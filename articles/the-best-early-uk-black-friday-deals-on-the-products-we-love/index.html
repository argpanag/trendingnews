Retailers aren’t rolling out their Black Friday discounts from the kindness of their hearts. It’s business. But if you can resist the urge to impulse-buy anything with a red ticket, and instead focus your attention on genuinely discounted items that you genuinely want, then it’s a potentially big win for you, too.

The whole shebang lasts a fortnight these days, but it’s not quite in full swing yet. At the time of writing, the actual Friday in question (27 November) is just over a week away, and many of the best deals haven’t landed yet. Plenty of brands have been at it for days, though, with John Lewis, Boots and, inevitably, Amazon launching their Black Friday sales when you could still smell fireworks in the air.

This is the third time we’ve rounded up Black Friday deals at the Filter, and a few items definitely seem to catch your eye year after year. A sonic toothbrush and classic Stanley flask are eternally popular, perhaps because they make good Christmas presents. At the hotter end of the price scale, a premium espresso machine, gym-quality treadmill and hybrid mattress are among your enduring Black Friday top dogs, thanks to the hundreds wiped off their prices.

Let’s see if this year’s newcomers, from weighted blankets to heated airers, can become your new Black Friday favourites. We’ll keep this page updated as more deals appear over the next 10 days, so drop in again soon.

How we selected these Black Friday deals (and excluded others)

Rather than just reprint whatever deals the retailers are pushing, we start by going back to our (and your) favourite products of the last year. Then we look – everywhere – to see if they have genuinely good Black Friday deals.

This often gives us a chance to re-recommend products we’ve rated highly, while wishing they weren’t so hair-raisingly pricey. “It’s so expensive,” wrote our best hair dryers tester Sarah Matthews about the Hershesons Great hair dryer. “The SmartSleep has a hefty price,” said Pete Wise of his favourite sunrise alarm for late-night reading. “Might feel like an investment too far,” mourned Lise Smith of the Dyson Airwrap Co-anda 2x multi-styler in her review. All are now discounted, and you’ll find their details below.

We always make sure that the discounts are real and noteworthy, too. A crossed-out RRP isn’t enough. We research every product’s price history using tools such as the Camelizer (very efficient but Amazon-only) and PriceRunner (all retailers) to ensure these price cuts are rare enough to recommend. In markets that have received short shrift for misleading sales tactics, such as mattresses, we’ve been careful to feature significant deals only.
