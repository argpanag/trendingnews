'Big jolt': Whanganui rattled by afternoon earthquake

TheTools · 26.10.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A GeoNet map shows the location of the magnitude 4.0 earthquake, which struck 30km south of Whanganui on Monday.

A magnitude 4.0 earthquake has struck near Whanganui this afternoon, with hundreds reporting feeling the shaking.

The quake hit at 3.33pm, about 30km south of Whanganui, at a depth of 12km, according to GeoNet.

The shaking was classed as light, although some residents reported feeling stronger tremors.

Within minutes, 853 people had submitted reports to GeoNet, including 30 who described the shaking as moderate, three as strong and one as severe.

Locals raced to social media to describe the shake, with several comparing the rumbling to a passing heavy truck.

One person said they heard the quake approaching before feeling a sudden jolt.

“Heard it coming, thought it was the wind then bang!” they wrote on Facebook.

Another described hearing “a bang, then a rumble then the shake”, while a resident in Aramoho reported a “big jolt”.

One resident said they were woken from a nap and initially thought the movement was their cat at the end of the bed.
