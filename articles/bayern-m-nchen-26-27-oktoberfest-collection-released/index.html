Bayern München 26-27 Oktoberfest Collection Released

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:27 · source · permalink

Alongside the official unveiling of their 2026-27 Oktoberfest kit, Adidas and Bayern Munich have released an extensive accompanying lifestyle collection. Designed to celebrate Munich’s world-famous folk festival, the new capsule merges Bavarian tradition with modern streetwear aesthetics.

The new Adidas Bayern München 26-27 Wiesn collection embraces a nostalgic retro aesthetic, highlighted by the Trefoil logo and an intricately designed Oktoberfest version of the club crest.

The centerpiece of the visual identity is the vivid, kaleidoscopic artwork introduced on the 26-27 Oktoberfest match jersey.

This multi-colored festival pattern is seamlessly integrated across several casual pieces, decorating the entire body of a standout knit cardigan and appearing as a framed graphic on the back of an off-white lifestyle t-shirt.

Alongside the official match kit, the collection includes the graphic back-print t-shirt, the full-pattern cardigan, a dark button-up jacket with classic stand-up collar detailing, traditional leather trousers, matching black track pants adorned with the Wiesn crest, and ribbed knitted socks finished with embroidered accents.

Following recent discussions regarding clubs using generative software for commercial rollouts, Bayern Munich took a noteworthy route for their digital catalog. The club's media team used AI to generate lookbook imagery of models wearing several pieces from the collection.

However, rather than presenting synthetic pictures as authentic photography, Bayern maintained complete transparency with consumers by distinctly labeling each artificial render with an "AI Generated" tag in the lower corner.

The full Adidas Bayern München 2026-27 Oktoberfest collection is officially available for purchase starting today, September 14.

Manchester United striker Matheus Cunha was spotted sporting an unusual kit detail during Sunday's Manchester derby against Manchester City. Eagle-eyed kit enthusiasts quickly pointed out that the Brazilian forward took to the pitch with his sleeve badges completely reversed on his 26-27 home shirt.

Under standard Premier League kit regulations, the official competition badge and "No Room For Racism" patch must be applied to the player's right sleeve, while the left sleeve is reserved for the club's commercial sleeve partner. On Cunha's long-sleeve jersey, however, the configuration was flipped: the sponsor logo was applied to his right arm, leaving the Premier League badge positioned on his left sleeve.

Side-by-side images comparing Cunha's jersey to teammate Marcus Rashford clearly showed the contrast between the standard layout and Cunha's swapped sleeves. Whether caused by a mix-up in the kit room or an accidental printing error during match preparations, the subtle mistake provided a unique talking point for shirt collectors.

Official images of the River Plate 26-27 away pre-match kit have surfaced, revealing a clean retro-inspired look produced by Adidas. Following the release of the club's tricolor away strip in August, this warm-up top expands River Plate's heritage-driven collection for the 26-27 season. Big thanks to @KamilBerberler.

The Adidas River Plate 26-27 away pre-match jersey features a crisp white body paired with bold red raglan sleeves and a matching white collar. The new River Plate 26-27 away pre-match jersey is scheduled to be worn by the squad during warm-ups for away fixtures and will be available to purchase in the coming weeks.
