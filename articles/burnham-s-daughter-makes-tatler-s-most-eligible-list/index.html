Burnham’s daughter makes Tatler’s most eligible list

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

The 24-year-old is described as Downing Street’s ‘diamond of the season’ and works as a Jägermeister executive in London

Andy Burnham’s daughter has been touted as one of high society’s most eligible young people.

In Tatler’s yearly assessment of prominent society figures, the 24-year-old was described as Downing Street’s “diamond of the season”.

Others on the Tatler list of the UK’s most “eligible” bachelors and bachelorettes include Jude Bellingham’s younger brother; a sprinting European gold medallist; and an 18-year-old lord.

Describing Rosie Burnham, 24, as a “northern lass”, Ines Jeveons, a Tatler writer, ranked the Prime Minister’s middle child as fourth on the magazine’s eligibility list.

She wrote: “The latest need-to-know from Westminster? Rosie Burnham, 24 – Downing Street’s diamond of the season and Prime Minister Andy Burnham’s eldest daughter.

“With her father newly installed in No 10, this northern lass is making waves of her own in the Big Smoke as a Jägermeister executive.

“A former netball captain and criminology graduate, she knows her way around both a court and a case. Good luck pleading yours.”

Camilla Tominey

Ms Burnham is the middle child of Mr Burnham and Marie-France van Heel’s three children. They also have a son, Jimmy Burnham, 26, and youngest Anne-Marie Burnham.

Mrs van Heel, often known as Frankie, is chief strategy officer at campaign agency Heavenly, where Ms Burnham is understood to have previously interned.

Until he returned to Parliament, Mr Burnham regularly posted social-media images of the family on dog walks and at Christmas.

She joined “young lords, gilded Gen Z heiresses, athletes in the ascendent and perennially partying It girls” on the list, according to Tatler.

Jobe Bellingham, the 21-year-old brother of England footballer Jude Bellingham, ranked second on the list.

Described as “the other half of football’s most glamorous sibling act”, the midfielder was put forward by Tatler as a possible future England player.

Henry Pollock, the rugby union player, 21, and Valentina Pinault, 19, the daughter of actress Salma Hayek and François-Henri Pinault, shared the number one spot.

It is the second year in a row Mr Pollock, who was described as “the Gen Z answer to David Beckham”, has appeared in the Black Book.

Moses Martin, the 20-year-old son of Gwyneth Paltrow and Chris Martin, the Coldplay frontman,, was ranked the ninth most “eligible” bachelor or bachelorette in the UK.

Isaac Goldsmith, the 18-year-old nephew of Zac Goldsmith, joined the list – the year after the former Tory MP’s daughter Thyra Goldsmith placed second on the annual ranking.

They were joined this year by Tunku Nadya Khyra, a member of the Malay Peninsula’s Royal family, and 18-year-old Lord Caspar Spencer-Churchill, who was described as a “gallant in the making”.
