EA FC 27 licenses – every league coming to the new game as 35+ leagues confirmed - AOL

TheTools · 13.09.2026 11:20 · source · permalink

EA Sports has unveiled the vast number of licensed leagues and competitions that will be playable in EA FC 27. More than 35 major leagues are included in this year's game, which officially launches on Friday, September 25.

There are more than 21,000 players from over 800 clubs and national teams in the game. Continuing the EA Sports tradition of holding more football licenses than other games, it'd be a struggle to find a title that challenges EA FC 27 for authenticity.

The sheer scale of licensed players is even bigger than in EA FC 26, when more than 20,000 were included. There are also more clubs and national teams and another 20 authentic stadiums - although the new additions are yet to be announced.

Players keen to get going in this unrivalled world of football immersion can play seven days early by pre-ordering the Ultimate Edition, which includes a handful of other bonuses besides early access. Those who bought the Ultimate Plus Edition - which stopped being available after August - will also get early access.

Furthermore, a host of continental competitions from Europe and South America are playable in-game. The UEFA Champions League, Europa League and Conference League are all back, as is Copa Libertadores.
