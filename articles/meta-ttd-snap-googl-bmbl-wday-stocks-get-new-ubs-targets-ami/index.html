UBS raised its price target on Meta to $884 from $715, citing opportunities to monetize AI-powered Business Agents and Muse.

The firm cut its Trade Desk target to $15 from $16, pointing to weaker ad budget allocation and competition from Amazon for advertiser spending.

It raised its price target on Snap to $7 but maintained a 'Neutral' rating, saying the platform has yet to benefit meaningfully from an improving digital advertising environment.

UBS on Wednesday revised its price targets on six stocks spanning digital advertising, pointing to artificial intelligence (AI) monetization opportunities at Meta Platforms, intensifying competition for The Trade Desk (TTD) and uncertainty over Workday's (WDAY) longer-term growth prospects.

The firm's latest assessments flagged the uneven recovery in digital advertising. While UBS sees opportunities for Meta to generate additional revenue through AI-powered tools and messaging platforms, it said advertisers were still not prioritizing Snap (SNAP) despite an improving industry backdrop.

Snap Struggles To Win Ad Dollars

UBS raised its price target on Snap to $7 from $5.70 but maintained a 'Neutral' rating. The firm said advertisers were still not prioritizing the social media platform, even as the broader digital advertising environment improves.

SNAP stock slipped 0.15% in premarket trading. Retail sentiment on Stocktwits shifted to 'extremely bullish' from 'bullish' territory over the past day. The stock has been on the rise for the last six sessions.

On Meta, UBS raised its price target to $884 from $715, citing opportunities to develop additional AI-driven revenue streams. The firm sees the rollout of Business Agents and increasing use of Muse supporting monetization across WhatsApp and Messenger, alongside potential gains in search advertising share.
