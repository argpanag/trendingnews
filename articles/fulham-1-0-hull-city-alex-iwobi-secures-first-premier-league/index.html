    FT: Fulham 1-0 Hull City

    Fulham

    Fulham manager Alvaro Arbeloa speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "I am very happy, especially for the supporters, they deserve this win, for the players, they were looking for this victory and have been working so hard. We deserved it in other matches, but we couldn't get it; today was finally the day.

    "We were having the same feeling as other matches, when we were controlling the game for long spells and having good chances but didn't score. Our rivals punish our mistakes or take their chances, but today we were brilliant for 90 minutes. We controlled the game, had many chances and didn't concede any clear chances to Hull, a very good team. They are tough to beat. We need to keep going and keep improving.

    "We have so many players with talent who can score. They will have chances. Players from the bench will be so important for us too. We have many chances and we need to try and take them,

    On Ryan Sessegnon's red card: "He was unlucky, he wanted to get the ball. I didn't see [the replay], maybe it was a clear red card. Everyone knows Ryan, he didn't want to do this challenge. It's unlikely for the Hull player; we are sorry for him."

    On using this first win as a springboard: "It's a massive game for us, a massive week because we have Coventry, then Palace in seven days. It won't be easy, but we know how we need to play and compete, we have to be competitive in this league. We have our way and hope we can in many games."

Alex Iwobi scored his first Premier League goal of the season for Fulham against Hull

Fulham earned their first Premier League win of the season and eased the scrutiny on Alvaro Arbeloa with a controlled victory over Hull City.

The Cottagers had been winless in their previous six league matches but secured the points thanks to Alex Iwobi's 33rd-minute strike.

The result offered some respite for head coach Arbeloa, who succeeded Marco Silva in the Craven Cottage hot seat in July but had not seen his side's promising displays translate into wins.

The result lifted Fulham's points tally to five and moved them off the foot of the table and out of the relegation zone, while Hull sit ninth.

Arbeloa's side took the lead just after the half-hour mark in the 100th meeting between the sides in all competitions.

Hull twice failed to clear their lines, allowing Sander Berge to slide the ball into the path of Iwobi on the edge of the box.

Iwobi curled a low shot that took a deflection off Lucas Herrington and squirmed under Hull goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis, who perhaps should have done better.

Hull struggled to create much of note, with Mohamed Belloumi dragging a shot wide of the post in the second half with arguably their best effort.

The only negative for Fulham was the dismissal of Ryan Sessegnon in added time after his studs caught the back of Robinio Vaz's calf from behind.
