Google Releases Gemini App for Windows

TheTools · 10.09.2026 17:24 · source · permalink

Five months after its release on the Mac, Google has brought a desktop version of its Gemini app to Windows 11.

“The new Gemini app for Windows lets you access AI help with a simple keyboard shortcut, directly from your desktop,” Google director of product management Erin Pettigrew writes. “Designed to work seamlessly alongside your favorite tools and daily applications, the new desktop app gives you instant assistance without breaking your flow.”
