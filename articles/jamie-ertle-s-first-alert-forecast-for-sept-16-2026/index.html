Jamie Ertle’s First Alert Forecast for Sept. 16, 2026

TheTools · 16.09.2026 23:51 · source · permalink

SAVANNAH, Ga. (WTOC) - Drier air is here under High pressure that’s dominating the Eastern Seaboard. There is a High Risk of rip currents for the Georgia beaches through this evening. We’ll see and feel temperatures closer to normal for this time of year with dry conditions through Friday. Rain chances will gradually increase across the Coastal Empire and Lowcountry through this weekend.

Thursday: 69/88 mostly sunny, 10% chance of a coastal shower south of I-16

Friday: 69/91 mostly sunny, 10% chance of a coastal shower south of I-16

This weekend, our weather pattern will begin to get a little messy. A trough of low pressure will develop and meander around the Gulf and Florida. It’ll increase our moisture, which will enhance sea breeze showers and storms.

Saturday: 71/90, mostly sunny with a 30% chance of late afternoon/evening showers and storms

Sunday: 72/88, mix of sun and clouds, 50% chance of afternoon showers and storms

Monday: 72/88, 60% chance of showers and storms

Tuesday, First Day of Fall: 73/88, 60% chance of showers and storms

Wednesday: 73/88, 60% chance of showers and storms

RIP CURRENTS: There is a moderate risk of rip currents for Thursday along our Georgia beaches, with a low risk of rip currents for our South Carolina beaches.

Stay Safe!

~JErtle
