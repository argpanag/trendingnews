Fantasy Football Week 5 Rankings: PPR Leagues | PFF

TheTools · 06.10.2026 19:05 · source · permalink
  • Michael Wilson is a must-start wide receiver: The Arizona Cardinals‘ top wide receiver has seen 30 targets over the last two weeks. In Week 5, he will face a Detroit Lions defense that has allowed the second-most fantasy points to wide receivers this season.
  • Kyren Williams is a top running back option: Williams has dominated playing time in competitive games despite the presence of Blake Corum. He's set to face the Buffalo Bills in Week 5, which will be a competitive game, and the Bills have allowed the fifth-most fantasy points to running backs this season.
  •  Most accurate rankings: Nathan Jahnke’s fantasy rankings have been the most accurate in-season per FantasyPros‘ rankings accuracy competition in each of the last six seasons among those who rank quarterbacks, running backs, wide receivers, tight ends, kickers, team defense and IDP.
