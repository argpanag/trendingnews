Avril Lavigne reveals she welcomed baby in surprise announcement

TheTools · 13.11.2026 21:21 · source · permalink

Avril Lavigne might have a lullaby album in her future.

The Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter surprised fans on social media when she revealed that she welcomed her first child, a daughter, in an Instagram post Friday, Nov. 13. Lavigne, 42, had not previously disclosed that she was expecting a child.

The pop punk songstress shared a trio of black-and-white photos of her baby girl, including a close-up shot of Lavigne and her partner holding onto the newborn's hand. The singer did not share the baby's name or date of birth in the post.

"Our mini ... princess has arrived 🎀👑🍼🩷," Lavigne wrote, adding in a cheeky reference to one of the lyrics from her 2007 hit "Girlfriend."

Another photo from Lavigne's post showed the singer cradling her daughter, who was dressed in a cheetah print onesie and matching headband.

And in a nod to her skate punk roots, Lavigne shared a playful snap of a skateboard stacked with three pairs of Vans sneakers. The first two pairs of checkered, black-and-white shoes were adult-sized, while the final pair were baby sneakers.

Jamie Foxx and Alyce Huckstepp welcome their first baby together

After teasing the news in a trailer for his album "QRÖMELIFE," Quavo confirmed in a Complex interview posted Sept. 29 that he's now a "proud" dad, having welcomed a daughter.

"It's a blessing that God gave me," the Migos rapper said. "It's a whole new state [of being]. It brings joy to me. It brings a new reason why I'm doing this. It brings a new hunger, a new fire, to me."

Jesse Williams got creative in alluding to himself and Alejandra Onieva welcoming their first child together. In a Sept. 28 Instagram carousel, the "Grey's Anatomy" alumnus reenacted a scene from Spike Lee’s "Do the Right Thing" as he reached into the fridge for a baby bottle while demanding that his Spanish partner speak English in front of their child.

Williams shares two children with his ex-wife, Aryn Drake-Lee.

Taylor Lautner, left, and wife Tay Lautner have welcomed their first child.

The “Twilight” star, 34, and the new mom, 29, revealed the arrival of their daughter, Lennon Taylor Lautner, in a joint Instagram post Sept. 23. The couple, who announced they were expecting in March, said Lennon was born Sept. 16.

“One week loving our sweet little Lenny🤍 Lennon Taylor Lautner🎀 9.16.2026,” they captioned the post.

Millie Bobby Brown and Jake Bongiovi welcomed their second child via adoption, a source confirmed to People. The news comes just over a year after the lovebirds, who married in May 2024, announced in August 2025 that they had adopted a baby girl.

“This summer we welcomed our sweet baby girl through adoption," the "Stranger Things" actress wrote on Instagram at the time. "We are beyond excited to embark on this beautiful next chapter of parenthood in both peace and privacy. And then there were 3.”

Lena Dunham and her husband, Luis Felber, are now a family of three. The couple, who have been married since 2021, welcomed a baby girl via surrogate, the actress revealed in a Vogue op-ed on Sept. 16.

The "Girls" creator recounted the emotional surrogacy journey, which came after 12 years of fertility issues. She recalled how she greeted her new baby: "You look nothing like I thought you would, but exactly right."

Sean Ono Lennon has his own "little darling" now.

The Grammy-winning musician, son of Beatles rocker John Lennon and singer-songwriter Yoko Ono, revealed he welcomed his first child with partner Charlotte Kemp Muhl in a Sept. 1 social media post. The birth also marks the first grandchild for the late John Lennon, who was fatally shot in 1980.

Diplo has let the cat out of the bag on his newest addition.

The Grammy-winning producer confirmed he recently welcomed a fourth child in an Instagram post Aug. 14. The post, which featured several photos from Diplo's family trip to Peru, included a lightly redacted photo of his baby girl.

"My daughter celebrated her first birthday with llamas wearing sunglasses," Diplo wrote. "[Son] Lazer couldn't find Peru on a map a week ago and walked out of the airport in a poncho and an alpaca hat looking like he'd been selling ayahuasca to backpackers. Watching your kid become someone new in real time is the whole thing."

"The McBee Dynasty: Real American Cowboys" stars Cole McBee and Kacie Adkinson welcomed their second child together.

The couple introduced their daughter, Tate Colette McBee, to the world in an Instagram post shared Aug. 5.

"Blair is the best big sister! We are so thankful for our two perfect, healthy girls!" they captioned the family photo.

Sebastian Stan and Annabelle Wallis are reportedly first-time parents. The "Captain America: The Winter Soldier" star and "Annabelle" actress welcomed a baby in July, TMZ and People reported Aug. 3. Representatives for the couple did not respond to USA TODAY's request for comment.
