Jemimah Rodrigues joins Hero MotoCorp and VIDA as brand ambassador

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:55 · source · permalink

The cricketer joins Smriti Mandhana, extending the brands’ association with Indian women’s cricket.

Hero MotoCorp and its mobility brand VIDA have appointed Indian cricketer Jemimah Rodrigues as their brand ambassador. The association follows the signing of Indian women’s cricket team captain Smriti Mandhana in July this year.

The partnership brings together the mobility brand and Rodrigues, who was part of India’s 2025 ICC Women’s Cricket World Cup-winning campaign. Rodrigues began her cricketing journey at an early age, joining the Mumbai Under-19 state team at 13.

With this appointment, Hero MotoCorp and VIDA continue their association with women’s cricket and sporting talent.

Speaking on the association, Sanjay Bhan, executive vice president, Hero MotoCorp, said: "At Hero MotoCorp, our brand philosophy is centered on empowering young talent, embracing dynamic performance, and connecting with the aspirations of young India. Jemimah Rodrigues embodies these attributes effortlessly. Having recently welcomed Indian captain Smriti Mandhana to the Hero fold, bringing Jemimah on board further cements our commitment to championing excellence and leadership in women’s sports. On the field, she is a fearless competitor with nerves of steel - evident in her historic, match-winning performances on the world stage. Off the field, her vibrant energy, genuine passion, and positive outlook connect seamlessly with the youth."

Himanshu Sharma, Head of Sales, JSW Sports, said: “We are happy to welcome a legacy brand like Hero MotoCorp to Jemimah Rodrigues’ growing brand portfolio as she continues to inspire a new generation of young athletes through her performances on and off the field. Partnerships like these reflect the growing opportunities for women in sport to build strong connections with leading brands and extend their influence beyond sport. It is encouraging to see Jemimah’s journey resonate with brands that share her values, and we look forward to seeing this association grow.”

As brand ambassador, Jemimah will represent Hero MotoCorp across upcoming multimedia brand campaigns, product launches, and grassroots youth engagement initiatives.
