Slate: Bearcats Gameday!

TheTools · 26.09.2026 14:21 · source · permalink
  • K-State Slate

Kansas State opens the Big 12 schedule with a night game at Cincinnati.

Kansas State (3-0, 0-0 Big 12) plays Cincinnati (3-0, 0-0 Big 12) tonight at Nippert Stadium. Kickoff is scheduled for 6 PM and the game will air on ESPN2. Full details are available in the How To Watch post.
The Big 12 opener for both teams is also the Wildcats’ first road game of the season and the first challenge against a Power 4 team. Should Kansas State win this game (the Wildcats are -6.5 favorites), it will be the program’s 4-0 start since 2012, when Collin Klein led the team on the field. Now he’s walking the sidelines and knows that “the margins are thin” in every game.

K-State’s 3-0 record so far was earned via a balanced offense and some stout defense. But the big scores and unblemished record are also due in part to a softer schedule that may not have prepared the team for the rigors of the Big 12 schedule. Whatever the outcome, this game will go far in answering some questions about the 2026 Wildcats.

For its part, Cincinnati has looked solid three weeks into the season, but their outing against Miami (OH) nearly ended in disaster. The Bearcats clawed their way back to victory from a 28-7 deficit, but their running game is statistically in the Top 10, and given the Wildcats’ struggle against the run last week, things could go awry if K-State is not solid in that department.

The Wildcats are a little thin on offense right now, especially with backup quarterbacks Jacob Knuth and Dillon Duff listed as questionable. Hopefully, nobody will need to step into Kody Cook role in this game.

In non-game news, a couple of Wildcats are getting early recognition on awards list. Safety Wesley Fair has been named a semifinalist for the William V. Campbell Trophy, which recognizes the best scholar-athlete in football. Meanwhile, punter Simon McClannan was named to the Ray’s 8 list for the eight best punting performances of the week. This qualifies him to be a Ray Guy Punter of the Week.

Since Kansas State does not take the field until later this evening, consider this your early game watch/commentary post.

Go Cats!
