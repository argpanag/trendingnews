JTBC, a comprehensive programming channel that is undergoing corporate rehabilitation procedures, will confirm the Hanyoung accounting firm as the organizer of the sale and begin the sale in earnest.

JTBC said on the 14th, "As a court investigator, the Korean-English accounting firm has already grasped the current status and major businesses of JTBC, so we expect to be able to proceed with mergers and acquisitions (M&A) quickly in the future," adding, "We will announce the sale within this month with the aim of selling the rehabilitation plan before approval."

Hanyoung Accounting Corp. cited JTBC's content production capabilities, intellectual property rights (IP), and competitiveness in the news sector as its major selling strengths. "JTBC is a large-scale sale with high brand value, including drama and entertainment content production capabilities, IP (intellectual property) assets, and news ratings of No. 1 general programming," said Han Young, an accounting firm. "Multiple original buyers have shown interest before the sale process, and market interest is expected to increase further when the process begins."

Earlier, JTBC fell into default in June when it failed to repay 20.6 billion won worth of floating loans. The deficit widened due to the financial burden of the Olympic and World Cup broadcasting rights coupled with the contraction of the TV advertising market.

Starting with the announcement of the sale in October, JTBC will proceed with procedures such as receiving letters of intent to acquire, due diligence, and bidding. After that, it plans to select preferred negotiators and complete M&A before approval of the rehabilitation plan through court procedures related to this contract.
