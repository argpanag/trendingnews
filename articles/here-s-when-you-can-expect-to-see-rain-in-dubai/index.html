Here's when you can expect to see rain in Dubai

TheTools · 07.10.2026 16:05 · source · permalink

Fog, rain, and humidity with just a touch of the occasional cool breeze – here’s what you can expect from Dubai’s weather this week

We’re in this awkward in-between phase of seasons, where it gets harder to decipher whether or not the weather is heading towards a cooler season. But from certified Dubai residents (like ourselves), we can guarantee that this is totally normal. It’s just the weather going through an angsty phase before the winter season makes its annual reappearance. But the question still persists: when will it rain in Dubai?

Here’s what the industry pros have to say about witnessing rain in Dubai

The National Centre of Meteorology  (NMC) predicted that the region is due to see some form of rainfall in the coming weeks.

While yes, temperatures and humidity is also set to rise overnight and well into the next day – on Thursday, October 8; we have hope for showers across the emirate. This might raise a few eyebrows on, ‘How can we expect rains when humidity and heat are still on the rise?’

The answer is quite simple. This phenomenon is possible due to a condition called convective clouds. This is when clouds are formed through warm, less dense air rising through cooler surrounding air and condensing or turning into liquid from air.

Back to our rain forecast in Dubai – while there isn’t an immediate forecast for the week, there is a slight chance you might see drizzles in some parts of the city. Now beware, because some coastal and inland areas are forecasted to experience fog and mist, which inherently reduces visibility for everyone – especially for those out on the road.

Caution for when you’re driving 

A caution to keep in mind whilst driving is to maintain safe distances, have your hazard lights on when necessary and drive with utmost caution to avoid any possibilities of accidents occurring. Speeding is something you’ll definitely have to keep a check on. Highly recommended for those who prefer not to be stuck behind an endless sea of traffic.

So, does this mean winter is close by?

While we are currently in the precipice of heading towards a cooler season, with noticeably breezier evenings and pleasant mornings, winter might still be a tad further than we can hope for.

We can expect to see the full force of winter in effect starting December 21, but till then we can still enjoy the gradual shift from hot afternoons to cooler and breezier days. After all, we are all looking forward to heading outdoors for the cooler season.
