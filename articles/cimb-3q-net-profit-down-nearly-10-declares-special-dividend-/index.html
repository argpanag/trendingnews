CIMB 3Q net profit down nearly 10%, declares special dividend as return on equity stays on track

TheTools · 25.11.2026 06:28 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 25): CIMB Group Holdings Bhd (KL:CIMB) declared a special dividend as the return on equity at the second-largest Malaysian bank by asset stays on track with its guidance.

The special dividend, involving RM596 million or 5.50 sen per share, will be paid on Dec 24, a bourse filing showed. Net profit for the three months ended Sept 30 (3QFY2026) was RM1.88 billion, down nearly 10% from the same quarter a year earlier, as both interest and non-interest income declined.

“We remain committed to disciplined growth amid macroeconomic uncertainties,” said Novan Amirudin, chief executive of CIMB. “We will continue to sharpen our portfolio, reallocate capital into higher-return opportunities and value-accretive growth.”

The dividend forms part of its multi-year commitment announced in November 2025 to return up to RM2 billion in excess capital to shareholders by the end of 2027. Including the latest, total dividends declared to-date have reached RM2.72 billion or 25.15 sen per share.

Shrinking net interest margins, the difference between what banks earn on loans and pay on deposits, have weighed on interest income while non-interest income was affected by weaker fees and trading income.

Still, the return on equity came up to 11% in the first nine months, within the bank's guidance of 11.0% to 11.5% for the full year. Net profit fell 3.5% year-on-year to RM5.73 billion. Apart from lower income, earnings were also hit by 19% rise in provisions and lower wholesale banking write-backs.

“We continue to invest and accelerate our four growth engines, which are profitable balance sheet growth, wealth, treasury client sales and fees, to sustain earnings momentum,” Novan remarked.

Common Equity Tier 1, the highest grade of regulatory capital, was 14.0% post-dividend — a level generally considered strong by analysts.

Shares of CIMB were up six sen or 0.85% at RM7.15 at the midday trading break on Wednesday, valuing CIMB at about RM77 billion ahead of the results and dividend announcement.
