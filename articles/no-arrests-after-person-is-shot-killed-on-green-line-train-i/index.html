No arrests after person is shot, killed on Green Line train in Minneapolis

TheTools · 04.10.2026 02:45 · source · permalink

One person was killed Saturday night in a shooting aboard a light rail train in southeast Minneapolis, police said.

Sgt. Garrett Parten, a spokesman for the Minneapolis Police Department, said an adult was shot on an eastbound Green Line train traveling from the Stadium Village station around 9 p.m.

5 EYEWITNESS NEWS captured the large police response at Westgate Station on University Avenue, where the train came to a halt after the shooting.

Medics performed first aid on the victim, but they were pronounced dead at the scene. Police have not released any information about the victim’s identity.

“I’d seen them giving CPR. I just walked away and started praying for the person, whoever it was,” said Melissa Martin, who was on the train that night.

Parten said the gunman left the train shortly after the shooting, and no arrests have been made. Minneapolis police also said investigators are still working to find out why the shooting happened.

Metro Transit said, “Riders will see an increased safety presence today, including additional MTPD officers at stations and on trains.”

“But it was a little more, definitely more fare checkers and security than a normal Green Line,” said Kyle Anderson, who rode the Green Line after the Twin Cities Marathon.

David Stiggers, president of Amalgamated Transit Union Local 1005, said he has been impressed by recent safety steps including increased patrols.

“I’d say this is an anomaly,” said Stiggers. “We don’t necessarily see this happening all of the time, so I would say we are a pretty safe transit system, but any loss of life is just too much.”

This was the fifth homicide on a transit line since 2021, according to Metro Transit.

The Green Line is set for expansion next year, and Stiggers said more security and more collaboration between Metro Transit and local police departments would help.

“I think the more presence that we have on our vehicles, the less chances are that actions like this would happen,” said Stiggers.

Metro Transit provided the following statement:

“Our hearts are with everyone affected by this senseless loss. Gun violence has no place on our transit system or in the communities we serve. Incidents like this are rare, but one is too many, and this fatality reinforces why our work to strengthen safety on transit is relentless and ongoing.

“We are working closely with our public safety partners to address the larger societal issues that show up on transit, because every rider deserves a safe trip. Riders will see an increased safety presence today, including additional MTPD officers at stations and on trains.

“With the skilled officers, advanced technology, and specialized training at our disposal, we are confident this suspect will be found and arrested. Let this be clear: if you commit a crime on our transit system, you will be identified, arrested, and held accountable.“
