Emma Willis' Radio 2 replacement confirmed as Strictly star takes over BBC show

TheTools · 19.09.2026 13:50 · source · permalink

It Takes Two host Ellie Taylor will also take over from Emma Willis on Radio 2(Image: Karla Gowlett/BBC/PA Wire)

Ellie Taylor will replace Emma Willis on her BBC Radio 2 show while Emma presents the new series of Strictly.

The 42-year-old comic was a contestant on the main show in 2022 and is also set to host Strictly Come Dancing spin-off It Takes Two. Ellie will present her first Radio 2 Saturday lunchtime show on September 26, when the first live show of this year's series of Strictly airs.

Taylor has presented on Radio 2 before, hosting its drivetime show. She said: "I cannot wait to be back at Radio 2 and am thrilled to be looking after Emma's Saturday show while she waltzes off to Strictly.

Emma is taking a break from Radio 2 as she's hosting Strictly with Josh and Johannes(Image: RAY BURMISTON/BBC)

"I always have so much fun with the Radio 2 listeners, who can expect plenty of great tunes, guests and surprises from me, but most importantly a lot of fun."

Willis hosted her final Radio 2 show (from 1pm to 3pm) on Saturday (September 19), where she was joined by Strictly judge Anton Du Beke.

Helen Thomas, head of Radio 2, said: "I'm so pleased Ellie will be joining us throughout the autumn and winter – it is always such a joy to hear her on Radio 2. Her warmth and laughter will be a great addition to our superstar Saturday line up, whilst Emma sashays over to the Strictly ballroom."

Ellie competed on Strictly and danced with Johannes(Image: BBC/Guy Levy)

Taylor will host her first episode of It Takes Two on Monday September 28. She will replace Janette Manrara and Fleur East as the presenter of the BBC Two show, with Manrara having confirmed her departure in June and East reportedly leaving the programme around the same time.

Willis will present her first Strictly main show alongside fellow new hosts Josh Widdecombe and Johannes Radebe on Saturday night.

The new main show presenters will replace Tess Daly and Claudia Winkleman, who announced they were leaving the show late last year.

Taylor was eliminated eighth in Strictly in 2022, when she was partnered by Radebe when he was one of the show's professional dancers.
