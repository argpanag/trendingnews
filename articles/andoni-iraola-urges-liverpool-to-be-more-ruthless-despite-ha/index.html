Andoni Iraola urges Liverpool to be more “ruthless” despite hammering Manchester United

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:57 · source · permalink

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola shared his reaction to their big 4-0 win against rivals Manchester United on Sunday, and the Spaniard was absolutely delighted with a statement win.

After Leny Yoro’s early red card Liverpool smelt blood in the water and scored twice right away and then finished it off in the second half with ease. They could have won by six or seven and didn’t get out of second gear.

Here’s the latest Andoni Iraola reaction from Anfield, as Liverpool have now won four of their last five Premier League games and are on a roll.

On his celebrations at the full time whistle and the Jurgen Klopp style fist pumps: “Obviously I enjoyed it. I have to do a bit more! But I think we wanted to celebrate in that moment and I think it is always nice to enjoy with the supporters.”

On if he felt Liverpool need a big win over a rival like that: “It’s not us a team, even the supporters deserved it, no? Because I think we’ve been very good away this season but at home we haven’t been good enough, and still not good enough. A big game against United, to finish like this, it is true that the conditions because they play with 10 most of the game. But still we’ve done our job, we’ve controlled the game absolutely. I think it is always nice when you can win like this in this kind of games.”

On the red card decision and what he makes of it: “I think it is the last man and a clear foul and it is a red card, yes.”

On what he made of the attacking performance and that part of the team coming together: “I think you have to take it carefully. Today we created a lot of clear chances, we missed against the ‘keeper three or four times. I think we created probably to score more than four. We missed chances that in other games you are remembering it right now, no? It is a good part and the result is part that we need to be more ruthless. Luckily today it was not needed and I think we finished the game well.”

On Bradley Barcola and how good he will be when adding that finishing touch: “We have to encourage him to keep making those runs in-behind, he misses against the ‘keeper but we will work on this. He is always a threat and a lovely decision also the assist to Rio. He has missed a big chance, he has the chance to score but he has the control in the situation to offer Rio the goal and I was happy for him, yes.”

On Florian Wirtz being man of the match and his levels: “Yes, Flo is in a lovely moment. We have to keep him there as much as we can. It’s a situation where against 10 players you need players like him to control the situation, to see the passes in-behind. It’s good he is also arriving more, he’s not just scoring but being a threat and not only Alex he is becoming another option to finish those crosses. I am happy for him.”

One-v-one, how hard is it to stop Rio Nguomha: “If you can put him in situations where they cannot help the full back a lot, I think it is difficult to stop, especially inside the box because he can go both ways. I think it was a great game. Players like Rio, like Flo, are players that if you are controlling the game and have a lot of possession, they will hurt opposition.”

On what he said to his players at half time to try and get out of a bit of a lull: “Especially to score a third one. We needed to score a third one quickly because always with 2-0 you can stay there and then you arrive to the last minute and then there is a strange feeling in this kind of game. I think we need to go, attack, don’t give them any hope or chance of believing. I think we did well.”

On it being a good week all around for them if you look at the table and what this team can achieve: “I think it’s still early days. It has been a good game week. I think we are now probably missing the two big games we lost here at home against Arsenal and City are the ones that are making the difference in the standings. I think especially away we’ve had very good results but this is still so early. And now it doesn’t stop because we have a very difficult game now, Brugge away, super physical and it’s going to be difficult. Then we have the big derby at the weekend. It is still a massive week for us.”
