Following last week’s long list of launches, and the release of NVIDIA DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, this week sees even more DLSS games and updates released, delivering the definitive experience for GeForce RTX gamers.

This week, Honeycomb: The World Beyond has just launched, and will be followed by the eagerly awaited WARDOGS on September 10th. Also on the 10th, PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS, one of Steam’s most played games, adds support for DLSS Super Resolution.

Then, next week sees the release of Active Matter and Aniimo, which are both supported and optimized by our newly-released GeForce Game Ready Driver.

Additionally, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered launches September 29th, enhancing visuals and adding path tracing, DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction and Dynamic Multi Frame Generation. And a new trailer debuts the first footage from The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Songs of the Past, launching next year.

Frozen Way and Snail Games USA’s Honeycomb: The World Beyond is a survival-sandbox game set on Sota7, an exuberant planet. Explore its vast world and face challenges on your bioengineering journey. Step into an unknown world as Hennessy, a bioengineer on a mission. Sota7 looks beautiful and peaceful, but don't let it deceive you, it's full of dangers! Discover alien flora and fauna, decode their mysteries, and deploy your expertise as one of the many instruments in your survival toolkit. Persevere against all odds, and be prepared to outsmart or outrun any dangers you might encounter.

Honeycomb: The World Beyond is available now, and GeForce RTX gamers can enhance visuals and accelerate performance with our suite of technologies, for the best possible experience.

DLSS 4.5 Super Resolution and Dynamic Multi Frame Generation will enhance image quality and maximize frame rates, while players activating the game’s ray-traced effects, can enable superior NVIDIA RTX Direct Illumination (RTXDI), upgrading the fidelity and detail of light sources. The quality of lighting and other ray-traced effects can then be taken to even better heights by toggling on the recently-released DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction, delivering the definitive Honeycomb: The World Beyond experience.

To see if Honeycomb: The World Beyond is for you, download the demo on DLSS and RTX-enhanced demo on Steam, then continue your progress in the full game, if you decide to pick up the new survival and crafting game.

BULKHEAD and Team 17’s WARDOGS is an All-Out Warfare FPS that combines tactical gunplay and combined arms combat with building and destruction mechanics in a large open map, where one hundred players split across three teams fight over the control zone. The team with the most players within the Control Zone earns points, and the first team to reach 100 points wins the match. The variables in zone location and individual player tactics mean no match ever plays out the same.

Each life, you purchase a custom loadout from a wide selection of weapons, gear, utility, and vehicles to help your team win the match. Every teamplay action you perform rewards cash. From reviving squadmates, transporting friendlies to the zone, or controlling the objective. Cash persists from match to match, so spending big on better gear, equipment, or a vehicle at the right time has the potential to turn the tide of battle and could yield massive returns.

WARDOGS has wracked up over 1.5 million Wishlists, over 500,000 gamers played the August closed beta, and BULKHEAD has announced that the eagerly-anticipated shooter will officially enter Early Access on September 10th.

GeForce RTX gamers entering WARDOGS’ warzones will discover day-one support for DLSS 4.5 Super Resolution and Dynamic Multi Frame Generation, enhancing image quality and accelerating performance, for the best possible experience. And NVIDIA Reflex will optimize PC latency, ensuring your actions occur faster, potentially giving you a competitive advantage on the game’s massive battlefields.

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS is the high-stakes winner-take-all free-to-play shooter that started the Battle Royale craze. Drop into diverse maps, loot unique weapons and supplies, and survive in an ever-shrinking zone where every turn could be your last.

On September 10th, the perpetually-popular PUBG will add support for DLSS Super Resolution, which players can upgrade to our latest DLSS 4.5 models by using NVIDIA app’s DLSS overrides, accelerating performance and enhancing image quality in every match and mode.

NVIDIA has continuously redefined the boundaries of PC gaming. From introducing real-time ray tracing to pioneering AI-powered graphics with DLSS Super Resolution, Frame Generation and Ray Reconstruction, our RTX technologies have transformed how games are rendered and experienced.

Now, we’ve released DLSS 5, which introduces 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, a major step forward in using AI to bring real-time graphics closer than ever to Hollywood-grade photorealism.

CD PROJEKT RED’s The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, one of the best-selling and highest-rated role-playing games of all time, is being improved with a slew of upgrades and enhancements to visuals, performance, gameplay, and more through The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Remastered.

The partnership between NVIDIA and CD PROJEKT RED began when the game was originally released in 2015, before it was updated with even more features in 2022 through the Next-Gen Update. And on September 29th, NVIDIA will be delivering even more cutting-edge features through Remastered.

The free upgrade includes a reimagined skill system, overhauled animations, improved combat, Cross Platform Mod Support, immersive meditation, an expanded photo mode, and much more. When it comes to NVIDIA technology in particular, players can upgrade to path tracing, with line swept sphere (LSS) path-traced hair rendering enhancing the game’s famous NVIDIA HairWorks technology. And to ensure these new path-traced effects look their very best, DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction is also being introduced.

Gaijin Entertainment's Active Matter is a hardcore military online PvPvE, PvE and PvP shooter set in a multiverse shattered by a quantum catastrophe. Each player controls an operative stuck in a time loop, always brought back from the dead to the same point, and enters quantum unstable zones rich with active matter - a substance capable of warping and ripping the very fabric of space and time.

Rivals from other timelines, physics-breaking anomalies that change gravitational force, and ethereal beings stand in the way. Players harvest active matter, collect the loot and extract to a safe area before the whole zone ceases to exist with everything inside it.

Instead of navigating traditional menus, players will interact with their Aniimo using natural language. Chat with them, teach them new skills, and dispatch them to gather resources via voice inputs. Each Aniimo will display dynamic emotions based on player responses, fostering a more authentic and meaningful connection with the creatures that live and work alongside them.

The NVIDIA ACE integration is powered by a locally-hosted, fine-tuned Nemotron Small Language Model (SLM) running on a player’s GPU. This powers each Aniimo's intelligence, allowing them to interpret player intent and trigger in-game behavior APIs for realistic actions and reactions.
