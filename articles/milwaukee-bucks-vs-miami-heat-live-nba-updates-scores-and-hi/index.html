Milwaukee Bucks vs. Miami Heat Live NBA Updates, Scores and Highlights

In a game that the scoreboard suggests was a lot closer than it actually was, Giannis Antetokounmpo welcomed his former team to his new arena with an easy win for the Miami Heat.

Miami beat the Milwaukee Bucks, 137-130, behind 27 points, 13 rebounds and nine assists from Giannis and a season-high 22 threes from the rest of the roster.

The Bucks were led by Kyle Kuzma (26 points on 9-of-16 shooting), but Milwaukee never seriously threatened for a win at any point in this one.

Bucks Starters in Vice City

Outside of an effort to juice their trade value, it's hard to fathom reasons why Milwaukee is starting veterans like Kyle Kuzma and Jericho Sims over Jaquez and Ware.

On the bright side, Brayden Burries is where he should be now. The Bucks need to give him as much developmental runway as possible.

Miami Embracing the GTA Connection

Tonight isn't just Giannis' first game against the Bucks and Herro, Jaquez, Ware and Jakučionis' first game back in Miami, it's the last Miami home game before the much anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto 6.

With "Vice City" being modeled after Miami, embracing the connection was a no-brainer for the Heat organization.

The First Game Back

The Miami Heat gave up all of Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez, Kel'el Ware and Kasparas Jakučionis in the Giannis Antetokounmpo trade.

This is their first chance at a "revenge game." And while the 1-13 Bucks are almost certainly going to lose, at least a couple of them might be able to prove a point.

Herro and Jaquez lead the Bucks in wins over replacement player and points per game. And they're first and fourth, respectively, in assists per game.

Miami's Finding Its Groove

After their 3-6 start, there were plenty of fans and analysts ready to hit the panic button over the new-look Miami Heat, but they're finding their rhythm now.

The Heat enter this game on a five-game winning streak. And while it's fair to take the first two, against the Sacramento Kings and Utah Jazz, with a grain of salt, the rest of the run includes wins over the Cleveland Cavaliers, Detroit Pistons and Houston Rockets.

And during these five games, Miami has several players cooking. Giannis Antetokounmpo is averaging 30.0 points, 12.2 rebounds and 4.6 assists, Klay Thompson is adding 19.3 points and shooting 47.6 percent from three, Bam Adebayo is at 18.0 points, 7.2 rebounds and 3.4 assists and Davion Mitchell is putting up 17.8 points and 11.0 assists.

This team suddenly looks like the contender many expected it to be. And that's unlikely to change against Giannis' former team on Wednesday.

Bucks Are Reeling

Milwaukee has a handful of interesting young (or at least youngish) players on the roster, but they haven't jelled into anything resembling a winning basketball team.

They're one of two one-win teams left in the NBA this season, and they have a worse winning percentage than the other one (the Sacramento Kings).

And given their struggles, the sooner they can unload veterans like Kyle Kuzma and Myles Turner, and give even more time an opportunity to up-and-comers like Brayden Burries, the better.
