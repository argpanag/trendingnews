LDU Quito and Palmeiras will battle for a place in the 2026 Copa Libertadores semifinals on Wednesday, September 16. The Verdão travel to Ecuador with the advantage after securing a 1-0 victory in the first leg at Allianz Parque.

The second leg will be played at the Estadio Rodrigo Paz Delgado, where Quito’s altitude once again becomes one of LDU’s biggest weapons. The Ecuadorian side need to win by one goal to level the aggregate score and keep their hopes of advancing alive.

LDU Quito vs. Palmeiras will be available LIVE on beIN SPORTS in the United States, with coverage beginning at 5:50 p.m. ET.

How LDU Quito Enter the Match

LDU Quito returned from Brazil with a narrow defeat that leaves the quarterfinal very much alive. Following the first leg, Los Albos fielded a rotated lineup and earned a draw against Deportivo Cuenca in domestic competition.

Home-field advantage could significantly change the picture. LDU know how to maximize the conditions in Quito and have a recent result that should provide plenty of confidence: in October 2025, they defeated Palmeiras 3-0 at this same stadium in the first leg of their Libertadores semifinal.

One goal would level the aggregate score, making the opening stages particularly important. LDU will look to impose their intensity early without leaving themselves exposed against one of the most dangerous attacks in South America.


How Palmeiras Enter the Match

Palmeiras arrive in Ecuador with the cushion provided by their 1-0 victory in São Paulo, although the Brazilian side know there is little room for error with such a narrow advantage.

The Verdão are also coming off a victory over São Paulo in the Brasileirão, using much of their first-choice lineup. Last season’s Libertadores runners-up are once again chasing a place among the final four teams in the competition.

Andreas Pereira, Jhon Arias, Flaco López and Vitor Roque are among their biggest threats, while the absence of Gustavo Gómez represents an important loss in defense. Palmeiras must manage their advantage without allowing LDU’s pressure and Quito’s altitude to quickly transform the tie.


How And Where To Watch LDU Quito vs. Palmeiras

The second leg of the 2026 Copa Libertadores quarterfinal between LDU Quito and Palmeiras will be played on Wednesday, September 16 at the Estadio Rodrigo Paz Delgado in Quito, Ecuador.

Fans in the United States can watch LDU Quito vs. Palmeiras LIVE on beIN SPORTS, with coverage beginning at 5:50 p.m. ET.

Final Verdict

Palmeiras hold the advantage after their 1-0 victory in Brazil, but this quarterfinal remains wide open. LDU are only one goal away from leveling the aggregate score and will have both the altitude and their home crowd on their side.

The 2025 meeting also shows just how dangerous the Ecuadorian side can be at Casa Blanca: Palmeiras’ most recent visit to this stadium ended in a 3-0 defeat.

The Verdão have the advantage and a squad built to challenge for the trophy, but LDU have already shown they can hurt Palmeiras in Quito. The final 90 minutes will determine which side keeps its continental title hopes alive.
