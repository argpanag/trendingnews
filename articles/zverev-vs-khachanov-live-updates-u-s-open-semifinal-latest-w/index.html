U.S. Open men's semifinals — Latest

Alexander Zverev, top seed in the men's singles draw, is facing No. 50-ranked Karen Khachanov for a spot in the U.S. Open final today.

Zverev is leading Khachanov after winning the opening set 6-3 before taking the second set 7-6(7) via tiebreak. Both players reached the U.S. Open semifinals by winning their previous matches in straight sets.
