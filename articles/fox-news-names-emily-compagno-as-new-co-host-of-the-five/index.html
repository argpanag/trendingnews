Fox News Names Emily Compagno as New Co-Host of ‘The Five’

TheTools · 24.08.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Compagno had been a host on 'Outnumbered' and will join 'The Five' in September.

Emily Compagno has been named a full-time co-host on Fox News Channel’s The Five. 

Compagno, who has been the co-host of Outnumbered since 2021, joins The Five starting Sept. 8, alongside co-hosts Greg Gutfeld, Dana Perino, Jesse Watters, and Harold Ford Jr. and Jessica Tarlov, who rotate in the liberal co-host chair. In joining the program, Compagno will step down from her role on Outnumbered, a panel show which features four women and one male, and a rotating Fox News personality will fill the seat until a full-time co-host is named. 

Compagno first joined Fox News as a contributor in 2018. Prior to joining the network, Compagno practiced law in criminal defense and civil litigation and later served as a federal managing attorney and acting director at the Social Security Administration. 

The Five typically ranks as the most watched cable news program. The show, which has been on air for 15 years, uses a roundtable format in which the panel debates politics, pop culture and other current events.

“Fifteen years later, The Five remains a powerhouse and appointment viewing for millions of Americans. Emily is a highly accomplished host whose energy and talent have made her a standout across Fox News Media, and we are thrilled to officially welcome her to the show,” said Suzanne Scott, CEO of Fox News Media.

“I couldn’t be more honored to join The Five, a team I have long admired and have been fortunate to work alongside over the years. Having an official seat at the table on what I truly believe is the most fun show on television is an incredible privilege. I am grateful for the opportunity and can’t wait to get started,” Compagno said.
