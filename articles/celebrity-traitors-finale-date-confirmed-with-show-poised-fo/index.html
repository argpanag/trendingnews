  • The second series of Celebrity Traitors is in full swing with the fifth episode due to air on the BBC on Wednesday night at 8pm.
  • It has now been confirmed that the final of the 2026 series is due to be shown on Friday October 30 at 8pm on BBC One.
  • The final episode is scheduled to last 80 minutes, making it the longest episode in the show’s history so far.
  • When the final episode finishes at 9.20pm, there is due to be a 40-minute episode of the spin-off show Uncloaked.
  • Uncloaked is hosted by comedian Ed Gamble, and the all-star cast is set to join him to review the series.
