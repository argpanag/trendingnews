Fleetwood Town drawn in Carabao Cup fourth round

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

We have been drawn against Fleetwood Town in the fourth round of the Carabao Cup, with the tie to take place at the League Two side's Highbury Stadium.

Having beaten Ipswich Town 4-2 at Portman Road on Tuesday night to advance in the competition, the draw for the next round has pitted us against the Fishermen, with the game scheduled for the week commencing October 26. It will be the first-ever meeting between the sides in competitive action.

Fleetwood reached this stage by beating fellow fourth-tier opposition Chesterfield and Shrewsbury Town, before then knocking out Championship side Sheffield United in the third round. All of their ties have been held at their 5,300-capacity home in the north-west.

We reached the final last season before Manchester City pipped us to the silverware at Wembley, missing out on having our name engraved on the trophy for a third time, with our two successes coming in 1987 and 1993.

Exact dates and kick-off times, plus ticket information, will be announced in due course.
