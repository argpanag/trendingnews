High surf, rough beach conditions expected along Florida's coast this weekend

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:46 · source · permalink

High surf and rough coastal conditions are expected along Florida’s east coast this weekend as onshore winds strengthen and beach showers return.

Beachgoers should prepare for building seas, with wave heights expected to increase through the weekend and into early next week.

On Friday, seas are expected to be 6 to 8 feet, with beach waves of 4 to 6 feet.

By Saturday, seas are expected to reach 7 to 9 feet, with beach waves of 5 to 7 feet.

Conditions are forecast to become rougher Sunday, when seas could reach 12 to 14 feet and beach waves could climb to 8 to 11 feet.

The increasing surf could create hazardous swimming conditions and stronger rip currents along the coast.

Winds and showers return

Coastal low pressure is expected to develop over the next few days as moisture increases across Central Florida. Showers are forecast to return this weekend, along with rising humidity and warmer morning temperatures.

Friday should remain mostly dry, with about a 10% chance of rain.

Rain chances increase to 30% Saturday and Sunday.

Afternoon temperatures should stay near 80 degrees through the weekend, while overnight lows climb into the upper 60s.
