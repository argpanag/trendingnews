Woman dies after being struck by vehicle in Dublin

TheTools · 03.10.2026 21:55 · source · permalink

A woman in her 30s has died in hospital after she was struck by a vehicle in Dublin city last Saturday.

The incident happened on North Wall Quay at around 11pm on 26 September.

The woman was brought to the Mater Hospital, where she died on Thursday.

No other injures were reported.

Gardaí have appealed for witnesses to come forward, including road users with dash cam footage from the area at the time to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Store Street Garda Station on 01 666 8000, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.
