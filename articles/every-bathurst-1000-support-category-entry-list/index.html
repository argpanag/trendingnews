Every Bathurst 1000 support category entry list

TheTools · 07.10.2026 00:18 · source · permalink

This year’s Repco Supercars Championship is supported by five categories – the Dunlop Super2 Series, Porsche Equity-One Carrera Cup Australia, Touring Car Masters, the Tyrepower V8 SuperUte Series, and the Toyota Gazoo Racing Australia GR Cup.

All entry lists are subject to change.

Dunlop Super2 Series

Tickford Autosport duo Lochie Dalton and Reuben Goodall lead the way in the Dunlop Super2 Series, split by 69 points.

The pair will be on double duty between Super2 and Supercars, with Dalton joining Erebus Motorsport’s Cooper Murray and Goodall with Tickford Racing’s Thomas Randle.

The field features 18 cars, including the additions of Team Kiwi Racing’s Brendon Leitch and Jonathon Webb at Anderson Motorsport.

The Dunlop Super2 Series will have two races across Friday (3:20pm AEDT) and Saturday .

Porsche Paynter Dixon Carrera Cup Australia

Porsche Equity-One Carrera Cup Australia will feature 24 cars at the Mountain.

Two drivers will be on double duty, with Harri Jones and David Russell racing in Supercars with Matt Stone Racing and PremiAir Racing respectively.

Touring Car Masters

Touring Car Masters will host the final round of its season at the Bathurst 1000, with a 34-car grid set to tackle Mount Panorama.

Nine New Zealand-based entries round out the full field, who are all regulars from the Central Muscle Cars series.

Headlined by Angus Fogg and Lance Hughes, two regular TCM contenders this season, the Kiwi contingent also includes the likes of Triple Eight part-owner Tony Quinn in his #101 Pontiac.

Tyrepower V8 SuperUte Series

The Tyrepower V8 SuperUtes Series will boast a 21-car field when the series returns to Mount Panorama for the Repco Bathurst 1000.

The series will welcome debutant Braydan Willmington on October 8-11 in the Jason Norris-owned Toyota HiLux with Apogee Motorsport.

Willmington is the son of Garry Willmington, who was a Bathurst 1000 staple between 1978 and 2000.

Matt Nolan will make his series return with the Sieders Racing Team, replacing Anthony Jewell who drove the Mitsubishi Triton at Wanneroo.

It’s the first time since Sandown last year that Nolan will have raced in the ute series.

Toyota Gazoo Racing Australia GR Cup

This year’s Toyota Gazoo Racing Australia GR Cup round at the Bathurst 1000 will feature three guest drivers as part of a bumper 29-car field.

The three guest drivers are TGRA Scholarship Series driver Harry Bresnehan, Lee Zeltwanger out of New Zealand’s Toyota GR86 Championship, and Australian Rally Championship winner Lewis Bates.
