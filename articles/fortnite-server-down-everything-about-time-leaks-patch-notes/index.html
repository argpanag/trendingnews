Fortnite: What you need to know about the server maintenance on October 15, 2026

What happens during the server downtime? The servers will be shut down. During this time, you cannot play Fortnite.

When does the server downtime start in Fortnite? The servers are expected to be shut down on October 15, 2026, at around 10:00 AM CEST. Shortly before that (about half an hour, around 9:30 AM CEST), matchmaking will be disabled.

When can I play Fortnite again? That depends on the size of the update and the duration of the downtime. Depending on the size of the update, it usually takes between 1 – 3 hours. So you can typically play again between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM. We will keep track of when exactly in the ticker.

Are there patch notes already? Epic Games only occasionally posts patch notes for updates online and usually only after the downtime. As soon as they are available, you can read them with us.

What content is coming in patch 42.40? In the last update of Season 4, there will be Halloween-appropriate collaborations once again. It is also possible that there will be first teasers for upcoming collaborations. We will accompany the server downtime with the corresponding leaks so you know what to expect.
