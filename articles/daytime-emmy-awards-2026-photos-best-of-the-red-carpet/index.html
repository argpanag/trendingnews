Daytime Emmy Awards 2026 Photos: Best Of The Red Carpet

TheTools · 30.10.2026 23:50 · source · permalink

Breaking News

The 2026 Daytime Emmy Awards celebrate the best of daytime television, and the Hollywood Palladium is the place where the celebration takes place.

CBS leads the award show nominees, with The Young and the Restless with 18 and Beyond the Gates with 15. ABC’s General Hospital has 13 nominations.

NBC’s The Kelly Clarkson Show leads talk shows with 10 nominations in its final year on the air.

RELATED: How To Watch The 2026 Daytime Emmy Awards

Genie Francis is being honored tonight with NATAS’ Live Achievement Award, as are this year’s Gold Circle and Silver Circle recipients.

Presenters and appearances are expected to include Eva zu Beck, Danielle Chang, Crystal Chappell, Matthew Hoffman, Sunny Hostin, Derek Hough, Pati Jinich, Star Jones, Michael Jr., Lesli Kay and Joe Lando.
