Actor spills more details on major blockbuster filmed in Suffolk

TheTools · 14.10.2026 16:49 · source · permalink

Nearly 30 years after The Mummy first became a box office hit, one of its original stars has spoken about reuniting with the cast for the franchise's latest chapter.

Actor John Hannah has revealed it feels as though no time has passed since the original The Mummy film, despite nearly three decades separating the blockbuster from its upcoming sequel.

The Scottish actor, who is reprising his role as Jonathan Carnahan, shared an update on the long-awaited fourth instalment during an appearance on Lorraine Kelly's ITV programme.

His comments come after part of the film's production was spotted at MOD Woodbridge in Suffolk, where Brendan Fraser was seen filming scenes for the movie last month.

Speaking about returning to the franchise, Hannah said filming was already several weeks under way.

He said: "Yeah, we've started now, we're a few weeks in.

"It's really lovely and it's extraordinary.

"The first one was, what, 28 years ago or something? But it's extraordinary how we've just kind of fallen back into it."

Hannah also joked about reuniting with co-star Rachel Weisz, who returns as Evelyn Carnahan after last appearing alongside him in The Mummy Returns.

He said: "Rachel was saying last night, we were shooting last night, how she just looks at me and starts to giggle.

"She's a very serious actress!

"I took it well, actually, you know and then she was like 'well whatever buffoonery you're about to do, let it commence'."

Fans were given their first glimpse of production in Suffolk when Oscar-winning actor Brendan Fraser was photographed filming at MOD Woodbridge.

The actor, who reprises his role as adventurer Rick O'Connell, was seen shooting what appeared to be a record-speed trials sequence behind the wheel of a silver-and-red rocket car.

Arnold Vosloo, Oded Fehr and Kevin J. O'Connor are also expected to return for the new film, which is due to be released in October 2027.
