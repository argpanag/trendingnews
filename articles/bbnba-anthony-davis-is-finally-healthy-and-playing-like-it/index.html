BBNBA: Anthony Davis is finally healthy and playing like it

TheTools · 15.11.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

KSR Cats in the NBA

Anthony Davis was probably just chilling in his Los Angeles mansion, minding his own business, figuring out which Hollywood party he should attend, when his entire world got flipped upside down. The discussion around the trade that sent Luka Doncic to the Lakers in exchange for Davis often focuses on the Luka aspect of it. How could the Mavericks trade away their one-in-a-lifetime superstar? But Anthony Davis had to be equally as shocked.

He wasn’t too far removed from helping the Lakers win a championship, and it looked like he was a staple in LA. However, when the prospect of attaining Luka arose, Davis became a trade commodity. His time in Dallas was brief and injury-plagued, so they shipped him off to Washington. Now with the Wizards, Davis has been in and out of the lineup with nagging injuries, but he finally appears to be healthy now and is playing like it.

Davis played three games this week, all of which he notched a double-double, and averaged 31.7 points. This included one game with 44 points and 13 rebounds, followed by a 30/15 performance, both against Detroit. Unfortunately, the Wizards lost both games, but it is good to see the Brow playing like the superstar he has been since his time at Kentucky.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander also put up 40, and Jamal Murray came close

Anthony Davis wasn’t the only member of the BBNBA to put up 40 this week. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, who has a knack for that kind of thing, did the same. SGA’s big game came against the Clippers, but it wasn’t enough for the Thunder to pull out a win.

OKC is still going to be a playoff team, but with two devastating injuries to Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace, their championship hopes have taken a huge hit. Their hopes primarily lie with SGA.

Meanwhile, Jamal Murray was a bucket away from 40 earlier this week. He put up 38 against the Rockets and followed it up with games of 30 and 23. The Cats could really use him right about now. Granted, he’s 29 years old and has been a pro for 10 years, but college basketball has no rules anymore, right?

Other BBNBA Notes

  • Overall, there were nine 30-point games across the BBNA this week. It wasn’t 83, but Bam Adebayo scored 37 against Cleveland on Friday. He also pulled down 13 rebounds, so that’s cool. Devin Booker was also in this group of bucket-getters.
  • Karl-Anthony Towns did not score 30, but he had a double-double in each of his three games this week. That makes 12 consecutive contests in which he’s put up at least 10 points and 10 rebounds. Not too shabby.
  • De’Aaron Fox had the best plus-minus number this week, coming in at 12.5. He hasn’t been putting up the same type of numbers he did in Sacramento, but he has been a key factor in the Spurs’ success.
