    13:19 BST

    Manchester United v Liverpool (13:30 BST)

    Manchester United

    Manchester United head coach Eva Olid, speaking to BBC One: "The pressure is always there because being at Manchester United, you have to perform and win.

    "It has been a few weeks, and it is calmer than the first ones. I think that's what the team needed. Little by little.

    "The attacking is a totally different style previously to what the team had.

    "Start looking at the team and I can see a lot of things we work on in the training and on the pitch.

    "Improvements are the most important thing."

  2. Man Utd's results so farpublished at 13:18 BST
    13:18 BST

    Manchester United v Liverpool (13:30 BST)

    Manchester United conceded seven goals in their opening two matches as they fell to defeat against London City Lionesses and Chelsea.

    Eva Olid's side were on course for the first win of the season when they travelled to Arsenal but conceded an equaliser in the 95th minute as they were forced to settle for a point.

    At the fourth time of asking, they beat West Ham but conceded a late penalty in the 92nd minute.

    The Red Devils will be looking for back-to-back wins and their first clean sheet of the season as they face rivals Liverpool.

    It was a bit of a surprise not to see Vivien Endemann in the Liverpool XI, let alone the squad, when the teams dropped.

  5. Confidence has risen - Taylorpublished at 13:14 BST
    13:14 BST

    Manchester United v Liverpool (13:30 BST)

    Liverpool

    In recent years, Manchester United would have come into this fixture as overwhelming favourites - but things are a little different this season.

    United are in a period of transition, while Liverpool look a much-improved side with manager Gareth Taylor determined to get them back competing with the top sides in the WSL.

    They pushed champions Manchester City all the way before falling to a late defeat and, after a Merseyside derby win last weekend, it has been a positive start to the campaign.

    “I think regardless of it being against Man Utd, whoever we're facing, I feel the confidence has risen," Taylor said.

    "I think the confidence level has risen between the players, the club, the supporters. I think that's really important.

    "And changing the mentality a little bit to actually realise that, yeah, we are good and we're moving in the right direction. We still have a lot of work to do."
