O'Donovan brought to hospital after falling ill at event

TheTools · 13.10.2026 13:43 · source · permalink

Minister for Culture, Communications and Sport Patrick O'Donovan has been brought by ambulance to hospital after becoming unwell at an EU Presidency meeting being held at the University of Limerick (UL).

An ambulance was called to UL when the minister took ill this afternoon.

The minister was hosting a meeting of EU telecommunications ministers which is being held at the UL Sports Arena.

A planned press conference with the minister has been cancelled.

A spokesperson for the minister said: "The minister became unwell during the EU informal meeting at the University of Limerick and has been taken off site for assessment and treatment."
