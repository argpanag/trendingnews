Kathmandu Gorkhas chase down 181-run target with two balls to spare

TheTools · 02.11.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Copyright © 2025 Nepal News. All Rights Reserved.

Nepal News

KATHMANDU: The Kathmandu Gorkhas chased down the 181-run target set by Karnali Yaks, scoring 181 runs for the loss of four wickets in 19.4 overs to win their Nepal Premier League (NPL) match by six wickets at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur on Monday.

Earlier, Karnali scored 180 runs for the loss of six wickets in 20 overs. Opener Ariful Islam top-scored with 44 runs off 22 balls, including five fours and three sixes. Hussain Talat made 40 off 30 balls, while Pawan Sarraf remained unbeaten on 30 off 23 balls. Gulshan Jha added 20 off 12 balls.

Talat and Sarraf steadied the innings with a 65-run partnership for the fifth wicket after Karnali slipped to 78 for four. Sarraf and Jha then added 28 runs off 16 balls.

Kathmandu captain Karan KC took two wickets, while Shahab Alam, Zahandaan Khan, Dev Shah and Santosh Yadav claimed one each.
