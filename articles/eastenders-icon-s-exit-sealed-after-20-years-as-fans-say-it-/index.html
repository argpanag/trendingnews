EastEnders icon's exit 'sealed' after 20 years as fans say it 'makes sense now'

TheTools · 10.09.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

EastEnders fans are convinced that one Branning family member is set to exit Walford (Image: CREDIT LINE:BBC/Jack Barns/Kieron McCarron)

EastEnders has aired the tragic death of Zoe Slater, and in another shock twist, fans think they know who will be blamed for her murder. Earlier this week, viewers of the BBC One soap witnessed Zoe (Michelle Ryan) fall and smack her head while trying to steal her grandfather's old taxi cab.

When she returned to The Queen Vic, Kat Slater (Jessie Wallace) fumed as her daughter messed up once again, but drew attention to her bloodied head after the vehicle's owner showed up and hit an innocent bystander, Zack Hudson (James Farrar).

In Thursday's episode (September 10), Kat's husband Alfie (Shane Richie) encouraged her to make amends with Zoe, who was also shown suffering from a headache. Upstairs in the pub, the mother and daughter made up and showed their love for one another, though Kat remained worried about Zoe's head injury.

Zoe Slater is dead on EastEnders, and a character might be blamed (Image: BBC)

Kat urged Zoe to seek medical attention as she remained in pain, but Zoe, who looked unwell, declined and urged her mother not to worry, saying she would seek help in the morning if it continued.

Elsewhere, the fallout of Max Branning's (Jake Wood) and Cindy Beale's (Michelle Collins) wedding continued as the distressed bride exposed his affair with Priya Nandra-Hart (Sophie Khan Levy) to the whole of the pub, leaving Avani (Aaliyah James) and Nugget (Juhaim Rasul Choudhury) in complete shock.

Moments later, Max left the pub and was reunited with the woman he really loves, Linda Carter (Kellie Bright), and their romance was ignited once more as they shared a passionate kiss in the barrel store.

Linda and Max's romance is back on in EastEnders (Image: CREDIT LINE:BBC/Jack Barnes/Kieron McCarron)

Looking worse for wear, Zoe then came downstairs looking for painkillers for her headache when she heard something in the basement, and caught her former lover, Max, with Linda, leaving her furious.

Zoe stormed up to her bedroom, and Max followed her, explaining his difficult love life with Cindy and Priya and his desire to keep his new romance with Linda a secret, which amused Zoe.

Max argued with her that she needed to keep quiet, but she shouted at him and weakly tried to push him to leave before he nudged her away, and she fell backward onto her bed in continued pain from her head injury.

Kat found Zoe dead in distressing EastEnders scenes (Image: BBC)

Zoe asked for Max's help, but thinking she was drunk, Max refused and left, slamming the door behind him. Left alone with the door closed, Zoe faintly wailed for Kat: "I want my mum."

Kat woke up in the middle of the night and checked on Zoe, who was lying on her bed fully clothed. She was horrified to discover Zoe was dead and broke down in tears.

As Max was the last person to see Zoe alive, some EastEnders fans are convinced he will be blamed for her death, which explains his forthcoming arrest. Max will be taken away by the police for the solicitation of murder on his wedding day on January 1, 2027, as seen in a flash-forward that aired earlier this year.

Max Branning is set to be arrested on New Year's Day 2027 (Image: BBC/Jack Barnes/Kieron McCarron)

Will this really be the end of Max after 20 years on the Square? Taking to X, one fan wrote: "On New Year's Day, Max Branning will be arrested for the solicitation to murder Zoe Slater #EastEnders."

A second wrote: "Max being the last to see Zoe alive explains that part in the flash forward #EastEnders", while a third asked: "Is Max being the last person to see Zoe alive going to be linked to his conspiracy to murder plot? #EastEnders."

"I wonder whether this is what Max gets arrested for on New Year’s Day? Does someone set him up for the murder of Zoe?! #eastenders", added one viewer. While another confidently said: "So THAT is who Max is accused of killing in the flashback episode!! IT'S ZOE! #EastEnders All starting to make sense now."

EastEnders airs Monday to Thursday on BBC One and iPlayer
