Honor X9e Pro is coming next week with an 11,000mAh battery

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:56 · source · permalink

Login

Siddharth 18 September 2026

Honor Android

Honor is gearing up to launch the X9e Pro in Malaysia and has been teasing the device on social media. The phone is set to launch next week, but several details about the handset have already surfaced online ahead of the unveiling.

The Honor X9e Pro will launch in Malaysia on September 24, according to the official Honor Malaysia website.

It will be available in Dawn Gold, Midnight Black, Reddish Brown, and Royal Purple colorways. Honor has also been teasing the phone’s extreme durability including the ability to be submerged in hot water.

The teasers also confirm that the Honor X9e Pro will feature a dual-rear camera setup with a 108MP primary sensor.

Meanwhile, a Malaysian news outlet has revealed the key features of the upcoming X9e Pro. The handset will feature a large 11,000mAh battery with support for 80W wired fast charging.

The phone is also said to offer a display with a peak brightness of up to 10,000 nits.
