MARSS welcomes Kaïs Mnif as Group President and Chairman

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:42 · source · permalink

MARSS has appointed Kaïs Mnif as Group President and Chairman. The company is expanding its leadership team as it scales to meet growing demand for counter-drone protection across Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

The appointment follows a succession of new contracts and customers for MARSS since being acquired by EOS in May 2026. These include a £370 million nationwide counter-drone network for Kuwait, signed in October and a multi-million dollar contract with an Arab nation to protect sites of national strategic importance, signed in the last few weeks. MARSS has added three new country customers in the Middle East in 2026.

This heightened demand reflects a shift in the threat environment. Recent conflicts have shown the damage that low-cost drones can inflict on critical national infrastructure (CNI) and governments are moving to put protection in place at speed.

How the appointment will drive integration and support opportunities for growth

Kaïs Mnif’s apointment as Group President and Chairman will help to drive MARSS’ continued integration within EOS to meet the urgent customer needs and continue the company’s growth.

Acting as a prime contractor, MARSS portfolio offers complete counter-drone protection from a single site, with NiDAR – its AI-enabled command and control (C2) platform – at its core.


Kaïs will report to Dr Andreas Schwer, CEO and Managing Director of EOS and will be based at the new MARSS office in Nice.

Johannes Pinl remains Chief Executive Officer of MARSS and will continue to lead sales and growth in new markets across EMEA.
