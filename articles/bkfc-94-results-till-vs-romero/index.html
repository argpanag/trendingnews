BKFC 94 Results: Till vs. Romero

TheTools · 26.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink
  • BKFC

Darren Till, Yoel Romero clash in BKFC 94 main event

MMA Fighting has BKFC 94 results at the Till vs. Romero fight card, a live blog of the main event, and more from the AO Arena in Manchester, England, on Saturday afternoon.

In the main event, UFC veterans Darren Till and Yoel Romero collide in a 190-pound catchweight contest.

BKFC middleweight champion Dave Mundell puts his belt on the line against Jack Cullen in the co-main event.

Check out the BKFC 94 results below.

BKFC 94 Main Card

Darren Till def. Yoel Romero via unanimous decision (49-46 x3)

Live blog | Highlights

Jack Cullen def. Dave Mundell via split decision (58-55, 56-57, 57-56)

Gaz Corran def. Mac Bygraves via third-round TKO (0:26)

Karl Thompson def. Andy Thornton via unanimous decision (50-45 x3)

Joe Lister def. Harry Bray via second-round TKO (1:58)

Mikey Henderson def. Matt Wiwczaryk via first-round TKO (5:00, doctor stoppage)

Stew Martin def. Joshy Williams via second-round TKO (5:00, doctor’s stoppage)

Bart Krol def. Luke Vaughan via first-round TKO (0:55)

Leigh Cahoon def. Ste Wallwork via second-round TKO (5:00, doctor’s stoppage)

BKFC 94 Prelims

Ben Lowe def. Dan McGraffin via first-round TKO (1:42)

Tyrone Hamer def. Liam Dooley via third-round KO (1:59)

Luke Bassnett def. Darren Williams via second-round KO (1:57)
