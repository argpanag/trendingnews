Two seriously hurt as crash closes highway south of Dunedin

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:13 · source · permalink
PHOTO

Two people have been seriously hurt in a two-vehicle crash which has closed a section of highway south of Dunedin.

Emergency services are at the scene of the two-vehicle crash at the intersection of SH1 and Tutu Hill Road.

Police were called to the crash about 4.20pm on Thursday afternoon, a spokesman said.

“Two people are reported to have serious injuries while a third has moderate injuries.”

Road users are advised to delay their journey or avoid the area, NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi said.
