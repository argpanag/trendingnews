Ben Affleck's Scrapped Batman Movie Gets Plot Tease From Deathstroke Actor

For the first time ever, we now presumably know a ton of details about the plot of Ben Affleck's solo Batman film, a movie that will unfortunately never be made (more on why in a moment). Back when Zack Snyder was the maestro of the DCEU for Warner Bros., one of the biggest upcoming projects in the stable, if not the biggest, was Affeck's goal of directing, producing and starring in his very own Batman feature film.

Years later, Joe Manganiello, the talented veteran actor seen portraying the infamous villain Deathstroke in a post-credits scene with Jesse Eisenberg's Lex Luthor in both versions of Justice League, was slated to reappear in a major way in the DCEU: as Batman's big bad in Affleck's Caped Crusader film.

And it was apparently going to be so brutal that it might've earned an R-rating, based on the beans Manganiello just spilled.

Plot Revealed for Ben Affleck's Scrapped DCEU Batman Movie

Per Entertainment Weekly: "Manganiello writes that Affleck planned to make the film 'a superhero version of David Fincher’s The Game' that would see Deathstroke 'dismantle Batman’s entire life' after being led to believe that the Caped Crusader murdered his son. ... The Magic Mike actor says that Deathstroke’s revenge would include killing Commissioner Gordon (played by J.K. Simmons in prior DC movies), taking Alfred Pennyworth (Jeremy Irons) hostage, and breaking into the Batcave."

It continued: "He also claims that the film would have featured a mid-movie fight in which 'Deathstroke would nearly kill both Batman and Batgirl before they would both escape via an aboveground train.' Manganiello says the film would have ended with a 'climactic one-on-one showdown through the streets of Gotham' that Affleck said would be inspired by a CGI fight sequence from the Batman: Arkham Asylum video game helmed by Tim Miller."

Holy sh-t ... inspired by the iconic Arkham games, too? Batman fans might never forgive Warner Bros. for robbing them of Affleck's vision for Batman and, finally, an utterly perfect live-action version Deathstroke.

Ben Affleck: Warner Bros. Is 'As Gross As It Gets'

“It sounded good to me,” Manganiello said, via EW. “I told Ben how great I thought his films were, especially The Town, and how I was really looking forward to seeing his Best Picture version of a Batman movie.”

In the end, Affleck called his time filming the DCEU with Warner Bros. (now named Skydance) "as gross as it gets", also rooted in the studio's handling of Zack Snyder following the death of his daughter by suicide. Add in the list of complaints from the other Justice League stars and the fact it all helped push Affleck to drinking, a demon he had to fight to overcome, and the odds of Affleck every going through with the Batman movie are literally zero.

"I really saw how not to make a movie like that, which is when the studio is at odds with a filmmaker. You're really making a mistake," he told the Happy Sad Confused Podcast. "That was what really doomed it. ... Boy, that was sh-tty — [Snyder] being taken off that movie, and the reshoots that we did, that was the lowest point of my career, was that. ... It was just like, Wow, this is about as gross as it gets."

That leaves us all wondering what Affleck's Batman film would've looked like but, thanks to his would-be co-star, Manganiello, being a man of the people, we now know the entire plot; and that's not nothing.
