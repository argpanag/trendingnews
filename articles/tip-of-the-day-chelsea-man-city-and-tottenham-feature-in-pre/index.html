Tip of the Day: Chelsea, Man City and Tottenham feature in Premier League treble

TheTools · 31.10.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

Saturdays Tip of the Day features three Premier League giants.

Last Update:

Saturday’s Tip of the Day features three Premier League clashes, with Chelsea and Tottenham backed to take all three points and goals expected when Manchester City face Brighton.

The first leg of our treble is for Chelsea to beat Manchester United.

The Blues will be looking to make home advantage count against one of their rivals, with the attacking quality in their squad giving them plenty of options in the final third.

United have the players to cause Chelsea problems, but the hosts are our pick to come out on top in what could be a fiesty early kick-off.

The second selection is for both teams to score when Manchester City take on Brighton.

City are always a threat going forward, particularly with Erling Haaland leading the line, but Brighton have shown they are capable of troubling the Premier League’s biggest sides with their attacking approach.

The Seagulls are unlikely to sit back for the entire contest, which should create opportunities at both ends. City may be favourites to win, but backing both teams to find the net looks a safe bet.

Our final selection is for Tottenham to beat Crystal Palace.

Spurs will be aiming to secure three points against their London rivals, with their attacking options capable of causing problems for the Palace defence.

Palace have the quality to make life difficult, particularly on the counter-attack, but Tottenham are our choice to get the job done and complete the treble.

Saturday’s Tip of the Day (odds are from bet365 and supplied as of Saturday, October 31)

  • Chelsea to beat Manchester United
  • Manchester City vs Brighton – Both teams to score
  • Tottenham to beat Crystal Palace
