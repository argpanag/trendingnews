Britain's Prince William, Prince of Wales, and Catherine, Princess of Wales | Photo Credit: PTI

Two men have been charged with burglary at the country home of Prince William and Princess Catherine, police said on Wednesday (November 25, 2026).

The Norfolk Police force says Mirgen Doku, 34, and Arilen Ndoci, 32, were charged with entering a dwelling with intent to steal and trespassing on a protected site. They are due in court later on Wednesday.

Police officers were called on Monday evening to reported “suspicious activity” at an address on the royal family's rural Sandringham estate in eastern England. After a search involving police dogs, drones and a helicopter, two men were arrested on suspicion of burglary and trespassing.

No royals were on the property at the time.

William and Kate’s Kensington Palace office declined to comment on security matters.

Anmer Hall, a 10-bedroom house on the Sandringham estate, was a wedding present to the couple from the late Queen Elizabeth II.

William and Kate use the secluded red brick Georgian mansion about 110 miles (171 kilometres) north of London as a country residence on weekends. The couple and their three children spend most of their time at Forest Lodge in Windsor, west of London, which they have described as their “forever home.”

Intruders have breached royal properties several times over the years.

In October 2024, burglars broke into the Windsor Castle estate west of London and stole a pickup truck and a quad bike from a farm building. Charles and Queen Camilla weren’t at Windsor Castle at the time, but it was reported that William and Kate were in their nearby house.

Three years earlier, a man armed with a crossbow was detained on the castle grounds. Jaswant Singh Chail was later sentenced to nine years in prison after admitting he had been encouraged by a chatbot to kill Elizabeth.

In 1982, intruder Michael Fagan managed to sneak into the queen’s private chambers while she was in bed. The queen spent 10 minutes chatting with the burglar before raising the alarm when he asked for a cigarette.

Fagan was sent to a psychiatric hospital, then released and was later jailed for unrelated criminal offenses.
