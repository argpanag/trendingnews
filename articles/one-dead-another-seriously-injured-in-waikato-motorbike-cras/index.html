One dead, another seriously injured in Waikato motorbike crash

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:21 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Emergency services were alerted to the single-motorbike crash on Morrinsville-Tahuna Rd at about 6.45pm Saturday. Image / Google Maps

One person has died and another is seriously injured after a crash involving a motorbike in Matamata-Piako.

Emergency services were alerted to the single-vehicle crash on Morrinsville-Tahuna Rd, between Paeroa-Tahuna Rd and Quine Rd, at about 6.45pm Saturday.

Police said one person died at the scene, while another rider on the same bike was transported to hospital in a serious condition.

The Serious Crash Unit carried out a scene examination and the road reopened before midnight.

Police are providing support to the families, while inquiries into the cause of the crash are ongoing.
