Ukraine uses laser weapon to shoot down Russian drone for first time

Ukraine has used a laser weapon to shoot down a Russian jet-powered drone for the first time, according to the country’s state-backed defence technology group Brave1.

Serhiy Beskrestnov, an adviser to Volodymyr Zelensky, shared footage earlier this week showing a drone crashing in a ball of fire, saying a Ukrainian laser system known as Blysk had successfully intercepted a Russian Geran drone.

Brave1 confirmed the use of the laser weapon in combat to AFP on Friday but cautioned that the technology was not yet ready for widespread deployment.

“A first successful interception does not mean the technology is ready for mass deployment,” it said.

“Laser air defence remains an experimental technology,” the group added, saying at least seven Ukrainian teams were working on developing laser weapons.

Nine Ukrainian children returned home after intervention by Melania Trump

Nine Ukrainian children were returned from Russia and Russian-occupied territories on Friday in the latest reunification operation mediated by US first lady Melania Trump.

Among them was an eight-year-old boy who was reunited with his mother after nearly five years apart at Ukraine’s northern border in the Chernihiv region.

“I want to show him everything that he missed and was taken away from him,” his mother, Alina Sydorenko, told the Associated Press.

Volodymyr Zelensky thanked the US first lady for her involvement in the reunification efforts, which the White House said had helped bring 43 children home so far.

Ukraine has secured the return of 2,875 children in total, according to Mr Zelensky.

“We remember each and every one of them. We are doing everything we can to find them and return them to their families,” he said.

Trump defends calling Putin his ‘friend’

Donald Trump has defended his friendly approach towards Vladimir Putin, saying it is necessary to secure a deal to end the war in Ukraine.

“We want to make a deal. We want to stop the killing,” the US president told reporters at his Mar-a-Lago residence on Thursday.

“I could walk in and say, ‘I don’t like you!’ and then we have the same problem that we have with Zelenskyy and Putin,” he said.

“The biggest problem with that settlement is the fact that you have two leaders that really hate each other.”

Zelensky questions Trump calling Putin a ‘friend’

Volodymyr Zelensky has expressed frustration with Donald Trump’s approach towards Vladimir Putin, questioning how the US president could describe the Russian leader as a friend while Moscow continues its invasion of Ukraine.
