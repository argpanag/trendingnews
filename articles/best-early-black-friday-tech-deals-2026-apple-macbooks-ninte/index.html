Best early Black Friday tech deals 2026: Apple MacBooks, Nintendo Switch 2, and more

TheTools · 11.11.2026 08:00 · source · permalink

You've dug out the winter clothes, the neighbors are putting up Christmas lights, and the stores have eggnog back on the shelves. In the world of shopping, that means it's almost time for Black Friday. If you've shopped the holiday sale period before, you know deals drop ahead of the actual Friday after Thanksgiving. That's great news if you have tech gifts on your list this year.

We're still a few weeks away from Black Friday, but we've scanned the ads and compiled a list of the best tech deals we'd buy ourselves before the official sale. Not only will some of these be great for using during the Thanksgiving break, but you'll also be on the team of early shoppers, which can often mean the best selection without shipping delays.

Check out Mashable's full Black Friday coverage as we keep a close eye on fresh deals.

The gaming console of the year at a $30 discount

Somehow, Woot is selling the Nintendo Switch 2 for a $30 discount, while it remains at full price everywhere else. Since Woot deals tend to sell out, hop on this one right now if you're interested. Grabbing the Switch 2 will put you in line for spending the holiday break with the new The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Amazon Prime members get free shipping at Woot.

A comfortable pair of earbuds for holiday travel

It's hard to beat the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) if you're searching for a pair that won't become bothersome after hours of wear. On Mashable's list of the best earbuds, the 2nd Gen QuietComfort Ultra earn their namesake as the most comfortable. The noise cancellation is excellent, and the adaptive ANC is great if you still need to tune into your surroundings. Consider these an early Christmas gift for yourself if you'll be taking off on holiday travel or need some solid earbuds.

Read our full review of the Bose QuietComfort Ultra earbuds (2nd gen).

A great MacBook for both students and professionals

At $200 off, the M5 MacBook Air is sitting at the same sale price we saw during October Prime Day. The MacBook Air proves itself to be a great machine for everyone, from students and professionals to frequent travelers. It's slim, lightweight, and comes with a stunning display and a powerful M5 chip.

Read our full review of the M5 MacBook Air.

A Kindle bundled with the new Kindle Click

Without making a peep, Amazon hiked the prices on all Kindles this year. While that's annoying and so on par for 2026, we do have something new and exciting in the e-reader world — the Kindle Click. The remote page-turner means you can now turn pages with your hands under the covers or while simply relaxing on the couch.

The glass Ninja Crispi air fryer

The Ninja lineup of kitchen appliances and gadgets consistently impresses. Take, for example, the new Ninja EverSear cookware, which rivals top brands but comes at a much better price. But if you or someone on your Christmas shopping list needs an upgraded air fryer, the Ninja Crispi is everything you want. With PFAS-free glass containers, the Ninja air fryer can reach up to 450 degrees for perfect crisping of your favorite meals and snacks. Plus, the glass containers come with lids, and they're dishwasher-safe.

A DJI bundle for vloggers

From pro level to just starting out, DJI continues to prove it's a brand designed for creators. Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth's review of the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 explained it's the gold standard for vlogging cameras. DJI has now released the Osmo Pocket 4, but you're looking at spending a lot more than the sale price of the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 paired with the DJI Mic Mini, which is on sale for $450.

A smart ring for tracking fitness goals in the new year

If you have 2027 health goals and don't love wearing a watch, a smart ring is a great alternative, and the Oura Ring 4 is on sale for $249 before Black Friday. The smart ring tracks sleep, activity, stress levels, heart rate, and plenty more to provide deeper insight into health and wellness. Plus, it gets up to eight days of battery life on a single charge. Keep in mind you'll want to sign up for Oura's subscription plan to get the full benefits, which costs $5.99 per month or $69.99 per year.

A budget tablet for upgraded streaming

If you're squinting to watch shows and YouTube videos on a phone, give your eyes a treat with the Amazon Fire HD 10 tablet. It's already on sale for 53% off, bringing the 10-inch tablet down to just $72.99. The 32GB storage is great for downloading shows, books, and games for travel or lounging around at home.

It's also a great option for kids if you're looking for something more affordable than iPads.

Hisense 85-inch TV for holiday movies and family gaming sessions

Deck the halls with a massive 85-inch Hisense TV. No one in your house will complain about the ability to watch Christmas movies on the giant display in UHD 4K. Gamers will also appreciate the 144Hz refresh rate, which is ideal for watching sports over the holidays.

The ultimate backyard upgrade

We have robot vacuums to take care of cleaning the floor inside, but plenty of us still dread hauling out the lawn mower and turning our white sneakers an ombre green shade. Instead of battling the lawn again next spring, make the upgrade to a robot lawn mower while the Mammotion Luba 3 AWD 3000 is on sale for the best price we've ever seen at Amazon.

The Mammotion is designed to tackle yards with a range of obstacles, such as roots, dips, rocks, and tennis balls the dog left out. It can run for up to 175 minutes on a single charge, covering up to 0.75 acres.
