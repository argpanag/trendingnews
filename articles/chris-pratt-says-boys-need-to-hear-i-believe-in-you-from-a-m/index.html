Chris Pratt Says Boys Need to Hear ‘I Believe in You’ From a Mentor as Way of the Warrior Kid Heads to Apple TV

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:49 · source · permalink

News

Chris Pratt has four words he hopes more boys will hear from a coach, pastor or teacher: "I believe in you." The actor spoke with ChurchLeaders about the difference a mentor can make ahead of the Nov. 25 premiere of Way of the Warrior Kid, in which he plays a Navy SEAL who spends a summer helping his nephew find his footing.

Pratt said encouragement can carry particular weight when it comes from an adult outside the immediate family. A boy who already knows his parents love him may hear those same words differently from a coach or teacher who has seen him struggle and still believes he can improve. Pratt included pastors among the adults who can play that role. 

That relationship drives the film. Jude Hill plays Marc, a middle schooler dealing with bullying, trouble in class and difficulty making friends. When his uncle Jake comes to stay with Marc and his mother, Sarah, played by Linda Cardellini, he is recovering from an injury. Jake draws on his SEAL training to devise a summer program called "Operation Warrior Kid." As Marc learns to face situations that scare him, Jake has fears of his own to confront. 

Hill told ChurchLeaders that Marc already knows his mother loves him. What changes over the summer is that Jake tells Marc he believes in him and then spends time helping him do things he thought were beyond him. For Hill, that combination of encouragement and practical help is what makes Marc the warrior kid of the title. 

Directed by McG and adapted from the bestselling novel by former Navy SEAL Jocko Willink, Way of the Warrior Kid premieres worldwide on Apple TV on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. Watch the official trailer on Apple TV.
