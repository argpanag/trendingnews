Adriana Lima sizzles in neon green lingerie at her 21st Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show

TheTools · 19.10.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Amazing

Lima remains one of the most emblematic Victoria's Secret Angels

Adriana Lima and Victoria's Secret go way back. The 45-year-old Brazilian model was featured on yesterday's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, hosted in California and featuring musical performances from Megan Thee Stallion, Katseye, and more.

Lima walked the runway for her 21st Victoria's Secret fashion show, wearing different outfits. Her most striking was a neon green lingerie set that showed off her toned thighs. 

The lingerie set was a one-piece neon lace set with a criss-cross pattern on the sides. Lima paired the look with matching green heels, matching bracelets, and some gorgeous floral angel wings. 

Lima returned to the stage once more, wearing a white lingerie set for her closing look. She paired it with striking beaded wings, playfully holding her hands in prayer and gazing skyward. 

Lima's history with Victoria's Secret

Adriana Lima is one of Victoria's Secret's most famous collaborators. She has appeared on every Victoria's Secret runway show from 1999, when she made her debut at 17, through 2018. 

The one exception was 2009, when she was on maternity leave. Her appearances have made her the longest-running Victoria's Secret model ever. 

Lima was promoted to an angel in 2000, enjoying her tenure for almost two decades. She officially retired in 2018, but made her comeback in 2023. "I'm coming home," she said ahead of the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in 2024, as reported by US Weekly. 

More details about this year's Fashion Show

This year's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show was a celebration of the brand's long history and cultural power in the mainstream, with a guest list that included stars like Nicole Kidman, Timothee Chalamet, and more. It was also a teaser of what's to come. 

The show featured many now-viral moments, including Barbara Palvin's post-partum catwalk, Kylie Jenner's Victoria's Secret debut in the $50 million sparkling Fantasy Bra, and a Gisele Bündchen teaser promising a reunion with the brand. 

“Are you ready for the show?” said Bündchen before the show. After, once all the models took the stage for the finale, the video of Bündchen was back, teasing her comeback. “Wasn’t that amazing?” she said. “You are going to be seeing more of me real soon. Ciao!” The years pass, but Victoria's Secret remains as relevant as ever.
