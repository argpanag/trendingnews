Longo: In or out - Milan expected to make Bondo decision after summer exit refusal

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:30 · source · permalink

Warren Bondo stayed at AC Milan in the summer, but this was not the plan. There is no visible role for him in the squad and a decision is expected today.

The mercato was not Milan’s best. Of course, there were good bits of business, but when it came to sales, the Rossoneri struggled. In fact, it’s a reason why the technical director conversation has restarted.

Bondo, as we know, was included in that. He was placed into the ‘bomb squad’ category and there was interest in the former Monza man. However, he remained at Milanello, just away from everyone else.

Bondo decision expected

As Daniele Longo reports (via @MilanPosts on X), it’s judgment day for Bondo, who will find out his fate today. Indeed, the French midfielder refused a move away from the Rossoneri in the summer, hoping to prove himself to the club.

However, he hasn’t been seen in the squad yet and whether he will find himself there moving forward is something else entirely.

Longo reports that the midfielder will find out today if he’s going to be reintegrated by Ruben Amorim – as Fikayo Tomori was – or whether he’ll remain outside of the project. Considering the depth of the midfield, you’d suggest he’s probably fighting an uphill battle.
