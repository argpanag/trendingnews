Graeme Hall makes bold wardrobe admission 'nobody needs to see that'

TheTools · 19.09.2026 12:50 · source · permalink

The Strictly Come Dancing contestant shared his concern over the Latin wardrobe option(Image: BBC)

This weekend, BBC audiences will witness Graeme Hall discovering which professional dancer he's been paired with for this year's series of Strictly Come Dancing. Renowned for his hit Channel 5 show Dogs Behaving (Very) Badly, the 59-year-old is trading pooches for the sparkle and glamour of the dance floor.

Prior to Saturday's launch show (September 19), he joined James Martin on Saturday Kitchen to discuss his forthcoming stint on the programme. During their chat, James reflected on his own Strictly journey when he reached the semi-finals back in 2005.

Graeme said: "I love the show, I can't wait, it'll be great! This is my thinking, I've had a couple of people say, 'What do you want to get out of it?' I know these things are good for your career.

"But the honest truth is, it's series 24, there's 15 celebs in every series, in the whole wide world, that's not a lot of people who have been given the chance to do what I'm doing. So, I'm going to love every minute and long may it last."

Graeme spoke to James Martin ahead of his Strictly Come Dancing debut(Image: ITV)

James observed: "I am going to chat to you in about six months' time and say, 'You could've said no!'. You don't even know what you're about to be entered into.

"You've got no idea, your whole life is going to change. You've got jeans, you've got turn-ups, that's your trademark look, you're going to come back looking like Liberace! You're going to be a changed fella, I tell you."

Graeme chuckled as he revealed to the ITV presenter that he'd already had a word with Strictly's costume department about some of the more revealing outfits featured on the programme previously.

The 'Dogfather' made a vow if he managed to get to the semi-fina(Image: ITV)

He clarified that there's absolutely no need to squeeze him into one of those plunging Latin shirts that expose everything down to the belly button, reports Wales Online.

He quipped: "Nobody needs to see that! I'm a man of a certain age!", with Danny O'Donoghue from The Script responding: "Wait until you're in the semi-finals and you're pointing at the phone, praying please, please."

When another celebrity chef on the programme wondered if he'd don such an outfit should he progress further in the contest, Graeme responded: "I tell you what, if I get to the semi-final, you're on. I'll do that!"

Craig spoke to BBC Breakfast ahead of the Strictly launch show(Image: BBC)

While audiences must wait to discover which professional dancer Graeme will be partnered with, judge Craig Revel Horwood admitted he was taken aback when he witnessed Graeme's moves during the pre-recorded launch episode.

Speaking on BBC Breakfast, when questioned about which celebrities viewers should keep an eye on as potential winners, he responded: "I've got to say, Delta Goodrem caught my eye, she seems to be very impressive very early on.

"Another surprise for me was Graeme, the dog guy. I just went, 'Wow, he's not bad'. I'm really looking forward to it. I think we're going to have a great competition."

Strictly Come Dancing airs Saturday, September 19 on BBC One at 7:25pm
