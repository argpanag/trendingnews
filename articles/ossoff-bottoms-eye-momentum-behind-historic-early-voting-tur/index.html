Ossoff, Bottoms eye momentum behind historic early voting turnout

TheTools · 19.10.2026 05:57 · source · permalink

1 / 30

With early voting off to a record-breaking start, Georgia’s top Democrats urged supporters to the polls saying they could make a pivotal difference in close races up and down the ballot.

“We need you to work,” U.S. Sen. Jon Ossoff said at a Cobb County rally with former Atlanta Mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms, who is running for governor.

“Some people just happen to live in times and in places where history is made, and this year, once again, Georgia, that’s us,” Ossoff said.

More than 796,000 voters have already cast ballots during the first week of early voting as of Saturday at 6 p.m. More people voted on Tuesday than the first day of early voting in the most recent midterm election in 2022.
