Champions League: Silva sparks Real Madrid’s demolition of Leipzig as Lask stun Sporting

TheTools · 21.10.2026 22:25 · source · permalink

  • Bernardo Silva opens scoring as Madrid win 4-1

  • Galatasaray sweep past Lille; 10-man Inter edge Shakhtar

Bernardo Silva opens scoring as Madrid win 4-1

Galatasaray sweep past Lille; 10-man Inter edge Shakhtar

Real Madrid claimed a third Champions League victory in as many games by thrashing RB Leipzig 4-1 on Wednesday, with Kylian Mbappé and Yan Diomande among the goals for José Mourinho’s side.

Bernardo Silva opened the scoring for Madrid from close range towards the end of a tight first half, with Arda Guler doubling the record 15-time European champions’ advantage soon after the interval.

Mbappé bent home a third before Tidiam Gomis pulled one back for the German side and Diomande rammed a volley into the top corner to cap Madrid’s triumph late on.

“We know every point is important to get into the top eight, we know we have to win as many games as possible – we’ve won three and there are five to go,” Mbappé said. The France forward, who hopes to win the Ballon d’Or next week, has netted in each of Madrid’s Champions League matches this season and has 12 goals in 12 games.

“I’m doing well [but] I think I can do more,” he said. “I’m one of the few players who always has to demand more from himself ... right now it’s a good moment, but we have to keep going and I have to do more.”

Lask launched an impressive comeback at Sporting to earn their first Champions League win of the season. The Austrian champions went behind when Luis Suárez scored from the spot after five minutes, but equalised 10 minutes later through Christoph Lang.

The visitors then stunned the Estádio José Alvalade as Sasa Kalajdzic and Krystof Danek put them 3-1 up, the former rounding off a smart team move and the latter firing in at the near post. Suárez scored again for the hosts late on, but it was not enough.

Galatasaray swept past Lille at the Stade Pierre-Mauroy, setting the tone with a goal after two minutes from Yunus Akgun.

Lille levelled the score via Soriba Diaoune, who then put them 2-1 up in the 39th minute. Aleksey Batrakov made it 2-2 just before half-time, however, before an own goal from Nabil Bentaleb and Victor Osimhen, in action against his former club, sealed a 4-2 victory.

Inter had Alessandro Bastoni sent off midway through the second half before Carlos Augusto’s late goal secured a 1-0 win at home to Shakhtar Donetsk, as the Italian champions got their first points of the league phase.

Bastoni was sent off for two yellow cards in as many minutes, putting Inter on the back foot. Carlos Augusto saved the day in the 77th minute, capping a lightning-fast counterattack with a low finish.

Real Betis, who beat Feyenoord 1-0 via a penalty kick from Isco, made it three from three in the Champions League to extend their strong start to the season.

Betis had the lion’s share of the chances, but only broke the deadlock when Jeremiah St Juste was adjudged to have handled in the box. Isco stepped up and fired low into the right-hand corner for what proved to be the winner.

Club Brugge defeated Lens 3-0 at the Jan Breydel Stadium in a ruthless home performance. The Belgians went 1-0 up through Hans Vanaken in first-half stoppage time before Joaquin Seys and Carlos Forbs secured the win in the second half.
