Qantas sets February 2027 sales for Sunrise flights

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:49 · source · permalink

Qantas plans to open bookings in February 2027 for its nonstop Sydney-London Project Sunrise service, with flights expected to begin in late October. Classic Flight Reward seats will be offered across all four cabins, including First, although Qantas has not confirmed the points prices.

Originally reported by 2paxfly.com · QANTAS: How to book Classic Rewards on Project Sunrise flights to London

Edited by Randy Petersen

Nov 21 · 2:49 AM · 1 min

The inaugural daily service is scheduled for late October 2027, with the booking window described as extending into January 2028. The nonstop outbound will use flight number QF1 and the return QF2; the existing Sydney-Singapore-London service is set to move to QF5 and QF6. Current one-way reward-chart estimates are 227,800 points in First, 151,800 in Business, 113,900 in Premium Economy and 58,900 in Economy.

The figures use existing Sydney-London Classic Reward rates and remain estimates; taxes, fees and carrier charges would be extra. The aircraft is planned with six First Class suites and 52 Business suites, but reward availability is expected to be limited. Before the London launch, the first A350 may fly commercially to Melbourne, Auckland and Los Angeles, with reward seats anticipated on some services.

A traveler needs substantially different balances by cabin, and the published figures exclude taxes and carrier charges, so points alone will not cover redemption cost. Scarce premium-cabin seats may require flexible dates and repeated searches after launch.
