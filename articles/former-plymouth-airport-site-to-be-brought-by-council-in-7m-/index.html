Former Plymouth airport site to be brought by council in £7m deal

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:21 · source · permalink

Land purchase is not a decision on future use, says city leader

The former airport site in Plymouth is to be taken back under council control in a deal worth £7 million.

Plymouth City Council is acquiring the long leasehold interest of the 95-acre site from Plymouth City Airport Ltd, a subsidiary of Sutton Harbour Holdings, for £4.387 million.

It is also buying an adjacent 15-acre parcel of freehold-owned land from the same company for £2.613 million.

The council says the move is part of a plan to influence, invest in and help shape the future of the site.

The council already owns the freehold interest in the site but Plymouth Airport Ltd has 127 years left on the lease, which gives it control of the site.

Holding both the freehold and the long leasehold will give the council complete control of future decisions about the site, the local authority said.

Council leader Tudor Evans said: "For over 15 years the city has been in a holding pattern over this site and while there has been a lot of talk, the fact is there is little anyone could do while the land was leased by a third party."

Tudor Evans said: "By owning the land outright, we are finally in control of decision making for the site that is of huge strategic importance to Plymouth. The Council has been working hard to acquire this land for a long time and I'm pleased we are now in a position to move forward. It has been a complex undertaking, but this is a significant moment in the history of the site."

Tudor added: “We need to be crystal clear about what this decision is and what it isn’t.

“This is a strategic land purchase and is absolutely not a decision on future use. The land is being bought at market value and any future discussions or specific land deals will be subject to a separate decision that need to reflect the city’s policies. This is protecting the future of this site.”
