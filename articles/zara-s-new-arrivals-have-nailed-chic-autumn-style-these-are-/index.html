*adds everything to basket*

It’s tough having to say goodbye to my denim shorts, flowy maxi dresses, flip-flops, and the warm, sunny weather, but I have to admit, I’m always excited when the autumn fashion season rolls around. There’s just something about swapping out my summer looks for cosy layers, new textures, and warm neutral colours that I love.

I’ve been doing some online window shopping to get a head start on my autumn wardrobe, and naturally Zara was my first port of call. The high street hero is always one of my go-to stops, but when I saw the new in section recently, I was genuinely taken aback by how good the selection is.

Not to be dramatic, but scrolling through the latest collection felt like Zara had somehow read my mind or had been stalking my Pinterest board. All the trends I’ve been seeing bubble up lately – like capris, funky cardigans and burgundy – are there, alongside the pieces that have officially become new staples for me – like peplum hems, funnel necks, rugby tops, and polka dots.

Surprise, surprise, it was very hard not to add everything to my online shopping basket. But after extensive internal debate, I managed to narrow it down to 12 pieces that stood out to me. From building out my new season work outfits to getting a jump on party szn with pieces I can add to my nights-out-with-friends rotation, these are what I’ll be reaching for all autumn long and beyond.

These 12 pieces are also the ones I think every fashion girl will have her eye on. And as Cosmopolitan UK is a no-gatekeeping zone, it only felt right to share. Below, find the best of the Zara new-in section for easy, effortless autumn shopping.
