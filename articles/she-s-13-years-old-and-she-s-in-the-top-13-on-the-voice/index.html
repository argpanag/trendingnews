She’s 13 years old — and she’s in the top 13 on ‘The Voice’

TheTools · 13.11.2026 23:52 · source · permalink

At 13 years old, Brianna Cameron is the youngest singer remaining in “The Voice” competition.

But that hasn’t put her at any sort of disadvantage — at all.

Going into “The Voice,” the rising star from New Jersey had already appeared on “The Jennifer Hudson Show,” had a one-time role in the CBS crime drama “The Equalizer,” and starred in a national touring production of Broadway’s “Tina: The Tina Turner Musical.”

Now, as a member of Kelly Clarkson’s team on “The Voice,” Cameron has become a strong contender to win the show’s 30th season.

Here’s a look at her impressive run so far.

Brianna Cameron auditions for ‘The Voice’

Cameron, who recently toured the country as young Tina Turner in the national tour of the Broadway musical, again channeled her inner Turner with a performance of “River Deep - Mountain High” during her blind audition for “The Voice.”

Clarkson was the first to turn, followed shortly by country singer Riley Green. When Cameron revealed her age, Clarkson ran up to the stage and gave her a hug.

“I thought you were grown!” she exclaimed.

“I could barely even speak when I was 13 years old,” Adam Levine joked. “That is quite a song for any human being to attempt.”

First-time coach Queen Latifah, who like Cameron is from New Jersey and also happened to be in the crime drama “The Equalizer,” shook her head with regret that she didn’t turn her chair around.

“You are meant to be on this show,” she said.

Although Cameron is only 13, Green said she has “the biggest voice” he’s heard on the show.

“I have no question that you can sing anything,” he said.

After some deliberation, Cameron ultimately chose to join Clarkson’s team.

“I can get you to the finale because you have that drive,” Clarkson told her. “And at 13, to have the sense you have already is incredible.”

Clarkson later told “Voice” producers she believes Cameron is already more prepared than she was when she first entered the music industry.

“I’m very excited to work with her,” said Clarkson, who was 20 when she won “American Idol.”

‘It’s like we’re watching young Whitney Houston’

“The Voice” coaches still appeared to be shocked by Cameron’s age during the Battle round of the competition, where she performed Duffy’s “Mercy” with fellow Clarkson member Saade.

“To be able to do this up here at your age is shockingly amazing,” Levine said.

“I love the ownership you took of the stage,” Latifah added.

After calling Cameron’s voice “insane,” Clarkson chose to advance the singer forward in the competition.

The teenager survived Cut Week — the largest single-week elimination in the show’s history — with a performance of Natalie Cole’s “Inseparable” that had Clarkson fighting back tears and Latifah standing up out of her chair.

“It’s like we’re watching young Whitney Houston,” Clarkson said.

“This is almost unfathomable that you can sing like this, with this poise, at this age,” Latifah added. “That was a beautiful performance of a beautiful song. This is when I thank God for being in this chair and being able to see what I see.”

What happens next on ‘The Voice’?

Cameron is one of seven singers who will compete for a spot in the top 10 during the live episode airing on Monday, Nov. 16.

Voting from viewers determines who from Clarkson and Green’s teams will round out the top 10.

Per the Deseret News, the five singers currently in the top 10 are:

  • Mia Knight, Team Queen
  • BigCountry, Team Queen
  • Lexi Stephens, Team Adam
  • Connor Closs, Team Adam
  • Dakota Cohen, Team Adam

The one-hour episode this coming Monday airs 8 p.m. MST on NBC.
