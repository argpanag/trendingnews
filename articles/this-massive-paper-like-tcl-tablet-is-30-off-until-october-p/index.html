This Massive Paper-Like TCL Tablet Is 30% Off Until October Prime Day Ends Tonight

Table of Contents

October Prime Day is here, and if you're looking for a versatile Android tablet, TCL's NXTPAPER 14, with a massive matte screen with different viewing modes for reading, streaming video, and writing, is 30% off during the sale.

With a 14.3-inch display, the NXTPAPER 14 might not be as portable as other tablets on the market, but the screen itself is the biggest perk. The matte display has a paper-like feel and three different viewing modes: You can switch from black-and-white Ink Paper Mode to Color Paper Mode (a must for comics) to full color (for streaming video or using other full-motion apps) with the push of a button. Under harsh, direct lighting, it does an impressive job of reducing glare and reflections. It also has 8GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, and a large 10,000mAh battery. TCL claims up to 10.8 hours of video playback, though actual battery life will vary depending on use.

The tablet comes with a folio-style case with a magnetic cover that doubles as a stand, a long charging cable, and a pressure-sensitive rechargeable stylus called the T-Pen, which offers 4,096 levels of pressure sensitivity. Combined with the matte texture of the display, the T-Pen creates a distinctive drag that you don’t get when using an Apple Pencil on an untreated glass screen. The tablet also has a useful Edge Bar feature: Swipe inward from the edge and hold to reveal a customizable mini dock containing shortcuts and tools. The 14.3-inch display makes these productivity features particularly useful, giving you enough room to keep reference material, notes, and other apps visible at once.

The NXTPAPER 14 isn't a true e-ink device; it remains a backlit LCD, so it won’t provide Kindle-like battery life or outdoor visibility, but the matte screen aims to be easier on your eyes. Its 60Hz refresh rate is also less fluid than the screens on many premium tablets, and it lacks a microSD slot to expand the included 256GB of storage. Whether you’re an artist, love sketching or reading comics, need to review and sign documents, or simply want a larger screen without all the bells and whistles of a premium tablet, the sizable NXTPAPER 14 is an excellent multitasking canvas with some of the reading and writing benefits associated with paper-like displays without the limitations of e-ink.
