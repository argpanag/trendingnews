Real Salt Lake takes on San Jose tonight, with kickoff set for 7:30 p.m. at America First Field. The match will celebrate Hispanic Heritage Month, highlighting the culture and community that help make Utah special.

With just six games remaining in the regular season, including three home matches, there’s still time for fans to cheer on RSL in person.

Kyle Schroeder, RSL’s vice president of community relations, joins Fresh Living to discuss tonight’s matchup, the Hispanic Heritage celebration, and what fans can look forward to as the season enters its final stretch.

Tickets are still available at RSL.com.
