GAME THREAD: Miami Heat (7-6) seek fifth straight win in showdown with Kevin Durant, Houston Rockets (11-3)

TheTools · 17.11.2026 00:35 · source · permalink

The Miami Heat are back in action tonight at Kaseya Center, hosting the Houston Rockets as they look to extend their winning streak to five games and continue their impressive turnaround.

After a difficult 0-4 start to the 2026-27 NBA season, Miami has found its rhythm. The Heat enter Monday night’s matchup with a 7-6 record following four consecutive victories, including an impressive 137-117 win over the Detroit Pistons on Saturday.

The victory was particularly encouraging considering Detroit had already defeated Miami earlier this season. Now, Erik Spoelstra’s squad has an opportunity to keep building momentum against one of the Western Conference’s hottest teams.

Houston enters tonight’s matchup with an 11-3 record, having established itself as an early contender behind the combination of Kevin Durant and Alperen Şengün.

The Rockets are coming off a dominant 147-119 victory over the Washington Wizards on Sunday, giving them plenty of confidence heading into Miami. However, Houston will be playing on the second night of a back-to-back, while the Heat have had a day to recover.

For Miami, the continued development of Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo as a frontcourt tandem remains one of the biggest storylines. The Heat have also benefited from improved contributions throughout their rotation, including the energy and defensive intensity provided by Myron Gardner.

Tonight presents another opportunity for Miami to prove that its recent surge is more than simply a favorable stretch of the schedule.

With Durant’s scoring ability and Şengün’s versatility presenting difficult defensive assignments, the Heat will need another complete team effort to extend their winning streak.

Can Miami make it five straight and take down one of the West’s top teams?
