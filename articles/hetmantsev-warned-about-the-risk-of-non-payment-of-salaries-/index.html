This is reported by Espresso, citing a statement from the head of the Verkhovna Rada Committee on Finance, Taxation, and Customs Policy, Danilo Hetmantsev, during the program "What’s Next?"

According to the member of parliament, by the end of the year, the military budget lacks about 27 billion dollars, and this deficit can only be covered with the support of international partners. At the same time, the Verkhovna Rada is not passing a number of bills that are crucial for the receipt of external funding.

Hetmantsev stated that due to the failure to meet obligations, Ukraine is already not receiving 4.2 billion euros in financial aid and also risks losing another 1.6 billion dollars under the programs of the International Monetary Fund. In his opinion, such a situation may directly affect payments from the state budget.

The MP criticized deputies who do not support the necessary legislative changes, emphasizing that the country currently needs unpopular but responsible decisions. In particular, this concerns the bill on the cancellation of the exemption for international parcels worth up to 150 euros, the adoption of which is linked to the conditions for receiving funding from the IMF and the European Union
