LG rolls out Lionsgate pop-up FAST channels in US

October 2, 2026

LG has announced a slate of new pop-up FAST channels on LG Channels in the US, in partnership with Lionsgate.

From now until the end of the year, LG Channels will be the FAST destination for viewers to find dedicated channels featuring three of Lionsgate’s most iconic film franchises: The Hunger Games, Saw and John Wick.

The channels will be available:

  • THE HUNGER GAMES (October 1st – October 15th)
    • Features all five films in the franchise, The Hunger Games pop-up gives diehard fans and new viewers alike the opportunity to catch up on the series before The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping lands in cinemas on November 20th.
  • SAW (October 16th – November 4th)
    • For the first time ever, the box office juggernaut franchise will be available as a standalone channel on FAST. The channel includes eight films to watch this spooky season, with all films also available on-demand.
  • JOHN WICK (December 17th – December 31st)
    • LG Channels will bring the action this holiday season by packaging the four-film franchise on a dedicated channel. This is the first time the John Wick franchise (pictured) will be made available as a FAST channel.

“By turning fan-favourite movies into curated, limited-time viewing events around moments when they’re most relevant – from Saw for Halloween to John Wick for the holidays – LG Channels is making premium entertainment easy to discover and enjoy for free, while adding even more value to the LG Channels and webOS experience”, commented Matthew Durgin, Vice President, Media Entertainment Services North America.

In addition to the exclusive pop-up channels, LG Channels expands its Lionsgate portfolio to include four new to LG FAST channels: Pulse, a medical drama channel featuring Nurse Jackie, Saving Hope and more; Ghosts, a single series channel dedicated to the popular CBS comedy, Ghosts; Lionsgate Network, featuring premium series and films, including Private Eyes and La La Land; and a single-series channel dedicated to Rookie Blue.
