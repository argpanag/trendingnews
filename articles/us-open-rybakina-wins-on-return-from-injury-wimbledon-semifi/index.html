US Open: Rybakina wins on return from injury: Wimbledon semifinalist Ferry eliminated; Lorenzo Musetti wins in straight sets

TheTools · 02.09.2026 04:22 · source · permalink

  • Sports
  • US Open: Rybakina Returns With Win | Fery Eliminated By Musetti
  • Copy link

Elena Rybakina made a winning return from injury in the first round of the US Open being played in New York, while Wimbledon semifinalist Arthur Fery's campaign came to an end.

Rybakina defeated 17-year-old American wildcard Thea Frodin 6-3, 6-2, while Britain's Fery was defeated by Italy's Lorenzo Musetti 6-3, 6-3, 7-5. Rybakina is now in the race to achieve the world number 1 ranking. Fery is out of the US Open after enjoying brilliant run of form over the last few months.

Rybakina returns after injury, wins match in 1 hour 14 minutes

Rybakina returned to the court for the first time since retiring from her quarter-final match against Iga Swiatek in Cincinnati. She had to leave that match unfinished due to a left ankle injury. Returning to the US Open after about two weeks, Rybakina showed no signs of injury against Frodin and registered a 6-3, 6-2 victory in 1 hour and 14 minutes. She won the first set 6-3.

In the second set, Frodin tried to make a comeback, but Rybakina maintained the pressure and took a 4-1 lead. The American player saved two match points and later fended off another opportunity, but Rybakina won it 6-2.

Only 434 points behind in the race to become No. 1

Rybakina is currently 434 points behind Aryna Sabalenka. If she reaches the semi-finals in this US Open, she will become the world's No. 1 player for the first time, even if Sabalenka wins the title. However, if none of Sabalenka, Coco Gauff, or Jessica Pegula become the champion, Rybakina could achieve the No. 1 ranking regardless of her own performance.

Rybakina said that she is motivated to become world number 1, but currently wants to take it match-by-match. She mentioned that after her injury, she did not play tennis for about a week and could not do much movement-based training either.

17-year-old Frodin's Grand Slam debut

Frodin was playing in the main draw of a Grand Slam for the first time. She earned a wildcard to the US Open by winning the USTA Girls' 18s National Championship.

Fery's journey ends at US Open after Wimbledon

In the match played on the Grandstand court, Fery showed aggressive play in the initial rounds, but Musetti won the first set 6-3.

In the second set as well, Musetti did not give Fery a chance to make a comeback and won the set 6-3. Fery struggled in the third set, but Musetti won the set 7-5 to clinch the match.

Reached the semi-finals at Wimbledon

Fery put up a brilliant performance at Wimbledon this year while playing as a wildcard. He became the fifth British male player and the second wildcard in the Open Era to reach the semifinals there. He was defeated by Alexander Zverev in the semifinals.

Before Wimbledon, Fery's ranking was 114, but after that performance, he became Britain's number-1 player and reached a new career high. He entered the US Open as the world number-33.

Musetti's next match is against Dane Sweeny

Musetti has secured a spot in the second round with this victory. He will now face Australia's Dane Sweeny. The Italian player had missed the last two major tournaments due to injury and has started his comeback with a win at the US Open.

Monfils wins on his 40th birthday

Playing in his final US Open on his 40th birthday, France's Gael Monfils registered a special victory. In New York, he defeated Paraguay's Adolfo Daniel Vallejo 2-6, 6-1, 6-2, 6-0 to advance to the second round. With this, Monfils became the oldest player to win a match in the US Open main draw since Jimmy Connors in 1992. After the match, the spectators present in the stadium made his victory memorable by singing 'Happy Birthday'.
