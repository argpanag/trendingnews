The Witcher 3 Remastered Mod Makes Movement and Combat More Responsive

TheTools · 14.10.2026 09:57 · source · permalink

Modder ‘bytevoid’ has released a new mod for The Witcher 3 Remastered that aims to make the game’s movement and combat more responsive. This is a mod that a lot of TW3 fans may appreciate. So, let’s take a closer look at it.

Going into more detail, the mod cuts input delay and helps Geralt react sooner to your commands. It also keeps the game’s original animations and balance.

Geralt will now stop sooner when you let go of a key or button. He also turns faster while running. Small changes in direction no longer slow him to a walk. Starting, stopping, and turning around feel faster, too.

These changes blend into the game’s own moves. This helps keep them smooth and natural. The mod does not add new animations. It also does not change damage or stamina.

Sword attacks will now flow into each other more quickly. The mod can also help Geralt hit the foe you aim at. His sword swings and signs can target the foe you picked, rather than miss and hit empty air.

You can also set his attacks to aim at the closest foe. Other options let Geralt turn toward a foe faster before a swing. You can also make attack lunges shorter and cast signs faster.

And that’s not all. Roach will also feel more responsive. The horse slows down sooner when you stop. It also turns faster and picks up speed more quickly. You can even make it gallop as soon as you press the key. You can also get off your horse with one tap. This works with both a keyboard and a gamepad.

It’s also worth noting that the mod has four settings pages: Movement, Horse, Combat and Targeting, and Other. Each page has its own switch. You can also pick a preset or change each option on its own.

Finally, there are five main presets for movement and combat. These are Off, Gentle, Recommended, Precise, and Unleashed.

Those interested can go ahead and download the mod from this link.
