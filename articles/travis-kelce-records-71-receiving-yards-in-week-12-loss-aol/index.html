Travis Kelce records 71 receiving yards in Week 12 loss - AOL

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:32 · source · permalink

Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce recorded four receptions for a team-high 71 yards with seven targets in Week 12 against the Buffalo Bills.

Why it Matters: The conditions were less than optimal, with a constant light rain in Western New York, but Kelce did a good job helping out his quarterback. Kelce and the Chiefs are back at it next Thursday on the road against the Los Angeles Rams. He remains a solid TE1 in most fantasy leagues.
