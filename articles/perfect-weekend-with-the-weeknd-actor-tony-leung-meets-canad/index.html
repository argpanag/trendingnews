‘Perfect weekend with The Weeknd’: Actor Tony Leung meets Canadian pop star in Hong Kong

  • The Weeknd arrived in Hong Kong for four After Hours Til Dawn shows at Kai Tak Stadium on Oct 24, 25, 30 and 31.

Canadian pop titan Abel Tesfaye, better known as The Weeknd, arrived in Hong Kong ahead of his four-night run at Kai Tak Stadium, part of his massive After Hours Til Dawn global tour.

In a “crossover” that has generated online chatter, the 36-year-old singer met and had dinner with Hong Kong movie superstar Tony Leung Chiu Wai and his actress-wife Carina Lau at the weekend.

Lau, 60, shared on social media on Oct 17 several photos of the trio in Hong Kong.

“What a perfect weekend with The Weeknd,” she wrote in English.

One of the photos she shared showed The Weeknd leaving a thank-you note on the dinner menu on Oct 16.

“Thank you for an incredible night, your friend The Weeknd. To Carina & Tony,” he wrote.

The Weeknd also shared on Instagram Stories a photo of him in conversation with Leung, 64. The post was set to the music of Hong Kong singer Faye Wong’s song Dream Lover (1994), a Cantonese cover of Irish rock band The Cranberries’ classic hit Dreams (1992).

Dream Lover was featured in Hong Kong movie Chungking Express (1994), starring Leung, Wong and now retired actress Brigitte Lin Ching-hsia. The film, directed by Hong Kong film-maker Wong Kar Wai, won Leung the Best Leading Actor accolade at the Golden Horse Awards in 1994.

According to Hong Kong media outlet The Standard, The Weeknd has previously shown an interest in Hong Kong culture, including sharing Faye Wong’s album Fuzao (1996) on social media in late September.

The After Hours Til Dawn global tour will be held in Hong Kong on Oct 24, 25, 30 and 31.

The Weeknd previously performed two sold-out nights in Singapore at the National Stadium on Oct 2 and 3. He then took the stage in Goyang, South Korea, on Oct 7 and 8, and in Bangkok from Oct 11 to 13.
