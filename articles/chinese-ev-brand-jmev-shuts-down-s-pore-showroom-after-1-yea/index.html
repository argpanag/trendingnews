Chinese EV brand JMEV shuts down S'pore showroom after 1 year

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:06 · source · permalink

It is the second Chinese EV brand to exit the Singaporean market recently.

November 23, 2026, 05:37 PM

Photo from JMEV

Chinese electric vehicle (EV) brand JMEV has shut down its showroom in Singapore, a year after opening.

Another Chinese EV brand, Neta, also shut down its Singapore showroom in April 2025.

The brand's distributor, Valor Auto, had its showroom situated at Telok Kurau Road near Joo Chiat, and showcased JMEV's first model on Jul. 29, 2025.

According to The Straits Times (ST), the showroom was empty and boarded up when reporters visited on Nov. 18.

Its Singapore website is also no longer accessible.

According to ST, Land Transport Authority (LTA) records show that only 28 units of the JMEV Elight saloon have been registered in Singapore since 2025.

According to the May 2026 price list, the cars were sold with a 10-year battery warranty, five-year vehicle warranty and six years of routine servicing, priced at S$208,777 inclusive of COE.

When launched, the brand launched a special SG60 promotion, offering the car for S$156,888 including COE.

What to do?

An automotive sector expert told ST that if a car dealership folds, the local portion of the warranty will no longer be valid, leaving only the factory warranty.

A lawyer also added that affected car owners have no legal recourse if the distributor closes.

This does not mean that the car is not serviceable, however.

One car workshop director said that the automobile supply chain in China was robust, and car owners could source their parts from online portals such as AliExpress.

Nevertheless, more proprietary issues, such as software updates, cannot be managed by third-party workshops.
