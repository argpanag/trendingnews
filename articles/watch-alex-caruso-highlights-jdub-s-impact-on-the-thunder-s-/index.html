WATCH: Alex Caruso highlights JDub's impact on the Thunder's defense - Community Post

TheTools · 03.10.2026 02:05 · source · permalink

All the talk about Jalen Williams being back and healthy for the Thunder has focused almost exclusively on his offense, not everything that he can do on the other end too.

Today at Oklahoma City Thunder training camp, I asked one of the best defenders in the world, Alex Caruso, about what getting back Jalen Williams will mean for an already elite Thunder defensive unit.

"Yeah, I mean, Dub's a pretty complete basketball player, you know what I mean? He was an All-Defensive team member as well as All-NBA that year he played the full year for us, so just getting a versatile player back. You know, that's something that I think that in the current age of basketball and state of the game. We need guys that can do a little bit of everything. And he does a little bit of everything at a high level."
