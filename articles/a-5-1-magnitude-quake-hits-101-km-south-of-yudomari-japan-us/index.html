A 5.1-magnitude quake hits 101 km south of Yudomari, Japan: USGS

TheTools · 13.09.2026 12:44 · source · permalink

BEIJING: An earthquake with a magnitude of 5.1 jolted 101 km south of Yudomari, Japan at 2032 GMT on Saturday (Sept 12), the US Geological Survey said.

The epicentre, with a depth of 45.5 km, was initially determined to be at 29.32 degrees north latitude and 130.38 degrees east longitude. - Xinhua
