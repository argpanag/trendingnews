UFC Vegas 124: Keiichiro Nakamura vs. Ollie Schmid Predictions & Odds

TheTools · 08.11.2026 00:19 · source · permalink

Featherweight contenders Keiichiro Nakamura and Ollie Schmid are scheduled to meet at UFC Vegas 124 on Saturday at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas.

Fresh off a win in his octagon debut, Nakamura heads into the bout as the betting favorite with -350 UFC odds, while Schmid, who dropped his first fight in the UFC, is going to be the +275 betting underdog.

UFC Vegas 124: Keiichiro Nakamura vs. Ollie Schmid Fight Odds

  • Keiichiro Nakamura: -350
  • Ollie Schmid: +275

UFC Vegas 124: Keiichiro Nakamura vs. Ollie Schmid: Tale of the Tape

UFC Vegas 124: Keiichiro Nakamura vs. Ollie Schmid: Prediction

My prediction is a TKO/KO win for Nakamura over Schmid because he’s the more measured striker, and his style fits Schmid’s perfectly. Nakamura has a karate style, which is a very kick-heavy approach.

Nakamura can chop down Schmid’s lead leg to limit his movement and reduce his power. Nakamura’s variety of kicks and strikes should also play a factor, but he needs to start fast.

Schmid’s last fight exposed that he’s very uncomfortable when pressured against the cage. It became his downfall because he threw strikes just to keep his opponent at bay, which is why he got countered and eventually finished.

Keiichiro Nakamura’s Recent Fight Results

Nakamura won the Road to UFC in 2025 and earned a UFC contract. He fought Sebastian Szalay in his UFC debut at UFC 325 in February and made the most of it by scoring a third-round TKO finish.

Ollie Schmid’s Recent Fight Results

The UFC signed Schmid earlier this year after an impressive run outside the organization. He was pitted against Marwan Rahiki in his octagon debut in May, but suffered a first-round stoppage defeat.
