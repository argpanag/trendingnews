Micron Technology (MU:NASDAQ) has lost 25% from its June high, even as its profits kept climbing. Needham thinks the market has it wrong: on Thursday, it reiterated its Buy rating and $1,650 price target, 81% above Wednesday's close.

AI jitters, then China

Micron closed at a record $1,213.56 on June 25, the day after its fiscal third-quarter results. Then worries over how long AI spending could last took hold, and the stock fell more than 40% from that peak before turning higher in August. (It's still up 350% over the past year.)

On Oct. 12, reports of progress at Chinese memory maker CXMT set off a two-day sell-off. Micron slid again before Thursday's open, along with other AI chip stocks.

None of that shows up in Micron's results: fiscal fourth-quarter revenue rose 379% to $54.2 billion.

Needham set its target at the top

Needham analyst Quinn Bolton raised his target to $1,650 in late June, just as the stock peaked. He valued Micron at 9x his fiscal 2028 earnings estimate of $184 a share. He reiterated the target on Oct. 1 and again on Thursday, when RBC Capital also stuck with its Buy and $1,500 target.

Does $1,650 add up?

Most of Wall Street agrees. The consensus target has climbed from $206 a year ago to $1,561, its high for the year…

Needham's target is a little above that, and well below D.A. Davidson's Street-high $3,000.

Earnings estimates keep rising, too. Consensus for fiscal 2027 is $176.50 a share, up from $159.46 a month ago. Fiscal 2028's is $206.85, well above the $184 Needham used in June…

Nothing in the recent selling should pull those down. Micron has already signed deals for most of its calendar 2027 HBM supply "with significant price increases year-over-year," CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said on the fiscal fourth-quarter call.
