Iconic British rock band announce first Australian tour in 15 years

TheTools · 07.09.2026 09:49 · source · permalink
  • Breaking
  • Entertainment
  • Showbiz

September 7, 2026 - 7.49am

Iconic British rock band Radiohead will return to Australia for the first time in 15 years, announcing a 16-show tour for 2027.

The band’s Australian tour will begin in Perth at the RAC Arena on Monday, May 3, followed by several shows at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne, and six shows at Afterpay Arena in Sydney to end the trip.

English rock band RADIOHEAD last performed in Australia in 2012. Edwina Pickles

The last show will be held on on Tuesday, June 1.

Registration for tickets opened at 5pm on Monday, and will close on Thursday at 5pm.
