EXCLUSIVE: Big Brother fans divided over eviction, but Harmon backs this one housemate to 'go far'

There’s no shortage of drama inside the Big Brother Australia house, and the latest eviction nominations have viewers scrambling to predict who will be next to go.

With seven housemates facing the public vote, fans are questioning whether Sunday could bring a double eviction, while others are frustrated by the show’s latest voting format.

But it’s not just viewers making predictions. Speaking exclusively to WHO, recently evicted housemate Harmon has revealed who he thinks could make it all the way to the end, and his pick might surprise fans.

As the competition heats up, the question is whether Australia will save their favourites or send another housemate packing.

Big Brother fans predict who will leave next

Anita, Kyrah, Pam, Tahlia, Ryan, Matty and Pheobe are all up for eviction, leaving viewers with plenty of decisions to make.

The current format asks fans to vote to save their favourite housemate, with the contestant receiving the fewest votes at risk of leaving. And with so many names on the chopping block, some viewers are wondering whether Sunday could see two housemates sent home.

View this post on Instagram

“Surely it’s a double on Sunday with over half of the house up like with the show only being 6 weeks it’s a good opportunity to get rid of at least 2 with that many up,” one viewer speculated.

Others are less concerned about the number of evictions and more frustrated with the voting system itself.

“Too many up for eviction. It needs to be vote to EVICT, not save,” one fan wrote.

Another agreed, commenting: “I agree the vote to keep is kind of weird, so much easier if you just vote for one person that you want evicted.”

Big Brother fans reveal who they want to save

Matty has emerged as a popular choice among fans, with viewers praising his ability to stir up drama without taking things too seriously.

“I love Matty, my favourite. He loves to stir the pot, without being rude or whiney, he’s just fun to watch,” one viewer wrote.

Another fan was equally enthusiastic, saying: “I saved Matty as he is good to watch and a good agitator in the house. He is what BB stands for.”

Ryan has also received plenty of support, with some viewers hoping he can survive the vote alongside Matty.

“I voted [to] save Ryan. I was tossing up between him and Matty. Hopefully Phoebe or Pam go….. Come on Australia save Matty and Ryan,” one fan commented.

Others think Pam, Pheobe or Anita could be in trouble.

Harmon reveals who he thinks will go far

While viewers are busy predicting the next eviction, Harmon has already shared his thoughts on who could make it deep into the competition.

Despite his own early exit, the former housemate believes one contestant is playing a strong game.

“I reckon Ryan’s playing a good game. I think Ryan will go pretty far,” Harmon told WHO.

Ryan and his sister Tahlia entered the house together while keeping their sibling relationship a secret from the other contestants. Harmon admitted he didn’t pick up on their connection until it was revealed.

“I remember sitting there thinking, ‘Far out’. I guess if it was an intimate relationship, maybe you could piece things together. But because they specified that it could be any relationship, it was actually kind of hard,” he said. “Maddie kind of threw a spanner in the works there. But Tahlia’s face went kind of red, so we knew it was her.”

With Ryan now facing the public vote alongside six other housemates, Harmon’s prediction could soon be put to the test.
