SHAREHOLDER ALERT: Levi & Korsinsky, LLP Notifies Stockholders of an Investigation into the Fairness of the Acquisition of The Baldwin Insurance Group, Inc. (BWIN)

PR Newswire

NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026

NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The following statement is being issued by Levi & Korsinsky, LLP:

Levi & Korsinsky, LLP announces that it has commenced an investigation into the fairness of the acquisition of The Baldwin Insurance Group, Inc. by Sequence Holdings and DFO Management announced on September 14, 2026. As a result of the acquisition, BWIN stockholders are expected to receive only $32.50 per share in cash in exchange for each share of common stock.

To request more information, go to:

https://zlk.com/cases/the-baldwin-insurance-group-inc-investigation-bwin 

or contact Joseph E. Levi, Esq. either via email at jlevi@levikorsinsky.com or by telephone at (212) 363-7500. There is no cost or obligation to you.
