India's Kishan ruled out of second New Zealand T20 with adductor strain

TheTools · 24.10.2026 10:39 · source · permalink

Sport

Cricket - Second T20 International - England v India - Old Trafford, Manchester, Britain - July 4, 2026 India's Ishan Kishan in action Action Images via Reuters/Craig Brough

Oct 23 : India batter Ishan Kishan has been ruled out of the second Twenty20 against New Zealand with a low-grade right adductor strain, the country's cricket board (BCCI) said on Friday.

The 28-year-old suffered the injury during India's four-wicket win in the opening match of the five-game series on Thursday, with the BCCI adding that its medical team is monitoring his recovery.

The second T20 will be played in Christchurch on Saturday.
