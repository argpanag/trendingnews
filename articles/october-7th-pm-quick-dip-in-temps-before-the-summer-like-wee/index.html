IA, NE, SD (KCAU) — Cooler day for the region with highs just in the 70s compared to yesterday's 80s. Still sunny for most of the area, as winds have been settling down for the region. Quiet conditions will extend through the evening hours, with cooler nighttime temps.

Warming up again for Thursday with some clouds, temperatures will be back in the mid and upper 70s for the region. A few showers and sprinkles are possible overnight into Friday morning, but the clouds will be brief, with sunshine still expected for the day. Temperatures will rise again, pushing into the upper 70s and low 80s, and warm further on Saturday.

Clouds then appear on Sunday, as temperatures fall, but the real drop in temperatures will be through the work week. Highs will fall into the 60s, along with cloudier skies for Siouxland. Those clouds bring some rain chances as well, so we could see wet conditions next week.
