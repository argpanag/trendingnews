Hanwha Systems Posts 831.6 Billion Won in Third-Quarter Revenue on Defense Growth - The Elec Inc.

TheTools · 27.10.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

Nine-month revenue reaches 2.36 trillion won

Hanwha Systems said on Oct. 27 that it posted third-quarter revenue of 831.6 billion won and operating profit of 99.1 billion won, driven by strong performance in its defense business. Revenue rose 30.1% from a year earlier, while operating profit increased 60.5%. Compared with the previous quarter, revenue declined 6.3% and operating profit fell 19.1%.

For the first nine months, cumulative revenue reached 2.3636 trillion won and operating profit totaled 302.4 billion won. In just nine months, the company generated revenue equivalent to 64% of its 2025 total and operating profit equivalent to 252%.

The defense division drove earnings through large-scale export projects, including multifunction radars (MFRs) for the Cheongung-II air defense system, as well as domestic programs involving active electronically scanned array (AESA) radars and avionics equipment for South Korea’s KF-21 fighter jet. The division’s order backlog stood at 8.3956 trillion won.

The information and communications technology (ICT) division supported earnings primarily through IT system implementation projects for affiliates, including Hanwha Life’s next-generation system and an enterprise resource planning (ERP) system for Hanwha Philly Shipyard. The division’s order backlog totaled 469.7 billion won.

Net profit fell 97.5% year on year to 3.8 billion won. The company classified an accounting loss arising from the sale of Hanwha Philly Shipyard under discontinued operations.

Hanwha Systems expects order growth in the 20% range in 2026 compared with 2025, supported by expanding overseas defense exports, additional space-related contracts and ERP implementation projects for affiliates in its ICT division.

In September, the company signed a heads of terms agreement with EDGE Group, a state-owned defense company in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), for an integrated air defense network project. Hanwha Systems aims to expand its defense business from supplying individual weapons systems to providing comprehensive defense systems. Follow-up negotiations are currently underway.
