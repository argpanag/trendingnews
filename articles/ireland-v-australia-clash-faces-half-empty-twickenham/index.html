Ireland v Australia clash faces half-empty Twickenham

TheTools · 23.11.2026 19:47 · source · permalink

Getty

Ireland's play-off clash with Australia could be played in front of a half-empty Twickenham as sluggish ticket sales look set to blight the first night of the Nations Championship finals this weekend.

Friday's double-header featuring Italy v Fiji and Ireland v Australia appears on course to be played out in front of a crowd of less than half the stadium’s 82,000 capacity.

The tournament’s official ticket website is showing swathes of tickets still available in most sections with the exception of the top tier, which is expected to be closed.

Officials acknowledged earlier this month that sales could be an issue, with fans having to wait until last weekend to discover the finalised schedule for the three-day showpiece.

Tickets for Saturday’s two games – Wales v Japan and England v New Zealand – are stronger, and they are also better for Sunday, when Scotland play Argentina before South Africa and France contest the final.

Earlier this month, RFU chief executive Bill Sweeney admitted Twickenham was in "unchartered waters" with regard to the ticket sales for the inaugural tournament, which brings together all the major northern and southern hemisphere nations.

Speaking prior to its opening weekend earlier this month, Sweeney added: "This is the first time we’ve done this.

"A lot of fans are waiting to figure out who is playing who and when. You’ve got to go through those pool matches before you know you you’re playing on finals weekend. It’s a bit like a condensed World Cup."

Nations Championship officials have been contacted for comment.
