Major weather shift for B.C and Alberta

TheTools · 25.09.2026 07:21 · source · permalink

Meteorologist Aman Purewal breaks down a significant weather pattern change for Western Canada. A strong low-pressure system will bring widespread thunderstorms, heavy rain, and strong winds to British Columbia and Alberta, followed by a sharp cooldown and even snow in the mountains.
