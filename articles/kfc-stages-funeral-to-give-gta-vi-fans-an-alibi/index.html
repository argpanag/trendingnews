KFC stages funeral to give GTA VI fans an alibi

TheTools · 16.11.2026 03:04 · source · permalink
  • Home
  • >
  • Marketing

The Go Full Chicken activation gives gamers a fictional farewell, a Gold Coast service and a nationwide Instagram stream

If you’re dying to get your hands on GTA VI but your boss won’t let you bury yourself in the game on launch day, KFC has cooked up an alibi: a funeral.

The fast-food chain will bid farewell to fictional character Ann Alibi at KFC Robina on the Gold Coast on Thursday, 19 November, with an 11am service conveniently scheduled during working hours.

KFC’s premise is that fans looking to spend the day playing can attend a farewell instead of calling in sick. The campaign is an independent initiative, with no official partnership with Rockstar Games or Grand Theft Auto VI.

A farewell during working hours

The Robina restaurant will become a funeral setting, complete with a book of condolences and an order of service. Attendees will be able to sign the book, take commemorative photographs and collect documentation of their attendance.

A KFC spokesperson said:

"We appreciate that Australians have responsibilities, but some occasions are simply too important to miss. And in accordance with Ann's final wishes, we've made sure the funeral takes place during working hours.

"Of course, we understand not everyone can make it to the funeral, which is why we're livestreaming the occasion and offering free KFC delivery for those wishing to pay their respects from home."

Taking the send-off nationwide

The service will be livestreamed nationally through KFC’s Instagram from 11am, extending the activation beyond the physical event.

In-person attendees will be offered free KFC, subject to availability. Those watching remotely can access free delivery through the KFC App throughout the day on orders over $30, subject to promotional terms.

The activation links KFC’s Go Full Chicken platform to anticipation around the game’s release, combining an in-person stunt, social livestream, and app-based promotion.

The public service is planned for KFC Robina at 118–120 Robina Town Centre Drive, subject to final site approvals, with funeral attire encouraged.

One qualification to the alibi: KFC’s campaign notes make clear that attending does not automatically qualify an employee for compassionate leave or other workplace leave.
