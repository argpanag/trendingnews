Miss Universe 2026: Minute-by-minute coverage of the grand finale

TheTools · 25.11.2026 01:05 · source · permalink

MISS UNIVERSE

The 75th edition of Miss Universe is underway and one of 116 contestants will be crowned the successor to Fátima Bosch

The night of the 75th Miss Universe pageant has arrived! Contestants from 116 countries and territories have gathered at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in Puerto Rico for the final competition, where one of them will take home the crown and succeed Fátima Bosch.

After weeks of events and preparation, the contestants are ready to take the stage and make their final impression on the judges. By the end of the night, one woman will be named Miss Universe 2026.

Another moment everyone will be watching is Fátima's farewell. The Mexican beauty queen will take the stage one last time as Miss Universe before passing on her crown and closing out a year that saw her represent Mexico around the world.

Follow along as we bring you the biggest moments, appearances, and updates from the 75th Miss Universe finale.

​The Miss Universe 2026 red carpet

Before the competition got underway, the Miss Universe red carpet brought out familiar faces, including Bosch. The reigning queen made her entrance wearing a jewel-encrusted gown, long diamond earrings, and the crown she has worn throughout her reign, known as "The Light of Infinity," created by Jewelmer; the crown features diamonds, gold, and pearls and is the first-ever Filipino-crafted crown for Miss Universe.

Queens reunite on the red carpet

Fátima wasn't alone for her final night as Miss Universe. The Mexican beauty queen was joined by three former titleholders: Sheynnis Palacios (Miss Universe 2024), Zuleyka Rivera (Miss Universe 2006) and Alicia Machado (Miss Universe 1996).

View post on Instagram
 

The four queens reunited on the red carpet and also spoke with ¡HOLA!, sharing a moment together ahead of the night's main event.

Victoria Kjær Theilvig attends Miss Universe 2026

Victoria Kjær Theilvig, who represented Denmark and won Miss Universe 2024, was also among the familiar faces at this year's competition. She joined the celebrations for the 75th edition of the pageant.

View post on Instagram
 

Jacky Bracamontes

Jacky Bracamontes turned heads on the red carpet in a bright pink gown. The Telemundo host has a special connection to this year's pageant, as she is returning to Puerto Rico 25 years after competing in Miss Universe in 2001. That year, Puerto Rico's own Denise Quiñones took home the crown.

View post on Instagram
 

The Miss Universe 2026 opening

La India, Denise Quiñones and Didi Romero took the stage for the opening performance of the night. As the three performers sang high above the stage, the 116 contestants made their entrance, walking across the stage and waving to the crowd at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot.
