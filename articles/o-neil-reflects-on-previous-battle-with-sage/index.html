O’Neil reflects on previous battle with Sage

TheTools · 11.09.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

The Ipswich’s manager stint at Strasbourg has aided his pre-match preparation.

Gary O’Neil offered his thoughts on Crystal Palace, the next opponents for Ipswich Town.

The Eagles earned their first points of the Premier League campaign with a 3-2 win over Fulham at Craven Cottage last week. Tyrick Mitchell bagged a brace to cancel out strikes from Josh King and Cesar Palacios before Ben Chilwell come off the bench to score the winning goal for the guests.

·‘They do have some similarities to how they were under Oliver Glasner’

O’Neil’s men are pointless since their 2-1 victory over Sunderland on the opening matchday, and the manager hopes that his experience of facing Palace’s new boss in France will help foil the opposition.

"It's an exciting game for us. We have respect for Crystal Palace and the quality that they have, but we'll go there full of optimism and intensity, trying to make it as difficult for them as possible and looking to put our first away win on the board.

"I played against Pierre Sage's Lens team last year at Strasbourg and he's trying to get his Crystal Palace group in the same shape. They do have some similarities to how they were under Oliver Glasner, keeping a back five, but Pierre Sage uses the midfielders a little bit differently.

"It's a tough place to go, Selhurst Park. A very small pitch, intense atmosphere. There'll be moments in the game where we need to suffer and dig in. We need to find a way to come through those difficult spells slightly better than we have in the last two games.

"I'm confident our group is in a good enough spot to go to compete with Palace and cause them some problems. If we go and nail how we want to do this then we have a real chance of getting something."
