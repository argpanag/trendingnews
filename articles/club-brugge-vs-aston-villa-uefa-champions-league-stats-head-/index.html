Match Facts

  • All three of the meetings between these two teams came in Aston Villa’s most recent UEFA Champions League campaign (2024-25). Club Brugge won their league phase meeting in that campaign (1-0), but lost both legs in the round of 16 against Unai Emery’s side (1-3 at home, 0-3 away).

  • Club Brugge have only won one of their last 17 games against English sides in major European competition (D3 L13), although that lone victory did come in a UEFA Champions League home match against Aston Villa (1-0 in November 2024).

  • Aston Villa have won 67% of their UEFA Champions League matches under Unai Emery (8/12). This is the second-highest win percentage for a manager with 10+ games in charge of an English side, only behind Liverpool under Arne Slot (68% - 15/22).

  • After winning each of their final two league phase games in 2025-26, Club Brugge will be looking to win three consecutive UEFA Champions League group stage games for just the second time in (also 3 between September and October 2022).

  • Since the start of 2024-25 in the UEFA Champions League, only Barcelona (44) have conceded more goals than Club Brugge (43). Among teams to play 15+ matches in the competition in that time, only PSV (2) are averaging conceding more goals per match than the Belgian side (1.95).

  • This will be Aston Villa boss Unai Emery’s ninth UEFA Champions League campaign, with Aston Villa being the third club he’s managed in multiple editions, after Valencia (2010-11 and 2011-12) and PSG (2016-17 and 2017-18). The Spanish coach has reached at least the quarter-finals in each of his last two UCL campaigns (semi-finals in 2021-22, quarter-finals in 2024-25).

  • Of the 22 goals that were scored by Aston Villa players in the 2024-25 UEFA Champions League, only 32% of them were netted by players who are still currently at the club (7).

  • 19-year-old George Hemmings could become just the second teenager to start for Aston Villa in a European Cup/UEFA Champions League game, after Mark Walters against Juventus in March 1983 (aged 18 in that quarter-final).

  • Over the last three seasons in the UEFA Champions League, Club Brugge goalkeeper Yann Sommer has prevented 9.5 goals, based on the xG of shots on target faced (excl. OGs) – this is the second-highest total in the competition in this period, behind only David Raya (+9.8).

  • Aston Villa’s Zion Suzuki could become the first ever Japanese goalkeeper to play in the European Cup/UEFA Champions League.

Aston Villa’s Zion Suzuki could become the first ever Japanese goalkeeper to play in the European Cup/UEFA Champions League.
