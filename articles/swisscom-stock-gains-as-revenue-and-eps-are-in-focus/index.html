Swisscom stock gains as revenue and EPS are in focus

TheTools · 21.09.2026 16:54 · source · permalink

Swisscom stock closed at CHF 673.00 on September 15, 2026, after rising 2.1 percent. Revenue was CHF 3.62 billion in Q2 2026 and EPS reached CHF 6.51.

Swisscom stock (CH0008742519) closed at CHF 673.00 on September 15, 2026, after rising 2.1 percent on the SIX Swiss Exchange. The move followed a session that left the shares at the upper end of their recent range and kept market attention on the latest reported numbers.

Revenue and EPS

According to finanzen.ch, Swisscom reported revenue of CHF 3.62 billion in the second quarter of 2026, down 1.95 percent from CHF 3.69 billion a year earlier. The same report put EPS at CHF 6.51, versus CHF 5.00 in the prior-year period.

That gives investors a clean comparison point: EPS increased by CHF 1.51 year over year, while revenue slipped by CHF 70 million. A first.

Price and range

At 09:28 on September 16, 2026, finanzen.ch showed Swisscom stock at CHF 671.00, down 0.3 percent from the open, with 2,724 shares traded. The same report placed the 52-week high at CHF 727.00 from March 10, 2026 and the 52-week low at CHF 545.00 from December 10, 2025.

That leaves the shares 8.35 percent below the 52-week high and 23.12 percent above the 52-week low. For investors, the gap to CHF 727.00 matters more than the small intraday setback.

Market cap and session

Swisscom's market capitalization was put at CHF 34.18 billion in a September 16, 2026 market overview, while Swissquote said the stock finished the previous session more than 2 percent higher. The stock now trades close to the top end of its one-year band, with the price and the Q2 earnings release both pointing to the same theme: cash generation still dominates the Swisscom story.
