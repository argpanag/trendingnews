New OTT releases this weekend: 9 new movies and shows coming on Netflix, JioHotstar, Prime Video and more - New OTT releases this wekend

TheTools · 09.10.2026 08:42 · source · permalink

    New OTT releases this wekend

    OTT releases this weekend, from October 9 to October 11, include a diverse lineup of Indian and international movies and web series. Netflix brings titles such as Pradhama Dhrishtya Kuttakkar, Animals, while Prime Video features Khalifa Part 1, Treasure Island. With crime thrillers, romance, action, comedy and adventure across several streaming platforms, viewers have plenty of new content to explore.

    Treasure Island (Prime Video / October 11, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 11, 2026

    Where to watch: Prime Video

    The Story: Starring Hayley Atwell, David Oyelowo, Jack Huston and Tomer Capone, this new adaptation of Robert Louis Stevenson's classic adventure follows young Jim Hawkins and his mother, Bess, who discover Captain Flint's legendary treasure map. Their discovery plunges them into a dangerous race for riches, where shifting loyalties and ruthless ambition threaten their survival aboard the Hispaniola.

    'Treasure Island' will stream on Prime Video from October 11.

    100 Days of Deception (Netflix / October 10, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 10, 2026

    Where to watch: Netflix

    The Story: Starring Kim You-jung, Park Jin-young, Kim Hyun-joo and Lee Mu-saeng, this Korean historical thriller is set in 1930s Gyeongseong under Japanese rule. Lee Ga-kyung, a skilled pickpocket saving money to escape to the United States, accepts a dangerous 100-day mission from the Korean independence movement. She goes undercover as an interpreter at the Japanese Government-General, where she encounters Kim Tae-woong, an elite interpreter whose background complicates her mission.

    '100 Days of Deception' will stream on Netflix from October 10.

    Animals (Netflix / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: Netflix

    The Story: Starring Ben Affleck, Kerry Washington, Steven Yeun and Gillian Anderson, this action thriller follows Los Angeles mayoral candidate Mark Kimball and his wife after their son is kidnapped. With only hours to raise the ransom and much of their money tied up in his political campaign, the couple is forced into desperate decisions that expose dangerous secrets.

    'Animals' will stream on Netflix from October 9.

    Reporting Live (Netflix / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: Netflix

    The Story: Starring Parineeti Chopra, Deven Bhojani, Adil Hussain and Shekhar Suman, this Hindi crime thriller follows a young reporter and her reluctant cameraman as they investigate a story that draws them into an illegal sand-mining racket controlled by a ruthless syndicate.

    'Reporting Live' will stream on Netflix from October 9.

    Khalifa Part 1 (Prime Video & ManoramaMAX / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: Prime Video and ManoramaMAX

    The Story: Starring Prithviraj Sukumaran, Mohanlal, Neil Nitin Mukesh and Indrans, this Malayalam action thriller explores the violent history of Mambarakkal Ahmed Ali, the grandfather of Aamir Ali. Titled The Intro, the first part traces Ahmed Ali's rise to power and the establishment of the Mambarakkal family's underworld empire, which is deeply involved in international gold smuggling.

    'Khalifa Part 1' will stream on Prime Video and ManoramaMAX from October 9.

    Eyes (SonyLIV / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: SonyLIV

    The Story: Starring Kani Kusruti, Nikhila Vimal, Shruti Ramachandran and Saniya Iyyappan, this Malayalam psychological crime thriller follows police officer Tina Sebastian as she investigates disturbing cases involving women. As the cases unfold, mental health professionals work alongside the police to understand stalkers, identify warning signs and prevent threats from escalating.

    'Eyes' will stream on SonyLIV from October 9.

    Pradhama Dhrishtya Kuttakkar (Netflix / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: Netflix

    The Story: Starring Parvathy Thiruvothu, Vijayaraghavan, Unnimaya Prasad and Mathew Thomas, this Malayalam crime thriller follows Aleena, who joins the police force through a compassionate appointment after her husband's death. What begins as a fresh start turns into a high-stakes struggle that tests her understanding of justice, loyalty and her own beliefs.

    'Pradhama Dhrishtya Kuttakkar' will stream on Netflix from October 9.

    Epic - First Semester (Netflix / October 9, 2026)

    OTT Release Date: October 9, 2026

    Where to watch: Netflix

    The Story: Starring Anand Deverakonda, Vaishnavi Chaitanya, Sivaji Sontineni and Vasuki Anand, this Telugu romantic drama follows Rohan, a middle-class student studying abroad under family pressure. As he navigates romance, identity and the challenges of growing up, he begins to discover more about himself and the life he wants.

    'Epic - First Semester' will stream on Netflix from October 9.
