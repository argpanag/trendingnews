Vincenzo Italiano delivered the bad news: He will not be available for the Başakşehir match.

TheTools · 24.10.2026 17:59 · source · permalink

23.10.2026 01:00

In the 3rd week of the UEFA Europa League, Beşiktaş head coach Vincenzo Italiano, whose team faced Crystal Palace, made statements after the match. 

Congratulating his players, the Italian coach said, "First of all, I congratulate all my players. We played against a good team, a team with quality players. I also played with 4 of my players who had very little playing time. Sometimes there can be problems while finding solutions regarding this. We started well in the first half but were slow in the second half. We conceded goals and then showed a good reaction. We got a point after going down to 10 men. We still have not lost in Europe. It was very difficult, but I think we did a good job." 

Drawing attention to the importance of the result, Vincenzo Italiano said, "I think what was important here was not losing and maintaining this continuity. Earning 1 point was also important for us. We had to go with this 11 because there can be a risk of injury. In this match Miretti got injured, and last week in the Hoffenheim match Orkun got injured. When you play matches in a short time, it can be dangerous. Today we got 1 point and we got a very important point." 

Continuing his words, the experienced coach said, "I think we are good right now. When you look, Hoffenheim, whom we beat last week, beat Lyon 3-0 this week. Crystal Palace is also a good team; we played against a good team. Marseille too. Our matches are really difficult. That is why we must do our best. Today I also rotated. I gave playing time to my players who had little time. I think we have done a good job in Europe so far. We are continuing unbeaten in Europe and we need to continue this way." 

Speaking about midfielder Fabio Miretti, who got injured and left the game in the first half, Italiano commented, "There is a possibility that Miretti will not be with us for a few weeks. He will not be in the Başakşehir match. I hope he returns quickly."
