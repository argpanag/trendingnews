Julia Roberts Will Make Small Mouth Sounds on Broadway

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:24 · source · permalink

There was a time when Scott Rudin was all but banned from Broadway for abusing his assistants. With Rudin now armed with a Tony win for Death of a Salesman, that time is gone: He revealed today that he is producing two plays on Broadway this season, and both are being directed by Joe Mantello. The buzzier one is Small Mouth Sounds, written by Beth Wohl (Liberation), which has the starriest cast imaginable. The six people in the play, set at a silent retreat, are Emmy winner Martin Freeman, Past Lives star Greta Lee, Tony winner Sarah Paulson, The Beauty’s Isaac Powell, Julia Roberts (known for being Julia Roberts), and Avenger Paul Rudd. In 2016, the play had a well-reviewed run Off Broadway, where it was directed by Rachel Chavkin, a Tony winner for Hadestown. It will begin performances this spring.

Before that comes Downstate. The Bruce Norris–written play follows four men who are convicted sex offenders and live in a group home in Illinois. Downstate played Playwrights Horizons in 2022 to rave reviews. It will feature a less well-known but still prestigious, cast. Original cast members returning are K. Todd Freeman and Francis Guinan. The rest of the cast will be filled out by Cassie Beck, Adrian Martinez, Al’Jaleel McGhee, Thomas Sadoski, Ivy Wolk, and Emmy winner Liza Colón-Zayas. Though Pam MacKinnon directed the Off Broadway run, Mantello will also be helming this one. Previews begin October 17 ahead of a November 15 opening night at the Booth Theatre. Joe Mantello is ready to get to work.
