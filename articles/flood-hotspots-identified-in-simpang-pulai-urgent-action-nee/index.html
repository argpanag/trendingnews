Flood Hotspots Identified in Simpang Pulai, Urgent Action Needed to Address Recurring Problem - Ipoh Echo

TheTools · 26.11.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

By Aida Aziz

IPOH: Several areas in Simpang Pulai were badly affected by flash floods following continuous heavy rain on Wednesday afternoon.

Simpang Pulai state assemblywoman Wong Chai Yi, who visited several flood hotspots to assess the situation, said the worst-affected stretch was along Jalan Sultan Nazrin Shah, from Lotus’s Hartamas towards the Bandar Cyber/Bandar Seri Botani junction.

“I have been on the ground since 4 pm inspecting several flood hotspots and identifying the causes of flooding in the Simpang Pulai constituency.

“The worst-affected area was Jalan Sultan Nazrin Shah, from Lotus’s Hartamas towards the Bandar Cyber/Bandar Seri Botani junction.

“Based on previous feedback from the Public Works Department (JKR), the drainage system in the area channels water discharge onto privately owned land.

“As such, JKR, the Ipoh City Council (MBI) and the Land Office must sit down together to examine the issue comprehensively and identify both short-term and long-term solutions,” she said.

She added that recurring floods required better coordination among the relevant agencies rather than each agency acting independently.

Meanwhile, Wong said the areas around Nam Thean Tong Temple, Kwan Yin Tong Temple and Taman Saikat were also affected by the heavy rain.

“Thank you to the Simpang Pulai state constituency service centre team, the Gopeng parliamentary team and the Village Development and Security Committees (JPKK) for being on the ground to monitor the situation and assist residents.

“I will continue to follow up with the relevant agencies to address the flooding problem in these areas,” she added.
