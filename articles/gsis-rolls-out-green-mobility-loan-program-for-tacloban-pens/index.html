GSIS rolls out green mobility loan program for Tacloban pensioners

TheTools · 27.11.2026 08:13 · source · permalink
  • Latest
  • Provincial

(Image courtesy of GSIS)

TACLOBAN CITY – The Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) on Friday launched the Ginhawa Bike and E-Mobility Loan (GBEL) for Pensioners, a financing initiative designed to provide retirees with affordable and environmentally friendly transportation options.

The program forms part of the state pension fund’s “Ginhawa for All” initiative under the Bagong Pilipinas program.

The launch was attended by GSIS Senior Vice President for VisMin Group Isagani Del Rosario, Vice President for Visayas Office Maria Rosanna Sanchez, Board of Trustees member Rolando Toledo, and Tacloban Branch Manager Rosenie Chavez.

“The program was created to recognize that retirement does not end a person’s need to move around. Many pensioners still visit family, attend medical appointments, run errands and take part in community activities,” Toledo said.

Under the program, qualified regular, disability, and portability pensioners below 80 years old may borrow up to 100 percent of the purchase price of a brand-new bicycle or electric mobility device, subject to a maximum loan amount of Php 300,000.

The financing scheme carries an annual interest rate of 5 percent, computed in advance, and is payable in 36 equal monthly installments over three years. A pensioner who avails of the maximum loan amount would have a monthly amortization of Php 9,583.33.

Eligible mobility devices include pedal-assist bicycles, electric bicycles, electric cargo bikes, electric mini-bikes, and other comparable electric personal transportation units intended for personal use.

To safeguard borrowers’ financial welfare, GSIS requires pensioners to retain at least 25 percent of their basic monthly pension after deductions.

Applicants falling below the prescribed threshold may still qualify upon providing written consent, provided their total monthly deductions do not exceed their actual pension.

GSIS clarified that the Php 300,000 maximum loan amount is not an automatic entitlement, as the approved financing will depend on the purchase price of the mobility device and the applicant’s eligibility assessment.

Qualified applicants must have received at least one monthly pension and have no outstanding amortizations under the Choice of Loan Amortization Schedule for Pensioners or the Program for Restructuring and Repayment of Debts at the time of application.

The age restriction was established under a GSIS Board-approved policy consistent with prudent lending practices and risk management measures aimed at protecting the pension fund.

Pensioners may submit their applications through the GSIS Touch mobile application without visiting a branch office. Required supporting documents include a quotation or official receipt for the intended purchase.

Once approved, loan proceeds will be credited directly to the borrower’s account within three business days.

GSIS emphasized that participation in the program is voluntary and encouraged pensioners to prioritize essential expenses, particularly food, medicines, and other basic necessities, before taking out additional loans.

The agency also advised borrowers experiencing repayment difficulties to immediately coordinate with GSIS for proper assessment and assistance.
