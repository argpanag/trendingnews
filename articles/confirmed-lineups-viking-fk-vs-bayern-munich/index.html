Confirmed lineups: Viking FK vs. Bayern Munich

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

With the Champions League making it's return this evening, Bayern Munich have made the trip north to Norway where they take on Viking FK from 21:00 CET at the Lyse Arena.

With around an hour to go before kick-off, both sides have announced their starting lineups – you can find all the details for the two teams below as the action approaches.

Confirmed Viking FK vs. Bayern Munich lineups:

Viking FK (4-3-3): Østbo – Heggheim, Falchener, Stensness, Haugen – Askildsen, Bell, Bjordal – Botheim, Christiansen, Tripić

Bayern Munich (4-2-3-1): Urbig – Laimer, Tah, Kim, Brown – Kimmich, Bischof – Karl, Olise, Diaz – Kane
