Amazon’s “House of the Dragon Meets Shadow and Bone” Show Will Be Its New King of Fantasy

TheTools · 30.08.2026 14:30 · source · permalink

In the wake of Game of Thrones, Prime Video is the streaming service that has the strongest fantasy offerings — well, if we're not counting HBO Max and its spin-offs, anyway. Despite multiple platforms rushing to capitalize on the success of the Song of Ice and Fire adaptation, few have succeeded in creating epic franchises on the same scale. Netflix's attempts with The Witcher and Shadow and Bone are not much to brag about, and Apple TV is leaning more into sci-fi right now (though its take on Brandon Sanderson's Cosmere will properly put it in the fantasy game).

Prime Video has multiple original fantasy series in its library, each boasting varying degrees of quality and success. The most notable is The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, which is an absolute spectacle in terms of production — but more divisive as a J.R.R. Tolkien adaptation, and feels like it's still finding its stride. The Wheel of Time is a contender that took its final bow, with Prime Video canceling it after Season 3. And The Legend of Vox Machina is probably Prime Video's most promising addition to the category to date, but it remains underrated among mainstream audiences.

Needless to say, it's hard to crown a new king of fantasy in Game of Thrones' wake, or even just among Amazon's productions. An upcoming series may clear things up, though, claiming that title with its "House of the Dragon meets Shadow and Bone" storyline.

The 'Fourth Wing' Show Is Poised to Become Prime Video's New King of Fantasy

It Has All the Ingredients to Become a Wider Hit

Prime Video is already ahead in the fantasy race, but the streamer isn't done releasing new projects — and one of its most exciting upcoming endeavors is Fourth Wing. Based on the viral TikTok sensation written by Rebecca Yarros, the TV show will be produced by Michael B. Jordan and his production company, Outlier Society. It will follow Violet Sorrengail as she embarks on a quest to become a dragon rider at Basgiath War College, despite spending her entire life training to become a scholar. Violet's journey includes trials, magic, military conspiracies, and obviously, dragons — all things that make it well worth going on with her.

With so many great ingredients for a fantasy story, Fourth Wing feels destined to become a wider hit once it arrives on Prime Video. There's a reason it's appealing to longtime fantasy readers and newcomers in book form, and that's something the TV series should be able to replicate. The charming characters, timely themes, and high stakes — and did I mention dragons? — make it all too easy to immerse yourself in. The show will cement itself as Prime Video's top fantasy title just because of how popular and accessible it is. There's also the fact that it meshes two very different series, offering something for multiple brands of fantasy lovers.

'Fourth Wing' Will Be "House of the Dragon Meets Shadow and Bone" in the Best Way

It Features Strengths From Both Fantasy Series

Fourth Wing is likely to dominate Prime Video, but it's a great blend of two shows from other platforms: House of the Dragon and Shadow and Bone. The House of the Dragon comparison feels obvious. Although Fourth Wing isn't as concerned with succession and power grabs as the Game of Thrones spin-off, it has all the dragon-riding action audiences could want. Its dragons talk, too, giving them more personality than even the best House of the Dragon creatures.

Meanwhile, Fourth Wing's romance story — complete with a shadow-wielding MMC — and military focus make it a solid choice for anyone looking to replace Shadow and Bone. The plots aren't quite the same, but they check a lot of the same boxes. They also have a similar feel, and with Shadow and Bone canceled, Fourth Wing can carry that forward. Hopefully, its range of strengths will work to its advantage, allowing it to resonate with a broader viewership. That's what fantasy needs to reach the heights of Game of Thrones, and Fourth Wing is one series that could actually pull that off.
