Ararat-Armenia–Sparta Praha match tickets now on sale

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:27 · source · permalink

1 minute read

Tickets for the UEFA Europa League league-phase opening-round match between Ararat-Armenia and Sparta Praha have gone on sale.

The match will take place in Yerevan at Vazgen Sargsyan Republican Stadium on September 16 and will kick off at 20:45.

Ticket prices are as follows:

North Stand – AMD 3,000

South Stand – AMD 3,000

East Stand – AMD 4,000–5,000

West Stand – AMD 6,000–10,000

Following the draw held earlier, Ararat-Armenia’s opponents are AZ Alkmaar (Netherlands), Milan (Italy), Sparta Praha (Czech Republic), Salzburg (Austria), Celje (Slovenia), Jagiellonia (Poland), N.E.C. (Netherlands) and Torreense (Portugal).
