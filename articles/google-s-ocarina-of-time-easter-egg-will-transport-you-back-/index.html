Google's Ocarina Of Time Easter Egg Will Transport You Back To The Internet Of Old

TheTools · 02.11.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

Links to the past

We love it when Google pulls out a little gaming Easter Egg. We had the inkable search engine screen for Splatoon 3, the various Pokémon collaborations, and even a Katamari crossover. But now, Google has delivered the most nostalgic one of them all.

If you Google 'Ocarina of Time', you'll now be met with a small Master Sword icon in the bottom right corner. Clicking on said icon pushes the sword back into its pedestal and transports the search engine into the past with it.

Just like that, your search looks pulled straight out of 1998, complete with blocky site links, a particularly nostalgic search bar, and an old-school exclamation-marked Google logo. Nice.
