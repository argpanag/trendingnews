Indi MP Helen Haines is in a critical condition in a Melbourne hospital after being "attacked" near her electorate office in Wangaratta, north-east Victoria.

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese is leading cross-party condemnation of the incident, as he reveals counter-terrorism police are investigating. 

Victoria Police said a 43-year-old Tarrawingee man has been arrested following the incident.

Cross-party well wishes for Helen Haines' recovery

C

By Claire Campbell

By Claire Campbell

Shock and sadness over the alleged attack onIndi MP Helen Haines has come from across political divides.

It has become clear from the amount of support and well wishes that have rolled in that the 65-year-old is a respected politician.

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese described Haines as a "thoroughly decent human being" who is "full of integrity", again calling for the temperature to be turned down in political discourse.

Opposition Leader Angus Taylor offered his full support to the government, calling the alleged attack on Haines "a shocking day for our nation".

Nationals MP Sam Birrell said Haines was one of the "kindest people in the parliament" and One Nation leader Pauline Hanson also said she was "horrified and saddened" by the incident and wished Haines a "full and speedy recovery".

PM said "this wasn't an accident"

A

By Andi Yu

By Andi Yu

The Prime Minister was asked last night on the ABC's 7:30 program whether Ms Haines was targeted due to her role as a politician and this is what he said:

"This wasn't an accident. This was a deliberate act," he said.

Anthony Albanese also said the joint counterterrorism unit had been activated but it was too early to say what was behind the Monday afternoon incident.

"It is too early in this investigation for me to provide you with details of what the motivation is behind this attack," he said.

Independent MP Helen Haines was airlifted to hospital in Melbourne late last night with critical injuries.
