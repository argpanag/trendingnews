by

West Indies head coach Daren Sammy

THIRU­VANAN­THA­PU­RAM, In­dia—West In­dies head coach Daren Sam­my will be us­ing the up­com­ing three-match One Day In­ter­na­tion­al se­ries against In­dia as a gauge to de­ter­mine how com­pet­i­tive the re­gion­al side can be against the top teams in the world.

The Windies will take on the home side in the first ODI on Sun­day, in search of a first se­ries win on the sub­con­ti­nent in 24 years.

Re­gard­less of the out­come of the se­ries, the West In­dies will have to book their spot in the 2027 ICC World Cup in South Africa, Zim­bab­we and Namib­ia, by go­ing through the Qual­i­fiers, which bowl off next Feb­ru­ary.

Speak­ing to the me­dia on Thurs­day, Sam­my said com­ing up against the num­ber one ranked ODI side in the world was a chal­lenge and a test the team was ea­ger­ly look­ing for­ward to.

“This is a great op­por­tu­ni­ty against the num­ber one team in the world…It’s been 24 years we haven’t won a se­ries here in In­dia, so it’s a great op­por­tu­ni­ty for us as a team to come out here and chal­lenge our­selves and mea­sure up our­selves as to where we’re at in One Day crick­et.

“I think it’s a great op­por­tu­ni­ty for us and the play­ers that we have to come and put in a good show against In­dia and look to win a se­ries,” Sam­my said.

“If you have to win a ma­jor ICC event, es­pe­cial­ly in the white ball for­mat, you have to beat In­dia and play­ing them here at home in their con­di­tions and per­form­ing well and win­ning gives us a fair idea as to what we can do as a team and how ca­pa­ble we are in com­pet­ing on the world stage and head­ing in­to the Qual­i­fiers in Feb­ru­ary.

“That is our num­ber one fo­cus; con­tin­u­ing to fine tune the team so that when we go to the Qual­i­fiers we put our­selves in the po­si­tion to book our tick­et to South Africa,” he added.

Sam­my re­vealed that with the ODI se­ries against In­dia be­ing the last of the year and pos­si­bly the last be­fore the Qual­i­fiers, every mem­ber of the 15-man squad would be giv­en an op­por­tu­ni­ty to con­tribute.

He said this would pro­vide a clear­er pic­ture of what the team tak­ing part in the Qual­i­fi­er would look like.

“…We want to give every­body an op­por­tu­ni­ty dur­ing those three games…every­body will be get­ting an op­por­tu­ni­ty be­cause this is our last ODI se­ries of the year and look­ing at our sched­ule go­ing in­to the Qual­i­fiers, we may not have an­oth­er ODI se­ries, so this last test against In­dia is very im­por­tant in the gen­er­al con­text of things to see where we’re at,” Sam­my said.↔(CMC)
