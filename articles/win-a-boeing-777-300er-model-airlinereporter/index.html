Win a Boeing 777-300ER Model!  : AirlineReporter

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:27 · source · permalink

By David Parker Brown

Do you want to win a Boeing 777 model with a Boeing livery? Of course you do – and you can! This model is from my own collection. I have two and I only need one. It is new in the box (except for me carefully taking it out for the photos). It is a 1/200 scale model, and that means it is almost 13″ from wingtip to wingtip and just over 14″ long. It is an officially licensed product, which means it is high quality (the white is a pearly white — purdy!). It comes with wheels and a nameplate, but I didn’t want to mess with those (AKA I didn’t want to break them).

HOW TO WIN THIS 777-300ER MODEL:

  1. Add a comment to this story sharing your favorite airline that is currently flying the 777
  2. Follow our AirlineReporter Instagram account
  3. That’s it!

At 5:00pm PST (Seattle, WA time) on December 20th, 2026, I will close down the comments. I will then use a random number generator and go to the comment that aligns with that number. I will verify that the person meets the requirements above and reach out to them to let them know they won. Once I have confirmation everything is good-to-go, I will update the story to say we have a winner!

QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT HAVE:  

Q: What if I don’t have an Instagram account?
A: Unfortunately, following our account is one of the requirements. You can always create one or wait for a future contest that doesn’t require it (I have more stuff to give away in the future).

Q: How do I know about future giveaways?
A: The best way is to join our newsletter.

Q: Can someone outside the U.S. win?
A: Yes!

Q: This is cool, I really hope that I win!
A: Not really a question, but I hope that you win too!

Good luck!
