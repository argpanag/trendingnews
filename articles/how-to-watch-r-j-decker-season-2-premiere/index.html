Decker is a former newspaper photographer who is disgraced and is an ex-con after an assault and giving false testimony. He reinvents his life as a private investigator in South Florida’s crime world. To help him fit into this new life, he has a whole cast of characters, including his ex and a woman from his past who could be his ticket to success or drag him back into his criminal past.
