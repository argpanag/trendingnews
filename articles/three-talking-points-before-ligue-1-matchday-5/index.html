Le Classique, Episode I

Olympique de Marseille vs Paris Saint-Germain.

On Sunday at 20:45, OM host PSG for the first Le Classique of the season. A special clash between the best of enemies...

OM are flirting with crisis after four defeats in five matches in all competitions this season; PSG will be desperate to avenge last season’s 1-0 defeat at the Vélodrome.

An explosive Le Classique, live on OneFootball!

Cahuzac’s first game

Appointed RC Lens head coach at the start of the week after the surprise dismissal of Dino Toppmöller, Yannick Cahuzac will take charge of the first match of his coaching career this Friday.

And what a match! Les Sang et Or travel to AS Monaco for a massive showdown. What a start to the weekend, yum!

A clash that could already be decisive for the UCL?

On Saturday at 20:45, Olympique Lyonnais host Stade Rennais in a match that already has the feel of a Champions League race showdown.

Rennes, third with ten points, against Lyon, fifth with eight points.
