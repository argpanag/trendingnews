Princess of Wales to make first long-haul trip since cancer diagnosis

TheTools · 15.11.2026 08:57 · source · permalink

Catherine to walk green carpet at Earthshot Prize awards during three-day Mumbai visit with her husband

The Princess of Wales will join her husband in India in their first joint overseas trip since her cancer diagnosis.

The Princess, who last travelled abroad on a work trip with the Prince of Wales in 2022, will undertake engagements in India and walk the green – not red – carpet at the Earthshot Prize awards ceremony, Kensington Palace confirmed.

She was “hugely looking forward to being alongside” her husband for “what is one of the most significant moments in his year”, a spokesman said.

It will be her first long-haul trip and second overseas visit since her cancer diagnosis and treatment, and her most high profile tour since the Caribbean in March 2022. The Prince and Princess attended the Earthshot Awards in Boston in December that year.

The Waleses will spend three days in Mumbai, a nine-hour flight from London, for engagements relating to the environment prize, as well as showcasing British-Indian cultural links.

They are expected to meet Bollywood stars to learn about India’s film industry and fashion, and celebrate the country’s passion for cricket.

The couple last visited India together in 2016, when they played cricket and posed for a photograph in front of the Taj Mahal.

A spokesman said: “Of course, returning to India together 10 years after their last visit is something they’re both really looking forward to.

“They have wonderful memories of their time here and are excited to see a very different side of India on this visit, through the extraordinary people and ideas shaping its future.”

It is not yet known whether the Prince will meet Narendra Modi, the Indian prime minister, or take on other work on behalf of the British Government while in the region.

Despite the Princess hoping to join her husband on the trip since its inception, the palace only confirmed her attendance one day before the couple were due to arrive.

It will be seen as a major milestone for the Princess, who has said that her recovery from cancer has not been linear, and has had “good days and bad days”. She announced that she was in remission in January 2025 and has been steadily returning to public life.

In May, she made an overnight visit to the Italian city of Reggio Emilia, near Bologna, on a fact-finding visit to learn about a unique form of early years teaching pioneered in the city.

The Prince said afterwards that she was “edging herself back” into doing more overseas visits, but that he and the palace team must “make sure she’s OK and rested”.

The Earthshot Prize is now halfway through its decades-long cycle to recognise and reward ideas to help “repair” the planet. Previous awards have been held in London, Singapore, Cape Town, Boston and Rio de Janeiro.

A spokesman for Kensington Palace told The Telegraph: “For The Prince, this week represents a hugely important moment in Earthshot’s journey.

“The Prince has long believed that the solutions to many of the challenges facing our planet already exist. The question is how we help them reach more people, more quickly, and that’s really what this week is about.

“There’s also a wider significance to the visit.

“The Prince is increasingly focused on how his role can bring people together, strengthen relationships and deliver meaningful outcomes, whether through Earthshot or his wider international work.

“India is an enormously important partner for the UK, and this visit provides another opportunity to celebrate the connections between our two countries.”

The spokesman added: “The Princess is hugely looking forward to being alongside him for what is one of the most significant moments in his year.

“She has seen Earthshot develop enormously since she last attended the Awards in Boston in 2022, and is excited to experience the scale of this year’s programme and meet the people whose work is making such a difference.”

The Earthshot Prize awards ceremony takes place on Tuesday night, with a performance by Take That.

Celebrity guests from across music, film, and sport include Sachin Tendulkar, the cricketer; actors Alia Bhatt, Rami Malek, Sonam Kapoor and Simone Ashley; Karan Johar, the host of Traitors India; and Earthshot ambassadors Robert Irwin and Nomzamo Mbatha.
