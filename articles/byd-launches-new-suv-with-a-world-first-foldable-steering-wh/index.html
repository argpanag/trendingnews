BYD launches new SUV with a world first foldable steering wheel

TheTools · 20.11.2026 22:54 · source · permalink

BYD has unveiled a new model with a completely foldable steering wheel, allowing for more space for the driver when resting, and for future steering-wheel-less operations as autonomous driving becomes common.

The company introduced this in its flagship Fang Cheng Bao sub-branded vehicle it calls the Tai 9, and it is the world’s first 0°-180° fold-away steering wheel – and it stows back into the dashboard within 10 seconds, creating more room for the driver.

A demo of this latest tech feature was shown to the public and shared by CNC on YouTube.

BYD was first spotted testing Fang Cheng Bao brand in Australia last year, with the Bao 5 PHEV SUV, which arrived locally late in 2025, under the more premium Denza brand.

BYD established its Fang Cheng Bao brand in June 2023, focusing on off-road vehicles. The first two models are mid- and large off-road SUVs driven by a PHEV drivetrain.

The Bao 5 was the brand’s first model, making its debut in 2023. This mid-size SUV quickly gained popularity across China, gaining 10,000 orders within three days of its launch. Over the next 6 months, the Bao 5 sold more than 20,000 units.

Then, in 2024, the brand released the larger Bao 8 SUV, which gained traction with buyers seeking a premium large SUV with some off-road capability.

By July 2024, BYD lowered Bao 5 prices as it began competing with rugged, small SUV-based off-roaders from ICE brands like GWM. This helped Bao 5 reach almost 50,000 sales in China in 2024.

In 2025, the off-road brand launched a new off-road model under its Fang Cheng Bao brand, called the Tai 3. This car was also the company’s first fully electric model aimed at the family adventure SUV market.

Earlier this year, the brand also unveiled some of the sleekest EVs at the Beijing Auto Show, targeting models like the iconic Porsche Panamera.
