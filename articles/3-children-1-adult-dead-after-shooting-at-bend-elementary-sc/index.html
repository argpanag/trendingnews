3 children, 1 adult dead after shooting at Bend elementary school; Suspected shooter dead

BEND, Ore. (KATU) — Three children and one adult are dead following a reported active shooter situation at Silver Rail Elementary on Thursday afternoon. The suspected active shooter is also dead.

The suspected shooter was a 16-year-old boy, according police confirmed.

Police responded to the school at about 12:59 p.m. after receiving a 911 call from person in a car who reported being shot at by a person armed with a long gun.

Within a minute, Deschutes County 911 dispatchers received multiple calls reporting that someone had entered Silver Rail Elementary and started shooting. Bend police officers arrived at 1:02 p.m. Seconds later, officers reported that the suspect had been shot and killed.

Officials held a press conference at 5:30 p.m. with additional information. During that press conference, authorities confirmed that five people, including the shooter, are dead.

The deceased include an adult teacher and children ages 5, 8, and 11. Two other victims, a 7-year-old child and a 54-year-old teacher, were taken to the hospital in critical condition.

The 16-year-old shooting is confirmed to have been shot and killed by a community member. According to police, that community member witnessed the suspect entering the school with a gun, before following them inside and engaging them.

Officials said they plan to hold another press conference with additional information Friday afternoon.

Bend-La Pine Schools buses took students to Journey Church at 61303 S. Highway 97 for reunification with their families. Police asked families and guardians to go there.

“Nobody knows what's going on there's somebody that's hurt whether that's a student or a shooter, whatever's going on in his head I mean somebody got hurt and that sucks,” said parent, Tyler Major, whose 10-year-old child attends school at Silver Rail.

Police asked the public to avoid the school and church areas because of a large law enforcement presence.

“We are stunned and heartbroken by this senseless tragedy,” Bend-La Pine Schools Superintendent Steven Cook said. “We extend our deepest sympathy to the families and to everyone affected by this violence. No words can adequately express the grief our community is experiencing. Our immediate priorities are caring for our students and staff and assisting law enforcement and emergency responders.”

All Bend-La Pine area schools and athletic events are canceled Friday due to the shooting.

Federal investigators from Portland’s FBI branch have responded to the scene, according to FBI Director Kash Patel.

“We will make every resource they need available,” Patel wrote on social media.

Silver Rail Elementary received “classroom door hardware upgrades” in 2022. According to the district, new classroom locks were installed as part of a districtwide safety project.

The new locks were designed to be secured from inside the classroom with a switch, and remain locked when the door closes, according to a description of the upgrades.

This is the first school shooting in Oregon in more than 10 years. It is also the second deadliest school shooting in state history.

Back in 2015, a gunman opened fire inside Umpqua Community College in Roseburg. Nine people were killed. The suspect died in a shootout with police. The college holds a day of remembrance every year.

This is a developing story. Check back with KATU News for updates.
