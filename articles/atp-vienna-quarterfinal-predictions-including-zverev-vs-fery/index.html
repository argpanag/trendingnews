ATP Vienna Quarterfinal Predictions Including Zverev vs Fery

TheTools · 29.10.2026 22:41 · source · permalink

We have reached the quarterfinals in Vienna, and by getting this far Alexander Zverev has guaranteed the World No. 1 ranking next week. But will he win his first match after achieving this crown? We predict that match, along with all of the others.

ATP Vienna Quarterfinal Predictions

Matteo Berrettini vs Alex de Minaur

Berrettini is playing well this week, which is great to see. When he’s healthy, a fast indoor court is his playground. De Minaur is a great counterpuncher who actually won this matchup at this exact same stage in Vienna last year, but I think Berrettini is healthier and more consistent right now than he was then.
Prediction: Berrettini in 3

Daniil Medvedev vs Adrian Mannarino

Sometimes it amazes me what Adrian Mannarino can do when he finds his rhythm. He plays the exact same game as he did over ten years ago, with the same efficient effectiveness. On a fast court it should work against Medvedev reasonably well, too, but the Russian has also upped his level recently and should have too much.
Prediction: Medvedev in 2

Carlos Alcaraz vs Tommy Paul

Despite a great run at the US Open, Alcaraz still has yet to look quite like himself in Vienna, or at any other stop on this comeback (including New York, if we’re fully honest). Tommy Paul has a good enough all-court game to stick with the Spaniard, and at this point the American is probably more consistent.
Prediction: Paul in 3

Alexander Zverev vs Arthur Fery

These two met at Wimbledon, after Fery’s amazing run to the semifinals. But the Brit hasn’t quite found that same level since then, and Zverev won that match handily. I expect the German to do the same this time.
Prediction: Zverev in 2
