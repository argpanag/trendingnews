One dead following morning crash in Walhalla, coroner says

TheTools · 17.09.2026 17:16 · source · permalink

WALHALLA, S.C. (FOX Carolina) - The Oconee County Coroner’s Office said they are investigating a deadly Walhalla crash on Thursday morning.

Officials said the crash took place at the intersection of South Highway 11 and Hefner Road around 8:49 a.m.

The coroner’s office said one person died on scene. He was identified as 69-year-old Ivan Arnold Brooks.

Brooks’ manner of death was ruled as an accident.
