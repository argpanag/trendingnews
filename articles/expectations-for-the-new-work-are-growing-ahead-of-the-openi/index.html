Expectations for the new work are growing ahead of the opening of Blizzard's fan festival "Blizzcon 2026. Blizzard is a game company affiliated with Microsoft.

Blizzard will hold BlizzCon 2026 at the Anaheim Convention Center in Orange County, California, for two days from the 12th (local time). Blizzard has been releasing new works at BlizzCon every year. However, it was acquired by MS and did not open BlizzCon after 2023.

BlizzCon, which will be held for the first time in three years, is interested in whether it will release a follow-up to the "Starcraft" series. Foreign media have reported that Blizzard plans to unveil a new StarCraft IP at BlizzCon this year.

According to the game industry, Nexon has also been discussing the development of games using StarCraft IP since signing a domestic publishing contract for "Overwatch" with Blizzard last year.

There are also expectations for a sequel to Blizzard's other masterpiece, World of Warcraft (WoW).

Along with the announcement of new works, performances and e-sports events will also be held at BlizzCon. According to Blizzard, K-pop girl group Le Seraphim will perform at BlizzCon this year. Le Seraphim also performed at BlizzCon in 2023.

The BlizzCon will also host the Overwatch World Cup and Blizzard game-based e-sports competitions.
