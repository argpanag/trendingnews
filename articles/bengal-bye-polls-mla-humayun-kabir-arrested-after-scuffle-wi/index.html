Bengal bye-polls: MLA Humayun Kabir arrested after scuffle with police in Rejinagar

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:31 · source · permalink

In Nandigram, the Congress alleged that the voters were being intimidated and polling agents prevented from entering booths.

Aam Janata Unnayan Party chief and MLA Humayun Kabir was arrested on Tuesday after a scuffle with police personnel in West Bengal’s Rejinagar, where voting was underway for a bye-election necessitated after he vacated the seat, The Hindu reported.

Kabir had won both Rejinagar and Nowda in the May Assembly elections. He retained Nowda.

Kabir had been having arguments with the police since polling began, alleging that a large number of personnel had been deployed near his home and at polling booths to intimidate voters and his party workers, The Hindu reported.

He cast his vote with his family in the morning and went to the Aam Janata Unnayan Party office in the constituency.

The police arrived at the party office and asked Kabir to leave, saying that he could not remain there during polling as he was neither a candidate nor an authorised polling agent.

An argument broke out between Kabir and the police personnel, and the MLA refused to go home.

“Do not work as their middleman,” The Hindu quoted him as saying. “Do not do vote chori. You are doing work for the thieves.”

The argument escalated into a scuffle, after which police personnel detained Kabir in a police vehicle.

Kabir was later arrested on allegations of attempting to influence voters and incite them, PTI quoted unidentified police officers as saying. It was not clear what charges the leader was booked under.

Allegations of poll irregularities in Nandigram

In Nandigram, Congress candidate Milan Pradhan alleged that his polling agents were being prevented from entering booths and accused the police of ignoring complaints of intimidation and other irregularities, PTI reported.

He also claimed that police had lathi-charged voters in Chakraberia.

Meanwhile, the police detained the Congress chief of Nandigram-I block over alleged attempts to create disturbances during polling, based on a complaint by Bharatiya Janata Party leaders, the news agency reported.

The bye-poll in Nandigram became necessary after Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari vacated his seat. Adhikari had won both Nandigram and Bhabanipur in the Assembly elections but retained the latter, where he defeated Trinamool Congress chief Mamata Banerjee.

The BJP has fielded Hasirani Rath, the mother of an aide to Adhikari who was shot dead two days after the Assembly election results were announced.

The Banerjee-led TMC faction has not fielded a candidate and is instead supporting Pradhan. He was arrested on September 18 in connection with a 2007 murder case and was subsequently shown as arrested in five other cases.

His arrest came hours after Banerjee announced that her faction of the TMC would support him in the bye-poll. The faction’s candidate had withdrawn her nomination shortly before the announcement, after photographs of her meeting Adhikari were shared online.

On September 28, the Calcutta High Court granted Pradhan interim bail in six criminal cases dating back to 2007, allowing him to campaign for the bye-poll.
