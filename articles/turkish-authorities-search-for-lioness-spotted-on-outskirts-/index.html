Turkish authorities search for lioness spotted on outskirts of Istanbul

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

Police and animal welfare teams in Istanbul are searching for a lioness seen prowling the city’s outskirts

ISTANBUL -- Police and animal welfare teams in Istanbul are searching for a lioness seen prowling the city’s outskirts, Turkish officials said Monday.

A resident spotted the animal Sunday evening in Uskumrukoy, an upmarket neighborhood on the northern edge of Istanbul’s European side, surrounded by forests. The lioness was later captured on security camera footage.

“The location of the lion has been identified, and the necessary care is being taken to capture it alive,” the Istanbul governor’s office said in a statement posted on social media.

Grainy images published by the Demiroren News Agency showed the lioness walking through a grassy area. It was unclear where the animal came from, though some media reports suggested it had escaped from a nearby home. The Istanbul Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office launched an investigation focused on identifying its owner.

The National Parks Directorate said specialist veterinarians and teams equipped with thermal imaging cameras were assisting in the search. More than 70 personnel, supported by drones, were searching in and around the nearby Belgrade Forest, which covers 54 square kilometers north of Istanbul. The forest was closed to vehicles and pedestrians.

Local resident Arda Koc said he saw the lioness after joining the search on Sunday evening.

“I took my car and went out for a drive hoping to see it,” he said in comments reported by the Cumhuriyet newspaper. “While I was driving, it suddenly jumped out in front of me. At first I was scared. After that, I immediately notified the gendarmerie.”

The big cat's appearance has gripped the public’s imagination, drawing people to help with the search. Yusuf Yalcin arrived wearing a lion costume beneath a jersey of local soccer team Galatasaray, nicknamed the Lions.

“Hopefully, the lion will be tranquilized and neutralized without anyone getting hurt,” he told Demiroren.

Residents were warned of the danger, and police were stationed on roads leading into and out of the neighborhood.

Sariyer District Mayor Mustafa Oktay Aksu noted that the lioness had not harmed chickens she passed at night, indicating she had been hand-reared. However, he warned that she should still be treated as dangerous.

Turkey has become a hub for the illegal trade in wild animals as traffickers exploit its air links to Africa and Asia. In December 2024, a young gorilla was rescued after being discovered at Istanbul Airport on a flight from Nigeria to Thailand.
