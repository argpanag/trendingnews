Brent Bambury retires from CBC after 47 years

  • 6 hours ago
  • Radio
  • Duration 4:24

Brent Bambury, host of CBC Radio's Day 6, signed off for the last time on Saturday after more than four decades with the public broadcaster. His career began in Saint John, N.B., and included everything from influencing the nation's musical tastes on Brave New Waves to helming more than one popular game show.
