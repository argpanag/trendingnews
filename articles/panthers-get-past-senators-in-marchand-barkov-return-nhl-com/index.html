Panthers get past Senators in Marchand, Barkov return | NHL.com

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:49 · source · permalink

Forward has three assists, captain scores; Ottawa point streak ends at nine

Senators at Panthers | Recap

SUNRISE, Fla. -- Brad Marchand had three assists and Aleksander Barkov scored in the return of both forwards for the Florida Panthers, who rallied for a 5-3 win against the Ottawa Senators at Amerant Bank Arena on Wednesday.

Marchand had missed 20 games since sustaining a knee injury in a 2-1 win at the Los Angeles Kings on Oct. 6. Barkov, the Panthers captain, rejoined the lineup after missing 22 games following a knee injury of his own, sustained in a 4-3 overtime loss at the San Jose Sharks on Oct. 1. 

“It was great, tough when you are sitting out,” Marchand said after being named the game’s First Star. “You want to be out there with the guys and competing especially early in the season when you want to lay the groundwork for how you are going to play all year. You want to build that chemistry with the team. So, it was great to be out there, but even better to see Barkov back out there.”

OTT@FLA: Lundell and Marchand combine for a 3-2 lead

Barkov, who scored the final goal of the game for the Panthers, said he was just happy to be back. He missed the entire 2025-26 season with a right knee injury before injuring his left knee in the second game of this season.

“It has been a while, so really happy to be back. Really excited,” Barkov said. “A little nervous, even. But it is easy to play for this team, to play in this building. It was a special night for us, and for me.”

Niko Mikkola and Anton Lundell each had a goal and an assist for Florida (14-8-3), which has won two games in a row and is 5-1-1 in its past seven. Jacob Markstrom made 25 saves.

Philip Tomasino had a goal and an assist, and Artem Zub and Jake Sanderson each had two assists for Ottawa (16-7-3), whose nine-game point streak ended (7-0-2). Linus Ullmark made 27 saves.

“I thought it was a hard-fought game,” Senators coach Travis Green said. “Obviously, (Florida) is a good team. There were parts of the game I did like tonight, and parts that I did not. I thought we dipped a little bit at the end of the second period. A couple of soft plays, and they end up scoring off of (them). We just weren’t at our best tonight. And it wasn’t everyone.”

OTT@FLA: Tomasino scores a breakaway goal to make it 2-1

Florida took a 1-0 lead at 16:16 of the first period. Marchand fought for a loose puck behind the net and skated out around the right side before sending a backhanded feed across the crease to Eetu Luostarinen, who lifted a shot inside the left post on his second attempt.

Ottawa took the lead by scoring twice in 41 seconds early in the second period.

Dylan Cozens tied it 1-1 at 1:12 when he redirected Zub’s shot in the right face-off circle, and Tomasino made it 2-1 at 1:53, racing through two Florida defenders and roofing a shot over Markstrom’s blocker.

Mikkola tied it 2-2 during a delayed penalty at 8:52 when he deflected a point shot from Matthew Tkachuk.

“We could have been better,” Cozens said. “It was an average game for us, and we still had a chance to win. So, that says a lot about our team. We know we have to be better, but we have more for sure.”

Lundell gave the Panthers a 3-2 lead at 18:55. Marchand made a slick move to cut to the middle and fed a backhand to Lundell, who had his initial attempt stopped by Ullmark but swept in the rebound.

Sam Bennett extended it to 4-2 at 9:17 of the third period. Markstrom came far out of his net to play the puck, pushing it up the right side before Marchand passed it to Lundell on the rush. Lundell then fed a charging Bennett, who poked the puck in to the stick side. 

“It was a big win with (Ottawa being) in first place (in the Atlantic Division), so, it was well done,” Florida coach Paul Maurice said. “We can play faster, but those guys have been out a little while. You have new lines.”

OTT@FLA: Bennett gives the Panthers a 4-2 lead with a PPG

After Barkov scored into an empty net at 16:08 for his first goal of the season to make it 5-2, Michael Amadio cut it to 5-3 at 19:09 with Ullmark pulled for the extra attacker.

“There was a lot of good stuff, but it has to be more like our first 30 minutes,” Tomasino said. “I think we had our chance today.”

NOTES: Marchand is the third Panthers player to record a three-assist game at age 38 or older, following Jaromir Jagr (44 on Dec. 20, 2016) and Gary Roberts (39 on Dec. 3, 2005). … Florida has won nine straight at home against Ottawa dating to March 3, 2022. It is tied for the second-longest such run in franchise history against a single opponent. The Panthers won nine in a row against the Montreal Canadiens from 2018-24. Florida’s longest home winning streak is 10, against the Chicago Blackhawks, an active run that was extended earlier this season.
