Today’s Moon (September 14): What Zodiac Sign Is the Moon In Today? Discover the Moon’s Astrological Influence, Meaning, Energy, Emotions, Love & More

Today’s Moon (September 14): The Moon, though always moving through the zodiac, and astrologers often link this movement to changes in our emotions, relationships, and general energy. On September 14, 2026, the Moon moves from Libra into Scorpio in Western tropical astrology, bringing a shift from harmony-focused Libra energy to the more intense and introspective qualities traditionally associated with Scorpio. The Moon is also in its waxing crescent phase, a time often associated in astrology with growth, momentum and building on new intensions.

What Zodiac Sign Is the Moon In Today, September 14?

On September 14, the Moon transitions from Libra to Scorpio, with lunar calculations placing the transition during the day depending on the time zone used. Once in Scorpio, the Moon is traditionally associated with deeper emotions, heightened intuition, privacy and a stronger desire to understand what lies beneath the surface. Its waxing crescent phase also makes the day symbolically connected with developing plans and moving forward after the recent New Moon.

What Does a Scorpio Moon Mean for Your Emotions and Love Life?

Astrologically, a Scorpio Moon is linked with emotional intensity, honesty and deeper connections. People may feel more private or sensitive and may prefer meaningful conversations over casual interactions. In love and relationships, the energy is traditionally associated with trust, intimacy and examining feelings that may have been left unspoken. At the same time, strong emotions can sometimes lead to overthinking or impulsive reactions, so astrologers generally encourage patience and honest communication.

What Does Today’s Moon Mean for the Zodiac Signs?

The Moon movement into Scorpio according to the astrologers can have varied implications as compared with other zodiac signs. Aries may be more concerned with money and trust, while Taurus may be more concerned with relationships. Gemini may be inclined to work and responsibilities, while Cancer could channel energy into creativity or personal goals. Leo may get more into home and family while Virgo may think more about communication. As the Moon departs Libra, they might turn more inward, and Scorpio could feel the Moon’s emotional effects more strongly. Sagittarius might be focusing on rest and reflection, Capricorn on friendships, Aquarius on career matters and Pisces on plans or personal growth.
