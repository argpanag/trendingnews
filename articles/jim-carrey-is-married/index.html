Jim Carrey Is Married?

TheTools · 02.10.2026 00:19 · source · permalink

Congratulations to Jim Carrey, who has reportedly tied the knot with his longtime girlfriend, Min Ah. This is especially exciting news for those of us who weren’t even aware that Carrey had a longtime girlfriend.

TMZ was first to report that Carrey and Min Ah were recently married in a private ceremony in Los Angeles. They first made their relationship public at the César Awards in February, when Carrey thanked his “sublime companion, Min Ah” when accepting the honorary César Award. “I love you, Min Ah,” he said in French. While the César Awards were the couple’s first public outing, they have been photographed together as far back as 2022.

Carrey has been married twice before. He and his first wife, Melissa Womer, with whom he shares a daughter, divorced in 1995. After that, he was briefly married to his Dumb and Dumber costar, Lauren Holly, but they also divorced in 1997. Of all Carrey’s relationships, the least is known about Min Ah — and something tells me it’s going to stay that way.
