Shelton: Jodar One of Best in The World In This Area

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

Count Rafa Jodar as among the best in this skill, says Ben Shelton.

By Richard Pagliaro | Thursday, November 19, 2026
Photo credit: Screen Shot

Even without the ball in his hand, Rafael Jodar is a disarming presence, says Ben Shelton.

US Open finalist Shelton saved two set points fending off Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5 in his final round-robin match of the ATP Finals in Turin today.

After the first pro meeting between the two former NCAA all-Americans, Shelton said the 20-year-old Jodar is already one of the most dangerous returners in the sport.

“In my opinion, he’s one of the best returners on tour. Both wings, can handle pace from up,” Shelton told the media in Turin today. “I mean, I’m serving 200 kilometers an hour into his body and he was putting it at my feet today.

“Definitely has one of the most aggressive second-serve returns on tour, and I see it over and over again throughout these tournaments. You know, when guys aren’t aggressive on second serves, he makes them pay consistently, puts the ball to the corners or with really good depth, and he breaks serve a lot.”

In fact, ATP Finals debutant Jodar is third on the ATP Tour in return games won this year. Jodar is breaking serve 29.10%, which is behind only Jannik Sinner (29.16%) and Tour leader Carlos Alcaraz (30.10) for the Tour lead.

Today, Shelton stung 14 aces, including three aces in a row to seal the match, and fought off five of six break points in the first of what may well be many meetings between the pair.

“I think that [the return], you know, that’s one of his biggest strengths,” Shelton said of Jodar. “It makes it tough and stressful, serving against him, because you know that he’s not there to put it in play and run.

“He’s there to take the ball out of your hands.”
