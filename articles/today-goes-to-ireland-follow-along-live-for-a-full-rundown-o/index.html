Jenna Visits an Irish-Speaking Girls Boarding School (and Learns a Phrase or Two)

Today’s Morning Boost unfolded at Coláiste Íde, the country’s only all-Irish girls boarding school.

Located in the idyllic town of Dingle, Coláiste Íde is nestled on more than 100 acres. Housed in the former residence of Lord Ventry, the school is renowned for its rich heritage and picturesque grounds overlooking Dingle Bay.

Jenna was joined this morning by guests Lorna de Barra and Nancy Ní Chonaill, two students at the school.

The pair gave Jenna a lesson in Irish and the TODAY co-host learns a few words and phrases including how to say "good morning" and "I'm on top of the world," which, in Irish, translates into "I’m on top of a pig’s back."

Savannah Guthrie Braves the Wild Atlantic to Take in the 'Incredible' Cliffs of Moher

Welcome to the Cliffs of Moher. Rising more than 700 feet above the Atlantic Ocean, the cliffs are a UNESCO Global Geopark and one of Ireland’s most popular tourist attractions.

Located along the west coast of the island in County Clare, the Cliffs of Moher are part of Ireland’s Wild Atlantic Way, a 1600-mile route that stretches from Inishowen’s Malin Head to the charming coastal town of Kinsale.

The Cliffs of Moher on a clear day — not like the rainy, drizzly day that Savannah visited on. MomentRF

To get there, Savannah took a choppy ferry ride from Kenmare. Ferry operator Liam O'Brien, who operates the Doolin Ferry with his wife Erin, warned her they were "just at the tail end of a storm," and while they don't normally do trips in this type of weather, they "made an exception for you."

Savannah described the boat trip to a "roller coaster" and, on the swell, said, "This is crazy."

Savannah on the Doolin Ferry with Liam and Erin O'Brien.  TODAY

After that ride, Savannah took in the stunning cliffs for the very first time.

Comprised of shale, sandstone and siltstone, the natural wonder is thought to be more than 300 million years old and offers one of the most breathtaking vistas in the world.

If the cliffs look familiar, you might recognize them from the 2009 movie “Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince,” in which they served as the backdrop for one of the film’s most pivotal scenes.

Savannah taking in the view.

After looking at the cliffs from the water, she then took in the view from above. "Wow," Savannah said. "Incredible. I can't believe I'm standing here."

Where Is TODAY's Ireland Extravaganza Being Filmed? Meet Kenmare

All four hours of TODAY are being filmed in Kenmare, the southwest corner of Ireland.

"It's a beautiful little spot. Kenmare falls at the foot of some mountains, right at the Kenmare Bay," Carson says, adding that the nickname for the area is "the little nest."

Looking at footage of the day before, Savannah said, “Every shot is beautiful and every person is beautiful."

Keir Simmons, in a segment, shared his insights on Ireland's rich history and heritage. Merging ancient traditions and modern technology, Ireland is a cultural mecca, drawing tourists from across the globe.

TODAY's Desk is a Stunning Custom Creation

Ireland is pulling out all the stops for the TODAY show right down to this custom wood desk made especially for the team.

Crafted by Kerry Hills, the Bespoke Marquetry Broadcast Table takes its inspiration from Kenmare's rolling hills and mountains. According to the crafter, rather than depicting a single motif, the TODAY desk "expresses the character and rhythm of place and feeling."

And while the TODAY logo is the focal point, "the rays of the sun end on the horizon line."

A Historic First for TODAY!

Throughout its 75 years, TODAY has traveled across the globe for a variety of segments and major events like the Olympics. However, the Oct. 16 show marks the first time in TODAY history that all four hours of the show will broadcast from Ireland.

Join Savannah, Craig, Al, Jenna, Sheinelle, Carson and Dylan live from Sheen Falls Lodge in picturesque County Kerry. While there, they’ll visit a variety of local attractions, sample some of the island's best cuisine, discover hidden shopping haunts and so much more.

The TODAY team will also be joined by notable Irish celebrities and guests, who share their insights on Ireland’s rich culture, heritage and history.

Whether you hail from the Emerald Isle, are planning to visit its rolling green hills, or simply want to learn more about the Land of a Thousand Welcomes, you don’t want to miss this once-in-a lifetime show!
