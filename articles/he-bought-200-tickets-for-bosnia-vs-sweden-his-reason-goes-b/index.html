A Bosnia and Herzegovina supporter has bought an extraordinary 200 tickets for Saturday's Nations League match against Sweden, with reports suggesting the visitors could have as many as 40,000 fans inside Stockholm's Strawberry Arena.

Muamer Makić, a Bosnian living in Sweden, wanted to make sure his family and friends would not miss the occasion. He secured the tickets through an early purchasing opportunity and distributed or sold every one of them within three days.

His story was featured by Swedish television channel TV4, which reported that demand among Bosnia supporters has been so intense that Makić continues to receive requests for tickets despite having none left.

The scale of the expected travelling support is extraordinary. With a crowd approaching 50,000 anticipated for the match in Solna on 14 November, Bosnian media estimates suggest that up to 40,000 spectators could be supporting the visitors. That figure has not been officially confirmed.

For Makić, the occasion means more than football. Sweden provided a new home for thousands of Bosnians who fled the war during the 1990s, and he believes Saturday's match offers an opportunity to express gratitude towards their adopted country.

Speaking to TV4, Makić emphasised the respect Bosnians have for Sweden and suggested that many visiting supporters might even join in singing the Swedish national anthem before kick-off.

His son Isak is also looking forward to the occasion, which will be his first visit to Sweden's national stadium.

The remarkable interest follows another memorable meeting between the countries. In October, Swedish supporters joined Bosnian fans in applauding Edin Džeko during his emotional international farewell in Zenica, where the teams drew 1-1.

Bosnia subsequently defeated Poland 1-0 to remain unbeaten in their Nations League group, setting up another important encounter with Sweden. As Nogomania previously reported, the victory demonstrated the team's ability to win without their long-serving record goalscorer.

Saturday's match kicks off at 20:45 CET. Whatever the final number of away supporters, the evening promises to carry particular significance for Bosnia's large community in Sweden.
