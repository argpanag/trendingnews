The podium speaks:

Leclerc: “It was quite a good race, unfortunately we couldn’t get the win. But at the end of the day, it was a positive race. It was a fun race.”

Verstappen: “Our overall pace was not good enough and the tyres we had available were not the right ones. But we still managed to get on the podium.”

Antonelli: “It was a result we needed to be fair, the whole team deserved it – all this time working to bring this new package and finally seeing the result is very and very pleasant. It was always a perfect execution of strategy to call the one [pit] stop. Today was a great team effort. We’re very close [to the title]. Hopefully we can clinch it soon.”

Antonelli takes a selfie with the Mercedes crew and is now off to get weighed and checked over. He’d had to wait a whole three races for a win. Standards are slipping.

Kimi is excited on the team radio: “Yes, woo. Let’s go! That’s how we [BAD WORD ALERT] we do it!”

Kimi Antonelli wins the US Grand Prix ...

… but not the championship just yet. The 20-year-old will have to wait after Lewis Hamilton and George Russell finished fourth and eighth respectively, keeping their very faint title hopes alive. That said, his ninth win of the season was a dominant one, reaffirming the fact that he’s been by far the best rider all year. And, as you may have heard 843 times before, he’s still only 20.
