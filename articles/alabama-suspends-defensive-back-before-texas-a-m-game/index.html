Alabama suspends defensive back before Texas A&M game

TheTools · 24.10.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

Alabama suspended junior defensive back Dre Kirkpatrick Jr. before Saturday's game against Texas A&M.

Alabama has suspended defensive back Dre Kirkpatrick Jr. for a violation of team rules, an athletics department spokesperson confirmed Saturday.

Kirkpatrick Jr., a junior, was added to Saturday's final SEC availability report as out for the game against Texas A&M. He did not appear on the availability report Wednesday, Thursday or Friday.

The length of Kirkpatrick Jr.'s suspension is not immediately clear.

Kirkpatrick Jr. has played 59 defensive snaps this season, mostly in a rotational role at the slot "Husky" position behind starter Red Morgan. He has four tackles and one forced fumble.

The most likely candidate to replace Kirkpatrick Jr. is sophomore Chuck McDonald, who could also be needed at cornerback with starter Zabien Brown out against the Aggies. Dijon Lee and Carmelo O'Neal are expected to start at cornerback.

Alabama has 10 players listed as out for Saturday's game against Texas A&M, including three injured players who were ruled out in Saturday's final report: Brown, starting center Racin Delgatty and freshman running back EJ Crowell. The Tide previously ruled out starting wide receiver Ryan Coleman-Williams, starting defensive tackle Terrance Green and four others, including outside linebacker Desmond Umeozulu.

This marks consecutive seasons in which Kirkpatrick Jr. has been suspended by Alabama. He was suspended last November and missed the remainder of the season after being arrested during Alabama's off week. He returned for spring practice earlier this year.

The Tuscaloosa News first reported Kirkpatrick Jr.'s suspension.
