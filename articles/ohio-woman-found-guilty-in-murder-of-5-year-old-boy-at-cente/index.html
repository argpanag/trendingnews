Ohio woman found guilty in murder of 5-year-old boy at center of Amber Alert

TheTools · 11.11.2026 15:59 · source · permalink

A jury has convicted an Ohio woman in the murder of a 5-year-old boy, whose disappearance in 2024 prompted a statewide Amber Alert.
