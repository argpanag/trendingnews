Toni Kroos stopped watching Bayern Munich’s demolition of RB Leipzig after just 30 minutes, then warned the beaten visitors to show more resistance against Real Madrid.

Discussing Saturday’s 6-1 defeat on Einfach mal Luppen, the podcast he presents with his brother Felix, Kroos criticised Leipzig’s passive approach. German reports say he switched off early and watched the highlights afterwards.

“It could have been 10-0 at half-time. That’s brutal,” Kroos said, according to TAG24’s account of the podcast.

Bayern actually led 3-0 at the break, with Luis Diaz, Aleksandar Pavlovic and Michael Olise scoring. Diaz completed a hat-trick in the second half, while Harry Kane also struck before Andrija Maksimovic scored Leipzig’s late consolation.

Kroos acknowledged Bayern’s quality but argued Leipzig had allowed their opponents to play too freely. For a club targeting a place among the Bundesliga’s top three, he considered the performance unacceptable, even against Bayern.

His warning ahead of Wednesday’s Champions League visit to Madrid concerned Leipzig’s attitude as much as their defending. He felt they had looked resigned to defeat from the start in Munich and needed greater belief to avoid a repeat.

The result represented a sharp reversal for Martin Demichelis’s side. Leipzig had beaten Eintracht Frankfurt 6-0 the previous weekend before defeating PSV Eindhoven 1-0 in the Champions League.
