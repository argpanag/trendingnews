WTA Wuhan Day 3 Predictions Including Sabalenka vs Li

TheTools · 13.10.2026 17:28 · source · permalink

The Wuhan Open schedule is packed for the third straight day, and this piece features three matches, including a clash between three-time champion Aryna Sabalenka and Ann Li, which will certainly be a fun match. Who will advance?

WTA Wuhan Day 3 Predictions

Iva Jovic vs Dayana Yastremska

Head–to–Head: First Meeting

Iva Jovic arrives in Wuhan with her back against the wall. The American looked sharp in Beijing until Iga Swiatek ended her run in the Round of 16, and now she needs a title here to keep her WTA Finals hopes alive. A first-round meeting with Dayana Yastremska is a tricky way to start that mission, but Jovic is well equipped to handle it.

Dayana Yastremska can light up any court with her fearless, first-strike tennis, and she will probably take a set off the shot-making alone. But her error count was high in a three-set grind with Cristina Bucsa, and she needed a late escape after racing to a 5-0 lead in the decider. Against a counterpuncher as steady as Jovic, that kind of leaky patch gets punished.

Expect fireworks early, then Jovic to settle into her rhythm and wear the Ukrainian down.
Prediction: Jovic in 3

Katie Volynets vs Hanyu Guo

Head–to–Head: First Meeting

Katie Volynets arrives in Wuhan with a lot of match play in her legs. The American is at her best on hard courts, where her flat, compact groundstrokes and sharp return game turn rallies into a scramble for her opponent. If she starts quickly and keeps the points short, Guo will have to find answers early.

But Hanyu Guo has the crowd, and the pace. Playing at home, Guo will look to open up the court with heavy hitting and make Volynets defend. The question is consistency: if the unforced errors pile up, Volynets will happily feed on them. This one should swing on a few key games, and Volynets’ recent rhythm and returning edge her through. Volynets has the momentum, and she’ll need it.
Prediction: Volynets in 3

Aryna Sabalenka vs Ann Li

Head–to–Head: Sabalenka 3 – 0 Li

On paper, this is a heavyweight against a counterpuncher, and Aryna Sabalenka should have the edge. The former world No. 1 plays big, hits first and asks opponents to survive her serve and forehand. If her first-serve percentage holds and she keeps the unforced errors down, Ann Li may struggle to get a foothold in rallies. Wuhan’s quick hard courts suit Sabalenka’s power, and she’ll look to end points early.

Ann Li won’t go quietly, though. The American is a clean ball-striker who takes time away and redirects pace well, and she can be awkward when an opponent gets impatient. Her route to an upset is to pepper the Sabalenka backhand, extend rallies and hope the errors start to pile up. It’s a tall order, but if Sabalenka’s serve wobbles, Li will be ready.
Prediction: Sabalenka in 3
