Discover Gordon Bay Cottage, now on Airbnb

TheTools · 09.09.2026 13:36 · source · permalink

This fall, Airbnb is opening the doors to yet another fan-favorite retreat – Gordon Bay Cottage, an iconic Canadian getaway that may look familiar – inviting guests to experience cottage country for themselves. Built as a summer hotel, Gordon Bay Cottage has a rich history that remains woven into the home today, from its century-old windows and hardware to the Muskoka-style screened porch overlooking the water. 

Muskoka is having a moment, thanks to star-studded summer escapes and a steady stream of pop culture cameos. As cottage country captures global attention, interest in Muskoka Airbnbs is climbing: searches from outside Canada for check-ins through November 2026 are up nearly 110 percent year over year, while overall searches, including from Canadians, are up more than 30 percent.1 

Exclusively on Airbnb, a select number of guests can book Gordon Bay Cottage for a stay this fall and experience the beauty of Muskoka and all of cottage country firsthand.

About the Stay

Gordon Bay Cottage offers a quintessential cottage country getaway with plenty of room to make the experience your own. Guests will have the cottage and private dock all to themselves, with plenty of ways to fill the hours.

Throughout the stay, guests will:

  • Take in the views from nearly every room, framed by the cottage's original windows.
  • Keep an eye out for local wildlife, from loons calling across the lake to chipmunks darting through the trees, alongside the occasional acorn.
  • Follow the granite steps to the private dock for a crisp morning dip or a paddleboard adventure.
  • Enjoy the 800-square-foot screened porch, made for taking in lake views, morning yoga, afternoon games, and everything in between.
  • Gather around the living room fireplace as the sun sets for a cozy night in.
  • Look up at the clear Muskoka sky after dark that offers spectacular views of the stars.
  • Stay connected with Wi-Fi as needed, though the cottage naturally draws attention to the moments that unfold beyond the screen.
  • For adventure seekers, the open waters can be explored by canoes, kayaks, and paddleboards.
  • Venture into the nearby town of Port Carling to discover a taste of Muskoka charm through local restaurants, coffee shops, and waterfront views.

How to Request to Book

For guests looking for an escape, three special promotional bookings will be available for October 2–5, October 9–12, and October 16–19 in Ontario, Canada. Guests will be able to book one of these three stays starting September 15 at 9:00 AM ET at $180 CAD per night. To book, visit airbnb.com/gordonbaycottage. 

"Gordon Bay Cottage has been on this lake since 1902, starting as a lodge for hunters and fishermen, with most of that original structure still in place. What I love about Gordon's Bay is exactly what makes cottage country so incredible. People come here to slow down and escape the hustle of everyday life. I can't wait to welcome the first Airbnb guests to take in the sights and sounds you can only find in cottage country," said Jayne McCaw, Airbnb host.

Experience the iconic Ontario Cottage Country

For generations, Canadians have made their way to cottages for lake days, long weekends, and a change of pace and scenery. Now, the tradition is capturing the attention of global audiences, with travelers from around the world discovering what locals have loved for decades. That interest is showing up in the local economy: Airbnb hosts in cottage country have helped generate over $83M in economic impact and supported nearly 1,300 jobs, bringing tourism spending to small businesses throughout cottage country.2

"Canadians have been escaping to Ontario’s cottage country for decades, and it’s exciting to see travellers from around the world discovering what makes this region so special. Tourism supports hundreds of businesses across Muskoka, and Airbnb hosts are an important part of welcoming visitors. We hope this incredible spotlight inspires even more people to come experience Muskoka for themselves, support local businesses and discover the region year-round," said Val Hamilton, Executive Director of Discover Muskoka. 

For travelers eager to embrace the cottage lifestyle, there are plenty of options – from cozy lakeside classics to elevated waterfront retreats waiting to be booked.
