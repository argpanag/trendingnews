Real Madrid are keen on Bayer Leverkusen's Jarell Quansah, Liverpool are monitoring Club Tijuana's teenage midfielder Gilberto Mora and Roma remain keen on signing Real Madrid striker Endrick.

Real Madrid are showing an interest in Bayer Leverkusen's 23-year-old England defender Jarell Quansah, who Arsenal made a bid for in the summer and was subject of an enquiry from Chelsea. (Bild - in German), external

Mexico's teenage star Gilberto Mora, 17, is on Liverpool's radar although Borussia Dortmund and Benfica are also keen on the Club Tijuana midfielder. (Teamtalk), external

Spain and Barcelona midfielder Dani Olmo, 28, wants to finish his career with Dinamo Zagreb. (Fichajes - in Spanish), external

Canada forward Jonathan David, 26, is starting to convince Atletico Madrid that they should make his loan from Juventus a permanent £21m transfer. (Fichajes - in Spanish), external

Inter Milan are keen on Italy midfielder Alessandro Romano and might make a move for the 20-year-old, who is on loan at Calgliari from Roma, if Turkey international Hakan Calhanoglu, 32, leaves the club at the end of his contract next summer. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external

Roma missed out on a loan move for Real Madrid striker Endrick, 20, in the summer but remain interested in signing the Brazil international in January. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external

Barcelona made an enquiry about Croatia defender Josko Gvardiol last season but it was clear the 24-year-old did not want to leave Manchester City. (Mundo Deportivo - in Spanish), external

Barcelona and Inter Milan are monitoring Stuttgart defender Finn Jeltsch, with the 20-year-old German also attracting interest from Premier League clubs. (Sky Sports Germany), external

Bologna have agreed a deal to sign former Manchester United defender Matteo Darmian, 36, on a free transfer after the Italian left Inter Milan at the end of last season. (Fabrizio Romano), external
