SunLive - Lotto First Division struck in BoP town - The Bay's News First

TheTools · 11.09.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

A winning Lotto First Division ticket has been sold in Kawerau.

The small Bay of Plenty town will be celebrating after a ticket worth $250,000 was sold via MyLotto for Wednesday night’s live draw.

Two other players in Hamilton and Canterbury also won a quarter of a million dollars in First Division.

A winning Strike Four ticket was sold on MyLotto to a player from Auckland, taking home $200,000.

Seven players won $29,673 each with Lotto Second Division, including one player from Tairua.

Meanwhile, Lotto NZ announced the $10 million Must Be Won Powerball draw, which will be held this Saturday. It will mark the final draw under the current 10-ball game, ahead of the launch of the new 14-ball game on Sunday.

The winning Second Division tickets were sold at the following stores:

MyLotto (x3) - Auckland

Riverhead Mini Market - Riverhead

Tairua Lotto & Stationery - Tairua

MyLotto - Hamilton

MyLotto - Wellington

Anyone who bought a ticket from any of the above winning stores should check their ticket as soon as possible in-store, on MyLotto, or through MyLotto App.
