Spanish media give their 'verdict' on Gordon, after the Englishman's first seven games with Barça

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:20 · source · permalink

The Spanish media have already made their assessment of Anthony Gordon after his seventh match with the Catalan club.

Gordon signed a five-year contract with Barcelona after completing a £69.3m move from Newcastle United before the World Cup.

It didn't take long for the Englishman to make his mark at his new club, registering four assists in his first four games.

Now, three La Liga matches later, Gordon has failed to register another goal contribution, despite his teammates giving opposing defenses nightmares.

Gordon started the game in Barcelona's 4-2 victory over Levante on September 13, with Lamine Yamali's brace ensuring the Catalans continued their perfect start.

Despite being left on the bench for the 7-2 win over Racing Santander in midweek, Gordon was brought on by coach Hansi Flick against Sevilla on Saturday (September 19), as Raphinha scored a hat-trick to give the La Liga champions a convincing 3-1 victory.

Yamal provided two assists, while Gordon struggled to make a positive impact in the match, missing two clear chances late on to score his first goal for the La Liga champions.

Gordon's recent form caused a huge reaction from the Spanish media after the match.

Mundo Deportivo described Gordon's performance as incomplete, writing: "He is a tactical blessing. But being a striker, as he is, he lacks that spark that distinguishes a star from a good player. He ended up playing as a center forward and missed a scoring opportunity, which he sought with more passion than finesse."

“Barca Universal” gave Gordon a 6.5: “Once again, Gordon appeared isolated on the wing and relatively far from building up the central defence. He didn’t complete many one-on-ones and didn’t produce much in terms of crosses. Gordon’s work was positive, but his impact has been on the decline in recent matches. He even moved into the centre as the match progressed, but failed to find himself in any promising scoring situations.”

"Barca Blaugranes" gave Gordon a 6/10, explaining: "He's starting to look a bit one-dimensional now, he works extremely hard, but his contribution in the final stages is a bit limited and he shot over the bar or missed a few good chances to score his first goal for Barca."

Sports Illustrated gave him a slightly higher score of 7.1, adding: "He had some good moments, but it's becoming increasingly clear that he's too eager to score his first goal for Barca. He missed a golden opportunity and was substituted shortly afterwards."

Meanwhile, "AS" wrote: "He tried, but nothing came of it. At least he doesn't give up and keeps trying, but he's unlucky in front of goal. He had two big chances when he played for a few minutes as a false 9, with Lamin and Adeyemi as his main suppliers."

“SPORT” gave the winger a 6/10, writing: “He moved all over the attack and ended up playing as a centre-forward. He wasn’t sharp, as evidenced by the two missed shots, and he needs to score to regain his confidence. However, he held the centre-backs, coped with the pressure and offered the tactical discipline that Flick requires. His profile complements that of Adeyemi. Barcelona remain unstoppable. They know how to score goals and they also know how to suffer. Flick’s team enters the international break without dropping a single point and with the feeling that this season could be very special.”

Barcelona will return to action at home against Getafe after the international break. /GazetaExpress/
