Choco Mucho sweeps Nxled, moves one win away from PVL Siklab bronze

TheTools · 28.11.2026 09:35 · source · permalink

Choco Mucho vented its ire on skidding Nxled, moving closer to a bronze finish after scoring a 25-14, 25-23, 25-22 sweep in Game 1 of the 2026 Premier Volleyball League All-Filipino Conference Siklab series third-place clash on Saturday at the Philsports Arena.

The Flying Titans, who fell short against twice-to-beat Creamline in the semifinals following a 21-25, 25-17, 25-17, 25-23 defeat, bounced back in emphatic fashion by sweeping the Chameleons, who absorbed their third consecutive loss, to move within a victory of securing the bronze medal.

“Naging maganda ‘yung simula talaga namin. Nakita ko sa players ko na talagang ‘yung sideout namin, nakuha namin ‘yung pasa namin,” said Choco Mucho head coach Dante Alinsunurin.

“Talagang mataas ang porsyento kaya siguro nagpatuloy-tuloy ‘yung game namin na nakuha ‘yung game. ‘Yun nga, sobrang ganda ng porsyento na ginawa namin, and tulong-tulong sila sa pagkuha ng panalo ngayon,” he added.

(C) PVL Images

Nxled made one last push to extend the match, with Brooke Van Sickle stuffing Caitlin Viray at the net before MJ Phillips followed with a quick attack to fuel a 3-1 run that brought the Chameleons within striking distance at 23-22.

However, the comeback bid came to a halt when Phillips sent her serve long, handing the Flying Titans match point. Eya Laure then delivered the finishing blow, powering through a double block to slam the door on Nxled and seal the straight-sets victory for Choco Mucho.

Sisi Rondina-Rosales led the Flying Titans with 12 points, all from attacks, while also recording 11 excellent receptions. Laure chipped in 11 points in the one-hour, 26-minute victory.

Maddie Madayag added nine points, while Viray contributed eight points on six attacks, one block, and one ace. Kat Tolentino, Jen Villegas, and Jewel Encarnacion also chipped in five points apiece.

Alina Bicar orchestrated Choco Mucho’s balanced offensive attack with 15 excellent sets and eight excellent digs, while Dawn Catindig anchored the defensive effort with 18 excellent digs.

The Flying Titans will look to close out the series in Game 2 on Tuesday at 3 p.m. at the same venue.

(C) PVL Images

Nxled, which swept the elimination round with a perfect 8-0 record to secure the top seed, continued its late-conference slide, absorbing its third consecutive defeat. The Chameleons had also been swept by Farm Fresh in the semifinals despite holding a twice-to-beat advantage.

Team captain Van Sickle posted 13 points, nine excellent digs, and eight excellent receptions, while Phillips and Myla Pablo added 11 and 10 points, respectively, in the defeat.

Gel Cayuna finished with 17 excellent sets and three points, while Bang Pineda recorded 11 excellent digs.
