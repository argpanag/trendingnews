Say Goodbye to a Major Streaming Service as Disney Quietly Confirms Phaseout

In 2025, Disney confirmed that Hulu's days were numbered, and that Disney+ would replace the app. Now, the phaseout is finally happening.

Disney+ has been streamlining the process for Hulu app subscribers to join the platform earlier this year. After over a year and a half, the process is finally happening. Per What's on Disney+, Disney+ will merge with Hulu in December.

Hulu Is Being Phased Out

While the Hulu service continues, the app will become obsolete. Disney+ already features multiple shared content with Hulu in a "unified app experience." In August, one year after it announced the plans to merge the two platforms, Disney offered a profile integration for Hulu subscribers on House of Mouse's app.

The plans to merge Hulu into Disney+ were always teased to happen in late 2026, and December is the best time to do it.

Disney+ has already added multiple features to improve the user experience and have both Disney+ and Hulu subscribers enjoy some of the same content. While the platforms still have some different titles due to copyright laws, it made it easier to use one service or both.

The profile integration allowed Hulu users to connect their profile to Disney+, even if they had a valid subscription or not. Those who had Hulu under a bundle could connect their Hulu profile to an existing Disney+ account. Those with Hulu or Hulu Premium subscriptions are automatically linked to Disney+ as part of what the company describes as a more "simple" and "unified" experience. The feature also supports the linking of 18+ profiles.

The new merger will help create a fully unified experience, which includes live TV and select premium add-ons, as well as shared content.

Disney has yet to detail when the Hulu app will be phased out, and if there are any changes moving forward. The biggest impact will be in the U.S., as, internationally, Hulu replaced Star as a brand and is already fully integrated.

Disney's full acquisition of Hulu happened in June 2026, after its deal with Comcast that had been a long time coming.

Hulu & Disney+ Recently Raised Their Prices

Since the streaming war started around 2020, owning multiple subscriptions comes with one guarantee: they will hike the prices every year. Services like Netflix even raised prices twice in 2026, with multiple other platforms to follow in September.

Disney+ and Hulu were among the streaming platforms to hike prices in September. The deals also incentivized bundling, as the differences between plans weren't so big.

Following the latest wave of price hikes, Disney+ Premium and Hulu + Premium individual plans were raised to $21.49 per month, a 13% increase. The ad-supported plans also saw a 50-cent increase to $12.49 per month.

The ad-free Disney+ and Hulu bundle went up by $2 to $21.99, while the ad-supported bundle kept its current monthly cost of $12.99, nearly half the cost of subscribing to Disney+ and Hulu individually. The Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN bundle with ad support was increased by $2 to $21.99 monthly.
