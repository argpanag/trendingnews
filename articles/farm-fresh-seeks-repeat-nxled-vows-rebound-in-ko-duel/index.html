Farm Fresh seeks repeat, Nxled vows rebound in KO duel

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:55 · source · permalink

Having shed the burden of protecting an unbeaten record, Nxled now faces a different kind of pressure – one that leaves no room for another misstep.

The Chameleons try to get back on track against a surging Farm Fresh side Thursday, as the two teams collide in a knockout showdown for the right to face Creamline in the PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series Finals at the Philsports Arena in Pasig City.

Game time is at 6 p.m., with both sides fully aware that everything they have worked for this conference now boils down to one all-important victory.

Farm Fresh, fresh from its thrilling five-set triumph over the erstwhile unbeaten Nxled last Tuesday, seeks to sustain its momentum and complete what could be a breakthrough march to the championship round.

Nxled, meanwhile, is out to prove that its first setback of the series will not define – or end – its campaign, determined to rediscover the form that powered its impressive sweep of the elimination round that netted the Chameleons the twice-to-beat edge in the semifinals.

For the Foxies, the opportunity is especially significant.

Since joining the Sports Vision-organized league in 2023, Farm Fresh has steadily built its credentials, with its fourth-place finish in the All-Filipino Conference last April marking its best campaign yet. The Foxies fell short of the bronze medal against PLDT but are now just one victory away from an even bigger breakthrough – a maiden Finals appearance.

And after demonstrating that they can go toe-to-toe with the previously unbeaten Chameleons, the Foxies have every reason to believe they can deliver another telling performance.

“Asahan nyo na lalaban ang Foxies. Kukunin namin ang Game 2, hindi lang para sa amin but para sa lahat ng sumusuporta sa Farm Fresh,” said Royse Tubino, whose explosive 18-point performance anchored the Foxies' stunning Game One victory.

That win not only put Farm Fresh in a position to advance but also gave the young franchise a huge confidence boost heading into the decisive encounter. With a Finals berth within reach, expect the Foxies to raise their level anew, draw inspiration from their breakthrough triumph and go all-out to finish the job.

But coach Koji Tsuzurabara is taking nothing for granted, keeping Farm Fresh grounded even after its impressive victory.

"It's just one win. But what's important is the next game," said Tsuzurabara, who, despite their win, maintains they remain the underdogs.

"As challengers, we want to make history for Farm Fresh. But if we want to win, we need to practice more. No rest," he added, referring to the team's Wednesday break, which they used instead as an opportunity for a tuneup.

But Nxled is hardly ready to surrender its championship aspirations.

The Chameleons had swept their way through the elims to secure one of the two semifinal incentives, putting themselves in prime position to book an early ticket to the Finals. Yet after failing to close out Game One despite having the advantage of an unbeaten record, they now find themselves facing a sudden-death situation.

While the defeat was a frustrating interruption to an otherwise dominant campaign, it could also serve as the wake-up call Nxled needs to rediscover its urgency, composure and winning rhythm.
The Chameleons know that their elimination-round sweep counts for little if they cannot translate that dominance into a Finals appearance. More than simply recovering from their first loss, they must show the resilience and championship temperament needed to deliver when the stakes are highest.

They also have the personnel and experience to respond, and do-or-die match offers them the chance to turn an unexpected setback into a defining moment in their campaign.

Beyond the immediate prize of a Finals berth, however, lies an even bigger challenge.

The winner will advance to face a formidable opponent in the championship round – a team that, given its quality, pedigree and competitive fire, is likely the side every contender would rather avoid in a Finals showdown.

Creamline marched to its 16th Finals appearance by thwarting Choco Mucho in four.
That makes Thursday's encounter even more compelling: Farm Fresh is chasing a historic breakthrough and a chance to carry its giant-killing momentum into the championship series, while Nxled is fighting to ensure that its impressive elimination-round sweep does not end in a heartbreaking semifinal exit.

One team will take another step toward history. The other will be left wondering what might have been.
