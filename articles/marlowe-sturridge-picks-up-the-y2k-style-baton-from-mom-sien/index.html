Marlowe Sturridge Picks Up the Y2K Style Baton From Mom Sienna Miller With This It-Bag

TheTools · 17.09.2026 15:57 · source · permalink

Sienna Miller's daughter Marlowe Sturridge might be only 14 years old, but she’s already taking after her mother when it comes to fashion, showing she, too, can rock a Y2K staple like no other.

On September 16, Sturridge made a rare appearance alongside her mother at the premiere of her upcoming film, War, at Leicester Square in London, and she made sure to dazzle in the same effortless chic style that made her mother a street style icon in the ‘00s.

For the occasion, Sturridge ditched the black tire attire, opting instead for a casual but cute, simple look anchored by a yellow vintage cami tee from the ‘70s featuring a graphic emblem proclaiming the MLB Pittsburgh Pirates as the 1979 World Series champions.

To balance the graphic T-shirt, Sturridge chose a low-rise satin maxi skirt with matching black lace trim for added texture. Lace-trimmed satin skirts and shorts have been all the rage this past year, with brands like Stella McCartney, Staud, Dries Van Noten, Prada, and many more offering their own take on the trend.

To match the skirt's cute allure, she added Miu Miu mesh Mary Jane ballet flats with thick silver buckle detailing. With three simple elements, Sturridge already managed a stylish formula worthy of her mom; however, she added the Y2K pièce de résistance with her bag: a black Chloé Paddington with gold brass hardware. Does it get any more ‘00s than that?

The Chloé Paddington is one of the most recognizable slouchy bags of the millennium era. Designed by Phoebe Philo, the style first launched in 2005 and was spotted on all the it-girls of the ‘00s, from Mischa Barton to Hilary Duff. Though Sienna Miller favored another ‘00s staple (the Balenciaga City bag) much more, she's a longtime fan of Chloé and has been spotted carrying a bunch of the brand's bags in the past, including the Paddington, and, hey, maybe she is precisely who Sturridge got the bag from.

It does seem like the 14-year-old has a knack for raiding her mom’s closet, after all. Speaking to British Vogue earlier this month, Miller admitted that Sturridge has a blast borrowing not only from her closet but also from Alexa Chung’s, who is currently in a relationship with Tom Sturridge, Miller’s former partner and Sturridge’s father. “She says she likes bottom halves from me and top halves from Alexa,” Miller told the publication. “She’s got it all going on. She’s very cool,” she added.

Tom Sturridge, Alexa Chung, Oli Green and Sienna Miller at the All England Lawn Tennis and Croquet Club on July 03, 2022, in London, England.

Clearly, Sturridge is already a style force to be reckoned with, and Miller also warned British Vogue that “she doesn’t hold back with the criticism” either. “The other day, I put on this Chloé suede jacket–a really beautiful blue, gorgeous, cape-y thing – and she just went, ‘Napoleoooon!’ So I took it off. I do feel like I’ve got this great usurper in my midst…” she recounted.

With London Fashion Week around the corner, we can only wonder whether Marlowe Sturridge will put on her fashion critic cap and attend some shows.
