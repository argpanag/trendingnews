    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent

    Kimi Antonelli clinched his first world title and became the youngest champion in Formula 1 history by finishing third at the Mexico City Grand Prix.

    Antonelli - 20 years and 68 days old - finished the race in third, behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton.

    His moment of glory came on a day when tensions at Ferrari between seven-time champion Hamilton and team-mate Charles Leclerc came close to boiling over.

    Antonelli drove a composed race amid some hectic action involving the other front-runners in a grand prix interrupted by a red flag that led to a 25-lap sprint to the finish.

  14. 'We have to have some serious talks' - Hamiltonpublished at 23:03 GMT 1 November
    23:03 GMT 1 November

    Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton who finished second at the Mexico City Grand Prix: "Firstly, look at this crowd. We have to acknowledge them. A huge congratulations to Kimi and the Mercedes team. It’s great to see them all up here celebrating their success. Youngest world champion ever, it’s pretty phenomenal, and I’m so grateful to be in the race to witness it.

    On his race: "Tough day. I think we had the pace to win today, but it wasn’t meant to be and we’ll take it offline and figure out what we can do in the background.

    “In the drivers’ briefing, we discussed as soon as you go off in Turn 1, you have to give the position back. Last year I got a 10-second penalty for that same move that he did. But ultimately, it was that that lost us the win today. I think we have to have some serious talks behind the scenes, for sure.

    “But the good thing is the team are working really hard. We’ve really improved our car. It’s great to be here, and we were on the old engine this weekend as well. Good results, good points. Shame we didn’t get a win, but some you win, some you lose.

    "I am hoping next year we have an engine to compete with them. This year they were flying past us on the straights through the year. We can fix that and keep this car, the base of it. I am relying on the team, I have 100% faith in them."

  15. 'It was tough' - Verstappenpublished at 22:59 GMT 1 November
    22:59 GMT 1 November

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen who won in Mexico: "It was very hectic out there today but the first start, already I think made the race for me. I just had a very good first lap, let's say it like that. And after that, yeah we had a bit of a different strategy, I do think we lacked a little bit of pace, but we just hung in there, made the right calls at the right time.

    "Of course the penalty of Charles put us into the lead and then you could see that they were always staying with me. It was tough, I was sweating at the end to try and keep Lewis behind.

    "As you know it is such a long run into Turn One and to pick your braking point there with the big bump in the middle of the track, I had to be quite careful, that is always a tradition in Turn One that there are two cars fighting for the lead.

    On World Champion Kimi Antonelli: "It is incredible of course, what a season he has had. To do that also at such an age I think is just really impressive and it is amazing to see the whole team celebrating with him, his family is here as well so I am sure this is a very emotional day for him and I am very proud of him."

'We have to have some serious talks' - Hamiltonpublished at 23:03 GMT 1 November
23:03 GMT 1 November

Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton who finished second at the Mexico City Grand Prix: "Firstly, look at this crowd. We have to acknowledge them. A huge congratulations to Kimi and the Mercedes team. It’s great to see them all up here celebrating their success. Youngest world champion ever, it’s pretty phenomenal, and I’m so grateful to be in the race to witness it.

On his race: "Tough day. I think we had the pace to win today, but it wasn’t meant to be and we’ll take it offline and figure out what we can do in the background.

“In the drivers’ briefing, we discussed as soon as you go off in Turn 1, you have to give the position back. Last year I got a 10-second penalty for that same move that he did. But ultimately, it was that that lost us the win today. I think we have to have some serious talks behind the scenes, for sure.

“But the good thing is the team are working really hard. We’ve really improved our car. It’s great to be here, and we were on the old engine this weekend as well. Good results, good points. Shame we didn’t get a win, but some you win, some you lose.

"I am hoping next year we have an engine to compete with them. This year they were flying past us on the straights through the year. We can fix that and keep this car, the base of it. I am relying on the team, I have 100% faith in them."
