Nuggets Rest DeMar DeRozan Against Clippers in Preseason

TheTools · 15.10.2026 02:57 · source · permalink

Denver will be without DeMar DeRozan for its Wednesday preseason matchup against the Los Angeles Clippers in Los Angeles, according to Altitude Sports reporter Katy Winge. The Nuggets are resting the veteran forward while evaluating their roster and giving younger players opportunities to strengthen their cases for regular-season minutes.

According to RotoWire, Denver's starters are expected to play despite DeRozan's absence, leaving the team without one of its leading bench options. DeRozan has averaged 4.7 points, 1.3 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 10.2 minutes per game during the preseason.

The Nuggets enter the matchup with two consecutive exhibition victories and a 2-1 preseason record, according to Sports Illustrated. Wednesday's game begins a two-game stretch in Los Angeles, with Denver scheduled to face the Los Angeles Lakers on Friday before the regular season begins.

With limited preseason opportunities remaining, several players are competing for places in coach David Adelman's rotation. The upcoming games provide additional chances to demonstrate their readiness in live competition, which Adelman has emphasized offers experiences that practices cannot fully reproduce.

DaRon Holmes is seeking a larger role in Denver's frontcourt after missing his entire rookie season with a torn Achilles. The 24-year-old center has made progress in his recovery but faces competition from Spencer Jones and Marvin Bagley III for backup frontcourt minutes.

Holmes described the remaining exhibition games as an opportunity to demonstrate his offseason development after Tuesday's practice.

"Opportunity. I think that's a big thing, not only for myself, but for some of the other players," Holmes said. "This is the time to showcase what we've been working on this summer. ...

I know for me, I'm already feeling a lot more comfortable from where I was last year to now. I feel like I've gotten a lot stronger. I feel like I'm understanding the game a lot better."

Holmes scored 11 points and collected five rebounds in Denver's previous game. His preseason performances could influence how much responsibility he receives as the team's third-string center.

Ryan Nembhard is also trying to improve his standing after receiving praise from Adelman during training camp and preseason. The 23-year-old two-way guard has yet to move ahead of veteran Tyus Jones, who has continued to impress as a ball-handler for Denver's second unit.

Nembhard's ability to distribute reliably and defend effectively could help him earn additional opportunities during the regular season. Denver's need for dependable ball-handling remains relevant as the coaching staff assesses its bench options.

Rookies Trevon Brazile and Bryce Hopkins are also competing for opportunities. Hopkins has already played alongside Denver's starters, while Brazile received substantial reserve minutes at power forward during an earlier preseason game when Spencer Jones was unavailable.

Both rookies offer defensive versatility, but securing consistent rotation minutes will be difficult. Hopkins, who is on a two-way contract, could receive additional playing time in the G League, while Brazile is expected to occupy a depth role toward the end of Denver's bench.

The Nuggets and Clippers are scheduled to tip off at 8:30 p.m. Mountain Time on Wednesday, with the game available on NBA TV. Denver will conclude its preseason schedule against the Lakers on Friday.
