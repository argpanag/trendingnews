Channing Tatum's New Near-Perfect Crime Thriller Set To Be One of 2026's Best in First Trailer

TheTools · 14.10.2026 16:02 · source · permalink

Channing Tatum is in the midst of a massive year in 2026, with much more coming soon for the bayou-based star. The year will end with Tatum reprising his dream role as Gambit in the MCU, starring in Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday alongside a host of legacy X-Men stars and more than a dozen Marvel legends from the last 18 years. Additionally, he is confirmed to return to the world of Jump Street next to Jonah Hill in 24 Jump Street in 2027. Before all that, however, the longtime fan-favorite will embrace a much more sinister and grounded story.

Tatum now joins director Beth de Araújo for a role in the new movie Josephine, which will come to theaters on Nov. 20. This movie will feature Tatum as a man named Damien alongside fellow MCU star Gemma Chan as his wife, Claire, and newcomer Mason Reeves as their daughter, the titular Josephine. This film had its world premiere at the Sundance Film Festival in Jan. 2026, winning multiple awards at this and other festivals earlier in the year. Now, this trio gets its first chance to show what's coming in this story in the first full look at the new movie.

Sumerian Pictures released an official trailer for the upcoming Josephine movie, starring Channing Tatum and Gemma Chan. The trailer teases a dark story in which Tatum and Chan, as parents, navigate a difficult situation when their child, Josephine, witnesses a crime in Golden Gate Park. While the trailer does not show the specific crime that takes place, Josephine's life is impacted heavily at a young age, as she has to testify in court and tell adult professionals what she saw in the park. The footage also teases the tension her parents feel having to watch their child endure something so traumatic.

While Tatum and Chan are no strangers to darker movies, this will put both of them in unique roles as parents to a young child who is going through something no child should have to endure. Chan even spoke about her experience on the movie in 2025, explaining that the story was deeply personal to de Araújo and expressing how she wanted to do justice to what was being shown on screen. Additionally, that story was inspired by an event from de Araújo's childhood, making it even more important to present it the right way. Now, after rave reviews for Tatum and Chan, the stage is set for the movie to reach a much larger audience.

Josephine will debut in the United States on Nov. 20, 2026.
