Orlando Magic at Phoenix Suns: Game Preview | Orlando Magic

TheTools · 06.11.2026 02:07 · source · permalink

The Orlando Magic are hoping their second road trip of the season goes similar to their first.

Orlando went 2-1 in its first action away from the Kia Center and is now aiming to pull off a similar feat when it heads out West for the first time this season.

The Magic (2-5) look to get back in the win column when they visit the Phoenix Suns (1-5) on Thursday at 9 p.m. ET.

“(We need to) keep our spirits high and play an intense game for 48 minutes,” said Magic forward Franz Wagner. “And clean up some of the execution stuff on offense.”

Injuries, turnovers and fluctuations in its 3-point shooting have been key factors in Orlando’s first seven contests of its 2026-27 campaign.

Ball handlers like Paolo Banchero and Jalen Suggs have been sidelined for extended stretches, and guard Anthony Black, who’s expected to play tonight, has missed three games with an ankle injury. That’s certainly played a role in Orlando’s ball security issues. But regardless of who’s on the floor, the Magic are looking to cut back on their 17.3 giveaways per outing.

“We haven’t started at home the way we’ve wanted to,” said Wagner. “So, these games are really important for us now. Clean up some of the turnovers and some of our execution in general. And keep our spirits high. It’s still early in the season and I think we have a good opportunity to go win some games.”

Phoenix, meanwhile, is also trying to get back in the win column after a slow start. Its offense is still led by guard Devin Booker, who’s averaging 24.7 points per contest. The Suns move the ball with efficiency, ranking both top 10 in assists (27.4) and fewest turnovers (14.7) per game.

“They’re aggressive on both sides of the ball,” said Magic guard Desmond Bane. “Hunting threes, hunting jump shots (and) defensively they try and pressure you. They have versatile guys that can switch. So, we have to execute.”

Orlando will look to get out and run against a Phoenix squad that’s giving up the second most points (124.3) per game on defense. The Magic will attempt to find the open man beyond the arc. Per Sportradar, the Suns are one of just two teams surrendering a 3-point percentage of at least 40 percent this season (the other is the Miami Heat).

IN AND OUT: Banchero (right low-grade high ankle sprain), Jonathan Isaac (left knee injury management), Izaiyah Nelson (left ankle injury recovery), Noah Penda (right partially torn hamstring) and Suggs (right knee contusion / bone bruise) are out for Orlando.

For Phoenix, Rasheer Felming (left ankle sprain), Jordan Goodwin (right calf soreness) and Mark Williams (left shoulder surgery) are out.

KEY STATS: During its 2025-26 campaign, Orlando went 8-6 on the road against Western Conference opponents and finished with a winning road record against them for just the fourth time in franchise history.

Tristan da Silva enters tonight's game averaging 3.3 made 3-pointers per outing, trailing only Grayson Allen for the top mark among bench players (per Sportradar).

QUOTE TO NOTE: “I think it all starts on the defensive side of the ball for us. That will allow us to get easy ones on offense. Stay together. The road is a great place to come together and spend time with each other on the court and off the court. So, I’m looking forward to that.” – Bane.
