Olympiacos vs. Iraklis: Match Preview

TheTools · 20.11.2026 18:57 · source · permalink

High-flying Olympiacos look to extend their five-match winning streak at the Karaiskakis Stadium as Jota Silva faces a struggling Iraklis side plagued by defensive vulnerabilities and poor away form.

A Super League Greece Round 11 fixture is set to be the event that will see Olympiacos host the club from Thessaloniki, Iraklis FC, in a game that will be staged at the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium, the home ground of the former. The match is set to be held on 22 November 2026. 

Currently placed second on the league table, the Red and Whites have accumulated 23 points after 10 games, and are 4 points behind table-toppers Panathinaikos. On the other hand, Iraklis FC sit in 9th position on the table, having managed to collect 10 points from an equal number of fixtures.

The form of Olympiacos while playing at the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium has been impeccable this season. In the last 6 games that Imanol Alguacil's men played on home turf, the team managed to register victories in 4 of them. It is also to be noted that the Thrylos are currently on a streak of five straight victories across all competitions. 

A major role in the Thrylos' recent successes has been played by their rock-solid defence, one which has conceded just 10 goals in 19 games across all competitions. Combined with their impeccable firepower that have netted no less than 30 goals in this duration, Imanol Alguacil will be confident about his team putting up a good performance against their opponents from Thessaloniki.

A player who has grown in stature at the club and been instrumental in the club's recent performances is striker Jota Silva, who is the Piraeus side's top scorer in the league with 5 goals. 3 of these came in a hat-trick scored against Kalamata. 

On the other hand, Iraklis FC, who were promoted at the beginning of the season, have not had a particularly bright start to their campaign. The team have found themselves on the losing end in 2 of their last 6 away games, and have only managed to get one win from this run of fixtures. Across all competitions, manager Walter Mazzarri’s men have managed to win one and draw one of their last 6 games, and have ended up being the losing side on 4 occasions.

The defensive vulnerability of Iraklis has been made very evident by the number of goals they have conceded. In the 11 games that they have played across all competitions in the 2026-27 season, the team have conceded 17 goals, and has managed to score just 12.

The top scorer in the league for Iraklis has been Portuguese striker Pedro Marques, who has netted 3 goals in 9 games.

For Olympiacos, manager Imanol Alguacil will be without the services of key forward Ayoub El Kaabi, who is recovering from a tibia-fibula fracture. Alongside El Kaabi, two players, Yusuf Yazici and Santiago Hezze, will also be unavailable. The former is out with a cruciate ligament tear, and the latter is recovering from a knee injury.

For Iraklis FC, midfielder Panagiotis Panagiotidis will not be able to take the field at the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium. He is out with a muscle injury. It is also to be noted that Walter Mazzarri's team sheet will also not see the inclusion of defender Giorgios Giannoutsos, who is also recovering from an injury.

Given the fact that the form of Olympiacos is much more impressive in comparison to that of Iraklis, and also the fact that the Red and Whites have historically dominated this fixture, it can be predicted that the match will result in a victory for the Piraeus club. A scoreline of 3-0 in favour of Olympiacos can be predicted.
