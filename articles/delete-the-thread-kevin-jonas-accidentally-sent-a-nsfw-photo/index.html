"Delete The Thread": Kevin Jonas Accidentally Sent A NSFW Photo To A Group Chat With His Brothers

TheTools · 29.10.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

    “This is my trauma,” Kevin Jonas said on the “Hey Jonas!” podcast.

    Kevin Jonas had a total SOS moment after accidentally sending a NSFW image of himself to a football group chat that included a couple of his friends — and much to Kevin’s horror, his brothers Nick and Joe Jonas as well.

    During Tuesday’s episode of the Hey Jonas! podcast, the 38-year-old singer confessed that he unintentionally sent a photo of his penis to their New York Giants fandom text thread.

    Kevin, the eldest of the brother trio, revealed he had mistakenly photographed his crotch in a mirror reflection while capturing a football announcement on his bathroom TV.

    “I did something that ruined my entire day,” Kevin told Joe and Nick during the episode. “I don’t even know how to start this… This is my trauma.”

    Joe quickly jumped in to explain the embarrassing ordeal. He said things took a turn after the news broke that football player J.J. McCarthy was going to start as quarterback.

    “Kevin sends through a picture to the group chat. It’s a TV, and it’s got the announcement on it, and nobody thinks much of it,” Nick recounted.

    Joe then said Kevin panic-texted them asking to delete the group thread after realizing that his penis could be seen in the photo he sent.

    According to the Jonas brothers, the group chat ― which also includes the original live drummer of their band, Alexander Noyes, and their former photographer, Rob Hoffman, among others ― “has been going for 15 years.”

     

    Joe, who was hanging out with Nick at the time, said they were initially confused by the messages. “I’m on the couch playing video games, and I look at Nick and I’m like… ‘Did [Kevin] post something he wasn’t supposed to?’” Joe said.

    Kevin then wrote several messages: “Guys delete the photo I’m sorry.” “Cuck my life.” “Fuck.”

    Joe teased that the alarmed texts were “the greatest series of messages,” while Nick called them “legendary.” Hoffman responded in the group chat with multiple laughing emojis, prompting Kevin to follow up with the texts: “I’m such a moron,” and “Well.”

    “The photo has mirrors, and I didn’t clock it,” Kevin explained on the podcast. “And it was in our bathroom, and I took the photo as I’m getting into the shower.”

    Joe added, “You have a massive TV in your bathroom, but there’s mirrors surrounding the TV and you can only see — it’s like the greatest thing that you didn’t know happened. There’s a big TV and right below is a mirror and it’s only your dick.”

    “Nobody wants to have a dick pic sent to them that they didn’t want,” Joe joked.

    Kevin said that when he realized his error, he “threw [his] phone into the kitchen.” When his wife, Danielle, found out what happened, she simply said, “Oh no.”
