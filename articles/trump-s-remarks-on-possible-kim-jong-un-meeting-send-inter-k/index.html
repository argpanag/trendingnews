Trump's Remarks on Possible Kim Jong Un Meeting Send Inter-Korean Cooperation Stocks Soaring; Hyungji I&C Up 25% — BigGo Finance

TheTools · 17.11.2026 00:55 · source · permalink

Inter-Korean economic cooperation stocks surged across the South Korean market after U.S. President Donald Trump, ahead of his Asia tour, mentioned the possibility of a meeting with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. As of 9:13 a.m. on the 17th, Indif was trading at 2,660 won (approximately $2), up 14.41% from the previous session. At the same time, Hyungji I&C rose 25.1% and Good People gained 18.31%, while Ananti (6.21%), Codes Combine (6.73%) and J.Estina (2.08%) also posted strong gains.

The rally was triggered by Trump's remarks to reporters as he left the White House. "We're going to be meeting with President Xi Jinping of China," he said, adding, "We're going to be meeting with President Vladimir Putin." Asked whether the North Korean nuclear issue could be included on the trilateral agenda, he replied, "Maybe we'll be with Kim Jong Un too. He could come." Asked where a meeting with Kim might take place, he said, "In China."

Trump is scheduled to attend the ASEAN summit in Manila, the Philippines, before traveling to Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) leaders' meeting. A U.S.-China summit and a U.S.-China-Russia trilateral summit are expected on the sidelines, with speculation that a U.S.-North Korea meeting could be added.

Whether the meeting will actually take place remains uncertain, however. China's Foreign Ministry has not specified what agenda items will be discussed at the trilateral talks. The White House has prioritized nonproliferation and a Middle East ceasefire while taking a passive stance on discussing the war in Ukraine. Russia, by contrast, insists that negotiations to end the Ukraine war should be a core agenda item.

The market is once again focusing on the past track records of inter-Korean cooperation-related companies. Indif has a history of entering the Kaesong Industrial Complex in 2008. Hyungji Group has accumulated production management experience and data from producing school uniforms in Pyongyang, Nampo and the Kaesong Industrial Complex, and Hyungji I&C recently set up a dedicated inter-Korean cooperation organization and is reviewing a production operating system in preparation for a possible restart of the Kaesong complex. Good People previously operated a local factory in the Kaesong Industrial Complex's pilot zone, while Ananti once held a golf course and resort in the Mount Kumgang tourism zone. Codes Combine and J.Estina are also classified as companies that operated in the Kaesong complex.

Note: Gains versus previous session as of 9:13 a.m. on the 17th.

Inter-Korean cooperation stocks are a representative theme group that reacts sensitively to geopolitical events. They have repeatedly seen sharp short-term swings whenever news emerged about inter-Korean summits or U.S.-North Korea negotiations. Analysts say this rally, too, is driven more by expectations and inflows of trading capital than by any actual resumption of business or earnings improvement. If the meeting falls through or talks make no progress, a rapid pullback cannot be ruled out.

Meanwhile, the trilateral summit is drawing international attention as the first case of the leaders of the three countries gathering in one place. With the deadline for the U.S.-China trade truce approaching in January next year, there is talk that an extension could be discussed at the meeting. However, with China's Foreign Ministry not disclosing specific agenda items, observers say it is too early to predict the outcome.
