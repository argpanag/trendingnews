Influencer 'with 55in bum' slammed for photoshoot in front of earthquake ruins

TheTools · 14.10.2026 10:36 · source · permalink

Gracie Bon has been criticised for her earthquake photoshoot (file)(Image: Ivan Apfel/Getty Images)

An influencer famous for her '55-inch bum' has been heavily criticised over her choice of location for a recent photoshoot. Gracie Bon, who boasts almost 17 million followers across her social media platforms, posed amongst the rubble and ruins caused by an earthquake in her own country, Panama.

A devastating 'quake with a magnitude of 7.7 struck the Central American nation on Friday (October 9), with more than 300 aftershocks recorded since - one of which on Monday had a magnitude of 6.1. Whilst no deaths have been reported, a national state of emergency was declared due to the significant damage to Panama's infrastructure.

Since then, 29-year old plus-size Gracie from Panama City has used the disaster to show off her incredible curves.

"The reality of my country," she began in a sombre Instagram post. "After the earthquake, we’re reminded that material things can be replaced, but life is priceless."

Gracie continued: "Hug your loved ones a little tighter, be grateful for what you have, live in the moment, and show up for those who need it most. Stay strong, Panamá. We got this, we'll get through it together."

She shared a series of snaps, the first of which saw her standing in the ruins in blue jeans and a white shirt that showed off her stomach.

Further images saw Gracie crouch to examine the remains of a property, while she also assessed the damage with her hands on her impressive hips.

Writing in response, one Instagram asked in Spanish: "Seriously? Was it really necessary to pose?" A second person pondered: "Imagine how empty you can be if you use this moment for interaction."

Gracie Bon attends Univision's 38th Premio Lo Nuestro at Kaseya Center on February 19, 2026 in Miami, Florida(Image: Ivan Apfel/Getty Images)

A third fumed: "How do I make this about me! What a need to go out posing in the middle of a disaster."

While a fourth penned sarcastically: "Let me find a good spot for the next photo... unbelievable. Just for looking for likes."

And a fifth Instagram user said: "I don't believe she actually asked someone to take pictures of her in front of someone's destroyed house."

Defending Gracie, however, one fan commented: "Thanks. We need more influencers who take things to the right place."

Whilst another concurred: "There's a big difference between documenting a tragedy to raise awareness and using the site as a backdrop for a photograph. It's not about judging your intentions, but about understanding that behind those ruins lie pain, loss, and the stories of people who deserve respect and empathy."

Gracie later returned to Instagram to hit out: "There are people who get more outraged by a photo than by seeing families lose everything. Sometimes the problem is not what we do, but the eyes with which you decide to see it. Let's help Panama."
