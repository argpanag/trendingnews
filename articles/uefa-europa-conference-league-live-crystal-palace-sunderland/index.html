  12. First home European game 'amazing for the club and fans' - Smithpublished at 19:44 BST
    19:44 BST

    Bournemouth v Sturm (20:00 BST)

    Bournemouth

    Bournemouth captain Adam Smith says he "would have never believed" he would be leading the side out for a European home game when he first joined the club 16 years ago.

    Smith, now 35, initially joined on loan from Tottenham for the 2010-11 season, just after the Cherries had been promoted from League Two.

    He then returned on a permanent basis in January 2014 with the club in the Championship and has been part of their journey ever since.

    "When I first joined I ever expected to be playing in Europe, or finishing in the top six like we did last season, and performing at this level. I would never have believed it," Smith told a news conference before Bournemouth host Sturm Graz on Thursday.

  13. 'I've been waiting for a European game for a long time'published at 19:43 BST
    19:43 BST

    Brighton v Kauno Zalgiris (20:00 BST)

    Brighton forward Georginio Rutter says his team-mates are "feeling pretty confident" after racking up a five-game unbeaten streak, but also "staying very humble" in the build-up to their first Conference League fixture of the season.

    "I play football to win trophies and we have the opportunity to do that this season, so why wouldn't we try? We're going to give everything we have, but it is important that we take it game by game," he said.

    "We need to keep being consistent like we are now. If we stay very humble but pretty confident at the same time, we can do something very good.

    "I've been waiting for a European game for a long time since I arrived here so I hope it's going to be a very good atmosphere.

    "We need the fans behind us. We have given them a lot this year but they have given us a lot back too, so hopefully the big spirit will match on both sides.

    "It will be a bit like a cup game. We will have to be careful about their strengths while also knowing our own strengths."
