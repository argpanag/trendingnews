  13. Maddison wrongly risked? Gallagher also booed onto the pitchpublished at 18:40 BST
    18:40 BST

    Chelsea 0-0 Tottenham

    Nizaar Kinsella
    BBC Sport senior football reporter at Stamford Bridge

    In these conditions, against that midfield and having only recently returned to the starting line-up, there were questions about whether Roberto De Zerbi was right to start James Maddison against Chelsea.

    The Italian admitted in his pre-match news conference that the midfielder had missed training sessions following the win over Coventry.

    In a game that looked likely to be more about competing than controlling possession, perhaps this was one he could have sat out.

    His replacement, Conor Gallagher, was once one of the most popular players among the home supporters, but some boo him as he comes on for Tottenham at Stamford Bridge.
