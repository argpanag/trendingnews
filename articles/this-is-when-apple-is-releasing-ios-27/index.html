This is when Apple is releasing iOS 27

Login

Vlad 09 September 2026

Apple iOS

Today Apple unveiled the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. These devices will go on pre-order on September 12 and will be released on September 18. They will both be running iOS 27, which Apple announced back in June and has been working on ever since.

Today Apple has also revealed when the stable version of iOS 27 will become available for eligible iPhones, following the usual months-long beta testing period. That date is Monday, September 14. That's when iOS 27 will be released, and it's safe to assume that iPadOS 27, watchOS 27, tvOS 27, and macOS 27 will also be released on the same day.


iOS 27

iOS 27 comes with the revamped Siri AI, but you do have to keep in mind that this will not be available in the EU and China. There are some other new features in iOS 27, and we went over them in our dedicated article.
