SALT LAKE CITY, Utah, Oct. 17, 2026 (Gephardt Daily) — After his Sept. 27 death in a Cache County BASE jumping accident, the family of Salt Lake City firefighter of Gabe Reilly decided to keep his memorial service family oriented and private.

But his “second family,” the Salt Lake City Fire Department, has posted a respectful tribute.

“Gabe Reilly was only with the Salt Lake City Fire Department for four years, but he accomplished more in that time than many do in an entire career,” the SLCFD statement says.

“Gabe grew up in Park City watching his dad Captain Dan Reilly serve this department, and he chose the same path himself. In 2025 he completed paramedic school, an accomplishment he was especially proud of. It allowed him to use his sharp technical mind with his strong sense of compassion to help people in their most frightening moments.

“Athletic and competitive since his days on the Park City Ski Team, Gabe brought that drive to the fire service. He would crush tasks and fitness tests in record time, then turn back around to encourage others and help them finish strong. That was Gabe. He wanted those around him to succeed just as much as he wanted to succeed himself.”

Reilly, 27, “lived every single day to the fullest,” the tribute says.

“He studied and trained hard with his crew, took friends on new adventures, and chased the mountains and open sky. He had no time for negativity, and you knew it the moment he walked into the station with his bright smile and contagious energy.

“Gabe was strong, kind, and dependable. If you needed him, he was there. His commitment to his fellow firefighters and the community, along with the friendship he shared, will not be forgotten.

“On behalf of the Salt Lake City Fire Department, we extend our deepest condolences to the entire Reilly family, his girlfriend Morgan, and all who loved him. Our thoughts and prayers are with you during this difficult time.

“Blue skies, Gabe. Rest easy brother.”
