The Manchester City coach denied that the financial fair play case had any influence on the 4-2 defeat to Paris Saint-Germain.

Maresca separates the defeat from everything happening off the pitch

Enzo Maresca insisted that the 4-2 defeat to Paris Saint-Germain had nothing to do with the ruling against Manchester City over charges related to financial fair play. The coach stressed that off-field issues do not affect his players and pointed out that the team had been in excellent form, coming off six straight Premier League wins and another victory in the Champions League, before this European setback.
