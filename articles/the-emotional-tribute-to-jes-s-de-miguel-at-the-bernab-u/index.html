The emotional tribute to Jesús de Miguel at the Bernabéu

TheTools · 21.11.2026 20:09 · source · permalink

The players of both teams wore jerseys with the number 9, formed a guard of honor to receive the family, and observed a minute of silence. 

The Santiago Bernabéu paid an emotional tribute to Jesús de Miguel before the match between Real Madrid and Celta de Vigo. The players of both teams took to the field wearing jerseys with the number 9 and the name of the CD Tenerife forward, who passed away on November 3 at the age of 30. They then formed a guard of honor to welcome De Miguel's family, who were accompanied by Real Madrid and Celta presidents, Florentino Pérez and Marián Mouriño.

Jersey Presentation
Fede Valverde, captain of Real Madrid, Iago Aspas, captain of Celta, Sergio Llull, captain of the basketball team, and Toletti, captain of the Real Madrid women's team, presented the family with a jersey from each team.

Minute of Silence
Finally, the Bernabéu observed an impressive minute of silence, and a large banner with the image of Jesús de Miguel was displayed in the South Stand. At Gate 9 of our stadium, the image of the Madrid-born player with a black ribbon was also projected.
