HOENIX - The Suns (2-5) snapped a four-game losing streak with a 117-104 win over Orlando on Nov. 5. Phoenix won the first quarter by seven as Devin Booker put up 12 points and two three-pointers. Booker had another double digit frame in the second quarter and entered halftime with 23 points. Phoenix shot 51.1% from the field and 52.6% from beyond the arc in the opening half as the Suns forced 13 turnovers leading to 17 points. The Suns won the third period by 11 points, with Dillon Brooks (12 points) and Miles Bridges (10 points) each totaling double digit scoring outputs. Phoenix took its largest lead of the game at 30 points in the fourth quarter where the Suns emptied their bench. Orlando did not lead outside of the first quarter, a quick six-point advantage. Booker led all scorers with 30 points, his second consecutive 30+ point outing. Bridges posted his second double-double of the season with 18 points and 13 rebounds to continue a strong four-game stretch. Collin Gillespie recorded 3 steals to get to 19 in the first seven games of the season, the most by a Suns player through the opening seven games of a campaign since Shawn Marion in 2007-08.
