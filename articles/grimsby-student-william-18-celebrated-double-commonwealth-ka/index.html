Grimsby student William, 18, celebrated double Commonwealth karate gold success

TheTools · 10.11.2026 10:07 · source · permalink

A Grimsby Institute student is celebrating winning a brace of medals after representing England at the Commonwealth Karate Championships. William Jervis, 18, has come from the competition in Glasgow with two gold medals after competing in the high profile championships, adding to an already impressive trophy tally.

William, a Level 1 Sport student, took top honours in the men’s Para K22 category at the Commonwealth Karate Championships, before going on to claim another gold medal at the Club Championships. The 12th Commonwealth Karate Championships took place in Glasgow from over the weekend, bringing together karate athletes from Commonwealth nations for competition across kata, kumite, and Para-kata categories.

The championships were held at Glasgow International Arena and included both Elite Championships for national-team athletes and Club Championships for WKF-affiliated Commonwealth karateka. The Commonwealth Karate Championships are separate from the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, which took place earlier in the year and featured a 10-sport programme.

The wins comes just over a year after William was selected to represent England at the 2025 Senior European Karate Championships in Armenia, where he competed in the male Para Kata category – in which competitors display a choreographed pattern of movements and techniques practised, representing a fight.

William’s success is the latest milestone in a journey that has seen him compete at national and international level, demonstrating the amazing dedication and determination he puts into his training, alongside his studies at Grimsby Institute, part of the TEC Partnership.

His mum, Clare Jarvis, said: “We couldn’t be prouder of William. Watching him step onto the tatami at the Commonwealth Karate Championships and come away with two gold medals across both the main event and the club championships is a moment we will treasure forever.

"He has worked so hard for this, showing incredible dedication, resilience, and independence throughout the entire competition. Seeing him thrive alongside his teammates and represent his country and club with such maturity is everything a parent could wish for.”

Jonathan Davy, Sport Programme Leader at Grimsby Institute, added: “Everyone at Grimsby Institute is incredibly proud of William and what he has achieved in Glasgow. To represent your country at this level is an incredible achievement in itself, but to come home with two gold medals is something really special.

“William’s success is a fantastic example of what young people from Grimsby and the surrounding area can achieve when they are given the opportunity to pursue their ambitions. We’re delighted to be able to celebrate this achievement with him and his family.”

William’s success marks another significant step in his journey as an international athlete.
