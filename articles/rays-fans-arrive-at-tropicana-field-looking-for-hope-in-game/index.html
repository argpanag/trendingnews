Rays fans arrive at Tropicana Field looking for hope in Game 4

TheTools · 27.10.2026 22:52 · source · permalink

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. (WWSB) - Fans gathered outside Tropicana Field on Tuesday ahead of Game 4 of the World Series, with the Tampa Bay Rays trailing the Milwaukee Brewers 3-0 in the series.

Gates opened at 5 p.m. First pitch is just after 8 p.m. Chief meteorologist Bob Harrigan spoke with fans as they arrived and said what they are looking for is hope.

Nate Ditchfield of Manatee County attended with his son, Timmy. Ditchfield said they came to represent Bradenton and Sarasota and to support the team.

“We are here to represent Bradenton, Sarasota,” Ditchfield said. “The guys need our help. They need our voices tonight. They need some inspiration. We’re here to do that. You can’t win all four games tonight, but you can win one, and that’s what we’re doing. We’re bringing one home tonight.”

Ditchfield said he was “pumped” for the game.

A group of fans from England also attended Game 4. Josh, one of the group, said the game is the second baseball game he has attended. He said he saw the Cubs play the Cardinals in London.

“It was a really fun experience, but World Series for a second game. Unbelievable,” Josh said. He said he was “really excited.”

Two students also were at the stadium. Jordan said he did a news broadcast Tuesday morning with his class at a school in Palmetto. He said he was excited for the game. Wesley said he was happy to be at the game.

The Rays need to win out to have a chance at their first World Series championship in franchise history. Griffin Jacks is the Rays’ starting pitcher.

Yandy Diaz had two of the Rays’ three hits in Game 3 on Monday night. He said Tuesday the team knows its backs are against the wall and is ready to embrace the challenge.

“It’s hard not to be upset, you know, being down here in the series 3-0,” Diaz said. “So, it’s a matter of, you know, being positive but also looking at it from a 50-50 standpoint, where, you know, I’m happy to be here but also, you know, just disappointed that we are down three games and now we have to win out to, you know, accomplish what we need to accomplish.”
