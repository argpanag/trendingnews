India will look to keep its dream of winning a record-extending 10th SAFF Championship title alive when it faces Bhutan in the first semifinal in Dhaka on Saturday.

Led by Under-23 head coach Naushad Moosa, the Blue Tigers are fielding a second-string team at the tournament as the senior side is in New Zealand for two friendlies.

India beat Pakistan 3-2 and Maldives 1-0 to top Group B, while Bhutan finished second in Group A with one point.

When will the India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match kick off?

The India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match will kick off at 2:30 p.m. IST on Saturday, November 14.

Where to watch the India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match?

The India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match will be played at the National Stadium in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Where to watch the India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match?

The India vs Bhutan SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal match will be livestreamed on the Veto app.
