The Brazilian is back in a LaLiga squad after his suspension, while Asencio, Rodrygo and Mendy remain sidelined.

Vinícius Returns to a LaLiga Squad

Brazilian forward Vinícius is the new addition to Real Madrid's squad for the thirteenth matchday of LaLiga against Celta this Saturday, in a list that still does not include Raúl Asencio, Rodrygo Goes and Ferland Mendy, who are recovering from their respective injuries.

The Brazilian had not appeared in a LaLiga EA Sports squad since October 10, when he was a substitute against Villarreal. He came on in the 52nd minute, watched Diomande's goal from the pitch and scored his own ten minutes later. However, the referee not only disallowed the Brazilian's goal for a previous foul, but also sent him off for pulling Nathan Saliba's hair.

Suspension and Return with Brazil

The Technical Committee of Referees punished the action with two matches of suspension for the red card and another two for the protests the Brazilian made afterwards. Since then, Vinícius had started in the three matches of the Champions League, in which he neither scored nor assisted, and he also started in two friendlies with Brazil during the FIFA window, in which he scored five goals.

Otherwise, the FIFA virus has not affected Real Madrid. Despite the "extreme fatigue" with which, according to Mourinho, his players went into the break, the Portuguese coach already confirmed at a press conference that "they are all available to play."

The available and unavailable

That includes Fede Valverde, who trained with the group this Friday for the first time after playing his last match with Uruguay in the early hours of Tuesday to Wednesday (Spanish time), and also Bernardo Silva and Aurélien Tchouaméni, who returned earlier than expected from their respective training camps due to muscle discomfort that is not serious.

Raúl Asencio, operated on for a stress fracture in his right tibia, and Rodrygo, recovering from a torn anterior cruciate ligament in his knee, are still not training with the group and are not on the list. The other absentee is Ferland Mendy. The Frenchman, despite having been training with his teammates for weeks, has not yet been included in any of Mourinho's squads.

Real Madrid's squad list for the match against Celta is made up of:

. Goalkeepers: Courtois, Lunin and Sergio Mestre.

. Defenders: Trent, Dumfries, Militao, Huijsen, Rüdiger, Konaté, Carreras and Cucurella.

. Midfielders: Valverde, Arda Güler, Tchouaméni, Camavinga, Bellingham, Bernardo Silva and Thiago Pitarch.

. Forwards: Vinícius, Endrick, Mbappé, Brahim Díaz, Carlos Espí and Yan Diomande. EFE
