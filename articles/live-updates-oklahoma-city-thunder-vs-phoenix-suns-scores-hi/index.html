News 9 meteorologist Lacey Swope teamed up with the Oklahoma City Thunder to give fans a look at Monday night’s weather ahead of the matchup against the Phoenix Suns.

Swope said temperatures in downtown Oklahoma City will be in the mid-70s during the day, with partly cloudy skies and temperatures dropping into the 60s as fans leave Paycom Center after the game.

Fans heading home could also encounter a few sprinkles, although Swope said the chance is very slight.

The Thunder shared Swope’s forecast on the team’s official Facebook page ahead of Monday night’s game.

Oklahoma City Thunder general manager Sam Presti confirmed Monday that Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace will both need surgery following injuries sustained during Sunday’s game against the Los Angeles Clippers.

Presti said Williams suffered a fractured fibula and dislocated left ankle, while Wallace sustained a torn labrum in his left shoulder. Both injuries will require surgery, but recovery timelines have not been determined.

The Thunder general manager said Williams was in good spirits following the injury and remains optimistic about his recovery.

“We’re extremely optimistic about the recovery for both the players,” Presti said.

The injuries present a significant challenge for Oklahoma City as the Thunder prepare to face the Phoenix Suns on Monday night.

The organization’s immediate focus is supporting both players and their families as they prepare for surgery, Presti said.

Monday night’s matchup at Paycom Center brings a familiar opponent back to Oklahoma City as the Thunder and Phoenix Suns meet for the first time since their 2026 playoff series.

The Thunder swept Phoenix in the first round of the NBA playoffs, ending the Suns’ postseason run in four games.

During that series, Oklahoma City moved within one win of completing the sweep after a Game 3 road victory in Phoenix.

The two teams also met five times during the 2025-26 regular season, with Phoenix winning two of those matchups.

Now, the Suns return to Paycom Center with an opportunity to turn the page on last season’s playoff disappointment, while the Thunder look to continue their success against Phoenix.

As the Oklahoma City Thunder prepare to face the Phoenix Suns following Jalen Williams’ injury, the team’s performance without him last season offers some perspective.

According to CBS Sports, Oklahoma City went 39-10 in games without Williams last season, a winning percentage equivalent to a 65-win pace over 82 games.

The Thunder also advanced to Game 7 of the Western Conference Finals despite playing much of the postseason without Williams.
