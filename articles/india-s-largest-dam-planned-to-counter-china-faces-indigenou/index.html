India’s largest dam planned to counter China faces Indigenous revolt

NewsFeed

The Indigenous people of India’s remote eastern Himalayan state of Arunachal Pradesh are protesting a massive dam planned to counter China’s colossal upstream hydropower project. They tell Al Jazeera the mega dam threatens their ancestral lands, culture and livelihoods.
