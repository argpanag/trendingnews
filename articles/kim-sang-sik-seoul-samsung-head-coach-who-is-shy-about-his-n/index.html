Kim Sang-sik, Seoul Samsung head coach, who is shy about his nickname "Common Sense Magic," says, "It feels like a burden, but I am happy and grateful" [Jamsil on-site] | STARNEWS

Kim Sang-sik, the head coach leading the surge of Seoul Samsung in professional basketball, smiled shyly at his nickname "Common Sense Magic."

Samsung, led by head coach Kim Sang-sik, will face Seoul SK in an away matchup in the 2026-2027 KCC Pro Basketball regular season at Seoul Jamsil Student Gymnasium at 4:30 p.m. on the 1st.

Samsung's momentum this season is formidable. The team has climbed to the top of the league standings with a nine-game winning streak since the start of the season, a first in KBL history. In particular, Samsung has shown a completely transformed appearance since head coach Kim Sang-sik took over this summer. This makes the record all the more surprising, as Samsung had finished last for five consecutive seasons up through last year.

If they win against SK as well, they will set another major record with a 10-game winning streak from the start of the season.

Head coach Kim Sang-sik, met before the game, cited "confidence" as the biggest change compared to the early days of his tenure.

Head coach Kim said, "At first, I would strongly reprimand them if I saw even a little bit of something unusual," adding, "But as we won one game, two games, and three games, confidence grew among the players. The atmosphere improved as well."

However, he emphasized, "Even so, if I see any sign of disorganization in that process, I continue to point it out."

Despite their unstoppable nine-game winning streak, they are not letting their guard down. Head coach Kim explained, "We are on a nine-game winning streak right now, but from a head coach's perspective, one must always think of it as a crisis. One must be worried and tense."

He continued, "The players have gained confidence, and I too have developed some degree of confidence compared to before. However, it is true that worry comes first. Because we do not know when another difficult situation will arise, I am always on edge."

Praise has poured in for head coach Kim Sang-sik amid Samsung's remarkable upward trend. Various nicknames have emerged among fans, including "Common Sense Magic."

Regarding this, head coach Kim said, "Personally, I really like it, but I also wonder if you are thinking of me as too great," looking shy.

He continued, "In fact, we are winning and doing well, but hearing such comments makes me a bit shy. My personality is just like that to begin with," adding candidly, "It is not a lie, but I also think, 'We could get blown away in one go at any moment.' Various emotions cross my mind."

He then said, "Anyway, it is really nice. I am grateful for watching us so closely and giving such positive evaluations," adding, "The players are doing very well too, but from my perspective, it is also a bit of a burden. Still, I am truly grateful," conveying his thankful feelings.
