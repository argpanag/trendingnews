Premier League today: How to watch Arsenal v Leeds United, TV channels & live stream

How will Arsenal and Leeds United fare today? Here is what to know, including kick-off time, TV channels and live stream options

Three points is all that separates Arsenal and Leeds United as the Premier League returns after a three-week international break, with the latter one of the surprise packages of the season so far.

Unlike Arsenal, Leeds have yet to lose a league game this season. Daniel Farke’s team have earned a reputation for being difficult to beat and have the quality to cause an upset in north London this weekend.

Mikel Arteta’s champions have had a long time to simmer on their shocking 3-0 defeat to Brighton in their final outing before the international break. The Gunners’ fans expect a response from their team to keep their title defense on track.

Here is your complete guide on how to watch today’s Premier League fixture, including kick-off times, broadcast networks and online streaming options.

Arsenal v Leeds United

Time: 12.30pm BST/7.30am ET

Stadium: Emirates Stadium (London)

How to watch

United States: Stream on Fubo

Canada: Stream on Fubo

United Kingdom: Watch on TNT Sports 1

What to watch for

Until their defeat at the Amex Stadium, Arsenal’s title defense had started perfectly. Four wins from four had Arteta’s side setting the pace once again at the top of the Premier League table. Brighton, however, may have exposed some weaknesses in the Gunners in a way that others could attempt to replicate.

Like Brighton, Leeds are dangerous in quick transition. They currently rank top for interceptions per 90 minutes in the Premier League, highlighting their determination to win the ball high up the pitch as often as possible.

Injuries suffered by Martin Ødegaard, Kai Havertz and Christos Tzolis over the international break could also be a problem. This is where Arsenal’s squad depth will be tested – although it should be noted Leeds haven’t beaten the north London outfit in the Premier League since 2003.

Player to watch: Bukayo Saka, Arsenal – The right winger returns to club duty on the back of bagging a brace for England in their 7-0 demolition of Croatia. With question marks over the fitness of Havertz and Odegaard, Saka could be even more crucial for Arsenal.
