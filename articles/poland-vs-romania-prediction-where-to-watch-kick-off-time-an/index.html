Poland vs. Romania - Head-to-head record

Recent encounters between Poland and Romania are few, adding unpredictability to this UEFA Nations League fixture. Their last competitive meetings were during the 2018 World Cup qualification campaign.

Poland dominated these two qualifying matches, securing victories in both. They achieved a 3-0 win on November 11, 2016, then followed it with a 3-1 triumph on June 10, 2017. The head-to-head record against Romania clearly favours the Poles, showcasing their superior performance.

This historical advantage gives Poland a psychological edge for the upcoming game. Conversely, Romania will aim to reverse past outcomes and prove their competitive ability against their rivals. Their strong motivation could be a decisive factor in this encounter.

Poland - In need of a result

Let’s take a closer look at Poland’s current situation heading into this match. We will analyse the team’s recent form and their latest performances in the UEFA Nations League for this crucial encounter.

Recent form

Poland has had a shaky start to their UEFA Nations League campaign. They kicked off with a goalless draw at home against Bosnia & Herzegovina, showing defensive organisation. However, this was followed by a disappointing 3-1 defeat away to Sweden, leaving them with just one point from two games.

This inconsistency is not new, as their form leading up to the tournament was also mixed, including a loss to Ukraine in a friendly. The clean sheet at home in their Nations League opener was a positive start on their own turf. In contrast, the three goals conceded against Sweden show a vulnerability that Romania will look to exploit.

A major boost for Poland is the availability of an almost full-strength squad. While star striker Robert Lewandowski seems to be a substitute, they have other threats like Sebastian Szymański, who got their only goal so far. Playing at home provides the perfect opportunity to find their attacking rhythm and secure a much-needed result.

Romania - Struggling for consistency

Let’s look at Romania’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their expectations for the match, and the standout players for this game.

Recent form

Romania faces a tough challenge in the UEFA Nations League, having lost both of their opening matches. They suffered a 2-1 away defeat to Sweden and a 4-2 home loss against Bosnia & Herzegovina. Conceding six goals in just two games highlights significant defensive frailties.

This inconsistent form extends beyond the current tournament. While they achieved a 2-1 friendly win against Wales, their overall results have been mixed across various competitions. Positively, Romania reports no injuries, allowing the coach to select the strongest available squad for this encounter.

Despite defensive concerns, Romania has scored three goals in their two Nations League fixtures. Attackers like D. Man and Daniel George Bîrligea have already found the net. However, securing a positive result away from home will require a much more solid and organised defensive performance.

Predictions for Poland vs. Romania - Home victory expected

Poland holds a strong historical advantage and will look to press early in front of their home fans. Considering Romania"s defensive frailties, there is a good chance for the hosts to be leading at half-time.

The visitors have conceded six goals in their opening two Nations League games, highlighting a struggling backline. This suggests Poland should create plenty of chances, making the “+1,5 Poland Total Goals" market an interesting option for the match.

Given Poland’s solid home record in the competition and Romania"s poor defensive form, our main prediction is a home victory. The hosts have the quality and motivation to secure all three points in this encounter.
