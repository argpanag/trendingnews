Brazilian GP: Lando Norris wins in the rain, podium for Isack Hadjar

TheTools · 08.11.2026 18:41 · source · permalink

The Briton, starting from pole position, held on to victory despite an attack from Antonelli at the start and the arrival of rain at the end of the race. Max Verstappen, who staged a brilliant comeback, finished second ahead of Isack Hadjar.

As usual, Brazil delivered a chaotic race! The drivers started on a wet track and saw the checkered flag in the rain. In between, the track had time to dry, and the majority of the race was run on slick tires. Despite all these changing conditions, Lando Norris ultimately took the victory, having secured his third consecutive pole position in São Paulo the previous day.

However, the 2025 world champion had a mediocre start, at the end of which Kimi Antonelli, the 2026 champion, decisively took the lead. The Italian led for a long time, until lap 20, when he made a mistake at the first corner, losing control in difficult conditions. This was an especially delicate moment for Mercedes because, three laps earlier, George Russell had lost control of his car exiting the pit lane. Spinning, the Briton was able to rejoin the race undamaged, but with a significant amount of time lost. This situation benefited the Red Bulls of Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar . Starting from the back of the grid, the Dutchman made an exceptional start to the race, rapidly climbing through the field. Lewis Hamilton , who also started at the back of the grid, also got an excellent start and was in 9th place before dropping back. After spinning out at the end of the race, just as rain began to fall again on the circuit, the seven-time world champion finished 9th, two positions behind Charles Leclerc (7th). The Monegasque driver did not have an easy race, culminating in a collision with Liam Lawson at the end, which could result in a penalty.

Although no Mercedes finished on the podium for the first time this season, Russell (4th) and Antonelli (5th) secured the German team's ninth Constructors' World Championship title. Antonelli put up a strong fight at the end of the race against Isack Hadjar, but the Frenchman was simply too strong on Sunday. Having kept pace with Verstappen for much of the race, the Parisian brilliantly kept Antonelli in his mirrors to secure his third podium finish of the season.

On the downside, Rafael Câmara had a very difficult first home Grand Prix and finished in 20th place, the last of the classified drivers. The rookie went wide several times, notably spinning out at the first corner. His compatriot Gabriel Bortoleto didn't fare much better (16th) after a poor qualifying session.

Now, see you in Las Vegas on November 22nd to kick off the final triple header of the season.
