Paris Saint Germain - On an incredible winning streak

Let’s take a look at Paris Saint Germain’s situation. We will analyse the team’s incredible form, recent results, and standout performers for the upcoming match.

Recent form

Paris Saint Germain are in unstoppable form, riding an eleven-match winning streak across all competitions. This incredible run includes dominant victories in both Ligue 1 and the UEFA Champions League. Currently sitting top of the league table, their performances on the road have been particularly impressive, where they remain unbeaten this season with four wins and two draws.

The Parisian attack is firing on all cylinders, with multiple players contributing to their success. Spanish forward Ferran Torres has been a standout performer, finding the back of the net four times. Adding to their threat, defender Achraf Hakimi has also been a major force, scoring three goals and providing two assists from the backline.

A significant advantage for the visitors is their clean bill of health, as they have no reported injuries ahead of this clash. This allows them to field their strongest possible lineup. With their formidable momentum, a potent attack, and a full-strength squad, PSG arrive at the Allianz Riviera as clear favourites to continue their dominant run.
