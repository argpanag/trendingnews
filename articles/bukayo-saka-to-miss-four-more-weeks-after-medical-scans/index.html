Bukayo Saka To Miss Four More Weeks After Medical Scans

TheTools · 21.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Bukayo Saka will miss another month of football. Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta confirms the talented winger needs four more weeks to recover from his muscle injury.

Bukayo Saka is set to miss out on yet another busy period on the sidelines. A big medical update from the manager about the forward has come through this Friday.

Manager Mikel Arteta gave a statement to the press ahead of the upcoming weekend matches. He announced that Saka will be out of action for another four weeks following the medical results obtained.

"He has done one other test; he's developing very nicely," Arteta revealed in the press conference. "But I think this is going to be an eight-week injury, give or take. At the moment, he's still four."

The Initial Injury For Arsenal Against Bayern

He had initially injured his quadriceps muscle playing against Bayern Munich on October 21st. He had to hobble off the field after only 10 minutes into the Champions League match.

Arsenal could manage a draw in Germany despite the absence of their key winger. The club's medical team immediately started the treatment for the upper leg injury to avoid any permanent damage to the muscles.

The medical team at the club had decided against performing surgery on the player. Instead, they had opted for a conservative approach that involved gym sessions and physical therapy.

Missing Important Matches For England And The Club

The severe muscle damage left him out of the entire November international fixture period, and rather than joining his national team, he remained in London getting treatment for his injury.

England had to play two very important matches without their star right winger. The national team doctors and club doctors concurred that he should get adequate rest to prevent making his leg muscle condition worse.

His current recovery period means that he will miss a number of very crucial matches. The club is set to face Newcastle United, Manchester City, and Tottenham Hotspur.

When Bukayo Saka Might Finally Return

The reporters asked the manager whether the player could play against Manchester United on December 20. However, the head coach denied giving an exact date for when the medical clearance would be completed.

"I cannot say it that precisely," Arteta said in his interview. "In four weeks' time, anything may happen. It can take three weeks, or it may even take five weeks."

The recovery process is entirely dependent on how the tissue behaves under training loads. The medical team will have to try the player's movements on the grass before he starts practising with the whole team.
