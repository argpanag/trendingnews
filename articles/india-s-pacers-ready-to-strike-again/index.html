India’s pacers ready to strike again

TheTools · 07.11.2026 09:51 · source · permalink

Saturday, November 07 2026, 03:21 PM

Nov 07, 2026 01:00 PM | UPDATED: Nov 07, 2026 01:39 AM | 5 min read

#Wellington

India’s inexperienced but promising pace attack will look to maintain its dominance over New Zealand’s batters and extend the visitors’ advantage when the teams clash in the second ODI here on Saturday.

Gurnoor Brar, Anshul Kamboj and Arshdeep Singh impressed in the series opener, exposing New Zealand’s struggles against pace and bounce. Kamboj stood out with 46 dot balls, consistently hitting testing lengths, while Arshdeep emerged as the leading wicket-taker.

The trio will be keen to exploit conditions at Hnry Stadium, where shorter square boundaries may require adjustments in bowling lengths. Mohammed Siraj could replace Auqib Nabi, the only Indian pacer to go wicketless in the opener, if available. However, the team management has not confirmed the combination.

On the batting front, Rohit Sharma will seek a return to form after falling in the first over, while Virat Kohli will look to build on his fluent innings in the opening match. Shreyas Iyer and Axar Patel, however, need to improve their response to short-pitched deliveries.

New Zealand have suffered another setback with Devon Conway ruled out after sustaining a concussion.PTI
