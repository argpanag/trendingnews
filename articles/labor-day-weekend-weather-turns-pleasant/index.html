Labor Day weekend weather turns pleasant

TheTools · 05.09.2026 03:00 · source · permalink

It was a muggy Friday in Baltimore, but the weekend is looking to be a better end to the summer.

On Saturday, isolated showers are possible south and west of Baltimore. Around the Baltimore metro, it should be a pleasant afternoon.

Sunday looks even better: more sunshine, less humidity and cooler temperatures.

Temperatures will remain in the low to mid-80s before temperatures warm up.
