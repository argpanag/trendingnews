Blast on Russian ship in Benghazi a suspected drone attack, say Libyan authorities

TheTools · 22.11.2026 16:55 · source · permalink

No one has claimed responsibility for the strike on the vessel, which had docked on its way back to Russia

An explosion on a Russian ship docked at the naval base in the Libyan port city of Benghazi was caused by a suspected drone attack, Libya’s eastern-based government has said.

There was no immediate claim of responsibility for the suspected strike, but it comes as Ukrainian forces have stepped up their long-range barrages against Russia far beyond the frontlines.

“Initial investigations indicate that the explosion occurred onboard a Russian ship docked in the port,” said the administration backed by the military commander Khalifa Haftar. “It is suspected that the ship was attacked by drones.”

Authorities in divided Libya’s east said the vessel had stopped at Benghazi port to refuel and ⁠resupply on its way back to Russia, ​under maritime cooperation agreements between ‌the two ‌countries.

Two medical sources said no deaths had been ‌reported from the blast, but there were injuries. The number and nationalities of those injured are not yet known.

There was no immediate reaction from Russian authorities, and no one has claimed responsibility for the suspected attack.

The ‌eastern-based Libyan authority said port and naval base facilities, including warehouses, were safe and that ​normal operations would resume once the fire was brought under control and the site secured.

It condemned the suspected attack as a violation of Libya’s sovereignty and ⁠a threat to maritime safety, saying it would take ​legal and ​diplomatic measures against ​anyone found responsible.

“The Libyan government will not ​allow Libyan ‌ports, territory or territorial ​waters ​to be turned into an arena for settling scores or exporting international conflicts,” it said.

If it is confirmed that Ukraine has been able to blow up or seriously damage a Russian warship in Benghazi, it would represent one of its most successful naval strikes during the five-year war.

But it also represents a risky extension of the fighting far beyond eastern Europe, in an incident that could affect civilians in another continent and the working of the port.

A small unit of Ukrainian special forces operatives has been based at Misrata, western Libya, and is believed to have coordinated drone attacks on Russian tankers and transport ships in the past year.

Initial reports and footage suggest the ship was struck from above, potentially by a drone, launched no more than a few kilometres away from the target in port.

Russia is close to the Benghazi authorities that control eastern Libya. It uses the port to supply Russian mercenary forces in Africa and as a base in the Mediterranean, which has a growing importance after it lost bases in Syria.
