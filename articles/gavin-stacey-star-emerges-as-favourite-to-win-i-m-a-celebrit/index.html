Gavin & Stacey star emerges as favourite to win I'm a Celebrity 2026

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

Henley resident Joanna Page has emerged as the favourite to be crowned the winner of the upcoming series of I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here!

The Welsh actress, 49, is best known for her role as Stacey Shipman in the hit BBC sitcom Gavin & Stacey.

The Gavin & Stacey actress is leading with 4/1 odds, according to ActionNetwork.com.

Paralympic basketball player Ade Adepitan follows closely with 9/2 odds, while comedian Jack Skipper is third with 5/1 odds.

On the other end, journalist Mariella Frostrup is the frontrunner for the first elimination with 5/2 odds, closely followed by model Leomie Anderson at 3/1.

The 2026 series of I'm a Celebrity starts today, (Image: ITV)

A spokesperson from ActionNetwork said: "Ahead of the launch of the new series of I'm a Celebrity, Joanna Page is leading the pack as the favourite to win, with odds of 4/1.

"That is not surprising given the huge affection for Gavin & Stacey and her warm, funny, down-to-earth personality, which is exactly what the viewers have rewarded in the past.

"Ade Adepitan isn't far behind with odds of 9/2 to win, with his positivity and inspirational story likely to win over viewers once the show gets underway."

he actress, who lives in Oxfordshire's Henley, will appear in the upcoming new series of the ITV reality show alongside a star-studded cast including former footballer Wayne Rooney, Bicester-born TV personality Chloe Burrows and soap star Claire Sweeney.

Ms Page, who has four children with husband actor James Thornton, said she jumped at the chance to appear on the show and hopes it will give her some time to just be herself rather than a mother.
