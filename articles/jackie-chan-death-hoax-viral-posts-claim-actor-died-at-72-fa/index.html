Chinese actor Jackie Chan has recently fallen for the victim-of-death hoax. Several social media posts are swirling around the internet claiming Jackie Chan has passed away. However, the posts about Jackie Chan’s death are untrue, and the 72-year-old actor is still alive, hale and hearty.

Jackie Chan is loved around the world, making him more than just a figure in the film industry. Consequently, the widespread rumours of Jackie Chan's death have spread rapidly over the past 24 hours.

Jackie Chan’s death rumours started spreading on social media, especially in the United States on September 28. A black-and-white picture of the actor started making the rounds, showing the text 'Jackie Chan' followed by '1954-2026.'

These posts gathered millions of views around the world within just hours.

Jackie Chan last appeared in public in July 2026 in Singapore. He made a surprise visit to the Pei Chun Public School and surprised the students and staff by singing his song, Hard to Say Goodbye.

Jackie Chan's Last Public Appearance 

According to Singapore's largest daily newspaper, The Straits Times, Jackie Chan's most recent public appearance took place on July 7 this year in Singapore. Chan showed up at Pei Chun Public School for an unexpected visit arranged by the Lim Family Foundation.

The report says the actor engaged with more than 900 students during the visit. A video shared from the event showed that he also sang the song Hard To Say Goodbye.

During the event, the veteran actor said, "Looking at you today made me remember my childhood years and humble beginnings. I had no hope. My only hope was my bowl of plain rice and to not be beaten by my teacher."
