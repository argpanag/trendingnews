Sudurpaschim set 121-run target for Janakpur in NPL

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:53 · source · permalink

Copyright © 2025 Nepal News. All Rights Reserved.

Nepal News

KATHMANDU: The Sudurpaschim Royals scored 120 for nine in 20 overs to set Janakpur Bolts a target of 121 in the Nepal Premier League (NPL) on Tuesday.

After winning the toss and choosing to bat at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, the two-time runners-up struggled against Janakpur’s bowling attack and failed to build substantial partnerships.

Brandon McMullen top-scored with 33 off 23 balls, while Tharindu Ratnayake remained unbeaten on 27 off 28 balls. Arif Sheikh scored 11, while openers Saif Ali Zaid and Binod Bhandari made 10 each.

Janakpur captain Harmeet Singh took four wickets for 17 runs in four overs. NPL debutant Harry Tector conceded just nine runs in his four overs and took one wicket. Rahul Yadav, Aditya Mahata and Abhishek Tiwari also claimed one wicket each.
