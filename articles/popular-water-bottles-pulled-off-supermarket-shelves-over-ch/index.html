Popular water bottles pulled off supermarket shelves over choking risk

TheTools · 03.11.2026 05:53 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • Recall

November 3, 2026 - 5.53am

A range of popular bottled waters sold at supermarkets across Australia have been urgently recalled due to a potential choking hazard.

The NSW Food Authority announced that Coca-Cola Europacific Partners Australia Pty Ltd is recalling Super Pump Spring Water 1.25L, as well as 750mL bottles of Pump Spring Water, Pump Berry Storm, Pump Lime Rush, and Pump Watermelon Hit.

The white sippers on the bottle can become a choking hazard when dislodged. NSW Food Authority

All the products with the best before date up to and including 22 January 2028 are being urgently recalled due to a packaging error that can cause choking if the white sipper were to become dislodged from the cap.

The products have been sold at Woolworths, Coles, Aldi, independent food retailers including IGA, petrol stations, convenience stores, newsagents, vending machines and retail outlets across the country and online.

Consumers are advised not to drink the product and should return it to the place of purchase for a full refund.

Anyone concerned about their health has been advised to get medical advice.

Nine.com.au has reached out to the Coca-Cola Europacific Partners for comment.
