Avalanche’s new dad Cale Makar returns to ice Wednesday night against Dallas Stars

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:02 · source · permalink

The defenseman missed the first night of the back-to-back while his wife gave birth to twin boys

Avalanche star defenseman Cale Makar returns to the lineup Wednesday night after missing the first game of a back-to-back with the Dallas Stars while his wife was in labor.

The team announced that Makar and his wife, Tracy, welcomed twin boys, Mattie and Rhett, on Wednesday morning.

Makar skipped Colorado’s Tuesday trip to Dallas. The Avs won the first game of their back-to-back, 3-2. Martin Necas scored twice, while Nathan MacKinnon had a pair of assists and a bloodied face after his second fight of the season. Mackenzie Blackwood made 24 saves in an impressive bounce-back performance.

A two-time Norris Trophy winner, Makar has three goals and seven points in five games this season. He signed an eight-year, $163.2 million contract with the Avs in August that begins next season and runs through 2035. It is the largest contract in NHL history.

The Avs are also recalling forward Gavin Brindley from the Colorado Eagles to play against the Stars. Zachary L’Heureux was a late scratch in Dallas with an upper-body injury, so the Avs had to play with 11 forwards and six defensemen in the win.

Brindley has a goal and five points in four AHL games with the Eagles this year. He had six goals and 13 points in 56 games with the Avalanche last season.
