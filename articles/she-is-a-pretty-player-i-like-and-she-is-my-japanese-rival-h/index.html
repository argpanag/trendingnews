"She is a pretty player I like, and she is my Japanese rival," Hwang Yu-min refuses to give up on the Rookie of the Year title... Tied for 5th with Hara, "Korea-Japan competition heats up" | STARNEWS

Hwang Yu-min, a rising star in Korean women's golf, is engaged in a fierce battle for the LPGA Tour Rookie of the Year award against Japan's Hara Erika.

On the 6th (Korean time), at the Daiheiyo Club Minori Course (par 72, 6,598 yards) in Ibaraki Prefecture, Japan, where the second round of the 2026 LPGA Tour Toto Japan Classic was held, Hwang Yu-min recorded a 5-under-par 67 with eight birdies and three bogeys.

With this, Hwang Yu-min moved up two spots from tied for 7th the previous day to tied for 5th with an intermediate total of 9-under-par 135. She trails solo leader Hataoka Nasa (Japan, 12-under-par 132) by three shots.

After finishing the first half at 1-under-par, Hwang Yu-min began to reduce her score significantly in the second half. Following a birdie on the 10th hole (par 4), she captured three consecutive birdies on holes 12 through 14 to climb the leaderboard. She added another birdie on the 17th hole (par 5) but committed a bogey on the final 18th hole (par 4), finishing the round tied for 5th.

The Rookie of the Year competition between Hwang Yu-min and Hara is also intriguing. Hara recorded a 7-under-par 65 with seven birdies and no bogeys on the day. Although she had remained at 2-under-par, tied for 26th, the previous day, she swung a powerful game to cut her score by seven shots in a single day.

Ultimately, Hara recorded an intermediate total of 9-under-par 135, placing her side-by-side with Hwang Yu-min in a tie for 5th. As both players challenge for a comeback victory, the Korea-Japan competition for the Rookie of the Year title has become even more intense.

Local Japanese media have also taken note of the two players' Rookie of the Year rivalry. The Japanese golf specialty outlet Golf Digest reported on Hwang Yu-min's performance along with an interview regarding the Rookie of the Year race.

The outlet stated, "There are only three tournaments left in the 2026 LPGA Tour season, including this week," and added, "In the Rookie of the Year competition, Hara is leading the charge toward a third consecutive Japanese winner, following Saigo Mao in 2024 and Yamashita Miyu in 2025."

In particular, Hwang Yu-min expressed familiarity with her rival Hara, saying, "I have played in the same group as Hara a few times. She is one of the pretty players I like. It seems she knows a little Korean as well."

Indeed, the two players were paired in the same group during the first round of the BMW Ladies Championship held in Korea last month.

However, there is no room for retreat in the Rookie of the Year competition. Currently, Hara leads with 559 points in the rookie award standings, while Hwang Yu-min is second with 441 points. The gap between the two players is 118 points.

The winner of this tournament will receive 150 points toward the rookie award. Second place earns 80 points, and third place earns 75 points. With few tournaments remaining until the end of the season, Hwang Yu-min's performance in this event is particularly important.

Hwang Yu-min has also not given up on her challenge for the Rookie of the Year title. She emphasized, "Since the season is not over yet, I am working hard with the goal of winning the Rookie of the Year award."

There is also a reason why this tournament, held in Japan, is welcome to Hwang Yu-min. According to reports, Hwang Yu-min has revealed that she is facing difficulties with language and food on the U.S. stage. In addition to having trouble communicating in English, she continues a lifestyle of visiting Korean grocery stores to find familiar foods and cooking for herself.

On the other hand, she showed a special affection for Japanese food. Hwang Yu-min smiled and said, "I love all Japanese food, such as ramen, sushi, and okonomiyaki." She also cited as a positive factor that Japanese grass is similar to that in Korea, meaning she faces little difficulty adapting to the course.

Meanwhile, Hwang Yu-min secured a spot on the U.S. stage by winning the LPGA Tour Lotte Championship last October as an invited player. She has been actively competing on the LPGA Tour this season, and her tied-for-5th finish at the opening event, the Hilton Grand Vacations Tournament of Champions, is her best result of the season.

At this Toto Japan Classic, she has reached the halfway point tied for 5th, challenging to surpass her season-best result and secure her second career victory on the LPGA Tour.
