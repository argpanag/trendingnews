Dog supplement recalled over possible salmonella contamination

TheTools · 11.09.2026 01:02 · source · permalink

Labradors during a visit by Sophie, Duchess of Edinburgh to The Yellow Labrador Club at The York Club on April 8, 2025 in Windsor, England. (Photo by Mark Cuthbert/UK Press via Getty Images)

A dog supplement brand is recalling two of their supplements after tests showed they may be contaminated with Salmonella. 

What we know:

The supplement, Fi, said the issue stems from an ingredient supplied to its manufacturer, which has suspended production while they investigate the source. According to the announcement from the Food & Drug Administration, the recall covers two 180-gram products, Fi Calming Supplement for Dogs and Fi 8-in-1 Formula Supplement for Dogs.  

The supplements were sold online through Amazon and Chewy, as well as directly to consumers.

Why you should care:

Salmonella can have a similar adverse effect on dogs as it does have in humans. Pets infected can become lethargic and show signs of diarrhea, fevers and vomiting with decreased appetite. 

Dig deeper:

Adverse reactions may be reported to the FDA’s Center for Veterinary Medicine through its online reporting system. 

What they're saying:

All affected customers were notified immediately. They also asked anyone who purchased these products should stop using it and dispose of it. Questions can be directed to Fi’s support line at 888-434-3647 or at support@tryfl.com. 


 

The Source: This story was written with information provided by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. This story was reported from Orlando.
