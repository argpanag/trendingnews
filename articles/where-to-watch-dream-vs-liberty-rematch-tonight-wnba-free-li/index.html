Why Dream vs. Liberty matters

Atlanta comes in with a 29-14 record and a chance to reach the 30-win mark for the first time this season. They also grabbed the upper hand in this week’s two-game set by defeating New York 95-84 on Monday behind 19 points from Jordin Canada.

The Liberty, meanwhile, have plenty of motivation to respond.
