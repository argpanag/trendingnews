Police in Middlesbrough have arrested 17 people in response to intelligence about suspected gang activity linked to last month's fatal wrong-way crash.

Multiple raids were carried out across Teesside on Monday by officers from Cleveland Police who seized drugs, weapons, cash and e-bikes as part of a wider crackdown on organised crime following the A66 collision that killed two police officers and five young men in August.

Some 17 arrests have been made so far, including on suspicion of drugs supply and theft of a motor vehicle. The force's Operation Artemis was expected to continue into Monday evening.

The operation saw roads cut off to allow a long line of police vehicles to pass through, and around 130 officers from forces across the country attended.

Cleveland Police officers arrest a person during a raid on a property as part of Operation Artemis - PA Wire

The Telegraph understands that the raids were launched after officers received intelligence about reported "precursor" offences and events leading up to the A66 crash. The collision was followed by a series of suspected gang-related incidents and saw hundreds of officers from across Britain deployed to Middlesbrough.

Pc Matthew Blades, 37, and Pc Tom Clough, 38, died in a patrol car in the head-on collision with a Volkswagen Passat that was evading police and travelling the wrong way on the dual carriageway in the early hours of Aug 22.

Makai Saddington, 18, Theo Rae, 17, Jakub Matusiak, 23, Michael Cahill, 23, and Cole Worthy, 17, who were in the VW, also died. It later emerged that the crash happened less than an hour after an SUV rammed a house belonging to Cole's father.

Money seized by Cleveland Police as part of Operation Artemis - PA

Since then, a seven-year-old girl and her aunt were killed in a house fire, a house in the town was rammed by a car and a 39-year-old man was stabbed. Police have not confirmed a link between the A66 crash and the three incidents. They had arrested 27 people in the wake of the crash by the end of August.

On Monday, officers made four arrests at an address in Grangetown on suspicion of drug supply offences. Three men aged 37, 43 and 60 and a 58-year-old woman were taken into police custody while an amount of suspected class A drugs was recovered, along with cash and a dog which is believed to be a banned breed.

At an address in Ormesby, officers forced open the door and recovered a knife, baton and a knuckle-duster and recovered suspected class C drugs.

Cleveland Police officers arrest a person during a raid on a property as part of Operation Artemis - PA Wire

Elsewhere, officers said they were "goaded" by youths on an electric bike being ridden illegally during the operation and subsequently arrested two 17-year-old boys on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle. They seized the Sur-Ron, another e-bike, a suspected stolen plate compactor and an electric scooter.

Supt Daryll Tomlinson, who briefed the officers before the operation, said on Monday: "Officers from across the region and across the country will be joining us today to take the fight back to the criminals in the area.

"We'll be executing drugs warrants, arresting people wanted on recall and warrants and sending a clear message out to the community."

A full update from Cleveland Police is expected on Tuesday.
