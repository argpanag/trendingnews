Talk Portland versus Minnesota tonight!

Tonight the Portland Trail Blazers will face another Western Conference foe on a hot streak to start the 2026-27 season. Portland travels to Minneapolis to take on the Minnesota Timberwolves at 5:00 PM, Pacific time. It’s the first game of a back-to-back. It’s always better to win that one, since there’s no guarantee who will play tomorrow in consecutive games!

You can talk about the action with friends and neighbors here as it unfolds. Here’s the basic info on the game:

Portland Trail Blazers (9-3) at Minnesota Timberwolves (9-4) - Monday, November 16th - 5:00pm Pacific

How to watch: BlazerVision in Oregon and Washington; League Pass everywhere else

How to listen: Rip City Radio 620AM

Trail Blazers Injuries: Shaedon Sharpe, Ja Morant, Vít Krejci, Chris Youngblood (Out).

Timberwolves Injuries: Anthony Edwards, Donte DiVincenzo, Jaylen Clark (Out).
