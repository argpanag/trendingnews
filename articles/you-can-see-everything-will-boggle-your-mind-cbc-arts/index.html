You Can See Everything will boggle your mind | CBC Arts

TheTools · 17.10.2026 08:23 · source · permalink

Film critic Adam Nayman, journalist Alyssa Bereznak and filmmaker Chandler Levack try to pull back the curtain

It’s the film nobody saw coming: comedian Nathan Fielder and filmmaker Lance Oppenheim teaming up for a documentary about Theranos founder and notorious fraudster Elizabeth Holmes.

You Can See Everything is in theatres now, and it has premiered to rave reviews. With a 100% critic score and a 99% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, the film is being called a masterpiece and is already considered a contender for best picture at the Oscars.

Today on Commotion, film critic Adam Nayman, journalist Alyssa Bereznak and filmmaker Chandler Levack try to pull back the curtain on You Can See Everything.
