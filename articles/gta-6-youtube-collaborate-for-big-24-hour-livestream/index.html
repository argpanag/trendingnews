GTA 6 & YouTube Collaborate For Big 24-Hour Livestream

TheTools · 12.11.2026 16:08 · source · permalink

GTA 6 is the biggest video game ever, so it makes sense to give people ‘the biggest livestream.’ YouTube carries on Grand Theft Auto 6’s incredible marketing campaign with a can’t-miss streaming event.

If you’re not playing GTA 6, or even if you are, it might be cool to check out YouTube‘s launch day livestream, featuring 24 hours of continuous action featuring a plethora of streamers and content creators.

YouTube & GTA 6 Livestream For 24 Hours Straight

‘The biggest launch of all time deserves the biggest livestream. 24 hours. 11/18. 11pm ET. YouTube.’
