How to watch NDSU play UNLV on Saturday, Oct. 10

TheTools · 09.10.2026 15:53 · source · permalink

FARGO — North Dakota State's game against UNLV on Saturday, Oct. 10 will be broadcast on The CW, the same network that aired last Saturday's Bison game at the Fargodome.

The game is slated for a 6:10 p.m. CT kickoff at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. Jack McMullen (play-by-play), Cody Kessler (analyst) and Marshall Newhouse (sideline) have the call on The CW.
