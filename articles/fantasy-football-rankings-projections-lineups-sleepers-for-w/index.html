Top Week 6 Fantasy football picks 

One surprise in Week 6: The model isn't high on Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins, despite him ranking sixth in Fantasy points for QBs this year. The veteran overachieved to start the year but is coming off his worst game with just 243 passing yards and 1 touchdown versus New England. Cousins is trending in the wrong direction as after having 3 passing TDs in each of the first three weeks, he then had two scores in Week 4 and then one in Week 5. The Raiders will face Buffalo on Sunday, and Cousins has five total fumbles (two lost) and three picks over his last two games against the Bills. Cousins doesn't even crack the top 15 at his position this week as he's one to fade in Week 6 Fantasy football lineups.

Another curveball: the model is high on Colts receiver Josh Downs, despite him being the WR43 in PPR leagues. Downs is coming off 3 catches for 47 yards on Sunday and has averaged 4.5 receptions for 53 yards over his last four games. However, his Fantasy floor and ceiling will be much higher in Week 6 versus the winless Titans. Downs has scored a touchdown in three of his last four games against Tennessee, and this year has seen the Titans allow eight different wideouts to post at least 60 yards or a touchdown in just five games.
