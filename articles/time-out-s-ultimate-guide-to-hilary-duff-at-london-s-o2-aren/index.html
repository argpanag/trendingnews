Time Out’s ultimate guide to Hilary Duff at London’s O2 Arena

TheTools · 16.09.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

London is getting a dose of Lizzie McGuire nostalgia as the The Lucky Me Tour hits the O2 this week. It’s Duff’s first tour in nearly two decades

Hilary Duff is finally back on the road and London is getting two nights with her this month. The Lucky Me Tour is Duff's first global headline run in almost two decades, and its first date at the O2 Arena in Greenwich was last Thursday (September 10). The second show is this week, on Tuesday September 15. 

The tour is in support of Duff's sixth studio album, luck... or something, which was released back in February. Hardcore fans will know this is a bit of a big deal coming from Lizzie McGuire herself, as it’s her first new record since 2015’s Breathe In. Breathe Out. It follows a run of comeback singles like ‘Mature’ and ‘Roommates’. 

Going to see Hilary Duff at the O2 this week – or hoping to grab last-minute tickets? Here’s everything you need to know, from start time and setlist to available tickets and travel.

What time do doors open?

Doors open at 6.30pm. However, the venue’s shops, restaurants and bars are open earlier.

What time will Hilary Duff come on stage?

TBased on other dates on the Lucky Me Tour (including the first date in London), expect Duff to start her main set at 9pm (or just before). 

What time does the show finish?

The tour’s average set has run for around one hour 40 minutes, with shows elsewhere wrapping up by roughly 10.50pm. This aligns with the O2’s curfew of 11pm.

La Roux is the Grammy-winning electropop artist that will be supporting Duff across her 2026 international dates, including the UK shows.

Last-minute Hilary Duff tickets for the O2

At the time of writing, there are still seating tickets available to purchase via Ticketmaster. There are further resale options via StubHub. 

How to get to the O2: tube lines and travel advice

London’s biggest indoor music venue is best accessed via tube at North Greenwich station, on the Jubilee line. It’s a short walk from there to the O2.

O2 Arena bag policy

Gig-goers are permitted to bring in bags smaller than A4 size to the O2. Backpacks, travel cases, laptop bags, camera bags and large tote bags are not permitted. There’s large bag storage for £10.
