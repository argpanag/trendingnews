Take your Halloween costume cues from celebs

TheTools · 22.10.2026 07:41 · source · permalink

Ready to up your Halloween costume game this year? Then look to the celebs for inspiration. Heidi Klum is known for elaborate transformations for her annual Halloween bash. From grotesque Medusa and Jessica Rabbit to E.T. and an elaborate peacock, she sets the over-the-top tone.

Her guests run with it, with getups ranging from cartoon characters and evil clowns to iconic movie characters.

So if you’re feeling ambitious, take some inspiration from a few celeb getups.

A mozzarella stick: This was a Lizzo look, and it wasn’t just a mozzarella stick, it was a melting one. A pre-made version of the costume doesn’t exist, but you can get a similar effect by wearing a cream-colored, strapless dress like The Efi Dress ($93.38 on bloomingdales.com) made by The Line by K. Just make sure to finish off the look with a flowy, cream-colored wrap like the Women’s Knit Twisted Faux-Fur-Trim Wrap ($40.80 on macys.com).

Pennywise the Clown: Last year Ed Sheeran turned up in terrifying form as the evil clown, and you can too if you put on something as easy-to-wear as the Pennywise IT Unisex Costume ($35.99 on walmart.com).

The Cat in the Hat: On the “Jennifer Hudson Show” last year, Janelle Monae showed up in lots of prosthetics to channel Mike Myers’ rendition of the Dr. Seuss icon. But you can just order the Dr. Seuss Cat in the Hat Deluxe Costume Kit ($20.98 on target.com) and have an equally big impact.

Britney Spears: In 2024, Paris Hilton showed up at Klum’s soiree in braids, wearing a bra-revealing button-down shirt, cardigan, and skirt. It’s a look that’s pretty easy to recreate with things you might already have in your closet, but if not, there the YIXAXKAO Pop Singer Costume for Women ($30.99 on amazon.com), which covers all of those bases. (Except the braids. Those are up to you.)

Dorothy from “Wizard of Oz”: In 2024, Olivia Culpo revived a costume she’d worn previously (in 2019), this time alongside her husband, Christian McCaffrey — who dressed up as the Tin Man. Get her entire look in a jiffy with the Women’s The Wizard of Oz Dorothy Costume – Kansas Edition ($46.99 on maisonette.com). (Again, though: The braids are up to you.)

Dread Pirate Roberts and Inigo Montoya: Neil Patrick Harris and husband David Burtka, known for their embrace of Halloween costuming, brought “The Princess Bride” to life at Heidi Klum’s 2018 party. Find your version of both with the Princess Bride Inigo Montoya Costume ($79.99 on halloweencostumes.com) and the Princess Bride Men’s Westley Costume ($69.99 on halloweencostumes.com).
