Fuel prices down anew this week

TheTools · 16.11.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

BUSINESS

by

Joann Santiago-Villanueva

Nov 16, 2026

6:58 PM GMT+8

PRICES DOWN. A busy gas station in Paco, Manila in this photo taken June 22, 2026. Domestic fuel prices are set for another rollback on Tuesday (Nov. 17), given the general decline in crude oil prices in the international market on easing Middle East tensions. (PNA file photo by Yancy Lim)

MANILA – Fuel prices are set for another cut for the second consecutive week, tracing the movement in the international market.

 

In separate advisories, Seaoil and Petron Corporation said they will slash gasoline prices by PHP1.50 per liter, diesel by PHP3.80 per liter and kerosene by PHP5.80 per liter, effective 6 a.m. Tuesday.

 

Earlier, Jetti Petroleum president Leo Bellas said the price reductions are effects of reports about the easing of tensions in the Middle East.

 

“Diplomatic headlines continue to push oil prices lower this week despite lingering supply risks. However, growing supply disruption worries as attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure and shipping through the Strait of Hormuz escalated have caused prices to rebound recently,” he said. (PNA)
