Slice of NY Pizza Now Available at Avengers Campus at Disneyland Resort

A new culinary offering is now available at Avengers Campus at Disneyland Resort. This comes in the form of a “Slice of NY” pizza and can be found at the second food cart in Avengers Campus, located by the Hyperion Theater. Pizza is sold by the slice, and there is cheese or pepperoni available. The pepperoni pizza is $9.99, and the cheese pizza is $9.49.

With the new pizza offerings arriving in Avengers Campus, friends of Daps Magic Mike and Matt stopped by to check out these new offerings. Below are photos and video of the new “Slice of NY” pizza. Check it out and then share what you think!
