WEATHER BLOG: Rain on Halloween? Here's what the early forecast shows

TheTools · 21.10.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

Rain on Halloween?

All Hallows' Eve is just 10 days away, and it appears there will be a seasonal chill in the air on the evening of the 31st.

I've also noticed rain chances lurking in the Oct. 30-Nov. 1 timeframe in several physics-based and AI/machine-learning models.

It's still a ways out, but I'm more confident about seasonal temperatures — in the 50s and 60s, with no extreme heat or cold — than I am about the precipitation forecast.

Parents: I wouldn't worry about trying to incorporate rain jackets into your children's costumes just yet. They'll probably need light base layers under their costumes, much like what they would typically wear on a late October evening.
