Denmark stun Portugal 4-1 to hand hosts 1st Nations League defeat

TheTools · 15.11.2026 01:41 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

Denmark handed Portugal their first defeat of the UEFA Nations League campaign Saturday with a 4-1 away victory, as Rasmus Hojlund scored twice to lift the visitors into second place in Group A4.

The Danes ended Portugal's four-match winning streak at Estadio Municipal de Braga in the hosts' first match since Cristiano Ronaldo's retirement from international football.

Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg opened the scoring in the 43rd minute, converting a penalty awarded after a video assistant referee review found Portuguese defender Ruben Dias had handled the ball inside the box.

The Danish captain sent his spot kick into the top-left corner, leaving goalkeeper Diogo Costa with no chance.

Denmark doubled their lead four minutes into the second half when Mikkel Damsgaard delivered a cross to the back post, where Hojlund struck a left-footed volley into the bottom corner.

Hojlund completed his brace in the 57th minute, collecting a through ball from Hojbjerg before turning past Renato Veiga and firing a right-footed shot into the far corner to make it 3-0.

Portugal made substitutions in an attempt to turn the match around, with Carlos Forbs replacing Joao Felix in the 64th minute.

The Club Brugge winger became the first player to wear Portugal's redesigned No. 7 jersey, introduced to honor Ronaldo following his retirement from international football.

Denmark extended their advantage in the 75th minute when Joachim Andersen headed home a free-kick delivery from Mathias Jensen to make it 4-0.

Portugal grabbed a consolation goal in the third minute of stoppage time when substitute Bernardo Silva converted from close range after Mads Hermansen saved Joao Neves' initial effort.

Portugal remained atop Group A4 with 12 points despite the defeat, having already secured qualification for the Nations League quarterfinals.

Denmark climbed to second place with nine points, three ahead of Norway.

Portugal will visit Wales on Tuesday in their final group match, while Denmark will host Norway.
