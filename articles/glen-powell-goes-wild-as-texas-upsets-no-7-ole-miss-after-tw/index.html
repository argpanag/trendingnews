Glen Powell goes wild as Texas upsets No. 7 Ole Miss after two straight losses

TheTools · 25.10.2026 18:18 · source · permalink

Glen Powell had every reason to celebrate. After watching his beloved Texas Longhorns endure two consecutive losses, the Hollywood star finally got the moment he had been waiting for Saturday night — and he made sure to share it with the fans. Powell erupted with joy as Texas defeated No. 7 Ole Miss 34-27 at Darrell K Royal-Texas Memorial Stadium, embracing Longhorns supporters along the sideline as the home crowd celebrated a much-needed victory. The actor, a regular presence at Texas games, was on hand as the Longhorns delivered one of their most consequential performances of the season in front of 104,306 fans. The win gave Texas its second conference victory and moved the Longhorns to 5-2 overall and 2-2 in SEC play. More importantly, it offered a response after consecutive defeats and gave the team momentum heading into the final stretch of the regular season.
