Dangerous bacteria found in smoked salmon: product recalled from sale in Latvia

TheTools · 08.10.2026 13:01 · source · permalink

According to the PVS, listeria was detected during laboratory control in packages of salmon weighing 100 grams. This concerns products with batch number **L0825-0828**. ### Salmon recalled from stores After receiving the test results, a decision was made to recall the corresponding batch from sale. Those who have already purchased this salmon are advised by the PVD not to eat it. The information about the detected violation has also been transmitted by the Latvian service to the European Union's rapid alert system. ### What is dangerous about listeria Listeria monocytogenes is a microorganism capable of causing an acute infectious disease - listeriosis. According to the Latvian Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (CDC), listeriosis is an infectious disease in animals and humans, which can affect the central nervous system, lymph nodes, and various internal organs. The PVS operates under the Ministry of Agriculture of Latvia and carries out state supervision and control in the field of food circulation and veterinary medicine.
