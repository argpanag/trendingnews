Golden Knights sign Braeden Bowman to six-year contract extension

TheTools · 10.09.2026 17:24 · source · permalink

The Vegas Golden Knights have handed out a long-term deal.

Forward Braden Bowman has signed a six-year, $20.4 million contract extension, the Golden Knights announced Thursday.

With still one year remaining on his two-year, entry-level deal, Bowman's new contract will take effect next season.

Bowman, 23, made his NHL debut with the Golden Knights last season, recording 26 points (eight goals and 18 assists) in 54 games. He was a healthy scratch during the playoffs but did appear in Game 6 of the Stanley Cup Final as the Golden Knights lost to the Carolina Hurricanes.

The Kitchener, Ont., native has also appeared in 88 career AHL games, all with the Golden Knights' affiliate, and has posted 62 points (26 goals and 36 assists).
