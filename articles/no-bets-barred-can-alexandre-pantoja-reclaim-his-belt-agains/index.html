Breaking down all the best bets for UFC 331: Joshua Van vs. Alexandre Pantoja II

Joshua Van and Alexandre Pantoja are set to run things back.

On Saturday, Van and Pantoja meet in a flyweight title rematch in the main event of UFC 331 in Los Angeles. With their first fight ending due to a freak injury that crowned Van champion, this matchup offers an opportunity to Van to prove the injury didn’t matter and for Pantoja to correct the record. But who will reign supreme?

On the latest episode of No Bets Barred, host Jed Meshew flies solo as he dives into the full UFC 331 card. Topics discussed include where Pantoja can reclaim his title or if #VanLife is here to stay, Arman Tsarukyan’s precarious position against Mauricio Ruffy, Patricio Pitbull and Doo Ho Choi banging it out, the rest of the undercard, The Climb, and more.

Tune in for episode 165 of No Bets Barred.
