T1 Doran: “What Makes You A Great Player Is How You Get Through The Pressure” - Hotspawn

Davide "Dovi" Xu

Share:

T1 at Worlds is a different beast. For the 11th consecutive time, the team has reached the Worlds semifinals, this time after beating the last hope of the West, Karmine Corp.

Starting with a strong 2-0, KC managed to get one back in Game 3, but it wasn’t enough to swing the momentum back in the LEC team’s favor. T1 regained composure and found great fights one after the other, including an incredible Neeko 5-man engage from Keria.

Now, the team moves on to face BLG in the semifinals, in what will be a big rematch from Worlds 2024 finals and MSI earlier this year. In the post-match interview, we spoke to Doran about how he’s been dealing with the pressure and what makes him enjoy League every day.

Davide: First of all, congratulations on the win.

Doran: Nothing special. I woke up and thought, ‘Oh, today’s match day.’ I got ready, took a shower, ate, and came to the venue.

Davide: You’re going to face BLG in the semifinals. It’s a rematch of MSI 2026, where you unfortunately lost 3–2. I want to ask you about Bin. He’s had quite a journey this season, and it feels like he’s been improving alongside the rest of BLG.

Doran: He’s been performing really well and consistently, and I think his performance has been solid at this Worlds too.

When I watched their quarterfinal, I thought their teamwork was really good, so we have to prepare thoroughly. If I can bring out my best performance, I think we can definitely win against them.

Davide: You’ve recently talked about the importance of handling pressure and stress.

Doran: Everyone goes through moments when they feel under pressure or experience a lot of stress. But I think the pressure you experience in the world of sports is a little different.

What makes you a great player is how you get through that pressure.

Davide: How aware are you of that pressure day to day? How often do you feel like you’re reaching your limit?

Doran: During a tournament, I always try to focus solely on the game. I try not to pay attention to unnecessary stress around me. And because I’ve been a professional player for a long time, I think I’m quite used to it now.

Doran: The bond I’ve been able to build with the T1 members. Those little moments every day make me really happy. Whenever I’m with my teammates or other people at T1, I have such a great time spending time with them.

Davide: I wanted to show you a photo. This is from Worlds 2025. A friend and I were talking about how your story is a little like Forrest Gump’s. He faced difficulties, but over time he kept looking for a way to break through them.

Doran: Once you go pro, it’s unavoidable that you’ll sometimes go through rough times, hardships, and a lot of stress.

But I think I can overcome those things by winning or performing really well.
Those are the moments when I feel happy and get a sense of freedom and release.

Doran: I don’t really know. Since we live together as a team, one thing I always keep in mind is not to make other people uncomfortable.

I don’t think there’s anything specific I consciously do for others. But we’re all going through this challenging time together as competitors, so it would be nice if we took care of each other and were kind to each other.
