Ferrari on 15 September: Rubens Barrichello’s 2002 Monza win

TheTools · 07.11.2026 20:51 · source · permalink

On 15 September 2002, Rubens Barrichello won the Italian Grand Prix at Monza, leading Michael Schumacher home in a Ferrari one-two. The result gave the tifosi a home victory with both Scuderia drivers on the podium.

Barrichello leads Ferrari home at Monza

Barrichello started fourth on the grid and finished first after 53 laps. Schumacher took second, with just 0.255 seconds separating the two Ferraris in the final classification.

The winning margin captures how closely the teammates crossed the line, while the finishing order places Barrichello at the centre of this particular Ferrari anniversary.

Under the scoring system then in use, first and second earned Ferrari 16 points: ten for Barrichello and six for Schumacher.

A podium with another Ferrari connection

Eddie Irvine finished third for Jaguar, 52.579 seconds behind Barrichello. The former Ferrari driver therefore joined his old team’s current pairing on the podium, adding another connection to the Scuderia’s recent history.

Monza’s 2002 result offers a reminder to examine individual races alongside broader championship records. On this afternoon, it was Barrichello who delivered Ferrari’s victory.
