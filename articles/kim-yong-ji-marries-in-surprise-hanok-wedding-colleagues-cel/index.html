Kim Yong-ji marries in surprise hanok wedding, colleagues celebrate

TheTools · 11.10.2026 23:10 · source · permalink

Actor Kim Yong-ji, who announced a surprise marriage without any prior dating rumors, has become the center of attention, and interest in her is heating up.

On the 11th, actor Bae Jung-nam shared on his social media, "Yongji, congratulations on your wedding," along with photos of Kim Yong-ji's wedding held against the backdrop of a serene hanok. In the released photos, Kim Yong-ji held a red bouquet while wearing an elegant ivory dress that revealed her shoulder line and wore a bright smile, and colleagues from the entertainment world, including Bae Jung-nam and close friend Kim Go-eun, attended to add to the celebrations.

So who is Kim Yong-ji, who surprised fans with the sudden news of becoming an "October bride"?

Kim Yong-ji debuted in 2015 as an advertising model and first drew significant attention in the advertising and modeling worlds with her distinctive exotic visuals and mysterious aura.

She later took her first successful step as an actor in 2018 in the tvN blockbuster drama Mr. Sunshine, playing Hotaru, a silent astrologer who stays by Gu Dong-mae (played by Yoo Yeon-seok). At the time, she rose instantly as a scene-stealer by perfectly completing the character's narrative with nothing but her eyes and aura.

Having solidified her position as an actor with exotic visuals and steady acting skills, Kim Yong-ji steadily built her filmography by showing growth in various works such as the dramas WATCHER, Everyone's Lies, The King: Eternal Monarch, Tale of the Nine Tailed, Somebody, Tale of the Nine Tailed 1938, and the film Dark Web. She proved a wide acting spectrum that spans the screen and the small screen. In one interview regarding her image of "mystery," she said, "I am grateful, but from now on I want to create my own image that is 'like Kim Yong-ji.'"

Having opened the second chapter of her life with a surprise wedding without public dating, heartfelt congratulations and support are pouring in from fans and colleagues.
