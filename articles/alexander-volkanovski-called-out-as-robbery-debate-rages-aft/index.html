Alexander Volkanovski called out as 'robbery' debate rages after Aljamain Sterling loses at UFC Qatar

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

Kevin Vallejos called for a title shot after picking up the biggest win of his career on Saturday night.

The 24-year-old rising star squared off against Aljamain Sterling in the UFC Qatar co-main event.

After a closely contested 15-minute featherweight fight, the three cageside judges scored in favor of Kevin Vallejos.

However, many people disagreed with their 30-27, 30-27, and 29-28 scorecards.

Fans and fighters react to the UFC Qatar co-main event

Aljamain Sterling raised his hand when the unanimous decision result was read out by UFC announcer Joe Martinez.

The former UFC bantamweight champion looked stunned when Kevin Vallejos was declared the winner.

Sterling’s view of the fight was quickly backed up by people on social media.

“Robbery,” UFC featherweight contender Lerone Murphy tweeted.

“That is a horrendous robbery, what the f–,” a fan fumed

“Lol what a robbery… I like Vallejos but that was incredibly suss,” someone else said.

“30-27 TO VALLEJOS? FIRE THOSE JUDGES IMMEDIATELY,” another X user wrote.

“Aljo got robbed lol actual robbery,” a fourth fan said.

“That was such a robbery, should be 30-27 Aljo,” another fan insisted.

Kevin Vallejos wants a title fight

Kevin Vallejos was asked what’s next after making it five wins in a row since joining the UFC

The Argentine fighter told Michael Bisping that he wants a shot at the featherweight king.

“I think the only logical option is Volkanovski,” Vallejos said via a translator during his post-fight interview.

“If he chooses to go up to 155lbs, I understand.

“But I would love to have the opportunity for him to fight me at 145lbs.

“I hope he gives me the opportunity, and he liked this fight.”
