Mavericks, who helped heal NBA-China rift, hold celebratory event ahead of exhibition

TheTools · 09.10.2026 01:55 · source · permalink

Mavericks owner Patrick Dumont, whose Las Vegas Sands company “played an integral role in the mending of relations” between China and the NBA, hosted a party for more than 200 at the Sands-owned Parisian Macao ahead of a pair of Mavs-Rockets exhibitions this weekend, according to Brad Townsend of the DALLAS MORNING NEWS. This marks the second straight year of NBA China Games in Macao, following a four-year hiatus in the aftermath of then-Rockets GM Daryl Morey’s 2019 tweet in support of Hong Kong pro-democracy protestors, which NBA Commissioner Adam Silver at one point said cost the league hundreds of millions of dollars. The exhibitions are expected to bring 400 journalists, most of them from mainland China and other Asian countries. Mavericks G Kyrie Irving said, “Patrick and Sands have done a great job of bringing the NBA back here, and being able to collaborate” (DALLAS MORNING NEWS, 10/7).

BEST FOOT FORWARD: Townsend in a separate piece wrote with Irving being backed by Chinese shoe brand Anta, there is not “any doubt” which player attracted the most cameras and reporter questions during Thursday’s media day. Irving is Anta’s chief creative officer and “plays an active role in brand direction, design and marketing.” Saturday will mark the global release of the Anta Macao Pack, debuting with two of what is expected to be four new colorways" for the KAI 3 shoe (DALLAS MORNING NEWS,10/7).
