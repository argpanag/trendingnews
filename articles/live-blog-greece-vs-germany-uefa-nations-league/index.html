Greece Looks to Surprise Germany Again

The UEFA Nations League continues this Sunday, October 4, with an intriguing matchup between Greece and Germany, two national teams looking to collect important points in the battle for the top spots in Group A2.


The Greek side has been one of the biggest surprises of the group and enters Matchday 4 as the leader with seven points. Its campaign began with a 2-1 victory over Serbia in Belgrade before delivering a surprising 1-0 win against Germany in Augsburg.

In its most recent appearance, Greece dropped points for the first time in the tournament after drawing 2-2 at home against the Netherlands. Despite losing its perfect record, the Greek side remains unbeaten and will now look to repeat the formula that helped it defeat Germany just a couple of matchdays ago.

Germany Seeks Revenge and a Move Toward the Top

Germany finds itself in a different situation. The team coached by Jürgen Klopp sits third in the group with four points and needs a victory to remain within striking distance of the top positions.



The Klopp era began with a 1-1 draw against the Netherlands. That was followed by a home defeat to Greece, one of the most unexpected results of the opening matchdays of the tournament.

However, the Germans responded in their most recent match by defeating Serbia 2-0, a result that gave them their first victory of this UEFA Nations League campaign.

Three Important Points in Group A2

The second meeting between Greece and Germany in this group stage could have a major impact on the standings. Another Greek victory would allow the hosts to remain at the top, while the visitors have an opportunity to close the gap and get revenge for their defeat in Augsburg.

The match will kick off this Sunday at 2:45 PM ET / 11:45 AM PT.
