Press Statement by His Majesty the King on the occasion of the State Visit to the Republic of Kazachstan | The Belgian Monarchy

TheTools · 13.10.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Mister President,
Excellencies,
Ladies and gentlemen,

Let me tell you, Mr. President, that Queen Mathilde and I are really happy to be in Kazakhstan for this State Visit at your invitation.

We are very curious to learn as much as possible about your beautiful country, and to bring the already very good relations at a higher level.

We know that over the past 30 years our countries have forged unbreakable ties. They are based on what, each in our own region, makes us special: diversity and connectivity.

When you, Mister President, visited Brussels and so cordially invited us for a State Visit to your country, there was no hesitation. I am particularly happy that the Visit takes place in the year celebrating Kazakhstan’s 35th Independence Anniversary.

I would like to thank you, Mr. President, and the authorities of Kazakhstan who have spared no effort, and made all the arrangements to receive us so well and organize such an interesting programme.

The three coming days will allow us to explore the potential and opportunities our countries offer each other. We will celebrate our friendship through culture. We will have the privilege to have a glimpse of the greatness and beauty of Kazakhstan.

 

Mister President, ladies and gentlemen,

I had the chance to visit Kazakhstan three times.

I witnessed the landing, now exactly 24 years ago, at Baikonur, bringing Belgium’s astronaut safely back to earth. I returned in 2009 to attend the launch of a space rocket, again carrying a Belgian astronaut, this time for a long-term stay in the International Space Station.

In 2010, I had the honour to lead, as Crown Prince, the first Belgian Economic Mission to Kazakhstan. This important mission heralded a period of growing bilateral relations, marked by numerous economic and academic exchanges. Our cooperation has also benefited from the Enhanced Partnership Agreement between Kazakhstan and the European Union, which entered into force in 2020, further strengthening our ties.

Today, Belgium is an important investor in Kazakhstan and many Belgian companies are solidly established here. There are innumerable partnerships in many areas, some of which we will get to know better during our stay in Astana and Almaty. I am particularly looking forward to the 4th Meeting of the Belgium-Kazakh Business Council tomorrow, together with you Mr President.

 

Mister President, ladies and gentlemen,

The continuing transformation of Kazakhstan into a world player is impressive. Only recently, the Kazakh people cemented a constitutional reform reflecting new ambitions for the country.

By all standards, Kazakhstan is today a modern society built on ancient foundations and honoured traditions. With a well-educated population, a large middle class and state-of-the-art infrastructure, Kazakhstan is ready to tackle the challenges of the future. It is at the forefront of digitalization. It has developed unique solutions, especially in the area of Artificial Intelligence. We are keen to visit the Alem A.I. center and discuss synergies between our two countries.

 

Mister President, ladies and gentlemen,

Most of all, this State Visit will allow us to understand Kazakhstan better. It is the perfect opportunity to appreciate the rich history and culture of this great country, its regional, ethnic and linguistic diversity.

I am looking forward very much to visiting iconic places and museums in Astana and in Almaty, offering a glimpse of the depth going back to the Golden Hord. With the Belgian delegation, I am enthusiastic to discover the natural and cultural wealth so beautifully described by the incomparable Abaï, and at the heart of Kazakh identity.

Kazakhstan is the epitome of hospitality, of “qonaqzhailyq” (KÔ-NAK JAH-I-LYK). It actively promotes internal but also regional and international harmony and understanding. It has always played this role. Kazakh people offered shelter and compassion to so many people of all backgrounds during the dark days of Communism. It has forged its rich multiethnic society. Kazakhstan has left the ‘Great Game’ status long behind it. It is now at the core of the ‘Middle Corridor’, literally in the heart of Eurasia.

As a bridge builder, Kazakhstan is more than ever the promotor of dialogue, the rules based international order and a firm belief in multilateralism as way to solve disputes. This is what fundamentally binds Kazakhstan with Belgium and the European Union.

 

Mister President, ladies and gentlemen,

“Shanyraghynyz biik bolsyn” or “Let the shanyrak stand high” is a befitting expression to describe the spirit guiding this Belgian State Visit to Kazakhstan. The shanyrak allows the sun rays to enter, enlighten and warm a beautiful home and make dwellers and guests alike feel welcome and happy. It reflects the high ambition to achieve positive results and move forward together.

 

Mister President,

I express again my gratitude for your warm reception and hospitality. The Queen and I and the entire delegation wish you and the people of Kazakhstan every happiness and success.

Thank you.
