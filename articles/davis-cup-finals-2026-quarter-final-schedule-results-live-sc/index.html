By Raja Keshri,

The Davis Cup Finals 2026 are here. The best eight nations are in Bologna. The atmosphere is electric. Every tie matters now. One bad day ends a season.

The Finals run from November 24 to November 29. All ties are at the SuperTennis Arena. The venue sits inside BolognaFiere. Italy are the defending champions. They have won the last three titles. They want a fourth straight crown.

This is knockout tennis at its purest. Each tie has two singles matches. Then comes one doubles match if needed. The first team to two wins advances. There is no room for a slow start.

Davis Cup Finals 2026 Quarter-Final Results Live

Quarter-Final 1: Czechia beat Canada 2-0

Czechia are the first semi-finalists. They were sharp from the first ball. They handled the pressure beautifully.

Tomas Machac beat Liam Draxl 6-2, 3-6, 6-2. He recovered well after dropping the second set. His returning was brave in the decider.

Jiri Lehecka then beat Felix Auger-Aliassime 7-6(4), 6-2. He served with real authority. He closed out the tie in style. The doubles was not needed. Canada fought hard. They could not find the big moments. Czechia now waits for their semi-final opponent.

Davis Cup Finals 2026 Quarter-Final Schedule

Wednesday, November 25, 4:00 PM CET: Italy vs South Korea

This is the big one for the home crowd. Italy start their title defence today. The arena will be loud. The fans will expect a win. Italy lead the head-to-head 3-0. They are heavy favourites on paper. South Korea are the underdogs. They have nothing to lose. That makes them dangerous.

Thursday, November 26, 10:00 AM CET: Great Britain vs Germany

This is a classic Davis Cup clash. Great Britain bring youth and fight. Germany bring power and experience. Alexander Zverev leads the German team. This tie could go to doubles.

Thursday, November 26, 5:00 PM CET: Austria vs Spain

Spain close the quarter-finals. They are the second seeds. Carlos Alcaraz leads their squad. Austria will need a perfect day. Spain will want a fast start.

Davis Cup Finals 2026 Semi-Final Picture

The semi-finals are on Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28. The final is on Sunday, November 29.

Czechia will face the winner of Italy vs South Korea. That semi-final is on Friday. The other semi-final is on Saturday. It will feature the winners of the two Thursday ties.

Teams To Watch During The Davis Cup Finals 2026

Italy

Captain Filippo Volandri leads a deep squad. Flavio Cobolli is the top-ranked Italian here. Lorenzo Musettibrings flair and variety. Luciano Darderi brings power and energy. Matteo Berrettini brings big-match experience. Andrea Vavassori is a doubles specialist. Jannik Sinner is not playing. Italy still have enough depth to win.

South Korea

Captain Jong-Sam Chung leads a brave team. Kwon Soonwoo is their top singles player. He is ranked around No. 156. South Korea waited decades for this stage. They will play with freedom.

Czechia

Czechia are already through. Lehecka and Machac are in fine form. Their doubles pair is strong too. They look like genuine title contenders.

Canada

Canada are out. Felix Auger-Aliassime gave everything. Liam Draxl fought hard in his debut. The team will learn from this loss.

Great Britain

Great Britain are young and hungry. They play with great team spirit. They will need their best tennis against Germany.

Germany

Germany are the third seeds. Zverev is the world No. 1 in their squad. Jan-Lennard Struff adds power. Yannick Hanfmann adds depth. Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz form a world-class doubles pair.

Austria

Austria are the outsiders in their tie. They will need early momentum. A fast start could change everything.

Spain

Spain are the second seeds. Alcaraz is the star name. David Ferrer captains the team. Spain have depth in singles and doubles. They are Italy’s biggest threat.

Players To Watch During The Davis Cup Finals 2026

Carlos Alcaraz is the headline act. He can change a tie in minutes. Alexander Zverev is the other superstar. His serve is a weapon. Flavio Cobolli is Italy’s leader this week. Jiri Lehecka is already in top form. Matteo Berrettini loves this event. He lifts his game for Italy.

How To Watch The Davis Cup Finals 2026

Fans in Italy can watch on SuperTennis. Fans in the UK can watch on TNT Sports. Coverage is also on HBO Max in selected regions.

Fans in the USA can watch on Tennis Channel. Live scores are on the official Davis Cup website. Live scores are also on the Davis Cup app. Highlights arrive after each tie.

Check local listings before each tie. Start times can shift slightly. The evening tie starts not before 5:00 PM CET.

End Of My Davis Cup Finals 2026 Rant: What Happens Next?

Italy play today. Two more quarter-finals follow on Thursday. Then the semi-finals begin. The final will decide the champion on Sunday.

If Italy win today, they face Czechia on Friday. That would be a blockbuster semi-final. If South Korea stuns Italy, it would become one of the biggest upsets in Davis Cup history.
