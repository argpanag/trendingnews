Milan vs Atalanta, probable line-ups, kick-off time and how to watch in the UK

Ruben Amorim is expected to recall Luka Modric and Goncalo Ramos when Milan host Maurizio Sarri’s Atalanta on Sunday. According to Sky Sport Italia, both are in line to start.

Milan seek a response after a 1-1 draw at Sassuolo last weekend. They beat RB Salzburg 4-0 in the Europa League on Thursday, with 19-year-old Alphadjo Cisse scoring a hat-trick.

OneFootball Videos

La Dea beat Venezia 3-1 in Serie A last weekend, then Pafos 3-1 in the Conference League on Thursday. They have nine points from six games and sit 10th.

Milan are expected to switch to a back four, with Mario Gila at right-back and either Diego Moreira or Pervis Estupinan on the left. Cisse may miss out, with Christian Pulisic in contention.

Atalanta are without Franck Kessie and Giacomo Raspadori, so Ederson and Mario Pasalic are expected in midfield. Sarri is likely to use Nikola Zalewski, Charles De Ketelaere and Jonathan Rowe behind Nikola Krstovic.

Kick-off is 17.00 BST. Probable line-ups, Milan 4-3-3, Maignan, Gila, De Winter, Pavlovic, Moreira, Musa, Modric, Rabiot, Chukwueze, Ramos, Cisse. Atalanta 4-2-3-1, Carnesecchi, Zappacosta, Kristensen, Kolasinac, Bernasconi, Pasalic, Ederson, Zalewski, De Ketelaere, Rowe, Krstovic.

In the UK it is live on TNT Sports and online via HBO Max and DAZN. In the USA it is on Paramount+.
