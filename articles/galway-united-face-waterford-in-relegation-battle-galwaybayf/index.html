Galway United Face Waterford in Relegation Battle | GalwayBayFM

TheTools · 19.10.2026 19:12 · source · permalink

Galway United are away to Waterford tonight in the League of Ireland Men's Premier Division, with John Caulfield's side currently sitting seventh in the table, seven points ahead of the Southerners who are ninth. Should Galway avoid defeat tonight they will mathematically secure Premier Division football again for next season. Galway come into the game after their first win over Shamrock Rovers in 20 years on Friday, with Jimmy Keohane and Aaron Bolger both scoring in a 2-0 win.

Waterford, despite currently sitting ninth in the relegation play-off spot, have had some impressive results recently, including a 2-0 win over Bohemians in their FAI cup semi-final. The Dublin outfit got the better of them in the league on Friday though winning 2-0 in Dalymount Park. John Caulfield's men are unbeaten against Waterford this season, drawing twice and winning 4-3 in the early weeks of the season. United have had a mixed bag of results down in the RSC in recent years. They failed to win either game last season, drawing 1-1 and losing 1-0, but they did get good joy in the two seasons beforehand winning two and drawing two.

This is the definition of a relegation six-pointer, with just seven points separating Galway, Waterford and Drogheda in the table. Drogheda are currently four points behind United and three ahead of Waterford, although they have played a game more. Galway will host third place St. Pats next Friday in their final home game of the season before travelling to Drogheda on the final day. Waterford meanwhile, will host Drogheda next Friday and then face Shelbourne in Tolka Park in their final game.

Galway have their destiny in their own hands, and John Caulfield will want to get the job done tonight with St. Pats, who are still in the title race, coming to Eamonn Deacy Park next week. While a last day trip to a hostile Sullivan and Lambe Park in a potential shoot-out for safety would be a far from ideal situation. Kick off tonight in the RSC is 7:45pm, with the game live on the Galway Bay FM website and live on FM from 9:00pm.
