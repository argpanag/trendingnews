Here's how many prizes could be won in Canada's massive Lotto Max draw | insauga

TheTools · 02.10.2026 17:11 · source · permalink

By Ryan Rocca

Published October 2, 2026 at 1:11 pm

Lottery players in Canada have a massive draw to look forward to Friday night.

The Lotto Max jackpot hasn’t been won for several weeks and now stands at an estimated $70 million, according to the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation.

In addition, there will be an estimated 10 $1 million maxmillion prizes that could be won.

And the massive $70 million jackpot means that there are also 70 $100,000 maxplus prizes up for grabs.

Overall, between those three categories, there are 81 major prizes that could be won Friday night.

In the most recent Lotto Max draw on Tuesday, as mentioned, the jackpot wasn’t won but there were still some big winners.

Two tickets matched six out of seven numbers plus the bonus in the main draw to win the second-place prize of $111,209.90.

And eight of the 65 $100,000 maxplus prizes were won by one ticket each.

In Ontario specifically, the OLG said a ticket sold in Richmond Hill was one of the second-place prize winners.

And three of the maxplus prizes were won in Ontario: one went to a ticket sold in Brantford, another in Elgin County, and the third in Simcoe County.

There was also a ticket sold in Brampton that won $100,000 on Encore.

Lotto Max is a national lottery game with draws on Tuesdays and Fridays.

It costs $6 per play, which gives four lines of seven numbers ranging from one to 52.

In Ontario, tickets can be purchased until 10:30 p.m. on a draw night.

Since 2009, more than $10 billion has been won on Lotto Max in Ontario, including 125 jackpot wins and 1,004 maxmillion prizes.
