Chase goes for 98 yards, scores vs. Steelers

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:29 · source · permalink

    Chase goes for 98 yards, scores vs. Steelers

    Ja'Marr Chase

    CIN Wide Receiver #1
    Player Stats

    Ja’Marr Chase caught 9-of-12 targets for 98 yards and a touchdown in the Bengals’ Week 3 loss to the Steelers.

    Chase’s 12 targets led the Bengals in this one. The star receiver caught all three of his targets on the Bengals’ first drive of the game, with his last target resulting in a three-yard touchdown. Chase would come up with a big 21-yard reception on a fourth-and-two in the third quarter that helped set up Evan McPherson for an eventual field goal and came up with two catches on third downs to extend drives. Chase has now scored in back-to-back games and has a dominant 16-173-3 line on 21 targets over that span. As usual, he’ll be a must-start in Week 4 against the Jaguars in a game that could have plenty of scoring.
    - Zachary Krueger
