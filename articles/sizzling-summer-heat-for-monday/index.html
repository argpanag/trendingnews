Sizzling summer heat for Monday

TheTools · 13.09.2026 12:26 · source · permalink

KANSAS CITY, Mo. (KCTV) - The 90s and near triple digits heat index values return Monday. Near daily rain & storm chances take place through the week with a First Warn in place Tuesday.

Tonight, partly cloudy skies remain with temperatures falling to the mid 60s. A few spotty showers will develop I-70 and north after midnight.

Monday Morning: Spotty Showers

We start off Monday with mild temperatures in the mid 60s but we do have a spotty rain & rumble chance. A 30% chance is on the table until around 8 AM for I-70 and north. Severe weather is not a concern, but some heavy downpours, lightning, and thunder are possible as kids are making their way to the bus stop. You’ll want to have the rain gear handy for the commute to work or for the kids at the bus stop. A quick heavy downpour or storm will be possible and may drop up to around 0.25″ of rain. We quickly dry out after the morning as a warm front moves through, resulting in a steamy afternoon.

Monday Afternoon: Hot, Steamy & Breezy

The summer heat is back with humid conditions. Sunshine takes over with a stout warm breeze between 10-15 mph, gusting to 30 mph. High temperatures reach 90 degrees, but heat index values will be near 100 degrees. It will be HOT for Chief’s tailgating and will be the hottest home opener in about 10 years.

By kickoff at 7:15, temperatures will return to the upper 80s, then cool into the low 80s through the rest of the game.

Monday Night -> Tuesday Morning

Rain & storm activity is possible along our northwestern border starting around 10 PM through Tuesday morning. A few storms may be strong to severe and would pose all threat - strong winds, hail and an isolated tornado. A level 1/5 severe risk is in place for our northwestern counties.

First Warn Tuesday

A 60% chance for rain & storm activity is in place for Tuesday as a cold front enters into the region. We have a level 1/5 severe risk for wind and hail highlighted across the area and a heightened flood risk. The best chance for rain & storms in the metro will likely be in the late afternoon and into the overnight, possibly spilling into Wednesday morning. High temperatures will be cooler with highs around the low to mid 80s with fairly cloudy conditions.

MID WEEK

We are watching a very messy pattern returning midweek. Wednesday and Thursday all hold rain chances as a front push south and stalls right over our area. This means continual rain chances and a growing flood threat each day, especially for northern Missouri. By next weekend, 3 to 5 inches are expected to fall across northern Missouri and 1 to 3 inches closer to the metro and points south. High temperatures remain seasonal in the low to mid 80s.
