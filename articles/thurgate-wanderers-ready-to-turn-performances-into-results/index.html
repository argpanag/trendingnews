Thurgate: Wanderers ready to turn performances into results

TheTools · 19.11.2026 01:23 · source · permalink

Home News

Western Sydney Wanderers FC

19 November, 2026

Western Sydney Wanderers midfielder Angus Thurgate believes the Red & Black are ready to make their return to A-League Men action this Friday night, with the squad determined to turn promising performances into results when they face Adelaide United at CommBank Stadium.

After an extended break between fixtures, the Wanderers have spent the past fortnight sharpening their preparations on the training pitch, with Thurgate confident the additional time has allowed the squad to continue developing under head coach Ufuk Talay.

Speaking to the media ahead of Friday night’s double header at Wanderland, Thurgate said the group was eager to return to competitive action and build on the foundations established in the opening weeks of the campaign.

“It feels good. We’ve had two good weeks on the training pitch, but the boys are ready to get back out there,” said Thurgate.

“Bit of an awkward timing of the break, but we’ve used it to keep honing in on what we’ve been working on.

“We’re ready to go.”

While the Wanderers entered the break following a disappointing result, Thurgate believes there have been plenty of encouraging signs in the team’s performances, with the midfielder confident the results will follow if the group continues to trust its approach.

The Red & Black have shown glimpses of their attacking potential and growing cohesion in the early stages of the season, with Thurgate emphasising the importance of maintaining consistency and continuing to improve.

“Obviously there’s stuff to improve on all the time, but we’re looking forward to getting back out there and getting a result,” he said.

“I think for the most part we’ve been performing well. We’ve been a bit unlucky in a couple of decisions, 50-50s haven’t gone our way.

“As long as we keep performing the way we have been and putting ourselves out there to get the result, I think we’ll be in good stead.”

With Adelaide United arriving at CommBank Stadium on Friday night, the Wanderers have used the extended preparation period to analyse their previous performances and fine-tune their approach for the encounter.

For Thurgate, the focus remains on taking the lessons from each fixture and ensuring the squad continues to progress as the season unfolds.

“We review every game and go into each week trying to improve on the week before,” he said.

“We’ve definitely done that, like we do every game, and just implementing what we’re going to do against Adelaide.

“We’ve had two weeks to do that, so we’ll be ready to go for Friday night.”

With the Red & Black returning to Wanderland for the first time following the break, Friday night presents another opportunity for the Wanderers to build momentum in front of their home crowd.

The Wanderers face Adelaide United this Friday 20 November at CommBank Stadium, with kick-off at 7:40pm AEDT, following the A-League Women’s Sister Cities Cup clash against Wellington Phoenix at 4:50pm.
