Rain this afternoon; chance of early snow forecasted for Thanksgiving

Rain is expected to start this afternoon and continue overnight dropping as much as two inches in the Hartford area, according to the National Weather Service.

A Special Weather Statement fog advisory from the U.S. National Weather Service was lifted at 9 a.m. due to a “locally dense fog” with “visibilities as low as one-quarter mile in spots” on Sunday morning.

The advisory called for “slow but gradual improvement to visibilities are expected through 10 a.m. Sunday morning,” according to the National Weather Service.

The National Weather Service calls for a chance of rain after 3 p.m. Sunday for the Hartford area. Sunday is forecasted to be cloudy with a high near 63 degrees.

During the afternoon, the chance of participation is 40% with “new precipitation amounts of less than a tenth of an inch possible.” However, tonight the Hartford area is expected as much as one to ttwo inches of rain, which will be heavy at times and will have winds gust as high as 20 MPH, the weather service said.

Mostly cloudy conditions are expected on Monday then becoming sunny with a high of 66 degrees. Tuesday and Wednesday are expected to be dry but cooler with highs in the 40’s and lows in the 20’s, according to the National Weather Service.

The National Weather Service is calling for “a chance of snow before 8 a.m. and then rain” on Thanksgiving morning.

In all, the National Weather Service is calling for an 80% chance of precipitation with northeast winds of 13 to 21 MPH and a peak forecasted gust of 37 MPH. Rain and breezy conditions are forecasted to continue Thanksgiving night into Friday.
