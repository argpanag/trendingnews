Country diary: Thrilling views of Iceland’s humpbacks, (perhaps) lured in by Céline Dion

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

For the first half-hour, Eyjafjörður, Iceland’s longest fjord, offered few signs of marine life – just three cormorants and a raft of common eiders. Then, suddenly, there it was. A distant spout of water vapour. A humpback whale.

As we motored out, more bushy plumes appeared to port and starboard, until we found the boat surrounded by about 15 individuals. Cutting the engine, the captain announced that he was about to employ a secret technique to coax the whales closer. There were raised eyebrows as the opening notes of Céline Dion’s My Heart Will Go On crackled over the speakers. But, as if on cue, two explosive exhalations erupted alongside the boat, prompting whoops of delight.
