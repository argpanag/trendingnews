Ted Lasso Returning For Season 5? Everything We Know About Renewal, Cast And Jason Sudeikis’ Plans

TheTools · 07.10.2026 12:43 · source · permalink

Spoiler alert: This story contains spoilers for the Ted Lasso Season 4 finale.

Ted Lasso Season 4 has officially come to an end, but fans may not have to say goodbye to AFC Richmond just yet. The 10-episode fourth season brought Jason Sudeikis’ Ted Lasso back to England, this time with a new challenge: coaching Richmond’s women’s football team.

And while Season 4 gave several characters emotional new beginnings, it also left enough story open for another chapter. So, is Ted Lasso returning for Season 5?

Here’s everything we know about a potential fifth season, including its renewal status, possible cast and Jason Sudeikis’ plans for the future of the show.

Will Ted Lasso Return For Season 5?

As of the Season 4 finale, Apple TV has not officially renewed Ted Lasso for Season 5. That means there is currently no confirmed release date, production start date or official episode count for another season. However, that doesn’t necessarily mean Season 4 is the end. The finale was presented as a season finale rather than a series finale, and the new season leaves several characters with storylines that could easily continue.

Jason Sudeikis previously indicated that he had another three-season arc in mind for this new phase of Ted Lasso, which began with the introduction of Richmond’s women’s team.

What Did Jason Sudeikis Say About Season 5?

Sudeikis has been clear that bringing Ted Lasso back for Season 4 was not simply about extending the original story. The first three seasons largely followed Ted and AFC Richmond’s men’s team before Ted returned to Kansas to be with his son. Season 4 instead created a new chapter around the women’s team.

Sudeikis described this new story as having a three-season arc, meaning there could potentially be much more to explore after Season 4. That could make Season 5 less of a traditional continuation and more of the second chapter of the larger story.

What Could Ted Lasso Season 5 Be About?

Ted has now stepped away from his role as head coach, while Alice Chilton has earned a major promotion. That immediately creates an interesting question about Ted’s future with Richmond. Would he remain involved with the women’s team? Could Alice become the team’s new central coach? And what happens to the relationship between the two?

Meanwhile, Roy Kent is heading to Chelsea, opening up an entirely new football world for Brett Goldstein’s character. Rebecca also has a new chapter ahead after accepting Matthijs’ proposal. The women’s team itself could be the biggest reason for another season.

Season 4 introduced players and coaches who were still finding their place within the Richmond universe. A fifth season could give characters such as Gemma, Boots, Lizzie and Alice much more room to develop. Tanya Reynolds and Abbie Hern have also already spoken about what they would like to explore if Season 5 happens. Reynolds suggested that an Arsenal-era flashback involving Alice and Gemma could be fun.

Who Could Return For Season 5?

Nothing has been confirmed because Apple has not ordered another season.

But if Season 5 does happen, the most likely returning names would include:

  • Jason Sudeikis as Ted Lasso

  • Hannah Waddingham as Rebecca Welton

  • Brett Goldstein as Roy Kent

  • Juno Temple as Keeley Jones

  • Brendan Hunt as Coach Beard

  • Jeremy Swift as Higgins

  • Tanya Reynolds as Alice Chilton

  • Abbie Hern as Gemma Jay

Season 4 also introduced several new members of the Lady Greyhounds, giving the potential fifth season a ready-made ensemble.

Apple’s official Season 4 announcement confirmed the return of Waddingham, Temple, Goldstein, Hunt and Swift, alongside Reynolds, Hern and the other new additions.

When Could Ted Lasso Season 5 Release?

There is no Season 5 release date yet. Even if Apple announces a renewal soon, fans should not expect new episodes immediately. The gap between Season 3 and Season 4 was nearly three years, although the longer break also involved the decision to revisit the series and develop its new storyline. Current reporting suggests that a potential fifth season could arrive in 2027 or 2028, depending on renewal, writing and production.

Is Season 4 The Final Season Of Ted Lasso?

Not officially. That is the most important distinction. Apple has not confirmed Season 5, but it also has not announced that Season 4 is the definitive end of the series. And Sudeikis’ comments about a three-season arc for the new era make the possibility of more episodes particularly interesting.
