Exit polls 2026 midterm elections US: When to expect results in Georgia, Florida, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Texas and other states

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

    Americans voted in midterm elections that determined control of Congress for the next two years. All 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats, and 36 governorships were contested. Exit polls indicated potential challenges for the Republican Party based on pre-election surveys. Polling occurred through various methods, with interviews conducted both before and on Election Day. Results from exit polls began releasing shortly after polling stations closed across different states.

    Most opinion polls suggest the results could be strongly unfavourable for the Republican Party
