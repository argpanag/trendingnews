‘PVL choking on his pickle’: Kylie Minogue leaves MCG spinning around in epic pre-Grand Final showcase

TheTools · 26.09.2026 07:21 · source · permalink

After four straight years of international headline acts, one of Australia’s greatest musical exports headlined the Grand Final pre-game entertainment as Kylie Minogue rocked the MCG.

Fittingly for Australia’s pop princess the sun came out right when she started, after it had railed in Melbourne all morning.

A video package celebrating Minogue’s love of Melbourne was fittingly followed by her opener, Love at First Sight before In Your Eyes.

She mocked the idea of a later Grand Final start time with On a Night Like This, with Andrew Dillon making up All The Lovers of that idea - which has most footy fans Spinning Around.

Things slowed down in the middle with Confide in Me - and then especially with Slow - before G Flip joined her for Padam Padam.

The biggest cheer of the performance came before The Locomotion though she mixed her transport modes by flying, rather than taking the train, into the sky as Can’t Get You Out Of My Head capped off the show.

The performance was widely praised on social media.

Full Grand Final pre-game entertainment preview and guide

It’s only fitting that Melbourne’s pop princess, 58, is performing ahead of the game between the Brisbane Lions and Fremantle given her links to the former.

Minogue’s musical career was kickstarted when, aged 18, she performed at a Fitzroy Football Club benefit concert at Festival Hall where a music producer scouted her.

She was soon on her way to London where a remarkable 40-year run, consistently topping the charts at home and abroad, got underway.

A decade later Fitzroy merged with the Brisbane Bears to create the Brisbane Lions, and another three decades on, they’re seeking their second AFL premiership three-peat of the century.

WHICH SONGS WILL KYLIE MINOGUE PERFORM AT THE AFL GRAND FINAL?

Minogue is expected to perform a 20-minute medley featuring between eight and 10 of her biggest hits from across her almost 40-year music career.

Simply performing songs which reached No.1 on the Aussie charts would give Minogue a huge range of options, from 80s tracks like The Locomotion, I Should Be So Lucky and Got to Be Certain, to 90s hit Confide in Me, to 00s tunes such as Spinning Around, On a Night Like This, Can’t Get You Out of My Head, In Your Eyes, Slow and 2 Hearts.

That doesn’t even include bangers like Better the Devil You Know, Love at First Sight, All the Lovers and her most recent big hit, Padam Padam.

WHO WILL PERFORM ALONG WITH KYLIE MINOGUE AT THE GRAND FINAL?

Minogue will get the spotlight in her pre-game show with a huge group of back-up dancers and performers alongside her.

Also performing will be singer-songwriter G Flip (opening act for Minogue), musical actor Michael Paynter (national anthem) and the iconic Mike Brady (Up There Cazaly during the retiree motorcade).
