A mention of a 120Hz mode has been found in the files of the PS5 Pro version of GTA 6.

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:11 · source · permalink

Rockstar Games has revealed technical details about Grand Theft Auto VI's performance on current-generation consoles ahead of the game's scheduled release on November 19, 2026. In an interview with TheGamer, company representatives revealed that engineers benchmarked an experimental build of the game running at 60 frames per second on the PlayStation 5 Pro. However, to achieve this, the developers had to completely remove cars and most non-player characters from the streets. Ultimately, the team abandoned this compromise and locked the target frame rate at 30 frames per second for all consoles, including the PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, and Xbox Series X and S.

The main reason for the performance limitation was the CPU load required to simulate heavy traffic, physics, and crowd behavior in the state of Leonidas. Although the PlayStation 5 Pro features a significantly more powerful graphics chip than the base model, its CPU has similar architecture and performance. Instead of a frame rate boost, owners of Sony's top-end console will receive exclusive graphical enhancements, including sharper images thanks to the PSSR 2 upscaler and shadow ray tracing. The developers considered maintaining the density and vibrancy of Vice City's open world more important than image smoothness.

Simultaneously, data miners examined the metadata of GTA 6 preload files and discovered a mention of a 120Hz high refresh rate mode for PlayStation 5 Pro without VRR support. In console games, this configuration is often used to implement an intermediate 40fps mode, which delivers a frame rate of 25ms instead of 33,3ms at the standard 30fps. Rockstar Games itself has not yet commented on this discovery in the game files. This entry could be a remnant of a canceled 60fps build, or it could be preparation for a 40fps patch after launch.
