France turned the game against Belgium around in just 15 minutes on Monday night to win 4-1, thanks in no small part to Michael Olise at his very best.

Brought on in the 60th minute, the Bayern Munich winger provided an assist and scored two stunning goals to help Les Bleus make a huge statement in their final outing before the next international break in November.

Two more goals and another assist that earn Olise a place in France's history books. Yes, it's that simple.

Les Bleus' number 11 has become the fastest player in France's history to reach both ten goals AND ten assists since a certain... Raymond Kopa, back in... 1958! 29 caps for Olise, 28 caps for Olise.

What's more, with ten assists in 2026, the former Crystal Palace player becomes the first player in the national team's history to reach double figures for assists in a single calendar year.
