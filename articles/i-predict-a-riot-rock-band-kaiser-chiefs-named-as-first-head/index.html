'I Predict A Riot' - Rock band Kaiser Chiefs named as first headliner for 2027 Laois music festival - Laois Today

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:10 · source · permalink
  • Lifestyle
  • Entertainment

The Greenfields Festival has announced its first headline act for 2027, with iconic British indie rock band Kaiser Chiefs set to perform in Stradbally over the May Bank Holiday weekend.

The announcement comes as the band’s frontman Ricky Wilson is currently in the Australian jungle, appearing on the latest series of ITV’s I’m a Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here!

The Leeds band, best known for hits such as ‘I Predict a Riot’, ‘Ruby’, ‘Everyday I Love You Less and Less’ and ‘Oh My God’, will take to the stage at the Ballykilcavan Estate in what is a major announcement for the Laois festival.

Having emerged as one of the biggest bands of the British indie rock scene in the mid-2000s, Kaiser Chiefs have enjoyed more than two decades of success, winning multiple BRIT Awards and performing at some of the world’s biggest music festivals.

The Greenfields Festival will return to Stradbally from Friday, April 30, to Sunday, May 2, 2027, following its relocation from Athy to Laois earlier this year.

Having outgrown its previous home in Athy, the festival moved to the Ballykilcavan Estate, on lands belonging to David Walsh-Kemmis, who runs the award-winning Ballykilcavan Brewery alongside his wife Lisa.

The festival’s first edition in Laois featured a host of well-known acts, including The Human League, Dec Pierce’s Block Rockin’ Beats, Vengaboys, Aslan and The Fureys.

Organisers are now looking to build on that with the announcement of Kaiser Chiefs, with further major acts set to be revealed over the coming months.

Announcing the news on social media, festival organisers said: “We predict a riot at The Greenfields Festival 2027!

“We are absolutely buzzing to announce that the legendary KAISER CHIEFS will headline The Greenfields Festival!

“Get ready for an unforgettable night of iconic indie anthems, massive singalongs and incredible festival energy!”

The announcement adds another major international act to the growing list of artists set to perform in Stradbally, which is already home to Electric Picnic.

Early bird tickets for the 2027 Greenfields Festival are on sale now, with organisers promising that the Kaiser Chiefs announcement is just the beginning.

“And we’re only getting started… More HUGE announcements coming soon!” they added.
