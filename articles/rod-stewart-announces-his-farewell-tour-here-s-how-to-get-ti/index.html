Rod Stewart announces his farewell tour, here's how to get tickets

TheTools · 30.09.2026 00:17 · source · permalink
  • Rod Stewart has announced his final rock 'n' roll tour, "The Final Encore," which will begin in 2027.
  • The tour will include stops across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Europe.
  • Tickets are currently on sale for the North American dates, with prices starting at $98 for a show in Colorado.

Rod Stewart has announced "The Final Encore," his farewell tour kicking off in 2027.

In the announcement video on Instagram, the 81-year-old rock legend said he's not retiring, but this will be his last rock 'n' roll tour.

"Ladies and gentlemen, after much deliberation and sadness I have to announce, next year will be my last tour forever, ever," Stewart said in the video. "I want to thank you for everything you've given me and hopefully I've given you something in return."

For "The Final Encore," Stewart will make stops across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe. His U.S. dates include performances in Georgia and Florida in March, followed by two nights in Colorado in September 2027.

With an iconic career spanning more than six decades and hits including "Maggie May," "Do Ya Think I'm Sexy," "Sailing," "Forever Young," and many more, Stewart's farewell tour is set to be a major event for fans. Here's everything to know about how to buy tickets to Stewart's final rock 'n' roll tour.

How to buy Rod Stewart farewell tour tickets

As of publication, tickets are available for all of Stewart's shows in North America. The cheapest tickets currently available start at $98 at the Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colo. on Sept. 3. See his 2027 schedule below.
