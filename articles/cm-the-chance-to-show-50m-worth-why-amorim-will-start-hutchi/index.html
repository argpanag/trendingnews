CM: The chance to show €50m worth - why Amorim will start Hutchinson vs. Benfica

TheTools · 16.09.2026 10:30 · source · permalink

Omari Hutchinson will certainly get some playing time in the Europa League against Benfica, and could even start.

According to Calciomercato.com, the Milan head coach Ruben Amorim needs to resolve his final doubts regarding the starting line-up he will field against his old club, but is tempted by the idea of ​​fielding Hutchinson from the start.

Eighteen minutes were enough to pique the curiosity of the fans and to satisfy Amorim. Hutchinson had a bright cameo on his debut at the Stadio Olimpico against Gennaro Gattuso’s Lazio, and it could earn him his first start.

The former Arsenal and Chelsea man got involved well, had a couple of nice dribbles, a cross that caused panic and even a long-range missile from a free-kick right at the end that was clutched by Mandas.

Even in training, despite a rather shy and reserved nature, Hutchinson is giving off good vibes. His characteristics make him a player who could find an interesting path in Amorim’s project as he can play in the half-spaces, linking the midfield to the attack, with Goncalo Ramos having looked isolated.

The chance to show his value

Barring any changes, Hutchinson will make his debut as a starter, paired with Alphadjo Cisse operating behind Ramos. Amorim has confidence in him and has highlighted his excellent physical condition despite being the last signing.

The player also spoke at his unveiling about the move: “I simply think the manager wanted me. I think he spoke to the management, they were also very interested, and it was quite easy because I scored against Amorim in the game against Manchester United.

“I think he was really impressed with me and said he needed a player in that position who was left-footed; he said I fit his criteria, so it wasn’t too difficult. Since I arrived, he’s been helping me a lot, he talks to me often: he’s been really great with me.”

According to sources close to the club, Hutchinson had been listed in Milan’s scouting database for at least three years. Once his candidacy was put forward by Amorim to Cardinale, he quickly worked to close the deal with Nottingham Forest and his agent Giuliano Bertolucci.

The loan deal cost €3.5m (plus €500,000 in potential bonuses), while the option to buy (which is not mandatory) is close to €50m. Omari will have to prove his worth starting tonight against Benfica.
