Family of Wallabies great in mourning after son’s death

TheTools · 18.09.2026 06:15 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • Rugby
  • Rugby Union

September 18, 2026 - 6.15am

The eldest son of Rugby Australia boss and Wallabies great Phil Waugh has died.

Jack Waugh passed away suddenly at the age of 21 last week, with his family remembering him as a “fun-loving young man”.

Phil Waugh with his sons Jack (left) and Charlie (right). Instagram/@charliewaughhh

They expressed gratitude for the outpouring of support from friends, family and the wider rugby community in Australia.

“Words cannot express the profound sadness we feel at the loss of our beloved son, Jack,” the statement read.

“Jack was a fun-loving young man whose warmth and spirit touched all who knew him, and he will remain forever in our hearts.

“As we come to terms with the shock of his passing, we kindly ask that the privacy of his family and friends be respected during this time of grief. We are deeply grateful for the messages of love and support we have received.”

The Sydney Morning Herald reports his death was caused by an accidental drug overdose.

Jack’s younger brother, Charlie, posted an emotional tribute on social media.

“To my big brother. I love you more than words could ever explain,” he wrote.

“You helped me grow into the person I am today, always looked out for me and protected me when I needed it most. I’ll carry everything you taught me for the rest of my life. I know you’ll be looking over me from wherever you are, and I know you’ll always be rooting for me. I hope I can make you proud.

“Rest easy Jack, I love you, always and forever.”

Waugh, who played 79 games for the Wallabies, has two other children from a second marriage.
