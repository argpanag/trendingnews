Champions League: Fenerbahçe-AS Rome, which channel broadcasts the match?

TheTools · 10.09.2026 14:11 · source · permalink

This first day of the Champions League, which is played over three days, concludes Thursday evening with six matches, including that between Sparta Prague and Lens (at 9 p.m.). But before seeing the Sang et Or at work, a beautiful poster pits Fenerbahçe against AS Roma.

Qualified for the league phase after an electric play-off against OL, the Turkish club has had a mixed start to the season in the Süper Lig, with two victories and two defeats. La Louve sits at the top of Serie A alongside Inter Milan and Lazio, with three wins.

How to watch Fenerbahçe-AS Roma live and streaming?

The kickoff for this match between N’Golo Kanté’s Fenerbahçe and Manu Koné’s AS Roma will be given at 6:45 p.m. The meeting, which will take place in Istanbul, will be broadcast live on the Canal+ Foot channel, also accessible in streaming via the myCanal platform.
