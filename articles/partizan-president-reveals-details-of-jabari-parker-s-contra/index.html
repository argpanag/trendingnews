Partizan Belgrade have parted ways with Jabari Parker after reaching a contract termination agreement. Club president Ostoja Mijailovic confirmed the resolution, noting that Partizan managed to save around 50% of the forward's salary for the current season.

Partizan Mozzart Bet Belgrade finally finished their Jabari Parker experiment after a year and a half.

Brought in as a highly-anticipated signing last season, Parker became one of the biggest disappointments in Partizan history.

After playing subpar under Zeljko Obradovic, new coach Joan Penarroya, who worked with Parker in Barcelona, refused to integrate highly-paid forward into the squad.

Parker finished last season on loan with Joventut Badalona, but didn't play a single second for Partizan during this campaign.

After a long saga, Parker has finally agreed to a deal with Serbian powerhouse to terminate the existing contract.

Partizan's president Ostoja Mijailovic talked for Mozzart Sport about the details of termination:

"We managed to agree the termination of Parker's contract and solved a very serious problem for our club," Mijailovic started.

"Parker had a biggest contract in Partizan's history, so it was our responsibility to find the solution that will reduce our financial burden and help us form the team."

Mijailovic revealed how much money will Partizan save with Parker's release:

"His last season was fully paid, with the amount of $3.100.100 gross. For this season we agreed to pay approximately 50% of his contract. Total amount paid to Parker will be a bit below $5.000.000 gross for two years," Mijailovic claimed.

"That's a huge sum for our club and it's important for people to be aware of the exact information, but also about the fact that we saved 50% of the money that was supposed to go to him this season."
