Cat Deeley and Holly Willoughby prove there's no bad blood

TheTools · 07.11.2026 12:57 · source · permalink

Cat Deeley and Holly Willoughby were all smiles as they posed for photos together, three years on from all the This Morning hullabaloo.

The two women, This Morning presenters, present and past, were pictured sharing a warm embrace at the Harper's Bazaar Women of the Year Awards at Claridge's in London.

They appeared delighted to see one another, proving there's no bad blood between them.

Chock-full of successful women, the awards saw Holly don a black tuxedo minidress by L'Agence, sheer black tights and a high black heel.

Her heel was no match for tall Cat, however, who opted for a long Grecian-style white satin dress, which she paired with a gold belt.

Both women wore their blonde hair in loose waves and sported a bold red lip for the occasion.

Cat attended the event solo, while Holly arrived at Claridge's with her husband of 19 years, Dan Baldwin.

Holly, 45, stepped down from This Morning in October 2023 after 14 years at its helm; the following year, she was replaced by Cat, 50.

At the time, Cat shared Holly's 'really classy' act of support. The mum-of-two said: 'Holly sent me a beautiful bunch of flowers and a handwritten card.'

'It was a really classy move; it really touched me. At some stage, once the dust settles, I’m sure we will get together,' Cat continued.

'People always talk about women being pitted against other women, but actually it’s just not true.'
