Grand Theft Auto VI Shatters Entertainment Box Office Records with $3.79 Billion in Day One Retail Sales

Launches as both the Highest Grossing Entertainment Property and Highest Rated Game of All Time

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 20, 2026-- Rockstar Games®, a label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI day one retail sales* reached $3.79 billion, setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.

This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261120446139/en/

Rockstar Games®, a label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI day one retail sales* reached $3.79 billion, setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.

Achieving a near perfect score of 99 on Metacritic and resounding critical acclaim, Grand Theft Auto VI sold 32.5 million units pre-release, a new record across all platforms.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”

Grand Theft Auto VI is available at the PlayStation® Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store, and global retailers and storefronts.

*based on company estimates through end of day November 19th worldwide

Grand Theft Auto VI is rated M for mature by the ESRB. Further details can be found at www.rockstargames.com/VI.

About Rockstar Games
Founded by Sam Houser in 1998, Rockstar Games pioneered the open-world genre of video games with the launch of Grand Theft Auto III in 2001. The series’ most recent iteration, Grand Theft Auto V, has sold-in nearly 235 million units alone. Alongside the other blockbuster entries in the series, including Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, and Grand Theft Auto IV, Grand Theft Auto has become one of the biggest-selling and most critically acclaimed and influential properties in modern entertainment, selling nearly 480 million units. Rockstar Games’ blockbuster Western series, Red Dead Redemption, has sold-in nearly 118 million units worldwide to date, with its most recent release, Red Dead Redemption 2, breaking new ground in fidelity and immersion. The latest Rockstar Games release is Grand Theft Auto VI. Follow Rockstar Games on X, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Discord, WhatsApp, TikTok, and BiliBili.
