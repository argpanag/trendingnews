A Dream Out of Reach... Gen.G's 7th Attempt Ends as Runner-Up

They came closer than ever on their seventh attempt, but in the end, it remained just out of reach.

Gen.G Esports finished as runners-up after losing 1-3 to BLG in the finals of the 2026 LoL World Championship (Worlds), held on the 15th KST at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York. Though they reached the finals after seven attempts, they ultimately fell victim to BLG's championship narrative of knocking out LCK teams.

Gen.G had made it to the Worlds stage without missing a single year since 2020, but the outcome was always cold. Despite consistently being named among the heavy favorites, they repeatedly failed to cross the semifinal threshold. Each time, they suffered unexpected defeats at the hands of different teams—G2, EDG, DRX, BLG, T1, and KT—forced to swallow the bitter pill of elimination.

For Gen.G, the 2026 Worlds was a golden opportunity. Fitting for title contenders, they swept through the Swiss Stage with a 3-0 record, then shut out Lyon and IG in the quarterfinals and semifinals, respectively, reaching the finals for the first time in a very long while. However, victory once again eluded them. After winning the first game, they conceded three consecutive losses to BLG, settling for second place.

Gen.G mid laner 'Chovy' also tasted the bitterness of defeat once more in his eighth attempt. Although he crafted a storyline of eight consecutive Worlds appearances and his first-ever final, his book remained unfinished once again. He had to watch his counterpart 'knight,' who had walked a similar path, lift the Summoner's Cup to bring a happy ending to his story.

With this, Gen.G concluded their seventh attempt as their seventh failure. Though they were the most formidable force throughout the season, they were ultimately missing that final edge. As the saying goes, 'fall seven times, stand up eight'—leaving fans curious whether an eighth chance will come for Gen.G and what their next move will be.
