Every word from Arteta's post-Lille press conference

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:48 · source · permalink

We made it two wins from as many games in the league phase of the Champions League on Tuesday night, overcoming Lille with a 3-0 win at Emirates Stadium.

A seven-minute second-half flurry of goals from Jurrien Timber, Ebere Eze and Bukayo Saka sealed the three points in N5, stretching our run of league phase victories to 14, spanning three separate seasons.

Mikel Arteta addressed the media after the victory in north London, and you can read every word from his post-match press conference:

on Ken Friar OBE:

Before we start, I would like to say a few words about Ken Friar, our life president. It's a really sad day for everybody that loves Arsenal Football Club, anybody who loves football, and anybody who has come across him. I had the privilege, the honour, to meet him and to spend time with him, to learn from him. There are people at football clubs who have a role and fulfil it, and others who create an identity, values, and they have a vision to create that future for the football club. The legacy Ken is going to leave, to all of us, to our football club, to everybody that has been close to him, is going to be forever. I want to pass on all our love to his relatives, his family, so they know that the way we feel about him cannot be anything but extraordinary.

on the emphatic second-half performance acting as a tribute:

I’m sure he is very happy to see how the boys played, how we won. That victory is for him. But not only that, I know he feels proud of what we built and especially the group of players that we have.

on the substitutes impacting the game:

We had moments in the game - in the first half especially- in certain moments we arrived in very dangerous positions with a lot of advantage. Our technical quality wasn’t good enough to deliver moments of real threat, and especially to be able to sustain attacks so well in certain transitions. The moment we made a change in terms of positions at half-time, our technical level increased, and we attacked the spaces where we thought we could hurt them. It was a joy to watch, the three goals were unbelievable, the connections, the timings, the quality they delivered to score. Three different players, and more who participated in those goals, so overall, I’m very, very happy.

on having the triple change ready before the team scores in successive games:

I’m going to do that more now! I felt it was the right moment to do it with the way the game was panning out, the way the opponent was behaving as well, that we needed in those moments and spaces different kinds of players. But that’s credit to the starters, and as well the ones who come in to create those moments. It’s about momentum.

on scoring lots of goals after long passing moves:

First of all, it depends on how the opponents behave. If they behave in a different way, the sequences of passes will be very short and you won’t have those kinds of moments. It’s something we have been training a lot because it’s a phase that we face a lot, and we have players evolving and understanding better, the level of chemistry we generate is better, and we need that because we cannot rely on other aspects. When other aspects aren’t working, we need to make sure we have a variety of ways to win football matches.

on Hincapie and Zubimendi’s fitness:

Zubi is much, much better. Piero is improving, but it looks more significant - I think it will be a matter of weeks, but we don’t know exactly how many. We have to see with Zubi how he deals with the pain on the pitch and the feelings he will face.

on Myles moving back into the left-back role:

He always gets love from the coaches, and he deserves massive credit because changing position is not only just what you have to deliver tactically but also the physical element of that, and the kind of efforts you have to make. But I thought he was brilliant, the way he competed, the way he carried himself. He was very consistent except for one action in the game, and that’s what we need. Myles is a player with a huge impact on the team.

on Michael Olise being suspended for our match against Bayern Munich next week:

I think the beautiful challenge will be Nottingham Forest away, and we’ve experienced that over the last few years - we’ll make sure we’re ready for that one first.

on Saka contributing off the bench:

I’m very happy and welcome if that’s the case. He wasn’t feeling too good yesterday and today because he had a sort of illness; he wasn’t ready to start, but he was really keen to contribute to the team if it was needed. He came on, had a real impact, with real quality.

on winning 14 consecutive games in the Champions League group phase:

It shows the level of consistency that we have. We carry on playing the way we do, performing that way in the competition. I’m sure we can do that more, but we have to earn it because it’s big.

on Madueke’s final product:

I said when you talk about players being ‘clinical’, sometimes that means scoring five in every 100. It’s about generating volume, more actions and getting him into the good positions, because he will decide matches. He had a really big contribution a few days ago, today he had a contribution in the last final pass as well, and that’s it. We need to get players to that level to be decisive in every game if possible.

on his conversations with Saka about his mindset:

After the press conference, I went and he was on the massage table, and I said: ‘Bukayo, I just killed you in the press conference, good luck tomorrow!’ and he was laughing, he knows me. He knows we have these kinds of things and he takes it because he wants to be the best version of himself. In order to do that, you have to set big objectives and challenges and try to raise them, by working, being consistent, and he has the ability to do it.
