Sixers vs Pacers: NBA Cup Clincher Friday Night in Indiana

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:21 · source · permalink

With a win Friday night in Indiana, the 76ers (15-4 overall, 2-1 NBA Cup) will clinch East Group B and advance to to the Knockout Rounds of the Emirates NBA Cup for the first time. Tipoff against the Pacers (6-12, 1-2) is set for 7:30 p.m. EST at Gainbridge Fieldhouse. Both teams are opening a back-to-back set, with the Sixers playing in Houston and the Pacers in Chicago on Saturday night.

Philadelphia currently leads East Group B over Miami, also 2-1, because the head-to-head record in Group Play games is the first tiebreaker. The Sixers are eliminated with a loss Friday.

The Knockout Rounds of the Emirates NBA Cup feature eight teams: the six group winners and one wild card from each conference. The quarterfinals on Dec. 4-5 and semifinals on Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9 will be played at the home arena of the higher-seeded team.

The championship game will be played at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, the iconic home of Butler University.

The Sixers snapped the Miami Heat’s seven-game winning streak Wednesday with a 111-104 victory at Xfinity Mobile Arena. In the absence of Joel Embiid (illness), along with Jaylen Brown (hamstring) for the second half, Tyrese Maxey scored 30 points, and LeBron James posted his 126th career triple-double with a season-high 25 points, 10 rebounds and a season-high 12 assists. VJ Edgecombe added 18 points, six rebounds, five assists and two steals.

The Pacers have been off since Monday, when they earned a 125-120 victory over the Oklahoma City Thunder. Pascal Siakam and Kelly Oubre Jr. each scored 20 points. Indiana had lost four straight before the win, including a loss to the Sixers two weeks ago.

By The Numbers

2.5: The 15-4 Sixers have a 2.5 game lead over the Boston Celtics and New York Knicks, who are both 12-6.

3: The Sixers and Pacers are meeting for the third time this season, with the Sixers winning each of the first two matchups: 114-109 on Oct. 28 in Indiana and 131-124 on Nov. 15 in Philadelphia.

27: Tyrese Maxey has scored 27 points in both games against the Pacers this season, including  17 in the fourth quarter of the comeback victory in October.
