OHL Wolves trade 15 draft pics to acquire Timmins star Adams

TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:06 · source · permalink

As a result, the Sudbury-bound Adams was not in the lineup when the Rock opened their 2026-27 NOJHL regular season with a 4-1 win over the Storm in Iroquois Falls

Article content

Tanner Adams stay with the NOJHL’s Timmins Rock proved to be short lived, as the Sudbury Wolves swung a deal to acquire the third overall selection in the 2026 OHL Priority Selection from the Brampton Steelheads, on Friday.

Adams maintained all along he would not report to the Steelheads and when it became apparent Brampton was not going to trade his rights to the Wolves — his preferred destination — he signed with his hometown Rock.

That all changed on Friday, however, as the Wolves shipped 15 draft picks to Brampton in exchange for Adams.

As a result, the Sudbury-bound Adams was not in the lineup when the Rock opened their 2026-27 NOJHL regular season with a 4-1 win over the Storm in Iroquois Falls.

“We were ready for every situation that could have happened with him,” said Rock coach and general manager Sheldon Reasbeck.

“We would have welcomed him with open arms if he was staying, but we are happy he is going to have this opportunity.

“He is a great kid and he is going to be an absolutely great hockey player and we wish him all the best, for sure.”

The deal sending Adams from Brampton to Sudbury will surely stand as one of the biggest blockbuster trades for the Wolves or any OHL team this season.

Adams, a 16-year-old forward from Porcupine, who starred for The Hill Academy U16 ‘AAA’ squad this past season, made it clear before and after the OHL Priority Selection he wanted to play in Sudbury and not in Brampton.

But the Steelheads still drafted him with their first-round pick this past June, two selections before the Wolves selected another top-end talent in Kash Kwajah.

He’s expected to arrive in the Nickel City next week.

To acquire his rights, the Wolves shipped the following draft picks to the Steelheads:

• OTT 2nd Round Pick, 2027;

• BFD 3rd Round Pick, – 2027;

• PBO 5th Round Pick, 2027;

• SBY 10th Round Pick, 2027;

• WSR 2nd Round Pick, 2028;

• OSH 3rd Round Pick, 2028;

• FLNT 4th Round Pick, 2028;

• PBO 5th Round Pick, 2028;

• PBO 2nd Round Pick, 2029;

• OSH 3rd Round Pick, 2029;

• WSR 4th Round Pick, 2029;

• SBY 6th Round Pick, 2029;

• BFD 2nd Round Pick, 2030;

• SBY 5th Round Pick, 2030;

• KIT 6th Round Pick, 2030.

Brampton will also get the fifth-overall pick in 2027, as well as another pick at the end of the second round as compensation.
