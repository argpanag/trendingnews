U.S. Open quarterfinals — Latest

The quarterfinals of the 2026 U.S. Open conclude today at Flushing Meadows.

In the women’s action, Coco Gauff (4) saved two match points to beat Mirra Andreeva (5) in dramatic fashion on Arthur Ashe Stadium. She will now face Elena Rybakina (2), who defeated Zheng Qinwen in three sets and earned the world No. 1 spot.

On the men's side, Karen Khachanov defeated Alexander Blockx (28) in straight sets after the Belgian retired while trailing in the third set after dropping the first two frames. Khachanov now awaits the winner of today's finale between No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev and Botic van de Zandschulp.
