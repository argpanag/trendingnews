Telugu Films Choosing Bollywood | Telugu Films Choosing Bollywood

TheTools · 04.09.2026 05:30 · source · permalink

South Indian cinema has many talented and beautiful actresses. However, when it comes to big pan-India Telugu films, filmmakers often seem to prefer Bollywood heroines. This has once again become a talking point in the industry because of the latest reports about Alia Bhatt.

Alia Bhatt is already a popular name across India. She acted in the Telugu film RRR and received a good response from audiences. Now, reports suggest that she may become part of two major Telugu projects.

According to the latest reports, Alia Bhatt is being considered for an important role in the Kalki 2898 AD sequel. She is also reportedly in talks for NTR and Prashanth Neel’s upcoming film Dragon. If these reports are true, Alia could be seen in two of the biggest Telugu projects in the coming years.

The news has also started a discussion among movie lovers. Many people are asking why Telugu filmmakers are not giving more opportunities to South Indian heroines for such big projects.

In recent years, several Bollywood actresses have been selected for Telugu and pan-India films. Deepika Padukone was part of Kalki 2898 AD and had an important role in the movie. However, she is no longer part of the sequel, according to reports. There have also been discussions about actresses such as Kiara Advani being part of major South Indian projects.

At the same time, South cinema has many talented actresses who can easily handle big-budget and pan-India films. Actresses like Samantha Ruth Prabhu, Rashmika Mandanna, Sreeleela, Pooja Hegde, Sai Pallavi and many others have already proved their talent and popularity.

Of course, choosing an actress depends on the story, character and the director's vision. A Bollywood actress can also be the right choice for a particular role. But fans feel that South Indian actresses should also get more opportunities in major Telugu films.

Telugu cinema has grown into a strong pan-India industry. Because of this growth, audiences are now expecting filmmakers to explore talent from all parts of the country while also giving more importance to the talented actresses from the South.

The debate is not about North versus South. It is about giving the right opportunity to the right talent. Fans will now be interested to see whether Alia Bhatt really becomes part of these two big Telugu projects.
