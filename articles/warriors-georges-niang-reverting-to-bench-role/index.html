Warriors' Georges Niang: Reverting to bench role

TheTools · 03.11.2026 07:50 · source · permalink

Niang won't start Monday's game against the Suns, John Dickinson of 95.7 The Game San Francisco reports.

Niang drew the start in the front end of this back-to-back set, though Draymond Green and Stephen Curry are back in action after getting the night off Sunday. Niang has averaged 7.0 points and 3.2 rebounds in 18.6 minutes per game across five appearances off the bench so far this season.
