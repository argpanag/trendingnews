Gençlerbirliği vs Galatasaray: How and Where to Watch the Match LIVE

Gençlerbirliği will host Galatasaray this Saturday, October 17, for Matchday 8 of the 2026-27 Turkish Süper Lig. The match will take place at Eryaman Stadyumu in Ankara, kicking off at 9:00 AM ET / 6:00 AM PT, and will be available to watch LIVE on beIN SPORTS and beIN SPORTS CONNECT in the United States. The visitors enter the encounter at the top of the standings with 16 points, while Gençlerbirliği sits in 13th place with eight points after seven league matches.



How Gençlerbirliği Comes Into the Match

Gençlerbirliği returns to Süper Lig action looking to secure its first victory since August after a difficult run of results.

The Ankara-based club started the season with consecutive wins over Fenerbahçe (2-1) and Göztepe (1-0), but has since struggled to maintain that momentum.

In its most recent league appearance, Gençlerbirliği earned a dramatic 2-2 draw against Amedspor at home. Rafael Luís scored the first goal for the hosts, while Thalisson rescued a point with an equalizer in stoppage time.

Before that draw, the team had suffered three consecutive defeats against Trabzonspor, Kasımpaşa, and İstanbul Başakşehir.

With two wins, two draws, and three defeats from seven matches, Gençlerbirliği will look to make home advantage count against the league leaders.


How Galatasaray Comes Into the Match

Galatasaray travels to Ankara looking to strengthen its position at the top of the Süper Lig standings.

The reigning Turkish champions have collected 16 points from their first seven league matches, with five victories, one draw, and one defeat.

In their most recent domestic appearance, Okan Buruk's team defeated Kasımpaşa 3-1, thanks to goals from Abdülkerim Bardakcı, Barış Alper Yılmaz, and Mario Lemina.

However, Galatasaray enters Saturday's encounter after a disappointing 4-1 home defeat against Barcelona in the UEFA Champions League.

The visitors will also be without Lucas Torreira and Lesley Ugochukwu, who are suspended, although head coach Okan Buruk will return to the touchline after serving his own suspension.

With another European fixture against Lille approaching, Galatasaray will aim to regain momentum before returning to Champions League action.



Gençlerbirliği vs Galatasaray: A Crucial Match for Both Teams

Saturday's encounter brings together two teams with contrasting objectives in the early stages of the season.

Galatasaray is looking to protect its lead in the standings, while Gençlerbirliği hopes to climb the table after failing to win any of its last five league matches.

One of the key factors could be Galatasaray's pressing. The Istanbul club has recorded 61 possession recoveries in the attacking third this season, the second-highest total in the competition.

With both teams seeking an important result for different reasons, the match at Eryaman Stadyumu promises to be an intriguing test for the league leaders.
