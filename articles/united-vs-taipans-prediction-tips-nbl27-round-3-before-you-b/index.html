United vs Taipans Prediction & Tips: NBL27 Round 3 | Before You Bet

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:21 · source · permalink

October 2nd 2026, 9:15pm, By: Jake Smit

Second up in the Saturday double-header for NBL27 Round 3 we have Melbourne United playing host to the Cairns Taipans. After suffering a tough loss on the road to begin the round, this stacked United roster will be desperate to bounce back with a big win in this one. 

Check out our United vs Taipans preview below and head to our NBL tips page for tips for every match this season!

Melbourne United return to John Cain Arena on Saturday night seeking an immediate response after a chaotic 84-82 road loss to the Tasmania JackJumpers on Thursday. Jacob Chance's squad was dragged into an absolute war of attrition in Hobart, with the whistle dominating a stop-start contest that featured a combined 72 free throws and six players fouling out. Despite the frustrating result, United still found reliable offensive production from their frontcourt, as Josh Oduro dominated with 22 points and eight rebounds, while veteran Joe Ingles provided a steady hand with 18 points and five assists. Backing up just 48 hours after such a gruelling, physical battle will undoubtedly test their legs, but returning to their home floor provides a massive boost. 

 

The Cairns Taipans arrive in Victoria desperately searching for answers after a brutal road trip on Wednesday night. Adam Forde's men were utterly dismantled in Auckland, suffering a 98-75 blowout loss to the New Zealand Breakers that dropped their early season record to 1-3. The Taipans looked completely disjointed offensively, heavily relying on isolation basketball as their perimeter shooting completely abandoned them. Star local Jack McVeigh forced the issue all night to finish with 16 points, shooting a miserable 1-of-11 from beyond the arc, while Keanu Pinder was held to just six points and seven rebounds. Leaking 98 points to the Breakers highlights serious defensive communication issues, leaving Cairns looking incredibly vulnerable as they step into another hostile environment. 

 

Laying 8.5 points with a team coming off a draining two-day turnaround is usually a risky proposition, but the stark contrast in class makes it a very appealing spot. Cairns has looked entirely disconnected on both ends of the floor this week, and asking a struggling offence to suddenly click against a legitimate title contender is a massive stretch. Even with heavy legs from their Thursday night slugfest, United boasts entirely too much firepower through Oduro, Ingles, and Cole Anthony for the visitors to contain over four quarters. Expect Melbourne to lock in defensively and run away with a comfortable home victory to steady their campaign.
