Best Children’s Day 2026 Deals to Check Out

Family Fun Deals  

Mandai Wildlife Reserve 

Hooray! Kids enter for free to Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure (RWA) with every paying adult from S$44 and S$50 respectively, valid for 2 visits from 2 to 18 October 2026 when you book by 4 October 2026. 

Highlights at Singapore Zoo include animal feeding, an in-park tram ride around the safari, and animal presentations (shows). For more information on the presentation timings, click here. 

At RWA, look forward to exploring 2 immersive zones (Asian and African rainforests), up to 5 Adventure+ activities, ranger talks (various timings), and spotting incredible wildlife! 

Click here to purchase your tickets today!

Singapore Science Centre

Kids grow up fast; celebrate this Children’s Day with something they’ll remember at Singapore Science Centre. Create wonderful family memories at KidsSTOP, fun experiences at ONE Ocean, or sit back and relax at Omni-Theatre from 2 to 4 October 2026. 

Good things come in pairs – purchase a bundle ticket covering KidsSTOP and one selected Omni-Theatre show (subject to availability), with S$4 off. The bundle starts from S$14 (U.P. 18) per child to S$9 (U.P. S$14) per adult. Parents can also unlock 50% off ONE Ocean Child Ticket. Simply subscribe to their mailing list for an exclusive promo code. 

For more information, head over to their social media page here to stay updated!

Golden Village

Parents, you’re in for a movie date treat! If your child is 6 and below, they get to watch a movie for free with every standard adult ticket purchased! This promotion is applicable for walk-in purchases at all Golden Village cinemas, anytime before 2pm from Monday to Friday. Do note that it is only valid for G, PG, and PG13-rated movies.
