Racing Bulls prospect to drive Liam Lawson's car this weekend

TheTools · 28.10.2026 23:06 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/sport/2212100%2Fracing-bulls-prospect-to-drive-liam-lawson-s-car-this-weekend

New Zealand driver Liam Lawson will hand his Formula 1 car over to prospect Nikola Tsolov at the Mexican Grand Prix this weekend, but only for an hour.

Racing Bulls have confirmed that the 19-year-old Bulgarian will drive Lawson's VCARB 03 in the first free practice session of the Mexico City Grand Prix at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

It will be Tsolov's first appearance in an official Formula 1 session.

Tsolov is currently running second in the F2 championship and is in contention for one of the Racing Bulls seats in 2027 with the team yet to confirm their lineup for next year.

Tsolov joined the Red Bull Junior Team in 2025, following an impressive junior career that saw him win the Spanish F4 Championship in 2022 and finish runner-up in the FIA Formula 3 Championship in 2025.

"Following my test with the team at Imola in August, I'm excited to take this next step, build on that experience and learn more about the car and the current regulations. I'll focus on making the most of the session, working closely with the team and providing useful feedback," Tsolov said in a statement.

Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane said it was important for Tsolov to get some F1 experience.

"It reflects our commitment to nurturing emerging talent and giving our junior drivers valuable track time and the support they need to learn and develop. Having already worked with Nikola at Imola, we're looking forward to seeing him build on that experience in Mexico and to the feedback he can provide to help support our preparations for the weekend."

Permane said earlier this month that they expected to announce their 2027 driver lineup "in the coming weeks".

All Formula 1 teams are required to give reserve drivers the opportunity to drive in four GP practice sessions a year.

Lawson is ninth in the 2026 drivers' championship with rookie team-mate Arvid Lindblad 11th, while Racing Bulls is fifth in the constructors' championship.
