Prince William and Kate Middleton made a glamorous red-carpet appearance at the Digger premiere in London. Their environmental-themed outing also drew attention to William's upcoming Earthshot Prize ceremony in Mumbai.

The couple joined Tom Cruise and the film's cast at Leicester Square on September 22. Kate wore a purple floor-length gown, while William opted for a black velvet blazer for the star-studded Royal Film Performance.

Prince William & Kate Middleton's premiere appearance invites comparisons with Earthshot

The Royal Observer suggested the premiere could serve as a subtle lead-in to the couple's next major royal moment. The publication connected their appearance with William's upcoming trip to Mumbai for the sixth Earthshot Prize ceremony. Digger followed oil tycoon Digger Rockwell, played by Cruise, as he tried reversing an environmental catastrophe. The satirical film therefore placed William at an event closely linked to his environmental work.

The premiere also gave the Waleses plenty of Hollywood sparkle. William and Kate greeted Cruise and director Alejandro G. Iñárritu before meeting other guests. They also interacted with children and shook hands with David Beckham and his family.

Kate's purple gown and historic jewelry became major talking points. She wore the Queen Mother's amethyst sautoir necklace alongside earrings once owned by Princess Diana. The timing also stood out because Mumbai will host the Earthshot Prize on November 17. As per PEOPLE, Indian filmmaker Karan Johar will host the ceremony, while 15 finalists will compete across five environmental categories.

The Royal Observer noted that the Princess of Wales could potentially join William in India. However, her participation had not been officially confirmed in the information reviewed. The couple last visited India together in 2016. During that royal tour, they visited the Taj Mahal, met Bollywood personalities and paid respects at India Gate.

Their potential return would therefore mark another significant royal visit. This time, William would arrive with the Earthshot Prize at the center of his agenda. The premiere itself was not presented as an official Earthshot event. Still, its environmental storyline created an obvious thematic connection.
