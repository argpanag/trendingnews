Follow Germany v Netherlands live as League A Group 2 draws to a close in the Nations League

League A Group 2 remains wide open, and Germany and the Netherlands have everything to play for on the final night. Their match at Berlin's Olympiastadion could determine which two teams reach the quarter-finals and whether a team enters the relegation play-offs, depending on Greece's result.

Germany sit second, level on points with Greece but behind on head-to-head results. If they beat the Netherlands, they could even finish top, provided Greece do not beat bottom-placed Serbia. If the teams finish level on points, the head-to-head mini-league would condemn Greece, who lost 7-0 to the Netherlands in the return fixture.
