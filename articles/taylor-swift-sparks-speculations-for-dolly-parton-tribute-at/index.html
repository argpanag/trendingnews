Taylor Swift sparks speculations for Dolly Parton tribute at CMAs

TheTools · 12.11.2026 07:34 · source · permalink

Published November 12, 2026

Taylor Swift fans are over the moon in excitement for her new red carpet appearance at the Country Music Awards, and an even exciting news took over social media when rumours arose that the pop icon might be giving a performance.

The 36-year-old pop superstar was speculated to be one of the participants in the Dolly Parton tribute at the award ceremony on November 18.

The news shook Swifties as they began connecting the dots to Swift's own return to country music for the release of her Debut(Taylor's Version) album.

Fans flocked to social media and started leaving comments like, "Aww, this would be so sweet of Taylor! Let's hope this is true."

Another echoed, "Please be true," while a third chimed in, "I hope she sings Here you come again or Islands in the Stream.. they’re both so singer/songwriter centric and will sound beautiful if she plays them acoustic . Just her and her guitar."

Someone added, "It probably going to be a medley of Dolly's songs alongside other country artist. It's going to be so beautiful." 

However, it was later confirmed that the rumours were not true, as a source from CMAs confirmed that a tribute will be performed but Swift won't be a part of it.
