    Barkley (stinger) still sidelined in 2nd quarter

    Saquon Barkley

    PHI Running Back #26
    Player Stats

    Saquon Barkley (stinger) is questionable to return to the Eagles’ Week 2 game against the Titans.

    Barkley was injured on the Eagles’ opening drive and immediately announced as questionable. He returned to the Eagles’ sideline in relatively short order with his helmet and a neck roll, but he has remained sidelined into the second quarter. He has also since shed his helmet. It appears it could be Tank Bigsby closing this one out for the Eagles. The only good news is, a stinger would be unlikely to sideline Barkley for Week 3. Stay tuned.
    - Patrick Daugherty
