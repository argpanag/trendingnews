Late Late Show reveals this week's guests

TheTools · 12.11.2026 15:52 · source · permalink

Nicola Coughlan will be among Patrick Kielty's guests on Friday's Late Late Show on RTÉ One and RTÉ Player.

Announcing this week's line-up, the Late Late said: "One of Ireland's biggest acting stars will join Patrick Kielty in studio on this week's Late Late Show.

"Galway Girl, Derry Girl, Barbie Girl - the multi-talented Nicola Coughlan will be in the house. She'll discuss her new hard-hitting drama I Am Helen, what it was like hosting Saturday Night Live (SNL) UK, hanging out with her comedy heroes, and heading back to the world of Bridgerton.

"Comedian John Bishop will drop by to talk about his brand-new show Let's Go Round Again, which will come to Ireland in May of next year. He'll also chat about heading to Patagonia with Jason Byrne, why raising pigs comes with its challenges, and why astronauts make better motorcyclists than comedians.

"Comic Neil Delamere will be in studio as he prepares to head out on the road with his latest stand-up tour, Neil's on Wheels. He'll speak about the audience members he loves to chat to, the Irish urge to play fast and loose with the rules, and making friends after 40.

"Kilkenny GAA legend TJ Reid will chat to Patrick about his retirement from inter-county hurling and celebrate his remarkable career as the all-time leading scorer of the championship.

"Cork pop powerhouse Lyra will give a musical tribute to Shane MacGowan, as well as sharing some exciting personal news.

"As well as all of this, one child in Ireland will get some very exciting Late Late Toy Show news."

The Late Late Show, Fridays, RTÉ One and RTÉ Player, 9:35pm
