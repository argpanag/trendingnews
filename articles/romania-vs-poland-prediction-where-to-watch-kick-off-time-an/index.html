Romania vs. Poland - Head-to-head record

Poland have dominated the recent meetings between these two nations, winning each of their last three encounters.

Their most recent clash came in October, when the Poles recorded an emphatic 6-0 home victory in the Nations League. That result followed two convincing wins during qualification for the 2018 World Cup.

Poland won 3-0 away to Romania in November 2016 before securing a 3-1 home victory in June 2017. The visitors will therefore arrive in Bucharest with considerable confidence, although their recent struggles on the road could make this a more competitive encounter.

Romania – Tricolours Facing a Crisis Under Hagi

Romania have endured a disastrous Nations League campaign, losing all four of their opening matches and conceding 13 goals. With pressure mounting on head coach Gheorghe Hagi, let’s examine their recent performances and expected line-up.

Recent form

Romania began their Group B3 campaign with a 2-1 defeat away to Sweden before suffering a 4-2 home loss against Bosnia and Herzegovina. Their situation deteriorated further in October, when Poland thrashed them 6-0 before Sweden secured a 1-0 victory in Bucharest.

Those results leave the Tricolours bottom of the group without a point, having scored just three goals and conceded 13. Hagi, who returned as national team manager in April, has faced increasing criticism after failing to deliver the improvement expected following his appointment.

The situation has also been complicated by public disagreements between Hagi and several senior players. Captain Nicolae Stanciu and goalkeeper Ionut Radu have both disputed the manager’s claims about their availability, while questions have been raised over the selection of his son, Ianis Hagi.

Romania will be without Stanciu and Radu through injury, while Razvan Marin is suspended. Alexandru Cicaldau is an option in midfield after scoring against Bosnia, while Daniel Birligea is expected to lead the attack following two goals in his last three club appearances for Frosinone.

Poland - Struggling on the road

Poland have collected four points from their opening four Nations League matches, with their emphatic victory over Romania providing the only real highlight of the campaign. Let’s take a closer look at their recent form and expected line-up.

Recent form

Poland began their Nations League campaign with a goalless home draw against Bosnia and Herzegovina before suffering a 3-1 defeat away to Sweden. They responded with an impressive 6-0 victory over Romania, but their momentum was short-lived as they lost 1-0 to Bosnia in their following fixture.

Jan Urban’s side have struggled throughout 2026, recording just two victories this year. Their failure to qualify for the World Cup was a major disappointment, and the Polish Football Association is expected to assess Urban’s future after the final two Nations League matches.

Their away record is particularly concerning, with six defeats in their last nine matches on the road and just one clean sheet in their previous 10. However, Poland hold a four-point advantage over Romania, meaning a draw in Bucharest would be enough to guarantee their place in League B for the next edition.

Robert Lewandowski is expected to lead the attack, having scored eight goals against Romania throughout his international career. Przemyslaw Frankowski is unavailable due to injury, while Piotr Zielinski and Marcel Regula have recently struggled with colds but are expected to be included in the squad.
