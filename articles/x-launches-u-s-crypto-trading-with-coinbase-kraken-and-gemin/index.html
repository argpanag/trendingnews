X Launches U.S. Crypto Trading With Coinbase, Kraken and Gemini - Crypto Economy

TheTools · 16.09.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

Starting now, social network X provides direct access to U.S. exchanges, including Coinbase, Kraken, and Gemini, alongside Interactive Brokers and Moomoo, through the integration of its Cashtag Partner Program. Via this feature, users searching for or engaging with a digital asset or stock Cashtag can view real-time price charts, explore related posts, and tap the “Trade” button to be redirected to the partner platform to execute their orders.

This strategy drastically reduces friction between social conversation and market execution. The integration capitalizes on massive financial discussion traffic across X, turning social sentiment into immediate liquidity within the country’s top regulated exchanges. Following an earlier pilot phase that drove an estimated $1 billion in volume in just three days, the rollout reinforces X’s ambition to become a financial super-app.

The initial rollout is fully deployed across the U.S. ecosystem for both traditional equities and cryptocurrencies. Looking ahead, plans include the gradual onboarding of additional global financial intermediaries and expanding the Cashtag feature to international jurisdictions.
