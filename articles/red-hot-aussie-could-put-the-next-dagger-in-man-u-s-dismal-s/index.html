Red-hot Aussie could put the next dagger in Man U’s dismal season

  • Sport

  • Football

November 25, 2026 - 1.21am

Manchester United are on the hunt for their first win under Mauricio Pochettino but an Australian rising star wearing Sporting CP’s colours is out to spoil the party.

Nestory Irankunda has been lighting up the Portuguese top flight, and meets the Red Devils on the back of a two-goal performance.

Nestory Irankunda celebrates scoring a double for the first time with Sporting CP. Instagram/@nestory._

The 20-year-old fired in their 4-0 Portuguese Cup win over fourth-tier team Uniao da Serra last week, earning the player of the match honours when the full-time whistle sounded.

While Irankunda’s dance moves and backflip celebration had the fans up and about, his coach was clear that he was just scratching the surface.

“He’s a player with some very interesting qualities ... but I think he’s a player who needs to develop aspects of his game,” Sporting manager Paulo Fonseca said.

“He’s an incredibly strong player in one-on-one situations, but he needs to start finding other solutions rather than just relying on taking players on.”

Irankunda had a taste of what it is like to come up against the best of the best while representing Australia at the World Cup, but he could be given the chance to develop his skills against one of the biggest clubs in world football.

The likes of Bruno Fernandes, Harry Maguire and Kobbie Mainoo are set to feature with Manchester United in the Champions League on Thursday.

A dire run of results in the Premier League means all eyes will be on the English giants, with United desperate to find a way out of the relegation zone.

Pochettino has been brought in to help United do exactly that, but in his first game at the helm he witnessed the team crumble to a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool.

The Argentine manager also inherited the noise of Fernandes’ contract situation, with the United captain rejecting two offers to remain at Old Trafford next season.

Fernandes says he will restart contract talks with United when they “start winning games” as he still has hope they can win a trophy this season. The club is still alive in the Champions League, Carabao Cup and the FA Cup, while the Premier League is well out of reach.

Pochettino will first put focus into instilling confidence into his playing group and there’s a sense of mystery about the changes he might make to snap their run of dismal form.

Fernandes has worked under five managers since joining United in 2020. Getty Images

It is that mystery around the team that has Fonseca on high alert.

“Eight minutes, a red card. It’s impossible to make an analysis,” the Sporting manager said, discussing United’s loss to Liverpool.

“I tried to see Pochettino with the United States and what kind of solutions he used. There is some information, but it is very difficult to predict.

“They have a new head coach with such world-class players, it’s extremely unpredictable to see if they will have a good day or bad day.”

Sporting CP will host Manchester United on Thursday, with kick off scheduled at 7am AEDT.
