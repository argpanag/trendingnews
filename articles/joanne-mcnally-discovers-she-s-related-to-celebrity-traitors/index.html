Joanne McNally discovers she's related to Celebrity Traitors co-star

TheTools · 16.10.2026 10:54 · source · permalink

It seems Celebrity Traitors has taken a bit of a Who Do You Think You Are? turn for Joanne McNally.

The Dubliner, who is set to appear on Jimmy Fallon's The Tonight Show, has been battling it out on the BBC show as a Faithful, attempting to catch out the Traitors and forge friendships in the process.

Today's top videos

By all accounts, Joanne forged a fast friendship with one of her fellow contestants, and their chats led them to discover that they're actually related!

Joanne and mathematician and author Hannah Fry are cousins, related by marriage. Go figure!

Speaking after her banishment, Hannah revealed: 'I really like Joanne. We actually discovered that we’re cousins, by marriage. So her cousin is married to my cousin.

'We’re basically sisters. I really loved her so much. She described herself as how she came out of the womb as a show pony. That’s the perfect description of that woman.'

'She is phenomenal. She knows exactly who she is, and she is totally unapologetic about it,' Hannah continued. 'I cannot tell you how much I admire her. She’s phenomenal.'

We wonder if there'll be a McNally-Fry get-together during the festive period.

Elsewhere, Hannah lifted the lid on who she wants to win the show, and it's someone who betrayed her at the Round Table, no less.

'Maya [Jama] as well. I really want Maya to win. I think Maya is playing the most amazing game,' Hannah said of her fellow Traitor. 'When someone looks like that, I think that you expect her to have a bit of a chip on her shoulder in some way or another, because, good God, she’s one of the most beautiful women alive, but she’s also just so much fun.

'No airs and graces, totally delightful to be around. I really want her to win. Leigh-Anne [Pinnock] too, my God, that woman hasn’t got a single thing wrong. Is she actually psychic?

'She’s the most perceptive person I’ve ever come across in my entire life.'
