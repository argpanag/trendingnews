Justus Hollatz to miss Germany’s World Cup qualifiers with injury

TheTools · 26.11.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Germany will be without Bayern Munich point guard Justus Hollatz for its upcoming FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 qualifiers against Latvia and Poland due to a knee injury.

The German Basketball Federation (DBB) confirmed that the 25-year-old has not recovered sufficiently to join the national team. The federation did not specify the nature of the knee injury or provide a timetable for his return.

Hollatz had already missed Bayern’s EuroLeague game against Zalgiris Kaunas on November 17 with the injury. His return was still a possibility when the German club issued its preview of Tuesday’s game against Maccabi Tel Aviv.

The guard helped Germany win the 2023 World Cup and EuroBasket 2025. In his only appearance in the current qualifying campaign, Hollatz recorded seven points, three rebounds, six assists and three steals against Israel in November 2025.

Bayern teammates Andreas Obst and Oscar da Silva have joined the national team, along with head coach Alex Mumbru. Nicholas Tischler of EWE Baskets Oldenburg has been dropped from the squad.

Germany can secure its place at next year’s World Cup in Qatar with a win over Latvia in Riga on Friday. The defending champion shares a 7-1 record with Croatia and Poland at the top of Group K, while fourth-placed Latvia is 4-4.

Germany has averaged 93.6 points and 41.5 rebounds per game in the qualifiers, compared with Latvia’s 83.4 points and 34.0 rebounds.

The two nations met in the quarterfinals of the 2023 World Cup, when Germany edged Latvia 81-79 before going on to win its first title.

Following Friday’s game in Riga, Germany will host Poland in Frankfurt on Monday, November 30.
