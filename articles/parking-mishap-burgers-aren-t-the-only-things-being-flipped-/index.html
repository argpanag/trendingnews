'Parking mishap': Burgers aren't the only things being flipped at a New Lynn McDonald's

TheTools · 09.09.2026 13:38 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A car has flipped onto its roof in a narrow McDonald’s drive-thru in Auckland’s New Lynn.

Photos have circled social media of a small white car on its roof, wedged on an angle in the fast food giant’s drive-thru lane, below a carpark embankment.

Police told the Herald the single-vehicle crash was a parking mishap and no injuries were reported.

In one video multiple police could be seen at the scene, which is adjacent to Kelston Mall shopping complex.

McDonald’s Kelston said it could not comment.

Fire and Emergency New Zealand was alerted at 12.39pm and sent two crew from Laingholm and Helensville as well as an assistant commander to the scene.
