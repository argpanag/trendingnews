Dwight Powell's run with the Dallas Mavericks has come to an end.

Ahead of the 2026-27 season, the Mavericks have waived Powell, making the 35-year-old big man an unrestricted free agent.

“Dwight was the consummate professional throughout his 12 seasons with the Mavericks and embodies everything you want in someone representing your organization," said Mavericks president Masai Ujiri. "He’s a tireless worker, an incredible teammate and someone whose impact extends well beyond the basketball court and into the Dallas community.

"We are grateful for everything Dwight has contributed to this franchise. He will always be part of the Mavericks family, and Dallas will always be a home for him. We wish him nothing but the best in this next chapter.”

The Charlotte Bobcats selected Powell with the No. 45 pick in the 2014 NBA Draft. Charlotte then traded his draft rights to the Cleveland Cavaliers, who dealt him to the Boston Celtics before his rookie season.

Powell appeared in five games with the Celtics in 2014-15, totaling nine points in nine minutes.

The Celtics traded Powell along with Rajon Rondo to the Mavericks in December 2014. Boston received Brandan Wright, Jae Crowder, Jameer Nelson and draft picks in return.

Powell spent 12 seasons with the Mavericks. He ranks fifth in games played, trailing Dirk Nowitzki, Brad Davis, Derek Harper and Rolando Blackman.

Powell holds career averages of 6.5 points, 4.2 rebounds and 0.4 blocks per game while shooting 60.6 percent from the field.
