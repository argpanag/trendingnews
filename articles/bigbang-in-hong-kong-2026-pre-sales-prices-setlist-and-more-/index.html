BigBang in Hong Kong 2026: Pre-sales, prices, setlist, and more you need to know

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:26 · source · permalink

Prices, ticket releases, seating plan, and more

OG K-pop fans will have been eagerly awaiting news ever since it was announced that BigBang will be coming to Hong Kong in concert later this year. Since this year celebrates the second-generation K-pop music act’s 20th anniversary, read on for everything you need to know about their concert here.

When and where are BigBang’s Hong Kong concerts?

Hong Kong is getting three nights with G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung. They will be performing at the Kai Tak Stadium on November 13 to 15.

How much are tickets to see BigBang?

Prices for seated tickets run from $699 to $2,699 for VIP2 category entry, which includes a VIP basic set of exclusive merchandise, and access to an express shopping lane. The highest VIP1 ticket tier costs $3,099 and includes standing entry in the middle of the stage area, a VIP plus set of merch, and an express shopping lane.

When are BigBang tickets available on sale?

Visa Infinite and BOC Visa cardholders can access the special pre-sale on September 15, running until 7pm. From 8pm onwards, all Visa cardholders will also be granted access to the pre-sale.

The TME pre-sale then runs on September 16 from 11am to 3pm, followed by pre-sales for Trip.com and Klook from 5pm to 9pm. Finally, tickets for the general public will then open on September 17, from 12 noon.

What tips should I know before buying BigBang tickets?

HKTicketing will open the online waiting room 30 minutes before each sale stage begins, so log on early. Make sure you’ve already registered for a HKTicketing account so you don’t have to waste time creating an account later. Also note that each registered account can purchase a maximum of two tickets for each show date across all sale phases.
