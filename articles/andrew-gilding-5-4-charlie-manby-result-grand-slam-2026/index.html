  2. 4.03pm

    Gilding takes out 124 on the bull to set up Searle

    Andrew Gilding beat Charlie Manby 5-4 in Group F, taking out 124 on the bullseye — 20, treble 18, bull — in the decider, per Sky Sports. Sky Sports had him averaging just over 81.

    It was a topsy-turvy one. Gilding led 2-0 after Manby missed 11 darts at double, then Manby came from behind, breaking on double 10, holding double 4, and taking out a 146 of treble 19, treble 19, double 16 to lead 3-2. Gilding levelled and went 4-3 with an 83 on double 10, then wired tops for the match at 4-3 before Manby held on double 8.

    Manby is 0-2 and out after two 5-4 defeats, leaving an 8-10 leg record. Gilding and Ryan Searle both have two points and play on Monday — the winner goes through.
