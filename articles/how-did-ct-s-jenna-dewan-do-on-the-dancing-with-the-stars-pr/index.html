How did CT's Jenna Dewan do on the 'Dancing with the Stars' premiere?

TheTools · 17.09.2026 21:58 · source · permalink

The female celebrities made their ballroom debut on the second night of the two-part Season 35 premiere of "Dancing with the Stars" Wednesday, Sept. 16 – and one New England-born competitor made a strong first showing.

Just like the male contestants the night before, each competitor danced to a No. 1 song from the year they were born. In a lineup that included athletes, models and influencers, actress Jenna Dewan represented New England.

While Dewan spent much of her childhood in Texas, she was born in Hartford, Connecticut. Best known for her work in "Step Up," Dewan is an accomplished dancer, and she used her experience to her full advantage in her first performance.

Here's how the Connecticut-born actress scored on her first night on "Dancing with the Stars."

How did Jenna Dewan do during the 'Dancing with the Stars' premiere?

For her first dance, Jenna Dewan performed a tango to "Call Me" by Blondie with her partner Val Chmerkovskiy.

"You put the 'go' in tango," judge Derek Hough told Dewan after she danced. "So much power, so much energy."

The other two judges agreed on Dewan's strong performance quality, but reminded her to watch technique.

"You were dancing a solo at moments," Carrie Ann Inaba said.

Dewan ultimately scored a 19 out of 31, earning her second place on the leaderboard after everybody danced. Together, her score and fan votes secured her spot in the next week of competition.

Who scored the highest on night 2 of the 'Dancing with the Stars' premiere?

At the top of the leaderboard at the end of night two was reality TV star Maura Higgins and her partner Mark Ballas, who both starred in Season 4 of "The Traitors." Their foxtrot to "Black Velvet" earned a 21 out of 30, two points higher than Dewan's score.

Who was eliminated on night 2 of the 'Dancing with the Stars' premiere?

Model and reality TV personality Sarah Jane Nader was eliminated at the end of the two-part premiere. Nader was partnered with new pro Hailey Bills, the second same-gender pairing in show history.

How to watch 'Dancing with the Stars' Season 35

New episodes of "Dancing with the Stars" drop every Tuesday at 8 p.m. on ABC and Disney+, with next-day streaming available on Hulu.

How to vote for 'Dancing with the Stars'

Want to see Jenna Dewan go all the way? Anyone 18 years of age or older can vote for their favorite couple(s) by logging in to the voting website or texting the first name of the celebrity they wish to vote for to 21523.

Voting is only available during each live show, opening at 8 p.m. and closing shortly after the final dance. There is a limit of 10 votes per couple, per voting method, for a total of 20 votes per couple.
