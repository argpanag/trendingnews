Gospel singer, Toluwanisings is dead

TheTools · 14.10.2026 14:52 · source · permalink

Nigerian gospel singer, Toluwanisings is reportedly dead.

His death was announced by the Convener of the Coalition of Public Interests Lawyers and Advocates, COPA, Pelumi Olajengbesi on Wednesday.

Expressing concern over the death of young Nigerians, Olajengbesi, in a post on Instagram, pleaded for God’s mercy.

While noting that the death of Toluwanisings is not God’s plan, he wrote, “We say no to untimely death.”

Sharing the singer’s picture, Pelumi said, “My God! What a life! No, Lord! We plead for Your mercy upon Toluwanisings.

“I am deeply troubled by the deaths of young men and women. This is not God’s plan for us.”

Reacting to the sad news, comedian Woli Agba said they are still praying for a miracle.

He wrote on his page, “Please hold on. Please don’t conclude. We are trusting God. Please.”

Responding in the comments section, singer Boyebest wrote, “Yes yes yes. Yeah I have faith in God. I do. God you’ll do this one.”

This came about two hours after the rumour first surfaced on social media.

DAILY POST reports that the circumstances surrounding the singer’s death have not been revealed as of the time of this report.
