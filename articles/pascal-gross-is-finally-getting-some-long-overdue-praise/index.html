Pascal Gross is Finally Getting Some Long-Overdue Praise!

TheTools · 19.11.2026 18:33 · source · permalink

Brighton’s midfield maestro has been named the Premier League’s 17th-best player by Sky Sports, with Der Kaiser finally getting some deserved recognition beyond Sussex.

It has been almost a decade since Pascal Gross first arrived at Brighton & Hove Albion, and somehow we are still having conversations about how underrated he is.

Albion fans have known his quality for years. We have watched the turns, the deliveries and the passes that nobody else seems to see. None of this is particularly surprising to anyone who has spent much time watching him. But with the Seagulls sitting second after a blistering start to the season, it seems a few more people outside Sussex are finally paying attention.

Brighton’s recent win over Brentford took them into second, continuing the club’s best-ever start to a top-flight campaign.

Sky Sports’ reporters and journalists have ranked the Premier League’s 25 best players, placing Gross 17th.

Related stories

Now, on current form, there is a very strong argument that he should be higher. There is probably a conversation to be had about a few more Brighton players deserving a place, too. Still, as an overall list, being recognised among the best 25 players in the entire league, and coming in at 17th, is significant. It is good to see his name getting that attention.

I mean, this is the same player who was putting up creative numbers alongside prime Kevin De Bruyne. The quality has been there for a long time.

Gross has registered 10 goal involvements in 10 Premier League games this season. He also leads the league for chances created with 29, ahead of Alex Iwobi and Morgan Gibbs-White on 24 each.

Those are outstanding numbers by any standard. For Brighton supporters, they are another reminder of just how fortunate we have been to watch him.

He also recently equalled Mesut Ozil’s tally of 87 Premier League goal involvements, making him the joint-highest German in the competition’s history.

Just take a moment to appreciate that. Gross now sits alongside Ozil, a player whose creativity has him regarded as one of the greatest of his generation.

There will always be disagreements over these lists. Most Albion fans could probably make a fairly passionate case for moving him up a few places. But seeing Der Kaiser get some deserved recognition is something we can all enjoy.

We have known for years. Good to see everyone else catching up.
