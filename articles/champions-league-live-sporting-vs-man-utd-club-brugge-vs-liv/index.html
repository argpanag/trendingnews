  1. 'I'm in a good place' - Isakpublished at 22:15 GMT
    22:15 GMT

    FT: Club Brugge 0-3 Liverpool

    Liverpool

    Liverpool striker Alexander Isak, speaking to TNT Sports: "Perfect away win, I would say. The performance until we got the second one was never obvious, which way it would go. They put on a good game. We spoke about having to get the second goal.

    "They were playing man on man, so I was against the defender the whole game. Ali tried to kick the ball the whole time so I'm glad he didn't stop trying.

    "It's always difficult to say exactly where you are and how good you are but I can say I'm in a good place and the team is in a good place, most importantly. Hopefully, it gets better from here.

    "The attack in general is beginning the click. We have a lot of different combinations and whoever comes in, we have a dangerous attack. It feels good."

  3. 'Mistakes are part of football'published at 22:11 GMT
    22:11 GMT

    FT: Sporting 2-2 Man Utd

    Manchester United

    More from Man Utd captain Bruno Fernandes, speaking to TNT Sports: "Obviously, I think we started the game well. We gave a penalty away where Senne [Lammens] saved it. We gave one or two counters, but we were very good in recovery positions.

    "They got the goal, but we were still in control of the game. We didn't have much people in the last third. Second half we got the two goals. That was the moment we should push a bit more.

    "Mistakes are part of football and from that you get goals. Everyone has to take the blame for everything we do good and we do bad as well. We need to be together in the good and in the bad moments."
