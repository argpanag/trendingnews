  4. 'No option other than to stick with Lammens'published at 22:52 GMT 25 November
    22:52 GMT 25 November

    FT: Sporting 2-2 Man Utd

    Simon Stone
    BBC Sport Manchester United reporter in Lisbon on BBC Radio 5 Live

    We were talking about where Manchester United need to strengthen and they took a big decision in the summer because they brought Karl Darlow in, an experienced keeper but not really someone who has played a lot of football at really, really high level.

    Obviously he has been an international player for Wales and played plenty in the Premier League but he's not a recognised number one in the way that say Kepa would be at Arsenal.

    Then behind that it's Tom Heaton who has hardly played a game for three or four years so you've got really no option other than to stick with Senne Lammens who, even going back to the World Cup, has proved to be fallible.

    He could become a very good goalkeeper, however he's really only into his third season of senior high level football of which was in Belgium and then last season at Manchester United.

    I don't know what option Manchester United have got, at least until January, if they want to bring another goalkeeper in.

  5. 'We showed the way we want to play'published at 22:50 GMT 25 November
    22:50 GMT 25 November

    FT: Sporting 2-2 Man Utd

    Manchester United

    Man Utd boss Mauricio Pochettino told TNT Sports: "It depends which way you prefer [to analyse the game]. So proud of the players. It was a very tough game. I like the team for the 90 minutes, the personality, the character.

    "We showed the way we want to play. We've only been working together one week.

    "Always we need to concentrate more and focus more. Try to recover better. We start to fix. With a performance like today it's the best medicine to prepare the next game.

    "The priority is next one. We need to believe more and more and more. Second half we recovered a lot of balls in the opposition half. That's the way we want to play.

    "We need to go to Old Trafford and show the commitment and play the way we want to play today."

  10. 'It is a very good performance'published at 22:41 GMT 25 November
    22:41 GMT 25 November

    FT: Club Brugge 0-3 Liverpool

    Liverpool

    Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola, speaking to TNT Sports: "It was a very important game for us. It was a key moment to put us in a good position. We are guaranteed to be qualified for the knockouts but now we want top eight. It was a more difficult game than it looks now after the result.

    "It was difficult to expect, it would be a very open game, they would man-to-man mark. Big merit in keeping the clean sheet. Overall, it is a very good performance.

    On Alexander Isak's goal: "I think it's a team that is very brave, they press a lot, so leave you with one v one. Alex turns very well and the finish is amazing. We play forward and he scores a goal.

    On Florian Wirtz's goal: "They always risk when they are losing, even more so, we were having good transitions. We weren't finishing properly but when we found the right pass.

    On Victor Munoz's injury: "Probably definitely the worst part of the game. He was in pain; now he is feeling a bit better. Let's hope it is not something big."

  13. A timeline of events...published at 22:32 GMT 25 November
    22:32 GMT 25 November

    FT: Sporting 2-2 Man Utd

    • 30' - Senne Lammens saves Luis Suarez's penalty after Lisandro Martinez catches Geny Catamo.
    • 31' - Lammens takes out Maxi Araujo in the Manchester United box and Suarez converts the spot-kick.
    • 40' - The referee blows the whistle before Amad Diallo finds the back of the net. He gives a foul against Youri Tielemans, who receives a first yellow card. Replays show Tielemans won the ball and pulled out of the challenge.
    • 47' - Martinez wins a penalty that Bruno Fernandes scores.
    • 49' - Fernandes puts United in front as his strike takes a bobble off a divot.
    • 80' - Lammens spills a high ball and Suarez finds the target with an overhead kick to make it 2-2. The goal is cleared following a quick VAR check.
    • 82' - Kaique pushes the ball into his own net but the goal is disallowed for a foul on the Sporting goalkeeper by Patrick Dorgu after a VAR check.
    • 90+3' - Tielemans is sent off for a reckless challenge on Sergi Altimira and United finish the game with 10 men.
