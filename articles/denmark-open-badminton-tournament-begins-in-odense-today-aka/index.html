Denmark Open Badminton Tournament begins in Odense today

Denmark Open Badminton tournament kicks off in Odense today. Two-time Olympic medalist P V Sindhu will take on Olympic champion and world number one An Se-young of South Korea in the Women’s Singles round of 32 match while Tanvi Sharma will be up against Natsuki Nidaira of Japan today. Ashmita Chaliha, Devika Sihag and Unnati Hooda will play their pre quarterfinal matches tomorrow.

In Men’s Singles, Lakshya Sen and former world number one Kidambi Srikanth will be in action today in the round of 32 matches, while Ayush Shetty will play tomorrow. In Men’s Doubles, Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty will be seen in action tomorrow.
