Flamengo's Pedro and Samuel Lino Called Up to Brazil Squad

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Flamengo colours will fly high at the Seleção! Attackers Pedro and Samuel Lino have been named in Carlo Ancelotti's 26-man Brazil squad on Wednesday for the nation's upcoming fixtures. The Rubro-Negro duo features among the players selected for the first matches of Ancelotti's new era with the Amarelinha, following the World Cup.

The inclusion of Pedro and Samuel Lino reflects their strong form with Flamengo and underscores the Rio club's standing in Brazilian football. Ten domestic-based players have been called up by Ancelotti for the trio of friendlies.

Pedro and Samuel Lino are among eight forwards in the squad. Alongside Flamengo's pair, the attacking options include Endrick, Estêvão, João Pedro, Raphinha, Rayan and Vinicius Junior.

Brazil's Schedule

The Seleção will fulfil three commitments across this international window. The first challenge comes against Australia on September 25 at Queensland Country Bank Stadium in Townsville. Four days later, Brazil face Australia again, this time at Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane.

The delegation then travels to India. On October 3, Brazil take on the hosts at Salt Lake Stadium in Kolkata. It marks the first ever meeting between the two nations.

Full Fixture List:

Sep 25: Australia vs Brazil — Queensland Country Bank Stadium, Townsville
Sep 29: Australia vs Brazil — Suncorp Stadium, Brisbane
Oct 3: India vs Brazil — Salt Lake Stadium, Kolkata.
