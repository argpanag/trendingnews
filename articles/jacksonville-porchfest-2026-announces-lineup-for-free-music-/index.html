Jacksonville PorchFest 2026 announces lineup for free music festival in Historic Springfield

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:23 · source · permalink

Local News

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. – Jacksonville PorchFest returns to Historic Springfield on Nov. 7, featuring more than 20 musical acts, food trucks, craft beer, art vendors and more.

The free, family- and dog-friendly festival will take place from noon to 8 p.m. in the Southwest Quadrant of Historic Springfield. The event is presented by Pearl Square.

  • 1blackfrend
  • Cacique Jakar
  • Douglas Anderson Guitar Ensemble
  • Flag on Fire

  • Los Muertos
  • MoonStalker
  • Mr. Dinosaur
  • Nikayla and The phuNk
  • Penthouse
  • School of Rock Mandarin House Bands
  • Split Tone
  • Sun Lowly
  • The Kreepy Tikis
  • The Queens of Tulua
  • Willie Evans Jr.

Jacksonville PorchFest is free to attend. Information about VIP tickets, parking and the festival is available at jacksonvilleporchfest.org.

The event is organized by Springfield Preservation and Revitalization, or SPAR, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated since 1974 to improving the quality of life in the Springfield Historic District. Proceeds benefit arts and culture programming in Jacksonville’s Urban Core.

For sponsorship information, visit jacksonvilleporchfest.org/sponsor.
