Bayern Munich’s Michael Olise finished 9th in Ballon d’Or voting

TheTools · 26.10.2026 23:38 · source · permalink
  • Bayern Munich Records and Achievements

FC Bayern did very well.

Michael Olise has finished ninth in the Ballon d’Or voting, earning a place among the top 10 players in world football alongside some truly elite company. Not a bad achievement for a player who has continued to elevate his game since joining Bayern Munich!

Olise has been outstanding, combining creativity, technical brilliance, and an eye for goal to become one of Bayern Munich’s most influential attacking players. His ability to unlock defenses, create chances for teammates, and produce moments of individual magic has made him a nightmare for opposing defenders. Finishing ninth is recognition of just how far he has come, with plenty of room for even more growth.

And what makes the evening even sweeter for Bayern Munich supporters? Olise’s teammate, Harry Kane, took home the top prize, finishing first in the voting. Having two Bayern Munich stars featured so prominently is a testament to the quality within Vincent Kompany’s squad.

@iMiaSanMia compiled the ranking based on votes:

  1. Harry Kane
  2. Kylian Mbappé
  3. Lamine Yamal
  4. Rodri
  5. Lionel Messi
  6. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia
  7. Ousmane Dembélé
  8. Erling Haaland
  9. Michael Olise
  10. Fabián Ruiz

Kane gets the crown, Olise cracks the top 10, and Bayern Munich has plenty to celebrate!
