Have the PS5 and Xbox Console Shortages Been Exaggerated Ahead of the GTA 6 Launch?

TheTools · 18.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

Most models are in stock at MSRP right now.

We made it, friends. Grand Theft Auto 6 now available! If you don't have a current PS5 or Xbox Series X|S but the universal acclaim for the game (including our 10/10 review) has your interest piqued, you might be wondering what your options are to play the game.

Unfortunately, your options are somewhat limited because stock of some models has sold out or is running low. So which consoles are available to buy right now? To answer that question, I visited all the major online retailers now that GTA 6 is out in the wild. And if you're wondering how the game runs, check out how GTA 6 performs on all platforms.

If you want to pick up a new console, it's worth noting that the MSRP for every console has gone up in recent years. That's thanks in large part to the parts shortages caused by the rush to build AI data centers. But if you can swing it, it’s not hard to find a new game console at retail price – unless you want a PS5 Pro or Xbox Series X. Let’s take a look at where things stand with every current model of every game console.

The Cheapest Way to Play GTA 6

  • Refurb PS5 Digital Edition - $499 at PS Direct
  • Refurb Xbox Series S - $399.99 at Xbox Store

If you're interested in parting with the least amount of money to play GTA 6, your best bet is to buy a digital-only console. You can get a refurbed PS5 Digital Edition at PlayStation Direct for $499 (not bad, as the MSRP on a new model is $599). Or you can get a less powerful refurbished Xbox Series S from Microsoft for $399.99 (MSRP on a new model is $499).

So there you have it. The console stock situation has seemed pretty dire in recent months. Xbox head Asha Sharma recently said there’s more demand than Xbox consoles available, and Microsoft told Xbox owners to trade in their dusty consoles. Meanwhile, over on the other side of the aisle, Sony wants to buy your PS5 for $400, and anyone in the market for a PS5 Pro may have to shell out as much as $1,400 to a reseller to get one.

With all that said, if you’re considering picking a new game console this holiday season, you may want to do it sooner rather than later. It’s absolutely quite possible that Sony and Microsoft are simply looking ahead at the Black Friday sales rush when issuing their warnings about stock. It’s possible a bunch of GTA 6 fans will rush out to get a console when the game launches on November 19 (preloads start November 10, by the way).

And it’s looking like RAMageddon will only get worse in 2027, meaning we could see further console shortages or price increases in the near future. So grab a console if you need it, because they’re readily available at the time of this writing.
