LIVE BLOG: Race Day at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026

TheTools · 25.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Sunday, 25 October 2026

Follow our live blog for the latest Race Day weather updates, track conditions, and MotoGP™ schedule changes at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026.

Key Race Day Highlights

  • Statement & refund information: The Australian Grand Prix Corporation has thanked fans for their support following today's cancellation and confirmed it is working with ticketing partners to facilitate full refunds for Sunday ticketholders and partial refunds for multi-day ticketholders.
  • MotoGP™ Grand Prix cancelled: Limited visibility has resulted in the cancellation of the MotoGP™ Grand Prix race. Jack Miller will be joining Jet on the Main Stage. For your safety, do not enter the track at any time.
  • Cancelled sessions: The MotoGP™, Moto2™, and Moto3™ races, the Legends Lap, MotoGP™ Warm Up, and Australian Superbikes Race 3 have all been cancelled due to the weather and visibility delays.
  • Traffic, parking, and transport: Catch-a-Coach services will be extended to align with track activity and longer gate opening times. The circuit car park is full, so fans driving to the venue should proceed to Cowes for the Park & Ride service and allow extra time for delays.

The Australian Grand Prix Corporation has issued a statement following the cancellation of Sunday's on-track action due to poor visibility preventing the medical helicopter from operating safely, thanking fans, riders, teams, officials, volunteers, partners, and the Phillip Island community for their support and contribution to the event's legacy.

The Corporation also confirmed it is working with ticketing partners to facilitate full refunds for Sunday ticketholders and partial refunds for multi-day ticketholders.

Read the full statement from the Australian Grand Prix Corporation

Limited visibility has resulted in the cancellation of the MotoGP™ Grand Prix race.

A MotoGP™ Riders Farewell Lap will take place at 3:45pm, finishing on the Main Straight, followed by Jack Miller joining Jet on the Main Stage.

For your safety, do not enter the track at any time.

Catch-a-Coach services will be extended to align with track activity and longer gate opening times.

As we wait for visibility to improve, the new provisional pitlane opening time for the MotoGP™ Grand Prix at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026 is now 4:00pm local time, and the race start time is now 4:30pm local time.

Due to the delays caused by the poor visibility affecting the operation of the medical helicopter, the Moto3™ and the Moto2™ races have both been cancelled.

Due to the delays caused by the poor visibility affecting the medical helicopter, the decision has been taken to cancel the Moto3™ race at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026.

Further updates regarding the provisional schedule for the MotoGP™ Grand Prix will be provided at 14:00 local time, as previously stated.

The Legends Lap with Mick Doohan, Casey Stoner, Wayne Gardner, and Kevin Schwantz, scheduled for 1:00pm to 1:20pm, has been cancelled.

Poor visibility continues to affect the operation of the medical helicopter at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026. It is now confirmed there will be no track action before 3:00pm local time.

Once track action is able to commence, the MotoGP™ Grand Prix will take place first.

The next update will be issued at 2:00pm.

Riders will be heading to the Fan Forum stage today, with Marco Bezzecchi appearing from 11:50am to 12:00pm, Joel Kelso and Senna Agius from 12:00pm to 12:20pm, Kevin Schwantz from 12:20pm to 12:35pm, Casey Stoner from 12:35pm to 12:50pm, Wayne Gardner from 12:50pm to 1:05pm, and Mick Doohan from 1:05pm to 1:15pm.

Directions to Fan Forum Stage

Traffic Update: Circuit car park is now full. Please proceed to Cowes for Park & Ride and follow traffic directions. Expect delays throughout the day.

There is no imminent improvement in visibility forecast at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026, therefore track action will not commence before 1:00pm local time.

MotoGP™ Warm Up has been cancelled.

Another update will be published at 12:30pm local time.

The visibility issues affecting the operation of the medical helicopter at the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026 have not yet improved.

Therefore, track action will not take place at 11:00. Further updates will follow once confirmed.

The MotoGP™ Warm Up has been postponed. A new time has not yet been scheduled, so stay tuned here for further updates.

Australian Superbikes Race 3, scheduled for 9:00 am to 9:30 am, has been cancelled due to wet weather conditions trackside.

Welcome to a soggy Race Day at Phillip Island for the Red Bull Grand Prix of Australia 2026, where classic coastal conditions have delivered a wet morning trackside. Keep this live blog locked in throughout the day, as any track inspections, delays, or weather-related schedule updates will be posted right here the moment they come through.
