FORECAST: Weather changes later this weekend

TheTools · 18.11.2026 22:47 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Temperatures will slide back down into the lower and middle 30s on the late night. More sunshine in store for Thursday with highs around 70 during the afternoon.

WARMING TREND: Temperatures are expected to stay very mild right on into the weekend. More clouds will be around on Sunday with some isolated showers possible during the afternoon. Rain totals look to be pretty light overall.

COOLER THANKSGIVING WEEK: Temperatures are going to be a bit cooler next week with highs in the 50s. It may only top out in the upper 40s on Wednesday. Rain chances look pretty subdued for Wednesday and Thanksgiving. We may end up with a better chance for rain right after the holiday, but we will continue to bring you updates on the forecast.
