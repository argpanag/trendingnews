"198cm tall" world-class middle blocker Jehra Gwynesch, who shed tears after losing to South Korea at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics, smiled broadly as she reached the top of Europe.

As a result, Turkiye won its second consecutive championship after winning the European Championship for the first time in history at the 2023 tournament.

Gwynesh, who was selected as the best middle blocker of this tournament, played a great role. He also led his country to its first-ever victory in the 2023 tournament.

Gwynesh, which we are familiar with, is gaining huge popularity not only for its skills but also for its appearance.

Gwynesh had shed tears on the court after losing 2-3 to South Korea in the women's volleyball quarterfinals at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics. He was 20 years old at the time.

With the victory, Turkiye even secured the right to participate in the 2028 Los Angeles (LA) Olympics.

"Now I have the goals of the World Championship and the Olympics," he said, expressing confidence, "I think this team can achieve all of them."
