Japan's Tamahome Employees Allegedly Filed Fraudulent Fire Insurance Claims in About 340 Cases Worth ¥23 Million; Insurers Demand Further Investigation — BigGo Finance

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:05 · source · permalink

Employees of Japanese homebuilder Tamahome (1419.T) fraudulently claimed fire insurance payouts, interviews with multiple non-life insurance companies have revealed. Using tactics such as reusing photos from past incidents and submitting repair cost estimates higher than actual, about 340 cases totaling approximately ¥23 million (approximately $140,000) in fraud have been confirmed. Multiple employees at a branch in western Japan are believed to be involved, and insurers are demanding a thorough investigation.

According to people familiar with the matter, there were cases in which damage to homes that had not actually occurred was made to look as if an incident had taken place by reusing photos submitted in past insurance claims. Cases of inflating repair costs to claim excessive insurance payouts have also been confirmed.

Multiple non-life insurers detected suspicious claims earlier this year and asked Tamahome to investigate. The company reported the results of its internal investigation to the insurers, but the insurers judged that the scope of the investigation was limited and insufficient to clarify the full picture. They are preparing to demand additional investigation. Some believe the fraudulent claims may extend to at least several hundred cases.

In addition to its custom-built housing and renovation businesses, Tamahome also sells fire insurance for homes and other products as an agency. For insurers, selling insurance through agencies is an important channel, and fraud on the agency side could undermine the appropriateness of insurance payouts and the assumptions underlying premium calculations. Non-life insurers plan to proceed with an industry-wide investigation on the view that similar tactics may have been used at other housing companies and agencies.

Tamahome is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market and posted revenue of ¥197.7 billion (approximately $1.2 billion) in its consolidated financial results for the fiscal year ending May 2026. It was established in June 1998. The number and amount of fraudulent claims confirmed are based on the investigation to date and may grow with further investigation.
