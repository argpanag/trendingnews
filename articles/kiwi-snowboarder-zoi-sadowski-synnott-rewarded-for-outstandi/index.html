Kiwi snowboarder Zoi Sadowski-Synnott rewarded for outstanding year

TheTools · 10.10.2026 06:54 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._Q.VKhexyL5oal5uCV3AUQmI1EOdryHi3hh73RdibV20lY/sport/1826261%2Fkiwi-snowboarder-zoi-sadowski-synnott-rewarded-for-outstanding-year

New Zealand's snow sports stars have celebrated an outstanding year, with Zoi Sadowski-Synnott winning the top prize, at the annual Awards night in Wānaka.

The snowboarder was crowned the overall athlete of the year for winning two Olympic silver medals, two X Games silvers, and the Natural Selection Tour championship title in 2026.

But her competition for the honour was fierce, with Ben Richards also a contender for becoming the inaugural FIS Freeride World Champion, as was skier Alice Robinson, for a career-first world cup victory in Super G.

Other award winners on the night included another Olympic medallist at Milano-Cortina, Luca Harrington, who took out Freeskier of the Year.

Harrington is currently ranked number two in the world in Freeski Big Air. He won two X Games golds in 2026 to go with his bronze at the Olympics.

Richards and Robinson didn't go home empty handed though, with Richards winning Freeride Athlete of the Year, while Robinson won the Alpine Ski Racer of the Year Award.

Other award winners included Adam Hall, who won the Adaptive Athlete of the Year Award, for winning silver at the Paralympics, while Dane Menzies secured Breakthrough Athlete of the Year.

The annual event was held at the Lake Wānaka Centre.
