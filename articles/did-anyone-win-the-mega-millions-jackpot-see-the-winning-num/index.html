Did anyone win the Mega Millions jackpot? See the winning numbers

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:32 · source · permalink

The winning numbers have been drawn for the Mega Millions jackpot on Tuesday, Sept. 15 and there's yet again no winner. After no one matched all six numbers the jackpot now climbs to $244 million with a cash option of $103.5 million.

The prize has been climbing since a Florida player won $800 million on July 28, making it one of the largest lottery jackpots currently available in the United States.

Here's what to know about Tuesday night's drawing and what's next for the jackpot.

How to play Mega Millions

Mega Millions tickets can be purchased at convenience stores, gas stations, grocery stores and other authorized lottery retailers. Some states also allow players to buy tickets online.

To play, select five numbers from 1 to 70 and one Mega Ball number from 1 to 24. Players who prefer not to choose their own numbers can request a Quick Pick or Easy Pick, which generates numbers at random.

Mega Millions tickets now include a built-in multiplier that automatically increases non-jackpot prizes by two, three, four, five or 10 times. Previously, players had to pay extra to add the Megaplier option.
