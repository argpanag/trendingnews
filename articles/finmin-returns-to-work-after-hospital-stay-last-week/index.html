Finmin returns to work after hospital stay last week

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

The deputy minister said that Valys was feeling well.

As reported, Valys felt unwell at work on 26 October and consulted his family doctor, who referred him to a cardiologist.

After his condition subsequently worsened, he went to the emergency department of Vilnius University Hospital Santaros Clinics.

The minister was still receiving hospital treatment on Tuesday and was discharged on Wednesday.

It was then reported that the finance minister had been granted sick leave until Friday and was temporarily deputised by Transport Minister Juras Taminskas.
