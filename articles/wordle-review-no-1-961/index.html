Today’s Word

Today’s word is VIPER, a noun. According to Webster’s New World College Dictionary, it refers to “any of a widespread family (Viperidae) of venomous snakes, including the pit vipers, the European adder, and the African puff adder; solenoglyph.”

Our Featured Artist

Cheryl Teo is a Singaporean paper artist whose handcrafted practice spans sculpture, illustration and installation. Her work explores themes of memory, identity and the emotional textures of daily life. Drawn to the tactile and familiar qualities of paper, Teo uses playful forms and visual metaphors to transform everyday subjects and construct whimsical, imagined worlds.
