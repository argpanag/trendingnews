Games Inbox: Will GTA 6 have tech problems at launch?

TheTools · 17.11.2026 01:20 · source · permalink

The Tuesday letters page is worried about what could go wrong on GTA 6’s launch day, as a reader is interested in renting a PlayStation 6.

Games Inbox is a collection of our readers’ letters, comments, and opinions. To join in with the discussions yourself email gamecentral@metro.co.uk


The final hurdle
We’re past the point where we have to worry about GTA 6 being delayed again but the last danger has got to be the launch itself. No one wants a Cyberpunk 2077 situation but while that seems unlikely remember that we had no clue about Cyberpunk before it came out, and CD Projekt Red were a very well trusted developer at that point, with not a blemish on them.

I don’t think it’s likely though. Two day one patches already says to me that Rockstar are all over this and actively trying to make sure we don’t have another Cyberpunk situation.

What I am worried about though is the internet falling over on Wednesday and Thursday. The early pre-loads is a good idea, but casual gamers aren’t going to know about that. They’re all going to buy the game and start downloading it at the same time and then everyone that has it is going to be staying up late to start it as soon as possible. I really don’t know if things are going to hold up.

It’d be a sad thing if people that had taken the day off don’t actually get to play it!
Xane

Rent-a-console
I can only assume the reason there’s not a GTA 6 and PlayStation 5 hardware bundle is because of the component crisis. I don’t see why that would make a difference (if there’s not enough to go around wouldn’t they rather sell them in a more expensive bundle?) but it’s the only thing that seems to explain it.

I am intrigued by the idea of renting a console though. I don’t need to on this occasion but if the PlayStation 6 comes out and is as pointless as people seem to think it will be then a quick rental is a good way to find out for myself. Although given the rumoured prices even renting it may cost an arm and a leg.
Franky


Vice versa
Can anyone tell me what the difference is supposed to be between a console that plays PC games and a PC that plays console games? Aren’t they both doing exactly the same thing? I guess they mean that it wouldn’t be as complicated to us as a PC, but if you can’t fiddle with the settings, to put things simply, then what’s the point?

There aren’t many games that are on PC only, and they’re all indie games, so really the main appeal of playing PC games on a console is the mods and the extra graphic settings and everything. We’ll have to see what Microsoft is cooking but at this point I have basically zero confidence in them.
Ishi

Endgame Encore
To quote Doctor Stephen Strange, we are in the endgame now. Grand Theft Auto 6 is releasing in three days; I pre-loaded the title on the 10th, at the time it went live. So it’s two more days of the waiting game. But even though I’m absolutely bewildered that I’ve made it this far in life, to see myself only this close to the release of the most anticipated video game of all time, I see it as a blessing personally.

But that being said, I’ve also been able to begin collecting retro consoles recently. Alongside the childhood joy of my PlayStation 2, the original PlayStation and the GameCube have officially joined the party – two consoles I never grew up with. As a matter of fact, I never grew up with the Nintendo 64, Mega Drive, or Game Boy Advance. But I will take great privilege in adding those to my collection, if I’m able to. Especially with the boon of physical media and the fact the world will be a very sombre place when 2028 arrives.

I’ve also ordered my first ever GameCube title. It’s one of the very best and a title that’s always eluded me. It’s Eternal Darkness: Sanity’s Requiem, recently in the news for a rumoured remaster by Nightdive Studios. It’s the one horror title I always wanted to play and own. From the renowned sanity effects to Nintendo publishing a horror title and breaking the mould to just the iconic vibe of it. Amnesia has nothing on this title.

It defined the sanity meter. When a title asks if you want to delete your save data, turns you into a zombie, tricks you in several ways, and still holds up today it’s a gem. It should be arriving on Wednesday. For my first ever GameCube experience, I’ve picked a brilliant entry point, that is Metal Gear Solid 2 indeed.
Shahzaib Sadiq


Software recreation
Love all your Amiga interviews, GC, and while I’ve never seen an Amiga 1200 in real life, I am very tempted by this recreation, especially if it can mimic any of the models. I do think it is a shame the Amiga has been ignored all these years and while there’s only so much that can be done to make up for lost time, I’m glad it’s getting at least some recognition at the moment.

I do feel that something needs to be done about the games though. Just hoping people will find the ROMs online isn’t good enough. We need compilations and remasters. I get the Alien Breed one was probably expensive to make but a compilation with a range of games would surely be worth it.
Tolly

GC: Thank you.


No compromises
You’re obviously allowed to make your own choices when it comes to not buying a game, because it only runs at 30fps, but I’ve seen no end of complaining about it for GTA 6, and I think it ignores some fundamental facts.

First, it seemed extremely clear to me, since before any footage of the new game was even revealed, that the chances of a 60fps version on hardware that costs less than two or three grand would be non-existent. Cut to people having bought a PS5 Pro for significantly less than that then complaining it’s not 60fps by default, and you’ve got the usual mix of over entitlement and unrealistic expectations.

It’s not that I think it was completely unreasonable to make any assumptions at all about the game running on premium hardware but in the current climate I’d be surprised if there’s a version of the game that runs at 60fps on any (relatively) affordable hardware in the next four or five years.

Second, it’s arguably even more clear that the unprecedented state of the art production values and attention to detail are extremely important, unique selling points for Rockstar games. The suggestion that these should in some way be compromised to allow for smoother gameplay just because the development of all games should be dictated by a 60fps standard would be cutting the legs out from devs like Rockstar and denying them the chance to fully pursue what makes them successful.

I’m not even defending GTA – if anything I’m more likely to sit in the camp that says there are far more important (and cheaper to implement) aspects of games that Rockstar doesn’t seem to focus on – and I’ve absolutely no doubt that plenty of people really can’t get on with 30fps in many types of game. But people like Trev need to reconcile their complaint with the fact that we’re talking about the most hyped and lucrative entertainment product in the history of any medium. You can’t observe that phenomenon as completely unrelated to the uncompromised production values and – in turn – the demands on increasingly expensive hardware.

Unless you’re one of those people who keep insisting the value drivers for Rockstar games always stem from the writing, but those people don’t seem to have fully thought things through either. The budget for this game doesn’t come from a bunch of people sitting round a table working on scripts.
Panda


Christmas tradition
The letters over the last couple of days have reminded me of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds. I usually play through it in the build up to Christmas, so have restarted and it’s as good as ever. I can feel a bit like I’ve overplayed it though and I don’t want to get fed up with it (like when you watch a good film too many times).

Would be nice for a port to appear somewhere though, as my 3DS is starting to show its age.
Simon

Preparations complete
I’ve finally preloaded GTA 6 and I wasn’t going to. I’m not riding its hype train and I was put off by the 30fps target that they’ve admitted to aiming for on console.

I was finally swayed by the thought that GTA 6 won’t be cheap for some considerable amount of time, and I do want to see how improved the game world is on the new edition. As it’s been so long since the last outing of number 5 originally came out. It’s not like you can just decide to get it on a whim either, if you feel like playing it one day, with a 200+ gig download, so I’ve freed up some space and installed it.

If you take Assassin’s Creed Shadow’s graphical options on Xbox Series X for instance – the 30fps, 40fps (if your TV supports it) and 60fps – then the higher frame rate with less graphical fidelity always wins out for me. The difference between 30 and 60 frames, for my eyes, is massive but the jump from 60 to 120 frames on something like Samurai Shodown is hardly noticeable.

At 30fps it’s the camera turning that bothers me the most and the motion blur effects. I think it probably depends what type of game you’re playing as well though. I hope the low frame rate isn’t too off-putting in GTA 6.

On another note, I read somewhere that the original Ocarina Of Time on the N64 ran at 20 fps and I would never have guessed it.
Nick The Greek

GC: The original Ocarina Of Time frequently goes below 20fps. People put up with a lot back in the day.

50 hours for the story in GTA 6 sounds a lot and it’s basically identical to Red Dead Redemption 2, which is not surprising. I do feel that game was a bit bloated at times, but I don’t think anyone’s going to be complaining about a lack of value for money.
Hammeriron

Absolutely loved the remake of Ocarina Of Time but I’m ready for Majora’s Mask. What’s that you say? It’ll probably be at least three years or maybe never? Damn you Nintendo!
Cisco
