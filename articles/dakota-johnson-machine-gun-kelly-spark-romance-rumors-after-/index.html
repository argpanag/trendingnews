Dakota Johnson & Machine Gun Kelly Spark Romance Rumors After Private NYC Hangout at Taylor Swift's Apartment

TheTools · 13.09.2026 13:51 · source · permalink

Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly are sparking romance rumors following a NYC hangout over the weekend.

The 36-year-old Fifty Shades actress and the 36-year-old “My Ex’s Best Friend” musician were both spotted out on Friday night (September 11) in New York City.

What Happened During Dakota Johnson & MGK’s NYC Hangout?

Dakota was first spotted leaving a black SUV in an all-black outfit and walking into Taylor Swift‘s apartment in the Tribeca neighborhood. (Taylor is currently out of town, according to TMZ.)

According to Deuxmoi, MGK arrived approximately 30 minutes later and was escorted in by Dakota‘s security guard. The outlet claims they spent several hours alone in the apartment before leaving together and going to her nearby hotel, where they reportedly stayed overnight.

Are Dakota Johnson & Machine Gun Kelly Dating?

There is no confirmation of the nature of their relationship at this time, or whether the meet-up was purely platonic or romantic.

How Did Dakota Johnson & Machine Gun Kelly Meet?

The two reportedly met at the star-studded wedding ceremony between Taylor and Travis Kelce held at Madison Square Garden earlier this year.

The sighting follows the news of Dakota‘s split from singer Role Model. She and the 29-year-old entertainer had been linked for several months dating back to a December 2025 sighting.

MGK shares a daughter with ex Megan Fox. He shared a rare glimpse of his 1-year-old daughter Saga Blade Fox-Baker in one of his Study Hall videos recently.
