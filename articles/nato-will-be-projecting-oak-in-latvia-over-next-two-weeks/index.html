NATO will be projecting oak in Latvia over next two weeks

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:29 · source · permalink

The training is intended to test and improve "the ability to move, deploy and maintain combat-capable units outside their permanent location."

"During the training, units will perform offensive and defensive tactical tasks, training their ability to operate in various conditions, ensure mutual cooperation, and maintain combat capabilities after moving to the training location," said a release from the National Armed Forces (NBS) .

The Oak Projection exercise is described as being part of the NATO Multinational Brigade in Latvia's preparations for larger-scale exercises this Autumn.

Residents are advised that the movement of military equipment and personnel to the Sēlija military training ground will take place from September 4th to 7th and will include tracked military equipment transported by rail, while columns of wheeled military equipment from the Ādaži military base will move by road in three separate groups to minimize the impact on traffic flow.

During the training period, flights of Canadian Armed Forces CH-146 Griffon helicopters will also take place between the Ādaži military base and the Sēlija military training ground. From September 11 to 14, military equipment and personnel will return to the Ādaži military base. The National Armed Forces call on residents to be understanding of the movement of military equipment and helicopter flights, as well as possible temporary traffic congestion.
