Photos: 'Space jellyfish' over Florida after SpaceX rocket launch

TheTools · 21.10.2026 23:05 · source · permalink

Photos of SpaceX's rocket launch from Florida on Oct. 20, 2026.

ORLANDO, Fla. - Did you see it? SpaceX launched its Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission from Florida's Cape Canaveral Space Force Station early Tuesday morning, which created a glowing "space jellyfish" over Central Florida.

What is a "space jellyfish?"

What we know:

The "space jellyfish" happens when sunlight reflects off a rocket's exhaust plume high in the atmosphere, which creates a glowing, bulb-shaped cloud that spreads across the sky – and can be seen from miles away.

It is most common during early morning or late evening launches.

The backstory:
