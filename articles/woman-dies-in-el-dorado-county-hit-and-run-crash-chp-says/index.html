Woman dies in El Dorado County hit-and-run crash, CHP says

TheTools · 19.10.2026 13:47 · source · permalink

A woman died Monday morning after a hit-and-run crash in El Dorado County, the California Highway Patrol Placerville division said.

The crash was reported at 3:27 a.m. along Lotus Road, just off of Highway 49, CHP said.

Lotus Road is closed, affecting traffic in the area, a KCRA 3 crew observed. CHP traffic logs state this will be a long-term closure.

The woman is being listed as a "Jane Doe" because her age and name are not known, CHP said.

KCRA 3 is working to gather details on what led to the crash.
