Vanoli (press conference): “Vital, but tomorrow is no watershed”

At 4:45 p.m., Paolo Vanoli spoke at the WindTre Media Center at Viola Park for the press conference ahead of tomorrow’s clash between Sassuolo and Fiorentina. Below are his comments, as reported by ACF Fiorentina.

Here is the press conference – Fiorentinauno.com

OneFootball Videos

The press conference

Here is what the coach had to say:

On the injury front, Ranieri is not back yet, but he has made significant progress. Atta is doing a bit more on the pitch, and we hope to have him back as soon as possible. Tomorrow is not a make-or-break game; it is a very important match, just like all the others. I think we need to look at the positives, not just the negatives. This team may have made some mistakes, but those were down to a lack of maturity, not attitude or anything else, as we saw in the last match. I have always said that we put in a great performance against Inter, and I said we could have won, because in my work I make my players believe that the most important thing is to dream and fight. Otherwise, there is no hope left. This is an extremely important match for us, for the team, for our position in the table and for what we are building.
