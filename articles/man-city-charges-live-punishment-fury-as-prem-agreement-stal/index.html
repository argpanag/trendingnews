Hello and welcome to the Manchester Evening News' Manchester City charges live blog on Monday, October 19.

The fallout from City's guilty verdict continues as reports suggest Premier League clubs have been left fuming over the uncertainty of the punishment. Enzo Maresca's men continued their winning start to the Premier League season by beating Ipswich Town to remain top of the table.

However, some of their top-flight rivals are said to be 'unsure' about what they are playing for with City continuing their phenomenal start to the season, while potential sanctions loom large.

The club have appealed against the decision and are expected to find out in early 2027 if that is successful, but a sports lawyer has explained why it could be difficult.

At the same time, a sanctions hearing is taking place with the potential punishments ranging from a fine to Premier League expulsion.

The three-person commission that deemed City in breach will be making that decision, with the latest claim being that they will know their possible sanctions before the end of the season.

With all of that being said, stay with us throughout the day for all the latest City charges news and updates...

Man City 'bullish' in PL appeal

The Telegraph have reported that sources with understanding of City's appeal against the Premier League are 'bullish'.

They have a strong outlook on their fight with the top-flight due to contemporaneous bank statements, government records and receipts that they beliee confirm the Abu Dhabi government as the source of funds.

It has also been stated that they have more recent evidence reaffirming the accuracy of the reports from the Abu Dhabi government.

They add that those with an understanding of the Premier League's case have expressed ongoing confidence in the verdict.

City are said to be arguing that that the panel overlooked almost all of their evidence that the sponsorship payments came from the Abu Dhabi government, shareholder of their sponsors.

City face 'uphill struggle' in appeal

Lawyer James Severn has claimed that Man City face 'substantial hurdles' in order to be successful in their appeal.

The club have argued that they have 'irrefutable evidence' to back their case. Yet he has outlined what the Premier League leaders need to do but face an uphill struggle.

He told BBC Sport: "City will need to identify a material error of law, principle or fact, or serious procedural unfairness, which materially affected the Commission’s decision.

“The Appeal Board nevertheless has broad powers to allow or dismiss the appeal, vary the decision or make any other order it considers appropriate.

“The bank statements, government records and contemporaneous payment documents could be powerful evidence if they establish a clear audit trail showing the disputed funds came from the Abu Dhabi government rather than the owners.

“However, if that evidence was already before the Commission, which nevertheless concluded that the arrangements were a sham, City will face an uphill struggle to demonstrate the finding was materially wrong.”

'Nobody can see it getting sorted'

According to The Telegraph, Manchester City's Premier League rivals believe an appeal verdict won't be delivered this season.

Their top-flight rivals are said to be angered at City's unwillingness to agree a settlement to bring the process to an end.

A source told the outlet: “The talk among clubs is that nobody can see it getting sorted out this season.

“That’s a massive problem because nobody is sure what they are playing for and whether or not the final positions will stand.”
