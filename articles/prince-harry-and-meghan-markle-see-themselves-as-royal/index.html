Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are determined to maintain their royal image and status.

Despite King Charles' firm reminder that the two no longer represent the monarchy, experts seem to think the Sussexes consider themselves members of the Royal Family on their own terms.

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle continue to see themselves as royals

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are outside the working monarchy. By: MEGA

Royal editor Emily Andrews addressed the Sussexes' position in a recent report, suggesting the couple have struggled to embrace their non-working royal status.

According to her, Harry and Markle dislike the label of "non-working royals" and prefer to present themselves as public figures who continue to carry out royal-style engagements. "They consider themselves to be royal on their own terms," Andrews wrote.

She added that the couple behaved as if Megxit never happened, suggesting their public appearances confuse people about their relationship with the monarchy.

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle reportedly disliked their non-working royal label. By: MEGA

Andrews further argued that the Sussexes seem determined to keep the visibility and recognition of royal life without their former institutional responsibilities.

Moreover, she claimed that the duo will not join the King and other senior royals for Christmas festivities. According to Andrews, Prince William is not interested in a reunion and is concerned about privacy and trust.

King Charles' letter caught Prince Harry and Meghan Markle by surprise

King Charles reaffirmed Prince Harry and Meghan Markle's private status. By: MEGA

The latest development comes after a September directive from Charles. A letter reportedly circulated and reiterated what Harry and Markle's position was outside the working Royal Family.

Lord Chamberlain's Office sent it and reaffirmed that the Sussexes remained private citizens and could not undertake official royal engagements.

Moreover, the two can no longer use their His and Her Royal Highness titles and any charitable or public appearances would take place in their personal capacity.

The message reinforced the terms of the 2020 Sandringham Agreement, which ruled out a half-in, half-out arrangement for the couple.

Prince William reportedly had little trust in Prince Harry. By: MEGA

However, the Sussexes did not expect the announcement to reach the public when it did, and their spokesperson said they "were a little surprised not to have been told about this in advance."

Royal sources offered a different account, claiming Harry and Markle knew about the letter before it was released. But since its release, the document has added another layer of ongoing tensions between the Sussexes and the Crown.
