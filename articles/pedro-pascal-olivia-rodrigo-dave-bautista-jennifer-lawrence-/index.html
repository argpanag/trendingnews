Pedro Pascal, Olivia Rodrigo, Dave Bautista, Jennifer Lawrence Among A-Listers Urging Fans to Vote: “Get Out There”

TheTools · 04.11.2026 01:15 · source · permalink

Celebrities posted photos and messages on their respective social media accounts ahead of the 2026 midterms.

As Election Day nears its end, A-listers are reminding their fanbases of the importance of voting.

Pedro Pascal, Olivia Rodrigo, Dave Bautista, Jennifer Lawrence, Chris Pratt, Bruce Springsteen and Samuel L. Jackson are among celebrities taking to social media, urging people to get out and vote in the 2026 midterms.

Heading into Election Day, people have their eyes on several key races — notably the Kansas Senate, Texas Senate, Michigan Senate, Ohio Senate and U.S. House races in several states — with a potential control flip of Congress at play. As for California, residents are also monitoring the Los Angeles mayoral and California governor’s races closely.

Check out the celebs’ social media posts below.

Pedro Pascal

The Last of Us and The Mandalorian actor shared a video on his Instagram Story Tuesday, telling fans, “I don’t think I’ve ever made a video at random like this, especially crossing a major intersection in Los Angeles. Vote! Please vote. Please vote.”

Olivia Rodrigo

On Oct. 29, the Grammy-winning singer shared that she voted early, posting a photo showing off her “I Voted” sticker. She added in the caption, “Get out there and vote!!!” She also shared a video on her Instagram Story Tuesday from her Daisy Chain Fields festival account, which displayed the word “Vote.”

Dave Bautista

The Guardians of the Galaxy and Blade Runner 2049 star posted a photo on Instagram Tuesday of him holding up his Florida vote-by-mail ballot. In the caption, he wrote, “Vote! #votethemout,” before encouraging people to text their friends to remind them to vote too.

View this post on Instagram

Jennifer Lawrence

The Oscar winner urged people to vote on her Instagram Story by referencing the iconic line her character, Katniss Everdeen, says in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1: “If we burn, you burn with us.” Lawrence posted a photo of herself holding up the three-finger salute from the film franchise, with the caption, “Midterms BB. Don’t forget who burns with us.”

Chris Pratt

On Instagram, the Guardians of the Galaxy and Parks and Recreation actor shared a photo of his “I Voted” sticker with the caption, “It’s like a gold star, but patriotic.”

View this post on Instagram

Carrie Coon

The Gilded Age and White Lotus star shared a photo of herself and husband Tracy Letts on X with their “I Voted” stickers. In the caption, she also endorsed Democrat Cait Conley, who’s running in the U.S. House’s 17th District in New York.

Kerry Washington

The Scandal alum shared a video on Monday dropping off her vote-by-mail ballot with her husband Nnamdi Asomugha. “Voting knowing you’re not canceling out each other’s votes,” she wrote over the clip before adding in the caption, “Voting team goals.”

Samuel L. Jackson

When encouraging fans to vote on Instagram, the veteran actor let his shirt do the talking, which read, “Fcuk you, I’m voting!” Jackson added in the post’s caption, “WHAT THE SHIRT SAYS!!”

View this post on Instagram

Bruce Springsteen

The Grammy winner posted a video on his Instagram Tuesday, reminding election and government officials of their “solemn duty” to “ensure votes are securely counted.” He added in the clip, “Our vote is our voice. And it is up to you to ensure that our voice is heard.”

Springsteen also wrote in the post’s caption, “If you haven’t voted already, thank you for voting today! And you can keep supporting democracy by taking the Oath to the Constitution yourself at oathproject.org.”
