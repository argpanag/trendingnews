The Beaver Supermoon 2026: online observation - 26 Nov. 2026 - The Virtual Telescope Project 2.0

TheTools · 23.11.2026 09:49 · source · permalink

Next 24 Nov., we will have the Beaver Supermoon 2026. We will show it live, online.

The Beaver Supermoon 2026: poster of the event.

Next 24 Nov. 2026, the Supermoon will be back, since its last occurrence in January.

We will be observing the “Beaver Supermoon” from Manciano, Italy, where our facility is based. Also, we will show the best full Moon images we collected over the years, particularly those with our satellite shining above the legendary monuments and skyline of Rome.

The online, free session is scheduled for 24 Nov. 2026, starting at 15:30 UTC.

To join the Supermoon live feed, you just need to enter, at the date and time above, our webTV page here!
