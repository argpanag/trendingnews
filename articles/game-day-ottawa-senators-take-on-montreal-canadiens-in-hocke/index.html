Game Day: Ottawa Senators Take on Montreal Canadiens in Hockeyville

TheTools · 21.09.2026 14:30 · source · permalink

The road to 103-2-0 begins today! (Because you know they're losing twice to Chicago.)

Remember, folks. It's preseason, so the results don't matter. Unless they win, of course.

The Ottawa Senators are in Trois-Rivières, Québec, this year's Kraft Hockeyville, for their first preseason game against the Montreal Canadiens, who have already swept a split-squad series against the Toronto Maple Leafs on Saturday.

There aren't a ton of roster spots up for grabs, but Stephen Halliday and Carter Yakemchuk are both waivers-exempt (despite the former being on a one-way deal) and are realistically going to have some competition in training camp for opening night jobs. With a suspended Ridly Greig slated to miss the first two games of the regular season, another spot on the forward corps is temporarily available as well.

Training Camp Notes:

  • A good mix of NHL regulars and guys competing for spots. Hayden Hodgson played 10 games for the Sens last season, while Sammy Blais was a point-per-game AHLer last year, who feels he was passed on for worse players on the Habs' roster last season.
  • Confirmed absences include the injured Artem Zub, Warren Foegele and Kurtis MacDermid, as well as Thomas Chabot, who's back in Ottawa with his wife, Marion, expecting their first child.
  • It appears the power-play network, at least to start, will be comprised of Tim Stützle, Dylan Cozens, Drake Batherson, William Eklund and Jake Sanderson on the first unit, with Shane Pinto, Andre Burakovsky, Claude Giroux, Yakemchuk and Chabot on the second. Expect to see Halliday fill in on the top unit tonight.
  • Montreal's going with the 'A' team tonight, with Chris Kreider being the lone absentee from their top-nine. Good call from both teams to ice competitive squads for Kraft Hockeyville.
  • Puck drop is scheduled for 7:00 pm EST on Sportsnet One.
