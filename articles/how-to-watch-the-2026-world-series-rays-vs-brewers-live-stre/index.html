When does the 2026 World Series start?

Game 1 between the Rays and Brewers is scheduled for Friday, October 23, at 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT. Milwaukee hosts the opener at American Family Field.

What channel is the World Series on?

Every game of the Rays–Brewers series will air on FOX, with Spanish-language coverage on FOX Deportes.

For DIRECTV subscribers, confirm that the appropriate channel is included in your selected package and available in your market.

2026 World Series schedule

The best-of-seven series follows a 2-3-2 format, with Milwaukee hosting the first two games and the final two, if necessary. Tampa Bay hosts Games 3–5.

Games 5–7 will be played if necessary. Dates are subject to change.
