Mild afternoons will switch to cold days

TheTools · 09.11.2026 12:50 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - A brisk start to the week with morning low temperatures in the 30s and 40s, we’ll warm up throughout the morning with ample sunshine and calm winds. Afternoon highs today will settle in the mid 60s, near normal for this time of year.

Tomorrow we’ll see more of the same: cold morning temperatures near 40 degrees, then sunshine and calm conditions for a comfortable autumn afternoon with highs near 70 degrees.

WEATHER PATTERN CHANGES: Wednesday will start off calm, but rain showers will make their way into the region as a low pressure system moves across the Midwest on Wednesday afternoon. An attached cold front will push showers in for Wednesday night and cold air for the rest of the week. Thursday will feature overcast clouds, and we’ll start to see the cold air take effect for the later half of the week.

Friday onward, we are expecting clear skies again, but it will also symbolize the start of winter as morning temperatures drop into the 20s. Afternoons will stay chilly with high temperatures only in the upper 40s. We’ll slowly begin to warm up again throughout the weekend, but only to the mid 50s.
