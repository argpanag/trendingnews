Taurus (April 20-May 20)

**** Today the Moon will move into your fellow Earth sign, which will encourage a more lighthearted approach to life. Enjoy socializing with others. Explore romance. Your involvement with sports and the company of children will be rewarding.

Gemini (May 21-June 20)

**** You have lots of mental energy today, which is why you want to share your ideas and information with someone. As the day wears on, home and family discussions will become more important.

Cancer (June 21-July 22)

**** For a large part of this day, the Moon is in your Money House. During the Moon Alert, be smart and restrict your spending to food and gas. If you want to buy something, fill your databank. Do your research -- than act once the Moon Alert is over.

Leo (July 23-Aug. 22)

*** Today holds the promise of fun socializing and romantic escapades. Reach out to someone you care about. Keep things light and enjoy talking to someone. After the Moon Alert is over, cash flow, shopping and money will be on your mind.

Virgo (Aug. 23-Sept. 22)

**** Today has a vague, lighthearted dreamy quality until the Moon Alert is over. (See above.) However, once the Moon moves into your sign to join the Sun and Mercury - it's all systems go! Go do that voodoo that you do so well.

Libra (Sept. 23-Oct. 22)

*** You're a social person, which is why you need others in your life. This day begins with a strong desire to talk to others or be involved in groups. However, after the Moon Alert, you need some peace and quiet. Treat yourself to solitude in beautiful surroundings.

Scorpio (Oct. 23-Nov. 21)

**** For the first part of this day, you're high viz. People notice you more than usual. You might have to do some damage control - keep this in mind. After the Moon Alert, make an effort to talk to a friend or be involved with a group because this will be rewarding.

Sagittarius (Nov. 22-Dec. 21)

*** Initially, you might feel a strong sense of escape today. You might want to run away and join the circus. You want to do something different! Take it easy, especially because this feeling will simmer during the Moon Alert, which makes you feel a bit rudderless. Later in the day - you're high viz.!

Capricorn (Dec. 22-Jan. 19)

**** Postpone important financial negotiations or banking decisions until the Moon Alert is over today. Initially, you can do your research and get your facts. But wait until you're on solid ground. Welcome any opportunity to travel, learn and explore your world.
