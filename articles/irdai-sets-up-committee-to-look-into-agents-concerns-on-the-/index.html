IRDAI sets up committee to look into agents' concerns on the proposed commission caps - Cafemutual.com

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:47 · source · permalink

IRDAI has set up an expert committee to review its proposed caps on insurance commissions. The committee must submit its report by December 31, 2026.

The move comes after IRDAI's consultation paper 'Recalibrating Economics of Insurance Distribution' drew an "overwhelming" response. By October 31, IRDAI received about 2.18 lakh answers to its 32 questions from over 13,000 people. That is a big jump. As Cafemutual reported, IRDAI had received just over 650 comments till October 24, a day before the original deadline.

Dr. Indu Bhushan chairs the committee. The members are Suresh Goyal (DG, NCAER), A.V. Girija Kumar (former CMD, Oriental Insurance), Praveen Vashishta (former CEO, Howden Insurance Brokers India) and Mahesh Kumar Sharma (former MD & CEO, SBI Life).

As Cafemutual reported earlier, the paper brings back product-wise commission caps. Insurers today pay agents an average of 51% of the premium on term plans. This could fall to 30%.
