Southport fire department to hold 9/11 remembrance ceremony

TheTools · 10.09.2026 22:03 · source · permalink

SOUTHPORT, N.C. (WECT) - To honor the hundreds of emergency personnel who ran toward danger 25 years ago, Southport first responders will gather Friday to ensure a new generation never forgets Sept. 11.

Each year, firefighters, police officers and neighbors assemble at Southport Fire Headquarters to pay tribute to the nearly 3,000 lives lost in the 2001 attacks. For Southport Fire Chief Charles Drew, preserving that tradition grows more urgent with each passing year.

“We started having this anniversary memorial just to make sure that people remember,” Drew said. “I saw a statistic today that says 40 percent of Americans are so young now that they were not born when that happened, so we want to make sure that no one ever forgets that terrible tragedy.”

Drew pointed to the extraordinary sacrifice of emergency personnel at the World Trade Center as a defining reason the department continues the observance.

“Three hundred forty-three firefighters, 72 police officers, and eight EMS workers gave the ultimate sacrifice on that day,” Drew said. “When everybody else was trying to run out of the buildings, they were running in to try and save lives.”

Beyond honoring those fallen first responders, Drew said the gathering offers comfort and community to people with direct ties to that day who have since relocated to southeastern North Carolina.

“We have people that attend that are relatives; we have people that attend that they were friends, that have moved to the area,” Drew said. “I just think it’s important that we do that to keep their memory alive.”

The remembrance ceremony begins just before 9 a.m. Friday at Southport Fire Headquarters.
