All Things Go, CBGB, Global Citizen 2026 Festivals Canceled

TheTools · 25.09.2026 17:42 · source · permalink

This post has been updated to include the cancelation of CGBG Festival.

All Things Go Festival has canceled its 2026 New York edition due to an incoming Nor’easter, which is forecasted to sweep through the area with gusty winds, rain, and coastal flooding.

The festival announced the news ahead of the event’s first day, with organizers writing in a statement that, following “consultation with local officials, Forest Hills Stadium, and trusted weather advisors, we have made the difficult decision to cancel All Things Go NYC on September 25, 26, and 27…due to severe weather conditions in the New York Metro area…. The safety of our fans, the artists and local crew is our top priority.” Refunds will be automatically issued to credit cards used to purchase tickets.

The New York edition of All Things Go included headliners Zara Larsson, Lola Young, Muna, and Ethel Cain, who were booked alongside Rebecca Black, Carly Rae Jepsen, Grace Ives, Sienna Spiro, Cara Delevingne, and more. The Maryland edition, also taking place on the same dates, will go on as scheduled.

The Queens event isn’t the only East Coast festival that’s been canceled this weekend due to weather. Brooklyn’s CBGB fest; Oceans Calling, scheduled to take place in Ocean City, Maryland; and Global Citizen, which was set to return to Central Park’s Great Lawn on Saturday, September 26, have all been scrapped.
