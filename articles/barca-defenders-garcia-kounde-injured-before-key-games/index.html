Barca defenders Garcia, Kounde injured before key games

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:32 · source · permalink

Both players finished the 5-2 win over Real Betis with discomfort ahead of the Clasico against Real Madrid and the Champions League clash with PSG.

Spanish centreback Garcia will miss both games and spend between “three to four weeks” out with a thigh injury, Barca said in a statement.

Right-back Kounde suffered an ankle sprain and Barca said he would miss the trip to face PSG on Tuesday but may still be able to feature in the Clasico on Sunday, Oct 25, depending on the “evolution of the injury”.

Both defenders ended the club’s 5-2 win over Real Betis in La Liga on Saturday with discomfort.

Hansi Flick’s side won a ninth consecutive league game to continue their 100% start to the campaign.

Double-defending European champions PSG announced a defensive absence of their own after Portuguese left-back Nuno Mendes picked up a knock in Saturday’s Ligue 1 win at Strasbourg.

“Having sustained an injury to his left thigh during last night’s match against RC Strasbourg Alsace, Nuno Mendes will be sidelined for the next few weeks,” PSG posted on their website.
