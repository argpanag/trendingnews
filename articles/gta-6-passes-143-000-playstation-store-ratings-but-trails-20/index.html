GTA 6 passes 143,000 PlayStation Store ratings, but trails 2026’s top-rated PS5 game | Межа

TheTools · 20.11.2026 18:41 · source · permalink

Photo source: Notebookcheck

GTA 6 has more than 143,000 PlayStation Store ratings on PS5, accumulated faster than any other game on the marketplace. Its 4.85/5 average is below Resident Evil Requiem’s 4.88.

GTA 6 has a Metacritic score of 99, tying the original Ocarina of Time for the highest score of all time. PlayStation Store users who own a digital copy can also rate the game.

This is reported by Notebookcheck.

PS Store DB tracked how quickly ratings for the PS5 version accumulated. Sony first-party game Marvel’s Wolverine had 4,703 ratings one day after release. GTA 6 received 49,627 ratings over the same period, then reached 122,755 24 hours later.

Among 2026 PS5 releases, GTA 6 has already surpassed Resident Evil Requiem’s 107,719 PlayStation Store ratings. Minecraft, however, remains far ahead with a historical total of more than 2 million submissions. The original says GTA 6 could realistically reach that mark, given projections of record pre-order sales.

Among 2026 games with at least 10,000 ratings, Resident Evil Requiem leads with 4.88 stars. GTA 6 is second at 4.85, followed by Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, Phantom Blade Zero and Pragmata, which complete the top five.

GTA 6 is rated higher than earlier Rockstar games listed in the original: GTA 5 has 4.46 stars and GTA 4 has 4.56. Red Dead Redemption and Red Dead Redemption 2 have nearly identical scores of 4.77 and 4.76, respectively.

PlayStation Store users can now submit written reviews of GTA 6, but only a handful are available, limiting insight into how players arrived at the average rating. Social media users generally praise Leonida’s detailed open world and say the many side activities keep them entertained beyond the roughly 50-hour campaign.

Performance is one of the most divisive topics. The PS5 and Xbox versions target 30 fps. Metacritic user reviews were expected to appear imminently at the time of the source report, offering a clearer picture of the community’s consensus.
