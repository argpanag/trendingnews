Espart scores his first goal in UEFA Champions League… | YSscores

TheTools · 20.10.2026 20:25 · source · permalink

Xavi Espart, a 19-year-old player for FC Barcelona, scored his first goal in the UEFA Champions League, equalising for the Catalan team against Paris Saint-Germain in the 55th minute, making the score 1-1.

The goal came after Xavi Espart made a run into the penalty area from the right side, before unleashing a powerful shot into the near corner that goalkeeper Matvey Safonov was unable to stop.

Fermín López played a key role in the goal, skillfully dribbling inside Paris Saint-Germain's half before accurately passing the ball to Espart, who successfully finished the move.

Xavi Espart puts in a strong defensive performance, successfully protecting his position, before taking advantage of one of his attacking attempts to score the equaliser.
