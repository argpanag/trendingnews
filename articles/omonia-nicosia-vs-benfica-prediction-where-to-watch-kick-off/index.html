Omonia Nicosia vs. Benfica - Head-to-head record

Omonia and Benfica have met only twice. Benfica won both matches between the clubs during the 1981 European Cup campaign.

The teams meet again with both having won their opening two Europa League league-phase fixtures. Omonia have impressed with their defensive organisation, while Benfica have demonstrated their attacking quality with victories over AC Milan and Celtic.

Although the historical record favours Benfica, Omonia’s recent home form suggests the hosts should not be underestimated.

Omonia Nicosia - Looking strong at home

Let’s delve into Omonia Nicosia’s current situation ahead of their clash with Benfica. We will now analyse recent form and the probable Omonia Nicosia vs. Benfica line-ups to see how they might shape up for this important European night.

Recent form

Omonia head into this fixture unbeaten in seven matches across all competitions, having recorded five wins and two draws during that run. Their latest outing saw them recover from two goals down to draw 2-2 with city rivals Olympiacos Nicosia.

Henning Berg’s side have also started their Europa League campaign impressively. A 1-0 home victory over Celta Vigo was followed by a convincing 3-0 win away to Celje, leaving them level on six points with Benfica after two rounds.

Their home record is particularly encouraging. Omonia have won 15 of their last 20 European home matches, drawing one and losing four, while keeping 11 clean sheets in that period. They have also avoided defeat in their last 15 matches at GSP Stadium across all competitions.

The hosts have no new injury concerns, although Carel Eiting remains sidelined with a serious knee injury and Fotis Kitsos is nearing a return from a cruciate ligament injury. Lois Diony is expected to lead the attack after scoring the equaliser against Olympiacos Nicosia, ending a six-match scoring drought.

Benfica - Riding a wave of victories

Let’s take a look at Benfica’s situation. We will analyse the team’s impressive momentum, their recent performances, and standout players for the trip to Cyprus.

Recent form

Benfica travel to Cyprus in outstanding form, having won 10 of their last 11 matches across all competitions. They have also won each of their last four continental matches, scoring at least twice in every game.

The Portuguese side began their Europa League league-phase campaign with a 2-0 victory away to AC Milan before beating Celtic 4-1 at home. Jhon Durán scored twice in the latter match, with Alexander Bah and Vangelis Pavlidis also finding the net.

Benfica’s attacking performances have made them one of the teams to watch in the competition. They will be keen to maintain their perfect start against an Omonia side that has conceded no goals in its opening two league-phase fixtures.

The visitors have some fitness concerns despite their strong overall squad. Jakob Kaminski is recovering from a thigh injury, Enzo Barrenechea is sidelined following meniscus surgery, and right-back Daniel Banjaqui is a doubt with a muscle injury.
