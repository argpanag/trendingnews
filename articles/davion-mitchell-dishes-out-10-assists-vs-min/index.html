    Davion Mitchell dishes out 10 assists vs MIN

    Davion Mitchell

    MIA Guard #2
    Player Stats

    Davion Mitchell produced 11 points (4-of-8 FGs, 2-of-2 FTs), 10 assists, one block and one three-pointer in Wednesday’s loss to the Timberwolves.

    The good news for fantasy managers was that Mitchell recorded a double-double, which included a game-high 10 assists. However, for category-league managers, the six turnovers hurt. Mitchell logged 33 minutes in Wednesday’s opener, with Pelle Larsson (two points, three rebounds, four assists, one steal and one block) taking on the playmaking responsibilities when “Off Night” was on the bench. Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo can also initiate the offense, and they’ll likely need to because Miami lacks depth at point guard.
    - Raphielle Johnson
