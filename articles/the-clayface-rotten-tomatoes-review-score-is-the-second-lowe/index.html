The ‘Clayface’ Rotten Tomatoes Review Score Is The Second-Lowest In The DCU

TheTools · 22.10.2026 15:02 · source · permalink

Clayface

The DCU is starting to amass a number of projects across TV and movies, no longer the nascent universe it once was. Now, we have the latest entry in the revived world, a pure horror film, Clayface, based on the gooey Batman villain, though with no Batman in sight. Now, reviews are in, and its Rotten Tomatoes score is okay, but it puts it almost at the bottom of the existing DCU projects so far. Here's the list:

  • Peacemaker (retconned to be DCU) – 93% critic score

  • Creature Commandos – 93% critic score

  • Lanterns – 91% critic score

  • Superman – 83% critic score

  • Blue Beetle (possibly retconned to be DCU) - 78% critic score

  • Clayface – 76% critic score

  • Supergirl – 52% critic score

Clayface

So, that's certainly not a bad score, and well above "rotten," though you're not exactly going to see headlines about how it's a giant critical win. Audience scores are not in yet, and will start coming in today as Thursday previews start. Then there's the all-important box office where somehow, its biggest competition is Nathan Fielder's Elizabeth Holmes documentary, You Can See Everything, which is set to see its total surge over last weekend. That said, Clayface is a horror movie released a week before Halloween, and the hope there is that the timing and genre will add to DC fans seeing it for comic book reasons.

It has been a banner year for horror, so Clayface has those to compete with on the critic side as well. And right now, that 76% puts it just outside the top 10 list for 2026, which includes:

  1. Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma – 94% critic score

  2. Obsession – 93% critic score

  3. It Ends – 93% critic score

  7. Hokum – 88% critic score

  8. We Bury the Dead – 88% critic score

  9. Backrooms – 87% critic score

  11. Clayface – 76%

So yeah, it's lower than the killer monkey movie right now. Not ideal. But again, if we're mainly talking box office, critics' reviews often don't matter in the genre. Other Mommy, the 19% scored horror film starring Jessica Chastain, has blown by its tiny budget to win its opening weekend, and it has made $82 million worldwide. However, I think A-lister Chastain helped carry interest there, and Clayface may be at a somewhat significant disadvantage compared to many of these other films, given that star Tom Rhys Harries is almost a complete unknown. That worked for Obsession, of course, but Clayface is not going to be Obsession. Nothing is Obsession.

There is only one other DCU project with a set date right now, next year's Man of Tomorrow, the Superman sequel out July 9, 2027, making three of the first four DCU about Kryptonians with Clayface here being the exception. The People vs. Gorilla Grodd is done filming and expected out in 2027, but there's no date for that show yet. We'll see how Clayface does. I am not the most bullish on its prospects, but it's horror, anything can happen.
