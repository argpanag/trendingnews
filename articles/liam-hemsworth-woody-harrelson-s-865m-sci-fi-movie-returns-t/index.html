Liam Hemsworth and Jennifer Lawrence's dystopian blockbuster is back in theaters today. The re-release comes ahead of another major premiere later this year.

The Hunger Games: Catching Fire returns to theaters on Friday, September 4, 2026. According to Fathom Entertainment, all five movies in the beloved franchise will get a limited re-release, ahead of the premiere of the upcoming prequel movie The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Following Catching Fire, The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 will return on September 5, and Mockingjay – Part 2 on September 6. The prequel movie The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes will return on September 7.

Catching Fire was released in 2013 and serves as the second installment of the original tetralogy. The movie picks up right after the events of The Hunger Games (2012) and follows Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark as they witness the brutal aftermath of winning the 74th Hunger Games.

The movie returns with Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss and Josh Hutcherson as Peeta. Other cast members include Woody Harrelson, Liam Hemsworth, Elizabeth Banks, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Donald Sutherland, Sam Claflin, and many more.

The Hunger Games: Catching Fire was a huge hit at the box office

The Hunger Games: Catching Fire became the highest-grossing film in the entire franchise. It has earned $865 million worldwide and was also the fifth-highest-grossing movie of 2013.

Additionally, Catching Fire holds the highest Rotten Tomatoes score in the series, at a whopping 90% on Tomatometer and 89% on Popcornmeter. It received overwhelmingly positive reviews with particular praise for Jennifer Lawrence's performance.

Meanwhile, fans are looking forward to the premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Based on Suzanne Collins' book of the same name, the movie serves as a prequel to the original Hunger Games series and a sequel to The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. It centers on the 50th Hunger Games, in which a young Haymitch Abernathy competed.

Joseph Zada stars as the teenage Haymitch, the adult version of whom was played by Woody Harrelson. Other cast members include Mckenna Grace, Ben Wang, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, and more.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping arrives in theaters on November 20, 2026.
