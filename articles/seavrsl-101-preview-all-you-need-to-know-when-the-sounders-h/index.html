SEAvRSL 101 Preview: All you need to know when the Sounders host Real Salt Lake in the 2026 Audi MLS Cup Playoffs Wildcard Round, pres. by Ticketmaster | Seattle Sounders

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:06 · source · permalink

The eighth-seeded Seattle Sounders are hosting ninth-seeded Real Salt Lake on Thursday in the Western Conference Wild Card Match. Here is everything you need to know:

WHEN AND WHERE: Kickoff is at 7 p.m. PT at Lumen Field (**TICKETS**).

WHERE TO WATCH: The match will be available to watch on Apple TV.

WHERE TO LISTEN: The match will air on iHeartMedia Seattle and in Spanish on El Rey 1360AM.

2026 MLS STANDINGS: Sounders: 12-12-10 (46 points, 8th West), RSL: 13-15-6 (45 points, 9th West)

FORM GUIDE (all competitions, most recent first): Seattle: WLLDD, Real Salt Lake: WLWWW

PREVIOUS MEETING: The Sounders defeated RSL 2-0 at Lumen Field on Sept. 23 behind a pair of goals from former Salt Lake attackers Danny Musovski and Albert Rusnák.

RAGEN SUSPENDED: The Sounders will be without key center back Jackson Ragen on Thursday as he serves the first of a two-game suspension. The MLS Disciplinary Committee issued the suspension on Saturday after reviewing Ragen’s tackle on LAFC’s Jacob Shaffelburg on Decision Day.

GOT IT DONE LATE: Both the Sounders and RSL needed victories on Decision Day (along with help around the league) to book their place in this year’s postseason. Seattle took down LAFC 2-1 at home while RSL knocked off the LA Galaxy at home by the same scoreline.

PLAYOFF HISTORY: 2026 marks the fifth time that Seattle and Real Salt Lake have met in the postseason. RSL won the aggregate Conference Semifinals in 2011, Seattle won the aggregate Conference Semifinals in 2012, the Sounders won the single-leg Conference Semifinals in 2019 en route to MLS Cup, and RSL won in the single-match First Round in the two clubs’ most recent playoff meeting in 2021.

THOMAS’ YEAR TO REMEMBER: In his first full season as the No. 1 in goal, Andrew Thomas proved why he was so highly regarded within the Sounders organization. The 28-year-old finished third in MLS in Goals-Expected Goals, preventing nearly 11 more goals than he should have. Seattle is hoping it can ride the waves of a hot goalkeeper in the postseason like it did with Stefan Frei in its 2016 and 2019 MLS Cup title runs.

LONGEST ACTIVE PLAYOFF STREAK: Despite all the flashy big-market teams around the league, it’s Pablo Mastroeni’s Real Salt Lake that owns Major League Soccer’s longest-active playoff qualification streak. RSL has made the postseason in each of the last six seasons since Mastroeni took over in 2021.

PLAYER TO WATCH: Sergi Solans, F

The rookie out of UCLA had a monster debut season, tallying 15 goals to lead RSL and record the second-most goals in MLS history by a SuperDraft pick in their first season out of college. The 23-year-old Spaniard has done his part to fill an attacking void left by the transfer of Zavier Gozo to Crystal Palace and the season-ending knee injury to Diego Luna.
