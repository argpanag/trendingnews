Tottenham 2-3 Aston Villa: Winless Spurs drop into relegation zone while visitors secure first win of Premier League season

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:09 · source · permalink

Report as Spurs suffer another defeat, making it their worst ever start to a Premier League campaign; Spurs only have two points and goal difference of -6, eclipsing their 2008-09 start (2 points, -3 goal difference)

Saturday 19 September 2026 17:19, UK

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Tottenham head into the international break without a win in the Premier League after losing 3-2 at home to Aston Villa.

Roberto De Zerbi's expensively assembled side were hit by goals from Johan Manzambi, Nicolas Jackson and Emi Buendia which handed Villa an important win - their first of the season.

Both clubs had made slow starts to the new campaign after summer rebuilds and while Spurs had failed to score in four league fixtures.

Spurs' sorry start in stats

  • Tottenham Hotspur have now lost 22 home league games since the start of 2024-25, just one fewer than in their previous four seasons combined (23).
  • Tottenham Hotspur have failed to win any of their previous 48 games in all competitions in which they’ve conceded the opening goal (D11 L37), with 37 of those games coming in the Premier League (D8 L29).
  • This is Spurs’ worst start to a Premier League season after five games, with two points and a goal difference of -6, eclipsing their 2008-09 start (2 points, -3 goal difference).

Conor Gallagher at least got Spurs' first goal of the season late on, followed up by a Jan Paul van Hecke header, but it was nothing more than a consolation and too little too late.

After a bright start from Spurs where they forced Zion Suzuki into four saves inside the first 20 minutes, Villa hit Tottenham with a sucker punch - with captain Micky Van de Ven off the pitch after he took a whack to the face from Tyrone Mings, Manzambi fired in.

Spurs' goal drought appeared over on the hour mark when Sandro Tonali played in Andy Robertson and Mohammed Kudus poked in his cross, but VAR ruled it was offside.

Jackson then made it 2-0 by finding the bottom corner.

A wonderful effort by Buendia from 25 yards made the victory safe for Villa and sparked 'you're getting sacked in the morning' chants by the away faithful.

Ahead of the extended break, Spurs find themselves in the relegation zone and have only won five of their last 36 matches at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

"The pressure heats up," said former Tottenham defender Michael Dawson on Soccer Saturday.

"That's what happens when you finish 17th twice, you spend all this money, there's a huge expectation around the football club. I believed there was going to be a shift but when there isn't, it breeds and now, unfortunately, for Spurs, they've got three weeks to wait until the next game."

De Zerbi admits he 'didn't speak to players' after defeat

Spurs boss Roberto De Zerbi on the timing of the international break:

"Maybe it's better, I didn't speak with the players today because I didn't want to say anything.

"But the players who go with the national team they have to play, they have to work and the other players like Gallagher, Solanke, Tosin, Senesi, Savio for sure we have to work more because of the physical condition I didn't like in this game."

He added: "I'm very disappointed, very sad, very frustrated for the performance because we can't lose this game in this way, absolutely. We can't concede the second goal, we can't lose balance just because in the first half we are losing in the beginning of the second half.

"I spoke with the four attackers to use their heads calmly, play calm, play, enjoy, don't take risks. When we conceded the second goal it was incredible."
