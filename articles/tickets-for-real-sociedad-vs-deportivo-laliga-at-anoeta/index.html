Planning to see Real Sociedad take on Deportivo de La Coruna? The LaLiga football match takes place at Anoeta Stadium in San Sebastián on 11 October 2026, with both clubs level on ten points after seven rounds. Check your travel route, plan your ticket purchase and arrive in time for the 16:15 kick-off

Real Sociedad and RC Deportivo meet on Sunday, October 11, 2026, at 16:15 at the Anoeta Stadium in San Sebastián, in the eighth round of LaLiga. At first glance, this is a match between two neighboring teams in the standings, but the numbers reveal different paths to the same points total: both clubs have ten points, with Deportivo in seventh place and Real Sociedad in eighth. The home side have recorded more victories, while the visitors have lost only once. Following the international break, both teams are looking to regain their rhythm, and the winner could take an important step towards the upper part of the table.

It is worth checking ticket availability and seating options in good time.

Equal points, different pictures of the season

After seven league matches, Real Sociedad have three wins, one draw and three defeats. They have scored nine goals and conceded thirteen. Deportivo have reached ten points with two wins, four draws and one defeat, scoring ten goals and conceding eight. This difference in defensive statistics explains why Deportivo are one position ahead of the hosts before their head-to-head encounter.

The season is still in its early stages, but the standings are tight enough for a single result to significantly change perceptions of the opening weeks. Real Sociedad want to prove that their victory in Valencia was not an isolated highlight, while Deportivo have an opportunity to show that their return to the top flight has been about more than just initial momentum.

Real Sociedad: victory in Valencia and defensive questions

The team managed by Pellegrino Matarazzo played their last league match before the international break on September 20, defeating Valencia CF 3-2. It was their second away victory in the league: at the beginning of September, Real Sociedad also beat Elche CF 3-2. Between those victories came a 0-3 home defeat against Atlético de Madrid, a reminder that attacking efficiency is not always matched by equally reliable defending.

In their home matches at Anoeta so far, Real Sociedad have beaten RCD Espanyol 2-1, drawn 0-0 with RC Celta and lost to Atlético de Madrid. This means Deportivo's visit represents not only an opportunity to collect points but also a chance to secure their second home league victory of the season. Matarazzo extended his contract in July until the summer of 2028, and his team face the task of finding a balance between their desire to control matches and the need to close down spaces more effectively after losing possession.

Luka Sučić enters the match as Real Sociedad's leading league scorer with three goals. Ander Barrenetxea has two, while Mikel Oyarzabal has recorded two assists so far this season. Carlos Soler, who links midfield and attack, and Sergio Gómez, the club's leader in league passes, are also important. Against a well-organized Deportivo side, particular attention will be paid to who can accelerate the movement of the ball from midfield into the final third.

Deportivo: solid start, but attack without its top scorer

Antonio Hidalgo has guided RC Deportivo to seventh place after seven rounds. The team from A Coruña began the season with draws against Elche CF and Málaga CF, then defeated Valencia CF 3-1 and Villarreal CF 3-2 away from home. A draw against Getafe CF followed, along with a 0-1 defeat to Sevilla FC and a 1-1 draw against Real Betis. That latest encounter demonstrated Deportivo's ability to fight back: Diego Villares equalized against Betis in the closing stages of the match.

The attacking statistics of this team contain a particularly important detail. Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang has scored five of Deportivo's ten league goals and provided two assists, but he will miss the match at Anoeta through injury. Zakaria Eddahchouri is also unavailable, leaving Hidalgo with an unusually limited selection of central strikers. The club's match preview stated that Nsongo Bil is the only available first-team striker for this fixture. A greater creative burden therefore falls on Mario Soriano, David Mella, Yeremay and Luismi Cruz.

Absences and squad availability

Before traveling to Anoeta, the visitors' biggest problem is not their points total but the physical condition of their attacking players. Deportivo confirmed three absences on October 10, while Real Sociedad had released a medical update concerning an important defensive player several days earlier.

  • Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang - Deportivo cannot count on their top scorer, who has scored five goals in seven league rounds.
  • Zakaria Eddahchouri - also misses the away fixture due to injury; his absence further limits the attacking options.
  • Marc Casadó - another confirmed Deportivo absentee, important for the team's options in central midfield.
  • Jon Mikel Aramburu - Real Sociedad announced an injury to the soleus muscle in his right leg; his availability depends on his recovery, so his participation should not be taken for granted.
  • Lorenzo Amatucci - after individual training during the break, he is available to Deportivo again, although the coach warned that an assessment is needed to determine how many minutes he can play.

Where the match could be decided

Real Sociedad have attempted 64 shots in their first seven matches, compared with Deportivo's 55, and the hosts also have a slightly higher total number of passes. These statistics suggest that Matarazzo's team may build attacks through possession more frequently, while Hidalgo will try to maintain compact distances between the lines and launch quick transitions after winning the ball. This is an expectation based on performances so far, not a confirmed indication of the starting lineups.

For Real Sociedad, the key could be patient build-up through the middle. If Deportivo close down the space around Oyarzabal, attacks down the flanks and Sučić's runs into the penalty area will become important. The hosts must not lose their defensive discipline during transitions: thirteen goals conceded already indicate that opponents are finding ways to threaten their goal. For Deportivo, the key is supporting Bil. If he remains isolated between the central defenders, the visitors could struggle to retain possession long enough for the rest of the team to move out of defence.

Head-to-head meetings offer an interesting reminder

The two clubs have not met frequently in the top division in recent years. Their previous league encounter took place in February 2018, when Real Sociedad won 5-0 at Anoeta. The last five league meetings before this fixture produced results showing that home advantage has not always guaranteed a convincing performance.

  • February 2, 2018 - Real Sociedad 5-0 Deportivo.
  • September 10, 2017 - Deportivo 2-4 Real Sociedad.
  • April 23, 2017 - Real Sociedad 1-0 Deportivo.
  • December 5, 2016 - Deportivo 5-1 Real Sociedad.
  • January 16, 2016 - Real Sociedad 1-1 Deportivo.

Real Sociedad won three of those five encounters, Deportivo won one, and one ended in a draw; the squads have changed considerably since then.

Anoeta: stands close to the pitch and pre-match activities

Anoeta Stadium is located at Paseo de Anoeta 1 in San Sebastián, on the coast of the Bay of Biscay. It opened in 1993, after Real Sociedad left Atotxa, and subsequent renovations brought spectators closer to the pitch by removing the athletics track. Its capacity is listed at approximately 40,000 seats. Although the stadium is also used for other events, on a league matchday the focus is on the football stands and the surrounding areas.

The loudest section of home support is associated with the southern Aitor Zabaleta stand, an area dedicated to singing and rhythmic support throughout the match. On the opposite side of the stadium is the family stand, while the central sections offer a different perspective of the game. First-time visitors should check the sector indicated on their ticket and choose their approach to the stadium accordingly.

Real Sociedad have also prepared a specific programme for this Sunday. Two Topagunea areas, near entrances 8 and 13, will operate from 14:15 to 19:00, featuring live music and food and drink offerings. Inside the stadium's lower concourse, Zalegunea will open with an expanded selection of refreshments when the gates open at 15:15. Some of the new sales outlets accept card payments only. Before the match, a tribute to goalkeeper Alazne Estensoro is also planned, and she will take the ceremonial kick-off as part of the pre-match programme.

Getting to the match: train, bus and parking

For visitors using public transport, it is important to know that Anoeta station on the Euskotren network is located next to the stadium. Specifically for the match against Deportivo, the new station entrance hall will be brought into use, with special arrangements for the return journey after the match. The direction of travel determines which entrance passengers should use to reach the platforms, so it is useful to remember this before entering the stadium.

  • Euskotren towards Hendaia - after the match, passengers should use the new entrance hall at Anoeta station.
  • Euskotren towards Lasarte-Oria - passengers should use the main entrance to the old entrance hall; trains towards Usurbil and Zumaia are accessed from the Gasca side.
  • Buses from the city centre - Dbus has announced routes 21, 26 and 28, as well as the special FT route from Boulevard and Urbieta, with increased services from 15:15.
  • Buses from other neighbourhoods - additional connections depart from Gros, Antiguo, Altza and the Intxaurrondo and Egia areas; route 17 and special departures are planned from Gros.
  • Car - parking spaces are available near the stadium, but there are also regulated on-street parking zones. The Illunbe car park can be a practical alternative, with pedestrian access to the Anoeta area.

After the match, city bus services towards the neighbourhoods will be increased again, although a section of the road network is scheduled to be temporarily closed for the first fifteen minutes for safety reasons. Those continuing their journey by train should follow signs for their direction rather than automatically heading towards the entrance they used on arrival. It is advisable to compare the announcement of special departures with current transport information if the journey continues beyond the city.

Entrances, arrival times and useful details

The gates at Anoeta will open at 15:15 for this match. The ticket offices near the membership office, close to the Atano III fronton, are scheduled to operate from 14:15 to 16:15. The club states that the stadium can also be accessed using a digital membership card in NFC format, and the updated version must be used for this season. Bicycle parking near the Amara Berri school will be available from the opening of the gates until half an hour after the end of the match.

It is particularly useful to know that the exhibition of the club's cup trophy, which can be visited on other days around the match, will be closed on Sunday precisely because the fixture is taking place. Visitors who had planned to combine a visit to the exhibition with attending the match should adjust their plans. On the other hand, the programme near entrances 8 and 13 offers activities before the referee's opening whistle, without having to spend the entire time waiting outside one's own sector.

For away supporters, it is important to remain in the section of the stadium indicated on their ticket, while all visitors should allow enough time to check their entrance and pass through security screening. It is worth securing tickets in good time and checking sector details before traveling, as arriving without a clear plan often leads to unnecessary walking around the stadium.

San Sebastián beyond the stadium

San Sebastián, also known as Donostia, lies on the northern coast of Spain and attracts visitors with its seaside promenades, historic old town and gastronomy. Those arriving early can enjoy a walk along La Concha Bay and explore the streets of Parte Vieja, where bars are famous for their pintxos. Monte Urgull offers views of the coast and the city, but visitors planning to explore it before the match should allow enough time to return towards Anoeta before traffic congestion begins.
