Fenerbahçe can't win in Europe, lets go of 2-0 home lead

TheTools · 20.10.2026 18:45 · source · permalink

Fenerbahçe drew 2-2 at home to Slavia Prague on Tuesday, having built a two-goal lead.

This result did not give them the opportunity to avenge the 6-0 defeat to Aston Villa last week nor any positive impetus before next Monday's big Turkish clash against Galatasaray.

With a beautiful arcing shot, sending the ball into the top left corner of the goal, Oguz Aydin gave the home team a dream start.

Then, Mason Greenwood scored from close range after a good move by Nélson Semedo.

However, Nathan Aké was forced to leave the game due to a knee injury, just before 20 minutes of play.

Slavia Prague quickly bounced back after a disastrous start, as Lukas Provod narrowed the lead to 2-1, following a careless mistake by Mattéo Guendouzi, who lost the ball inside his team's penalty area.

Guendouzi, Aydin and Vedat Muriqi came close to scoring Fenerbahçe's third goal, but the first half in Istanbul ended goalless.

After the break, the guests rushed in search of an equalizer.

The Turkish defense stopped Wiktor Nowak at the last moment after an hour of play, while a little later Romelu Lukaku replaced Muriqi in attack.

Slavia Prague was the better team in the second half.

As Fenerbahçe fans were increasingly confident of victory, Slavia Prague significantly increased the pressure in the final minutes.

Left-back Archie Brown was quite active on the left wing, but the 3-1 result, which the home team wanted, was not achieved.

Slavia Prague continued to attack until the end, while Fenerbahçe focused mainly on counterattacks.

Then, Pavel Kacor beat Ederson from close range, after a precise arc from the right wing, causing the Turkish team another disappointment in the Champions League. /GazetaExpress/
