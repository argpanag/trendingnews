Fnatic's 2011 LoL World Champion Cyanide Dies at 31

TheTools · 26.10.2026 18:18 · source · permalink

Lauri "Cyanide" Happonen, the Finnish player who won the first League of Legends World Championship with Fnatic in 2011, has died at 31, Finnish newspaper Ilta-Sanomat reported Monday.

(Riot Games)

Lauri "Cyanide" Happonen, the Finnish player who won the first League of Legends World Championship with Fnatic in 2011, has died at 31, Finnish newspaper Ilta-Sanomat reported Monday.

Happonen died July 31, according to Ilta-Sanomat, which said his death was confirmed by Finland's Digital and Population Data Services Agency, Dexerto reported. His cause of death has not been disclosed.

A Teenage World Champion

Happonen was 16 when Fnatic won the inaugural League of Legends World Championship in 2011. He went on to win three European league titles with the organization in 2013 and 2014, playing alongside some of the game's most recognizable names, including xPeke and YellOwStaR, before retiring from professional competition.

That first world title came 15 years before this year's World Championship, which is being played in Allen, Texas, with the knockout stage beginning Nov. 3, as RG.org reported.

Former Teammates Pay Tribute

Former Fnatic teammate Bora "YellOwStaR" Kim was among those who paid tribute after the news broke.

YellOwStaR said the two had last seen each other at a League of Legends showmatch in July, days before Happonen's death. "We saw each other again at the showmatch in July, and he was still full of dreams, plans, and projects for the future," he wrote.

Happonen is the second member of Fnatic's 2011 championship roster to die, following Maciej "Shushei" Ratuszniak, who died in April 2025 at 36, according to Dexerto.
