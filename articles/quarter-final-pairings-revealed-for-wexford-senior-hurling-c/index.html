Quarter-final pairings revealed for Wexford Senior hurling championship | Wexford Weekly

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:05 · source · permalink

We’re now into the quarter-final stages of the Wexford Senior hurling championship…

The preliminary quarter-finals took place over the week. This saw both Faythe Harriers and St Annes progress to the final eight. They defeated Oulart-The Ballagh and Glynn-Barntown respectively.

Meanwhile, the relegation final also took place. The Rapparees have been relegated to the Intermediate ranks after losing out to St Mogues Fethard. Just five years ago, the Rapps won the Wexford SHC — which shows just how competitive the Wexford SHC race is.

Quarter-finals – Wexford SHC:

Shelmaliers vs Faythe Harriers

Rathnure vs St Annes Rathangan

St Martins vs Ferns

Oylegate-Glenbrien vs Naomh Eanna

_

The quarter-finals are set to be played over the weekend of September 25th-27th.
