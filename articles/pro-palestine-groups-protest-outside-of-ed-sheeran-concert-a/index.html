Pro-Palestine groups protest outside of Ed Sheeran concert at Lincoln Financial Field

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:46 · source · permalink

PHILADELPHIA (WPVI) -- A protest took place outside Ed Sheeran's concert at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia on Saturday.

The protest was planned by a coalition including Shut Down D&Z, Writers Against the War on Gaza (WAWOG), No ICE Philly, and the Philadelphia Students for Justice in Palestine Coalition.

The protest follows the removal of rapper Macklemore, after he called for a "Free Palestine" and criticized Israel during an earlier show at MetLife Stadium.

Several other opening acts have also pulled out of the tour in response to Macklemore's removal.

Sheeran said the decision to cut Macklemore from the Loop Tour was the promoter's, not his own.

The Philadelphia Police Department said, as it does with all large events, it is working closely with the Linc and event organizers to ensure the safety of concertgoers, the employees and the people who live in the nearby neighborhoods.
