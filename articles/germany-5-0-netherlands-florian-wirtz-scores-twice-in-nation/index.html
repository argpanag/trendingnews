Germany 5-0 Netherlands: Florian Wirtz scores twice in Nations League

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:14 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores

Florian Wirtz (right) has scored eight goals for Germany since joining Liverpool - only one behind his total for the Reds

Florian Wirtz scored twice as Germany thrashed the Netherlands in their final Nations League game - with both sides qualifying for the quarter-finals.

Jurgen Klopp's side started the day third in Group A2 but overtook Greece and the Netherlands into top spot.

All three teams ended on 11 points - with results between them being used as the tiebreaker instead of goal difference.

The Netherlands would have finished top of the group had Germany only won 4-0.

Greece, who beat Serbia 1-0 thanks to a Giorgos Masouras goal, placed third.

Freiburg's Derry Scherhant scored his first international goal, curling a fine effort into the top corner in Berlin.

Liverpool attacking midfielder Wirtz won the ball before firing in Germany's second from outside the box.

Borussia Dortmund defender Nico Schlotterbeck headed in Brajan Gruda's free-kick to make it 3-0.

Gruda then cut the ball back for Wirtz to lash in his second goal.

And Gruda, on loan at Leipzig from Brighton, made it two goals in two caps - to go with his two assists in Berlin - as he made it 5-0.

It was Germany's biggest victory against their neighbours since 1959 when they won 7-0.

"We hope that we can make it fun for the fans. It was definitely fun for us, especially in the second half," Wirtz told German broadcaster ARD.

"We don't mind who we play. We want to play attacking football. We want to score goals and dominate the opponent - and I think we did that perfectly.

"We want to keep going next year. We want to get better. I think we've taken some great steps in our development as a team."

The Netherlands, who beat Greece 7-0 on Friday, finished second after their first defeat under Xavi Hernandez.
