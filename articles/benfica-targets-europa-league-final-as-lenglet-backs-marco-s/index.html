Benfica Targets Europa League Final as Lenglet Backs Marco Silva

TheTools · 26.11.2026 18:17 · source · permalink

Benfica are pursuing a place in the Europa League final after winning their first four league-phase matches, but coach Marco Silva has warned that Viktoria Plzen will pose a serious challenge. Ahead of their trip to the Czech Republic for the fifth round, defender Clément Lenglet also expressed confidence in the Portuguese side's ability to reach the final in Frankfurt.

According to 24horas.pt, Benfica face a Viktoria Plzen team that has recorded four wins and one draw across its last five matches. Silva has rejected any suggestion that his players can approach the fixture without full concentration.

"There is no reason for us to think it will be easy," said Marco Silva, Benfica's head coach.

The coach also declined to confirm whether Trubin or Samuel Soares would start in goal. He addressed Gianluca Prestianni's absence, explaining that the Argentine experienced adductor fatigue following the match against Torreense.

"It was a matter of management and not taking risks," Silva said.

Silva expects Prestianni to return for Benfica's match against Famalicão on Monday. He also acknowledged the importance of Fredrik Aursnes and Leandro Barreiro, whose absences have affected the team's available options.

"I miss both of them very much," Silva said.

Aursnes has partially returned to team training, while Barreiro is expected to need several more weeks before becoming available for competitive matches. Silva also maintained that Benfica still have considerable room for improvement despite their progress, reserving a definitive assessment of the team's development until the end of the season.

In separate comments to Sport TV ahead of the Viktoria Plzen fixture, Lenglet said the weather in the Czech Republic should not distract Benfica from their immediate objective. Temperatures had fallen to minus 1 degree Celsius, with light snowfall expected.

"The biggest obstacle is not the weather, it is the opponent. Of course, it is colder here than in Portugal, but we are professionals and must adapt to these conditions," said Lenglet.

The French center back believes Benfica have the quality to compete for the Europa League trophy, although he stressed that the team must demonstrate its capabilities throughout the competition.

"There is a long way to go to reach the final. We have the conditions to get there, but we have to prove it in every game. We are at the beginning of a long journey, we have to win today and take it step by step. Only then will we be happy at the end of the year," Lenglet said.

Lenglet was named among the substitutes against Viktoria Plzen, with Circati ahead of him in the defensive selection. He nevertheless praised Silva's approach to squad rotation, highlighting the opportunities given to players throughout the season.

"The coach works well with the entire squad. Everyone has had playing time since the beginning of the season, which is difficult to achieve, and we have also been winning games. The last game we lost was against FC Porto, mainly because of my sending-off. We have a group of 23 or 24 players, not just 13 or 14," Lenglet said.
