The Mandalorian and Grogu opens big on Disney Plus - Fantha Tracks | Daily Star Wars News

TheTools · 18.09.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

It’s finally docked on Disney Plus, and that last port of call for The Mandalorian and Grogu has seen the film amass 727 million minutes of viewing time, enough to put it at number 2 on the movie streaming charts, behind only The Whisper Man on Netflix. Not too shabby, and it shall be interesting to see the knock-on effect it has on The Mandalorian and the other streaming shows on the network.
