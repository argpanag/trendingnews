Team news: Tzolis returns to action at Anfield

TheTools · 01.11.2026 16:41 · source · permalink

Winger Christos Tzolis is straight back into the starting line up to face Liverpool at Anfield in the Premier League on Sunday.

Our summer signing had missed the last six games after suffering a hamstring injury while on international duty at the start of last month.

Unsurprisingly manager Mikel Arteta makes a host of changes to the side that played in the Carabao Cup in midweek. Indeed of the XI who started at Fleetwood, only Jurrien Timber and Kai Havertz retain their places.

Riccardo Calafiori and Bruno Guimaraes are both recalled to the starting line up after neither were named from the start in our last Premier League game, against Everton last weekend. Noni Madueke is also back to complete the front three with Tzolis and Havertz.

The hosts also make several changes after their midweek cup game – which they won on penalties against Chelsea at Anfield.

Goalkeeper Alisson returns, as do Jeremy Jacquet, Milos Kerkez, Florian Wirtz, Alexis Mac Allister, Bradley Barcola and Alexander Isak. Dominik Szoboszlai is fit enough to return to the bench.

Arsenal: Raya, Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori, Rice, Bruno, Odegaard, Madueke, Tzolis, Havertz.

Subs: Arrizabalaga, Mosquera, White, Hincapie, Eze, Gyokeres, Merino, Zubimendi, Lewis-Skelly.

Liverpool: Alisson, Araujo, Jacquet, van Dijk, Kerkez, Mac Allister, Nyoni, Wirtz, Gakpo, Barcola, Isak.

Subs: Mamardashvilli, Gomez, Endo, Szoboszlai, Tsimikas, Munoz, Gravenberch, Koumas, Ngumoha.
