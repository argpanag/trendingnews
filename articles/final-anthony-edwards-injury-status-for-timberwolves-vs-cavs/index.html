Anthony Edwards (knee) will play for the T-Wolves against the Cavs.

Anthony Edwards still gets the green light for the Minnesota Timberwolves this Monday against the Cleveland Cavaliers.

Ahead of the key showdown in Cleveland against the Cavs, the Wolves have upgraded Edwards' status to available (Right Knee Injury Maintenance).

Anthony Edwards has been initially listed as questionable against the Cleveland Cavaliers. Despite worries by many regarding the sudden appearance of the knee issues, the superstar will still suit up for the Minnesota Timberwolves.

Chris Finch already informed reporters that Edwards' knee woes aren't a long-term concern.

Edwards serves as the driving force of the Timberwolves' hot season start, averaging 28.7 points, 5.0 rebounds and 3.3 assists. Behind his lead, Minnesota holds a perfect 3-0 record heading into the first week of the new NBA season.

With him available, the Wolves can't be more confident about extending their win streak and keeping their momentum going.
