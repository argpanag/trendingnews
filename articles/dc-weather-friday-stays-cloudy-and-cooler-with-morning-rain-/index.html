WASHINGTON (7News) — Washington, D.C., will begin Friday, Sept. 11, with clouds and a small chance of a lingering morning shower. That means anyone heading to a morning 9/11 remembrance should consider an umbrella just in case, but rain chances will fade quickly.

The rest of the day stays mostly cloudy, with temperatures climbing into the low 80s. A light northeast breeze will help lower the humidity through the afternoon, making Friday evening feel noticeably more comfortable. Expect temperatures to fall into the lower 70s during the evening and 60s overnight.

As we pause to remember the lives lost and the courage shown on Sept. 11, 2001, the weather looks quiet for outdoor ceremonies after the morning hours.

The weekend is not a washout, but keep the umbrella nearby. Saturday brings the better chance for scattered showers and thunderstorms, especially late afternoon into Sunday morning. Some places may receive a soaking rain, while others see only a passing shower. Sunday will turn warmer and more humid, with a few showers or storms still possible—mainly early in the day.

  • Friday forecast: Mostly cloudy after a few early showers. High near 83. Lower humidity by afternoon.
  • Friday night: Mostly cloudy and comfortable. Low near 68.
  • Saturday: Humid with scattered showers and thunderstorms, especially later in the day. High near 81.
  • Sunday: Warmer and more tropical, with a few scattered showers or storms possible. High near 86.
