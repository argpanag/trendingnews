Semi-truck fire causes delays on foggy Highway 97C east of Merritt

TheTools · 18.11.2026 17:16 · source · permalink

November 18 2026 - A semi-truck fully engulfed in flames is causing minor delays on Highway 97C (Okanagan Connector) this morning between Kane Valley Road and Highway 5A, near Aspen Grove east of Merritt.

DriveBC lists the incident as minor, with traffic control in place in both directions while crews assess the scene.

Drivers are advised to expect delays and watch for emergency vehicles.

The fire is burning in heavy fog, with motorists also reporting icy conditions on the Connector between Kelowna and Merritt.
