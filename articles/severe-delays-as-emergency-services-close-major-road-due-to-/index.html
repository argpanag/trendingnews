Severe delays as emergency services CLOSE major road due to incident

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:39 · source · permalink

Motorists travelling towards Essex are facing major disruption after a crash brought traffic to a standstill on the M25.

All lanes have been stopped on the anticlockwise carriageway between Junction 3 for the M20 at Swanley and Junction 2 for the A2 at Darenth following a collision.

The incident was first reported at around 10.06am on Sunday, October 4, with traffic unable to pass the scene while emergency services respond.

The closure is likely to have a knock-on effect for drivers heading towards the Dartford Crossing and Essex, with delays expected to build quickly on the approach to the incident.

It is not yet known how many vehicles are involved or whether anyone has been injured.
