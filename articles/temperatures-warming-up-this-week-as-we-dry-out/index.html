Temperatures warming up this week as we dry out

TheTools · 25.10.2026 14:24 · source · permalink

Actions

  • Facebook
  • Tweet
  • Email

Showers and maybe a couple of thunderstorms will move through tonight and continue into early Monday morning. The best chance for rain will be across the eastern plains and mountains, while the Denver area should see less activity. Some areas could pick up a decent amount of rain, but most of Denver will likely stay fairly dry.

Mountain areas will see a little snow overnight, with generally light accumulations expected. The northern mountains could pick up a few inches, while areas farther south will see less. A few of the higher mountain passes could be slick through early Monday morning, so keep that in mind if you’re traveling.

Showers should move out early Monday, with some sunshine returning later in the day. Low clouds and patchy fog could stick around through the morning, keeping temperatures on the cool side. Overall, Monday will be a little chilly before things start to improve.

Tuesday through Thursday look much nicer, with mostly dry weather, more sunshine, and a gradual warm-up. Temperatures will climb through the week, with highs getting close to 70 degrees across the plains and Denver area by Wednesday and Thursday. It should be a pretty pleasant stretch of fall weather.

Looking ahead to next weekend, there’s a chance unsettled weather could return, though the forecast is still uncertain. For now, western and southern Colorado look more likely to see rain and snow, while areas farther north and east may stay mostly dry. We’ll have a better idea of how things shape up as the weekend gets closer.
