Larry’s BC Handicapping Challenge

The rules are each contestant will handicap both the Breeders Cup Turf and the Breeders Cup Classic. Plus 4 other races. Our contestants Joe Withee, John Lindley and Vince Bruun each took 4 of the remaining 12 races and you can see their picks below or go to our Breeders Cup coverage on the link above and you can read their reasoning behind their picks.

Each contestant has 6 races selected so Larry will also compete and will use the Turf and Classic along with four randomly selected races. Each selection will be assigned a $2 win, place, and show, or the $6 combo. Total winnings will determine the winner.
