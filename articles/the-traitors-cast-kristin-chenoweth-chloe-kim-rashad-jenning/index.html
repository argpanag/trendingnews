‘The Traitors’ Cast: Kristin Chenoweth, Chloe Kim, Rashad Jennings, Whitney Leavitt and More Join Peacock Hit

TheTools · 18.09.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

The reality television hit, which was just renewed for three additional seasons, is set to return in 2027.

The Traitors‘ next celebrity season is set to be released in 2027, and Peacock has unveiled its star-studded cast.

Musical theater icon Kristin Chenoweth, Olympic gold medalist Chloe Kim, The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives Leavitt and former NFL player Rashad Jennings are among the upcoming season. The next season will feature much more Hollywood talent, including several actors, than normal. There’s also plenty of love for the Bravo-universe this season, although that’s no surprise.

The new season’s full cast is: RuPaul’s Drag Race’s Bianca Del Rio, content creator Boman Martinez-Reid, The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City’s Bronwyn Newport, actress Casey Wilson, Kim, singer Cody Simpson, Southern Charm’s Craig Conover, The Real Housewives of Atlanta’s Cynthia Bailey, actor Dominic Monaghan, Next Gen NYC’s Emira D’Spain, The Real Housewives of Potomac’s Gizelle Bryant, Love Island’s Iain Stirling, Big Brother and Amazing Race’s Jag Bains, Love Island USA’s JaNa Craig, The Bachelor’s Joey Graziadei, actor Johnny Sibilly, Chenoweth, Survivor and The Challenge’s Michele Fitzgerald, Survivor’s Q Burdette, The Challenge’s Rachel Robinson, Jennings, Big Brother and Amazing Race’s Taylor Hale and Leavitt.

The Traitors — based on a Dutch format — is a competition where contestants are split into two categories: Faithfuls and Traitors. All 23 contestants will start as Faithfuls before Cumming decides which (typically) three players will become Traitors. The titular role then works to sneak past the rule-following players who are attempting to figure out who the Traitors are while choosing a Faithful player each night to “murder.” Studio Lambert produces the series, which utilizes reality TV stars and other media personalities for its contestant pool.

Its fourth season, which found Love Island U.S. alum Rob Rausch as its victor, scored the show its biggest ratings to-date, according to Peacock. Viewership was reported to have surged 72 percent over the prior season.

The Traitors has already been renewed for another three seasons following the upcoming celebrity season, bringing its total expected seasons to at least nine. In the wake of The Traitors success, NBC has debuted a civilian version of the series, The Traitors: New Blood, which premiered Thursday (Sept. 17). This is technically considered the show’s fifth season, making the celebrity version its sixth. The Traitors: New Blood streams next day on Peacock.
