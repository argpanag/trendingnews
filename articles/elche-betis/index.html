Real Betis have a great opportunity to cement their top-four position when they travel to Elche, who are fighting to avoid spending the international break in the relegation zone.

Match News and Current Form

Elche suffered one of their worst results of the season in the last round when they lost 1-0 away to fellow strugglers Valencia. They start the round level with the bottom-three on points and given that they play two of the current top-four in their next three games, a spot in the relegation zone could well await them. Elche were unbeaten in five matches across all competitions prior to the Valencia loss (W3, D2), but they’re yet to win a league game at home this season (D1, L4) and are subsequently on the verge of recording their longest winless home run in La Liga since 2022/23.

Real Betis have been on the slide since opening their La Liga campaign with five wins from their first six games (L1), picking up just two victories from their five matches since (D2, L1), while they were also beaten in midweek UEFA Champions League action by Borussia Dortmund. Manager Manuel Pellegrini will be putting his personal unbeaten away record at Elche in La Liga (W2, D2) on the line as he heads to Estadio Martínez Valero with a team on their longest unbeaten away run of 2026 in this competition (W1, D2), but failing to win their last three road fixtures in all competitions (D2, L1) points to a growing vulnerability on their travels. 

Head-to-Head History

Elche are winless in four La Liga H2Hs (D1, L3), last going longer without a win against Betis in this competition in 1962. Betis are unbeaten in their last six La Liga trips to Elche (W2, D4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Ten of Elche’s 14 league goals this term came after half-time.

Elche opened the scoring in only three La Liga games this season but are unbeaten when doing so (W2, D1).

Five of Betis’ last six competitive away matches saw both teams score.

Betis have trailed at half-time only once this league season (HT: W5, D5).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Fer Niño has scored two of Elche’s four home La Liga goals this season, but his impact may be felt later on with only one of his six league goals in 2026/27 being their first of the match. The same can be said for Betis’ Pablo Fornals, who has seen each of his last 12 goals for the club come beyond the 45th minute.

Elche’s Thomas Lemar was forced off injured last week, while Betis’ Álvaro Fidalgo is a doubt.

Betting Analysis

Betis’ strong starts have underpinned their success, so there is a compelling case to back the Real Betis half-time/full-time double.
