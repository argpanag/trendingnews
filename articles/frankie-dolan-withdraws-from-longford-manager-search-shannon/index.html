Dolan withdraws from Longford manager process

Frankie Dolan has pulled out of the process to become new Longford SFC manager.

Dolan took to social media to announce his withdrawal from the process. Dolan having told Shannonside radio he was in the mix for the jobs just two weeks ago.

Dolan posted on his facebook page.

“Hi All, following a week of speculation and numerous messages and calls of support and encouragement, I just wanted to give everyone an update on my position in relation to the Longford senior football manager role.

After careful consideration, I spoke with the chairman of the Longford county board earlier this week and have decided that I won’t be going forward for the role.

I’d like thank everyone who has been in touch, and also Longford GAA for approaching me about the position. I wish them all the very best going forward”.

Frankie.
