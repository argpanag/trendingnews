  1. 'I've been living my dream'published at 16:51 BST
    16:51 BST

    More from Solheim Cup-winning captain Anna Nordqvist on how she felt when Europe clinched victory: "I think there's been so many emotions bottled up. It's been such a long road. It's been a lot of hard work.

    "Last night was hard because I really hadn't had a moment to stop and I think I sat down to eat for 10 minutes all day yesterday, so my vice-captains really picked me up. There's been a couple of good speeches in the team room last night and everyone was so excited to go and play today."

    On who made the speeches: "I had a little bit of a short and sweet speech and Anne [van Dam], the comeback the European team had in the juniors was incredible, so she shared some thoughts and mentality and Mel [Reid] and [Caroline] Hedwall. It's been amazing doing this journey with them. I couldn't be more proud and more honoured to do this journey with them.

    "I've been living my dream. I had the opportunity to go to the United States in 2006 and I'm forever grateful for everything that's happened. Living my dream, it doesn't feel like I've worked a day in my life. Having my family support from far away, living in the US alone but having them here at every single Solheim Cup and now sharing it with my nieces and nephews, seeing my brothers cry, my sister-in-law it's always been a family affair for me. I said it in my speech that what makes the Solheim Cup special is sharing it with others."

  2. 'I was impressed with every single player'published at 16:49 BST
    16:49 BST

    Solheim Cup-winning captain Anna Nordqvist: "This feels so good. This has been so much hard work this week and in the last two years leading up to this, not only me but all the background team. The help has been amazing this week. We had a really good week,. Everyone's been good teammates and leaving the course yesterday, we were really excited to come here today and wow, was I impressed with every single player and caddy turning up to the first tee ready to play.

    "The rain was good mojo for us. There was a lot of blue early. The way the golf course was, I'm just really proud of the players for starting strong and finishing strong.

    "This was the no-brainer to put Charley [Hull] first. We saw the stats yesterday and Charley and Lottie [Woad] were actually top four players who made the most birdies in the week. They didn't score many points but they played really tough matches.

    "People don't understand how good these players are and how good the golf has been in the last three days. It was a no-brainer and then finishing strong with Carlota [Cigunda], we know she's done it in the past and to see Lottie finish a good week. She's been playing so good this week and not really getting anything off it. Holing the winning putt, I think that's going to be hard to forget for her."

  5. Singles scorespublished at 16:34 BST
    16:34 BST

    Here's the scores on the doors - and none of the 12 singles matches ended up all square today.

    A 7-5 singles success stretches Europe's unbeaten record in Sunday sessions to six Solheim Cups, with America's last success coming back in 2015 in Germany.

    That middle bunch of Europe wins did the damage for Anna Nordqvist's team, with Nanna Koerstz Madsen returning to form to pick up a point, then three rookies in a row doing the business.

    Nastasia Nadaud, Julia Lopez Ramirez and then Lottie Woad sealing the deal - brilliant stuff from that trio as Solheim Cup singles action is not for the feint hearted.

    They build rookie differently these days.

  6. Europe win the Solheim Cuppublished at 16:31 BST
    16:31 BST

    Europe 15-13 USA

    And there we have it - it's official. Europe win the Solheim Cup with a 15-13 success over the USA at Bernardus Golf Club in the Netherlands.

    It's a close one, as we knew it would be, but Europe really showed up in the singles today and on the balance of the week they've played the better golf so that result looks and feels just about right.

    Well played to Anna Nordqvist's side - the rookies were superb and the big names played their part today. The rain couldn't dampen the crowd's spirits either.

    What a day.

  10. 'Very happy to do it for Anna'published at 16:20 BST
    16:20 BST

    Team Europe's Carlota Ciganda on how it feels to make the win official for her team: "So happy. Very happy for Anna [Nordqvist], the rookies. I think it's been one of the best teams we've had in the last few years. Everyone is a unit, a team, that's what Europe is about and we had a great time. Some great matches, lots of birdies and very happy to do it for Anna.

    "I don't know what happens at Solheim but I make a lot of putts. I need to take this mentality into regular tournaments.

    "Today I was really motivated to beat her [Kupcho] because I knew it was going to be important. I did very well and very happy."

    On Nordqvist: "She's a good friend. I've known her for more than 20 years. We played lots of amateur golf, professional, lots of Solheims. I think she's one of the most competitive players that I know. I think she was prepared, very organised. She had everything in her mind. She had a game plan and I'm so happy that everything worked out. I couldn't be happier for her.

    "Vamos."
