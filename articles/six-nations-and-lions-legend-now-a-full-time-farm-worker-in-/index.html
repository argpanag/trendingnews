Six Nations and Lions legend now a full-time farm worker in new life

TheTools · 09.09.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

As a player, Rory Best was one of Ireland's finest captains.

Across 124 caps, the hooker achieved plenty. He won Six Nations titles, toured with the Lions twice and captained Ireland to victories over Australia, South Africa and New Zealand.

But, following his retirement from the sport in 2019, Best moved back to his first dream - following in his father's footsteps on the family farm.

As a youngster, Best only had humble ambitions of playing locally while working on the family farm in Poyntzpass, County Armagh.

“When I was young there was no professional rugby – it only started in ’95,” he told AgriLand previously.

"Playing rugby, you come into contact with a lot of different people. You start off your life wanting to be a farmer who plays a bit of rugby for Banbridge and then that changes because you become professional."

He works alongside his father, John, and brother, Simon, managing an arable and beef enterprise that features a pedigree Aberdeen Angus herd.

Being tech-savvy, he's also become an ambassador for the Herdwatch agricultural app, using it for day-to-day stock management across the family operation.

“You’ll not have the same challenges you have in rugby; things will be very different," he said previously about moving into farming.

"I think you have to be realistic and know you will never fill that void – so you don’t try to. You just try to do something you can get your teeth into and enjoy.

“It will be nice to spend a little bit of time with my family and be able to enjoy Christmas now like a normal person – not worrying about training the next day.”

He's stayed involved in rugby too following his retirement - doing some media work and coaching.

In April 2021, Best was announced as a technical and high-performance skills coach for the Seattle Seawolves alongside former Ireland international and ex-Ospreys coach, Allen Clarke.

Then, last year saw him named Ulster's general manager - with Best being heavily involved in tempting former Ireland and Wallabies coach Joe Schmidt to Belfast for this season as coach development advisor.
