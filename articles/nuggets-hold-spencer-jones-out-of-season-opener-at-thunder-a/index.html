Nuggets hold Spencer Jones out of season opener at Thunder as hamstring injury lingers

TheTools · 23.10.2026 00:52 · source · permalink

Jones was on track to play all week, but the Nuggets decided to play it safe Thursday after he participated in their morning shootaround

OKLAHOMA CITY — After spending the week expecting Spencer Jones to be available for their season opener, the Nuggets decided last-minute to hold him out against the Thunder, fearing that his hamstring injury could worsen if he played.

“He went through the whole day, the whole process. We’re not gonna play him tonight,” coach David Adelman said Thursday night before the season tipped off at Paycom Center. “It’s not a serious thing, just precautionary. It’s one of those injuries that could turn out to be a serious thing.”

Jones, 24, missed the entire preseason with a left hamstring strain that he suffered during a player development workout at training camp. He told reporters last Sunday that he was on track to play in Oklahoma City but hadn’t been officially cleared yet. As late as Wednesday, Adelman was encouraged the 3-and-D wing would be available.

But after Jones participated in a shootaround Thursday, the Nuggets reevaluated him again and decided to play it safe.

“Today was one of those ‘longest days ever’ kind of things in the NBA. So we had a lot of opportunity to get him checked by the medical people,” Adelman said, referencing the late-night tip for national TV purposes. “And there was enough concern that you can’t lose your backup power forward on night one. I just think it’s a smart play. Not to mention, sometimes when guys don’t play, it brings opportunity. … I think it does educate to see your roster. So being careful with Spence, bringing opportunity to other people.”

Trevon Brazile played the lion’s share of minutes at backup four while Jones was sidelined this preseason. Jones is the second Nuggets reserve to start the year hurt, along with shooting guard Julian Strawther (knee).
