OKC Thunder fans urged to arrive early for Paycom Center home opener

TheTools · 22.10.2026 11:43 · source · permalink

Thunder fans heading to Paycom Center for Thursday night’s home opener against the Denver Nuggets should arrive early, follow arena bag restrictions and prepare for potential downtown traffic congestion from nearby construction.

Oklahoma City Thunder fans heading to the team’s home opener Thursday night should arrive early and review Paycom Center’s security policies before heading downtown, as large crowds and nearby construction could cause congestion.

The Thunder will host the Denver Nuggets at 8:30 p.m. at Paycom Center, marking the team’s return to Oklahoma City after a win on the road in San Antonio to open the season on Tuesday.

Inside the Paycom Center, arena officials are encouraging fans to arrive early as thousands are expected to gather downtown for the game.

The Paycom Center has a strict bag policy. Bags must measure less than 6 inches by 9 inches by 2 inches, including clear bags. Fans may bring Thunder jerseys and individual basketball cards into the arena. Signs and posters are permitted but cannot exceed 11 inches by 17 inches.

Construction near the arena, including that of the Continental Coliseum, could add to downtown traffic and congestion. Drivers are encouraged to plan ahead before commuting downtown for Thursday’s game.
