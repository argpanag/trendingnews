  • Tyler Perry's hit show Beauty in Black, previously canceled, is set to return for a fourth season with filming scheduled for fall 2026 in Atlanta and Chicago.

One of Tyler Perry's biggest hits on Netflix may be returning for a new season sooner than expected. The third season of the previously canceled Beauty in Black premiered on the streaming platform last month. After a hit season, it appears fans won't have to wait long to watch season 4 of the show.

Production schedules for fall 2026 suggest filming for Beauty in Black season 4 is scheduled to take place between October 19 and 30, 2026 (via What's on Netflix). The report also reveals that filming will take place across two major locations: Atlanta, Georgia, and Chicago, Illinois. As for when fans can expect the season to be released, the report speculates that Netflix may release it in spring or summer 2027 after wrapping up post-production work.

What else do we know about Tyler Perry's Beauty in Black Season 4?

Beauty in Black follows two women from the Bellary family as they navigate the complex maze of politics, crime, and haircare. The third season recently concluded its run with eight episodes, leaving plenty of questions for the next season to address.

While plot details are under wraps, it is expected to deal with the fallout of Horace and Norman's potential deaths, Charles and Varney's relationship, and the shooting at the club. Fans will have to wait for the next season to find out if Kimmie and Mallory made it out alive.

The show stars Taylor Polidore Williams, Crystle Stewart, Ricco Ross, Xavier Smalls, Amber Reign Smith, Julian Horton, Steven G. Norfleet, and more. It is produced by Angi Bones and Tony Strickland for Tyler Perry Studios, with Perry serving as director and executive producer.

The series has surpassed 1 billion hours of watch time, with the third season debuting at No. 2 on Netflix's English TV charts with 13 million views and remaining on the chart for over two months consecutively.

All three seasons of Beauty in Black are streaming on Netflix now.
