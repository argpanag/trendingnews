Real Madrid says Valverde may play after Lee Kang-in tackle sparks controversy

TheTools · 06.10.2026 11:20 · source · permalink

This only amplifies suspicions. News says Federico Valverde (28, Real Madrid), who was said to have been seriously injured by a tackle from Lee Kang-in (25, Atlético Madrid), is already preparing to return.

Spanish outlet Madrid Universal reported on the 6th (KST), "Real Madrid expects its three injured key players to be able to play in the Villarreal match. The Madrid club is waiting for the players' conditions to become clear ahead of the Saturday match," it reported.

The outlet quoted Melchor Luis of Spain's Cope, saying, "Real Madrid hopes Kylian Mbappé, Ibrahima Konaté and Valverde will be able to play in the La Liga match against Villarreal, and is closely monitoring the three players' physical conditions," adding, "Given the multiple problems already affecting the squad, whether they can play is particularly important."

The most striking name is Valverde's. Mbappé and Konaté were originally reported to have minor injuries, but Valverde's case is different. On the 22nd he had his ankle stepped on hard by a tackle from Lee Kang-in in the 2026-27 La Liga seventh-round Madrid derby against Atlético Madrid.

Lee Kang-in was lucky. Because he dangerously stepped on an opponent's ankle, a red card could have been issued. But because Valverde did not fall and continued playing, referee Ortiz Arias let it pass. Video assistant referee (VAR) did not intervene either.

The decision sparked major controversy after the match. Real Madrid posted the tackle clip on the club's official social media account and protested strongly, and Real Madrid coach José Mourinho angrily pulled out a printed copy of the incident after the game, saying it was "clearly a red card."

He even announced that Valverde had suffered a "serious trauma," repeatedly criticizing Lee Kang-in and the referee's decision. In the process, some fans' critical messages toward Lee Kang-in also appeared. Later, when Real Madrid revealed after additional tests that Valverde had been diagnosed with an "ankle third-degree sprain," Real Madrid fans' anger grew even more.

But questions arose. While Real Madrid described it as the most severe third-degree sprain, they also predicted Valverde could recover in two to three weeks. Since such injuries normally require about two months to heal, that was hard to accept. Ruben Uria also said, "Two to three weeks? I've never seen this. It's Coach Pintus's miracle at Real Madrid. Another miracle?" expressing disbelief.

Moreover, the fact that it happened during an international break added to suspicions. Fans of other clubs suspected Real Madrid had exaggerated the announcement to prevent Valverde from being called up to the Uruguay national team. It is also mysterious that Valverde reportedly played the full 90 minutes afterward without issue.

Former Spain national team goalkeeper Santiago Cañizares also pointed out, "With a third-degree sprain you cannot continue playing after being injured," saying Valverde walking off seemingly fine did not match a diagnosis of a third-degree sprain.

As expectations grew locally that Valverde could play in the Villarreal match on the 11th, criticism of Real Madrid increased. The criticism is that the club intends to have him rest only during the international break and then play him.

Madrid Universal reported, "Valverde's situation could be the most difficult among the three players," while adding, "Real Madrid initially expected Valverde would need about two to three weeks to recover. If recovery follows this schedule, playing in the Villarreal match would be tight, but the club still hopes he can return in time."
