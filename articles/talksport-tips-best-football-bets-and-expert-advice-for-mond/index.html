Bet of the Day

Newcastle United to beat Leeds United @ 15/8

Prior to a ball being kicked this season, many believed Newcastle would struggle due to Eddie Howe leaving along with the trio of Anthony Gordon, Bruno Guimaraes and Sandro Tonali.

Under new coach Matthias Jaissle, however, the Magpies have enjoyed a decent start to the new campaign. Not only are they unbeaten across five games in all competitions, but Jaissle has the club playing some excellent football.

Leeds have also begun the season in decent fashion, winning once and drawing two of their three league matches. Daniel Farke’s side did suffer a 6-3 defeat to Chelsea in the EFL Cup last time out though, despite leading 2-0.

Newcastle have not lost to Leeds since January 2021 and an away win would allow Jaissle to keep building momentum.

Villarreal vs Real Betis - Troy Parrott to score, Both teams to score and over 2.5 goals @ 5/2

Both teams enter this clash after contrasting results in the Champions League. Villarreal suffered a 3-2 defeat to Borussia Dortmund, while Real Betis sealed an impressive 3-2 triumph over Lille.

Troy Parrott scored in that victory for Betis, four days after netting the winner against Real Madrid in La Liga. There is no doubt he will be filled with confidence, so backing him to find the back of the net once more is the perfect way to start this bet builder.

Six of the previous seven games between these sides has seen both teams scoring. Both teams have scored in four of Villarreal’s five games this term, while two of Betis’ last three games would have seen this bet land.

Regarding goals, three or more have also been scored in two of Betis’ three games, while the only match that hasn’t featured at least three goals for Villarreal was their 1-0 loss to Alaves.

Bet Builder

Roma -1 vs Torino, Como to defeat Parma and Inter Milan to win and both teams to score vs Udinese @ 7/1

Roma have started the Serie A campaign in wonderful fashion, opening up with two 4-0 wins over Fiorentina and Lecce, before defeating Atalanta 2-1 last time out.

Given the fact Torino have lost three of their first four league games, expect the capital side to secure another comfortable victory.

Another team who has begun the Serie A campaign well is Como. Cesc Fabregas’ men will have to contend with Champions League games this season also, but two wins and a draw from the first three games is solid.

Como have also won two of their last three meetings with Parma and with the away side sitting 16th in the table, it should be win number three for Fabregas and co.

Played three, won three. Inter Milan’s title defence has begun perfectly. Two of their Serie A wins have featured both teams scoring and with Udinese scoring eight goals in five matches, don’t be surprised to see them find the back of the net at the San Siro as they did on their last two trips to the champions.
