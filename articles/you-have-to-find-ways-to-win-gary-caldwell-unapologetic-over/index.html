‘You have to find ways to win’ – Gary Caldwell unapologetic over Wigan’s success

TheTools · 14.11.2026 21:20 · source · permalink

Gary Caldwell felt Wigan’s 2-1 victory over fellow League One strugglers Barnsley was “a huge victory” – particularly as he felt the Tykes had been the better side.

Fraser Murray put Wigan ahead nine minutes before the break with a cool finish into the far corner.

But Wigan were unable to build on the lead, and Barnsley found an equaliser 12 minutes after the interval through Cameron McGeehan.

With both sides desperate for the points to climb away from the foot of the league, it was Wigan who found a second goal with 15 minutes to go as Jensen Weir headed home Morgan Fox’s cross.

“It was a very tough game,” said Caldwell. “We knew before that Barnsley would prove to be very difficult opponents, and that proved to be the case.

“We were very good for five minutes in that first half, we managed to score in that time, but the rest of the half I thought both teams cancelled each other out.

“I didn’t think either side played well in the first half, and I thought they were the better team in the second half.

“But if we want to become the team we want to be, you have to find ways to win football matches.

“And I thought our spirit, our effort, our togetherness, our desire to win the game was sensational.

“Three or four weeks ago, there’s no doubt we lose that game, and I think that shows the progression the team is making.

“We found a way to win, we’ve scored two goals, our home form continues to be very good.

“So lots to take from it. It’s another win, but I know we can get better, and we will continue to improve.

“But that is a huge victory in terms of personality.”

Barnsley boss Daniel Stendel accused some of his players of being passive, and felt a third game in the space of seven days was a bridge too far.

“We were so passive in the first half,” he said. “We told them to be compact but that does not mean you don’t press or attack.

“We came back and scored then had momentum, but we conceded a second goal and had not really a chance for the second goal.

“Maybe for some players it was too much from the game on Wednesday. We had the feeling we would beat them after our goal.

“I had the feeling Wigan stood in front of the box and tried to defend.

“You can see also the pressure on them. But we conceded the goal.

“I will keep positive, work with the guys and be the best example but I can understand everybody is not happy.

“This was not the last big game but was a big game against a team also from the bottom and we didn’t take points so it’s frustrating for everybody.”
