Gardaí assessing credibility of threat sent to schools

TheTools · 14.10.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

Gardaí are assessing the contents and credibility of a threatening email sent to a number of schools today.

The Department of Education said it has consulted with gardaí, who have advised any school that received the email to call 999 and report the threat.

The department said schools can "continue to operate as normal" unless otherwise advised by gardaí.

Gardaí said they are engaging with a number of educational establishments and that "no further information is available at this time".

The Irish National Teachers' Organisation said it is "not in a position to comment" but is advising members to report any concerns to gardaí and follow their advice.
