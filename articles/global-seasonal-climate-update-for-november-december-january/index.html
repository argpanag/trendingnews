Global Seasonal Climate Update for November-December-January 2026

TheTools · 21.10.2026 08:53 · source · permalink

Outlook for Oceanic Drivers for the Next Season: For the November 2026–January 2027 (NDJ 2026–27) season, multi-model ensemble forecasts indicate a further intensification toward stronger El Niño conditions. The seasonal average Niño 3.4 SST ensemble mean anomaly is forecast to reach approximately +3.8°C during NDJ 2026–27, with the warming trend peaking around December 2026. The spread among individual forecast systems generally remains narrow, reflecting high forecast confidence.

In the Indian Ocean, a positive IOD is predicted to decline toward climatology, yielding a seasonal mean anomaly for NDJ 2026–27 of +0.3°C. Meanwhile, the equatorial Atlantic basin is forecast to remain warm, with SSTs in both the North and South Tropical Atlantic staying above normal.

About the Global Seasonal Climate Updates series

The Global Seasonal Climate Update (GSCU) is WMO's flagship seasonal climate outlook, providing authoritative information on the current state of the climate system and expected temperature and rainfall patterns for the coming three months. Issued monthly, the GSCU brings together the latest global climate science to support informed decision-making worldwide.
