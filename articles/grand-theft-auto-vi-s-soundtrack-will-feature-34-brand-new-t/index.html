Grand Theft Auto VI’s soundtrack will feature 34 brand-new tracks

Six singles are dropping today from the likes of Keith Richards, Travis Scott, and Yung Lean.

After teasing a music-related reveal a few days ago, Rockstar has officially announced the soundtrack for Grand Theft Auto VI. Called Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the soundtrack will feature 34 songs, which Rockstar describes as “brand-new, original tracks.” Based on the first six singles, which are available today, it seems that the collection will span a pretty wide range of genres, with everyone from Keith Richards to Travis Scott contributing.

Here’s what’s been confirmed so far:

  • Yung Lean - “That’s It” (feat. Future & Metro Boomin)
  • Travis Scott - “RHYNO” (prod. by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo)
  • CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. & Etienne de Crécy - “Sexy Magic”
  • Morgan Wallen - “Last Thing You Need”
  • Rauw Alejandro - “Macacoa 2000”
  • Keith Richards - “Bright Lights, Big City”

The soundtrack will be coming to all major streaming services, and Rockstar says that it’ll be available on both CD and vinyl. It’ll be launching on November 19th, the same day as the game.

The reveal of the album is just the latest Rockstar announcement as GTA VI approaches it’s long-awaited and oft-delayed launch. Last month the studio debuted a lengthy first-look at gameplay, which premiered on Netflix before being released on YouTube. The anticipation around the game is so big that almost every major game publisher — with the notable exception of Nintendo — has gotten out of its way.
