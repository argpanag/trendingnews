Moneyview IPO Allotment Date

As per the timeline, the Moneyview IPO allotment date is September 29, 2026, Tuesday. The investors can check the IPO allotment via the registrar website of MUFG Intime or the BSE NSE website. Investors can check the allotment status online and offline with easy steps given below:

Moneyview IPO Allotment Links

Check Moneyview’ IPO Allotment Status on Registrar site?

  1. Visit MUFG Intime India Pvt. Ltd. website
  2. Select IPO Name ‘Moneyview’ from the drop-down menu on the page
  3. Enter details required like PAN Card, Application Number, or Demat Account Number
  4. Click on the ‘Search’ Button, and;
  5. Get your allotment on the your screen

Check Moneyview IPO Allotment Status on BSE?

  1. Visit BSE India IPO Allotment Page
  2. Select the issue type: equity/debt
  3. Select IPO Name ‘Moneyview’ from the drop-down menu on the page
  4. Enter details required like PAN Card or Application Number
  5. Click on the ‘Search’ Button, and;
  6. Get your allotment on the your screen

Check Moneyview IPO Allotment Status on NSE?

  1. Visit NSE India IPO Allotment Page
  2. Select the issue type
  3. Select IPO Name ‘Moneyview’ from the drop-down menu on the page
  4. Enter details required like PAN Card or Application Number
  5. Click on the ‘Search’ Button, and;
  6. Get your allotment on the your screen

Business Overview

Founded in 2014, Moneyview is a leading Indian financial technology (fintech) company. It is India’s top-rated all-in-one financial app that offers all kinds of financial and lending services. It offers personal loans, credit tracking, and financial management services through its mobile platform. Moneyview uses technology and data analytics to check creditworthiness to ensure quick loan approvals. 

Its platform operates as a 2-sided network where it connects users who are looking for financial products with banks, NBFCs, insurers, and financial institutions that provide such products. Moreover, Moneyview has expanded its product range across multiple product categories, such as insurance, credit cards, digital gold, payments, and earned wage access.
