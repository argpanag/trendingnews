This watch is a matcha-inspired collab with Cafe Kitsune

TheTools · 05.09.2026 13:53 · source · permalink

Casio has teamed up with Franco-Japanese lifestyle brand Café Kitsuné for a limited-edition watch inspired by matcha and Japanese tea culture.

As seen on Casio’s website, the collaboration features the Casio Vintage AQ-230ECK-3A, which comes with a matcha-inspired green dial and Café Kitsuné’s signature fox symbol.

The fox appears on the watch’s dial and case back, while the Café Kitsuné logo is engraved on the case back and clasp.

The watch also comes with special packaging in a matching matcha-inspired color scheme.

According to Casio, the design is meant to reflect Café Kitsuné’s Franco-Japanese identity and its appreciation for simple moments shared in a warm, international setting.

“This collaboration with Café Kitsuné translates the brand’s universe into a watch featuring matcha green tones inspired by Japan’s iconic tea culture and Café Kitsuné’s distinctive aesthetic codes,” it said.

The watch has a silver case and a self-adjustable band. It is also water resistant and designed as a unisex timepiece.

The Café Kitsuné x Casio Vintage Watch is listed on the Maison Kitsuné website for P8,618. —Carby Rose Basina/JCB, GMA News
