Exact date vital winter payment worth over €1,000 will return in September

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink

The Fuel Allowance payment is set to resume in just a few days, with the 2026/2027 season expected to commbegin nce at the end of September as usual.

Fuel Allowance is a financial support intended to help with heating costs during the colder months and last season, over 470,000 households benefitted from the scheme. Only one person per household is entitled to receive it.

The Fuel Allowance season typically operates from late September through to April, providing €38 per week over 28 weeks or as two lump sum payments totalling €1,064.

The return date has been confirmed as Monday, September 28, 2026 and it will run until April, reports RSVP Live.

To qualify for Fuel Allowance you must:

  • Be aged 66 or over
  • Be under 66 and get a qualifying social welfare payment
  • Live alone (or only with certain people)
  • Satisfy a means test

You must also be living in Ireland. You won't be eligible for Fuel Allowance if your heating costs are fully covered as part of your accommodation arrangements.

To receive Fuel Allowance, you must be living alone, or with:

  • A spouse, civil partner or cohabitant who qualifies for an increase on your pension or social welfare payment, or is getting a qualifying payment in their own right
  • Dependent children
  • A person who gets Carer's Benefit, and who is caring for you or for your spouse, partner or cohabitant, or for a qualified child dependant on a full-time basis
  • A person getting short-term Jobseeker's Allowance (less than 312 days) or basic Supplementary Welfare Allowance (less than 364 days)
  • A person getting a qualifying payment (for example, long-term Jobseeker’s Allowance) and who would be eligible for a Fuel Allowance in their own right
  • A person aged 66 or over
  • A person renting a room from you, where this doesn’t affect your entitlement to Fuel Allowance
  • A person you provide accommodation to in your own home, and for whom you get the Accommodation Recognition Payment

If you're aged 66 or older, you don't need to be receiving a qualifying social welfare payment to access Fuel Allowance. However, you will need to pass a means test.

If you're under 66 years of age, you must be in receipt of a qualifying social welfare payment:

  • State Pension (Contributory)
  • Bereaved Partner's (Contributory) Pension
  • Incapacity Supplement under the Occupational Injuries Benefit scheme
  • Invalidity Pension
  • Guardian's Payment (Contributory)
  • Death Benefit under the Occupational Injuries Scheme
  • A pension or benefit from a country covered by EU Regulations or a country with which Ireland has a bilateral social security agreement (provided there is an equivalent Irish payment)
  • One Parent Family Payment
  • Carer's Allowance
  • Disability Allowance
  • Blind Pension
  • Bereaved Partner's (Non-Contributory) Pension
  • Deserted Wife's Benefit or Deserted Wife's Allowance
  • Guardian's Payment (Non-Contributory)
  • Farm Assist
  • Jobseeker's Transitional payment (JST)

You may qualify if you've been receiving Basic Supplementary Welfare Allowance for over a year. You're also eligible if you've been on Jobseeker's Allowance for more than 312 days.

You may hold on to your Fuel Allowance if you move to Jobseeker's Allowance from One Parent Family Payment, Carer's Allowance or Jobseeker's Transitional payment.

From September 2026, those transferring from Disability Allowance or Blind Pension to enter employment will retain their Fuel Allowance for 5 years.

Further variations on qualifying payments exist and you can check all the relevant details here.

How much is Fuel Allowance and how to apply

  • Fuel Allowance is currently €38 per week. The Fuel Allowance season ordinarily runs for 28 weeks, although the 2025/26 season was extended by an additional four weeks.
  • You can apply for Fuel Allowance online via the MyWelfare website here. You must hold a MyGovID account to apply online.
  • Should you be unable to apply online, you may instead complete a physical form. For those aged under 66, fill in the NFS1 form to apply for fuel allowance (linked here).
  • If you are aged 66 or over, fill in the NFS2 form to apply for fuel allowance (linked here).
  • A form can also be collected from your nearest post office or by ringing 0818 200 400. Make sure you request the correct form for your relevant age group.
  • Should you need help filling out your application form, assistance is on hand at your local Intreo Centre, Social Welfare Branch Office or Citizens Information Centre.
