FIRST ALERT | Chris Bailey has a much colder setup on the way

TheTools · 19.11.2026 19:26 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT)- This incredibly mild pattern continues through the upcoming weekend before a dramatic change takes place. Temps take a big dip behind a cold front arriving Sunday night. That sets the stage for a colder than normal Thanksgiving Week with the chance for some winter.

Temps today through Saturday are mainly in the 60s with the chance for 70 in a few spots Friday and Saturday. Yowza.

Speaking of this warm pattern, our September/October combo was the warmest ever recorded in Kentucky history.

To make things even wilder, that system I’ve been talking about off the southeastern seaboard actually has the chance to become something tropical. The National Hurricane Center is highlighting this potential.

That system will hug the east coast through the upcoming weekend.

It’s at this time, we track a cold front into Kentucky from the west. This boundary moves from west to east late Sunday into Sunday night, bringing a gusty round of rain along for the ride.

Much colder air then presses in from the north for the rest of the week, but the active southern storm track keeps cranking out systems. It’s so energetic that the forecast models have little hope of figuring this out for several more days.

Still, we are likely to see another storm system or two come from the south and lift northward impacting our region over the extended Thanksgiving Holiday Weekend. With a cold high to the north, the potential for some winter weather is there with any system that rolls our way.
