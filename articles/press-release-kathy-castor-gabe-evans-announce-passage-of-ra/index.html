Press Release: Kathy Castor, Gabe Evans Announce Passage of Ratepayer Protection Act | Stock News

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:15 · source · permalink

By: Quiver CongressRadar

Castor and Evans promote bipartisan legislation making large data centers cover their own energy infrastructure costs.

Quiver AI Summary

Bill passage: U.S. Reps. Kathy Castor and Gabe Evans said the House passed their bipartisan Ratepayer Protection Act. The measure would require state utility regulators to set rules so communities do not pay for power plants, transmission lines or other upgrades built to serve large data centers.

What the bill would do: The release says data centers with demand of 100 megawatts or more would face guardrails requiring them to cover the full cost of infrastructure and provide financial assurances. Castor and Evans said this is intended to protect families, small businesses and utilities from higher electric bills if data center demand grows.

Related context: Castor said she opposes some data center development in Hillsborough County because of electric bills and environmental concerns, and linked the bill to earlier legislation she introduced with Rep. Mike Levin on electricity-price hikes tied to large load demands.
