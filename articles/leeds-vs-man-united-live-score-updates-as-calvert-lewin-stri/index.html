United: Lammens, Yoro, Maguire, Martinez, Dalot, Santos, Baleba, Tielemans, Fernandes, Mbeumo, Amad

Leeds: Trafford, Bogle, Muharemovic, Elvedi, Justin, Gudmundsson, Ampadu, Tanaka, Stach, Okafor, Calvert-Lewin

HALF-TIME! Leeds 0-1 United

That's the whistle for the break here.

Mbeumo's goal is the difference.

42' - LEE 0-1 MUN - Tielemans has been superb

This has been Tielemans best performance in his United career, by some distance. He was good in midweek, creating chances for Mbeumo and Fernandes, and he’s created two more for Mbeumo today. Dovetailing well with Fernandes at times, both moving wide to offer combinations with the wing-back, which is how the goal came about. Santos and Baleba good again as well.

39' - LEE 0-1 MUN - Professional away display

This has been a professional away display so far. The game has been low on quality, but United have found a way to get ahead with a well-worked goal.

34' - LEE 0-1 MUN - GOAL! 1-0 UNITED! (Mbeumo)

Mbeumo did brilliantly to get in front of his marker and poke home. Carrick rarely celebrates, but he fist pumped that goal like a man who really needs a win. He urned to his staff afterwards to enjoy the moment.

32' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Trafford clears

Amad was sent over the top and almost got there before Trafford, but the Leeds stopper won the race.

30' - LEE 0-0 MUN - First touch in Leeds' area

That Mbeumo header was United’s first touch in the Leeds penalty area. Mbeumo has to do better. Well picked out by Tielemans but United had two players unmarked in front of goal. Mbeumo not renowned for his heading but he has to do better with that chance.

28' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Huge chance

Mbeumo has just missed a huge chance, heading wide after another wonderful cross from Tielemans. Any striker worth their salt puts that opportunity away, but Mbeumo is not a centre-forward.

26' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Poor game

Both sides have lacked quality in this first half. It's been a scrappy game with plenty of fouls.

22' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Fernandes needs to settle

Fernandes has looked moody and frustrated since this game kicked off, despite playing what you would think is a more natural position for him. He was limping at one point and his yellow card then only came from his own heavy touch. Hard to argue with the decision, but argue he did, and pretty vociferously. Needs to get his head back in the game.

20' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Tactical instructions

Carrick deep in conversation with Baleba and Dalot. Fair play to him for tweaking the system today - Leeds wouldn’t have expected it

16' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Fernandes booked

14' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Baleba doing well

7' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Yoro cleared the danger

Yoro has got his head onto two Leeds crosses in just six minutes. There will be plenty more to deal with today.

5' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Confusing set up

A confusing set-up from United. Looks a back three out of possession, with Dalot and Amad the wing-backs. Fernandes is playing as a striker, with Tielemans pretty close to him, and Mbeumo more off the left.

3' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Back five?

It looks like United have started with a back five, with Dalot at LWB and Amad at RWB. Fernandes has started down the middle. Well done if you predicted that before the game.

2' - LEE 0-0 MUN - Physical start

It's been a physical start, which is no surprise in this fixture. Tielemans just conceded a free-kick and Martinez was perhaps lucky not to give one away in the previous passage of play.

Carragher on Martinez saga

"There would have been big focus on Man Utd today anyway, because of their start to the season, they lost in Madrid midweek, but the Lisandro Martinez situation has ramped things up. What it's also done is make everyone forget how well United competed midweek. Poor result. But they actually played quite well. This is a tough game for them. If Man Utd lose today on the back of what has been said, this will ramp up to levels Michael Carrick has never had to deal with as a manager before. Talk around the manager will go to new levels."

Carrick explains Martinez decision

Michael Carrick reiterated that "what's done is done" when asked about Lisandro Martinez being selected to start for United against Leeds today.

Martinez tore into the United hierarchy following United's defeat to Atletico Madrid in the Champions League on Tuesday. The United defender took aim at Jason Wilcox, saying he expected more from the club in the transfer window.

Martinez released a statement on Wednesday explaining his comments before sitting down for talks with Omar Berrada, Wilcox and Carrick on Thursday. He would apologise later that day for the timing of his comments but did not take back what he said.
