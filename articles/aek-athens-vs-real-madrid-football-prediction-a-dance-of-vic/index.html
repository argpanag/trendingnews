AEK Athens vs Real Madrid Football Prediction: A Dance of Victory

TheTools · 04.11.2026 17:10 · source · permalink

AEK Athens vs Real Madrid Football Prediction: Real Madrid aims for a perfect winning record in the Champions League when they face AEK Athens, with Vinicius under close scrutiny.

Real Madrid, according to football analysts , have a solid basis to leave Athens with their 7th consecutive Champions League victory (00:45 on November 5th). But against AEK, what's more important than the three points is the response from Vinicius, who is trying to regain his place in Jose Mourinho's team.

A run of six consecutive wins – highlighted by the El Clásico victory over Barcelona – coupled with a perfect record after three Champions League matches, allowed Real Madrid to enter the game with confidence.

AEK Athens just lost 0-2 to Panathinaikos and only have 3 points in this year's Champions League. Despite Mourinho's warnings about the pressure of playing away from home, the difference in quality is still clearly in favor of the Spanish team.

In the previous Champions League qualifying round match, AEK suffered a humiliating 1-5 defeat at the hands of Man City, another factor demonstrating their level of performance on the continental stage.

For Vinicius, Athens means something different. Since establishing himself as a regular starter in the "Los Blancos" squad, he has never entered a match in such a precarious position.

With only one goal and three assists in 11 appearances this season, and his attacking runs becoming monotonous and predictable, the Brazilian star is going through a worrying slump.

While Vinicius was absent due to a four-match suspension in La Liga, Real Madrid continued to progress strongly. In fact, this helped Mourinho find balance within the team.

Yan Diomande increased that pressure. The Ivorian player scored 4 goals in 22 days, all of them spectacular, and provided 3 assists, bringing dynamism and effectiveness to the attack.

Diomande continued his scoring form against Racing Santander, contributing to Real Madrid's 3-0 victory. The left wing, which was once almost exclusively Vinicius's, now has a real competitor.

Diomande will most likely be rested to conserve energy for the busy schedule. Mourinho has confirmed Vinicius will start in Athens in tonight's Champions League match.

However, this opportunity doesn't guarantee he'll immediately regain his place upon returning to La Liga. Vinicius needs to demonstrate more of his desire to score goals.

Vinicius needs to choose the right moments to dribble, combine quickly with Kylian Mbappe and Jude Bellingham , and actively provide support when he loses possession.

A goal could relieve the pressure. Vini needs a team-oriented performance to convince Mourinho once his suspension ends.

Real Madrid are expected to win against AEK Athens by a large margin. However, what Mourinho and the Madridistas are most concerned about right now is Vinicius's mental state, while Diomande may be rested on the bench.

Predicted lineup:

AEK Athens (4-4-2): Brignoli; Rota, Moukoudi, Relvas, Pilios; Majer, Marin, Vitalis, Koita; Varga, Jovic.

Absent: Joao Mario (injury).

Suspects: Rota, Strakosha.

Real Madrid (4-2-3-1): Courtois; Dumfries, Rudiger, Huijsen, Carreras; Camavinga, Bernardo Silva; Brahim, Bellingham, Vinicius; Mbappe .

Absent: Rodrygo, Raul Asencio (injured); Arda Guler, Tchouameni (rested).

Absent: No.

Prediction: Real Madrid wins 3-1 .
