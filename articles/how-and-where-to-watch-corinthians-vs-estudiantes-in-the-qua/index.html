Don’t Miss Corinthians vs. Estudiantes LIVE on beIN SPORTS


Corinthians and Estudiantes de La Plata will battle for a place in the 2026 CONMEBOL Libertadores semifinals on Wednesday, September 16, with everything still to play for following the opening leg in Argentina.

The first meeting finished 1-1 in La Plata, with Alexis Castro scoring for Estudiantes before Kaio found the net for the Brazilian side. The tie will now be decided at the Neo Química Arena, where neither team has any margin for error.

Corinthians vs. Estudiantes de La Plata will be available LIVE on beIN SPORTS in the United States, with coverage beginning at 8:20 p.m. ET.


How Corinthians Enter the Match

Corinthians, coached by Fernando Diniz, earned a valuable result away from home and will now have the opportunity to finish the job in front of their own supporters. Any victory will send the Timão directly into the semifinals.

Their domestic form provides a different picture, however. Corinthians are coming off a 2-1 defeat to Flamengo and currently sit 13th in the Brasileirão. The Libertadores therefore offers an opportunity to change the momentum of their season.

Memphis Depay, Rodrigo Garro, Gustavo Henrique and Breno Bidon are among the key players to watch. Corinthians are also chasing their first Libertadores semifinal appearance since 2012, the year they went on to lift the trophy.


How Estudiantes Enter the Match

Estudiantes travel to São Paulo knowing that a victory will secure their place in the semifinals. If the teams finish level again, the tie will be decided in a penalty shootout.

Alexander Medina’s side are coming off a 2-1 victory over Platense in their most recent domestic match and will look to carry that momentum into their continental challenge.

Guido Carrillo, Joaquín Correa, Alexis Castro and Joaquín Tobio Burgos are among the players who could make the difference for El Pincha. There is also history at stake: Estudiantes have not reached the Libertadores semifinals since 2009, when they captured their fourth continental title.


How And Where To Watch Corinthians vs. Estudiantes

The second leg of the 2026 CONMEBOL Libertadores quarterfinal between Corinthians and Estudiantes de La Plata will be played on Wednesday, September 16 at the Neo Química Arena in São Paulo, Brazil.

Fans in the United States can watch Corinthians vs. Estudiantes LIVE on beIN SPORTS, with coverage beginning at 8:20 p.m. ET.

Final Verdict

After the 1-1 draw in the first leg, there is virtually nothing separating these two teams. Corinthians will have home-field advantage, but Estudiantes have already shown they can compete on equal terms in what promises to be another tense knockout match.

There is also plenty of history on the line. Corinthians have not reached the semifinals since winning the competition in 2012, while Estudiantes last made the final four during their title-winning run in 2009.

There is no advantage left to protect. A victory sends either team into the final four of South America, while another draw will take the quarterfinal to penalties.
