Alejandra Gere and Richard Gere’s Autumn plans are what dreams are made of

The family moment comes after Richard and Alejandra returned to the United States following a period living in Spain

Alejandra Gere has offered a rare glimpse into family life with Richard Gere, and her idea of the perfect plan is refreshingly simple.

The Spanish philanthropist and businesswoman shared images from an autumn outing with her husband and their children, showing the family enjoying nature away from red carpets, premieres and Hollywood events. Instead of an elaborate getaway, their day involved muddy paths, puddles, fresh air and a mission to head outdoors to look for frogs.

View post on Instagram
 

The family moment comes after Richard and Alejandra returned to the United States following a period living in Spain. Now back at their country home outside New York, they appear to be embracing the change of season and the quieter routines that come with it.

Alejandra Gere Shares Her Favorite Autumn Family Activity

For Alejandra, fall offers the ideal excuse to slow down and spend uninterrupted time with the children. She explained that she especially enjoys the first rainy days of autumn, when temperatures are still relatively mild. Going outside with the children to search for frogs and spending time together in nature has become something more meaningful than a simple walk.

According to Alejandra, the activity "helps us reconnect as a family."

The accompanying family images capture exactly that kind of low-key day. Richard, Alejandra and the children are seen surrounded by an unmistakably autumnal landscape, with wet trails and puddles forming part of the adventure.

Why Richard Gere and Alejandra Gere Returned to the US

Richard and Alejandra spent roughly a year living in Madrid before returning to the United States. Alejandra has described the decision as one influenced primarily by family and logistical considerations, while making it clear that Spain remains important to them.

Their move to Spain had brought the family closer to Alejandra's roots, but their return has put them within easier reach of Richard's professional commitments and family connections in the United States. The actor also has an adult son, Homer Gere, from his marriage to Carey Lowell.

Alejandra and Richard have generally been careful about how much of their children's lives they share publicly.

The couple, who married in 2018, have two sons together, Alexander and James. Alejandra is also the mother of Albert, her son from a previous marriage.
