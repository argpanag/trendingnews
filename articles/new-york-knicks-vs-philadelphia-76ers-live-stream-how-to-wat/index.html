The defending champion New York Knicks open their preseason with a visit to the retooled Philadelphia 76ers on Monday night

The New York Knicks put together a playoff run for the ages, capturing their first NBA championship since 1973, and enter the 2026-27 season as the hunted rather than just one of the hunters. New York opens its preseason on Monday night as it travels to meet the Philadelphia 76ers at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia. The 76ers made a couple of huge moves as they chase a first title since 1983, trading for two-time All-NBA wing Jaylen Brown and signing ageless LeBron James as a free agent to team with their returning core of Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey and VJ Edgecombe.

After going down 2-1 to the Atlanta Hawks in the first round of the playoffs, the Knicks won 15 of their next 16 to claim the championship. New York lost big man Mitchell Robinson in free agency and look to replace him with former Sixer backup Andre Drummond, a move that could affect the defense from the second unit. New York looks to become the first team to repeat as champions since the Golden State Warriors in 2017 and 2018.

Philadelphia, meanwhile, pushed all-in to get Embiid his elusive ring. The oft-injured superstar played just 96 regular-season games over the last three seasons and, with Drummond gone, third-year man Adem Bona becomes the primary backup. James, meanwhile, put up unprecedented numbers in his age-41 season, averaging 20.9 points, 7.2 assists and 6.1 rebounds in 33.2 minutes per game, surpassing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar for the highest such figures at that age. Ball distribution appears to be James’ role on a team that features two players in Brown and Embiid who each logged usage rates of more than 32% last season.

New York plays five times in the preseason while the 76ers set four warmup games. The teams tip off the regular season against each other at Madison Square Garden on Oct. 20.
