  4. 'We looked in control the whole innings'published at 11:45 GMT 15 November
    11:45 GMT 15 November

    England captain Harry Brook: "We put in a really good performance there, really pleasing to see us bounce back like that.

    "The whole innings, we looked like we were in control. We countered their bad balls, put them under pressure when we needed to and Tom Banton played an exceptional innings.

    "He played to his strengths and put away the bad balls. The depth that we have means we were able to go on and get that 350."

    On talks after Perth: "You want to learn from your mistakes but we knew, coming here, that this was going to be a much better wicket and thankfully some lads cashed in."

    On fielding: "That's the template we want to set every time we go out in the field. It's been a massive discussion in the group in the last year and a half.

    "We want to be one of the strongest and fittest fielding sides in the world and I thought that display out there was awesome, bar a couple of dropped catches.

    "Mis-execution you can deal with but the energy you can control."

  5. 'Tried some different plans'published at 11:43 GMT 15 November
    11:43 GMT 15 November

    Australia captain Mitch Marsh: "We obviously bowled first thinking there might have been a little bit in the wicket, which probably sounds weird to say at the Adelaide Oval, and try to maximise those early overs. I thought they batted really well. We tried a lot through the 50 overs, a few different plans with Riley Meredith bowling bumpers, and all sorts of stuff.

    "Credit to England. They played well. We know they are a good side. A really rounded side and they showed that tonight."

    On the toss: "I do bowl first a lot. Maybe I'll start batting first!"

    On why their batting struggled: "Any time you are chasing 350 you are the under pump from ball one. We needed two of our top order to really set the platform to have any chance. Credit to England, they played really well today so it's 1-1 going to Hobart."

  6. 'A special feeling'published at 11:39 GMT 15 November
    11:39 GMT 15 November

    Player of the match, England's Tom Banton: "I've watched a lot of cricket in Australia over the years, the Ashes and white-ball cricket, so to come here today was a special feeling."

    On his innings: "50-over cricket is hard, sometimes you get off to a flyer then you have to rein it back in and visa-versa. It's just picking moments and match-ups."

    On partnership with Joe Root: "I've played with Rooty a little bit now and, having him at the other end, he's such a calm head and makes it much easier for me.

    "He's one of the greats of the game so to be at the other end and watch him go about his business is pretty cool."

    On his bowling: "I've been working on my bowling in the nets but it's a lot harder in a game!"

  9. 'Proud moment'published at 11:33 GMT 15 November
    11:33 GMT 15 November

    England batter Tom Banton, speaking to Test Match Special: "I think the most important thing is getting the win. Coming here today if we didn't win, the Hobart match wouldn't have mattered. It's been a great day and I'm very happy.

    "The toss in Perth was a big thing, they bowled well and put us under pressure. That's how cricket works sometimes. It can change really quickly. We spoke about sticking to what we know well and do well.

    "Looking back at the last ODI series we started slow and then came back stronger. It's been a great day so it'll be a nice day off tomorrow, some training then a big game on Wednesday."

    On scoring a century in Australia: "Growing up I watched so much cricket - the Ashes, one-day cricket and T20s - so do that is a very proud moment. For me I am trying to win games for England, and do as much as I can."

    On whether he has nailed down the opener's spot: "I'd like to think so but cricket is funny old game. It can change very quickly. I'm taking each game as it comes and trying to score as many runs as I can."
