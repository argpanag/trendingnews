    FT: Scotland 21-43 New Zealand

    Player of the match Quinn Tupaea tells STV: "Very satisfying win. It's a tough week for the group but we rallied together well and got a good result at a difficult place to play.

    "They had a lot of momentum, but we went into half-time feeling pretty positive. We knew we could come out on top, so we're very pleased.

    "It's been pleasing, we had a tough tour in Africa, came back and played some pretty impressive rugby against Australia. We love playing this style of rugby, keeping the ball on the park.

    "[Beauden Barrett]'s a special player, think he's second all-time in Tests now so I'm pleased for the guy."
