Liverpool star may never recover from Iraola call, reckons Carragher

TheTools · 01.11.2026 15:59 · source · permalink

Andoni Iraola has made a bold move by start Trey Nyoni for Liverpool against Arsenal on Sunday, a decision which could do serious damage to Ryan Gravenberch, believes Jamie Carragher.

19-year-old Nyoni has been handed a first Premier League start and it comes in a huge clash at Anfield.

The midfielder has already made 20 appearances for the Reds, 14 of which have come this season and he has already earned a starting place in the Champions League.

It is a huge opportunity for the exciting talent, but Carragher sees it as a ‘mighty blow’ for Gravenberch who has been left on the bench while Nyoni comes into the starting XI.
