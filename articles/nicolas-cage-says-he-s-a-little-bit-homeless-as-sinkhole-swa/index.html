Nicolas Cage says he’s ‘a little bit homeless’ as sinkhole swallows his Malibu home

TheTools · 03.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

Nicolas Cage said he is “a little bit homeless” thanks to a sinkhole that has consumed a part of his Southern California home.

Talking about his Halloween plans at a Los Angeles Comic Con panel over the weekend, Cage said he is “currently a little bit homeless.”

“Something happened in my house in Malibu. So, I’m just sort of living in hotels, so I don’t have the normal trick-or-treaters I would like to enjoy tonight,” he said, according to a fan video of the panel.

A huge sinkhole formed in the driveway of Cage’s home on the Southern California coast in September. At the time, authorities were investigating what caused the sinkhole to open up.

City officials said the sinkhole also caused a gas leak, but no one was hurt.

Video shows a massive crater in the driveway that appeared to swallow up everything sitting on top. It was not clear from aerial imaging whether the house itself was damaged.

The city of Malibu said in an emergency proclamation that the sinkhole started to form Sept. 7 and fully emerged Sept. 8, measuring 25 to 30 feet deep and 30 by 60 feet wide.

Seven homes were “red-tagged” because of hazardous conditions brought on by the sinkhole, the proclamation said, as were an additional 24 homes “due to the loss of safe vehicular access.” Gas services in the area were shut off.

The next day, the city said the housing association that controls the area would be working on repairs. It said repairs were expected to be completed within five weeks, “depending on conditions.”

Representatives for Cage did not immediately respond to a request for comment. It was not clear how long he has been out of the home and when he will be able to return.

The city of Malibu has not released any further information about the sinkhole, and it did not immediately respond to a request for more information.
