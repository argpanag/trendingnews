Kane defended amid claims he fails to show up for England in key moments

TheTools · 29.09.2026 10:56 · source · permalink
  • Amy Hamerslagh, Apprentice Sports Writer
  • Published: 11:56, 29 Sep 2026
  • Updated: 11:56, 29 Sep 2026

talkSPORT's Inside England panel argued over whether Harry Kane steps up for his country in tournament-defining matches.

Kane stands as the Three Lions' all-time leading goalscorer, yet debates remain over whether he struggles in high-pressure moments.

This was after he hit the crossbar from the spot against Spain on Saturday in what was a game-changing moment.

The Bayern Munich striker often faces critics who point out crucial missed opportunities on the grandest stages, including another missed penalty against France in the 2022 World Cup quarter-final.

The 33-year-old only scored in the group stages and the Round of 16 at the 2018 World Cup, and failed to register a goal in the next two rounds of the tournament.

Additionally, the former Tottenham forward was unable to add his name to the scoresheet in both the Euro 2020 final against Italy, and the Euro 2024 final against Spain.

On Inside England, Flex argued: "He’s England’s record goalscorer, the scrutiny of being at that level is that you go into a very exclusive club.

"It's a club that has Kylian Mbappe, who scores in decisive moments, players like Vinicius Jr, and Rodri in his own way. These guys decide trophies.

"And for Harry Kane, unfortunately he spent a lot of his career at Tottenham. He spent a lot of time where the goals weren’t for nothing, but they were for personal accolades so he didn’t experience the adulation.

"Now he’s stepped out of Spurs, and he’s winning trophies galore, and he’s been able to outscore Robert Lewandowski.

"He’s been a fantastic player, but for his country, he loses it in key moments, and we look at him because he’s THAT guy for us."

Kane's iconic England moments

Despite the scrutiny, Kane's international career is filled with defining moments for the Three Lions.

He enjoyed a fairytale debut under Roy Hodgson in 2015, needing just 79 seconds to score after replacing Wayne Rooney in the 72nd minute against Lithuania.

His form at club level impressively continued, and the then-Spurs forward wore the captain's armband for the first time in 2017 - he completed a comeback from two goals down to Scotland and made it 2-2.

Additionally, Kane surpassed Rooney as England's all-time record goalscorer in March 2023, with a penalty against Italy in a Euro qualifier.

He also became the country's top goalscorer at the World Cup, beating Gary Lineker's previous record of ten.

The scapegoat for England

talkSPORT host, and Spurs fan, Abbi Summers argued back and said: "He’s not the first player to be captain or lead England in a golden generation to not step up. He doesn’t just statpad against s*** teams, that’s not true.

"He does get a lot of pens but what do you want him to do, not take it. He might not have scored against France when it matters but okay."

Pundit Kweku Afari added: "You’re right, you think about Wayne Rooney, he’s a better player than Harry Kane, but he never delivered at an international tournament."

Summers responded: "There’s not many strikers in world football who can do what Harry Kane can do. He has the passing ability, he has range, and he can drop deep.

"He actually did it at Spurs because he was injured quite a lot. Under Jose, he did play deeper because he’s such a great passer of the ball. There’s not many No. 9s who can do that, and I just think he’s the scapegoat for England."

Kane will have the chance to add to his England goal tally further on Tuesday night, as the Three Lions take on the Czech Republic in the Nations League.
