Nations League: Haaland reaches 65 goals as Norway falls to Portugal

TheTools · 29.09.2026 07:28 · source · permalink

Erling Haaland scored his 65th goal in 57 appearances, but Norway lost 2-1 to Portugal in Group A4.

SUMMARY

  • Three goals shy of Gerd Müller

Erling Haaland scored his 65th goal for Norway on Sunday, September 27, in Oslo, but it was not enough to prevent a 2-1 defeat against Portugal in the second round of the Nations League. The Manchester City forward achieved this milestone in 57 appearances.

João Félix opened the scoring in the 17th minute. Haaland then equalized for Norway before Gonçalo Ramos secured victory for Portugal with a chipped shot.

Four days earlier, Haaland had netted a brace in the 3-2 victory for Norway against Denmark, bringing his total to 64 goals in 56 appearances and surpassing Zlatan Ibrahimović as the top Nordic scorer in national team history.

Three goals shy of Gerd Müller

With 65 goals in 57 matches, Haaland boasts an average of more than one goal per game. He is now just three goals away from Gerd Müller’s total of 68 goals in 62 appearances for West Germany.

After two rounds, Portugal leads Group A4 with six points. Norway and Denmark have three points each, while Wales remains without a point.
