Chelsea vs Spurs, Women's Super League | How to watch, team news, kit colours, key information

TheTools · 18.10.2026 10:38 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

After a positive international window for our players, Women’s Super League action returns this weekend as we face Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on Sunday.

Here’s all you need to know ahead the London derby with the Blues…

What time does the match start?

Kick-off is at 12noon BST on Sunday.

How can I watch the game?

Sunday’s match at Stamford Bridge will be televised live in the UK on Sky Sports Football, Sky Sports Ultra HDR and Sky One.

Alongside this, supporters can keep fully up-to-date with all the action via live text during the game on our Match Centre.

Video highlights will be posted on official club channels from four hours after the final whistle, while a full match replay will be available to watch on SPURSPLAY from 24 hours after the game.

Predict the score | Chelsea vs Spurs

What is the team news?

Martin Ho revealed that Olga Ahtinen is set for a small spell on the sidelines after picking up a little knock.

The Finland international missed our last outing in the WSL and will be unavailable for this weekend's London derby.

Players that are also returning from international duty on Friday (16 October) morning will be assessed before the short trip across the capital to Stamford Bridge.

For Chelsea, Sonia Bompastor reported that she has the entire squad to choose from for our WSL clash on Sunday.

Who is the referee?

Kirtsy Dowle will take charge of Sunday’s match.

Georgia Ball and Anastasiya Voloshchuk are her assistants, with Alice Parker the fourth official.

Which kits will the teams be wearing?

Chelsea will be wearing their usual home kit with blue shirts, blue shorts and white socks.

We will be wearing our 2026/27 home kit, featuring Lilywhite shirts, navy shorts and white socks.

Our goalkeeper will be in green, with Chelsea’s in orange.

What will the weather be like?

The forecast is mostly dry on Sunday but with plenty of cloud and moderate breeze. Temperatures at around 15 degrees.
