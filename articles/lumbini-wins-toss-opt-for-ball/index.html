Lumbini wins toss, opt for ball

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:29 · source · permalink

By TRN Online, Kathmandu, Nov 14: Lumbini lions won toss and elected to ball first in the Nepal Premier League (NPL) Season 3 match being played at the Tribhuvan University Cricket Ground in Kirtipur today.

After wining the toss, Lumbini lions choose ball, allowing Biratnagar Kings to bat first.
