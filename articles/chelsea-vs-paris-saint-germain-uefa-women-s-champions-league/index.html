  3. England defender Carter joins Bay FCpublished at 21:09 GMT
    21:09 GMT

    It was announced earlier this evening that England defender Jess Carter has completed a move to Bay FC after leaving Gotham FC as a free agent.

    The 29-year-old has signed a contract that will keep her with Bay FC - a club based in the San Francisco Bay Area - until 2028, with the option for an additional year.

    Carter joined New York-side Gotham in July 2024 after a six-year spell with Chelsea that saw her win the Women's Super League on five occasions.

    "Coming to Bay FC is a new challenge, and that's something I'm excited about," said Carter.

    "I've been fortunate to be part of some successful teams, and I know how much work goes into winning. I'm looking forward to getting to know this group, earning the trust of my teammates and doing my part to help us move forward."
