Derrick Henry passes Ray Rice, ties Mark Andrews in Ravens record book

TheTools · 27.09.2026 21:56 · source · permalink

Two touchdowns against Dallas moved Derrick Henry past Ray Rice and into a tie with Mark Andrews for a Ravens franchise mark.

Derrick Henry’s first touchdown against the Dallas Cowboys tied Ray Rice on the Ravens’ career rushing list. His second moved him past Rice and gave him another place in the franchise record book.

Henry now has 38 rushing touchdowns with Baltimore, one more than Rice. His two-score performance Sunday in Brazil also tied Mark Andrews for the most games with multiple touchdowns in Ravens history, with 12, Ravens Communications said. The second touchdown also added to a league-wide mark. Henry has 34 career games with multiple rushing touchdowns, the most among active NFL players. Josh Jacobs and Jonathan Taylor are next with 18 each, according to the team.

Henry entered Sunday with 36 rushing touchdowns as a Raven. He tied Rice with his first score against Dallas, then took sole possession of second place with his next one. His fifth rushing touchdown of this season also gave him 11 career seasons with at least five, an NFL record according to Ravens Communications.

The touchdowns were part of a milestone first half for Henry. A 5-yard run to open the second quarter moved him past Eric Dickerson and into ninth place on the NFL’s career rushing list. That ranking covers Henry’s entire career; the marks he reached alongside Rice and Andrews cover his time with Baltimore.
