Stars make Strictly history after all eight couples top leaderboard

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

Every remaining couple on Strictly Come Dancing has topped the leaderboard in a first at this point for the series.

The BBC One dance programme returned to screens on Saturday night for its ninth week which was broadcast live from Blackpool.

At the start of the episode, presenter Emma Willis revealed that all the remaining celebrity contestants and their professional dancers have each topped the leader board – a first at this point in the dance programme, making Strictly history.

Willis said: “We are nine weeks into the competition and for the first time ever at this point, every single one of our remaining couples has topped the leaderboard.

“That has never happened before in 22 years of Strictly.”

The episode opened with a music festival-inspired performance from Basement Jaxx featuring a medley of their biggest hits, accompanied by the Strictly cast.

Singer Delta Goodrem was the first celebrity to take the ballroom floor as she reunited with professional dancer Kai Widdrington, who missed last week’s show because of an illness.

The pair received 36 points for their quickstep to I Wanna Be The Only One by Eternal & Bebe Winans.

Anton Du Beke said Goodrem was a “star” and praised the performance as “divine”.

Fellow judge Craig Revel Horwood added that the dance was “absolutely brilliant”, and noted that Goodrem and Widdrington make “an incredible partnership”.

Meanwhile, actress Jaime Winstone and professional Carlos Gu performed a samba to Freedom! ’90 by George Michael.

The couple scored 32 points and were praised by Revel Horwood for maintaining “fantastic rhythm”.

The show also saw TV personality Dani Dyer and Nikita Kuzmin score 35 points for their couple’s choice to songs Love Sex Magic by Ciara and Justin Timberlake, Me Against The Music by Britney Spears and Madonna, and Word Up! by Cameo.

Judge Motsi Mabuse said Dyer was a “different woman” while Shirley Ballas said she could be a “popstar on Wembley”.

“I thought the whole thing was great, just the transitions here and there,” Ballas said before appearing emotional, adding: “You are a different woman and it’s really remarkable to watch.”

Du Beke said Dyer was a “force to be reckoned with”.

Strictly Come Dancing continues on BBC One.
