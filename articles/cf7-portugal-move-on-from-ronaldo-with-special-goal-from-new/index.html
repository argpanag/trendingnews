CF7: Portugal move on from Ronaldo with 'special' goal from new No. 7 Forbs

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:32 · source · permalink

It was out with old and in with the new for Portugal on Tuesday as the team secured a much-needed first win since Cristiano Ronaldo's international retirement, courtesy of the player given the responsibility of wearing the No. 7 jersey he made legendary.

Carlos Forbs -- wearing a No. 7 shirt featuring a line across the number to honor Ronaldo -- scored the winner as Portugal beat Wales 3-2 in the Nations League on Tuesday.

"It's a special feeling; I won't forget today," Forbs said after what was his first international goal. "The most important thing is that we managed to win; I'm happy about that.

"We needed the goal to calm the game down, and it just so happened that I scored."

The 22-year-old winger is a very different player to "CR7," not least because he's still making his name in the Belgian league with Club Brugge and all his three Portugal games have been off the bench.

"I know it's a number with special significance, but I didn't feel any extra weight wearing it," Forbs added.

Ronaldo finished his Portugal career as the all-time leader in men's international soccer in both appearances (234) and goals (146). Forbs, with one goal in three appearances, has some way to go.

"I have to keep working, not just here with the national team, but also with my club to show the coach he can keep calling me," Forbs said of his future prospects.

Poor defending and discipline were issues for Portugal, but there were still far more positives than in Saturday's 4-1 loss to Denmark. Portugal finished their Nations League group with five wins from six games in a campaign overshadowed by the absence of Ronaldo.

The victory ensured Portugal finished top of their group and will face a runner-up from another group in the quarterfinals next March. The draw will take place on Friday.

"We showed off our mentality, our character, and the win was deserved today," Forbs said. "The aim today was to get the three points, finish first in the group, and that is what we did."

Tribute banners were again on show among the Portugal fans in Cardiff, nearly two months after Ronaldo's last game for Portugal, a 1-0 win over Wales in September.

Ronaldo fell out with coach Jorge Jesus when he was an unused substitute for the following game against Norway, and then walked out on the squad. He declared his retirement from the national team Nov. 5.

Bruno Fernandes gave Portugal the lead when he curled the ball into the top corner but it lasted just two minutes before David Brooks leveled for Wales. When Sorba Thomas put Wales ahead on a rapid counter just before halftime, it was Portugal's turn for a quickfire equalizer from Pedro Neto.

Forbs then put the visitors in front in the 70th minute with smart movement to set up a low shot.

It looked like Portugal might give the lead away again after Chelsea winger Neto was sent off for two reckless tackles in as many minutes, but Wales struggled to create many chances after that.

The Associated Press contributed to this report.
