Kim Hellberg reveals what he said to Finn Azaz after controversial celebration

TheTools · 28.11.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

Head coach Kim Hellberg of Middlesbrough has words with Finn Azaz of Southampton

Kim Hellberg has revealed what he said to Finn Azaz in the aftermath of his decisive goal for Southampton.

The former Boro man netted Southampton's second of the game as they won 2-0. After scoring, the attacking midfielder ran the length of the pitchto celebrate in front of Boro fans.

The incident sparked angry scenes from Boro fans and players, and Hellberg could be seen having words with the Ireland international after too.

Revealing what was said, Hellberg said: “With Finn Azaz, I told him, ‘I think you have to do better, you cannot do things like that’. That was a key thing to tell him.

“I know it's a lot of emotions and I know emotions can get the better of you, but that's what I told him, ‘you have to be better on that occasion.

Like I said to him, it wasn't a good move, but players are young people and emotions come into it, but you can handle it and should handle it in a better way."

Meanwhile, assessing Boro’s performance, he said: “We were good. We were, for me, for 60 minutes the better side and created a lot of good opportunities.

We won the ball high, our pressing was very good, we were brave on the ball. For a long time they've tried to make this a final.

I'm very proud of how we handled that and we were the better team for 60 minutes. We should have scored in those moments and should have found a pass in some moments where we could have hurt them a bit more, maybe shooting instead of looking for the extra pass.

Then I'm disappointed with the goal and it comes more of a scrappy game after that. I'm impressed by our players and take control of the game. I'm disappointed with the result.”
