Nicola Willis discusses Pre-election Economic and Fiscal Update

Follow the latest with RNZ's live coverage:

This week, Treasury gave a pre-election update on the government's books, giving Nicola Willis a bit of an economic reprive.

Labour leader Chris Hipkins called the PREFU is "the verdict" on National's track record, with government debt, unemployment, and inflation staying higher for longer, ACT leader David Seymour said it "vindicated" his party's drive for savings.

Meanwhile, the Electoral Commission sounded the alarm over how few young people are enrolled to vote. We're expecting to hear more on this issue during campaigning on Wednesday.
