  19. 'We won the game'published at 08:29 GMT
    08:29 GMT

    Australia v New Zealand (08:35 GMT)

    New Zealand had the upper hand for much of the match when they faced Australia in the opening match of the World Cup on 15 October.

    They perhaps paid for a lack of ruthlessness, and a touch of carelessness, particularly in the loose pass that allowed Jack Bostock to score a breakaway try - the first of his three, as the Kangaroos won 24-22.

    New Zealand's pack is arguably the best in the world, with James Fisher-Harris, Moses Leota, Joe Tapine and Naufahu Whyte up front.

    Australia front-rower Tino Fa'asuamaleaui, though, has dismissed the idea that the Kiwi forwards will have the upper hand today.

    "We definitely discussed [after the opening game] that we needed to be better," he said.

    "But we won the game. We were really proud of our effort, our scramble defence and making things happen."
