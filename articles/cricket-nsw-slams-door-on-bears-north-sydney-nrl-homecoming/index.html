The NRL was hoping the Bears could play their first pre-season game at their spiritual home.  (AAP: Dan Himbrechts)

In short: 

Cricket NSW will not allow NRL side the Perth Bears to play a trial match at North Sydney Oval.

The expansion side was hoping to play a pre-season game at the venue but cricket officials denied the request.

A match would have rendered the ground unusable for cricket for at least five weeks.

Cricket NSW has slammed the door on a Bears NRL pre-season homecoming, ruling out making North Sydney Oval available for Perth to play at in February.

Cricket officials informed the Bears today that they would not accept any financial compensation for use of the venue, given it would remove cricket for at least five weeks.

It comes after NRL executive chair Peter V'landys last week said it would be "beyond comprehension" for cricket to deny the expansion club a romantic homecoming.

V'landys also claimed a $200,000 compensation fee would be "silly", after Cricket NSW indicated that would be the cost to take the pitch out and put it back in.

The Bears will play in the NRL in 2027.  (Getty Images: Paul Kane )

But Cricket NSW chief executive Lee Germon said ultimately, money was not a factor, and instead his sport had to look after the North Sydney Cricket Club and stop them being moved for four games.

"It became very evident the impact to cricket was too great to agree to this request," Germon said.

"North Sydney have been playing at North Sydney Oval since 1895, so it is very much a key home and a spiritual home for them as well.

"We want them to play as much cricket as we can there.

"But because it is such a great cricket ground we have WBBL and international women's matches there as well, which also restricts accessibility for the club."

Bears unlikely to be able to play at oval in future

Germon warned it was unlikely cricket would be moved out of North Sydney in the future for the Bears due to the annual schedule.

That would leave Perth never able to play at the Bears' spiritual home, given North Sydney is unlikely to be considered fit for a regular-season NRL match.

"It's a key cricket ground," Germon said.

"We are faced with this every year. We would love to host a Sheffield Shield final at the SCG, but we can't because of the AFL.

"It's the world we live in. Cricket has the rights to North Sydney Oval in cricket season, and cricket is a summer sport.

"So it's very unlikely we would be able to accommodate any request of this nature, moving forward."

Germon said he had some sympathy for the Bears, who are scheduled to play their first pre-season match at the little-known western Sydney ground, Eric Tweedale Stadium.

But asked how he thought V'landys may feel about the situation, Germon said he was not fussed.

"I don't know, I don't know him. And I don't really care," he quipped.

"It's a decision we have made and it is a decision in the best interest of our sport."
