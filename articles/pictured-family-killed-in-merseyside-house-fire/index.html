Pictured: Family killed in Merseyside house fire

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:08 · source · permalink

‘Devoted and loving’ mother and three of her children died in early morning property blaze in Bootle

A family killed in a house fire in Merseyside have been pictured for the first time.

Elisha Brabin, 36, and her children Leo, 16, Ellis, eight, and Prudence, seven, died in the blaze in their home on Stone Square in Bootle on Tuesday.

Merseyside Fire and Rescue Service previously said four people were found “showing no signs of life” in an upstairs bedroom at around 3.45am.

In a tribute issued on Wednesday, the family said: “Our whole family is devastated by the loss of Elisha, Leo, Ellis and Prudence.

“Elisha was our beloved daughter, wife, mother, sister, aunty, niece and cousin. She was a devoted and loving mother who lived for her children. They were her whole world, and she meant everything to us.

“Our beautiful babies, Leo, Ellis and Prudence, were loved beyond measure and brought so much joy, happiness and love to our family. They filled our lives with laughter and precious memories that we will cherish forever.

“The tragic loss of Elisha and our three precious children has left us heartbroken. They have all been taken from us far too soon. The loss we are experiencing is unimaginable, and the impact on our family will be felt forever.”

The family thanked those concerned for their “kindness, support and understanding”.

A neighbour, who did not want to be named, told The Telegraph: “I heard a lot of screaming and shouting. One man was shouting, ‘Get them out’.

“I didn’t realise what it was at first, then I saw all the blue lights and knew it was serious. It’s so sad.

“I’m a grandmother of eight and can’t imagine losing any of them. The family must be absolutely devastated.”

Neighbours spoke of attempting to reach those inside using a stepladder but the flames and smoke were too intense.

Eight firefighters later wore breathing apparatus to enter the house and search for occupants.

Another neighbour who knew the family said: “They were lovely. We’re a close-knit community and most people knew them. They were great and got on with everyone.”

Ms Brabin is understood to have had four children with her husband Kane.

Neighbours said their fourth child was staying elsewhere at the time.

The fire service said one man had been taken to hospital after being found outside the property.

Flowers and cards, family photographs and other tributes have been left near the scene.

“Sleep tight forever in our hearts. So heartbreaking,” one read.

A GoFundMe fundraising page said the community had been “left heartbroken”. So far it has raised more than £5,000.

Nick Searle, chief fire officer at Merseyside Fire and Rescue Service,  said in a statement from the scene: “Firstly, our thoughts are with everyone who’s been affected by the tragic incident this morning.

“After a 999 call to Merseyside Fire and Rescue Service fire control room at 3.30am this morning, crews from surrounding stations were informed and mobilised to the scene with the first fire engine arriving at 3.45am.

“The initial fire crews arrived to find the semi-detached house well alight. Crews were wearing breathing apparatus, entered the building to search the property for occupants.

“Six fire engines were in attendance at the height of the incident. On arrival of the fire crews, a casualty was already outside of the property, and was taken into the care of North West Ambulance Service and subsequently transferred to hospital.

“During the course of the search of the property, firefighters discovered four casualties, showing no signs of life, and it’s now been confirmed that all four have tragically lost their lives.

“There will now be a full joint investigation with Merseyside Fire and Rescue Service and Merseyside Police into the cause of the fire. Our thoughts remain with the loved ones of those lost, and fire crews will maintain a visible and reassuring presence in the community over the coming days.

“There will now be a full joint investigation with Merseyside Fire and Rescue Service and Merseyside Police into the cause of the fire.”
