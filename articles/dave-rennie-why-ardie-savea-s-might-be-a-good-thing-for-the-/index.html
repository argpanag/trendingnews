Dave Rennie: Why Ardie Savea's might be a 'good thing' for the All Blacks

TheTools · 04.10.2026 18:10 · source · permalink

Dave Rennie believes that Beauden Barrett still has plenty to offer the All Blacks and has issued an update on Ardie Savea.

Dave Rennie has issued an update on All Blacks captain Ardie Savea and says that Beauden Barrett still has plenty to offer the team going forward.

Savea dislocated his shoulder in the third Rugby’s Greatest Rivalry series Test match against the Springboks in Soweto after scoring a brace of tries in the opening quarter.

Ardie Savea update

The 32-year-old has subsequently been ruled out for the remainder of the international season, with Rennie revealing that he could even miss the start of the Super Rugby Pacific campaign, with the back-rower signing with the Auckland-based club following the demise of Moana Pasifika.

“He has had an operation, and he won’t return until next year in Super Rugby, and I’m not even sure whether that’ll be in time for the start of Super Rugby, but he’s got a fair bit of time rehabbing,” Rennie told Newstalk ZB’s Weekend Sport with Jason Pine.

The All Blacks boss provided the update when an answering caller’s question with the listener theorising that the series would have panned out differently had Savea not sustained the injury.

“I agree with you around the impact that he made in South Africa. He did a great job leading our guys. He’s inspirational,” the head coach said.

“He was on absolute fire that day, scored a couple of tries, and I think got injured maybe 25 minutes into that game.”

Rennie added that there could be a silver lining from Savea’s injury, with the back-rower having an extended rest period while the All Blacks get the opportunity to explore more of their depth.

“So look, we’ve got really good depth,” he continued.

“We’ve got a lot of good players, but it’s hard to replace someone of that calibre. We will miss him, but it’s an opportunity to grow others over the next few months, and he’s had a big couple of years.

“So maybe we might reflect back in February that the break’s been really good for him, so he can have a big year leading into a World Cup.”

Beauden Barrett still has plenty to offer

Another player that has been ruled out for the remainder of the season is Ruben Love, with the Hurricanes’ fly-half sustaining an ankle injury in South Africa. The first-five has started all but one of the All Blacks’ Test matches, with Richie Mo’unga starting the RGR finale in Baltimore.

That was Mo’unga’s first appearance in the black jersey since the 2023 Rugby World Cup final and he is primed to don the number 10 jersey again versus Australia in the Bledisloe Cup.

Beauden Barrett also played his first Test match of the year in the USA, coming off the bench in the second half. While the experienced playmaker has fallen down the pecking order since Rennie took over from Scott Robertson, the head coach believes that he still has plenty to offer the team and once again hailed his mentorship of Love.

“I hadn’t coached Beauden prior to this and coached against him a lot. He is impressive; he trains the house down and is a big driver of the culture and the legacy,” he said.

“The amount of assistance he gave Ruben through that series- he sat down a lot and was really open and sharing with him, and a guy that he’s competing with.

“It was really impressive; he’s a really good man. I think that we played him a fair bit of 15 in South Africa, and he played really well, and so he’s still got a lot to offer New Zealand rugby.

“We’ve played Ruben in Tests and Richie there this year, and probably utilise Beauden more at 15, and that’s where he’s playing for Taranaki this week, but I still think he’s got a massive amount to offer the black jersey.”
