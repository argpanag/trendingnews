HILLSBOROUGH COUNTY, Fla. (CBS12) — Two adults and a child were rescued from Florida waters after their boat became disabled on Sunday.

A little before 6:30 p.m., the Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office marine unit rescued two adults and a child from a disabled boat near Fantasy Island that began to take on water.

Body camera video shows deputies approaching the disabled boat and hoisting the three people up from the disabled boat.

Deputies say one of the adults and a child were showing signs of hypothermia but were able to warm up inside the cabin. Deputies say all three were taken to the Davis Island Boat ramp.
