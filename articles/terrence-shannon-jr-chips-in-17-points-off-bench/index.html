Terrence Shannon Jr. chips in 17 points off bench

    Terrence Shannon Jr. chips in 17 points off bench

    Terrence Shannon

    MIN Guard-Forward #4
    Player Stats

    Terrence Shannon Jr. provided a boost off the bench with 17 points (7-of-11 FGs), one rebound, five assists and two three-pointers against the Nets on Monday.

    Shannon hasn’t been able to match his 34-point outing from last week, but he continues to be a reliable source of offense off the bench for Minnesota. He has scored in double figures in six straight games, which matches his longest streak of the season. Shannon hasn’t been able to contribute much value in other categories consistently, but he has been able to provide buckets, just as he did in this game.
