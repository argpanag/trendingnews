Christian Pulisic Nears Major Contract Extension With AC Milan

Christian Pulisic is reportedly on the verge of securing his future at AC Milan. The American forward has reached an agreement in principle to extend his contract until June 2031, a move that would keep him at San Siro throughout what should be some of the best years of his career.


According to ESPN, the 28-year-old is preparing to sign a four-year extension to his current deal, which expires in June 2027. An official announcement from Milan is expected in the coming days, although the agreement has not yet been formally confirmed by the club.

The new contract would also reportedly include a significant salary increase. Italian media reports suggest Pulisic could earn around €5 million net per season plus bonuses, up from his current salary of approximately €4 million.

Why AC Milan Is Determined to Keep Christian Pulisic

Since arriving from Chelsea in 2023 for approximately €22 million, Pulisic has established himself as one of Milan's most important attacking players. The move to Italy revitalized a career that had been disrupted by injuries and inconsistent playing time during his final seasons in England.

The American has rediscovered his confidence and consistency at San Siro, contributing goals, assists and creativity across domestic and European competitions. His performances have made him a central figure in Milan's attack, convincing the club to prioritize his long-term future.


Christian Pulisic's Career Revival After Leaving Chelsea

Pulisic arrived in Italy with an impressive résumé that included winning the 2021 Champions League with Chelsea, but his final years at Stamford Bridge had raised questions about his ability to maintain his best form over an entire season.

His time at Milan has helped answer those doubts. With greater responsibility and regular opportunities to influence matches, Pulisic has rebuilt his reputation as one of Europe's most effective American players, proving he can play a leading role at a major European club.

What Christian Pulisic's New Deal Means for the USMNT

The potential extension would also provide stability for one of the most important players in the United States men's national team. Pulisic has remained a key figure for his country, featuring at both the 2022 and 2026 World Cups and continuing to lead the national team's attack.

For Milan, securing his services through 2031 would represent a major commitment to the club's sporting project. For Pulisic, it would mark another milestone in a remarkable career turnaround, allowing him to focus on competing for trophies rather than uncertainty over his future.
