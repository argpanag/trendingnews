Barbara Palvin Walks 2026 Victoria’s Secret Runway 6 Weeks After Giving Birth

TheTools · 19.10.2026 06:01 · source · permalink

Barbara Palvin just made her first official appearance since welcoming her daughter with Dylan Sprouse back in September! And she didn't just show up at an event; she was the event. Barbara walked the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show just over a month after giving birth, strutting down the runway in a cute dress while Megan Moroney sang.

Barbara and Dylan announced the arrival of their baby just a few weeks ago, writing on Instagram, "We took Labor Day literally this year 👼🏻."

Dylan announced they named their daughter Sylvie on his podcast, saying, "There's a moment when I look at Sylvie, and I'm like, 'This is the coolest thing ever.' She's looking at me, and her eyes are opening. She's got a small wake window during the day, and she's looking at me, and she smiles or something, and it's the cutest thing."

Barbara has since shared photos of her motherhood journey and also included baby Sylvie in her VS casting announcement.

Meanwhile, Dylan reacted to the news by posting, "Proud of my missus, jumping right into the vs fashion show post birth. Built different. I don't know where you find the energy but damn!"
