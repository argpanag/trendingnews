In pictures: Thousands flock to Radio 2 in the Park in Stirling

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:20 · source · permalink
  • Published
    13 September 2026

Thousands of music fans partied over the weekend at Radio 2 in the Park with Stirling Castle as the backdrop.

A DJ kicked things off on Friday night with the crowd dancing along to classic singalong anthems and crowd-pleasing favourites, with presenters Sara Cox, Gary Davies, DJ Spoony, Michelle Visage and Rylan.

On Saturday, acts included Chaka Khan, Lulu, Texas, Emile Sande, Jack Savorreti, ABC, The Script and Alexandra Burke.

Simple Minds headlined on Sunday, topping a bill which also included Deacon Blue, Level 42, Blue, Cast, James Blunt and Appleton.
