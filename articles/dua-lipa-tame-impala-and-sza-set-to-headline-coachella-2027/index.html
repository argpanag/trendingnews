Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA set to headline Coachella 2027

TheTools · 11.11.2026 15:07 · source · permalink

The California festival has unveiled its lineup, which also features Madonna, Phoebe Bridgers and Robyn

The lineup for the 2027 Coachella festival has been revealed after months of speculation, with Dua Lipa, SZA and Tame Impala set to headline the California event.

The festival will also see Madonna headlining the dance tent with Zara Larsson, Robyn, Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer and Daddy Yankee set to perform. The festival will take place in the Empire Polo Club in Indio on 9-11 April and 16-18 April.

In recent weeks, rumors have swirled surrounding the 2027 lineup. SZA seemingly confirmed her booking earlier this week with an Instagram story teasing “SZAChella” as well as a new album and tour coming next year.

The event marks Tame Impala’s return to the desert after the group previously headlined in 2019, while SZA guested during Justin Bieber’s headline set earlier this year. Dua Lipa previously popped out during Martin Garrix’s 2017 appearance; her 2027 slot marks the first time a British female solo artist has headlined the festival.

This year’s Coachella was headlined by Bieber, Karol G and Sabrina Carpenter, with the latter’s performance praised by the Guardian’s Adrian Horton as “a dizzying full-scale hybrid production of undaunted vision … and immaculate performance”.

As well as US names, the 2027 festival also showcases global talent including Adéla, Becky G, Jisoo, Drain Gang, NCT and Måneskin.

In addition to Dua Lipa, British artists are well represented with Jorja Smith, EsDeeKid, Underworld, Shygirl and Lambrini Girls all set to perform.

Breakout talent on the lineup includes the young pop artist Underscores, rapper Nettspend, showgaze duo After and hyperpop artist Tiffany Day.

Madonna’s slot comes 21 years after she first headlined the dance tent around the release of her 2005 album Confessions on a Dance Floor; earlier this year, she released a sequel titled Confessions II which hit number one on the US and UK charts.

In August, Emily Eavis denied rumors that Madonna was set to play Glastonbury next year.

Passes for the 2027 Coachella festival go on sale on 13 November at 12pm pacific time. For the fifteenth year, YouTube will return as Coachella’s livestream partner for both weekends.
