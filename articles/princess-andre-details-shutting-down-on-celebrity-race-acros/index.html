Princess Andre details 'shutting down' on Celebrity Race Across the World

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:37 · source · permalink

Princess Andre opened up about experiencing a distressing flashback while taking part in this year's Celebrity Race Across the World. Currently, the content creator is taking part in the beloved BBC show with her brother, Junior.

The duo are competing against three other pairs as they all race from Borneo across Southeast Asia to Melbourne, Australia. During last week's episode, Princess and her brother were travelling at night to try to reach their next checkpoint.

When their taxi unexpectedly pulled over at the roadside, Princess suffered traumatic flashbacks. Though their driver was merely stopping for fuel, the incident triggered memories for Princess, who quickly began to panic.

It followed a violent carjacking she endured in 2018 alongside her mother Katie Price and Junior. During that incident, six men held the family at gunpoint, with the attackers demanding Katie hand over her children.

In the episode, BBC viewers witnessed Princess becoming visibly distressed as her brother offered reassurance. Discussing the experience with Louise Minchin and Gethin Jones on Wednesday's Morning Live (November 17), the 19-year-old reflected on what she was experiencing in that moment, reports the Mirror.

Gethin remarked: "This is a very emotional part of the show; you're on a car journey, which brings back lots of memories for both of you. You're very open and honest about what happened, aren't you?"

Princess responded: "Yeah, it was a scary time, to be honest, and I didn't realise that it still affected me until that happened. Afterward I felt silly though."

The ITV star revealed that memories from her past were triggered during their time on the BBC show, when they were travelling at night and pulled over at the roadside — a situation strikingly similar to something she experienced as a child.

She added: "I was like, 'Oh my God, what is happening?' I kind of just shut down. But you [Junior] were there for me, as always."

Elsewhere in the interview, Princess disclosed that her brother had been ringing her in the weeks leading up to their journey, ensuring she felt prepared and ready for what lay ahead.

She told the Morning Live presenters: "I was like, 'I'm fine!' but I got there, hadn't worn my shoes in, I forgot my watch, I forgot my fan, I forgot all these things. When I got there, I was like, 'This is a reality check; I'm not ready for this'."

At present, the siblings sit at the top of the leaderboard, having outpaced their fellow celebrities to reach the checkpoint in the most recent leg of the competition. However, with a fresh episode airing tonight (November 18), the standings could shift dramatically.

Junior and Princess told hosts Louise and Gethin that they have remained close with the other contestants, keeping in touch via a group chat to discuss their shared experience. Junior also revealed he was planning to host some of them at his home later that evening to watch the new episode together.

Morning Live continues weekdays on BBC One from 9:30am. Celebrity Race Across the World airs Wednesday's on BBC One from 8pm.
