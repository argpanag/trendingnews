“THIS IS ONE OF THE HARDEST DAYS OF MY LIFE” Željko Mitrović Fires 150 People and Announces: “I Will Have to Lay Off Another 1,000” | Serbiantimes.info EN

TheTools · 30.10.2026 13:49 · source · permalink

Željko Mitrović, owner of Pink Media Group, announced that October 29 was one of the hardest days of his life.

As he stated on the social network Instagram, 150 people have lost their jobs, and he will have to lay off around 1,000 more.

“One of the hardest days of my life, full of emotions, love, warmth, and understanding. I have disbanded all the ensembles at the Odeon Theatre, 150 people have lost their jobs, and across the entire system I will have to lay off around a thousand people, actually from around ten companies that have not yet managed to get back on their feet. See you, dear friends, in some better times! Your Žex loves you,” Željko Mitrović wrote.
