Travis Kelce Just Revealed The 1 Wish He Had After His Wedding To Taylor Swift

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:25 · source · permalink

    The NFL star also named “just the f**king best person on this planet” for all they did at his wedding.

    Travis Kelce is finally telling all about his wedding to Taylor Swift.

    The Super Bowl champion spoke about the couple’s big day on the season premiere of New Heights, the podcast he shares with his brother, Jason.

    “It’s a night out we’ll never forget,” Travis said of the July 3 nuptials.

    “Me and Taylor are so thankful for everybody that showed up. From the vows to just seeing everyone, to how much fun we had throughout the entire evening and hearing that kind of be relayed through everybody’s stories of the night,” he shared, calling it all “just so magical, man.”

    While the NFL star said he didn’t have any regrets, he did have one wish.

    “I wish it could have lasted even longer than it did,” he said. “It felt like it just happened in the blink of an eye.”

    “Well, once the drinks stopped being served, everyone kinda just starts to, you know, mosey out,” Travis quipped.

    Travis also made sure to give a big “special thank you” to the couple’s officiant, Adam Sandler, who he called “just the fucking best person on this planet.”

    “For him to be up for getting ordained in the state of New York and marrying me and Taylor in the way he did, in the very, you know, ‘Sandman style’ and giving us more than we could have ever imagined in that moment, knowing that — it was the biggest night of both of our lives,” he said, calling it all “absolutely insane.”

    Swift and Travis Kelce were married at Madison Square Garden in New York in front of about 1,000 guests.

    Conan O’Brien recently opened up about attending the exclusive nuptials and also shouted out his former Saturday Night Live co-worker.

    LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - JUNE 10: Conan O'Brien attends SiriusXM's Front Row with the cast of "Toy Story 5" at SiriusXM Studios on June 10, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Emma McIntyre/Getty Images for SiriusXM)

    “Adam Sandler was brilliant and really sweet and perfection,” O’Brien said this week on his Conan O’Brien Needs a Friend podcast. “And I keep bothering him because we’re friends. I keep telling him how it’s one of the finest things I’ve seen a person do. He just hit the tone. He was funny. He was sweet. It was perfection.”

    He also added that the wedding itself was both “lovely” and “very heartwarming.”

    “The whole thing was just absolutely beautiful,” the former late-night host said. “And people have said this, but I’ll just double down — it was really sweet and real.”
