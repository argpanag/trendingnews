Hurricanes teammates visit pickleball championships with Stanley Cup | NHL.com

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:45 · source · permalink

Aho, Svechnikov, Chatfield surprise fans with trophy in Cary, N.C.

© Philip Pritchard

Pickleball craze has officially reached the Stanley Cup.

The coveted NHL trophy stopped by the Veolia Pickleball National Championships in Cary, North Carolina, on Thursday to surprise fans.

The #Stanleycup surprised fans at the @PPLTOUR. Pretty cool event for champions @HockeyHallFame @Canes @NHL pic.twitter.com/Bt4vELADcY

The #Stanleycup surprised fans at the @PPLTOUR. Pretty cool event for champions @HockeyHallFame @Canes @NHL pic.twitter.com/Bt4vELADcY

The Cup took center court during the event and posed with some of the pros who are competing for the pickleball national championship.

Carolina Hurricanes players Sebastian Aho, Andrei Svechnikov and Jalen Chatfield were in attendance with Lord Stanley, and even got in on the pickleball action themselves.

View this post on Instagram

The Hurricanes teammates also selected the order of a fantasy football draft out of the Cup, live from the pickleball event.

View this post on Instagram

The Hurricanes will begin their title defense on Tuesday, Sept. 29 (5 p.m. ET; ESPN) against the Florida Panthers to officially drop the puck on the 2026-27 NHL season.
