The 8 Most Expensive Fantasy Bras in Victoria’s Secret History—Starting at $3 Million

The 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show is set to take place in Los Angeles on October 18.

Returning after an eight-year hiatus, the custom-made Fantasy Bra is often worth millions and remains one of the show's most coveted honors.

InStyle looks back at the most expensive Fantasy Bras in Victoria's Secret history, starting at a minimum of $3 million.

It's almost time for my Super Bowl, aka the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show. With highly anticipated musical performances, some of the world's top models preparing to hit the runway, and the return of a beloved tradition, this Sunday's show is shaping up to be one for the books. Naturally, there's no better time to pay homage to the Angels who came before. Enter: the Fantasy bra. Custom-made over hundreds of hours, dripping in diamonds, and often worth millions, the dazzling creation is one of the show's most iconic traditions.

From Heidi Klum to Adriana Lima and Jasmine Tookes, wearing the Fantasy Bra has long been considered one of the highest honors a supermodel can receive. It all started in 1996, when Claudia Schiffer debuted the first-ever Fantasy Bra in a Victoria's Secret advertising campaign, valued at $1 million. Since then, the designs (and their price tags) have only gotten more extravagant. After an eight-year hiatus, the Fantasy Bra is finally making its grand return for 2026, with the brand partnering with Jacob & Co. on a design featuring an Angel-cut diamond reportedly valued at up to $22 million.

To celebrate its comeback, we're taking a trip down memory lane to revisit the most expensive Fantasy Bras in Victoria's Secret history.

01 of 10

Jasmine Tookes's Bright Night Fantasy Bra (2016)

Credit: Getty Images

Jasmine Tookes had a $3 million moment when she stepped onto the 2016 runway wearing the Bright Night Fantasy Bra. Designed by Eddie Borgo and crafted by A.W. Mouzannar, the dazzling creation featured 9,000 gemstones and took a whopping 700 hours to complete.

02 of 10

Marissa Miller's "Harlequin" Fantasy Bra (2009)

Credit: Getty Images

Marisa Miller took the runway in the $3 million Harlequin Fantasy Bra in 2009. Designed by Damiani, the piece featured 2,300 diamonds and a 16-carat heart-shaped pendant, taking 800 hours to create.

03 of 10

Selita Ebanks's "Very Sexy Holiday" Fantasy Bra (2007)

Credit: Getty Images

Selita Ebanks took holiday dressing to a whole new level in the 2007 Very Sexy Holiday Fantasy Bra, valued at $4.5 million. Designed by Mouawad, the extravagant set included a bra, thong, garter, bracelet, and hair clip adorned with nearly 9,000 diamonds and colorful gemstones, taking over 880 hours to create.

04 of 10

Adriana Lima's Black Diamond Fantasy Miracle Bra (2008)

Credit: Getty Images

Adriana Lima took center stage in the $5 million Black Diamond Fantasy Miracle Bra in 2008. Designed by Martin Katz, the bra featured nearly 3,900 gemstones, including two black diamond drops and 120 round white diamonds, with a total weight of 1,500 carats.

05 of 10

Karolína Kurková's Star of Victoria Fantasy Bra (2002)

Credit: Getty Images

In 2002, Karolína Kurková' wore the $10 million Star of Victoria Fantasy Bra, designed by Mouawad. Inspired by the brand's Very Sexy collection, the floral design featured thousands of rubies and emeralds arranged into roses and leaves, with a 60-carat diamons at its center.

06 of 10

Heidi Klum's Millennium Bra (1999)

Credit: Getty Images

In the early days of Victoria's Secret Fantasy Bra tradition, Heidi Klum wore the $10 million Millennium Bra in 1999. The design featured 2,000 diamonds set in platinum, accented by an array of sapphires.

07 of 10

Tyra Banks's Diamond Dream Bra (1997)

Credit: Getty Images

Just one year after the Fantasy Bra made its debut, Tyra Banks wore the $3 million Diamond Dream Bra in 1997. Designed by Harry Winston, the piece featured 100 diamonds along its sweetheart neckline, with a dangling 42-carat pear-shaped diamond serving as the centerpiece.

08 of 10

Candice Swanepoel's Royal Fantasy Bra (2013)

Credit: Getty Images

For the 2013 show, Candace Swanepoel wore the $10 million Royal Fantasy Bra, custom-fitted to her body. Designed by Mouawad, the 18-karat gold creation featured more than 4,200 gemstones, including diamonds, sapphires, and rubies, with a 52-carat pear-shaped ruby at its center.

09 of 10

Heidi Klum's Heavenly Star Bra (2001)

Credit: Getty Images

Heidi Klum made Fantasy Bra history in 2001 when she became the first model to wear one on the Victoria's Secret runway. Valued at $12.5 million, the Mouawad-designed Heavenly Star Bra featured more than 1,200 pink sapphires, 2,300 white diamonds, and a 90-carat diamond centerpiece, complete with a matching diamond belt.

10 of 10

Gisele Bündchen's Red Hot Fantasy Bra (2000)

Credit: Getty Images

Topping the list at $15 million, Gisele Bündchen's Red Hot Fantasy Bra remains the most expensive in history (although it was reserved for the VS holiday catalog rather than the runway). Featuring 1,300 diamonds and Thai rubies, the design earned a Guinness World Record for the priciest piece of lingerie ever made at the time.
