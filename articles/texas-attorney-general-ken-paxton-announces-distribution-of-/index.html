Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton announces distribution of more than 7 million free eggs statewide

TheTools · 09.09.2026 23:03 · source · permalink

Three egg producers agreed to donate more than 7 million eggs in an antitrust settlement

Egg prices CRASH! Farmers STILL losing money

FOX Business correspondent Grady Trimble reports from Matteson, Illinois, highlighting the struggle of local egg farmers like Marty Thomas. Despite surging egg supply driving consumer prices down, farmers face losses as their production costs remain high, forcing them to adapt.

More than 1.7 million free eggs have already been delivered to Texas food banks, with another 5.3 million slated to arrive in the coming weeks as part of an antitrust settlement with three major egg producers, Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton announced Tuesday.

The statewide distribution stems from settlements reached in June with Cal-Maine Foods, Centrum/Versova and Hickman's Egg Ranch, resolving antitrust claims brought by the Trump administration's Department of Justice and a coalition of state attorneys general over allegations the companies conspired to inflate egg prices between 2022 and 2025.

Under the agreements, the companies will donate more than 7 million eggs to Texas food banks and pay a combined $3.3 million to participating states.

"After securing 7 million eggs for the people of Texas, I am excited to announce that Texans will now have an opportunity to receive cartons of free eggs in communities across the state," Paxton said. "I will continue to help our families put food on the table and do everything within my power to lower prices and help Texans who are struggling to make ends meet."

The distributions are being coordinated with Feeding Texas, the state's largest hunger-relief organization. Seventeen food banks are participating in the effort.

The Houston Food Bank is expected to receive the largest allocation of 1,544,400 eggs, followed by the North Texas Food Bank in Plano and the Tarrant Area Food Bank in Fort Worth, which will each receive 842,400 eggs. Other participating food banks are located across the state, including Austin, San Antonio, Tyler, Corpus Christi, El Paso, Laredo, Odessa, Pharr, Beaumont, Amarillo, Lubbock, Victoria, Abilene and Wichita Falls.
