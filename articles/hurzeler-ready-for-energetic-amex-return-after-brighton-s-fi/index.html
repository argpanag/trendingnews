Hurzeler ready for energetic Amex return after Brighton's five-game away run

TheTools · 08.11.2026 14:23 · source · permalink

Fabian Hurzeler is looking forward to feeling the energy of the Amex after two weeks of travels.

The Seagulls squad flew home from Spain yesterday following Thursday’s late night at Getafe.

They now prepare to face a Brentford side who have made a strong start to the season after being pipped to a European place by Albion last term.

The Seagulls have completed a run of five successive long-distance away games and head coach Hurzeler said: “We are playing in front of our fans, and I think that's also what we are looking forward to after five away games in a row.

“To play in front of our fans, in the Amex, that has to give everyone a push.

“This has to give the fans a push. It has to give us a push.

“I'm looking forward to seeing everyone with a very, very energetic atmosphere in the stadium.”

Albion could not fly straight back to England after the game due to the kick-off time.

They left the Coliseum stadium at about midnight local time and had a late breakfast on Friday before returning to Lancing for a recovery session.

The Thursday-Sunday routine is becoming quite normal but Hurzeler said: “I'm not sure if the body ever gets used to these kinds of travels, because it's definitely a lot of travel.

“But it's something we can adapt to and we can manage quite well, because we have a lot of great staff.

“We have a lot of great staff members who take care of the recovery from the players.

“We have a good organisation who do a brilliant job of organising everything and it makes everything really smooth, and I think that's the most important.

“The most important now will be that we are mentally and physically ready for Sunday, and that's our responsibility.”

Brentford, who are sixth, have drawn all four of their Prem away games to date.

Hurzeler said: “We expect a quite physical Premier League game, a completely different game to today.

“We know it's also a game of long balls.

“Brentford tries to go in the back of us, they try to be very physical for the second ball.

“They have a clear style of play, very physical.

“We have to be ready for the physicality. I think that's the most important that we are ready for this.”
