HCFG, HCD, and SCB out of Kurashev race

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:35 · source · permalink

National League

The future of the 27-year-old national team player and his potential return to Switzerland has been a hot topic in the National League for weeks. 

Icon Sportswire / Matthew Huang

According to a report in 24 heures, HC Fribourg-Gottéron, HC Davos, and SC Bern have reportedly withdrawn from the race to sign Philipp Kurashev. According to the newspaper, this leaves two clubs still in the running: Lausanne HC and EV Zug. Blick had already speculated yesterday that LHC was the favorite to sign him—partly because, as reported yesterday, Kurashev was spotted on the ice at the Vaudoise aréna and visited the game in the evening.

The Südostschweiz also reports that Kurashev is no longer in the running for a spot with HCD. According to the report, the Graubünden-based club reportedly offered him a lucrative contract. However, Kurashev had asked for more time to consider the offer. The Graubünden-based club didn't have that time, given its current on-ice and personnel situation—yesterday, the club announced it had signed Canadian forward Joseph Blandisi.
