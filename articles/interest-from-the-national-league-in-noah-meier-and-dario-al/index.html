Interest from the National League in Noah Meier and Dario Allenspach

TheTools · 16.10.2026 12:13 · source · permalink

National League

According to information from the "Blick", the two SCL Tigers players have attracted other clubs' attention.

dieBildmanufaktur.ch

The SCL Tigers are hard at work building their roster for the upcoming season. Team manager Pascal Müller has announced several extensions in recent weeks. Müller also wants to renew the contracts of defenseman Noah Meier and forward Dario Allenspach. However, other clubs are also interested in the two players.

In Meier’s case, HC Fribourg-Gottéron is reportedly showing particular interest, Blick writes. As for Allenspach, EHC Biel is believed to be keen on signing him. The Seeland-based club is said to have taken an interest in him even before he moved from EV Zug to the Emmental.

It remains to be seen whether Müller will succeed in keeping both players around. The team manager aims to have the lion's (or Tiger's) share of the roster for the upcoming season in place by Christmas.
