Independiente Santa Fe v Vasco da Gama, first leg of the CONMEBOL Sudamericana quarter-finals: kick-off time, teams and how to watch the match | CONMEBOL Sudamericana

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:43 · source · permalink
  • Independiente Santa Fe, who eliminated River Plate in a historic penalty shoot-out, welcome Vasco da Gama to Bogotá to kick off the tie.
     
  • The 'León' have suffered just three home defeats in 44 knockout-stage matches in CONMEBOL competitions (27 wins and 14 draws).

Three decades after their only previous meeting, Independiente Santa Fe and Vasco da Gama meet again in a knockout tie. Back then, in the semi-finals of the 1996 Copa CONMEBOL, the 'León' advanced on penalties in Bogotá after the tie finished 2-2 on aggregate. History repeats itself at 'El Campín', with the 2015 CONMEBOL Sudamericana champions at home and a Vasco side buoyed by their emphatic win in Asunción.

Santa Fe are coming off the most impressive qualification of their campaign: they eliminated River Plate. Following a goalless draw in Bogotá, Pablo Repetto's side drew 1-1 at the 'Monumental' — Sebastián Driussi put the 'Millonario' ahead before Franco Fagúndez equalised in the 80th minute — and prevailed in the penalty shoot-out, with 41-year-old Hugo Rodallega wearing the captain's armband. Rodallega and Fagúndez, with four goals apiece, have scored two-thirds of the 'Cardenal's' goals in continental competition this season, with almost all of them coming after half-time: a side that grows into matches as the minutes go by.

Independiente Santa Fe in the CONMEBOL Sudamericana 2026: results, squad and schedule

Vasco, managed by Pedro Emanuel, came through their tie against Olimpia with a display of character. After a 0-0 draw at São Januário, they fell behind in Asunción to Esteban Matus' goal but turned the game around in emphatic fashion: Thiago Mendes equalised, David put them ahead before the break, and Adson and Claudio Spinelli completed a 4-1 victory. The 'Cruzmaltino' are one of the teams to have collected the most points in this competition in matches in which they conceded first, while Adson, with nine involvements in sequences ending in goals, is one of their most dangerous attacking weapons.

For Santa Fe, the tie is a test of their strength at home: just three defeats in 44 knockout-stage matches in CONMEBOL competitions, along with six wins in seven meetings with Brazilian opponents. For Vasco, it is a chance to prove that the version seen in Asunción — a side that refuses to give up — is the one capable of taking them back to a continental semi-final.

The tie is about to begin: the winner on aggregate will progress to the semi-finals; if the scores are level after the two legs, the tie will be decided on penalties. The second leg will be played in Rio de Janeiro next week.

Match facts

  • The only previous meeting between Independiente Santa Fe and Vasco da Gama in continental competitions came in a knockout round, when they faced each other in the semi-finals of the 1996 Copa CONMEBOL. The tie began in Brazil, finished 2-2 on aggregate and the León advanced on penalties (6-5) at home.
     
  • Independiente Santa Fe have suffered just three home defeats in 44 knockout-stage matches in CONMEBOL competitions (27W 14D). Against Brazilian opponents alone, the León have won six of their seven meetings (1D).
     
  • Adson (Vasco) is the player with the most involvements in sequences ending in goals (9) since the start of the CONMEBOL Sudamericana 2026, alongside Alejandro Hohberg (Cienciano), although the Cruzmaltino player has played three fewer matches than his Imperiales counterpart (8 compared with 11).
     
  • Vasco are among the four teams to have collected the most points since the CONMEBOL Sudamericana 2026 group stage in matches in which they fell behind, with seven points. The Cruzmaltino have avoided defeat on all three occasions when they conceded first in this edition (2W 1D).
     
  • Two-thirds of Santa Fe's goals this year across the CONMEBOL Libertadores and Sudamericana (8/12) have been scored by Hugo Rodallega and Franco Fagúndez, with four apiece. Seven of those eight goals have come in the second half.
