Meet the Parents of Rising U.S. Tennis Star Alex Michelsen

TheTools · 08.09.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Both of his parents played tennis competitively in college.

Alex Michelsen first picked up a tennis racket at age three, hitting backhands in the family garage in Aliso Viejo, California. Two decades later, he is one of the most promising young Americans on the ATP Tour, currently ranked in the top 50 in the world.

It turns out tennis runs deep in the Michelsen family as his parents introduced him to the sport and shaped the way he plays it. “My parents had contrasting game styles,” Michelsen told The New York Times in 2023. "My mom would stay at the baseline and make every ball. I took that from her. My dad likes to serve and volley, come to the net and be more creative, so I took that from him. I feel like I’ve combined those two very well.”

Below, get to know more about Alex Michelsen’s parents, Sondra and Erik.

Meet Alex’s mom, Sondra:

Raised in Mission Viejo in a community far more focused on swimming, Sondra did not pick up a racket until around age 11, as she noted in an interview earlier this year. She eventually traded the pool for the court, and started playing junior events throughout Southern California at age 12, competing for her high school, and then as a walk-on at San Diego State University—the only school she applied to—without a scholarship or a guaranteed spot on the roster. She worked her way to the top of the lineup, eventually becoming the team’s No. 1 player.

After college, she coached tennis for a few years, later earning her teaching credential. Sondra taught third grade for more than a decade teaching third grade at St. Mary’s School in Aliso Viejo, where Alex and his older brother Jake attended. She was also Alex’s first coach. “She taught me how to play and I was hitting with her almost every day until I was 15 or 16,” reads a quote from Alex’s ATP Tour bio. “My earliest tennis memory was when I was four and it started raining and I was crying to her because I couldn't play.”

Meet Alex’s dad, Erik:

Erik Michelsen took a more traditional path to college tennis. A three-time All-American at the University of Redlands near San Bernardino, California, Erik followed in his own father’s footsteps at the school. He later went on to build a career as a lawyer.

Erik, like Sondra, often stays home with Sondra while Alex travels on the tour these days. “It’s a healthy balance,” said Eric Diaz, who was Alex’s coach when speaking to The New York Times in 2023. “His mom and dad taught him so much about the game and they also know when to give him time to grow on his own. Alex likes that and it is [sic] worked very well for him.”
