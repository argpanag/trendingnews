Cork lottery player wins over €77k in Saturday night's draw

TheTools · 25.10.2026 16:45 · source · permalink

As there was no winner of Saturday’s €3.5m jackpot, Monday’s prize is heading for an estimated €3.6m.

A Cork lottery player won €77,437 after they matched five numbers and the bonus in Saturday night’s draw.

The winning Lotto Quick Pick ticket was purchased on the Saturday at Collins SuperValu on Main Street in Carrigaline.

In Kerry, the same amount was won in the same draw at Centra on Muckross Road in Killarney.

The winning numbers in Saturday’s Lotto draw were: 2, 15, 24, 25, 30, 43, and the bonus was 16.

Winners are advised to contact 1800 666 222 or email claims@lottery.ie.

National Lottery spokesperson Emma Monaghan said the lottery operator was advising anyone who purchased a Lotto ticket in either shop on Saturday to check their tickets.

“If you’re one of our winners, sign the back of your ticket, keep it somewhere safe and contact our prize claims team,” she said.

As there was no winner of Saturday’s €3.5m jackpot, Monday’s prize is heading for an estimated €3.6m.

In 2023, Premier Lotteries Ireland, the company that holds the exclusive licence to operate the Irish National Lottery, was sold to French gaming company and lottery operator La Française des Jeux in a deal worth €350m.
