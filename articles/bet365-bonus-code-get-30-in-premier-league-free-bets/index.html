Fulham vs Man United preview (Sunday 4.30pm)

Manchester United travel to Craven Cottage in the middle of a wretched week, and with the pressure on Michael Carrick rising sharply. Wednesday’s Carabao Cup exit to Brighton was as bad as it gets: 2-0 up inside ten minutes through 19-year-old Shea Lacey’s debut goal and Mason Mount’s finish, then beaten 3-2 as Charalampos Kostoulas, Pascal Groß and Maxim De Cuyper turned the tie around at Old Trafford.

It was the first time in 50 years that United have led 2-0 at home and lost, having won 143 of the previous 145 such matches, and the second consecutive season they have gone out of the competition at the first hurdle. Loud boos greeted the final whistle three days after the derby defeat to a ten-man Manchester City.

Fulham, by contrast, will fancy this more than any fixture so far. Alvaro Arbeloa’s side are still without a win, sharing the division’s longest winless run with Aston Villa, Bournemouth and Tottenham, but they earned a creditable goalless draw at Anfield last weekend and have been competitive rather than overrun in their defeats, 3-2 at home to Chelsea, 3-2 to Crystal Palace.

Seven conceded in four is not the record of a side in freefall. Carrick made eight changes for the cup tie, so his strongest XI should be fresh, and Bruno Fernandes remains the man everything runs through. But United have won once in four league games, and a Fulham side yet to get going could hardly ask for a better moment to face them.
