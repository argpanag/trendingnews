PBA: Meralco spared from major breakdown after holding off Blackwater

TheTools · 16.10.2026 12:17 · source · permalink

Meralco let its advantage slip away but still managed to find ways to fend off Blackwater's fightback for a 120-115 victory in the PBA Season 50 Governors' Cup on Tuesday at the Ninoy Aquino Stadium.

Import Jordon Varnado delivered in the endgame to save Meralco from wasting a 20-point lead, finishing with 31 points, seven rebounds, seven assists, two steals, and two blocks as the Bolts revived their playoff chances by improving to 4-5 in Group B.

Chris Newsome registered 24 markers, including a crucial three-point play in the final 90 seconds, together with six boards and three dimes while Bong Quinto and Cliff Hodge chipped in 15 and 12 points apiece.

"You know, you credit Blackwater. We were in our flow and then they changed their lineup, they went small ball, they used players who could switch. That switching worked for a while, they were able to catch up," Meralco head coach Luigo Trillo said.

"But we were able, in the last two minutes, we made a couple of basketball plays. A lot of heroes from us."

Meralco showed no signs of rust early on despite playing its first game since August 14. Better, they turned a slim 68-58 halftime lead to as big as 86-66 midway in the third period after Newsome hit a three-pointer.

However, Blackwater had no plans of backing down in the face of that huge deficit. The Bossing slowly caught up with Sedrick Barefield hitting a triple and a layup to bring his side to a two-point game, 101-98.

Barefield, Dalph Panopio, and import Kentrell Barkley continued to spark the Blackwater fightback with Barefield completing a jumper that gave the Bossing a 111-109 lead.

Newsome answered with a three-point play before Varnado dished out an assist to Cliff Hodge for a layup, then added one of his own to give Meralco a 118-113 lead with 27.3 seconds left.

Varnado delivered the finishing blow with two free throws as Meralco held on to avoid a late collapse and secure its fourth win in nine games.

Barefield led Blackwater's brave stand with 31 points and five rebounds while Barkley chimed in with 29 markers, 11 boards, and five assists.

Scores:

Blackwater 115 – Barkley 31, Barefield 29, Ilagan 15, Paonopio 10, Una 9, Tratter 7, David 5, DiGregorio 3, Escoto 3, Murrell 3, Demusis 0, Austria 0, Cruz-Dumont 0, Mallilin 0.

Quarters: 32-36, 68-58, 97-86, 120-115.

—JKC, GMA News
