Suns vs. Blazers predictions, odds and best bets: Dame is home, and the model likes Portland on opening night

TheTools · 21.10.2026 01:20 · source · permalink

For the first time since 2023, Damian Lillard will run out of the Moda Center tunnel in a Portland uniform. He’s 36 now and back from the torn Achilles that cost him all of last season, and Wednesday’s season opener against the Phoenix Suns (7:00 PM PT) is the kind of night Rip City has been waiting three years for.

The franchise’s all-time great isn’t the only headliner. Ja Morant, acquired from Memphis this summer for Jerami Grant and Kris Murray, makes his Blazers debut alongside Lillard in a backcourt with two players who have combined for 11 All-Star selections. First-year coach Micah Nori has one of the more interesting rosters in the West.

Opening-week injury worries have already reached Portland. Deni Avdija, the Blazers’ projected leading scorer, is questionable with right ankle soreness, and his status should become clearer after shootaround. It would be a surprise if he missed the home opener, but if he does, Sidy Cissoko likely moves into the starting lineup and Morant would take on an even bigger share of the offense. Jrue Holiday is also a minor concern after sitting out the preseason finale with right-hand swelling (his X-ray was negative), and Morant has played just 79 games over the past three seasons. Portland went 42-40 last season and lost to the Spurs in five games in the first round.

Phoenix arrives with a point to prove after Oklahoma City swept it out of the first round, led by Devin Booker (26.1 points per game last season) and Dillon Brooks (a career-high 20.2). Deep Betting Market Analysis and Model Angles:

Outright Win Probability: Trail Blazers (60.4% Model Probability). The models give Portland a 60.4% chance to win its home opener, slightly above the 58.7% that DraftKings’ -142 price requires. The edge is small but real, and an opening-night crowd for Lillard’s return could add to it. The Suns at +120 need a 45.5% win rate, well above the model’s 39.6%, so there’s no value on the upset.

Point Spread Angle: Trail Blazers -2.5 (-110 at DraftKings). This is the best number on the board for Blazers fans. The simulations project a 117-114 Portland win, and at the wider -3.5 they still give the Blazers a 53.4% chance to cover. DraftKings is dealing a point less, so Portland only needs to win by three. That moves the model’s cover probability comfortably above the 52.4% break-even. The spread is the most efficient way to back the Blazers. Avdija’s status is the biggest risk to the pick, because the model’s projection assumes he plays. If he’s ruled out, expect the line to move toward Phoenix and the edge to shrink.

Total Points Angle: Pass at 231.5. The model projects 231 combined points, almost exactly on the DraftKings total. It rates the Under slightly more likely (51.5%), but the -115 price requires 53.5%. The Over at -105 needs 51.2%, against a model probability of 48.5%. Neither side has value. Both teams are working in new pieces on opening night, which adds uncertainty to the total.

Featured Prop Value: Ja Morant 20+ Points (+107). The model projects Morant for 20 points in his Blazers debut, right at DraftKings’ 20+ line. Because he only needs 20 or more, a projection that lands exactly on the number gives him slightly better than even odds of hitting, and he’s priced as an underdog at +107, which needs only 48.3%. Phoenix’s Dillon Brooks 18+ Points (-106) is the other strong prop on the board, since his 19-point projection clears the line by a point. Lillard 20+ (-116) is harder to back: the model has him at 19 points, and the price is steeper. Avdija’s ankle adds to the case. (If Portland’s projected leading scorer (23 points) is limited or out, Morant becomes the clear first option.)
