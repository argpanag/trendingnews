Who Is the New Below Deck Med Deckhand Replacing Max? Meet Massi (Bio and Photo) | Bravo

TheTools · 08.09.2026 01:01 · source · permalink

Get to know Massi Scarcia, who joined Below Deck Med Season 11's deck crew after Max Salvador's dramatic exit.

Key Takeaways

  • Massimiliano “Massi” Scarcia joins Below Deck Mediterranean Season 11 as the new deckhand replacing Max Salvador, bringing more than a decade of yachting experience that includes work as a deckhand, bosun, and captain.
  • The 26-year-old Italian yachtie from La Spezia described himself as highly experienced and adaptable, with interests including motorcycles, diving, fishing, and off-roading, while also sharing memorable charter tips and favorite destinations in the Bahamas and Sardinia.
  • Captain Sandy and several crew members reacted positively to Massi’s arrival and quick adjustment to the team, though his presence also introduced tension among the deck crew and sparked early social dynamics onboard.

A new deckhand named Massimiliano "Massi" Scarcia has officially come aboard for Below Deck Mediterranean Season 11, replacing Max Salvador, who abruptly departed amid a dangerous situation involving his father. During Season 11, Episode 14 of Below Deck Med, Massi shared details on his life and extensive experience in the yachting industry.

How to Watch

Below Deck Mediterranean airs Mondays at 8 p.m. ET on Bravo. Episodes stream next day on Peacock.

"If you couldn't tell, I'm from Italy," the 26-year-old newbie said, while introducing himself in a confessional. "I don't have any blood in my veins. I've just got espresso, cigarettes, and stress."

Adding that he's usually "the guy in charge," Below Deck Med's new deckhand continued, "I've been in yachting for 10 years. I've been on small boats, big boats. I've been a deckhand; I've been a bosun a few times."

Trending Shorts

In fact, Massi was already serving as a bosun on a different boat just one day before joining Captain Sandy Yawn's crew. He said that he even planned to return to that same vessel after wrapping his time on M/Y Akira One. As he met his new fellow yachties, Chef Joy Lefaucheur immediately caught Massi's eye.

Massi Scarcia's bio for Below Deck Down Med Season 11

The Below Deck Med Season 11 cast reacts to Massi Scarcia joining the crew

Upon arrival, Massi presented Captain Sandy with his two-page CV. In a producer interview, she said, "He has loads of experience. He's been a captain, so I'm hoping that he can just come on deck, pick up like he's always worked here, and just help us get through the rest of the charter season."

Aesha Scott's first impression of Massi? "He seems lovely!" the chief stew gushed to Kayley Smith.
