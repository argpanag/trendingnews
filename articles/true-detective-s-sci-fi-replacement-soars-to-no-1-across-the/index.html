HBO appears to have firmly embraced its franchise era with shows such as House of the Dragon, The Penguin, and the upcoming Harry Potter. Harry Potter, in particular, is being regarded as the biggest television event of the year, with an ambitious decade-long plan already in motion. In the meantime, HBO is also doubling down on spin-off shows inspired by some of its other major movie franchises, such as the True Detective-inspired DC Universe offshoot Lanterns. The series premiered to critical acclaim in August, and with the first season nearing its climax, a clearer picture of its viewership has emerged.

According to the latest Nielsen report, the seventh episode of Lanterns drew approximately 7 million viewers across the United States in three days. The show retained a spot on the acquired series streaming rankings as well, amassing around 550 million minutes watched for the week. The DC Universe series, starring Aaron Pierre and Kyle Chandler, has averaged around 12 million viewers per week in the United States so far, and around 19 million viewers globally. It should be mentioned that Nielsen tracks only television viewership (excluding mobile and other streaming device metrics).

By comparison, It: Welcome to Derry averaged nearly 20 million views per episode in its first season, while the third season of Euphoria averaged 25 million viewers per week, The Penguin averaged 27 million weekly viewers, and The Pitt's second season averaged 15 million viewers per week globally. Created by Chris Mundy, Damon Lindelof, and Tom King, Lanterns has received critical acclaim. The series now holds a "Certified Fresh" 92% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus reads, "Lanterns lights the way to a controlled and fantastical escape, in which Kyle Chandler and Aaron Pierre bring audiences into their world of wonder through fearless performances, grounded writing, and charismatic chemistry." Also starring Poorna Jagannathan, Garret Dillahunt, Paul Ben-Victor, and Laura Linney, the series will conclude its first season this Sunday.
