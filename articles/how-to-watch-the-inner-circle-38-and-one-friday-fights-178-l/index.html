How To Watch The Inner Circle 38 And ONE Friday Fights 178 Live On November 13

TheTools · 10.11.2026 06:12 · source · permalink

After opening its blockbuster November 2026 lineup with a heart-racing triple-header weekend, ONE Championship is back with a second dose of compelling Asia primetime events, including the opening round of the ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Grand Prix.

The world’s largest martial arts organization will broadcast The Inner Circle 38 and ONE Friday Fights 178 live to over 195 countries and territories around the globe this Friday, November 13.

First, viewers can watch The Inner Circle 38 exclusively on live.onefc.com beginning at 6:30 p.m. ICT.

Then, viewers can watch ONE Friday Fights 178 live via ONE Championship’s global broadcast partners at 8:30 p.m. ICT. The show can also be viewed on ONE Championship’s YouTube channel, ONE Championship’s Facebook page, and watch.onefc.com.*

To view your local listings and start times by region, check below.

*Geo-restrictions apply
