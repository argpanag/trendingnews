An Improving Game?

I think it’s fair to say that the reaction to this game was initially mixed. Our own reviewer Simon Cardy praised the game for its characterisation of its protagonist, but criticised it for overly flashy combat that grew a bit tiresome as the game wore on.

There was also the rather amusing community backlash to the so-called “fart gas” scent trails in the game. Players didn’t like these for a number of reasons. First, they felt it was a little too hand-holdy, and their creative exploration was being stifled. Secondly, they just kind of…looked quite bad.

Thankfully, now we’re a few patches down the line, and Insomniac have allowed players to be able to toggle these hints on and off to their hearts’ content.
