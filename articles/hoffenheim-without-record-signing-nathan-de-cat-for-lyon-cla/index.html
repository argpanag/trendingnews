Hoffenheim without record signing Nathan De Cat for Lyon clash

According to Le Progres, Hoffenheim will be without three players for Thursday’s Europa League tie against Lyon at 21:00, including record signing Nathan De Cat.

The Bundesliga side beat bottom club Mönchengladbach 2-1 on Sunday, but head into matchday three under pressure after defeats to OFI Crete 0-2 and Besiktas 2-3.

OneFootball Videos

Austrian coach Christian Ilzer, who has alternated between 4-3-3 and 4-4-2 this season and arrived from Sturm Graz in 2024, will be missing defender Albian Hajdari with a foot injury and winger Patrick Wimmer with a hamstring problem, the latter joint-top scorer with two in six. De Cat, 18, a Belgium international signed from Anderlecht for €18.5m this summer, has a meniscus injury and is ruled out until the end of 2026.

Goalkeeper Oliver Baumann, forward Adam Hlozek and veteran Andrej Kramaric, a 2018 World Cup finalist against France, are available.

Ilzer said the weekend win helped and that performances have improved, though progress is still needed across all three competitions. He described Lyon as in form and top of Ligue 1, having beaten Anderlecht and Crystal Palace, tactically flexible, and very physical with pace across every line.
