Bianca Manalo opens up on breakup with Win Gatchalian

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

Bianca Manalo opened up on her breakup with Senate President Sherwin “Win” Gatchalian.

On “Fast Talk with Boy Abunda” on Thursday, the beauty queen and actress said they broke up because Win is too focused on serving the country.

“I understand that Tito Boy because he’s very hardworking,” Bianca said in a mix of English and Filipino.

Saying it's "better that way" instead of Win having another vice, the Kapuso celeb said, "Who am I to stop him? So okay, I’m happy for him and I will forever support him.”

Bianca also said they continue to remain friends and "share custody of our three dogs.”

“When I need help, I still talk to him, he is still the one who helps me. Someone from his staff calls me if I need something. He still takes good care of me,” she added in a mix of Filipino and English.

When asked why Bianca is always rumored to be the third party when celebrity relationships end, she simply said, “I don’t know.”

“Now that I’m single, I hear a lot of stories that are like what—why is it that when I’m single, I am always linked to whoever. I think I need to be taken again, joke,” Bianca said joked.

But turning serious, she said she is not ready to have a boyfriend yet.

“I’m happy. I’m focusing my happiness with my nephews and my nieces and my mom,” Bianca said.

“So I’m okay for now. I’m not in a rush.”

Gatchalian has always kept his relationship with Bianca private, often responding with “no comment” when asked about them.

In 2025, he arrived at the State of the Nation Address alone. — LA, GMA News
