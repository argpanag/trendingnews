Minnesota Wild Games Available to Spectrum Customers | Minnesota Wild

TheTools · 26.09.2026 22:36 · source · permalink

SAINT PAUL, Minn. - The National Hockey League (NHL) today announced an agreement with Spectrum that will allow Spectrum TV customers in the Minnesota Wild local broadcast territory to watch Wild games this season.

“We’re thrilled to bring Wild+ to Spectrum customers throughout the Minnesota Wild broadcast territory this season,” said Minnesota Wild Chief Revenue Officer and Executive Vice President Mitch Helgerson. “Adding Spectrum is another important step in our efforts to ensure broad distribution of Wild+ throughout the State of Hockey.”

The NHL is in discussion with additional cable and satellite providers regarding distribution of Wild+. More information on Wild+ channel availability will be shared as agreements are finalized. Fans should contact their provider to make sure they can watch games on Wild+ this season.

Fans within the Minnesota Wild local broadcast territory can also watch Wild games on Wild+ on Prime Video by purchasing either a monthly ($19.99) or annual ($99.99) subscription. Wild+ is available to all Amazon customers as a standalone subscription on Prime Video and is not included with an Amazon Prime membership. Fans can subscribe using any free Amazon account; a paid Prime membership is not required.

More details on subscriptions will be announced before the start of the regular season at watch.wild.com.

Minnesota Wild telecasts on Wild+ will feature Anthony LaPanta as play-by-play announcer, Ryan Carter as lead analyst, Kevin Gorg as rink-side reporter, and Katie Storm and Wes Walz serving as the primary hosts of the Wild pre- and postgame shows. Alex Stalock and Lou Nanne will also serve as analysts for select games.

For more information on Wild+, the Minnesota Wild multimedia television network, please visit watch.wild.com.
