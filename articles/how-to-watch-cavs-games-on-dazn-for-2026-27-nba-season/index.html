How to watch Cavs games on DAZN for 2026-27 NBA season

CLEVELAND, Ohio -- The Cleveland Cavaliers will have a new streaming home when they hit the hardwood for the upcoming NBA season this fall. The team announced on Sept. 1 they will broadcast their games on DAZN for the 2026-27 campaign.

DAZN offers several subscription tiers. Details on the various plans are below.

What is DAZN?

DAZN is a rising subscription-based streaming service dedicated to live and on-demand sports, including boxing, MMA, soccer and more recently, the streaming home for select NBA teams in the 2026-27 season.

How many Cavs games can I watch on DAZN?

Of the 82-game regular season slate, it was announced in the deal that 17 nationally televised games will be excluded from DAZN. Streaming details are still being finalized for 15 games to be available for free through a to-be-announced over-the-air partner. This makes it likely there will be at least 50 available Cavs games only aired on DAZN.

What plans does DAZN offer?

Here’s a breakdown of your DAZN plan options:

Monthly Pass: $19.99 per month - two streams in one location, includes downloads.

Monthly Pass Ultimate: $24.99 per month, five streams in two locations, includes multiview, downloads, alternate angles and feeds.

Season Pass: $119.99 per year - two streams in one location, includes downloads.

Season Pass Ultimate: $139.99 per year, five streams in two locations, includes multiview, downloads, alternate angles and feeds.

DAZN also will distribute select Rock Entertainment Sports Network (RESN) programming for free, including the Cleveland Monsters, Cleveland Charge, high school games and regional college sports.
