Initial agricultural losses from Storm Champi at NT$133 million - Focus Taiwan

TheTools · 24.10.2026 11:48 · source · permalink

Taipei, Oct. 24 (CNA) Tropical Storm Champi had caused an estimated NT$133.36 million (US$4.2 million) in agricultural losses across Taiwan as of Saturday afternoon, the Ministry of Agriculture (MOA) said Saturday.

The brunt of the losses reported to date have occurred in Yunlin County, accounting for more than 80 percent of the total, with garlic crops suffering the heaviest losses, the MOA said.

As of 5 p.m., Yunlin reported estimated losses of NT$109.6 million, followed by Yilan County at NT$8.39 million, New Taipei at NT$7.24 million and Taoyuan at NT$3.46 million, according to the ministry.

Crop losses to date have totaled about NT$133.07 million islandwide, with 2,405 hectares of farmland affected, ministry data showed.

Garlic accounted for the largest share, with damage estimated at NT$53.07 million across 326 hectares. Other affected crops included peanuts, onions, sweet corn and tea.

Chen Shang-ren (陳尚仁), director-general of the Central Branch of the Agency of Agriculture and Food, toured damaged fields in Yuanchang, Dongshi and Dapi townships in Yunlin County and pledged swift disaster relief.

Chen said he would quickly report the damage to the ministry and expedite the issuance of relief payments so that affected farmers can receive assistance soon and resume farming as quickly as possible.

(By Chang Hsiung-feng, Chiang Yi-ching and Lee Hsin-Yin)
