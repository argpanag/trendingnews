Ellidy Pullin announces engagement to Brock Wadsworth

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:22 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Aussie stars

September 8, 2026 - 9.22pm

Ellidy Pullin has shared the beautiful news that she’s engaged to her partner, Brock Wadsworth. 

The Darling, Shine! Podcast host made the big announcement on Instagram, sharing a snap of herself with her man, flashing her new rock to the camera.

Ellidy Pullin is engaged to partner Brock Wadsworth, sharing the beautiful news on social media.  Instagram/@ellidy_

“HE WANTS TO BE STUCK WITH ME FOREVER 😭😭😭💍💍💍,” she captioned the joint post.

In one snap she kissed him on the cheek and held her ring finger up proudly to the camera, as they celebrated with champagne.

In another, the couple embraced in a hug, soaking in the special moment.

The post has been flooded with so much love and congratulatory messages for the couple.

“Oh this is just the best. And that ring!! Wow,” commented Today’s Renee Bargh.

“Nawwwwww so so happy for u both! Xxxxxxx,” replied Elyse Knowles.

“Love it!! Massive congrats guys!!!!!” wrote DJ Chris Lake.

“This has made my day. You deserve all the happiness,” commented one follower.

Others, who have never actually met Pullin, celebrated this special moment for her and wished her all the best for this new chapter of her life.

Wadsworth proposed in Europe, where the couple are currently on holiday with Pullin’s daughter Minnie.  Instagram/@ellidy_

Wadsworth proposed while the couple are on a trip to Europe with Pullin’s daughter Minnie.

In some posts shared on TikTok the podcaster and model has flashed her stunning new ring, and celebrating their engagement.

The news of her engagement comes six years after the tragic death of her partner, Olympic snowboarder Alex “Chumpy” Pullin.

Wadsworth shared a photo of “his girls” after proposing to Pullin.  Instagram/@brockwad

Opening up about this new chapter of her life and finding love again in a December episode of her podcast, Pullin admitted that there were times when she did feel guilty for being so happy.

Pullin said she knew Wadsworth for “years” before they dated, and explained he’s her good friend’s brother.

Pullin admitted that initially she accidentally led her new boyfriend on, knowing she didn’t want anything serious.

Wadsworth turned up halfway through a group Bali trip after an invite from Aussie DJ Paul Fisher, who is married to Darling, Shine! podcast co-host Chloe Fisher.

Pullin has previously spoken about how hard it was at first to navigate a new relationship and the guilt of moving forward.  Instagram

But she confessed it wasn't the perfect start, admitting it took a long time for her to want to date him, citing her own fears and anxieties that she "didn't want to hurt him."

“I was making things up in my head [about why he wasn’t my person], now I’m just like all of those things were in my head... I was just scared of my feelings,” she said.

Despite things fizzling out initially, they both ended up in Europe at the same time months later and he flew to London to see her – which is when she realised that she had actually missed him and things changed.

Elsewhere in the podcast episode she explained it was hard to navigate her grief and guilt about the passing of her late partner while they were dating.

Pullin and Chumpy dated since 2012, but he tragically died in 2020 in a spearfishing accident.  Instagram

“Chumpy passed away five years ago. Grief is absolutely f---ed,” Pullin confessed.

“Of course, I love Chumpy, I still love him so much. I’ll never not love him.”

The couple had been dating since 2012 but never married, so she legally changed her surname to Pullin after his death in 2020 in his honour.

Pullin welcomed her ‘miracle’ daughter, Minnie, in October 2021. She was conceived via IVF using sperm retrieved from her late partner, and born 15 months after his death.
