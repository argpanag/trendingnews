Saquon Barkley Clears Injury Report for Eagles Cowboys Game

TheTools · 26.11.2026 21:06 · source · permalink

Philadelphia will have Saquon Barkley available for its Thanksgiving showdown against the Dallas Cowboys after the running back was removed from the injury report. The Eagles cleared the 2026 season's injury concern surrounding Barkley, who had been dealing with a neck issue following the team's Week 11 victory over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Barkley participated in limited practices during the week but ultimately avoided an injury designation. According to the injury report shared on November 25, 2026, he was among the Philadelphia players no longer listed as unavailable or questionable for Week 12.

The running back had also experienced an illness, while Dallas Goedert (shin) and Quinyon Mitchell (hamstring) were among the other Eagles players whose names appeared in the injury update.

Barkley expressed confidence about returning earlier in the week after sustaining an injury against Pittsburgh.

"I believe so." Barkley said when asked whether he expected to play in Week 12, according to RotoWire Fantasy Football's November 22, 2026, report.

His availability comes after an injury-disrupted campaign that has restricted his participation to eight games. According to his 2026 season statistics, Barkley has played fewer than 40 percent of Philadelphia's offensive snaps but has still recorded four rushing touchdowns and averaged 4.3 yards per carry.

Against Pittsburgh in Week 11, Barkley carried the ball 17 times for 75 yards and a touchdown despite dealing with a hamstring problem and a stinger. His performance extended his scoring streak to four consecutive games.

That run includes his previous meeting with Dallas in Week 7, when Barkley produced 62 rushing yards and a touchdown on only seven carries. He played just 15.2 percent of the Eagles' offensive snaps in that matchup.

The Cowboys' defensive struggles against the run could also influence Philadelphia's offensive approach. According to the season statistics cited by Sports Illustrated, Dallas ranks 31st in the NFL in EPA per rush and has conceded 15 rushing touchdowns, the fourth-highest total in the league.

Philadelphia will enter the NFC East rivalry with Barkley available as quarterback Joe Milton prepares to lead the offense during the Thanksgiving matchup.
