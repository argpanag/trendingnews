UNL’s bid to rejoin American Association of Universities faces pushback

TheTools · 21.09.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. (KOLN) - The president of the American Association of University Professors urged the American Association of Universities to withhold consideration of the University of Nebraska–Lincoln’s readmission to the organization in a letter on Monday.

The AAUP warned that recent actions at UNL, including faculty terminations and department closures, have compromised its standing as a leading research institution.

Last December, NU regents approved the termination of 58 faculty members, including 39 tenured professors, through the closure of four academic departments: Educational Administration; Earth and Atmospheric Sciences; Statistics; and Textiles, Merchandising and Fashion Design.

According to the AAUP, these decisions have had immediate consequences for students, research programs, and faculty leadership, including members of the UNL AAUP chapter.

“I urge the AAU to withhold consideration of UNL’s readmission,” wrote Todd Wolfson, the AAUP president. “Unless and until the institution demonstrates a clear and credible commitment to restoring shared governance, protecting tenure, and aligning its practices with the principles that define leading research universities.”
