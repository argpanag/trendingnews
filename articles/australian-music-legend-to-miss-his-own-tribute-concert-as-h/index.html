Australian music legend to miss his own tribute concert as health worsens

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:41 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Australian singer John Farnham is most famous for 1986 hit You’re the Voice. Photo / Getty Images

The health of iconic Australian singer John Farnham has deteriorated in recent months, according to a statement from his family.

Farnham, who had a decorated solo career as well as a period fronting Little River Band in the early 80s, has battled oral cancer and underwent extensive surgery on an aggressive tumour in 2022.

A tribute concert at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena is planned for this weekend but Farnham’s health will prevent him from attending.

“John’s health has deteriorated in recent months. He now requires continuous oxygen support and uses a wheelchair for mobility.

“It has also become necessary for John to receive 24-hour nursing care at home, ensuring trained support is always available when he needs it,” read a statement on son Rob Farnham’s social media.

The statement said Farnham was “deeply moved” by the tribute concert and public concern.

“If things were different, John would have loved nothing more than to attend the concert. He would undoubtedly have been a little overwhelmed by all the fuss, but he would also have wanted to thank everyone involved in person.

“As a family, we have decided that it is best for John to remain at home, where he can be safe and comfortable,” read the family’s statement from wife Jill and sons Robert and James.

Farnham’s career took off in 1967 with his hit Sadie the Cleaning Lady. He joined Little River Band in 1982 before finding further solo success in 1986 with Whispering Jack – which included the smash hit You’re the Voice.

The Melbourne tribute will feature artists such as Keith Urban, Richard Marx, Jimmy Barnes, Vanessa Amorosi and Robert Farnham, among others.
