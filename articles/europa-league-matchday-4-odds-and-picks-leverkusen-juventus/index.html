Europa League Matchday 4 Odds And Picks: Leverkusen, Juventus

TheTools · 03.11.2026 12:38 · source · permalink

The Europa League league phase reaches its halfway point on Thursday, and several favourites look shaky. Leverkusen host winless Marseille, Ferencváros take a perfect record to Milan, Juventus visit a struggling AZ, and Crystal Palace, Celtic and Sunderland arrive out of form. The best Canadian prices on each game and six picks.

Form is the theme of Matchday 4. Several of Thursday's favourites have been losing on the weekends, while a handful of underdogs are winning almost everywhere. That gap between reputation and results is where the best prices are on this card, and the six games below are the ones where it is widest.

Every club plays eight league-phase games, so this is the midpoint. Five teams have a perfect nine points after three rounds (Benfica, Leverkusen, Bournemouth, Ferencváros and Olympiacos), while Marseille, Celtic, Viktoria Plzeň and Ararat-Armenia are still on zero. The early games kick off at 12:45 p.m. ET, the late games at 3 p.m. ET.

Bayer Leverkusen Vs Marseille Prediction

Leverkusen have nine points and an 8-0 goal difference in Europe. The Bundesliga has been rougher lately, with a 3-0 loss at Hoffenheim and a 4-2 home defeat to Stuttgart. Marseille are 34th with one goal from three European games, yet they have won four straight Ligue 1 matches and scored 13 times doing it, including 5-2 at Angers and 4-3 against Le Havre.

A Leverkusen defence that has just shipped seven goals in two league games against a Marseille attack averaging more than three goals a game over its last four in Ligue 1 points one way: goals at both ends. Both teams to score at 1.69 at 20Bet, Granawin and Tonybet is the bet. Leverkusen at 1.45 looks too short to touch.

AC Milan Vs Ferencváros Prediction

Ferencváros are one of the surprise packages, with three wins, nine goals and a 3-1 victory at Celtic among them. Milan are 24th on three points after losing 3-0 at Bournemouth in Matchday 3. They do arrive in a better mood after beating Inter 3-1 on Saturday, three days after a 4-2 home loss to Bologna.

Milan should win, but by two goals? That is a lot to ask against a side that has scored at least twice in every European game this season. Ferencváros +1.5 at 2.04 at 1xBet is the value play, with 2.00 at 22Bet and BetLabel close behind. Milan at 1.31 offers nothing.

AZ Alkmaar Vs Juventus Prediction

(Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

AZ have six European points, but their domestic slump is hard to ignore: no goals in three of their last four Eredivisie games, with defeats to Feyenoord, Twente and Ajax. Juventus are unbeaten in the league phase on seven points and won 2-0 against Napoli on Sunday, though losses to Lazio and Genoa in October show they can still have bad days.

Juventus at 1.96 is the strongest pick of the night. It is available at Granawin, 1xBet, 20Bet and Tonybet. An AZ side that has stopped scoring in the Eredivisie is unlikely to trouble Juventus enough to justify an even-money price on the visitors. Juventus's own inconsistency is the main risk.

Crystal Palace Vs Hoffenheim Prediction

Palace have one point from their last four games in all competitions, a 2-2 draw at Besiktas, and have lost 5-0 at Brighton, 2-0 to Newcastle and 2-0 at Tottenham in that spell. Hoffenheim beat Lyon 3-0 in Matchday 3 and then Leverkusen 3-0 three days later, before a 2-0 loss at Werder Bremen.

Palace being favourites here is the biggest mismatch between price and form on the card. Hoffenheim at 3.73 at 1xBet is the bet, with 3.64 at BetLabel and 22Bet the next best. The value here is with the in-form away side; the risk is that Palace finally click at home.

Celtic Vs Besiktas Prediction

Celtic have lost all three European games and conceded nine goals, and the weekend brought a 2-0 defeat at Falkirk after a 2-2 home draw with Dundee United. Besiktas have seven points, won 3-2 at Hoffenheim and drew 2-2 with Palace.

Besiktas at 2.23 at 1xBet is the pick. They have scored in every European game, and Celtic have kept no clean sheets in Europe. Celtic Park is a hard place to go, and that is the reason to keep the stake sensible, but the form gap is too wide to ignore.

Sunderland Vs Dinamo Zagreb Prediction

Sunderland have seven points in Europe from three tight games, scoring only four goals. They are winless in four Premier League matches with one goal in that run. Dinamo Zagreb have four points from a 0-0 draw, a 1-0 win over Anderlecht and a 4-2 home defeat to NEC Nijmegen.

The moneyline is not a serious option at 1.47 for a team scoring this rarely. Under 2.5 goals at 2.29 at 1xBet, or 2.25 at BetLabel and 22Bet, fits how Sunderland's games have gone in both competitions. This is one for small stakes; a single early goal changes the picture.

Prices move quickly before European nights, so compare sports betting odds on Thursday morning. Bettors in Alberta can also compare the best Alberta Sportsbooks before the late games.

Europa League Matchday 4 Best Bets

Juventus at AZ is the top pick. Hoffenheim at Palace and Besiktas at Celtic are the two away sides with the form to back at plus money, and Ferencváros +1.5 and both teams to score in Leverkusen vs Marseille take on favourites that look overrated. Sunderland's under is the smallest stake of the six.
