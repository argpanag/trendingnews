At the Mapei Stadium in Reggio Emilia, Sassuolo host Fiorentina in a Serie A meeting between two teams in urgent need of points. Neither side's league position reflects their initial ambitions.

Alberto Aquilani's Sassuolo are struggling. A win over Juventus raised hopes among their Emilia-based supporters, but they have taken just one point from their last five league matches. A heavy 6-2 defeat against Lazio followed in the most recent midweek round. Now 15th, Sassuolo need to turn things around.

With three straight league defeats and seven goals conceded across those games, Paolo Vanoli's Fiorentina sit 17th. Their only recent positive result is a 2-2 draw against Atalanta, hardly enough to mask a disappointing season so far.

Vanoli must find makeshift solutions, particularly in defence, with Parisi, Sottil, Ranieri, Atta and Pongracic all unavailable. Sassuolo have several absences of their own, and Thorstvedt joins the list of unavailable players after being sent off against Lazio.

For both sides, three points would help ease the pressure near the bottom of the table. Here's what you need to know about watching the match live on TV and via streaming.

Sassuolo are without Ismael Koné, Fedde Leysen, Daniel Boloca, Edoardo Pieragnolo, Yeferson Paz and Jay Idzes through injury, while Kristian Thorstvedt is suspended. Aquilani is expected to start Turati in goal, with Caleta-Car, Odenthal, Cinquegrano and Doig in defence, Matic, Bakola and Adzic in midfield, and Berardi, Esposito and Laurienté as the attacking trio.

For Fiorentina, Parisi, Sottil, Ranieri, Atta and Pongracic are out injured. Vanoli is expected to field De Gea in goal, with Jimenez, Dragusin, Viery and Joao Mario in defence, Ndour, Brescianini and Oulai in midfield, and Mastantuono, Pellegrino and Njie in attack.

Recent form

Sassuolo have no wins, one draw and four defeats from their last five league matches. They most recently lost 6-2 to Lazio at the Mapei Stadium, having previously fallen 2-0 to Como. A 1-1 draw against AC Milan brought their only point in that spell. Sassuolo have scored seven goals and conceded 13 across those five matches.

Over their last five outings, Fiorentina have no wins, two draws and three defeats. They most recently drew 2-2 with Atalanta, having lost 2-1 to Inter before that. Fiorentina also lost 3-1 to Como and 2-1 to Genoa. Across the five matches, they have scored seven goals and conceded 11.

Head-to-head record

The teams last met in April 2026, when their Serie A match ended 0-0. Sassuolo have the better record across their last five meetings, with two wins to Fiorentina's one and two draws. Sassuolo won 3-1 in December 2025, while Fiorentina won 5-1 in April 2024.

League table

Sassuolo sit 15th in Serie A, while Fiorentina are 17th. Both must look over their shoulders in the fight to avoid relegation.
