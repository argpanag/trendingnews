U2 have just kicked off a 'secret' gig in the school yard at Mount Temple

TheTools · 25.09.2026 14:06 · source · permalink

U2 ARE PERFORMING a ‘secret’ gig this afternoon to mark the 50th anniversary of the band’s formation.

The concert kicked off within the last few minutes.

The four-piece are performing in the yard at Mount Temple, the north Dublin school where they first formed in 1976 after 14-year-old Larry Mullen posted his ‘drummer seeks musicians’ message on the school noticeboard.

They played their last gig as Mount Temple students in the school yard in 1978. 

Microphones were set up on top of a porta-cabin earlier this morning, with a drum kit on a nearby platform. 

Around 1,000 students are watching the gig, with some waving towards cameras and holding pieces of paper and homemade placards. 

Friends and family of the band are seated in a separate section.

This includes their former manager Paul McGuinness, painter and sculptor Guggi, who grew up with Bono in Glasnevin, and broadcaster Dave Fanning, who was among the first to play the band’s music on radio and has premiered several of their tracks over the years. 

Over 100 invited journalists are also present, alongside around 100 members of staff. 

The group announced a new album, Carnaval De Luz, earlier this week.
