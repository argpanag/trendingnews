Confirmed line-ups | Forest vs Spurs

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:47 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

We can confirm the team line-ups for today’s Premier League visit to Nottingham Forest (3pm BST).

Roberto De Zerbi makes three changes to the team that started against Newcastle United last weekend with Savio back in the frame and coming in for his first start in our colours, joining Destiny Udogie and Conor Gallagher in the team.

Archie Gray, Andy Robertson and Mateus Fernandes drop to the bench.

Deadline day loan signing Mykhailo Mudryk is also named among our substitutes.

Here’s the team in full…

Starting XI: Kinsky, van Hecke, Gallagher, Tel, Udogie, Tonali, Savio, Marmoush, Pedro Porro, Bentancur, van de Ven (c).

Substitutes: Dubravka, Senesi, Robertson, Gray, Bergvall, M Fernandes, Mudryk, Kudus, Solanke.

Nottingham Forest, meanwhile, line up as follows…

Starting XI: Sels, Williams, Murillo, Gibbs-White (c), Ndoye, Delap, Schlager, Cunha, McAtee, Milenkovic, Aina.

Substitutes: Victor, Diomande, Hudson-Odoi, Dominguez, Wood, Igor Jesus, Yates, Netz, Munoz.
