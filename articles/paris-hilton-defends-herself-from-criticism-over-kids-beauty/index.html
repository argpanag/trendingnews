Paris Hilton has defended herself against criticism over her Halloween costume inspired by Beauty and the Beast.

The star dressed herself as Belle and her kids as Chip and Lumière from the Disney classic.

"London said she wanted to dress up as Chip," Hilton commented in a response to a viral video about the costumes.

Though negative reactions to her family's Beauty and the Beast Halloween costumes was ever a surprise, Paris Hilton issued a defense for herself amid ongoing blowback.

After specific corners of the internet disavowed Hilton's decision to dress herself for the occasion as lead character (and Disney princess) Belle while her children sported costumes inspired by supporting characters Chip and Lumière, Hilton explained the decision Wednesday in a social media comment.

"Beauty and the Beast is one of their favorite movies," Hilton wrote of her kids, London and Phoenix, who posed alongside their mother for a portrait commemorating their 2026 Halloween costumes.

Paris Hilton and her family as Belle, the Beast, Chip, and Lumiere from 'Beauty and the Beast' for Halloween
Credit: Brian Ziff

The comment came under a viral video in which an Instagram user spoke out about people misidentifying Hilton's daughter as Mrs. Potts (voiced by Angela Lansbury in the beloved movie) when she was actually Chip (Mrs. Potts' teacup child).

Hilton, 45, added that "London said she wanted to dress up as Chip" because "she said it was her favorite character in the movie," which was released by Disney in 1991.

Still, Hilton's initial post highlighting the family's costumes generated intense pushback from the public.

"Cute but maybe the kids should have been belle and beast and the adults the side dishes I mean it is a kids holiday," one commenter responded.

Another added: "Main character when it should be your kids moment," while one said, "Why not let your daughter be Belle? Attention has to be on her! Poor little girl!"

Though she received criticism over her family's Halloween costumes, the Beauty and the Beast-inspired outfit wasn't the only one Hilton sported for the 2026 holiday.

Paris Hilton's family Halloween costumes inspired by Disney's 'Beauty and the Beast'

Earlier this week, Hilton shared photos and video of herself while dressed as her friend and pop star Britney Spears, with whom Hilton was regularly photographed in the early aughts.

"Oops… I #Slayed it again," Hilton wrote in a caption, referencing the look inspired by Spears' performance with a live snake from the 2001 MTV VMAs.
