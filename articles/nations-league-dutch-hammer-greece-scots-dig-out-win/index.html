Nations League: Dutch hammer Greece, Scots dig out win

TheTools · 13.11.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

Getty

John McGinn's double got Sebastien Pocognoli off the mark and off the hook in Scotland's 2-1 victory over Group B1 makeweights North Macedonia at Barclays Hampden.

Visiting goalkeeper Stole Dimitrievski denied the Scots three times in a goalless first half and Billy Gilmour missed an open goal from eight yards before fellow midfielder McGinn broke down the North Macedonia rearguard in the 54th minute – only for the limited visitors to level through striker Elmin Rastoder three minutes later.

However, the Aston Villa playmaker scored from the spot in the 73rd minute to give Pocognoli his first home win as Scotland head coach.

Captain Andy Robertson equalled Sir Kenny Dalglish’s 102-cap record and will beat it away to Switzerland on Monday while by taking his international goal tally to 24, McGinn joined Hughie Gallacher as joint-third on the all-time Scotland list.

Scotland moved on to seven points from five fixtures, one point behind second-placed Slovenia who drew 0-0 at home to Group B1 winners Switzerland, but the aim of the runners-up place which brings a promotion play-off spot and a more favourable seeding for the 2028 European Championship qualifiers, is tough.

The Scots play the dominants Swiss in St Gallen while Slovenia will be odds-on to take care of North Macedonia, who, with no points from five games, will finish bottom of the table.

Germany's bid to reach the Nations League quarter-finals will go to the wire as they defeated nine-man Serbia 3-1 and the Netherlands did a demolition job on Greece.

Jurgen Klopp’s side moved level on eight points in their group with Greece thanks to an own goal from Srdjan Babic and strikes from Florian Wirtz and Brajan Gruda at Belgrade’s Rajko Mitic Stadium.

The hosts pulled one back through Babic in the fixture – played behind closed doors due to racist and discriminatory behaviour by Serbian fans in September – which ended in farce when second-half substitutes Vanja Dragojevic and Andrija Zivkovic were given their marching orders in the last quarter of an hour.

Dragojevic was initially given a yellow card but was upgraded after it was deemed he stood on Joshua Kimmich’s ankle, while Zivkovic saw red for foul and abusive language later on.

Tijjani Reijnders scored a hat-trick, Jurrien Timber bagged a brace and Crysencio Summerville and Donyell Malen were also on target as the Netherlands made sure of their spot in the knockout stages by thrashing Greece 7-0.

Germany will host the Dutch in their final group game on Monday, with Greece welcoming Serbia. Greece currently sit in second spot in the group by virtue of a better head-to-head over Germany.
