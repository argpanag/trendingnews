What the autumn equinox on September 23 means for your zodiac sign

TheTools · 22.09.2026 11:52 · source · permalink

The autumn equinox on September 23 officially means that the days will begin to grow shorter and temperatures will start to drop—even though India will experience a second summer in October. Astrologically and spiritually, the autumn equinox marks a key turning point of transition and balance. This time invites us to slow down, let go and engage in introspection. “For astrology enthusiasts, this also marks the sun’s passage from Virgo to Libra, bringing themes of balance, relationships and harmony to the forefront,” explains astrologer Efimera.

Aries

Your season begins with the spring equinox. The autumn equinox is therefore almost the opposite of your natural energy. When all you want to do is charge ahead, this time of year invites you to slow down, take a step back and seek balance. As the sun enters Libra, relationships and compromise take centre stage. Approach the equinox by resisting the urge to do everything yourself and making room for others’ opinions. Even an Aries can take a step toward others.

Taurus

You love stability and balance, so the autumn equinox is one of the most fulfilling times of the year for you. As the seasons change, you’re already thinking about comfort, daily rituals and a perfectly cosy home. Venus, your ruling planet, is currently in Scorpio, which gives your relationships a deeper and more intense dimension. Prepare by making your home a sanctuary and surrounding yourself with everything that gives you a sense of security—preferably with a drawer well-stocked with hot chocolate.

Gemini

You’re the most sociable sign of the zodiac. The calmer, more contemplative energy of the autumn equinox may take you a little while to adjust to. Fortunately, the sun’s entry into Libra emphasises communication, interaction and social connections, which suits your rhythm much better. Get ready by reorganising your schedule and checking in with everyone you’ve promised to reach out to. This period may be calmer, but your schedule doesn’t have to be.

Cancer

As a sign associated with family and a cosy haven, you see the arrival of autumn as an invitation to retreat to your favourite spot. This year’s autumn equinox aligns particularly well with your energy. Mars continues to transit Cancer until September 28, giving you an extra boost of motivation and determination. Get ready by preheating the oven, breaking out a salad bowl and inviting your loved ones over. Your heart is at home.

Leo

Your sign is ruled by the sun, and the autumn equinox marks the moment when your ruling planet leaves Virgo to enter Libra, centring relationships, balance and cooperation. After months of shining brightly, it’s in your best interest to step out of the spotlight a bit. Approach the darker months by taking pleasure in being less visible and living more fully. You don’t need to be the centre of attention to leave a lasting impression.

Virgo

The autumn equinox marks the end of Virgo season and offers you the perfect opportunity to take stock of things. After a period devoted to organisation, routines and tidying up, the sun’s entry into Libra encourages you to aim for balance rather than perfection. Get ready by wrapping up your administrative tasks and allowing yourself not to get everything done all at once.

Libra

This is your moment—the autumn equinox coincides with the sun’s entry into your sign and marks the beginning of your astrological season. With equal amounts of light and darkness, it perfectly reflects your natural taste for balance and harmony. Prepare by identifying what feels off in your life and deciding what needs to change. And yes, you have every right to make it all aesthetically flawless.

Scorpio

This time promises to be particularly intense for you. Venus entered your sign on September 10 and is still there at the time of the equinox, bringing relationships, attraction and emotional intensity to the forefront. Prepare yourself by being honest about what you truly want and about the people who matter to you. This isn’t the season for superficial relationships: if something is important to you, go the extra mile.

Sagittarius

You love movement, thrills and the idea of the next big adventure. So the calmer, more contemplative atmosphere of the autumn equinox may feel a bit foreign to you. The sun’s move into Libra invites you to pay more attention to your relationships and your balance before diving back in headfirst. Prepare by treating yourself to an evening or two of quiet time. You don’t need to go on a trip to feel like you’re making progress.

Capricorn

Capricorn, you are one of the most determined and ambitious signs, and balance doesn’t always top your list of priorities. The autumn equinox serves as a helpful reminder that productivity isn’t everything. As the sun enters Libra, cooperation and relationships take on greater importance. Prepare by scheduling real moments of rest, rather than promising yourself you’ll take a break ‘when everything is done.’ Here’s a little spoiler: it’s never quite over.

Aquarius

You’re one of the most independent signs, but the big picture sometimes absorbs you to the point where you forget to check in with yourself. The autumn equinox is an opportunity to restore the balance between what you give to others and what you keep for yourself. Prepare by asking yourself a simple question: Am I spending all my energy on others, to the point where I find myself completely drained? Take advantage of this change of season to make self-care a part of your routine, rather than thinking about it only when you’ve had enough.

Pisces

Sensitivity, romanticism and deep intuition define you—so the pragmatic, balancing energy of the autumn equinox may seem a world away from your dreamy universe. The sun’s entry into Libra encourages you to find harmony without sacrificing your sensitivity. Prepare for this change of season by spending time in nature, taking long walks and giving yourself the space you need to let your emotions flow. You don’t need to sort through every single one of them—sometimes, it’s enough just to feel them.
