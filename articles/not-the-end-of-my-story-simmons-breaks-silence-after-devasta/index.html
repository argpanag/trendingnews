‘Not the end of my story’: Simmons breaks silence after devastating season-ending injury

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:59 · source · permalink

Just days away from making his much-anticipated NBA return after nearly two years away from the game, Australian All-Star Ben Simmons has suffered a season-ending injury during a training session, with the 30-year-old taking to Instagram to address the devastating setback.

According to ESPN insider Shams Charania, Simmons suffered a torn right Achilles tendon during training, with an MRI scan confirming the injury that will rule him out for the entire NBA season.

The injury has now put his long-awaited NBA comeback on hold, dealing a significant blow not only to his hopes of returning to his best form, but also to his ambitions of representing the Australian Boomers at next year’s FIBA World Cup in Qatar.

Simmons has since broken his silence on Instagram, sharing an emotional message about his latest setback while remaining determined to return to the court, declaring that this injury will not be the end of his story.

“I’ve faced challenges before that tested me mentally, physically, and emotionally, and I’ve found a way through every single one of them. This injury is another chapter in my journey, not the end of my story,” Simmons wrote.

“It’s never easy being away from the game I love, especially after everything it took to get back here. But if there’s one thing I’ve learned, it’s that adversity doesn’t define you. How you respond to it does.

“I’ve been knocked down before, and I’ve always found a way to get back up. This time will be no different.”

The news comes on the same day his documentary, ‘Being Ben’, was released, which follows the highs and lows of his career and his journey back to the NBA, making the latest setback all the more difficult to swallow.

Simmons, who signed with the Sacramento Kings to begin his NBA comeback, had been performing well throughout the pre-season, showcasing his talents and reminding everyone why he is a former All-Star.

Just two days ago, Simmons delivered an impressive performance against the San Antonio Spurs, finishing with 14 points, eight rebounds and seven assists in what would prove to be his final pre-season appearance before the devastating injury.

This was a comeback almost two years in the making, with Simmons’ appearance at an Australian Boomers minicamp putting him back in the spotlight and giving the basketball world a glimpse of his healthy body after months of rehabilitation from the injuries that saw him miss the entire 2025-26 NBA season.

The minicamp ultimately paved the way for the Sacramento Kings to sign the Australian star on a one-year deal, making the timing of his latest injury all the more devastating.
