Gray man killed in single vehicle crash, police say

TheTools · 18.09.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

A Gray man was killed Wednesday in a single vehicle crash, according to the Cumberland County Sheriff’s Office.

Deputies were alerted to the crash on Long Hill Road in Gray at around 7:50 p.m., according to Sgt. Todd Meslin.

Deputies arrived to find a 2012 Ford Escape in a wooded area off the road.

“It appears, while traveling westbound on Long Hill Road, the operator was unable to negotiate a sharp curve, which caused the vehicle to go off the right side of the road,” Meslin stated. “The vehicle became airborne, colliding with several trees, and came to rest on its right side against a large tree.”

The driver, Kevin Austin, 57, of Gray, was pronounced dead at the scene, according to Meslin.

The crash is under investigation, Meslin said, “however, it does appear that speed and alcohol were a factor.”
