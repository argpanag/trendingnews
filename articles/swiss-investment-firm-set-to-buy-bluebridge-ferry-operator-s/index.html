Swiss investment firm set to buy Bluebridge ferry operator StraitNZ

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:48 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Business

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v4.laWzzQVil69j0DGqQk4Ywh3JMAvc5jXoA1Fn8ldP8DY/business/2751553%2Fswiss-investment-firm-set-to-buy-bluebridge-ferry-operator-straitnz

The Bluebridge ferry operator StraitNZ looks set to change ownership, with Swiss-based global investment firm Partners Group agreeing to buy the company from Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners.

StraitNZ operates two ships that provide daily freight and passenger services on the Cook Strait.

Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners bought StraitNZ back in 2022.

Partners Group says it will work with management on a plan that aims to enhance StraitNZ's customer offering and further grow its revenue.

Partners Group managing director, infrastructure, Nicholas Kuys, said StraitNZ carries traffic across the Cook Strait, a key backbone of New Zealand's economy.

"We look forward to partnering with an experienced management team to continue delivering safe, reliable, and efficient services for customers."

Partners Group said it will invest in StraitNZ from select pools of capital that it has.

It says the transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to approval of the Overseas Investment Office.
