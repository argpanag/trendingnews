BABYMONSTER HK concert set for Jan. 9, 2027, priority booking starts Sept. 22

TheTools · 15.09.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

BABYMONSTER is coming to Hong Kong! The K-pop girl group will perform at AsiaWorld-Expo on Jan. 9, 2027, with MONSTIEZ MEMBERSHIP (GLOBAL) priority ticket registration opening on Sept. 14.

Members will be able to purchase tickets on a priority basis from 11 am on Sept 22. Ticket prices range from HK$799 to HK$2,299, with VIP tickets priced at HK$2,299.

Fans can get ready to secure their tickets when sales open, with several priority booking periods scheduled before the general sale.

2026–27 BABYMONSTER WORLD TOUR [춤 (CHOOM)] IN HONG KONG

Date: Jan. 9, 2027 (Saturday), 6 pm

Ticket prices: HK$2,299 (VIP) / HK$1,699 / HK$1,399 / HK$999 / HK$799 (All seats)

Ticketing details

-Ticketing platform: Cityline

-MONSTIEZ MEMBERSHIP (GLOBAL) priority ticket registration

  • 12 pm, Sept. 14, 2026 to 11:59 pm, Sept. 17, 2026

-MONSTIEZ MEMBERSHIP (GLOBAL) priority booking

  • 11 am to 11:59 pm, Sept. 22, 2026

-HSBC Mastercard priority booking

  • 11 am to 11:59 pm, Sept. 23, 2026

-Trip.com early-bird booking

  • 11 am to 11:59 pm, Sept. 23, 2026

-Live Nation member priority booking

  • 11 am to 11:59 pm, Sept. 24, 2026

General sale

  • 11 am, Sept. 25, 2026
