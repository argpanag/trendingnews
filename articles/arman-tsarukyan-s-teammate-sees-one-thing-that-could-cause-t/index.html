Arman Tsarukyan's teammate sees 'one thing' that could cause trouble at UFC 331

TheTools · 19.09.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

UFC lightweight contender Grant Dawson thinks the result of the UFC 331 co-main event depends entirely on Mauricio Ruffy's improvements.

Dawson knows that Arman Tsarukyan (23-3 MMA, 10-2 UFC) is going to show up prepared for Saturday's five-round fight at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles (Paramount+), because he worked with his fellow 155-pound standout during training camp for Ruffy (14-2 MMA, 5-1 UFC) at American Top Team in Coconut Creek, Fla.

"Arman's always in the gym, getting better and doing what Arman does," Dawson told MMA Junkie. "You see the stuff on social media and that's just on social media. The guy's a very hard worker, always in the gym, always ready to go. It worked out really well for me because I'm fighting a wrestler (at UFC 333), and Arman is somewhat of a very good wrestler. Getting a couple of rounds – not necessarily sparring – but grappling rounds in with him really helped us both. Because I've got good takedown defence and he's got good takedowns. We both benefitted from it."

One of the big questions around Tsarukyan going into UFC 331 is whether his lack of activity in MMA – one fight since April 2024 – has compromised his game in any way. Tsarukyan has kept busy with grappling matches, though, and Dawson has felt improvement to what he already considered an elite skillset.

"He's always been a good wrestler," Dawson said. "He's always been strong and a very physical guy, great cardio. He's definitely pushing a much better pace I feel like. Not that he wasn't before. But it just feels like the gas tank is a little more confident. He knows he can keep pushing that pace, which is great because I like to push a high pace. When we go together it's like two Tasmanian devils trying to fight over a steak."

Although Dawson is naturally biased toward his American Top Team brethren, that doesn't mean he discounts Ruffy's ability to pull off an upset at UFC 331. There are questions around the Brazilian, too, mainly how much he has improved his grappling since a submission loss to Benoit Saint Denis in September 2025.

Dawson said he approves of Ruffy's decision to relocate his training camp to Australia at Freestyle MMA alongside UFC featherweight champ Alexander Volkanovski, but ultimately he doesn't see it as enough.

"The one thing that makes this interesting is the size and physicality of Ruffy," Dawson said. "And the fact he's training with Volkanovski. I think he's in a much better room. Not shade on The Fighting Nerds at all, they're a great room. But I just imagine they have a lot of good wrestlers in that room to push you when you fight wrestlers. I think it was a good decision for Ruffy to leave after he lost to Benoit Saint Denis.

"I'm very interested to see if Ruffy has fixed those holes. If he has, this could be a very interesting fight. If he hasn't, this is not going to be very interesting fight. ... I think Arman will win by finish. Late. Very, very late. I think end of the fourth round or early fifth round. Just waring on him. But I'm telling you, these first two rounds are going to be interesting."
