Nepal to Play Japan in Asian Games Cricket

TheTools · 26.09.2026 01:18 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. Nepal is playing Japan on Saturday in its second match in the men's cricket of the 20th Asian Games ongoing in Japan.

The match to be held at Kōrogi Sports Park in Nishin, Aichi, Japan, has not been able to toss due to rain.

Nepal had defeated Afghanistan by 5 wickets in its first match on Friday. Japan was defeated by Afghanistan in its first match.

If Nepal wins the match against Japan, it will qualify for the quarterfinals as group winner. 

The group winner will play Sri Lanka in the quarterfinals, while the group runner-up will play India.
