Thunder Sign Anthony Pritchard To Exhibit 10 Contract

TheTools · 07.10.2026 00:34 · source · permalink

The Thunder have signed free agent guard Anthony Pritchard to a non-guaranteed Exhibit 10 contract, the team announced today.

A native of Tulsa, Oklahoma, Pritchard went undrafted out of Central Michigan in 2025 and spent his first professional season with the Thunder’s G League affiliate, the Oklahoma City Blue.

In 39 total appearances for the Blue, Pritchard averaged 11.2 points, 5.5 assists, and 4.3 rebounds in 28.8 minutes per game. The 6’2″ point guard made 52.9% of his attempts from the floor but hit just 10-of-41 three-pointers (24.4%) and shot 52.7% from the free throw line.

It’s a safe bet that the plan is for Pritchard to report back to the Blue this fall. If he’s waived by the Thunder and then spends at least 60 days in the G League, his Exhibit 10 deal will make him eligible for a bonus worth up to $91K on top of his base NBAGL salary.

Oklahoma City waived guard Robert McCray V earlier in the day in order to open up a spot on its preseason roster. Having signed Pritchard, the club is back to the 21-player maximum.
