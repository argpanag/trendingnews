Will Best Juggles Big Brother and Strictly Come Dancing Duties

TheTools · 26.09.2026 18:05 · source · permalink

Television presenter Will Best is balancing dual broadcasting commitments this autumn by co-hosting Big Brother on ITV while competing as a contestant on the 24th series of BBC's Strictly Come Dancing.

Viewers raised questions about how the television personality would manage overlapping production schedules, given that both reality competition franchises feature live broadcasts.

Production logistics allow the presenter to maintain both roles because both shows are filmed in London. The BBC confirmed that Best rehearses for Strictly Come Dancing during the week before presenting Big Brother live on Friday evenings. He then performs on the live Strictly Come Dancing broadcast on Saturdays during a break in the Big Brother airing schedule.

Big Brother launched its 23rd season on September 13, while Strictly Come Dancing aired its pre-recorded launch show on September 19 before beginning live broadcasts on September 26. Best is paired with professional dancer Dianne Buswell on the dance competition.

Alongside his television commitments, the presenter is also preparing for major changes in his personal life after announcing that he and his fiancée, Tobi Rose, are expecting their first child.

Best shared the family news with his co-hosts Fleur East and James Barr during an appearance on The Hits Radio Breakfast Show on Hits Radio.

"Tobi and I are having a baby! They’re going to be bopping about soon and they can’t wait to come on the radio and chat to everyone," said Will Best, presenter.

He joked about his future responsibilities while discussing the upcoming arrival with his radio colleagues.

"People won’t be able to tell the difference, to be fair, between the baby and me, I’d imagine," said Will Best, presenter.

He expressed humor about managing child care arrangements alongside his busy work routine.

"I was hoping you guys could be permanent full-time nannies – for me and that means that Tobi can then look after the baby!" said Will Best, presenter.

The baby is expected to arrive while the celebrity competition progresses, adding to a demanding period for the host.

"Obviously I’ll be missing the little nipper and you know, I’ll be tired," said Will Best, presenter.

He acknowledged feeling emotional during early preparations for the dance contest.

"I mean, I’ve already done some little interviews for Strictly, where I’ve talked about Tobi and stuff, and I’ve already cried. So I’m going to be a blubbering mess," said Will Best, presenter.

Best has co-hosted Big Brother alongside AJ Odudu since the reality franchise was rebooted on ITV in 2023, while also fronting its Celebrity spin-off series.
