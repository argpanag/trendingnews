  1. Report: Antonelli wins in US but has to wait to secure titlepublished at 22:56 GMT 25 October
    22:56 GMT 25 October

    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent in Austin

    Kimi Antonelli just missed out on clinching his first world title in a dominant victory in the United States Grand Prix.

    The 20-year-old Italian leads Mercedes team-mate George Russell by 123 points after coming home ahead of Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Ferrari's Charles Leclerc.

    Antonelli stamped his authority on this race in the same way as he has on the season, and was out of touch even by the first corner as his rivals all battled behind him.

    He took control of the race from the very beginning, an excellent start combined with a gripping scrap through the first lap between Verstappen and Leclerc leaving him more than two seconds clear by the end of the first lap.

    No-one got near him from then on.

  3. 'Race was really lost from first two stints' - Hamiltonpublished at 22:54 GMT 25 October
    22:54 GMT 25 October

    Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton speaking to Sky Sports: "To be honest, the VSC wasn't ideal but I felt like the race was really lost from the first two stints. I lost so much time. I couldn't turn the car in the lower speed particularly. So Turn One, Turn 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 just massive understeering, so the cars were just blown away. I was adjusting my settings and by the time we got to the last stint, I think we got closer to the balance but I think we should have started with that balance in the beginning.

    "I think, I've lost a lot of points in the last five races or so, and I think it's really come from set-up or just not perfecting the set-up and that's something I've really got to get on top of. I think that's really where I've lost out. I think performance wise, I feel like I've been driving well. I think today, I feel like the last stints were pretty good. The first two stints, I just couldn't do anything about that.

    "Hopefully when we go to Mexico we can improve on some of where we've got wrong with the set-up."

  4. Hadjar says Red Bull needs 'redesign'published at 22:51 GMT 25 October
    22:51 GMT 25 October

    Red Bull's Isack Hadjar speaking to Sky Sports after the race: "Just watching my race start you know... I do everything perfect in terms of procedure and just have clutch over-delivery and these things are just out of my control. I have poor radio coverage, I can't hear what's happening in the pits. I leave the pits with the wrong map and then I'm losing a second a lap every lap on the hard tyre. It's just..."

    On why solutions to improve the start are hard to come by: "It needs a redesign. That's it. The clutch needs a redesign because at the moment it's too fragile, it's not coping well with temperatures. It's a real struggle."

  5. 'The mindset doesn't change' - Antonellipublished at 22:49 GMT 25 October
    22:49 GMT 25 October

    Championship leader Kimi Antonelli speaking to Sky Sports after the podium ceremony: "It was the perfect way to end the weekend. It was an incredible team effort especially from strategy to believe in me and go for one stop because I wasn't sure whether one or two stop was the quickest. But really happy that we made it stick. Today was a really important victory for me, for the team, especially after having a couple of difficult races, being back on top feels very good."

    On how he feels going to Mexico: "The best way, the perfect way would be to finish off in style and then just enjoy the rest of the season. I'm very aware that it's very close but the mindset doesn't change. I want to finish the season on a high in order to then go with high hopes into 2027 but we'll first focus and worry about finishing this well and then we'll see."
