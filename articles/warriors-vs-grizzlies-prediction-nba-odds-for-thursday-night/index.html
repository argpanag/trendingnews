Warriors vs Grizzlies Prediction & NBA Odds for Thursday Night | Predictem

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:39 · source · permalink

by Joe Jensen | Nov 19, 2026 | NBA Picks

The Memphis Grizzlies are 7.5-point favorites over the Golden State Warriors with a total of 225.5 at most books. My play is Grizzlies -7.5.

Ask yourself one thing before you grab the points: what has Golden State shown you in the last week that says it can hang for 48 minutes? Since Stephen Curry tore his right oblique in Houston, the Warriors have played three full games and lost them by 38, 21 and 22. They haven’t cracked 100 points in any of them, the first time they’ve done that three straight games in almost five years, per the Associated Press.

This is the last stop on a five-game trip, and the Warriors are 0-4 on it. They got punched in the mouth by San Antonio, 123-85, then trailed New Orleans 35-11 after one quarter on Monday and shot 11 of 46 from deep. Wednesday in Dallas was more of the same: Cooper Flagg went for 33, the Mavericks led by as many as 29, and Golden State shot 24% from three.

Put those three games together and the Warriors are scoring about 90 points per 100 possessions. That isn’t a bad offense. That’s a team that can’t find a second option. Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg (20.3 points a game) is doing what he can, but he went 6 of 18 in Dallas, and the injury list behind him is brutal: Curry, Jimmy Butler III (knee), Kristaps Porzingis (illness), Moses Moody (knee), Brandon Williams (hamstring) and Al Horford (back) are all listed as out.

The Grizzlies are 11-4 and have won four straight, all without Ty Jerome (25.4 points a game), who has missed three straight with a sore right hip and is listed out again. It hasn’t mattered. Memphis dropped 142 on New Orleans, 129 on Brooklyn and 136 on Washington, then went into Cleveland Wednesday and won 124-121 behind 26 from Cam Spencer and 20 from Taylor Hendricks. That was a franchise-record eighth straight road win.

The depth is the story. Rookie Cameron Boozer gives them 16.6 points and 9.4 boards a night, Cedric Coward is a matchup problem at 14.5 and 8.4, and Zach Edey can wall off the rim. Across 15 games, Memphis is putting up roughly 118.5 points per 100 possessions and pushing the pace at about 103 possessions a game. Against a Warriors team that has allowed nearly 117 per 100 in its three games without Curry, that’s a lot of easy offense.

FedExForum hasn’t been much of a fortress. Memphis is 3-4 at home against 8-0 on the road, with home losses to New Orleans, Indiana, Oklahoma City and Denver. The Grizzlies are also on the second night of a back-to-back after a 22-lead-change grinder in Cleveland, and Isaiah Stewart (illness) is out along with Jerome. The line has already ticked down from 8.5 to 7.5, and a desperate Draymond Green on the last night of a miserable trip is the kind of thing that turns a blowout into a nine-point game late. Memphis only beat this same Warriors team 111-108 in October, though Curry played that night and scored 24.

Both teams are tired. Only one of them can still score. Memphis has more bodies, more shooting and a better offense by a wide margin right now, and I’ll lay the number.

Projected score: Grizzlies 121, Warriors 107

The Pick: Grizzlies -7.5 (-110)
