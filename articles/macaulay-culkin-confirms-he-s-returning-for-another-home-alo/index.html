Macaulay Culkin confirms he’s returning for another ‘Home Alone’ movie

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

Macaulay Culkin has confirmed that he will be returning for another "Home Alone" movie.

It has been 36 years since "Home Alone" was released in theaters, with Culkin as 10-year-old Kevin McCallister, who is left home alone while his family goes to Paris for Christmas.

The movie was a massive hit, and multiple sequels were released in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Culkin is working on a new standalone sequel with Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg’s production company, Point Grey Pictures, which is writing and producing it, according to an announcement from Deadline last week.

Macaulay went on Instagram and confirmed the news. “It’s true ... ” he wrote on Nov. 21.

While there are no details about what the "Home Alone" standalone sequel could entail, the outlet noted that Macaulay has already pitched an idea to Disney Motion Pictures boss David Greenbaum.

Per the report, Macaulay wants to explore where Kevin is now, given how he’s “older and wiser” and has “survived abandonment and burglars as a child.”

This isn’t the first time Macaulay has reprised Kevin this year. He played the character in a new McDonald’s campaign that will launch a limited-edition Home Alone meal starting on Dec. 1.
