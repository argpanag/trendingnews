Dan Walker put forward a Michael Sheen Traitors theory and got everyone thinking

TheTools · 16.10.2026 08:33 · source · permalink

Celebrity Traitors superfan Dan Walker has come up with a theory about who will make it to the final for the show - and Michael Sheen is part of it.

The Channel Five presenter believes that the Welsh actor will make it to the final, alongside comedians Romesh Ranganathan and Joanne McNally.

He is not basing his musings on anything he has heard, but is basing on airtime and the narrative so far. After five episodes Michael Sheen has been very quiet and only got one vote at a round table from actual Traitor James Acaster. Read here about what Michael Sheen is doing right.

Dan Walker has picked that up too writing on social media: "Someone told me last year that they edit Traitors in reverse order… so, they start with the final episode and then work back to the beginning.

"With that in mind - based on airtime, narrative so far and the fact that it’s good to keep someone who is so sure but wide of the mark -my stab in the dark is that Michael, Romesh and Joanne are there at the end. *I will delete this if I’m wrong*." Not everyone agrees with him, one fan wrote: "Joanne threw herself off the tower last night with the bizarre claim she was at the hide and seek game."

Another said: "Sheen probably Romesh I feel like he'd been murdered before then Joanne will be banished soon."

Another said: "Joanne will come under a lot of heat now, didn't vote either traitor our and said she was at the hide and seek, think you could be right about the other two, think Maya might make the final 3."

Dan's prediction comes as details of the final have been confirmed with the 2026 series due to be shown on Friday, October 30, at 8pm on BBC One.

The final episode is scheduled to last 80 minutes, making it the longest episode in the show’s history so far. When the final episode finishes at 9.20pm, there is due to be a 40-minute episode of the spin-off show Uncloaked.

Meanwhile, after Rob Beckett was murdered in plain sight, and mathematician Hannah Fry was banished, YouTube star King Kenny has been recruited as a traitor.

The 29-year-old accepted Maya Jama and James Acaster’s offer to join them following the dramatic roundtable on Thursday night.

After seeing his fellow traitors for the first time, the social media star was shocked but then asked them for loyalty.

“Guys, just don’t do me dirty. Let’s try playing clean game where all three of us get to the final,” he said.

During a confessional on the BBC programme, Kenny added: “I think being a faithful was fun, but being a traitor is going to be better.

“The faithful are doomed!”

Celebrity Traitors is on again on Friday, October 16, from 8pm.
