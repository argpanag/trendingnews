Apple reports outages affecting iCloud Mail, AppleCare and more

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:41 · source · permalink
  • November 17, 2026
  • 02:24 PM

Apple is reporting service disruptions affecting several of its services, including iCloud Mail, iCloud Account & Sign In, Apple School Manager, and Schoolwork.

According to Apple's System Status page, at least six services were experiencing issues or outages as of 2:10 PM ET on November 17, 2026.

The most widespread issue affects iCloud Account & Sign In, which Apple lists as an ongoing issue affecting all users.

The company warns that the service may be slow or unavailable. The disruption started at approximately 11:45 AM ET.

Apple has also reported outages affecting iCloud Mail and iCloud Web Apps (iCloud.com).

Both incidents started at around 2:03 PM ET, with some users unable to access the services.

Apple School Manager, Schoolwork and AppleCare are also affected

The problems aren't limited to iCloud. Apple School Manager and Schoolwork are also experiencing outages, with some users affected.

Both incidents started at approximately 1:40 PM ET.

AppleCare on Device is experiencing another outage that affects all users.

According to Apple, purchasing AppleCare directly on a device is temporarily unavailable. This issue started at approximately 11:15 AM ET.
