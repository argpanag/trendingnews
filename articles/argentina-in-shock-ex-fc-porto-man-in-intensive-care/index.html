Argentina in shock: ex-FC Porto man in intensive care

Daniel Osvaldo is in intensive care at Lavallol Hospital after being diagnosed with a pleural effusion in his right lung. The news was reported by the local press, which says the former striker was found at home, in poor condition, by his sister.

The incident took place on Thursday, with a friend helping take the Argentine to hospital. According to medical reports, his right lung had a significant build-up of pus and fluid, leading the medical team to decide to keep the former footballer in hospital.

Osvaldo played for FC Porto in the 2015/16 season, during which he made 12 appearances, scored one goal and provided one assist. He is currently 40 years old.
