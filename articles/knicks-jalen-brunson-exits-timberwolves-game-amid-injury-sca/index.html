Knicks’ Jalen Brunson exits Timberwolves game amid injury scare

TheTools · 20.11.2026 01:33 · source · permalink
  • NBA on ClutchPoints
  • New York Knicks

New York Knicks superstar Jalen Brunson left Thursday night's game against the Minnesota Timberwolves in the first quarter.

Superstar Jalen Brunson left the New York Knicks' showdown against the Minnesota Timberwolves in the first quarter. Per Jack Borman, Brunson left for the locker room at the 9:19 mark after getting hit in the face amid a LaMelo Ball drive. Brunson had gone scoreless in his first three minutes of action.

However, that injury does not appear to be too serious as he made his way back to the game late in the first quarter. He assisted on a Karl-Anthony Towns bucket and hit a 3-pointer to help the Knicks to a 33-26 lead at the end of the first.

The Knicks are riding a two-game winning streak after back-to-back wins over the Toronto Raptors, improving to 8-6 on the season. Brunson hit a clutch basket in the first game, which resulted in a 112-108 win in overtime.

After a somewhat slow start to the season, Brunson has found his groove in November. He's averaging 28.1 points while shooting 46.7% from the field and 41.5% on 3-pointers during the month. He's topped 30 points in two of his last three games and is now averaging 27 points on the season.

Brunson's consistent production has been crucial for the Knicks, especially amid the continued offensive struggles of fellow Villanova alums Mikal Bridges and Josh Hart. However, Bridges has shown signs of life in recent games, as he dropped a season-high 18 points against the Pacers and 17 in the second game against the Raptors.

Should Brunson and the Knicks go on to beat the Timberwolves, they will have a five-game home stand coming up to extend their win streak further.
