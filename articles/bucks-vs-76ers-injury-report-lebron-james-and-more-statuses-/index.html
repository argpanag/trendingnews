Bucks vs 76ers Injury Report: LeBron James and More Statuses to Monitor

TheTools · 24.10.2026 19:14 · source · permalink

Will LeBron James play against the Milwaukee Bucks?

The Milwaukee Bucks are looking to get into the win column when they take on the Philadelphia 76ers in the City of Brotherly Love on Saturday. The game begins at 7:30 PM ET and will air on NBA TV, NBCS-PH, PCK-PHI, WCGV, and Bucks+.

The Bucks fell to 0-2 on Friday in an afternoon showdown against the Chicago Bulls, losing 132-125 in their home opener. The game was moved up to an earlier start time to accommodate Game 1 of the World Series between the Milwaukee Brewers and Tampa Bay Rays.

The 76ers are coming off a historic 29-point comeback against the Cleveland Cavaliers, putting them at 2-0 on the season after defeating the New York Knicks on opening night. This is the second of three home games for Philly.

This is the first meeting of the season between the teams.

The Bucks once again have a clean injury report with no one listed. Their injury report was clean against the Bulls as well.

For the second straight game, the team started Jaime Jaquez Jr., Ryan Rollins, Tyler Herro, Myles Turner, and Kyle Kuzma against the Bulls on Friday.

Kasparas Jakucionis, AJ Green, Ousmane Dieng, Kel’el Ware, and Brayden Burries all played over eight minutes off the bench against the Bulls.

Rookie Nate Ament, who was selected 13th overall, has yet to make his NBA debut.

LeBron James is OUT with low back and sciatica management. James has played in both games for the Sixers this season, averaging 15.5 points, 9.5 rebounds, and six assists. He has struggled a bit from the field, shooting 37% overall and 14% from three.

Dean Wade is QUESTIONABLE with a left ankle sprain. He has yet to play this season. He joined the Sixers as a free agent in the offseason after spending last year with the Cleveland Cavaliers. He averaged 5.8 points, 4.2 rebounds, and 1.5 assists on 43% shooting and 36% from three last season.

Dominick Barlow is OUT with a low back stress reaction. On October 19, it was reported that he would be out for two weeks.

Ariel Huckporti is OUT after suffering a right Achilles tendon tear in preseason. He is out for the season.

Tacko Fall is OUT with a left quadriceps strain.

Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown, VJ Edgecombe, and Joel Embiid will start for the Sixers. Kentavious Caldwell-Pope could start in place of LeBron James if he is unable to play.
