Marseille predicted XI v Angers: Bruno Genesio to restore strongest side after midweek rotation

Marseille are expected to revert to their strongest XI against Angers on Sunday afternoon after midweek rotation under Bruno Genesio.

According to GetFootballNewsFrance.com, Genesio made five changes to the team that beat ESTAC Troyes when facing Olympiacos in the UEFA Europa League on Thursday. Several of those introductions were second-string options and academy products.

OneFootball Videos

Neal Maupay, Keyliane Abdallah and Bamo Meité are expected to return to the bench.

CJ Egan-Riley, Amine Harit and Amine Gouiri are expected to come back into the line-up, with Gouiri expected to lead the line.

Angel Gomes has drawn criticism this season, yet he is expected to continue in the No.10 role against Le Sco.

Predicted Marseille XI v Angers: Jeffrey De Lange, Emerson, Nayef Aguerd, CJ Egan-Riley, Timothy Weah, Tochukwu Nnadi, Himad Abdelli, Igor Paixao, Angel Gomes, Amine Harit, Amine Gouiri.
