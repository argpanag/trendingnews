How many times has Elche beaten Real Madrid at home? When was the last time?

TheTools · 15.09.2026 09:55 · source · permalink

Elche’s Martínez Valero is celebrating its 50th anniversary, with Real Madrid playing a major role in the stadium’s long and painful history.

The Martínez Valero is in party mode. Elche’s stadium is still celebrating its 50th anniversary after reaching the half-century mark on September 8. There was already a tribute before the game against Real Sociedad to mark the occasion, but the coming weeks will be particularly significant, with a calendar packed with events commemorating the milestone.

Elche and the 1:50 Madrid ratio

Real Madrid feature prominently in the Martínez Valero’s history because they have been one of Elche’s biggest bogey teams over those 50 years. Elche have beaten Los Blancos just once at their current home, back in March 1978. Goals from Antón, Félix Palomares and Gómez Voglino secured a 3-1 victory over a Madrid side coached by Luis Molowny. But that was Elche’s first – and remains their only – home win over Real Madrid at the Martínez Valero.

That means it has now been 48 years since Elche last beat Madrid in front of their own fans. Since that famous upset, Real Madrid have left the Martínez Valero unbeaten, recording seven wins and two draws in LaLiga. Elche did manage a draw last season, but historically Madrid have dominated this fixture.

Madrid’s punishing goals record

The teams have met 55 times in all competitions, with Elche winning six, 13 ending in draws and Madrid claiming 36 victories. The numbers are even more daunting when it comes to goals: Elche have conceded 142 against Real Madrid, more than against any other opponent.

There will also be a first when the teams meet again. José Mourinho has never previously coached a team at the Martínez Valero, with his spells in Spain and Elche’s time in the top flight never having coincided until now.
