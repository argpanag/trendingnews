Steph Curry Addresses NBA Retirement and Explains Pay Cut on New Warriors Contract

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

Golden State Warriors star Stephen Curry knows the end of his legendary career is drawing closer.

So when the four-time NBA champion faced a contract decision over the offseason, he didn't hesitate to take a slight pay cut, telling ESPN's Anthony Slater that he wanted to eliminate distractions and give the Warriors some flexibility for what could be his final two years in the league.

"All of it," Curry said. "I assumed that it would probably be one of, if not the last, contract I signed. I'm not afraid to say it. But maybe I'll surprise myself and want to keep going."

Curry agreed to a two-year, $116 million extension that gave $10 million in cap space back to the Warriors next summer and included a player option in the final year, potentially keeping him in Golden State for 20 straight seasons. While he would've been eligible for a max contract extension, the 38-year-old isn't focused on the finances at this stage in his career.

The two-time NBA MVP remains one of the best scorers in the game, but he told Slater that he would've been willing to accept a complementary role if his skills started to fade with his age. He still remains open to that possibility.

"It would not kill my ego," Curry said. "I want to win. Whatever that means, you make decisions based on what's in front of you."

Slater noted that Curry "made clear that there hasn't and won't be a retirement decision soon." However, he acknowledged that the end is coming, and he wants to enjoy it while it lasts.

"I want to appreciate what's happening right now because I don't want to just go through the motions," Curry said. "Then, it passes you by."
