Cristiano Ronaldo joins U.S. sports billionaires Michael Jordan and Vince McMahon in the $2bn club as African fans track his 1,000-goal race

TheTools · 23.10.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

Forbes has revealed that Cristiano Ronaldo is the first active athlete to surpass $2 billion in career earnings (on and off the field) before taxes and agents' fees.

  • Cristiano Ronaldo is the first active athlete to surpass $2 billion in career earnings before taxes and fees, according to Forbes.
  • He joins US sports legends Michael Jordan and Vince McMahon in the select group of sports figures with multi-billion-dollar earnings.
  • Ronaldo's wealth comes from his record contract with Saudi club Al-Nassr and lucrative global endorsement deals.
  • He is also the world's most-followed individual on social media, amassing over 1.05 billion followers across major platforms.

By reaching this landmark, the Portuguese superstar joins elite U.S. sports figures such as basketball icon Michael Jordan and wrestling entrepreneur Vince McMahon.

As of October 23, 2026, Forbes’ real-time estimates put the net worth of Michael Jordan, the first athlete to become a billionaire, at $4.7 billion.

Meanwhile, Forbes estimates Vince McMahon’s net worth at $3.3 billion as of October 23, 2026.

A new sports billionaire era unites champions from the United States, Portugal, and Saudi Arabia

Ronaldo's rise into multi-billionaire territory is fueled by his record-setting contract with Saudi Arabian club Al-Nassr and a powerful portfolio of commercial ventures.

His playing deal in Saudi Arabia generates over $235 million (Forbes on-field earnings) each year, while off-field sponsorship deals with prominent global brands like Nike, Lego, Panini, and Whoop contribute over $65 million annually (Forbes off field earnings).

Millions of fans in Africa and across the world watch as the most-followed superstar eyes 1,000 goals

Alongside his wealth, Ronaldo stands as the most-followed person on Earth with a combined digital audience of over 1.05 billion followers across Instagram, Facebook, X, and YouTube.

His unprecedented online audience features millions of passionate fans throughout Africa who actively track his performances and cheer on his quest to reach 1,000 official career goals.

Having accumulated 981 official senior goals for club and country, he is now only 19 strikes away from becoming the first player in men's football history to hit four digits.

Discussing the media hype surrounding the milestone during the Portugal Football Summit, Ronaldo shared his perspective by explaining that reaching 1,000 goals is an objective rather than an obsession.
