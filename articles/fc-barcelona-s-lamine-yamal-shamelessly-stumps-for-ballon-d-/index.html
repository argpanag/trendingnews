FC Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal shamelessly stumps for Ballon d’Or, doesn’t consider Bayern Munich’s Harry Kane top competition

TheTools · 14.09.2026 13:00 · source · permalink
  • Bayern Munich Records and Achievements

The FC Bayern star just goes about his business.

There are plenty of ways to make your case for the Ballon d’Or.

You can score insane goals. You can carry your club on your back through an entire season. You can win trophies, break records, and consistently produce when the biggest games arrive.

Or, apparently, you can just tell everyone that you are the best and then explain why you deserve the award.

FC Barcelona star Lamine Yamal, who is extremely good and obviously not some hack, was asked about his status in the Ballon d’Or race and his answer sounded less like an assessment of the competition and more like a campaign speech. Transfer journalist Fabrizio Romano captured this shameless bit of self promotion:

“🚨🌕 Lamine Yamal: “Kylian Mbappé and I, we are the two BEST in the world…

…but I think I AM the one who deserves to win the Ballon d’Or”, told Movistar. 🔥☝🏽”

“🚨🌕 Lamine Yamal: “Kylian Mbappé and I, we are the two BEST in the world…

…but I think I AM the one who deserves to win the Ballon d’Or”, told Movistar. 🔥☝🏽”

Well, at least Yamal is not lacking confidence.

The problem is that Yamal completely discounted the greatness of Harry Kane, who won two trophies, scored more than anyone in the world, and led England to a third-place finish in the World Cup. Kane, though, recently said that the Ballon d’Or race is “out of my hands” and was not going to fall into the trap of making the kind of grandiose statements that Yamal and his idol, Real Madrid star Kylian Mbappé have made.

Yamal and Mbappé want to make sure that everybody knows exactly where they rank themselves, and let the Ballon d’Or voters know that they would very much like their attention.

Kane, meanwhile, has taken the slightly less theatrical approach. The Bayern Munich striker does not need to campaign for himself every time a microphone appears. He does not need to declare that he is the best player in the world or explain why an individual award belongs on his shelf. He scores goals, wins trophies, and lets the numbers make the argument for him.

There is definitely something refreshing about that.

Kane’s case for the Ballon d’Or can be made without a self-promotion campaign. The Englishman’s performances speak for themselves. His goal totals speak for themselves. His consistency speaks for itself.

So while Yamal and Mbappé are busy making sure everyone knows they deserve the Ballon d’Or, Kane can be over here doing the old-fashioned thing — playing football, scoring goals, and winning games.

What a concept.
