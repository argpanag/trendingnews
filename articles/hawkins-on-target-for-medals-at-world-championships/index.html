Hawkins on target for medals at World Championships

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:21 · source · permalink

British windsurfer Finn Hawkins is looking to fine tune tactics after staying in medal contention at the Musto iQFOiL World Championships 2026 in Weymouth & Portland. The athlete from St Austell ensured he remains in the running at the Weymouth & Portland National Sailing Academy after making the gold fleet on the second day of racing, finishing in 17th place after the preliminary series.

Former European medallist Hawkins was met with a strong 20-knot wind in Portland Harbour, and a sixth-place finish was the highlight of the Brit’s day with eighth-place, 11th-place and a discarded 21st-place finish the rest of his results on Tuesday. Hawkins ends his preliminary series with an upwind sprint slalom race win and four top 10 performances from eight races in Dorset.

He made the top-half cutoff and will race in the elimination series from Wednesday to Friday, in order to decide the top 10 of the gold fleet who will have the right to race for medals.

“The racing was nice. It was really windy today, shifty and dusty. It was hard to know where to go all the time. I had an okay day, nothing that’s going to ruin my event. I’m going to need a big performance in the gold fleet," said Hawkins.

“It was a completely different type of day compared tro Monday, but we’re used to the conditions here. It was about getting the tactics right. There’s a bit of confidence but there are also learning points and takeaways that I need to shift in, if I want to start punching up front."

The British Sailing Team windsurfer will fight to make the finals series at the windsurfing and sailing venue for the London 2012 Olympics. All the action is building towards the finale on Saturday, with a Fan Zone at Weymouth Pavilion bringing windsurfing to spectators and families by the Jurassic Coast seaside.

The elimination series start on Wednesday 9 September in both men’s and women’s competitions, with 10 British Sailing Team and British Sailing Squad athletes in the running. Islay Watson is the leading British woman while Duncan Monaghan the leading British man, with five more British Sailing Team and British Sailing Squad men in the running for the medal finals this weekend.

The Musto iQFOiL World Championships 2026 are taking place from 7-12 September. Get closer to the action on the final day by visiting the Fan Zone on Weymouth Pavilion to enjoy a full day of live racing, big-screen coverage, music, family activities, food and opportunities to get out on the water.
