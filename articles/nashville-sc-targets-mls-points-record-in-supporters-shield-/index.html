Nashville SC Targets MLS Points Record in Supporters Shield Chase

TheTools · 11.10.2026 00:13 · source · permalink

Nashville SC entered the final month of the 2026 Major League Soccer regular season chasing two major achievements: its first Supporters Shield and the league's single-season points record. With 60 points from 27 matches, the Tennessee club held a 10-point lead in the Supporters Shield race and was positioned to challenge Inter Miami CF's record of 74 points.

According to MLSsoccer.com on October 7, Nashville had recorded 18 wins, three losses and six draws. The club needed 15 points from its final seven regular-season matches to reach 75 points and establish a new league benchmark, requiring five wins or four wins and three draws.

Nashville began that closing stretch with a road match against Austin FC at Q2 Stadium on October 10. The game marked its return to Austin following the 2025 U.S. Open Cup final, when Nashville defeated the hosts 2-1 to win the first major trophy in the franchise's history, according to Nashville SC's official preview published October 8.

The October 10 matchup also followed a two-week break for Nashville. The club's most recent league appearance before facing Austin was a 2-0 victory over Toronto FC on September 26, with Hany Mukhtar scoring both goals, according to The Tennessean's live coverage published through Yahoo Sports.

Nashville's pursuit of the Supporters Shield had gathered momentum after consecutive shutout victories, according to a Fox Sports preview published October 8. The club had also lost only once in its previous 10 matches, according to MLSsoccer.com, strengthening its position ahead of the final seven fixtures.

The remaining schedule included three home games and four away matches. After Austin, Nashville was scheduled to host Sporting Kansas City on October 14, visit the San Jose Earthquakes on October 17 and travel to CF Montréal on October 24. The club would then host the Philadelphia Union on October 28 and the New York Red Bulls on November 1 before visiting FC Cincinnati on November 7.

Nashville's attacking production has been led by designated players Sam Surridge, Mukhtar and Cristian Espinoza. MLSsoccer.com reported that the trio had combined for 55 goal contributions, with Surridge recording 16 goals and two assists, Mukhtar contributing 13 goals and seven assists, and Espinoza adding four goals and 13 assists.

The club's defensive performance has also been central to its campaign. Nashville had conceded just 21 goals, the lowest total in MLS at the time of the October 7 report. Goalkeeper Brian Schwake and defender Andy Nájar were among the club's 2026 MLS All-Stars, alongside Mukhtar and Surridge.

Schwake returned from international duty after receiving his first United States men's national team call-up. Nashville SC reported that he made three saves in his first international start during a shutout against Canada, while backup goalkeeper Xavier Valdez also represented the Dominican Republic during the international window.

Midfielders Patrick Yazbek and Ahmed Qasem also returned to the squad following international commitments. Their availability gave head coach B.J. Callaghan additional options as Nashville prepared for a demanding run of matches, according to the club's October 8 preview.

The club's championship ambitions extend beyond the regular season. Nashville won the U.S. Open Cup in 2025 and entered the final stretch of 2026 pursuing its first Supporters Shield and MLS Cup titles. Its strongest previous MLS Cup playoff runs came in 2020 and 2021, when it reached the conference semifinals, according to MLSsoccer.com.

As of the October 7 report, Nashville was the only MLS team to have secured a place in the 2026 MLS Cup playoffs. Maintaining its season average of 2.22 points per match across the final seven fixtures would put the club on course to finish with 75 points, surpassing the 74-point record established by Inter Miami CF in 2024.
