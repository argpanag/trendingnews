Ex-IGB REIT CEO Elizabeth Tan passes away

TheTools · 22.09.2026 07:49 · source · permalink

Elizabeth is the daughter of property tycoon Robert Tan, whose family controls IGB Bhd.

Elizabeth, 42, is the daughter of property tycoon Robert Tan, whose family controls IGB Bhd and its listed real estate investment trusts – IGB REIT and IGB Commercial REIT.

IGB REIT Management announced her passing in a notice to tenants, business partners and colleagues, expressing sadness over the loss of a leader whose contributions left a lasting impact on both REITs.

The company also extended its sympathies to Robert, who is the executive chairman of IGB REIT Management and a major unitholder of both IGB REIT and IGB Commercial REIT.

“On behalf of the board, management and staff of IGB REIT Management Sdn Bhd, we extend our deepest condolences to the family and loved ones during this very difficult time,” said IGB REIT Management CEO Chai Lai Sim in a statement.

Elizabeth was appointed interim CEO of IGB REIT Management on Jan 1, 2024, following the retirement of Antony Patrick Barragry. She stepped down as CEO in March 2025 due to medical reasons.

She also served as an alternate director of IGB Bhd, a position she resigned from in January this year, citing health issues.

After graduating with first class honours in Business Administration from Cardiff University, Wales in 2004, she joined Mid Valley City Gardens Sdn Bhd, an IGB subsidiary.

Elizabeth also served as joint chief operating officer and head of operations/leasing at The Gardens Mall.

IGB REIT’s portfolio includes Mid Valley Megamall and The Gardens Mall in Mid Valley City, Kuala Lumpur, and The Mall, Mid Valley Southkey in Johor Bahru.
