Form & Styles

Pinas stands 185 cm and has built his short run on early knockout threat, converting five of seven wins inside the distance. Pulyaev offers a veteran dimension at 196 cm with 193 cm reach, though his submission-heavy game—21 tapout wins—may struggle to find entries against a rangy orthodox opponent who carries power. Both men share the orthodox stance, so the bout likely turns on Pulyaev's cage-craft versus Pinas's ability to land clean before the grappling exchanges begin.

Prediction

Books side decisively with Pinas, and the lopsided pricing is hard to fault given the knockout rate and the 30-win gulf in experience favouring a fighter who has been stopped more often than he has finished inside the distance recently. Pinas ought to press the range advantage and hunt the finish early, whilst Pulyaev's best path runs through survival and the occasional scramble. Of course, a veteran's nous can trump raw numbers on any given night, and MMA remains the most volatile sport to price.
