Helen Skelton shares 'emotional' back-to-school update as she returns to screens

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:27 · source · permalink

CUMBRIAN TV presenter Helen Skelton has shared a heartfelt update with fans as all three of her children returned to school.

The 43-year-old, best known for her work on BBC’s Morning Live, Countryfile, and Blue Peter, took to Instagram on Wednesday (September 3) to reflect on the emotional milestone.

Her children, Ernie, 11, Louis, nine, and Elsie, four, from her previous marriage to rugby player Richie Myler, all returned to school after the summer holidays.

She wrote: "I could write an essay on the thoughts going through my head today.

"Back to school, starting school and changing school.

"No one warns you about the emotional roller coaster.

"Grateful they want to go. Grateful they can go. Grateful they have wonderful teachers and school staff they race to see.

"Still feels like I have lead weights in my pockets though, the garden is unnervingly quiet and the snack and soggy sock bag we have carried for six weeks is redundant."

She revealed that her youngest, Elsie, was starting school for the first time, while her eldest, Ernie, was beginning at a new school.

The TV star reflected on how different the house now feels, marking the first time in more than a decade that she has been at home without one of her children.

She added: "First time in 11 years I haven’t had a babe at home.

"A decade of different hats: a stay-at-home mum, a living abroad with her babies mum, a working mum, a single mum and a pandemic mum (weird that wasn’t it!).

"I hope your September is a month of fabulous firsts for your little people.

"To the people who now get to spend their days with our kids, I envy you and am in debt to you."

Helen shares glimpses of her life away from the camera with her more than 500,000 Instagram followers.

Earlier this week, she posted photos from a staycation in Cumbria, featuring family moments and time spent relaxing in a hot tub overlooking the mountains.

She returned to screens this week on Morning Live, co-hosting alongside Gethin Jones.
