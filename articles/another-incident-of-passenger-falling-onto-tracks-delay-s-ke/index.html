Another Incident Of Passenger Falling Onto Tracks Delay's Kelana LRT Line

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:02 · source · permalink

Rapid KL reported temporary delays along the Kelana Jaya LRT Line this afternoon after a passenger believed to have fainted fell onto the tracks at Kerinchi station.

In a statement, the rail operator said the incident occurred at approximately 4.40pm, prompting the immediate activation of the Platform Intrusion Emergency Stop (PIES) system.

Electricity supply to the tracks was temporarily cut off as a safety precaution, while the emergency brakes of an approaching train were activated to prevent an accident.

Rapid KL said station personnel promptly assisted the affected passenger out of the track area before taking the individual to the station’s emergency room for initial medical attention.

The incident caused delays to train services along the Kelana Jaya Line while rescue and safety procedures were carried out.

However, incidents involving LRT passengers falling onto the tracks have become increasingly frequent in recent times, resulting in injuries and disruptions to train services. Although proposals to install protective barriers along station platforms have been raised, such measures have yet to be implemented.
