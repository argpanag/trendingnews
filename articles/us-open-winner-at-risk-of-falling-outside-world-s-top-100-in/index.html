US Open winner at risk of falling outside world's top 100 in latest setback - AOL

TheTools · 15.09.2026 18:53 · source · permalink

Emma Raducanu has watched her WTA ranking drop further after she decided to skip the US Open to focus on regaining full fitness.

The British tennis star, who shocked the sports world with her first Grand Slam triumph in New York five years ago, withdrew from this year's tournament due to a stress fracture in her right leg. The injury surfaced during the clay-court season and deteriorated throughout the summer, leaving Raducanu unable to prepare adequately for the US Open.

She hasn't competed professionally for 92 days, since falling to Donna Vekic in the Queen's Club final before Wimbledon. As a result, her global ranking has taken a substantial hit.

After her defeat to Vekic, Raducanu sat at 31st position, but has now plummeted to 96th, sliding seven places since the US Open commenced.

This downturn leaves her at risk of dropping below the top 100 worldwide, although a solid performance at the upcoming China Open could help turn things around.

Raducanu's entry was confirmed on the tournament list earlier this month, and she'll be keen to rebuild momentum and stop her rankings decline.

During last year's China Open, she reached the third round, defeating Cristina Bucsa initially before falling to Jessica Pegula.

Raducanu won the first set against Pegula but failed to sustain her lead after her opponent elevated her play, eventually losing 3-6, 7-6, 6-0. Following the match, Pegula said: "That was a crazy match. It was a lot of fun.

"The tiebreak was intense, but I got myself back in and kept the pressure on. When she (Raducanu) hit the double fault, I knew I was still playing some good tennis, and it was right there.

"I got a little lucky with my two backhand winners. Other than that, I tried to keep fighting for as long as I could.

"The crowd was awesome, this court has been good to me, and I'm excited to play here. I'm happy I got through tonight, and I'll try to rest and recover."
