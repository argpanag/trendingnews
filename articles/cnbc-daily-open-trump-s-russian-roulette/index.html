CNBC Daily Open: Trump's Russian roulette

Hello, this is Leonie Kidd coming to you from London. Welcome to another edition of CNBC's Daily Open.

It's a massive political gamble ahead of the midterms.

President Trump's announcement of a diesel deal with Russia has stunned the global community. And now, questions are being asked about whether Moscow can supply the U.S. with enough diesel for the agreement to be felt at the pump.

It's a risk, but so far oil is down and futures are up.

TikTok CEO sees major scientific breakthroughs from AI within three years

TikTok CEO Shou Zi Chew expects artificial intelligence to drive major scientific breakthroughs within the next three years, transforming a broad range of sectors.

"In the next two to three years, we're going to have a real scientific breakthrough: physics, biotech, chemistry, material science, because of AI. It's very, very, very possible," Chew said at the DBS Asia Future Forward Conference in Singapore on Monday.

Chew's remarks come as ByteDance, TikTok's Chinese parent, expands its artificial intelligence research beyond consumer applications. In April, the company's Seed research team unveiled Protenix-v2, an AI model focused on biomolecular structure prediction and design, with potential applications in drug discovery.
