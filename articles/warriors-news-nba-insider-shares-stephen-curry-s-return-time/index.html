Warriors News: NBA Insider Shares Stephen Curry’s Return Timeline After Abdominal Tear - BasketballNews

Nov 25, 2026, 5:24 AM CUT

The 9-8 Golden State Warriors might just have to take on the Sacramento Kings without rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, cutting their offense narrower than it has been throughout Stephen Curry’s absence. The only good news for the Warriors fans and admirers is that Curry is getting reevaluated soon and might just be able to hit the paint sooner rather than later.

“They really need Steph back,” national NBA insider Brett Siegel reported. “He's going to be re-evaluated … we're going to get that update sometime between now and Thursday on how many more weeks we're looking at here. I've heard that they're (the Warriors) pretty confident that he's going to be back sometime within the next two weeks here.”

Ever since the Warriors' cornerstone went down with a right oblique tear on November 11 during a game against the Houston Rockets, the team’s offense has basically collapsed. They suffered a 5-game losing streak and failed to score more than 100 points in any of them. Although they managed to pull off a narrow 88-87 victory over the Boston Celtics recently, there hasn’t been any true progress in the situation.

Imago

Credits - Imago

And as Siegel noted, it has been a historical trend for the Warriors. Last season, Curry missed a total of 39 games due to a knee injury. The Warriors fell to a 37-45 record and missed the playoffs entirely. Jimmy Butler and Moses Moody’s absence made things worse, as the offense went off the rails altogether.

“They needed Steph Curry in order to be a very successful team,” Siegel added. “We saw it last year. As soon as he went down, granted they were without Jimmy Butler and Moses Moody as well, but once Steph went down, the season was lost for the Warriors. And it's kind of the same thing right now. If he’s out for a prolonged period of time, this team just doesn’t have that sustainability.”

The 38-year-old superstar was averaging 27.8 points, 3.9 assists, and 3.7 rebounds across 10 games this season before his injury, and even hinted at retirement amid his eclipsing career. But, knowing how much the team needs him, Siegel noted that Curry himself is eager to return.

“Steph himself is really pushing to get back as soon as possible,” Siegel concluded. “He knows that the Warriors need him. He wants to be out there on the floor. He doesn't want to be missing any more games. And so right now, I think we're looking somewhere within 14 to 21 days to get Steph Curry back on the court.”

Several reports have noted that Butler and Moody will return by February 2027 or earlier. If Siegel’s prediction lands on the mark, the Golden State Warriors will be in for a stellar season powered by Butler and Moody, anchored by Curry.
