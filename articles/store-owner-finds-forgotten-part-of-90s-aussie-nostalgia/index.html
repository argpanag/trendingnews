Store owner finds forgotten part of 90s Aussie nostalgia

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:36 · source · permalink

The owner of the Kolmar House General Store discovered a thirty year old piece of Aussie nostalgia. Photo: Facebook / Realestate.com.au

A small town general store has discovered a blast from the past, and the hype it has caused could mean the return of a favourite Aussie snack.

A thirty year old Magnum Ego has been frozen in time in the back fridge of a historic rural general store in Harrow, Victoria.

Owner of Kolmar House General Store, Ash Stanbridge, was preparing the store for summer when he stumbled across a part of Aussie 90s nostalgia.

“I took over Kolmar House about 10 months ago. I’m fairly new to running the shop, but the building itself is over 145 years old and has been a central part of Harrow’s history for generations,”

“We were doing a clean-out in the stables next door … We were defrosting and cleaning out one of the older freezers left by previous owners and buried right down at the bottom under decades of ice, there it was,” he said.

Frozen inside the old freezer was an original packaged Magnum Ego.

“(I was in) complete disbelief. I honestly had to look twice and check Google to confirm because I haven’t seen that packaging in decades.

Kolmar House is located in one of Victoria’s oldest inland townships, and claims the title of the longest continuously running licenced grocery store opening in 1881, yet Mr Stanbridge did not expect to discover this kind of history at the back of the store.

“Finding a piece of 90s nostalgia completely frozen in time after decades was surreal,” he said.

“A few locals have joked about tasting it, but after nearly three decades, it’s strictly an antique!”

Mr Stanbridge has left the antique untouched and intact in the freezer at Kolmar House. He reached out to the ice cream company who confirmed that the original Magnum Ego is an Australian invention which launched in 1998 and the packaging looks “remarkably consistent” with the original packaging from the late 90s and early 2000s.

“It holds a unique place in the country’s ice cream culture, quickly becoming one of the most celebrated and decadent icons of late-90s corner store snacking,” a spokesperson for the Magnum Ice Cream Company said.

Inside the old freezer was the Magnum Ego. Photo: Supplied

Since sharing the footage on the Kolmar General Store Facebook page, the discovery has made its way to the AustralianNostalgia subreddit with many reminiscing about the 90s treat and packaging.

“Since Magnum got in touch to say they’re looking into the find, it’s been incredible seeing the massive wave of memories Aussies have been sharing online about the original Ego,” Mr Stanbridge said.

“I know they don’t make them anymore, but like thousands of other nostalgic snack fans out there, I really wish they would!”

Like Mr Stanbridge, Aussies are now calling for the return of the Magnum Ego since the flavour was phased out at the beginning of 2026.

The Magnum Ego was an iconic creation conceived as the ultimate indulgence, designed to elevate the classic Magnum experience.

The comparison of the two. Photo: Supplied

“At its heart, Magnum has always been built on indulgence, fun and bringing a little extra pleasure to everyday life. The Ego delivered on that double crack Magnum chocolate, creamy vanilla ice cream and a rich caramel sauce,” The Magnum Ice Cream Company said.

The company has not ruled out the potential return of the iconic ice cream and said the response to finding the antique has been impossible to ignore.

“We know how passionate the Magnum Ego fanbase is and the overwhelming response to Ash’s discovery has certainly made that loud and clear. We’re paying close attention so watch this space,” the Magnum spokesperson said.

“We’ve loved seeing the conversation unfold and hearing everyone’s memories of one of Australia’s most iconic ice creams.

“We also love that a 145-year-old general store in Harrow has brought so much joy and nostalgia to snack fans across the country.”
