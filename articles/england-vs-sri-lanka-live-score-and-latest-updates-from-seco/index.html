England review

Atkinson lbw b Fernando Hit on the toe by a hooping, reverse swinging yorker. 

OVER 43: ENG 275/5 (Jacks 37 Atkinson 9)

Wellalage replaces Colombage and the Surrey boys milk him for three singles off his first three balls, all down the ground, until Atkinson whips two off his toes through square leg. 

England need 47 off 42 and the probability of their victory stands at 76 per cent at the end of the over according to the algorithm. 

OVER 42: ENG 269/5 (Jacks 35 Atkinson 5)

Atkinson cannot pierce the infield from Madushanka’s first two deliveries then works the third, shorter one, for a single through square leg. Jacks has to raise himself on to his toes to swat he slow bouncer for a single through cover. 

Atkinson drives the left-armer for a single to long-on and Jacks pinches the strike with another tip and run single with the field spread. 

OVER 41: ENG 265/5 (Jacks 33 Atkinson 3)

Sri Lanka bring back Colombage and Jacks cuts his first ball for a single. Atkinson hops on to the back foot to get off the mark with a stir of the handle to divert the ball through mid-on. Rathnayake saves thre runs with a fine, diving half-stop at cover when Jacks flayed his drive towards him. They run a single as they do when Atkinson drives through mid-off and finish of with two more nudged into the legside to keep up with the rate. 

OVER 40: ENG 259/5 (Jacks 30 Atkinson 0)

Madushanka replaces Malinga for the last over before they can put five men outside the circle. Jacks slaps a drive off the back foot just beyond the leap of Asalanka at cover point. They run two and Jacks then gives Rehan the strike by tucking a single off his hip. Rehan falls to his sixth ball without scoring and SL must be edging ahead in the betting now.

OVER 39: ENG 256/4 (Jacks 27 Rehan 0)

OVER 38: ENG 255/4 (Jacks 26 Rehan 0)

After a couple of delays for treatment and then for Buttler to limp off, England require 67 off 72 balls.
