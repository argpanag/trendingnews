First Look: Martin Scorsese Is Directing A Horror Film With Leonardo DiCaprio And Jennifer Lawrence

TheTools · 26.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

We now know why Leonardo DiCaprio was sporting a moustache at this year's Oscars.

The actor has shared a first look at his new film What Happens at Night, which is being directed by Martin Scorsese and also stars Jennifer Lawrence.

The image shows DiCaprio and Lawrence playing a married couple walking hand in hand through a snowy and isolated landscape.

Production is now underway on the film, which is being described as a haunted marriage drama with psychological horror elements.

 

 

A post shared by Leonardo DiCaprio (@leonardodicaprio)

The story follows an American couple who travel to a small European town to adopt a baby. However, things quickly become strange, with the couple finding themselves trapped in an unsettling and mysterious world. As they try to claim their baby, they begin to question their marriage, themselves and what is actually happening around them.

The film is based on the novel What Happens at Night by Peter Cameron. The cast also includes Mads Mikkelsen, Patricia Clarkson and Jared Harris.

For DiCaprio, the movie marks another collaboration with Scorsese. The pair have previously worked together on Gangs of New York, The Aviator, The Departed, Shutter Island and The Wolf of Wall Street.

Jennifer Lawrence, meanwhile, is working with Scorsese for the first time.

The two stars previously spoke about the director during Variety's Actors on Actors, when Lawrence asked DiCaprio what she should expect from working with Scorsese.

DiCaprio warned her that the director has a habit of recommending films to his actors.

"He’s going to give you a lot of film references," he told her.

He explained that Scorsese sometimes organises screenings of entire films, or even individual scenes, to help actors understand the tone or style he is looking for.

DiCaprio also suggested they could watch Japanese ghost films while working on What Happens at Night.

"You’re going to have an amazing time," he told Lawrence.

The new film comes from Apple, which previously worked with Scorsese on Killers of the Flower Moon, starring DiCaprio and Robert De Niro.

There is currently no release date for What Happens at Night, but filming is now underway.
