Hull City 2-2 Ipswich Town: What Gary O'Neil said

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:29 · source · permalink
  • Published
    8 minutes ago

Ipswich Town boss Gary O'Neil, speaking to Match of the Day, said: "We were the better side, we should have punished them more in the first half. We gifted them two goals, then we responded unbelievably well.

"The goals [we conceded] were the only real disappointing part, a lot of errors for goals which has cost us but in terms of coming away from home, dominating the game, defending, attacking, I thought the lads were very good today.

"We knew they [Hull] would change [at half-time] and they were better than in the first half but they still were not better than us, we were the better team. It's a point on the road and I've watched a lot of Hull's games and not a lot of teams were as good with the ball as we were.

"It's tough to go away from home, we keep moving forwards and get ourselves ready for Bournemouth in the next one. The boys were excellent and mistakes will happen and it's just unfortunate that the only two errors we made both led to goals - I didn't like either of their two goals and the second one is preventable.

"I'm really pleased with where my team is at after that today. We were the better team away from home and that takes some doing. It's tough to be in control and find yourselves 2-1 down with the place bouncing. Our lads have some character and we just need to keep it up. Hull are a very good side and I'm really pleased with how we did against them."

Did you know?

  • Ipswich have avoided defeat in successive Premier League matches for the first time since January 2025, while it is the first time that manager Gary O'Neil has done so in the competition since a run of four in October/November 2024 with Wolves.

  • Ipswich have won 8 points from losing positions in the Premier League this season - more than any other team.
