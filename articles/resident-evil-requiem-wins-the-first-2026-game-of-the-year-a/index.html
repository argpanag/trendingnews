Resident Evil Requiem Wins the First 2026 Game of the Year Award

TheTools · 11.11.2026 20:45 · source · permalink

Top accolade at the Golden Joystick Awards

Resident Evil Requiem has been crowned the Ultimate Game of the Year at the Golden Joystick Awards, the first major games award ceremony of 2026.

Alongside the biggest award, it was also named the Console Game of the Year, and it won categories celebrating the best lead performer (Angela Sant'Albano as Grace Ashcroft) and the best game trailer. Most of the Golden Joystick Awards are decided by a public vote, and the race for Ultimate Game of the Year was extremely close.

Resident Evil Requiem won the award with 17% of the overall vote, but Phantom Blade Zero was right behind it with 16% and then Crimson Desert got 15%.

Obviously, since it's not out yet, GTA 6 wasn't eligible for any categories.

Elsewhere, the award for the Best Remake / Remaster went to The Witcher 3 Remastered, then Dispatch won the Best Soundtrack and Best Storytelling categories. Onimusha: Way of the Sword has the Best Audio Design according to the voters, Valheim was the year's Best Indie Game, and The Witcher 4 is the Most Wanted Game.

Below are all the category winners:

  • Best Storytelling: Dispatch
  • Best Audio Design: Onimusha: Way of the Sword
  • Best Multiplayer Game: Meccha Chameleon
  • Best Visual Design: Control Resonant
  • Best Game Expansion: Doom: The Dark Ages Revelations
  • Best Indie Game (Self-Published): Meccha Chameleon
  • Best Indie Game: Valheim
  • Best Gaming Hardware: Steam Controller
  • Studio of the Year: Capcom
  • Best Early Access Game: Subnautica 2
  • Best Soundtrack: Dispatch
  • Still Playing (Mobile): Balatro
  • Still Playing (PC & Console): Minecraft
  • Streamer's Choice: Meccha Chameleon
  • Best Game Trailer: Resident Evil Requiem - Evil Has Always Had a Name
  • Best Remake/Remaster: The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered
  • Developer's Choice: Meccha Chameleon
  • Best Game Adaptation: Iron Lung
  • Best Supporting Performer: Grace Saif (Diana, Pragmata)
  • Best Lead Performer: Angela Sant'Albano (Grace Ashcroft, Resident Evil Requiem)
  • PC Game of the Year: Crimson Desert
  • Console Game of the Year: Resident Evil Requiem
  • Critic's Choice: Big Walk
  • Most Wanted Game: The Witcher 4
  • Ultimate Game of the Year: Resident Evil Requiem
