Nicole Scherzinger, Fiancé Thom Evans Break Up After 7 Years

TheTools · 27.11.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

Nicole Scherzinger’s fiancé Thom Evans announced the couple have broken up seven years after finding love on The X Factor: Celebrity, noting, “This is not the outcome I had ever hoped for.”

Nicole Scherzinger is back to being a solo artist.

Three years after the Pussycat Dolls alum and rugby player Thom Evans became engaged, Evans announced they have broken up.

“It is with a heavy heart that l share that Nicole and l are no longer together,” the 41-year-old wrote in a Nov. 27 Instagram Story. “After seven years of love, commitment and believing in a future together, this is an incredibly difficult time.”

Evans’ statement also seemingly revealed that their parting of ways was not necessarily mutual.

“Whilst this is not the outcome I had ever hoped for,” he wrote, “I respect Nicole's decision and genuinely wish her every happiness in the next chapter of her life.”

Scherzinger, for her part, has not yet addressed their breakup.

Evans and the Broadway star first met in 2019 while filming The X Factor: Celebrity, during which Evans formed part of the boy band Try Star. And though the former professional athlete finished fifth in the competition, he left with an even better prize: Scherzinger’s heart.

Though the pair initially kept their romance buttoned up, they were spotted spending time out and about together in late 2019 before making their red carpet debut as a couple at a Golden Globes after party in Jan. 2020.

Then, three years later, Scherzinger revealed Evans had popped the question in a romantic, beachside proposal.

Alongside photos of Evans down on one knee in the sand before a surprised Scherzinger, the singer captioned her June 2023 post, “I said yes.”

In the time since, the pair have shared the occasional glimpse into their romance on social media.

For his part, Evans showed off his and Scherzinger’s epic summer with a July Instagram dump heavily featuring the singer. He captioned the post, “Lately.”

Meanwhile, the last time Evans appeared on Scherzinger’s Instagram grid was in late September. During a trip to Paris, the then-couple took the time to dance to the music of street performers on the city’s streets.

She wrote over the clip, “Dancing in the city of love with my love,” quipping in the caption, “And getting shown up by the locals.

But Scherzinger and Evans aren’t the only celebrity couple for whom 2026 marked the end of their love. Read on for more.
