Beauty queen Katrina Johnson expressed her excitement as she’s set to represent the Philippines in the upcoming Miss International 2026 pageant.

In an Instagram post, Johnson marked the milestone as she claimed it’s a prayer she’s currently living.

“Today marks the culmination of years of believing, becoming, and refusing to give up on the possibilities of my own life,” Johnson expressed.

More than winning the seventh crown of the country in the Miss International stage, Johnson is hopeful that her story will serve as an inspiration for others out there who dare to dream big.

ADVERTISEMENT

“Regardless of what happens tonight, I hope my journey reminds someone out there that there is no expiration date on a dream, that trying again is something to be proud of, and that sometimes, the most beautiful chapters of our lives begin when we find the courage to put ourselves out there one more time,” she added.

“Pilipinas, ito na ang ating panahon,” she ended.

Johnson is competing with over 70 candidates in the Japan-based pageant and is eyeing to take home the seventh crown of the country. The last time the Philippines won the Miss International crown was in 2016 with actress-beauty queen Kylie Versoza.

Meanwhile, the Final Gala of Miss International 2026 is set to happen today, November 25, at the Kanadevia Hall in Japan. The Miss International YouTube channel will stream the Final Gala in real time, 4:30 p.m. (Philippine time).
