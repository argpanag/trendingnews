Northeasterly winds bring coastal hazards including tidal flooding and rough surf

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. – Tonight will be partly cloudy and chilly with temperatures in the 50s and 60s.

Monday will feature a mostly cloudy sky and it will be breezy. Highs will be near 70 degrees and winds could gust up to 30 mph at times, especially along the coast.

The strong northeasterly wind will bring coastal hazards to our area. These include tidal flooding, rough surf and a higher risk for rip currents.

An area of high pressure be north of our area and will keep a northeasterly wind around through most of the upcoming week. This will keep the humidity low and keep temperatures below normal.

Regarding the drought, the latest drought monitor reflects improvement across our area. Several areas have been downgraded to the lowest category (abnormally dry).

While we are still in a deficit, rainfall this week will once again lead to more improvement in the next outlook.

TONIGHT: Mostly Cloudy. Windy. Low 57.

MONDAY: Partly Cloudy. Breezy. Mainly Dry. High 70, Low 55.

TUESDAY: Mostly Sunny. Breezy. High 75 Low 56.

WEDNESDAY: Mostly Sunny. High 76, Low 56.

THURSDAY: Mostly Sunny. High 77, Low 58.
