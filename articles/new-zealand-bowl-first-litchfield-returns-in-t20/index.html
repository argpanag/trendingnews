New Zealand bowl first, Litchfield returns in T20

TheTools · 20.10.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

New Zealand have won the toss and will bowl in the second Twenty20 International against Australia at North Sydney Oval.

Australia's top order batter Phoebe Litchfield returns to the side for Tuesday night's match after overcoming a minor hip issue.

She replaces Talhia McGrath in the only change to the team which won Sunday's opening game by two wickets.

Victory will ensure Australia retain the Rose Bowl with a game to play in the three match series, a trophy the home side has held for 26 years.

Teams:

Australia: Beth Mooney, Georgia Voll, Phoebe Litchfield, Ellyse Perry, Ashleigh Gardner, Georgia Wareham, Annabel Sutherland, Nicola Carey, Sophie Molineux (c), Kim Garth, Lucy Hamilton.

New Zealand" Georgia Plimmer, Flora Devonshire, Melie Kerr (c), Maddy Green, Izzy Sharp, Brooke Halliday, Jess Kerr, Polly Inglis, Rosemary Mair, Nensi Patel, Bree Illing.
