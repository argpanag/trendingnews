Pierce Brosnan's wife Keely, 63, shows off her slimmer figure

TheTools · 26.10.2026 08:40 · source · permalink

Pierce Brosnan's wife Keely Shaye Smith showed off her slimmer figure in a satin slip dress as she posed for a sweet family photo on Sunday.

The actress and writer, 63, looked stunning in the purple gown as she posed alongside her mother Sharon to celebrate her 82nd birthday.

Also joining the festivities were James Bond star Pierce, 73, who has been married to Keely for 25 years, and the couple's sons Dylan, 29, and Paris, 25.

The family smiled for the camera while posing in front of Sharon's birthday cake, which was covered in candles.

Keely wrote in the caption of her Instagram post: 'Please join me in wishing a very Happy 82nd Birthday to my beloved mother Sharon Rose!'

She has been showcasing her slimmer frame since 2024, and it has been reported that she has lost around 100lbs (45kg). 

Back in 2006, the actress previously told Vogue: 'I never shy away from... my curves. I never hide in baggy clothing.' 

007 icon Pierce has also come to his wife's defence in the past when a social media troll had pointed out changes to her appearance.

'I strongly love every curve of her body. She is the most beautiful woman in my eyes. And also because she had our children,' he said.

'In the past, I truly loved her for her person, not only for her beauty, and now I'm loving her even more that she is my children's mother. And I am very proud of her, and I always seek to be worthy of her love.'

Keely previously told People she immediately found the Bond actor 'captivating' when they met and said that he had a 'mischievous sparkle in his eyes'.

In the same interview, the MobLand said of his wife: 'I found a great woman in Keely Shaye. Not if I searched a million times over would I find one as good.'

Last year, Pierce said that Keely still makes his 'world turn', telling Fox News: 'She still makes my heart sing and she still makes my world turn.

'She allows me to go out into the world and create what I do as an actor, and that takes strength and stamina. We just enjoy each other's company.'

In January, fans jumped to Keely's defence after a troll uploaded comparison pictures of the couple.

One image had been of the couple at the Governors Ball for the Academy Awards in 1996, while the other showed them at the Mamma Mia! premiere in 2008.

The troll then used the photos as a 'reminder to avoid marriage'. 

However, one X user said: 'I don't get some men on X devotion to degrade Brosnan's wife. They're both happy, aging gracefully, and she was and is absolutely gorgeous.'

Another wrote: 'Pierce Brosnan's won the lottery of life. Dude's been married 30 years and they've both aged gracefully.'

Brosnan is also father to son Sean, 43, whom he had with first wife Cassandra Harris, who passed away in 1991 at age 43 following a battle with ovarian cancer. 

The actor adopted Harris's two other children, Christopher and Charlotte, from her previous relationship. In 2013, Charlotte passed away at age 41 from ovarian cancer.

Pierce and Keely first crossed paths in 1994 while attending a party in Cabo San Lucas, Mexico. 

After welcoming their two sons, they tied the knot in August 2001 during a romantic ceremony in Ireland. 

In August, the couple celebrated their 25th wedding anniversary, with Keely sharing a series of snaps of the pair from over the years on Instagram.

She also penned in the caption: 'Happy 25th wedding anniversary my love! What an adventure life is with you. Looking forward to the next 25 years beside you @piercebrosnanofficial.'

Last month, the couple made a rare appearance together as they attended the Los Angeles premiere of Seniors alongside their two sons and their girlfriends.
