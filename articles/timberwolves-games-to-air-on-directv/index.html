Timberwolves games to air on DIRECTV

TheTools · 26.10.2026 17:13 · source · permalink

The Timberwolves will now be available to view on DIRECTV for those with “Choice” or higher-tiered packages.

A deal was announced Monday making Minnesota’s broadcasts, previously only available to be streamed on the DAZN app, will now also be broadcast on DIRECTV channel 668 for those who have purchased a qualifying package.

The Wild reached a similar agreement in recent days for their Wild+ broadcasts.

Minnesota’s games on Friday in Orlando and Sunday against Toronto were stream-only, which led many fans — including those with DIRECTV — to purchase subscriptions.
