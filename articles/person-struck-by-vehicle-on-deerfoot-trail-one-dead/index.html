Person struck by vehicle on Deerfoot Trail, one dead

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:02 · source · permalink

Deerfoot Trail closed between Peigan Trail and 17 Avenue S.E.

A person was struck by a vehicle along Deerfoot Trail on Tuesday, according to Calgary police.

The incident occurred shortly after 1 p.m. in the northbound lanes of Deerfoot Trail and 17 Avenue S.E.

As a result, all northbound lanes of Deerfoot Trail S.E. are closed between Peigan Trail and 17 Avenue S.E.

“We are asking drivers to avoid the area,” CPS said in a statement.

Postmedia photographers at the scene observed a body at the crash site.

A witness told Postmedia Calgary the man had been changing his tire before the collision took place.

The City of Calgary alerted drivers on social media to expect delays due to the crash and resulting road closure.
