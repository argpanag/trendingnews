Hayen issues updates on Hatate and Julio ahead of Bristol City clash

TheTools · 05.09.2026 13:53 · source · permalink

Nicky Hayen wants his Burnley side to build on their second half against Middlesbrough as they return to Turf Moor to host Bristol City.

Ugo Raghouber scored a stunning equaliser as the Clarets fought back to claim a point against Boro on Wednesday night.

Despite the improvement after the break, Hayen’s side are still searching for their first win and only have two points on the board.

“We finished the game actually quite well - especially the start from the second half and the way we scored that goal with a lot of energy, a lot of positive energy,” said Hayen.

It is set to be another tough test against Bristol City, who are already on seven points after a positive start under Michael Skubala.

“They play totally different and they have managed quite well, they have the points at the moment, so they have a lot of confidence,” the Clarets chief continued. “But it doesn't affect actually our mindset, we just have to continue the way how we are working.

“I know it is with ups and downs, but what we have to remember is how we came out of that dressing room. This has to give us the energy, the boost and confidence to start this game well tomorrow.

“Every piece has to fall into that puzzle and it is also about the physical part and the mental part, we have already discussed it enough.

“But what we really need is just a win to change everything, and again we have an opportunity to show it tomorrow.”

Josh Laurent is back from suspension but Lucas Pires remains unavailable due to the concussion protocol. The left-back could return at Wrexham next week.

Largie Ramazani made his debut on Wednesday night, coming off the bench for the second half against Boro.

“With these 45 minutes, not having a reaction is a really good sign,” said Hayen. “This is what we try to build up gradually going up to 60, 75, up to 90. 

“But the problem is also you see some players – for example, Anel (Ahmedhodzic) - who played 30 minutes in pre-season and 75.

“He played already three games, three consecutive games, 90 minutes, so his body feels it also. But we try to push our players as far as possible without having injuries.”

Reo Hatate and Igor Julio could also come into the squad after joining from Brighton and Celtic respectively on deadline day.

“Reo had a real good pre-season and has played already games,” the head coach explained. “So he is ready to play, to start if it’s necessary. 

“And Igor needs, of course, a little bit more time because he already played a long time without games. 

“In pre-season he did training sessions, but of course he needs a little bit of time. But step by step we will let him grow.”
