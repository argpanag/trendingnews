Work week weather: Temperatures rise to 70s by Friday in Christian County - Christian County Now

TheTools · 16.11.2026 15:43 · source · permalink

Christian County forecast: Although this week will be off to a chilly start, expect it to gradually warm up by the time the weekend rolls around.

CHRISTIAN COUNTY, KY (CHRISTIAN COUNTY NOW) – Expect a sunny and cool start to the week, with highs near 59 degrees Monday and overnight temperatures dropping to around 33.

Temperatures will gradually warm throughout the week, reaching the mid-60s Wednesday before climbing to near 70 degrees Thursday and Friday with sunny skies, according to the National Weather Service.

Christian County forecast

Here’s the day-by-day outlook.

  • Monday: Sunny, with a high near 59. East wind 3 to 6 mph.
  • Monday night: Clear, with a low around 33. Light east wind.
  • Tuesday: Sunny, with a high near 63. Light and variable wind.
  • Tuesday night: Clear, with a low around 34. Calm wind.
  • Wednesday: Sunny, with a high near 66. Calm wind becoming south around 6 mph in the morning.
  • Wednesday night: Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. Light south southeast wind.
  • Thursday: Mostly sunny, with a high near 70. South wind around 5 mph becoming calm in the afternoon.
  • Thursday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 41. Calm wind.
  • Friday: Sunny, with a high near 70. Calm wind becoming southeast around 5 mph.
  • Friday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 42. Light east southeast wind.
