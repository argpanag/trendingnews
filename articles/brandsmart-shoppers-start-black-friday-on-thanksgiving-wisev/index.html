BrandsMart Shoppers Start Black Friday on Thanksgiving | Wisevoter

Shoppers in South Florida began Black Friday deal hunting in stores on Thanksgiving Day, comparing in-store prices with online offers.

Updated on Nov. 27, 2026 in Retail

Shoppers in South Florida started Black Friday shopping on Thanksgiving Day, filling carts at stores including BrandsMart USA in Miami Gardens. Shoppers said in-store deals were cheaper than online prices.

Why it matters

The early start to holiday shopping reflects how retailers and consumers are adjusting to a shopping season that now stretches across Thanksgiving weekend. Local shoppers are weighing in-store and online options as they plan holiday budgets.

The National Retail Federation projects about 183 million people will shop over Thanksgiving weekend, fewer than last year, and 69% of shoppers plan to make Black Friday purchases. Final local sales totals for Miami Gardens stores have not been reported.

The players

BrandsMart USA

BrandsMart USA is a South Florida electronics and appliance retailer with multiple locations across the region.

National Retail Federation

The National Retail Federation is a Washington-based trade association that publishes annual holiday shopping forecasts for the U.S. retail industry.

The details

Shoppers filled carts with gifts in store aisles on Thanksgiving Day, with some buying appliances such as an ice maker and an air fryer. Others visited stores to look for big screen televisions and compared prices against online listings before buying.

Season Trajectory

The National Retail Federation's annual holiday forecasts have tracked how Black Friday has expanded into Thanksgiving Day and the full holiday weekend. The projected 183 million Thanksgiving weekend shoppers, below last year, suggests that the expansion of the shopping window has not yet translated into rising shopper counts.

Shoppers comparing in-store and online prices on Thanksgiving may find better deals on appliances and electronics by checking both before buying. Holding off on purchases until Black Friday or Cyber Monday can still matter for households with a fixed holiday budget.

The takeaway

Comparing in-store and online prices before buying can help holiday shoppers stretch their budgets across the full shopping weekend.

What happens next

Small Business Saturday and Cyber Monday follow later in the holiday weekend, and shoppers continue watching for deals on both days.
