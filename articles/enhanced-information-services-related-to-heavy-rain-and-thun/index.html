Enhanced Information Services related to Heavy Rain and Thunderstorms

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:45 · source · permalink

10 September 2026

The Hong Kong Observatory has enhanced the information services today (10 September 2026) to help the public more quickly identify the areas that may be affected by heavy rain and thunderstorms. When the Thunderstorm Warning is in force and thunderstorms are expected to only affect certain areas, a red dot will be marked on the icon of Thunderstorm Warning on the homepage of the HKO website. Besides, when the Special Announcement on Flooding in the northern New Territories is in effect, users can access the “Regional Information on Heavy Rain and Thunderstorm” webpage via the link provided on the website homepage to view the locations of affected areas on map.

Push notifications for the Special Announcement on Flooding in the northern New Territories and Thunderstorm Warning sent through the “MyObservatory” mobile application will also include regional information. Members of the public can visit the HKO website or download the “MyObservatory” app at the following links (iOS, Android, Huawei) to obtain the latest weather information.
