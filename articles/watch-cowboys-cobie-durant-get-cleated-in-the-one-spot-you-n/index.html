Getting stepped on is never a good feeling. Cleats can only add to that pain. For Dallas Cowboys cornerback Cobie Durant, he was cleated by a teammate in the one spot where the sun doesn't shine.

The ensuing moment was a pain-ridden one for Durant, and it was a moment of bad luck. He stayed in the game after taking a second to regather himself, but the damage was done.

Perhaps it will be a moment he and his teammate, safety Markquese Bell, can laugh about in the future.

Here's a look at the unfortunate misstep that Durant didn't seem to enjoy too much.

Cobie Durant gets cleated by teammate
