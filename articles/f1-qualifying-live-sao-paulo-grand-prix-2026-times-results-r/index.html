Lorraine McKenna and Tasnim Chowdhury

  1. 'We should've finished the lap' - Russellpublished at 20:46 GMT
    20:46 GMT

    Mercedes' George Russell, who qualified P2 but will start P7 with a five-place grid penalty: "Obviously congrats to Lando [Norris] but that was definitely the pole lap. Misjudgment on our side, we need to understand what happened. And yeah, the rain was coming and we should've just finished the lap."

    On what he can do in tomorrow's race: "It depends on the weather. It's the first time in a while I've felt confident in the car; I felt more confident in the wet than I do in the dry, so I don't really understand why that was.

    "I felt more like myself and could fight for pole every single lap. But unfortunately, when it mattered, we couldn't execute."

  2. 'I think I figured it out pretty quick' - Norrispublished at 20:41 GMT
    20:41 GMT

    McLaren's Lando Norris who will start the race in pole position: "It has been a long day. It has been a great qualifying, to be on pole again here, two years in a row. Different conditions to last year but I am very happy, it's not easy conditions out there, especially when you know it is going to rain a lot more. It was about getting two laps in, in the beginning and not make a mistake, that is exactly what we did so it has been a very good day.

    "This was my first time driving the car in the wet so I think I figured it out pretty quick. Full wet, inters, all different tyres, it was very windy as well the last couple of laps so it is certainly a good day for learning for me and good to take into tomorrow. These guys [Mercedes] are also very quick. Today is just about getting on pole, doesn't matter how you do it so very pleased with that."
