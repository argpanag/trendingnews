Who is Maya Jama? Love Island host to switch iconic villa for Traitors castle

Maya Jama is one of the most recognisable faces in the world of reality TV, but how much do you know about her?

She’s the current host of Love Island, a judge on The Masked Singer, and now she's heading into The Celebrity Traitors castle.

Sharing why she was keen to be a part of the second series of the show, Jama said: “I love the show.

“I watched the last celebrity series and I've always wanted to do some kind of challenge game show, but there’s never really been the right one.

Who is Maya Jama?

Maya Jama is a TV presenter and businesswoman, as she co-owns Sproud, a dairy alternative to milk made from pea protein.

She also owns a skincare brand called MIJ Skincare, which is “high-quality, high-performance skincare, for every face & every moment”.

Her TV career has seen her appear on shows such as Love Island, The Masked Singer, Glow Up: Britain’s Next Make-up Star and more.

She’s passionate about fashion and beauty and has collaborated with various brands including British Vogue, Rimmel London and Grazia UK.

Jama also stars as Aisha in series two of The Gentleman, which is available to watch on Netflix.
