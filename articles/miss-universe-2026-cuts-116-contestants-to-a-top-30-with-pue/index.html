Miss Universe 2026 cuts 116 contestants to a Top 30, with Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto advancing on home soil

TheTools · 25.11.2026 01:53 · source · permalink

México, Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela and USA were among the contestants selected

Thirty delegates advanced to the semifinals of Miss Universe 2026, narrowing a field of 116 contestants during the pageant's 75th anniversary competition in San Juan, Puerto Rico. Thirteen of them were from Latin America, including Mexico, Puerto Rico, Venezuela, Cuba and Colombia, as well as Miss USA.

The announcement came during Tuesday's live finale at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot. The contestants had already taken part in private interviews and the preliminary competition, which included swimsuit and evening gown presentations. Those performances helped determine who would continue in the race for the crown.

One place in the Top 30 was reserved for the contestant who received the most votes through Miss Universe's official fan-voting platform. The organization said that the delegate would advance automatically. The other semifinalists were selected through the competition's judging process.
