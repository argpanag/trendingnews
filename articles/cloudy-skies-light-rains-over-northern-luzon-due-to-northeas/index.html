Cloudy skies, light rains over Northern Luzon due to Northeasterly Windflow

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

The Northeasterly Windflow will bring cloudy skies with isolated light rains over Northern Luzon on Tuesday, PAGASA reported.

Forecast Weather Conditions

Batanes, Cagayan, and Isabela will have cloudy skies with light rains due to the Northeasterly Windflow but with no significant impact.

Ilocos Region, Cordillera Administrative Region, and the rest of Cagayan Valley will have partly cloudy to cloudy skies with isolated light rains due to the Northeasterly Windflow but with no significant impact.

Metro Manila and the rest of the country will have partly cloudy to cloudy skies with isolated rain showers or thunderstorms due to localized thunderstorms and flash floods or landslides during severe thunderstorms.

Forecast Wind and Coastal Water Conditions

The wind speed forecast for Extreme Northern Luzon is moderate to strong and moving northeastward while coastal waters will be moderate to rough.

The rest of the country will experience light to moderate wind speed moving northeastward with slight to moderate coastal waters.

Sunrise will be at 5:50 a.m., sunset at 5:30 p.m. — BAP, GMA News
