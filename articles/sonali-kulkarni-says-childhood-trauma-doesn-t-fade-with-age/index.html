Sonali Kulkarni says childhood trauma doesn't fade with age

TheTools · 07.11.2026 16:13 · source · permalink

Updated On: 03 November, 2026 03:21 PM IST | Mumbai | IANS

Actress Sonali Kulkarni has opened up about her upcoming short film ‘Like Maa.’ She reflected on how childhood traumas can leave a lasting impact and do not simply fade with age.

Pic/Instagram

Actress Sonali Kulkarni has opened up about her upcoming short film ‘Like Maa.’ She reflected on how childhood traumas can leave a lasting impact and do not simply fade with age.

Sharing her experience, Sonali told IANS, “I was very touched by the subject Neeru has chosen for her first short film. It is a very sensitive subject, and her direction is also very subtle. I am happy that I could add value to her script because she was open to improvisations and suggestions.”
