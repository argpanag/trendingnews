Venezuelan singer Gus Ormo found dead in pool at Weston home, reports say

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:25 · source · permalink

Gus Ormo, a Venezuelan musician and singer, died Friday morning in a “suspicious incident” where he was found submerged in a backyard pool in Weston, reports say.

Someone called 911 about 9 a.m. Friday to report a possible drowning near the 2100 block of Quail Roost Drive in Weston, Sheriff’s Office spokesperson Gerdy St. Louis said in an email Friday afternoon.

Deputies and firefighters arrived and found a man submerged in the pool. He was pronounced dead there, St. Louis said.

The home is owned by the Venezuelan musician and singer Gustavo Enrique Ormo, who is known professionally as Gus Ormo, Broward County Property Appraiser records show. Ormo is the registered agent of an inactive company associated with the Quail Roost Drive address that appears on various music streaming sites as Ormo’s record label, state business records show.

The person who called 911 reported that the man was “tied up” and at the bottom of the pool, according to first responders’ radio communications archived by the site Broadcastify.

Ormo’s sister confirmed his death to Telemundo 51 Miami and CBS Miami, the stations reported. She told the news stations Ormo’s family did not have additional information about his death.

Ormo’s relatives did not respond to a Sun Sentinel reporter’s voicemails Friday night.

Located in the Weston Hills neighborhood, the home is about three miles from The Club at Weston Hills, a private golf and country club that features two 18-hole championship courses in the community.

Homicide and Crime Scene Unit detectives are investigating the “suspicious incident,” St. Louis said.
