Maple Leafs' Anthony Stolarz to start against Predators

TheTools · 06.10.2026 11:23 · source · permalink

The Toronto Maple Leafs will maintain their goalie rotation on Tuesday.

Anthony Stolarz will start against the visiting Nashville Predators, head coach Jim Hiller told reporters at morning skate.

Sergei Bobrovsky played the first and third games of the season, while Stolarz played Game 2 for Toronto.

Stolarz has the only win so far, making 26 saves in a 2-1 win over the New York Islanders last week.

The Predators are 1-1 on the season, having beaten Dallas in their last game on Saturday.
