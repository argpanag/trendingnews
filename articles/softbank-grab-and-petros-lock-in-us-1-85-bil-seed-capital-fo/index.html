Softbank, Grab and PETROS lock in US$1.85 bil seed capital for AI infrastructure in Malaysia

TheTools · 09.11.2026 13:59 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 9): Petroleum Sarawak Bhd (PETROS), SoftBank Group Corp and Grab Holdings Ltd have entered into a joint development agreement (JDA) for US$1.85 billion in initial combined seed capital to develop AI infrastructure in Sarawak and Peninsula Malaysia.

Building on the framework agreement signed in Kuching, Malaysia, on Oct 8, 2026, the JDA marks the next phase of the partnership to advance plans to develop AI infrastructure in the nation.

Of the proposed initial investment of US$1.85 billion, US$1 billion will be invested in Sarawak and the other US$850 million in Peninsula Malaysia.

Of the US$1 billion earmarked for Sarawak, US$800 million is from SoftBank Group, US$750 million from Grab and US$100 million from PETROS. Meanwhile, SoftBank Group and Grab will invest US$650 million and US$200 million, respectively, for the project in the Peninsula.

The proposed investment will be deployed in stages, subject to projects achieving key development and customer contract milestones, and the necessary closing conditions.

The partnership combines SoftBank Group's global AI and technology capabilities, Grab's digital and AI expertise, and PETROS' local energy and infrastructure development experience.

The collaboration aims to build towards a long-term target of five gigawatts of AI compute capacity and will also explore opportunities across the wider AI value chain, including AI compute and services, advanced technologies, robotics, local workforce and community development.

"The investment forms part of a multi-year plan to develop AI compute capacity in Sarawak, and the parties will also seek additional investors and hyperscaler customers to support the project's development and long-term expansion," according to a joint statement on Monday.

The proposed investment will be deployed in stages subject to projects achieving key development and customer contract milestones, and the necessary closing conditions. 

SoftBank Group is the majority shareholder of the Sarawak AI project, with an option for Grab to increase its stake in future—reducing SoftBank Group's share while it remains the majority shareholder.

Grab will lead development of the Peninsular Malaysia project as a founding shareholder, with future funding expected through third-party equity and non-recourse project financing.

Given the parties' mutual interest in developing the AI ecosystem in Malaysia, SoftBank Group has agreed in principle to a five-year lock-up in respect of Grab shares that will be held directly by an entity within SoftBank Group, with the lock-up to take effect in accordance with the agreed terms and subject to customary exceptions.

Once fully operational, the facilities are expected to be among the largest AI infrastructure of their kind in Asia, and the project will be developed in close collaboration with Malaysia's federal government and the Sarawak government, aligning with the National AI Action Plan 2026–2030 and Sarawak's Post Covid-19 Development Strategy 2030.

Through the Sarawak Gas Roadmap, PETROS is advancing critical gas infrastructure to strengthen Sarawak's energy security and support its industrial expansion.

 As the master developer of the Kuching Low-Carbon Hub, PETROS is also building an integrated low-carbon industrial ecosystem that brings together upstream development, carbon capture solutions, LNG-to-power, downstream processing, industrial parks, gas terminals and deep-sea port infrastructure.

Sarawak's abundant energy resources, established power generation capacity and growing digital economy ambitions provide a strong foundation for the development of large-scale AI infrastructure.

These investments will support Malaysia's ambition to become a regional AI hub, while creating opportunities for Sarawak to diversify its economy and move towards higher-value industries.
