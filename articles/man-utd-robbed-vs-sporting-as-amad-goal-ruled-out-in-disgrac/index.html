Man Utd robbed vs Sporting as Amad goal ruled out in 'disgraceful decision'

TheTools · 25.11.2026 20:59 · source · permalink

United fans were furious

Manchester United fans were left fuming during the first half of their Champions League match away at Sporting CP. First, referee Szymon Marciniak gave the Portuguese side two penalties, with Senne Lammens saving Luis Suarez's first spot-kick but failing to stop the second. Then right when the Premier League outfit thought they got themselves level, they saw a goal ruled out in confusing circumstances. Amad scored at the second time of asking after his initial effort was pushed onto the woodwork. However, Marciniak had already blown his whistle, ruling Youri Tielemans had fouled Goncalo Inacio in the build-up.

United supporters certainly didn't see things the same way as the Polish official. "Is the referee kidding, what is he doing?" asked one. "That Manchester United goal should stand. There is no foul whatsoever there, Youri Tielemans got the ball. This has to be a joke. What a robbery." Another said: "We just got robbed. No foul and Amad was played onside," while a third called it a "disgraceful decision". To make matters worse, VAR couldn't look at the incident because the whistle was blown before Amad converted the rebound.

"What a joke of a decision," another United fan wrote. "Youri Tielemans clearly got the ball, so where exactly is the foul? That Manchester United goal should have stood. To make matters worse, Bruno [Fernandes] gets shoved hard on the edge of their box and the referee looks the other way."

"That could be one of the worst decisions we have ever seen in the Champions League," was the direct verdict from one fan, while another suggested this was far from a one-off. "We get robbed every week. What did Manchester United do to deserve this robbery? How is that Amad goal not given," they asked.

TNT Sports' studio broadcast team shared United fans' frustrations. “It’s shocking. Shocking from the referee," Karen Carney said.

"The referee blows the whistle before the ball goes in so it can’t be overturned. When you look at this angle, Tielemans wins the ball and doesn’t make any contact.

"The Sporting player makes the most of it because he knows if he rolls around, which he does, he’ll disallow the goal and give the foul which is what happens. I feel that should be 1-1 and I’d be furious if I was Manchester United because that should have been a goal for Amad and it should be 1-1.”

It was not the first time this season United have been on the wrong end of a contentious decision. The red card earned by Leny Yoro in their most recent league game at Liverpool has been overturned, witth Mauricio Pochettino's team successful in their appeal.

There were also complaints over the first penalty awarded to Sporting for the Martinez foul. However, the Argentina international would go on to win a spot-klck himself early in the second half, with Bruno Fernandes converting from the spot and scoring his second of the evening soon after.

United couldn't hold on, though, with a Senne Lammens error allowing Suarez to score his second of the evening. The visitors then had a second goal chalked off in contentious circumstances, with Sporting keeper Kaique Pereira a relieved man after VAR ruled he was fouled in the process of punching into his own net.

"On first viewing I thought his foot must have hit him on the follow through but when you look at it again, Tielemans actually tries to take his foot away. He doesn't touch him because he knows the midfielder is coming towards him.

"He tried to pull his foot out of the way. It is one of the worst decisions I've seen with a referee giving that decision and even VAR not saying to him to go back and review it because it's not a foul."
