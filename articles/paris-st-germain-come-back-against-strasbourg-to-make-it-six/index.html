Paris St-Germain come back against Strasbourg to make it six wins in a row

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:12 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores
  • Tables
  • Line-ups
  • Match Stats
  • Head-to-head
  • Live Text

Dembele assisted a goal, missed a penalty and scored after coming off the bench

Paris St-Germain extended their winning run in all competitions to six games as they came from behind to win at Strasbourg.

Luis Enrique changed eight players of the team that beat Manchester City 4-2 in the Champions League.

And the rotated team fell behind after Giovanni Reyna's deflected shot ended up in the back of the net.

Enrique brought on five of his stars in the second half and they helped turn the game around.

Ousmane Dembele crossed for Illia Zabarnyi to score the leveller - but Ballon d'Or holder Dembele then put a penalty wide.

However, the Frenchman put them ahead with five minutes to go with a low driven effort from 16 yards out.

And Desire Doue added a third in injury time from Ferran Torres' ball.

Having been 13th in Ligue 1 after three games, the victory takes PSG to the top of the table ahead of their rivals' weekend matches.
