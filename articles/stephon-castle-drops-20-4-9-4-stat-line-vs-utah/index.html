    Stephon Castle recorded 20 points (6-of-11 FGs, 8-of-9 FTs), four rebounds, nine assists and four steals in Sunday’s win over the Jazz.

    Castle’s windmill dunk with 47.4 seconds left in the fourth quarter extended the Spurs’ lead to six and essentially ended the game. The third-year guard had an excellent night, shooting high percentages from the field and the foul line and dishing out nine assists. The Spurs didn’t have De’Aaron Fox, which gave Castle and Dylan Harper more room to make plays, and they were both excellent against the Jazz.
