Erling Haaland is fascinated by two PSG players

TheTools · 14.10.2026 19:14 · source · permalink

As he faces Paris Saint-Germain this evening in the Champions League, Erling Haaland hopes to help Manchester City win against the two-time reigning European champions. Which does not prevent the Norwegian striker from appreciating the Rouge et Bleu, their game and especially two of their players.

“They are excellent in the Champions League and Ligue 1. They have been champions for many years and European champions twice in a row, which is not common. They have incredible players and play exceptional football. The whole team is special, especially the full-backs with their constant movement. Nuno Mendes and Achraf Hakimi are truly fascinating to watch. I already played against them, I lost”he declared on the microphone of Canal+.
