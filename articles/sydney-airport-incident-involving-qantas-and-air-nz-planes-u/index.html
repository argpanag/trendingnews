Sydney Airport incident involving Qantas and Air NZ planes under investigation

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:38 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / World

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v4.laWzzQVil69j0DGqQk4Ywh3JMAvc5jXoA1Fn8ldP8DY/world/2809117%2Fsydney-airport-incident-involving-qantas-and-air-nz-planes-under-investigation

The Australian Transport Safety Bureau (ATSB) is investigating an incident at Sydney Airport involving Qantas and Air New Zealand aircraft.

Qantas flight QF426 from Melbourne abandoned its landing approach after crew in the cockpit saw an Air New Zealand aircraft crossing the runway.

The incident happened at 10.15am local time on Wednesday morning and involved a Qantas Boeing 737 and an Air New Zealand Airbus A321.

In a statement, the ATSB said air traffic controllers had advised the Qantas flight crew to expect a late landing clearance due to aircraft crossing the runway.

The aircraft had not been cleared to land.

"As the 737 continued the approach, and prior to receiving a clearance to land, the crew reported seeing an Air New Zealand A321 crossing the runway at taxiway Golf. The crew of the 737 elected to conduct a go-around.

"The evidence collection phase of the investigation will involve interviewing involved flight crew and air traffic controllers, retrieving and reviewing available recorded data such as air traffic control audio, movement guidance and control system recordings and any available CCTV, and the collection of other relevant information including manuals and procedures.

"In particular, the investigation will seek to ascertain what information was provided to the crews of both aircraft, and what mental models those crews held," the ATSB said in a statement.

Qantas has said it will support the ATSB as it conducts its investigation.

"Our pilots were following Air Traffic Control instructions and elected to conduct a go-around as the other aircraft was crossing the runway," a Qantas spokesperson told RNZ.

The aircraft landed safely shortly afterwards.

Earlier this year, a number of incidents occurring at Sydney Airport made headlines, including a near-collision in July that investigators described as one of the most severe runway conflicts on record.
