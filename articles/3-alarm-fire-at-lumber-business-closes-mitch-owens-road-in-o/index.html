3-alarm fire at lumber business closes Mitch Owens Road in Ottawa's south end | CBC News

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

Road closed between Bowesville and Manotick station roads

Mitch Owens Road is closed between Bowesville and Manotick Station roads in Ottawa's rural south end while first responders deal with a major fire at a business, Ottawa police say.

Photos shared online show smoke billowing from a large barn-like structure housing a lumber business.

An Ottawa police spokesperson told CBC the emergency call came from 6178 Mitch Owens Rd., where The WoodSource is located.

An Ottawa Fire Services spokesperson told CBC around 4:30 p.m. that firefighters were taking a defensive approach to the three-alarm blaze, meaning they're no longer attempting to enter the building and are instead trying to suppress the flames to prevent them from spreading.

On Wednesday afternoon a large plume of smoke could be seen from as for away as downtown Ottawa.

Police are asking motorists to avoid the area near the fire and plan alternative routes.
