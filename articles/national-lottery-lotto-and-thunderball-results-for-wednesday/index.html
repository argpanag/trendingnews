National Lottery Lotto and Thunderball results for Wednesday, November 18

TheTools · 18.11.2026 20:53 · source · permalink

There is a £2.8 million jackpot up for grabs in tonight's Lotto. Players can take part in the November 18 game by using two sets of six main numbers and a bonus ball drawn from two separate machines.

Players are automatically placed into two separate rounds in every draw meaning two chances of winning a prize. The Lotto have made 168 millionaires so far this year.

Earlier this year, one lucky player scooped the £5.8 million jackpot in Saturday's National Lottery Lotto draw, with three other ticket-holders each winning £1 million after matching five main numbers and the Bonus Ball.

Meanwhile, a married couple from the Vale of Glamorgan won £1m on the Lotto back in August. The couple in their 60s, said they played the same numbers for decades.

As well as the Lotto, the Thunderball also takes place tonight. The draws take place between 8pm and 8.30pm.
