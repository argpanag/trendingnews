Eagles injury update: Dallas Goedert ruled out vs. Cowboys

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:35 · source · permalink

After Cooper DeJean went down in the first quarter, another Eagle has an injury status early in the game.

ARLINGTON, Texas — Eagles tight end Dallas Goedert has been ruled out against the Cowboys with a shoulder injury.

Goedert was initially listed as questionable to return. He got hurt in the first quarter and then jogged into the tunnel.

Shortly after Goedert went inside, the Eagles gave up their second first-quarter touchdown. So now the Eagles are in the hole two touchdowns and might have to try to mount a comeback without one of their best offensive players.

Without Goedert, the Eagles have Zach Ertz, Johnny Mundt and Eli Stowers available in this game. They entered this game with four tight ends after having five active against the Steelers. Grant Calcaterra is a healthy scratch today.

The Eagles’ first offensive possession was going well until they failed to convert on a 4th-and-1 in Cowboys territory. The Eagles opted to hand the ball off to Saquon Barkley from a fullback position instead of a QB sneak.
