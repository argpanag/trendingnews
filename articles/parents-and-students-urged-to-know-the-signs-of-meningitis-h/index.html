A charity is marking World Meningitis Day today by highlighting the speed at which the disease can become life-threatening.

Early symptoms, including headache, fever, vomiting and muscle pain, can be similar to flu-like symptoms.

Galway-based ACT for Meningitis is urging parents, students and older people to know the symptoms and act quickly if someone becomes unwell.
