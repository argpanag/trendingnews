Evacuate UdeG 227 university students participate in institutional drill

TheTools · 22.09.2026 09:25 · source · permalink

The university network community participated this Friday at noon in the 2026 Institutional Drill. In total, there were 227 people, including students, teaching, operational and administrative staff of la Universidad de Guadalajara (UdeG) in more than 200 buildings; this, in commemoration of the 41st anniversary of the earthquake of September 19, 1985 in Mexico City.

 

The General Coordination of University Services (CGSU) and the University Civil Protection Unit started from the hypothesis of an earthquake with a magnitude of 7.7 degrees with epicenter in the municipality of Tehuacán, Puebla, with considerable damage in 14 federal entities, including Jalisco.

 

In the building of Rectoría General 635 people were evacuated in a record time of 6 minutes and 35 secondsCGSU head Juan Carlos Guerrero Fausto explained, acknowledging the community's participation in the preventative exercise.

 

“From the central building, we recognize the effort of students, teaching staff, administrative staff, directors, and all citizens who were in the university facilities during this exercise,” he stated.

 

Regarding the university network, the estimated evacuation time was 9 minutes, said Guerrero Fausto. “More than 227 people in 110 municipalities across the state achieved this time. We must emphasize that the evacuation was orderly, and evacuation routes and protocols previously rehearsed were used at all times ,” he noted.

 

He also highlighted the organization of the internal brigades , the proper signage of evacuation routes, and the correct assembly at designated points. “Our university Civil Protection network has more than 500 volunteer leaders committed to the safety of our institution. These types of drills can be greatly improved, and what gives us peace of mind is the awareness and commitment of all university members during this period of exercises, which will surely result in saving more lives ,” he said.

 

Guerrero Fausto expressed that, unfortunately, society has had to learn from bitter and complicated experiences, referring to the earthquake of September 19, 1985. “But we also see with hope the responsibility, empathy, and solidarity of our colleagues, especially the volunteers, who are driven by the need to serve their fellow citizens ,” Guerrero Fausto concluded.
