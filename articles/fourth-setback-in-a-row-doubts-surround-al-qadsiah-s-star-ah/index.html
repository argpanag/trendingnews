Fourth setback in a row: doubts surround Al-Qadsiah's star ahead of Al-Hilal | Goal.com

TheTools · 23.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

Nacho sparks concern at Al-Qadsiah

Al-Qadsiah host Al-Hilal on Friday evening in Matchday 11 of the Saudi Roshn League.

The hosts sit top of the standings with 25 points from 10 matches, a single point clear of Al-Hilal, who have played nine fixtures.

According to the newspaper "Arriyadiyah", doubts surround Spain's Nacho ahead of the clash with Al-Hilal, due to injury.

Nacho has already missed Al-Qadsiah's last three matches across all competitions, against Pakhtakor, Abha and Al-Taawoun, after sustaining an ankle joint injury.
