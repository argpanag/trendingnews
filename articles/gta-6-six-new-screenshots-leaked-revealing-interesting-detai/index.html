GTA 6: Six new screenshots leaked – revealing interesting details

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:48 · source · permalink

With just one day to go until the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, new screenshots are circulating on social media. Their exact origin remains unclear, although Rockstar insider and dataminer Tez claims they came from the backend of a review website. Insider Gaming reported the claim, citing a Discord conversation. Six screenshots have surfaced in total, and two are particularly interesting.

One image appears to show Lucia holding a man hostage. Another shows Jason and Lucia entering a store where an armed woman is apparently in the middle of a robbery. On Reddit, fans are speculating that GTA 6 (available for pre-order on Amazon) might allow players to use hostages as human shields or encounter crimes committed by NPCs. In both cases, the question is whether these situations are limited to scripted mission sequences or can also occur dynamically in the open world. Unsurprisingly, many fans are hoping for the latter. The two screenshots can be seen below.

The remaining screenshots leave less room for speculation, but they offer further glimpses into the world of Grand Theft Auto VI, including new hairstyles for the main protagonists and weapons that haven't been shown before. One particularly interesting image is set in a gym, where a bald, muscular man is performing a bicep curl with a 95-pound dumbbell while a woman does pull-ups in the background. Whether the two are actually Jason and Lucia is unclear. Other screenshots show more of Leonida's swamplands, as well as Jason and Lucia relaxing on inflatable rings in the ocean.
