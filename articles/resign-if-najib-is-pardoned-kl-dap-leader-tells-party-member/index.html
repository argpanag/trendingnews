Resign if Najib is pardoned, KL DAP leader tells party members in Cabinet

TheTools · 10.09.2026 05:13 · source · permalink

Ben Fong says it would be 'untenable' for DAP to remain in government if the former prime minister is released.

Ben Fong said it would be “untenable” for DAP to remain in government if Najib is released.

“This is a red line that we cannot cross,” Fong, the Kuala Lumpur DAP treasurer, said in a statement.

It was reported that the Federal Territories Pardons Board (FTPB) is expected to meet tomorrow to consider, among other matters, Najib’s bid for a pardon of his convictions linked to the 1MDB scandal.

Najib, 72, was convicted six years ago of misappropriating RM42 million in SRC International funds. He was fined RM210 million and handed a 12-year jail term which he has been serving at Kajang prison since August 2022.

The FTPB later halved his prison term to six years and reduced the fine to RM50 million.

In December 2025, Najib was convicted of abuse of power and money laundering involving over RM2 billion linked to 1MDB. He was sentenced to 15 years’ jail and fined RM11.38 billion. He is appealing the conviction.

Fong said that leaving the Cabinet did not necessarily mean bringing down the government.

He said DAP could continue supporting the government from outside the Cabinet to preserve political stability and push for institutional reforms.

“But stability cannot become an excuse for accepting everything. Political leaders must recognise that some lines cannot be crossed.”

There are five ministers and seven deputy ministers from DAP in Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim’s administration.
