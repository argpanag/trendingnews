Last game thoughts…

The less said about that Coventry away performance the better. We were slow, demotivated and second best in most ways. And the gaffer did sweet FA to fix it until it was way too late.

The only positive thing is that Regis didnt get his P45 as a result of that, but he will need to put on a master class the next few games to hold on to the grace he has been given.

Tonight is his first chance to show he has what it takes, and that the players are willing and able to play for him. Whether those who play this evening get a sniff at the weekend could be part of his demise, even if we do win this Europa home game.

Hopefully Nordi will be back in the team after his day off (assume that was down to concussion protocols) and with Talbi now in the side in place of Diarra, this is a chance to see some green shoots to be nurtured for the Chelsea game to come very soon.
