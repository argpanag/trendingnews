    England v Spain (19:45 BST)

    So, anything been happening since the international break started?

    Nah didn't think so. The first international break of the year is upon us after what feels like only a couple of weeks of the season.

    England begin their Nations League campaign tonight against world champions Spain and there will likely be a sense of 'what if' on the Wembley terraces after the Three Lions' World Cup campaign ended with defeat by Argentina in the semi-final. Victory there would have seen them take on La Roja in the final.

    But, alas, here we are.

    Three months on and with a new tournament to look forward to.

  3. Trafford set to start for Englandpublished at 17:47 BST

    England v Spain (19:45 BST)

    Sami Mokbel
    BBC Sport Senior football correspondent

    Leeds goalkeeper James Trafford is set to start for England against Spain at Wembley.

    The former Man City stopper has previously started just twice for his country but is expected to be given the nod ahead of regular first choice Jordan Pickford.

    Elsewhere, Bayer Leverkusen's Jarell Quansah is set to start at right back - ahead of Trent Alexander-Arnold - while Arsenal's Myles Lewis-Skelly is also expected to start.

    Spain are the defending European and World champions and go into the Nations League match unbeaten in 38 matches.

    Expected: Trafford; Quansah, Konsa, Guehi, O’Reilly; Anderson, Lewis-Skelly; Saka, Bellingham, Gordon; Kane

England v Spain (19:45 BST)

Sami Mokbel

Leeds goalkeeper James Trafford is set to start for England against Spain at Wembley.

The former Man City stopper has previously started just twice for his country but is expected to be given the nod ahead of regular first choice Jordan Pickford.
