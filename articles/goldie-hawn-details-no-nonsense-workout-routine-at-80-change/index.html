Goldie Hawn opened up about her workout secrets and how she remains super fit at the age of 80. The actress appeared on Khloé Kardashian'sKhloé In Wonder Land podcast to discuss her fitness regime, which blends low-impact exercises, such as Pilates and Yoga, with weight-lifting. 

"What is your favourite type of body movement or workout?" Khloé asked the star. "Oh god, there's so many. But I like Pilates, I think Pilates is great and I started as a ballet dancer and did Pilates before hardly anyone knew about it," replied Goldie. 

"It was created by a dancer for injuries. I remember the first time I was on a reformer and they didn't look like they looked today. But I loved the idea that I was able to, you know, the pulleys, and you'd be able to get your body, pull long on this thing. For a dancer, there's no explaining how fantastic that felt. That was one of my favorite ways, the isolation. That was one style."

Goldie Hawn appeared on the Khloé In Wonder Land podcast (@ YouTube)

Goldie continued: "Yoga, I was never madly in love with yoga. You have to be careful, my back wouldn't take an inversion. You have to know how much to do. Yoga is good for stretching. And then I walk."

Khloé then asked the 80-year-old if she enjoys lifting weights. "I lift weights. I've been lifting weights for 50 years," replied Goldie. "I think weight lifting is critical for women," said Khloé.

Goldie Hawn opened up about her workout secrets (@ via Getty Images)

"It is critical. What it does for your bones, really strengthens your bones and it builds muscle. And it changed my body completely as I had very slopey shoulders and I remember when I started when I was very young, and it changed my body. So great," explained the actress. "Then I do plank every day for a minute and a half. And then I do my weights every other day."

How does Goldie Hawn's workout routine compare to her daughter Kate Hudson's?

Kate Hudson's toned physique is hardly surprising given her disciplined fitness routine. "I need to be moving to feel good," she told People. "Even just making time for a little movement every day – going for a walk, doing a few minutes on the treadmill, some Pilates, or if I have more time for it, I love hot yoga." 

Goldie Hawn and her daughter, Kate Hudson, follow similar workouts (@ Getty Images)

She continued: "I really enjoy sweating it out, and it helps me clear my mind. It's not just about trying to look good physically, it's important to get oxygen to my brain and feel like my blood is really circulating. I love skiing, walking, hiking and especially riding my bike. It makes me feel like a kid again!." 

Kate explained that dieting "puts so much pressure on people to lose weight quickly". "Getting healthy is not a two-week process, it's a change of life-style," she shared. The star trains with Brian Nguyenm with the fitness routine focused on strength exercises. "One day, I tuned in and I was like ... I don't feel strong, I feel long and I love my Pilates but as I get older... am I doing enough (strength) exercises?," she told TODAY.
