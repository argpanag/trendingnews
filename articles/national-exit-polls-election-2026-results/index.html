National Exit Polls: Election 2026 Results

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

NBC News, with a group of other news organizations, conducted exit polling across the country in 45 key states. The poll includes in-person interviews on Election Day, and it combines the in-person poll with other methods–mail, phone and online–to account for in-person early and mail-in voters. The exit polls are not vote results, but instead a look at the thinking and motives of voters across the country. Exit polls help the public better understand the makeup and motivations of the electorate.
