THE 2026 Toyota AFL Grand Final is just one sleep away.

Fremantle and Brisbane will meet at the MCG on Saturday in what promises to be an incredible finale to the season, as the Dockers chase their first premiership and the Lions hunt their third successive flag.

Then, the Fremantle and Brisbane players will be on show on Friday in the traditional Grand Final parade as final preparations are put in place, finishing at Yarra Park outside the MCG where they will be presented to fans along with the premiership cup.
