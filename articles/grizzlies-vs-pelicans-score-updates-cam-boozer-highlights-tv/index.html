Grizzlies vs Pelicans score updates, Cam Boozer highlights, TV channel

TheTools · 02.11.2026 22:01 · source · permalink

The Memphis Grizzlies are going for a franchise record on Nov. 2 against the New Orleans Pelicans (7 p.m. CT, DAZN-MEM).

Memphis (6-0) is the only unbeaten team remaining in the NBA. The start is tied for best in franchise history with the 2014-15 Grizzlies.

The Pelicans (2-4) aren't off to a strong start, but they're playing better than their record indicates. New Orleans is 5-1 against the spread this season and has won its past two games.

Grizzlies guard Ty Jerome is back in the lineup after missing the previous game due to upper back soreness. Guard Scotty Pippen Jr. (lower back soreness) is doubtful to play, and center Quinten Post (right ankle sprain) is listed as questionable.

The Pelicans have a 46-44 advantage in all-time meetings.
