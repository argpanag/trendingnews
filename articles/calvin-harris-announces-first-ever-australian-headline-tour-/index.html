Global electronic powerhouse, GRAMMY Award winner, and multi-platinum record-breaking DJ and producer, Calvin Harris, announces his first ever Australian headline tour. Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 will light up major venues across the country next February!

Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 Dates

  • February 18, 2027, – Langley Park, Perth WA
  • February 19, 2027, – RNA Showgrounds, Brisbane QLD
  • February 20, 2027, – The Domain, Sydney NSW
  • February 26, 2027, – Flemington Racecourse, Melbourne VIC

When do tickets go on sale?

Telstra Plus Presale: 11am on Monday, 21 September

Artist Presale: 12pm on Wednesday, 23 September

General Onsale: 1pm on Thursday, 24 September

*local time

Who is eligible for each presale?

Telstra Plus Presale: Only active Telstra customers will be able to access this presale. Head to telstra.com.au/plus for more details. 

Artist Presale: All fans who sign up for first access tickets via Untitled Group Co:Brand before Thursday 24 September, 11:00am* will be eligible to access the Artist Presale.

*local time for the venue you’re wanting to purchase for.

Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 Ticket Tips

What is the ticket limit for Calvin Harris?

You may purchase a maximum of 4 tickets per transaction during the Telstra Plus Presale and 6 tickets per transaction during the Artist Presale and General Onsale.

How can I purchase accessible tickets for Calvin Harris?

Tickets can be purchased through the Ticketmaster Website, or by submitting a request through the Ticketmaster Help Centre following these steps:

  1. From the dropdown menu, select Accessible Bookings as the topic.
  2. Then select Calvin Harris as the event name.

Alternatively, you can call our Accessible Booking Line on 1300 446 925, available Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am – 5:00 pm AEDT. Submitting a request doesn’t guarantee a ticket, availability and type of accessible tickets will vary by venue. A Ticketmaster representative will be in touch if further information is required. 

For more information, visit our help page Accessible Tickets: Everything you need to know.

How will I get my tickets for Calvin Harris?

Tickets for these shows will be mobile-only. Your phone is your ticket. Once tickets are ready to download, you can do so by logging into your Ticketmaster account on the Ticketmaster app.

Find out more about Ticket Transfer – including how to accept and open a transferred ticket here.

 

What are the age restrictions for Calvin Harris?

This tour will be 18+. Valid I.D will be required for entry. 

Hints and tips for improving your chances of getting Calvin Harris tickets

Check Your Account

Create or sign in to your account and check that your email, phone and payment details are up to date.

Keep Your Phone Close 

You will be asked to enter a sign-in verification code, which will be sent by text. To avoid any issues accessing the queue, please check your account now and verify that your mobile phone number is correct.

Prepare for Checkout

Notify your bank for any required purchase authorisations or to check spending limits.

Stick To One Device & One Tab Only

Join the queue from one browser on one device. Additional attempts to join could kick you out of the queue. Accessing the sale from multiple tabs may also cause you to lose your place in line.

Remember: there are a lot of fans

We’re expecting this to be a very popular event. When a popular event goes on sale there are hundreds, and sometimes thousands, of customers all attempting to purchase tickets at the same time. Tickets are sold as long as seats are available, and when an act is this popular, sometimes they go very quickly.

Be sure to follow the above tips to ensure you have the best chance of snapping up some tickets.
