Women: Matchday news #B04FCB | Bayer04.de

TheTools · 14.09.2026 16:55 · source · permalink

A range of interactive activities and games will be available for all fans at the match. Among other things, the Bayer 04 Cube will be set up. Stadium visitors can look forward to a wheel of fortune where the top prize is a jersey signed by the women’s team. The winner will receive the jersey from a player after the match.

There will also be a photo booth in the piazza featuring images of the players, football goals and a bouncy castle. At half-time, Bayer 04 will be hosting an exclusive members’ challenge for the first time as part of a women’s matchday. Two fans, accompanied by the mascot Brian the Lion, will compete in football bowling on the stadium pitch.

As part of the DFB’s ‘Gemeinsam Gutes Spiel’ campaign matchday, Bayer 04 are inviting interested fans to a sustainability stadium tour. The 90-minute tour of the BayArena starts today, Monday, at 16:00 CEST and is free of charge. HERE is the link to register. There will also be an information stand run by the Bayer 04 sustainability team on the stadium concourse. Before kick-off, both teams will present a banner for the campaign as a joint gesture of support for sustainability. During the half-time break, fans will then have the opportunity to find out about the new car-share platform, WERKSELFgo.

In the run-up to the match, Bayer 04 are conducting a survey on fan travel. This voluntary survey takes the form of an online questionnaire which can be completed at the stadium. QR codes for the survey will be displayed on posters around the piazza. The survey is anonymous. Once the survey has concluded, a home jersey will be raffled off amongst all participants who voluntarily provide an email address.

Fan travel on matchdays is the biggest source of emissions for professional football clubs. The previous 2025 travel survey has already provided valuable information and is now to be updated so that Bayer 04 can continue to be actively involved in travel issues, based on the latest data. This information is of great importance for managing and optimising environmental efforts in the context of travel. Thank you very much for taking part.

As has been the case on every home matchday since the start of this season, the Ulrich Haberland Stadium will open 90 minutes before kick-off, i.e. at 16:30 CEST. This time, entry will be via the north-west entrance (Stelzen side) and the south entrance. In standing area S (South Stand), tickets are also available for home supporters in a separate block. From 16:30 CEST, the away ticket office will be open alongside the Service WEST ticket office. Tickets are available in advance HERE. As usual, Kiosk 9 will be offering a range of hot food and drinks. Payment can be made using Girocard, Mastercard, Visa, Maestro, V-Pay, Google Pay and Apple Pay, as well as using prepaid cards.

Information on the clash with the German champions can be found in the latest issue of Werkself Live. The matchday magazine, which provides a concise summary of the key facts and figures about the match, is available at the UHS. Werkself Live is also available free online HERE.

Bayer 04 earned a 2-2 draw at FC Augsburg on Bundesliga Matchday 3 with a late equaliser. The Werkself led at half-time through Christian Kofane's strike but the hosts, who went in the game as league leaders, turned it around with a quickfire double from Michael Gregoritsch after the break. The Black-and-Reds didn't give up, though, and salvaged a point thanks to substitute Patrik Schick's flick in the 89th minute.

After the first win of the season against 1. FC Union Berlin (4-0) last weekend, the Werkself are aiming to collect another three points at FC Augsburg on Saturday 12 September (kick-off 15:30 CEST). The hosts, meanwhile, are enjoying their best-ever start to a Bundesliga campaign and go into Matchday 3 top of the table for the first time. The Matchday News.
