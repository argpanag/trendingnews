LISA Drops 'SaWaDiKa' Video: Read Lyrics & Translation of Song Title!

TheTools · 04.09.2026 00:20 · source · permalink

LISA is back with new solo music and her track “SaWaDiKa” is bound to be a hit!

The song is the first single off the 29-year-old singer’s upcoming PRESS PLAY EP, which is set to be released on October 23.

Lisa, whose real name is Lalisa Manobal, is one-fourth of the K-Pop group BLACKPINK and she’s also an actress who appeared in season three of HBO’s The White Lotus. She’ll next appear in the action film Tygo. In November, she will take the stage in Las Vegas for four shows as part of a residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace.

What does “SaWaDiKa” mean in Thai?

In Thai, “SaWaDiKa” is normally written out as “sawatdee ka” and it translates to “hello.” Simple!

LISA‘s label says that the new song sees her “returning to her roots with her rap prowess and core hip-hop sound.”

The music video was shot across Bangkok, Thailand, featuring her in iconic locations across the city.

LISA sings in the chorus, “Hello, sawatdee ka / Yeah, you know my wrist is cold / Got this timepiece from Geneva / If you know it, then you know / I’m that bitch on your TV / In my Ferrari, indigo / La Lalisa, la diva / Sawatdee ka, there she go.”
