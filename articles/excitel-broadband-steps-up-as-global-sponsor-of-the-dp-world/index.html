Excitel Broadband steps up as Global Sponsor of the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:25 · source · permalink

Gurugram, 19 November 2026 – Excitel Broadband, India’s most exciting home broadband player, today announced that it is the Global Sponsor of the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026. The Asian Cricket Council tournament opens in Doha, Qatar, today, with eight teams from across Asia playing 15 matches before the final on Sunday, 29 November.

It is Excitel’s second Asian Cricket Council tournament this year. In September, the brand had an on-ground presence at the DP World Women’s Asia Cup 2026 in Dubai, where India beat Sri Lanka by 72 runs in the final to win a record eighth title. In Doha, Excitel steps up to Global Sponsor, backing the young players expected to shape the next chapter of Asian cricket.

For fans watching at home in India, Excitel will be part of the picture all tournament. The brand appears behind the wicket on the pitch mat, along the perimeter boards around the ground and on the backdrop at post-match presentations, press conferences and player interviews. Every match is live on Sony Sports Network and Sony LIV.

Commenting on the sponsorship, Aditya Jain, Chief Marketing Officer, Excitel Broadband, said, “We are grateful to the Asian Cricket Council for welcoming Excitel back, this time as Global Sponsor. Cricket is where Indian fans are at their most passionate, and few platforms let a brand share in that emotion at this scale. We look forward to being a part of this exciting tournament over the next eleven days.”

Varun Pasricha, CEO, Excitel Broadband, added, “Excitel started out as a young challenger with big ambitions, so a tournament built around rising talent feels very close to who we are. These players have worked for years to earn their place on a bigger stage, and we are glad to support them at such an important point in their journey. Cricket has always brought Indian homes together, and we are proud to be part of that as we stand with the next generation of Asian cricket.”

Haider Raza, Head Of Media, Communications and Operations, ACC said, “We are pleased to welcome Excitel on board as a Global Sponsor for the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars. The players at this tournament reflect how cricket is played today, and Excitel’s energy and ambition align well with the qualities that define modern cricket – intent, speed, and a confident mindset. We look forward to a successful tournament together.”

The Asia Cup Rising Stars adds to Excitel’s growing presence across the sports calendar. In October 2026, the company became the Official Broadband Partner of Sporting Club Delhi in the ISL, and in 2024 it partnered JioCinema on “Speed Ka Man of the Match” during the IPL.
