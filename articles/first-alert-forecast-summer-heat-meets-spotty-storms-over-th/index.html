FIRST ALERT FORECAST: Summer heat meets spotty storms over the weekend

TheTools · 17.09.2026 12:17 · source · permalink

HAZARD, Ky. (WYMT) - Summer heat and humidity are still holding strong across Central and Eastern Kentucky, but storm chances will mix into the forecast through the weekend. Most areas will still see plenty of dry time and sunshine, though a passing shower or thunderstorm could pop up at times.

Today will be hot and mostly sunny, with highs in the low to mid 90s. It could feel closer to the triple digits because of the humidity, while isolated and quick-moving showers or storms remain possible during the afternoon and evening.

Tonight will stay warm and muggy, with lows only dropping into the lower 70s. Humidity is not going anywhere just yet, and a stronger cold front that could bring a more noticeable break is still not expected anytime soon.

Tomorrow will bring more clouds, helping keep high temperatures in the mid to upper 80s. Storm chances will be a little higher than today, so it is a good idea to keep an eye on the sky if you have outdoor plans.

The weekend will stay warm, with highs in the mid to upper 80s. Showers may develop Saturday night and continue into Sunday, with partly cloudy skies expected during the breaks between showers.
