Celebrity Traitors' Joanne McNally is out as one murdered and one banished

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:04 · source · permalink

Irish comedian Joanne McNally has been banished while actress Sharon Rooney has become the latest celebrity "murdered" in a dramatic penultimate episode of The Celebrity Traitors.

The departures followed an intense roundtable confrontation where both players faced suspicion. McNally was ultimately eliminated after fellow contestant King Kenny exercised the deciding vote, utilising a "double dagger" advantage that granted him two votes to seal her banishment.

Following McNally's exit, the secret Traitors—content creator King Kenny, television host Maya Jama, and comedian James Acaster—selected Rooney as their overnight murder victim in a targeted move to sow confusion among the Faithfuls.

Reacting to her elimination from the BBC reality series, Rooney immediately pointed suspicion toward one of the culprits, stating: "I think this was Maya Jama, I think that is her love heart and her kisses."

After her fate was decided, McNally said: “I’ve had such an amazing time with all of you, it’s been one of the best experiences of my adult life, I actually feel emotional…

“It has been amazing, thanks so much for all making it what it was, I’m premenstrual.

“I’ve had such a great time, I am a hardcore faithful.”

During Thursday’s show, Jama attracted heat when she toasted her fellow contestants by saying: “To killing them, banishing them even.”

For the mission, the celebrities were tasked with matching the severed ‘heads’ of celebrities who had left the show with their buried bodies, with teams splitting into one in the lab, where the heads were, and one in the garden where the bodies were.

Those in the lab had to relay screams to their contestants in the garden in order to match the head and bodies.

The Celebrity Traitors continues on BBC One.
