Today, developer RedOctane Games dropped their 14th Setlist reveal for their up-and-coming instrumental rhythm title ‘Stage Tour’, coming to PC via Steam, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PlayStation 5 consoles in December.

Now part of a Setlist of 65(!!!) songs (we’re heading for 90!), here’s three familiar tracks that you might just love to play to:

  • “Lola Montez” – Volbeat –  pairing driving guitars with a chorus built for singing along, this one is perfect for getting the whole band involved! 
  • “Negative Space” – Silverstein – this Canadian band has spent more than two decades making their mark on post-hardcore, and bring their 2025 offering from the Pink Moon album to the setlist.
  • “The Heart From Your Hate” – Trivium – want heavy riffs and a huge, melodic chorus? Trivium’s track has got your back. This one also houses a personal memory for one of the Stage Tour developers – find out more in the Stage Tour blog!

You can learn more over on the game’s official blogpost about this drop.
