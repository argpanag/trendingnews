Thun host Hajduk Split at Arena Thun in a UEFA Conference League fixture that pits a struggling Swiss side against a Croatian outfit riding a wave of confidence.

Hajduk arrive in Switzerland off the back of a statement result. Gonzalo Garcia's side beat Ajax 2-1 in their last Conference League outing, a win that announced them as genuine contenders at this level and lifted them into the upper half of the competition standings.

Thun's situation is more precarious. Gian-Luca Privitelli's squad have lost three of their last five competitive matches, including a 4-2 defeat to FC Zuerich in the Swiss Super League, and their Conference League campaign has already suffered a setback following a 1-0 loss to FC Twente.

The contrast in momentum between the two sides is stark. Hajduk have won four of their last five matches across all competitions, while Thun head into this fixture with serious questions about their defensive resilience after conceding eleven goals in five games.

For Hajduk, this is an opportunity to build on the Ajax result and cement their standing in the competition. For Thun, it is a chance to arrest a run of form that threatens to derail their European ambitions entirely.
