Grand Theft Auto VI: Here Are All The Songs We Heard In Rockstar's In-Game Radio Preview

TheTools · 09.10.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, and ahead of its release, Rockstar Games has created a webpage where you can preview six in-game radio stations. The stations feature Latin music, southern hip hop, country, metal, AfroBeats, and pop, and I just finished listening to each preview. 

While listening, I used my phone's Shazam feature to create a list of all the songs I heard in the previews. Below, I'll list each station and the songs I caught. 

Cocotea FM

  • EoO - Bad Bunny
  • Si Es Trucho Es Trucho (Remix) - Axel Rulay featuring Farruko & El Afla
  • Canalla - Romeo Santos & El Chaval de la Bachata

Back Country Radio

  • Need a Little Time Off for Bad Behavior - David Allan Coe
  • Don’t Come Home a Drinkin’ - Loretta Lyn
  • The Conversation - Hank Williams, Jr & Waylon Jennings

AfroBank FM

  • Joha - Asake
  • Gye Wani - Pat Thomas featuring Ebo Taylor
  • Shay’sana - DJ Lag & Robot Boii featuring Kamo Mphela

The Chamber 106.6

  • I - Black Sabbath
  • I’m Broken - Pantera
  • Raining Blood - Slayer

Flash FM

  • Good Ones - Charli XCX
  • Electric Feel - MGMT
  • Midnight Sun (Girls Trip) - Zara Larsson & PinkPantheress

Dirty South Classics

  • Like a Pimp Mix - JefeEdai
  • Who Dat - JT Money
  • Born N Raided - DJ Khaled (featuring Pitbull, Trick Daddy, & Rick Ross) 

And those are all the songs I heard on these stations.
