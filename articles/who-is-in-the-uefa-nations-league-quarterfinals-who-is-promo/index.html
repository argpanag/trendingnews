The group stage of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League is complete. We can now flip the page to the quarterfinals for League A, as well as the promotion/relegation playoffs. Here's what you need to know. 

What Is The UEFA Nations League?

The UEFA Nations League was created to provide more competitive matches for European national teams outside of major tournaments such as the World Cup and the European Championships. Those tournaments are held every four years (the next Euros is in 2028 and the next World Cup is in 2030), so the Nations League rounds out the calendar. 

The Nations League format is similar to what we see at the club level across Europe, where teams can be promoted or relegated to higher tiers of competition. 

UEFA's 55 eligible teams (Russia remains suspended from international competition) are divided into four leagues. There are 16 teams in groups of four teams for Leagues A, B, and C, with six teams in two groups of three in League D. League A is the highest group and features the historically elite teams such as England, France, Italy, Germany, Spain, and Portugal.

When Is The 2026-27 UEFA Nations League Quarterfinals? 

In League A, the top two teams from each group advance to the quarterfinals, which will be held across two legs on March 25 and 30, 2027. The four winners of those catchups advance to the semifinals. The losers remain in League A for the 2028-29 edition. 

When Are The 2026-27 UEFA Nations League Semifinals And Final?

The four winners then contest the semifinals on June 9, 2027, with the final on June 13, 2027. The host nation for those games has yet to be determined. 

Who Was Promoted Or Relegated In The 2026-27 UEFA Nations League? 

Promotion: The four group winners in League B automatically get promoted to League A for the 2028-29 edition. Similarly, the four group winners of League C move up to League B. All six teams in League D will automatically join League C due to upcoming format changes for the 2028-29 edition. 

Relegation: The two lowest-ranked fourth-placed teams in League A will be automatically relegated to League B. The other teams in those leagues will head to the promotion/relegation playoffs in March 2027. 

These will involve the four runners-up from League B against the two lowest-ranked third-placed teams and the two highest-ranked fourth-placed teams from League A. The winners will play in League A in 2028-29, with the defeated teams going in League B.

The four bottom-placed teams in League B will play the four runners-up from League C. The winners remain or join League B, while the defeated are slotted into League C. There will be no Group D in 2028-29. 

Does The Nations League Affect Qualifying Euro 2028?

Yes, because Nations League rankings will determine seeding for the 2028 Euro draw. That tournament will feature 24 teams and will be hosted in England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland. Sixteen of those teams will book spots through Euro 2028 qualifying, while the other eight will be determined by a playoff. Any defending Nations League group winner that didn't book a direct Euro 2028 spot will get a playoff spot. So it helps to do well in the Nations League. 

Who Has Previously Won The UEFA Nations League?

Portugal has won twice (2019, 2025) while France (2023) and Spain (2021) have one title each. Spain has two runner-up finishes (2021, 2025) with the Netherlands (2019) and Croatia (2023) each with a second-place finish. 

Who Are The UEFA Nations League All-Time Goalscorers?

Norway superstar striker Erling Haaland reigns supreme as the tournament's top goalscorer, with the likes of England's Harry Kane, France's Kylian Mbappé and Portugal's recently retired Cristiano Ronaldo also on the table.
