    Stephen Curry moves into 16th on NBA scoring list

    Stephen Curry

    GSW Guard #30
    Player Stats

    Stephen Curry tallied 25 points (6-of-10 FGs, 9-of-9 FTs), five rebounds, two assists and four three-pointers in Monday’s win over the Pelicans.

    Curry only played 21 minutes in Monday’s blowout victory, none in the fourth quarter. That’s all the time he needed to notch another career milestone, as the Warriors guard moved past DeMar DeRozan into 16th place on the NBA’s all-time scoring list. Thus far, Curry’s production has exceeded his preseason ADP by a safe distance, as he’s been a top-5 player in nine-cat formats. The Warriors end their week with games against the Rockets and Spurs, two teams that should offer up greater resistance than the Pelicans did.
    - Raphielle Johnson
