DeeJay Dallas: Reverts to Minnesota's practice squad

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:53 · source · permalink

Dallas reverted to the Vikings' practice squad Monday, per the NFL's transaction log.

Dallas played just six offensive snaps in Sunday's win over the Packers. The running back made a larger impact on special teams, logging one tackle on kick coverage over 14 special-teams snaps. Dallas can only be elevated two more times, and after that point he may only play for the Vikings if he is signed to the active roster.
