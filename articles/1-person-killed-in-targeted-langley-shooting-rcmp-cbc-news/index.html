1 person killed in 'targeted' Langley shooting: RCMP | CBC News

TheTools · 07.10.2026 07:01 · source · permalink

Police responded to shots fired call at around 9:45 p.m. PT on Monday

Police say one person was killed in a shooting in Langley, B.C., on Monday night.

Officers responded to a call of shots fired outside a "busy restaurant" in the 6000-block of 200 Street at around 9:45 p.m. PT, said Sgt. Freda Fong with the Integrated Homicide Investigation Team (IHIT).

First responders found a 23-year-old man in medical distress, who died of his injuries at the scene.

"We are very fortunate that nobody was injured inside the restaurant, it's a stark reminder of when there's gun violence in the community it could affect anybody — you and I could have been in the restaurant at that time," Fong said.

"There were multiple vehicles in the lot that did get hit by stray bullets."

Just after 10 p.m. PT, officers were then called to a vehicle fire near 232 Street and 75 Avenue.

Homicide investigators discovered a burnt sedan, which they believe was used in the shooting.

Police are investigating if there is any connection between a vehicle fire and a fatal shooting that occurred in Langley on Monday night. (Curtis Kreklau)

IHIT says the motive in the shooting is still under investigation, but that the attack is believed to have been targeted.

"[The victim] does have history for drug charges in Manitoba," Fong said.

"Within B.C. he's had interactions with police for gang and weapons associations, however he did not have a criminal record."

Anyone who witnessed anything suspicious, or who has video shot in the area between 8:30 p.m. and 10:30 p.m. on Oct. 5, is asked to contact IHIT.
