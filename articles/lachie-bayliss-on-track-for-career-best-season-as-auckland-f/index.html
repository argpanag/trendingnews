Lachie Bayliss on track for career-best season as Auckland FC chase turnaround

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v4.laWzzQVil69j0DGqQk4Ywh3JMAvc5jXoA1Fn8ldP8DY/sport/2772361%2Flachie-bayliss-on-track-for-career-best-season-as-auckland-fc-chase-turnaround

Auckland FC's newest goal-scoring threat, Lachie Bayliss, is on target for a career best season in the A-League despite the competition only being five rounds old.

Bayliss has scored three goals in five games for his new club, to be sitting only two goals behind his tally from last season with the Newcastle Jets.

The All White made the move to Auckland to switch A-League clubs before coach Pedro Moreira was signed but said he made the right move.

"A big reason why I wanted to come to Auckland and play in this team is because I thought I'd fit in well.

"It's nice to score a few goals off the back of that but it's still early days in the season and we've got a lot of footy left to play."

Bayliss is in joint second in the golden boot standings at the early part of the season, one goal behind Sydney FC's Al Hassan Toure.

He is one of four scorers for Auckland so far this season.

The 24-year-old said he had set himself a goal-scoring target for the season but did not want to make the magic number public.

"I only sort of played the end of the last season so I sort of knew if I had a whole season under my belt I could definitely produce a lot more."

In 27 games with the Jets in the 2025-26 season Bayliss scored five goals for the team that finished at the top of the table after the regular season.

That stands as his best return since joining the A-League in the 2023-24 season.

Bayliss also scored his first international goal against India for the All Whites this month and jokes he wishes that could be added to his A-League season tally.

The style of football Moreira has Auckland playing could be contributing to Bayliss' hot scoring form.

"I think for me it suits me greatly because I play in the pocket and I want to get the ball in the half-turn and play forward, I mean, it's perfect for me."

Pressure and passion

Pressure and passion is how Moreira sums up the situation the team is in ahead of an away game against Central Coast Mariners on Saturday.

Auckland are in an unfamiliar place of eighth on the A-League ladder with one win, one loss and three draws.

Bayliss scored late, in the 89th minute, to secure a draw against Melbourne Victory last round to keep Auckland's unbeaten home record going this season.

But Moreira acknowledged he felt the pressure of being in the bottom half of the competition.

He said everything from the messages he verbally passed on to his players to his facial expressions could influence how his playing group responded, so he wanted to get it right.

Moreira said he could not "control" where Auckland were currently on the competition ladder but he said they were working hard on the training pitch to get into a position they "deserved" to be.

He is definitely seeking three points this round against the Mariners who are in 10th.

"We need to put the ball on the net to solve this and not suffer goals easily, it's very important.

"Probably we were close to winning, [against Victory] but we were also close to losing the game. So it's really something we can improve, especially on the last third of the pitch.

"The chances that we create, the last pass, the precision of the cross, the quality of the delivery, that's a detail that we really must improve on the next game."

Moreira often references the "fight" of his team as they battle to improve this season. An actual skirmish broke out against Victory and the coach saw it as two teams showing how much a result matters.

"It's the passion of the game."
