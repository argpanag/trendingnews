Jay-Z at the Stade de France this Thursday: how to get there by public transport

TheTools · 10.09.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

Which songs is Jay-Z planning to perform this Thursday night at his one-and-only show at the Stade de France ? What staging can fans expect from the American rapper this week ? Will any high-profile guests join the performer for the occasion? For now, the suspense remains intact. But Jay-Z is determined to light up the vast Stade de France in Seine-Saint-Denis this Thursday, September 10, 2026, just days after his two dates at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London. The rapper, behind countless hits, will use the moment to toast three decades of making music, and to celebrate the 25th anniversary of his album “The Blueprint,” acclaimed by both critics and fans.

Jay-Z to perform at the Stade de France in September 2026
Eight years after lighting up the Stade de France alongside Beyoncé, Jay-Z is back in Paris. This time, the American rapper is set to celebrate three decades in the business. He invites fans to a one-night-only concert on Thursday, September 10, 2026 — a show not to be missed at the iconic Stade de France in Seine-Saint-Denis. [Read more]

Due to the limited number of European dates announced by Jay-Z, the rapper's fans are expected to be out in force in the capital this week. And many of them plan to reach the Stade de France by public transport this Thursday, September 10.

As noted by the RATP, Different modes of public transport can get you to the Stade de France, namely the RER B and D, the metro lines 12, 13 and 14, or the Transilien Line H.

Heads up: note that traffic is suspended on weekdays from 11:35 p.m. on the RER D between Paris Gare du Nord and Creil. "A replacement bus service is being laid on, with service to intermediate stations," says Transilien SNCF. After the concert, the 14 line is therefore to be preferred.
