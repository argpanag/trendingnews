RFP ISSUED FOR MARKETING AND PR SERVICES | ICC

TheTools · 21.10.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Media Release

The ICC is inviting proposals from entities interested in providing Marketing and PR services for the inaugural ICC Women’s Champions Trophy 2027, which will be held in Mumbai and Vadodara, India, from 14 to 28 February. 

 The Request for Proposals (RFP) invites proposals from agencies and other qualified entities with experienced teams and a proven track record of delivering Marketing and PR campaigns at major sporting events.  

The successful Applicant will be responsible for developing and delivering and integrated marketing and PR campaign build awareness, drive audience engagement and generate excitement for the inaugural tournament, bringing together six of the world’s leading women’s teams. 

The full RFP with an in-depth description of the ICC’s requirements and expectations are available on the page here. 

Proposals should be submitted to MarketingRFP@icc-cricket.com. 

The deadline for submission of proposals is 1700hrs GST, 4 November 2026
