JTBC launches sale, to pick preferred buyer in January

TheTools · 09.11.2026 01:25 · source · permalink

JTBC, which is undergoing corporate rehabilitation, has formally launched an open, competitive tender to sell its management control. It will accept letters of intent through next month, then conduct preliminary due diligence and a main bid before selecting a preferred buyer candidate in January next year.

EY Hanyoung Accounting Corporation, JTBC's sale manager, said in a sale notice on the 9th that it will run the process as an open, competitive bid "by attracting external capital through a third-party allotment paid-in capital increase and bond issuance."

This deal is closer to injecting new funds into a company under rehabilitation than a typical transfer of existing shareholders' stakes to sell control. The buyer would inject new capital into JTBC to obtain management control.

Letters of intent (LOIs) are due by the 4th of next month. The seller plans to conduct preliminary due diligence from Dec. 7 to Jan. 8 next year for candidates that submit LOIs. The deadline to submit final acquisition proposals is Jan. 15 next year.

JTBC plans to select a preferred negotiating partner in January next year through the competitive bidding process. Candidates' financing capacity and plans for operating the business are expected to be key factors.

A JTBC official said, "We will conduct the sale transparently and fairly in accordance with relevant laws and the rehabilitation court's procedures," adding, "Through the M&A, we will build a foundation for sustainable growth and create an opportunity to strengthen our content and channel competitiveness."
