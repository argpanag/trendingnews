How to secure tickets to A$AP Rocky's Australian tour - RUSSH

TheTools · 12.10.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

Culture / Music

It's been eight years since A$AP Rocky blessed Australian stages with his presence, but in December this year, that drought will end. The rapper has officially announced Australian dates for his Don’t Be Dumb tour, with shows scheduled for Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth.

Tickets will be available through a series of presales from Wednesday October 14, ahead of the general onsale on Friday October 16. Here’s everything you need to know to get tickets in your hot little hands.

 

A$AP Rocky’s 2026 Australian tour dates and cities

The Don’t Be Dumb World Tour will visit four Australian cities in December.

  • Melbourne: Saturday, December 5 at Rod Laver Arena
  • Sydney: Wednesday, December 9 at Afterpay Arena
  • Brisbane: Monday, December 14 at Brisbane Entertainment Centre
  • Perth: Thursday, December 17 at RAC Arena

 

How to get pre-sale tickets to A$AP Rocky’s Australian tour

If you’re hoping to get in early for tickets (and we suggest you do), there are several presale options to know about.

  • The Mastercard presale opens on Wednesday October 14 at 12pm local time and closes Friday, October 16, at 12pm. Mastercard cardholders can also access Preferred tickets from Friday, October 16, at 1pm, subject to availability. You can find more information on all things Mastercard presale here.
  • The Vodafone presale begins on Wednesday October 14 at 12pm local time and runs for 48 hours. This is available only to eligible Vodafone mobile customers. You can find out more at Vodafone.
  • The Live Nation presale kicks off on Thursday October 15 at 12pm local time and closes on Friday October 16 at 12pm. You’ll need to register with Live Nation to access thid presale.
  • The Westfield members presale opens on Thursday October 15 at 12pm local time, and will give Westfield members exclusive access to reserved tickets. You can sign up to become a member through the Westfield app.

 

When do general tickets go on sale?

Of course, if your presale efforts fail, there's always the general ticket sales. General onsale begins on Friday October 16 at 1pm local time via A$AP Rocky’s official website.
