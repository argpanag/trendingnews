Coach Bev Priestman calls for ruthless Phoenix attack against Perth Glory - Friends of Football

TheTools · 31.10.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Wellington Phoenix head coach Bev Priestman wants her side to be more ruthless in front of goal as they chase their first win of the women’s A-League season.

The Phoenix have travelled to Western Australia to face unbeaten Perth Glory on Sunday, with the match kicking off just before midnight (NZT).

Priestman believes her players can exploit weaknesses in a Glory defence that has yet to concede a goal this season.

“They’re undefeated,” Priestman told media via Zoom from Perth.

“You always get an aggressiveness from them.

“I see some really good opportunities and we’ve been working on those but I don’t take them for granted.

“They seem to come alive at the death and you’ve got to be switched on to some of their players.”

Priestman says the Phoenix have concentrated on improving the small details that can make the difference between winning and losing.

“I do think although they haven’t conceded, there has been opportunity to concede, and that’s for us to unpick and get after.

“This week it’s about us. We’re working on the fine margins. Can our set pieces be better? All of the little things that can win you football matches.

“The goal that we’ve scored in the last game, that’s as simple as it needs to be for us.”

The Phoenix could receive a boost with new signings Milly Clegg and Kam Pickett potentially available after the squad was depleted for their opening two fixtures.

Priestman believes both players will provide different attacking options as Wellington look to turn promising performances into results.

“I got to see a little bit more of Milly on the pre-season and she’s a true number nine, works really well in the way that we play.

“Kam, on that left-hand side, brings me a little bit of the Brooke Nunn type. The dribbler, the ability to get closer to goal to pass more balls into the box.

“I think both give us the ability across 90 minutes to really get after teams.”

Priestman will make a late decision on how to use the pair.
