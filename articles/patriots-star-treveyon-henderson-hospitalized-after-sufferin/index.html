New England Patriots star running back TreVeyon Henderson announced that he recently had an "unusual" medical emergency that left him hospitalized for several days.

The 24-year-old said the medical emergency involved a series of unexplained neurological symptoms that affected some of his physical functions. Henderson said the cause of the issue remains unknown and unexplainable.

"Recently, I experienced an unexpected and unusual medical emergency involving a series of unexplained neurological symptoms that required hospitalization for several days.
