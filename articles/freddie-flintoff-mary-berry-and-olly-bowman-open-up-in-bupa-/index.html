Freddie Flintoff, Mary Berry and Olly Bowman open up in Bupa’s global Mindplace Sessions campaign via Story Studio, London

TheTools · 20.11.2026 01:58 · source · permalink

 

In the series, O’Kane sits down with former England cricket captain Freddie Flintoff, Aussie internet personality Olly Bowman, food journalist and chef Dame Mary Berry and British television personality Miquita Oliver for a series of candid, unscripted conversations about the emotions we often keep to ourselves.

Alongside the series, a global OOH, OLV and social campaign will run across the UK, Spain and Australia. It showcases real people and the real questions we ask ourselves every day, but are often unable to share publicly.

The launch comes as new research from Bupa reveals more than 80 per cent of Australians hide how they really feel from loved ones. The findings also highlight a contrast between respondents’ views on seeking mental health therapy and their current use of that support.

Bupa’s research found anxiety (24 per cent), insecurity (21 per cent) and loneliness (21 per cent) were among the hardest feelings to talk about. While 85 per cent of Australians believe talking-based therapy could be useful, just 11 per cent are currently engaging in therapy or receiving mental health support.

The survey also found that 55 per cent of respondents would be reluctant to speak to a therapist, with one in five (18 per cent) saying their problems do not feel serious enough. For more than one in four people (28 per cent) who have never engaged in therapy, having a clearer idea of what happens during a therapy session would make them more likely to consider it. The research was conducted by Opinium between 9 and 23 October 2026, with a sample of 6,000 adults: 2,000 each in the UK, Spain and Australia.

Mindplace Sessions aims to destigmatise therapy and make it feel less intimidating to take the first step.

Says Freddie Flintoff, international cricketer, coach and broadcaster: “I didn’t really know what to expect going into it, and if I’m honest, I surprised myself by how much I enjoyed the conversation. I spoke about things I probably wouldn’t normally talk about and Owen helped me look at some of them in a different way. There was something quite freeing about just sitting down and being honest.”

Says Kerri Stratford, Interim Director, Mindplace: “Therapy doesn’t have to be reserved for moments of crisis. Talking to a professional can support us through the everyday ups and downs of life.

“Mindplace Sessions aims to show people what speaking to a mental health professional can look and feel like, helping to make professional support less intimidating, and more familiar. Together with the support available through Bupa’s Mindplace services, the series is a small part of our broader commitment to making caring for our minds an everyday part of healthcare.”

Bupa is also helping make mental health care more accessible through its Mindplace low-cost offering, where a session with a registered psychologist at a Mindplace clinic will cost no more than $129. Bupa also continues to invest in community mental health programs through its Community Grants program.

Says Owen O’Kane, Psychotherapist and Author: “We spend a lot of time trying to push away emotions that feel uncomfortable, but often those feelings are trying to tell us something. Sometimes the most powerful thing we can do is slow down, stay with an emotion and become curious about what’s underneath it.

“Mary, Freddie, Olly and Miquita brought very different experiences to these conversations, but the feelings underneath are ones many people will recognise. I hope Bupa’s Mindplace Sessions encourage people to become more curious about their emotions, talk about them more openly, and feel more comfortable seeking support.

“Each conversation goes beneath the surface, exploring how experiences have made them feel. Freddie explores trauma, pain and finding balance, Mary reflects on grief, loss and Miquita delves into her feelings of anxiety, shame and pride.”

All four episodes are available to watch on Bupa’s YouTube channel.

Client: Bupa
Agency: Story Studio
