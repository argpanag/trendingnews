Celebrating historic triumphs and unstoppable rise of SLT-MOBITEL Brand Ambassador Rumesh Tharanga on global sport arena

SLT-MOBITEL proudly congratulated Brand Ambassador, Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage, on his historic back‑to‑back victories which have redefined Sri Lanka’s place in world athletics and set a defining chapter in Sri Lanka’s sporting history.

Following his sensational Diamond League Championship Finale win, in Brussels, where he became the first Sri Lankan athlete to clinch the prestigious title with a throw of 89.04 metres, Rumesh stunned the sporting world once again emerging victorious in the World Athletics Ultimate Championship 2026 title in Budapest, producing a commanding throw of 90.12 metres to secure his crown against the globe’s finest. These triumphs echo the determination, discipline, and excellence Rumesh embodies.  

From a promising fast bowler to a world‑class javelin thrower Rumesh’s journey is one of resilience, ambition, and relentless pursuit of excellence. His victories in Rome, Doha, Lausanne, Zurich, Brussels, and now Budapest, cement his place among the world’s elite and elevate him as a symbol of national pride.

Upon arriving at Bandaranaike International Airport, Rumesh was honoured with a special welcome ceremony. He was warmly received by Naleen Jayasuriya, Chief People Officer, and Anuruddha Rathnayake, Chief Network Officer, SLT-MOBITEL who celebrated his achievements as a moment of immense pride for Sri Lanka.

The gesture demonstrates SLT-MOBITEL’s deep commitment in honouring Sri Lanka’s sporting heroes and to nurture athletic talent. As Brand Ambassador, his elevation mirrors SLT-MOBITEL’s resolve to dedication and excellence in building Sri Lanka’s sporting future, symbolizing the company’s broader mission to drive progress, connectivity, and opportunity for Sri Lankans islandwide.

As Rumesh’s profile continues to grow on the global stage, SLT-MOBITEL is determined to support communities to advance and grow. His relentless hard work, strengthened by SLT‑MOBITEL’s steadfast belief in his potential, establishes him to reach even greater heights and inspire a nation.
