Woman, 37, dies after falling from height at ION Orchard, police investigating

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Investigations are ongoing.

November 18, 2026, 03:33 PM

Photo from CapitaLand’s website.

A 37-year-old woman was found dead at ION Orchard on Nov. 18.

In response to media queries, the police and Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) said they were alerted to a case of a fall from height at about 6:50am at 2 Orchard Turn.

Mothership understands that the incident happened outside the mall.

The woman was found lying motionless and pronounced dead at the scene by an SCDF paramedic.

Preliminary investigations do not suggest foul play, and investigations are ongoing, the police said.

Separately, an ION Orchard spokesperson said they are aware of an incident involving a member of the public that occurred on Nov. 18.

"As the authorities are investigating the matter, we are unable to comment further," the spokesperson said.
