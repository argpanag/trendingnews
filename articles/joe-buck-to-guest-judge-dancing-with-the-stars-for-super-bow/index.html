Joe Buck to guest judge 'Dancing with the Stars' for 'Super Bowl Night'

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:19 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Joe Buck is going from the broadcast booth to the ballroom.

The ESPN "Monday Night Football" announcer is bringing his football expertise to "Dancing with the Stars" next week as a guest judge.

Buck will join Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli at the judges' table for the show's "Super Bowl Night."

Buck, who is the son of legendary sportscaster Jack Buck, became a household name for NFL fans when he made his debut during the 1994-95 season, when he called a full slate of games for FOX Sports, according to ESPN.

He's currently on the mic in his 29th season calling NFL games.

In 2022, he was inducted into the Sports Broadcasting Hall of Fame.

His career also includes calling 24 World Series, 22 MLB All-Star games, five of golf's U.S. Opens and hosting his own shows.

"DWTS" will host its first-ever "Super Bowl Night" on Oct. 13, where the 11 remaining couples will perform routines set to iconic songs from past Super Bowl halftime shows.

The Super Bowl theme comes ahead of Super Bowl LXI, which will air on ABC and ESPN on February 14, 2027, from SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles.

See the 11 remaining couples and see what they will be performing below.

  • Jackson Olson and partner Emma Slater will perform a Salsa to "Uptown Funk" by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars
  • Guillermo Rodriguez and partner Witney Carson will perform a Salsa to "Tití Me Preguntó" by Bad Bunny
  • Harry Shum Jr. and partner Jenna Johnson will perform a Samba to "Yeah!" by USHER ft. Lil Jon & Ludacris
  • Ezra Frech and partner Daniella Karagach will perform a Salsa to "Hot in Herre" by Nelly
  • Tyler Cameron and partner Sharna Burgess will perform a Jazz to "The Time (Dirty Bit)" by Black Eyed Peas
  • Connor Wood and partner Rylee Arnold will perform a Samba to "Hips Don't Lie" by Shakira ft. Wyclef Jean
  • Maura Higgins and partner Mark Ballas will perform a Tango to "Only Girl (In the World)" by Rihanna
  • Julia Stiles and partner Ezra Sosa will perform a Jazz to "Jenny from the Block" [Track Masters Remix] by Jennifer Lopez ft. Jadakiss & Styles P
  • Jenna Dewan and partner Val Chmerkovskiy will perform a Paso Doble to "Bad Romance" by Lady Gaga
  • Tatyana Ali and partner Jan Ravnik will perform a Salsa to "squabble up" by Kendrick Lamar
  • Amber Glenn and partner Pasha Pashkov will perform a Samba to "Mi Gente" by J Balvin & Willy William

"Dancing with the Stars" airs Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and streaming on Disney+. Episodes stream the next day on Hulu.
