Strictly pro: Show changes 'not what people are used to'

TheTools · 07.10.2026 13:32 · source · permalink

Former Strictly Come Dancing professional Brendan Cole said the programme is "not what people are used to" since its recent shake-up, adding: "I don't think people are loving it."

The hit BBC dancing competition returned for its 24th series last month with its three new presenters Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe at the helm.

The stars replaced Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly as the faces of the show, and are among a string of changes as five professional dancers also joined its latest instalment.

New Zealand dancer Cole, who featured on Strictly from 2004 until 2017, discussed the changes to the flagship entertainment programme while appearing on Jeremy Vine on 5 on Wednesday.

The 50-year-old said: "There’s no investment.

"You’ve got all the new pros. There’s no investment in them because people don’t know them yet.

"They will become household names, but it’s going to take some time. You’ve also got the new hosts."

Cole noted that the presenting trio has faced "massive backlash" and said: "It’s not what people are used to.

"I think the signings are interesting, let’s say. I’m not enjoying it so far."

According to the BBC, last week’s episode saw a peak of 5.6 million viewers while its first live episode had a peak of 5.8 million viewers and an average of 5.3 million.

Cole went on to describe the shake-up as a "massive risk", and said: "I don’t disagree with the moving on. I think you’ve got to change your show.

"I think you’ve got to increase it to make it better to move it on.

"I think making those changes – the contestants and then the hosts – it’s a massive change for people and I don’t think people are loving it."

"People are watching TV in a different way these days," he added.

"I struggle to get through two and a half hours on the main show on a Saturday night.

"It’s long. It’s a big watch."

Source: Press Association
