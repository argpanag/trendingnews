Hailey Bieber admits she considered suicide because of the hate campaign launched against her by Selena Gomez's fans

TheTools · 21.11.2026 12:54 · source · permalink

Speaking candidly with Andrew Ross Sorkin at “The New York Times” DealBook Summit in New York City, the 29-year-old Rhode founder revealed how relentless online harassment and real-world confrontations pushed her to a dark breaking point

While appearing on stage at The New York Times DealBook Summit in New York City on Wednesday, November 18, Hailey Bieber opened up about the severe psychological toll of being pitted against Selena Gomez for years, admitting that at the height of the backlash, she thought “it would be better if [she] wasn’t here anymore.”

During her conversation with journalist Andrew Ross Sorkin, the 29-year-old model and beauty entrepreneur explained that the hostility went far beyond routine celebrity gossip, spilling out of comment sections and into her daily life.

“I had people telling me to kill myself in the comments every single day. I was going through a time where people were yelling at me in the street,” the Rhode founder shared during her panel at the New York Times’ DealBook Summit in New York on Wednesday.

Even grabbing a routine cup of coffee became a reminder of the public rivalry fueled by social media.

“People were putting tip jars up in Starbucks of me versus this person,” the model recalled, referring to Gomez, who had an on-again, off-again relationship with her husband, Justin Bieber, for nearly a decade.

Unlike celebrities who claim online criticism never bothers them, Bieber acknowledged that she was “too sensitive” to brush off the wave of vitriol, allowing dark thoughts to take hold.

“I had never experienced anything like that before. So the way that people were making me feel was that maybe it would be better if I wasn’t here anymore,” she continued. “Because that’s how intense I felt other people’s feelings were towards me at the time.”

Navigating social media boundaries, motherhood, and a years-long fan feud

Today, Bieber, who welcomed her son, Jack Blues, with Justin in August 2024 and saw her skincare company Rhode acquired by e.l.f. Beauty in a deal valued at up to $1 billion, actively works to “regulate” her screen time. Even so, she admitted it remains “tough to try to limit yourself” when social apps are “so available.”

She also confessed that she continues to “deal with comparison” when scrolling online, adding that she refuses to hide those vulnerabilities so she can eventually guide her own children through the pressures of digital culture.

“I want to be able to have [that conversation] with my kids so that when they’re feeling that way, they can relate to mom,” she shared, “and know that I feel that way too sometimes and we can be in that together.”

Looking back on years of relentless scrutiny, a dynamic that briefly resurfaced even during Wednesday’s summit when Sorkin asked her in a rapid-fire round whether she would buy Gomez’s Rare Beauty, Fenty, Skims, or Kylie Cosmetics, prompting her to choose Skims, Bieber noted she has gradually developed a “resilience” after “so much time of enduring and going through so much intense scrutiny.”

The fixation among sections of the internet dates back to Justin Bieber and Gomez’s high-profile romance, which began in 2010 and spanned numerous breakups and reconciliations, with Justin briefly dating Hailey during some of their off periods.

Their on-and-off chapter closed permanently in 2018 when the “Boyfriend” singer proposed to Hailey just months after splitting from the “Only Murders in the Building” star.

Although Justin and Hailey married in September 2018, and Gomez later moved on, marrying producer Benny Blanco in September 2025, many fans refused to let the past go.

Attempts to project unity never fully quieted the noise. During a September 2022 appearance on “Call Her Daddy,” Hailey insisted it was “all love” between her and the “Hands to Myself” singer, and the two women even posed together for a photograph two months later.

Yet subtle social media digs kept speculation alive, and by June 2025, followers noticed the pair had unfollowed each other on Instagram. Throughout the turbulence, Justin reportedly stood firmly behind his wife.

“Justin trusts Hailey to say what she wants to say and if she gets them into some drama, he’s going to ride or die with her, whether it is about Selena or not,” a source told the Daily Mail in October 2025.
