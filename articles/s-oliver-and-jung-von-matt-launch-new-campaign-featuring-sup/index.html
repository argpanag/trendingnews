s.Oliver and Jung von Matt launch new campaign featuring supermodel Heidi Klum and her younger self

TheTools · 12.09.2026 05:41 · source · permalink

Mumbai: s.Oliver has launched a new international brand campaign with Jung von Matt BEAUTY COLLECTIVE, featuring supermodel and brand ambassador Heidi Klum alongside a younger version of herself. The campaign, built around the idea of ‘CASUAL SINCE FOREVER’, brings together Klum’s personal history and s.Oliver’s brand heritage through fashion, pop culture and AI technology.

The campaign explores what happens when an icon encounters her own past. It draws a parallel between Klum’s evolution from an aspiring model to a global fashion personality and s.Oliver’s journey of evolving over the decades while retaining its casual identity and down-to-earth character.

The creative idea uses AI face-swap technology to bring the two versions of Klum together in a single image. The campaign aims to connect the face of the brand with its heritage while presenting the brand story through a contemporary format.

“With this campaign, we are further sharpening our positioning as a 100% casual brand. The story demonstrates in an emotional way that, while much changes, the true casual spirit remains. Together with Heidi Klum, we are interpreting precisely this idea in a surprising and previously unseen way,” says Johannes Rellecke, Co-CEO of the S.OLIVER GROUP.

Nicole Grözinger, Creative Director at Jung von Matt NECKAR and Creative Lead at Jung von Matt BEAUTY COLLECTIVE, said, “Sometimes the strongest ideas are the simplest: Heidi Klum meets Heidi Klum. We’re bringing together defining phases in the life of one of Germany’s greatest pop culture and fashion icons and using her story to retell the s.Oliver story as well. AI makes this encounter possible, but for us it remains a means to an end. The key is to use it in a way that makes the creative idea even stronger. In this way, brand heritage becomes not a nostalgic look back, but contemporary storytelling,”

The campaign has been designed as a long-term communications platform that can be extended across different stories, products and activations. It seeks to reinforce s.Oliver’s positioning as a 100% casual brand beyond individual campaign moments.

The creative development was led by the Jung von Matt BEAUTY COLLECTIVE at the Jung von Matt NECKAR office. The team used AI to bring to life still images shot by Rankin. Tempo handled the technical production of the film, while Alex Feil led the direction and creative AI direction.

The campaign is anchored by a brand film that will run across relevant touchpoints as part of a broader international activation. The campaign will roll out across international markets.
