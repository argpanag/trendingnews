Travis Kelce's Ex Was Accused Of Shading Taylor Swift With Her Halloween Costume, And 13 Other Times Celeb Costumes Started Serious Drama

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:46 · source · permalink

    One iconic reality star was called out by the very celebrity she dressed up as!

    The day after Halloween, I love scrolling through my social media feeds and judging all the best (and worst, lol) celebrity Halloween costumes. Usually, most of them are pretty much forgotten by the next week, but some celeb costumes stir up a TON of discourse.

    Here are 14 celebrity Halloween costumes that started drama with other celebs:

    1. In 2025, Julia Fox dressed as Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis in a pink suit and a pillbox hat splattered with blood, referencing the former first lady's appearance moments after surviving the 1963 assassination of her husband, President John F. Kennedy.

    Online commenters criticized Julia's costume as "tasteless" and "inappropriate." Writer and political commentator Jack Schlossberg, the grandson of Jackie and JFK, also called her out, tweeting, "Julia Fox glorifying political violence is disgusting, desperate and dangerous. I'm sure her late grandmother would agree."

    Addressing the backlash via Instagram, Julia wrote, "I'm dressed as Jackie Kennedy in the pink suit. Not as a costume, but as a statement. When her husband was assassinated, she refused to change out of her blood-stained clothes, saying, 'I want them to see what they've done.' The image of the delicate pink suit splattered with blood is one of the most haunting juxtapositions in modern history. Beauty and horror. Poise and devastation. Her decision not to change clothes, even after being encouraged to, was an act of extraordinary bravery. It was performance, protest, and mourning all at once. A woman weaponizing image and grace to expose brutality. It's about trauma, power, and how femininity itself is a form of resistance. Long live Jackie O. ♥️"

    2. In 2023, Megan Fox and her then-partner, Machine Gun Kelly, dressed as Gogo Yubari and the Bride from Kill Bill. At first glance, these costumes may seem pretty innocuous, but here's some important context: This was in the middle of the SAG-AFTRA and WGA strikes. The actors' union instructed its members to refrain from dressing up as characters from struck content in order to "use our collective power to send a loud and clear message to our struck employers that we will not promote their content without a fair contract." The union said to "choose costumes inspired by generalized characters and figures" instead.

    SAG-AFTRA also said, "Don't post photos of costumes inspired by struck content to social media." However, in a since-deleted Instagram post, Megan flagrantly disregarded her union's guidelines and straight-up tagged them in her caption!

    In response, Abbott Elementary actor and SAG-AFTRA negotiating committee member Lisa Ann Walter tweeted, "What a rebel. Keep posturing for stupid shit, pretty lady. Meanwhile we'll be working 10 hours a day - unpaid - to get basic contract earners a fair deal."

    She continued, "I bet she likes turnaround rules and relocation fees and our ongoing battle for AI protections & streaming residuals… The union did that. But sure - shit on them over nonsense."

    Lisa later told Page Six, "Her adding the union on that day, that was the flip-off. That was the issue. And it's just bratty, it was just bratty. ... [The union is] not for rich people. This is for the people that can't negotiate a contract." However, she also said that Megan, whom she's never met, is likely "lovely," adding, "I didn't mean for it to be such a big deal. So now I feel bad. So I apologize."

    Megan and MGK never publicly responded to Lisa's comments.

    3. In 2026, North West, Kim Kardashian and Kanye West's 13-year-old daughter, dressed up as Baddies star Natalie Nunn. Notably, she wore prosthetics to exaggerate her chin. Online commenters called the costume "mean-spirited" and accused her of mocking Natalie's appearance. They also criticized Kim's parenting.

    However, Natalie herself wasn't upset. On Instagram, she reposted one of North's videos, adding, "I GOTTA GET @northwest her baddie chain now! ❤️❤️❤️❤️ SHE GETS A BADDIE CHAIN HAPPY HALLOWEEN MONTH !!"

    Then, in an "Uno reverse," Natalie dressed up as North! North commented, "omg."

    4. In 2025, Happy Death Day actor Rachel Matthews recreated Katy Perry's astronaut look from the singer's viral 11-minute trip to space with Jeff Bezos's Blue Origin. While in costume, Rachel happened to cross paths with Orlando Bloom, aka Katy's ex-fiancé. She innocently posted a picture with the actor to her Instagram story, captioning it, "will tell my kids about this one day."

    At first glance, you might think, "Oh, what a wild coincidence, but surely the internet was TOTALLY normal about this!" Well, of course, you'd be wrong. A viral tweet from a Katy stan account shared Rachel's pictures with the caption, "Orlando Bloom's new gf dressing up as Katy Perry in her space costume." The tweet was quickly debunked — from Rachel's caption, it seems obvious she's simply a fan of Orlando — but that didn't stop the rumor from spiraling out of control.

    5. In 2025, media personality Kayla Nicole recreated Toni Braxton's iconic look from the "He Wasn't Man Enough" music video. On Instagram, she posted a video of herself dancing as this part of the song played: "What are you thinking? / Did you know about us back then? / Do you know I dumped your husband, girlfriend? / I'm not thinking 'bout him / But you married him / Do you know I made him leave? / Do you know he begged to stay with me? / He wasn't man enough for me."

    Many commenters interpreted the moment as a "petty" dig against Travis Kelce, whom she dated for five years, and his now-wife, Taylor Swift. Kayla previously faced online harassment from Swifties simply for being Travis's ex.

    On her podcast, The Pre-Game, Kayla revealed that her costume was inspired by Taylor — just not that Taylor! She said, "I had a white best friend. Her name was Taylor. And she [had] the only house I could go over to as a kid because my mom didn't play them type of games. I specifically remember driving home from private school in my uniform in the back of a paddy wagon with Taylor, and her mom was playing this song." Young Kayla had an "a-ha moment" where she thought, "Wow, white people listen to Black people music as well."

    On another episode of her podcast, Kayla further clarified that her Halloween post had absolutely nothing to do with Travis and Taylor. She said, "I feel like Halloween is an opportunity for you to let your creative flag fly. People will try to project their own narratives, but for me, personally, I am not in the business of tearing other women down. I'm in the business of celebrating them, and for me, this moment was about celebrating an icon in my culture."

    6. In 2014, Lily Allen had a pop culture-inspired costume — a gynecologist named Dr. Luke. Her look appeared to be a reference to producer Dr. Luke (Lukasz Gottwald), whom Kesha accused of sexual abuse, which he denied.

    For context, that same year, Kesha sued Dr. Luke for alleged sexual abuse, and the two spent years in a complicated legal battle. Because she was locked in a contract, Kesha was unable to record new music until 2016, when Sony finally permitted her to record without the producer. In 2023, Kesha and Dr. Luke settled their legal battle a month before it was set to go to trial. In her statement, Kesha said, "Only God knows what happened that night. As I have always said, I cannot recount everything that happened. I am looking forward to closing the door on this chapter of my life and beginning a new one. I wish nothing but peace to all parties involved."

    In his statement, Dr. Luke said, “While I appreciate Kesha again acknowledging that she cannot recount what happened that night in 2005, I am absolutely certain that nothing happened. I never drugged or assaulted her and would never do that to anyone. For the sake of my family, I have vigorously fought to clear my name for nearly 10 years. It is time for me to put this difficult matter behind me and move on with my life. I wish Kesha well."

    The announcement came several months after the release of Gag Order, Kesha's final album with Dr. Luke's Kemosabe Records. She officially parted ways with the label that December.

    While some people interpreted the costume as Lily supporting Kesha and mocking Dr. Luke, others criticized her for trivializing the allegations. Regardless of Lily's intent, the costume has landed her on lists of offensive celebrity Halloween costumes for over a decade now. For what it's worth, in 2016, amidst Kesha's legal battle with Dr. Luke, Lily tweeted, "My thoughts are with @KeshaRose today. I'm sorry you've had to endure this experience."

    7. In 2013, Julianne Hough from Dancing With the Stars went as Suzanne "Crazy Eyes" Warren from Orange Is the New Black for Halloween (you can see the photos here). Here's the main issue: Julianne, a white woman, wore blackface to portray Uzo Aduba's character. Following criticism, she apologized, tweeting, "I am a huge fan of the show Orange Is the New Black, actress Uzo Aduba, and the character she has created. It certainly was never my intention to be disrespectful or demeaning to anyone in any way. I realize my costume hurt and offended people and I truly apologize."

    Wendy Williams called Julianne out on The Wendy Williams Show, pointing out that she "should know better." She said, "She showed her ignorant side when she showed up to a Halloween party wearing blackface. Like, who does that? I don't know what part of stupid — or, excuse me, less than smart — she is. Who walks out of the house in blackface? Like, wow, Julianne. I don't know whose party she was at, but you have to figure this: This was, like, an intentional move. This was not something that happened all of a sudden, and she was like, 'Oh, is this racist?' You put thought into your Halloween costume. I've known what I've wanted to wear for over 40 days..."

    Wendy continued, "You talk about your Halloween costume. You don't just show up in blackface. Which means she's not just less than smart off the cuff — she's less than smart even when she thinks about it. ... She grew up Mormon. She only thought that there was one kind of alcohol until I guess she arrived to Hollyweird and discovered you can drink something different every minute of the day. Does she get a pass if she grew up out there? Because I'm thinking there's books and there's internet and TV…the world is your oyster."

    Julianne didn't publicly respond to Wendy.

    Uzo later told E! News, "I think maybe it was an unfortunate event, but she apologized, and I feel like we can all move on."

    8. In 2018, Olympic gold medalist Shaun White shared (and later deleted) an Instagram photo of himself as Simple Jack, a Tropic Thunder character that has long been criticized for mocking people with intellectual disabilities. Shaun's costume was called out by commenters as well as the Special Olympics, with senior director of global youth engagement Soeren Palumbo telling HuffPost, "We are truly disappointed that Shaun White, an acclaimed Olympian, would choose this costume, which is so offensive and causes so much pain. Disability is not a joke nor should it be a punchline. We hope that Shaun White and others learn that this just continues stigma, stereotypes, and discrimination."

    Shaun apologized on Twitter, writing, "I owe everyone in the Special Olympics community an apology for my poor choice of Halloween costume the other night. It was a last minute decision. It was the wrong one. The Special Olympics are right to call me out on it. They do great work supporting many tremendous athletes and I am so sorry for being insensitive. Lesson learned." The official Special Olympics account replied, "Thank you @shaunwhite for listening to our community. You always have an invitation to shred with our athletes at #XGames Aspen!"

    However, while Shaun was willing to admit he was wrong, Ben Stiller, the actor who played Simple Jack, felt the need to defend him. Replying to a tweet joking about a Tropic Thunder boycott, Ben said, "Actually Tropic Thunder was boycotted 10 years ago when it came out, and I apologized then. It was always meant to make fun of actors trying to do anything to win awards. I stand by my apology, the movie, Shaun White, And the great people and work of the @SpecialOlympics."

    9. In 2022, Kylie Jenner dressed up as Halloween icon Elvira. She was praised for the accuracy of her costume and even went so far as to recreate the Mistress of the Dark's photoshoots (you can see Kylie's pictures here).

    However, there's one person who wasn't 100% impressed — Cassandra Peterson, aka the real woman behind Elvira! She told TMZ, "I didn't get a heads up that Kylie was doing the costume, but she did the costume justice, and it was very flattering. ... It would have been even more flattering if she tagged me."

    Kylie never publicly responded.

    10. In 2020, Lil Nas X — who used to run a secret Nicki Minaj stan account — paid homage to the Queen of Rap in a drag look that recreated her "Super Bass" music video outfit (you can see his pictures here). This was before Nicki's MAGA turn, so the backlash had nothing to do with that — sadly, he was bombarded with anti-gay comments.

    It wasn't just anonymous internet users, either — some of his music industry peers joined in. Rapper Dave East called him a derogatory Jamaican patois term for queer men. However, he misspelled it, and responding on Twitter, Lil Nas said, " ... can't even be homophobic the proper way smh butchering the hell out of the patois."

    Shortly after, he added, "bro i don't bother a soul in this industry. all i do is tweet and make bangers. leave me alone damn."

    Additionally, 50 Cent — who's previously denied being homophobic — tweeted Lil Nas's Halloween pictures, adding, "👀what the fuck, Nikki come get him !!! Lol." In a since-deleted quote tweet, Lil Nas replied, "Why u in barb business."

    11. In 2025, Nina Dobrev dressed as Tiffany Valentine, aka the Bride of Chucky, for Halloween. In the comments, fans interpreted the look as shade against another famous redhead — Nina's ex-fiancé, Shaun White.

    While Nina never publicly commented on the speculation, her posts revealed that the costume was actually a joint effort with her friend, Erica Barstein, who dressed as Chucky!

    Sometimes, the "drama" surrounding a celebrity's Halloween costume is actually pretty lighthearted. So, let's end on a positive note with a few:

    12. In 2021, Khloé Kardashian let her then-3-year-old daughter, True, choose their Halloween costumes, and the Disney-inspired look wasn't exactly what Khloé would've chosen for herself. She told The Ellen DeGeneres Show, "True's gonna be Moana, and she wants me to be Pua, the pig. So, it's a little shady of her, but it's okay. I will be Pua for True."

    Unfortunately, Khloé and True ultimately spent that Halloween in quarantine after testing positive for COVID-19.

    13. Similarly, in 2017, Kristen Bell — who famously voiced Anna in Frozen — dressed up as Elsa. On Instagram, she said, "When your daughter demands you BOTH be ELSA for Halloween...you GRIN AND FORKING BEAR IT."

    View this photo on Instagram

    14. And finally, in 2024, "Angel of My Dreams" singer JADE dressed as Little Bo Peep from Toy Story. Her then-partner, Jordan Stephens, had a matching look, but rather than being Bo's onscreen love interest, Woody, he was Mr. Potato Head (you can see the photos here).

    On Late Night With Seth Meyers, JADE said, "Basically, every year, I ask — in brackets, force — my boyfriend to be my sort of accomplice. And he always forgets. I'm always the one that sort of instigates what we're doing. So, on the night, I was like, 'You're Mr. Potato Head.' I bought him — which he then refused — a black morph suit and then just the head. ... We went to the party, and as we left — 'cause everyone was laughing, obviously — he literally whispered in [my] ear, 'That's the last time I'm ever your accessory!' I was like, 'Fair enough.'"
