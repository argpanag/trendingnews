MobLand's Helen Mirren addresses why she had to change Essex accent

TheTools · 29.09.2026 08:51 · source · permalink

MobLand star Helen Mirren has revealed why she ditched her Essex accent. The 81-year-old was born in London but raised in Leigh-on-Sea, in Southend, Essex. With the accent often heard on screen in shows such as The Only Way Is Essex, it's hard to believe Dame Helen is from the area.

Speaking about why she sounds so different from many people from the county, Helen said she toned down her accent when she joined the Royal Shakespeare Company as a young actress.

Speaking to Essex's Mark Wright for Extra TV, she said: "I did [change it], I had a real, not a strong Essex accent, but definitely Essex.

"My first job was with the Royal Shakespeare Company in England and in those days... nowadays you can do Shakespeare with any accent, which is much better I think, healthier and more truthful, but in those days to do Shakespeare, you had to talk like that a bit," she added, while demonstrating a posh accent.

"When I look at pictures of myself young now and hear myself talking, in an interview I did with Michael Parkinson, I kind of talk [posh], it's awful," she added.

Helen's latest role is in MobLand, in which she plays Maeve Harrigan, the matriarch of the Harrigan family.

The role is a far cry from another character she is known for, portraying Queen Elizabeth in the 2006 film The Queen.

Asked which type of role is easier to slide into, she said: "I loved playing the Queen because she's so varied.

"I thought of the Queen as a submarine and occasionally her periscope comes up and looks around the world like this, observes it all and then disappears back into the submarine and continues, so she was very submerged emotionally and psychologically, the Queen," she continued.

"Maeve is the absolute opposite, Maeve is bouncing about on the waves, this way and that way, every way, which way," she continued, before her co-star Pierce Brosnan chimed in and joked: "[With] bags of cocaine."

"Bags of cocaine help a lot," she added, laughing. "I've absolutely loved playing Maeve, it's such a treat, it's like the icing on my cake honestly."

Opening up about working with Pierce, her on-screen husband, who stars as Conrad in the crime series, she described him as a "wonderful" man and actor.

She said of his character on the show: "He's brilliant as Conrad, just as he is brilliant in everything. He's terrifying as Conrad, this terrible, violent man on the edge of tearing everything down around him."

"But off camera, Pierce is the most fun, lovely and peaceful man you could ever hope to meet," she added to Best Magazine. "I love working with him."
