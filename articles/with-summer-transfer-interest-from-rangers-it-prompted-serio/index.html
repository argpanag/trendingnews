With summer transfer interest from Rangers, it prompted serious think on my future

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:06 · source · permalink

Lewis Ferguson has admitted he had a "long think" about his future amid interest from Rangers over the summer.

The Scotland midfielder, who currently plays for Bologna, was a target for the Ibrox side.

However, no official offer was made.

The 27-year-old has featured regularly for the Italian side this season, after struggling for game time last term.

He has played 90 minutes and captained the side in every Serie A game so far.

On international duty with the national team, Ferguson was asked about the links to Rangers.

He said: "I had a long think after the World Cup.

"I had a bit of time spent on holiday and I thought about my future.

"I was a little bit unsure, but nothing ever was really that close, to be honest.

"When I went back for pre-season with Bologna, we had a new manager and it was like a fresh start again.

"Although we've changed since, so it's been, again, another... I've had three new managers in the last two months.

"So, yeah, it was just that. I had a long think about my future and what that was going to look like.

"In the end, it was still at Bologna.

"I've loved every moment that I've had there.

"So, yeah, it was just about continuing that journey and seeing where it takes me."

Ferguson was then asked if playing for Rangers is still an aspiration of his.

He said: "Yeah, I think one day. It's no secret I grew up a fan. That's absolutely no secret.

"My family's involved in the club and it was an aspiration of mine, a dream of mine as a kid, and that's not changed.

"But I don't want to speak about that while I'm still a player at Bologna. I've got too much respect for them.

"But, yeah, in the future, you never know. It might happen, it might not, but it's always been something in the back of my mind."
