Preorder the Stunning Zelda-Themed Nintendo Switch 2 Before It Sells Out - CNET

TheTools · 08.09.2026 19:59 · source · permalink

Nintendo's limited-edition Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary console arrives Oct. 29, and preorders are now live.

Associate Writer

Aashna Gheewalla

Associate Writer

September 8, 2026, 3:59 pm ET

Nintendo is giving its Switch 2 the collector’s treatment with a special-edition console inspired by The Legend of Zelda. The limited-edition Zelda Switch 2 is available to preorder now, ahead of its Oct. 29 release. We’d strongly suggest getting your order in now before it’s too late.

Along with an eye-catching black-and-gold design, it includes the same upgraded hardware found in the standard Switch 2. If you’ve been waiting for a reason to upgrade your Switch, this collector’s edition might be it.

The Legend of Zelda Switch 2: $520

The Nintendo Switch 2: The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Edition is one of the most striking special-edition consoles Nintendo has released. It has a custom black-and-gold finish, plus it features Triforce-inspired artwork across the dock, console and Joy-Con 2 controllers. Underneath the themed design, you’re getting the same upgraded Switch 2 hardware, including a vivid display, faster performance and support for up to 4K output when docked with compatible games and TVs.

The system comes with the matching dock and themed Joy-Con 2 controllers, though the game itself is sold separately. At $520, it costs more than the standard console, but the exclusive design makes it an appealing option for Zelda fans and collectors alike. Limited-edition Nintendo hardware has a history of selling out quickly, so preordering is the safest way to secure one before launch.
