  2. "There is good in all of us"published at 08:52
    08:52

    Owain joined Naga Munchetty and Charlie Stayt live on BBC Breakfast this morning, with an honest update on how he’s feeling as he tackles Day 4.

    “You’re looking good!” was the welcome from Naga - but Owain wasn’t quite feeling as positive.

    “I’m not feeling good today, hon. It’s been a long old couple of days,” he admitted. With his knee playing up, a bad back and hills everywhere, today was a stretch he’s been dreading.

    But the people he’s met along the way and those who benefit from BBC Children in Need are what is keeping him going.

    “In the run-up, I’ve met a lot of people who benefit from Children in Need,” he said. “They can only fund one in 6 of the projects that approach them. Wouldn’t it be nice to get more money in that pot?”

    Reflecting on the welcome across all four nations, Owain thanked everyone who has come out to cheer, offer support and put money in the buckets, despite the financial pressures many are facing.

    “There is good in all of us, and I’ve seen so many good people.”

    And Charlie had one final bit of encouragement for Owain:

    “I think you look quite cool!”

  12. "I know we've got some big hills..."published at 08:19 GMT
    08:19 GMT

    Owain Wyn Evans
    BBC Radio 2’s 4 Nations Cycling Superstar

    Owain will be cycling through Wales on Day 4 of his 4 Nations Challenge, and if anyone knows just how hilly Wales can be, it's Owain.

    We're here now this morning in Welshpool. Wherever I look outside the windows, I can just see hills. So that means whichever direction I'm travelling this morning, I've gotta get over some sort of hill.

    We did a road called Long Mountain last night... I called it something else last night - couple of extra words in between ''long'' and ''mountain''...

    Gotta keep pushing. I'm scared about today because I know we've got some big hills. It's on the hill the knee hurts, and it's on a hill that I came off the bike the other day, so that's always in the back of my mind. When you travel slow on a bike as you have to do when you're going up these steep hills, and you're clipped into the pedals, one wrong twist of the handle bars and you're over.

  13. Our new Lord of Welshpool!published at 08:13 GMT
    08:13 GMT

    A rather grand start to the morning for Owain as Bill Rowell, the Mayor of Welshpool presented him with a scroll and a new title for the day.

    “We are very proud of Owain and proud of what he’s doing, and we felt that we needed to express our thanks, so we’re going to make him Lord and Bailiff of Welshpool for the day.”

    Reading from the scroll, the Mayor proclaimed:

    “Be it known that Owain Wyn Evans, broadcaster, drummer extraordinaire and intrepid cyclist of the four nations, is hereby bestowed with the entirely unofficial and magnificent title of honorary Lord and Bailiff of Welshpool, in recognition of his extraordinary determination, boundless enthusiasm and remarkable efforts in cycling nearly 400 miles across four nations in support of Children in Need.”

    And when asked what powers the title actually bestowed, the Mayor explained: “I think he can drive sheep over bridges and that!”

    Probably best to stick to the bike for now, Owain.
