NBA, sportsbooks to ban and restrict certain wagers

TheTools · 16.10.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

The NBA has agreed with multiple prominent sportsbooks to ban and restrict specific wagers this season, including parlays involving the under on some players' stats.

According to multiple reports citing league memos, the NBA instructed its authorized gaming operators -- including DraftKings and FanDuel -- to not allow bets that the league has deemed problematic in the wake of recent betting scandals.

The prohibited wagers include parlays that combine bets on the same player’s statistics going under, “first of” bets such as who wins the opening tip or records a player’s first rebound, and under bets on players expected to play fewer than 12 minutes.

The changes add to earlier restrictions on bets involving players on 10-day or two-way contracts, turnovers, fouls and missed free throws, according to the report.

DraftKings has released a statement on the decision, saying in part, "The NBA’s guidance formalizes many of the market restrictions already reflected in DraftKings Sportsbook’s practices. DraftKings supports the NBA’s efforts and is committed to continued collaboration with the league to maintain game integrity, including through appropriate limits on betting markets. DraftKings’ Code of Responsible."

Engagement is fully aligned with the league’s stance on the harassment of athletes, coaches or officials.”

The NBA has pushed to limit some player-performance bets amid concerns that a player could influence a narrow statistic more easily than the outcome of a game.

The scrutiny has grown after a series of gambling investigations, including cases involving former Toronto Raptors player Jontay Porter and Miami Heat guard Terry Rozier.

The league has said it wants to reduce opportunities to manipulate player performances and protect players from harassment tied to wagers.
