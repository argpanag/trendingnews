Random thoughts on TFC: Jobs on the line during final stretch

TheTools · 24.09.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Toronto FC's last eight games of the season are as much about players securing their futures as they are about clinching a playoff spot.

John Molinaro

Some random thoughts I had on Toronto FC ahead of Saturday's road match vs. Nashville SC (8:30 p.m. ET/Apple TV).  

A big opportunity this weekend for Nicksoen Gomis 

Toronto is going to be short-handed on the defensive side for Saturday's match vs. the Supporters' Shield leaders in Tennessee.

Richie Laryea and Richard Chukwu (Canada) and Benjamin Kuscevic (Chile) have been called up by their respective senior national teams and will miss the game in Nashville – it falls on the same day as the international friendly between Canada and Chile at BMO Field.
