PlayStation Store listings show a staggered global unlock for Grand Theft Auto 6, clarifying that access will vary by region. The GTA 6 digital release will roll out across 63 regions, beginning in New Zealand and progressing to Australia, Japan, South Korea, and others. This regional unlock affects Vice City access times, with some players starting up to 19 hours before those in later time zones. Rockstar Games' open-world title is slated for November, with digital pre-orders on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S ahead of the November 19, 2026 launch. Midnight local releases and no disk edition place timing under platform scheduling. Players switch between Jason and Lucia.
