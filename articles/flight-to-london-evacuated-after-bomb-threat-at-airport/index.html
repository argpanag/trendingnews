Flight to London evacuated after 'bomb threat' at airport

TheTools · 25.09.2026 21:24 · source · permalink

Passengers were ordered out of a London-bound Jet2 flight following a bomb threat.

Some flights were briefly disrupted at Tenerife South Airport this afternoon following the incident.

Guardia Civil officers checked a Jet2 plane to London Stansted after reports that one person had made a bomb threat, according to Atlantic Hoy.

Passengers had already boarded the plane before being told to disembark.

Luggage and passengers were checked as a precaution before the aircraft was given an all-clear.

The incident involved flight LS1536, which was scheduled to leave the Canary Islands’ second-largest airport at 11.20am.

But the Boeing 737 took off from Tenerife at about 2pm instead, according to the tracking website Flightradar24.

The plane landed at Stansted at 5.49pm.

A spokesperson for Jet2 told Metro: ‘We can confirm that customers were asked to disembark a flight at Tenerife South Airport today following an incident involving a passenger onboard.
