Savannah leaders honored at Men and Women of Action Dinner

TheTools · 04.10.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

Savannah Alderwoman Linda Wilder-Bryan hosted her annual Men and Women of Action Dinner at the Savannah Civic Center on Tuesday night.

The event recognized state and city officials, the Chatham County sheriff, Savannah’s police chief and several other community leaders for their service.

Wilder-Bryan honored the attendees as the “Sheroes and Heroes” of Savannah and Chatham County.
