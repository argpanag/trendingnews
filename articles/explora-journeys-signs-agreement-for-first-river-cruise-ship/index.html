Explora Journeys signs agreement for first river cruise ships

TheTools · 25.09.2026 07:20 · source · permalink

 

You are viewing 1 of your 2 free articles

Explora Journeys is set to make its debut in the river cruise market.

 

The luxury ocean line said it has signed a letter of intent with Dutch shipbuilder Den Breejen Shipyard for the construction of its first luxury river ships.

 

The brand said: “Since launching, Explora Journeys has sought to change the way people experience ocean travel through our Ocean State of Mind philosophy – combining the freedom and discovery of travelling by sea with the elegance and refinement of European hospitality and design.

 

“We now intend to bring that same approach to Europe’s most iconic rivers.”

 

Further details of ship design, onboard experiences, delivery schedules and initial itineraries will be announced “in due course”, with sales expected to open next year.

 

The news comes a month after the line’s third vessel Explora III embarked on its preview sailing, during which president Anna Nash (pictured) hinted the ocean fleet may be “just the beginning” for the brand.  

 

The plans emerged days after Celebrity Cruises held a float-out ceremony in Rotterdam for the inaugural ship of its new river fleet, due to enter service in August 2027.
