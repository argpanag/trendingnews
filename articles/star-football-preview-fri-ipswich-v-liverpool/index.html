Liverpool are still searching for their first Premier League win under Andoni Iraola after being held to 2-2 draws by Newcastle United and Nottingham Forest in their opening two games.

Ipswich have found the net in each of their opening two league games and should provide a threat at home, particularly after scoring twice against Manchester United despite eventually losing 5-2. Liverpool’s defensive vulnerabilities were again evident in the draw with Forest, making goals at both ends an appealing angle.

Liverpool have dominated the recent meetings, winning the last two encounters 2-0 and 4-1, while their overall record against Ipswich is also strongly in their favour. The Reds should have enough attacking firepower to finally get their first victory of the Iraola era, but I expect Ipswich to contribute to the scoreline.
