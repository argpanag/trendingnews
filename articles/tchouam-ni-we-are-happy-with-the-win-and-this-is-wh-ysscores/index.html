Tchouaméni: We are happy with the win, and this is wh… | YSscores

TheTools · 10.10.2026 22:02 · source · permalink

Aurélien Tchouaméni, a player for Real Madrid, confirmed his happiness with the 1-0 victory over Villarreal in the eighth round of La Liga, emphasizing the importance of continuing to win, despite the difficulty of returning to match rhythm after the international break.

Aurélien Tchouaméni expressed satisfaction after the match: "We are happy because we know that Villarreal is a big team. Of course, we can improve, but after three weeks of stoppage, it is not easy to reach our best level, and the most important thing for us today is to achieve the victory."

Real Madrid player addressed the incident of his teammate Vinícius Júnior's red card, expressing his confusion over the referee's decision. He said: "Certainly, when we have the support of our fans in the stadium, things become easier, but in the end, I do not understand what is happening with the referees, and I do not understand that red card, but the most important thing is to win."

Speaking about the importance of the victory after the international break, Tchouaméni explained: "The win is very important, and we must continue to achieve victories. We did that today, and now we have to look ahead."

The French player expressed his happiness at playing his 200th match for Real Madrid, stating: "It is a dream I live every day. I thank the fans and the president of the club, and I hope to play more matches and achieve more victories."

Aurélien Tchouaméni praised the importance of the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium after Real Madrid secured five wins in five home matches this season, stating: "It is our home, and we must continue to win here."

Aurélien Tchouaméni concluded his remarks about the upcoming clash against AS Roma on Wednesday, stating: "We did not show our best in our first match, and we must go to Rome and win."
