Police Car Hits Road Divider in Geylang Bahru; Officer, 31, Assisting Investigations

TheTools · 10.10.2026 20:25 · source · permalink
  • News

A police car hit a road divider in Geylang Bahru on 10 October. The male officer driving it, aged 31, is assisting investigations.

Accident Happened At About 7:45am

Police told 8world that the accident occurred at about 7:45am along Geylang Bahru Road towards Bendemeer Road.

No one was injured. Investigations were ongoing, police said.

Footage Shows Damaged Car And Fallen Railings

According to Lianhe Zaobao, footage shared on Xiaohongshu showed damage to the front of the police car and fallen railings in the rightmost lane.

Two officers were beside the vehicle, while another police car was parked across the road.

The woman who filmed the scene told Zaobao that she drove past after the accident had happened and did not know how it occurred.
