Another oil price rollback set today

TheTools · 17.11.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — Another fuel price rollback will greet motorists today following last week’s large price drop for gasoline.

The Department of Energy (DOE) has ordered oil companies to implement minimum price cuts of P1.50 per liter for gasoline, P3.80 for diesel and P5.80 for kerosene this week.

The latest adjustments reflect movements in the international oil market amid geopolitical developments in the Middle East, which continue to influence global supply and prices.

Hopes of diplomatic talks to end the months-long conflict have helped ease global oil prices, as prospects of a resolution temper concerns over potential supply disruptions.

However, renewed attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure have prompted oil prices to rebound.

With the latest adjustments, gasoline prices in Metro Manila are expected to range from P77 to P102.80 per liter, while diesel will sell for P84.70 to P94.19 per liter.

Kerosene prices, meanwhile, are projected to range from P109.20 to P138.14 per liter.

The DOE said the government continued to explore ways to beef up the country’s fuel stockpiles.

As of Nov. 6, the country had an average fuel inventory equivalent to 50.33 days of consumption, latest DOE data showed.

Meanwhile, state-run PNOC Exploration Corp. signed preliminary agreements with Indonesia’s PT Interport Mandiri Utama and The Shell Co. of Thailand Ltd. to explore possible cooperation on oil-ticketing mechanisms.

The initiative will facilitate pilot projects to explore the use of existing oil storage facilities in Indonesia and Thailand to give the Philippines access to petroleum reserves during supply disruptions.

Under an oil-ticketing arrangement, a participating entity pays for the right to access oil stored in another country during an emergency, subject to agreed conditions.
