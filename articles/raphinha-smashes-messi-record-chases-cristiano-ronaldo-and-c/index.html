Raphinha smashes Messi record, chases Cristiano Ronaldo and could make history

TheTools · 20.09.2026 09:37 · source · permalink

FC BARCELONA

With 12 goals through the opening seven matchdays, the Brazilian has surpassed Leo Messi’s best scoring start of 11, sits just one goal behind Cristiano Ronaldo and has another historic milestone within reach

    Journalist

Journalist

Actualizado el 20/09/2026 05:37 EDT

Raphinha, 29, continues to prove there are no limits to what he can do. After arriving at Barcelona in the summer of 2022 to play and thrive on the right wing, the Brazilian reinvented himself on the left when Lamine Yamal emerged on his preferred side. Now, following the departures of Robert Lewandowski and Ferran Torres, combined with the failed pursuit of Julián Álvarez, Barça’s No. 11 has decided to become Hansi Flick’s No. 9, and not just any No. 9.

The Brazilian celebrated his new contract extension with Barcelona, which will add two more years to his current deal and keep him at the club until June 2030, by smashing a record previously held by the greatest player of all time: Lionel Messi.

With his perfect hat trick against Sevilla, the Porto Alegre native moved clear at the top of the LaLiga scoring charts with 12 goals through the opening seven matchdays, surpassing Messi’s best ever start. During the 2017/18 season, the Argentine scored 11 goals over the same seven game span.

But Raphinha’s remarkable numbers do not end there. He matched the best seven game scoring start of four other players: Mariano Martín with Barcelona, Pruden with Atlético Madrid in 1940/41, Campanal with Sevilla in 1940/41 and Quini with Sporting in 1979/80, according to MisterChip.

In fact, only two players in LaLiga history have scored more than Raphinha through the opening seven matchdays: Cristiano Ronaldo, who had 13 goals in 2014/15, and Echevarría, who scored 14 for Oviedo.

Following the international break, during which Raphinha will represent Brazil in friendlies in Australia and India, the Brazilian will face an enormous challenge. In that same 2014/15 campaign, Cristiano reached 16 goals through the opening eight matchdays. That means Raphinha would need to score four times against Getafe at Spotify Camp Nou on October 10 to match his mark.

Another Hat Trick Would Put Him Ahead of Messi and Suárez

Even if Raphinha falls short of four goals, another hat trick would allow him to make history in another way.

So far, only Messi has recorded back to back hat tricks twice, while Luis Suárez did it once. If Raphinha produces another three goal performance, he would surpass both the Argentine and the Uruguayan, neither of whom ever managed three consecutive hat tricks.

He would also equal a historic feat previously achieved by Lángara, with Oviedo in 1934/35, and Zarra, with Athletic Club in 1946/47.Spectacular Shooting Efficiency

Raphinha’s numbers become even more impressive when his shooting efficiency is taken into account.

Including Barcelona’s Champions League match against Feyenoord, the Brazilian has attempted 22 shots this season. Only one has missed the target, two have hit the woodwork and, of the 19 shots that were on target, 14 ended up in the back of the net, according to data from Catalunya Ràdio’s TDT.

Those are numbers that, until now, would have been associated with elite finishers such as Romário, Ronaldo, Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Suárez, Lewandowski, Haaland or Kane, to name some of the most prolific scorers of recent decades.

Raphinha can now be added to that conversation. He has transformed himself into a genuine No. 9 and emerged as Flick’s major solution at center forward for Barcelona.
