League of Nations: Draws at the Stade de France and at Jack's farewell

TheTools · 02.10.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

In the evening derby of the League of Nations on Friday, France and Italy played 1-1 at the Stade de France in the 3rd leg of Group A1.

France took the lead with a beautiful goal from Oliseh (55) from a free kick, and Bastoni (68) equalized by taking advantage of a careless pass from Rabiot in front of his own penalty area.

In the second match of this group, Belgium defeated Turkey 3-0 at home with two goals from De Bruyne (10, 59) and one from Lukaku (76).

France leads the standings with seven points, Belgium has six, Italy has four, while Turkey has no points.

In Zenica, Edin Džeko said goodbye to playing for the national team of Bosnia and Herzegovina, and the home selection played 1-1 against Sweden on the captain's farewell. Gyokeres (61) gave the guests the lead just before Džeka left the field, and Alajbegović (80) equalized.

Sweden is at the top of Group B4 with seven points, Bosnia and Herzegovina is second with five. Poland follows with four points, while Romania has no points.
