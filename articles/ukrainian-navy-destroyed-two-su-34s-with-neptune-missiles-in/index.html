Ukrainian Navy Destroyed Two Su-34s with Neptune Missiles in Volgograd Region

TheTools · 29.10.2026 17:47 · source · permalink

Units of the Ukrainian Navy carried out a successful missile strike using the Neptune coastal missile system against the Russian military airfield Marinovka in the Volgograd region of the Russian Federation.

The strike hit the deployment site of the 11th Mixed Aviation Regiment, according to the Ukrainian Navy.

As a result of the strike, two Su-34 front-line fighter-bombers were destroyed.

The Su-34 is one of the main platforms of Russian tactical aviation for launching guided aerial bombs (GABs).

As a reminder, after a drone threat was reported at the Russian Marinovka airfield in the Volgograd region, satellites detected a fire on the night of October 29.
