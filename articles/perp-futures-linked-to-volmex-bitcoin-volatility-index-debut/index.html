Perp futures linked to Volmex bitcoin volatility index debut on Hyperliquid

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:54 · source · permalink

Deployed via Kinetiq's Markets frontend and led by Volmex CEO Cole Kennelly, Hyperliquid's new BVIV perpetuals let traders easily go long or short on 30-day bitcoin volatility.

  • Hyperliquid debuts perpetual futures to the Bitcoin Volmex Implied Volatility Index.
  • These perps allow traders to bet directly on the magnitude of bitcoin’s price swings rather than its direction.
  • Markets by Kinetiq led the listing with Volmex and Perps.fun, marking the first onchain perpetual futures market for Volmex’s Bitcoin volatility index.
