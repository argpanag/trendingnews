Kiwanis high school Key Clubs to host pickleball tournament fundraiser

TheTools · 16.11.2026 23:52 · source · permalink

CASPER, Wyo. — The 307 Kiwanis Club, the Natrona County High School Key Club and the Kelly Walsh High School Key Club will host a pickleball tournament on Saturday, Nov. 21 to fundraise for both Key Clubs’ travel costs to district and international conferences.

The pickleball tournament will be at the Casper Pickleball and Sports Complex from 9 a.m. until 7 p.m. Entry costs $50 per person.

The tournament is for all ages and abilities. Anyone who is interested is invited to come play and support the Key Clubs.

In addition to pickleball, there will be a 50/50 raffle and a silent auction at the event. Little Caesars Pizza has also sponsored the event as a vendor.

The mission of the high school Key Clubs, sponsored by the Kiwanis Club, is to develop leadership skills through service projects. Usually, Key Club members participate in fundraisers and community service projects, such as Coats for Kids. The pickleball tournament sponsors travel to international and district conferences that offer club members opportunities to learn and grow as leaders.

All proceeds from the pickleball tournament and donations will be split evenly between Natrona County High School and Kelly Walsh High School to cover travel and hotel costs.  

“We are trying to be positive influences in the communities that we live in. Just helping one child in one community at a time is our focus,” Darci Andersson, the secretary for the 307 Kiwanis Club, said. 

Participation in Key Clubs not only develops adolescents’ leadership skills, but can also provide them with opportunities to find their passions and future employment, Andersson said. 

“I think they become stewards of the community, not just in a leadership role, but they know that there are people in the community that need our support,” she said.

Andersson and her fellow Kiwanis Club members have been preparing for the pickleball tournament for five to six months. Most of that time was spent gathering donations to fund the tournament itself, she said. 

The 307 Kiwanis Club is still looking for donations to fund the pickleball tournament and to go toward both Key Clubs’ conference participation. To donate, the community should contact Andersson or mail checks to 3939 Fox, Casper, WY 82604.
