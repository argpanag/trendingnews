Brewers' Dustin May starting Game 4 of World Series vs. Rays

TheTools · 27.10.2026 15:56 · source · permalink

The Milwaukee Brewers are handing the ball to Dustin May as they go for the sweep.

The team announced its starter for Game 4 of the World Series on Tuesday, hours before first pitch (7:30 p.m. ET / 4:30 p.m. PT, Sportsnet, Sportsnet+).

May, 29, has a 5.63 ERA and 1.25 WHIP over eight innings pitched in four games over the 2026 post-season, along with five strikeouts.

The Brewers acquired the right-hander on Aug. 3, ahead of the MLB trade deadline, from the St. Louis Cardinals, and he made 10 regular-season appearances for the club.

Milwaukee is seeking its first World Series title in franchise history.

May previously won a championship with the Los Angeles Dodgers in 2020.
