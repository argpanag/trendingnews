Bestway group launches new vehicles in Pakistan

TheTools · 24.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

ISLAMABAD: Bestway Group has launched two variants of electric vehicles and one hybrid vehicle in Pakistan. This NEV was launched in collaboration with Chinese manufacturer Geely. They have established a plant in Karachi having capacity to produce 25,000 units.

In the first phase, they will establish eight dealerships in different major cities of the country, including Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. Bestway Group officially introduced Chinese automotive giant Geely Auto to Pakistan at a launch ceremony here in Islamabad on Saturday night and was attended by the Group founder Sir M Anwar Pervez, the Group Chairman Lord Zameer Choudrey and the senior executives from both organisations and others.

Bestway Automotive introduced three Geely models to the Pakistani market – the EX2, EX5 and Starray EM-R. The Geely EX2 is fully electric designed while the EX5 is a fully electric SUV. The Starray EM-R features range-extended electric vehicle (REEV) powertrain technology, combining electric driving with the flexibility of an onboard petrol engine.

Through this partnership, Bestway and Geely aim to build lasting customer relationships and contribute to the development of Pakistan’s automotive industry.
