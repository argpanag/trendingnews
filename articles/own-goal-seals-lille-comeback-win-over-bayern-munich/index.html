Pavlovic's own goal cost Bayern in France

Aleksandar Pavlovic scored a comical own goal as Bayern Munich suffered their first defeat in Europe this season at the hands of French side Lille.

The German champions surrendered a first-half lead as their hopes of a top-eight finish took a hit while Lille secured their first victory in the Champions League this season.

Bayern, missing Harry Kane, Michael Olise and regular keeper Manual Neuer through injury, were in front after 15 minutes as Tom Bischof's corner was turned in by Dayot Upamecano.

Manager Vincent Kompany, thought his side had surrendered their lead just after the restart, but Joshua Kimmich's own goal was chalked off after a VAR review for a foul by Ayase Ueda.

However, Ueda got his goal in the 62nd minute with a fine finish after a good run and pass from Nathan Ngoy.

A quick counter then saw the French side take the lead three minutes later as a throw out from the keeper Berke Ozer saw Lille break away and Ethan Mbappe burst through.

Mbappe's shot came off the post but Pavlovic swiped at it to clear only to fire it into his own net.
