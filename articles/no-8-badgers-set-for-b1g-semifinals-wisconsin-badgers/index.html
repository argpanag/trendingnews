No. 8 Badgers set for B1G Semifinals

November 23, 2026 | Volleyball

No. 3-seeded Badgers take on No. 2-seeded Penn State

MADISON, Wis. — The No. 8 Wisconsin volleyball team continues its Big Ten Tournament run with a semifinal matchup against No. 2-seeded Penn State in Fishers, Indiana, on Tuesday, Nov. 24. 

First serve is set for 7 p.m. (CT) at the Fishers Event Center, following the 5 p.m. semifinal matchup between No. 1-seeded Nebraska and No. 5-seeded Purdue. Fans can stream the match live on Big Ten Network. 

The Badgers advanced to the Semifinals after completing a four-set victory over Michigan State in its first Big Ten Tournament match on Sunday evening, improving the program's streak to 95-straight victories over unranked opponents. 

Wisconsin clipped a .344 (60-18-122) hitting percentage while tallying 11 blocks to hold the Spartans to a .070 (34-25-129) hitting percentage. Three Badgers totaled double-digit kill performances, as Big Ten Freshman of the Year Decelise Champion notched a match-high 16 kills with Grace Egan and Audrey Flanagan adding 14 and 12, respectively. Egan's 14 kills helped her past the 500 career kill mark. 

In their last 15 matches against Penn State, the Badgers are 8-7 and hold a 6-3 record since 2021. Wisconsin faced the Nittany Lions at home earlier this season, where they suffered a setback in a five-set thriller. 

The Nittany Lions earned a double-bye into the Big Ten Tournament as the second-overall seed, where they turned in 17 blocks to complete a 3-1 victory over Illinois to advance to the Semifinals. 

The winner of Tuesday's matchup will advance to the Big Ten Tournament Championship on Wednesday at 7 p.m., where they will face the winner of the Nebraska/Purdue match.
