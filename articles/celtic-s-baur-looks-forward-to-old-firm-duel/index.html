Celtic's Baur looks forward to Old Firm duel

TheTools · 10.09.2026 15:28 · source · permalink

Getty

Mika Baur was delighted that his first goal for Celtic helped generate further momentum ahead of Sunday's Old Firm showdown with Rangers in the Premier Sports Cup.

The German midfielder notched in the second half as the Hoops defeated St Johnstone 1-0 on Wednesday to make it a perfect six William Hill Premiership wins out of six this season.

The Hoops’ only blemish this term remains their Champions League play-off capitulation away to LASK, and Baur is optimistic they can keep tracking in the right direction by overcoming their Glasgow rivals at Ibrox this weekend to reach the semi-final of the Premier Sports Cup.

"It will be a special match," said the 22-year-old, who joined from Bundesliga side Paderborn last month.

"Everyone knows it’s a special match against Rangers. They’re a good team, it will be a tough game, difficult, but we will try our best to get into the next round.

"I think now we have good momentum because of the last matches in the league, we can be confident for sure.

"That’s why it’s important that we won against St Johnstone, to have that confidence and to look forward to the Rangers match and to go into the match really confident.

"We know we have the quality and we are capable of scoring, capable of defending.

"It was not a special goal, it was a tap-in, but I’m really happy that I scored it and that we won because it was a tough match, especially in the first half."

Baur admitted Sunday’s match is likely to be an eye-opener for him in terms of scale an intensity compared to anything he has experienced before in his career in the German lower leagues.

"I really don’t know what to expect," he said. "For sure I expect it will be special, but I think I’ve never experienced something like this.

"I’m just looking forward and being excited about what’s happening on Sunday.

"I played two derbies, but it was during my time in Dresden. They were also special, there are some really tough derbies, but I think you cannot compare it to something like Sunday."

Baur is unfazed by the prospect of there being no Celtic fans at Ibrox on Sunday after the recent decision not to allow away supporters to attend any SPFL matches between the two Glasgow sides this term.

"We have to accept it, it’s not our decision," said Baur. "It should not affect us on Sunday, but sure, if you have some fans with you to give you energy, it helps a lot, but it’s not our decision.

"We accept it and try to do our best. You know everyone still watches it in front of the TV, so you want to do your best, you want to win and maybe it also gives you a bit of energy.

"I’ve never experienced something like that before, especially if there are just fans there that maybe hate you and don’t like you, but I think I can handle that."
