NFL Games Today: Is There a Football Game on Friday? A Look at Tonight’s Slate and Complete Week 1 TV Schedule - AOL

TheTools · 13.09.2026 15:54 · source · permalink

The NFL has already played its two Week 1 curtain-raisers, and now the league goes dark until Sunday.

Wednesday brought the New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks in a Super Bowl LX rematch; Thursday sent the San Francisco 49ers and Los Angeles Rams halfway around the world for the league’s first-ever regular-season game in Australia, but tonight there is nothing on the NFL schedule.

College football picks up the slack, and it will do that with one of the sport’s oldest rivalries taking center stage on national television.

Catch College Football Action On Friday

The headline game is a Border War rematch nobody expected to see back in Lawrence. No. 23 Missouri travels to Kansas at 8 p.m. ET on FOX, with both teams entering 1-0.

Branded the StorageMart Border Showdown, this is the 122nd meeting in a rivalry dating back to 1891, tied for the 10th-most-played series in college football history, and the first time the two programs have met in Lawrence since 2005.

The game was originally scheduled for Saturday before both schools agreed to move it to Friday night to secure standalone national TV coverage.

Missouri has won the last four meetings, including last year’s renewal in Columbia after 13 dormant seasons in the series, and the Tigers hold the historical edge depending on which school’s record book you trust. Kansas lists it as 57-55-9; Missouri counts it as 58-54-9, with the gap tracing back to a disputed forfeit ruling from 1960.

Eli Drinkwitz enters his seventh season leading Missouri, while Lance Leipold, in his sixth year at Kansas, carries a 28-35 record.

NFL Week 1 Schedule, Dates & Streaming Details

The Patriots and Seahawks opened the season Wednesday at Lumen Field, where New England fell to defeat, with the game airing on NBC and Peacock. Thursday’s 49ers-Rams matchup at Melbourne Cricket Ground streamed exclusively on Netflix and NFL+ as the Niners came out on top.

The rest of Week 1 resumes Sunday, September 13, the busiest day on the calendar. FOX carries Chicago Bears at Carolina Panthers, Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Cincinnati Bengals, and New Orleans Saints at Detroit Lions at 1 p.m. ET, with additional windows filling out across CBS and FOX through the afternoon and evening.

NBC closes the day with Sunday Night Football, sending the Dallas Cowboys to the New York Giants. Denver Broncos at Kansas City Chiefs wraps up the full week Monday night on ABC and ESPN, a rematch with real weight after the Broncos won the AFC West at 14-3 while the Chiefs fell to 6-11 a year ago.
