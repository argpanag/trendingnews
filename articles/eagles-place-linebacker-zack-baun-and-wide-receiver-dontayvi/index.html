Eagles place linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks on IR

TheTools · 21.11.2026 21:44 · source · permalink
  • Eagles
  • News

AP Photo/Matt Slocum

Injuries continue to pile up for the Eagles.

The Philadelphia Eagles have announced that they are placing linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks on IR, causing them to miss at least the next four games.

We are activating RB Will Shipley from reserve and elevating DT Gabe Hall and LB Anfernee Orji for the game. We have also placed LB Zack Baun and WR Dontayvion Wicks on the Injured Reserve List. pic.twitter.com/x6UPtYFGSR

— Philadelphia Eagles (@Eagles) November 21, 2026

We are activating RB Will Shipley from reserve and elevating DT Gabe Hall and LB Anfernee Orji for the game. We have also placed LB Zack Baun and WR Dontayvion Wicks on the Injured Reserve List. pic.twitter.com/x6UPtYFGSR

It’s two huge losses for the Eagles squad, as Baun has been arguably the team’s not only best defensive player but overall best player this year, and Wicks, in his six games this season, has been a fantastic addition as a No. 2 receiver for Philadelphia.

Baun has been the Eagles’ green-dot playcaller on defense every single game since joining the team back in 2024. While he was used as an on-the-line linebacker, Phialdelphia and Vic Fangio felt that he’d be better off the ball in the middle, and it’s safe to say they were correct.

He’s been a Pro Bowler in his first two seasons with the Eagles, and was on his way to potentially being named for a third straight time, and a one-time first-team All-Pro. In his three seasons in Philadelphia, Baun has led the Eagles in tackles every season, totaling 339 in that time.

Baun’s injury that is causing the IR stint is the back injury that he suffered in the third quarter of the Eagles’ week nine loss to the New York Giants. While he is eligible to return this season, this injury could be season-ending for the linebacker, as the Eagles are 2- 7 at the moment and the playoffs seem very unlikely.

Jereimaiha Trotter Jr. will likely get the green-dot playcalling duties moving forward, as he and Jihaad Campbell are the only two healthy linebackers left from the beginning of the season.

As for Wicks, it just continues to be a disaster of a season for the receiver in his first in Midnight Green, as after suffering an ankle injury that caused him to miss the last four weeks, he suffered a new foot injury that was likely sustained during his rehab from his ankle injury and has caused him to miss at least another month.

Wicks has recorded 19 receptions for 284 yards and one touchdown this season, filling the hole on the outside left by AJ Brown. The Eagles will at least get DeVonta Smith back to help Joe Milton on offense moving forward, but the Eagles’ wide receiver room will still be short for at least the next month.

As for good news, the Eagles are activating Will Shipley off IR, giving the Eagles back one of their backup running backs. Shipley has missed the last five games with a partial plantar fascia tear that he suffered in the London game against the Jaguars.
