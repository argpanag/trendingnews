Apple TV Has Become a Hub for Sci-Fi

By now you've probably guessed that we're talking about Silo. The show holds an overall 92% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes. The third season, which premiered this past July, set a new benchmark with a "Certified Fresh" 96% score. The aggregator's consensus for the latest season reads, "Silo codifies its third season as a masterful dystopian tale with expertly driven character arcs and tighter narrative strings, fulfilling its promise of science fiction excellence." In her review, Collider's Tania Hussain praised the new season for elevating the show to "essential viewing" status. And that's exactly what audiences are doing. According to FlixPatrol, Silo has now spent 600 days on the domestic Apple TV viewership chart, despite competition from platform-defining hits such as Slow Horses and Ted Lasso, both of which returned with new seasons recently.

The final season of Silo will be released in 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
