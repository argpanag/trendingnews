Real Sociedad fans brave Oslo deluge as 430 head for Lillestrom

Real Sociedad supporters are enduring a sodden build-up in Oslo and Lillestrom, with a steady downpour greeting their arrival ahead of the Europa League match. Around 430 txuri-urdin fans are expected at Arasen Stadium.

According to Diario Vasco, the rain has barely eased, temperatures hover around 11 degrees and umbrellas have stayed up, a stark contrast to the sunshine in Brussels and Leuven a week ago.

OneFootball Videos

Some fans noted it was still dark early in Oslo, and Lillestrom is the northernmost city among all participants in this season’s Europa League. David, president of the Peña realista de Vinaros, plans to sleep in Oslo then catch the last train back from Lillestrom. Supporters say public transport, especially the trains, runs like clockwork.

In the morning many visited Slottplassen, where the royal palace stands. The downpour sent plenty back to their hotels for lunch after stocking up on provisions at supermarkets. Some paid 30 euros for pizzas last night.

Even so, the txuri-urdin will back their team, who need a win to reach four points and view the Europa League table differently. The meeting point is Fuser Bar at 16:00, roughly a 20-minute walk from the ground. It is about five minutes from Lillestrom’s train station.

Source: Diario Vasco
