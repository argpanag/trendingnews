Huang to Stay or Leave?

Nvidia's Board to Investigate

The development follows Huang's admission that he had a romantic relationship with a woman U.S. officials suspected of being tied to Chinese intelligence.

Huang reportedly told employees that he takes "full responsibility" and apologized for what happened.

Nvidia's board said it will form an independent committee and bring in outside legal help to examine the matter.

Meanwhile, the company's head of public policy communications said that Huang would continue to lead NVIDIA as CEO without interruption.

U.S. officials had earlier suspected that Alice Huang, an executive at a Singapore-based data-center company, was helping China evade restrictions on advanced AI chipe, while maintaining a close relationship with Jensen Huang.
