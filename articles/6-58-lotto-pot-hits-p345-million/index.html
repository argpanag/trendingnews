6/58 lotto pot hits P345 million

TheTools · 29.09.2026 01:41 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The jackpot in the Ultra Lotto 6/58 is estimated to reach P345 million, the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office announced yesterday.

PCSO general manager Melquiades Robles said no bettor guessed the winning combination 57-40-09-54-56-27, which had a prize of P337,819,635.42.

Four bettors picked five digits to win P375,000 each.

Another 659 lotto players won the third prize of P2,124.43 each.

The PCSO reminded the public that under the Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion Law, lottery prizes exceeding P10,000 are subject to a 20-percent tax.
