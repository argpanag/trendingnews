Another oil price cut looms next week

NATIONAL

by

Anna Leah Gonzales

11:47 AM GMT+8

ROLLBACK LOOMS. A pump attendant refuels a car at a gasoline station in this undated photo. Jetti Petroleum president Leo Bellas said Friday (Nov. 27, 2026) that diesel and gasoline prices may go down by PHP3 to PHP3.50 per liter in the first week of December. (PNA file photo) 

MANILA – Domestic pump prices are expected to go down for the fourth consecutive week due to supply surplus. 


Citing oil prices and foreign exchange developments as of Thursday, Jetti Petroleum president Leo Bellas forecast diesel and gasoline prices to decline by PHP3 to PHP3.50 per liter next week. 
He said these forecasts exclude week-on-week ocean freight and insurance costs.


"Oil remained relatively lower this week amid expectations of a supply surplus as Middle East crude loadings from terminals outside the Strait of Hormuz rose, overshadowing the shipping risks as attacks inside the strait continue," Bellas said. 


"Product prices also eased further this week as strong supply outlook amid rising Chinese exports continues to loosen tight prompt availability of both gasoline and diesel in the region," he added. 


Last week, oil firms also slashed the prices of gasoline and diesel.


Final price adjustments will be announced on Monday and will take effect Tuesday next week. (PNA)
