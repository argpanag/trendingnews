'Secret' behind Barbera Thornhill's 'MAGA makeover' amid outcry over her looks

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:42 · source · permalink

All eyes were on Barbera Thornhill as she received a new role in the Trump administration, but not for the reasons she'd have hoped.

Thornhill was announced as Donald Trump's new Ambassador-at-Large to Monitor and Combat Trafficking in Persons earlier this week. To celebrate her new role, an official portrait of Thornhill was shared on social media to congratulate her, but attention was soon drawn to her looks.

X users accused Thornhill of undergoing a "Mar-a-Lago" makeover as she seemed to have a wrinkle-free forehead and a noticeably fuller pout. Her eyes were also opened wide, with no wrinkles visible at the side of her face. Aesthetics and skincare expert Amish Patel has now spoken out about what work Thornhill might have undergone to achieve her look.

Speaking exclusively to The Mirror US based on pictures of Thornhill, Patel began, "Looking through photos of Barbera over the years, Barbera‘s face seems to change after 2009 because in 2014 we can see that her eyes look different so it’s possible that she may have had some surgery around her eye area.

"Blepharoplasty can open the eye more by giving an eye lift, but a brow lift can also smooth the forehead and help open up the eyes." It wasn't the only difference he noted.

Patel suggested, "Her chin and jawline look sharper in later pictures, which again could suggest some aesthetic intervention, perhaps surgery or other procedures which tighten the skin, such as radiofrequency and perhaps strategic filler placement."

The expert concluded, "For someone of her age, we would expect to see more wrinkles, but her face is very smooth, so again this could be down to Botox and maybe chemical peels."

Thornhill's appearance gained widespread attention on social media, with people wondering if she'd undergone a so-called MAGA makeover. One X user suggested Thornhill's new look is the result of "the Mar-a-Lago effect."

Someone else admitted, "Had to check and see if this was a satire account," as a third person echoed, "This is a real picture?" Another social media user trolled, "Are they planning to scare the traffickers to death?"

"MAGA loves to humiliate their women members," another person fumed. "Posting a picture of this woman when you know the audience is going to tear apart her appearance, is cruel."

The X user added, "The MAGA men enjoy watching their own women get humiliated and will even participate. These people eat their own kind."

"Jeez, put a trigger warning next time," a sixth person begged. Someone else joked, "This is getting better and better. More lizards coming in."

Amish Patel, BEM, is an award-winning practitioner and skincare expert at Intrigue Cosmetic Clinic
