All the Stars at the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show

TheTools · 18.10.2026 21:51 · source · permalink

The guest list was just as starry as the runway

The focus of the evening may be on the angels, but there will be just as many stars sitting front row at the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show this evening as there will be models.

Two years after the event made its grand return in New York City, the location has moved to Los Angeles for 2026, where it is being held at Sony Pictures Studios in Culver City. This marks the first time the VS show has been held in L.A. in 19 years.

The 2026 iteration will see the reappearance of veteran models like Adriana Lima, Anok Yai, Candice Swanepoel, Ashley Graham, Paloma Elsesser, Alex Consani, Devyn Garcia, Liu Wen, and Gigi Hadid. Elsa Hosk will also notably hit the catwalk after an eight-year hiatus.

Last year brought a few surprise cameos too, from stars like WNBA player Angel Reese and Olympic gymnast Suni Lee. Not to mention, Jasmine Tookes brought her own scene-stealing moment when she walked the runway while nine months pregnant. If I had to wager, there will likely be a few surprises in store tonight, too.

The performers for the evening will include Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Katseye, and Megan Moroney.

But before all the excitement begins, we’ll have our eyes locked on the pink carpet, where all the A-list attendees will be walking before the show begins.

(You can stream the show for free on the official Victoria’s Secret YouTube channel, as well as on the brand’s official Instagram and TikTok accounts.)
