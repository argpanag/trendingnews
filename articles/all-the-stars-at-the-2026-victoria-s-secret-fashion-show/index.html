All the Stars at the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:11 · source · permalink

This evening, the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show kicks off at the Sony Studios in Culver City, California. Top models—otherwise known as the “Angels”—will be hitting the runway in the brand’s finest lingerie wears, including Gigi Hadid, Alex Consani, Precious Lee, Adriana Lima, Anok Yai, Candice Swanepoel, and more. (To name a few.)

During the show, there will also be a roster of buzzy musical guests, including live performances from Katseye, Tate McRae, Megan Maroney, and Megan Thee Stallion.

Before all of the striking intimates made their theatrical debut, however—not to mention those bespoke angel wings!—the Victoria’s Secret red carpet is delivering a whole other style spectacle. Celebrities all turned up for the event in style tonight—bringing their best statement looks to the occasion. (Every year, the show garners a star-studded front row: Last year, stars such as Sarah Jessica Parker, Patrick Schwarzenegger, Chloë Sevigny, Jeremy Pope, and more showed up.)

Don’t want to miss all of the action from this year’s event? Below, scroll on for all of the celebrities who showed up for the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show red carpet.
