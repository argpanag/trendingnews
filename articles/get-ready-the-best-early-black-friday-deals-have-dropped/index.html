What is Black Friday?

Traditionally, Black Friday refers to the major shopping event that takes place on the Friday after Thanksgiving in the United States. Now, that major shopping event has been embraced worldwide, marking the start of the Christmas shopping period.

In Australia, it’s evolved into one of the busiest shopping events of the year. No longer is this sale event four days of impressive deals; in recent years, it has expanded to a month of savings, typically ending not long after Cyber Monday.

When is Black Friday and Cyber Monday?

This year, Black Friday falls on Friday, November 27, but retailers will start discounting from the end of October, both in-store and online. We promise to keep you updated as soon as the official deals drop.
