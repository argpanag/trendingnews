The Trade Bell has been rung once more, this time on Tuesday morning - the first day of September.

SEN's Sam Edmund responded to texts from listeners and provided some further info on SEN’s Whateley.

Read the latest below:

“I would have Essendon as a very, very good chance to land Arty Jones,” Edmund said.

“A decision has not been made. Essendon have the bigger offer as things stand, and valuing this player has been the challenge for all parties.

“What the Dogs are offering now is essentially what the Jones camp were pushing for mid-season.

“I know this is the art of negotiation, but it’s taken time for the Dogs to get to the here and now, and that has let other clubs like the Bombers in. It’s a three-year offer from the Dogs at the moment.

“There is also a fear at the Kennel that after his Wildcard Final exploits, that Essendon could go north again and might even sweeten their offer further.

“It is a real battle for this guy here, and considerable speculation that the Bombers’ offer is more than tempting.

“Pretty hard to call at the moment this one.”

“Adelaide, I’m not sure, but what we can say is that he, via his management, is once again considering his playing options.

“Jordan De Goey’s management has contacted rival clubs this year to ask, ‘are you interested in our guy’, who is contracted until the end of next year at Collingwood.

“De Goey was subject to strong trade speculation last off-season, and it amounted to nothing.

“He has been chased by St Kilda multiple times in recent seasons and stayed put. But there have been fresh approaches in the back half of this year.

“So, again, you’d have some level of question mark around his immediate playing future.

“His management haven’t returned calls over the last two days, but I know Jordan De Goey is a question mark at the moment, again.”

“It’s still one destination and one destination only. A lot has changed in the last 12 months; what hasn’t changed is his destination club.

“I think this gets done.”
