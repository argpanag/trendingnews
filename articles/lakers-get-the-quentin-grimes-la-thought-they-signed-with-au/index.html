Lakers get the Quentin Grimes LA thought they signed with Austin Reaves out

TheTools · 15.11.2026 23:48 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Lakers defeated the Phoenix Suns 131-123 on Sunday afternoon behind a 43-point masterpiece from Luka Doncic. Austin Reaves was out with a heel injury, which moved Quentin Grimes into the starting five, and the Lakers saw the player they thought the franchise was giving a $60 million contract to this offseason. Grimes struggled to start his Lakers tenure, but this could be the game that finally busts him out of the slump.

The 26-year-old wing put up 22 points, six rebounds, one assist, and two steals in 37 minutes against the Suns. He made seven of his 10 field goal attempts, including sinking five 3-pointers. The Lakers outscored Phoenix by 19 with Grimes on the floor. They needed someone to step up, and Grimes got a chance to be more aggressive. Q even delivered a clutch three to seal the Lakers eighth straight win.

He was averaging just 8.0 points on 43.1 percent shooting from the field and 24.2 percent on his 3-point attempts coming in. It is still early, but there were questions about the signing. Grimes is the fourth-highest paid Laker this season, but he wasn’t playing like it. He has only played well when one of the two stars has been out. Hopefully, Sunday in Phoenix jumpstarts him.

Lakers thought Quentin Grimes would be a versatile offensive weapon

LA is light on ball-handlers and shot creators this season. They are relying on Doncic, Reaves, and Collin Sexton to run the offense. Things get tricky when one of those three players misses time. The Lakers are relying on Grimes to be a secondary creator and floor spacer. They can't count on Bronny James or Jaden Hardy. LA needs Grimes.

He hadn’t done either coming in. Grimes was averaging just 2.5 assists per game and making just 0.7 3-pointers. That is not enough.

Grimes was exactly what LA believed they added. He was the secondary creator next to Doncic and Sexton throughout the contest. The Lakers gave him chances to bring the ball up the floor and found him for six 3-point attempts.

The Lakers need this version of Grimes. If he’s making shots and being a versatile option, Los Angeles is even better. This is already the best offense in the NBA. Grimes gives them another weapon to add to the danger.

Quentin Grimes’ path to starting is defense

Grimes was expected to start when the Lakers signed him, but head coach JJ Redick gave the nod to the best perimeter defenders. Matisse Thybulle and Ziaire Williams earned the spots to start the season. Jake LaRavia has replaced Williams, but LA is focused on becoming a top-ten defense.

The Lakers need the Grimes defense fans saw in the first three quarters. He got two steals and was defending multiple positions. LA was 5.0 points per 100 possessions better defensively with him on the floor coming into the game. Grimes needs to be a standout on the defensive end to get more minutes and make a larger impact.

The Lakers have nine new players and are still figuring things out. They are 12-2 this season, which is incredible. This roster should only get better from here. Grimes has to improve. Fans saw a glimpse of what it could look like against the Suns.

Lakers need this Quentin Grimes when they are healthy

Grimes had 23 points and four steals in the blowout win over the Clippers on Oct. 28 without Luka Doncic. Q had 12 points and two stocks in a victory over Detroit when The Don sat again. He has stepped up without the stars, but the Lakers need this version when the roster is healthy.

The biggest difference is shot attempts. Grimes has gotten at least 10 tries in the three games where Luka or Reaves sat. He hasn’t had more than seven in the other contests. The Lakers need to get Grimes more involved to get the most out of him.

Los Angeles is bringing Grimes off the bench, but they want him to be in the running for Sixth Man of the Year. He has virtually disappeared to start this season. The 26-year-old has to be better. Hopefully, the win in Phoenix was the start of Grimes showing the Lakers exactly what he can do in LA.
