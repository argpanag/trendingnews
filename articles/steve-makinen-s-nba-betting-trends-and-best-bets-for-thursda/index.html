Strategies Using NBA DraftKings Betting Splits Data

One of the most touted features on the VSiN.com website, and a feature that we believe to be a fantastic resource for bettors, is the betting splits pages. These are the pages that we have built utilizing the data that DraftKings so graciously provides to us, detailing the breakdowns of the money & ticket splits for point spreads, money lines, and totals. 

In an article published on the VSiN.com website on opening day, Steve Makinen outlined 14 different systematic strategies for successfully using the DK Betting Splits Data that were developed in the last three seasons. Here are the systems and qualifying plays for today’s games as of 11:30 a.m. ET. These can and will change, so continue to track and qualify the systems up until tip-off for best usage.

DK NBA Betting Splits system #1: When 63% or more of the handle was on the side of a home team playing as a four-point favorite or less (or as an underdog) on ATS wager, this “super” majority group has gone 236-206 ATS (53.4%) over the last four seasons, good for +9.4 units of profit and an ROI of 2.1%. This is fairly a profitable system that the public seems to get right consistently.
System Match (PLAY): ATLANTA (-1.5 vs CLE)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #4: In another somewhat rare situation but when there was a majority handle backing the team with fewer wins on the season in the NBA over the last 3+ seasons, that group produced a 570-514 ATS record (52.6%), good for +4.6 units of profit and an ROI of 0.4%.
System Matches (PLAY ALL): ATLANTA (-1.5 vs CLE), PORTLAND (-7.5 vs MEM)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #7: The number 63% or higher has been a key super-majority number in non-divisional conference games over the last 3+ seasons, with handle groups of that size boasting a 679-601 ATS record, good for 53%. This is about a 3% improvement on the overall majority figures and has netted +17.9 units of profit and an ROI of +1.4%.
System Matches (PLAY ALL): ATLANTA (-1.5 vs CLE), NEW ORLEANS (-5.5 vs SAC)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #9: There has been a massive disparity of success levels for majority groups on handle when they back the home or road team on money line wagers. On home teams, these majorities have gone 1744-780 over the last three seasons but for -310.98 units and an ROI of -12.3%. On road teams, these majorities have gone 1337-879 but for just -47.67 units and an ROI of -2.2%. This 10%+ disparity warrants fading ML majorities on home teams consistently.
System Matches (FADE ALL): NEW ORLEANS ML (-238 vs SAC), PORTLAND ML (-290 vs MEM)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #11: One of the best, simplest, and most frequent angles I have been able to uncover is when a majority of handle bettors were backing the Under in an NBA game. When any majority has favored the Under option over the last four seasons, the results have been strong, with Under the total on these games going 558-464, good for 54.6%, +47.6 units, and an ROI of +4.7% since January ’23.
System Matches (PLAY UNDER ALL): CLE-ATL (o/u at 237.5), SAC-NOP (o/u at 236.5), MEM-POR (o/u at 232.5)
