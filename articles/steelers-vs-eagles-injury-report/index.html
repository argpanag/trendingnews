Steelers vs. Eagles Injury Report

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:36 · source · permalink

Wide receiver DeVonta Smith is good to go for Sunday's game against the Steelers, but Dontayvion Wicks remains out.

The Eagles are healthier coming out of the bye week and will welcome the return of wide receiver DeVonta Smith to the lineup on Sunday against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Smith, who has missed five of the last six games with a hamstring injury, was a full participant in practice on both Thursday and Friday this week.

However, the Eagles will be without Smith's positionmate Dontayvion Wicks for the fourth game in a row. Wicks was limited in practice all week with an ankle injury. The team added a foot injury for Wicks on Friday's report.

Linebacker Smael Mondon is doubtful for Sunday's game after missing the past two practices with an ankle injury. Running back Will Shipley did not practice on Friday due to an illness. He is questionable as he remains on Injured Reserve with a foot injury. He would need to be activated off Injured Reserve to the 53-man roster by 4 PM on Saturday to be eligible to play against the Steelers.

Everyone else on the active roster is good to go for Sunday.

Linebacker Jihaad Campbell is set to return to the lineup and start alongside Jeremiah Trotter Jr. with Baun out and Mondon doubtful. Campbell missed the past two games with hamstring and quad injuries. Defensive back Michael Carter missed three of the last four games with an abdomen injury. Wide receiver Britain Covey was out for the last two games with a knee injury. Cornerback Jonathan Jones is healthy after missing the past two games with a hamstring injury.

The Steelers listed six players as questionable, including outside linebacker Nick Herbig, who suffered injuries to his hip and ribs last Sunday night in Cincinnati. Herbig did not practice all week.

In fact, no one practiced on Friday for the Steelers as the team held a walkthrough. The participation level is an estimation.

Three of the players who are questionable – guard Gennings Dunker, wide receiver Michael Pittman, and wide receiver Roman Wilson – are on Injured Reserve and, like Shipley for the Eagles, would need to be activated in order to play against Philadelphia.

Thursday's Injury Report

There were two notable changes on Thursday's injury report for the Eagles.

Wide receiver DeVonta Smith was upgraded to a full participant on Thursday after practicing in a limited capacity Wednesday. Smith told reporters after Wednesday's practice that he was back to 100 percent after missing five of the last six games with a hamstring injury.

Linebacker Smael Mondon joined fellow linebacker Zack Baun on the sideline Thursday. Mondon was a full participant with an ankle injury on Wednesday, but did not participate in practice Thursday. Mondon stepped in during the Week 9 game against the Giants before the bye week after Baun suffered a back injury and Jihaad Campbell was out with hamstring and quad injuries.

Baun has yet to practice this week, while Campbell is on track to return.

Wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks missed the Eagles' last three contests with an ankle injury.

Running back Will Shipley, practicing for the first time since the Week 5 game in London, was limited for the second straight day. The Eagles would need to activate him off of Injured Reserve to be eligible for Sunday's game.

The Eagles will release their final injury report on Friday afternoon.

In Pittsburgh, outside linebacker Nick Herbig has been sidelined with hip and rib injuries suffered Sunday night in Cincinnati.

Wednesday's Injury Report

Head Coach Nick Sirianni expressed optimism during his Wednesday press conference about the possible return of wide receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) this Sunday against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Both players returned to practice coming out of the bye week as they were limited participants on Wednesday.

Smith has missed five of the last six games with his hamstring injury. He said he "felt good" after Wednesday's practice and that he's back to 100 percent. Smith has 26 catches for 310 yards and two touchdowns on the season.

Wicks was sidelined for the last three games prior to the bye with his ankle injury. In his first year in Philadelphia, Wicks has posted 19 receptions for 284 yards and a touchdown.

Running back Saquon Barkley, who has played through hamstring and back injuries, was a full participant on Wednesday. He is no longer listed as having a back injury. Fellow running back Will Shipley is on Injured Reserve with a foot injury, but returned to practice on Wednesday as a limited participant. Shipley has been sidelined since the Week 5 game in London.

Defensively, linebacker Zack Baun suffered a back injury against the Giants before the bye week and did not practice on Wednesday. He was the only Eagle on the active roster who did not participate in some capacity. Good news is that linebacker Jihaad Campbell is set to return to the lineup after missing two games with hamstring and quad injuries.
