No need for changes as Vanie Gandler backs Capital1’s rebuilding process

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:47 · source · permalink

Expectations for Capital1 skyrocketed after the Solar Spikers acquired Most Valuable Player winners Erika Santos and Vanie Gandler during the offseason.

However, the Solar Spikers fell well short of those expectations in the 2026-27 PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series despite fielding a loaded lineup that also features Bella Belen. Capital1 ended its campaign on Thursday with a 3-5 record, although it closed the series on a high note with a 26-28, 25-19, 21-25, 25-19, 15-10 victory over the Galeries Tower Highrisers at the SM Mall of Asia Arena.

While the disappointing finish was undeniable, Gandler understands that building chemistry with her new teammates and adjusting to the system will take time.

“We said naman we’ll be patient before this conference, and now more than ever is the time that we need to be patient,” said the 2026 PVL AFC MVP.

“Just because we didn’t reach the expectations of all the people making it to the playoffs, it doesn’t mean our team isn’t growing and getting better,” she continued.

(C) PVL Images

Among the challenges Santos and Gandler had to navigate during the conference was the all-to-play rule, which limited their opportunities to develop chemistry with their teammates on the court.

The duo led the Solar Spikers in their final victory of the first leg of the AFC. Santos finished with 26 points, while Gandler recorded 20 points, 16 digs, and 10 receptions.

“Coming into this first conference, we said naman na we’re still trying to get our momentum. And I know that a lot of people have a lot of expectations for this team, and I understand why because we are a good team, but volleyball is really a team sport. We’re still struggling with our connection, but each loss teaches us more,” shared the Ateneo de Manila University product.

“Of course, the all-to-play was also a challenge to us because we really have to build a connection with everyone in the team and not just the starting six.”

“Of course, the all-to-play was also a challenge to us because we really have to build a connection with everyone in the team and not just the starting six.”

That will no longer be a hurdle when the Sigla Series begins in January 2027, as the all-to-play rule will be lifted, allowing teams to field their preferred starting six.

Gandler believes that the difficult campaign, which saw Capital1 suffer four consecutive losses before Thursday’s victory, is part of the process of building a cohesive unit and realizing the team’s full potential.

“I don’t think there’s anything that needs to be changed or what. It’s really part of the process; as an athlete, we really have to go through these losses, setbacks, and of course everyone looks at the wins,” said Gandler.

“Us, we have to look at how we improve. If I didn’t perform as well today, it means I know what more I can do next game. We’re discovering more about ourselves, especially on the side of our maturity, because Capital1 is a team that is still building a culture, so how we deal with these losses and challenges is what will make us Capital1 in the future.”
