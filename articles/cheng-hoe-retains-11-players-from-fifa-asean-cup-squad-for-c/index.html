Cheng Hoe Retains 11 Players From FIFA ASEAN Cup Squad For China, Yemen Friendlies

TheTools · 11.03.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR, Nov 3 (Bernama) -- National interim head coach Tan Cheng Hoe has retained 11 players from the 23-man squad that finished third in the recent FIFA ASEAN Cup for next week’s Tier 1 international friendlies against China and Yemen.

The Football Association of Malaysia (FAM) said in a statement that Cheng Hoe had also recalled three players from the 2026 ASEAN Hyundai Cup, which was held in July and October, while naming 10 other players, including two newcomers, namely Ahmad Haziq Kutty Abba (Terengganu FC) and A. Selvan (Selangor FC).

The Harimau Malaya players will report for duty on Monday (Nov 9) and leave for China on Tuesday (Nov 10).

Malaysia will take on China on Nov 14 at 8 pm before facing Yemen on Nov 17 at 2 pm. Both matches will be held at the Zhaoqing New District Sports Centre Stadium in Guangdong, China.

Based on the latest FIFA world rankings, Malaysia are currently ranked 133rd in the world, while China are 96th and Yemen 144th.
