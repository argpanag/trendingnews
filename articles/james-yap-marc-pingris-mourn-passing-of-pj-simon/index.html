James Yap, Marc Pingris mourn passing of PJ Simon

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:52 · source · permalink

James Yap and Marc Pingris mourned the passing of their longtime friend and teammate PJ Simon.

Simon passed away on Friday. He was 46.

In a post, Yap thanked Simon for all the memories they shared.

“Grabe kalit kaayo ni, Part. Wala gyud damha. Salamat sa tanan ka maayo nimo, Part, sa tanan nato panag uban na dili jud nako makalimtan. Daghan pa man ta unta basketball tour na buhaton. Sakita ani Part,” Yap wrote.

(This happened so suddenly, man. I never expected this at all. Thanks for all your kindness—for all the time we spent together, which I’ll never forget. We still had so many basketball tours to do together. This hurts so much, man.)

“Please continue to watch over your family and give them strength during this difficult time. You will surely be missed,” Yap added.

Pingris, meanwhile, said Simon was an inspiration to the youth and described him as a kind person.

“‘Tol gusto ko lang magpasalamat sa’yo. Salamat dahil madami ka pinasaya na mga kabataan at mga manunuod sa PBA man or sa labas,” Pingris wrote.

“Napakabait mo na tao, kaibigan, anak, ama... Masakit man na ganito ka nawala pero nanatili ka sa puso ng bawat napasaya mo na tao, isa na ako d’on.”

(Brother, I just want to thank you for bringing happiness to so many people, especially the youth and those who watched you play in the PBA and beyond. You were a kind person, friend, son, and father. It hurts that you had to leave us this way, but you will remain in the hearts of everyone whose lives you touched. I am one of them.)
