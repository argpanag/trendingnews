Taylor Swift Breaks Spotify Records With "Patient Zero"

TheTools · 26.09.2026 13:32 · source · permalink

The previously unreleased song released by Taylor Swift yesterday as part of The Life of a Showgirl: Encore record has already scored some records of its own.

Spotify says that one of the four Swift songs released yesterday – “Patient Zero” – immediately became Spotify’s most-streamed song by a female artist in a single day in 2026 so far. And Swift herself became the most-streamed female artist in a single day this year.

As of this morning, the four new songs from Swift’s album occupied the top four spots on Spotify’s Top 50 – Global list. So far, “Patient Zero” has been played 13,709,109 times its release yesterday. “Cleveland” is at 9,620,630; “Pink Clouding” has 8,785,117; and “Babylon” has 8,419,108

We can expect more streamings of “Patient Zero” – along with the three other “new” songs included in The Life of a Showgirl: Encore expanded release, including “Babylon,” “Pink Clouding” and “Cleveland!” – when Swift unveils the full, world premiere music video for “Patient Zero” during Sunday night’s MTV Video Music Awards (on CBS and MTV, streaming on Paramount+ Premium in the U.S.).

Watch on Deadline

A teaser clip of the “Patient Zero” video dropped Thursday, and even that brief bit seemed to provide enough clues to keep Swiftophiles scrambling for hidden messages. We know stars Colin Farrell and Dakota Johnson made appearances in the clip, but that’s about all we know for sure.

The Swifitian conjecture has been brewing since Swift appeared in a recent Law & Order: SVU-themed Emmy telecast skit with Mariska Hargitay. Some strange words spoken by Swift – “Saccharide. Aries. From the vineyard. North or south” – that have had die-hards deciphering ever since.

Maybe all will be revealed Sunday. Or maybe not.
