Probe ongoing after boy killed in fatal e-bike crash with bus in Norwich city centre

TheTools · 07.09.2026 12:14 · source · permalink

An investigation is "ongoing" into the death of a boy who died after the electric bike he was riding was involved in a crash with a bus in Norwich.

A probe was launched after a crash between the boy, who was riding a black e-bike, and a bus at the junction of All Saints Green and Surrey Street in Norwich.

The teenage cyclist was airlifted to Addenbrooke's Hospital in Cambridge following the crash, which happened just after 6.50pm on Monday, June 29.

But he died from his injuries two weeks later on Monday, July 13.

Police are now appealing for witnesses and anyone with relevant dashcam footage.

No arrests have been made but a Norfolk Constabulary spokesman said the investigation was "ongoing".

The bus driver was unharmed.

Anyone with any information is asked to get in touch with police via 101 quoting reference NC-29062026-514.
