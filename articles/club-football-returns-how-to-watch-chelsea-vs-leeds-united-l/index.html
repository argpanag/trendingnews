Club football returns! How to watch Chelsea vs Leeds United - Leeds United

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

Leeds United return to Premier League action this weekend as Daniel Farke’s side travel to Stamford Bridge to face Chelsea on Saturday, with kick-off scheduled for 3pm.

The Whites, currently ninth in the table, will be looking to build on the 1-0 win over Tottenham in the last outing before the international break.

Chelsea, meanwhile, sit fifth and will also be aiming to return to winning ways after suffering a 1-0 defeat against Sunderland last time out.

Here is everything you need to know about how to watch the fixture live.

PRE-MATCH SHOW

Coverage starts from 2:30pm with the official pre-match show, live on LUTV and across the club’s digital platforms.

Leeds United alumni Simon Grayson and Michael Bridges will be in the studio, previewing the action with their thoughts on team news, tactical set-up and the key battles to watch at Stamford Bridge.

LUTV AUDIO COMMENTARY

Supporters unable to watch live can still stay across all the action via LUTV’s live audio commentary.

Bryn Law will be your guide from Stamford Bridge alongside Leeds United legend Tony Dorigo, offering expert insight and analysis throughout the 90 minutes.

FOLLOW ON SOCIAL MEDIA

Fans can also keep across all the latest via the club’s official social media channels, with live updates, behind-the-scenes access and exclusive matchday content on Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, WhatsApp.

From team news through to key moments and post-match reaction, Leeds United’s digital platforms will ensure supporters remain connected to the action at the Stamford Bridge wherever you are in the world.
