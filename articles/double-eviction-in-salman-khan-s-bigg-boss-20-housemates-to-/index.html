'Bigg Boss 20' will reportedly see a double eviction for the first time. Step inside to learn which contestants are leaving the 'Bigg Boss 20' house this week.

By Prajeta Roy Published: Nov 22, 2026  •  Updated: Nov 22, 2026 | 20:22:10 IST

Bigg Boss 20 has created a significant buzz due to its unique theme this season. Each contestant began their journey inside the Bigg Boss 20 house with two lifelines. As of now, all remaining contestants of Bigg Boss 20 have one lifeline left. It was Mahhi Vij who was holding two lifelines. 

advertisement

However, last week, after receiving the fewest votes, Mahhi lost her extra lifeline. In the last two weeks, housemates escaped the eviction process. Now, according to the latest buzz, Bigg Boss 20 will reportedly see a double eviction for the first time.

Bigg Boss 20 to have double eviction for the first time after two weeks of 'no elimination'? Here's what we know

This week, the contestants who have been nominated are Love Gill, Kanika Mann, Mahhi Vij, Gullu, Aamrapali Dubey, Qazi Touqeer, Manish Goel, Yung DSA, and ScoutOP. Week 9 of Bigg Boss 20 didn’t show any eviction as it was Diwali. Next week, that is Week 10, Mahhi Vij received the fewest votes, and there was no eviction. Now that it's Week 11 of Bigg Boss 20, as per social media buzz, there will be a double eviction.

If the buzz is true, it will be Bigg Boss 20’s first double elimination. Some other reports claim that Manish Goel will allegedly exit the show. Manish, who entered the show as a wildcard, will reportedly exit due to a serious leg injury. A Bigg Boss 20 insider page claimed that Salman Khan will clarify that Manish was not the contestant with the lowest votes. The page also claimed that Mahhi Vij is again safe and there will be one eviction this week.

Manish Goel and Qazi Touqeer's recent fight inside Bigg Boss 20 house explained

For the last few weeks, the audience has been seeing an ongoing clash between Qazi Touqeer and Manish Goel. A few days ago, a promo surfaced online in which Manish was seen losing his cool as Qazi called him 'professor.' Soon, Manish’s wife, Poonam Narula, came in support of her husband and explained what made Manish lose his temper.

advertisement

"Ever since Manish entered the house, Qazi has constantly poked him with names like Gyani Baba and Professor. Qazi kept taking it further with condescending remarks and age-shaming. When Manish finally asked him to stop and draw a line, Qazi told the camera that he would make Manish cry and wouldn't stop."

Poonam shared that Manish never approaches Qazi to pick a fight. She mentioned that her husband is emotional and is genuinely a nice family man. Things escalated further inside the Bigg Boss 20 house during the recent nomination task. When Qazi called Manish a 'professor' again, he lost his cool. Later, he got emotional and broke down.

advertisement.

At the time, Manish recalled one of the darkest phases of his life. He said to the fellow contestants that an incident took place when he was 12 or 14, which he never wants to revisit. He went on to explain to Gullu, Love Gill and Kanika Mann that he felt Qazi had deliberately touched upon something deeply personal that triggered him.

Manish got emotional and struggled to share his feelings. He said that the incident was very dark and happened when he was in school. He mentioned that he tries not to think about it at all. Cutting the conversation short, Manish said that the dark incident prompted him to try to commit suicide.
