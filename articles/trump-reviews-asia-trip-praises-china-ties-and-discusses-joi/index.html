Trump reviews Asia trip, praises China ties and discusses joint AI safeguards

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

By YIFAN XU in Washington | chinadaily.com.cn | Updated: 2026-11-21 14:23

US President Donald Trump reviewed his recent Asia trip at a White House news conference on Friday, speaking about his meeting with President Xi Jinping, his impressions of Shenzhen, the first-ever trilateral summit involving China, Russia and the United States, and cooperation on artificial intelligence safeguards.

Trump described his meeting with Xi on Thursday as "very productive", saying the two leaders had discussed progress in economic relations. He spoke positively of his relationship with Xi and recalled that the Chinese president had personally welcomed him at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport on Wednesday afternoon.

"We have a very great relationship," Trump said.

Trump praised Chinese cities as clean and pleasant, and said that "there's no crime" in them.

He also singled out Shenzhen for its rapid development. "It's clean. It's immaculate," he said. Trump noted that Shenzhen had been a fishing village decades ago and had since developed into a modern city. He said he had closely examined the roads during his visit and found them "flawless". "I have great respect for the job they've done," Trump said.

When asked whether he was considering easing restrictions on chip exports to China, Trump said, "We're talking about a very comprehensive deal with China." He did not provide details of the proposed agreement.

According to China’s Foreign Ministry, the two sides believed the meeting was positive and constructive. They agreed to formulate an action plan on building a constructive relationship of strategic stability, implement the consensus between the two presidents, and build a constructive China-US relationship of strategic stability on the basis of respect, fairness and reciprocity.

Trump also gave a positive assessment of Thursday's first-ever trilateral summit with Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen. "And the relationship was fantastic between the three of us," he said.

Xi, Putin and Trump held a China-Russia-US summit in Shenzhen on Thursday afternoon, on the sidelines of the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen.

Following the meeting, the three countries issued the China-Russia-US Heads of State Declaration toward Peace.

The three heads of state reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought. The three countries will conduct a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons. The declaration also addressed United Nations reform and energy supplies, among other issues.

Referring to the technology by his preferred term — "superintelligence" — Trump said the United States and China were ahead of other countries in the field and that he and Xi had agreed to work together on safeguards.

"So President Xi and I agree that we're going to do some joint guardrails," Trump said. He did not specify what the safeguards would involve or when they might be introduced.

On the same day, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC that Trump and Xi had agreed that humans should always be involved in any military decisions made by AI and that the technology should not be allowed to act autonomously in major military actions.

According to a readout of Thursday's meeting in Shenzhen released by China's Foreign Ministry, Xi said China and the United States should strengthen communication and coordination, prevent the security risks of AI, and ensure that AI is always under human control.
