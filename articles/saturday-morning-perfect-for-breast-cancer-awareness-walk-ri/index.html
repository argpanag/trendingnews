Saturday Morning Perfect For Breast Cancer Awareness Walk – RiverScene Magazine

TheTools · 04.10.2026 05:04 · source · permalink

The Cancer Care Resource Foundation hosted its Breast Cancer Awareness Walk Saturday morning under the London Bridge in Lake Havasu.

Participants wore pink head to toe in honor of loved ones that have fought the battle of breast cancer. Jerry Aldridge emceed the event and Mayor Cal Sheehy, along with city council woman Michele Lin, read a proclamation from the Mayor’s office.
