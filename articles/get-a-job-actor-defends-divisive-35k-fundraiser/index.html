‘Get a job’: Actor defends divisive $35k fundraiser

TheTools · 08.11.2026 22:05 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Showbiz

Updated November 8, 2026 - 10.19pm. First published at 10.05pm

Former child star Brandon ‘Bug’ Hall has defended his decision to ask fans for financial support as he and his wife prepare for the arrival of their sixth child.

Hall, who is best known for his role as Alfalfa in the 1994 film Little Rascals, launched a GoFundMe fundraiser on Friday after falling on hard times.

Brandon ‘Bug’ Hall has asked fans for financial support for him, wife Jill and their children. GoFundMe/Brandon Hall

In the days since, he has copped significant backlash from critics urging him to “get a real job” instead of asking strangers on the internet for money.

“Put your pride down and get a real job,” one X user commented on Hall’s post about his fundraiser.

“This is embarrassing, stop impregnating your wife when you can’t even keep your family afloat. Set your priorities straight.”

Former child star Bug Hall played Alfalfa in The Little Rascals. Universal Pictures

Hall replied, “I’m going to keep impregnating my wife”, but clarified in another post that he “mostly worked full-time” while also building a farm and home for his family this year.

Some critics slammed him as a hypocrite based on a post made on X in 2025, where he claimed “I beg and mooch from no-one. You clearly missed the important details of how I actually provide for my family”.

Others called Hall “lazy” and “a grifter” for asking fans to support him financially.

Hall’s GoFundMe started with a goal of $US2000 ($2900) and had raised nearly $US24,000 ($35,000) at the time of writing.

He claimed on X that he planned to pull the fundraiser down once it hit the original goal, but it had been reported so many times that GoFundMe had paused the funds for review.

Hall with three of his children. X/@bug_hall

“The fundraiser is currently being mass reported by all the usual suspects. Can’t delete until the first funds transfer,” he wrote.

“Oh irony of ironies … this would have been closed down within 48 hours, had not the usual seethers and teeth gnashers mass reported it.

“Now it has to remain up until the first funds transfer. Even the demons do God’s will in their malice. Deus Vult [God wills it].”

Hall launched the fundraiser last week with a plea to fans to help him catch up on bills before his wife gives birth to their sixth child.

“Self-cancelled” child star Bug Hall and his wife Jill are preparing to welcome their sixth child.  X/@Bug_Hall

“We’re all overjoyed to be where we are, and we’re even enjoying roughing it, but baby six is less than a month away, and I really need some help getting the last few things buttoned up beforehand,” he wrote on GoFundMe.

“My hope is to finalise everything here over the next two weeks so I can take some work until the baby arrives.

“Times are hard for everyone, and I’m no more deserving than anyone else, but I ask in your charity that you help where you can, whether monetarily, by sharing this, or by prayer and sacrifice for the Hall-Barnetts of the Holy Cross.”

Hall stepped away from Hollywood after “self-cancelling” six years ago and started over as a “radical Catholic extremist” with a focus on “medieval morals”.

He moved his wife and five kids to an 80-acre property in rural Arkansas, US, to live off-grid and announced in January that he had taken a “vow of poverty”.

He donated the money he made as a child star to charity, according to a report by the Daily Mail, and gave away most of his belongings with the goal of living “free of any need for personal income as possible”.

Hall moved his family to an off-grid proprtey in rural USA. GoFundMe/Brandon Hall

Hall and his wife Jill married in 2017 and currently have four daughters and one son, with another baby due next year.

Hall sparked outrage when his son was born in 2024 by announcing the baby boy’s arrival with a post captioned “I have an heir!“, despite already having four daughters.

When followers questioned why Hall only considered his son an heir, he replied, “I said heir, not dishwasher”, which only ignited more fury.
