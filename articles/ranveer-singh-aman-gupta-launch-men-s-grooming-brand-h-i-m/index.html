Ranveer Singh, Aman Gupta launch men’s grooming brand H.I.M.

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:55 · source · permalink

The brand enters the men’s personal care market with eight products across face, body and hair care, powered by its proprietary IN·XT™ molecule.

Ranveer Singh and Aman Gupta have launched H.I.M. (Handsome Indian Man), a men’s personal care brand developed for Indian skin, climate and biology. The brand debuts with eight products across face, body and hair care.

The products are powered by IN·XT™, a proprietary molecule developed exclusively for the brand. The formulations are based on research into Indian male skin and are designed to address conditions such as heat, humidity, pollution and hard water.

The launch is accompanied by a brand film featuring both co-founders.

H.I.M. aims to address what it describes as a gap in the men’s grooming category, where products have traditionally been adapted from formulations designed for other consumers. The brand positions self-care as an essential part of the modern Indian man's routine.

Speaking about the launch, Ranveer Singh, co-founder of H.I.M., said, “Someone once told me I was ‘too handsome for an Indian.’ Being Indian should never make being handsome a surprise. I’ve always taken pride in looking after myself, but I started to realise my products were never really made for me, or for our skin, our weather and our water. Aman and I worked with scientists to understand Indian male skin and develop H.I.M. I tested every formulation, from the first samples to the final products. I want a man to pick up H.I.M and know that someone has put that thought and care into it for him. Because handsome and Indian were never two different things.”

Aman Gupta, co-founder of H.I.M., added, “Almost every Indian man has borrowed a face wash from his sister, wife or girlfriend at some point. Me included.

I look at consumers and categories for a living, and very few categories in India are moving like this one. India’s beauty and personal care market is ₹2.2 lakh crore today and is projected to nearly double to ₹4 lakh crore by FY2031. By 2030, 

Even searches for men’s skincare routines are up 850% over the last five years. Indian men are finally talking about their skin, reading labels and building routines, and it’s one of the hottest conversations in the industry right now. With the category growing at over 12% a year, we see an enormous opportunity to build brands that are relevant to the next generation of consumers.

For all of this to happen I wanted the right partner for it. Ranveer has been one of the most obsessive co-founders I have ever worked with. We have all seen the passion he brings to his films. I have now seen his rigour, involvement and obsession up close while building H.I.M."

The H.I.M range is priced from ₹299 to ₹899 and is available from today on HIM Science Website, Amazon and Blinkit.
