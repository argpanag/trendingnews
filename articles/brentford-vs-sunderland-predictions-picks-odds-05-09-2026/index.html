Brentford vs Sunderland – Best bets of the match

Brentford vs Sunderland Preview

Brentford head into this fixture sitting third in the Premier League table, a remarkable position built on four wins from their last five outings. Keith Andrews will be without Antoni Milambo and Sepp van den Berg through knee and groin injuries respectively, while Fábio Carvalho continues his recovery from a cruciate ligament tear. The Bees have been devastating at the Gtech Community Stadium this season, and their 3-0 demolition of Sunderland back in January underlines their superiority in this fixture.

Sunderland occupy 13th place and arrive in west London with mixed recent results, collecting two wins, two draws, and a defeat from their last five matches. The Black Cats did beat Brentford 2-1 at the Stadium of Light in August 2025, proving they can trouble this opponent on their day. However, replicating that performance away from home against a side in such commanding form represents a significantly tougher proposition for Régis Le Bris and his squad.
