Persib Beats FC Seoul 3-1 as Beckham Seals Asian Campaign Win

Bandung. Persib Bandung secured an impressive 3-1 victory over South Korean side FC Seoul in the AFC Champions League Two (ACL Two) group stage on Wednesday, capitalizing on home advantage at Gelora Bandung Lautan Api Stadium.

Mariano Peralta, Luka Menalo, and Beckham Putra Nugraha scored for the Indonesian champions, while Leonardo Ruiz netted FC Seoul's only goal in the Group Stage clash on Nov. 25.

Persib made an aggressive start in front of a packed home crowd and took the lead just six minutes into the game. Peralta finished off a well-delivered pass from Sandy Walsh to put the hosts ahead 1-0.

FC Seoul responded by stepping up their attacking efforts, seeking to break through Persib's defense. The visitors eventually found the equalizer in the 37th minute when Ruiz converted to make it 1-1.

The parity lasted only four minutes, however, as Persib restored their lead in the 41st minute. Peralta turned provider, delivering a precise pass that Menalo finished clinically to put the hosts 2-1 ahead heading into halftime.

The contest remained competitive after the break, with both teams exchanging attacks in search of another goal.

FC Seoul's hopes of mounting a comeback suffered a major setback in the 71st minute when Moon Seon-min was sent off, leaving the South Korean side with 10 men.

Persib took advantage of their numerical superiority and sealed the victory in the 79th minute. Young midfielder Beckham Putra Nugraha found the net to extend the lead to 3-1, effectively putting the game beyond FC Seoul's reach.

The visitors were unable to find another goal before the final whistle, as Persib comfortably secured all three points on home soil.
