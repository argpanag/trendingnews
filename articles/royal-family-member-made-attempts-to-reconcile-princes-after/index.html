Royal family member made attempts to reconcile princes after years of tension

TheTools · 30.10.2026 08:18 · source · permalink

A member of the royal family reportedly attempted to mend the relationship between Prince William and Prince Harry.

The brothers' relationship remains strained and was said to deteriorate after the Duke and Duchess of Sussex stepped back from royal duties in 2020 and issued a series of public criticisms of the royal family.

Their last public appearance together was at their father's coronation in 2023.

The then Prince Charles walks to church at Sandringham with William, Kate, Meghan and Harry on Christmas Day, 2018 (Image: PA)

It is understood that rugby player Mike Tindall, who is married to the cousin of Harry and William, Zara Tindall, is said to have made repeated efforts to bring the brothers together.

This included inviting both to dinner or drinks in hopes of brokering reconciliation, according to reports.

The royal editor of Woman & Home, Emily Andrews, wrote in a recent issue: "It came to naught.

"And Mike recently made a quip about a time long ago 'when Harry was fun', perhaps implying how he feels about the Duke now."

The Tindalls are expected to attend Christmas at the Sandringham Estate this year as they did in 2025.

Harry and Meghan are reportedly set to return to the United States with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, for the festive season.

It is believed that they will not attend King Charles' traditional Christmas at the Norfolk estate, a royal custom dating back to 1870.

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex's decision to move to the UK earlier this year had fuelled speculation about their future living arrangements.

Norfolk had been considered a possible location due to its association with the royal Sandringham Estate and the Prince and Princess of Wales' residence at Anmer Hall.

The pair have now settled in the Cotswolds and are not thought to have visited the king or his brother since this move.
