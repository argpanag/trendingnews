Statement following the publications by NOS Sport and In de Waaier - Team Picnic PostNL

TheTools · 15.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

We have taken note of the publications by NOS Sport and In de Waaier about Team Picnic PostNL. The accounts shared by current and former riders and staff members affect us. At the same time, we do not recognise ourselves in the overall picture presented in the publications.

The fact that experiences differ does not diminish the seriousness of the accounts that have now come to light. We will therefore commission an independent investigation into how our team culture is experienced in practice and to what extent it reflects the culture we strive for.

The team and our three promises

Since the team was founded in 2008, three promises have formed the foundation of our approach to professional cycling.

The first promise: fair, healthy and responsible sport.

Our anti-doping policy is an important part of this promise. It is not only about what we communicate externally, but also about the mindset and ethical considerations that guide our decisions in practice.

The second promise: safe collaboration based on shared values.

We want everyone within the team, on and off the bike, to be treated with respect and to feel free to express an opinion, encourage one another, ask questions and raise concerns. Mutual commitment and integrity are central to this, helping us create an environment where people can develop, perform and enjoy working together.

The third promise: continuity and job security.

We have made a commitment to do everything we can to secure the future of the team and, in doing so, preserve jobs within professional cycling.

While this may sound self-evident, job security in professional cycling is anything but. The sport’s economic model falls seriously short. Every year, several teams disappear, with the associated loss of jobs. This has become almost normal, even though it should never be the case in a sport of this scale.

That is why we have been working to secure the future of the team since 2008. Over the years, we have provided employment for hundreds of people.

The NOS investigation

According to NOS, it spent months trying to speak with current and former riders and staff members. As soon as we received indications that the investigation was taking place, we offered to meet with NOS to discuss our philosophy and vision, and to give the broadcaster the opportunity to speak with current team members and colleagues. NOS chose not to take up that offer. We regret this.

Last Monday, we were then given only a few days to respond to a number of questions, either in writing or on camera. In response, we made several further attempts to speak with NOS. These attempts were unsuccessful.

Independent investigation into team culture

In a performance-driven environment such as professional sport, things can go wrong. That is why there are several ways for people within our team to raise problems and concerns. Riders have a personal coach, there is a Rider Committee, and staff members can turn to confidential advisers. If people have felt unsafe within our organisation or have felt they lacked sufficient opportunity to raise concerns, we want to understand how that could have happened.

We are proud of what we have built since 2008 together with the many people who are, or have been, part of and associated with this team. Precisely for that reason, we believe it is important to take a critical look at ourselves.

We will therefore commission an independent, professional investigation into our team culture. We will take its findings seriously and implement any improvements that may be needed, out of respect for everyone who is, or has been, part of Team Picnic PostNL.
