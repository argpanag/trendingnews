Paris Hilton made another head-turning appearance during New York Fashion Week. The media personality stepped out for the Alice + Olivia Spring/Summer 2027 NYFW presentation in an all-black ensemble that blended sheer elegance with dramatic flair. Naturally, the see-through lace dress became one of the standout fashion moments, especially combined with her luxe Gucci bag.

Paris Hilton elevates completely see-through lace dress with chic Gucci bag for NYFW show

Paris Hilton attended Stacey Bendet's "Welcome to Wonderland" NYFW presentation on September 10 in a see-through lace dress paired with a dazzling Gucci bag. She later shared photos from the event on Instagram, writing, "Sliving in Wonderland with @AliceAndOlivia and @ParivieBeauty for #NYFW."

For the occasion, Hilton wore a black floral lace dress by Alice + Olivia. The high-neck design featured intricate sheer lace throughout, layered over opaque underwear for coverage. Meanwhile, a flowing floor-length black cape added movement and extra drama to the monochromatic look.

The Gucci bag brought a glamorous finishing touch to the outfit. She carried a silver Crystal Medium Evening Top Handle Bag, which sparkled beautifully against the all-black ensemble. The shimmering accessory added contrast while elevating the statement look.

Hilton also accessorized with sleek black sunglasses and delicate diamond stud earrings. Her signature platinum-blonde hair was styled pin-straight with a center part, creating a polished finish. In addition, she opted for glamorous makeup featuring softly defined eyes, glowing skin, and nude-pink lips that complemented the sophisticated styling.

The presentation celebrated Alice + Olivia's Spring/Summer 2027 collection with a whimsical "Welcome to Wonderland" theme. Guests stepped into an immersive fairytale-inspired setting featuring romantic designs and interactive AI-powered virtual try-on experiences. The event drew celebrities, fashion insiders, and industry tastemakers throughout the evening.

The NYFW appearance perfectly balanced bold fashion with timeless glamor. Her sheer lace dress delivered plenty of drama, while the flowing cape kept the silhouette elegant. Paired with the dazzling Gucci handbag, the look proved that Hilton continues to embrace statement dressing with confidence.
