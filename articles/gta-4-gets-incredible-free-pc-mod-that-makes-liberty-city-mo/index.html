GTA 4 gets incredible free PC mod that makes Liberty City more alive and dangerous than ever

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:13 · source · permalink

Free GTA 4 PC mod brings a Nemesis-style system to Liberty City, with returning enemies, gang wars and NPCs who remember your actions

Richard Breslin

Liberty City is one of the most popular and nostalgic locations in the Grand Theft Auto series, having featured in multiple games, most notably GTA 3 and GTA 4. However, with 2008's GTA 4, starring Niko Bellic, the immersion of this fictional take on New York City was taken to another level.

Yet, thanks to a free PC mod, GTA IV is becoming even more immersive with a gameplay mechanic inspired by the legendary Nemesis system, made famous by Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor and later patented by Warner Bros.

Following a similar Nemesis-style mod for Red Dead Redemption 2, enRAGE IV – Living City and Nemesis System brings the concept to Liberty City, giving players another reason to revisit Rockstar Games' beloved open-world classic. And best of all, it's free to download right now.

GTA 4's free Nemesis-style mod makes Liberty City more dangerous than ever

Thankfully, modder mcchickenwingg has brought their own take on the legendary Nemesis system to the PC version of GTA IV, available to download for free via Nexus Mods. With enRAGE IV – Living City and Nemesis System, NPCs can remember your actions, retain injuries and even have family members seeking revenge, alongside an evolving criminal underworld.

One of the best features of the Nemesis system is that enemies remember previous encounters, whether you've humiliated them, left them with scars or defeated them in battle. As a result, they can develop personal grudges and seek revenge when you least expect it.

The Nemesis system also features a hierarchy in which Orcs can rise through the ranks by defeating rivals or taking advantage of another captain's death, creating an ever-changing power struggle.

It was a gameplay mechanic way ahead of its time when it debuted in 2014's Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor. Yet, despite Warner Bros. patenting the system, it hasn't appeared in another released game since 2017's Middle-earth: Shadow of War, which is a shame considering its potential in other open-world games.

The best features of GTA 4's enRAGE IV – Living City and Nemesis System

According to the mod's Nexus Mods page, here are some of its best features:

  • Enemies remember you: NPCs remember previous encounters, including threats, robberies and humiliation, which can lead to personal grudges.
  • Revenge and rivalries: Surviving enemies can return with scars, backup and a personal vendetta against you.
  • Family revenge: Kill someone, and their relatives may come looking for payback.
  • Gang hierarchies: Eight criminal organisations feature bosses, lieutenants and captains who can rise through the ranks.
  • Gang wars: Rival gangs fight for control of Liberty City's neighbourhoods, even when you're not around.
  • Gang interrogations: Hold gang members at gunpoint to uncover secrets, but be warned, some may lie and lead you into an ambush.
  • Dynamic street events: Encounter gang patrols, drug deals, funerals and police raids throughout Liberty City.
  • Local newspapers: Five newspapers report on gang activity, killings and other events happening across the city.
  • Endless gang wars: An optional feature introduces new criminal organisations to replace those you've wiped out.

How to download and install GTA 4's free Nemesis-style mod

  • Visit Nexus Mods and download enRAGE IV – Living City and Nemesis System for free.
  • Make sure you have a compatible version of GTA IV: Complete Edition (1.2.0.59) or versions 1.0.7/1.0.8.
  • Install the three required tools: ASI Loader, ScriptHook and ScriptHookDotNet, ensuring they're compatible with your game version.
  • Extract the downloaded mod files.
  • Copy enRAGE_IV.cs and enRAGE_IV.ini into the scripts folder in your GTA IV installation directory. Create the folder if necessary.
  • Launch GTA IV and press F7 to access the mod's menu.
  • Press U to interact with NPCs and begin creating your own rivalries across Liberty City.

You'll need all three additional tools for the mod to work, but there's no need to install the original Red Dead Redemption 2 enRAGE mod.
