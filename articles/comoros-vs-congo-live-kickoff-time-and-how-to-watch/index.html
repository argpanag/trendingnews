Comoros vs Congo: How and Where to Watch LIVE

Comoros will host Congo on Friday, November 13, for Matchday 3 of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualifiers.

The encounter will take place at Stade Omnisports de Malouzini in Moroni, Comoros, with kickoff scheduled for 8:00 AM ET / 5:00 AM PT.

Fans can follow the match through beIN SPORTS CONNECT, with English-language streaming coverage scheduled to begin at 7:50 AM ET.

Comoros has collected three points from its opening two matches, while Congo sits at the bottom of Group G with one point.



How Comoros Comes Into the Match

Comoros enters Friday's encounter looking to build on its first victory of the qualification campaign.

The team coached by Hubert Velud began Group G with a 3-1 defeat against Cameroon on September 24.

Warmed Omari scored for Comoros, but goals from David Roi Nyengue, Bryan Mbeumo, and Danny Namaso secured all three points for the Indomitable Lions.

However, the Coelacanths recovered five days later with a 1-0 home victory over Namibia.

Benjaloud Youssouf scored the decisive goal in the 53rd minute, allowing Comoros to collect its first three points.

Velud has announced a 24-player squad for the November double-header, featuring several important returning players.

Faïz Selemani, Rafiki Saïd, and Ismaël Boura are back in the national team, alongside Saïd Bakari and Anfane Saïd Djambae.

Their returns provide additional options for a team hoping to secure another important home victory.


How Congo Comes Into the Match

Congo travels to Moroni searching for its first victory after a difficult start to the qualifiers.

The Red Devils opened their Group G campaign with a 1-0 defeat against Namibia on September 24.

Bethuel Muzeu scored the only goal of the encounter early in the second half, leaving Congo without points after its opening fixture.

Five days later, Congo earned its first point with a 1-1 draw against Cameroon.

Geltany Bantsiele opened the scoring in the 24th minute, but an own goal by goalkeeper Melvin Zinga in the 53rd minute brought the visitors level.

Head coach Claude Le Roy has named a 25-player squad for the upcoming double-header.

The selection includes Thievy Bifouma, Gaïus Makouta, Morgan Poaty, and Mons Bassouamina.

With one point from two matches, Congo will need a positive result to improve its position in the qualification race.



Comoros vs Congo: A Crucial Double-Header in Group G

Group G remains extremely competitive after the opening two rounds.

Cameroon leads with four points, while Comoros and Namibia have collected three each. Congo follows with one point.

The top two teams will qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, which will be hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

Friday's match will be the first of two encounters between Comoros and Congo during the November international window.

The teams will meet again on Tuesday, November 17, at Stade Alphonse-Massamba-Débat in Brazzaville, Congo.

With only three points separating the group leader and the bottom-placed team, both matches could have significant implications for qualification.
