'I'm not gonna lie' - Ward-Prowse says what we're all thinking after beating Nuno

TheTools · 25.10.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

SAINTS star James Ward-Prowse admitted getting one over former boss Nuno Espirito Santo made the 3-2 win over West Ham that little bit better.

The midfielder endured a difficult spell at odds with Espirito Santo at London Stadium, before rejoining Saints in a bargain deal this summer.

It followed also being bombed out of the squad at Nottingham Forest by Nuno, when he had been on loan at the Premier League side previously.

But he produced an impressive performance on his West Ham return, recording 11 defensive contributions – a total not beaten by any other player.

And Ward-Prowse made no secret of his satisfaction at helping Saints claim a statement victory against a West Ham side tipped for promotion.

“Yeah, I'm not gonna lie, of course it does,” he said, when asked whether beating his former manager added extra satisfaction.

“You're coming into the game wanting to play well first and foremost for yourself and the team effort, but yes, it's a fantastic win.

“I think personally it's always a big game because you're coming back to a team you used to play for and you want to do well and equip yourself well.

“But the bigger picture is about the team, and obviously West Ham are a good team, a lot of good individuals.”

Saints arrived in East London believing they could take all three points, with Ward-Prowse revealing boss Tonda Eckert had made that ambition clear.

“He made it clear to us that this was a game that we can win and that was the mindset from the first training in the week,” said the 31 year old.

“That's been the mindset throughout, so it's a huge win."

Saints raced into a two-goal lead inside 10 minutes through Kuryu Matsuki and James Bree, before former Saint Kyle Walker-Peters pulled one back.

Lewis Dobbin restored the two-goal advantage before half-time, with Tomas Soucek’s stoppage-time strike proving only a consolation.

Ward-Prowse also praised the positive atmosphere at Saints since his return, pointing to the belief instilled by Eckert and the club’s wider ambitions.

“There's nothing but positivity here,” he said. “There's a real vision right from the top down in terms of what we want to do.

“We've got an unbelievable manager that's instilled such belief in us. It wasn't coming here and trying to respect them and settle for a point.

"It was to come here and win, and we executed that from minute one.”
