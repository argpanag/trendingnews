Through Telegram, Kim Nok-wan (34), the chief who ran the sex exploitation crime group "Vigilante," was sentenced to life imprisonment. The vigilante group is an online sexual violence organization that has caused the biggest damage ever.

On the 10th, the Supreme Court's second division (Chief Justice Kwon Young-joon) confirmed the original ruling that Kim Nok-wan, who was indicted on 25 charges of violating the Youth Sex Protection Act (such as producing sexual exploitation), violating the Sexual Violence Punishment Act (filming using cameras), violating the Child Welfare Act, forced harassment, and rape.

It also maintained an order of 30 years of electronic device attachment, 10 years of employment restrictions on children, adolescents, and disabled-related institutions, and 10 years of personal information disclosure and notification. However, he was acquitted of the crime of organizing a criminal organization and activities.

The two accomplices who worked as so-called "preachers" in the vigilante group and participated in the crime were confirmed to be imprisoned for a long period of two years, a short period of one year and June, and two years and six months in prison, respectively.

From August 2020 to January 2025, Kim Nok-wan organized a vigilante group by imitating 'Room N' and called himself a 'pastor'. He was arrested and charged in February last year on charges of sadistically sexually assaulting minors and producing and distributing sexual exploitation.

The vigilante group committed the crime by threatening to distribute it to the person concerned after finding out the personal information of a woman who posts body photos on social media or meets conditions, and a man who tries to enter the Telegram "Yadongbang" or "Jindin Ambulbang." Threatened victims were taken naked pictures or used to produce sexual exploitation, and some were actually sexually assaulted.

He also laundered criminal proceeds by threatening to distribute personal information, taking money from victims and turning it into a Google gift code.

The number of victims of the vigilante group is 261, more than three times that of the similar case, "Doctor's Room" (73). Kim Nok-wan and members of the organization made more than 2,000 sexual exploitation products.

In November last year, the first trial sentenced Kim Nok-wan to life imprisonment, and 10 accomplices were sentenced to imprisonment or probation.

However, innocence was maintained for the crime of organizing and activities of criminal organizations. The reason was that as accomplices also participated in the crime under the threat of Kim Nok-wan, it was difficult to see the vigilante group as a criminal organization that combined the common purpose of committing a crime. The second trial in April this year maintained the same judgment.

At the time, the second trial court criticized, "The defendant (Kim Nok-wan) did not have a minimum respect for human dignity," adding, "It is difficult to even dare to estimate the physical and mental pain, shame, humiliation and despair that the victims must have felt in the course of the crime." "For such crimes against human rights, severe punishment is needed, and severe punishment is needed to prevent copycat crimes by sounding the alarm in society," he pointed out.
