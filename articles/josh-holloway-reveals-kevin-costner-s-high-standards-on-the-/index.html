Josh Holloway is pulling back the curtain on what life was like on the set of "Yellowstone."

The neo-Western series created by Taylor Sheridan dominated TV for all five of its seasons. The success of Sheridan's crowning achievement also led to multiple prequels and spinoffs.

The "Yellowstone" universe is a powerhouse, and Holloway played a major role. He starred as villain Roarke Morris in season three of the series, and very briefly appeared in season four.

Josh Holloway reveals what Kevin Costner was like on the "Yellowstone" set.

Now, he's shining a light on what Kevin Costner was like on set, and the high standards and work ethic the legendary actor brought to the series.

Holloway recently appeared on the "Inside of You" podcast hosted by Michael Rosenbaum, and explained that Costner wasn't afraid to call himself out on set when he didn't perform at a high level.

"What I was impressed with with him, he did a close-up with me where he’s telling me about the rules or something, and he did it, and he just went inside and he was like, ‘Ah, that sh*t was lazy.’ And then he came up and I was like, ‘He cares. He’s still working.’ And he brought it. He's in this. So, I was impressed with that. You know, that he’s still keeping his standard high. I respected that. I was like, ‘Hell yeah,'" Holloway explained.

It's worth noting that while Costner might have held himself to extremely high standards on set, his time on "Yellowstone" didn't end in great fashion.

Costner infamously split from the show during the mid-season split in season five, and never returned to ever play John Dutton again.

His exit, which led to plenty of reports of feuds behind the scenes, resulted in the final "Yellowstone" episodes feeling a bit rushed and an ending that never truly felt complete.

Don't get me wrong. The conclusion of "Yellowstone" was always clear to anyone paying attention, but without John Dutton, something just felt a little unsatisfied.
