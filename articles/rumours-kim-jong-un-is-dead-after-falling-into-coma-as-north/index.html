Rumours Kim Jong un is dead after 'falling into coma' as North Korea risks 'war'

TheTools · 19.10.2026 11:10 · source · permalink

Is he dead or alive?(Image: KNS/AFP via Getty Images)

Rumours are swirling that Kim Jong-un has died. The strange claims gained traction over the weekend, and claim that the leader of the world's most secretive nation was left in a “coma after suffering a brain haemorrhage”.

According to Wanjun Xie, Chairman of China Democracy Party (CDP), medical experts had been “dispatched” to treat him. He posted on X: “It is rumored that recently, Kim Jong-un, General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea and Supreme Leader of North Korea, suffered a severe cerebral hemorrhage and fell into a coma.

Although a team of medical experts from Peking Union Medical College Hospital was dispatched to North Korea to assist in his treatment, the poor medical conditions in North Korea forced Kim Jong-un to be transferred to Peking Union Medical College Hospital for emergency treatment.

He was last seen around five days ago

“However, due to the severity of his condition and the fact that the optimal window for treatment had passed, Kim Jong-un succumbed to his injuries and died. His body has been secretly transported back to North Korea, and the news of his death is being kept under wraps.”

He later added: “Rumours circulate that Kim Jong-un has died, thwarting Kim Ju-ae's succession plans and potentially plunging North Korea into civil war. The Chinese Communist Party has reportedly dispatched 9,000 troops to Pyongyang to assist Kim Ju-ae in stabilising her regime.

“In preparation for a potential civil war in North Korea, the Northern Theater Command of the Chinese People's Liberation Army has entered a state of high alert (Level III), and all troops within the theatre command have been ordered to...Leave, family visits, and demobilisation/retirement are suspended; personnel who have left the area are recalled; and strict control over personnel entry and exit is enforced!”

He could be dead

Another account on X claimed that the rumours stemmed from defector Ji Seong-ho, who it had been claimed had spoken to Yonhap News.

However, the Daily Star has found no trace of any interview with him, and found that he actually hasn't spoken to the media for several years – and stopped using his own YouTube channel two years ago.

Kim Jong-un's last public appearance was on October 13, opening a new museum filled with some of the biggest missiles most humans have ever seen.

The National Defence Revolution Museum under the Second Economy Commission of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea was inaugurated with “due ceremony on the occasion of the 81st founding anniversary of the WPK,” officials said at the time.

There are currently no updates on North Korea's main news site, PyongYang times, and there are no concrete pieces of evidence to back up the claims – but we will be keeping an eye on the situation given the “Tubby Tyrant” was recently warned about his diet, given his sizeable weight.

The despot – who many call a “Tubby Tyrant” – was the subject of a warning by South Korean spies that he is now so fat he is at risk of dying of a heart attack, like how his father died.
