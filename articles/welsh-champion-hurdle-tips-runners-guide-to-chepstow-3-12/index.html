Welsh Champion Hurdle tips & runners guide to Chepstow 3.12

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:09 · source · permalink

/

/

Le Beau Madrik spearheads the Skelton charge…

A bumper field of 17 runners take their chance in the Welsh Champion Hurdle at Chepstow on Friday. Champion trainer Dan Skelton fires four darts including handicap debutant Le Beau Madrik.

Welsh handler Sam Thomas and jockey Dylan Johnston are hoping to claim the spoils for the third year in a row. The combo are pinning their hopes on Doctor Blue, who races from 1lb out of the limited handicap.

Morebattle winner Captain Hugo is seen for the first time since finishing second in the Scottish Champion Hurdle at the tail end of last term. Laafi makes the trip across the Irish Sea to head the weights, Gibbs Island brings C&D form to the table after scoring at this meeting last season and Pioneer Pete attempts to complete a hat-trick.

Find Welsh Champion Hurdle odds on the Paddy Power website and app. Otherwise, Timeform’s guide to the runners and betting tips are below.

Welsh Champion Hurdle 2026 verdict

Timeform: GIBBS ISLAND made a winning reappearance in good style at this fixture 12 months ago, and he may well repeat the feat off the same mark here. Sam Thomas has won the previous two renewals of this race and Doctor Blue could have a big say if back on song. Le Beau Madrik is likely to have been primed by the all-powerful Skelton team, while Captain Hugo and Serious Challenge would be serious players if ready to roll.

Welsh Champion Hurdle 2026 runners and riders

The following horses have been declared for the Welsh Champion Hurdle:

Laafi

Trainer: William Durken Jockey: Philip Byrnes Age: 6 Weight: 11st 12lbs

Smart hurdler. First past the post in a competitive handicap at Aintree (disqualified due to rider breaking whip rules). Enhanced his record in big-field valuable handicaps with narrow defeat in Swinton before smooth-travelling eighth in the Galway Hurdle in July. Has to be respected on return.

Kateira

Trainer: Dan Skelton Jockey: Jack Andrews Age: 9 Weight: 11st 9lbs

Very useful mare on her day. Landed a match in a listed event at Wetherby (2m) on reappearance last November. Finished the season in form with a more-than-creditable sixth in the BetMGM Cup at Cheltenham. Harry Skelton now looks elsewhere and likely has bigger targets down the line.

Knickerbockerglory

Trainer: Dan Skelton Jockey: Harry Atkins (5) Age: 10 Weight: 11st 6lbs

Likeable front runner who has an excellent record on seasonal reappearance. Back to his best when narrowly denied on first crack at 2½m (good) at Bangor in March before a forgivable end-of-season run in France. Would ideally prefer more rain on return to 2m, though.

Le Beau Madrik

Trainer: Dan Skelton Jockey: Harry Skelton Age: 5 Weight: 11st 5lbs

Sole defeat over hurdles when pulled up in Kelso Group 2 on hat-trick bid in February. Overcame some less-than-fluent jumping to come clear in impressive style at Haydock (21f) subsequently. Gets the nod from Harry Skelton on handicap bow and has every chance if picking up where he left off.

Serious Challenge

Trainer: Fergal O’Brien Jockey: Liam Harrison Age: 6 Weight: 11st 4lbs

Borderline smart on Flat in Ireland in 2024. Confirmed himself a top-end handicap hurdler last season, chasing home Captain Hugo in the Morebattle before a fine third in Sussex Champion Hurdle at Plumpton. Can make an impact if settling (keen on reappearance previous 2 seasons).

Captain Hugo

Trainer: Philip Hobbs & Johnson White Jockey: Sean Houlihan Age: 6 Weight: 11st 3lbs

Improved a chunk when making a winning handicap debut in the Morebattle at Kelso (soft) in February. Still looked ahead of his mark when runner-up (jumped none too fluently) in Scottish Champion at Ayr (heavy). Dangerous if firing on all cylinders for his return (yard 3 winners from 6 this season).

Gibbs Island

Trainer: Tom Lacey Jockey: Stan Sheppard Age: 5 Weight: 11st

Made a winning return/handicap hurdle debut in clear-cut style at this fixture 12 months ago. Back on track with a highly encouraging seventh in the County prior to doing all he could from another poor position in the Swinton. Races off the same mark as last year and may well make it 2-2 over C&D.

Sony Bill

Trainer: Faye Bramley Jockey: Harry Cobden Age: 5 Weight: 11st

Developed into a useful hurdler for Willie Mullins last season, however, has regressed over fences for current yard this year. Back hurdling and hard to be confident that he’ll snap a losing sequence stretching back to his 3-y-o days in France.

Sageborough

Trainer: Paul Nolan Jockey: Sean Flanagan Age: 5 Weight: 10st 12lbs

Low-mileage 5-y-o. Much improved when doubling his career/Wexford tally in a conditions event in May (17f). Subsequently ran too badly to be true in the Galway Hurdle. Has been given 10 weeks to get over that, however, will need to get back on the up to defy his inflated British mark.

Filibustering

Trainer: Harry Derham Jockey: Paul O’Brien Age: 5 Weight: 10st 11lbs

Strong-travelling type. On the up when landing his third successive hurdle success in emphatic fashion at Cartmel in late-June. Was given plenty to do when 7¾-length third at Perth subsequently, though every chance he’s high enough in the weights now in any case.

Hamlet’s Night

Trainer: James Owen Jockey: Jonathan Burke Age: 5 Weight: 10st 10lbs

Ran a blinder behind Rubaud in Grade 2 Elite at Wincanton last season. Likely had enough for the season when down the field in the Swinton in May. Notable that he was better than ever when making a winning return on the Flat last month and excellent yard likely to have targeted this. Hood back on.

Listentoyourheart

Trainer: Dan Skelton Jockey: Charlie Todd Age: 7 Weight: 10st 9lbs

Useful mare. Given a considerate reappearance in this corresponding event 12 months ago prior to winning a valuable 2½m handicap at Windsor on New Year’s Day. Drop back in trip/jockey bookings suggest this may again be a springboard to later-season targets.

Queenie St Clair

Trainer: Nick Scholfield Jockey: Sean Bowen Age: 6 Weight: 10st 8lbs

Strong traveller. Improved again to notch a third success for current yard in a Go North Series Final handicap at Kelso in March. Handicapper has offered some respite following heavy end-of-season defeat and arrives fit from a spin on the Flat. Probably stable first string on jockey bookings, too.

Morning Mayhem

Trainer: Nick Scholfield Jockey: Kielan Woods Age: 5 Weight: 10st 8lbs

Another success story for up-and-coming yard. Found further improvement when landing Cartmel Cup in refitted headgear in August. Another personal best when narrowly denied at Market Rasen latest but could well be nearing his ceiling (now 38 lb higher than his breakthrough success in December).

Pioneer Pete

Trainer: Jonjo & AJ O’Neill Jockey: Jonjo O’Neill Jr Age: 6 Weight: 10st 7lbs

Fairly useful 6-y-o. Landed the odds in a trio of maiden/novice hurdles either side of a cracking handicap debut run at Punchestown in May, when only narrowly denied in a fiercely competitive affair. Still relatively unexposed and has race fitness on his side (significant at this time of year).

Doctor Blue

Trainer: Sam Thomas Jockey: Dylan Johnston Age: 6 Weight: 10st 6lbs

Very promising second in Cheltenham Grade 2 on hurdling debut/return last year. Tame finishing effort on latest start in February (has also bled/refused to race). Tongue strap refitted following a breathing operation and feared if back on song for yard that has won the last 2 renewals of this race.

Tennessee Tango

Trainer: Joe Tizzard Jockey: Brendan Powell Age: 6 Weight: 10st 6lbs

Fairly useful bumper winner. Third to a couple of above-average types over C&D prior to landing the odds at Plumpton in February. Found wanting for stamina in 2½m events subsequently. Out of the weights back up in class but should at least benefit from return to 2m following a breathing operation.
