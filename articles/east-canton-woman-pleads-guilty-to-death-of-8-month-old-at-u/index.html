East Canton woman pleads guilty to death of 8-month-old at unlicensed daycare

TheTools · 27.10.2026 17:58 · source · permalink

STARK COUNTY, Ohio (WOIO) - The woman charged with the death of an eight-month-old boy at an unlicensed daycare, pleaded guilty in Stark County Court of Common Pleas Tuesday.

Melanie Dimarzio pleaded guilty to involuntary manslaughter and child endangering for the March death of Grant Simmons.

Dimarzio was sentenced to five years in prison. The judge ordered her to start serving her jail time on Nov. 9.

She was arrested by East Canton police officers and the U.S. Marshals on July 21.

On March 10, East Canton officers and Osnaburg Township EMS responded to a home in the 100 block of Market St. South for a report of a baby not breathing.

According to the 911 call, Simmons was taking a nap, but when they went to wake him up, he was blue and not breathing.

When paramedics arrived, they began life-saving measures before taking Simmons to Aultman Hospital, where he died later that day.

Inside the home, officers learned the day care facility operators were unlicensed.

One of two operators disclosed she has between five and 10 children she babysits at the home in exchange for approximately $45 per day, paid in cash.

On March 20, Judge Frank Forchione granted a temporary restraining order filed by the village of East Canton and the state of Ohio to shut down the day care.
