Malaysia lifts haze emergency in Sarawak, but warns pollution risks remain

TheTools · 07.09.2026 07:13 · source · permalink

King Sultan Ibrahim revokes the emergency status after rain lowers air pollution levels in Serian district

Kuala Lumpur on Friday activated the emergency after obtaining royal consent when pollution levels reached critical levels in Serian, about 65 kilometres (40 miles) south of the Sarawak capital, Kuching.

“The haze situation in Sarawak has not yet fully recovered, and the air pollution index (API) reading in several areas ... particularly Serian and Samarahan districts, is still at very unhealthy levels,” it said in a statement.

The federal government and the Sarawak state government will continue to closely monitor the situation, and residents in affected areas are advised to limit outdoor activities and to always comply with instructions from the authorities,” it said.

API figures in Serian passed the critical threshold of 500 on Friday, peaking at 519 with the town cloaked in a thick cloud of smoke.

Under Malaysian guidelines, API levels above 300 are classified as hazardous, while readings beyond 500 constitute the threshold for declaring a local emergency.
