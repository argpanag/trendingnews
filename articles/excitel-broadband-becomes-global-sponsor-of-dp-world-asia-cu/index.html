Excitel Broadband Becomes Global Sponsor of DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 | EVENTFAQS India

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:06 · source · permalink
  • Industry Watch
  • Articles
  • News
  • Events
  • Sports

Excitel Broadband has announced its association as the Global Sponsor of the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026, which commenced in Doha, Qatar. The Asian Cricket Council (ACC) tournament features eight teams competing across 15 matches, with the final scheduled for November 29.

This marks Excitel’s second association with an Asian Cricket Council tournament in 2026. The brand previously had an on-ground presence at the DP World Women’s Asia Cup 2026 in Dubai in September, where India defeated Sri Lanka by 72 runs to claim its eighth title.

As part of the sponsorship, Excitel will feature across the tournament through branding on the pitch mat behind the wicket, perimeter boards around the ground and backdrops at post-match presentations, press conferences and player interviews. All matches will be broadcast live on Sony Sports Network and Sony LIV.

Commenting on the sponsorship, Aditya Jain, Chief Marketing Officer, Excitel Broadband said, “We are grateful to the Asian Cricket Council for welcoming Excitel back, this time as Global Sponsor. Cricket is where Indian fans are at their most passionate, and few platforms let a brand share in that emotion at this scale. We look forward to being a part of this exciting tournament over the next eleven days.”

Varun Pasricha, CEO, Excitel Broadband said, “Excitel started out as a young challenger with big ambitions, so a tournament built around rising talent feels very close to who we are. These players have worked for years to earn their place on a bigger stage, and we are glad to support them at such an important point in their journey. Cricket has always brought Indian homes together, and we are proud to be part of that as we stand with the next generation of Asian cricket.”

Haider Raza, Head of Media, Communications and Operations, ACC, said, “We are pleased to welcome Excitel on board as a Global Sponsor for the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars. The players at this tournament reflect how cricket is played today, and Excitel’s energy and ambition align well with the qualities that define modern cricket – intent, speed, and a confident mindset. We look forward to a successful tournament together.”

The sponsorship adds to Excitel’s growing presence in sports. In October 2026, the company became the Official Broadband Partner of Sporting Club Delhi in the Indian Super League (ISL). It had also partnered with JioCinema in 2024 for the ‘Speed Ka Man of the Match’ feature during the Indian Premier League (IPL).
