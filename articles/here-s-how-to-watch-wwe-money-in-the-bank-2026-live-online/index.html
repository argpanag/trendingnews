After an epic two-night SummerSlam event with Roman Reigns defeated Seth Rollins for the World Heavyweight Title and Liv Morgan defeated Iyo Sky to retain the Women’s World Title, WWE travels to New Orleans for WWE Money in the Bank, a premium live event on Saturday, Oct. 10.

The event features five bouts, including two title matches for the World Heavyweight Championship and Women’s World Championship.

In 2025, WWE was in the top 10 in nearly 40 country’s for Money in the Bank. The company hopes that this year’s major would bring in even more viewers. In addition, it’s likely there would be celebrity fans in-attendance during the event, such as New Orleans Pelicans forward Zion Williamson, New Orleans Saints running back Alvin Kamara and others.

When Is 2026 WWE Money in the Bank? Date, Time

Money in the Bank takes place at Smoothie King Center in New Orleans, Louisiana on Saturday, Oct. 10. The start time is 6 p.m. ET/3 p.m. PT.
