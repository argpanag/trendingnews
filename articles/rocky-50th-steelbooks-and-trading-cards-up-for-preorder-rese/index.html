Rocky 50th SteelBooks and Trading Cards Up for Preorder - Resell Calendar

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

The film returns to theaters next month

By RC Staff

14 October 2026

Key Points

  • The Rocky 50th Anniversary 4K SteelBook is $47 on Amazon and ships December 15

  • Cardsmiths’ Rocky trading cards start at $19.99 for two packs, with eight-pack Mega Boxes at $59.99

  • Card packs can contain on-card autographs from Sylvester Stallone, Mr. T, and Dolph Lundgren

The Rocky 50th Anniversary 4K SteelBook is $47 on Amazon and ships December 15

Cardsmiths’ Rocky trading cards start at $19.99 for two packs, with eight-pack Mega Boxes at $59.99

Card packs can contain on-card autographs from Sylvester Stallone, Mr. T, and Dolph Lundgren

Fifty years after Sylvester Stallone first jogged through the streets of Philadelphia, the Italian Stallion is getting the full anniversary treatment. Rocky returns to theaters in November for a short 4K run, and merchandisers are lining up limited releases to celebrate the franchise. Two of them are open for preorder right now: a collector’s SteelBook from Amazon MGM Studios and a premium trading card set from Cardsmiths. One is about as safe as a reseller bet gets, since you can back out before it ever ships. The other is a final sale gamble with a handful of ultra-rare autographs sitting at the top.

Rocky 50th Anniversary SteelBook Details

Amazon MGM Studios and Alliance Home Entertainment are releasing a two-disc collector’s edition of the 1976 Best Picture winner, and preorders are live on Amazon. The set includes the film on 4K Ultra HD and Blu-ray in SteelBook packaging, along with a souvenir mini-poster commemorating the anniversary.

The extras are where this one earns its shelf space. The 4K disc carries three commentary tracks featuring Stallone, members of the cast, filmmakers, and boxing experts. The Blu-ray adds more than three hours of bonus material, including archival 8mm home movies shot in 1975, a three-part making-of documentary, and featurettes on Bill Conti’s score, the makeup, directing, and cinematography.

The disc lands December 15, about a month after Rocky’s theatrical run through Fathom Entertainment on November 7 to 11. That timing puts it right in the middle of holiday gift season, which never hurts demand.

Cardsmiths Rocky 50th Trading Cards

Cardsmiths has a Rocky 50th Anniversary trading card collection covering all six original films. Packs pull from more than 150 unique designs, mixing scenes from the movies with original artwork. Subsets include Culture Shokz cards with art by Gonzalo Ordonez, Gallery Edition artist interpretations, Ring Masters, and movie poster cards for every film in the saga.

The chase list is stacked. Cardsmiths is producing six uniquely designed 1-of-1 Stallone on-card autographs, plus another Stallone design numbered to 3. Mr. T autographs as Clubber Lang and Dolph Lundgren autographs as Ivan Drago are limited to 200 pieces.

Below that, collectors can hunt Parallel Holofoils, gold-stamped short prints, and Gemstone Refractor cards, with Collector Boxes and Mega Boxes each carrying their own exclusive variants. Select boxes also come in gold-themed packaging serial-numbered out of 1,976 per variation, a nod to the original release year.

Is This Worth Reselling?

The SteelBook is the low-risk play. Amazon lets you cancel a preorder any time before it ships, and its pre-order price guarantee means you’ll pay the lowest price offered before release. Even after it arrives, an unopened copy falls under Amazon’s standard return window.

Limited edition SteelBooks have flipped for up to $150 in the past, and Rocky has a fanbase that will pay for the right collectibles. There’s no published unit count for this edition, so it’s hard to say how scarce it really is. Preorder now, watch whether Amazon sells out, and cancel if it doesn’t.

The cards are a much riskier bet. Cardsmiths’ return policy says all sales are final on trading card boxes, cases, and packs, so once you check out, they’re yours. Cardsmiths also notes that autographs are expected but not guaranteed. If a signer can’t fulfill their obligations, those cards may be omitted or replaced.

Where this could pay off is scarcity. If the print run ends up small, sealed boxes (especially the serial-numbered gold variants) could carry a premium with Rocky fans and set collectors. A Mr. T or Lundgren auto numbered to 200 is the kind of hit people chase, and a Stallone 1-of-1 would be a grail for anyone collecting this set. Just remember those are lottery odds, not a plan. Grab a box if you want the rip, but don’t buy in bulk expecting a guaranteed sealed flip.

Don't Forget Rocky Popcorn Buckets

The anniversary run is also bringing a Rocky staircase popcorn bucket to participating theaters from November 7 to 11. We broke down the bucket’s resale outlook last month, and with only a five-day window to buy, we expect these to hit eBay fast. Like the cards, buckets are final sale once you’re at the counter.
