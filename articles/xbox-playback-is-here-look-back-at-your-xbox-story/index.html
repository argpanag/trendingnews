XBOX Playback is Here: Look Back at Your XBOX Story

TheTools · 12.11.2026 16:05 · source · permalink

Your games. Your milestones. Your story. Playback lets you look back at your history with XBOX – across 25 years of gaming.

Starting today, XBOX Playback brings highlights from your available XBOX history across every generation in a personalized experience. For the first time, you can look back and re-live the part you played in XBOX’s story. See when your journey began, revisit the games you spent the most time with, and rediscover achievements and moments along the way. 

Your Playback includes your first XBOX game, total time played, most-played game, top years, longest streak and more. As no two players are the same, everyone’s Playback will be different. Whether you’re an OG who first played using a Duke controller, or you booted up your first console this year, you’ll have a personalized story waiting for you. 

Starting today, you can watch your Playback directly on your console (look for the option on your dashboard) or watch your personalized video on the web by signing in with your XBOX account here.  

Want to dig a little deeper? Head to your Playback Archive on the web to explore more of your available history. Dive into what some of your first milestones were, your biggest achievements and more. XBOX Playback will roll out gradually throughout the day, so don’t worry if you don’t see it right away. 

After watching your Playback, scan the QR code at the end of the console video or select “share” on web to get your personalized Playback card. Share it with friends or post it with #XBOXPlayback on social.  

XBOX Playback brings together years of XBOX history in a brand-new way. We hope Playback evokes a few memories, surprises you, or reminds you of a game you haven’t thought about in a while. Playback is based on available account data, so some parts of your history may be incomplete or take time to appear. Playback is available in 15 countries* from today through December 31. 

Whether you’ve been playing with us since the beginning or are just getting started, thank you for being part of XBOX.  

*Available until December 31, 2026 in Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, Colombia, France, Germany, Hungary, Italy, Mexico, Poland, South Africa, Spain, UK and US. Requires at least 1 XBOX Gamerscore, 1 achievement, and 1 minute of qualifying gameplay linked to your Xbox profile; access on console requires at least 1 minute of qualifying gameplay on console. Based on available data; some data may be incomplete or delayed.
