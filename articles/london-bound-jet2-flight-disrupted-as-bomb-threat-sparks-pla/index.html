London-bound Jet2 flight disrupted as 'bomb threat' sparks plane evacuation in Tenerife - AOL

TheTools · 27.09.2026 07:48 · source · permalink

A Jet2 flight with destination London Stansted had to be evacuated on Friday. The plane was at the Tenerife South Airport preparing to depart on the late morning of September 25, when passengers were ordered out for security reasons.

Guardia Civil officers checked the plane bound for London following reports one person had made a bomb threat, according to Spanish news outlet Atlantic Hoy. Passengers who were already taking their seats were told to disembark as a security measure. All customers set to board the flight and their luggage were checked before the aircraft was given the green light for departure.

The Jet2 LS1536 flight was scheduled to leave at 11.20am local time and reach the UK by 3.40pm, but the departure was instead pushed back to 2.01pm, tracker Flightradar24 shows.

It safely landed at Stansted at 5.49pm.

A spokesperson for Jet2 confirmed an incident had disrupted the schedule of one of the company's flights.

They told the Metro: "We can confirm that customers were asked to disembark a flight at Tenerife South Airport today following an incident involving a passenger onboard.

"Customers have since reboarded and the flight has now landed at London Stansted as planned. We would like to apologise to everyone onboard for the delay and any inconvenience caused."

The incident also seemingly prompted some disruption to other flights at Tenerife South Airport, with some delays recorded in the afternoon.

This comes after an easyJet flight from Manchester to Hamburg had to return to its departing airport due to a technical issue with the aircraft.

The company said the plane was "met by emergency services as a precaution only and customers disembarked normally".

Last week, a Virgin Atlantic flight also departing from Manchester had to return to the British airport following a "minor technical issue", which disrupted its journey to New York.
