​Strikers name unchanged 13-player squad for Adelaide Oval clash | Adelaide Strikers

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:13 · source · permalink

​The Adelaide Strikers have named an unchanged 13-player squad for tomorrow night’s Weber WBBL|12 clash against the Melbourne Renegades at Adelaide Oval.

​The fourth-placed Strikers will take on the fifth-placed Renegades in a crucial finals race showdown, with the match also set to be a historic night for spinner Amanda-Jade Wellington. 

​Wellington will make her 159th consecutive WBBL appearance, equalling Perth Scorchers star Beth Mooney’s record for the most consecutive matches played in WBBL history.

​Tomorrow night's fixture will be the Strikers' 20th WBBL match at Adelaide Oval and the only regular-season WBBL game to be played at a Tier One stadium this season.

​Fans can enjoy a Friday night fan fest atmosphere, with player signings, giveaways, a pre-match K-pop DJ and the Strikers Kids Zone, featuring inflatables, face painting, hair braiding and more. 

​Entry is free for children under 18.

Match Details
