GREEN BAY (WLUK) -- The Green Bay School Board on Monday evening accepted the resignation of Preble High School special education teacher Joey Demerath.

Demerath submitted his resignation last week after he was placed on administrative leave amid an investigation into allegations that he sent inappropriate messages to a former student.

The district said it and Green Bay police were investigating the claim. No arrests have been made in the case.
