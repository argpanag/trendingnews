Popular PS5 & PS4 Fighting Game 75% Off on PlayStation Store With DLC

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

Bandai Namco's fighting game based on a popular anime has received a major discount on the PlayStation Store. With a 75% discount, the PS4 and PS5 title also includes a DLC expansion and additional content.

Jujutsu Kaisen Cursed Clash Deluxe Edition is now available for $19.99 on the PlayStation Store. However, the offer is only available as part of a limited-time deal, which ends on October 8. Once the offer ends, the game reverts to its standard retail price of $79.99.

Meanwhile, the standard edition is offered at $59.99 on the storefront, which currently holds a 4.04 average rating based on 23,000 reviews there. This offer is a great choice for fighting game enthusiasts who also have an interest in the anime.

What comes with the Jujutsu Kaisen Cursed Clash Deluxe Edition on PS5 & PS4?

The Deluxe Edition includes three items: the base game, the Hidden Inventory/Premature Death DLC, and Jujusta 2024. The DLC adds a story chapter from the anime's second season. It explores Gojo Satoru and Suguru Geto's past during their Jujutsu High student days.

Student versions of Gojo and Geto are introduced in the DLC alongside Toji Fushiguro. Meanwhile, Jujusta 2024 is an exclusive 8-bit, retro-style baseball mini-game.

The base game is a fast-paced 2-on-2 fighting adventure built around each character's cursed techniques and abilities. Players can also team up to launch a joint attack. The character roster includes 16 fighters at launch, with more added through updates and paid expansions.

Ryomen Sukuna, Satoru Gojo, Suguru Geto, Yuji Itadori, and Yuta Okkotsu are among the characters. Cursed spirits such as Jogo and Hanami also appear.

Bandai Namco released the title in February 2024. The game also includes a story mode, which adapts the anime's first season and the prequel film, Jujutsu Kaisen 0. It follows the protagonist Yuji Itadori as he fights off various monsters to defend humankind in modern-day Japan.
