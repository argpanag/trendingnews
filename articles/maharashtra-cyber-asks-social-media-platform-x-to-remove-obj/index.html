Maharashtra Cyber asks social media platform X to remove objectionable posts

Mumbai, Oct 21 (PTI) The Maharashtra Cyber has issued formal directives to social media platform X to take down objectionable posts and provide account details in connection with an ongoing inquiry into cyber offences, officials said on Wednesday.

The action follows an FIR registered at the Nodal Cyber Police Station in Navi Mumbai on Sunday.

According to the Nodal Office, which functions as the apex authority for cyber-related crimes in Maharashtra, the case has been registered under Sections 352 (intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of the peace) and 353 (statements conducing to public mischief) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), along with Section 67 of the Information Technology (IT) Act.

Authorities noted that the investigation was initiated after fraudulent accounts operating on the platform were found hosting defamatory allegations, offensive remarks, and content capable of outraging the dignity of women and provoking public unrest, a Cyber official said.

As part of the ongoing probe, specific URLs and account details have been submitted to the platform’s grievance and compliance teams, with directions to immediately disable the offending content and furnish subscriber logs and technical identifiers to aid further legal proceedings. PTI ZA NSK
