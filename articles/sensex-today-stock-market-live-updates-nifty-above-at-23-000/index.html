Market on Monday | Nifty below 22,900, Sensex tanks 876 pts

Indian benchmark indices snapped their two-day winning streak and witnessed heavy selling on October 19, amid elevated crude oil prices, weak global cues and selling in private banking stocks following disappointing earnings.

At close, the Sensex was down 876.37 points or 1.17 percent at 73,852.90, and the Nifty was down 257.20 points or 1.11 percent at 22,888.95.

The broader markets also remained under pressure with Nifty midcap and smallcap indices fell 1 percent each.

Biggest Nifty losers were Axis Bank, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles, Bajaj Finance, Trent, Infosys, while gainers included JSW Steel, Eicher Motors, SBI, L&T and ONGC.

Except PSU Bank (gained 2.2%), all other sectoral indices ended in the red with FMCG index down 1.2%, IT index down 1.3% and Private Bank index shed 1.8 percent.
