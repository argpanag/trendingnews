David Pope Named MoAD’s 2026 Political Cartoonist of the Year

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:57 · source · permalink

The Museum of Australian Democracy [MoAD], as part of their annual Behind the Lines cartoon exhibition named David Pope as the 2026 Political Cartoonist of the Year. Said the MoAD judging panel:

“Pope’s work was singled out for its excellent draughtsmanship, astute political commentary and clever use of references. His cartoons covered the big topics of the year, including climate change, the fuel crises, the rapid spread of AI and the rise of One Nation, as well as various other stories that made news in 2026.”

The Canberra Times profiles Pope and celebrates their cartoonist being honored (or here).

Along with honoring Pope MoAD has opened their 2026 Behind the Lines cartoon exhibit for online viewing with concise explanatory text.

“In Australian federal politics it’s been a year of shifting alliances and power struggles: One Nation asserted itself as a major presence, a new ‘Teal’ party was born, the Coalition broke up – then made up again – and the Liberal party got a new leader after less than a year.

“Our cartoonists have helped us make more sense of the year than any number of textbooks could.”

Along with Pope the roster of featured 2026 Behind the Lines cartoonists are Joe Benke, Peter Broelman, David Rowe, Warren Brown, Mark Knight, Cathy Wilcox, Johannes Leak, Fiona Katauskas, Megan Herbert, Matt Davidson, Brett Lethbridge, Lindsay Foyle, First Dog on the Moon, Sean Leahy, Matt Bissett-Johnson, Dean Alston, Ed Iffland, Philip Somerville, Danny Eastwood, Alan Moir, Sam Wallman, Aaron Billings, Chris Downes, David McArthur, Greg ‘Smithy’ Smith, Badiucao, Pat Hudson, Jess Harwood, John Spooner, David Blumenstein, Paul Peart-Smith, Shaun Tan, Scott Wrigg, Harry Bruce, Holly English, Nordacious, Judy Horacek, Oslo Davis, Andrew Weldon, and Fionn McCabe.

In addition MoAD celebrates the centennial of The Old Parliment House, where MoAd is located, with cartoons from 1927 marking the opening by Ted Scorfield, Tom Glover, Jack Waring, Percy Leasing, Will Donald, Stan Cross, and a few unknowns.
