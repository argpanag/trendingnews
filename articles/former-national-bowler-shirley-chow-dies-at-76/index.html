Former national bowler Shirley Chow dies at 76

TheTools · 02.11.2026 14:53 · source · permalink

The Asian Games gold medallist and 1979 Sportswoman of the Year passed away in Ipoh, Perak, after battling lung cancer.

The Malaysian Tenpin Bowling Congress (MTBC), which confirmed her death earlier today, said her passing was a great loss for the bowling community.

“Chow was a respected figure in Malaysian bowling, having contributed to the sport both as an athlete and a coach.

“Her dedication, passion, and commitment to bowling will be fondly remembered by the Malaysian bowling fraternity and all those whose lives she touched throughout her journey in the sport,” it said in an Instagram post.

At the 1989 SEA Games in Kuala Lumpur, Chow won silver medals in the women’s doubles with Pearly Chong, and the trios with Chong and Lily Chua.

In 1991 in Manila, she took silver in the singles and gold in the doubles with Lisa Kwan.

In 1993 in Singapore, she won gold in the trios with Shalin Zulkifli and Kwan, gold in the team event, and the all-events title, along with silver in the singles.

Chow won gold in the women’s trios at the 1994 Asian Games in Hiroshima, Japan, with Lydia Kwah and Shalin, and silver in the doubles with Kwah.

After ending her career as an athlete, she served as a coach in Perak.

In October last year, the MTBC presented Chow with the RM5,000 cash prize promised when she was named Sportswoman of the Year in 1979.
