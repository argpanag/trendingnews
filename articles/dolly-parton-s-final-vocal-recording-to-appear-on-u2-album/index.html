NASHVILLE, Tenn. (WZTV) — Dolly Parton’s final recorded vocal performance will be heard in a new duet with U2 frontman Bono, with a country version of the song set to premiere on radio Thursday.

The song, “Torn,” is the final track on U2’s upcoming album, Carnaval De Luz, scheduled for release Friday, Nov. 13, according to an announcement on Parton’s website.

Parton recorded her vocals in Nashville in June, before her death on Aug. 25. The song was co-written by U2, Parton and Johnny McDaid and is described as a song about broken family ties.

A country version, titled “Torn” (Kent Wells Version), is scheduled to air throughout Thursday on several radio groups, SiriusXM channels and Music Choice. Parton’s label partner, Big Machine Records, will promote the version to country radio, according to the announcement.

The track was produced by Jacknife Lee and features Daniel Lanois on pedal steel guitar.
