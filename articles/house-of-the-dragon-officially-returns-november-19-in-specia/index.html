House Of The Dragon Officially Returns November 19 In Special Event

TheTools · 07.11.2026 09:48 · source · permalink

From Screen Rant on November 7, 2026 | OpenCritic

House of the Dragon's latest season proved to be an improvement over its predecessor, offering climactic spectacle and intriguing character development that has helped pave the way for what will undoubtedly be an intense and exciting final season. Indeed, the final episode of the season was an immense success, garnering 34 million viewers, a 58% increase over the season's first episode. However, as great as the third season of House of the Dragon was, it is now sadly over, and fans are left twiddling their thumbs, awaiting the next entry.

Well, that is, of course, unless they're interested in the numerous Game of Thrones spin-offs. There is an abundance of spin-off media that fans have been consuming long before and since House of the Dragon season 3 aired, the vast majority of which has been well-received by fans. One of these projects is running a limited-time House of the Dragon event that fans can delve into completely for free. The only downside is that you'll need to be present for the duration that it is active in order to make the most of it.
