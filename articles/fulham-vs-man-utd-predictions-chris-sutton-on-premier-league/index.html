Fulham vs Man Utd predictions: Chris Sutton on Premier League game

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:43 · source · permalink
  • Published
    4 hours ago

Watching Fulham draw at Anfield last weekend changed my view on them.

I had not seen Alvaro Arbeloa's side over 90 minutes before and, while their other results were poor, this time they were excellent.

They cut through Liverpool and had some nice patterns of play, but also defended well too. Arbeloa said afterwards that they played with more balance and I think that is a good way to describe their display.

Then I went to Manchester United's defeat by Brighton in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday, and their performance was very poor.

Michael Carrick really needs a response with a win here to take into the international break, but how can I give United a chance after the way the Seagulls absolutely dismantled them?

Three times in the past four seasons, United have gone to Craven Cottage and nicked the win late on, but I can't make a case for that happening.

As well as having problems up front, United are struggling defensively and I can see Fulham creating plenty of opportunities against them too.

Sutton's prediction: 2-2
