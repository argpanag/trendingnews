GTA VI leaks continue with a lengthy (and very nude) gameplay video

GTA VI’s main characters can seemingly go through the whole game naked in a new leak.﻿

The GTA VI leaks keep coming — and the latest is almost as long as the game’s official “extended look” on Netflix. Only instead of showing off all the various gameplay details, this one is more of an “extended look” at Jason running around Vice City naked. As Kotaku reports, GTA leaker Cyberleek, who cashed out on a memecoin and seemingly disappeared earlier this year, is back with a new 25-minute video of GTA VI gameplay.

The video suggests players will be able to play through GTA VI with their characters completely unclothed. According to Kotaku, Jason is naked throughout almost the whole video, including while on a motorbike, “at a beach-side coffee store,” and while talking to other characters. Lucia is also mostly naked in a clip at the end of the video, where Cyberleek reportedly briefly shows her from behind.

Some of the leaked footage appears to include light story spoilers, with Kotaku noting that the video opens with a cutscene of Jason and Lucia leading into a dirt bike race for one of the game’s scripted missions. Cyberleek goes on to show some first-person driving footage from Jason, followed by a street race. Kotaku also says Cyberleek “seemingly conjures a tank out of the air” and, of course, uses the video to advertise a crypto coin.

Cyberleek previously shared a slew of other GTA VI clips in the lead up to the game’s Netflix special. Rockstar Games eventually responded, calling the leaks “heartbreaking,” and Take-Two has requested multiple subpoenas as it tries to find the “alleged infringers” leaking the game footage. Before that, a massive collection of videos leaked in 2022, and the hacker was eventually sentenced to life in hospital prison. Grand Theft Auto VI is available for preorder now and set to launch on November 19th.
