Rabiot to start alongside Modric against Lazio, Sky reports

TheTools · 12.09.2026 11:39 · source · permalink

According to Sky, there are important updates regarding the Milan line-up that will start tonight against Lazio at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome. Contrary to the reports that circulated in recent hours, Yunus Musah will not partner Luka Modric in midfield. Instead, Adrien Rabiot will start alongside the Croatian, meaning he will not play in the attacking midfield role he occupied against Juventus in Turin. Behind Gonçalo Ramos, Alphadjo Cisse will be joined by Ruben Loftus-Cheek.

The rest of Ruben Amorim’s line-up is confirmed. Mike Maignan will start in goal, with Mario Gila, Koni De Winter and Strahinja Pavlovic forming the back three. Samuel Chukwueze will operate on the right, with Pervis Estupinan on the left, as pointed out via Milan News.

Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Pavlovic; Chukwueze, Modric, Rabiot, Estupinan; Loftus-Cheek, Cisse; Ramos.
