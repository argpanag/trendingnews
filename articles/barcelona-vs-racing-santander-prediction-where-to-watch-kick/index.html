Recent form

Barcelona leads the Spain Primera Division, having won all five league matches. They show remarkable attacking strength, scoring 17 goals and conceding just two. This dominant domestic start sets a formidable tone for any opponent at Camp Nou.

Their strong form extends to the UEFA Champions League, with a 5-1 victory over Feyenoord. Raphinha, with six league goals, and Lamine Yamal are in exceptional form. The team is at full strength with no injuries, boosting their presence on the pitch.

This excellent start, coupled with a perfect home record in the league, makes them clear favourites for the Barcelona vs. Racing Santander fixture. Racing Santander - Struggling on the road

Now, let’s look at Racing Santander’s situation. We will analyse their current form, recent results, and the key players for their trip to Barcelona.

Recent form

Racing Santander currently sits 10th in the Spain Primera Division, having secured seven points from five matches. Their start to the season has been inconsistent, with two wins, one draw, and two defeats. A notable difference exists between their home and away performances, as they remain unbeaten at home but have lost both fixtures on the road.

Their fluctuating form is evident in recent results, including a 2-1 victory over Alaves and a subsequent 3-2 loss to Rayo Vallecano. While the team has displayed attacking prowess, scoring nine goals, their defence has also conceded nine. Attacker M. Zabiri stands out as a key player, having netted three goals.

Racing Santander’s struggles away from home pose a significant challenge for the upcoming Barcelona vs. Racing Santander encounter at Camp Nou. Despite having no injured players, their defensive vulnerabilities could be exposed. Overcoming their poor travel record against a strong opponent like Barcelona will be a formidable task.
