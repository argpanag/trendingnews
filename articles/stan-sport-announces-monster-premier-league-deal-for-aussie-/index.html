Stan Sport announces monster Premier League deal for Aussie fans

TheTools · 09.09.2026 23:03 · source · permalink
  • Breaking
  • Sport
  • Football
  • Football

September 9, 2026 - 11.03pm

Stan Sport will be the home of the English Premier League in Australia until the end of 2034 after Nine Entertainment announced a fresh six-year deal.

Aussie fans will be able to watch the world’s premier football league on Stan Sport for the next eight years.

Ollie Watkins of Aston Villa celebrates scoring. Aston Villa FC via Getty Images

“Football is the ultimate global game, followed with remarkable intensity by millions of fans across Australia,” Nine CEO Matt Stanton said.

“Bringing the Premier League to Stan has been a genuine game-changer for our business, and we are very pleased to be extending the partnership for a further six years.”

Stan Sport first secured the Premier League rights last year, as well as the Champions League and a host of other football competitions.

The 2026-27 season kicked off last month.

“As the sole rights holder in Australia, Nine is uniquely placed to activate our assets across streaming and broadcast, publishing and outdoor to promote awareness of Premier League, deepen fan engagement, and tell the game’s stories across the entirety of the season,” Stanton said.

“This agreement further strengthens Nine’s premium sport offering and reflects Nine’s long-term strategy of investing in premium live sport and delivering the sporting moments that matter most to Australians.”

Football’s new home, Stan Sport, is the only place to watch the Premier League, UEFA Champions League, FA Cup, WSL, J.League, NWSL and K League
