English Athletes Reach European Table Tennis Draws

TheTools · 13.10.2026 20:30 · source · permalink

English athletes competed across European table tennis and football coverage as reports highlighted the 2026 European Championships in Ljubljana, Slovenia, West Ham's Championship campaign, and ongoing EFL season developments from October 2026.

According to Table Tennis England, Liam Pitchford, Connor Green, and Tom Jarvis reached the men's singles main draw at the European Championships. Pitchford and Green topped their groups with two victories each.

Table Tennis England reported that Sam Walker exited the men's singles event after losing to Anders Eriksson of Sweden and neutral athlete Nikita Artemenko. Tianer Yu won her opening match before a 3-1 defeat to Katarzyna Wegrzyn of Poland.

The organization also reported that Tin-Tin Ho entered the women's singles main draw as the 24th seed and will face Romania's Elena Zaharia in the round of 64.

In doubles events, Table Tennis England reported that Green and Ho lost 3-1 to Sweden's Elias Ranefur and Filippa Bergand in the mixed doubles round of 32. Ho and Anna Hursey advanced to the women's doubles last 16.

West Ham's Championship title prospects were discussed by EFL expert George Elek on the Not The Top 20 podcast, as reported by Claret and Hugh. He also raised concerns about the team's control during matches.

"I think on the balance of probability I’m going to reluctantly say that West Ham are the likeliest league winners." said George Elek, EFL expert.

Claret and Hugh reported that West Ham retained Jarrod Bowen, Taty Castellanos, and Konstantinos Mavropanos while signing Arne Engels for £22 million. The report said automatic promotion remained the club's target.

BBC Sport reported that the Championship season had reached a point where most teams had completed eight of their 46 fixtures before football returned after an extended international break. The outlet also published a quiz covering the season so far.
