FIRST ALERT WEATHER DAY: Storm Chances and Flooding Concerns This Evening

TheTools · 10.09.2026 18:26 · source · permalink

HAZARD, Ky. (WYMT) - We’ve got a First Alert Weather Day in effect for tonight and Friday. Here’s what’s happening: a cold front is slowly creeping through the region, and it’s bringing warm, moist air that’s perfect for storms to develop. Don’t worry. We’re not expecting anything severe, but you’ll definitely want to keep an eye on things and stay with us for updates.

Tonight Through Friday Night

Tonight could bring some storms with mostly cloudy skies. Lows will drop to the mid-to-upper 60s. That cold front moving slowly southward is creating the right conditions for storms to pop up, so keep your phone nearby. Here’s something to watch: if storms keep hitting the same spots, we could see some locally heavy rainfall. It’s been a while since we’ve had significant rain, so that’s something to keep in mind.

Friday is our second Alert Day with another chance of storms. Highs will reach the low 80s. We’ve got another wave of energy moving in from the west that’ll keep storm chances in the forecast. If you’ve got outdoor plans, just stay weather aware and check back with us often.

Friday night things should calm down a bit. The storm chance drops to just slight, mostly cloudy skies, and lows around the mid-60s.

The Weekend and Beyond

Saturday starts off with a chance of storms early on, then partly cloudy skies take over. Highs in the low 80s, lows in the upper 60s. That cold front is still hanging around, keeping things a bit unsettled.

Sunday is looking like your best day. Sunny and pleasant with highs in the low 80s and lows in the upper 60s. Perfect for getting outside. High pressure is moving in and that’ll really cut down on storm chances.

Monday stays mostly sunny and warm. Highs in the mid-80s, lows in the mid-60s. This is honestly your best day of the entire week.

Tuesday gets unsettled again with a slight chance of storms in the afternoon and evening. Highs will climb to the upper 80s. That’s the warmest day we’ll see. It’ll feel muggy too. Lows around the mid-60s.

Wednesday and Thursday both have chances of storms with highs in the mid-to-upper 80s and lows in the upper 60s. Another cold front could move through, bringing more storm chances. Stay alert and keep checking back with us.

Next Saturday and Sunday shower chances stick around with highs in the low 80s and lows in the upper 50s to low 60s.

What You Need to Do

  • Keep your phone charged. We’ll send alerts if storms develop.
  • Have a plan. If storms arrive during your commute or outdoor activities, know where to take shelter.
  • Check updates often. Timing can change, so stay with us for the latest.

Stay safe and stay weather aware.
