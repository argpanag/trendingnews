Employee at Punggol motor show allegedly found vaping, HSA investigating

TheTools · 24.09.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

The Health Sciences Authority (HSA) has launched an investigation after a man working at a motor roadshow in Punggol was allegedly filmed vaping.

The incident reportedly occurred at around 7.20pm on Aug 30 at a Dongfeng Motors roadshow in Waterway Point, according to an anonymous user who sent the footage to sgfollowsall.

The video, which was uploaded to Instagram on Tuesday (Sept 22), shows a man, reportedly a salesman working at the event, seated alone at a table with a vape-like device concealed in his hands.

A family with children can be seen at the table behind him while he takes several puffs from the device.

"Before this video was taken, we noticed he kept taking puffs from his device," the user wrote, adding that there were many people walking around him.

The user added that a report with HSA was also filed, but that they have not received any updates.

Volt Auto, the authorised distributor for Dongfeng Motors in Singapore, told Chinese publication Lianhe Zaobao that it has identified the man in the video.

It is aware of the video circulating online and that incident is being handled internally by the company, adding that it will cooperate with the authorities.

In response to queries, HSA said that it is aware of the incident and has launched an investigation, reported Zaobao.

In an Instagram story on Friday, HSA said that it has identified the 32-year-old individual in the video, and that he is currently assisting with HSA's investigations.

AsiaOne has reached out to Volt Auto and Dongfeng Motors for additional information.
