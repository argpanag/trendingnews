What's Coming to Netflix This Week: September 28 to October 4, 2026

TheTools · 28.09.2026 12:15 · source · permalink

From Florence Pugh’s highly anticipated 'East of Eden' to the debut of 'LEGO ONE PIECE,' here is everything coming to Netflix this week.

Picture Credit: FOX / Netflix

Welcome to your weekly look ahead at what’s coming to Netflix US over the next seven days! Spooky season is officially upon us, and as we cross over into October, Netflix is delivering an absolutely massive first-of-the-month drop. With an enormous wave of horror classics, beloved movie franchises, and highly anticipated Netflix Originals, your autumn watchlist is about to get seriously packed.

Here is a breakdown of everything coming to Netflix between September 28th and October 4th, 2026, including three expanded highlights you definitely won’t want to miss. For the full list of October 2026 releases, keep it locked here on What’s on Netflix.

Best New Shows and Movies Coming to Netflix This Week

East of Eden (Limited Series)

Coming to Netflix: Thursday

Florence Pugh leads the cast in this highly anticipated, ambitious new adaptation of John Steinbeck’s classic American novel. Exploring the intricate, multigenerational saga of the Trask family, this limited series promises sweeping drama, stunning period details, and powerhouse performances. If you love a sprawling, emotionally heavy historical drama, clear your schedule for this massive Netflix Original release.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Infinity Castle (2025)

Coming to Netflix: Monday

Anime fans, the beginning of the end is here. Dropping Tanjiro and the Hashira straight into Muzan’s mind-bending lair, this first chapter of the epic finale trilogy is pure, adrenaline-pumping action. As the Demon Slayer Corps goes head-to-head with the terrifying Upper Rank demons in a brutal battle for survival, the animation and high-stakes fights go completely supernova. It’s an absolute must-watch event.

LEGO ONE PIECE (Season 1)

Coming to Netflix: Tuesday

The legendary Straw Hat Pirates are getting a brick-tastic makeover! In a hilariously meta twist, Netflix is expanding its smash-hit ONE PIECE universe with a brand-new LEGO animated series. Adapting the beloved live-action adaptation—which itself adapted the iconic anime and manga—this blocky adventure brings Luffy, Zoro, Nami, and the rest of the crew to life in a highly destructive and utterly hilarious way. Expect plenty of clever visual gags, swashbuckling action, and that signature, irreverent LEGO charm.

Murder in a Small Town (Seasons 1-2)

Coming to Netflix: Tuesday

Picture Credit: FOX

If you’re looking for your next crime binge, this is exactly what the doctor ordered. This atmospheric FOX mystery series follows a burned-out, big-city detective who retreats to a quiet, picturesque coastal town in desperate need of some peace and quiet. But, as is always the case in sleepy, idyllic TV towns, he quickly discovers that dark secrets, local scandals, and brutal murders wash up on its shores just as often as the tide. With the first two seasons dropping together, it’s the perfect moody mystery to curl up with on a rainy autumn evening.
