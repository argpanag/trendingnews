Costco Reveals Its Full Black Friday Ad and Sale Dates for 2026, Discounts Include Frozen Food and Gaming PCs

TheTools · 31.10.2026 04:51 · source · permalink

Wholesale deals.

Costco has officially revealed its annual Holiday Savings ad and you can already start browsing what will be discounted this year. Similar to other retailers, Costco is offering multiple waves of savings leading up to Black Friday and Cyber Monday. The first wave of sales kicked off on October 30 and will run through November 15.

Costco Black Friday Sale Schedule

According to Costco's official page, most the deals will be available both in-warehouse or online except for on Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday. You can check out the full schedule below and click on each link to check out the ads for each sale.

  • Pre-pre-Thanksgiving Day Sale - October 30 - November 15
  • Pre-Thanksgiving Day Sale - October 16 - November 30
  • Thanksgivng Day Sale - November 26 (Online Only)
  • Black Friday Sale - November 27-30
  • Cyber Monday Sale - November 30 (Online Only)

What are some of the best deals?

The different sales have a pretty varied selection of deals, so there are savings to be had pretty much all month long. The pre-pre-Thanksgiving sale that's happening now and the pre-thanksgiving sale both have a good amount of deals on snacks, frozen foods, and kitchen essentials. There's also a decent number of discounts on TVs and gaming monitors during this time, all of which will last until Cyber Monday if they stay in stock.

As you might expect, some of the best deals on tech and other big-ticket items don't arrive until the actual Black Friday sale kicks off after Thanksgiving. This is when you'll find an RTX 5060 gaming PC for $300 off, a Dyson vacuum under $300, and a great 4K TV for under $1,000. This is also when some of the best frozen food deals happen, including $5 off Costco's signature frozen lasagnas.

Both the individual Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday sales offer a few interesting items, but the bulk of the sale is not there. If you need a pair of Dickies men's overalls or cargo pants for $30, you won't want to miss the Thanksgiving sale. And if you plan on eating at Applebee's a lot in the future, the Cyber Monday gift card deal is definitely worth taking a look at.

More Black Friday Sales Announced

Costco isn't the only one currently running a holiday sale before Halloween. Best Buy just kicked off its DoorBOOster sale after announcing the full Best By Black Friday schedule for 2026. Both Walmart and Target have also announced their sale dates for November. It's already looking like a busy month for anyone looking to save as much as possible this year.
