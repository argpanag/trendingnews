Two taken to hospital after fire involving EV battery at Bishan workshop

TheTools · 19.11.2026 12:56 · source · permalink

Two people were taken to hospital for smoke inhalation after a fire broke out at a workshop in Sin Ming Auto City in Bishan on Wednesday (Nov 19).

A video posted on the SG News Warehouse Sale Facebook page showed flames burning inside the workshop, with thick black smoke billowing from the premises.

The video also captured several loud noises as sparks appeared to come from inside the workshop.

Numerous people were seen standing outside the workshop, watching the fire, with some using their clothing to protect themselves from inhaling the smoke.

The post's caption claimed that a hybrid battery had caught fire, allegedly causing the blaze.

In another video shared by witness Eillron Goh, several SCDF vehicles were seen at the scene as firefighters responded to the incident.

The Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) said it was alerted to a fire at 160 Sin Ming Drive at about 1.35pm.

The fire involved an electric vehicle (EV) battery unit at a workshop on the fifth floor of the industrial building. The SCDF extinguished the fire using two water jets and conveyed two people to Singapore General Hospital (SGH) for smoke inhalation.

The cause of the fire is under investigation.

EV fires in Singapore

There were 125 vehicle fires in the first half of 2026, of which 10, or 8 per cent, involved EVs.

In a social media post on Nov 14, Senior Minister of State for Home Affairs Goh Pei Ming said none of the 10 cases involved EV batteries.

He advised vehicle owners to service their vehicles regularly at authorised workshops and avoid unauthorised modifications.

Goh also reminded motorists not to attempt to extinguish fires involving EV batteries, or fires that are too large to handle. Instead, they should move to a safe distance and call 995.
