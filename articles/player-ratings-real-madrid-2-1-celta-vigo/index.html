Player Ratings: Real Madrid 2–1 Celta Vigo

TheTools · 22.11.2026 01:53 · source · permalink
  • Real Madrid CF: La Liga

Jude Bellingham scored the equalizer and Carlos Espí came off the bench to win it for Real Madrid. Thibaut Courtois kept the comeback within reach, while Arda Güler’s move into a deeper role and Vinícius Júnior’s introduction helped turn the game in the second half.

Starters

Thibaut Courtois — 8. Made two outstanding saves to keep Madrid in it. The biggest came after Bernardo Silva gave the ball away with Celta already a goal up.

Ibrahima Konaté — 7.5. Recovered well from one poor giveaway. Konaté was strong in his challenges and finished with a game-high four tackles.

Dean Huijsen — 7.5. Madrid’s high line looked shaky early on, and Huijsen was often the one left to cover the space behind it. He showed both strength and composure in those situations.

Trent Alexander-Arnold — 6.5. Looked sluggish early and misplaced passes he usually makes comfortably. Grew into the game, won a couple of strong defensive challenges and played some dangerous balls into the final third.

Álvaro Carreras — 6. Celta’s press gave him problems. He rarely looked sure of his next pass when they closed him down on the left.

Eduardo Camavinga — 6. Lost track of his runner on Celta’s goal. He won some good challenges in midfield, though, and did a better job of helping Madrid play through pressure in the second half.

Bernardo Silva — 5.5. Uncharacteristically loose on the ball. His worst giveaway handed Celta a chance to make it 2–0, and he had Courtois to thank for keeping it at one.

Arda Güler — 8. Came alive when he dropped deeper in the second half. Receiving the ball earlier in the move gave him more influence over Madrid’s buildup, and he played a big part in the sequence that led to Bellingham’s equalizer.

Jude Bellingham — 8.5. Defended relentlessly and still found the legs to keep crashing the box. Some of his best touches came with barely any room to move. His reward for all those runs was the equalizer, which he scored from Mbappé’s pass.

Yan Diomande — 7.5. Quieter than usual, though he still looked capable of beating his man whenever Madrid found him. Carried a threat on either wing.

Kylian Mbappé — 6.5. Missed a penalty and struggled to find his range as a shooter. His most important contribution came as a provider when he picked out Bellingham for the equalizer.

Substitutes

Marco Cucurella — 7.5. Brought energy and fight from his first involvement. His combinations with Vinícius gave Madrid more life down the left.

Carlos Espí — 10. Five minutes, five touches, and his third match-winner of the season. What else are you supposed to give him?

Fede Valverde — 7. Ideally, he’d have had the full 90 minutes off. Mourinho needed his energy, and Valverde gave him that, even on a day when his passing was off.

Vinícius Júnior — 8. Gave Madrid a lift as soon as he came on. Combined well with Cucurella on the left and had a hand in both goals.

Endrick — 7.5. Made several good runs down the right, giving Madrid an outlet on that side while Vinícius and Cucurella combined on the other.
