Aer Lingus axing their credit card and gives customers a year to use perks

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:18 · source · permalink

Bank of Ireland and Aer Lingus are to discontinue their popular Aer Credit Card, with customers being given just over a year’s notice before the travel rewards scheme comes to an end.

The two companies have confirmed that the card will no longer be available from November 30, 2027, following a review of their credit card offerings.

Customers can continue using their cards as normal until that date, meaning they can still collect Avios points and enjoy the travel benefits currently attached to their accounts.

However, there are some important changes cardholders will need to be aware of. The Aer Credit Card currently offers perks including two return European flights, airport lounge passes, fast-track security and priority boarding.

Customers will be able to book their two return flights up to November 30, 2027, with Aer Lingus agreeing to honour bookings made before that deadline for travel up to 11 months later.

However, lounge passes and fast-track and priority boarding passes must all be used before the November 2027 cut-off.

Cardholders will also stop earning Avios through the credit card after that date, although any points already collected will remain available to spend as normal.

Another major change concerns travel insurance, with the worldwide multi-trip cover attached to the Aer Credit Card ending at midnight on November 30, 2027.

Bank of Ireland says customers will be offered an alternative credit card, with further details about the transition expected in early 2027 and those who wish to keep travel insurance can consider switching to the Bank of Ireland Platinum Credit Card, which also offers multi-trip cover.

There is currently a €76.18 annual fee for the Platinum card, compared with the Aer Credit Card’s monthly charge of €6.50 and customers do not need to do anything immediately and can continue using their existing cards until the withdrawal date.

Alternatively, they can switch to another Bank of Ireland credit card or choose to close their account if they no longer need it.

Anyone who decides to switch or close their account before November 30, 2027, should be aware that most of their Aer Credit Card benefits will end immediately. However, flights already booked will still be honoured by Aer Lingus, subject to the terms and conditions.

Bank of Ireland says it will contact customers again in early 2027 with more information about moving to an alternative card. The bank has thanked customers for their loyalty and said it remains committed to providing credit card products and services that meet their needs.
