The world’s best pizza has been crowned - and it’s not in Italy

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:33 · source · permalink

Founded by chef Michele Pascarella, London's 'Napoli on the Road' took the top spot ahead of the reigning champ 'Una Pizza Napoletana'.

London has officially got the world's best pizza. Napoli on the Road, the Chiswick pizzeria founded by chef Michele Pascarella, has taken the top spot at the 50 Top Pizza World Awards 2026, beating out hundreds of contenders from around the globe.

The win was announced in Naples – the original birthplace of pizza – where Pascarella celebrated a remarkable journey. He launched Napoli on the Road in 2016 from a three-wheeled Piaggio Ape fitted with a wood-fired oven, serving pizzas at London’s streets and farmers’ markets.

A decade later, the business has grown into restaurants in Chiswick, Richmond and Soho, with the latter branch offering a pizza tasting menu inspired by Pascarella’s childhood in Caserta in the south of Italy.

"I’m in shock. I probably need to have a few drinks and a sit down to process this. I can’t explain how big this is. It’s like the Oscars but for pizza," Pascarella said at the ceremony in Naples.

After two consecutive years at number one, Anthony Mangieri’s Una Pizza Napoletana in New York has dropped to second place. In third is Milan's Confine, ran by Francesco Capece and Mario Ventura.

The results of the competition, now in its tenth year, are based on almost 1,000 anonymous inspectors, with each restaurant visited twice and judged on its pizza, ingredients, service and hospitality.
