  7. 'Very controversial' at Emiratespublished at 16:00 GMT
    16:00 GMT

    HT: Arsenal 0-0 Hull

    Dale Johnson
    Football issues correspondent

    That is a very controversial VAR intervention at the Emirates.

    A player who is in an offside position only becomes active when they commit an offside offence.

    Kai Havertz must be "attempting to play the ball, or challenging an opponent for the ball" - otherwise the shirt pull by Nobel Mendy takes precedence. The foul comes before any offside offence.

    Havertz did not commit an offside offence, and this should have been a spot-kick to Arsenal regardless of the offside.

    We saw this a couple of seasons ago between Newcastle and Bournmouth. Fabian Schar was just offside but pulled back as he moved to run onto a free-kick delivery. A penalty was awarded after a VAR review.
