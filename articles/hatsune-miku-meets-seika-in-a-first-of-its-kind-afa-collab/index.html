Hatsune Miku Meets Seika in a First-of-Its-Kind AFA Collab

TheTools · 26.11.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Anime Festival Asia (AFA) Singapore 2026 has paired its mascot Seika with Hatsune Miku for the Hatsune Miku × Seika SJ60 merchandise collection, with original artwork by Japanese illustrator LAM. The collection will be sold through AFA SHOP during the festival at Suntec Singapore from 27 to 29 November 2026, and pre-orders are open on AFASHOP.co now, so Miku fans can sort their haul before the doors open on Friday.

AFA calls it a first-of-its-kind crossover between its mascot and the virtual superstar, created especially for SJ60. That’s the marker for 60 years of diplomatic relations between Singapore and Japan. The organisers frame the collab as a celebration of the Japanese pop culture fandom that links fans across Singapore, Japan and Southeast Asia, and few characters fit that brief better than a singer who began life as software.

Meet the Two Leads

Seika is the face of the festival, sitting front and centre on this year’s key visual. AFA launched in Singapore in 2008, and this year’s festival (AFASG26, as it styles itself) is its 18th edition here. The Singapore flagship has collected three Singapore Tourism Awards: Best Leisure Event in 2019, then Outstanding Leisure Event in 2023 and 2024. For a refresher on what an AFA weekend typically packs in, our look back at Anime Festival Asia 2023’s five standout highlights is a handy primer.

Miku’s origin story is stranger, and better. So many users made songs with her and posted them online that she grew into a cultural phenomenon, moving into merchandise and live performances as a virtual singer. VOCALOID, the label the fandom and AFA’s Sunday concert run under, is a trademark of Yamaha Corporation.

What’s in the Drop

AFA says the collection runs from stylish apparel and premium framed art canvases to everyday accessories and collectibles, all carrying LAM’s exclusive artwork.

If you’re choosing one piece, make it a canvas. The whole draw here is new LAM art of two characters AFA has never put together before, and a framed print is the only format that keeps that art at full size instead of shrinking it onto a keychain. Apparel is the easy alternative if you’d rather wear your Miku than hang her on the wall of your HDB bedroom!

AFA says the collection will be available through AFA SHOP during the festival. Pre-ordering is the calmer route, since nobody wants to build their Saturday around a merch queue.

Miku’s Sunday Night

AFA’s three concert nights close on Sunday, 29 November with the AFA VOCALOID NIGHT feat. Hatsune Miku. Go in expecting a celebration of the music Miku made possible, played by the people who write it.

That same Sunday hosts the HoneyWorks Vocal All Stars Festival, which brings CHiCO alongside members of HaKoniwalily, TAKANE NO NADESHIKO and Karen na Ivory, backed by the HoneyWorks live band. Sunday is easily the strongest night for anyone raised on Vocaloid and online-born music.

The earlier nights pull a different crowd. Friday, 27 November is Yuki Kajiura LIVE vol.#22 ~precious pieces~ in Singapore -SPECIAL SETLIST-, the composer’s first concert in Singapore, with a setlist curated for the city. Saturday, 28 November brings the AFASG26 Music Fest powered by Crunchyroll, with Kyary Pamyu Pamyu, May’n, Aooo, KAF and Isekaijoucho. The full night-by-night breakdown sits in our day-by-day guide to AFA Singapore 2026.

The Other 200 Booths

The Creators Hub is where independent artists, illustrators and makers sell original art and handmade collectibles, so budget for some impulse buys there too.

Cosplayers get their own stage with the Cosplay Singles Competition on 29 November, run through the AFA Cosplay Hub. It’s another reason Sunday stacks up as the day to go if you can only manage one.

Two big set pieces round out the floor. Toei Animation is bringing a 7-metre Goku inflatable, and YOASOBI has a dedicated booth ahead of the duo’s concert at the Singapore National Stadium on 20 February 2027.

Merch hunters with tastes beyond Miku have options as well.

Tickets Before Friday

The cheapest tickets are gone. The Super Early Bird phase has sold out, a point we covered when AFA Singapore 2026 tickets first went on sale.

Options still run from single-day exhibition tickets to multi-day stage access, sold across the Early Bird, General Sales and Show Day phases on AFA’s official AFASG26 website. All prices exclude booking fees, so factor that in before you check out.

A virtual singer born from fan-made songs, a festival mascot from Singapore and a fan-favourite illustrator all meeting on one canvas is exactly the kind of crossover that makes fandom fun. If Miku has soundtracked any part of your life, this is the AFA weekend to show up for. Lock in your pieces on AFASHOP.co, grab your tickets on the AFASG26 official website, and follow Anime Festival Asia on Facebook and Instagram for the latest updates before Friday. See you in the queue for that canvas!
