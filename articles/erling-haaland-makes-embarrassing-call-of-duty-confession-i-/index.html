Manchester City superstar Erling Haaland has revealed an unexpected side of his gaming personality, admitting that he has spent his entire life using one of the most unpopular tactics in Call of Duty.

The Norwegian striker made the amusing confession while playing the popular video game alongside British rapper Aitch, openly acknowledging that he is the type of player other gamers love to complain about.

Haaland Revisits His Childhood Gaming Days

The revelation came during a relaxed multiplayer session captured in a recently shared YouTube video. With a controller in hand, Haaland reflected on the memories that Call of Duty brought back from his younger years.

“This gives me so much nostalgic feeling when I was young,” Haaland remarked.

The conversation soon turned to his preferred approach to the game, prompting an admission that the Manchester City forward initially seemed reluctant to make.

“And this is a bit embarrassing to say, but I’ve been a camper my whole life. I’m that guy that you hate.”

In Call of Duty, camping refers to a strategy in which players remain in a particular location, waiting for opponents rather than actively moving around the map. Although effective in certain situations, the tactic has long been a source of frustration among gamers.
