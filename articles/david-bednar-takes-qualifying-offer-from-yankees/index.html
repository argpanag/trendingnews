    David Bednar

    NYY Relief Pitcher #53
    Player Stats

    David Bednar accepted the qualifying offer on Tuesday, locking him in as a Yankee in 2027.

    It’s not the three-year deal he might have gotten without having his market curtailed by the qualifying offer, but Bednar’s $23.15 million salary next season gives him the highest AAV for a reliever ever, overtaking Edwin Díaz at $23 million. Bednar will almost certainly remain the Yankees’ closer. He had a 2.47 ERA and 35 saves in a career-high 69 1/3 innings last season.
    - Matthew Pouliot
