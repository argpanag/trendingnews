'Loud explosion' heard in early hours as Glasgow fire crews rush to scene

TheTools · 09.10.2026 15:19 · source · permalink

Glasgow residents were woken to a "loud explosion" in the early hours of Friday morning (October 9) when a van caught fire in the city's southside. Emergency crews were alerted to the incident on St Edmund Court at around 2.36am.

Locals reported hearing a "bang like a bomb going off" at the time of the incident.

One fire appliance was dispatched to the scene, and crews departed at 3.48am after it was confirmed the area was safe.

Photos of the scene shared with Glasgow Live show flashing blue lights from the fire appliance as what appears to be smoke billows into the surrounding streets.

No injuries were reported as a result of the fire.

A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: “We were alerted at 2.36am on Friday, 9 October, to reports of a fire affecting a van on St Edmund Court, Glasgow.

“Operations Control mobilised one fire appliance to the scene.

"There were no casualties and crews left the scene at 3.48am after ensuring the area was made safe.”
