Bigg Boss 20 has entered a new week, offering viewers yet another round of drama. The new drama took place during the new captaincy task.

In the recent episode, the current captain, Mahhi Vij, was removed from the captaincy race after 6 contestants voted against her. Although she was appointed the sanchalak of the captaincy task, the task involved square and circle shapes placed in the garden area, and contestants had to move to the shape announced by Bigg Boss.

Contestants who failed to make the shape were eliminated from the task.

Also Read: Bigg Boss 20: Love Gill's team alleges character assassination, ScoutOP's team issues clarification after Kaashvi questions their 'brother-sister' bond

While all the contestants were seen putting up a tough fight for captaincy, it resulted in a major fight between Gullu and Yung DSA.

Yung and Gullu became involved in a physical altercation, and the two were seen shoving each other during the dispute.

During this ugly fight, Manish Goel had a sudden asthma attack inside the house. Nevertheless, Gullu and Yung remained unaffected by Manish's worsening health.

The viral tweet reads, "During the fight, #Manish suffered an asthma att@ck, yet the fight continued even after that."

— Livefeed Updates (@BBossLivefeed) October 27, 2026

Things escalated when Gullu lashed out at Yung and said, "Tere jaise log kachhe dhote hain... Ek maarunga, saare baal gir jayenge!"

Yung replied, "Baal pe mat jaa tujh jaise ko salary pe rakhta hu."

While a few days are still left in this week, it will be interesting to see if this will lead to any midweek elimination on Bigg Boss 20.

Meanwhile, as the captain task has been drawing attention, it is being reported that Kanika Mann will become the new captain of Bigg Boss 20.
