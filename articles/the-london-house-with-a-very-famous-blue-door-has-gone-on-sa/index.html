The London house with a VERY famous blue door has gone on sale for £7.5million

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:32 · source · permalink

One of London's most famous houses has gone on the market for a staggering £7.5 million – and film fans might recognise its iconic blue front door.

The Notting Hill property featured in the 1999 romantic comedy of the same name, starring Hugh Grant and Julia Roberts, and was the on-screen home of Grant's character, William Thacker.

Located on Westbourne Park Road in Notting Hill, the three-bedroom house once belonged to film director and screenwriter Richard Curtis.

The listing reads: "Behind one of the most recognisable front doors in London lies a remarkable three-bedroom house, once home to screenwriter Richard Curtis and immortalised as William Thacker's residence in the 1999 film Notting Hill.

"Extending to approximately 2,928 sq ft, the property pairs a unique cinematic history with exceptional architecture, voluminous interiors and two substantial outside spaces."

The home features two bathrooms, an open-plan kitchen, and a 34ft-long courtyard.

It also includes a roof terrace measuring approximately 29ft by 14ft, accessible via a spiral staircase.

Now being marketed by Sotheby's International Realty, it is described as "remarkable" home and a chance to own "unique cinematic history" (Image: William Lailey / SWNS)

The listing describes the property as "remarkable" and offers buyers the chance to own a piece of "unique cinematic history."

It is understood that director Richard Curtis was living at the address during filming, and production designer Stuart Craig selected the house for the film without initially realising it belonged to the writer.

The original 19th-century door was sold at Christie's for charity.

Its replacement has since been painted the same shade of blue that made it famous.

There was no answer at the door when SWNS visited yesterday (Nov 9).
