The singer announced the release of her new song and opened the pre-sale period on her website for 24 hours.

aylor Swift has announced the release of “Patient Zero”, her new single, which will be available next September 25. The singer shared the date through her official channels and confirmed that the song can now be pre-ordered on her website.

“I’ve been eagerly waiting to tell you that my new single ‘Patient Zero’ will be released on September 25,” Swift wrote in the message with which she introduced the song. The presale was enabled for 24 hours, as the artist herself explained.

“Patient Zero”, with a release date

The announcement arrives with a title that refers to the expression used to identify the first known case of a disease within an outbreak. In Swift's case, the term appears as the name chosen for her new song.

Ver esta publicación en Instagram

The artist made a post on her social media where she announced the release of her new single accompanied by a series of zeros, a visual reference to the name of the new song.

Swift has built much of her career on songs inspired by personal experiences and romantic relationships. In the case of “Patient Zero”, we will still have to wait for the release to learn about the content and the meaning that the singer has given to the title.
