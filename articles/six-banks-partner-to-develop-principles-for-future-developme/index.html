Six banks partner to develop principles for future development of agentic commerce

TheTools · 23.09.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

Six banks have tied up to publish a set of joint principles related to the future development of agentic commerce.

The banks are NatWest Group NWG RBSPF, ASB Bank, Bank of America BAC, Capital One COF, Commonwealth Bank of Australia CBAUF CMWAY, and ING Group ING INGVF.

The framework is expected to help ensure both that customers and merchants retain choice, control, and flexibility in how they pay and get paid, along with safety and security.

"Agentic commerce has the potential to transform how people and businesses interact with the economy, but its success will ultimately depend on the strength of the underlying payments infrastructure," said Jonathan Oram, executive general manager of corporate banking and payments at ASB Bank.

"As agentic commerce continues to evolve, establishing trust and confidence across the ecosystem will be critical to its long-term success. Building confidence among consumers, merchants, and financial institutions will require thoughtful approaches to identity, authorization, fraud prevention, liability management, and customer protection," said Mark Monaco, head of global payments solutions at Bank of America.
