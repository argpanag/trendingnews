Everything you need to know about the Rugby League World Cup

TheTools · 14.10.2026 00:48 · source · permalink

The 2026 Rugby League World Cup kicks off tomorrow evening in Sydney

The first match reignites one of sports great rivalries when New Zealand takes on Australia. 

The Aussies are defending men’s and women’s champions, having won every tournament since 2008, but the Kiwis and the Kiwi Ferns look strong and there are some good looking challengers from the Pacific nations.

With Jesse to explain everything you need to know about the competition is journalist, author & rugby league expert Will Evans.
