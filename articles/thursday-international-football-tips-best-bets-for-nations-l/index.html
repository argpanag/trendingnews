Bellamy’s side are winless in five since a 1-0 victory in Liechtenstein last November and failed to beat Bosnia at home in the World Cup qualification play-offs.

Bellamy also held talks to move to Burnley in the summer until deciding not to take the role, and that saga is still fresh in the mind of supporters.
