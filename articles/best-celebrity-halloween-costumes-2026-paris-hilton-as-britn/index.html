Best Celebrity Halloween Costumes 2026: Paris Hilton as Britney Spears, Lisa Rinna as Kylie Jenner and More

TheTools · 31.10.2026 21:28 · source · permalink

Stars are embracing the spooky holiday with creative, nostalgic and pop culture inspired costumes. The Hollywood Reporter has compiled a list of our favorites.

Hollywood stars are celebrating Halloween with a wide range of costumes, from recreating iconic on-screen characters to paying tribute to fellow celebrities.

Whether it’s Camila Mendes and Madelaine Petsch channeling Betty and Wilma from The Flintstones, Kim Kardashian transforming into her daughter, North West, or Paris Hilton debuting multiple costumes throughout the holiday, celebrities are getting into the Halloween spirit.

Below, see a roundup of this year’s costumes from a host of stars, including some of The Hollywood Reporter’s favorites. (This is an ongoing story that will be updated throughout the weekend as more celebrities reveal their costumes.)

Today Show Hosts

The Today show brought NBC’s 100th-anniversary celebration to Halloween, as the morning show hosts dressed up to highlight memorable programming from the past century. Dressed as an NBC page, Savannah Guthrie led a group of tourists, who closely resembled some Today hosts, through 30 Rockefeller Plaza. They first encountered Willie Geist as Tonight Show host Johnny Carson’s Carmac the Magnificent, followed by Dylan Dreyer as I Dream of Jeannie’s title character, played by Barbara Eden, and Al Roker as Eddie Murphy’s Saturday Night Live character Gumby. When the tour got to Must-See TV, Guthrie donned a second costume, dressing as Friends’ Rachel Green alongside Jenna Bush Hager’s Phoebe Buffay. The Today show also honored itself with Carson Daly dressing as Ella Langley to celebrate his Today PopStart segment. And NBC’s unscripted programming was also highlighted, specifically its Olympic coverage: Laura Jarrett dressed as Simone Biles and Craig Melvin transformed into Snoop Dogg, who’s become known for his memorable Olympics appearances in recent years. The real Martha Stewart even joined Melvin’s Snoop Dogg. And, to celebrate NBC’s holiday traditions, including the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade and Christmas in Rockefeller Center, Sheinelle Jones joined the Radio City Rockettes for a sparkly dance routine. Guthrie’s page even got a visit from 30 Rock page Kenneth Parcell, with Jack McBrayer reprising his role from the beloved NBC comedy that recently celebrated its 20th anniversary.

Live! With Kelly and Mark

Live! With Kelly and Mark brought back former co-host Ryan Seacrest for its New Year’s Eve-inspired Halloween Shockin’ Eve. Seacrest appeared at the beginning of the show as the hosts honored Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Consuelos, dressed as the webslinger, swung through the New York streets as Ripa’s Yelena Belova held on. The hosts took the stage as Bad Bunny and Lady Gaga from the 2026 Super Bowl halftime show before changing costumes at the commercial break to Anne Hathaway’s Penelope and Matt Damon’s Odysseus in The Odyssey before they ended the show joined by producer Michael Gelman and DJ Deja Vu, with the whole Live team all dressed as Toy Story 5 characters. Live’s Halloween episode featured more than 60 costumes, including pretaped segments in which Ripa dressed as Inde Navarrette’s Nikki from Obsession, who can’t get enough of a different kind of Bear in Consuelos’ squishy creature, and Elizabeth Holmes as shown in You Can See Everything as part of a Traitors parody. Guest Lisa Rinna also got in on the action, dressing as Kylie Jenner alongside Gelman’s Timothee Chalamet, as Rinna and Gelman sported Jenner and Chalamet’s memorable orange looks from Marty Supreme’s Los Angeles premiere late last year.
