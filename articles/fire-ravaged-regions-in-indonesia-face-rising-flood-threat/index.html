Fire-ravaged regions in Indonesia face rising flood threat

The warning comes after months of drought and widespread forest and peatland fires, fuelled by prolonged dry conditions under the influence of El Nino.

  • Indonesia’s fire-hit regions now face a higher flood risk as the rainy season starts, because burned land and damaged peatlands cannot absorb water well.
  • BNPB warned of flash floods and landslides, while BMKG said rain will begin in most areas between November and December, with El Nino possibly delaying the peak.
  • Experts and activists urged peatland restoration and corporate accountability, but doubts remain over whether the revived peatland agency has enough power and resources.
