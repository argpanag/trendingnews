Your pictures of the Harvest Moon glowing in the UK sky

TheTools · 27.09.2026 21:56 · source · permalink

A beautiful sight from both the plane and on the ground in Buckinghamshire

September's full Moon glowed across the UK sky on Saturday and many people seemed to have perfect conditions for viewing it.

It's also known as the Harvest Moon because of its association with the time of year people traditionally gather crops.

The Moon reached its fullest point at 17:49 BST on Saturday but it was later, when the sky got darker, that it made its biggest impact.
