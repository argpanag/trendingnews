    Semi goes up in flames on foggy, icy Connector Wednesday morning

    There were minor delays on the Okanagan Connector (Hwy 97C) this morning.

    A semi caught fire near the Hwy 5A turn off, just east of Merritt, and traffic control was at the scene.

    There has been no official word on the status of the truck driver.

    Along with that notable event, drivers also reported foggy and icy conditions during the Hwy 97C commute.

    The highway remains open and DriveBC expects to provide another update at 10 am.
