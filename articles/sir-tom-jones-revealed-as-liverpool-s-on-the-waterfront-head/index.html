Sir Tom Jones revealed as Liverpool's On the Waterfront headliner

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:53 · source · permalink

Sir Tom Jones is set to return to Liverpool next summer as a headliner for On the Waterfront 2027.

The Welsh singer, whose hits include It's Not Unusual and Delilah, will perform at the city's Pier Head on 20 June.

He joins Jamie Webster in the festival line-up, who has already been announced as the headline act for 18 June.

Sir Tom recently returned to performing after being forced to cancel a number of concerts because of illness.

He told fans earlier this month he feared his life was "over" after being admitted to hospital in the US.

He spent a week at the Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center in Los Angeles and cancelled a number of concerts, but has since returned to stage and told the audience it was a serious "bad case of the flu".

The 86-year-old also recently made headlines after revealing ITV had "made the decision" to "fire" him from The Voice UK coaching panel, having served as a coach on the reality show since it first debuted in 2012.

Throughout his career, Sir Tom has explored different musical styles, collaborating with artists from Stevie Wonder, Aretha Franklin and Van Morrison as well as Dolly Parton and Ed Sheeran.

He received a knighthood from the Queen in 2006 and has won multiple Brit Awards.

The four-day festival will also see a headline performance from Cream Classical on 19 June, which organisers said would be a set that "reimagines some of the biggest dance anthems of all time through spectacular orchestral arrangements and powerful live vocals".

The opening headliner on 17 June is yet to be announced.
