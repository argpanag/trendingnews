University of Buffalo installs weather stations in Plattsburgh

TheTools · 16.10.2026 21:50 · source · permalink

The University of Buffalo has partnered with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation to install weather stations in Plattsburgh as part of a statewide climate study.

The weather stations are being placed in five different climate zones across New York to monitor local conditions, including temperature and relative humidity patterns. The data collected will be compiled in the spring to create a statewide design guide, which will provide information to help communities adapt their streets to rising temperatures and prepare for extreme heat.

“This is a really important project, largely because it's putting the needs of communities first and thinking about what are the solutions that they can actually adopt to deal with changing climatic conditions and giving them information about what those changing climates might be at the street level, which is where most of us actually experience the environment,” said Lauren Ames Fischer, a faculty member at the University of Buffalo.

The project is focusing on rural communities that often receive less attention in climate studies. It is funded by the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation.
