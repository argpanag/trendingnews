See the best celebrity Halloween costumes of 2026

TheTools · 01.11.2026 02:07 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Halloween 2026 is here, and Hollywood's biggest stars are getting into the spooky spirit!

From elaborate transformations and nostalgic throwbacks to clever pop culture references, celebrities are once again pulling out all the stops with their Halloween costumes.

Ashley Graham made a splash dressed as Ariel from Disney's "The Little Mermaid," while Kelly Ripa and Mark Consuelos showed off multiple looks during their Halloween-themed episode of ABC's "Live with Kelly and Mark," including costumes inspired by Lady Gaga and "Toy Story."

Keep scrolling to see how your favorite celebrities are celebrating Halloween 2026, and check back throughout the day as we update this roundup with more costumes and photos.

Kelly Ripa and Mark Consuelos

For their epic Halloween episode, Ripa, Consuelos and their guests donned several costumes, including characters from "Toy Story," "Spider-Man," "Obsession" and more.

Ashley Graham

Ashley Graham lip-synced to "Part of Your World," while revealing she was dressed as the Little Mermaid.

Danielle Fishel, Rider Strong, and Will Friedle

Co-hosts Fishel, Strong and Friedle recreated the Halloween costumes their characters wore during the beloved ABC sitcom's first season.

Paris Hilton and family

Paris Hilton and her family dressed as characters from "Beauty and the Beast" and "The Addams Family" for two epic themed costume reveals this Halloween.

Lisa Rinna

Lisa Rinna dressed as Kylie Jenner and was joined by Live with Kelly and Mark executive producer Michael Gelman as Timothee Chalamet.

Keke Palmer

Palmer and son, Leo, dressed as Janet and Michael Jackson for an homage to the music video "Scream."

Zendaya

Zendaya honored Halle Berry with a throwback costume, shared by her stylist, Law Roach.

Disney is the parent company of "Good Morning America" and ABC.
