2 PS5 Games Are Being Delisted on December 31

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink

From GameRant (Written by Dominik Bošnjak) on October 30, 2026 | OpenCritic

Two Street Outlaws games for the PlayStation 5 are being delisted on December 31, 2026, according to a late October publisher notice. The PS5 is one of several platforms they are leaving on that date.

Street Outlaws is a Discovery Channel reality TV franchise that debuted in 2013, originally focusing on Oklahoma City's underground street-racing scene. Its central conceit revolved around "The List," a ranking of the area's 10 fastest cars, with drivers modifying their vehicles and racing one another to move up the standings. The series later expanded beyond that format and Oklahoma City while retaining its focus on highly modified cars, drag racing, and competition between established crews. Its popularity eventually led to two licensed video game adaptations from publisher GameMill Entertainment and now-defunct Dutch developer Team6 Game Studios.

Unless you straight-up buy consoles, these games are hard to get nowadays and have become rarities.
