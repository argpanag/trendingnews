Man killed in shooting on Detroit’s west side

TheTools · 26.10.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

Local News

No information provided on possible suspect

DETROIT – A man was killed in a shooting on Detroit’s west side on Monday.

The shooting happened near Livernois and Buena Vista Street at around 1:30 p.m. on Oct. 26.

Police responded to the area for a report of a shooting. When officers arrived, they found a man suffering from a gunshot wound.

Medics arrived and pronounced the man dead.

The investigation is ongoing. No further information was provided.

Anyone with information is asked to call Crime Stoppers at 1-800 SPEAK UP, DetroitRewards.tv or the Detroit Police Department Homicide Unit at (313) 596-2260.
