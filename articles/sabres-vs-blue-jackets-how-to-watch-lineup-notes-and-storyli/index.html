Sabres vs. Blue Jackets | How to watch, lineup notes, and storylines | Buffalo Sabres

TheTools · 23.09.2026 00:08 · source · permalink

Rasmus Dahlin, Tage Thompson and the rest of Buffalo's regulars will make their preseason debuts.

Rasmus Dahlin, Tage Thompson and the rest of the Buffalo Sabres’ regulars are expected to make their preseason debuts against the Columbus Blue Jackets at KeyBank Center on Tuesday.

With a shortened preseason, the Sabres have forgone the usual practice of blending prospects and veterans to start their exhibition schedule. Tonight’s lineup should closely resemble the one we’ll see when the regular season opens in Columbus on Oct. 1.

Buffalo opened the preseason Monday with a 4-1 loss in Pittsburgh. That lineup consisted mostly of depth players and prospects. Read more on that game here.

The puck drops at 7 p.m. Here’s what you need to know.

  • Tickets for tonight

How to watch

Streaming: Sabres.com

Radio: WGR 550

Tonight’s roster

Forwards: Zach Benson, Sam Carrick, Josh Doan, Konsta Helenius, Peyton Krebs, Jiri Kulich, Beck Malenstyn, Ryan McLeod, Josh Norris, Noah Ostlund, Jack Quinn, Tage Thompson

Defensemen: Louis Crevier, Rasmus Dahlin, Owen Power, Mattias Samuelsson, Conor Timmins, Olen Zellweger

Goaltenders: Colten Ellis, Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen

Storylines

1. Debuts for Zellweger, Crevier

Tonight will offer a first look at Buffalo’s two offseason additions on defense, Olen Zellweger and Louis Crevier.

Zellweger has opened training camp on a pair with Owen Power, occupying the spot vacated by Bowen Byram’s departure. Zellweger’s shared characteristics with Byram – speed and offensive instincts – have already shown through a few days of practice and in Saturday’s Blue and Gold Scrimmage, but he’ll still have to earn the job full-time.

Crevier, meanwhile, has been a full participant through the early days of camp after undergoing offseason shoulder surgery. The 6-foot-8 defenseman has been skating on a pair with Conor Timmins.

2. A second showing for Kulich

The new collective bargaining agreement dictates that veterans of 100 of more career NHL games can play a maximum of two preseason contests.

One player unaffected by that rule: Jiri Kulich, who is the lone skater from Monday’s game set to play again on Tuesday. At age 22, Kulich has 75 career games on his resume.

The Sabres are capitalizing on the opportunity to give Kulich as much runway as possible after he missed most of last season due to a blood clot. He led Sabres forwards with 19:39 of ice time during Monday’s game and had six shot attempts. Coach Lindy Ruff said he'll "in all likelihood" dress Kulich for all four exhibitions.

3. The Ostlund line

The forward line of Zach Benson, Noah Ostlund and Jack Quinn – all of whom are candidates for breakout seasons in 2026-27 – was Buffalo’s best in Saturday’s Blue and Gold Scrimmage. Ostlund and Quinn scored goals while Benson was his usual, tenacious self.

Assuming the line stays together, tonight will be a chance to see how it fares against outside competition – and a possible preview of a look we’ll see during the regular season.

4. Columbus' lineup

The Blue Jackets are bringing a game-night roster with a good mix of NHL and minor-league players, the NHLers including Miles Wood, Dmitri Voronkov, Carson Soucy and goalie Cam Talbot. See their full group here.
