Calvin Harris Announces First Ever Headline Australia Tour

TheTools · 15.09.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Calvin Harris has announced he will head to Australia next year for his first-ever headline tour of the country

Supplied

Calvin Harris has announced he will head to Australia next year for his first-ever headline tour of the country.

Having played at events like Stereosonic, Big Day Out and Festival X around the country over the years, the Scottish DJ superstar will perform his own dates next February in Perth, Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne.

Live Nation said in a statement on Wednesday: “The Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 will celebrate his expansive repertoire, seamlessly blending his seminal, record-breaking studio albums – such as 18 Months, Motion, and the critically acclaimed Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 – with his latest club bangers, massive chart-topping collaborations, and his recent compilation album 96 Months.”

Having already performed at major festivals like Coachella and Glastonbury, Harris this year made his long-awaited debut at Tomorrowland in Belgium. More recently, he dropped his latest track, “Satisfy,” with Irish singer-songwriter, Jazzy.

Pre-sale registrations kick off at 11am local time on Monday, September 21st, before general public tickets go on sale from 1pm local time on Thursday, September 24th.
