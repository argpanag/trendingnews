South Africa defeated Australia by 67 runs in the first ODI. | Photo Credit: AP

Australia will be looking to level its three-match ODI series against South Africa when they play at the Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg on Sunday, September 27.

The visitor lost the first ODI in Durban by 67 runs after collapsing from 129 for one to 230 all out in pursuit of 298. Matthew Breetzke continued his impressive start to life in ODI cricket with another ton for the Proteas, while Marco Jansen put in an all-round display as well.

Josh Inglis has been ruled out of the series with a fractured finger, but Pat Cummins may return for Australia in this match.

When is the South Africa vs Australia second ODI?

The South Africa vs Australia second ODI is scheduled to begin at 1:30 PM IST.

What time is the toss for the South Africa vs Australia second ODI?

The toss for the South Africa vs Australia second ODI is scheduled to happen at 1:00 PM IST

Where will the South Africa vs Australia second ODI be streamed live?

The South Africa vs Australia second ODI will be streamed live on the Fancode app and website, and will not be televised live in India.
