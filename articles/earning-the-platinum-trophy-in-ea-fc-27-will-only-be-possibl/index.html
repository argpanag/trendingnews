Earning the Platinum Trophy in EA FC 27 Will Only Be Possible Through Payment

TheTools · 09.09.2026 14:06 · source · permalink

No one forces you to do it, of course, but the fact remains.

EA Sports FC 27 will be released in early access on September 18, with a full release a week later. Meanwhile, the complete table of Achievements and Trophies for the game has already appeared before launch, and one of the tasks has particularly caught the public's attention.

This refers to the achievement "All Aboard the Premium Track." To obtain it, players must fully complete and unlock all levels of the Premium Pass – a paid battle pass that will be used in EA FC 27.

However, the standard edition of the game does not include any Premium Pass. Ultimate Edition buyers will receive the first pass for free, while Ultimate Plus Edition owners are promised the first five Premium Passes immediately.

If a player purchases an edition without a free pass or simply fails to complete it within the allotted time, they will have to buy a new Premium Pass separately – either with real money or a substantial amount of in-game Ultimate Team currency.
