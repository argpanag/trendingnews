New outright favourite as Melbourne Cup barriers locked in

TheTools · 31.10.2026 07:41 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • Horse Racing
  • Horse racing

October 31, 2026 - 7.41am

The final field and barriers have been confirmed for this year’s Lexus Melbourne Cup and it’s tough news for one leading overseas hope.

James J Braddock, who was among the betting favourites before Saturday night’s draw, will jump out of the widest gate in barrier 24.

James J Braddock (Joseph O’Brien/Craig Williams): Lightly-raced gelding having his first Australian start and brings a solid career record to the Cup, with two wins and three placings from eight starts. The four-year-old presents as a good chance and Willow in the saddle can’t hurt either. Getty Images

On the flip-side, the Ciaron Maher-trained Middle Earth has drawn barrier one and will come from the inside gate at Flemington over the 3200m trip.

Point Of Law, who rocketed into favouritism after winning the Geelong Cup, has drawn nicely in barrier nine for the race that stops the nation.

The lightly-raced stallion, who joined the Maher camp recently, has now been installed as outright $6 favourite with bookmakers after the draw.

Local hopes Juja Kibo (14) and Mr Monaco (16) drew in midfield, while the in-form So You Are drew barrier seven after earning a late inclusion.

Knight’s Choice, who won the famous race two years ago, drew barrier two.

Here’s the full field and barriers for the 2026 Melbourne Cup.
