SnakePit Post-season GDT, #8

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:00 · source · permalink
  • MLB News

The final four-pack of games today. All four Division Series are in action for the final time, and all four could finish today. Or none of them could. Or any number in between. The two American League Division Series are both 2-0, with the Rays and White Sox sitting on the edge of going into the Championship Series. Meanwhile, in the American League, the Dodgers and Brewers will both seek to clinch, having a 2-1 advantage. Los Angeles have the momentum back, having one Game #3 in Atlanta, while Milwaukee fell just short in San Diego, but have two more chances. All three games there have been decided by one run, and I’d expect another close contest tonight.

Here are the games on order today. There’ll definitely be some overlap here, so be prepared to channel surf. Or, I guess these days, switch browser tabs.

  • Cleveland Guardians @ Chicago White Sox (0-2): 1:00 pm, TBS/truTV
  • Los Angeles Dodgers @ Atlanta Braves (2-1): 3:00 pm, FS1
  • Tampa Bay Rays @ New York Yankees (2-0): 5:00 pm, TBS/truTV
  • Milwaukee Brewers @ San Diego Padres (2-1): 7:00 pm, FS1
