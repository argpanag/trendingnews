Apollo Micro Systems Q2 FY27 Results: Record Revenue and PAT

TheTools · 24.10.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

Apollo Micro Systems (NSE:APOLLO) filed an investor presentation on 24 October 2026 under Regulation 30 of SEBI's LODR Regulations, disclosing consolidated revenue from operations of Rs 6,394.4 million for H1 FY27, a 78.2% year-on-year increase, alongside record quarterly and half-yearly profit figures.

Key Highlights

  • Consolidated revenue from operations for Q2 FY27 reached Rs 3,881.5 million, up 72.3% year-on-year, marking an all-time high across all quarters reported by the company.
  • Consolidated PAT for Q2 FY27 stood at Rs 461.5 million, a 53.7% year-on-year increase, with H1 FY27 consolidated PAT reaching Rs 713.7 million, up 49.6% year-on-year.
  • Consolidated EBITDA (excluding other income) for H1 FY27 was Rs 1,649.1 million, reflecting a 64.7% year-on-year rise, with an EBITDA margin of 25.8%.
  • The order book stood at Rs 17,650 million as on 30 September 2026, as disclosed in the investor presentation.

About the Company

Apollo Micro Systems (NSE:APOLLO), headquartered at Plot No. 128/A, IDA-Mallapur, Hyderabad, Telangana, is a defence electronics company incorporated in 1997. It designs and manufactures electronic systems, sub-systems, and products for land, water, air, and space applications, serving India's defence sector. The company is engaged in indigenised missile programmes, naval systems, arms and ammunition, and OEM weapons supply under CIN L72200TG1997PLC026556.

Announcement in Detail

The investor presentation covers Q2 and H1 FY27 consolidated and standalone financials. On a consolidated basis, Q2 FY27 revenue from operations rose 72.3% year-on-year to Rs 3,881.5 million, while EBITDA (excluding other income) climbed 87.8% to Rs 1,111.8 million, yielding an EBITDA margin of 28.6%. Consolidated PBT for Q2 FY27 was Rs 924.8 million, up 114.3% year-on-year, against a tax charge of Rs 463.2 million.

On a standalone basis, Q2 FY27 revenue from operations grew 36.0% year-on-year to Rs 3,062.9 million, with standalone EBITDA (excluding other income) rising 81.6% to Rs 1,075.6 million and an EBITDA margin of 35.1%, the highest ever across all quarters per the presentation. Standalone PAT for Q2 FY27 was Rs 477.3 million, up 44.4% year-on-year. Working capital days improved to 353 days in H1 FY27 from 359 days in FY26 on a consolidated basis.

Impact on Investors

The filing shows the consolidated order book was Rs 17,650 million as on 30 September 2026, which investors will note provides a quantified revenue pipeline. The presentation also discloses that consolidated borrowings reached Rs 5,432.6 million as of FY26, and consolidated cash flow from operating activities was negative Rs 1,297.6 million in FY26, driven by working capital requirements. Shareholders will observe that H1 FY27 consolidated cash flow from operations was Rs 224.4 million, indicating a partial improvement in cash generation relative to the prior full year.

The disclosed terms indicate that the consolidated diluted EPS for H1 FY27 was Rs 2.1, compared with Rs 1.5 in H1 FY26. Investors will note that the company's R&D spending as a percentage of revenue was 8.0% in FY26, a figure the presentation flags as part of the company's long-term programme development profile across indigenised missile and naval programmes.

Sector / Market Context

India's defence capital budget has expanded consistently in recent Union Budgets, with the Ministry of Defence allocating a higher share toward domestic procurement to support indigenisation goals under the Defence Acquisition Procedure. According to SEBI-listed disclosures and industry bodies such as FICCI, domestic defence electronics firms have benefited from the government's push to reduce import dependence, a policy backdrop that shapes order flow for companies operating across land, naval, aerial, and space defence segments in India.
