Highly Cited Researchers 2026

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:10 · source · permalink

65 Max Planck researchers are on the "Global Highly Cited Researchers" list compiled annually by Clarivate Analytics

With 65 highly cited researchers, the Max Planck Society is the only German organisation in the top ten of international research institutions. The Chinese Academy of Sciences tops the list, ahead of the US universities of Harvard and Stanford. The only other European institution in the top ten is the University of Oxford (with 61 highly cited researchers).

The top 10 institutions at a glance

© Clarivate Analytics

Clarivate Analytics’ analysis shows which researchers have published the most highly cited papers over the past ten years. It also lists their fields of research and the institutions they work for.

This year, Clarivate has made 7,090 Highly Cited Researcher awards to 6,802 individuals. Some researchers were recognised in more than one field, which is why there are more awards than recipients. The researchers are based in 62 countries and regions, but 86 per cent of the awards go to just ten countries, and three-quarters go to the top five alone. Germany is among them in fourth place with 373 particularly influential researchers (ten more than in 2025), behind the USA, China and the UK. Other European countries in the top ten are the Netherlands (7th), Switzerland (8th) and France (10th).

The USA has 2,606 highly cited researchers, a fall of 0.5 per cent, while China has gained 0.9 per cent (1,484 researchers). The top five European countries account for 1,364 highly cited researchers, and the total for Europe as a whole exceeds 1,700. The Max Planck Society has 65 highly cited researchers, placing it 8th in the institutional ranking. Together with the University of Oxford (10th), there are only two European institutions in the top ten.

Alongside Harvard University (+17), it is mainly Chinese universities and organisations that are gaining highly cited researchers: Zhejiang University (+15), Tsinghua University (+10), the Chinese Academy of Sciences (+9) and China Agricultural University (+8). The University of Copenhagen (+9) has also made gains, which is why Denmark is among the top 54 institutions this year.

The method used to identify highly cited researchers – those who rank in the top one per cent of their field in terms of the number of citations – takes into account not only the number of publications and citations, but also factors relating to researchers who expand knowledge at the frontiers of research and have a community-wide influence and impact. Original articles are therefore given greater weight than review articles. Furthermore, all articles whose authors had been removed from the previous year’s Highly Cited Researchers list due to issues with research integrity were excluded.
