Team news | Porro, Tonali available for Villa clash

TheTools · 19.09.2026 13:51 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

Roberto De Zerbi reports that both Pedro Porro and Sandro Tonali are available for selection for our Premier League clash against Villa on Saturday (12.30pm).

Pedro has missed our last two matches against Everton and Liverpool after starting against Newcastle and Forest while Sandro didn't make the midweek trip to Anfield due to a knock.

Roberto was asked in Friday's pre-match press conference if Pedro, Sandro and James Maddison were ready to start against Villa - Madders came on for the final 15 minutes at Anfield.

He said: "Porro, Tonali, Maddison are all available to play. Normally, we have to understand the situation in terms of physical condition, but all of them are ready."

Asked more about Madders, Roberto added: "James is working with the team the last 10, 15 days, maybe less than 15 days. Even if he is a technical player, he needs to be fit, to stay in a good condition. When he arrives to the best condition, it is very important for us, as a personality, as a characteristic, as a quality, as an experience, the quality of his passes are different than the others."

Xavi Simons, Dejan Kulusevski, Mykhailo Mudryk and Wilson Odobert remain out.
