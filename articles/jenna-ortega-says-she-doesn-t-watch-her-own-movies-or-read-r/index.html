Jenna Ortega says she doesn't watch her own movies or read reviews

TheTools · 22.10.2026 14:54 · source · permalink

Jenna Ortega has revealed she doesn't watch her own films or read their reviews as she admitted she thinks it would 'send her into a psychosis'.

The actress, 24, insisted she tries to stay as detached as possible and only tunes into some of the chatter through social media, but also tries to limit her time online.

Jenna's reluctance to engage with what is being said about her is to maintain firm boundaries around her own relationship with her public image. 

'I think that that would send me into a psychosis of some sort,' she said when asked if she watches her own movies or reads reviews in a new Vogue interview.

Jenna also purposely avoids opening the box office updates her team sends because she 'doesn't want it to become a numbers thing'.

She only encounters chatter about her movies through word of mouth or Instagram, 'which I'm on less and less So much of what you see on there nowadays is just garbage AI. We all look at ourselves too much.'

She  put on a dazzling display in a daring fringed dress as she attended the premiere for her new film Klara And The Sun at New York's Crosby Street Hotel on Monday. 

Jenna was joined on the blue carpet by her co-star Amy Adams, 52, as the two continued to promote the Taika Waititi-directed film despite the scathing, one-star reviews.

The movie, based on Kazuo Ishiguro's book, sees Jenna play the titular character, a robot girl designed to prevent loneliness.

While posing in the store's window, Klara catches the eye of Josie (Mia Tharia), who persuades her mother Chrissie (Amy Adams) to buy her as a companion.

Klara befriends the teen in an alternative future where technological advances in genetic engineering have divided humans into strict, isolated classes. Steve Buscemi and Natasha Lyonne also star in the movie.

The movie premiered Thursday at the British Film Institute London Film Festival and has racked up a stream of lacklustre reviews in the days since.
