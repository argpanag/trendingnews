Carlos Espí, the hero again: late goal gives Madrid the win

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:12 · source · permalink

Real Madrid beat Celta 2-1 with Espí, 21, who had only six minutes on the pitch, and extend their run to nine straight wins.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 21, 2026 at 04:12 PM

Carlos Espí came off the bench once more to decide a match. The 21-year-old, with barely six minutes on the pitch, scored the 2-1 in the 85th minute that gave Real Madrid victory over Celta, after Pablo Durán had put the visitors ahead in the 6th minute. According to El Español, Mourinho's side had produced a grey first half with little fluency or intensity.

The match turned against Madrid early. Durán profited from a low cross by Swedberg while Trent and Carreras were badly positioned. Madrid then earned a penalty for a hand on Mbappé by Javi Rodríguez, but the Frenchman sent it over the bar. Radu palmed away a Bellingham header, and the equaliser came in the 66th minute, when Jude struck a shot after a move involving Vinícius.

Changes that turned it around

Mourinho reshuffled his bench in the second half, bringing on Vinícius, Valverde and Cucurella, and Madrid began to gain ground. Espí replaced Camavinga in the 81st minute. His header was saved by Radu, but at the second attempt he found the net with his foot to make it 2-1.

The win keeps Madrid's run at nine straight victories and keeps the pressure on Barcelona, the league leaders. Before kick-off, the Bernabéu paid tribute to Jesús de Miguel, with his wife, sister and father on the pitch, joined by Florentino Pérez and Marián Mouriño. In the stands, Rafa Nadal made his debut in his new role as a Madrid board member.
