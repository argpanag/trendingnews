Clemson-Washington in Maui: TV info, Vegas odds, team previews

TheTools · 24.11.2026 13:26 · source · permalink

Clemson men's basketball (4-2) looks to bounce back from a tough start out in the Maui Invitational with the start of consolation bracket action versus Washington (4-1).

Tipoff in Lahaina Civic Center is set for 8:00 p.m. ET. Kanoa Leahey and Dane Bradshaw will call the broadcast for ESPNU.

Clemson is a 1.5-point favorite.

Clemson is No. 83 in the KenPom ratings (46 offense/147 defense), facing a No. 87 Washington team (68 offense/118 defense).

Brad Brownell's Tigers were blitzed out in Hawaii on Monday in an 82-62 loss to the Ole Miss Rebels. It took Clemson nearly 12 minutes to reach double-figure scoring while the Rebels had already scored 33. The Tigers went 5-of-25 from the 3-point line over the game.

In its tournament opener, Washington led by eight at the half on BYU, but the Cougars scored 52 points in the second half in an 83-77 BYU win.

The Huskies have four averaging double-figures scoring with 6-5 guard Lejuan Watts (15.6), 6-7 guard Steele Venters (14.6), 6-10 forward Nikola Dzepina (12.2) and 5-11 guard Ryan Beasley (12).

Clemson SID Notes

• This will mark just the third meeting all-time between Clemson and Washington. The Tigers trail the series 2-0.

• The last time both teams played was Dec. 22, 2000, in the San Juan Shootout. A 77-76 overtime win for Washington. The only other meeting was in the Great Alaskan Shootout, a 76-66 win for the Huskies.

• Both matchups came with Washington as a member of the Pac-10, now a member of the Big Ten.
