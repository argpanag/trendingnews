Mancini's Italy Line-up for Belgium: Coppola in Defence, Pio Up Front, Surprise Wingers

TheTools · 25.09.2026 18:22 · source · permalink

National Team

Ahead of their opening match, Italy's squad sees many changes, particularly in defence and on the wings, with Cambiaghi and Maldini expected to start.

While defence and midfield see familiar faces, there are changes in attack. Roberto Mancini is meticulously preparing for the opening match of his second spell in charge of the Azzurri, carefully assessing the options at his disposal. He knows full well that with four matches in eleven days, he will need to rotate his squad. However, his immediate focus is singular: to kick off with a victory and reignite enthusiasm around the national team. This is why his selections for the Belgium match will not be made with the subsequent trip to Turkey in mind, but rather to pose the greatest possible challenge to Van Bommel's side.

Scalvini Available

First, the good news from the treatment room: after missing Tuesday afternoon's session as a precaution, the Atalanta defender trained with the group yesterday, completing the entire session alongside his teammates. The minor ankle scare he suffered on Monday is now a thing of the past, and Scalvini will be available to 'Mancio' for tomorrow evening's match at the Olimpico (with over 32,000 tickets already sold).

Experienced Defence

Scalvini's recovery does not, at present, suggest any changes to the back four playing in front of Donnarumma. At least, the same players who were tested together in Tuesday's drills were seen yesterday: Di Lorenzo on the right, Mancini and Coppola in the centre, and Calafiori on the left. Kayode hopes to overtake Di Lorenzo at the last minute, but currently, the Napoli captain holds the advantage over the Brentford talent. For the suspended Bastoni's replacement, however, Coppola is ahead: the coach went to watch him play live in Paris against Lyon, and after securing a starting spot immediately upon arriving in the French capital (in February), Diego has not relinquished it. Calafiori will play in the left-back role he usually occupies at Arsenal. Could there be any surprises, perhaps not from the start? Ahanor on the left flank, which would see Calafiori shift to a marking role (with Coppola out).

Midfield Pillars

In midfield, the most favoured trio, tested yesterday during set-piece drills, features debutant Romano in the centre, Barella as the right-sided midfielder, and Tonali as the left-sided midfielder. Frattesi, who is in excellent form with Lazio, will start on the bench and may be a starter in Bursa to utilise his ability to make runs into the box in what promises to be a 'heated' encounter. The former Inter player explained in a press conference: "In the new group, there are young players with great quality. We need to get in tune with them because they can help us. Romano, with certain movements and that left foot, reminds me of Matic. I also really like Inacio, who has quality and intensity. Mancini? He's the same as when we left, and I agree with his sentiment that we must focus on the first match and not the subsequent ones. Like the whole squad, I'm happy the coach is back. Italy has a competitive squad, even to challenge other European giants, despite France, for example, having many more alternatives."

Forward Options Tested

And now to the attack, where there are still some doubts. These may be resolved in this morning's final training session, before the team departs for the capital this afternoon. Pio Esposito remains the favourite for the centre-forward role, although Kean was heavily tested yesterday. Who will play on his flanks? On the left, Maldini was preferred over Raspadori, while Zoma will start on the bench. On the right, it's a three-way race between Zaniolo, Cambiaghi, and Vergara. The former remains 'Mancio's favourite', but due to an injury on the first matchday of the league, he has played little and might be rested for the Turkey match. In this scenario, Cambiaghi is favoured over Vergara. Sebastiano Esposito could come on during the match and operate between the lines, transforming the 4-3-3 into a 4-2-3-1.
