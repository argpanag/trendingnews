Week 8 AP Top 25 Poll sees Big Ten gain equal footing on SEC and unbeaten Utah earn major respect

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:55 · source · permalink

Miami holds the No. 1 spot for a second straight week while Utah climbs to No. 6 after improving to 7-0

While Week 8 of the college football season couldn't possibly live up to the total chaos that unfolded the week prior, we still saw three Top 10 teams in the country all fall on the road, which has yet again resulted in some serious shuffling in the latest AP Top 25 Poll.

To no surprise, the biggest faller in this week's poll belongs to the BYU Cougars, who dropped six spots to No. 8 after losing to UCF on the road in double overtime. On the other side of the spectrum, Texas is this week's biggest jumper, with the Longhorns moving up eight spots to No. 11 after holding on and beating Ole Miss at home, 34-27.

The Big 12 saw a bit of cannibalization in Week 8, but when all the dust settled, Utah improved to 7-0 on the year after its late-night win over Houston, and was rewarded by moving up to No. 6 in the nation. Next up for the Utes is a huge matchup at Cincinnati, the same Cincinnati team that knocked off No. 8 Texas Tech on Saturday in a stunner.
