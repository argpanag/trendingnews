Inter vs. Parma, Chivu's press conference on Friday

TheTools · 10.10.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

First Team

The Nerazzuri Coach will answer questions from journalists on the eve of Serie A Matchday 6

Serie A is finally set to resume after the long international break: Inter will take on Parma at San Siro on Saturday 10 October at 18:00 CEST in their Matchday 6 fixture.

On the eve of the league clash, Inter head coach Cristian Chivu will answer questions from journalists at a press conference at the Inter Training Center in Appiano Gentile.

The press conference is scheduled for Friday 9 October at 14:30 CEST.

The press conference will be streamed live on the Club’s official channels.
