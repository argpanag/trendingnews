Late Barcelona withdrawals! Flick and Cubarsí to miss Ballon d'Or gala

FC Barcelona’s defeat to Real Madrid in El Clásico has prompted changes to the club’s plans for the Ballon d’Or gala. According to Cadena SER’s Què T’hi Jugues program, Hansi Flick decided not to travel to the ceremony, while Pau Cubarsí also opted to stay home and rest after the physical demands of recent weeks.

The German coach and the defender will not be the only talking points surrounding Barcelona’s delegation. Lamine Yamal, Rodri, Dani Olmo and Joan García do plan to attend the event, where Barcelona’s young forward could once again take center stage.

Hansi Flick to miss ceremony after El Clásico defeat

The defeat to Real Madrid changed Barcelona’s plans for the ceremony, according to information published by Cadena SER.

Flick decided not to travel to London for the gala, while Cubarsí preferred to rest after playing significant minutes for Spain and Barcelona.

The defender had been tipped as one of the leading candidates for the Kopa Trophy, which recognizes the best young footballer. However, his absence from the ceremony is fueling expectations that the award will once again go to Lamine Yamal.

Lamine Yamal eyes another Kopa Trophy

The Barcelona winger has already won the Kopa Trophy in the last two editions and could now become the first footballer to claim it and the Ballon d’Or in the same year, according to the scenario outlined in the original report.

Although there appears to be a clear favorite for the best young player award, the race for the Ballon d’Or remains open and far less certain.

Lamine will not travel with Barcelona’s official delegation. As he did last year, the footballer took a private flight to attend with family and friends.

The rest of Barcelona’s delegation will travel to London

While Flick and Cubarsí will miss the ceremony, other club representatives will attend the event.

Lamine Yamal, Rodri, Dani Olmo and Joan García are among the players who will travel. Barcelona’s official delegation will include board members, executives and players from the men’s and women’s teams.

According to the report, Flick and Cubarsí are the last-minute absentees, although their reasons differ: the coach decided not to attend after El Clásico, while the defender prioritized rest after a heavy workload.

The spotlight will be firmly on Lamine Yamal, who could add another Kopa Trophy to his individual honors as he awaits the outcome of the vote for world football’s highest individual award.
