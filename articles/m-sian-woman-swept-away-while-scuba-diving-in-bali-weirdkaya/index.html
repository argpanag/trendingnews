M’sian Woman Swept Away While Scuba Diving In Bali | WeirdKaya

TheTools · 18.09.2026 23:58 · source · permalink

Community news hub for today's generation of Malaysians

The victim was 40-year-old Siti Nuraini Rusidi, who was reportedly swept away by strong currents while scuba diving in Crystal Bay, Desa Sakti at about 11.30am local time (12.30pm Malaysia).

Was reportedly unwell

According to Indonesian media outlet BaliNews, Siti Nuraini had her first diving activity at 8am along with a dive master, a boat captain, and several other divers.

Siti Nuraini assured him that she was fine and went on to complete the dive.

Swept away

While en route to the next dive site in Crystal Bay, Siti Nuraini was again asked whether she felt fit to continue and she replied by saying she was okay.

Unfortunately, the group met strong currents and three of them, including Siti Nuraini, were swept away. She was later found dead by authorities.

News of her death didn’t reach Malaysian shores until a post about it was posted on Threads and subsequently went viral.

Siti Nuraini’s family have refused to comment on her passing so far.
