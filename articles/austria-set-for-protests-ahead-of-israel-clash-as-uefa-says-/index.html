Austria set for protests ahead of Israel clash as Uefa says game is ‘high risk’

TheTools · 23.09.2026 18:11 · source · permalink

Austrian security officials have confirmed that two demonstrations will take place around tonight’s game with Israel in Linz.

Uefa have classified the fixture as ‘high-risk’, according to Austrian news agency APA, and a security operation has been put in place.

Austrian FA CEO Bernhard Neuhold said: “The aim is to take precautions in all areas. We want to ensure that all the different groups can enjoy a safe experience at the stadium.”

While Austria hosted Israel in Vienna in 2019 and Klagenfurt in 2021 without incident, Neuhold added that circumstances were different ahead of their latest meeting.

“We are aware of the implications this time as well, but we are also confident that we will be able to manage it well,” he said.

“We have an experienced organizing team, and for years we have relied on proven procedures and close communication with the authorities when it comes to high-risk matches.”

The FAI moved their home fixture against Israel on October 4 out of Dublin's Aviva Stadium and to Bačka Topola in Serbia due to "operational challenges that could impact on the delivery of the game on home soil."

They confirmed the venue switch at the end of June, meaning both games against Israel will be played in a third country. Israel will use Debrecen as their base.

Irish protesters are now set to target the visit of Austria on October 1 for demonstrations against the Israel games.

Austria hosted this year's Eurovision Song Contest, which was boycotted by Ireland over Israel's participation.
