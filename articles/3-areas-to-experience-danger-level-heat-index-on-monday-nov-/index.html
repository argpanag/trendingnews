3 areas to experience 'danger' level heat index on Monday, Nov. 16 — PAGASA

TheTools · 15.11.2026 23:53 · source · permalink

Three areas across the country are expected to experience "danger" level heat index on Monday, November 16, the state weather bureau reported.

In its latest heat index forecast, the Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration (PAGASA) forecast Puerto Princesa, Palawan, to record the highest heat index at 45 degrees Celsius.

This was followed by Coron, Palawan, and Zamboanga City, Zamboanga del Sur, which are both expected to record heat index levels of 43 degrees Celsius.

PAGASA classifies heat index levels between 42°C and 51°C under the “danger” category, where heat cramps and heat exhaustion are likely, while heat stroke becomes possible with prolonged exposure.

Meanwhile, more than 60 other areas across the country are forecast to experience “extreme caution” heat index levels ranging from 33°C to 41°C.

According to PAGASA, prolonged physical activity outdoors under these conditions may also lead to heat-related illnesses.

The heat index refers to the apparent temperature or the level of discomfort felt by the human body when air temperature and humidity are combined.

PAGASA urged the public to take precautions against heat-related illnesses by limiting exposure to direct sunlight, drinking plenty of water, wearing light clothing, and staying in shaded or cooler areas.
