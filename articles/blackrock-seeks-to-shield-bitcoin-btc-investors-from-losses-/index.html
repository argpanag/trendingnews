BlackRock seeks to shield bitcoin (BTC) investors from losses after Calamos fund weathers 50% crash

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:05 · source · permalink

BlackRock's proposed fund follows similar products from Calamos that helped investors avoid steep losses during bitcoin's selloff.

  • BlackRock filed Monday to offer a bitcoin-linked ETF that seeks to limit losses while allowing investors to benefit from price gains.
  • Calamos' protected bitcoin ETF lost just 1% since October 2025, even as bitcoin tumbled more than 50% from its peak.
  • The filing follows BlackRock's June introduction of a bitcoin income ETF as the firm expands beyond funds that track bitcoin's price.
