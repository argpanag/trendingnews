AI investments must be judged by returns, not fear of missing out: Kumar Mangalam Birla

TheTools · 14.10.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

    Kumar Mangalam Birla discussed the importance of evaluating potential returns on artificial intelligence investments. He suggested a more gradual approach to adopting AI technologies within the Aditya Birla Group. The group is assessing how AI can enhance various business aspects, from customer interaction to manufacturing efficiency. Birla also highlighted the challenges related to capital allocation amidst geopolitical changes. Overall, maintaining employee motivation and collaboration remains crucial in today's business environment.
