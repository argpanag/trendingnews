  • Anthony Philip Bruccoliere, a music teacher who claimed to have worked as a personal assistant to actors Charlie Sheen and Brooke Mueller, was convicted of 18 felony sexual assault crimes involving two young girls between 2008 and 2019.
  • An Orange County Superior Court jury deliberated for just one day before finding Bruccoliere guilty and confirming true findings on sentencing enhancements for multiple victims.
  • Prosecutors demonstrated that Bruccoliere subjected the victims to years of sexual abuse at his Costa Mesa home after luring them under the guise of babysitting and sleepovers, using his size to overpower them and forcing them to rehearse cover stories.
  • Authorities arrested Bruccoliere during a covert police operation after the victims confronted him in a wiretapped phone call, during which he laughed and tried to blame the young children for the abuse.
  • Bruccoliere faces a maximum sentence of 180 years to life in prison and is scheduled to be sentenced on January 8.
