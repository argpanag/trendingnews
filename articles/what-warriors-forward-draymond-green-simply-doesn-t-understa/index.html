What Warriors forward Draymond Green simply doesn’t ‘understand’ about massive GTA 6 hype

TheTools · 23.11.2026 16:52 · source · permalink
  • NBA on ClutchPoints
  • Golden State Warriors

Warriors star Draymond Green reacts to GTA 6 hype, admitting he doesn't understand fans taking time off work to play the game.

Golden State Warriors forward Draymond Green isn't buying into the massive excitement surrounding Grand Theft Auto VI. While the Warriors veteran regularly shares his opinions, he admits he struggles to understand why some people would miss work just to play a video game.

Green reacted to the GTA 6 craze, explaining that video games have never been a major part of his life. His latest discussion took him away from basketball and into the gaming world.

“I'm not a video game player myself. I think the last GTA I remember playing is like San Andreas,” Green admitted.

Despite his limited gaming experience, Green acknowledged the enormous attention surrounding Rockstar Games' highly anticipated release.

“The excitement level, it's unbelievable,” he said.

However, one particular aspect of the anticipation left the four-time NBA champion completely puzzled.

“I don't understand people calling out of work to play a video game,” Green explained.

Still, Green made it clear that he wasn't criticizing anyone for enjoying something he personally doesn't follow.

“To all you GTAers out there, kudos to you. Much respect, much love. Your thing is your thing,” he said.

Green's reaction reflects his unfamiliarity with modern gaming culture rather than opposition to the franchise itself.

The outspoken forward has never shied away from sharing his perspective, whether discussing basketball controversies or topics outside the NBA.

For GTA 6 fans, taking time off to play might sound reasonable. For Green, however, the excitement remains something he can appreciate without fully understanding.

His comments also highlight how differently people approach gaming today.
