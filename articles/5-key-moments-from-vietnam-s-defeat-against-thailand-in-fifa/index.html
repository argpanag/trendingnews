5 key moments from Vietnam's defeat against Thailand in FIFA ASEAN Cup

TheTools · 01.10.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

Thailand score early

Thailand took an early advantage in the 10th minute as Sarach Yooyen (6) capitalized on a defensive lapse. A clearance from a cross fell directly to his feet as he fired a thunderous shot straight into the top corner.

Vietnam goalkeeper Patrik Le Giang managed to get a hand on the ball, but the sheer power of the strike made it impossible to keep out. This opening goal forced Vietnam to change their tactics early in the match.

Vietnam's goal ruled out for offside

Vietnam responded by pushing higher up the pitch in search of an equalizer before halftime. The breakthrough appeared to come in the 37th minute when Nguyen Tai Loc (13) showcased his pace, receiving a through ball and breaking through Thailand’s defense.

The run concluded with a chip over the Thailand goalkeeper, seemingly leveling the score.

The celebrations were cut short, however, as a VAR check confirmed Loc was offside, keeping Thailand's lead at the break.

Free kick hits the post

Thailand nearly doubled their advantage shortly after the second half started. In the 52nd minute, Yooyen stepped up to take a clean free kick from roughly 20 m out and hit the post.

Patrik sustained an injury after colliding with the post during his attempt. Despite the discomfort, the Vietnamese keeper stayed on the pitch.

Although Vietnam controlled the majority of possession in the second half and subbed in fresh attackers, Thailand remained a constant threat on the counterattack. They nearly sealed the game once more in the 80th minute with a dangerous close-range header from Peeradol Chamrasamee.

Le Giang makes stunning save

However, Patrik demonstrated great reflexes, producing a stunning save to deny the point-blank attempt. His late-game save kept Vietnam within a single-goal margin.

Late goal seals the match

When Vietnam put most of their players forward in a desperate attempt to salvage a late draw, Thailand exploited the open space in the third minute of stoppage time. A quick counterattack released substitute Chaiyaphon Otton onto a perfectly weighted through ball.

Otton remained composed under pressure, slotting the ball cleanly past Patrik to finally put the game to rest.

The late strike secured a 2-0 victory for Thailand in their second match of Group B, giving the team a great chance to top the group and advance to the final.
