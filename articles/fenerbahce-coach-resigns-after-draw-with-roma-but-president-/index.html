Club president: ‘I am his senior, I told him to continue’

The Fenerbahce head coach, Ismail Kartal, announced his resignation on Thursday after ⁠his team drew 1-1 at home to Roma in the Champions League – but minutes ⁠later, the club ⁠president ​said Kartal would stay in the job.

Kartal shocked the post-match press conference by claiming he was ⁠leaving Fenerbahce “never to return”. The 65-year-old added that he was taking the drastic decision “to clear the way for the club” – but the Fenerbahce president, Aziz Yildrim, quickly moved to play down his manager’s remarks.

Minutes after ⁠the press conference, Yildirim told reporters that ​Kartal “continues in his role” with the Turkish side. “Some ‌fans threw a bottle ‌at the coach’s head this evening during the game. He ‌is upset about it,” the club president added.

“I just spoke to Ismail Kartal. He mentioned the bottle incident and he also complained about being under pressure,” he said. Asked whether the manager had told him he would stay, Yildirim said: “There’s no need for him ‌to say that he’d continue. I am his senior, I told him to continue and that’s ​it. We are a family.”

Kartal, who spent a decade at Fenerbahce as a player, coached the club in 2014-15, then on an interim basis in 2021-22 before returning for a full ⁠campaign in 2023-24. He was the assistant manager at Fenerbahce the ​past two ​times the club won ​the Turkish title, and returned for a fourth ​managerial spell in ‌June.

Fenerbahce have ​finished second ​in the Turkish Super Lig in each of the past five seasons, and reached the Champions League’s league phase this season for the first time in 18 years. The team sit ninth in the Turkish top-flight, having lost twice in their opening four games, including Saturday’s 2-1 home defeat to Besiktas.

Roma took the lead in the 39th minute when Donyell Malen rolled a pass to ​Bryan Cristante, who beat Fenerbahce keeper Ederson with a bouncing finish. The hosts levelled before half-time when Mason Greenwood slipped a ​pass through to Archie ​Brown, who clipped a finish over ​Mile Svilar.
