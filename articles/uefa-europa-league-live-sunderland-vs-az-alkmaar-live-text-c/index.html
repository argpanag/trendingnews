Match Facts

  • This will only be Sunderland’s fifth ever game in European competition, after four games in the 1973-74 Cup Winners’ Cup – the Black Cats beat Vasas SC in the first round (3-0 aggregate) before losing to Sporting CP in the round of 16 (2-3 aggregate).

  • AZ have lost all 11 of their previous European matches away to English opposition by an aggregate score of 9-31, losing in England five times across the last four seasons (vs West Ham, Aston Villa, Spurs twice and Crystal Palace).

  • This will be Sunderland’s first game in major European competition since November 7th 1973, a gap of 52 years and 313 days; only Go Ahead Eagles (59y 353d) and Union Saint-Gilloise (57y 336d) have had longer gaps between matches in such competitions.

  • AZ are winless in each of their last eight UEFA Europa League away games (D2 L6), since a 1-0 at Napoli on MD1 of the 2020-21 campaign. Their last ended in a 3-1 loss to Tottenham Hotspur.

  • Only eight players have made more UEFA Europa League appearances (since 2009-10) than Sunderland captain Granit Xhaka (63), with his most recent game coming in the 2023-24 final for Bayer Leverkusen, which they lost 3-0 to Atalanta.
