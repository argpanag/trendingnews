Some of the most glamorous women from around the world are in Puerto Rico this week for the official 2026 Miss Universe pageant.

This year’s pageant brings together contestants from 116 countries as they compete for the coveted diamond crown, designed by Venezuelan jewelry designer George Wittels in collaboration with Paris-based Maison d’OR. TV host, Mario Lopez, and Miss Universe 2022, R’Bonney Gabriel, will serve as hosts.

Where to Watch Miss Universe 2026

This year’s Miss Universe pageant airs live from San Juan, Puerto Rico on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 8 p.m. ET on Telemundo. You can watch the competition on TV with any cable package that includes the Spanish-language network.
