Celebrity Race Across The World star Princess Andre reveals heartbreaking way fame changed her - AOL

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:11 · source · permalink

Celebrity Race Across The World stars Princess and Junior Andre reflected on their famous upbringing - and the heartbreaking way it changed her as a person.

The brother and sister duo - who are the children of Peter Andre and Katie Price - started tonight's episode in first place, having beaten all the other pairings to the latest checkpoint.

Other pairs racing are Jeff Stelling and his son Matt, as well as Eurovision star and singer Sam Ryder with tour manager and best friend Kaz. Singer Ella Eyre and her best friend from childhood Nana complete the line-up.

During tonight's episode, they opened up about how difficult it was to grow up in the spotlight with their famous parents. Princess explained: "The biggest challenge for me growing up in the public eye was everyone knowing about my life.

"There was a lot of people at my school who I would think are my best friends but they were not. People would say horrible stuff about my family. School is meant to be your escape."

Princess explained she used to hate everything about herself and struggled with her confidence. She described herself as self-critical, saying that she regularly stopped herself from talking as she didn't want to come across as stupid.

Princess and Junior were forced to reflect on the terrifying carjacking they experienced whilst in South Africa with their mum Katie when six men held the family at gunpoint. The incident happened in 2018 but Princess has been open about how it affected her durig candid appearances on Race Across The World.

During their travels through Indonesia for the BBC show, they took a taxi overnight but when the driver stopped to pick up petrol, a terrified Princess had flashbacks to what happened in South Africa.

In a discussion on Morning Live today about the moment viewers saw on screen, Princess said: "It was a scary time, to be honest, and I didn't realise that it still affected me until that happened. Afterwards I felt silly though. I was like, 'Oh my god what is happening?' I kind of just shut down. But you [Junior] were there for me, as always."

The sibling duo had previously spoken on how they wanted to step out of their famous parents' shadow.

"Everything we do, we get compared to our parents but we actually have our own thing that we do," Princess said, with Junior adding: "We've kind of got a lot to prove. I'm so excited for this. I cannot wait to do this, we're going to win."

This year's Celebrity Race Across The World will see the four pairings travel all the way from Kuching in the Malaysian state of Sarawak, Borneo to sunny Melbourne, Australia on a 7500km journey.
