Major fried chicken chain franchisee files Chapter 11 bankruptcy

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Although consumer traffic at fried chicken restaurants increased by 3% in 2025, according to data firm Circana, certain major franchisees have continued to struggle with declining sales and rising costs.

Declining sales led a KFC and Taco Bell franchisee to file for bankruptcy to seek a sale of its assets.

Tasty Chick'n files Chapter 11 bankruptcy

Major KFC and KFC/Taco Bell co-branded franchisee Tasty Chick'n LLC filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, facing a potential termination of its franchise agreements over $15.6 million in unpaid royalties.

The Plano, Texas-based fried chicken dining chain franchisee and three affiliates filed their petition in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, listing $50 million to $100 million in assets and $100 million to $500 million in liabilities, according to court papers.

Debtor counsel Jason S. Brookner of Gray Reed in Houston was not immediately available for comment.

Tasty Chick'n was formed in 2021 to develop, acquire, own, and operate KFC and Taco Bell branded restaurants through franchise agreements with KFC, Taco Bell, and parent Yum Brands, according to a declaration by Tasty Chick'n CEO Thomas M. Scott.

Debtor seeks a sale of its assets

The franchisee is seeking a sale of its assets through the Chapter 11 bankruptcy case, the declaration said. It plans to market its restaurants as an entire portfolio, by individual market, or in combinations of different markets to maximize buyer participation and transaction value, court papers said.

The debtor will also seek $20 million in debtor-in-possession financing from prepetition lender MidCap Financial Trust, consisting of $5 million in new money and a $15 million rollup of prepetition debt.
