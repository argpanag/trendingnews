Liam Lawson seals top 10 start for Vegas GP after securing F1 future

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:29 · source · permalink

Kiwi Formula 1 driver Liam Lawson has secured another top-10 qualifying result and will start seventh for tomorrow's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The Racing Bulls driver went eighth fastest in the final stage of qualifying (Q3) with a fastest lap time of 1m 35.090s, but was promoted one place following a 10-place grid penalty for McLaren's Oscar Piastri.

It marked another impressive qualifying result for the Kiwi, although his teammate Arvid Lindblad went quicker during Q3 and would start tomorrow's race from fifth - his highest qualifying result in F1.

Further up the grid, Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli produced a stunning final lap of 1m33.893s to claim pole position around the dazzling street circuit.

However, a five-place grid penalty will see Antonelli drop to sixth, handing pole position to Red Bull's Max Verstappen. Mercedes' George Russell will join him on the front row.

The pair had earlier clashed during the final practice outing (FP3), making wheel-to-wheel contact as they battled for position.

Verstappen accused Russell of pushing him towards the barriers, with stewards later issuing the Red Bull driver a warning for erratic driving.

It was a less exciting outing for Ferrari, with both Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton failing to reach the final stage of qualifying.

Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson during a press conference at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit, Las Vegas, USA. Picture date: Wednesday November 18, 2026. (Source: Getty)

Lawson's strong result came after Racing Bulls confirmed his Formula 1 future, securing his seat with the team for the 2027 season.

Speaking ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, the 24-year-old admitted he felt "a little bit of relief" after weeks of uncertainty surrounding his future.

"It's obviously nice and for me I'll try and enjoy the last few races even more," he told reporters

The Kiwi described the contract negotiations as "an intense few weeks", but said he had been enjoying his racing, particularly during the second half of the season.
