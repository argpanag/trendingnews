How to watch Linda Noskova v Elina Svitolina: TV channel & live stream

TheTools · 14.11.2026 21:11 · source · permalink

Linda Noskova will be hoping to extend her excellent WTA Finals debut when she takes on 2018 champion Elina Svitolina in the semi-finals.

Noskova was one point away from sweeping her group following straight set wins over Marta Kostyuk and Coco Gauff, only for a late Jessica Pegula fightback to end her flawless start.

Svitolina makes her first appearance in the last four of the WTA Finals since 2019 and the Ukrainian sealed her progression with a hard-fought victory over world number two Aryna Sabalenka.
