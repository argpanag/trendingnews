New Hunger Games Movie Prequel Gets Official Verdict From Author

TheTools · 14.11.2026 14:56 · source · permalink

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is facing an honest review from its creator.

The Hunger Games film series is a major dystopian franchise that has grossed over $3.3 billion worldwide across its adaptations. The movies began in 2012 with The Hunger Games, followed by The Hunger Games: Catching Fire in 2013, which was a major box-office peak, grossing over $864 million worldwide. The franchise is celebrated for revitalizing interest in young adult dystopian books and paving the way for others, including Divergent and Maze Runner.

Suzanne Collins, author and creator of the Hunger Games franchise, has written a letter addressed to fans of her novels and the movies, giving her official verdict on Francis Lawrence's retelling of Sunrise on the Reaping. The author stated, "Our incredible director, Francis Lawrence, does double duty as our third producer. A brilliant visual storyteller, he’s helmed an extraordinary film, which maintains the book’s narrative and themes, while bursting with his own distinctive style." Collins continued and even confirmed the return of some major franchise stars to the upcoming adaptation:

"Woody’s return to reprise his character, along with our amazing original stars, Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson, adds such poignancy to the film. It’s such a gift to watch them bridge Haymitch’s Hunger Games to Katniss’s tale.

Led by a remarkably powerful and moving performance by Joseph Zada, our new cast is astonishingly good by every measure. From feisty young tributes Mckenna Grace, Ben Wang, Molly McCann, Iona Bell, Percy Daggs IV, Laura Marcus and Jhaleil Swaby, to conspiratorial victors Kelvin Harrison Jr, Maya Hawke and Lili Taylor, to soulful District 12 denizens Whitney Peak, Kara Tointin, Smylie Bradwell, Melody Chikakane Brown, Scot Greenan, Grace Ackary, Jeffrey Lee Hallman, Serafin Mishiev and Jefferson White, to stellar Capitol standouts Glenn Close, Ralph Fiennes, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Billy Porter, Iris Apatow and Edvin Ryding, and to everybody who sparkled in the Capitol or gave a three-finger salute in District 12, the performers have brought such understanding, humor, pathos and pizazz to their characters. Gems, every last one. I truly can’t imagine a better cast."

Collins continued and thanked the crew involved that helped to transform Sunrise on the Reaping novel into the upcoming release. "Transforming a novel into a script requires great energy, akin to turning ice into steam, and we were fortunate to have two of our powerhouse screenwriters, Billy Ray and Michael Lesslie, return and deliver such a rich screenplay. And once it was in the can, our terrific editor, Mark Yoshikawa, completed the metamorphosis with his dynamic pacing and eye for propulsive storytelling," stated the author.

In ScreenRant's cover story for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, movie franchise director Lawrence and producer Nina Jacobson went as far as to state that this novel by Collins is one of their "favorite books in the franchise." Zada, who is portraying the younger counterpart to Harrelson's Haymitch Abernathy also expressed his joy on joining the movie and shared that the actor had provided two movies as his advice going forward including "Cool Hand Luke, the Paul Newman film. He just said that everything that he's ever done, he's trying to do that."

There has been speculation surrounding the potential success for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which will follow Haymitch Abernathy's Hunger Games. Opening forecasts as reported by Deadline have stated that its opening weekend could welcome over $60 million in the U.S., and even potentially surpass upwards of $70 million. With a forecast such as this, it would be no surprise if a global opening weekend of $100 million is in the cards for the Hunger Games prequel.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping arrives in theaters on November 20.
