FPL Semenyo Replacement for GW8: Cherki, Mbeumo or Rogers After 8-Week Blow

TheTools · 19.10.2026 19:15 · source · permalink

Enzo Maresca says Antoine Semenyo will be out for probably eight weeks, so the 13.8% of FPL managers who own him need a replacement before Friday's 6.30pm deadline. Cherki, Mbeumo and Rogers are the main options.

Enzo Maresca has said Antoine Semenyo will be out “for probably eight weeks”, so the 13.8% of FPL managers who own him need a replacement before the Gameweek 8 deadline at 6.30pm UK time on Friday 23 October. FPL has now cut his chance of playing to 0%, with the news line reading “Knee injury – Unknown return date”.

The City boss made the comment at his Monday press conference before the AEK Athens game. “The only one that will be out for probably eight weeks is Antoine [Semenyo],” he said, as reported by Goal. Our earlier Semenyo injury update covered the doubt. This is the confirmation. FPL’s own data shows more than 96,000 managers have already sold him this Gameweek.

Source: official FPL API, Monday 19 October.

Cherki: the like-for-like swap

Cherki costs £8.0m, the same slot Semenyo filled at £8.3m, and he’s owned by 37.3% of managers. He has 44 points, form of 6.0 and an xGI of 0.69 per 90, and more than 100,000 managers have brought him in this Gameweek. He keeps your City exposure, with Aston Villa away on Saturday 24 October (12.30pm UK time), then Brighton and Fulham at home either side of a trip to Forest. With Semenyo gone, he’s the obvious man to take the attacking minutes. We weighed him against Semenyo in Cherki or Semenyo, and that choice has now been made for managers. Verdict: the safest move, though plenty of managers are making it.

Mbeumo: the value pick

Mbeumo is £7.8m, owned by 20.0%, with 41 points, form of 6.0 and the best xGI per 90 of the four in our table at 0.70. United host Bournemouth on Sunday 25 October (2pm UK time) before away trips to Chelsea and Liverpool either side of a home game with Villa. FPL rates both of those away games as 4 on its difficulty scale, and that’s the case against him. He’s the one to take if you want to save £0.2m and avoid the crowd.

Rogers: the template move

Morgan Rogers is the most-owned of the three at 47.7%, priced at £7.9m, with 40 points and form of 5.5. His xGI of 0.51 per 90 is the lowest of the main three and his fixtures are mixed: Spurs at home (5.30pm UK time on Saturday 24 October), Man Utd at home, Sunderland away, Leeds at home. More than 85,000 managers have bought him this week. He’s a fine fixture play, but we wouldn’t pay for the numbers alone, and following 47.7% of the game isn’t a plan.

Other options

Cunha is the left-field choice at £7.9m, with only 6.7% ownership and the best form of the group at 7.0. His xGI of 0.32 per 90 is well below Mbeumo’s, though, and he shares those United fixtures. If you’d rather leave the midfield alone, our GW8 midfielders guide has more names. Barcola (Liverpool, £8.0m) is owned by just 3.1% and has form of 3.0, so we’d pass.

Our pick

Cherki, with Mbeumo as the cheaper alternative. Cherki keeps you in City’s attack with the best price-to-output match for Semenyo’s old slot, and he’s in the form to justify it. Rogers is the one we’d leave to everyone else.

Deadline: Friday 23 October, 6.30pm UK time. City’s next match is Aston Villa away on Saturday 24 October at 12.30pm UK time.
