Frankie Pangilinan confirms relationship with 'rumored BF' Dan Laurel

TheTools · 08.09.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Frankie Pangilinan has confirmed her relationship with her "rumored boyfriend," Dan Laurel.

In a YouTube interview with Ogie Diaz, the daughter of Sharon Cuneta and Senator Kiko Pangilinan said she couldn't help but laugh when she saw headlines in previous months referring to Dan as her "rumored boyfriend."

"I saw the articles that came out, and they were saying 'rumored boyfriend' or 'alleged,' but no one actually asked me to confirm," she said in Filipino.

"The articles kept saying that I had not confirmed anything, but no one had actually asked me," she added.

So, on Dan's birthday in May, Frankie greeted him on Instagram, cleverly referring to him as her "rumored boyfriend."

"He's wonderful," she said about Dan, who is the great-grandson of former Philippine President Jose P. Laurel.

Frankie also revealed that her parents are excited about her new relationship, particularly her mother, Sharon.

"More than anything, they're excited for me," she said. "Of course, Mom is more excited than Dad. Dad is still a little strict."

Frankie is set to star in the upcoming film "Edjop" opposite Elijah Canlas. Directed by Katski Flores, it comes out in cinemas on September 16. —Hermes Joy Tunac/MGP, GMA News
