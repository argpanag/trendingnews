Cool air briefly affecting the eastern half of the U.S. will lead to weekend freezes at least as far south as the Tennessee Valley, reaching many areas that have not yet experienced a season-ending freeze. However, above-normal temperatures will return (or continue) across much of the country early next week. During the next 5 days, precipitation will be limited to some edges and corners of the country, including the Pacific Northwest, the Atlantic Seaboard, areas in the vicinity of the Great Lakes, and portions of the south-central U.S. Some of the heaviest precipitation, locally 2 to 6 inches or more, will fall at higher elevations of western Washington, including the northern Cascades. Rainfall in the lower Rio Grande Valley could total 1 to 2 inches or more. Meanwhile, dry weather will prevail through the first half of next week across much of the Plains, Midwest, and mid-South.
