Pisilli could start behind Malen and Dybala

Could Niccolò Pisilli be the surprise package for Fenerbahçe vs. Roma?

The Giallorossi midfielder has always started on the bench in the first three Serie A matches, coming on as a substitute against Fiorentina and Lecce.

OneFootball Videos

He also scored the goal that sealed the 4-0 victory for Fiorentina, while at the Via del Mare he came on for 23 minutes without making an impact.

The number 61 could likely make his Champions League debut today, though it remains to be seen whether, surprisingly, he’ll start or start during the match.

Gian Piero Gasperini appears to have selected his starting lineup for the match against Fenerbahce, but surprises are lurking.

As Il Messaggero reports, Molina is expected to replace Lulli, while the other nine players are the same ones who won against Atalanta. The only possible option is between Soulé and Mora. There’s one caveat. This week, the Grugliasco-born coach tried Niccolò Pisilli as an attacking midfielder. It could be a quick move, but surprises can’t be ruled out.
