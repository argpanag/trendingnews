Stock Market Today Live Updates: BSE Sensex plunges over 1,000 points, NSE Nifty50 slips below 22,300 - The Times of India

TheTools · 01.10.2026 08:35 · source · permalink

BSE Sensex fell 272.48 points, or 0.38%, to 72,207.81, while NSE Nifty declined 77.90 points, or 0.34%, to 22,542.55.

Stock market today: Stock recommendations for October 1

Benchmark indices ended lower for the third consecutive session, with late-session selling erasing the gains seen during the trading day. The 30-share BSE Sensex declined 48.78 points, or 0.07%, to settle at 72,480.29. The index had risen as much as 533.16 points, or 0.73%, during the session to touch 73,062.23 before giving up those gains. The 50-share NSE Nifty also closed lower, falling 95.75 points, or 0.42%, to 22,620.45. Among Sensex constituents, Eternal, Sun Pharma, Titan, Adani Ports, Tata Steel and HDFC Bank were among the major laggards. Kotak Mahindra Bank, ICICI Bank, InterGlobe Aviation and Axis Bank were among the gainers.
