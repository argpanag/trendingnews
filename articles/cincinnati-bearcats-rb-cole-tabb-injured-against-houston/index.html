Cincinnati Bearcats RB Cole Tabb injured against Houston

TheTools · 08.11.2026 00:18 · source · permalink
  • Cincinnati Bearcats running back Cole Tabb was injured during the third quarter against No. 17 Houston.
  • Before the injury, Tabb had 60 rushing yards and a receiving touchdown in the game.

Cincinnati Bearcats running back Cole Tabb was injured in the game at No. 17 Houston with 12:46 remaining in the third quarter.

JC French tried to complete a pass toward the Bearcats' sideline and Tabb was sandwiched between two Houston Cougars. UC's Sports Medicine staff surrounded him, and he was eventually able to walk to the examining tent with some help.

Gi'Bran Payne entered the game in place of Tabb, who was having his usual productive game. The Bearcats scored on the series with JC French hitting Gavin Grover on a 34-yard scoring pass.

Tabb had 60 yards rushing at the time for the Bearcats and three catches for 34 yards, including one for a touchdown. Tabb has scored 14 touchdowns for UC this season with 10 on the ground and four by air.

Including his day at No. 17 Houston, Tabb has 752 rushing yards for the season and 17 catches for 155 yards.
