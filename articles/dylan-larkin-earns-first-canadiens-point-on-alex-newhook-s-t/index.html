Dylan Larkin earns first Canadiens point on Alex Newhook’s tying goal

TheTools · 24.11.2026 02:49 · source · permalink

Alexandre Texier had the primary assist and Larkin the second as Montreal drew level with Los Angeles in the second period of his debut.

Dylan Larkin recorded his first point for the Montreal Canadiens on Monday, earning the second assist on Alex Newhook’s goal at 7:28 of the second period against the Los Angeles Kings at Bell Centre.

Montreal trailed 1-0 when the puck went off Larkin’s skate behind the Los Angeles net. Alexandre Texier found Newhook in front, where he beat Darcy Kuemper for his seventh goal of the season.

It was Larkin’s fifth assist of the season. His first four came in 10 games with the Detroit Red Wings, who traded him to Montreal on Sunday.

The Read

Dylan Larkin had 67 points (34 goals, 33 assists) in 74 games in 2025-26. The Canadiens are 14-6-4 with 32 points through 24 games, 3rd in the Atlantic Division and 9th in the NHL.

Figures as of Nov. 23, 2026.
