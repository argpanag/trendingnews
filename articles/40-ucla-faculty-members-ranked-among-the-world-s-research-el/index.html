40 UCLA faculty members ranked among the world’s research elite

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:10 · source · permalink

Forty UCLA faculty members have earned a place among the world’s most influential scientists and social scientists, underscoring the global reach and impact of the university’s lifesaving and transformative research. The scholars are part of an elite group representing just one in every 1,000 researchers worldwide.

The annual Highly Cited Researchers list, published by international data and analytics firm Clarivate, recognizes scholars whose work has helped shape research in their respective fields. Honorees have authored multiple papers that rank among the top 1% worldwide in citations over an 11-year period, reflecting how frequently other researchers have drawn on their findings.

“We celebrate the Highly Cited Researchers 2026 for extending the frontiers of knowledge and inspiring scientific and academic progress around the world,” said Bar Veinstein, president of academia and government at Clarivate.

This year’s list includes 6,802 researchers from more than 1,200 institutions across 62 countries and regions. As in 2025, the U.S. led the world in highly cited researcher honors, followed by mainland China, the U.K., Germany and Australia.
