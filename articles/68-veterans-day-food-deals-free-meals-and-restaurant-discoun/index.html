68 Veterans Day Food Deals: Free Meals and Restaurant Discounts

TheTools · 10.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Chrissy Callahan

Every year, Americans celebrate Veterans Day to honor veterans and active-duty military members for the sacrifices they’ve made for our country.

This year, the occasion falls on Wednesday, Nov. 11, and many many restaurants are offering active-duty members of the military and veterans a free or discounted meal, coffee or dessert to show their appreciation.

Below, we’ve rounded up a list of some of the many deals you can find at chain restaurants on Veterans Day.

7 Brew

7 Brew is giving veterans 50% off any drink all day on Nov. 11.

Anthony’s Coal Fired Pizza & Wings

Anthony’s Coal Fired Pizza & Wings is giving veterans and active-duty military members 10% off their dine-in purchase with a valid military ID on Nov. 11.

Applebee’s

Applebee’s is giving active-duty military, veterans, Reserves and National Guard members a free full-sized entrée from a special holiday menu while dining in on Nov. 11.

These customers will also get a $5 Bounce Back Card that can be redeemed for their next dine-in, to go or delivery order through Nov. 29.

Aroma Joe’s

Veterans who show a valid ID at Aroma Joe’s on Nov. 11 will get a free 24-ounce coffee or AJ’s Rush energy drink.

Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar

Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar is giving veterans and active-duty service members a free BD’s All-American Burger with cheese and a classic side on Nov. 11 between 11 a.m. — 4 p.m. The offer is valid when you dine in and show a valid military ID or proof of service.

Baya Bar

Baya Bar is giving veterans and active-duty military members a free smoothie on the holiday.

BJ’s Restaurant & Brewhouse

BJ’s Restaurant & Brewhouse is giving current and former military members a free Chocolate Chunk Pizookie when they dine in on Veterans Day.

Blaze Pizza

Participating Blaze Pizza locations will offer veterans andactive-duty military personnel a free one-topping pizza on the holiday.

Bubbakoo’s Burritos

Bubbakoo’s Burritos is offering veterans and active-duty military members a free chicken or beef burrito or bowl when they show a valid military ID and order in restaurant on Nov. 11.

Buffalo Wild Wings

Buffalo Wild Wings is giving veterans and active-duty military members 10 free boneless wings plus fries when they dine in and show proof of service at participating Buffalo Wild Wings sports bars locations on Nov. 11. The same offer is valid for walk-in orders with a $5 minimum purchase at participating Buffalo Wild Wings GO locations.

All year long, Buffalo Wild Wings also offers active-duty military, veterans and first responders at 15% discount at participating locations.

California Pizza Kitchen

Veterans and active-duty service members who dine in at participating California Pizza Kitchen locations on Nov. 11 can get a free entrée and beverage from a special prix fixe menu.

They will also score a buy one, get one bounce back card that’s valid for a pizza, pasta or salad (of equal or lesser value) when they buy any pizza, pasta or salad on a future visit between Nov. 12 — 25.

Cheddar’s Scratch Kitchen

Cheddar’s Scratch Kitchen is offering veterans and active-duty military members a free appetizer or dessert on Nov. 11.

Chili’s is giving all veterans and active-duty military personnel a free entrée (beverages and gratuity not included) when they dine in at any location on Nov. 11. They can choose from the following meals: Oldtimer with Cheese and Fries, Chicken Bacon Ranch Quesadillas, Three-Count Chicken Crispers and Soup with Side Salad.

Condado Tacos

Veterans who show a valid military ID at Condado Tacos on Nov. 11 can get a free taco with a purchase of equal or greater value.

Cracker Barrel

Veterans and active-duty military members who show a valid military ID can get a free Sunrise Pancake Special at participating Cracker Barrel locations on Nov. 11.

Dave & Buster’s

Dave & Buster’s is running the following Veterans Day deals:

  • Free unlimited gameplay for veterans and active-duty military members with a valid military or veteran ID
  • Free dessert with the purchase of an entrée
  • Half-price games for all customers
  • Veterans and active-duty military members also get a 15% off discount on food and non-alcoholic beverages all year
