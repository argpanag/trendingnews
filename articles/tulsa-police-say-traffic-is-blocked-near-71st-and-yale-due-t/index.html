TULSA, Okla. — The Tulsa Police Department said traffic is currently blocked near East 71st Street and South Yale Avenue due to a gas leak.

The department said all eastbound and westbound traffic is blocked at this time.

Authorities said the road will be reopened as soon as it is safe to do so.
