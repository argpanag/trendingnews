Iceland goalkeeper Ingvar Jonsson dies aged 37 a decade after iconic England upset - AOL

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:41 · source · permalink

A goalkeeper who was in the Iceland squad that famously eliminated England from Euro 2016 has tragically passed away aged 37. Ingvar Jonsson has died following a cancer diagnosis earlier this year. Jonsson was an unused substitute throughout Iceland's extraordinary journey to the quarter-finals in France and was in the matchday squad against Roy Hodgson's Three Lions. His club, Vikingur Reykjavik, confirmed the heartbreaking news in a statement on Thursday.

Vikingur said: "It is with great sadness and deep sorrow that we announce that our dear friend and team-mate Ingvar Jonsson, Vikingur's goalkeeper, passed away on Wednesday, November 25, surrounded by his loved ones.

"The thoughts of the club and all Vikingur are with his family and loved ones. We send them our deepest condolences."

Jonsson secured the Icelandic championship five times, including two titles with Vikingur, which clinched qualification for the Europa League and Conference League. He also enjoyed stints in Denmark and Norway.

He claimed the title for the first time in 2014 with Stjarnan and was named Players' Player of the Year. Stjarnan went the entire season undefeated with Jonsson in goal.

The shot-stopper earned eight caps for his country between 2014 and 2019 and played against Spain and South Korea.

Jonsson is survived by a wife and three children. Tributes have flooded in from across the globe following the tragic news of his death.

Belgian side Cercle Brugge commented: "Our deepest condolences." One fan posted: "One is deeply saddened. I extend my condolences to Ingvar's family, friends, loved ones and the Viking community."

While another wrote: "Heartfelt condolences to you all." A third added: "He is a legend in Icelandic football! Rest in peace, dear friend!"
