A-League football: Auckland FC 'calm and confident' heading into Mariners clash

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink
  • On Air
  • The All Sport Breakfast

New recruit Marley Leuluai is adamant Auckland FC's high on confidence despite a rocky start to the A-League football season. 

The eighth placed Black Knights face the Central Coast Mariners away tonight with one win, three draws, and one loss to start their title defence campaign. 

Leuluai told D'Arcy the team's leaders have kept any early season nerves under control.
