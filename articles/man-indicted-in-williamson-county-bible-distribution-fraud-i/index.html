Man Indicted in Williamson County Bible Distribution Fraud Investigation

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:53 · source · permalink

A man has been arrested in Williamson County following an investigation into alleged theft, forgery and computer offenses involving a company that distributes religious materials.

More Crime News

The Tennessee Bureau of Investigation began investigating Giancarlo Trejo, 37, on Oct. 12, 2025, at the request of 21st Judicial District Attorney General Stacey Edmonson.

Investigators allege Trejo directed fraudulent contacts to receive referral fees, asked Bible recipients to send payments to his personal account and used his employer’s credit card for personal expenses and travel.

On Oct. 7, a Williamson County grand jury indicted Trejo on one count of felony theft, three counts of felony computer offense, 10 counts of forgery, seven counts of computer offense and one count of criminal simulation.

Trejo was arrested Tuesday and booked into the Williamson County Jail on a $250,000 bond.

The charges are allegations, and Trejo is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
