10 times the royal wore jewellery with a secret significance

TheTools · 06.10.2026 13:06 · source · permalink

Sparkling jewellery is not just an accessory for the royals – it is a way to pay tribute to loved ones or even express affection for a partner...

From tiaras to statement earrings, Princess Kate and Queen Camilla often wear subtle tributes to the countries they visit during royal tours or pair outfits with accessories that hold a hidden meaning.

The royal family does jewellery best, wearing the most sparkly and elaborate pieces around. So we've scoured the royal archives to find the sweetest sentimental items in the royal ladies' jewellery boxes. No, you're tearing up...

1/10

Princess Kate's special Eton moment

Marking over 30 years since Princess Diana dropped Prince William off at Eton College, Princess Kate made a very touching tribute. On September 8, 2026, Kate and William's son, 13-year-old Prince George, was following in his father's footsteps by starting at the same prestigious all-boys school. To show the special significance of the day, Kate wore a pair of Cartier Trinity hoop earrings, with Diana being one of Cartier Trinity collection's most famous fans. If there was ever a time to pay tribute to Diana, dropping George off at school for the next chapter of his life felt pretty special. 

2/10

Duchess Sophie's personalised necklace

Duchess Sophie keeps her loved ones close to her heart, literally! In January, she accessorised her classic black outfit with a delicate gold necklace featuring the three initials of her family members - an 'E' for her husband Prince Edward, an 'L' for her daughter Lady Louise Windsor and a 'J' for her son, James, Earl of Wessex. The 61-year-old has actually worn the necklace before, but it also featured a shell charm which she has since removed.

3/10

Queen Elizabeth II's engagement ring

It comes as no surprise that the Queen's engagement ring held a special place in her heart. The three-carat solitaire diamond is flanked on each side by four smaller diamonds, and it was made using jewels from a diamond and aquamarine tiara that belonged to Prince Philip's mother, Princess Alice of Battenberg. She wore the special piece of jewellery every day for over 70 years.

4/10

Meghan Markle's star sign

The mother of two loves to add a personal touch to her outfit. While Prince Harry and Meghan may have been briefly separated to attend different royal engagements in Canada and England, Meghan ensured he remained at the forefront of her mind with her gold necklace. Embossed with the word 'Virgo', which is Harry's star sign, the jewellery paid tribute to her husband's birthday on September 15.

5/10

Sarah Ferguson's special gold cuff

While many brides and grooms opt for personalised rings, Sarah Ferguson ensured Princess Eugenie knew how much she was loved during her wedding day to Jack Brooksbank back in 2018. Alongside her striking emerald green Emma Louise Design ensemble, she accessorised with a gold cuff which spelt out 'Eugenie' and 'Beatrice' in diamonds.

6/10

Meghan Markle's gold pendant

For a visit to an Invictus Games Friends and Family reception in April, 2022, Meghan donned a gorgeous gold pendant by Sophie Lis. The designer took to Instagram to explain the meaning behind the design, which featured a diamond plus sign and a ruby minus sign. She said: "Honored to see the stunning Duchess of Sussex wearing our Love Pendant. 'Every day I love you more, more than yesterday less than tomorrow.'"

7/10

Princess Kate's engagement ring

Kate's gorgeous sapphire and diamond engagement ring has become one of the most popular ring designs since Prince William proposed in 2010. However, it is particularly sentimental to the couple, as it once belonged to William's late mother Princess Diana.

8/10

Princess Diana's present

How adorable was Princess Diana's pendant necklace? After the birth of their first son, Prince Charles gifted Diana a sweet gold medallion engraved with 'William'. Diana wore it to the polo in 1983.

9/10

Princess Kate's midnight moon necklace

In a sweet nod to her children Prince George, Princess Charlotte and Prince Louis, Kate sported a beautiful gold disc necklace featuring their initials. The personalised Gold Midnight Moon design - which was also engraved with three small stars - was from British brand Daniela Draper and retails for £1,070.

10/10

Meghan Markle's tribute to Suits

Perhaps a nod to her former career as an actress on Suits, Meghan has often worn several delicate gold bands on her right-hand index finger and thumb. They appear to be very special to her, as she not only wore them during season seven of the show but also during her early public appearances with Prince Harry.
