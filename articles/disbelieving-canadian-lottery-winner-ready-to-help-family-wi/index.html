Disbelieving Canadian lottery winner ready to help family with $1M jackpot | Daily Hive | National

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

Seeing may be believing, but it still took some time for a Canadian lottery player to realize that he had won the jackpot.

Jiawei W., of Richmond, B.C., shared that he couldn’t believe his eyes when he learned that he had won a $1-million Lotto 6/49 prize from the Nov. 4, 2026 draw.

He had scanned his ticket on the BCLC Lotto! Mobile App, and the moment left him stunned.

@atlanticlottery/Instagram

Jiawei bought the winning ticket at the 7-Eleven on Bridgeport Road and Great Canadian Way in Richmond.

The first person the Metro Vancouver resident told about his windfall was his wife, who was also skeptical at first.

“She thought I was joking,” said Jiawei. “I didn’t believe it.”

When asked if he had any immediate plans to spend his $1 million jackpot, Jiawei said that he has no immediate plans. However, he does want to help his family.

“I’m surprised and happy,” Jiawei added when asked how it felt to win.

A trip to the store to buy a light bulb also ended up shining a light on a brighter future for another recent B.C. lottery winner.

The route led Mehrdad P. of Maple Ridge, B.C., past a gas station, where he decided to check his ticket for the Oct. 9, 2026, Lotto Max draw.

BCLC

Mehrdad used a self-scanner to check his ticket and discovered he won a $1 million Maxmillions prize.

Upon realizing how much he had won, he got back in his car and drove back home to share the good news.

The Metro Vancouver resident first told his wife, who thought that he was being a bit strange.

“She said, ‘You are acting weird,’ and I told her, ‘I am weird,’” said Mehrdad.

“I told her I won $100,000, but later we checked on the [BCLC] Lotto! App and saw it was one million.”
