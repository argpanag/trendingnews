Packers and Falcons tied at 7 after first quarter

TheTools · 25.09.2026 01:17 · source · permalink

Christian Watson catches a touchdown in third straight game

GREEN BAY – Christian Watson caught his fourth touchdown in three games, as the Packers and Atlanta Falcons ended the first quarter tied at 7.

Packers and Falcons tied at 7 after the first quarter

Green Bay won the opening coin toss and elected to defer. Atlanta gained the initial first down with a 15-yard screen pass to Bijan Robinson. On second-and-10, safety Xavier McKinney picked off Michael Penix over the middle.

Jager Burton started in place of Aaron Banks at left guard, as Green Bay took over at its 45.

The Packers opened in a two tight end look, gaining six yards on a first-down pass to Tucker Kraft. However, they were forced to punt it away after back-to-back incompletions. The Falcons took over at their 17 after a 41-yard Daniel Whelan punt.

Robinson gained 10 yards and a first down before Zaire Franklin and Brenton Cox Jr. stopped him for a 1-yard loss on second-and-10. Atlanta punted it away after an incomplete pass on third down.

On third-and-11 from the Green Bay 12, Matthew Golden made a great downfield adjustment to a Jordan Love deep ball for a 43-yard completion.

The Packers then worked out of a second-and-20 situation after a holding penalty with a 10-yard screen to MarShawn Lloyd. Harold Perkins was then flagged for unnecessary roughness for excessive force out of bounds.

Lloyd gained six yards on a draw from the Atlanta 28. After a timeout, Love threw a play-action pass to Kraft in the flat for a first down. After a 9-yard pass to Skyy Moore, Lloyd gained two yards to set up first-and-goal at the Atlanta 4.

Christian Watson came uncovered on a playaction pass for the touchdown, putting the Packers up 7-0 with 5 minutes, 13 seconds left in the first quarter.

It's the first time since 2018 a Packers player had a TD catch in the first three games of a season (Davante Adams).

Atlanta started its next possession at its 15. An 8-yard pass to Kyle Pitts and 6-yard Robinson run got the Falcons a first down, but Lukas Van Ness forced Penix into an intentional grounding penalty to set up third-and-11.

However, Brenton Cox Jr. was flagged for unnecessary roughness gave Atlanta a first down. Robinson busted a 55-yard run on the next play to the Green Bay 5.

Robinson ran in a 3-yard touchdown two plays later to tie the game at 7 with 1:32 left in the first quarter.

The Packers went three-and-out at the end of the first quarter.
