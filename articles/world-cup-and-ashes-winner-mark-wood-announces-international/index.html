  • England fast bowler Mark Wood has announced his immediate retirement from international cricket at the age of 36 following recurring knee injuries.
  • The decision comes after medical advice rendered Test and 50-over cricket unfeasible, following knee swelling during the first Ashes Test in Perth last November.
  • A two-time World Cup winner, Wood was a central figure in England's 2019 50-over World Cup triumph on home soil and their 2022 T20 World Cup victory in Australia, while he also won the Ashes with England in 2015.
  • Since making his debut against New Zealand in 2015, he claimed 119 wickets in 38 Tests, 80 wickets in 70 ODIs, and 54 wickets in 38 IT20s.
  • Rob Key, Managing Director of England Men's Cricket, praised Wood's remarkable career, highlighting his ability to act as a point of difference in major series and thrill crowds.
