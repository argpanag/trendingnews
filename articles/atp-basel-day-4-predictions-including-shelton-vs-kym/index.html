ATP Basel Day 4 Predictions Including Shelton vs Kym

TheTools · 28.10.2026 18:33 · source · permalink

The ATP 500 in Basel second round continues on Day 4, after an emotional day that saw the end of Stan Wawrinka’s remarkable career. But there are points to be earned and ATP Finals spots up for grabs. As always, we predict all of the matches. Who do you think will win?

ATP Basel Day 4 Predictions

Jiri Lehecka vs Daniel Altmaier

Lehecka is a strong talent who finds himself competing on bigger and bigger stages, though he hasn’t quite made the jump to consistently elite yet. That jump is coming, though, and it could be very soon. Altmaier is a solid player who can trouble better athletes, but I don’t think he’ll be able to withstand Lehecka for too long.
Prediction: Lehecka in 2

Alex Michelsen vs Arthur Fils

This is a battle of future stars. Fils is slightly further along in his development than the American, but it’s not by much. Both play powerful tennis from the baseline with some solid all-court ability. Fils can comfortably play somewhat more aggressive tennis, but Michelsen is also more comfortable on defense. Ultimately, I expect this to be on Fils’ racket, but don’t be stunned if there’s an upset.
Prediction: Fils in 3

Ben Shelton vs Jerome Kym

Ben Shelton’s serve on an indoor court is just unfair. He seems to have moved on from his US Open final loss and is now racing to a chance to win the ATP Finals on courts that suit his game. See how he does here and in Paris-Bercy leading up to it, because that will tell us if he’s ready to take home an elite title.
Prediction: Shelton in 2
