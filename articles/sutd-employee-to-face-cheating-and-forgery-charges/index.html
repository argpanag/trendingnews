SUTD employee to face cheating and forgery charges

TheTools · 22.09.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

In Singapore, a 41-year-old man is to be charged with cheating and forgery over allegedly unauthorized bookings of football facilities at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD). As Channel NewsAsia reports, citing Singapore police, the charges are set to be brought in court on September 23.

More than 180 bookings

Police said they received a report about possible violations on July 3, 2025. According to them, the man used his position as an assistant manager at SUTD to make several unauthorized bookings.

Preliminary investigations, according to law enforcement authorities, found that over approximately three years, the man made more than 180 unauthorized bookings of the university's football facilities. Police said the bookings resulted in SUTD losing more than S$130,000 in revenue, or about US$102,000.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Alleged deletion of records

Police also believe that the man deleted booking records in an attempt to conceal his actions. Cheating in Singapore carries a penalty of up to three years in prison, a fine, or both, while forgery carries a penalty of up to four years in prison, a fine, or both.

Singapore police said they take seriously the abuse of trust to commit criminal offences.
