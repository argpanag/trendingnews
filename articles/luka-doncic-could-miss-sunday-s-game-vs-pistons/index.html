Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Doncic is questionable for tonight's game against the Detroit Pistons due to lower back tightness.

The Los Angeles Lakers could be without their superstar Luka Doncic in their upcoming game against the Detroit Pistons.

Doncic has been listed as questionable for Sunday night's game in Detroit due to lower back tightness.

The 27-year-old Slovenian superstar has already missed one game earlier this season against the Clippers for the same reason, although he appeared in the other nine games for the Lakers.

In those nine appearances, he has been sensational, averaging 40.1 points, 7.8 rebounds, and 7.7 assists, while shooting 49.8% from the field and 41.9% from three.

Meanwhile, the Lakers have started the season strong as well, currently sitting at the top of the Western Conference with an 8-2 record.

The Slovenian superstar has entered the Lakers' history books as only the fourth player in franchise history to score 40 points in fourth consecutive games, joining Elgin Baylor, Kobe Bryant and George Mikan.

Doncic's 361 points through the first nine games to start the season is the most in the modern NBA and the most since Wilt Chamberlain scored 472 in the 1962-63 season.

Luka has also recorded his 11th career 50-point game passing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and tying Allen Iverson for 10th most in NBA history.

Doncic has also recorded his 62nd game with at least 40 points, five rebounds and five assists. He ties Michael Jordan for the most such games in NBA history, passing LeBron James in Wilt Chamberlain in the process.
