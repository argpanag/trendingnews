Summary

  • US Open quarter-finals - listen to BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra commentary (UK only)

  • Frances Tiafoe comes from two sets down to beat fellow American Alex Michelsen 5-7 3-6 7-5 6-3 7-6 (10-6)

  • Tiafoe reaches New York semi-finals for third time

  • Two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka beats Linda Noskova 7-6 (7-1) 3-6 7-6 (10-7) to reach women's last four
