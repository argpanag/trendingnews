  3. What's happened so far on day 10 of the US Open?published at 00:18 BST 9 September
    00:18 BST 9 September

    Two players have booked their place in the semi-finals of the men's and women's singles.

    • Aryna Sabalenka fought back from a break down in the final set to beat Linda Noskova and keep her title defence alive.
    • Men's 11th seed Frances Tiafoe recovered from two sets and 5-3 down to beat fellow American Alex Michelsen.

    Elsewhere, Briton Neal Skupski and American partner Christian Harrison beat German duo Jakob Schnaitter and Mark Wallner in straight sets in round three of the men's doubles.

    Joe Salisbury and his partner Ranjeev Ram also advanced, with a three-set win over Brandon Carpico and Nikita Filin.

    In the men's wheelchair doubles quarter-finals, Alfie Hewett and Gordon Reid progressed in straight sets. Andy Lapthorne won his quad doubles opener alongside Leon Els but Gregory Slade and Heath Davidson bowed out against top seeds Niels Vink and Guy Sasson.

  4. 'Michelsen is a hell of a player, he easily could have won'published at 00:17 BST 9 September
    00:17 BST 9 September

    Tiafoe 5-7 3-6 7-5 6-3 7-6 (10-6) Michelsen

    Tiafoe speaking after his win against Michelsen: "Super tough game. In the end I slowly started to get my rhythm. Snuck out that third set and got better and better. He's a hell of a player and he easily could have won today. Shout out to Alex Michelsen."

    On the crowd: "Everyone was asleep in the first couple of hours, I was too. As it went along the energy was incredible as it is always. You guys are rooting for me and I appreciate that."

    On the match between Alcaraz and Shelton: "For sure I'll watch their match, but I'm going to enjoy this one though. Let's go!"
