  4. US quarter-final hopespublished at 19:28 BST
    19:28 BST

    Tiafoe v Michelsen

    Frances Tiafoe's match against Alex Michelsen means at least one American man will be guaranteed to reach the US Open semi-finals.

    If Ben Shelton beats two-time champion Carlos Alcaraz in the evening session, he could make it two.

    Tiafoe has reached the last four at Flushing Meadows twice before, but has never got to a Grand Slam final.

    Michelsen, meanwhile, is playing in his first Grand Slam quarter-final and is happy to have gone under the radar so far in New York.

    "First, second, third rounds, all matches I'm feeling nerves. It's a human experience," he said.

    "But it's just about going out there and managing them to the best of my ability. I feel like I've done that very well."

    Great Britain's Andy Lapthorne is through in the men's quad wheelchair doubles alongside South African partner Leon Els.

  7. 'I was pushing myself to the limit'published at 19:14 BST
    19:14 BST

    Sabalenka 7-6 (7-1) 3-6 7-6 (10-7) Noskova

    Sabalenka speaking after the match: "What a battle, what an incredible player she is. I'm speechless right now because I was pushing myself to the limit in this match especially the third set. I honestly thought that's it, the match is over.

    "Super happy to get this win, thank you guys for being amazing. Thank you for the support."

    On not losing a tie-break at the US Open: "This has boosted my energy and confidence. I'm super glad I made that Ace it's a very important point. It just shows that I'm ready to fight and I'm happy to see that."

    On what she will work on for the semi-finals: "A better game than that, I think I approached the net a couple times. I will work a bit extra on that part of my game. For now I just want to relax and recover and reflect what has just happened."
