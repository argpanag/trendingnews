EAST FLATBUSH, Brooklyn (PIX11) — A man was fatally stabbed twice late Friday afternoon in Brooklyn, according to police.

The deadly assault happened at around 5:30 p.m. near 882 Albany Ave. in East Flatbush, according to NYPD.

 Get ongoing crime and safety coverage on our homepage 

The 25-year-old victim was stabbed multiple times in the body, according to police. He was taken to a local hospital where he succumbed to his injuries.

The victim was identified as Christopher Davey and lived in the area, according to NYPD.

Police are looking for two men who fled in a black sedan.

The investigation is ongoing, according to police.
