In case you haven't heard: Victoria's Secret is searching for a new Angel. Following their open casting call earlier this year, VS debuted its first-ever docuseries, Angels Among Us, on YouTube, following the journey to find its newest Angel. The winner will be revealed live during this year's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show on Sunday, October 18—and I've gotta say, the competition is fierce.

After Brielle Reimann was eliminated during episode 3 and Kassandra Marie Crespo was eliminated during episode 4, we now have eight finalists in the running going into episode 5 (which premieres on Sunday, October 11, at 7 p.m. ET on YouTube). Get to know them all, below!

Andreína Martínez Founier

Originally from the Dominican Republic, Andreína was crowned Miss Dominican Republic 2021 and represented the Dominican Republic at Miss Universe 2022, reaching second runner-up.

Instagram: @_andreinamart

TikTok: @_andreinamart

Aria Ocean DuPont-Andrew

The youngest of the potential Angels, 18-year-old Aria is an aspiring actor and model from Pennsylvania.

Instagram: @ariaocean70

TikTok: @ariasguitar70

Kailyn Shepherd

New Jersey native Kailyn has modeled for brands like Aeropostale, Oh Polly, and Mirror Palais, and she also made her acting debut on the CBS police comedy Elsbeth.

Instagram: @kailynshepherd

TikTok: @itgirlkailyn

Kyani Mekōkualani Bateman

Hawai'i native Kyani represented her island as Miss Maui Teen USA 2021 and has since walked runways for both the Hawai'i Swim Show and Miami Swim Week.

Instagram: @kyanibateman

Mar Supremacy

Picked out of the crowd at casting by Paloma Elsesser (casual), Mar hails from Los Angeles and—when she's not modeling—is currently studying finance.

Instagram: @mmarsupremacy

TikTok: @marsupremacy

Riley Midgett

Riley is a recent grad from Eastern New Mexico University, where she was on the track and field team.

Instagram: @riley_midgett

Solange Omon

Originally from Cameroon, Solange is currently based in Maine, where she walks for multicultural fashion shows and is signed with Port City Models.

Instagram: @solange_omon

TikTok: @solangeomon

Tatyana Tinsley

Born and raised in NYC, Tatyana already has done tons of commercial, editorial, and runway modeling, most recently walking in the Sherri Hill Spring 2027 Runway Show during New York Fashion Week.

Instagram: @_tatyanatinsley_

TikTok: @_tatyanatinsley_
