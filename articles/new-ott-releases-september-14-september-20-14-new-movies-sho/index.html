New OTT releases (September 14-September 20): 14 new movies & shows arriving on Netflix, JioHotstar & more

TheTools · 15.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

This week's lineup brings a fresh mix of OTT releases (movies and web series) across Amazon Prime Video, SonyLIV, JioHotstar, Apple TV and more. Lust Stories 3, the third instalment in the Emmy-nominated anthology film series, premieres on Netflix this week. Also arriving are Thudakkam, an action thriller starring debutant Vismaya Mohanlal; Neagley, an action-packed investigative drama led by Maria Sten; Kattalan, a Malayalam thriller featuring Antony Varghese; and the much-awaited new season of the espionage drama Slow Horses. Scroll down to explore the complete list of titles premiering this week.

This six-episode period drama is a modern adaptation of Boleslaw Prus’ classic Polish novel. It follows ambitious merchant Stanisław Wokulski (Tomasz Schuchardt), whose obsession with aristocrat Izabela Łęcka (Sandra Drzymalska) drives him to use his wealth to gain entry into Warsaw's elite social circles.

After the mysterious death of a close friend, Frances Neagley (Maria Sten) sets out to uncover the truth herself. Her investigation takes her deep inside a sinister cult and eventually puts her on a collision course with a formidable enemy. An action-packed spin-off of Reacher, the series sees Sten reprise her role as the sharp private investigator.

Season 6 of Slow Horses continues to focus on Jackson Lamb (Gary Oldman) and his dysfunctional team of disgraced MI5 agents as they find themselves caught up in a dangerous game of retaliation and revenge, orchestrated by Diana Taverner (Kristin Scott Thomas). With their own agency hunting them, the Slough House team must navigate another high-stakes mission. The series is based on Mick Herron's novels Joe Country and Slough House.

The Fixers is a Taiwanese action-comedy series that revolves around a down-on-his-luck former gangster, Felix Chiang (Christopher Lee), who reluctantly teams up with Louis Hsueh (Shou Lou), the arrogant son of a politician. Together, they are tasked with handling the dirty secrets of powerful clients from both sides of the law.

Created by Ryan Murphy and Ian Brennan, Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story is an eight-episode anthology series inspired by the infamous 1892 murders of Andrew and Abby Borden, allegedly committed by their daughter Lizzie Borden (Ella Beatty). Set against the backdrop of Victorian-era America, the series explores family trauma, paranoia and obsession.

After being forced out of her position at a hospital, gifted emergency surgeon Fumi Numata (Mayu Matsuoka) finds herself working in the world of cosmetic medicine. There, she clashes with celebrity cosmetic surgeon Rin Tohyama (Riisa Naka), ultimately exposing the greed and desire beneath the glamour of the industry.

Journalist Charlie Cannon and his wife Larissa are stunned when their daughter Katie returns home eight years after mysteriously disappearing in Egypt. What begins as an emotional reunion soon turns into a terrifying nightmare when the couple discovers that Katie is host to an ancient demonic parasite. As supernatural forces close in, Charlie decides to sacrifice himself, setting the stage for a chilling and ambiguous conclusion.

This coming-of-age comedy explores the lives of childhood best friends Maya (Maitreyi Ramakrishnan) and Anjali (Priyanka Kedia) as they join UCLA’s highly competitive Bollywood-fusion dance team. As they train for the U.S. National Championship, the duo must navigate fierce rivalries, personal conflicts and the pressures that come with competing at the highest level.

Set at a strict military school in Naples, this Italian teen drama follows three cadets — Salvo, Vincenzo and Camilla (Ciro Minopoli, Biagio Venditti and Margherita Buoncristiani, respectively) — as they struggle with discipline, family expectations and the pressures of school life. With phones and romance banned, the students find themselves caught up in a series of secrets that challenge the academy’s rigid rules.

Directed by the distinctive filmmakers Vikramaditya Motwane, Shakun Batra, Vishal Bhardwaj and Kiran Rao, the third installment of Netflix's anthology film series Lust Stories features four short stories that explore modern relationships. The ensemble cast includes Konkona Sen Sharma, Radhika Apte, Vijay Varma, Abhishek Banerjee, Gurfateh Pirzada, Sana Thampi, Ali Fazal, Radhika Madan, Aditi Rao Hydari and Siddharth.

Inspired by the Pierre Choderlos de Laclos' French novel Les Liaisons dangereuses, the upcoming period romance K-drama revolves around Lady Cho (Son Ye-jin), a clever noblewoman, and Cho Won (Ji Chang-wook), who orchestrate a high-stakes seduction game set in the Joseon Dynasty. However, the wager spirals out of control when their target, Ahn Hui-yeon (Nana), becomes entangled in their plot.

Based on Emma Green's best-selling trilogy, You+Me - Against the World delves into the life of Alma, an aspiring filmmaker who secretly pursues her passion from her wealthy family, and Vadim, a rebel with a troubled past. The two are brought together while working on a film project and, despite their differences, gradually fall for each other. Their relationship takes a dangerous turn when an anonymous stalker begins sending them threatening messages.

Thudakkam centres around Meenu (Vismaya Mohanlal), a skilled martial arts practitioner who lives a quiet life with her father (Sai Kumar) in a village in Idukki. Her world is upended after a terrifying encounter with a contract killer (Ashish Joe Antony) leads to her sudden disappearance. What follows is a tense fight for survival as Meenu puts her martial arts skills to use to protect herself and her family.

Set along the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border, Kattalan focuses on the violent turf war between rival ivory-poaching syndicates led by Maari (Sunil) and Eddy (Kabir Duhan Singh). Determined to dismantle Eddy's operation, Maari enlists Antony (Antony Varghese), a notorious poacher known for hunting elephants for their tusks. What follows is a brutal confrontation between competing criminal networks.
