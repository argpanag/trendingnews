Your daily horoscope: September 26, 2026

TheTools · 26.09.2026 04:01 · source · permalink

Libra.iStockPhoto / Getty Images

IF TODAY IS YOUR BIRTHDAY

A sun-Pluto link on your birthday will inspire you to take charge of your life and turn your big dreams into even bigger realities. Aim high, move fast and, most importantly of all, never give up. All things are possible if you want them enough.

ARIES (March 21 - April 20):

If you have relied heavily on other people making decisions for you in recent weeks, then you now need to step up to the plate and take the initiative. It’s most unlike you, an Aries, to be this passive, so make up for it over the next 48 hours.

TAURUS (April 21 - May 21):

There is no point trying to keep your thoughts and feelings to yourself this weekend, because you are sure to say or do something that makes it obvious to everyone what is worrying you. Don’t be ashamed to admit that you need advice and assistance.

GEMINI (May 22 - June 21):

If something in your life isn’t working then now is the time to get rid of it and replace it with something that does. A terrific sun-Pluto link this weekend will make it easy for you to be ruthless, both with yourself and with other people.

CANCER (June 22 - July 23):

The planets warn if you stick to a rigid plan of action this weekend, you won’t get far. Be flexible in your approach, especially at work, and don’t think of it as a sign of failure if you have to rip up your agenda and start all over again.

LEO (July 24 - Aug. 23):

The sun, your ruler, makes a marvelous link to power planet Pluto this weekend, making this the ideal time to push the boundaries, both at home and at work. Certain people need to be reminded that it’s you who should be calling the shots.

VIRGO (Aug. 24 - Sept. 23):

If others make it clear they disagree with what you intend to do this weekend, then get up, get out and leave them behind. Don’t waste your precious time trying to persuade them, because you don’t need their help, though they may need yours.

LIBRA (Sept. 24 - Oct. 23):

The time for thinking is over and the time for action has arrived. The full moon in your opposite sign will give you plenty of excuses to do nothing, but if you don’t act decisively now, you will always regret it. Don’t do it tomorrow, do it today!

SCORPIO (Oct. 24 - Nov. 22):

The sun in the most sensitive area of your chart at this time of year can undermine your confidence a bit, but you are still more than capable of making good things happen – good for yourself, that is. Seize the moment and create your own luck.

SAGITTARIUS (Nov. 23 - Dec. 21):

You may be tempted to ignore that nagging voice at the back of your brain that says something in your world isn’t quite right, but that would be a big mistake. Make it your one and only priority this weekend to find out why you feel so unsettled.

CAPRICORN (Dec. 22 - Jan. 20):

You will have to get tough this weekend, especially with colleagues who seem determined to do as little as possible for as much as possible. Just because they don’t share your work ethic does not mean they are entitled to share less of the load.

AQUARIUS (Jan. 21 - Feb. 19):

This is one of the best days of the year for getting out into the world and making things happen. Don’t listen to those who urge caution, because the planets promise that even when something goes wrong, the end result will still be very much in your favour.

PISCES (Feb. 20 - Mar. 20):

If you speak from the heart over the next 48 hours, then even people who disagree with what you say will be impressed by your passion. Don’t be so passionate though that you go wild and spend money on stuff you only use once and then throw away.
