Inside Singapore’s fake review trade

TheTools · 01.10.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

How easy is it to buy a fake review in Singapore?  The competition watchdog says businesses buying fake reviews range from restaurants to doctors, lawyers and tuition agencies. CNA found listings offering S$1 to S$2 per review — and tested how it works.
