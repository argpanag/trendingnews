Gwyneth Paltrow Stars In 'The Apology' Netflix Series (Exclusive)

TheTools · 19.10.2026 18:23 · source · permalink

EXCLUSIVE: The aftershocks from the big Thursday shakeup at Netflix that saw Chief Content Officer Bela Bajaria being replaced by Peter Rice continued over the weekend, with Head of Film Dan Lin also let go.

In contrast, Head of U.S. and Canada scripted series Jinny Howe made a big statement over the weekend with a two-season straight-to-series order to The Apology, a thriller drama from A24 & studio-based Cut To, starring Oscar winner Gwyneth Paltrow (Shakespeare In Love) in her first lead TV role and directed by Oscar winner Thomas Vinterberg (Another Round) in his second U.S. television directing effort following Netflix’s upcoming Kennedy series.

Netflix bought the competitive project preemptively, Deadline has learned. The streamer declined comment.

Watch on Deadline

There is a symbolic full-circle timing in the pickup. Netflix announced itself as an original programming player with its two-season straight-to-series order to House of Cards, starring another Oscar winner, Kevin Spacey, in 2011. Fifteen years later, the streamer is looking to reestablish its dominance amid Wall Street concerns over slowing engagement growth and coming off a tough week marked with layoffs of some 1,000 people and Bajaria’s ouster.

I hear Rice, who officially started today, has spoken with Howe, who is believed to be safe in her job for the foreseeable future. Rice is not said to have been involved in the decision on The Apology, on which Howe communicated with Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos, sources said.

The Apology, written by Joe Murtagh (Say Nothing), follows Evelyn (Paltrow) and Caleb Amspoker whose son was murdered 18 years ago in a cold case that gripped the nation. When a stranger suddenly appears and confesses, justice finally seems possible — until they learn the killer may get off with a reduced sentence. Faced with that unbearable truth, Evelyn and Caleb decide to take matters into their own hands.

Executive producing are Joe Hipps for Cut To, Murtagh who serves as showrunner, Vinterberg and Paltrow.

Hipps’ A24-based Cut To has emerged as one of the top sellers of big packages with A-list talent attached. Since the company’s launch two years ago, Hipps has landed four straight-to-series orders for A24: Discretion, starring Nicole Kidman and Elle Fanning, at Paramount+; Trigger Point, headlined by Joel Edgerton, at Netflix; and an espionage thriller starring Millie Bobby Brown and David Harbour from Adolescence co-creator Jack Thorne, also at Netflix.

All four sales were competitive. Netflix landing three of the projects is indicative of how aggressive the streamer has been over the past year, winning a large portion of the big TV bidding wars.

This marks Paltrow’s second series-regular role following her supporting turn on another Netflix show, The Politician. She also did a recurring arc on Fox’s Glee. On the feature side, Paltrow recently starred in Marty Supreme. She had stayed largely away from acting in the past several years while focusing on growing her Goop lifestyle brand. Paltrow is repped by Lighthouse Management + Media and CAA.

British TV writer Murtagh created the 2023 series The Woman In the Wall. He is repped by UTA and Lark in the UK.

Vinterberg is directing the first and last episodes of Netflix’s upcoming series Kennedys, about America’s political dynasty. He repped by Gersh and Independent Talent Group in the UK.

A24’s upcoming TV slate also includes Hulu’s The Spot and Peacock crime drama Superfakes. A24 is behind Peacock’s breakout series Crystal Lake and Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s documentary You Can See Everything, which opened big with $15 million at the weekend box office. The studio is about to take out a new Jesse Eisenberg series, written, executive produced and to be directed by Eisenberg, executive produced by Rice under his A24 deal.
