Davis: Underdog Roughriders aren't bluffing, they're 'all-in' for CFL playoffs

TheTools · 30.10.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Jameer Thurman: "Ain't nobody picking us to win this game. People in Regina aren't picking us to win this game."

In poker parlance, the Saskatchewan Roughriders have “a chip and a chair.”

All they’ve got is a seat in the CFL playoffs and one chip to strategically play when they visit the B.C. Lions for the West semifinal on Saturday in Vancouver, where the Roughriders are long shots to win the showdown.

“We’re all-in anyway,” said Roughriders all-star linebacker Jameer Thurman, continuing with the poker metaphor. “It doesn’t matter what’s in our hand right now. We’re going all-in.
