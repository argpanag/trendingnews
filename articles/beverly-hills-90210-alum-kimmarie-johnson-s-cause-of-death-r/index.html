"Beverly Hills, 90210" actress Kimmarie Johnson's cause of death has been revealed following her death at 50.

Johnson died after being diagnosed with a "heart aneurysm condition," her niece, Rhonda Scott, revealed to TMZ on Thursday.

Scott told the outlet that Johnson suffered a "heart event" at her house in Los Angeles on Oct. 27, prompting her to be rushed to Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, where she received her diagnosis.

"Beverly Hills, 90210" actress Kimmarie Johnson (pictured above in West Hollywood in July 2015) died after being diagnosed with a "heart aneurysm condition" at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. Getty Images

Johnson (seen above in Beverly Hills in 2014) suffered a "heart event" at her house in Los Angeles on Oct. 27 and was subsequently rushed to the hospital, where she received her diagnosis. Getty Images

She added that Johnson died peacefully on Wednesday afternoon.

Johnson remained active on social media in the months leading up to her death.

In her final Instagram post, shared in August, Johnson posed for a photo wearing a black coat adorned with silver floral detailing, paired with sunglasses.

A month earlier, she shared a Reel giving followers a look at her time at an event celebrating ESPN's docuseries "Life in the W."

Johnson was best known for her appearance on "Beverly Hills, 90210," where she played the character Judy in the Season 9 episode "The Following Options," which aired in 1998.

Among her other acting credits was an appearance in the 2000 comedy film "Dude, Where's My Car?," starring Ashton Kutcher and Seann William Scott.

Johnson is among several "Beverly Hills, 90210" stars to have died in recent years.

Luke Perry, who portrayed Dylan McKay on the hit series, died in March 2019 at the age of 52 after suffering a massive stroke.

Joe E. Tata, who played Peach Pit owner Nat Bussichio on the series, died in August 2022 at 85. He had been diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease in 2014.

Shannon Doherty, who portrayed Brenda Walsh on the show, died in July 2024 at the age of 53 following a lengthy battle with stage 4 breast cancer.
