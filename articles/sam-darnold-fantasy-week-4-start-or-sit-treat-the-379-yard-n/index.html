Sam Darnold Fantasy Week 4 Start or Sit: Treat the 379-Yard Night as an Outlier - AOL

TheTools · 04.10.2026 19:59 · source · permalink

The Chargers come to Seattle with Darnold 19th among quarterbacks in PFN’s Week 4 rankings, a week after he returned from a glute injury and went all gas no brakes: 45 passes, 31 completions, 379 yards, four touchdowns and two interceptions. That game turned when the team clearly lost confidence in Jadarian Price after the rookie lost a fumble on his third carry, and it is far from what this offense draws up. One weirdo game does not change the view, so he remains a floor rather than a ceiling play, with access to one of the best receivers in the game, and I expect him to revert to that here. Just be careful about chasing the points you watched from your bench.

Should You Start or Sit Sam Darnold in Week 4?

Darnold comes back from a glute injury against a weak opponent … silly me for expecting a high-floor game without a ton of upside.

Instead, it was all gas no brakes against the Commanders as the team clearly lost confidence in Jadarian Price after the rookie lost a fumble on his third carry.

Well, that wasn’t the expected script. It’s always good to see him let his hair down when the situation asks for it (three players had a 20-plus-yard catch, seven had a catch of at least 13 yards), but it’s far from what this team draws up.

It’s going to take more than one weirdo game for me to adjust how I view Darnold. He’s to be viewed as a floor, not a ceiling play. He has access to one of the best receivers in the game, and that naturally gives him a look at upside that players like Drake Maye (right now) and Daniel Jones lack.
