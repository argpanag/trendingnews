The Miss International 2026 grand coronation night is officially underway, with 73 candidates vying for the opportunity to become the pageant’s 64th titleholder and begin a new reign.

After weeks of activities and preliminary competitions in Japan, the candidates are set to face the final stages of the competition, where the field will be narrowed down from the Top 20 until one woman ultimately claims the coveted crown.

Reigning queen Catalina Duque of Colombia will have the honor of crowning her successor, marking the end of her journey as Miss International 2025.

The Miss International 2026 grand coronation night officially opened with all 73 delegates taking the stage for their introductions. Dressed in their respective national costumes, the candidates proudly presented themselves and introduced their home countries, marking the start of the highly anticipated finale.
