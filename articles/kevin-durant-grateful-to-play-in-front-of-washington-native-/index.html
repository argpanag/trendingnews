Kevin Durant ‘Grateful’ To Play In Front Of Washington Native Crowd

TheTools · 16.11.2026 05:58 · source · permalink

Kevin Durant makes his feelings known about playing in front of his native Washington crowd following a win over the Wizards

For his entire NBA career, Kevin Durant has had a special place in his heart for Washington. Being his native place, Durant has long-standing roots with the place, which prompts him to bring his best game on the court against the Washington Wizards. Over the years, the 38-year-old has shared multiple stellar moments in this place.

From facing injuries to nearly recording a triple-double in 33 minutes against Washington, Durant has seen numerous memorable chapters of his career unfold in this place. However, even after playing so many years in the NBA, Durant’s excitement to play in front of his native crowd hasn’t faded.

The 2-time NBA champ returned to the Capital One Arena to face the Wizards. Following the Houston Rockets’ win in the game, Durant opened up about his emotions about playing in Washington.

“Every time you put on an NBA jersey, man, it’s a blessing,” Durant said. “Grateful to do it here in front of my family and friends, and they come to support me every game I play here in my whole career. It’s amazing that we still got to do this, man. So, it’s great.”

Kevin Durant’s night was made even more special with the presence of his mother, Wanda, in the arena. The Rockets forward shares a close bond with his mother and immediately went to greet her near the sidelines after the game as well.

Notably, Durant didn’t disappoint the Washington crowd upon his homecoming. The Houston forward recorded 25 points on the night along with 4 rebounds and 5 assists. The 38-year-old went 8 of 13 from the field and 7 of 8 from the free-throw line, eventually concluding the game with a plus-minus of +24.

Alperen Sengun, Amen Thompson, and Tari Eason scored in double figures as the Rockets clinched a 147-119 blowout win. The Houston Rockets were collectively relentless on offense in the game, scoring 58% from the field and 45% from downtown in the game to blow away the Wizards to win their fourth straight game.

Kevin Durant Is Still Going Strong At 38

Nearly 20 years have passed since Kevin Durant's rookie season for the Seattle SuperSonics. From that point, KD has changed franchises, won NBA Championships, earned MVP honors, and eventually moved up the ladder as one of the greatest to grace the sport of basketball. But what hasn’t changed in these 20 years? Durant’s relentless scoring spree.

Even at the back end of Durant’s career, the 38-year-old is putting up good numbers. In fact, in the last 6 games for the Rockets, Durant has recorded 20+ points in each of them. Durant is scoring north of 48% from the field and 36% from beyond the arc currently.

This clearly showcases the longevity that Durant has managed to produce across his career. The Houston Rockets team clearly revolves around Durant on the court, and with him in the flow, the franchise is looking threatening. They are currently 11-3 in the season, with their next game scheduled against the Miami Heat.
