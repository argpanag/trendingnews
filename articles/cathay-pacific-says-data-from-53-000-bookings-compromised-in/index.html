Cathay Pacific says data from 53,000 bookings compromised in Amadeus cyberattack

TheTools · 30.10.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

Carrier says most cases involved exposed passenger names and flight details, with no evidence financial data was accessed

Hong Kong flag carrier Cathay Pacific Airways has revealed that 53,000 bookings were compromised after hackers breached the system of its technology service provider.

The carrier is among hundreds of airlines worldwide that use technology solutions provided by Amadeus IT Group.

The revelation follows similar disclosures by Korean Air and Japan Airlines.
