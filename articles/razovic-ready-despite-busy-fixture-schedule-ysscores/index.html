Razovic: Ready despite busy fixture schedule | YSscores

TheTools · 19.10.2026 15:53 · source · permalink

Al Taawoun host Al Qadsiah on Tuesday at the club's stadium in Buraidah, as part of the 10th round of the Saudi Pro League, amid a busy schedule of matches for the team.

Razovic explained that all his players represent significant importance to him, pointing out his need to implement a rotation policy among them to reduce fatigue and maintain their ability to deliver good performances.

The Al-Taawoun coach revealed he did not want the match postponed, stating his team is ready to play it on schedule despite the consecutive fixtures in a short period.

The coach stated that he relies on professional players who pay attention to all aspects of their lives, explaining that, based on his experience as a former player, he prefers to play more matches instead of increasing the number of training sessions.

Fok Razović, the coach of Al-Taawoun, emphasized his team's readiness to face Al-Qadisiyah, despite the pressure of matches and playing every three days. He expressed confidence in his players' ability to handle this schedule.
