More content from Rockstar Games’ highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI, including previously unseen assets from its predecessor GTA V, has reportedly surfaced, according to Insider Gaming. Some of the information may have been uncovered in previous leaks, but the latest dump appears to have brought new data with it.

Dataminers are currently sifting through the ginormous 192 GB data dump, but what has been reported so far includes a rough 3D look at the GTA 6 map, an outline of the scrapped Liberty City expansion for GTA V, and more assets from both games, including the cancelled Agent Trevor project.

More than a decade ago, rumours and datamined information suggested that Rockstar was working on a Liberty City map for GTA V, potentially as DLC or a map expansion. It never materialised, however, with fans theorising that Rockstar’s increasing focus on GTA Online eventually led to the project being shelved.

With the recent major leak, we now have a closer look at the cancelled Liberty City project, with TJGM sharing the discovery on X and describing the expansion as massive and much larger than the version featured in GTA IV. Some fans have even speculated that Liberty City could eventually return in GTA VI.

The 192 GB data leak also includes assets from the reportedly cancelled Agent Trevor DLC for GTA V, including the order and names of its missions, as well as the Stems OST for the missions. A shot of a mansion interior supposedly intended for the Agent Trevor DLC was also shared.

The user also shared screenshots of the upcoming GTA VI map “rendered in low quality”. The images appear to show the map’s fundamental layout, with some users noting that it aligns with locations shown in GTA VI’s trailers so far.

Rockstar Games is now gearing up for the launch of what could be one of the biggest video games in history, with GTA VI set to release on 19 November 2026, just two months from now. It will launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, before heading to PC at a later date.
