CAUGHT ON CAMERA: Meteor caught on camera over Cambridge

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

A Cambridge resident captured cosmic activity that lit up skies across southern Ontario on Wednesday evening.

Video submitted by West Galt resident Alicia Brown, taken around 7:40 p.m. on Wednesday, shows a sudden appearance of a glowing blue-green ball streaking across the sky for just a few seconds. 

Media reports show footage of this meteor was captured all around southern Ontario including Barrie, Simcoe County, London and parts of Michigan.

The Great Orbax, a science communicator from the University of Guelph's department of physics and local science education advocate, confirmed via email this was a meteor, specifically a fireball meteor.

He explained a meteor occurs when Earth passes through debris in space typically left behind from a comet or asteroid.

"Typically the size of these pieces of debris are a few mm, but when these pieces are very big, sometimes up to a metre in size, they heat up a lot and generate light on the level of brightness of the planet Venus," he wrote. "That's what a fireball is. This large piece of debris burning up."
