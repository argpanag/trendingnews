SLU is no. 2 top-performing school in the September 2026 Psychometricians Licensure Exam, produces 9 topnotchers - Saint Louis University, Philippines

TheTools · 23.09.2026 13:45 · source · permalink

Saint Louis University (SLU), through its School of Teacher Education and Liberal Arts (STELA), is the second Top-Performing School in the September 2026 Psychometricians Licensure Examination, achieving 97.46% passing rate (346/355) for the “with 300 or more examinees and with at least 95% passing rate” category.

Additionally, nine Louisians placed in the top ten:

Rank 6 (93.20%)
Giruelle Sophia Velicaria Paduit

Rank 7 (93.00%)

Hanna Mikaella Magsi Caplis

Rank 8 (92.80%)
Justin Paolo Canias Balen

Rank 9 (92.60%)

Rank 10 (92.40%)
Vanessa Lorraine Floresca Espejo
Harvey Ford Gallate Marcos

These were announced by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) on 23 September 2026.

Congratulations to the STELA- Department of Psychology, for this outstanding achievement!
