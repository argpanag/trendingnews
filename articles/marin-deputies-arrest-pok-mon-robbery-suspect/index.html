Marin deputies arrest Pokémon robbery suspect

TheTools · 09.11.2026 23:51 · source · permalink

A man who stole Pokémon cards at the Target store in Marin City was booked on suspicion of robbery, according to the Marin County Sheriff’s Office.

The incident occurred Friday morning, when the suspect allegedly stole about $100 worth of the cards and pushed a security guard who confronted him. The penal code defines robbery as theft of property using “force or fear.”

Cesar Rudolph Garcia, 27, of Marin City remained in custody at the county jail on Monday in lieu of $75,000 bail.
