Toney eyed up for Premier League return in January - two years after £40m exit

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:14 · source · permalink
  • Alex Crook, Chief Football Correspondent, talkSPORT
  • Published: 11:14, 20 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 11:39, 20 Nov 2026

Ivan Toney is emerging as a potential January target for Crystal Palace, talkSPORT understands.

England international Toney played under new Palace boss Thomas Frank at Brentford and is currently at Saudi Pro League side Al-Ahli.

The 30-year-old is believed to be open to a return to the Premier League to boost his chances of a place in England’s Euro 2028 squad.

Toney's chances of making the squad recently suffered a blow as he failed to move a court date which clashes with the event.

The striker scored 36 goals in 85 top-flight appearances before leaving the Bees in a £40million transfer during the summer of 2024.

His form in front of goal has been even more prolific since moving to Saudi Arabia.

Toney has scored 86 goals in just 108 appearances across all competitions for Al Ahli.

He also helped his side win back-to-back AFC Champions League Elite titles.

Frank has been promised tools to bolster Palace’s relegation-threatened squad.

His new club are currently rooted to the bottom of the table with just seven points from their opening 10 matches.

However, the Eagles could move out of the drop zone and leapfrog Coventry City by beating the Sky Blues on Saturday.

The two sides meet at the CBS Arena in Frank's first match as manager, with kick-off at 3pm.

Palace have struggled in front of goal in the Premier League so far this term.

Their opening 10 fixtures have seen the Eagles score just 10 goals, including just one in their last four outings.

They have also failed to get on the scoresheet in half of their league matches this season.

Meanwhile, no Palace player has scored more than four goals in all competitions.

With last season's top scorer Ismaila Sarr sidelined until December through injury, they have failed to fill the void in front of goal.

Jean-Philippe Mateta found the net 19 times last term, but has just one goal in all competitions so far this season.

In contrast, Toney has 14 goals in 15 appearances for Al Ahli during the current campaign.

Those include 13 strikes in just nine Saudi Pro League matches to top the scoring charts.
