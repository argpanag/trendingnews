  3. 'I couldn't fail in front of all of those legends'published at 01:50 BST
    01:50 BST

    Sabalenka 7-5 6-2 Pegula

    Aryna Sabalenka, speaking on court after beating Jessica Pegula to reach the US Open final: "No complaints [about her performance], I am just super happy that I was able to pull off such good tennis.

    "Last time I played against Jessica, it was almost impossible for me to get the win so I am super happy to play such tennis, in front of you all. Thank you for the experience."

    To the crowd: "You guys are the best and your respect for me is priceless, I know you wanted her [Pegula] to win but thank you for being so respectful to me.

    "I was so desperate to win, I wanted it badly and I was just trying to talk to myself. Look at my box, I couldn't fail in front of all of those legends, beautiful girls and my team."

    On her rituals before a match: "I do have rituals, I have my favourite breakfast and dinner that has to be done. Just because I have my ritual, they really squeezed me in, my routine means a lot for me."

  11. Hewett and Reid into men's wheelchair doubles finalpublished at 01:33 BST
    01:33 BST

    Hewett/Reid 4-6 6-3 10-8 Fernandez/Oda

    British duo Alfie Hewett and Gordon Reid have just reached the final of the men's wheelchair doubles, beating Gustavo Fernandez and Tokito Oda in a match tie-break.

    Hewett and Reid are going for a sixth US Open doubles title together as a partnership. They'll face Spain's Martin de la Puente and Dutch player Ruben Spaargaren in the final.

    Earlier in the day, Japan's Oda beat Reid 6-1 6-0 in their singles quarter-final. Oda is the top seed and defending champion in that event.

    Hewett, the second seed, defeated American Casey Ratzlaff 6-2 6-0 to reach the last four.
