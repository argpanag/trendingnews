Airbus secures major jet deals with two Vietnamese airlines

TheTools · 14.09.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Vietnam Airlines and Vietravel Airlines signed non-binding agreements for up to 55 Airbus jets, including A350s and narrowbodies. Deliveries start in 2029-2034. These orders reflect Vietnam’s aviation expansion as it becomes Southeast Asia’s second-largest market by seat capacity.

By InsideFlyer

Sep 15 · 1:34 AM · 1 min

Vietnam Airlines will receive five A350-900s starting in 2033, supporting its plan to add 30+ widebodies over a decade. Vietravel Airlines, currently operating five planes, intends to acquire 50 narrowbodies-A220s and A321neos-to expand domestic and regional routes. Both agreements are letters of intent, not firm orders.

The deals were announced during Vietnam’s State President Tô Lâm’s visit to Paris, alongside broader defense aerospace talks. Airbus already serves Vietnam Airlines with 80 aircraft. The new orders aim to meet rising demand as Vietnam surpasses Thailand in regional seat capacity, according to OAG data.

Travelers may see expanded route networks and more frequent flights from both carriers starting late this decade. Delays or cancellations of these orders could limit service growth and competitive pricing across Southeast Asia and beyond.
