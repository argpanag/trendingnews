Meta Reportedly Rebranding Horizon+ to 'Meta VR+', Adding Popular 3D Films to Game Service

TheTools · 10.11.2026 14:06 · source · permalink

Meta may soon rebrand Horizon+, its VR game subscription service for Quest, to ‘Meta VR+’, as the company appears to be bundling a number of 3D films to better appeal to Meta VR Glasses owners.

Ostensibly sourced from the latest HorizonOS 2.10 release which began rolling out late last month, dataminer ‘NT2182’ (via ‘NoriDoesVR’) has uncovered an image that suggests Meta is getting ready for another rebrand.

This time, the company is reportedly refocusing its Horizon+ game subscription service for Quest to include 3D films, and renaming it Meta VR+ the process.

Notably, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023), Project Hail Mary (2026) and Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019) are all actually owned by three different entities: Amazon MGM Studios, Sony Pictures, and the Disney-owned Marvel Studios, respectively.

This suggests that Meta could be hand-picking specific films to license individually rather than offering a wider swath of exclusive content, like Disney+, Prime Video, or Netflix tends to do.

Meta announced in September it would begin hosting 3D Disney films via Disney+, soon to be available to subscribers in select countries. In the past, the company also inked a multi-year partnership with James Cameron’s Lightstorm Vision to develop new stereoscopic content.

This follows the unveiling of Meta VR Glasses in September. The company is differentiating the $1,300 device from its comparatively cheaper Quest 3/3S gaming platform by focusing on casual content consumption, as well as its use as a private productivity device/virtual screen.

The headset (or glasses, rather) are exceedingly light at just ~100g on-head weight, which is done by offloading the compute and battery to a tethered, pocketable puck.

While VR Glasses runs the same version of Horizon OS as Quest, the device doesn’t come with Touch controllers in the box despite supporting them as additional add-ons, focusing more on hand and eye-tracked input. You can learn more about Meta VR Glasses in our hands-on from Connect 2026.
