It’s time for Basketball: San Antonio Spurs vs Sacramento Kings

TheTools · 24.11.2026 02:30 · source · permalink
  • San Antonio Spurs Discussion

The short handed Spurs face the dangerous Kings before their two NBA Cup games later in the week

Welcome to the Game Thread. Veterans of the Game Thread know how we do things around here, but for all you newbies we have a few rules. Our community guidelines apply and basically say be cool, no personal attacks, don’t troll and don’t swear too much.

The Spurs didn’t play particularly well on Saturday night, despite blowing out the Pelicans 114-91. You could say it’s the hallmark of a good team that they can win big when they’re not playing great, but in truth, it was all Victor Wembanyama, who dominated the Zion-less shorebirds all by himself with a 50 point masterclass, along with 18 rebounds, 5 blocks, 4 steals and d0minating just about every stat that you can read in a box score. Well, the big guy is questionable tonight, and with Dylan Harper still rehabbing his sore hip and Fox questionable with a shin contusion, this is a chance for Castle to lead the team to a win, and for Champagnie and Vassell to make some major contributions. Also, Jordan McLaughlin is listed as questionable, so we could see some Ja’Kobi Gillespie minutes, who has only played a little over 4 minutes in the NBA so far, but has been tearing up the G-League with the Austin Spurs.

The opponent tonight is the Sacramento Kings, who only have two wins so far in the season. One of those wins was just a few days ago, as they beat the Celtics 97-96 in the Golden 1 Center. The Celtics rested several of their players, including Jason Tatum, but it shows that in the NBA, good teams can’t take wins for granted just because they’re playing a team that has a bad record, especially if you’re missing key players. The Spurs will have to play with poise and keep their focus to come out with a win tonight, and a fast start is key. If the Silver and Black can take the Kings out of the game early, they could keep the home crown from giving the Kings enthusiasm and come out with a comfortable win. If the Kings hang with the Spurs into the fourth quarter, it could end up with a purple laser beam piercing the sky as the home team lights the beam for the third time this season.

GO SPURS GO!! It’s another late night game, which is going to be on the menu all week, as the Spurs face the Portland Trail Blazers on Wednesday night at 9:00 in the Moda Center, and return to the Frost Bank Center to face the Lakers on Friday night with an inexplicably late 8:30 PM start time. Both games are NBA Cup games, and if San Antonio wins both, they will win their group outright. The Spurs will still have a good shot at advancing into the knockout rounds if they win one of the two games, but winning outright is better.

Game Prediction:

Ja’Kobi Gillespie has his first extended run in the NBA and makes a strong case for being added to the regular roster. [They can’t all be funny, folks.]
