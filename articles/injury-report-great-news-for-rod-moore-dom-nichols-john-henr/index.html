Injury Report: Great News for Rod Moore, Dom Nichols, John Henry Daley for Michigan vs. MSU

TheTools · 07.11.2026 22:36 · source · permalink

In this story:

ANN ARBOR, Mich. — Michigan is set to host Michigan State for the battle for the Paul Bunyan Trophy. The game will be played under the lights for the fifth year in a row, and the Wolverines have taken down the Spartans in four straight games.

But Michigan must be prepared for the Spartans to pull out all the stops in the game on Saturday night. Michigan State has two wins this season and the Wolverines should expect Pat Fitzgerald to pull out every stop to try and take down the Wolverines.

And Michigan has some positive/negative news ahead of the game against Michigan State when the injury report came out.

What it means

This is about the cleanest bill of health Michigan could've asked for ahead of its rivalry game with Michigan State. The Wolverines will get both Dom Nichols and John Henry Daley back this weekend, with neither being listed on the report. Neither played last week at Rutgers, and Nichols hasn't played the last couple of weeks.

Also, Michigan will get Rod Moore back, who also didn't play last week against the Scarlet Knights. Moore wasn't listed on the injury report against Rutgers, but after he suffered an injury against Penn State, he has been very limited.

On the offensive side, Michigan will have its full complement of offensive linemen available. Blake Frazier, Jake Guarnerea, and Brady Norton aren't listed. There were rumblings of Michigan being out another starting lineman this weekend against Michigan State. That's still possible, but the Wolverines aren't listing anyone on the report. Avery Gach has made some noise and seeing a switch in the lineup is more than possible against the Spartans.

Tight end Hogan Hansen is listed as a game-time decision and that means he is going to give it a go. The Wolverines got Mason Bonner involved more last weekend and he played more snaps than he saw all season against Rutgers. It's obvious that Michigan is wanting to get its tall true freshman involved moving forward, and if Hansen can't play, expect more from Bonner.

The big news for rival Michigan State is that quarterback Alessio Milivojevic isn't listed on the report, which indicates he will be back in the lineup for the Spartans.
