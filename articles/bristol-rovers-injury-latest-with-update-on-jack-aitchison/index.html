Bristol Rovers injury latest with update on Jack Aitchison

TheTools · 01.10.2026 10:09 · source · permalink

Steve Evans provided an update on Jack Aitchison ahead of Bristol Rovers' trip to Crewe Alexandra with the Scottish attacker set for a spell on the sidelines.

The former Exeter City midfielder started his first league game of the 2026/27 season against Gillingham on September 19. But was left out of the 18-man squad for the 1-0 win against the Grecians last weekend.

When discussing Aitchison's fitness, Evans said: "Yeah, Jack Aitchison will be out for a few weeks. Jack's had a hamstring injury. He's had a little operation on that, so we're probably not going to see Jack now for six weeks or so, but it is what it is.

"The one position we're blessed with, you know, we've got the maverick back next week - Tommy Leigh comes back after the week out - we've got Shaq Forde, Russell-Denny, I've got Bobby [Kamwa] who can play there. The one thing I'm not short of is options in that position."

Meanwhile, Keenan Gough was an unused substitute against Exeter - returning to the matchday squad for the first time since the 5-3 win against Newport County in August.

"Oh, listen, he's a son-in-law material, isn't he? With the way he conducts himself," he said when asked by Bristol Live about how big a boost it is to have Gough back. "But it's quite interesting because as soon as we scored last week and he came back from a warm-up, and I said to him, you're not getting another pitch.

"And every day, I've seen him this week, I kept saying you were never getting on last week, but you could see, Will, he was our best player by some distance for the early part of the season, so obviously we're pleased to get him back in here.

"But if we go through Friday unscathed, he's had a proper now eight or nine days of full training, and therefore he's ready."

The Scotsman also told local media on Thursday that 'there's been a bit of illness in the camp,' but assured it was not serious and that the worst of it was earlier this week.

"No, there's been a bit of illness in the camp," he said. In terms of, we didn't have an afternoon session on Tuesday. We cleared the place because everyone had it - nothing significant. I had it, everyone had it, which was this head cold, I would call it.

"I listened to one or two of my players, who were not sure whether they were going to make it through 24 hours not worrying about anything else, but no, I think there's a lot of that around, but I think that's in general.

"I think kids went back to school last week, don't they? And of course, when kids go back to school. We know that these little soldiers carry viruses."

Rovers will be looking to win back-to-back League Two games on Saturday, with a tricky trip to the Mornflake Stadium awaiting them. So far this season, Lee Bell’s side are unbeaten in League Two - winning two and drawing six.
