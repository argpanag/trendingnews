Embattled Nvidia (NVDA) CEO Jensen Huang was allegedly involved in a second extramarital affair.

The company's board is reportedly meeting to determine if it will oust Huang as chief executive, the Wall Street Journal reports. Nvidia stock fell in after-hours trading following the report.

According to The New York Times, Huang, whose relationship with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy has embroiled the company in scandal this week, also had a relationship with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell.

She joined Nvidia in 2008 and heads up the company's healthcare business.

The Times report says that Huang and Powell were seen being affectionate at a hotel during a company conference in March, and that when a bystander took a photo of the interaction, a security guard "sequestered them" and made them an offer to sign a nondisclosure agreement.

Last month, The Times reported that US intelligence services believed a woman, Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive, may have acted as an intelligence asset, sharing information with the ruling party in Beijing.

On Sunday, Huang issued a statement apologizing to his family and Nvidia employees for having an affair, though he didn't name with whom.

In response, Nvidia's board said it is launching an independent investigation into the matter.

"While the Board has no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, the Board's inquiry will examine all available information," the board said in a statement Sunday.
