State Bank of India is investigating a suspected exposure of customer data found on the dark web. A preliminary analysis of around 3.75 lakh records identified 3,897 records containing personal information, including names, mobile numbers, dates of birth, email IDs and residential addresses. The bank has activated its cyber incident response protocols and engaged an independent cybersecurity agency to assess the authenticity, source and extent of the exposure. SBI says its operations remain unaffected, but the investigation is ongoing and the identities of potentially affected customers have not yet been established. The disclosure raises fresh concerns about banking data security and the risks of personal information being misused for fraud. Customers should remain vigilant, avoid suspicious links and never share OTPs, PINs or banking credentials.

A viral video allegedly showing racial harassment and inappropriate touching of a tribal youth from Tripura in Delhi has triggered widespread outrage. Union Minister Kiren Rijiju has warned of action against social media influencer Amir Shokeen, while the Tripura Students’ Forum Delhi has filed a complaint seeking an FIR and an urgent investigation. Tripura Chief Minister Manik Saha has also directed the state police to coordinate with Delhi Police. Shokeen issued an apology after the clip went viral.
