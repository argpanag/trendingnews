Confirmed Liverpool line-up v Tottenham Hotspur - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:04 · source · permalink

Share

Joe Gomez starts Liverpool’s Carabao Cup third-round tie with Tottenham Hotspur this evening.

The defender makes his first appearance of the season after recovering from injury as Andoni Iraola makes 10 changes, with only Ronald Araujo retaining a place in the XI from the weekend draw with Fulham.

Liverpool: Mamardashvili, Gomez, Kerkez, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Frimpong, Araujo, Nyoni, McConnell, Koumas, Ngumoha.

Subs: Alisson, Endo, Jacquet, Szoboszlai, Isak, Munoz, Barcola, Gravenberch, Chambers.
