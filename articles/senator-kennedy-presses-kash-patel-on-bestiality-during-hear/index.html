Senator Kennedy presses Kash Patel on bestiality during hearing

TheTools · 16.09.2026 11:06 · source · permalink

FBI Director Kash Patel defended the agency’s decision to roll back restrictions on applicants with a history of bestiality during an exchange with Republican Senator John Kennedy. Patel said the FBI did not want to automatically disqualify people who had been victimised.

Patel added he learned of cases in which otherwise qualified applicants had been barred from consideration because they were forced to engage in bestiality while being trafficked.
