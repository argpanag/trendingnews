Cue Clothing, Veronika Maine go bust despite turnaround efforts

TheTools · 15.09.2026 06:24 · source · permalink

Cue Clothing has collapsed nearly six decades after it was founded with its British owner Hilco Capital appointing receivers in the hope of restructuring and selling the label.

Cue and its sister brand Veronika Maine have 51 stores, including 42 boutiques and nine discount outlets. They also have concessions in Myer and David Jones department stores as well as New Zealand’s Ballantynes.
