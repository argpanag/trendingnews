Tim Westwood in court to deny two more sexual offence charges

TheTools · 12.11.2026 11:36 · source · permalink

Former Radio 1 DJ accused of new offences against women in London and now faces trial in January for 17 charges

The former radio DJ Tim Westwood has denied two additional sex offences dating back to 1991 and 2004.

His latest not guilty pleas mean he now denies 17 charges against nine women between 1983 and 2016.

The 69-year-old appeared at Southwark crown court on Thursday, where he pleaded not guilty to indecently assaulting a teenage girl aged 16 or 17 in Hammersmith and Fulham, west London, in 1991.

The former BBC Radio 1 and Capital Xtra DJ also denied sexually assaulting an 18-year-old woman in Wandsworth, south London, in May 2004.

Westwood, of Fitzrovia, in London’s West End, was charged in October last year with offences against seven women, which he denies.

He is accused of four indecent assaults in the 1980s in London, as well as three indecent assaults at the BBC in the 1990s. He is also alleged to have raped a woman in a hotel in 1996.

Westwood is further accused of two indecent assaults and one count of rape from the early 2000s at a London address, and two counts of rape at a London address in the 2010s.

He is also alleged to have committed a sexual assault at a nightclub in Stroud, Gloucestershire, in 2010, and at a London music festival in 2016.

He has entered not guilty pleas to all counts and is due to face trial for all 17 charges at Southwark crown court in January. He was granted conditional bail.

Westwood left Radio 1 and Radio 1Xtra in 2013 after nearly 20 years. He stepped down from his show on Capital Xtra in April 2022.
