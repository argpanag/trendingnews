  • Survivor Greece contestant Stavros Floros is suing producers for $100 million after losing his left leg in a freak accident while spearfishing off Saona Island in the Dominican Republic.
  • Flores claims producers required contestants to spearfish in heavily trafficked waters without safety measures, despite a recent death in the same area, leading to his life-threatening injury.
  • Production for Survivor Greece season 13 was immediately halted after the accident, and there is no indication of filming resuming as Flores continues his physical rehabilitation in Greece.

Survivor Greece contestant Stavros Floros is suing producers of the hit reality series after a freak accident during season 13, TMZ reported.

During a break from filming in May, Flores was spearfishing in the waters off Saona Island in the Dominican Republic when a propeller struck his body, resulting in his left leg being severed above the knee.

Flores was transported to Miami Hospital, where he underwent multiple surgeries to repair his injury. After 61 days, Flores was released from the hospital. Now, he's suing production for $100 million.

In court documents obtained by TMZ, Flores claimed that he lost his leg in the ocean and that only a "tourniquet applied by a stranger aboard the offending tourist boat kept him alive."

The Survivor Greece star also claimed that producers required contestants to become accustomed to spearfishing. He noted that producers were aware that the water in which contestants spearfished was heavily trafficked by tourist boats.

Flores further claimed that despite a recent death in the same body of water, producers failed to supply contestants with safety boats, protected zones, or adequate warning markers.

Instagram / stavrosflo1

After the accident, the show's production company, AcunMedya, released a statement obtained by the Toronto Sun. "According to the information available so far, the accident appears to have happened when a tourist boat injured the contestant while he was spearfishing outside the competitive process of the reality show," the statement says.

"From the very first moment, there was an immediate response to provide assistance and safely transport the injured contestant, while the competent port authorities are investigating the causes of the incident in order to fully determine the circumstances," continued the statement.

After Flores was struck in the water by a propeller, production for the current season was immediately shut down. Filming remains inactive, and there are no signs of cameras picking back up for the 13th season.

Since returning home to Greece after his hospital stay, Flores has appeared to be in good spirits, as seen in photos and video updates from his Instagram. During his physical rehabilitation, Flores has been surrounded by loved ones as he adapts to life with one leg.
