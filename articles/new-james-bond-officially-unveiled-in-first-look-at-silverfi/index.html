New James Bond Officially Unveiled In First Look At Silverfin

TheTools · 14.10.2026 16:47 · source · permalink

There is a new James Bond in town.

The James Bond franchise stands as the most popular spy thriller movie series of all time. Even with strong competitors emerging over the years, such as Tom Cruise's Ethan Hunt in the Mission: Impossible movies or Matt Damon's Jason Bourne in the franchise named after the character, Bond has remained the most iconic spy in fiction. Many actors have played James Bond in live-action and other mediums. The most recent major portrayals of the spy came in the form of Daniel Craig's final movie as 007, No Time to Die, and the 2026 video game 007 First Light. Now, a new Bond rises.

On Instagram, Big Finish shared the first look at Isaac Rouse as a young James Bond, with the actor set to voice the character in Young Bond: SilverFin Part 1, a new audio drama based on the Charlie Higson novel of the same name.

SilverFin is planned as the first in a new series of James Bond audio dramas that will adapt the Young Bond series of novels created by Higson. SilverFin is set to follow a young Bond during his time studying at Eton College, with the audio drama consisting of 16 half-hour episodes. SilverFin will be released on October 29 as two box sets. To celebrate the upcoming release, Big Finish shared a photo shoot at Eton with Rouse in costume as the young version of James Bond he will voice, donning a fitting 1930s school uniform. Higson penned the first five Young Bond novels, starting with 2005's SilverFin, and concluding his run with 2009's Young Bond By Royal Command. After that, Steve Cole assumed the franchise, penning an additional four books, of which Young Bond: Red Nemesis, which was released in 2017, is the latest. Higson is directly involved with Big Finish's new audio drama, as not only is the first Young Bond audio series based on his SilverFin book, but the author is also a consultant on the James Bond series and one of the project's stars. Higson is set to voice Michael Merriot.

Merriot is the classics tutor at Eton College, serving as an early mentor to James Bond, which makes him the perfect character for Higson, who penned the Young Bond books, to play in the upcoming audio drama. Rouse's James Bond and Higson's Michael Merriot are joined by a wide cast that also includes names like Charlotte McBurney (Wilder Lawless), Miriam Margolyes (Cleek MacSawney), Harry Marcus (Red Kelly), Christopher Nathan (George Hellebore), William Hope (Lord Hellebore), Paterson Joseph (Dr Perseus Friend), Tim McInnerny (Uncle Max), Miranda Raison (Aunt Charmian), and more. The Young Bond series' cast also includes a few major connections to James Bond's history.

Playing Madame Ouspenskaya is Lucy Fleming, the niece of James Bond creator Ian Fleming. Other Bond-related castings include that of Tim Bentinck, who has voiced James Bond in video game form, as Sergeant White, and Penelope Rawlins, who is related to the spy that served as an inspiration for James Bond, R. H. Bruce Lockhart (per Big Finish), as the voice of Alfie Kelly. Young Bond: SilverFin Part 1 helps in the wait for the casting of the next James Bond movie, which will be directed by Dune franchise's Denis Villeneuve.
