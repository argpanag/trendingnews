Bend park district working with designers to replace Harmon Park boat - The Bulletin

TheTools · 16.11.2026 19:54 · source · permalink

Published 11:54 am Monday, November 16, 2026

By Noemi Arellano-Summer

Bend Park & Recreation is working to add a boat-themed piece of playground equipment at Harmon Park, after the previous P.S. Happy Harmon metal boat was removed this summer.

The previous boat was removed due to age and safety issues. Its fame made Harmon Park known as “the boat park.” It was a lifeboat that park employee Ken Lehto converted to a tugboat in 1974.

The park district is working with three playground representatives and their design teams. Each team will design one boat-themed concept using the same project parameters. Concept renderings will be placed on the park district’s website and the park district will review the options before presenting them to the community.

A park district project webpage provides the opportunity for community members to be involved and updated. Community members can review designs when available, provide feedback and sign up for project emails. Feedback will help inform the park district’s choice of design.

Design concepts are expected to be available by the end of the year, with installation tentatively scheduled for summer 2027.
