Sunday’s Football Bet of the Day sees me head to the MLS, and I’ve highlighted a goals-based selection in one of the fixtures.

⚽️ Chicago Fire vs Charlotte FC

🇺🇸 MLS

⏰ Sunday 22 November, 23:00

The Chicago Fire host Charlotte FC in Game 1 of their best-of-three MLS Cup Playoff series at Soldier Field this evening, a fixture I'm looking for goals in.

Gregg Berhalter’s hosts have scored 64 goals across their opening 34 matches in the MLS this campaign, with 33 (52%) of them coming in front of their supporters.

The Fire have found the back of the net in all 17 (100%) of their contests in front of their supporters this term – scoring on multiple occasions in nine of their past 13 – and will be confident of breaching a Crown backline that has kept just two clean sheets on their travels this season, shipping multiple goals in each of their last four.

Dean Smith’s hosts have scored 66 goals across their opening 34 contests in the MLS this term, with 29 (45%) of them coming on their travels.

The Crown have blanked just twice on their travels all season – scoring on multiple occasions in eight of their past nine – and will also be confident of breaching a Chicago defence that is without a clean sheet in each of their past 10 MLS clashes in front of their supporters.
