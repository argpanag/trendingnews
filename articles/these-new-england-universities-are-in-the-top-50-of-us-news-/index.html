These New England universities are in the Top 50 of US News rankings. See the list. - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 23.09.2026 05:46 · source · permalink

Take a look at the rankings of US News & World Report’s 2027 lists of the nation’s best universities and liberal arts colleges, released Tuesday, and you’ll find lots of schools from New England near the top of the charts.

Yes, there’s MIT, ranked No. 1 for the first time ever, and its neighbor Harvard University, ranked in third place for the fourth consecutive year. Harvard’s historic rival Yale holds its place in fourth for a second year.

Elsewhere in the top 25, Dartmouth College jumped up one spot and tied for 12th, while Brown University dropped three places from last year to land a four-way tie for 16th.

Boston College and Tufts University both jumped five places this year, into a three-way tie for 31st. Just below them was Boston University, which rose a whopping eight ranks from the previous year to 34th. Northeastern University rose one step in the ranks, tying for 42nd.

Just outside the top 50 sat Brandeis University and University of Massachusetts, Amherst, both of which climbed into a tie for 59th. That put UMass Amherst as the highest-ranked public university in New England.

New England has also continued to dominate the liberal arts ranking, and is home to each of the top three schools: Williams College, Amherst College and Bowdoin College.Wellesley College placed 10th this year, while Wesleyan University was tied for 14th, while Middlebury College slotted in at 19th. Colby College, College of the Holy Cross, and Smith College joined a tie for 22nd. Bates, Trinity, Mount Holyoke, and Connecticut Colleges all also made the top 50 liberal arts colleges.
