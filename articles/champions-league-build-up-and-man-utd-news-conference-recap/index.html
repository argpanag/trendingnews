  5. Napoli v Arsenal preview: Key stats and player infopublished at 14:55 BST
    14:55 BST

    Napoli v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Matt Jones
    BBC Sport journalist

    After coming up agonisingly short in last season's Champions League final, Arsenal begin what they hope will be a glorious European campaign at Napoli.

    The Gunners were beaten on penalties by Paris Saint-Germain in May, with Gabriel missing the decisive kick after a 1-1 draw.

    That was after Mikel Arteta's team topped the league phase with eight wins from eight and then went unbeaten during the knockout stages.

    As for Napoli, their Champions League adventure was a big disappointment last term, as they finished in a lowly 30th place in the league phase.

    New head coach Massimiliano Allegri, who replaced Antonio Conte this summer, will be confident of a better show from what is still a talented squad.

    Did you know?

    • Napoli won their first Serie A game of the season, but go into this game after back-to-back losses.
    • Arsenal have only won on one of their last six away trips to face Italian sides in the Champions League (D1 L4), failing to score in five of those.

    Man Utd v Sabah (Thu, 20:00 BST)

    Manchester United

As long as I can play and perform for the team, I'm happy."
