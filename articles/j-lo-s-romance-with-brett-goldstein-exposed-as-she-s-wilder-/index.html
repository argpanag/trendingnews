J-Lo's romance with Brett Goldstein exposed as she's 'wilder than she looks'

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:34 · source · permalink

Brett Goldstein and Jennifer Lopez are said to be casually dating(Image: Getty Images)

Jennifer Lopez and Ted Lasso star Brett Goldstein are said to be casually dating. The pair met when they starred together in the Netflix romcom Office Romance.

Hollywood's hottest new couple were seen leaving a glitzy Los Angeles event in the same car on Friday night (October 16). But is J-Lo's latest romance doomed to fail or destined to stand the test of time?

Celebrity psychic and astrologer Inbaal Honigman, speaking exclusively via Casino, took a closer look at the couple to see if they're a good fit. Her reading of the stars revealed that both J-Lo, 57, and Brett, 46, love "casual dating" and that they have the "same approach to love".

The pair seem to be very well suited, according to their stars(Image: Getty Images)

She said: "Jennifer Lopez's passionate, fiery birth chart is a refreshing contrast to Brett Goldstein's sensitive, sentimental astrological chart, but there's one very special thing that the two have in common. J Lo's chart is very much the superstar's stars.

"Both her Sun sign, which is her public persona, and her Mercury sign, which represents her communication skills, are in the proud and loud sign of Leo, showing that she was destined for the stage, and she can live her life in public without feeling overexposed.

Jennifer is "wilder than she appears"(Image: Getty Images for Netflix)

"Jennifer's Scorpio moon reveals that deep down she's even wilder than she appears. Brett's astrology is quite different, as both his Sun and Mercury, which are his public face and his way of communicating, are in the caring and emotional sign of Cancer. This means that Brett is all about the home and the family and that he enjoys feeling safe and secure."

Honigman said that Brett leads with "brains not vibes" when it comes to "work, investments and saving for a rainy day". This is because he "has three important planets in the responsible and sensible sign of Virgo, they are the moon, Jupiter the wealth planet, and Saturn planet of discipline."

Inbaal Honigman said the "main thing that ties this couple together is their Venus sign"(Image: Andrew H. Walker/Shutterstock)

She continued: "But the one main thing that ties this couple together is their Venus sign. Venus is the planet of love, named for the Roman goddess of love and beauty. It is the planet that determines how each person would be around their family, the home, and their inner circle of closest friends. Both Jen and Brett have this planet in the fun loving, cheeky and experimental sign of Gemini."

The expert explained that this means that casual dating is their favourite type of dating as Gemini is "so easygoing". She said that it means they can flirt with other people with no risk of jealousy, adding: "They'd rather laugh things off and stick together, than get too serious and suffer."

Honigman concluded: "The fact that both of them have the same Venus sign, means that they have the same approach to love, families and domesticity. In fact, Gemini is a dual sign, and this duality under Venus means that both Brett and Jennifer would rather date someone from a different background and live in multiple locations on planet Earth, so it is the ideal placement for long-distance and age-gap relationships!"
