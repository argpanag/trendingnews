Ledger investigates potential wallet tampering after reports of $86 million in crypto stolen

TheTools · 10.10.2026 06:52 · source · permalink

The hardware wallet maker said it is investigating devices sold by a Southeast Asian reseller as social posts swirl about crypto assets drained from Bitcoin, Ethereum and Tron addresses.

  • Ledger is investigating reports that more than $86 million in cryptocurrency may have been stolen from hundreds of wallets linked to devices sold by CryptoBilis, a Southeast Asian reseller, though the losses and connection have not been independently verified.
  • Ledger asked CryptoBilis to halt sales and shipments and advised recent buyers not to set up their devices, while customers who already activated wallets should consider transferring their assets to a new device with a newly generated recovery phrase.
  • A supply-chain attack involving tampered devices could be one possible explanation, but the cause, number of affected users and total losses remain unconfirmed.
