Eng v Ire U15 WC match abandoned after alleged racism

TheTools · 26.10.2026 17:28 · source · permalink

A match between Republic of Ireland and England at at FIFA's new Under-15 World Cup has been abandoned after the English players walked off the field following an allegation of racism.

Footage from the match appears to show England coach Matt Thorpe beckoning his players off the field after a confrontation between one of their players and an Irish opponent.

Words also appeared to be exchanged between the two coaching teams, and the game at the tournament in Azerbaijan was stopped with the Republic of Ireland leading 4-1.

A FIFA spokesperson said: "FIFA is aware of an incident at the FIFA Under-15 World Cup and Festival match between England and the Republic of Ireland.

"The match was abandoned by the referee following England's refusal to continue.

"The official reports are being gathered, and the matter will be referred to the independent disciplinary committee who will decide in due course."

The FAI have been contacted for comment.

Ireland beat Cambodia in their final group stage match 4-1 to finish ahead of England. The young Lions beat Zimbabwe 2-0 in their final game.

The new tournament is played in an eight-a-side format with smaller pitches and 20 minute halves.
