Regulation (EU) 2026/2269 of the European Parliament and of the Council on temporary trade-liberalisation measures for certain products originating in Armenia was published in the Official Journal on 9 October 2026. It was adopted on 7 October 2026 on the basis of Article 207(2) TFEU.

The recitals refer to large-scale trade measures introduced by the Russian Federation since May 2026. Those measures affect imports and transit of key Armenian export products, including alcoholic beverages, mineral water, fruits and vegetables. The recitals state that it is appropriate to grant trade concessions in a spirit of solidarity with Armenia, within the framework of the Comprehensive and Enhanced Partnership Agreement.
