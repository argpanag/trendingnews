Perfectly-formed 19ac holding near Punchestown and Naas on market for €1.95m

TheTools · 09.09.2026 09:04 · source · permalink

The impressive residence is a big focal point, offering 341sq m of living space and finished to an exceptional standard, according to the selling agents.

New to the market with Naas-based auctioneers Coonan Property is a compact but perfectly formed 19-acre holding at Eadestown — just southeast of Naas.

According to selling agent Will Coonan, the land here is excellent, typical of its environment. Laid out in six paddocks, with water laid on throughout and defined by stud fencing.

The impressive residence is a big focal point, offering 341sq m of living space and finished to an exceptional standard, according to the selling agents.

“The house is approached via a mature tree-lined avenue, with established beech hedgerows and lime trees creating a wonderful sense of arrival,” Mr Coonan says.

But the property has an even more unique feature in the presence of a 4,500-year-old granite genuine standing stone — just one of many such stones that stretch from Punchestown to Forenaughts.

The proximity to Punchestown is complemented by the property’s equestrian facilities, that include an American-style barn incorporating eight loose boxes, as well as mare and foaling box, feed room and tack room. There is also an all-weather arena, an Emerald horse walker, a lunge ring and a woodchip arena.

The entire impressive package is guiding €1,95m.
