Nicole Taylor, a singer in the country trio Taylor Red, has died. She was 35.

On Monday, Nika and Natalie Taylor, the other members of the group and Nicole's sisters, announced her death in a TikTok post.

"We’re heartbroken to announce the passing of our sister, Nicole Taylor," Nika said. "We honestly thought she’d be healed here on earth, but now she’s healed in heaven."
