Erling Haaland relishing ‘fun’ battle with Gabriel as Man City strive to improve

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:24 · source · permalink

Erling Haaland is relishing another showdown with Gabriel but knows Manchester City must improve to get anything out of Sunday’s clash with Arsenal.

City will face the Premier League champions at the Emirates Stadium having won just one of their previous seven games in all competitions and defeat would leave them trailing the Gunners by nine points.

Haaland is also without a goal in three games and he expects a tough battle as he prepares to renew his rivalry with centre-back Gabriel.

The Norwegian told the club’s website: “It is always good to play against the best teams – Arsenal have been the best team in the last year and one of the best teams in the last five years and, yes, it’s always fun to play against Gabriel.

“But we have to perform better. We need a top performance on Sunday. It is the most difficult game of the season, to go to the champions away, so we need everyone to have a top performance to get something out of the game.”

City are winless in four Premier League games and endured further frustration as they needed to fight back to snatch a 2-2 draw with Napoli in the Champions League at the Etihad Stadium on Tuesday.

Enzo Maresca’s side trailed twice in the second half, with former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund and Noa Lang on target for the Italian side either side of an Ayyoub Bouaddi equaliser.

City salvaged a point with a fine individual goal from Rayan Cherki four minutes from time but it was a largely uninspiring display from the hosts.

City began the season strongly, and won their first seven Premier League games, but their form has dipped considerably in the past month, with creativity lacking and defensive errors proving costly.

Maresca said: “In this moment it is like this. In the first 10 games we didn’t concede, we always scored first and then in this moment it is exactly in the opposite way.

“We just we need to find a way to avoid that but I don’t think it affects the players in terms of confidence.

“Avoiding goals, that is important, but on the ball all the numbers, all the stats, are for us. The main thing is the balance between what we concede and what we create.”
