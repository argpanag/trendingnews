Miandoab: 15-Year-Old Zahra Jadidi Murdered by Her Fiancé | Hana Human Rights Organization

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

According to reports received by Hana Human Rights Organization, on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, Zahra Jadidi, a 15-year-old girl from Miandoab, was stabbed to death by her 21-year-old fiancé, Ali-Asghar Fayazian, at the city’s Coastal Park.

According to information received, Zahra had been engaged to this individual during her childhood but intended to end the relationship. Hana’s sources identified her fiancé’s opposition to her decision to separate as one of the factors linked to the murder.

According to the same reports, Ali-Asghar Fayazian took his own life using a hunting rifle after committing the murder. The Miandoab police commander also confirmed the incident and stated that the bodies of both individuals had been found near the scene.

Hana Human Rights Organization considers the murder of Zahra Jadidi a shocking example of violence against girls, underscoring the urgent need to combat child marriage and guarantee women and girls the right to leave unwanted relationships. The perpetrator’s death must not prevent an investigation into the circumstances of Zahra’s childhood marriage, the factors that led to this crime, and the possibility of preventing it.
