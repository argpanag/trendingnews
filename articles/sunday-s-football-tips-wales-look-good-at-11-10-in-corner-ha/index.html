After losing their first two group games away to Portugal and the Danes, the bookies wrote Craig Bellamy's men off at home to Norway.

But they beat the World Cup quarter-finalists 2-1 and that will give them a confidence boost ahead of the return clash with Denmark.

Wales vs Denmark top betting tips

Denmark eased to a 2-0 win when these sides faced off in Copenhagen last Sunday and Bet365 make them 19/20 favourites to do the double over Wales.

But Bellamy's side will take heart from that win over Norway, even though Torbjorn Heggem's red card played a big part in their victory.

The Welsh always have a go under Bellamy so I can see goals at both ends in this one, with BTTS and over 2.5 strikes paying evens.

We only saw six corners in the last meeting and four of them went to Wales in Copenhagen. That suggests backing the Welsh at +1 in the corner handicap tonight could pay off at 11/10.

Portugal vs Norway

Portugal host Norway in the other Group A4 game tonight and the Nations League champions are looking to keep up their perfect record.

Cristiano Ronaldo's hissy fit cast a cloud over their last game against Denmark but the Portuguese showed their class by claiming a 4-2 win in Copenhagen.

The unity in the camp should improve without Ronaldo and they now take on a Norway side they beat 2-1 in Oslo.

Bet365 make Portugal 8/13 favourites for another win and it's hard to back the Norwegians as they've been leaking goals.

It's now 11 games without a clean sheet for Norway, who have been relying on Erling Haaland and Co outscoring the opposition.

Goncalo Ramos has scored in back-to-back games since replacing Ronaldo up front. He's 10/11 with Bet365 for another goal tonight and adding on over 1.5 shots on target boosts the odds to 27/20.

Greece vs Germany

Greece have been upsetting the odds in Group A2 and will fancy their chances of doing the double against Germany tonight.

The Greeks ruined Jurgen Klopp's first home game as German boss when they claimed a shock 1-0 win last Sunday.

It looked like they would follow it up with another victory at home to the Dutch on Thursday when they were 2-0 up at the break.

The Netherlands recovered to scrape a 2-2 draw but belief will still be high in the Greek camp, especially with Klopp using the Nations League for a bit of experimentation.

Plenty of German big hitters will sit this one out so I'm surprised to see Greece as big as 31/10 for a win on home soil.

I'm playing it a bit safer and backing the Greeks to win either half at 5/4 with Bet365. That bet has landed in all three of their Nations League games so far.
