The uniform worn by "Basketball Emperor" Michael Jordan in the championship match, which won the last championship, was sold for $14 million (about 18.7 billion won). It's the highest price ever for a basketball souvenir auction.

The Associated Press reported on the 13th (Korea Standard Time) that Jordan wore when he played in the 1998 NBA championship game for the Chicago Bulls was sold for $14 million.

The buyer is Ron Furman, a real estate investor in Miami, USA.

The handwritten uniform was worn by Jordan in the second round of the championship game against the Utah Jazz at the time. Chicago won the game and put the series record at 1-1.

Jordan's 1998 championship uniform is trading at a high price every time it goes up for auction. The first game uniform was sold for $10.1 million (about 13.5 billion won) in 2022, and the third game uniform was sold for $12.3 million (about 16.4 billion won) last month. This second game's uniform exceeded both records.

Expanding the scope of the sport as a whole, Babe Ruth, a legend of the New York Yankees in the U.S. professional baseball, holds the record for the highest price of a game suit auction. The uniform, which Ruth wore when she hit a so-called "preview home run" against the Chicago Cubs in Game 3 of the 1932 World Series, was sold for $24.12 million (about 32.2 billion won) in 2024.
