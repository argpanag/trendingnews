Is Taylor Swift Singing About Tom Hiddleston on 'Pink Clouding'? Look Back at Their Relationship Timeline

TheTools · 25.09.2026 06:22 · source · permalink

Taylor Swift dated actor Tom Hiddleston for a few months back in 2016 and now fans think she’s singing about him on her new song “Pink Clouding,” released 10 years after their breakup.

Taylor and Tom had a whirlwind romance in the summer of 2016, with sightings of them across the world.

What are the lyrics for “Pink Clouding” and what do they mean?

Taylor didn’t want to let this guy down, so she ended things and left him on a “pink cloud.”

In the chorus, she sings, “‘Cause I was pink clouding from the thrill of it (Pink) / Skydiving as you wrapped your arms around my body / Gray rocking where the arrow hit (Gray) / Years later, I still wonder how to tell you sorry / I didn’t want to let you down (Pink cloud, pink cloud) / So I left you up there on a pink cloud (Pink, pink cloud).”

Taylor seems to apologize to this ex on the bridge.

At the end of the song Taylor, says she hopes this guy “landed safely on the ground” and “I hope you’re so happy now.”

May 1, 2016 – Taylor and Tom first spotted together

Taylor and Tom met for the first time while attending a pre-Met Gala dinner at Anna Wintour‘s home.

May 2, 2016 – They danced together at the Met Gala

There’s a video of the two of them on the dance floor together at the gala.

June 1, 2016 – Taylor’s breakup with Calvin Harris confirmed

At the time, it was reported that there was “no drama” amid their breakup, but that didn’t appear to be the case.

June 15, 2026 – Taylor and Tom spotted kissing in Rhode Island

Taylor has a home in Rhode Island and they were spotted sharing kisses there in early June.

June 23, 2016 – Taylor and Tom spotted on double date in Nashville

At this point, there had already been multiple sightings of the couple in Nashville, where Tom reportedly met Taylor‘s parents for the first time.

They also went to a Selena Gomez concert while in the city.

June 26, 2016 – Taylor and Tom spotted with his family in England

Taylor and Tom visited his family in England.

That same week, they went to Rome as well.

July 4, 2016 – Tom joins Taylor and friends at annual Fourth of July party in Rhode Island

Tom was spotted wearing his infamous “I <3 TS” tank top that weekend.

July 6, 2016 – Taylor and Tom spotted in Los Angeles

Taylor and Tom were spotted together at LAX Airport. It appears they were on their way to Australia at the time.

July 10, 2016 – Spotted in the Gold Coast in Australia

While in Australia, they were seen visiting a children’s hospital and also enjoying the sights and restaurants.

July 14, 2016 – Tom speaks out about his relationship with Taylor

Tom confirmed their relationship in an interview with THR.

“Well, um. How best to put this? That notion is—look, the truth is that Taylor Swift and I are together and we’re very happy. Thanks for asking. That’s the truth. It’s not a publicity stunt,” he said at the time.

July 24, 2016 – Tom makes another comment

“I don’t know, it comes down to being authentic,” he told People. “Everything you do you have to make sure you truly believe in it and as long as you know that, it doesn’t matter what anyone else says about it because the nature of being a public figure is that everyone will have an opinion about anything you do, and as long as you know why you’ve done something and you’ve committed to it with authenticity then you’re okay.”

August 13, 2016 – Yet another comment from Tom

“I’ve learned that there are many sides to a story, and that sometimes there are a lot of stories out there which are false and the hardest thing is to try to not let those falsehoods affect your own life, that’s what I would say,” he told THR.

September 6, 2016 – Breakup reported

It was reported that they broke up after three months together and that it was “amicable.”

Rumors swirled that they were having difficulties with their schedules, with Tom filming Thor: Ragnarok at the time in Australia.
