US Open Day 5 Men's Predictions Including Zverev vs Halys

TheTools · 02.09.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

Despite rainy conditions at the start of the 2026 US Open, the second round is scheduled to wrap up on Day 5 as 16 men’s matches get underway. No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev survived a Round 1 scare and will look to bounce back against veteran Frenchman Quentin Halys. Zain Mustafa, Yesh Ginsburg, and Shane Black offer their insights. But who will advance?

US Open Day 5 Men’s Predictions

Jakub Mensik vs Jurij Rodionov

Zain
Coming all the way from the qualifying round, Jurij Rodionov has played his lefty baseline game well so far this tournament, and he now gets a step up in competition against Jakub Mensik. The Czech has the bigger weapons in this match, and if he plays his game, it is hard to see him not win, despite his underperformance at the Majors at times.
Prediction: Mensik in 4

Yesh:
Rodionov has looked so impressive this tournament, and Mensik looked very shaky closing out his opening match. If those nerves remain then Rodionov could punish him, but I think the Czech is just the better player right now.

Shane
Mensik is a Mixed Doubles Grand Slam champion. Will that help him at the US Open? It definitely can’t hurt. Rodionov has impressively won four matches in New York, but Mensik’s serve and first-strike tennis will be too much for the Austrian.
Prediction: Mensik in 3

Learner Tien vs Gael Monfils

Zain
In potentially his career-last match at a Major, Gael Monfils takes on Learner Tien in what will be a baseline battle. At his best, the Frenchman would have been favored to physically win this match, but at this stage in both players’ careers, Tien should be able to outlast Monfils, but not before the Frenchman wins a couple of popcorn points.
Prediction: Tien in 4

Yesh:
Learner Tien has played in some big matches in his young career so far, but nothing at all (not even the Next Gen Finals) will have prepared him for what the atmosphere will be like in New York for this one. Monfils will thrive off the crowd. Assuming he doesn’t get fatigued, he should ride a wave of emotion past the American.
Prediction: Monfils in 4

Shane
This will be a fun one. It’s Monfils against arguably the best young American in the sport! What more could you want? The Frenchman will push Tien early, but ultimately the American should be able to outhit and outlast Monfils.
Prediction: Tien in 4

Alejandro Tabilo vs Alexei Popyrin

Zain
Alexei Popyrin had a pretty solid showing in the first round, gaining some momentum in what has been a subpar season so far. Against Alejandro Tabilo, this match comes down to whether the Aussie can take the racket out of Tabilo’s hand with his big serve and forehand. Tabilo is the more consistent player here, and if he plays up to his level, this should be a straightforward win for the Chilean.
Prediction: Tabilo in 4

Yesh:
Popyrin looked really strong against Grigor Dimitrov, leaving the Bulgarian with no answers for his powerful baseline game. I can’t see Tabilo doing much against him, either.
Prediction: Popyrin in 3

Shane
Both men looked confident and poised in their Round 1 US Open wins. Tabilo is the seeded player, but Popyrin, who has had some Grand Slam success, is the better hard court player. Give me the Aussie in a tight one.
Prediction: Popyrin in 5

Alexander Zverev vs Quentin Halys

Zain
Alexander Zverev survived a big scare in his opener, as he continues to struggle on the hard courts this summer. Up next will be Quentin Halys, a player whose aggressive baseline style has troubled Zverev in the past. The German should still advance, but I think this can get trickier for him than he would like.
Prediction: Zverev in 4

Yesh:
Zverev did not look good in his first match. He got through, but he’ll need to play better to beat the Frenchman. He says he can do it, so who am I to disagree?
Prediction: Zverev in 4

Shane
Zverev left the court at 2 AM after his first-round five-setter. For some players, this may be a poor sign of things to come. However, I think this is just the scare the German needed. This US Open draw is open. He will advance through it.
Prediction: Zverev in 3
