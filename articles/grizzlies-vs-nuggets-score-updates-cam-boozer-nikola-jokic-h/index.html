Grizzlies vs Nuggets score updates, Cam Boozer, Nikola Jokic highlights

TheTools · 11.11.2026 22:01 · source · permalink

The Memphis Grizzlies are entering a stretch of more challenging games after starting the season red hot.

Memphis (7-3) play the Denver Nuggets on Nov. 11 (7 p.m. CT, DAZN-MEM) at FedExForum. This is the first meeting between the Grizzlies and Nuggets this season.

This matchup will be a true clash of styles.

Denver (5-4) is led by three-time MVP center Nikola Jokic and point guard Jamal Murray. The duo remains one of the best in the NBA. Murray has scored at least 35 points in four of the team's nine games.

Memphis will need depth at the center position to deal with the crafty Jokic. Grizzlies center Zach Edey has been playing limited minutes while building up following an ankle procedure last March.

Two backup centers put the Grizzlies' depth in question. Quinten Post is out due to being in the concussion protocol, and Isaiah Stewart is questionable to play (right knee bone bruise).
