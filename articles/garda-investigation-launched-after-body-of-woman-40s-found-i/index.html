Garda investigation launched after body of woman, 40s, found in Wexford 

TheTools · 12.10.2026 05:45 · source · permalink

A scene was sealed off in Wexford on Sunday night as gardaí probe the death of a woman in the town.

A scene was sealed off in Wexford on Sunday night as gardaí probe the death of a woman in the town.

The woman, who was aged in her 40s, died in a house in Coolcotts in the town on Sunday morning.

Gardaí say they are “currently investigating all the circumstances” of the death.

It is understood a post mortem examination is expected to take place on Monday. 

The house has been sealed off and the local coroner has been notified.

A garda statement said on Sunday night that no further information is available at this time.
