Toy Story 5 Arrives on Disney+ September 23 — Here's What to Know

TheTools · 19.09.2026 15:52 · source · permalink

To infinity and beyond and straight to your living room! Toy Story 5 is hitting Disney+ on September 23, 2026, and if you missed it in theaters or just want to watch it again from the couch, the wait is almost over.

The fifth installment in the beloved Pixar franchise brings a fun new wrinkle to the toy world: a frog-shaped tablet named Lilypad arrives at Bonnie’s house and starts disrupting everything the traditional toys know about playtime. Woody, Buzz, Jessie, and the whole gang have to figure out what their role looks like in a world that is increasingly run by tech. It is the kind of story that feels timely without losing any of that classic Toy Story heart.

Tom Hanks is back as Woody, Tim Allen returns as Buzz Lightyear, Joan Cusack reprises Jessie, and Tony Hale is back as Forky. The new additions are equally exciting Greta Lee voices Lilypad, Conan O’Brien plays a tech toy called Smarty Pants, Craig Robinson voices Atlas the GPS hippo, and Bad Bunny makes a cameo as Pizza with Sunglasses.pa The film is directed by Andrew Stanton and Kenna Harris, and Taylor Swift contributed an original country song called “I Knew It, I Knew You” to the soundtrack.

Mark your calendars Toy Story 5 arrives on Disney+ September 23, 2026. Will you be watching? Let us know in the comments!
