Well, it's about damn time, Netflix. Four years ago, the streaming giant delivered what turned out to be one of the best rides of 2022 to audiences, namely, The Watcher, before walking away from the series after the conclusion of its first season. But, finally, it's on its way back to Netflix subscribers around the world.

Created by Ryan Murphy and Ian Brennan, The Watcher stars award-winners Bobby Cannavale (Boardwalk Empire, Scarpetta) and Naomi Watts (The Divergent Series: Allegiant) as a married couple that chose to move their family to a dream home in Westfield, New Jersey suburbia, but are then terrorized by letters from a stalker — unable to figure out which of their neighbors might be the culprit.

It concluded after seven episodes and with an explosive cliffhanger, but now fans of the series will get a continuation.

Netflix Finally Renews The Watcher for Season 2

Per Variety: "Netflix has announced that Season 2 of the mystery horror series is set to begin production this fall in New York. In addition, Season 1 stars Naomi Watts, Bobby Cannavale, Margo Martindale, and Isabel Gravitt will all return and reprise their respective roles. The news comes roughly four years after the first season of the series aired back in October 2022."

This news comes after several not-so-promising updates over the years.

What makes The Watcher even more bone-chilling is the fact it is loosely based upon a true story, connecting in a real way with audiences by warning that it could happen to anyone, including the ones watching The Watcher. Four years after its cliffhanger, Season 2 looks to build on the story that kept the series in the top-10 list of most-watched shows on Netflix for several weeks.

What Makes The Watcher So Popular With Netflix Viewers?

Speaking to the show's aforementioned ability to resonate with viewers, Cannavale explained how Murphy convinced him to sign on for The Watcher in the first place.

"I think Ryan Murphy, who's sort of very good at having his finger on the zeitgeist, just thought, 'You know that would be a good idea to make a show about it,'" he told People, in 2022. "But how he initially approached me about it — it was during COVID — and he was like, 'I don't know about you, man, but COVID made feel really powerless to protect my family.'

He continued: "And I think for him, it was more of an allegorical thing to tell the story in the guise of it's about a house and getting these letters, but really it's about fear, and it's about people being unable to control what they can't control," he added. "And I think that's what people respond to."

Maybe, just maybe, if Season 2 of The Watcher can repeat that success, there will be a Season 3 waiting in the bushes like, well, some sort of anonymous stalker.
