Gold prices rise for the first time in 4 days in India

TheTools · 05.09.2026 00:53 · source · permalink

Gold prices rise for the first time in 4 days in Indiashort by Ashley Paul / 12:05 pm on Thursday, 3 September, 2026The price of gold rose for the first time in four days as it closed ₹2,757 higher at ₹1,53,941 per 10 grams on Thursday. Gold prices had last closed higher on August 28, and have fallen by ₹8,394 per 10 grams since then. Meanwhile, silver also broke its three-day losing streak by rising ₹4,137 to close at ₹2,32,533 per kilogram.read more at IBJA
