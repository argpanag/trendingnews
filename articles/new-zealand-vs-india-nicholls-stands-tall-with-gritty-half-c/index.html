  1. Head to sportnation.nz or open the Sport Nation app, select the match in the available streams and turn the volume up. Note: Most live sport will be on the main stream, but some events will be exclusively on Sport Nation Extra if there's a scheduling clash.
  2. Tune into the same match on your TV (MySky) or video streaming app (SkyGo or Sky Sport Now) and turn up the volume.
  3. For cricket, pause your TV or stream when you see/hear the bowler's front foot land during their delivery stride. For rugby league or football, pause your TV or stream when you hear the referee blow the whistle for the opening kick-off.
  4. Listen to the Sport Nation stream, immediately unpause your TV or video stream when you hear the same sound (the scuff of the bowler's foot landing or referee's whistle) on the Sport Nation stream. Video and audio should now be in sync with each other, or at least very close to it.
  5. If you're using a soundbar with your TV, the Sport Nation stream can be sent to it via Bluetooth.
