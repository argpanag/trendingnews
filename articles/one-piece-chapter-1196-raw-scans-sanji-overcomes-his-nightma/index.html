One Piece Chapter 1196 raw scans: Sanji overcomes his nightmare as the fight against Killingham intensifies - AOL

TheTools · 16.10.2026 01:05 · source · permalink

One Piece Chapter 1196 raw scans are finally here, and we see that this installment is mostly focused on Sanji's battle against Killingham. More than anything, what is going to keep readers hooked about this chapter is how Sanji is about to confront and rise above his weakness that seems to have haunted him since the two-year time skip.

Titled “There’s No Worth in a Love That Longs for Death,” One Piece Chapter 1196 is going to feature a flashback to Kamabakka Kingdom, where Emporio Ivankov taught Sanji an important lesson about self-love.

Disclaimer: This article has major spoilers from One Piece Chapter 1196. Reader discretion is advised.

Sanji struggles against Killingham

One Piece Chapter 1196 begins with Sanji desperately trying to suppress the memories of his time on Kamabakka Kingdom. He tells himself to remain mentally strong and refuses to let those painful memories resurface.

However, Killingham continues attacking him, stabbing his hands and taunting him about how important his arms are to a cook. More importantly, when Sanji asks him to stop, Killingham just keeps on mocking him. He even suggests that Sanji should beg Luffy for help. However, Sanji refuses to accept the possibility of disappointing his captain.

Killingham summons Sanji's greatest nightmare

As Sanji struggles to remain conscious, Killingham uses his dream-related ability to summon his opponent's nightmare. Surprisingly, the nightmare that appears is Ivankov rather than Sanji's Germa family. Killingham admits that he does not understand why this particular figure appeared, but he immediately uses the manifestation against Sanji. The situation becomes even stranger when Killingham merges with Nightmare Ivankov, taking on a bizarre appearance featuring a kirin-like body and Ivankov's head.

Ivankov's lesson about self-love in One Piece Chapter 1196

During the flashback sequence that takes place in One Piece Chapter 1196, we see that Sanji kept on winning his fights against the inhabitants of Kamabakka Kingdom. But for some reason, he kept on sustaining unnecessary injuries, which Ivankov took notice of.

He asks Sanji whether this was either reckless or careless behaviour because he has a habit of jumping right into the face of danger without having regard for his life. Sanji counters by saying that he is dedicated to loving women and does not see the point in loving himself.

That is when Ivankov warns Sanji that constantly fighting this way will not yield anything good for him. Love should offer protection but not at the cost of self-destruction. It is clear proof that Ivankov is the one who understood Sanji's weakness the best, even when he refused to acknowledge it.

Sanji overcomes his weakness and challenges Killingham

Back in the present, Sanji in One Piece Chapter 1196 finally understands what Ivankov wanted to say. He now recognizes that if he chooses to care for himself, it does not translate to him abandoning his friends. So, now the awareness has finally set in that he could choose himself and still want to protect everyone he cares for. Basically, the core of the message was to stop treating oneself as insignificant.

As Sanji comes to a level of acceptance about this message, Ivankov in this chapter disappears, and without missing a beat, he continues to make a bold declaration that Killingham will not be able to land another hit on him. Moreover, he also criticizes this God Knight because of how they looked down on the people of Elbaph.

Finally, in this chapter, Sanji lights up a cigarette and once again lands a brutal insult on Killingham by calling him names. One Piece Chapter 1196 ends with Eiichiro Oda leaving a hint that Sanji might be about to awaken his Conqueror's Haki. While this is still unconfirmed, there are hints all over this battle that suggest that there might be developments in his Observation Haki too.

Unfortunately, fans will have to wait longer as the manga will be going on a break after the release of this chapter for a week. Overall, this chapter is important for Sanji, as he is overdue for a power boost. While the main battle against Imu has been sidelined for now, fans are still hyped to see what Oda has planned for the rest of the Straw Hat crew members.
