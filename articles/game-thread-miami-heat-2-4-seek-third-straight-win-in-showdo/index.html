GAME THREAD: Miami Heat (2-4) seek third straight win in showdown with Luka Dončić, L.A. Lakers (5-2)

TheTools · 03.11.2026 03:05 · source · permalink

The Miami Heat are heading west with some much-needed momentum as they visit the Los Angeles Lakers tonight at Crypto.com Arena, looking to extend their winning streak to three games.

After an ugly 0-4 start to the 2026-27 NBA season, Miami has finally begun finding its rhythm. The Heat followed up their first victory over the Atlanta Hawks with an impressive 145-125 win against the Indiana Pacers on Friday night.

Giannis Antetokounmpo has continued to lead the way, but the recent improvement has been about more than Miami’s biggest offseason acquisition. Bam Adebayo is beginning to find his offensive groove, while Klay Thompson is coming off his best shooting performance in a Heat uniform.

Tonight presents a considerably tougher challenge.

The Lakers enter the matchup with a 5-2 record, led by Luka Dončić, who has been putting up remarkable scoring numbers to open the season. Los Angeles is also coming off a 131-125 victory over the Toronto Raptors on Sunday, meaning the Lakers will be playing on the second night of a back-to-back.

For Miami, the matchup offers another opportunity to build chemistry between Antetokounmpo and Adebayo while testing its improving offense against one of the Western Conference’s most dangerous teams.

The Heat also have the benefit of an extra day of rest as they begin a three-game California road trip.

Can Miami keep its winning streak alive and make a statement against Dončić and the Lakers?
