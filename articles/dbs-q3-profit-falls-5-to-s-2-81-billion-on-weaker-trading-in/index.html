DBS Q3 profit falls 5% to S$2.81 billion on weaker trading income; misses forecasts

    [SINGAPORE] DBS’ net profit for its third quarter fell by 5 per cent as growth was offset by lower markets trading income amid unfavourable equity market conditions, it said on Thursday (Nov 4).

    Net profit for the three months ended Sep 30, 2026 stood at S$2.81 billion, compared with S$2.95 billion from the year-ago period.

    The earnings missed the S$2.95 billion consensus forecast in a Bloomberg survey of eight analysts.

    The lender declared a dividend of 81 Singapore cents per share for the period, comprising an ordinary dividend of S$0.66 and a capital return dividend of S$0.15.

    This is up from the S$0.75 per share paid out in the year-ago period, comprising an ordinary dividend of S$0.60 and a capital return dividend of S$0.15.

    Group net interest income for the quarter rose 4 per cent to S$3.71 billion, as hedging and strong balance sheet growth more than offset the impact of lower Sora.

    Group net interest margin was down 9 basis points (bps) to 1.87 per cent for the quarter, from 1.96 per cent in the previous corresponding period.

    For the commercial book, net fee and commission income was up 6 per cent to S$1.43 billion, while treasury customer sales and other income rose 19 per cent to S$685 million.

    Meanwhile, markets trading income was down 65 per cent to S$152 million due to unfavourable market conditions, said DBS.

    The bank’s non-performing loans (NPL) ratio was 0.9 per cent, down from 1 per cent the same-period a year ago.

    DBS CEO Tan Su Shan noted that outlook has become more uncertain amid elevated energy prices, tighter financial conditions and geopolitical risks, even as the global economy has remained resilient.

    “As we enter 2027, we will stay nimble to capture opportunities while prudently managing risks,” she said.

    In a separate filing on the Singapore Exchange, the bank also announced that Peter Seah will retire from his role as chairman of DBS’ board after 16 years in the role. He will be succeeded by current board member Anthony Lim from Jan 1, 2027.

    Shares of DBS closed 0.1 per cent lower at S$70.78 on Wednesday, before the release of the results.
