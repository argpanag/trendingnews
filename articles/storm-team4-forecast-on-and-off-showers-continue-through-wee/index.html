Storm Team4 Forecast: On-and-off showers continue through weekend

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:52 · source · permalink

Storm Team4 has the weather forecast for D.C., Maryland and Northern Virginia.

Storm Team4 Meteorologist Gary McGrady has your morning weather and a look at the 10-day forecast.

4 things to know about the weather:

  1. Unsettled pattern through the weekend
  2. No washouts
  3. Temperatures stay seasonable
  4. Improving weather by Monday

Stream NBC4 newscasts for free right here, right now.

Periods of showers and thunderstorms will affect the D.C. metro area from Friday through Saturday. Sunday looks drier for most of the day.

While there will be dry periods each day, scattered showers and thunderstorms may develop, especially Saturday afternoon through Sunday morning.

Afternoon highs will generally range from the low to mid-80s through Monday with mild overnight temperatures near 70°.

Drier air arrives late Sunday, bringing more sunshine, lower humidity, and pleasant conditions to start the new week.
