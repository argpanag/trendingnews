PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for November: Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, Atomfall, Dragon Quest III HD-2D  Remake and more

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:30 · source · permalink

Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars  Remastered join PlayStation Plus Premium. 

This month, embark on an inspiring mission of human connection in Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, assemble a one-of-a-kind crew as you explore the open sea in Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, navigate the cult-controlled ruins, natural caves, and nuclear bunkers of a green and unpleasant British countryside in Atomfall, or explore a vast world map with an abundance of captivating locations in Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake. All these titles and more are available in November’s PlayStation Plus Game Catalog lineup*. Meanwhile, Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars Remastered land into PlayStation Plus Premium. The full lineup will be available to play from November 17, unless otherwise noted.

Navigate a living environment through day and night cycles, natural disasters including earthquakes, sandstorms and forest fires, and Timefall – a rain that instantly ages everything it touches. To traverse this dangerous landscape, you’ll need to build new types of infrastructure, master specialised equipment, and confront (or evade) the otherworldly creatures that roam the fractured world.

An update to the PlayStation Plus app on PC

We wanted to let you know that from December 10, we will be retiring the PlayStation Plus app on PC.

PlayStation believes in providing high-quality experiences to our players, and we regularly review offerings to ensure we are focusing on experiences our members value most. With a very small number of eligible PlayStation Plus Premium members (less than 0.4%) using the PlayStation Plus app on PC, making this change allows us to focus on enhancing high-quality experiences that more of our members want to use. As a result, the PlayStation Plus cloud streaming app on PC will no longer be available.

This change does not affect PlayStation Plus memberships or benefits available on PS5 and PS4 consoles. We appreciate everyone who used the app on PC and will be notifying affected users directly.
