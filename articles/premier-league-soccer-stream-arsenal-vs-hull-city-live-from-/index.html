Can I livestream Arsenal vs. Hull City in the UK?

No broadcaster in the UK has the rights to show this game live because of the traditional Saturday 3 p.m. kickoff blackout. This prohibits matches from being shown in the region at that time to protect attendance throughout the English football pyramid.   

That also means that if you’re traveling in the UK, you’re unlikely to be able to watch the game as you normally would at home due to geoblocking.
