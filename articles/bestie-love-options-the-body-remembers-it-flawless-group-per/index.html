BESTie, "'Love Options,' the body remembers it"... Flawless group performance defying 13-year hiatus | STARNEWS

Girl group BESTie took the stage of a music show as a complete unit for the first time in over 4,000 days.

BESTie (Hye-yeon, Yu-ji, Da-hye, Hae-ryeong) appeared on MBC's "Show! Music Core," which aired on the 31st of last month, and performed their comeback hit "Love Options."

On that day, BESTie matched black-and-white styling with high heels and delivered their signature upbeat yet powerful performance. Their solid vocal skills, relaxed stage presence, and seamless coordination among the four members made the long hiatus seem insignificant. In particular, having matured over 13 years, they added a more refined charm, offering a different viewing experience from their past activities.

Above all, this performance carries significance in that the four members, who have been active in their respective fields, gathered again at a practice room to revive choreography they had long internalized. Previously, the members also sparked fans' delight by posting photos of themselves together at the practice room on their personal social media accounts, hinting at their upcoming music show appearance.

Recalling the moment they reunited for the first time in a long while, BESTie shared, "At first, I felt overwhelmed thinking about handling both the choreography and live vocals, but once we started practicing, I realized our bodies had remembered it." The countless hours of practice and stage performances from the past shone again, culminating in their complete group performance.

"Love Options," released in 2013, has been re-evaluated recently, centered on short-form content, and continued its reverse chart run on domestic and international music charts. This interest led to their complete reunion, the release of a choreography video, and a music show appearance on the 17th, and even their stage after over 4,000 days received enthusiastic response, demonstrating BESTie's unwavering strength.

Attention is now on what steps BESTie will take next, having revived the memories of 13 years ago through today's stage.
