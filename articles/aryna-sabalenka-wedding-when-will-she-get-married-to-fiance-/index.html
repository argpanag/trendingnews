Aryna Sabalenka Wedding: When Will She Get Married to Fiance Georgios Frangulis?

TheTools · 12.09.2026 20:42 · source · permalink

Aryna Sabalenka reached the women’s singles final at the 2026 US Open, putting herself just one win away from making history with a three-peat at Flushing Meadows.

The top-seeded Belarusian star earned her spot in the final after defeating Jessica Pegula 7-5, 6-2 in the semifinals. The win also marked her 19th consecutive victory at the tournament.

The defending champion is attempting to become the first woman to win three consecutive US Open titles since Serena Williams achieved the feat from 2012 to 2014.

On her way to the final, Sabalenka also endured a tough quarterfinal against sixth seed Linda Noskova, beating the Czech star in a dramatic 7-6 (1), 3-6, 7-6 (10-7) battle.

Today, the Belarusian faces second seed Elena Rybakina, who defeated Coco Gauff in the other semifinal.

The final also serves as a rematch of the 2026 Australian Open final, which Rybakina won in three sets.

One of Sabalenka’s biggest supporters during the tournament has been her fiancé, Georgios Frangulis, whom the tennis star got engaged to earlier in March 2026.

Fans have been curious to learn more about the star couple and their plans for the future. Sabalenka, before the US Open, also revealed something that is sure to excite her fans.

Speaking to Vogue, the 28-year-old revealed details about the moment when Frangulis popped the question. However, before describing the proposal, she also revealed details about their planned wedding.

“Now it’s planned for September next year, so we really have to start planning after US Open,” she revealed.

She then went on to describe how her fiancé first asked her about marriage.

The two-time US Open champion revealed that she was not at all prepared for the special moment and jokingly pointed out her choice of attire.

“But about the proposal, did you see how I looked? Probably not. So I was wearing a pair of jeans, beautiful jeans, but like, white t-shirt, and the flip flops. And I did like this little ugly braid. It was like after two practices, gym, and then I was doing my treatment, like with my face down on the table. I still had my marks from the table.”

With Sabalenka revealing that the wedding is planned for September next year, tennis fans now have another reason to look forward to what comes after the US Open as the star prepares to tie the knot with her longtime partner.
