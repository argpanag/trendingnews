US stocks rose on Tuesday as the AI trade, earnings, and an upcoming Fed decision remained in focus.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI) rose above the flat line, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC) climbed 1.2%. The S&P 500 (^GSPC) gained 0.7% as semiconductor stocks gained.

(^DJI )

AI stocks continued to benefit from revived enthusiasm after Anthropic's (ANTH.PVT) IPO investor prospectus, which the company filed publicly on Monday, highlighted the promise — and risks — of artificial intelligence. In Asia, the resurgent AI trade helped lift South Korean chip stocks and the KOSPI (^KS11) index.

Another catalyst for tech investors arrives Wednesday, when Alphabet (GOOG) and Microsoft (MSFT) report their third quarter results. In the meantime, on Tuesday, investors digested better-than-expected earnings from Coca-Cola (KO) and fintech SoFi Technologies Inc. (SOFI).

Anticipation also continues to build ahead of the Federal Reserve's interest rate decision on Wednesday. Policymakers began their two-day meeting on Tuesday to discuss whether hotter-than-expected inflation warrants a rate hike. Investors aren't convinced the Fed will move, pricing in just 10% odds of a hike, per CME Group.

  • Oracle, in effort to reduce reliance on Nvidia, adds AI chips from Google, Cerebras, and SambaNova

    Yahoo Finance's Dan Howley reports:

    Oracle (ORCL) is diversifying its AI cloud infrastructure, signing deals to bring chips from Google (GOOG, GOOGL), Cerebras (CBRS), and SambaNova (SANS.PVT) onto its networks as it looks to diversify beyond Nvidia's processors.

    The move marks the first time Google is selling its Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) for use within a third-party data center rather than simply renting capacity to companies.

  • Oil prices ease on hopes of a deescalation in Middle East conflict

    Oil prices eased on Tuesday as markets priced in the possibility of a deescalation in the Middle East conflict.

    Brent crude oil futures (BZ=F) eased to trade below $110 per barrel while WTI (CL=F) also declined to around $100 as Fed policymakers initiated a two-day policy rate meeting.

  • UPS stock jumps on quarterly results, CEO says 'outlook for holiday demand is strong'

    United Parcel Service (UPS) stock rose 2% on Tuesday after the courier posted third quarter results and raised its full-year guidance, signaling solid demand during the upcoming holiday season.

    Revenue is expected to come in at $92.6 billion this year, up from a prior forecast of $91.2 billion. UPS also increased its adjusted earnings guidance.

    Despite inflation pressures and high diesel costs, UPS is optimistic heading into the last couple of months of the year.

    The courier is in the middle of a multiyear turnaround plan aimed at cost-cutting and reconfiguring its delivery network.
