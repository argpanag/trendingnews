Applications for enrolment in electoral roll for MLC poll allowed after Nov 6: T'gana CEO

Depth, context and analysis

Hyderabad, Nov 3 (PTI) Telangana Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) C Sudharshan Reddy on Tuesday said applications for enrolment in the electoral roll for the upcoming MLC poll can still be submitted after the November 6 deadline.

The CEO said in a release that the draft electoral rolls for the poll from Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda Graduates’ constituency and Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad Graduates’ constituency would be published on November 25.

“In order to avoid confusion among the citizens, it is hereby clarified that applications in Form-18 can be submitted up to 6/11/2026; during the period for filing of Claims and Objections from 25/11/2026 to 10/12/2026; and also, from the date of final publication of electoral rolls – from 30/12/2026 to the last date of filing of nominations for the Graduates’ constituencies,” it said.

As per the schedule given by the Election Commission for de-novo preparation of electoral rolls for the two constituencies, the last date for receipt of applications in Form 18 is November 6, it said.

The final publication of electoral rolls would be on December 30.

The biennial election to the Council from the two Graduates’ constituencies would be held in March next year. The date of the election is yet to be announced. PTI SJR SJR ROH
