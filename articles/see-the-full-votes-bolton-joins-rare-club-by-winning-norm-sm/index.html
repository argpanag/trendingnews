See the full votes: Bolton joins rare club by winning Norm Smith in losing team

TheTools · 26.09.2026 22:32 · source · permalink

Fremantle midfielder Shai Bolton has won the Norm Smith Medal

FREMANTLE star Shai Bolton has become just the fifth player in the history of the Norm Smith Medal – and the first in 21 years – to win the coveted best on ground award in a losing Grand Final.

Bolton adds his name to legendary figures Chris Judd (West Coast, 2005), Nathan Buckley (Collingwood, 2002), Gary Ablett snr (Geelong, 1989) and Maurice Rioli (Richmond, 1982). 

GRAND FINAL Full match coverage and stats

The two-time premiership Tiger starred in his first decider for the Dockers, finishing with 26 disposals – most on the ground for Fremantle – 10 contested possessions, nine score involvements, nine inside 50s, seven marks and five tackles. 

In one of the most evenly battled Norm Smith voting games in recent history, the man who won the previous two – Will Ashcroft – finished equal second alongside Brisbane veteran Dayne Zorko. 

Zorko and Bolton both polled three votes from two of the five judges, while Ashcroft collected votes from three different judges. 

Bolton polled votes from each of Andrew McLeod, Shaun Burgoyne, Chris Judd, Michael Voss and Lauren Wood.

The 27-year-old reached half-time with 13 disposals to his name when the scores were level, finishing with eight touches in a tense final quarter to do everything to push the Dockers to a maiden premiership. 

But it just wasn't enough, even despite the game looking almost over when Wharfie Time was activated with four minutes left after Pat Voss put the Dockers 17 points up 22 minutes into the fourth quarter.

"To be honest, it's a bit hard," Bolton said.

"You don't really play (for individual honours), you play to win premierships.

"I'm honoured to get the Norm Smith, but I'm gutted for our boys.

"We didn't get the job done today, but we'll see them next year."

The South Fremantle product is proving to be a sensational recruit since crossing from Richmond at the end of 2024 in a deal that saw the Dockers send three first-round picks to Punt Road in exchange for Bolton and a first-round pick and a future third-round pick. 

Bolton was named All-Australian for the second time last month after first collecting a blazer in 2022. 

Bolton will return to Western Australia without the medal he wanted when he flew to Melbourne on Wednesday, but after producing the best season of his career to date.

Fremantle coach Justin Longmuir said Bolton was a deserving Norm Smith medallist.

"He's probably emulated his whole final series," Longmuir said.

"He's been in this position a lot, and I think that experience showed today his not only ability to win the ball, but then make sure it either gets to the outside through his own endeavours or feeding it to others on the outside.

"He's had a super final series, and today was a big part of that. We had a lot of winners on the day. I thought most of our players can hold their heads up high with the way they performed, and clearly Shai was one of them.

"It's a credit to him the way he's performed across the whole final series, and I thought it was well deserved, the medal today."
