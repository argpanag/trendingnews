NEA Orders Sadia Supermarket To Stop Selling Red Bull Without Deposit Labels

TheTools · 21.11.2026 06:35 · source · permalink
  • News

The National Environment Agency (NEA) has ordered Sadia Supermarket to immediately stop selling Red Bull drinks without the required Beverage Container Return Scheme (BCRS) deposit labels.

The retailer, located at Tuas South Recreation Centre, has complied and removed all affected drinks.

Supermarket Did Not Apply For Grace Period

NEA said in a Facebook statement on 21 November that Red Bull beverages imported and sold by Sadia Supermarket lacked the required deposit mark, 8world reported.

The agency instructed the supermarket to stop selling the unlabelled drinks immediately and gave it a stern reminder about the offences involved. Sadia had not applied for the earlier grace period to clear unlabelled stock, NEA said.

NEA had allowed eligible smaller retailers to apply for extra time to clear drinks without BCRS labels. Approved retailers could continue selling their remaining stock until 31 October 2026.

The arrangement came with conditions. Retailers granted an extension were told not to bring in more unlabelled stock and to clearly identify the drinks to customers. Those drinks were not subject to the 10-cent deposit.

Other Registered Products Remain Unaffected

NEA stressed that the matter concerns Red Bull beverages imported and sold by Sadia Supermarket. Other products registered under the BCRS are unaffected.

Consumers can continue returning eligible empty bottles and cans at reverse vending machines to get back their 10-cent deposit.

The scheme entered full implementation on 1 October 2026, following a six-month transition period from 1 April to 30 September.

It covers pre-packaged drinks in plastic bottles and metal cans with capacities from 150ml to three litres. Containers covered by the scheme must bear the deposit mark.
