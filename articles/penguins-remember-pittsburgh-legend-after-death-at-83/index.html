The Pittsburgh Penguins shared a heartfelt post on social media on Monday to remember a legend of the Steel City.

Donnie Iris has died. He was 83.

This is what the Penguins shared on their X account:

If the visual doesn't show up properly — it's of Iris singing the national anthem in a Penguins sweater at a Penguins game.

Donnie Iris Bio

Donnnie Iris was originally from Ellwood City, Pennsylvania.

He's best known for the song "Ah! Leah!"

Iris had been diagnosed with bladder cancer in 2023, but he kept performing until very recently.

Iris announced his retirement less than a month ago, on Sept. 28.

"After much thought, and in light of recent health issues, I have made the difficult decision to retire from future concert performances," Iris wrote on social media after his final show. "Music has given me far more than I could ever put into words. From the earliest days to every chapter of my life, your support has been a constant source of joy and inspiration."

Iris was a part of multiple bands in his career.

He wrote and sang the No. 1 song "The Rapper" in 1970.

He then joined Wild Cherry, where he met Mark Avsec to form Donnie Iris & The Cruisers. Their album "Back On The Streets" included that famous song "Ah! Leah!"

CBS News shared this additional information:

Iris was christened the "new king of cool" by a reviewer after a show in 1980, and from that name, the follow-up album "King Cool" was born. The album had songs "Love Is Like A Rock," "That's The Way Love Ought To Be" and "My Girl."

During their time as a band, Donnie Iris & The Cruisers toured with names like Journey, Loverboy, Foreigner, Bon Jovi, Joan Jett and Hall & Oates.
