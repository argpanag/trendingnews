A beloved horror classic is finding new life in the world of streaming. It may even be a sign that The Craft deserves another sequel.

Per FlixPatrol, The Craft has officially managed to crack the top ten list of the most watched movies on HBO Max in the United States. While The Craft hasn't managed to make it past the tenth place spot on the list yet, the fact that it has returned to the charts at this point is a testament to the film's lasting popularity with audiences.

Why Audiences Can't Get Enough of The Craft

Directed by Andrew Fleming from a screenplay by creator Peter Filardi and Andrew Fleming, The Craft stars Robin Tunney as Sarah Bailey, a high schooler who moves from San Francisco to Los Angeles and soon befriends a trio of local girls rumored to be witches. Before long, Sarah discovers just how more powerful she and her new friends are together than they ever were apart.

Alongside Fairuza Balk's Nancy, Rachel True's Rochelle, and Neve Campbell's Bonnie, Sarah uses her newfound abilities to her own benefit, only for things to go horribly wrong for each of the young witches. As tensions mount and bodies pile up, Sarah finds herself fighting for her life against the very same clique that so quickly embraced her as one of their own.

Upon its release in 1996, The Craft was a massive hit with audiences, grossing more than $55.6 million at the box office against a budget of just $15 million. Perhaps unsurprisingly, The Craft received generally middling reviews from critics, with favorable comparisons to the likes of Heathers being hampered by complaints over its unsteady thematic storytelling and occasionally excessive special effects.

What the Future Could Look Like for The CraftIn 2020, writer-director Zoe Lister-Jones'The Craft: Legacy was released in theaters, serving as the long-awaited follow-up to the original cult classic.The Craft: Legacy stars Cailee Spaeny as Lilith "Lily" Schechner, a young woman who, like Sarah before her, moves to a new town and finds fast friends in a trio of budding witches.

The Craft: Legacy features a star-studded ensemble cast led by Fideon Adlon, Lovie Simone, and Zoey Luna as young witches Frankie, Tabby, and Lourdes, as well as Nicholas Galitzine, Michelle Monaghan, and David Duchovny. The Craft: Legacy also features a cameo appearance by original The Craft star Fairuza Balk in a reprisal of her iconic role as Nancy Downs.

Unfortunately for fans, The Craft: Legacy was an absolute dud in theaters, grossing just $2.3 million against an $18 million production budget. The sequel was also widely bemoaned by both critics and audiences, with The Craft: Legacy currently holding a 47% "rotten" rating via review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, as well as a 24% audience-generated "Popcornmeter" rating on the platform.

Even after the negative response to The Craft: Legacy, fans remain hopeful for a third entry in the series. Ahead of the film's release, Lister-Jones opened up about having written The Craft: Legacy with plans for a third film in mind, explaining that the next The Craft would revolve around an intergenerational story with stars from both previous films taking center stage alongside one another.

The Craft is available for streaming on HBO Max.
