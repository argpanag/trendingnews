Blazers’ Injury Report Remains Unchanged for First Matchup With T-Wolves

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:49 · source · permalink
  • Blazers Injury Report

The same four players will be out for Monday night’s game.

The Portland Trail Blazers have released the injury report ahead of their first matchup against the Minnesota Timberwolves on the road Monday night, with no new updates to report. The same four Blazers players who missed Portland’s 120-107 win over Sacramento on Friday — Ja Morant, Vit Krejci, Chris Youngblood and Shaedon Sharpe — will once again be out in Minnesota.

Here’s the full report:

INJURY REPORT 11/16 @ MIN:

OUT
Vit Krejci (L Ankle Fracture)
Ja Morant (L Hamstring Strain)
Shaedon Sharpe (R Meniscus)
Chris Youngblood (R Foot Bone Bruise)

INJURY REPORT 11/16 @ MIN:

OUT
Vit Krejci (L Ankle Fracture)
Ja Morant (L Hamstring Strain)
Shaedon Sharpe (R Meniscus)
Chris Youngblood (R Foot Bone Bruise)

The Blazers are scheduled to tip off against the Timberwolves on Monday at 5 p.m. PST. Then Portland will meet the Timberwolves again in Minnesota on Tuesday at the same time.

One thing to monitor in this two-game series against Minnesota will be if the Trail Blazers elect to play reserve center Robert Williams III in both games of the back-to-back. Williams, who has typically sat out at least one leg of back-to-backs as a precautionary measure to manage his health, played in both games of a back-to-back last week for the first time since 2022 and for his first time as a Trail Blazer.

As of the latest Timberwolves injury report submitted to the NBA, Minnesota star Anthony Edwards and guards Donte DiVincenzo and Jaylen Clark will be out for Monday’s contest.
