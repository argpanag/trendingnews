Al Pacino to be honored for his career achievements at the Actor Awards

TheTools · 17.10.2026 22:02 · source · permalink

Al Pacino will become the 62nd honoree of the SAG-AFTRA Life Achievement Award, the American actors’ union’s highest honor. The announcement was made on October 15, 2026. He will receive the prize at the 33rd Actor Awards ceremony on February 28, 2027, at the Shrine Auditorium & Expo Hall in Los Angeles, i.e., March 1 at 2:00 a.m. Paris time.

This award honors the entirety of a career and the humanitarian commitments of its recipient. It transcends a single performance or a season’s TV. For Al Pacino, it acknowledges more than six decades of work—from cinema to television, not forgetting the stage. His track record already includes an Oscar, two Tony Awards, two Emmys, and two Actor Awards.

SAG-AFTRA also highlights its commitment to disaster-relief efforts and the fight against poverty. The union reiterates its pledge to the Shakespeare Center of Los Angeles: theatre performances and charity shows are also part of the journey this tribute aims to celebrate.
