What are the benefits of a sunrise alarm clock?

Light is one of the biggest triggers for your body clock. A lack of light signals it's time to sleep, while bright lights help you feel alert. It's a reason why many of us (including me) find it easier to wake up in summer, when the sun has naturally risen by the time you're getting out of bed.

In winter, however, dark mornings often lead to sleep inertia. Instead of being slowly woken by the rising sun, you're jolted awake by an alarm screeching in the middle of your sleep cycle.

There are two potential solutions. First, you can shift your wake up until the sun naturally rises. Appealing, but potentially disruptive and on some overcast days, you might never get out of bed.

Second, a sunrise alarm clock. The Hatch Sleep Clock steps up when the sun isn't around. It lights up slowly (over a preset period) meaning when the built-in alarm starts to chime, you've already received key signals that it's time to get going. And instead of blinking awake in darkness, you open your eyes to a well lit room.

Convenient and comfortable sleep tracking

The Hatch Sleep Clock adds an additional feature: contactless sleep tracking. I think it's one of the best sleep trackers you can buy for convenient insights into your sleep.

I'll level with you. The Sleep Clock won't give the deep insight, minute-by-minute metrics, and all-round health data of something like the Oura Ring 5.

However, the user-friendly interface is super accessible for anyone looking to learn about their sleep for the first time, while contactless tracking is both more comfortable than wearables and means no charging.

Plus, there's no subscription required. So you don't have to worry about your data being paywalled. At it's lowest price ever of just $187.49, the Sleep Clock is also competitively priced for a tracker, making now the time to shop.
