SA vs BAN: Temba Bavuma Left Out As Proteas Announce Playing XI For 2nd Test

TheTools · 23.11.2026 04:52 · source · permalink

Temba Bavuma has been left out of the South Africa playing XI for their second Test against Bangladesh. The Proteas announced Tony de Zorzi as his replacement in the squad. The second Test begins on the 23rd of November, 2026 in Centurion.

The successful Protea skipper Temba Bavuma has once again been left out of the South Africa squad for his injury as the Proteas announce its playing XI for their second Test against Bangladesh which is all set to begin on the 23rd of November, 2026 at the SuperSport Park in Centurion. Bavuma has been ruled out due to a right calf strain that he sustained during the first Test in Johannesburg. Tony de Zorzi has been named as his replacement.

During the ongoing first Test in Johannesburg against Bangladesh Bavuma sustained a strain on his right calf muscle while batting in South Africa’s first innings. Eventually, he had to leave the field being retired hurt on 49. But he came back later in the innings to finish on an unbeaten 68. After that he did not step up on to the field for Bangladesh's second innings and hence was ruled out of the second and final Test. Initially, all rounder Senuran Muthuswamy was called in the squad as a replacement for Bavuma.

But eventually before the beginning of the second Test the Proteas announced Tony de Zorzi as the replacement for Temba Bavuma in the playing XI so that he can bat in the middle order. Anrich Nortje is also returning in the second Test from his injury to light up the Protea pace attack by replacing seamer Dane Paterson in Centurion. Due to the same calf injury, Bavuma has also been left out of South Africa's squads for the upcoming white-ball series against Bangladesh, where Keshav Maharaj will captain the ODI side.

Aiden Markram will lead the Proteas in the second Test. South Africa already hold a 1-0 lead in the two match series after they defeated Bangladesh on Day 5 in Johannesburg. It marked just the third time in their Test history they won a match after conceding 400+ runs in the first innings. The reigning World Test Championship title-holders sit at the top of the WTC standings and are aiming for a 2–0 series sweep.

Bavuma frustrated on his injuries

Temba Bavuma expressed his frustration of getting injured again and again in the post match press conference after the 1st Test. “I'm obviously frustrated that I find myself in this situation. With that being said, it's a big part of my game, I guess. I will continue managing it under the supervision of the medical team. I guess it's a nice maybe little reset for me to just take stock of where I am as a batter. What are the things that I'm doing well?,” he said.

“What are the things that the team is doing well? And then probably I'll have a little bit of a forward view of what's to come in December in that challenge. It's something that's been happening regularly and I'm kind of starting to get used to it. I guess no one is surprised that Temba's injured again, but I won't say it's something that I'm expecting with every game. I have a way of dealing with it mentally,” Bavuma added.

South Africa Playing XI for the 2nd Test

Aiden Markram (C), Marques Ackerman, Zubayr Hamza, Tony De Zorzi, Matthew Breetzke, Ryan Rickelton, Wiaan Mulder, Marco Jansen, Simon Harmer, Kagiso Rabada, Anrich Nortje
