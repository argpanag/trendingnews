Picks And Predictions For Week 4 vs. the Los Angeles Chargers

TheTools · 04.10.2026 19:57 · source · permalink

The Seattle Seahawks take on the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 4 at Lumen Field.

The Seattle Seahawks face the Chargers on October 4 at Lumen Field as part of Week 4 of the 2026 NFL season. Kickoff is set for 1:25 p.m. PT. Here's what local and national media are predicting:

Mike Florio, NBC Sports: Seahawks 27, Chargers 13

"There's plenty of Harbaugh DNA in this one, literally and figuratively. That won't matter much, because the Seahawks got an unexpected wakeup call against the Commanders."

Vic Tafur, The Athletic: Seahawks

"The Chargers made a stand last week but still lost against the Bills to fall to 0-3. Seattle is a tough place to make another stand, especially with the champs coming off a loss."

Pete Prisco, CBS Sports: Seahawks 30, Chargers 20

"The Chargers are 0-3 and coming off a long trip in losing to the Bills. The Seahawks were upset last week by the Commanders. That will get their attention, which won't be a good thing for the Chargers. Look for that Seattle defense to come up big against Justin Herbert."

Pamela Maldonado, EPSN: Seahawks 20, Chargers 17

Eric Moody, EPSN: Seahawks 28, Chargers 21

Seth Walder, EPSN: Seahawks 26, Chargers 23

Jeremy Bergman, NFL.com: Seahawks 28, Chargers 19

Brooke Cersosimo, NFL.com: Seahawks 30, Chargers 24

Gennaro Filice, NFL.com: Seahawks 27, Chargers 15

Bobby Kownack, NFL.com: Seahawks 25, Chargers 17

Dan Parr, NFL.com: Seahawks 26, Chargers 17
