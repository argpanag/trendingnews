Heavy to very heavy rainfall is likely to continue in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal on Saturday, October 24, as the northeast monsoon remains active over southern India.

Kerala and Mahe are also likely to receive very heavy rain, while parts of Karnataka and Rayalaseema may see heavy showers, according to the India Meteorological Department (IMD).

The IMD has forecast heavy rain at isolated places over South Interior Karnataka and Rayalaseema, while the Andaman Islands are likely to receive light to moderate rainfall at most places, with heavy showers at isolated locations.

Squally weather is expected over the Andaman Sea and adjoining areas, with winds reaching 45–55 kmph and gusting to 65 kmph.

Meanwhile, a low-pressure area over the east-central and adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a depression on Saturday, a deep depression on Sunday and a cyclonic storm by the morning of October 26.

The system is likely to move west-northwestwards towards the north Andhra Pradesh and adjoining south Odisha coasts over the following two days.

The developing system is expected to bring heavy rainfall to parts of eastern India in the coming days. Odisha could receive heavy to very heavy rain from October 27, with extremely heavy rainfall possible at isolated places on October 28.

North Andhra Pradesh is also likely to experience heavy to very heavy rainfall, with extremely heavy showers possible at isolated places on October 27 and 28.

The IMD has advised fishermen at sea to return to the coast by Saturday morning and not venture into the east-central and adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal as conditions deteriorate.

North, central and western India are expected to remain largely dry, with no significant weather activity forecast over most of these regions.
