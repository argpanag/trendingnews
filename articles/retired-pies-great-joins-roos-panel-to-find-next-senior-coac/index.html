Retired Pies great joins Roos panel to find next senior coach

TheTools · 09.09.2026 03:58 · source · permalink

North Melbourne has revealed the six-person panel that will decide on the club's next senior coach, and a former Magpies star was a surprise addition

RECENTLY retired Collingwood premiership star Steele Sidebottom will have a say on who will become North Melbourne's next senior coach after the Kangaroos unveiled their selection panel on Tuesday.

Sidebottom's 375-game AFL career came to an end less than a fortnight ago when Collingwood lost its wildcard finals match to the Western Bulldogs in an after-the-siren thriller.

The 35-year-old has been named on a six-person selection panel to find the next Kangaroos coach following the club's drama-charged decision to sack Alastair Clarkson with one year left on his contract.

Sidebottom will be joined on the panel by former North Melbourne captain Andrew Swallow, Kangaroos chief executive Jennifer Watt, football manager Todd Viney, AFLW coach Darren Crocker and vice-president Nick Dowling.

North Melbourne says it is seeking candidates who have recent and relevant AFL coaching experience from successful environments and who can take the Kangaroos to their next premiership. 

The Kangaroos were widely panned for the manner in which they sacked Clarkson.

Watch the full media conference as North Melbourne president Dr Sonja Hood and football boss Todd Viney speak after the sacking of Alastair Clarkson

The four-time premiership coach said North had danced on his grave and publicly humiliated him and his family in the way they explained the reasons behind his sacking.

Those reasons included North wanting to seek out a coach who can keep up with the modern game.

Viney also said there were some gaping holes in the club's gameplan under Clarkson, and that when Clarkson went into overdrive in an attempt to fix those issues, it led to "more meetings, more information, and players ... becoming a bit more confused".

Premiership coaches John Longmire and Adam Simpson have been floated as options to take over from Clarkson due to their stature as champion former North players.

But as reported by AFL.com.au's Damian Barrett, there has been a strong belief among key decision makers at North Melbourne that it is time to employ an untried coach. 

Jaymie Graham (Fremantle) is one of the most highly-rated assistant coaches in the game, while Corey Enright (St Kilda) and James Kelly (Geelong) were also in contention for the Carlton job before Fraser's hot results as interim boss made him the only choice for the Blues.
