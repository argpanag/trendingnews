The leaves are covering the ground and the holidays are creeping ever closer, which means it’s time to settle in with a fresh wave of shows on Hulu and Disney+. This November brings an exciting lineup to the streaming service siblings, led by a splashy returning favorite, a lavish new K-drama and a long-awaited chapter of a fan-favorite fantasy series.

Kickstarting the month on Nov. 4, season 2 of Ryan Murphy’s “All’s Fair” premieres on Hulu. The campy courtroom drama reunites Kim Kardashian, Glenn Close, Sarah Paulson, and the rest of the all-female divorce firm for another round of high-stakes scandals, secrets, and stilettos. Dropping that same day on Disney+ and Hulu is “The Remarried Empress,” a live-action K-drama adaptation of the wildly popular webtoon about an empress who boldly rewrites her own destiny after her husband brings home a new concubine.

Later in the month on Nov. 20, “Percy Jackson and the Olympians” returns for its third season. Adapting Rick Riordan’s “The Titan’s Curse,” this new installment follows Percy and his friends as they race against time to rescue a kidnapped Annabeth and save Olympus from the encroaching Titans. Want to see what else is hitting your watchlist this month? Keep reading to explore our top picks and the full list of everything coming to Hulu and Disney+ in November.

New on Hulu and Disney+ in November 2026: Top picks

'All's Fair' season 2

Ryan Murphy’s campy courtroom saga returns for a second season, back inside the all-female divorce firm of Grant, Ronson & Greene. After the attorneys split from their old male-led practice to strike out on their own, season 2 drops them into a fresh storm of scandals, secrets and stilettos, as new allegiances form, friendships are tested and a shocking revelation threatens the very existence of the firm. You know, no pressure or whatever.

The same group from season 1 is back, including Kim Kardashian, Naomi Watts, Niecy Nash-Betts, Teyana Taylor, Sarah Paulson and Glenn Close, with Cauã Reymond joining the cast this season.

Stream on Hulu beginning November 4

'The Remarried Empress'

This lavish Korean period drama, based on the hugely popular webtoon, follows Navier, the beloved and impossibly composed empress of the Eastern Empire, who has built a prosperous land alongside her husband, Emperor Sovieshu. Her world is turned upside down when Sovieshu brings Rashta, a runaway slave, back to the palace with plans to make her his concubine.

Rather than let heartbreak rule her, Navier keeps her composure, and when Prince Heinrey, a charming rival leader from the Western Kingdom, takes an interest in her, she makes a life-changing choice to rewrite her own destiny. Shin Min-a stars as Navier, with Lee Jong-suk as Heinrey, Ju Ji-hoon as Sovieshu and Lee Se-young as Rashta. You've got plenty of time to catch up with the webtoon if you start reading now.

Stream on Hulu beginning November 4

'Percy Jackson and the Olympians' season 3

Disney+’s hit adaptation of Rick Riordan’s beloved book series returns for a third season, this time bringing “The Titan’s Curse,” the third novel, to the screen for the first time. When Annabeth (Leah Sava Jeffries) suddenly vanishes and the Oracle of Delphi delivers a Great Prophecy that could decide the fate of Olympus, Percy Jackson (Walker Scobell) and Grover (Aryan Simhadri) set off on a perilous new quest, racing to rescue Annabeth and the goddess Artemis (Dafne Keen) from the clutches of the Titan Atlas (Holt McCallany).

Expect new allies, including the di Angelo siblings, and a noticeably darker turn for the young demigods -- perfect for scratching that YA fantasy itch if you're a big reader or have kids in the house who can't get enough of Jackson.

Stream on Disney+ beginning November 20
