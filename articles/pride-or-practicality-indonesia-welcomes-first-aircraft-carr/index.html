Pride or practicality? Indonesia welcomes first aircraft carrier

TheTools · 18.09.2026 07:36 · source · permalink

Jakarta has approved US$450 million for the aircraft carrier programme but some lawmakers warn the final bill could be higher

The 41-year-old former ITS Giuseppe Garibaldi, with capacity for short take-off and vertical landing aircraft and helicopters, was welcomed with a military ceremony upon its arrival at a naval base in North Jakarta’s Tanjung Priok seaport.

“We intend to use the ship primarily for military operations other than war, although it can also be deployed for combat missions if necessary,” Indonesian Navy chief Admiral Muhammad Ali told reporters after the ceremony. He said the carrier could serve the navy for another 15 to 20 years and could accommodate 14 helicopters or more, depending on their size.
