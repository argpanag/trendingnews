Christopher Eccleston and Carole Ann Ford to star in the Ninth Doctor's Big Finish Christmas special | Doctor Who

TheTools · 14.10.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

Products

October 08, 2026

The Ninth Doctor and Susan bring Christmas to the Doctor Who YouTube channel in a new audio adventure...

Christopher Eccleston and Carole Ann Ford star together for the first time in The Ninth Doctor Adventures: The Wash, a special full-cast audio drama streaming free on the official Doctor Who YouTube channel this Christmas Day.

Written by Robert Valentine, The Wash reunites two generations of the Doctor's family – the Ninth Doctor himself and his granddaughter Susan – in a story that begins, improbably, in a launderette on the Powell Estate. Avoiding Jackie Tyler's early Christmas preparations, the Doctor heads to The Wash Inn; there, alongside unexpected dangers, he finds himself in conversation with a voice from the past. 

The Wash will stream live on the official Doctor Who YouTube channel on Tuesday 22nd December, giving fans the chance to listen along together. It will remain free to stream for one month afterwards – part of a selection box of Doctor Who treats from Big Finish arriving over the Christmas period, with further announcements to come.

When Rose and Jackie start early Christmas prep, the Doctor opts to do the laundry. The Wash Inn is a hub and haven for lonely souls, and who is more lonely than the last of the Time Lords?

But why has the memory of his granddaughter, Susan, begun to trouble him? What terrible force lurks in an innocent washing machine? And is there enough Brite White in the world to deal with it? It will all come out in the Wash…

Big Finish listeners will also have the chance to purchase this special adventure as a digital download and as a collector’s edition CD, now available to pre-order exclusively from the Big Finish website.

Christopher Eccleston said: “This was a brilliant, really outstanding script, I thought. I love that notion that as we go through our lives, particularly if you’re a Time Lord, everybody you meet remains with you.

“And working with Carole was really exciting. I’d been looking forward to it. I think that An Unearthly Child is the greatest episode ever made – when I made the TV series, that was the one I watched as inspiration. And she’s a pro, she was absolutely brilliant – a real honour.”

Carole Ann Ford added: “Acting opposite Chris was wonderful, marvellous. It’s such a strong story. Susan pushes this Doctor to try and sort himself out and not be so touchy with people. I think listeners will be cheering Susan on!”

Doctor Who – The Ninth Doctor Adventures: The Wash is now available to pre-order from bigfinish.com.
