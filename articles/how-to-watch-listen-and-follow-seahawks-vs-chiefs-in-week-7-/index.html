How To Watch, Listen And Follow Seahawks vs. Chiefs In Week 7 On Sunday, October 25

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:55 · source · permalink

Time, TV, radio, live stream, and everything else you need to know to follow the Week 7 action vs. the Kansas City Chiefs at Lumen Field.

The Seattle Seahawks face the Kansas City Chiefs on October 25, 2026 at Lumen Field as part of Week 7 of the 2026 season. Kickoff is set for 5:20 p.m. PT. Here's everything you need to know to follow the action:

Game Center | Buy Tickets | NFL+

The Matchup: Seahawks vs. Kansas City Chiefs

How To Tune In:

  • TV: NBC
  • Radio: Seattle Sports 710AM, KIRO Newsradio 97.3 FM, Seahawks Mobile App (for fans in the Seattle-area)
  • Stream: NFL+
  • International:

* Listen Online: Seahawks Mobile App

Watch On TV: NBC

Seahawks vs. Kansas City Chiefs will be carried on NBC. Mike Tirico will call the play-by-play while Cris Collinsworth provides color analysis with Melissa Stark reporting from the sidelines.

Listen On Radio: Seattle Sports 710AM, KIRO Newsradio 97.3 FM, Westwood One (National), Sirius XM Channels 85 or 192 and Seahawks Mobile App

On the radio, you can hear the Seahawks on Seattle Sports 710AM, KIRO Newsradio 97.3 FM and in the Seahawks Mobile App. Steve Raible and Dave Wyman will call the action with Jen Mueller as the sideline reporter.

On the national radio, Westwood One, Ryan Radtke and Mike Golic will call the action.

Listen Online: Seahawks Mobile App

For fans in the local Seattle market and international fans in Canada, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Australia and New Zealand, the game will be available to watch on mobile devices via the Seahawks Mobile App and Seahawks.com.

For international fans, NFL Game Pass is the ultimate way to watch the NFL around the world*, with options for every NFL fan out there for the 2026 Regular Season, the Playoffs and Super Bowl LXI.

*Geographic and device restrictions apply. Local & primetime games only. Please check your local TV listings to confirm availability. iOS mobile web and app users should "Allow Location Access" (Settings > Privacy > Location Services > Safari). Data charges may apply.

Seahawks.com, the Seahawks Mobile App, and Seahawks social media channels will provide game coverage throughout the day. Follow the Seahawks on Instagram, Twitter/X, and Facebook for live gameday updates, videos and behind-the-scenes photos.
