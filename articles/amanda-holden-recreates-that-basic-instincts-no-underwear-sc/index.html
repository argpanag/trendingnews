Amanda Holden recreates THAT Basic Instincts 'no underwear' scene in mini dress

TheTools · 29.10.2026 12:26 · source · permalink

Amanda Holden recreated the iconic scene from Basic Instinct (Image: noholdenback/Instagram)

Amanda Holden treated Instagram fans to a recreation of the famous no underwear scene from Basic Instincts. In the 1992 Hollywood classic, Sharon Stone uncrosses and re-crosses her legs to reveal she is not wearing underwear while being interrogated by police.

The scene became one of the most famous and discussed moments in cinema history – and Amanda isn't letting the discussion stop as she herself donned a white mini dress similar to Stone's. In the erotic thriller, Stone donned a skin-tight white satin mini dress complete with a high neck and asymmetric hemline.

Amanda, on the other hand, wore a white long sleeve mini dress, which showed off her long legs. She completed the look with a face full of glamorous make up and wore her hair styled down.

Amanda Holden channelled her inner Sharon Stone (Image: noholdenback/Instagram)

In her video, the Britain's Got Talent judge held a pen to imitate smoking a cigarette. She also crossed and uncrossed her legs, giving her followers a tease.

She said Stone's line "It's nice". The clip from the movie was played below her version and the post was captioned: "Some call it Basic Instinct, I call it outfit of the day".

Amanda tagged the dress brand, Odd Muse. The dress is available to buy online for £135. Fans were left delighted with the video, with several taking to shower her comment section with compliments.

Amanda crossed and uncrossed her legs...

...just like Sharon Stone in the film

One wrote: "You legend". Another said: "You look beautiful Amanda". A third particularly excited fan noted: "This woman always looks fabulous wow". A fourth said: "Gorgeous dress you look stunning".

A fifth exclaimed: "Why did you make this video two hours long, Amanda?" accompanied with a laughing face emoji.

Amanda's video came just days after her ex Les Dennis opened up about their marriage. The former couple were married from 1995 to 2003, but were together for a total of 10 years.

Sharon Stone in the original scene

Appearing on the Very Very Sherrie podcast earlier this year, the 73-year-old was asked about his marriage to Amanda and their 17-year age gap.

Host Sherrie Hewson recalled her reaction to their relationship, telling him: "I remember going to you, 'She's too young what are you doing? You silly old fool'".

Les went on to recall an encounter with a plumber who visited their home in Highgate. While discussing the Grand National at the time, he said the plumber referred to Amanda as a "kid".

Fans were delighted with Amanda's post(Image: noholdenback/Instagram)

"So yeah I should've known," he laughed. Sherrie replied: "I felt that about you, I think I said it you as well, 'It's not going to work'".

"Probably," he laughed. "Everybody said it to each other but nobody said it to me!"
