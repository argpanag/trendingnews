Gigabyte Announces Onimusha: Way of the Sword Bundle for AMD Products

TheTools · 03.09.2026 11:15 · source · permalink

Gigabyte has partnered with AMD to announce a special bundle for Capcom’s latest title, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, which will be available to users who purchase eligible products.

Users who purchase select eligible products from participating retailers or e-tailers will receive a coupon code from AMD for Onimusha: Way of the Sword. The code will allow users to claim the game for free and must be redeemed by October 24, 2026. Meanwhile, the special bundle offer will remain available until September 26, 2026.

Here are the list all eligible products:

  • AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D2
  • AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D
  • AMD Ryzen 9 9900X3D
  • AMD Ryzen 7 9850X3D
  • AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D
  • AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT
  • AMD Radeon RX 9070 GRE
  • AMD Radeon RX 9070

For more details regarding the campaign, users can check out the campaign page.

Onimusha: Way of the Sword is an upcoming action-adventure game where players take on the role of Miyamoto Musashi, a samurai wielding the mystical Oni Gauntlet as he battles the monstrous Genma in Edo-period Kyoto. On PC, the game will be available for ₹4,399, with the Premium Deluxe Edition priced at ₹5,699. It will also launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

In other news, users can also check out the special AMD game bundle for Onimusha: Way of the Sword, which is available with the purchase of select AMD processors and graphics cards.
