Joe Milton on his return to Dallas: 'Just going to play another football game'

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

The quarterback showed marked improvement in his second start, helping the Eagles get back in the win column.

Progress is measured in ways we all see – like the third-and-6 throw and completion to wide receiver DeVonta Smith that quarterback Joe Milton made in the final minute of Sunday's win over Pittsburgh to seal the deal – and there are ways that are hidden in the shadows of long film study, repetition on the practice field, and a breakdown and buildup of the way to play the game's most important position.

In his second NFL start, Milton took another step forward on both fronts. He handled a Pittsburgh defense that has a relentless approach to pressuring the line of scrimmage and the passing game with a same-page plan with his offensive line and receivers, and he delivered some on-the-money passes that moved the sticks and added a big-play element to the passing game.

Was it perfect? No. But Milton impressed to win his first game in the NFL and that is what mattered.

"What I'm most excited about is that regardless of a good play or bad play, he always played the next play and that really showed itself in the very last play of our game before the kneeldowns, where we needed him to make a play on third-and-6 in a four-minute (situation) versus pressure and he executed right there," Head Coach Nick Sirianni said. "He's going to want some throws back. He made some good plays, going to want some plays back, that's football, but I just loved his response throughout. He's confident and he should be. He's a talented guy."

Milton is a piece of clay the Eagles are molding, step by step. Philadelphia is his third team and his first where he is gaining measurable practice reps and valuable playing time in games. This is Milton's first taste of football stability and he is taking full advantage.

Throughout the two weeks of Milton's time as the starting quarterback here, he has talked about his growing confidence, the way the coaching staff and his teammates are building him up. He asks a lot of questions and the Eagles give him all of the answers he needs.

"I feel more comfortable than I did last week," Milton said in the days leading up to the Steelers' game. "Just the verbiage and everything that we needed to do that we are leaning towards to solve the problem and fix it. I feel way more confident for sure."

The questions Milton is asking are specific. He wants to know his responsibility in every aspect of the situation after joining the team in the middle of the season and being elevated to the starting role after three weeks in Philadelphia.

It is as challenging a task as you can imagine: Learning a whole new language and developing chemistry with new teammates around him literally on the fly.

"It's been fun and I'm happy to be here and having this opportunity," Milton said. "I'm taking advantage. I have a lot of work to do. I know that. I feel like I'm getting all the help I need."

The next step for Milton is here: A short week of preparation and a return to the team he played with earlier this season – the Dallas Cowboys. A national game. Thanksgiving in Dallas. It's a big moment in a series of big moments for Joe Milton, who is doing what he said he wanted to do when he first came to Philadelphia: Stay where his feet are. Enjoy the moment.

"Just going to go play another football game," Milton said of returning to Dallas. "I mean, obviously, I was just there a month ago, but I mean, it's going to be a great game. It's going to be a game where we battle, we come out there, and we just have to execute our one-eleventh. That's all it's going to come down to. Know those guys' mentality a little bit, so therefore we just got to come with it. We've got to show up and show out no matter what, whatever play is called, have to play on their side of the ball, and you just have to execute. Can't shoot yourself in the foot."

The Eagles return from the bye week looking to start the second half of the season strong against their in-state rivals, the Pittsburgh Steelers.
