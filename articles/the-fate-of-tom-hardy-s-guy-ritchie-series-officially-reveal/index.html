The Fate of Tom Hardy's Guy Ritchie Series Officially Revealed After Season 2 Finale

TheTools · 20.11.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

There are few families on television quite as vile as the Harrigans, and unfortunately for everyone who crosses their path, their retirement plans appear to be, well, as alive as some of their rivals. Tom Hardy has spent two seasons of MobLand trying to keep the criminal family from tearing itself apart, which usually involves threats, violence, and cleaning up whatever disaster they've created that week. With Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren leading the family, it's hardly surprising that things rarely go according to plan. Now, with the second season having reached its conclusion, fans concerned about the fate of the most volatile family in the world of crime have some news to process.

Paramount+ has officially confirmed that production on MobLand Season 3 began this week, with Hardy, Brosnan, and Mirren all returning. The announcement arrives alongside the explosive and shocking Season 2 finale, which is now streaming on Paramount+, and thankfully that means we don't have to play the old waiting game wondering if it'll ever be renewed. We prefer a speedy turnover of our favorite shows, thank you very much.

Who Stars in 'MobLand'?

The series stars Hardy (Venom) as Harry da Souza, Brosnan (GoldenEye) as Conrad Harrigan and Mirren (1923) as Maeve Harrigan, alongside Paddy Considine (House of the Dragon), Joanne Froggatt (Downton Abbey), Lara Pulver (Sherlock), Anson Boon (Pistol), Dhillon (After Life), Jasmine Jobson (Top Boy), Johnny Flynn (Emma), Janet McTeer (Ozark) and Toby Jones (Captain America: The First Avenger).

Hardy returns as Harry Da Souza, the Harrigan family's loyal fixer, whose job frequently involves dealing with problems that can't exactly be taken to the police. Brosnan reprises his role as the loose cannon, Conrad Harrigan, the family's patriarch, while Mirren returns as Maeve Harrigan, who has actually proven herself to be one of the most unlikeable characters on television, and that's a true compliment. Mirren is screen royalty so for her to actually produce a performance like this is a testament to her abilities.

Created by Ronán Bennett, MobLand is executive produced by Guy Ritchie, Jez Butterworth, Hardy, and several others, with Paramount Television Studios and 101 Studios producing the crime drama.

MobLand Seasons 1 and 2 are streaming now on Paramount+, including the Season 2 finale. Season 3 is officially in production, with a premiere date yet to be announced.
