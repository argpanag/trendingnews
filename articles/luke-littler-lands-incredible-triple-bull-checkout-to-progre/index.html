Luke Littler lands incredible triple-bull checkout to progress in Wolverhampton

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:31 · source · permalink

Luke Littler landed an incredible triple-bull checkout as he booked his place in the last 16 of the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton.

The two-time defending champion was beaten 5-4 by Dutchman Niels Zonneveld but bagged the three legs he required to ensure his progression from the group stage.

And with the pressure off, Littler showcased his skills with his remarkable 150 finish to level the match at 4-4 immediately after Zonneveld had missed his first match dart.

Television footage even appeared to capture the audacious 19-year-old asking the crowd whether he should attempt the unorthodox finish moments before he stepped back up to the oche.

Littler later tweeted: “Brain rot – didn’t know if I was through or not… 3 bulls will do to definitely secure my spot in the next round>”

The already-eliminated Zonneveld at least had the bonus of winning the deciding leg and handing Littler his first ever defeat in the tournament.

There was also drama as Gerwyn Price dispatched Sebastian Bialecki 5-2 to progress to the knockout stages from Group E.

Price bust his score in the sixth leg of the match, but after hitting double 20 to top out at 10 over his required tally, the Welshman was given the leg on account of having been given incorrect information by the referee.

Alan Soutar missed eight match darts to beat Jermaine Wattimena and progress from Group B, before the Dutchman grasped his chance to find double eight and progress from Group B with a thrilling 5-4 victory.

There was no such drama for Ryan Searle or Rob Cross, both of whom won their groups with 5-1 wins over Andrew Gilding and Anton Ostlund respectively, while Kevin Doets beat Tommy Morris by the same score to progress from Group C.

Cameron Menzies produced three consecutive 100-plus finishes to beat Stephen Bunting 5-2 in a winner-takes-all Group D tussle, and Niko Springer rounded off an eventful night by beating Wessel Nijman 5-3 to top Group H.
