Sheriff Arrests Nine in San Bernardino Train Cargo Theft Probe

TheTools · 29.10.2026 12:27 · source · permalink

Nine men identified by the San Bernardino County Sheriff's Department as residents of Mexico were arrested in San Bernardino, California, on October 16, 2026, after investigators searched a residence and recovered more than $333,000 in allegedly stolen retail merchandise. Authorities said the investigation targeted an organized criminal network connected to the Sinaloa Cartel and suspected of burglarizing freight trains over the previous 12 months.

Investigators from the Sheriff's Department Rural Crime Task Force, assisted by the Specialized Enforcement Division, executed a search warrant at a residence in the 1200 block of East 35th Street, according to a department statement. The investigation focused on alleged burglaries targeting Burlington Northern Santa Fe Railway trains.

The recovered merchandise included JBL speakers, Logitech computer hardware, Victoria's Secret merchandise, Nike shoes, New Balance shoes and Husky tools. The department valued the stolen retail goods at more than $333,000.

Authorities also seized four semiautomatic handguns, a drone described as being used for surveillance, a large amount of cash, tools allegedly used to access train cargo containers, fentanyl suspected to be intended for sale and four vehicles connected to recent train cargo burglaries, officials said.

The nine arrested suspects were identified as Jose Navarrete, 24; Brian Iran Fontes-Carrillo, 27; Mauro Ruiz-Medina, 30; Pastor Pena-Lerma, 43; Alfredo Covarrubias-Navarrete, 24; Jose Alfredo Castillo-Baca, 24; Jose Ismael Palafox-Bernal, 18; Cesar Daniel Navarrete-Martinez, 31; and Cristian Erubiel Navarrete-Zapien, 23.

According to the Sheriff's Department, all nine men were listed as residents of Mexico. Authorities booked them into the Central Detention Center on charges including conspiracy, theft of railroad property and possession of a controlled substance while armed.

The investigation centers on suspected organized thefts involving freight shipments transported by Burlington Northern Santa Fe Railway. Officials said the alleged burglaries occurred over a 12-month period preceding the arrests, while the seized vehicles were connected to recent incidents involving train cargo.

The Sheriff's Department said investigators will continue efforts to identify and arrest additional suspects and recover stolen property. The department has not provided further details about additional arrests in the investigation.
