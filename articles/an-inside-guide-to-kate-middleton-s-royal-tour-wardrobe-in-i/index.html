An Inside Guide To Kate Middleton’s Royal Tour Wardrobe In India

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:29 · source · permalink

India had sat at the top of Catherine, the Princess of Wales’s bucket list ever since she married into the royal family in 2011. Or so said Prince William during their 2016 tour of the subcontinent, for which the then Duchess of Cambridge packed a wardrobe that brought together the fashion heritage of both India and the country she represented. First came an Alexander McQueen dress, its paisley print drawing on the textile traditions of India and Persia, followed by a pink-and-pistachio tunic dress by Mumbai-based designer Anita Dongre, worn to play cricket at the Oval Maidan with children supported by local charities. Then along came a Jenny Packham gown, hand-beaded by Indian artisans, to a Bollywood gala, and a blue-and-white embroidered sheath dress by Cumbala Hill-born designer Naeem Khan for a visit to the Taj Mahal. Together, the looks offered an early indication of Kate’s understanding of clothing as a diplomatic tool.

Catherine, the Princess of Wales, wearing Alexander McQueen at the Taj Hotel in Mumbai, 2016.

Catherine, the Princess of Wales, at the Oval Maidan in Mumbai, 2016.

A decade on, the couple returned to India on Monday morning, where Prince William is hosting the sixth Earthshot Prize summit and awards ceremony in Mumbai. The annual Earthshot Prize gives £1 million each to five winners tackling some of the world’s most pressing environmental problems, and India has produced more finalists than any other country since its founding in 2020. It is Catherine’s first appearance at the event since Boston in 2022, while the inaugural Children’s Earthshot Awards should provide familiar territory for the princess, whose charitable work has long focused on the next generation. Beyond the prizes, though, the Prince and Princess are undertaking a series of engagements across Mumbai (and New Delhi), highlighting the cultural ties between Britain and India, including cricket, film and, of course, fashion.
