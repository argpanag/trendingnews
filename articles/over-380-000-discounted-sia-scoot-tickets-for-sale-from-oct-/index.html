SINGAPORE – Travellers can look forward to discounts on more than 200,000 Singapore Airlines (SIA) return tickets and over 180,000 Scoot one-way tickets during the Time To Fly travel fair from Oct 23 to 30.

Destinations include Bangkok, Hangzhou, London, Los Angeles, Madrid, Seoul and Sydney, as well as new Scoot routes to Belitung, Chiang Rai, Guiyang and Okinawa, the two carriers said in a joint release on Oct 13.

For the discounted SIA tickets, the travel period is from Jan 1, 2027, to Sept 30, 2027, across business, premium economy and economy classes on flights from Singapore to 78 destinations.

For Scoot, the travel period is from Nov 9, 2026, to Oct 15, 2027, across ScootPlus and economy class flights between Singapore and 70 destinations.

The airlines said tickets will be available online through the SIA and Scoot websites and mobile applications, as well as through appointed travel agents.

There will also be a three-day physical Time To Fly travel fair from Oct 23 to 25 at the Suntec Singapore Convention and Exhibition Centre, halls 401, 402 and 403.

The fair will feature exclusive deals from more than 30 travel agents and partners.

Among other perks, visitors to the fair can enter a lucky draw for a pair of SIA business class return tickets, with hotel accommodation, for Cebu, Hangzhou and Sydney.

Other prizes include SIA business class return tickets with hotel stays to London Gatwick, Madrid and Male, as well as Scoot vouchers worth up to $2,000 and KrisShop vouchers worth $800.

Vinod Kannan, SIA’s senior vice-president for sales and marketing, said: “Our annual Time To Fly promotion gives Singapore-based customers more choice and value as they plan a long-awaited holiday, reconnect with loved ones or discover a new destination.”

Scoot’s chief commercial officer Calvin Chan pointed out that discounted tickets to popular destinations such as Jeju, Nanjing and Osaka will be on sale, and added: “With more choice and attractive fares, customers can start planning for their next getaway.”
