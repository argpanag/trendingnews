EU: Martin says Europe's security 'fundamentally reshaped' by war in Ukraine

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Europe's security has been "fundamentally reshaped" by Russia's war in Ukraine, Taoiseach (Irish prime minister) Micheál Martin has told 50 leaders who are in Dublin for a summit.

The Republic of Ireland is chairing the EU presidency until the end of the year and its main showpiece events, the European Political Community (EPC) and informal meeting of the European Council, are taking place this week.

The country has been preparing for the events for months, with gardaí (Irish police) carrying out the largest security and policing operation in the history of the state.

Attendees include Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky and heads of state across the EU.

The Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) will support its Irish counterparts in policing the summit.

Even though officers from abroad will not be involved in frontline policing, gardaí said they are being supported by the PSNI, police from Latvia and Portugal and other international law enforcement agencies.

Parts of Dublin city centre are closed off and there has been widespread traffic disruption in city in recent days.

The state has also been dealing with a number of bomb threats, which have included hoaxes, on schools and media organisations in recent days.

'Brutal war of aggression'

On Thursday, about 50 international leaders gathered at the Convention Centre in Dublin for a meeting of the EPC.

They included European Commission president Ursula von der Leyen and Nato chief Mark Rutte.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham is expected to attend as part of his efforts to form closer ties with Europe.

Martin told those attending on Thursday that "our European security environment has been fundamentally reshaped by Russia's brutal war of aggression in Ukraine".

"What is happening in Ukraine goes against all of our values and all of what we stand for."

Addressing leaders at the EPC, Zelensky said Europe needs to "pay attention" when there is talk about security guarantees.

He said: "Europe really needs to pay attention when the Americans and Russians talk about this, it's not just about security guarantees for Ukraine in fact it's more about some kind of new security framework between Europe and Russia and we find this approach a bit strange."

He said Russian President Vladimir Putin's wars are about "his attitude toward others not some kind of permanent confrontation with Europe that was impossible to avoid".

Ukraine, AI and hybrid threats across Europe are expected to dominate key EU meetings in Dublin on Thursday and Friday.

The EPC is an informal forum for European leaders to hold political and strategic discussions on issues of shared concern.

It was set up in 2022 after Russian's full-scale invasion of Ukraine and meets twice a year.

The theme of the summit in Dublin is "Europe – strong, secure, and connected".

Leaders will discuss economic security, Ukraine, hybrid activity in Europe and democratic resilience.

Hybrid warfare or hybrid attacks are loose terms to describe non-military methods, for example cyber attacks, sabotage or misinformation, designed to exert pressure and destabilise an adversary.

It is believed there will be a "handful" of no-shows, including Turkey as it is hosting the COP31 climate summit.

Earlier, Martin said that discussions for a European voice to represent the EU at US meetings with Russia on ending the war will "happen on the margins" of the summit.

He said Europe is "united" and "doesn't believe in war", and is "appalled" by the "Russian invasion of Ukraine".

"Many member states [of the EU] feel an existential threat because of what has happened in Ukraine and the potential, if Ukraine was to fall, for further aggression on the European continent," he told reporters.

Martin added that he hopes there is progress amid talks with US envoy and Russia.

US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff are flying to Dublin on Thursday to update Zelensky on talks with Putin.

Separately Martin and Zelensky have indicated that they may cooperate on the production of unmanned drones.

The two leaders met in Dublin on Wednesday.

There will also be a meeting of a Friends of Ukraine group, co-hosted by the UK and Ukraine on the margins of the EU meeting, where Zelensky is expected to brief the leaders.

It is understood that meeting is not part of the EU meetings happening this week.

On Friday, a meeting of the informal European Council will take place, also in the Convention Centre.
