Up to 5 inches of rain recorded in parts of North Ga. in last 24 hours, NWS says

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:45 · source · permalink

Areas of North Georgia, including parts of Fulton County, got 3 to 5 inches of rain between Friday and Saturday, the National Weather Service said.

Saturday storms that began during daybreak continued into the afternoon and are predicted to taper off completely around 8 p.m. Even with mostly overcast skies, highs reached the mid-80s by the afternoon in Atlanta. The feels-like temperature topped off at 94 degrees around 3 p.m.

From Carroll County, west of Atlanta, to Fulton and DeKalb counties, the Weather Service said radar estimates revealed up to 5 inches of rain shortly before 3 p.m. In the city of Villa Rica, nearly 8 inches of rain fell in the last 24 hours.
