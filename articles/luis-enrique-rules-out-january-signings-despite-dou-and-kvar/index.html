Luis Enrique rules out January signings despite injuries

Spain's Luis Enrique Martínez, coach of Paris Saint-Germain (PSG), ruled out going to the winter market on Tuesday despite the injuries to two of his key attacking players: Frenchman Desiré Doué and Georgian Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, who will be out for several months with respective knee injuries.

"We are not obliged to go to the market, we are not going to sign a player for six months, it is not our style. We have a team," said Luis Enrique at the press conference ahead of the clash in Paris against Italy's Roma.

"It is true that we have to be more attentive, but with these players, this coaching staff and these fans we are not afraid of anything. We trust in doing our job and overcoming the difficulties," he added.

PSG leaders of the Champions League

PSG, leaders of the first phase of the Champions League with four wins from four matches, level on points with Real Madrid under José Mourinho, face AS Roma, who are twentieth in the table, close to the elimination places.

Luis Enrique praised his Roma counterpart, veteran Gian Piero Gasperini (68), and the Italian club's forwards, including Dutchman Donyell Malen, who has just scored four goals in Serie A against Parma (1-5), and already has 16 in all competitions so far this season.

"We know all the AS Roma players, especially the forwards. Malen used to play on the wing and, when he moved to the centre, he showed his ability to score goals. His goal tally is incredible," he said.

Luis Enrique's memories of Roma

Luis Enrique coached Roma 15 years ago, a brief spell that left its mark on him, despite the lack of good results.

"It was very special for me. I have good memories of my year in Roma. It is a very nice feeling every time I have the chance to visit Rome, it is magnificent. Things can go better or worse, but that is life. You have to accept it. I keep very positive memories," he concluded. EFE
