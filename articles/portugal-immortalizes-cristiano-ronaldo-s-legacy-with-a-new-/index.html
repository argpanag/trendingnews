Portugal Immortalizes Cristiano Ronaldo's Legacy With a New No. 7 Jersey Design

TheTools · 16.11.2026 06:11 · source · permalink

Summary

The Portugal Football Association has announced a graphic design update to its No. 7 jersey to honor Cristiano Ronaldo

Because FIFA regulations prevent the outright retirement of the number, the new design features a small cross on the digit

The updated jersey will debut during the team's upcoming Nations League match against Denmark following Ronaldo's recent retirement

The Portugal Football Association has officially announced a design update to its iconic No. 7 jersey, serving as a permanent tribute to Cristiano Ronaldo‘s storied international career. Debuting during the team’s upcoming Nations League match against Denmark, the design shift marks Portugal’s first game since the 41-year-old forward announced his retirement from the national team earlier this month.

Concluding his international run with a staggering 146 goals across 234 matches, Ronaldo stepped away from the squad on November 5 following a fallout with coach Jorge Jesus. Because strict FIFA stipulations prevent national federations from officially retiring jersey numbers, the governing body opted for a visual workaround to honor the athlete’s legacy. The newly updated No. 7 kit features a modified graphic design that incorporates a small cross on the digit, symbolically drawing a line after Ronaldo’s historic era.

“From today onwards, we will have a different number 7 on the jersey, but with the same essence and the same pride, reserving the previous one for the immortalization of a story that will never be forgotten,” the federation shared in an official statement. While forward Rafael Leão wore the number during Portugal’s previous outing, a permanent successor for the new No. 7 has yet to be named.
