Week 4 football roundup: Ithaca, Montabella, Mt. Pleasant stay unbeaten

TheTools · 19.09.2026 03:17 · source · permalink

Week 4 of the high school football season brought another busy slate across Mid-Michigan, with unbeaten teams staying hot, others getting back in the win column, and conference races beginning to take shape.

Here’s a look at the action involving teams from The Morning Sun coverage area.

No. 1 Beal City 35, McBain 16

Beal City improved to 4-0 for the second consecutive season, picking up another conference victory to move to 2-0 in league play.

No. 1 Montabella 65, Coleman 7

Division 1 8-player No. 1 Montabella continued its unbeaten start with another dominant performance, cruising past Coleman to improve to 4-0 overall and 2-0 in the MSAC.

Montabella piled up 530 rushing yards in the win, led by Austin Jensen with 165 yards and three touchdowns on eight carries. Josh Arganbright added 109 yards and two scores on five carries, while Ethan Greene rushed for 104 yards and a touchdown on just three attempts. Quarterback Gabe Kauffman completed all three of his passes for 78 yards and a touchdown while adding 64 rushing yards and another score.

The Comets’ lone touchdown came on a 1-yard run from Brennan Glazier. He also completed 16 of 25 passes for 132 yards with one interception.

Sincear Czarnecki had seven receptions for 78 yards, while Konar Curtis rushed 10 times for 40 yards and recorded a team-high eight tackles.

“Montabella is bigger, faster and played harder than us,” Coleman head coach Chad Klopf said. “We have to get back in believing in ourselves and stop focusing on things we cannot control.”

Coleman dropped its second straight game and fell to 1-3 overall and 0-2 in conference play.

No. 3 Mt. Pleasant 35, Traverse City Central 20

Division 3 No. 3 Mt. Pleasant improved to 4-0 for the second consecutive season behind another strong performance on both sides of the ball.

Quarterback Freddie Makinen threw two touchdown passes, both to receiver Zay McGinnis, while Prosper Enehiroana and Dev Murray each added rushing touchdowns.

The Oilers are now 4-0 overall and 2-0 in the SVL-North.

No. 5 Ithaca 47, Saginaw MLS 6

The Yellowjackets improved to 4-0 overall and 2-0 in conference play after a dominant victory over Saginaw Michigan Lutheran Seminary.

No. 10 Harrison 24, Centreville 14

Division 7 No. 10 Harrison improved to 4-0 after building a 16-0 halftime lead and holding off Centreville in the second half.

Running back Nyxon Rhodes led the way with 20 rushes for 145 yards and two touchdowns, while also catching a 9-yard touchdown reception from Parker Latunski.

Head coach Jamie Lipovsky recorded his 50th win at Harrison.

Chippewa Hills 36, Grant 12

Chippewa Hills won its fourth straight game, improving to 4-0 for the first time since 2016.

Quarterback Logan PeLong completed 3 of 7 passes for 36 yards and a touchdown while rushing 23 times for 231 yards and four scores.

Running back Clay Farrell added 168 yards and a touchdown on 24 carries.

Clare 60, Standish-Sterling 6

Clare snapped its two-game losing streak dominantly, rolling past Standish-Sterling for its first Jack Pine Conference victory of the season.

The Pioneers built a 27-0 halftime lead and continued to pull away from there.

Quarterback Dane Alexander completed 3 of 4 passes for 61 yards while rushing six times for 177 yards and four touchdowns.

Donovan Hoogerhyde and Luke Gyulveszi also found the end zone.

Gunner West made a big impact defensively with eight tackles, while Blake Rodgers added five tackles and two sacks.

“We knew with it being their homecoming, we would have to dictate the tempo and emotion early,” Clare head coach Steven Spranger said. “Thankfully we did that and took control of the game right away.”

The Pioneers improved to 2-2 overall and 1-1 in JPC-1 play.

Fulton 50, Potterville 13

The Pirates improved to 2-2 behind a huge offensive night on the ground.

Fulton racked up 404 rushing yards as a team and outscored Potterville 28-0 in the second half.

Lucas Podolak led the way with 12 carries for 183 yards and three touchdowns. Zach Litwiller rushed seven times for 150 yards and a touchdown while also throwing two touchdown passes to Eli Coblentz.

Jacob Alexander led the defense with nine tackles, a forced fumble and a fumble recovery. Litwiller also recorded an interception, while Coblentz added a fumble recovery.

Farwell 58, Pinconning 0

The Eagles improved to 2-2 on the year with their first win in conference play.

Gladwin 38, Shepherd 20

The Blue Jays suffered their second straight loss, falling to 2-2 overall and 0-2 in conference play.

Essexville Garber 16, Alma 14

The Panthers fell to 1-3 on the season after a conference loss Friday night. Alma was held to just 65 rushing yards, while the Dukes finished with 189 yards on the ground.

Quarterback Penn Seeley completed 9 of 21 passes for 112 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions. He also rushed 15 times for 32 yards and a score.

Linebacker Jake Johnston led the defense with nine tackles and two tackles for loss.

Alma falls to 1-1 in conference play.

Burton Bendle 41, Vestaburg 14

Vestaburg’s two-game winning streak came to an end as the Wolverines were held to their lowest scoring output of the season.

The Wolverines led 8-6 at the end of the first quarter, but Burton Bendle then scored 35 straight points.

Lucas Warczinsky and Koffee Arntz scored both touchdowns for the Wolverines. Arntz completed 4 of 12 passes for 44 yards and two interceptions in the loss. Warczinsky rushed 24 times for 63 yards while also catching three passes for 41 yards.

Brehm Warczinsky led the team with 14 tackles.

Vestaburg falls to 2-2.

Sanford Meridian 56, St. Louis 0

St. Louis suffered its fourth straight loss and fell to 0-2 in JPC play.

“Sanford Meridian was as advertised, and they are very well coached,” St. Louis head coach Ben Brock said. “Our players never gave up and played as physical as we could against a very good team. I am proud of a few of our guys who won their battles, as we had moments of success that we can build on their effort moving forward.”

Merrick Brock led the Sharks offensively with 16 carries for 49 yards and two receptions for seven yards.

Brock also recorded seven tackles, a sack and a tackle for loss on defense. Marcus Hoyt finished with seven tackles and a tackle for loss.

St. Louis falls to 0-4 on the season.

St. Bernard’s 46, Sacred Heart 0 (THURS.)

Sacred Heart welcomed St. Bernard’s of Eureka, California, to Mt. Pleasant for a rare cross-country matchup, but the Crusaders took control early and handed the Irish a shutout loss.

Sacred Heart falls to 2-2 on the season.

Portland St. Patrick 49, Breckenridge 14 (THURS.)

Breckenridge suffered its second straight loss as Portland St. Patrick pulled away for a 49-14 victory.

Quarterback Gavin Corbat completed 25 of 40 passes for 323 yards and two touchdowns with four interceptions, while also rushing 12 times for 61 yards.

Carter Suhr had nine receptions for 187 yards and two touchdowns, while Ely Chapin added nine catches for 102 yards.

Suhr also recorded eight tackles and a forced fumble on defense. Andrew Clark added six tackles and three tackles for loss.

“We learned a lot about our team tonight and didn’t perform to our standard,” Breckenridge head coach Isaac Gregory said. “There’s plenty of blame to go around, but we’ve got a talented St. Charles team to get ready for.”

The Huskies fall to 2-2 on the season.
