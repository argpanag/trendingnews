CONFIRMED: Nations Championship fixtures for finals weekend

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

SPOTLIGHT: The inaugural Nations Championship will reach its conclusion next week when the Northern Hemisphere battles the Southern Hemisphere in the final Tests at the Allianz Stadium in Twickenham.

The final pool round of the competition concluded on Saturday with six blockbuster fixtures.

South Africa booked their spot in the final of the competition after finishing atop the Southern Hemisphere pool, despite losing to Ireland 17-20 in Dublin at the Aviva Stadium.

Continues below…

They will face France after they topped the Northern Pool, having dispatched Argentina 45-17 at the Stade de France in Paris.

England and Ireland finished in second and third places, respectively, in the Northern Hemisphere, and they’ll face New Zealand and Australia.

Scotland will battle Argentina as the respective fourth-place teams, while Wales and Japan will lock horns for a second time in as many weeks.

Meanwhile, Italy and Fiji will play for the wooden spoon, having faced each other already in the competition.

The Nations Championship finals weekend will run from Friday, 27 November until Sunday, 29 November.

The fixtures:

Friday, 27 November:
Italy vs Fiji
Ireland vs Australia

Saturday, 28 November
Wales vs Japan
England vs New Zealand

Sunday, 29 November
Scotland vs Argentina
France vs South Africa – FINAL
