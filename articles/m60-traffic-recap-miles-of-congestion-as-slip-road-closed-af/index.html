M60 traffic RECAP: Miles of congestion as slip road closed after lorry overturns

TheTools · 04.11.2026 23:12 · source · permalink

There are long delays on the M60 this evening after a lorry overturned with a stretch of the motorway to stay shut 'for hours', travel bosses have said.

The HGV overturned on the anticlockwise exit slip road at junction 5 for Princess Parkway earlier today, resulting in damage to a barrier and a diesel spillage. All traffic was initially stopped on the main carriageway, but has since been released.

However, the exit slip road remains closed and is expected to be shut for 'several hours' whilst the lorry is recovered and the spillage is cleaned up, in what is described as a 'complex' and 'protracted' operation.

Diversion

Drivers heading anti-clockwise on the M60 are advised to follow the solid square diversion symbol on road signs and:

  • Continue on the M60 anti-clockwise from J5 and follow to J2.
  • Leave the M60 at J2.
  • At the M60 J2/A560 roundabout take the 6th exit to rejoin the M60 clockwise.
  • Follow the M60 clockwise back to J4 and leave the M60 clockwise to join the M56 westbound.
  • Continue on the M56 to J3A to continue your journey.#

National Highways says:

If this closure impacts on your planned route, please allow extra journey time. Plan ahead, you may wish to re-route or even delay your journey. "
