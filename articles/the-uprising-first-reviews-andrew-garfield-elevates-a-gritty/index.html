The Uprising First Reviews: Andrew Garfield Elevates a Gritty but Uneven Epic

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:24 · source · permalink

News

Critics say director Paul Greengrass' style isn't always a great fit and some of his choices are questionable, but the action is immersive, the pacing is brisk, and the acting is spot on.

by Christopher Campbell | September 9, 2026

TAGGED AS: movies

Andrew Garfield stars in The Uprising, a medieval action movie opening this weekend, and the reviews are now online and quite mixed. Written and directed by Oscar-nominated filmmaker Paul Greengrass (United 93, Captain Phillips) and based on both history and legend, the epic period piece depicts the origins of a hero during the 1381 Peasants’ Revolt in England. Reactions are divided on whether the style fits the material or if its premise is especially relevant today, while an effort to align the story with a famous fictional hero seems mostly agreed upon as misguided.
