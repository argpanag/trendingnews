What Is Kalshi?

Kalshi is a prediction-market exchange regulated by the CFTC. Rather than placing a conventional bet against a sportsbook, users buy and sell event contracts with other traders.

Each contract is usually tied to a clear yes-or-no question, such as whether a fighter will win, whether an NFL team will make the playoffs or whether a particular economic event will occur. Contracts trade between $0 and $1, with the price representing the market’s implied probability of the outcome.

If a contract resolves in your favor, it settles at $1 per share. If it resolves against you, it settles at $0. You can also sell a position before the market settles, allowing you to lock in a gain or limit a loss as prices change.

How Kalshi Contracts Work

Suppose a “Yes” contract costs $0.60. That price implies the market sees about a 60% chance of the event happening.

If you buy 10 contracts, your cost is $6. If the outcome is correct, the contracts settle at $10 total, producing a $4 profit before any applicable fees. If the prediction is wrong, those contracts settle at $0. Since the market price can shift before resolution, users may also sell their contracts early rather than hold through the final outcome.
