WA Police have taken a person into custody after they swarmed the semi-rural suburb of Keysbrook, south of Perth, on Saturday morning, responding to a major incident.

A police source told WAtoday that a person had been shot and authorities asked the public to stay away from the area.

A police spokesperson said “significant police resources” were sent to the suburb, later reporting that there was no ongoing threat to public safety.

“One person has been apprehended at the scene and is currently assisting with inquiries,” they said.

“The investigation remains ongoing. More information will be provided when available.”

Three St John Ambulance crews were sent to the area but have not been utilised and are on standby.

Keysbrook sits along the South Western Highway roughly 62 kilometres south of the Perth CBD.
