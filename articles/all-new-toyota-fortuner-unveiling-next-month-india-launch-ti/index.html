All-New Toyota Fortuner Unveiling Next Month: India Launch Timeline and Other Details

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

Next-gen Fortuner global debut on December 1, 2026 India launch expected in early-to-mid 2027

Toyota is preparing to launch the next-generation of its flagship SUV, the 2027 Toyota Fortuner. After nearly a decade on sale, the second-gen model has been the best-selling SUV its category. The third-generation Fortuner will come with a comprehensive overhaul. It is scheduled to make its global debut in South Africa on December 1, 2026.


Following its international debut, the all-new Toyota Fortuner is expected to arrive in the Indian market in early-to-mid 2027. The new model will introduce sharper design, a modern digital cockpit and LEVEL-2 ADAS suite.

 

Key Details

2027 Next-Gen Toyota Fortuner

Global Debut Date

December 1, 2026 (South Africa)

Expected India Launch

Early to Mid-2027

Platform

Upgraded IMV Body-on-Frame Chassis

2.8L Turbo-Diesel with 48V Neo Drive Mild-Hybrid Tech

Specs & Transmission

~201–204 PS / 500 Nm (6-Speed MT & 6-Speed Torque Converter AT)

Drivetrain

Rear-Wheel Drive (4x2) & Four-Wheel Drive (4x4) with Low-Range

Estimated Price Range

₹38.00 Lakh – ₹52.00 Lakh (Ex-Showroom)


The next-gen Toyota Fortuner will adopt a bolder, squarer stance inspired by the latest-generation Toyota Hilux pick-up. The spy images confirm that the SUV will retain its signature high ground clearance and imposing road presence. It will have a wider front grille, flatter bonnet, slim LED DRLs, and split headlamp setup, an upright A-pillar, squared wheel arches, a flatter tailgate and a full-width connected LED bar.


The SUV will come with dual 12.3-inch screens comprising a floating touchscreen infotainment system paired with a fully digital driver’s instrument cluster. The infotainment unit will be compatible with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.


In terms of features, the SUV is likely to get a panoramic sunroof, ventilated and power-adjustable front seats, a 360-degree surround-view camera, multi-zone climate control, and wireless smartphone charging.


The next-gen Toyota Fortuner will come with LEVEL-2 ADAS tech with features like Adaptive Cruise Control, Autonomous Emergency Braking, Lane Keep Assist, and Blind Spot Monitoring.


The next-gen Fortuner will be powered by the tried & tested 2.8-litre, 4-cylinder, turbo-diesel engine paired with a 48-volt Neo Drive mild-hybrid system. The belt-driven motor-generator improves low-end throttle response, and start-stop operations, and boosts fuel efficiency. It is also expected to retain the 2.7-litre naturally-aspirated petrol engine. Built on the revised IMV ladder-frame chassis, the next-gen Fortuner will retain its core off-road hardware, including a proper 4x4 drivetrain with a low-range transfer case, and revised suspension setup.


The next-gen Fortuner is expected to be priced around Rs 38 lakh to Rs 52 lakh, ex-showroom. It will rival the likes of the MG Majestor, Jeep Meridian and the Skoda Kodiaq.
