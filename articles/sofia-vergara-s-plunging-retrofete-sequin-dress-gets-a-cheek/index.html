Sofia Vergara shared behind-the-scenes images from America's Got Talent, highlighting a new green sequin-and-lace look that added to her ongoing on-show fashion moments. The judge appeared in the Retrofete Ellen Sequin Dress in Pine during the latest episode of America's Got Talent. Sofia Vergara later posted the look on Instagram, writing, "See you next week! What a season it has been!!! @agt season 21!" The strapless midi features dark green sequins, black lace trim, and a plunging neckline; it retails for $598 and is currently listed at $293 on Retrofete's site. She paired understated jewelry with voluminous waves and neutral makeup, and shared clips with Mel B and Howie Mandel.
