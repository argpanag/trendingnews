FOX 8 to simulcast 20 Pelicans games free during 2026-27 season

TheTools · 30.09.2026 20:56 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS (WVUE) - New Orleans Pelicans fans will be able to watch 20 games free over the air on FOX 8 during the 2026-27 NBA season.

The Gulf Coast Sports & Entertainment Network announced Wednesday that FOX 8 will simulcast 20 Pelicans games this season, giving viewers across the New Orleans region another way to watch the team without a cable or streaming subscription.

The FOX 8 schedule includes 11 home games and nine road games.

Coverage begins with the Pelicans’ season opener Wednesday, Oct. 21, when New Orleans hosts the Indiana Pacers at 7 p.m.

“We are extremely excited to simulcast more Pelicans games on WVUE-FOX 8 this season than ever before,” FOX 8 and GCSEN General Manager Mikel Schaefer said. “Pelicans fans are extremely passionate and loyal and we all are looking for an exciting season.”

Among the biggest games on the FOX 8 schedule are two home matchups against the Los Angeles Lakers, on Dec. 2 and New Year’s Eve.

FOX 8 will also carry home games against the Oklahoma City Thunder, Philadelphia 76ers and Phoenix Suns, along with road games against the Golden State Warriors, Boston Celtics and New York Knicks.

“We are committed to delivering a high-quality broadcast experience that brings Pelicans basketball to as many fans as possible across the Gulf Coast,” Pelicans Team President Dennis Lauscha said. “Building on last season’s significant viewership growth, we look forward to expanding our reach and giving more fans the opportunity to watch, follow and connect with the team.”

Pelicans games airing on FOX 8

The schedule is subject to change. All listed times are Central.

Play-by-play announcer Joel Meyers and color analyst Antonio Daniels will return to the Pelicans television booth for their 14th and seventh seasons with the team, respectively.

New Orleans native Andrew Lopez returns for his third season as sideline reporter.

Kelly Crull joins the Pelicans television broadcast team this season as host of the pregame, halftime and postgame studio shows. Crull previously worked with NBA on TNT, NBA TV, Major League Baseball on TBS and ESPN, along with several NBA and MLB teams.

The 20 FOX 8 simulcasts are in addition to the Pelicans’ full slate of locally televised games available on GCSEN throughout the season.
