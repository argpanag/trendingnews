Are Conor and Harley Benn related? Family history including dad Nigel

TheTools · 12.09.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Harley Benn is set to finally make his highly-anticipated Misfits Boxing debut on Saturday night when he takes on rival Joe Laws.

The two rivals were due to go head-to-head on the KSI vs Dillon Danis undercard last year, but the bout was scrapped after KSI was forced to cancel the event after pulling out of his fight with Danis.

Over a year later, they will reignite their feud and have already clashed on multiple occasions in the build-up to fight night. On Wednesday, tensions boiled over as Laws threw his water and then launched a table at Benn - sparking chaotic scenes. Both fighters have a professional background - with fans chomping at the bit to see the two foes trade leather in the ring.

Many will no doubt be keeping an eye on Benn, largely due to his name. Fans within the boxing fraternity will immediately be wondering if there's any relation to Connor and his legendary boxing father Nigel. And so, Mirror Fighting takes a look at whether both Conor and Harley are related....

Are Conor and Harley Benn related?

In short, yes. Both Conor and Harley are half-brothers. Former middleweight champion Benn Sr conceived Harley during an affair with his mum Lisa Andrew, while also going through IVF with his second wife Carolyne.

Carolyne reportedly didn’t know about the affair when she gave birth to twins Conor and India, shortly after Harley was born. Benn Sr refused to accept that he was Harley's father until a paternity test proved so.

Harley followed in his father's footsteps by venturing into the world of boxing. The 29-year-old - who is four days older than Conor - made his professional boxing debut back in 2017. The British brawler cruised to comfortable victories in his first six fights before suffering a shock loss to Lee Hallett back in 2019. He followed it up with three consecutive wins, but his last fight came in a defear to Faizan Anwar over in Dubai three years ago.

Half-brother Benn has carved a somewhat eye-catching career. The 'Destroyer' famously engaged in two epic bouts with Chris Eubank Jr last year. On Saturday night, just hours after Harley's fight with Laws, Benn will take on Ryan Garcia for the American's WBC welterweight title.

Are Conor and Harley Benn on speaking terms?

In recent years, Harley reconnected with father Nigel and half-brother Conor. The 29-year-old would regularly post training snaps with them. But two years ago, the boxer revealed that he cut his dad out of his life and admitted that he no longer speaks to Conor.

In an interview with The Sun, he said: “The relationship was sort of was rocky away from boxing. It would have taken a lot more than boxing to fix it on both sides and I cut my dad off last year. That’s how it is at the minute. I’m more at peace with how I was when I was younger in my early 20s, it used to affect me, having a rocky relationship with my dad and everyone coming up to me, ‘Oh your dad’s amazing, your dad’s this, your dad’s that.’

“OK, yeah, he’s an amazing boxer, but he has not been a good dad to me, so it was hard to hear that and people screaming his praises at me. And I was like, yeah, he was an amazing boxer, one of the most entertaining boxers I think Britain’s ever seen, but when it comes to our relationship, it’s a whole lot deeper than boxing.”

Despite the rather strained relationship with both Conor and dad Nigel, Harley hopes to one day set things right with Benn Sr: “I mean, my dad, it’s one of them. I’m grateful for the life that he gave me," he added.

“There’s a lot going on that could be fixed, might not be fixed, but me and Conor? I’ll be honest, it would just be fake if we were to get on. We come from completely different places, we don’t value the same things and if I’m honest, he probably doesn’t see me as a brother and I don’t see him as a brother unfortunately, but it is what it is.”
