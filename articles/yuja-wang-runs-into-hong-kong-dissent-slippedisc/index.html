Yuja Wang runs into Hong Kong dissent - Slippedisc

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:55 · source · permalink

norman lebrecht

November 12, 2026

Members of her audience were offered a full refund after a concert with the Mahler Chamber Orchestra fell short of the expected length.

Here is what the concert organisers are telling ticket holders:

Dear Patrons,

Refund procedure for the 10 November performance of “Yuja Wang Play-Conducts the Mahler Chamber Orchestra”

We understand the frustration and disappointment that some of our audience felt following the programme change at the concert on 10 November. We recognise how eagerly anticipated this concert was, and we apologise if the evening fell short of expectations.

Following careful consideration, the musicians made the artistic decision to withdraw the Tsfasman Jazz Suite from the programme for the entire Asian Tour. The rest of the programme was performed as advertised.

Unlike other cities, we did not immediately accept the withdrawal of the Tsfasman Jazz Suite. In the days leading up to the performance, we continued to negotiate for its inclusion while exploring alternative repertoire options. As Hong Kong was the opening stop of the tour, discussions of the final tour programmes were still ongoing until the day of the performance.

We were assured that to balance the shortened programme, extended stage time for Yuja Wang would be provided in the form of encores. Unfortunately, this was not fully realised, leaving our patrons with both very late notice and a performance that some may feel fell short of expectations.

As a gesture of goodwill, Premiere Performances will offer a full refund. Please fill in the refund form by 25 November 2026, 11:59pm. The refund will be issued to your original payment method within 8 weeks of receipt of your completed form.

Refund form: https://forms.gle/YbhYW2RghrvDY45E9

For those who purchased tickets through bank transfer / FPS transfer, please contact [email protected] to arrange a refund.

We sincerely apologise for any disappointment and frustration this has caused, and we thank our community for their understanding.

If you need further assistance regarding the refund process, please contact our Ticketing Manager at [email protected].

Premiere Performances of Hong Kong

The original advertised programme was:
Sergei Prokofiev – Symphony No. 1 in D major, Op. 25 “Classical”
Nikolai Kapustin – Piano Concerto No. 4, Op. 56 (with Yuja Wang)
*Interval*
W. A. Mozart – Symphony No. 29 in A major, K. 201
Alexander Tsfasman – Jazz Suite for Piano and Orchestra (with Yuja Wang)

UPDATE: South China Morning Post reports that members of the audience promptly reported the event to the Consumers Council.  PP told the paper that negotiations with Wang had continued ‘until an hour before the concert began’. It adds:

(An) audience member said ticket holders who had paid up to HK$1,980 (US$252) were denied the advertised musical experience because Wang’s scheduled appearance in the second half had been dropped entirely, describing the arrangement as deeply unprofessional…

The controversy contrasted sharply with the warmer audience reception for Wang’s first concert with the Mahler Chamber Orchestra on November 9, also organised by Premiere Performances.

Wang performed two Brahms piano concertos back-to-back without an interval while also conducting the orchestra. In an encore that thrilled the audience, Tarmo Peltokoski, the new music director of the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra, joined her on stage for a four-hand piano performance.
