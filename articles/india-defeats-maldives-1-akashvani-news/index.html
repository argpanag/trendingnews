India defeats Maldives 1-0 in SAFF Championship Group B match in Dhaka

India today defeated Maldives 1-0 in SAFF Championship Group B match at the National Stadium in Dhaka. Suhail Ahmad Bhat scored the match’s lone goal from the penalty spot in the 38th minute. With this the Blue Tigers entered the semi-finals.

It was India’s second and final group match of the South Asian Football Federation (SAFF) Championships.

India beat Pakistan 3-2 in their opening Group B match on 5th of this month.
