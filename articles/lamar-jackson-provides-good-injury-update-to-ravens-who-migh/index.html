The Baltimore Ravens suffer quite a scare when they saw Lamar Jackson suffer an ankle injury that will sideline him in Week 5. However, the injury might not be as gruesome as initially thought.

Per Ian Rapoport of NFL Network, Lamar Jackson has a “real chance” to return in Week 6 against the Browns. Rapoport also stated Jackson has been “working like crazy” to get back on the field next week.

Initially, the Ravens thought this was going to be a multi-week absence. However, it seems like the 2x MVP might be back sooner rather than later. Still, Jackson is unlikely to suit up in Week 5 vs Falcons.

Lamar Jackson’s stats in 2026 NFL season so far

Jackson has guided the Ravens to a 3-1 record, completing 68/96 passes for a 70.8 completion percentage. Jackson has 967 yards, six touchdowns, just one pick.
