Semi-Final 2: Sydney Sixers vs Sydney Thunder – Squads, Players to Watch, Live Streaming, Pitch Report - Female Cricket

TheTools · 19.10.2026 17:52 · source · permalink

The second semi-final brings us a high-stakes Sydney derby, with the Thunder heading into the clash in stellar touch. Sydney Thunder wrapped up their group stage in second place with seven points, remaining unbeaten across four games with three victories and a rained-out wash-out. They also boast a towering net run rate of +2.206, the best in the entire competition. Meanwhile, the Sydney Sixers secured third spot on four points with two wins and two losses, narrowly edging out the lower mid-table pack thanks to a slightly superior net run rate of +0.246.

The Thunder have been dominant with both bat and ball, crushing opponents with high-margin wins that have made them look like serious title contenders. Their top order has set strong foundations, allowing their bowlers to attack with plenty of runs to play with. The Sixers, conversely, stumble into the semi-finals on a two-match losing streak after starting the tournament with back-to-back wins. They’ll need to turn around a sudden dip in form very quickly if they want to halt their cross-town rivals.

Local derbies always carry an extra layer of intensity, and adding a spot in the final makes this clash even more compelling. Thunder start as firm favorites given their form and raw firepower, but the Sixers have plenty of big-stage experience to draw upon. If the Sixers can strike early and disrupt Thunder’s early rhythm, we could be in for a tense, tight finish; otherwise, Thunder’s momentum might prove too much to stop.

Players to watch out for :

Anika Learoyd

Anika Learoyd has provided high-quality consistency for the Sydney Thunder, compiling 97 runs across three innings while being dismissed only once. Operating with an excellent average of 48.50 and a healthy strike rate of 136.61, Learoyd has displayed impressive shot selection and composure under pressure. Whether setting up a foundation or accelerating toward the end, her measured approach has given the Thunder middle order crucial stability throughout their fixtures

Jemma Barsby

Jemma Barsby has emerged as the most lethal bowler in the tournament so far, heading the wicket-taking charts with 8 wickets in just 4 matches for the Sydney Thunder. Her standout performance came in a game-changing spell where she picked up 5 for 10, completely unraveling the opposition’s middle order. Delivering her overs with great precision and maintaining a tight economy rate of 5.70, Barsby has proven to be an absolute nightmare for batter lineups looking to hit out

Elsa Hunter

Elsa Hunter has provided class and composure for the Sydney Sixers, scoring 111 runs in three batting outings at an average of 37.00. Highlighted by a top score of 65, Hunter has stroked 15 boundaries while maintaining a solid strike rate above 126. Her ability to build structured innings without getting bogged down has made her one of the most dependable top-order run-getters for the Sixers in this campaign

Courtney Sippel

Courtney Sippel has been a primary strike weapon for the Sydney Sixers, collecting 7 wickets across four matches to sit near the top of the wicket charts. Maintaining a stingy economy rate of 5.20 and boasting best bowling figures of 3 for 13, Sippel has constantly tied batters down while picking up crucial scalps early in the innings and during death overs. Her control and discipline have given the Sixers attack a genuine edge throughout the tournament
