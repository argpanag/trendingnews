Luka Doncic in rarefied air as Lakers entertain Hornets

Luka Doncic in rarefied air as Lakers entertain Hornets

On an eight-game winning streak with the best offense in the NBA, the Los Angeles Lakers return home Tuesday to take on the struggling Charlotte Hornets.

The Lakers finished off a perfect four-game road trip with a 131-123 victory over the Phoenix Suns on Sunday. NBA-leading scorer Luka Doncic led the way with 43 points, while Quentin Grimes stepped into the starting lineup and added 22 points.

The run of success has come with second-leading scorer Austin Reaves missing two of the past three games. Reaves sat out on Sunday with a left heel contusion and bone bruise.

While Doncic is scoring 39.0 points per game, the Lakers are putting up 122.8 points as a team while opening the season with a 12-2 record to keep pace with the San Antonio Spurs for the top mark in the Western Conference.

Doncic has scored at least 40 points in seven of the 12 games he has played this season and his 468 total points are the most in the first 12 games of a season since Wilt Chamberlain in the 1962-63 season.

And with nine assists Sunday, Doncic passed former teammate LeBron James for fourth on the all-time list with 65 games of at least 40 points and five assists.

Those are just a part of the milestones Doncic reached in the game. He also has 30 points in 10 consecutive games. The other Lakers players to accomplish the feat are only some of the best to ever play the game: Elgin Baylor, Jerry West and Kobe Bryant.

Despite all of the early-season success, Doncic is confident the Lakers can be even better.

“Honestly, I think we have a lot of space for improvement,” Doncic said. “Obviously, one thing for now is blowing some leads. But I think (it’s with) every aspect of the game. I feel like we have a lot of improvement left.”

The Hornets will be playing for the second time in three days in the Los Angeles area after falling 113-105 to the Clippers on Sunday for their eighth loss in nine games. The score was tied 90-90 with 9:33 remaining before it got away when the Clippers went on a 10-0 run.

Kon Knueppel has missed 12 of Charlotte’s 13 games with a left hamstring strain.

Coby White scored 21 points for Charlotte, while Naz Reid had 17 and rookie Christian Anderson Jr. added a career-high 16 off the bench.

“Just feeling comfortable on both ends of the floor and being confident,” said Anderson, the No. 18 overall selection out of Texas Tech. “Game by game it’s just getting better for me.”

With the Hornets leading 75-72, head coach Charles Lee was ejected after receiving two technical fouls in 17 seconds of the third quarter.

“It was a little frustration. He wants to be better, and we all want to be better,” said assistant Lamar Skeeter, who took over as acting coach after Lee was ejected. “We had moments where we all kind of got caught up on the wrong thing. We just got to stay focused.”
