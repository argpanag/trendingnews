Quiver AI Summary

Boebort office rejects ethics allegations: A spokesperson for Rep. Lauren Boebert said there is “zero truth” to allegations raised in a Sept. 17 filing to the House Ethics Committee. Boebert said the claims are “utterly false” and described the filing as politically motivated.

Office cites committee rules: The release says House Ethics Committee Rule 15 bars complaints filed within 60 days of an election in which the member is a candidate, and says the filing fell inside that window. It also says no House member certified the submission in writing, which the release says is required for a non-member complaint.

Spokesperson criticizes coverage: The spokesperson said the document should not be treated as an investigation and called it a “political smear.” The release also says the allegations follow “recycled sensational claims” that were previously fact-checked as false.
