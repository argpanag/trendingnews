Scout Selection: Best Fantasy team for Gameweek 11

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

See the dream line-up featuring double-ups from Bournemouth, Brighton, Man City and Spurs

There are double-ups on four sides – Manchester City, Brighton & Hove Albion, AFC Bournemouth and Tottenham Hotspur – in the best team for Gameweek 11 of Fantasy Premier League.

Man City’s Erling Haaland (£15.8m) and Rayan Cherki (£7.8m) host Fulham in the first match of the Gameweek on Saturday lunchtime following the 11:00 GMT deadline.

Later that afternoon, Bournemouth pair Djordje Petrovic (£4.5m) and Marcus Tavernier (£6.7m) entertain Nottingham Forest, while Spurs’ Jan Paul van Hecke (£5.1m) and Mohammed Kudus (£6.6m) are at home to Ipswich Town.

On Sunday, meanwhile, Brighton’s Maxim De Cuyper (£5.5m) and Pascal Gross (£6.3m) visit Hull City. 

Haaland is named as the captain, with De Cuyper selected as the vice-captain. 

Although managers have been advised to move to four or five at the back for their strategies over the season, it’s worth bearing in mind that the Scout Selection is essentially a “Free Hit” side which is specific to each Gameweek and focuses far more on players with attacking upside.

The Bournemouth goalkeeper hosts Forest in fine form, with three clean sheets across the last four Gameweeks. When it comes to conceding big chances – situations where a player is expected to score – the Cherries have the second-best record in the league, with their total of 16 more than only the 12 of Arsenal. 

A visit to Newcastle United gives the Arsenal centre-back the chance to extend his fine away form. Gabriel has blanked just once over his five road trips, producing four clean sheets and six defensive contribution (DC) points to total 32 points. 

Listed as a defender in Fantasy, his role as a midfielder for Brighton has helped De Cuyper top the overall player standings on 75 points. With two goals, four assists and seven clean sheets to his name, the Belgian's potential at both ends of the pitch makes him one of the best alternatives to Haaland for the captaincy.

The Spurs centre-back has amassed 55 points over the last eight Gameweeks due to his multiple routes to points. Van Hecke produced two goals, four clean sheets and eight DC points across that period, averaging 6.9 points per match.

If you’re playing a Free Hit chip and are willing to take a risk on the Frenchman, his recent upturn in goal output suggests he can reward you as a double-up pick alongside Man City team-mate Haaland. Cherki has scored in three of his last four league starts, totalling 28 points.

Facing a Hull side who are without a clean sheet over their last seven matches, the midfielder could also be in line for another big haul.

Despite his back-to-back blanks in the last two Gameweeks, very few of Haaland’s huge 78 per cent ownership will be deterred from captaining him at home to Fulham. Having returned to form for Norway over the international break, you can’t overlook the Man City forward for the captaincy right now.
