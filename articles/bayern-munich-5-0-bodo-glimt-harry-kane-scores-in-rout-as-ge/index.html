Olise scores twice as Bayern thrash Bodo/Glimt

England captain Harry Kane scored his 55th Champions League goal and Michael Olise struck twice as Bayern Munich began their campaign by comfortably beating Bodo/Glimt.

Vincent Kompany's side will be looking to improve on last season, when they reached the semi-finals.

They made the ideal start to the league phase with this victory against Norwegians Bodo/Glimt, who put up stubborn resistance in the first half but were then overwhelmed.

Jamal Musiala - making his first start of the season - struck early in the second period when Kane's shot deflected kindly for him.

That was a historic goal as it was Bayern's 900th in Europe's premier club competition, with only Real Madrid having scored more.

Kane then got his goal when he headed in a free-kick, taking his haul to 55 in 71 Champions League appearances.

It means he is the third fastest player, in terms of games played, to reach 55 goals in the competition - after Erling Haaland in 49 matches and Ruud van Nistelrooy in 70.

It was also his 34th European goal in 39 games for Bayern Munich.

Bodo/Glimt rarely threatened but did go close to a spectacular goal midway through the second half when Andreas Helmersen spotted Manuel Neuer off his line and went for the lob from halfway, but the ball landed on top of the net.

Alphonso Davies added a third for Bayern in style with a rocket of a shot from the edge of the box, before Olise sent a neat finish into the top corner.

A difficult night got even worse for Bodo/Glimt in the final few minutes when Odin Bjortuft was sent off for a foul on Olise, who got his second goal in stoppage time.

Match Facts

  • Bayern Munich have faced Norwegian sides eight times previously in major European competition without losing (W6 D2) – their most recent meeting was in a 1-1 draw with Rosenborg in the first group stage of the UEFA Champions League in November 2000.

  • Bodø/Glimt are winless in all three of their major European matches against German teams, losing 6-1 on aggregate to Werder Bremen in the 1999-00 UEFA Cup (0-5 home, 1-1 away) and drawing 2-2 with Borussia Dortmund in last season’s UEFA Champions League.

  • Bayern Munich have won their opening game in each of their last 23 major European campaigns (22 in the UEFA Champions League, 1 in the UEFA Cup), stretching back to 2003-04. Spanish side Deportivo de La Coruña were the last to stop Bayern winning, beating them 3-2 in 2002-03.

  • Last season was Bodø/Glimt’s first ever UEFA Champions League campaign, winning five of their 12 matches (D3 L4) and reaching the last 16. All five of their wins came in succession, between January and March.

  • Bayern Munich have won 36 of their last 38 home UEFA Champions League group/league phase matches (D2) since losing 3-2 to Manchester City back in December 2013 under Pep Guardiola.

  • Bodø/Glimt had a five-game winning streak in the UEFA Champions League last season – this was the longest run by a team from outside of Europe’s big five leagues since Ajax in 2021-22, who won six in a row.

  • Only Barcelona in 1999-00 and Paris SG last season (both 45) have scored more goals in a single season of the UEFA Champions League than Bayern Munich in 2025-26 (43). Bayern scored four or more goals in six different matches, the joint most of any side in a European Cup season (since 1955), along with Barcelona in 1999-00 and Real Madrid in 2013-14.

  • Since the 2023-24 season when he joined Bayern Munich, Harry Kane has scored more UEFA Champions League goals than any other player (33). Kane has scored in his last seven appearances – Robert Lewandowski was the last player to score in more consecutively, netting in nine in a row between November 2020 and November 2021 also for Bayern Munich.

  • Michael Olise created 33 chances for Bayern Munich in the UEFA Champions League last season, with only Real Madrid's Arda Güler creating more (34). His six assists were the joint most by a Bayern player in a season, along with Hasan Salihamidzic in 2001-02 and Leroy Sané in 2021-22.

  • Jens Petter Hauge scored six goals for Bodø/Glimt in the UEFA Champions League last season, the most by any player for a Norwegian club in a single edition.
