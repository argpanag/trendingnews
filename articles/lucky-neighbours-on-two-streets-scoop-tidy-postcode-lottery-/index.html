Lucky neighbours on two streets scoop tidy Postcode Lottery prize

TheTools · 26.09.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

NEIGHBOURS in two Rudheath streets are celebrating after their postcode came up trumps in yesterday’s Postcode Lottery draw.

In the draw on Friday, September 25, subscribers who live in Foxglove Way and Mulberry Court and have the postcode CW9 7XT scooped £1,000 for every winning ticket.

The prize means neighbours could be raising a toast together after finding out they had struck lucky.

The Postcode Lottery is a subscription game which aims to raise money for charities while also bringing cash prizes to homes across the UK.

Prizes are announced every day of the month – with £30,000 jackpots, and £1,000 prizes for 20 different postcodes each day.

Players sign up with their postcode and pay £12.50 per month for a chance to win when they are automatically entered into a draw.

A minimum of 33 per cent from each ticket goes to charity, and players have raised more than £950 million for more than 9,000 charities and good causes since 2005.
