Springfield Man Charged With 20 Crimes Following Investigation Into Secret Recording Device

TheTools · 12.09.2026 00:02 · source · permalink

BELCHERTOWN — Jovante Gonzalez, 22, of Springfield, was arraigned today in Eastern Hampshire District Court on 20 crimes following an investigation by the Amherst College Police Department (ACPD).

Gonzalez is charged with the following:

  • Photographing an Unsuspecting Nude Person (9 counts)
  • Unlawful Wiretap (9 counts)
  • Criminal Harassment
  • Trespassing

Gonzalez is alleged to have installed a secret recording device inside a gender-neutral bathroom in the Amherst College Science Center. ACPD discovered the device in mid-April 2026 and their subsequent investigation led to the issuance of an arrest warrant for Gonzalez on September 10. Gonzalez was arrested on that warrant later that same day by the Springfield Police Department.

Assistant District Attorney Alexa Pascucci represented the Commonwealth during the arraignment. Gonzalez was represented by Attorney Jeffrey Burnstein of Springfield. The arraignment was heard before District Court Judge Rebecca Michaels.

Gonzalez was released on personal recognizance with conditions to have no contact with the victim(s) or potential Commonwealth witnesses, undergo a mental health evaluation and comply with recommended treatment, stay off Amherst College property, and be monitored by GPS.

Gonzalez is scheduled to be back in court on November 4 for a pre-trial hearing.

Gonzalez has been trespassed from Amherst College and all Amherst College properties.

The investigation remains active and ongoing. Anyone concerned they may have been recorded by the device is encouraged to contact ACPD at (413) 542-2291. The Amherst College Office of Civil Rights and Title IX and the Office of Student Affairs are also available to assist students who may have been affected by the incident.

All defendants are presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
