Isa Briones, Joshua Henry, Sarah Pidgeon Make the 2026 Time100 Next List

TheTools · 01.10.2026 08:35 · source · permalink

See why Lin-Manuel Miranda, Sarah Michelle Geller, and more think these theatre stars have earned a spot for the annual honor.

September 30, 2026 By Margaret Hall

Time Magazine's annual Time100 Next list of emerging leaders in the world is out, and among those recognized are stage faces Isa Briones (Just in Time, Hadestown), Tony winner Joshua Henry (Ragtime, Carousel), and Sarah Pidgeon (Stereophonic).

The list features introductions written by established luminaries, highlighting the potential of each selected candidate. Katherine LaNasa honored Briones, Lin-Manuel Miranda honored Henry, and Sarah Michelle Gellar honored Sarah Pidgeon.

Said LaNasa, "Isa Briones is the coolest kid I know. The literal funny bone of The Pitt (sorry, pun intended), she moves between vulnerability, deadpan comedy, and gritty “don’t even think about messing with me” moments with the ease of an actor far beyond her years. And she can sing like nobody’s business. This spring’s Broadway turn as Connie Francis in Just in Time left me wondering, “Is there anything she can’t do!?” Her fierce dedication to accurate portrayals of Filipino women onscreen is an inspiration to many. But I personally admire Isa because she has the balls to be herself. She dresses in her own unique style, speaks truth to power, and she is kind. She shows us all that you don’t have to shape-shift to win."

Said Miranda, "Everyone who meets Josh Henry becomes a Josh Henry evangelist. Robin de Jesús was the first to tell me: “Write his name down. He’s the TRUTH. You HAVE to see him for In the Heights.” A month later, Josh walked in, quiet and confident. He started singing. His humanity and virtuosity laid bare, all in that voice. The Truth. My form of evangelism has been casting him in almost everything I’ve worked on: In the Heights; two companies of Hamilton; tick, tick…BOOM!—while cheering for him on Broadway in Violet, Shuffle Along, Carousel, and so many more. This season, The Truth transcended the arts page, as clips of his blazing portrayal of Coalhouse Walker Jr. in Ragtime made the rounds. TikToks of folks from all walks of life, melting at the sound of his booming baritone. At last The Truth had found a role that lined up perfectly with the Josh we have been lucky enough to know in life: a singular voice and a paragon of decency. Write his name down. He’s the TRUTH. You HAVE to see him."

Said Gellar, "By definition, an It girl is someone with an ineffable quality—culturally relevant, with that unique blend of beauty, confidence, and magnetism that can’t be taught or bought. So it is only fitting that Sarah Pidgeon was chosen to embody the ultimate It girl, Carolyn Bessette Kennedy. Like Carolyn, Sarah is one of one. A Carnegie Mellon graduate, she effortlessly jumps between stage and screen.

"Her Tony-nominated performance in Stereophonic showcased both her powerhouse vocals and her heartbreaking vulnerability. And her role in this year’s Love Story not only earned Sarah a much deserved Emmy nomination, but also introduced a global audience to how truly captivating she is.

"Whether she’s delivering a 17-page scene or smoking a cigarette in silence, you simply can’t take your eyes off her. Just as Carolyn remained her authentic self despite all eyes being on her, Sarah possesses that same rare individuality. She is one of the kindest, most gifted performers and carries herself with such effortless poise, she seems as though she were plucked from another era. She is timeless. And that’s exactly what makes an It girl impossible to manufacture. Sarah Pidgeon simply has it in spades."

See the full 2026 list at Time.com.
