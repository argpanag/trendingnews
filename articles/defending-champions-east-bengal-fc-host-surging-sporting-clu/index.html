Defending Champions East Bengal FC Host Surging Sporting Club Delhi In Kolkata

The Red and Gold Brigade Return To Salt Lake For Their First Home Fixture Of The Season, While The Visitors Aim To Extend Their Winning Run.


Kolkata: Defending champions East Bengal FC will play at home for the first time this season when they take on a resurgent Sporting Club Delhi at the Salt Lake stadium, in Kolkata, on Friday, 20 November. The Red and Gold Brigade were on the road for three straight games, and a fixture at home couldn’t have come at a better time.

East Bengal have made an explosive start to their title defence, scoring 12 goals in their first three league matches. Antonio López Habas’s men dismantled Churchill Brothers FC Goa 4-0 in their opener before cruising past Inter Kashi FC 5-1. However, that blistering start hit a sudden roadblock in Chennai, where a defensive lapse saw them fall 5-3 to Chennaiyin FC. Habas described the performance as an “accident”, admitting that his team had lost its playing identity. “After the match against Chennai, we lost the idea, we lost the style and for that we have to recover immediately,” he said.

Despite the setback, East Bengal remain the league’s highest-scoring side. Aké Loba and Dani Ramírez have scored three goals apiece, with Loba also contributing two assists. Bipin Singh has added two goals. With Anwar Ali unavailable, Habas believes the situation presents an opportunity for another player to step up. The East Bengal head coach has also stressed the importance of squad depth, saying: “We need one squad with good players in all positions on the pitch.”

Sporting Club Delhi, meanwhile, arrive in Kolkata with wind in their sails after shaking off a rocky opening stretch. Following narrow setbacks against Bengaluru FC and Mohun Bagan Super Giant, alongside a hard-fought draw against FC Goa, Tomasz Tchórz’s side ignited their campaign with a stirring 3-2 comeback triumph over Mumbai City FC before dispatching Inter Kashi FC 3-0. Sitting fourth with seven points from five matches, the visitors hold a narrow one-point cushion over sixth-placed East Bengal FC, though the champions boast two games in hand.

Tchórz acknowledged East Bengal’s history and stature, but made his own ambitions clear. “We are going to Kolkata with the purpose to win the game,” he said, underlining his attacking intent: “We want to be offensive. We want to create a lot of chances and score a lot of goals.”

Juan Peña leads Delhi’s scoring charts with three goals, while Carlos Fortes and Rodrigo da Silva have scored twice each. Rodrigo has also registered two assists. With 10 goals in five matches, Delhi have shown they can pose a threat going forward.

Recent meetings have favoured East Bengal. They recorded a 4-1 victory over Sporting Club Delhi in February 2026, having also won 2-0 in the previous league season. The visitors will hope their improved form can make this a different contest.

Three points on Friday would lift East Bengal FC to nine points and vault them into the top four, while another statement result would allow SC Delhi to continue their charge to a playoff spot.

The match kicks off at 5:00 PM IST on Friday at the Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan, Kolkata. Fans can watch live on the MG ISL YouTube channel and SonyLIV.
