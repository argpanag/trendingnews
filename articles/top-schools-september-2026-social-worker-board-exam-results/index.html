TOP SCHOOLS: September 2026 Social Worker Board Exam Results

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:41 · source · permalink

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) releases the top-performing schools and a summary of schools’ performance in the September 2026 Social Worker Licensure Exams (SWLE) three (3) working days after the last day of exams.

The Social Worker board exam was conducted on September 9-11, 2026, at PRC testing centers located in NCR, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, and Zamboanga.

The Board for Social Workers is led by its Chairperson, Hon. Evangeline M. Guinto, and its members, Hon. Fe J. Sinsona, Hon. Maria Lyra T. Del Castillo, Hon. Vincent Andrew T. Leyson, and Hon. Jerusa C. Arnante-Homo.

Release of Results

According to the Professional Regulation Commission’s official 2026 schedule of licensure examinations, the results of the September 2026 Social Workers Licensure Examination are targeted for release on September 16, 2026.

The SWLE was conducted from September 9 to 11, 2026. However, results may be released earlier or later than the target date without prior notice.

During the previous SWLE held in September 2025, PRC released the results three (3) working days after the last day of examinations. We advise examinees to bookmark this page and stay connected with us on our social media channels for the latest and most accurate result announcements.

Schedule of Registration

The PRC will announce the online registration schedule for issuing Professional Identification Cards (ID) and Certificates of Registration to new Social Workers after the release of the September 2026 SWLE results.

Successful examinees are advised to register through the official PRC online services portal at https://online.prc.gov.ph/ and follow the instructions for initial registration.

Registrants are generally required to prepare the following:

  1. Notice of Admission (NOA) – for identification purposes
  2. Completed Oath Form or Panunumpa ng Propesyonal
  3. Two (2) passport-sized ID photos on a white background with complete name tag
  4. Two (2) sets of documentary stamps
  5. One (1) short brown envelope

Successful examinees should also follow any additional registration requirements announced by PRC together with the official September 2026 results.

Verification of Rating

Online verification of ratings and examination results for the September 2026 Social Workers Licensure Examination will be available after the results are officially released.

Examinees may check their ratings through the official PRC Verification of Rating service.

To verify their examination results, candidates are generally required to provide information such as the examination name, examination date, application number, first name, last name, and date of birth.

Oath-Taking Ceremonies

The PRC will announce the schedule and venue for the oath-taking ceremony of the new Social Workers after the release of the September 2026 SWLE results.

Successful examinees who wish to attend a face-to-face mass oath-taking should follow the registration instructions and deadline stated in the official PRC announcement.

Those who cannot attend the face-to-face mass oath-taking may participate in a virtual oath-taking or request a special oath-taking, subject to the schedules and guidelines announced by PRC.

For virtual oath-taking, passers may secure an online appointment through https://online.prc.gov.ph/ and select “e-OATH” as the transaction when a schedule becomes available.

After completing the oath-taking requirement, successful examinees may proceed with Initial Registration according to PRC’s instructions.
