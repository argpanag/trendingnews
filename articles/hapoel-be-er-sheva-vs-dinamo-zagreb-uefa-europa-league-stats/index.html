Match Facts

  • Hapoel Be'er Sheva and Dinamo Zagreb met in the 2018-19 UEFA Champions League qualifying phase, with the Croatians progressing 7-2 on aggregate (5-0 home, 2-2 away).

  • Although Hapoel Be'er Sheva have only won five of their 20 UEFA Europa League matches (D3 L12), three have been on MD1 of the group stages in 2016-17, 2017-18 and 2020-21.

  • Dinamo Zagreb have won their opening game in three of their last four major European campaigns, beating Chelsea in 2022-23, Astana in 2023-24 and Fenerbahçe in 2025-26; the exception was a 9-2 defeat to Bayern Munich in 2024-25.

  • Dinamo Zagreb conceded 22 goals in last season's UEFA Europa League, their joint most ever in a single major European season; they also shipped 22 in the 2011-12 UEFA Champions League.

  • Dion Beljo has netted 10 goals in 18 major European appearances across the last two seasons (5 in 10 for Rapid in 2024-25, 5 in 8 for Dinamo Zagreb in 2025-26), though all five of his goals in last season’s UEFA Europa League were scored at home.
