Maher Cup galloper faces final inspection

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:43 · source · permalink

Ryan Reynolds

All 22 final acceptors for Saturday’s $6.55 million Sportsbet Caulfield Cup have been inspected by Racing Victoria veterinarians, with Saint George the only runner requiring a further examination on race day.

Green Spaces and second emergency Juja Kibo were inspected on Friday morning following their arrival in Melbourne from Sydney and were passed suitable to race.

That completed the inspection process for the entire Caulfield Cup field after the remaining 20 acceptors were examined on Thursday and also passed suitable to race.

The Ciaron Maher-trained Saint George will, however, be re-inspected after arriving on course at Caulfield on Saturday following a re-plating.

The additional examination means Saint George will need to satisfy RV veterinarians on race day before taking his place in the $6.55 million Group 1 over 2400 metres.

Bjorn Baker-trained Green Spaces and Maher’s Juja Kibo require no further veterinary examination following Friday morning’s inspections.
