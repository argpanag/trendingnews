Sweden - Building on recent success

Here is a closer look at Sweden’s current situation and their performance leading up to this match. We will analyse the team’s recent momentum and their most recent results as they prepare to face Poland in this crucial UEFA Nations League game.

Recent form

Sweden kicked off their UEFA Nations League campaign with a solid 2-1 victory over Romania on home soil. This positive start contrasts with their mixed fortunes at the recent World Cup. While they managed a big 5-1 win against Tunisia, they suffered heavy defeats to the Netherlands and France, exiting in the round of 32.

This inconsistency is reflected in their overall record of nine wins and eight losses in their last 21 matches. However, they have shown they can step up when it matters, as seen in their World Cup qualification campaign. They secured their spot by defeating Ukraine and then Poland in the final.

Heading into this clash, Sweden will be buoyed by that opening Nations League win and the advantage of playing at home again. The fact that they have a fully fit squad with no injuries is another significant boost. These factors combined should give them plenty of confidence for the upcoming Sweden vs. Poland fixture.

Poland - Looking for a victory

Let’s review Poland’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum and recent form, including their performance in past matches, for their trip to Sweden.

Recent form

Poland started their UEFA Nations League campaign with a goalless draw at home against Bosnia & Herzegovina, earning them one point. This result means they are still seeking their first win in the competition as they prepare for this away fixture. Their recent competitive form has been inconsistent, notably including a 2-1 victory over Albania in the World Cup qualifiers.

Overall, the Polish side has shown variable form, recording nine wins, seven losses, and five draws in their last 21 matches across all competitions. This mixed run features a friendly draw against Nigeria, two draws against the Netherlands in World Cup qualifiers, and a 2-0 defeat to Ukraine. Such fluctuating performances make it challenging to anticipate their approach in this encounter.

The memory of their 3-2 defeat to Sweden in the World Cup qualification final will likely be a strong motivator for Poland. Crucially, the visitors arrive with a full-strength squad and no reported injuries, providing a significant boost as they aim for a positive result in this Sweden vs. Poland fixture.
