Supreme Court Proposes Panel on Assault on Women Journalists, Bars Arrest

The Supreme Court of India has indicated that it will constitute a committee to investigate allegations of assault on female journalists by police officers during protests. The Chief Justice directed senior advocate Indira Jaising to submit documents in two sealed envelopes containing the names of allegedly assaulted female journalists along with proposed retired female judges and police officers for the panel. The top court also directed the Delhi Police not to arrest a woman journalist against whom an FIR was filed under the SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act by an Assistant Commissioner of Police. The court is considering framing guidelines to govern such incidents and distinguish journalists at protest sites.

Supreme Court Pulls Up INDIA Bloc Leaders Over October 12 March, Commotion

The Supreme Court has sought an explanation from opposition INDIA bloc leaders, including Rahul Gandhi, Mallikarjun Kharge, Akhilesh Yadav, and Mahua Moitra, over their march and the subsequent commotion inside the court premises on October 12. The top court questioned the conduct and intention of the leaders and co-petitioners who entered the premises with a crowd after the registry had closed, leading to disruption at the filing centre. The hearing involved multiple petitions concerning the protest, alleged illegal detentions, metro closures, railway disruptions, and attacks on journalists. While issuing notices in the matters to allow parties to respond, the bench underscored that it was not condoning the actions that transpired during the march.

Centre Mandates Caller Name Display on 4G, 5G Networks to Curb Spam Calls

In a major change to caller identification, the government has directed telecom service providers, including Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea, and BSNL, to display a caller's registered name alongside their mobile number. Set to launch next month, the Calling Name Presentation service will be introduced on 4G and 5G wireless networks, pulling user identities directly from subscriber databases. The initiative aims to help mobile phone users identify incoming unfamiliar numbers and reduce promotional and spam calls from sectors like banking, insurance, and automobiles. Implementation on 2G, 3G, and landline networks will be considered separately, while guidelines regarding privacy options for callers remain under development.

Pakistani Fake News Account Banned on X Over Fabricated Videos on Delhi Protests and Rahul Gandhi

A notorious social media handle operating under the name Anushi Tiwari Indian has been banned on platform X in India following an India Today investigation. The Pakistan-based account circulated inflammatory content, fabricated posts, and old videos from 2019 during opposition and Congress protests in Delhi. The profile falsely claimed that Delhi Police opened fire, that 13 people were killed, and that Rahul Gandhi was injured in firing. Delhi Police Cyber Cell confirmed that the IP address traced back to Pakistan. Authorities and intelligence agencies have identified several similar Pakistani propaganda accounts that will face bans as ongoing action against misinformation networks continues.
