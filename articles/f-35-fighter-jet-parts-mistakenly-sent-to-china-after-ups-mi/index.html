F-35 fighter jet parts mistakenly sent to China after UPS misses warning email | Fox Business Video

TheTools · 08.10.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

F-35 fighter jet parts were mistakenly sent to China after a UPS employee missed a critical export warning email. The Pentagon and State Department are investigating how sensitive military stealth technology was shipped.

A missed export warning email at UPS leads to highly sensitive F-35 fighter jet parts being mistakenly shipped to China. Gerri Willis reports on how the military components, which included critical stealth canopy technology, were rerouted through Hong Kong instead of South Korea. The Pentagon and State Department are currently investigating this major security breach.
