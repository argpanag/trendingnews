Unranked Auburn upsets top-ranked Georgia with walk-off field goal, marking wild feat not seen in 36 years

TheTools · 18.10.2026 10:38 · source · permalink

Georgia had won 53 straight games against unranked opponents

Being ranked No. 1 in the country might actually be a curse.

For the first time since 1990, the No. 1 team in the nation lost to an unranked opponent in consecutive weeks after Georgia fell at home to Auburn, 27-24.

It looked like all Georgia early, as they got out to a 14-0 lead in the first quarter. But the Dogs would not score on their next five drives, punting three times, missing a field goal and throwing a pick. The Tigers used that all to their advantage and scored 24 unanswered points to put Georgia on upset alert.

The Bulldogs entered the fourth quarter down 10, but an early field goal cut the deficit, and they were able to force two Auburn punts. After the second, they embarked on a 13-play, 94-yard touchdown drive to tie the game.

Auburn was stuffed and had to punt the ball away for a third consecutive drive, giving Georgia the ball at its own 39 with over a minute to go and all the momentum. They got a gift with a defensive pass interference on the first play.

But suddenly, all that momentum shifted.

On the next play, Gunnar Stockton lost a fumble, giving Auburn and all three of its timeouts the ball with 58 seconds at its own 44.

The Tigers got 22 yards on three straight carries and then drew a roughing the passer penalty, putting themselves in chip-shot range.

Alex McPherson came on for a 34-yard field goal, and he drilled it to complete the upset.

Texas lost the Red River Rivalry to Oklahoma last week, causing it to drop all the way to No. 7, in case you are wondering how far Georgia may fall.

This is also Georgia's first unranked opponent loss at home under Kirby Smart since 2019.

Georgia, now 5-1, had taken the top spot after it defeated Alabama last week in a dogfight, but the spot is now up for grabs for squads like No. 2 Notre Dame, No. 3 Miami and No. 4 Ohio State.
