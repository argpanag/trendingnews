As the top two women's tennis players in the world—Aryna Sabalenka and Elena Rybakina—aim to win the final Grand Slam of the year, celebrities have descended on Arthur Ashe Stadium for the afternoon match. "Everything that people associate with New York—the passion and the energy and the excitement—feels very much endemic to the U.S. Open itself," Nicole Kankam, the Managing Director, Pro Tennis Marketing and Entertainment for the USTA, told Town & Country. "It's loud, it's tough, it's hot, but it's also everything that makes it great and exciting."

A-Listers have increasingly become part of the atmosphere of the tournament. Among those spotted arriving this afternoon include Amy Adams, Fran Drescher, and Billie Jean King herself. Here, see all the celebrities in attendance for the U.S. Open women's finals:

King, a four-time U.S. Open singles champion, was all smiles at she arrived.
