EGT Digital expands Philippine offering with new PAGCOR accreditations | Yogonet International

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:31 · source · permalink

EGT Digital is expanding its offering in the Philippines after its local entity, EGT Philippines, secured new accreditations from the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR).

The approvals cover casino game and sports betting content provision, along with game aggregation. PAGCOR’s list of accredited gaming affiliates now names EGT Philippines as an approved provider for the three categories.

The approvals allow EGT Digital to offer its proprietary casino games and Sportsbook solution to PAGCOR-licensed operators, broadening its product offering in the Philippine market.

EGT Philippines can also introduce its game aggregation solution, allowing operators to access content from multiple providers through a single integration. This expands EGT Digital’s offering beyond its proprietary products and gives operators access to a wider range of casino content.

"These accreditations are an important milestone for EGT Philippines and reflect our long-term commitment to the local market,” said Mariana Manchina, Director of EGT Philippines. “They create new opportunities for us to grow our presence, deepen our partnerships with local operators, and contribute to the continued development of the regulated gaming sector in the country."

The new accreditations bring EGT Digital’s casino content, Sportsbook technology, and aggregation solution under its offering for PAGCOR-licensed operators in the Philippines.
