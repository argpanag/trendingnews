Davis Cup Day 1 Predictions Including Moller vs Dimitrov

TheTools · 18.09.2026 13:29 · source · permalink

We have a packed slate of Davis Cup ties ahead, as players detour towards international tennis in between what is a packed ATP tour schedule. This article features arguably one of the ties of the round, as Grigor Dimitrov’s crafty game takes on the counterpunching of Elmer Moller. But that is far from all, with the return of Holger Rune back on court set to be another prominent story. As always, we here at LWOT will be predicting each and every match, but who do you think will win?

Davis Cup Day 1 Predictions

Alex Knaff vs Zsombor Piros

Head-to-head: First meeting

This matchup is a great opportunity for either player, as both of them are ranked outside the Top 100. Alex Knaff plays his best tennis on hard courts and will have the added benefit of playing in front of his home fans here. But Zsombor Piros is the more solid player here. If the Hungarian implements his game from the back of the court, he should win this match.
Prediction: Piros in 3

Gustavo Heide vs Stan Wawrinka

Head-to-head: First meeting

In what is his last year on Tour, Stan Wawrinka is set to take on the aggressive game of Gustavo Heide at the Davis Cup. Although this will be played in Brazil, I expect Wawrinka to enjoy the atmosphere of this occasion given the circumstances. On an indoor hard court, this match will likely become a battle of aggression from the baseline. I expect a back-and-forth contest, but one in which the more experienced player manages to handle the pressure points better and gets the win.
Prediction: Wawrinka in 3

Holger Rune vs Iliyan Radulov

Head-to-head: First meeting

After almost a full year of absence due to an Achilles injury, Holger Rune is back on a tennis court, with his first assignment being to tackle the aggressive game of Iliyan Radulov. Radulov will want to test Rune’s movement early on and take advantage of any court rust the Dane has here. But in front of a home crowd, and given how much he has practiced to be here, I think Rune should grow into the match and eventually come out on top.

Prediction: Rune in 3

Elmer Moller vs Grigor Dimitrov

Head-to-head: First meeting

This matchup pairs the all-court variety of Grigor Dimitrov against the baseline counterpunching game of Elmer Moller. If Moller makes this contest physical, he can push Dimitrov to the limit on the back of the energy from a home crowd. But Dimitrov is not only the more experienced player here, but also has the higher floor. If the Bulgarian serves well and uses his variety, he should win this contest.
Prediction: Dimitrov in 2
