    HT: Hibernian 1-2 Celtic

    Pat Bonner
    Former Celtic goalkeeper on BBC Sportsound

    Hibs lack the confidence to go and exploit Celtic when they win the ball. They go, and then turn back. You can see and hear the frustration from the crowd.

    They don't have that willingness to get a shot away or play a penetrating pass. They just want to keep the ball rather than take the risk.

    The conditions are poor. It looks nice and calm but the wind is up and it's so difficult for players, especially for defenders.

  4. Kerjota, McPake and Kingsley start for Heartspublished at 12:58 GMT
    12:58 GMT

    Motherwell v Hearts (14:00)

    Three changes for Hearts from Thursday's trip to Turkey, two of them potentially enforced.

    Christian Borchgrevink seemed to suffer a hamstring injury in Trabzon and he's replaced at right-back by stand-in captain Stephen Kingsley. Rogers Mato seems to remain at left-back.

    The other two changes come on the wing. Calvin Miller was a rumoured doubt and Sabah Kerjota makes a rare start in his place.

    Josh McPake returns to the side after being rested, coming in for Sabri Guendouz.

    Miller is on the bench at least, as is Aron Benjaminsen - an 18-year-old summer signing from the Faroe Islands. Laurent Mendy is only fit enough for the bench.
