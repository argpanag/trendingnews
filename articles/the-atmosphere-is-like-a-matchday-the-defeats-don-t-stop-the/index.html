Despite a complicated start, the Franchi stadium could be at capacity.

Despite a weak standing, temperatures expected to be above average, and a less than stellar fixture (despite a solid friendship with the Granata fans that has lasted for decades), the Viola fans have decided to turn out for today's match at the Franchi stadium against Torino . As of yesterday, over 21 tickets had been sold for the match against Abate's team, confirming the enthusiasm still palpable around the Viola and the confidence that De Gea and his teammates will be able to reverse their trend this afternoon. The Curva Ferrovia is sold out for today, while a few hundred tickets for two sections of the covered grandstand (the armchairs and the lateral stand) remain on sale.

A substantial response, however, is poised to arrive, after this Saturday, also next week, when Fiorentina will be playing away against Venezia on Friday at 8:45 PM (on a day that will be truly chaotic for the Veneto capital, given that it will be the penultimate day of the Film Festival). Despite the restrictions imposed by the GOS (namely, the possibility for Viola supporters to purchase tickets only in the away section of the Penzo stadium and only with a fan card), the 1.000 tickets made available by Venezia for organized cheering are expected to sell out quickly (at least that's what the fans are predicting, hoping that a positive result today will fuel their enthusiasm).

The Ministry has also imposed restrictions for the Coppa Italia derby against Pisa: Nerazzurri fans will only be able to purchase tickets for the away section and only if they have a valid membership card.
