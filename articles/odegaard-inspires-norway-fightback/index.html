Odegaard inspires Norway fightback

Martin Odegaard delivered a captain's performance to help Norway come from 2-0 down to beat Denmark 3-2 and book a spot in the Nations League quarter-finals.

After falling two goals down, Odegaard chased down goalkeeper Mads Hermansen, forcing the ball into the path of Erling Haaland, who tapped home.

Heading into the final 10 minutes and needing a win to qualify for the last eight, Odegaard fired in an equaliser before Haaland smashed in a stoppage-time penalty to turn things around.

Our skipper then led the celebrations, banging the drum in front of the travelling support as they performed their famous row celebration that was seen over and over again at last summer's World Cup.

Viktor Gyokeres' scoring run may have ended for Sweden, but he did provide an assist in a 1-1 draw with Poland in Warsaw.

After falling behind, Viktor set up Hugo Larsson to equalise as Sweden rounded off their campaign undefeated and secured back-to-back promotions.

Over in Asia, Bruno Guimaraes captained Brazil for the first time as his country ran out 7-0 winners versus Singapore.

After producing two assists in their 3-2 defeat against Japan, Bruno dished out another assist for Vinicius Jr. to get the scoring going at the National Stadium.

Gabriel Magalhaes was also starting his second game in a row and he also provided an assist for Chelsea's Estevao in the second half after Vinicius had already completed his hat-trick. 

Endrick, Rayan and an own goal rounded off the scoring as Gabi came off after 60 minutes and Bruno did the same a minute later. 

Piero Hincapie rounded off the international window by playing 64 minutes in a 2-0 victory over Bolivia in Quito.
