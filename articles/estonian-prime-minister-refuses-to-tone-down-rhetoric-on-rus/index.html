Estonian prime minister refuses to tone down rhetoric on Russia despite U.S. requests: ‘Putin is a dictator who kills women and children’ — Meduza

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:56 · source · permalink

Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal told Delfi that he intends to keep speaking openly about the threat Russia poses.

Reuters reported that Pentagon officials urged the Baltic states, including Estonia, to avoid overly sharp statements toward Moscow. Michal told reporters he had received no such requests directly from the U.S.

“I’m not going to hold back. Putin is a dictator who kills women and children. And he will keep doing so until he is forced to change course,” Michal said. Currying favor with dictators, he said, leads only to humiliation and ultimately does nothing to protect freedom.

Estonia is doing its part to defend NATO’s eastern flank, allocating almost 7% of its GDP to the alliance’s needs, he said. A willingness to “call a wolf a wolf, rather than a forest dog” is also necessary for ensuring security. Michal added that Estonia’s firm stance and clear statements “give Russia no peace.”

Reuters earlier reported, citing sources, that Pentagon officials had approached Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia with a request to tone down their rhetoric toward Moscow.

Elbridge Colby, head of the Pentagon’s policy directorate, cited the phrase “cornered like a rat” as an example of inappropriate rhetoric. Michal used it to describe Putin in late September while discussing a rise in sabotage incidents.
