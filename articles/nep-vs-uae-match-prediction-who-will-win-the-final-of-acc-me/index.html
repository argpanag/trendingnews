NEP vs UAE Match Prediction: Who Will Win the Final of ACC Men's Premier Cup 2026?

Nepal and the UAE are set to meet in the final of the ACC Men’s Premier Cup 2026, with both teams entering the title clash after dominant semi-final victories. As both sides are coming off convincing knockout wins, the final promises to be a closely contested battle.

The match also carries added importance as the winner of this clash will secure a place in the 2027 Asia Cup against some of the best cricket teams, giving both sides plenty of motivation to produce their best cricket in the final.

NEP vs UAE Match Preview

Nepal booked their place in the final with an impressive 87-run win over Oman. After being bowled out for 147, Nepal’s bowlers completely turned the game around, restricting Oman to just 60. Dipendra Singh Airee was the standout performer with both bat and ball, scoring a half-century before contributing three wickets, while Sandeep Lamichhane also picked up three wickets.

UAE, meanwhile, produced an even more comfortable result in their semi-final against Hong Kong, securing a seven-wicket victory. Hong Kong managed only 70 before UAE completed the chase in just 11.1 overs.

NEP vs UAE Pitch Report

As per the NEP vs UAE match prediction, the Bayuemas Oval in Kuala Lumpur is likely to provide a competitive contest between bat and ball in the ACC Men’s Premier Cup 2026 final. The surface can offer some help to spinners, particularly as the game progresses. A score around the 200-run mark could give the team batting first a useful platform.

NEP vs UAE Weather Report

The weather in Kuala Lumpur is expected to be hot and humid, with plenty of cloud cover around the match. Rain remains a possibility, particularly during the afternoon, so interruptions cannot be completely ruled out. If the rain stays away, the warm conditions should allow for a full contest.

NEP vs UAE Head-to-Head Stats & Records

Nepal have the upper hand in their ODI rivalry with the United Arab Emirates, winning 11 of their 19 meetings, while UAE have secured eight victories. Neither side has had a no-result in these encounters.

NEP vs UAE Predicted Top Batter

  • Dipendra Singh Airee: He could be Nepal’s key batter in the final. Airee has been in good touch during the tournament and showed his value in the semi-final against Oman with an impressive 51 off 76 deliveries and was the leading scorer for the team.

NEP vs UAE Predicted Top Bowler

  • Sandeep Lamichhane: He is Nepal’s leading bowling threat for the final. The leg-spinner delivered an excellent spell against Oman in the semi-final, returning figures of 3/9 and playing a major role in Nepal’s convincing victory.
