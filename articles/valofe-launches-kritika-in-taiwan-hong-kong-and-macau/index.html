VALOFE Launches 'Kritika' in Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau

VALOFE announced on September 10 that it has launched official service for the action RPG 'Kritika' in Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau.

Prior to the official service launch, VALOFE conducted a test with local players to inspect the game's content and operational system. According to VALOFE, during the test that concluded last July, the day-one retention rate averaged around 65%, with an average play time of approximately four hours.

The official service incorporates improvements based on feedback from test participants. Key systems were upgraded, including enhancing overall game accessibility and balancing content.

VALOFE explained that it shared user feedback and data with local publisher Phoenix Games, applying them to updates to establish content and operational systems tailored to local preferences.

The company is also pushing to enter mainland China. VALOFE stated that it has submitted an application for a Chinese publishing license for 'Kritika' alongside a local partner, with its Shanghai subsidiary leading development. The company expects a high likelihood of securing the license within the year, as administrative procedures are currently underway.

Additionally, it reported that 'Kritika Mobile' (working title) is under development through China's Jiangsu Huanyu, with plans to begin service in the first half of 2027.

A VALOFE official said, "Based on our collaboration with our Shanghai development studio and publisher Phoenix Games, we are now able to offer local players a highly polished Kritika service," adding, "Along with this official launch, we will deliver stable service while continuing to expand our service regions."
