FC Seoul is trying to win five consecutive league titles.

Seoul will face Incheon United in the 27th round of the K League 1 of the 2026 season at 7 p.m. on September 5 at Seoul World Cup Stadium.

Seoul will play a 4-4-2 formation. Klimala and Son Jung-beom work together in front. Babets and Lee Seung-mo will form the midfield, while Andersson and Jung Seung-won will play left and right midfielders. Ross and Park Sung-hoon are responsible for the central defense. Kim Jin-soo and Choi Jun will start as left and right fullbacks. Goals are protected by Koo Sung-yoon.

"Players at the Asian Games will head to Japan after the match against Incheon," Seoul coach Kim Ki-dong said. "There is a regret that the squad is not thick, but we will have to find a good alternative."

Coach Kim continued, "Yazan falls into accumulation of warnings. He has won the match against Incheon this season with a combination of (Park) Sung-hoon and Ross. Sung-hoon is going to challenge for the Asian Games after today's game. I asked Sung-hoon, "Shouldn't you join by raising your senses as much as possible?" I'm looking forward to it," he said.

Seoul has 17 wins, five draws and four losses (56 points) in 26 K League games this season. Seoul, the lone leader, is 14 points ahead of second-place Ulsan HD.

Seoul is approaching its first league championship since 2016.

Incheon coach Yoon Jeong-hwan, the head coach of the opposing team, also praised Seoul's momentum. Coach Yoon said before the game, "The atmosphere in Seoul is really good," adding, "No matter who plays, it brings a lot of goals and wins." He then said, "The winner this season is Seoul."

Kim, who heard Yoon's words, smiled brightly and replied, "I'll work harder."

Manager Kim was wary of being careless.

Coach Kim said, "Incheon is not an easy opponent. Mugosa, who will lead a single chance to a score, is ahead. Gersault, who looks to come in as a replacement, is really fast. When facing Incheon, you must not show any gap. He stressed to our players that "space should not be allowed." We have to focus until the end so that we can bring three points in front of our fans," he said.
