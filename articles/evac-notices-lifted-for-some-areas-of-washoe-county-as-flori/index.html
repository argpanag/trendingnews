Evac notices lifted for some areas of Washoe County as Floriston Fire reaches 640 acres

TheTools · 17.09.2026 15:49 · source · permalink

FLORISTON, Cali. (KOLO) - Update at 10:16 a.m.: The Floriston Fire is now 640 acres, per CAL FIRE.

CAL FIRE NEU will be holding a virtual community meeting on the fire tonight at 6:00 p.m. on their Facebook page. You can watch it here.

Update at 9:11 a.m.: Notices to be ready to evacuate have been lifted for some areas of Washoe County.

Areas from Quilici to Verdi are now no longer under any kind of notice.

Original Article: The Floriston fire did not have major growth overnight, according to an update provided by Cal Fire NEU at 8:00 a.m. Thursday, Sep. 17, 2026.

The wildfire burning along the Interstate 80 corridor near the California-Nevada state line has grown to 640 acres, with 5% containment.

Cal Fire NEU said fire crews will focus on the north side of the fire, toward Reno, as down-canyon winds are expected through Friday.

The Sacramento Regional Incident Management Team assumed command of the incident.

CHP - Truckee said Westbound Interstate 80 remains closed from the state line through the fire area due to safety concerns.

Evacuation warnings and orders remain in place in Nevada county, Sierra county, and Washoe county.

Check emergencywashoe.com for the latest evacuation map.
