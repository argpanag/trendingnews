Mary Berry's easy family favourite lasagne tastes 'absolutely fabulous'

TheTools · 13.11.2026 04:58 · source · permalink

Lasagne is a timeless family dish that many of us enjoy. While numerous recipes exist, one from Mary Berry has been described as "absolutely fabulous".

The television chef's family-friendly recipe is straightforward and easy to prepare. Home cooks who have already attempted the recipe have heaped praise upon her vegetarian mushroom lasagne.

Mary's recipe is meat-free, though meat can be added if desired. Her recipe description states: "This is a classic veggie pasta dish and a real favourite. Full of flavour, it has a traditional cheese sauce with a tomato-based mushroom filling. It is best to soak the sheets of lasagne in hot water before assembling."

Mary's recipe, featured in her Mary 90: My Very Best Recipes book, has been published by Happy Foodie, which noted: "Mary Berry's veggie lasagne is layered with a tomato and mushroom sauce filling and a traditional cheese sauce. It's the perfect meat-free family-friendly recipe."

The lasagne can be prepared up to 24 hours beforehand and frozen while uncooked. One individual who has tried the recipe commented: "I love the recipe - it was so tasty."

Another remarked: "Absolutely fabulous. The only thing I did was soak pasta sheets in hot water too long; they stuck together, so I did soak some more, but only for a short time, and they were fine."

One person remarked: "It doesn't claim to be a vegetarian dish but rather just a simple veggie mushroom lasagna. Anyone can switch out non-vegetarian ingredients for those more suitable!" Another added: "Lovely. Added chickpeas and red pepper for extra veg. Scrumptious."

Mary Berry's Veggie Mushroom Lasagne recipe

Serves six people.

Ingredients

  • Six to eight dried lasagne sheets
  • Two tbsp olive oil
  • One large onion, diced
  • 500g button mushrooms, sliced
  • Two large garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 30g plain flour
  • Two 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • One tbsp chopped thyme leaves
  • Two tsp balsamic vinegar
  • Two tbsp sun-dried tomato paste
  • 200g baby spinach

Cheese sauce ingredients

  • 55g butter
  • 55g plain flour
  • 600ml hot milk
  • Two tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150g mature Cheddar, grated
  • 55g Parmesan, grated

Method

Submerge the lasagne sheets in hot water for 15 minutes until softened, then drain thoroughly.

Heat the oven to 200C/180C Fan/Gas 6. You'll need a 2.25 litre rectangular ovenproof dish.

Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over a high heat. Fry the onion for several minutes. Add the mushrooms and garlic, cooking for three to four minutes. Add the flour and combine thoroughly. Add the tomatoes, thyme, vinegar, tomato paste and spinach, cooking for a further three to five minutes. Season with salt and pepper, remove from the heat and leave to one side.

For the cheese sauce, melt the butter in a saucepan over a medium heat. Mix in the flour, then gradually whisk in the hot milk. Stir through the mustard and two-thirds of the cheeses. Season and bring to the boil; whisk for several minutes until the mixture has thickened.

To assemble the lasagne, spread a third of the mushroom and tomato mixture across the bottom of the dish. Cover with a third of the cheese sauce, then lay half the lasagne sheets over the top in a single layer. Continue building up the layers with the sauces and pasta until you've created three layers of mushroom and tomato, three layers of cheese, and two layers of pasta. Sprinkle with the remaining cheese.

Bake in the oven for approximately 40 minutes, until golden brown, bubbling around the edges, and the pasta is tender.
