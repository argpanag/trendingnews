In Kramatorsk, State Emergency Service bomb disposal experts recovered an unexploded piece of aircraft ordnance

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:51 · source · permalink

Following yet another airstrike on Kramatorsk, an unexploded ordnance was discovered near a nine-story residential building, stuck in the ground. The explosive device turned out to be a D-30SN UMPB aerial bomb.

At the scene, State Emergency Service bomb disposal experts removed it from the ground and transported it to a demolition site, where they destroyed it in compliance with all necessary safety measures.

Reminder: If you discover an explosive object, do not approach it and do not attempt to move it yourself. Move to a safe distance and call the Emergency Service at 101.

Based on information from the Main Directorate of the State Emergency Service of Ukraine in Donetsk Oblast
