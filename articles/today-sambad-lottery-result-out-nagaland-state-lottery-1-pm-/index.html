Today Sambad Lottery Result LIVE: Tax Deductions & Payout Realities

If you hold a winning ticket, the amount listed on the prize chart is not the final amount you take home. Indian tax laws apply strict rules to winnings from games of chance:

  • Tax Threshold: Any prize exceeding ₹10,000 is subject to a flat 30% Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) under Section 194B of the Income Tax Act.

  • Additional Charges: Surcharges and educational cesses are applied on top of the flat 30% for massive payouts like the ₹1 Crore prize, meaning a jackpot winner typically takes home roughly ₹65 Lakh to ₹70 Lakh after all statutory deductions.
