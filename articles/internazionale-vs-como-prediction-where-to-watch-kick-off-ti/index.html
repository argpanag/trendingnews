Internazionale - Looking to build on recent win

Internazionale bounced back from their derby defeat with a convincing win during the week. Here, we will take a closer look at their current form and recent results. We will also analyse their tactical approach for the upcoming match and reveal the expected Internazionale vs. como line-ups.

Recent form

Internazionale currently hold second place in Serie A with 25 points, enjoying a formidable season. Their home record at the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza is perfect, with five wins in five league matches. At home, they show a potent attack, scoring 17 goals.

Despite a recent 3-1 derby loss to AC Milan, Internazionale quickly bounced back. They secured an impressive 3-0 away win against Feyenoord in the UEFA Champions League. This result demonstrates their resilience and strong overall momentum.

With no reported injuries, Inter can rely on their key players. Attacker Marcus Thuram has five goals and two assists in the competition so far. This attacking strength, coupled with their solid home form, suggests they will dictate play and pressure the opposition’s defence.

como - Enjoying a winning streak

Let’s review Como’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their expectations and standout players for the trip to Milan.

Recent form

Como are in sensational form, currently sitting fourth in Serie A with 23 points. They are on an impressive winning streak, having won their last six matches across all competitions. This run includes an astonishing 9-1 victory away to Lens in the UEFA Champions League, a result that showcases their current attacking firepower.

Their away form in the league has been particularly strong, with four wins in six games on the road, scoring 13 goals in the process. Attacker T. Douvikas has been a standout performer, finding the back of the net five times in the competition. Midfielder Nico Paz has also been crucial, contributing with three goals and three assists so far.

With no injuries to worry about, Como arrive at the San Siro with a full squad and immense confidence. Their recent goal-scoring prowess suggests they will adopt an attacking approach. This makes them a significant threat to the home side’s defence, promising a very entertaining match.
