Projected Lineup: October 27 vs. Boston | Carolina Hurricanes

TheTools · 27.10.2026 23:38 · source · permalink

Hall, Jankowski on the cusp of returning

RALEIGH, N.C. - The Carolina Hurricanes are hoping to have at least one returning face in their lineup on Tuesday when they take on the Boston Bruins.

Mark Jankowski, who has been out of the lineup since Oct. 17 with a lower-body injury, and Taylor Hall, who has been sidelined since Oct. 13 with an upper-body injury, are both considered options for this evening.

As of yesterday, Hall looked like the more likely option, saying he felt good to go after fully participating in Monday's practice at Lenovo Center. The veteran forward took the ice with the team this morning, but left about five minutes in and did not return. Rod Brind'Amour did not seem concerned and said he thought Hall would be alright.

As for Jankowski, he started this morning's skate as an extra, but when Hall left the ice, he began working with the group in normal duties. The 32-year-old told reporters that he, too, felt ready to return.

Jankowski has goals in his last six games, the longest active streak in the NHL before his injury.

Among the forwards who are certainly healthy, Logan Stankoven will try to turn his three-game goal streak into four. With eight total on the season, he enters Tuesday's play tied for eighth among all NHL skaters.

With no changes expected on defense, Pyotr Kochetkov should get the net for a second straight game. Turning in a 24-save shutout on Saturday in St. Louis, the 26-year-old backstop is a perfect 7-0-0 thus far this season.

Catch the battle between division leaders tonight at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN.
