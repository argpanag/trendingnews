Man Utd news: Benjamin Sesko pulls out of Slovenia international duty

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:30 · source · permalink
  • Published
    22 September 2026

Manchester United striker Benjamin Sesko says he is "very sorry" after pulling out of Slovenia's forthcoming Nations League matches so he can "fully dedicate" himself to his recovery.

The 23-year-old missed the 1-1 draw at Fulham on Sunday with a shin injury sustained against Brighton in the Carabao Cup.

He joins United team-mates Kobbie Mainoo, Marcus Rashford and Karl Darlow in withdrawing from international duty.

"I have always played for Slovenia and will always play with heart and pride. The most important thing now is to fully dedicate myself to recovery," Sesko posted on Instagram.

"I am already working on getting back on the field as soon as possible and helping the national team and the club again."

United's next match is at home to Tottenham on 10 October.

"Benjamin will undergo tests this week before defining his individual recovery plan," said a club statement.
