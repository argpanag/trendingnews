Mourinho Welcomes Militão Back for Sevilla Clash

Real Madrid have received news they have been waiting months to hear. Éder Militão is back in the squad after recovering from an injury that kept him sidelined for almost half a year.

José Mourinho confirmed the Brazilian defender's return during his press conference ahead of the LaLiga Matchday 9 encounter against Sevilla.

The news comes as a major boost for the Portuguese manager, who regains one of his most experienced center-backs at a time when the demanding schedule is beginning to test the squad's depth.

The match will take place on Sunday, October 18, at the Santiago Bernabéu, where Militão could make his long-awaited return in front of the Madrid supporters.

However, his inclusion in the squad does not necessarily mean he will start. After such a lengthy absence, the priority will be to gradually rebuild his match fitness.


A Recovery That Began in April

Éder Militão's last appearance for Real Madrid came on April 21, 2026, during a match against Deportivo Alavés.

The Brazilian was forced off after suffering a tear of the proximal hamstring tendon in his left leg, an injury that required surgery on April 28.

That marked the beginning of a lengthy rehabilitation process that kept him away from competitive action for several months.

His recovery progressed gradually, allowing him to return to full team training in late September. Over the following weeks, the defender increased his workload and completed sessions alongside his teammates.

Now, after nearly 180 days without playing an official match, Militão is once again available for Mourinho.

His return carries additional significance given the physical setbacks he has endured in recent seasons, including two serious anterior cruciate ligament injuries since 2023.

For that reason, the coaching staff will need to carefully manage his return to competitive football.

UEFA Champions League

El Clásico Looms for Real Madrid

Militão's return comes during a week that could have a major impact on Real Madrid's ambitions in both domestic and European competitions.

After hosting Sevilla, the Spanish giants will face RB Leipzig in the UEFA Champions League before traveling to meet Barcelona in El Clásico on October 25.

Having the Brazilian available again gives Mourinho more defensive options, alongside players such as Dean Huijsen, Ibrahima Konaté, and Antonio Rüdiger.

Beyond his ability to win possession and dominate individual duels, Militão brings valuable experience in high-pressure matches and a strong physical presence in both penalty areas.

The Portuguese manager must now decide whether to give him his first minutes against Sevilla or wait a little longer before increasing his involvement.

The main objective will be to avoid unnecessary risks while helping the defender regain confidence and consistency.

For Real Madrid, his return is a welcome development during a season in which every point could prove decisive.

And for Militão, it marks the end of a long wait and the beginning of a new opportunity to reclaim his place in the Madrid defense.


Key Takeaways

  • Éder Militão returns to the Real Madrid squad after nearly six months out following an injury suffered in April.
  • José Mourinho regains an important defensive option, although Militão's inclusion against Sevilla does not guarantee a place in the starting lineup.
  • Real Madrid face upcoming matches against Sevilla, RB Leipzig, and Barcelona, making the Brazilian's return particularly timely.
