Ballon d’Or: Five countries, players with most awards

TheTools · 25.10.2026 23:23 · source · permalink

News

October 26, 2026 by Wale Gbadebo

Since its introduction in 1956, the men’s Ballon d’Or has become one of football’s most prestigious individual honours, celebrating players whose performances have stood out across a season. Over the decades, several countries and legendary footballers have shaped the award’s rich history.

On the individual list, Messi leads with eight Ballon d’Or trophies, while Ronaldo follows with five wins. France international Ousmane Dembélé won the latest edition in 2025.

Germany, the Netherlands, and Portugal also rank among the leading nations in the award’s history, reflecting their contributions to football across generations.

Here is a look at the countries that have recorded the most men’s Ballon d’Or victories up to 2025.

1. Argentina – 8 awards

All eight of Argentina’s Ballon d’Or victories belong to Lionel Messi, who won the prestigious award in 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2023.

Messi made history by winning four consecutive titles between 2009 and 2012. His eighth triumph came in 2023, following Argentina’s victory at the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

2. France – 8 awards

France’s eight Ballon d’Or victories have been shared by six players: Raymond Kopa, Michel Platini, Jean-Pierre Papin, Zinedine Zidane, Karim Benzema and Ousmane Dembélé.

Platini won three consecutive titles from 1983 to 1985, while Dembélé became France’s latest winner in 2025 after an outstanding season with Paris Saint-Germain.

3. Germany – 7 awards

Germany has won seven Ballon d’Or awards, including those won by West German players before the country’s reunification.

Franz Beckenbauer claimed the honour twice, in 1972 and 1976, while Karl-Heinz Rummenigge secured back-to-back titles in 1980 and 1981. Gerd Müller, Lothar Matthäus and Matthias Sammer complete the list of German winners.

4. Netherlands – 7 awards

The Netherlands’ seven Ballon d’Or victories owe much to Johan Cruyff and Marco van Basten, who each won the award three times.

Cruyff claimed the honour in 1971, 1973 and 1974, while Van Basten triumphed in 1988, 1989 and 1992. Ruud Gullit’s victory in 1987 completed the country’s tally.

5. Portugal – 7 awards

Portugal’s seven Ballon d’Or titles have been won by three players: Cristiano Ronaldo, Eusébio and Luís Figo.

Ronaldo leads the Portuguese tally with five victories, secured in 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017. Eusébio won the award in 1965, while Figo claimed the honour in 2000.
