Top 10 Passers: September 2026 Geodetic Engineer Licensure Exam Results

TheTools · 28.09.2026 21:47 · source · permalink

GELE RESULTS 2026 – The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) named the topnotchers or top 10 passers in the September 2026 Geodetic Engineers Computer-Based Licensure Exam (GELE).

PRC administered the Geodetic Engineer board exam in Manila, Baguio, Butuan, Cebu, Davao, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Rosales, Tacloban and Tuguegarao on September 23 and 24, 2026.

Previous Examinations

In the September 2025 Geodetic Engineers Computer-Based Licensure Examination, Miguel Bongon Rasco of FEATI University topped the examination with a rating of 89.00%. Niron James Famador Abas of the University of Southeastern Philippines (USeP)-Davao City placed second with 88.80%.

In the October 2024 Geodetic Engineers Licensure Examination, Hannah Mae Rudio Guimbongan of Nueva Vizcaya State University-Bayombong and Vincent Ray Tojong Pascua of USeP-Davao City shared the top spot after both earning ratings of 87.60%. Josephine Niña Daipan Balilahon of USeP-Davao City and Adrian Philip Alarcio Campos of Saint Louis University tied for second place with 87.40%.

In the October 2023 Geodetic Engineers Licensure Examination, Joshua Carl Mari Jocoy Fajardo of USeP-Davao City ranked first with a rating of 89.60%. Gennie Marie Ceruela Geñoso of the University of the Philippines (UP)-Diliman followed in second place with 89.40%.

In the October 2022 Geodetic Engineers Licensure Examination, Ronald Estrada Diaz Jr. of USeP-Davao City topped the examination with a rating of 92.40%. Paul Henry Torrigue Camacho of UP-Diliman placed second with 91.00%.
