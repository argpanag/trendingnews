Watch: Kylian Mbappé handed his MOTM award to Real Madrid teammate after PSV romp - Get Spanish Football News

TheTools · 24.11.2026 22:30 · source · permalink

Real Madrid star Kylian Mbappé handed his MVP award for his performance in the club’s latest outing to a fellow member of Los Blancos’ attacking ranks.

José Mourinho’s troops were of course back in action on Tuesday evening, welcoming PSV Eindhoven to Spain’s capital for a Champions League clash.

The aforementioned Mbappé featured from the off in the fixture, and played a starring role in his side’s eventual 2nd-half comeback, notching a brace in a 5-1 rout.

Also on target late on was Vinícius Jr, bringing to an end a dry spell for the Brazilian at club level dating all the way back to August.

Vinícius’ teammates were therefore quick to showcase their delight for the under-fire winger during his celebration, mobbing ‘Viní’ with hugs and congratulations.

And as alluded to above, post-match, Kylian Mbappé took things a step further. After being handed the official Man of the Match award for his showing, the Frenchman passed his trophy to Vinícius, a classy gesture on Mbappé’s part:
