The visitors are still without Ishan Kishan, who has missed the last two games due to a low-grade right adductor strain and had not regained match fitness as of the BCCI's most recent update.

Auckland weather forecast for New Zealand vs. India 4th T20I

Friday is forecast as a mild spring day in Auckland with a high of 18°C and a low of 12°C and a daily precipitation chance of 15%. The wetter weather in this stretch is expected on Thursday, which carries a 40% rain chance, with Friday the drier of the two days.

Temperatures should sit at around 16°C by the toss and drop steadily through the evening, reaching roughly 13°C by the close. That averages out near 14°C across the hours of play, which is noticeably milder than the 9°C the players finished in at Wellington.

A colder change is forecast to follow rather than precede the match, with Saturday's overnight low in Auckland expected to fall to around 8°C.

Chances of rain interrupting play in Auckland

At 15% for the day, the odds of rain disrupting this match are slim. The system bringing the wetter weather to Auckland is forecast to pass through on Thursday, leaving Friday on the clear side of it.

The outlook on either side supports that reading, with Saturday at 10% and Sunday at 5%, so the drying trend is expected to continue through the weekend rather than break.

What happens if the New Zealand vs. India 4th T20I is stopped by rain?

Should rain arrive despite the forecast, the match could either be delayed or shortened, with the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern method used to set a revised target. A minimum of five overs per side is needed for a result, and a complete washout would see the game abandoned, as there is no reserve day.

Neither side has much riding on the outcome. India have already won the series with two to play, and New Zealand are now playing for the consolation of avoiding a 5-0.

Eden Park pitch report and dew factor for IND vs NZ 4th T20I

Eden Park is unlike any other international ground because it was built for rugby, which leaves the straight boundaries at around 55 metres and the square boundaries at roughly 65 metres. Batters tend to target down the ground here rather than square, the reverse of most venues.

The scoring record reflects that. The highest T20I total at the ground is Australia's 245 for 5 against New Zealand in 2018, which also stands as the highest successful chase here, in a match where the hosts had made 243 for 6 batting first.

A mild, humid Auckland evening in late October makes dew a genuine factor in the second innings, and the side bowling last should expect the ball to get wet. India have chased in all three matches of this series, and the toss winner is likely to field again.

What time does the New Zealand vs. India 4th T20I start?

The fourth T20I between New Zealand and India takes place at Eden Park in Auckland on Friday, October 30, 2026.

Play is expected to begin at 8 PM local time, which is 12:30 PM IST, with the toss half an hour earlier at 7:30 PM local time, or 12 PM IST.
