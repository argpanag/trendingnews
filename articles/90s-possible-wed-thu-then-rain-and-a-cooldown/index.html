90s possible Wed-Thu, then rain and a cooldown

TheTools · 30.09.2026 10:17 · source · permalink

EVANSVILLE, Ind. (WFIE) - Temperatures will be about 10 to 15° warmer than normal for the final day of September and the first day of October, but then rain rolls through Friday and cools us back down.

TODAY

We are off to a mild start this morning with temperatures ranging from the mid 50s to low 60s under partly to mostly cloudy skies.

The clouds will break up some as the day goes on, becoming mostly sunny this afternoon. While that sunshine will help warm us up, what is really driving our temperatures today is a nice breeze from the south.

That wind is pulling warmer air from the south up into the Tri-State, which will push our temperatures into the upper 80s to around 90° this afternoon.

TONIGHT

Tonight will be partly cloudy and mild. Temperatures will fall back out of the 80s this evening, then through the 70s overnight, bottoming out in the mid 60s by early Thursday morning.

TOMORROW

Thursday will be partly to mostly cloudy, but it looks like the daylight hours will probably be dry. Thursday will also be a bit breezy with winds from the south-southwest at around 9 to 18 mph. That southerly breeze will once again push our temperatures into the upper 80s to near 90° Thursday afternoon.

RAIN LIKELY FRIDAY

Rain will push into the Tri-State late Thursday night as a cold front approaches our region. Rain is likely throughout Friday morning, but it seems that rain may taper off Friday afternoon, drying out just in time for Friday night football. The clouds will then clear as we head into the weekend.

COOLER TEMPERATURES RETURN

The cold front swinging through the Tri-State Friday morning also ushers in much cooler temperatures. Highs will be in the mid 70s Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

The first couple days of the West Side Nut Club Fall Festival look fantastic with plenty of sunshine and highs in the lower 70s Monday and Tuesday of next week.
