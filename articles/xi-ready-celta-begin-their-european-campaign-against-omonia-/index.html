🚨XI ready: Celta begin their European campaign against Omonia Nicosia

Celta de Vigo make their UEFA Europa League debut this Thursday (18:45) away to Omonia Nicosia at the GSP Stadium. Claudio Giráldez’s side head into the match in urgent need of a win after six league games without victory. Hit by the absence of Iago Aspas and with the clash against Racing de Santander in mind, the coach will opt for heavy rotation, including Altay Bayindir’s debut in goal.

Omonia, managed by Norwegian coach Henning Berg, arrive unbeaten in their domestic league and with a perfect home record in Europe. The Cypriot side will rely on their physical strength and aerial play, led by Muamer Tankovic and Loïs Diony, to exploit Celta’s recurring weaknesses from set pieces. With Hungarian referee Balázs Berke in charge, the Galicians are looking for a win to put any doubts to rest.
