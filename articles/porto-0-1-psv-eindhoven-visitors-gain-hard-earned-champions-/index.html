Porto 0-1 PSV Eindhoven: Visitors gain hard-earned Champions League win

TheTools · 21.10.2026 03:57 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores

  • Line-ups

PSV Eindhoven have won one, drawn one and lost one of their opening three Champions League games this season

PSV Eindhoven gained their first win of this season's Champions League at the expense of a Porto side who have now lost all three of their matches.

The Dutch visitors took a 57th-minute lead when they got in down the right and Guus Til was at the back post to convert Sven Mijnans' shot, which was trickling wide.

Porto, who lost their opening two games - 2-0 at home to Manchester City and 3-2 at Real Betis - pushed for a late equaliser.

In second-half injury time, Pepe fired wide for the hosts, Hwang In-beom had an effort blocked and Alberto Costa's header was saved by Matej Kovar.

But PSV will feel they deserved their three points as they hit the woodwork through Filip Kostic and then Esmir Bajraktarevic when the match was goalless.

Porto have won all eight of their Primeira Liga matches and are top of the table domestically, but they sit 33rd in the 36-team Champions League standings.

PSV, with four points from three games, entertain Club Brugge on Wednesday, 4 November, with Porto at home to Napoli on the same night.
