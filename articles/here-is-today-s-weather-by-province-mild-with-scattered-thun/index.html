Good morning South Africa. Spring is showing its mixed moods today, with sunshine in the Cape and cloud spreading across much of the interior. Cape Town enjoys clear skies and a pleasant 24°C, while Johannesburg sits partly cloudy between 9°C and 21°C. Durban and Mbombela see slight rain, and the Free State, Northern Cape and parts of the west face showers and thundershowers. Winds are mostly light to moderate from the northeast.

The warmest spots reach 24°C in Cape Town, Polokwane and Mahikeng, while Johannesburg is coldest at a chilly 9°C. The wettest weather sits over the Free State and Northern Cape. Do note the severe thunderstorm warnings for Tsantsabane, Emthanjeni, Siyancuma and !Kheis, so loved ones there should take care.
