  • Sabalenka and Medvedev headline the rest of the seeds. Both look to continue building momentum toward the second week.
  • Australia’s singles hopes rest entirely on Sweeny. With de Minaur, Schoolkate and Popyrin all out, Friday’s schedule reflects the shift toward doubles as the main source of Australian interest.
