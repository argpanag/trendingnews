Roy Keane could quit ITV and Sky as Man United icon waiting for imminent call

TheTools · 27.09.2026 17:01 · source · permalink

ITV and Sky Sports pundit Roy Keane has admitted that he would entertain a return to management amidst growing speculation linking him with a potential move to Celtic. Supporters are awaiting updates regarding the future of manager Martin O'Neill, who explained he will reflect on his position during the international break.

A loss to Rangers marked the third loss in seven days for the Scottish champions - having also been hammered by their rivals in the Premier Sports Cup before launching their Europa League campaign with a home loss to Ferencvaros. Supporters fuelled speculation over a possible succession plan after the Manchester United legend Keane was spotted in the stands during the 1-0 league reverse at Celtic Park.

However, Celtic chief executive Michael Nicholson has subsequently backed O'Neill. That means the choice will ultimately rest with the 74 year old - with whom Keane collaborated during O'Neill's spell in charge of Ireland and Nottingham Forest.

The football legend has been absent from the dugout for 15 years since departing Ipswich Town, where he spent two years at the helm following success in his maiden managerial role at Sunderland AFC.

However, while speaking to Micah Richards in a Sky Sports Premier League A-Z feature released on Saturday, Keane left the door ajar for a dugout comeback. He said: "Strangely enough, at weekends when I'm watching games, there are teams I watch and you go, 'I think I could go in there and do a job.'

"But it's mostly at weekends. It's not through the week. It's not Monday morning when I'm having my scrambled egg at the local cafe, just reading a paper.

"All these managers driving up and down the motorways and stresses. But yeah, I do. I think there's always something there.

"The pilot light is still on for something. If someone gave me a phone call and told you, you know what? Who knows? It's going to be unlikely."

Richards suggested to Keane that management is "still in you", highlighting how the Irishman responds while analysing matches on punditry duties in the Sky studio. To which, he replied: "Yeah. Because even when we're watching games, you look at it from a manager's point of view.

"Yeah, maybe, maybe. But then to actually go in there and do it, the logistics of everything."

The Manchester United legend also revealed there had been opportunities in recent years that he has turned down to continue with his current broadcasting work. He said: "It's well and good sitting here going, 'Yeah, yeah.'

"But the reality is then, and I've had opportunities the last few years, but then there's contracts and staff issues and you go, 'Really?' And sometimes you go, 'Stick to what you're doing.'"

Keane - who concluded his playing career at Celtic Park with a brief stint in 2006 - notably rejected the Hoops' position in 2014 before Ronny Deila was appointed - with the Irishman claiming that Dermot Desmond told him the job was his if he wanted it.
