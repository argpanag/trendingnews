Two students taken into custody after weapons located at Magruder High

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:55 · source · permalink

The Rockville school was placed on a ‘hold status’ Thursday morning, police say

A 15-year-old boy at Colonel Zadok Magruder High School in Rockville was charged with assault and possession of a dangerous weapon after he was found with a BB gun on campus Thursday morning, Montgomery County police said in a news release.

The student was one of two students who were taken into custody in two separate incidents at the school on Thursday morning while school security staff investigated reports of a student with a gun, the release said. During the investigations, the 15-year-old boy assaulted school security and staff members, and the second student was found in possession of a knife.

Shortly after 8:45 a.m., county and sheriff’s officers responded to the school at 5939 Muncaster Mill Road after receiving reports of the two weapons incidents, police said. As police and school security personnel investigated, Montgomery County Public Schools (MCPS) placed the school on a “hold status” Thursday morning, according to police. Eventually, the school returned to normal operations around 10 a.m. after officers cleared the building.

When a school implements a hold status, students must clear the hallways and remain in a room or area until an “all clear” is announced, according to the MCPS website. Staff are required to close and lock classroom doors and account for all students and other adults, but classes should continue. The hold response is meant to be used to keep hallways clear during situations such as medical emergencies.

The incident comes during the second week of school for the 2026-2027 school year. In 2022, there was a shooting at Magruder High School that left one student with life-threatening injuries. Last school year, there were two shootings at county high schools, including at James Hubert Blake High School in April and Thomas S. Wootton High School in February. Both resulted in student injuries.

According to police, when an MCPS administrator and security personnel were investigating the gun report, the 15-year-old boy was identified and later assaulted the staff members. MCPS spokesperson Liliana López said Thursday morning in an email to Bethesda Today that during the altercation, a BB gun was found in the student’s possession.

County police took the teen into custody and charged him with first- and second-degree assault, possession of a dangerous weapon on school property and disruption of a school day, according to the news release.

Discipline for the student will be handled by the Department of Juvenile Services, which has “authorized detention of the student,” the release states.

The second student was found in possession of the knife on campus while school security investigated the initial gun report, according to police. After discovering the weapon, the knife was confiscated.

According to police, disciplinary action for the student in possession of the knife will be handled administratively by MCPS.

According to López, the school plans to send a message to families summarizing the incident.
