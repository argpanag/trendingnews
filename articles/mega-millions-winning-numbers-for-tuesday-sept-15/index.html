Mega Millions winning numbers for Tuesday, Sept. 15

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:32 · source · permalink

The winning Mega Millions numbers in the Tuesday, September 15, drawing for the $227 million jackpot were 27, 36, 43, 57, 58 and the Mega Ball is 16.

The cash value is $96.3 million.

Numbers are drawn at 11 p.m. ET Tuesday and Friday. The next drawing is Friday.

Mega Millions tickets cost $5 per play, with a multiplier included. Players can buy tickets until 10:45 p.m. the night of the drawing.
