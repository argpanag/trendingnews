Hang Seng Closes Higher as Hong Kong Holds Its Morning Gains

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:10 · source · permalink

As seen on the 24/7 Wall St. homepage on September 22, 2026.

  • 🇭🇰 Hang Seng+0.13%
  • 🇨🇳 SSE Composite+0.11%

Hong Kong held its gains through the close, finishing 0.13% higher with Shanghai up 0.11%, so the morning bid never faded. Quiet sessions like this are where positioning builds ahead of the next catalyst.

The Hang Seng closed up 0.13% at 3,196.22, recovering from an early dip to hold positive ground for most of the session.

The index peaked near a 0.5% gain in the late morning, then buyers stepped back in during the afternoon to keep the close above the flat line. Dips being absorbed rather than extended points to a market still building positions.
