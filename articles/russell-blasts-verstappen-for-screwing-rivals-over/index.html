Russell blasts Verstappen for ‘screwing’ rivals over

TheTools · 21.11.2026 06:15 · source · permalink

The pair made contact while fighting for position at Turn 14 late in FP3, with the stewards later giving Verstappen a warning for changing direction unpredictably under braking.

Russell felt the punishment was sufficient but criticised what he described as a regular pattern of behaviour from the Red Bull driver when others are preparing to begin flying laps.

“I think a warning was fine. There’s nothing in the rules to say you cannot overtake someone who is starting their lap. But when you do this, you are screwing this person over,” Russell told Sky Italia.

“And he does this week in, week out with many drivers. And everybody accepts it. I’m not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they’re bigger and better than everyone else. So somebody’s got to stand up to that guy.”

The stewards reviewed the drivers’ movements as they approached the corner, placing little weight on the informal agreements drivers have about respecting one another’s preparations for a timed lap.

They determined that Verstappen’s change of direction led Russell to move to the inside in an effort to avoid a collision, but decided the incident did not merit a more serious sanction.

Verstappen also faced a separate investigation for weaving while Franco Colapinto was behind him earlier in the session, although no further action was taken after the stewards considered several mitigating factors.

Those decisions come ahead of a wider change to Formula 1’s stewarding system, with the FIA planning to introduce full-time officials from 2027 in an effort to improve consistency.

Russell was also asked about that move following his clash with Verstappen and expressed his support.

“I think permanent stewards is absolutely the right decision. So that’s good.”

The FIA will initially recruit a small group to work alongside the existing rotating panels before expanding the operation from 2028, with a longer-term target of 12 to 15 full-time stewards.

Verstappen and Russell will renew their rivalry in tomorrow’s Las Vegas Grand Prix, with the pair starting alongside each other on the front row.
