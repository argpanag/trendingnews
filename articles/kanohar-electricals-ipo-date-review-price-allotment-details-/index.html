About Kanohar Electricals

Kanohar Electrical Ltd is one of the leading companies in transformer manufacturing in India, catering to a diverse range of end-user industries,
including power transmission, railways, renewable energy, and power distribution. The Company operates across two principal business segments (i) transformer manufacturing and (ii) engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) services. It is among the four manufacturers in India certified by the Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO), the research and development organisation of Indian Railways, for the manufacture of 100 MVA, 132 kV Scott transformers.

Kanohar Electricals Limited operates two manufacturing facilities situated
at Rithani and Gangol in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. As of September 30, 2025,
these facilities had a combined transformer manufacturing capacity of
19,200 MVA.
