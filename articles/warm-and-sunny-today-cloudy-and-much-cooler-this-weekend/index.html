Warm and sunny today. Cloudy and much cooler this weekend!

TheTools · 05.11.2026 10:27 · source · permalink

East Texas (KLTV/KTRE) - Good morning, East Texas! You may want to grab the light jacket or flannel this morning! It is a chilly start to the day with overnight lows ranging from the upper 40s to lower 50s areawide due to calm winds and clear skies. Sunshine sticks around throughout most of the day as south winds bring highs into the middle to upper 70s. Some in Deep East Texas might even hit 80° this afternoon! Today will mark the warmest afternoon for the next seven days as our next cold front sets up to quickly move through the area starting later this evening. A few spotty showers will be possible along the front tonight into tomorrow morning, but most of the area likely won’t see much of any type of meaningful rain. Highs on Friday will trend a bit cooler in the 70s for all with a mix of sun and clouds in the afternoon. Temperatures should cool quickly in the evening, so be prepared for a slightly chilly second half if you’re heading out to any high school football games. Clouds will increase Friday night and will likely persist into Saturday and most of Sunday as a weak upper-level low tracks over the southern plains. This upper low will also bring a chance for rain across East Texas this weekend but will likely only amount to spotty showers across the area as rain battles through drier air in the middle levels of the atmosphere as well as closer to the ground. Due to the continued north winds and extra cloud cover, temperatures will trend much cooler over the weekend with highs on both Saturday and Sunday likely only climbing into the 60s! 60s could continue into early next week, though a few “warm spots” will likely start to briefly top off in the lower 70s by Tuesday.
