Due to forecast adverse weather

This afternoon's sailing to Liverpool, and its return leg, have been cancelled.

The Steam Packet Company says it's due to forecast adverse weather, including gale-force winds.

Affected passengers can amend their booking via their online account on the ferry's website or by calling the Reservations Team on 01624 661661.

As a result, two additional sailings have been added to tomorrow's schedule (Saturday 19 September).

Tomorrow's 10am sailing has been rescheduled and will now leave at 7.15am, 'to assist passengers affected by the weather cancellation'.

There will then be a sailing from Liverpool to the Island at 11.15am, and a return to the Merseyside port at 3pm.
