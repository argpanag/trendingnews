Al Khaleej vs Al Nassr Preview – prediction, team news, lineups | Saudi Pro League 2026-27

TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:08 · source · permalink

Saudi Pro League

The Big Yellow are unbeaten in their last 10 meetings with the Pearls!

Al Khaleej are gearing up to take on Al Nassr for their matchweek 7 fixture of the Saudi Pro League 2026-27 season. These sides are going to come up with an interesting clash, taking place at the Prince Mohammed Bin Fahd Stadium.

The José Gomes-led side have been on a lacklustre run of form. They have failed to secure a win in their first six games of the league campaign. Al Khaleej at home will be looking to use these conditions to their advantage for their upcoming game, although it is not going to be an easy match.

The Pearls have not been able to perform well all across the field. They need to put in more urgency in the final third. Their efforts in the defensive back line have to be on point.

Ange Postecoglou’s men are back to winning ways with their 2-1 victory against Abha Club in their last Saudi Pro League 2026-27 clash. Cristiano Ronaldo and company had full control of the game. They will be looking to do so against Al Khaleej.

Al Nassr are aiming for the top spot on the league table. With a win, they can get there. Despite being away from home, the Big Yellow will be fully confident due to their better squad quality.

Players to Watch

Angelo Fulgini (Al Khaleej)

Angelo Fulgini will be looking to step up his game so that he can help his side secure its first win in the league this season. He is going to be on the left flank once again and can threaten Al Nassr’s defence with his pace. The 30-year-old can also compete in different positions on the field, making him crucial for Al Khaleej.

Cristiano Ronaldo (Al Nassr)

The Portuguese talisman was the star of the show for Al Nassr in their recent victory against Abha Club. He netted in a sensational header to open the scoring for his side. Cristiano Ronaldo has now scored three goals in five Saudi Pro League appearances this season.

He is going to be a constant threat for opponents once again.
