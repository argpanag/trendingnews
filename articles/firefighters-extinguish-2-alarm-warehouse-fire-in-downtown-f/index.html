Firefighters extinguish 2-alarm warehouse fire in downtown Fresno

TheTools · 18.09.2026 04:10 · source · permalink

FRESNO, Calif. (KFSN) -- Firefighters have extinguished a two-alarm warehouse fire that broke out in downtown Fresno on Thursday night.

The fire broke out just after 8 p.m. on H Street, between San Joaquin and Calaveras streets.

When crews arrived, they found heavy smoke coming from the back of the warehouse.

At one point, firefighters knocked down the flames in one part of the warehouse before they spread to another.

Part of the warehouse that caught fire is a City of Fresno property.

"When we arrived, we noticed a majority of the fire was to the back side, which was some storage of plastic materials, plastic totes and styrofoam," Fresno Fire Battalion Chief John Creasy said.

The blaze required multiple agencies, including Fresno Fire, Fresno County Fire and CAL FIRE. Around 30 firefighters in total responded to the scene.

Fire officials say no one was inside the warehouse when they arrived and no injuries were reported.

Power was knocked out for nearly 1,000 residents during the battle, according to PG&E's outage map.

The cause of the fire is under investigation.
