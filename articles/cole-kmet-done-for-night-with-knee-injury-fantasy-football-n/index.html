Cole Kmet Done for night with knee injury - Fantasy Football News

TheTools · 23.10.2026 04:02 · source · permalink

Kmet exited Thursday night's game against the Patriots with a right knee injury and will not return.

Kmet caught a short pass near the sideline and was tackled by a pair of defenders, but he immediately grabbed at his knee and was carted to the locker room. Prior to going down in the fourth quarter, Kmet caught all three targets for 28 yards. For the season, the blocking specialist has 10 catches for 87 yards. Sam Roush will be in line for more snaps alongside Colston Loveland.
