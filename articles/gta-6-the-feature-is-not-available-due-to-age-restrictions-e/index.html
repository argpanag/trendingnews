GTA 6 The Feature is Not Available Due to Age Restrictions error on PS5: Possible fixes

TheTools · 18.11.2026 10:54 · source · permalink

PS5 players trying to access GTA 6 may encounter the "The Feature is Not Available Due to Age Restrictions" error. This issue is generally linked to parental controls or age restrictions on the PlayStation Network account. However, incorrect account settings can also trigger it.

Here are some possible fixes to resolve the error on PS5 and restore access to the affected features.

Possible fixes for GTA 6 "The Feature is Not Available Due to Age Restrictions" error on PS5

The "The Feature is Not Available Due to Age Restrictions" error on PS5 can prevent GTA 6 players from accessing certain features. It is associated with PlayStation's age restrictions and parental control settings.

Players can try several solutions to address the issue:

  1. Check parental control settings
  2. Verify account details
  3. Review communication settings
  4. Sign out and sign back in

1) Check parental control settings

Follow these steps:

  • Go to Settings > Family and Parental Controls > Family Management on your PS5.
  • Select the affected account and review its content restrictions.
  • Check the permitted age level for games and applications.

If the account has restrictions, ask the family manager to adjust them. Save the changes and check if GTA 6 works properly.

2) Verify account details

Check the date of birth registered with your PlayStation Network account. An incorrect birth date can affect the age category assigned to the account. If the account belongs to a minor, its age restrictions will remain active.

PlayStation does not offer a standard option to freely edit the date of birth after account creation.

3) Review communication settings

Some PS5 features require access to communication and user-generated content.

  1. Open Settings > Family and Parental Controls > Family Management
  2. Select the affected account.
  3. Review the Communication and User-Generated Content settings.

Ask the family manager to adjust restrictions if they are blocking access to the required features.

4) Sign out and sign back in

If you have changed parental control settings, sign out of your PSN account and sign back in. Sony recommends this step to apply updated restrictions while a game is running. Restart GTA 6 after logging in and check if the error persists.
