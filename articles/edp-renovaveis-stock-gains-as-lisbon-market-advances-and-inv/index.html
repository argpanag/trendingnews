EDP Renovaveis stock gains as Lisbon market advances and investors eye growth targets

TheTools · 07.09.2026 15:29 · source · permalink

EDP Renovaveis stock edged higher on the Lisbon exchange as investors digested recent portfolio moves in Brazil and focused on the companys growth targets and valuation relative to its 52-week range.

EDP Renovaveis stock (ISIN ES0144580Y14) traded around 13.82 euros on the Euronext Lisbon exchange as of September 1, 2026, with the share price up about 1.17 percent on the day according to a market overview of the Portuguese stock exchange.

Lisbon gains support EDP Renovaveis

According to a market report on the Lisbon stock exchange dated September 1, 2026, the benchmark index moved higher with energy names among the main contributors, and EDP Renovaveis shares climbed 1.17 percent to 13.82 euros as investors rotated into utilities.

The same overview shows that the broader energy sector in Lisbon also advanced, with large peers in electricity and oil logging gains between about 1.1 percent and 1.5 percent, underscoring that EDP Renovaveis participated in a sector-wide move rather than an isolated spike.

Portfolio reshaping in Brazil underpins the story

A separate report on mergers and acquisitions activity in Portugal published on September 2, 2026 highlights that one of the most notable recent deals was the purchase of 100 percent of EDP Renovaveis Brasil from EDP Renewables by EDP Brasil for around 700 million euros, integrating Brazilian generation, trading and supply activities under EDP Energias do Brasil SA.

For investors in EDP Renovaveis, this transaction is relevant because it signals a deliberate portfolio reshaping in which Brazilian operations are consolidated locally while the listed renewables arm can focus capital on projects in Europe and other core regions, potentially improving the alignment between growth investments and the balance sheet.

Onshore wind and solar pipeline remains central

EDP Renovaveis is best known for its onshore wind and solar projects, and the company continues to emphasize a pipeline of new capacity to support medium term growth in output and cash flow while maintaining discipline on returns.

Stock valuation and investor perspective

With a share price of about 13.82 euros as of September 1, 2026, EDP Renovaveis stock sits in a range that investors often compare to medium term growth targets and recent transaction values in order to gauge whether the implied valuation multiples remain attractive relative to peers in the European renewables sector.
