Foo Fighters to announce Murrayfield Stadium show in Edinburgh

Live entertainment promoter PCL Presents has teased a potential Foo Fighters Murrayfield Stadium show on social media.

A post showed the bands logo projected on to prominent Edinburgh landmarks such as Edinburgh Castle, Murrayfield Stadium, St Giles’ Cathedral the Usher Hall and the National Museum of Scotland with a caption saying ‘16 November 9am’. 

Foo Fighters are currently on the road on their Take Cover Tour 2026 which will see them travel Europe, North America and Australia. 

The band fronted by Dave Grohl currently has tour dates up until early March with their final performances to take place in Peru.

It now looks as though the tour will be extended as the band are expected to announce a show at Murrayfield Stadium next summer. Foo Fighters last played in Scotland when they headlined Hampden Park in June 2024. It has been over a decade since the band last appeared in Scotland’s capital.
