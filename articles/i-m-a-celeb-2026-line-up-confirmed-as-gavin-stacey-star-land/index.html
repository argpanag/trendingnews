I'm A Celeb 2026 line-up confirmed as Gavin & Stacey star lands in Australia

TheTools · 09.11.2026 10:22 · source · permalink

The famous faces for ITV's I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! are starting to touch down in Australia ahead of a brand-new season. ITV previously confirmed that new episodes will be on our screens from Monday, November 16, for the 26th series, hosted by Ant and Dec - and recognisable faces are seemingly on their way to the jungle.

Last year saw Angry Ginge crowned King of The Jungle, beating out stars like Manchester rapper Aitch, Jack Osbourne and Ruby Wax as new celebrities get ready to face their fears in the jungle.

ITV previously confirmed that, as the new series returns on Monday, November 16 at 9pm on ITV, I’m A Celebrity…Unpacked will also return live on ITV2 & ITVX, hosted by Sam Thompson and Kemi Rodgers.

But with just days to go until the beloved series returns, the official lineup has been confirmed, with the celebrities landing in Australia including Gavin & Stacey star Joanna Page and Wayne Rooney. Here's everyone we know so far.

Wayne Rooney

Wayne Rooney was the first celebrity to land in Australia ahead of the new series, following in the footsteps of his wife Coleen, who finished as runner-up in 2024. However, the star refused to give anything away about his reasons for landing at Brisbane Airport, instead admitting: "I've never been to Australia."

The former Manchester United striker didn't give anything away when asked if he was looking forward to the jungle, but did share a smile when asked about the jungle karaoke - the camp singalongs and musical challenges.

Wayne, who is a favourite for success, is expected to follow in his wife's footsteps and win over the hearts of the nation, having previously shared his enthusiasm for the ITV show - publicly supporting Coleen during her I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! stint.

Ahead of his own TV appearance, a friend admitted Coleen was a huge influence in his I'm a Celebrity decision, adding: "He has no shortage of advice from Coleen, who became a firm favourite with viewers during her own stint in camp."

Sharky

YouTuber Sharky, whose real name is Sharmarke Mohamud, has also landed in Australia. Sharky rose to fame back in 2015 when he launched his own YouTube channel, becoming known for his football content, pranks and FIFA gameplay.

In 2019, he co-founded the British YouTube group Beta Squad alongside Chunkz, Niko Omilana, AJ Shabeel, and King Kenny, becoming a familiar name to many.

The group has now amassed more than 12 million subscribers on YouTube, but Sharky isn't the only star to make the jump to mainstream TV. King Kenny has recently appeared on Celebrity Traitors, following in the footsteps of his co-star Niko Omilana.

Now, Sharky is set to make his TV debut, landing in Australia ahead of the new series as he was spotted wearing a blue jumper, black beanie hat, and headphones through the arrivals hall.

When asked his reasons for being in Australia, though, Sharky said: "I’m actually joining the Socceroos (the Australian Men's national team). I’ve been called up for the national team for the international break."

He continued: "I actually did my DNA test the other day, and I found out I’m 2% Australian, so the manager called me up."

Ade Adepitan

Ade is a former Paralympian wheelchair basketball player who won a Bronze medal back in 2004 and a Gold medal in 2005. Also a TV presenter, he was appointed an MBE in 2005 for his services to disability sport.

The 53-year-old has presented programmes such as The Travel Show, Climate Change: Ade on the Front Line as well as Children in Need. His arrival in Australia makes him the third celebrity to appear ahead of the new ITV series.

But of course, he gave nothing away when asked why he was there, instead insisting: "I am here to advise on the 2027 Australian tapdancing festival. I am here to dance." When asked what he would miss most, he admitted it would be "time with my family."

Leomie Anderson

Leomie Anderson is the fourth celebrity to land at Brisbane Airport, but of course gave nothing away on the true reason for her being there.

Waving to fans as she arrived, Leomie is a Victoria's Secret model, making history back in 2019 when she became a Victoria's Secret Angel, as she now prepares to swap the catwalk for the jungle.

She was also seen on Netflix's Celebrity Bear Hunt last year before her sudden elimination in the first round.

Claire Sweeney

Coronation Street star Claire Sweeney has touched down in Australia, smiling for the cameras as she arrives at Brisbane Airport. It appears she, too, is joining I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! line-up, having previously given up her dream on-stage role.

Claire was famed for her role as Cassie Plummer on the Weatherfield soap, making her Cobbles debut back in 2023. Revealed as the long-lost mum of Tyrone Dobbs and estranged daughter of Evelyn Plummer, Claire left Coronation Street earlier this year.

Following her exit, Claire went on to return to the Liverpool stage as Annie at the Liverpool Empire - but it appears she is leaving that behind for a stint in the iconic jungle.

Declaring she was in Australia to "get my extensions done", the 55-year-old added: "I couldn't get my extensions down in Liverpool, so I got an appointment over here to get my nails and my hair done. I love Australia, the people are cool and friendly. It's a home from home."

Of course, she refused to give anything away about her jungle debut, instead stating: "I am a big fan of the show; I watch the show you are talking about. I regularly watch."

Joanna Page

Gavin & Stacey star Joanna Page landed at Brisbane Airport on November 10. Known for her role as Stacey Shipman, fans may also recognise the star for her portrayal as Judy in hit festive film Love Actually.

In the past year, she has also won comedy competition Taskmaster on its 21st series as well as being a regular on ITV's Loose Women. Now though, she could be taking on a series of savage challenges in the jungle for the brand new series.

The Gavin & Stacey star was all smiles as she headed through the airport donning her leopard print trousers.

This story will be updated as new stars land in Australia.

I’m A Celebrity…Get Me Out Of Here! starts Monday 16th November at 9pm on ITV1, STV, ITVX & STV Player
