TRON: Post-Quantum Cryptography Hits Testnet | Flash News Detail

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:20 · source · permalink

TRON DAO confirmed post-quantum cryptography deployment on its testnet to counter quantum computing threats, with founder Justin Sun noting the network could activate mainnet protection at any time.

Analysis

TRON DAO has deployed post-quantum cryptography technology to its testnet, directly addressing emerging quantum computing threats as stated in the October 12, 2026 announcement from the official account.

Founder Justin Sun highlighted ongoing monitoring of quantum and AI advances, adding that TRON retains flexibility to move the protection to mainnet without delay. Bitcoin and Ethereum are pursuing parallel quantum-resistant measures.

TRX holds at $0.33 on the 4h chart inside a bearish structure where EMA50 sits at $0.33 and EMA200 at $0.34, establishing clear resistance overhead. MACD registers a golden cross at -0.0 while RSI(14) prints 43.09 in neutral territory, and price remains pinned between Bollinger Bands both at $0.33, pointing to volatility compression that favors a test of the 50-EMA support before any sustained directional break.
