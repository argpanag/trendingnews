Aspyn Ovard is finally revealing the "10 year secret" that ended her marriage to ex-husband Parker Ferris.

After the couple's shocking split in 2024, Ovard told fans on social media that she divorced Ferris after learning he had been keeping something from her throughout their entire marriage.

Despite fans rampant speculation, Ovard never revealed what the secret was—until now.

During the premiere episode of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, the influencer claimed Ferris had been secretly drinking behind her back throughout their entire marriage, despite the fact he claimed to be sober and Ovard was strictly against drinking.

"When Parker and I were getting married, we were definitely, I thought, actually, we were on the same page about where we stood with drinking and smoking and things like that," Ovard said.

Although Ovard is not a member of the Mormon Church, which prohibits members from consuming alcohol, Ovard explained she's strongly against drinking due to watching her father struggle with alcoholism and die from an alcohol-related illness.

"My dad struggled with alcoholism, so the only version of him I knew was like, some level of intoxicated," she said. "Finding out that Parker had been drinking behind my back for years, it was literally earth-shattering."

Ovard added that she found out her ex-husband's secret just two months after her dad died from alcoholism.

Ferris, for his part, said during a later episode that he tried to get Ovard to experiment with alcohol throughout their marriage to no success.

"She would tell me, 'If you ever drink, we are getting divorced,'" Ferris said, explaining that he started drinking "because it's fun."

"She was very 'Molly Mormon' in that way," he added of his ex-wife.

Ovard and Ferris were married from 2015 to 2024 and they share daughters Cove, 6, Lola, 4, and Elle, 2. In the premiere episode, Ovard also shared that one of their daughters is battling terminal cancer.

The couple did not share which of their children is fighting cancer, nor the type of cancer she has.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, a new spinoff from the original Mormon Wives series, follows a new group of moms and influencers living in Orange County, Calif.

The other cast members include influencers Bobbi Althoff, Avery Woods, McCall Dapron, Salomé Andrea, Madison Fisher, Jordan McClanahan, and Ashleigh Pease.

The series is currently streaming on Hulu and Disney+.
