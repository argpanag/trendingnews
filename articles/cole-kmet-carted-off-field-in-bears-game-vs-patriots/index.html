Cole Kmet carted off field in Bears' game vs. Patriots

TheTools · 23.10.2026 03:58 · source · permalink

Chicago Bears tight end Cole Kmet was carted off the field in the fourth quarter of Thursday's game after suffering an apparent injury

CHICAGO, ILLINOIS – OCTOBER 22: Cole Kmet #85 of the Chicago Bears is carted off the field after being injured in a play against the New England Patriots during the fourth quarter of the game at Soldier Field on October 22, 2026 in Chicago, Illinois. (Photo by Michael Reaves/Getty Images)

Chicago Bears tight end Cole Kmet was carted off the field after taking a hit to his legs in the fourth quarter of Thursday’s game against the New England Patriots.

Kmet had caught a pass on second down from quarterback Caleb Williams and was moving toward the first-down marker when he was hit by two Patriots defenders.

Kmet immediately began grabbing his knee on the sideline, and was tended to by trainers for the Bears.

After the TV timeout, Kmet was seen being carted off the field, giving a thumbs up to Bears fans as he left the field.

Kmet has been a key reserve player for the Bears this season, serving as a mentor to Colston Loveland and still playing significant snaps for Chicago during the season.

If Kmet misses any time, rookie Sam Roush would likely see more action in head coach Ben Johnson’s offense.
