Jemimah Rodrigues joins Captain Smriti Mandhana as Hero Vida brand ambassador - Exchange4media

TheTools · 16.11.2026 10:57 · source · permalink

Jemimah Rodrigues will represent Hero MotoCorp across upcoming multimedia brand campaigns, product launches

  • Hero MotoCorp has appointed Indian cricketer Jemimah Rodrigues as its Brand Ambassador, reinforcing its commitment to women's athletic excellence.
  • This partnership follows the earlier signing of Smriti Mandhana, the captain of the Indian Women’s Cricket Team, in July 2023.
  • Jemimah expressed pride in joining Hero MotoCorp, highlighting shared values of grit and empowerment in sports, and aims to inspire young Indians.
  • As Brand Ambassador, she will participate in multimedia campaigns, product launches, and youth engagement initiatives for the company.

Hero MotoCorp and its emerging mobility brand VIDA has announced the appointment of Indian cricket superstar Jemimah Rodrigues as its Brand Ambassador. This association further cements the company's support for women’s athletic excellence, building on the landmark signing of Indian Women’s Cricket Team Captain Smriti Mandhana in July this year.

The association brings together the global mobility brand, Hero MotoCorp with one of Indian sport’s most dynamic trailblazers. Jemimah, who etched her name into cricketing folklore during India’s victorious 2025 ICC Women’s Cricket World Cup campaign, is a teenage prodigy who broke into the Mumbai Under-19 state cricket team at the age of 13.

Expressing her excitement on joining Hero MotoCorp as their Brand Ambassador, Jemimah Rodrigues said:

"I am incredibly proud and honored to partner with Hero MotoCorp, a brand that has championed Indian sports and empowered Indian athletes across multiple disciplines. Cricket has taught me to play with grit, joy, and conviction, and I see those exact values reflected in Hero MotoCorp’s journey. I look forward to this exciting partnership as we encourage young Indians to break boundaries, trust their journey, and ride forward with confidence."

Speaking on the association, Sanjay Bhan, Executive Vice President, Hero MotoCorp, said:

"At Hero MotoCorp, our brand philosophy is centered on empowering young talent, embracing dynamic performance, and connecting with the aspirations of young India. Jemimah Rodrigues embodies these attributes effortlessly. Having recently welcomed Indian captain Smriti Mandhana to the Hero fold, bringing Jemimah on board further cements our commitment to championing excellence and leadership in women’s sports. On the field, she is a fearless competitor with nerves of steel—evident in her historic, match-winning performances on the world stage. Off the field, her vibrant energy, genuine passion, and positive outlook connect seamlessly with the youth. Jemimah represents a modern, unstoppable generation of achievers, and we are thrilled to welcome her to the Hero family as we continue to inspire millions to chase their dreams."

Himanshu Sharma, Head of Sales, JSW Sports, said:

“We are happy to welcome a legacy brand like Hero MotoCorp to Jemimah Rodrigues’ growing brand portfolio as she continues to inspire a new generation of young athletes through her performances on and off the field. Partnerships like these reflect the growing opportunities for women in sport to build strong connections with leading brands and extend their influence beyond sport. It is encouraging to see Jemimah’s journey resonate with brands that share her values, and we look forward to seeing this association grow.”

As brand ambassador, Jemimah will represent Hero MotoCorp across upcoming multimedia brand campaigns, product launches, and grassroots youth engagement initiatives.
