As we look back on legendary punk guitarist Cheetah Chrome, who died Sunday at 71, it’s worth revisiting Crash ’n’ Burn, the 1977 punk film that captured Chrome and the Dead Boys alongside fellow underground firebrands including the Viletones, Teenage Head, the Diodes, and others. As a document of punk in its rawest, most chaotic form, Crash ’n’ Burn is about as essential as it gets—a noisy, unruly snapshot of a scene that was loud, dangerous, and completely unwilling to play by the rules. And this particular clip is all Dead Boys, with Cheetah Chrome’s guitar attack front and center.
