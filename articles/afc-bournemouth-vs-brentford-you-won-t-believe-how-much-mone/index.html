AFC Bournemouth vs Brentford: You won’t believe how much money tickets cost for this Premier League game

TheTools · 12.09.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

Bournemouth’s ticketing is divided into three categories: A, B and C. Demand is high which means it can be tricky getting a seat.

Bournemouth welcome Brentford to Dean Court on Saturday as the 2026/27 Premier League enters Week 4. After two draws and a defeat, the Cherries are one of six teams still seeking their first win.

It’s been a different story for Brentford who kicked off their campaign with a 3-0 win over Spurs and drew both of their last two games, against Leeds and Sunderland after conceding late goals. Keith Andrews’ team sit fifth in the the table.

As it’s only Bournemouth’s second home game of the new season, and for that reason, demand for tickets will be high. Dean Court was almost at full capacity for Everton’s visit in Week 2 and a similar crowd is expected for Brentford.

Staggered pricing

Bournemouth’s ticketing is divided into three categories: A, B and C. Category A is for games against the league’s ‘big 8′ (Arsenal, Aston Villa, Chelsea, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United, Newcastle and Tottenham). For obvious reasons, these are the most expensive tickets, ranging from $16 for junior level to $82 for a seat in the main stand.

Brentford are one of the eight teams in Category B. Family price tickets in this category range from $42 to $53, the most expensive tickets - central seats in the main stand cost $78 while seats behind the goals in the north and south stands are prices at $58.

In the resale market, tickets are selling fast and at the time of publication, were available starting at $107. However, the next team to visit Dean Court is Liverpool - resale tickets are currently range from $935 to $2,780.
