Fiorentina-Atalanta 2-2 ratings and report: Njie and Joao Mario score

The match at the Franchi ends 2-2: Vanoli’s Fiorentina draw with Maurizio Sarri’s Atalanta. Below are our match report and player ratings.

The first midweek round of the year is now over for Fiorentina and Atalanta. Vanoli, facing a defensive injury crisis, took on an Atalanta side struggling badly away from home. Sarri returned to Florence after intense transfer speculation, while the Franchi awaited its first home league win of the season.

Fiorentina-Atalanta 2-2: match report

First half:

The game sparks into life from the opening exchanges. Pellegrino and Jimenez immediately look to test the goalkeeper, while Atalanta respond by trying to find space in Fiorentina’s half. An early injury forces Kristensen off, with Scalvini coming on. Elmas receives the first yellow card, followed by Viery for pulling back Krstovic in open play. In the 14th minute, Mastantuono has a chance, aiming a right-footed shot at the near post but failing to find the net. Fiorentina keep pressing and, in the 15th minute, Fagioli tries his luck after a corner is cleared. In the 19th minute, Atalanta threaten: Elmas shoots left-footed from around the penalty spot, but Viery blocks to keep Fiorentina’s goal intact. Vanoli’s side threaten again in the 23rd minute, when Jimenez crosses from the right and Pellegrino fails to apply the finishing touch. Atalanta respond through De Ketelaere, who finds himself all alone in front of goal but fires wide from an offside position. In the 29th minute, Ndour comes close to putting Fiorentina ahead: after a scramble, the midfielder shoots with his left foot. His effort takes a deflection, but Carnesecchi manages to save with his feet and prevent a goal. Fiorentina continue to push for the opener and, in the 33rd minute, Joao Mario shoots towards goal, only for Zappacosta to deflect it. Three minutes later, Gaetano wastes a good opportunity, sending his close-range right-footed effort high over the bar. In the 38th minute, Mastantuono produces a brilliant piece of skill in the penalty area, beating his man before curling a left-footed shot just wide. The decisive moment comes in the 41st minute. Fagioli makes a mistake in the build-up and Atalanta immediately capitalise: Zalewski surges down the left and cuts the ball back for De Ketelaere, who has all the time and space he needs to shoot. The Belgian places his effort into the far corner past De Gea, putting the Bergamo side ahead. Fiorentina almost concede a second straight away. In the 42nd minute, the rampant De Ketelaere floats a perfect cross towards Krstovic, whose header looks certain to go in. De Gea produces an extraordinary save to deny the striker and keep Fiorentina’s hopes alive.

Second half:

Fiorentina return with an immediate substitution and an aggressive approach, determined to overturn the deficit. Fagioli comes off, with Oulai replacing him. In the 50th minute, Mastantuono goes for goal from a free kick, sending his effort narrowly wide. Fiorentina equalise in the 53rd minute: Jimenez delivers a perfect cross into the penalty area and Njie appears at the far post, beating the Atalanta defence to the ball and heading home to throw the game wide open again. Atalanta try to respond in the 56th minute through De Ketelaere. The Belgian produces a superb piece of skill and sends in a low cross, but Viery makes a crucial intervention to clear the danger. In the 61st minute, Fiorentina erupt as Joao Mario completes the turnaround to make it 2-1. The wide man advances down the left, takes on his opponent, cuts inside and fires towards goal. His shot deflects off Obric, just enough to wrong-foot Carnesecchi and find the net. Atalanta immediately look for a response and threaten again in the 65th minute. De Ketelaere’s wonderful dummy frees Krstovic on the edge of the area, but the Montenegrin striker shoots wide. Atalanta remain dangerous, particularly through De Ketelaere’s creativity, and the game stays wide open until another equaliser arrives in the 74th minute: from the Belgian’s assist, Zalewski shoots left-footed from the right, catching De Gea out at his near post. Shortly afterwards, Mastantuono has two efforts but cannot find the net, though Atalanta still cannot get to grips with him.
