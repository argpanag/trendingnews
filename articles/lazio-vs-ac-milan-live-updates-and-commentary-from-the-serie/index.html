Gennaro Gattuso’s men are in the lead after 45 minutes, deservedly so as they were much better than Milan, who barely created anything when going forward.

Ruben Amorim has to change something for the second half, otherwise the Rossoneri are doomed.

Three minutes have been added to the first half by the referee.

Loftus-Cheek’s deflected shot from distance nearly beats the Lazio goalkeeper. Corner at least.

That’s a very unlucky goal, depending on how you look at it. Taylor’s cross was deflected by Estupinan onto the bar, then headed in by Noslin as De Winter tried to clear the rebound.

Lazio 2-0 Milan.

Frattesi sends it just wide. Milan need to be careful here, otherwise they could have a mountain to climb.

Unreal save by Maignan on Zaccagni, making himself big, after a fantastic move by Lazio. That looked like it was going to be 2-0 for the hosts.

Cancellieri opens the scoring for Lazio. Really nice move to create the chance, BUT Estupinan definitely should have defended that cross better.

Lazio 1-0 Milan

Noslin had to stretch a little to reach that cross, but that was still a fantastic chance that warranted a better finish (straight at Maignan).

Nice pass through to Zaccagni, who forces Maignan to a good stop. Chukwueze could have done better there it has to be said.

Estupinan was a little behind Cancellieri there, but man, that is a crazy dive from the Lazio striker.

Chukwueze’s header is saved by the goalkeeper. It wasn’t a terrific chance by any means but the Nigerian did well to get power on that.

The game is underway at Stadio Olimpico. Matteo Marcenaro is the referee for the evening.

Lazio 0-0 Milan

Do you want to see more quality in midfield?

“I think we need quality in every area, quality on the ball. I think we did well against Juventus at keeping the ball, but we lacked danger. We weren’t aggressive in the final third, and we need players between the lines: I think we lacked that.

“So it’s not just about the quality of the players, it’s about the quality of the positioning at the right times, in the right space to be dangerous. We worked on that during the week.”

What did you ask of Rabiot?

“Compared to Musah, he’s a little different, also because of his foot: one is left-footed, the other right-footed. I think Rabiot is not only a player who can often get into the penalty area, but he’s also capable of dictating the tempo of the game.

“He’s very experienced, knows how to make the right runs, and is also good around the box. Today, however, I believed that the characteristics of Modrić and Rabiot would be crucial to supporting Cissé and Ruben Loftus-Cheek,” he said in his pre-match interview.

What’s Amorim’s football like? And what is your position?

“When I arrived, we talked about my position on the pitch, both as an attacking midfielder and a midfielder, so I’ve been training more or less alternating between the two roles. It also depends on how they want me to play on the weekend in question and what the team needs.

“Personally, I feel good both in midfield and a little higher up the pitch, and then I always try to give my all. In Turin, I didn’t perform well, partly because my physical condition still isn’t at 100% yet.
