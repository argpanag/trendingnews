Woman dies after falling from height at Ion Orchard

TheTools · 18.11.2026 04:21 · source · permalink

A 37-year-old woman was found dead at Ion Orchard on Wednesday (Nov 18) morning.

In response to AsiaOne's queries, the police said they were alerted to a case of a fall from height at 2 Orchard Turn, the address of the shopping mall, at about 6.50am.

The woman was found lying motionless and was pronounced dead at the scene by a Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) paramedic.

Police said preliminary investigations do not suggest foul play, and investigations are ongoing.
