CD Projekt RED has released patch 5.01b for The Witcher 3 Wild Hunt Remastered

TheTools · 15.10.2026 09:06 · source · permalink

CD Projekt RED has released the 5.01b hotfix for the game. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered. This update is exclusively for PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 5 Pro consoles. The developers noted that this hotfix addresses critical bugs and introduces various stability improvements designed to ensure a more reliable and smooth gameplay experience while exploring the Continent on the target platforms.

The hotfix release followed shortly after the release of the larger numbered patch 5.01, which had previously adjusted visual parameters, graphics options, and interface elements. In the new update, the team focused on targeted technical adjustments to the engine for Sony's current-generation consoles. The developers chose to limit the full list of minor changes to system code optimizations and fixes for crashes affecting overall performance.

In the comments below the announcement, users expressed mixed reactions due to the lack of a patch on other systems. In particular, Nintendo Switch 2 owners noted issues with cross-platform saves, which are temporarily blocked due to incompatibilities with updated builds. Xbox Series X|S and PC players are also continuing to wait for similar stability and lighting fixes for their platforms.
