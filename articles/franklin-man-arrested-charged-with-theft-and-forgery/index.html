Franklin Man Arrested, Charged with Theft and Forgery

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:22 · source · permalink

FRANKLIN– An investigation by special agents with the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation has resulted in the arrest of a man in connection to theft, forgery, and computer offenses.

On October 12, 2025, at the request of 21st Judicial District Attorney General Stacey Edmonson, TBI agents began investigating Giancarlo Trejo (DOB 2/23/1989). Investigators presented evidence that, while working for a company that distributes religious materials, Trejo funneled fraudulent contacts to receive referral fees. He distributed complimentary Bibles, asking recipients to send payments to his personal account. Additionally, he used his employer’s credit card for personal expenses and travel.

On October 7th, the Williamson County Grand Jury returned an indictment charging Trejo with one count of Felony Theft, three counts of Felony Computer Offense, ten counts of Forgery, seven counts of Computer Offense, and one count of Criminal Simulation. On Tuesday, he was arrested and booked into the Williamson County Jail on a $250,000 bond.

The charges and allegations referenced in this release are merely accusations of criminal conduct, not evidence. The defendant is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty beyond a reasonable doubt and convicted through due process of law.
