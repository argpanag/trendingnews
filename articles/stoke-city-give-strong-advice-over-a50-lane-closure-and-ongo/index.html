Stoke City give 'strong advice' over A50 lane closure and ongoing roadworks

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:11 · source · permalink

Stoke City fans are being urged to give themselves plenty of time to get to tonight's crunch clash against Middlesbrough due to potential congestion around the stadium. The club says it expects 'increased pressure on the road network' near the bet365 Stadium due to 'ongoing roadworks and infrastructure repairs'.

As such and the Potters say there will be some changes to help with post-match congestion in the area. It comes as following a smash last month and a lane is out on the A50 Trentham Lakes junction off-slip road. That collision happened on September 18 - but the restriction won't be lifted any time soon.

A post on Stoke City's website states: "The club is in regular dialogue with Highways England and has been advised that, due to awaiting the manufacture of required parts for the fixing of the damaged parapet, this lane closure is expected to remain for a minimum of three months."

As such the club has offered 'strong advice' for fans to arrive early and leave plenty of time to reach the stadium in time for the 8pm kick off against Championship promotion-chasers Middlesbrough.

"As you enter the estate, only one lane is in use approaching the traffic lights. After making the right turn and passing the bridge, both lanes are available again on approach to the Lexus roundabout. Please use both lanes to aid traffic flow.

"We anticipate heavy traffic on the A500/A50 slip road into the estate from 7pm onwards. Please allow extra time for your journey.

"Shuttle bus services will begin from approximately 6.15pm. Later buses to the stadium are likely to be affected by congestion, so we strongly recommend using earlier services where possible."

"The club is aware of traffic problems caused by matchday queues for the Nuffield car park backing up onto the adjacent roads, causing delays across the surrounding road network. Supporters should be aware that, following discussions between all involved parties, should this car park reach capacity, road users will be asked to move their vehicles on, as opposed to queuing and waiting for access."

"For eastbound exit via the A50 bridge, the two outbound lanes introduced as part of the new system will remain in use towards the traffic lights. Short holds may be necessary to bring shuttle buses or emergency vehicles onto the estate safely. This may cause brief delays while the outbound lane is paused."

A spokesperson for the club added: "Stoke City, together with its traffic management provider CSP, will be monitoring conditions live on the day and will make operational adjustments to keep traffic moving as much as possible. Please plan, allow additional travel time, and follow the instructions of stewards and signage to help keep everyone moving safely.

"Thank you for your patience, cooperation and support."
