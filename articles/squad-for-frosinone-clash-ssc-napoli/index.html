Squad for Frosinone clash - SSC Napoli

TheTools · 10.10.2026 10:12 · source · permalink

The squad for Napoli’s upcoming Serie A home match against Frosinone, scheduled for Saturday at 20:45 CEST at the Stadio Maradona, has been announced: Badiashile, Beukema, Contini, De Bruyne, Di Lorenzo, Gilmour, Giovane, Hojlund, Lang, Lobotka, Lucca, Marianucci, Rafa Marin, McTominay, Meret, Milinkovic-Savic, Milton Pereyra, Neres, Politano, Rrahmani, Spinazzola.
