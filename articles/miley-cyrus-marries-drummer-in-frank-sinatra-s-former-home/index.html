Miley Cyrus marries drummer in Frank Sinatra’s former home

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:59 · source · permalink

Pop star holds outdoor ceremony on private estate in California

Miley Cyrus has married musician Maxx Morando in a private estate formerly owned by Frank Sinatra.

The singer, 33, and the 28-year-old drummer held the private outdoor wedding in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday.

The ceremony, officiated by Wayne Coyne – the lead singer of rock band the Flaming Lips – was attended by the couple’s closest friends and family, including Billy Ray Cyrus, the singer’s father.

Photographs from the pop star’s weddings show her in a long-sleeved, white satin gown standing under an arch adorned with white flowers. Morando, the drummer for rock band Liily, wore a black tuxedo for the ceremony.

Guests included Elizabeth Hurley, who is dating Billy Ray Cyrus, and her son Damien Hurley, as well as Noah Cyrus, the singer’s younger sister.

The wedding details were kept secret, with no date or location previously released.

Lily-Rose Purcell, Cyrus’s stepsister, hinted at a wedding when she shared pictures from the rehearsal dinner on Friday night.

On Saturday morning Tish Cyrus, the singer’s mother, was seen arriving at the wedding venue.

The couple, who have largely kept their relationship out of the public eye, first met on a blind date arranged by mutual friends in 2021.

Cyrus previously spoke about meeting Morando in an interview with British Vogue in 2023, saying: “We got put on a blind date. Well, it was blind for me and not really for him.”

They were first seen together in public in November, 2021, at the Gucci Love Parade fashion show in Los Angeles.

They became engaged in December last year, and Cyrus was seen wearing a diamond ring at the premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash in Los Angeles shortly afterwards.

Morando later revealed that he had proposed during a trip to Kyoto, Japan.

The wedding is Cyrus’s second marriage. She previously married actor Liam Hemsworth in December, 2018, but the couple separated in August, 2019. Their divorce was finalised in January, 2020.

Cyrus, who rose to fame as Disney Channel’s Hannah Montana, is said to still be struggling with the death of Dolly Parton, her godmother.
