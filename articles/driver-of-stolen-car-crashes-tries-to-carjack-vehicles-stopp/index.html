Driver of stolen car crashes, tries to carjack vehicles stopped at Auckland lights

TheTools · 24.09.2026 03:56 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A driver of a crashed stolen vehicle evading police allegedly attempted to carjack multiple cars stopped at lights at a busy South Auckland intersection.

Police said they received a report of a vehicle stolen from Roscommon Rd just after 4pm.

The vehicle was seen on Lambie Drive, where police said it “took off at speed” and crashed shortly afterwards at the intersection of Ormiston Rd and Te Irirangi Drive.

Police said the driver fled the damaged vehicle on foot and attempted to get into other cars waiting at the lights.

The carjacking attempts were unsuccessful.

“Upon arrival, police located the alleged offender, and he was taken into custody without issue,” the spokesperson said.

A Hato Hone St John spokesperson told the Herald they were notified of the incident at 4.18pm but were not required to respond.
