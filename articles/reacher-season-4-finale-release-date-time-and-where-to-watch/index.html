'Reacher Season' 4 Finale Release Date, Time and Where to Watch

TheTools · 09.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Jack Reacher’s latest war is about to reach its breaking point. After weeks of Philadelphia conspiracies, betrayals, and brutal fight scenes, ‘Reacher‘ Season 4 is finally closing in on its finale, and fans are eager to know exactly when they can watch it.

The eighth and final episode, fittingly titled ‘Cut,’ promises to answer the biggest questions the season has raised, from who is really behind Anna’s death to what happens to the shadowy figures pulling the strings. Here’s everything currently known about the release date, time, and where to stream it.

‘Reacher’ Season 4 Episode 8 Release Date

‘Reacher’ Season 4 premiered on Amazon Prime Video on August 12, 2026, with its first three episodes dropping together before the show shifted to a weekly rollout for the remaining five episodes. That schedule culminates with the season finale.

Episode 8, titled ‘Cut,’ is set to release on September 16, 2026. That means the full eight episode season will have unfolded over just over a month, with the finale capping off Jack Reacher’s newest fight against a Philadelphia based conspiracy.

Fans should note that new episodes of ‘Reacher’ typically drop on Prime Video at 12 a.m. Pacific Time and 3 a.m. Eastern Time, meaning night owls on the East Coast may want to steer clear of spoilers late Tuesday. The consistent midnight Pacific release has held throughout the season, so there is little reason to expect a change for the finale.

Where to Watch the ‘Reacher’ Finale?

‘Reacher’ Season 4 remains exclusive to Amazon Prime Video, and it is not available on Netflix, Paramount Plus, or any other streaming service. Every subscription tier, including the ad supported plan, carries the show, so viewers do not need a premium package to catch the finale.

That also means cable subscribers or anyone hoping to catch the episode through a traditional broadcast will need to sign up for Prime Video directly, since there is no alternative way to stream ‘Cut’ when it lands.

For anyone who has kept pace with the season so far, the finale should be available to stream the moment it drops rather than arriving through a scheduled broadcast window, matching how every previous episode of Season 4 has rolled out.

What to Expect from the ‘Reacher’ Season 4 Finale?

According to the official episode description, Reacher, Tamara, and Jacob take the fight to the enemy as the full extent of their plot is revealed. Reacher faces off with those responsible for Anna’s death, while Jacob faces a moment of truth and Plum gets his shot at redemption.

That synopsis suggests the finale will finally tie together the threads that have been building since the season premiere, including the fallout from the massive cliffhanger in episode six and the political entanglements teased in the penultimate episode, ‘Vote for Sampson.’

Given the show’s track record, episode seven was expected to lean more into tension building rather than an all out showdown, saving the biggest confrontations for episode eight. That pattern lines up with how previous ‘Reacher’ seasons have paced their own finales, typically saving the biggest physical confrontations for the very last hour.

Critically, the season has been performing well, holding a 93 percent approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes based on 29 reviews, with critics praising the show for delivering brawn alongside just enough narrative depth to keep the franchise moving. That reception suggests the finale has a lot of goodwill to build on heading into its biggest moments.

What Comes After ‘Reacher’ Season 4 Ends?

Fans will not have long to sit with the finale before the franchise expands even further. A new spinoff series, ‘Neagley,’ starring Maria Sten as private investigator Frances Neagley, will premiere on September 16, immediately following the ‘Reacher’ Season 4 finale.

Unlike the flagship series, ‘Neagley’ will not follow a weekly release format, since all eight episodes of the spinoff will be available to stream at once. Alan Ritchson is also set to make a guest appearance as Jack Reacher in the new series, giving fans an immediate continuation of the story world even after Season 4 wraps.

‘Reacher’ has already been renewed for a fifth season, so the Philadelphia conspiracy closing out in ‘Cut’ will not be the character’s last outing. Between the finale and the launch of ‘Neagley’ on the very same day, September 16 is shaping up to be a major date for the franchise’s growing universe.
