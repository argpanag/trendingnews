Rehab facility where Presley Gerber died had prior overdose incident months earlier

TheTools · 21.10.2026 14:07 · source · permalink

Police reports indicate that officers were dispatched to Resolutions Living in February 2026 after staff expressed concern that a resident had suffered a drug overdose, TMZ reports.

During the February incident, emergency responders and facility personnel administered multiple doses of naloxone to revive an unresponsive female resident. While the individual regained consciousness and did not require hospital transport following on-site evaluation, the event highlights previous emergency interventions at the facility seven months prior to Gerber’s death.

The twenty-six-year-old model and son of Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber died at the Santa Monica facility on September 20, just hours after checking into the home. First responders performed life-saving protocols upon arrival, but Gerber was pronounced dead at the scene. Reports regarding his admission indicate that facility staff were informed that Gerber was actively under the influence of narcotics prior to his arrival, yet proceeded to admit him into the sober living environment.

Following Gerber’s death, internal personnel changes occurred at the facility, including the termination of an admissions officer. The facility’s owner declined to provide public comment regarding admission protocols or past medical emergencies at the location. Local authorities continue to investigate the circumstances surrounding Gerber’s death as a suspected overdose while awaiting final toxicology results from the medical examiner’s office.
