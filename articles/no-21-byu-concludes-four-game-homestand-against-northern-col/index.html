Wednesday will be the first all-time meeting between the two schools.

PROVO, Utah - No. 21 BYU concludes its four-game homestand on Wednesday, Nov. 18, when the Cougars host Northern Colorado. Tip is scheduled for 7 p.m. MT.

WHERE TO WATCH
Wednesday's game can be seen on ESPN+ with Dave McCann, Blaine Fowler and Spencer Linton on the call. 

FIRST TIMER
Northern Colorado is one of two teams BYU will face for the first time in program history this season. The Cougars are also scheduled to take on Northwestern on Saturday, Dec. 5, at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City.

While this will be the first meeting between BYU and Northern Colorado, the Cougars are no strangers to the Big Sky Conference. BYU is 60-9 against Big Sky opponents since 1980, including an 86-71 victory over Montana State in its last outing to extend its winning streak against the conference to 10 games.

AIN'T IN BIG SKY COUNTRY
BYU improved to 4-0 for the second time under third-year head coach Kevin Young with an 86-71 victory over Montana State on Friday evening in front of 17,561 fans at the Marriott Center. 

The Cougars scored 43 points in each half for their 29th straight non-conference home win, shooting 58.2 percent from the field, 40.9 percent from 3-point range and 54.2 percent from the free throw line. 

Robert Wright III led the team in scoring for a fourth time this season, one less than he did in 35 games last season. The junior went 9-of-15 from the field and 4-of-8 from the free throw line while dishing out a game-high seven assists. He also tied a career high with five steals, four of those coming after halftime. 

Bruce Branch III tied a career high with 17 points, going 6-of-8 from the field, 2-of-2 from behind the arc and 3-of-7 from the charity stripe. He had five rebounds, two of those coming on the offensive glass.

PROTECT THE FAMILY
Robert Wright III and Brooks Bahr both rank in the Top 100 in the nation in assist-to-turnover ratio as the duo has combined for 31 assists and only seven turnovers. Prior to the Montana State game, Wright III was one of three Big 12 players since 1996 with at least 75 points and only two turnovers in their first three games. 

BETTER TURN THE BUS AROUND
Heading into Wednesdayís doubleheader, BYU men's and women's basketball are a combined 8-0 at the Marriott Center this season. In those eight victories, the programs are outscoring opponents by 23.3 points per game and shooting 48.3 percent from the field and 34.0 percent from behind the arc. 

LIGHTS OUT
The Cougars went 32-of-55 from the field in the win over Montana State, marking the fifth time under Young that the Cougars have shot 58.0 percent or better from the field. 

Through two weeks of the season, BYU ranks first in the Big 12 and second in the nation in effective field goal percentage (65.7 percent). They also rank second in the league and eighth nationally in field goal percentage (55.0 percent). 

CROWN THE KING
With 22 points, seven assists and five steals in Thursday's win over Montana State, Robert Wright III became the fifth Cougar since 1996 to score at least 20 points with five or more assists and five or more steals in a game. 

The junior's 30.8 usage rate ranks him in the 96th percentile in NCAA Division I men's basketball, while also ranking in the 91st percentile in assists/game, 96th in assist-to-turnover ratio (4.75), 96th in fouls drawn per 40 minutes 97.6) and 88th in true shooting percentage (67.7). 

HE IS A GOOD GUY, JUST A LITTLE DEFENSIVE
The Cougars are 13-1 when Khadim Mboup has five or more defensive rebounds, including 12 straight victories. Five of his six rebounds in the win over Montana State came off the defensive glass as he tied for the team lead with Kohler and Betsey. 

Last season, Mboup had the sixth-most double-digit rebounding performances by a freshman in program history, while his 53 offensive boards are tied for the 10th most. His 14 rebounds in a win over Kansas State in the opening round of the Big 12 Championship are the second-most by a Cougar in a conference tournament in program history.
