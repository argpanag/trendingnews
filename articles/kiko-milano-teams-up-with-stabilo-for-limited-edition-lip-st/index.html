KIKO Milano Teams Up with STABILO for Limited-Edition Lip Stain

TheTools · 06.11.2026 17:55 · source · permalink
  • Beauty & Cosmetics
  • Branded Products
  • Greetings & Stationery
  • News

Inspired by the stationery brand’s iconic highlighter design, the format allows users to build color intensity.

November 6, 2026

KIKO Milano has unveiled a playful new beauty collaboration with stationery brand STABILO, introducing a limited-edition matte lip stain available in five colors.

The product, called the STABILO x KIKO Milano Lip Mark(er), takes inspiration from STABILO's iconic highlighter design, translating it into a fun, marker-style lip product. The format allows users to build color intensity to their liking.

The brands are encouraging users to mix and match the new lip stain with other KIKO lip products, enabling countless color combinations and the ability to create a personalized look.

The limited-edition is available at Kiko Milano in the U.K.
