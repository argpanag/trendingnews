India face Sri Lanka in the Asian Games 2026 men's semi-final on Thursday, October 1, a repeat of last year's Asia Cup final. It's also a rematch neither side has had to wait long for. Once again, the weather has dominated the build-up, rather than team news.

Shreyas Iyer's side reached this stage without bowling a ball, after their quarter-final against Afghanistan was washed out and India advanced on superior ICC rankings. Sri Lanka arrived the same way, with their tie against Nepal also abandoned without a toss.

Nisshin weather forecast for India vs Sri Lanka semi-final

Conditions in Nisshin have improved noticeably since the washed-out quarter-finals earlier this week. Local trackers show Tuesday's rain chance dropping to as low as 2 per cent during the day, a sharp turnaround from the persistent downpours that wiped out Monday's fixtures.

The improvement is not expected to last all day, however. AccuWeather's outlook for Thursday points to showers returning by evening, with overcast and humid conditions building through the afternoon before heavier rain arrives later. The match window itself, scheduled for early afternoon local time, looks to fall in the drier part of the day.

  • 12:00 PM: 27°C, rain chance around 20%
  • 1:30 PM (start): 27°C, rain chance around 25%
  • 3:00 PM: 26°C, rain chance around 30%

  • 7:00 PM: 23°C, rain chance around 55%

Chances of rain interrupting play in Nisshin

This is the first genuinely improved forecast of the knockout stage so far. Where the quarter-finals faced rain chances well above 90 per cent for most of the day, Thursday's outlook shows a considerably lower risk through the toss and the early overs of the match.

The risk does not disappear entirely, and forecasters remain divided on exactly how much rain returns by evening. If the match runs long, or if the start is delayed for any reason, the increasing rain chance later in the day could still force an interruption.

Even so, this is the best window either side has had all tournament, and a completed semi-final looks a realistic prospect for the first time since the knockouts began.

What happens if the India vs. Sri Lanka semi-final is washed out?

Asian Games cricket does not use a reserve day. If the match is abandoned with no result possible, the team ranked higher on the ICC's official T20I rankings is declared the winner and advances to the final.

India currently sit well above Sri Lanka in those rankings, meaning a washout would send India through without a ball being bowled, continuing the exact pattern that has decided every knockout tie at this tournament so far.

What time does the India vs. Sri Lanka semi-final start?

The Asian Games 2026 semi-final between India and Sri Lanka will be played at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan.

The match is scheduled to begin at 1:30 p.m. local time (JST) on Thursday, October 1, which is 10:00 a.m. IST for viewers in India.
