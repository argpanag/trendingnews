Days of Our Lives star Robert Kelker-Kelly has died at 62, according to reports.

Kelker-Kelly died October 3 in Missouri. A cause of death has not been revealed.

TMZ was first to report the news.

Kelker-Kelly was best known for playing Bo Brady on the hit soap opera. He took over the role from Peter Reckell in 1992 and starred opposite Kristian Alfonso and Lisa Rinna.

Reckell then reassumed the role in 1995.

Kelker-Kelly went on to star on General Hospital, appearing in 42 episodes of that daytime soap opera as Stavros Cassadine.
