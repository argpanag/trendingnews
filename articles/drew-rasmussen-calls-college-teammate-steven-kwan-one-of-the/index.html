Drew Rasmussen Calls College Teammate Steven Kwan 'One of the Greatest Human Beings' - AOL

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Drew Rasmussen will face a familiar opponent when the Tampa Bay Rays open the American League Championship Series against the Cleveland Guardians on Monday. The right-hander is preparing to pitch against Steven Kwan, his former college teammate and longtime friend at Oregon State.

Rasmussen spoke warmly about Kwan ahead of the matchup, praising the Guardians outfielder’s character as well as his ability. The two played together in college before establishing themselves in the major leagues.

“There’s not enough great things I could say about the kid. He’s perfect. He’s unbelievable. He’s one of the greatest human beings in the entire sport,” Rasmussen said, according to ESPN reporter Buster Olney.

Rasmussen also described the familiar exchange between the two when they meet on the field. “I definitely acknowledge every single time,” he said. “Usually a little smile gets shot back in my direction. I mean, shoot, I was 19 the first time I faced him in fall ball at Oregon State.”

The matchup carries added significance because Rasmussen enters the series after a dominant postseason start. He held the New York Yankees to one hit over eight scoreless innings in Game 1 of the American League Division Series, striking out 10 and allowing two walks as Tampa Bay won 1-0.

Rasmussen also delivered a strong regular season, finishing 2026 with a 16-5 record, a 2.67 ERA and 174 strikeouts across 172 innings. His performance gives Tampa Bay an experienced starter to lead its rotation into the matchup with Cleveland.

Kwan finished the regular season with a .282 batting average, a .382 on-base percentage, 152 hits and 10 stolen bases. The left fielder also helped Cleveland reach the ALCS after the Guardians defeated the Chicago White Sox in five games in the Division Series.

The Rays finished 98-64 and swept the Yankees in their ALDS, while Cleveland advanced after rallying from a two-game deficit against Chicago. The teams begin their best-of-seven series Monday at Tropicana Field, with Rasmussen scheduled to start for Tampa Bay.

The friendship between Rasmussen and Kwan will add a personal element to the opening game, but both players will have a clear job to do. Rasmussen will try to keep Cleveland’s lineup in check, while Kwan will look to help the Guardians take an early lead in the series.
