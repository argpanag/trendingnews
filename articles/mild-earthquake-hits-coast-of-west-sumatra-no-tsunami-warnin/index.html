Mild Earthquake Hits Coast Of West Sumatra, No Tsunami Warning Issued

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:32 · source · permalink

A mild earthquake measuring 5.2 on the Richter scale struck off the western coast of northern Sumatra, Indonesia, at 10:59 am today, with no tsunami threat to Malaysia, according to the Malaysian Meteorological Department (MetMalaysia).

The earthquake was recorded at a depth of 10km, with its epicentre located at 3.2 degrees north and 92.7 degrees east, approximately 364km west of Simeulue, Indonesia.

MetMalaysia confirmed that the tremor posed no tsunami threat to Malaysia, with no tsunami warning issued.

The department shared the earthquake information through its official earthquake monitoring channels, advising the public to refer to its updates for the latest information.
