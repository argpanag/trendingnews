World of Warcraft: Forever revamps WoW: Classic with new lands & quests in November 2026

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:47 · source · permalink

At BlizzCon 2026, Blizzard Entertainment showed off prominent reveals about the future of World of Warcraft, including a new adventure from the team working on World of Warcraft: Classic. The next big MMORPG chapter for that team is World of Warcraft: Forever, which sounds like another reboot, but this time with a reimagined Azeroth, new races, locations, and a massive collection of new quests. It will release on November 4, 2026 on PC, and a beta will open next week.

Blizzard Entertainment announced the details of World of Warcraft: Forever at BlizzCon 2026. A beta where players can play up to Level 30 will launch on September 16, 2026 and players can sign up now. The grand pitch for the game is as follows:

On its social media, the World of Warcraft team also shared a bullet list of what players can expect in Forever:

  • New dungeons
  • 10 & 20-player raids
  • Skyborne playable race
  • New & expanded zones
  • Account-wide perks
  • Updated world visuals & lighting
  • New race & class combos
  • 1000+ new quests
