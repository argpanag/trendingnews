Yankees, Mets, Phillies' MLB Top Free-Agent, Trade Targets After Teruaki Sato's Cubs Contract

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

One of the top hitters in the 2027 MLB free-agent class is now off the board.

ESPN's Jeff Passan reported Japanese slugger Teruaki Sato agreed to a six-year, $120 million deal with the Chicago Cubs. Sato is the No. 5 overall player in Bleacher Report's free-agent rankings.

The New York Yankees, New York Mets and Philadelphia Phillies were all linked with Sato earlier this month. If strengthening the offense remains a focus, the three will have to pivot to other targets.

Here's who could be on the radar for the trio of contenders.

New York Yankees

The Bronx Bombers are heading into 2027 with an infield that could start Ryan McMahon at third and potentially Anthony Volpe at second. McMahon has slugged just .349 since his move to New York, while Volpe has been a below-average hitter since debuting in MLB in 2023.

Alec Bohm or Brandon Lowe would provide an immediate offensive upgrade.

Bohm is coming off a disappointing 2026 season in which he posted a .249/.315/.383 slash line. Hopefully, that's just a one-year blip. Lowe could regress in a different direction after hitting 35 home runs and driving in 96 RBI but is still a proven commodity. His 150 appearances were also a career high.

On the pitching front, the Yankees have pursued Japanese high schooler Shoki Oda. The 18-year-old wouldn't provide immediate help to the starting rotation, but he could be a long-term contributor at a manageable cost if he decides to make the jump to the United States

New York Mets

The Mets might be done big-game hunting after lining up a $450 million deal for two-time Cy Young Award winner Tarik Skubal.

The Athletic's Will Sammon reported Monday that "some kind of short-term arrangement" is New York's likely route to add any further pitching. The New York Post's Jon Heyman and Joel Sherman suggested Nick Martinez could be a solution:

Japanese pitcher Kaima Taira, on the other hand, might be a little too pricey for the Mets' tastes. Teams that missed out on Skubal could go after the right-hander even more aggressively.

If New York is willing to keep expanding the payroll, Taira's versatility as a starter and reliever could be worth it.

Philadelphia Phillies

Phillies president of baseball operations Dave Dombrowski didn't shy away from laying out his top priority.

"I will say one thing: We have to have an outfielder," he told reporters earlier this month. "Right now, we don't have an outfielder. But is that more important than some other things? I guess it depends upon who that outfielder is."

Randy Arozarena and Seiya Suzuki are the best two outfielders available. In the case of the latter, Sato's agreement with Chicago could mean a reunion to the Cubs is off the table.

Especially if the bidding climbs into the nine figures, Philly would be right to have some reservations about signing either player. But the fans will voice their frustration if the team fails to bring serious reinforcements to the outfield.
