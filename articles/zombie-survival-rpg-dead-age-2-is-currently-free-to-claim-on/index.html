Zombie Survival RPG Dead Age 2 Is Currently Free to Claim on GOG

TheTools · 28.11.2026 05:20 · source · permalink

Developer Silent Dreams and publisher Headup have launched a limited-time giveaway on GOG, allowing PC players to claim a free DRM-free copy of Dead Age 2: The Zombie Survival RPG. Interested players can secure their permanent digital copy by logging into their GOG account, visiting the game’s storefront page, and clicking the giveaway banner to add it directly to their library.

Post-Apocalyptic Factions and Survival Gameplay

Set 10 years after the initial zombie outbreak, Dead Age 2 follows Jack’s surviving group after fleeing to Freedom City, where a supposed cure for the virus sparks bitter conflict rather than peace. Players must scavenge scarce food and resources while navigating a brutal power struggle among three rival factions: the ruthless military, cunning smugglers, and civilian Independents.

Turn-Based Combat and Roguelike Mechanics

Drawing inspiration from Darkest Dungeon, Fallout, This War of Mine, and The Walking Dead, the title blends fast-paced turn-based battles with base management, permadeath stakes, and roguelike progression. Every decision carries lasting consequences for survivor morale, faction reputation, and camp survival against both flesh-eating hordes and hostile looters.

GOG: https://www.gog.com/en/game/dead_age_2
