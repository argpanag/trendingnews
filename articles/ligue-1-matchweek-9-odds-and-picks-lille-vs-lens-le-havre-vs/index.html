Ligue 1 Matchweek 9 Odds And Picks: Lille Vs Lens, Le Havre Vs PSG

TheTools · 29.10.2026 11:25 · source · permalink

Nine Ligue 1 games from Friday to Monday: Lille vs Lens, PSG at Le Havre, Paris FC vs Monaco and Marseille vs Toulouse lead the card. Best decimal prices at Canadian sportsbooks for every game, a clear view on each and a best-bets table to finish.

Eight rounds in, three points cover the top four in Ligue 1: PSG on 17, Rennes on 16, Lille and Monaco on 15. Matchweek 9 sends the leaders to bottom side Le Havre, puts Lille against Lens on Friday night and closes on Monday with Lorient against Brest.

The best value on the card sits with home sides the market is underrating: Lille against a Lens team with a long absence list, Nice against a Rennes side missing its leading scorer, and Paris FC, still unbeaten at home. Lyon vs Angers is the one to skip, because the Lyon price leaves nothing on the table.

Kickoffs are listed in Eastern time. Prices are decimal and taken from the Canadian books in the tables, and the wider market is on the sports betting odds page.

Friday, October 30

Le Havre Vs PSG: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Friday, October 30, 2:00 p.m. ET, Stade Océane.

The leaders visit the only winless team in the league. Le Havre are 0-2-6 with two points, eight goals for and 15 against, and they lost a 4-3 thriller at Marseille last weekend.

PSG are 5-2-1 and beat Lyon 2-0 on Monday, but Ousmane Dembélé, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes all sit on the injury list. Ferrán Torres is the club's top league scorer with four.

At 1.26 on PSG there is nothing to do on the result. The total is a different story. Le Havre's games have gone over 3.5 goals only three times in eight, and all five PSG wins have been by one or two goals. Under 3.5 at 2.05 at 1xBet is the bet. The danger is a repeat of the Marseille game, where Le Havre chased and the match opened up.

Nice Vs Rennes: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Friday, October 30, 2:00 p.m. ET, Allianz Riviera.

Rennes arrive second at 5-1-2 and level with Marseille on a league-best 18 goals, but Esteban Lepaul, top of the scoring chart with seven, is listed with an ankle injury and goalkeeper Brice Samba is listed as suspended for yellow cards.

Nice are 3-2-3 and won 2-1 at Brest last Friday. Their home wins over Lille and Strasbourg both came 2-1.

Rennes moved out from 2.45 to 2.53 at 1xBet on October 27, while Nice shortened slightly. The market is drifting the right way, but not far enough. Nice on the draw-no-bet line at 2.05 at Tonybet, 20Bet and Granawin is the value: a win pays and a draw refunds. The risk is that Rennes have still scored 18 goals, with Sebastian Szymański chipping in three.

Lille Vs Lens: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Friday, October 30, 4:05 p.m. ET, Decathlon Arena - Stade Pierre-Mauroy.

Lille are 4-3-1, third in the table, and have conceded just six times, level with Lorient for the fewest in the league. They drew 1-1 at Monaco last weekend. Tanguy Nianzou serves a red-card suspension, while Ayase Ueda and Tiago Santos are injured.

Lens are 4-1-3 with 16 goals and beat Paris FC 2-1 on Saturday. Florian Thauvin has four goals and eight assists. The squad is stretched, though: Odsonne Édouard, Abdallah Sima, Jonathan Gradit, Pierre Ganiou and Maik Nawrocki are all unavailable. Lille won the last league meeting 3-0 in April.

Lille at 2.55 at 1xBet is the price to take. The home side has the better defence and the healthier squad, and the market has this close to a coin flip. The main threat is Thauvin, who has had a hand in 12 of Lens' 16 goals.

Saturday, October 31

Troyes Vs Le Mans: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Saturday, October 31, 3:45 p.m. ET, Stade de l'Aube.

Troyes have the weakest attack in Ligue 1 with five goals in eight games and sit 17th at 1-2-5. They drew 0-0 at Toulouse last time out.

Le Mans are 15th at 1-4-3, with Louis Mafouta on four goals, and drew 1-1 at Auxerre.

Troyes are favourites at 2.39, which asks a lot of a team that has scored twice in a game only twice all season. The better angle is Le Mans on the draw-no-bet line at 2.14 at Granawin, 20Bet and Tonybet. The risk: Le Mans have only one win and have conceded 16.

Strasbourg Vs Auxerre: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Sunday, November 1, 9:00 a.m. ET, Stade de la Meinau.

Identical goal columns here: 12 scored, 18 conceded for both. Strasbourg are 2-1-5 after a 3-0 loss at Rennes and have a long absence list that includes Joaquín Panichelli and Fabio Baldé. Auxerre are 3-1-4, with Cameron Archer on five goals and Daniel Namaso on three.

Both teams have scored in six of Strasbourg's eight league games and seven of Auxerre's. Both teams to score at 1.57 at 20Bet, Tonybet and Granawin is the pick. Strasbourg at 1.87 to win looks short for a team with five defeats. The risk is a scrappy game where one side keeps a rare clean sheet.

Lyon Vs Angers: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Sunday, November 1, 11:15 a.m. ET, Groupama Stadium.

Lyon are 4-2-2 with only seven goals conceded but lost 2-0 at PSG on Monday, and Nicolás Tagliafico, Pavel Šulc and Orel Mangala are among several players listed as out. Angers are 2-2-4, nine goals for and 15 against, after a 0-0 with Lorient.

The best Lyon price is 1.44 at 1xBet, which is thin for a side that was held 1-1 at home by Le Havre. Angers at 7.70 does not appeal either. This is the game to sit out.

Paris FC Vs Monaco: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Sunday, November 1, 11:15 a.m. ET, Stade Jean Bouin.

Paris FC have taken 10 of 12 points at home (3-1-0), including a 4-3 win over Rennes and a 3-0 win over Nice. Lassine Sinayoko has six goals and four assists. Their last match was a 2-1 loss at Lens.

Monaco are fourth at 4-3-1 with seven goals conceded, and drew 1-1 with Lille last time. Ansu Fati and Eric Dier are listed as injured; Paris Brunner has four goals.

Paris FC at 2.39 at 1xBet is the play. The home record supports a shorter price than that. The risk is Monaco's defence, joint third-tightest in the league with Lyon's.

Marseille Vs Toulouse: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Sunday, November 1, 2:45 p.m. ET, Stade Vélodrome.

Marseille are 4-0-4 and have yet to draw. All four losses came against current top-six sides (PSG, Rennes, Monaco and Paris FC), and all four wins came against teams in the bottom half, the latest a 4-3 win over Le Havre. Amine Gouiri leads the side with five goals.

Toulouse are 11th at 2-4-2 after a 0-0 draw with Troyes. Casper Tengstedt has five goals, and Aron Dønnum is listed as suspended after a sending-off.

Marseille at 1.95 at 1xBet is the bet, against a side from the half of the table they have beaten every time this season. The risk is the draw: Toulouse have drawn half of their games.

Lorient Vs Brest: Prediction And Odds

Kickoff: Monday, November 2, 2:45 p.m. ET, Stade du Moustoir - Yves Allainmat.

Lorient have played the tightest games in the division: seven goals scored, six conceded, three 1-0 wins and two 0-0 draws, the latest at Angers. Five of their eight games have stayed under 2.5 goals.

Brest are 2-3-3 with 14 goals and lost 2-1 at home to Nice. Kamory Doumbia has four goals, Ludovic Ajorque three.

Under 2.5 goals at 1.89 at 1xBet is the pick, in line with how Lorient set up. The risk is a Brest attack capable of big totals, as their 5-1 win over Angers showed.
