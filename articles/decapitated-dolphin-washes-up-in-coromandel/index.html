Decapitated dolphin washes up in Coromandel

TheTools · 10.09.2026 03:51 · source · permalink

A decapitated dolphin has washed up on Pauanui beach in the Coromandel and the Conservation department wants to hear from anyone who knows what happened.

Conservation department investigation officer Hayden Loper said although the dolphin's body was decomposed, it appeared some kind of tool or implement had been used to remove its head.

Loper said marine scientists believed it was a bottle nosed dolphin, a species fully protected since 1978.

"We are asking anyone who saw what happened to this dolphin to come forward," Loper said.

"Pauanui is a small but busy settlement with a popular beach, so we're hopeful someone can provide us with information which may lead us to whoever is responsible."

He said anyone with any information should contact the Department of Conservation on 0800 362 468, or email compliance@doc.govt.nz, and quote CLE-12503.
