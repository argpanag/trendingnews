Kelly Osbourne Details Facing "Cruel" Body-Shamers When She Was "So Sick"

TheTools · 01.09.2026 03:19 · source · permalink

Kelly Osbourne, 41, detailed how she responded to online body shaming amid being "so sick" following the death of her father Ozzy Osbourne in 2025.

Kelly Osbourne's path to healing hasn't been easy. 

Following the death of her father Ozzy Osbourne in 2025 from a heart attack, the talk show host confessed how difficult it was to deal with outside scrutiny about her body on top of her family's heartbreak.

"I didn't navigate it—I wasn't well," Kelly told Hello Magazine in an interview published Aug. 31 of her response to body shaming that spiked after she appeared at the Brit Awards in February. "How can you navigate something so cruel when you're so sick?"

Despite the negative attention, the 41-year-old has been able to make progress on her health, remarking, "I'm getting a lot better. I've got all the help I need. I’m grateful for the support I have in my life."

And with her support system, including mom Sharon Osbourne as well as a therapist, Kelly has been able to put "one foot in front of the other" after relocating back to the U.K. from Los Angeles.

"You've got to fix the head before you can fix the body. It's a slow process. It's going to take me a minute," she continued. "I'm not going to be fixed in a day. I'm not going to be fixed on one therapy session. This is a lifetime of work I've got to do and continue to do for the rest of my life."

As such, Kelly—who is mom to 3-year-old Sidney with ex Sid Wilson—said she is discovering "the reasons why I do certain things that stem from my childhood."

As she put it, "I'm getting to know myself in a whole new way, but it's a lot more healthy."

While the reality star is nurturing her relationship with herself, she also finds strength in taking care of her son, who she welcomed in 2022 after she began dating the Slipknot musician.

"I have so many reasons to keep going," Kelly shared. "I've got my baby boy, and he makes me the happiest person in the world."

Aside from her family—who are making progress on producing Ozzy's biopic, per her younger brother Jack Osbourne—Kelly praised an "incredible team of people" who have stepped up during the tough time mourning the Black Sabbath rocker, including pal Melissa Rivers, who she met during her days working alongside Joan Rivers on E!'s Fashion Police.

"What you'll find when you're grieving is that people don't know how to handle it," she explained. "You do lose a lot of friends. But the ones who stick around are the real ones."
