A train has been taken out of service after it was targeted in a graffiti attack at Athlone Train Station.

The incident happened in the early hours of Saturday morning, while the train was stabled at the station.

Six windows on one trainset were graffitied.

Irish Rail have confirmed the train did operate one service this morning, but has since been withdrawn so it can be cleaned.

Gardaí in Athlone are investigating, and CCTV footage has been provided to assist with their inquiries.

It's believed to have taken place between 8pm on Friday, October 2nd and 6.30am on Saturday, October 3rd.

Irish Rail is appealing to the public not to engage in this type of vandalism, warning that when trains have to be taken out of service for cleaning, it reduces the number of trainsets available to operate services.
