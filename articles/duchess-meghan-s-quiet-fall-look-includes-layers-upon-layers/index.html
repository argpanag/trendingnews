Duchess Meghan didn't invent quiet luxury, but her wardrobe is the textbook definition of the trending term. Just look at her latest outfit.

In the newest Instagram post on the duchess' lifestyle brand, As Ever's grid, Meghan is seen wearing a laid-back but totally glamorous ensemble that pulled pieces from her minimalist designer wardrobe.

Up top, a cream-white sweater with ribbed details kept her cozy, while below, a silky olive-green midi skirt zhuzhed up the look just a bit. Meghan eschewed heels in exchange for sweet baby-pink ballerina flats with little bows. But the real luxury came courtesy of her jewelry: stacks of gold and diamond jewelry that amounted to more than $300,000, but somehow looked completely casual on her.

The standouts were Jennifer Meyer's large double dome tennis necklace ($61,250) and Logan Hollowell's reverse water drop martini studs ($5,575). Other pieces included her gold Cartier love bracelet ($7,950), her three-stone engagement ring from husband Prince Harry (which has an estimated value of $260,000 to $350,000), and her solid-gold Cartier Tank Française (its price ranges from approximately $17,850 to $35,700), which previously belonged to Harry's mother, Princess Diana.

As readers know, Meghan recently moved from Montecito, California, (the quiet-luxury mecca), to England with Harry and their two kids; but despite the change of culture, landscape, and weather, the duchess' style doesn't seen to have changed at all. And while she may not be a senior member of the British royal family any longer, we just know the late Lady Di would be proud of her exquisite jewelry collection.

Meghan's As Ever post, where she debuted her quietly luxurious ensemble, was shared to celebrate the launch of As Ever and Los Angeles chocolatier Compartés's limited-edition holiday advent calendar inspired by Meghan's Montecito home and garden. You can purchase it on asever.com starting today.
