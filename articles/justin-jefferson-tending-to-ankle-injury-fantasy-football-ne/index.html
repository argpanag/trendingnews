Justin Jefferson Tending to ankle injury - Fantasy Football News

TheTools · 27.09.2026 21:54 · source · permalink

Jefferson is questionable to return to Sunday's game at Tampa Bay due to an ankle injury.

Jefferson picked up the issue in the first half of this Week 3 matchup, and if he's unable to reenter the contest, he'll end it with two catches (on two targets) for 32 yards. WR Jordan Addison and TE T.J. Hockenson will take on more sway in the Vikings' passing game for as long as Jefferson is sidelined.
