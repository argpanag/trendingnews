How much money do players get paid at 2026 Presidents Cup?

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

There is no prize purse at the Presidents Cup, but the players still help generate millions for charities around the world.

There are plenty of reasons for the world's best golfers to want to play in the Presidents Cup.

National pride is one. 

The chance to compete alongside some of the biggest names in the game is another. 

And then there is the opportunity to lift one of golf's most recognisable team trophies.

But there is another obvious question whenever 24 of the world's best players gather for a major team event: how much money are they actually playing for?

The Presidents Cup is a rather unusual event in that respect.

Unlike a PGA Tour tournament, there is no individual leaderboard with millions of dollars waiting for the winner. 

How much money do Presidents Cup players get paid?

There is no prize money at the Presidents Cup.

Players are not paid for their participation. 

Instead, each competitor receives an equal portion of the funds generated by the event and allocates that money to charities of their choice.

It means the financial impact of the Presidents Cup extends far beyond the players and the competition itself.

Since the biennial contest was first staged in 1994, more than $56.4 million has been generated from Presidents Cup proceeds, while more than 475 charities across 18 countries have benefited over the past 25 years.

That charitable element is one of the things that makes the Presidents Cup different from a normal professional golf tournament.

Why are LIV players missing?

There is another important talking point surrounding this year's Presidents Cup.

The competition is run by the PGA Tour, meaning players currently competing on LIV Golf are ineligible to participate.

That has kept a number of big names out of the action at Medinah this week, none bigger than Bryson DeChambeau, Cameron Smith, Joaquin Niemann, Louis Oosthuizen and Lucas Herbert.

The absence of so many LIV players has particularly affected Geoff Ogilvy's International side.

The rules are different for the Ryder Cup, however, where LIV Golf players remain eligible for Team USA under the current rules.

That's because the Ryder Cup is run by the PGA of America and DP World Tour / Ryder Cup Europe. 

What is the Presidents Cup format?

The Presidents Cup is a four-day match-play competition featuring 30 matches between a 12-player United States team and a 12-player International team.

Unlike the Ryder Cup, which is a three-day event starting on a Friday, the Presidents Cup gets underway on Thursday.

There's also two more points available, with 30 at a Presidents Cup opposed to 28 at a Ryder Cup.

Thursday features five matches of fourballs. Friday then uses the opposite format, while Saturday features both fourball and foursomes matches across the morning and afternoon sessions.

Everything then comes down to Sunday, when all 24 players take part in singles.

Each match is worth one point.

The first team to reach 15½ points wins the Presidents Cup.

Presidents Cup Past Results

Despite the International team featuring players from countries around the world outside Europe, the competition has been heavily dominated by the United States.

The International team's only outright victory came in 1998 at Royal Melbourne, where they won by nine points.

The event produced one of its most memorable moments five years later in 2003.

Tiger Woods and Ernie Els were locked together after the final singles matches in South Africa, leading to a sudden-death playoff.

With darkness approaching, captains Jack Nicklaus and Gary Player ultimately agreed to share the Presidents Cup rather than continue the playoff.

Since then, the United States has continued to dominate.

USA has won the Presidents Cup 13 times, including 10 in a row on the eve of this week's contest. 

While the players at Medinah will not be competing for a traditional prize purse, the Presidents Cup still carries plenty of significance — and its charitable impact means there is considerably more at stake than simply the trophy.
