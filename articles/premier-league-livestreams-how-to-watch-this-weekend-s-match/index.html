Where Can You Stream the Premier League Matches?

Thankfully, this is an easy one. NBC has the rights to televise Premier League matches in the United States. For the 2026-27 season, it will be broadcast on Peacock streaming service, as well as its USA Network and NBC traditional television channels. To tune into these matches, you'll need a cable subscription or a live TV streaming service. Separately, the Spanish-language broadcast rights are held by Telemundo and Universo. Here's how to watch:

  • DirecTV: DirecTV's $90-per-month Entertainment package includes USA Network and Telemundo. You'd need the $125 Ultimate tier for Universo.

  • Fubo: The Fubo Latino plan costs $20 per month and includes access to Telemundo and Universo.

  • Hulu + Live TV: For $90 per month, you get USA Network and NBC Sports. An additional $ 4.99-per-month Español add-on can get you select Spanish-language programming, including Universo. Telemundo is an additional $11.99 per month to any plan, which seems excessive.

  • Peacock: You'll need the $ 10.99-per-month Peacock Premium plan to watch Premier League matches broadcast on NBC's platform.

  • Sling: Sling's $46 per month Sling Blue plan includes access to USA Network.

  • YouTube TV: Though the standard plan for YouTube TV costs $83 per month, you can actually now pay for the cheaper $65 Sports package to get USA Network, NBC Sports, and Telemundo. Universo can be added via the $14.99-per-month Spanish Plus add-on.

Which Teams Are Competing in the 2026/27 Premier League?

In total, 20 teams compete in the Premier League each year, including 17 of last season's top teams and three clubs promoted from the EFL Championship. Those promoted include Coventry City, Ipswich Town, and Hull City. Of note, Coventry is returning to the league after a 25-year absence. West Ham United, Burnley, and Wolverhampton Wanderers have been relegated to the second division.

The other clubs participating in the 2026-2027 Premier League season include defending champions Arsenal, Aston Villa, Bournemouth, Brentford, Brighton & Hove Albion, Chelsea, Crystal Palace, Everton, Fulham, Leeds United, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United, Newcastle United, Nottingham Forest, Sunderland, and Tottenham Hotspur.
