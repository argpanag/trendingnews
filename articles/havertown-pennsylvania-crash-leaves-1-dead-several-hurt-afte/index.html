Havertown, Pennsylvania, crash leaves 1 dead, several hurt after driver has possible medical emergency, police say

TheTools · 03.10.2026 20:50 · source · permalink

Updated on: October 3, 2026 / 3:50 PM EDT / CBS Philadelphia

One man died and several people were hurt in a crash in Haverford Township, Pennsylvania, Saturday afternoon, police said. 

Investigators believe the driver of a pickup truck may have been experiencing a medical emergency. The man was driving erratically on West Chester Pike from Darby Road to Eagle Road around 12:15 p.m., Haverford Township Police Chief John F. Viola said. 

The pickup truck sideswiped several cars along West Chester Pike before slamming into a U-Haul truck near the intersection with Eagle Road, Viola said.

The driver of the pickup truck died, and eight people were taken to area hospitals with non-life-threatening injuries, Viola said.

Fourteen vehicles were involved in the crash. The full crash scene spanned about 2 miles, Viola said.

West Chester Pike/Route 3 westbound is shut down in that area for the investigation. One lane is getting by on the eastbound side. Police plan to use a drone to take aerial images of the scene.

Many police officers were already in the area for Haverford Township Day when they received the call, Viola said.

This is a developing story and will be updated.
