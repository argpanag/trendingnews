Complete EA FC 27 Trophy List—How To Get All Trophies & Achievements

TheTools · 09.09.2026 08:29 · source · permalink

The new football season is in full swing, which naturally means a new EA Sports title is around the corner, and the full list of Trophies and Achievements in EA FC 27 is now available.

EA FC 27 is the latest entry in EA Sports‘ long-running franchise, landing across a plethora of platforms and bringing a heap of changes, including the new open-world mode, The Grounds.

For a complete list of all the tasks that lie ahead for Achievement and Trophy collectors in EA FC 27, Insider Gaming has you covered.

All EA FC 27 Achievements & Trophies

EA FC 27 has a stacked list of Achievements and Trophies to earn, scattered across all major game modes, some of which will require particular focus.

While a vast majority will be ticked off through general progression, like scoring a certain type of goal or completing basic objectives in Ultimate Team, others will require a lot of time, like completing five seasons with a single club in Career Mode, although you can likely skip ahead and simulate matches.

Unsurprisingly, there’s also a fair chunk of tasks to complete in the new The Grounds mode, the majority of which are for completing objectives tied to recognisable names from the football world.

How Many Achievements & Trophies in EA FC 27?

There are 47 total Achievements and Trophies to collect in EA FC 27, including the coveted Platinum Trophy on PlayStation, which may not have an equivalent on PC or XBOX.

The total number on last-gen platforms like PlayStation 4 and XBOX One is lower, as that does not include all of the game modes available on other platforms, including The Grounds.

The leak also suggests there are no secret Achievements or Trophies in EA FC 27, so you won’t be sweating trying to work out what there is left to complete.

You can expect to sink many hours across plenty of game modes, and an online connection is required for a vast majority of the tasks you need to tick off.
