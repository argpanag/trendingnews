Shanti Pereira marries lawyer and former sprinter Tan Zong Yang

TheTools · 21.11.2026 08:37 · source · permalink

Shanti Pereira is hitched!

The national track and field athlete tied the knot with her lawyer and former sprinter beau Tan Zong Yang at a ceremony at Church of Our Lady of Lourdes in Rochor on Saturday (Nov 21).

Zong Yang, 32, shared a series of Instagram Stories from their friends and family about their big day.

Thirty-year-old Shanti looks stunning in a strapless floral white gown with a sweetheart neckline and corseted bodice, while Zong Yang wears a beige suit with a dark bowtie and light pink boutonniere.

"Baby sis be married," reads a Story by Shanti's sister Seema, showing the couple making their exit through the aisle.

Shanti and Zong Yang announced their engagement back in June 2024.

The Asian Games gold medallist has been dating the SEA Games 2022 bronze medallist since 2021.
