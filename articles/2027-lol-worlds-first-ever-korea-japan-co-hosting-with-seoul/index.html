2027 LoL Worlds — First Ever Korea-Japan Co-Hosting with Seoul Arena Finale

TheTools · 14.11.2026 00:12 · source · permalink

The final chapter of 2027 LoL eSports will unfold in Seoul, South Korea. Before that, a notable milestone is the first-ever Korea-Japan co-hosting in the 17-year history of the League of Legends World Championship (Worlds).

Riot Games announced the detailed hosting locations and operational plans for three international LoL eSports tournaments scheduled for next year through the ‘2026 LoL Showcase’ held in Capital, New York. Chris Greeley, Head of LoL eSports, revealed the major roadmap for the 2027 First Stand (FST), Mid-Season Invitational (MSI), and World Championship just a day before the 2026 Worlds final.

The core of this announcement is undoubtedly the co-hosting of the 2027 World Championship by Korea and Japan. South Korea has previously hosted the Worlds finals three times as a single host nation, starting with the Seoul Sangam World Cup Stadium in 2014, followed by the Incheon Munhak Stadium in 2018, and the Seoul Gocheok Sky Dome in 2023. Meanwhile, Japan has operated its independent LJL league but has never hosted the main stage of the prestigious Worlds tournament.

Through the 2027 tournament, Riot Games has chosen a strategy to combine South Korea’s unmatched e스포츠 infrastructure with the growing fandom energy of the Japanese market. The Swiss Stage, which opens the tournament, will be held in Nagoya, Japan, for the first time in history, while all matches of the knockout stage (quarterfinals, semifinals, and finals)—the flower of the main tournament—will take place in Korea.

Once the quarterfinalists are decided in Nagoya according to the schedule, the winners will travel to Korea to compete in the quarterfinals and semifinals at the Suwon Convention Center in Gyeonggi Province. The long-awaited final to determine the year’s ultimate champion is scheduled to be held at ‘Seoul Arena,’ a next-generation specialized performance hall currently under construction in Chang-dong, Dobong-gu, Seoul. As a result, domestic fans will directly witness the crowning of the Summoner’s Cup on home soil for the first time in four years since 2023.

Changes will also be applied to the overall annual international tournament system. The FST, a season-opening international tournament established in 2025, will be held under a scaled-up special format called ‘First Stand: All In’ exclusively for 2027. Due to an increased number of participating teams and players compared to before, the hosting location has also been changed from Southeast Asia, which was initially planned, to China, while the Southeast Asia hosting has been postponed to 2028. Detailed schedules and formats will be revealed in January next year.

The 2027 MSI, the mid-year concluding tournament, will be held in Valencia, Spain, one of the core strongholds of the Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) region and the home base of LEC team Movistar KOI (MKOI).

Head Chris Greeley shared, “Korea has played an indispensable role in the history of LoL eSports, and I am thrilled to present the most important moments of the 2027 World Championship back in Korea.” Attention is focused on whether the Worlds, continuing its 17-year legacy, can set another milestone in Asian eSports through this novel attempt of Korea-Japan co-hosting.
