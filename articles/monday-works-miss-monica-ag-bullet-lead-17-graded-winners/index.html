Monday works: Miss Monica, Ag Bullet lead 17 graded winners

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:54 · source · permalink

Miss Monica, who won the Grade 1 Del Mar Debutante on Sept. 5, returned to work Monday for her first drill since the race. She worked four furlongs in 47.6 seconds over the Santa Anita dirt. The Bob Baffert trainee is undefeated in two starts, after a 5 1/2-length maiden win on Aug. 9 followed by her 6 1/4-lengths blowout in the Del Mar Debutante.

Grade 1 winner Ag Bullet also returned to the work tab for the first time since her last race. The daughter of Twirling Candy, trained by Richard Baltas, drilled four furlongs in 47.8 seconds over the all-weather training track. Ag Bullet won both the Jaipur (G1) and the Kentucky Downs Turf Sprint (G2) last year, and was the runner-up in the Jaipur this year, but still seeks her first victory since the 2025 Kentucky Downs meet. She was most recently eighth as the favorite in the 2026 Kentucky Downs Turf Sprint (G1).

These 17 graded-stakes winners worked across the country Monday morning:

Ag Bullet (Twirling Candy), a Grade 1 winner (2025 Jaipur), worked four furlongs in 47.8 seconds at Santa Anita on Monday morning. It was the 10th fastest of 33 works at the distance. This was her first work since her last race. In her last start, she placed eighth in the Kentucky Downs Ladies Turf Sprint (G1) going 6 1/2 furlongs on the turf at Kentucky Downs on Aug. 29 for trainer Richard Baltas.
