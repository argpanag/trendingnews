Inter Miami - Carrying solid momentum

Inter Miami begin their MLS Cup title defence looking to become the first Eastern Conference club this century to win consecutive championships. Kily González’s side will hope their attacking quality can make the difference against Columbus.

Recent form

Inter Miami finished second in the Eastern Conference with 60 points from 34 matches, just two points behind the leaders. They won two, drew two, and lost one of their final five regular-season fixtures.

Their latest outing ended in a 3-3 home draw against Charlotte FC, continuing a mixed run of results. Miami have dropped points in three of their last four league matches but have suffered only one defeat since October.

The Herons led MLS in scoring during the regular season, netting 80 goals at an average of 2.4 per match. Lionel Messi finished as the league’s top scorer for the second consecutive year, highlighting their considerable attacking strength.

González faces several injury concerns, with Mateo Silvetti and Tadeo Allende struggling with shoulder and knee problems, respectively. Israel Boatwright remains sidelined, while César Abadía is doubtful. Messi, Luis Suárez, and Germán Berterame are expected to lead the attack.

Columbus Crew - Enjoying a winning streak

Columbus Crew enter the MLS Cup playoffs in excellent form, having transformed their season with an impressive run of victories. Laurent Courtois’s side will be looking to maintain that momentum against the defending champions.

Recent form

Columbus finished seventh in the Eastern Conference with 47 points, securing their playoff place after a difficult start to the campaign. They failed to win their first five league matches but ended the regular season with six consecutive victories.

Their latest result was a thrilling 4-3 away win over Chicago Fire, with Brais Méndez scoring the decisive goal in stoppage time. The Crew have now scored at least twice in nine consecutive MLS matches.

Despite their attacking form, Columbus have struggled defensively away from home. They conceded at least three goals in seven away league matches this season and have lost their last three playoff fixtures on the road.

Courtois has relatively few injury concerns, although Wessam Abou Ali is doubtful with a knee problem. Gonzalo Tapia scored twice against Chicago, while Anass Zaroury and Brais Méndez also found the net in that victory.
