Brooklyn Beckham & Nicola Peltz’s Tense Red Carpet Moment Caught on Camera

TheTools · 11.09.2026 13:48 · source · permalink

Brooklyn Beckham, widely known as David Beckham’s son, has made headlines lately because of his marriage to Nicola Peltz and reported family tension with his mother. Amid all this, the model recently appeared with his wife on the red carpet in a moment caught on camera.

Brooklyn and Nicola were filmed in an awkward exchange at the Deauville American Film Festival

Recently, the power couple attended the Deauville American Film Festival in France on Tuesday, September 8, 2026. However, as always, fans did not focus on their designer outfits this time. Instead, their red carpet moment left fans wondering whether something was going on between Brooklyn Beckham and Nicola Peltz.

According to coverage from News.com, everything appeared fine at first, as the model kissed and hugged his wife. However, shortly after, the Foosball star stepped aside so that the Lola actress could enjoy the spotlight on her own. Before leaving Nicola Peltz, Brooklyn Beckham also fixed the back of her gown so that the actress could continue walking the red carpet without any trouble.

However, according to the source, Beckham’s move appeared to slightly irritate Peltz. The video clearly showed the 31-year-old staring at David Beckham’s son until he had walked away and reached the barrier nearby.

For context, Nicola Peltz attended the Deauville American Film Festival to support her friend and Prime co-star Faye Dunaway, who received the Icon Award that night. Meanwhile, the former footballer’s son supported his wife at the event.

Brooklyn Beckham married Nicola Peltz on April 9, 2022, at the actress’s family estate in Palm Beach, Florida. Since then, no reports of any major conflict between the power couple have emerged. However, the model and Bates Motel star’s marriage has reportedly coincided with growing tension between the 27-year-old and his parents, David Beckham and Victoria Beckham.
