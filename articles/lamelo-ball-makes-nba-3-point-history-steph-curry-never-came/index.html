Minnesota Timberwolves guard LaMelo Ball became the fastest player ever to 1,000 career 3-pointers, getting there in his 311th game in the win over the Bucks.

Minnesota Timberwolves star guard LaMelo Ball made history in the last game against the Milwaukee Bucks on Monday.

Ball led the Timberwolves to a convincing 112-100 home win, finishing with 34 points (12/21 FG, 6/13 3PT, 4/5 FT), 6 rebounds, and 3 assists, while also clinching a historic three-point mark.

The 25-year-old guard not only made his 1,000th career three-pointer, becoming the 188th player in history to reach this mark, but also became the fastest to ever do it, getting there in his 311th career game.

Previously, Duncan Robinson held this title as the fastest player to ever hit 1,000 three-pointers, doing it in 343 games.

By comparison, Golden State Warriors superstar Stephen Curry, arguably the greatest shooter to ever live, reached his 1,000th career three-pointer in his 369th career game back in 2015.

Other players who have reached the 1,000 career three-pointer mark include Buddy Hield (350 games), Luka Doncic (351 games), Anthony Edwards (353 games), Donovan Mitchell (355 games), and Tyler Herro (368 games).

Stephen Curry is by far the leader in all-time three-pointers made, as the 38-year-old currently sits comfortably first with 4,275 career three-pointers, over 800 ahead of James Harden, who sits second with 3,408 career makes.

LaMelo Ball hasn't found his groove yet in Minnesota after joining the Timberwolves following the offseason trade from the Charlotte Hornets.

In his eight appearances so far this season, Ball is averaging 16.9 points, 3.6 rebounds, and 5.9 assists, while shooting 42.7% from the field and 36.6% from three-point range.

Meanwhile, the Minnesota Timberwolves, who currently are without Anthony Edwards, who is set to miss significant time due to injury, are sitting with a 5-3 record in the middle of the Western Conference standings.
