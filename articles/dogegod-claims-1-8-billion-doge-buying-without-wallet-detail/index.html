DogeGod Claims 1.8 Billion DOGE Buying Without Wallet Details | TokenPost

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

The claim gives no wallet addresses, transactions or measurement period. Dogecoin’s Nov. 11 UTC daily aggregate close was below its Nov. 3 close.

  • DOGE Dogecoin

Dogecoin (DOGE) closed at $0.067467 in its Nov. 11 UTC daily aggregate, below $0.075172 on Nov. 3, while a separate large-holder buying claim lacked wallet-level details.

DogeGod claimed large holders accumulated 1.8 billion DOGE. The claim identifies no wallets, transactions, holder cohort or measurement period, so it does not establish net buying.

The two daily prices are volume-weighted aggregates across exchanges and markets, rather than closes on a particular exchange. They provide price context but do not verify the buying claim.

At 2:15 p.m. ET (19:15 UTC) Nov. 12, the 100 largest Dogecoin addresses held 103.7 billion DOGE, equal to 66.14% of the mined supply listed in that snapshot. The concentration figure does not show that those addresses, or their owners, bought the 1.8 billion DOGE in DogeGod’s claim.
