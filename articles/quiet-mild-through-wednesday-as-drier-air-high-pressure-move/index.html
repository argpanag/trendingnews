Quiet, mild through Wednesday as drier air, high pressure move in for a couple days

TheTools · 15.09.2026 01:56 · source · permalink

Showers and a few thunderstorms will continue late Monday night into Tuesday morning before we have a chance to dry out Tuesday. Winds will be shifting Tuesday and less humid air will be moving into southern Minnesota and northern Iowa.

Cloud cover decreases through Tuesday morning, making for a brighter and comfortably mild Tuesday afternoon. Some thin, high clouds will linger into the afternoon Tuesday but our skies will still be bright with filtered sunshine. Winds will be a bit gusty out of the northwest, but sunshine and comfortable temperatures will help offset that wind.

Overall fall-like conditions will continue through this week as temperatures continue to run a bit cool, just slightly below average for mid-September. High temperatures will be around 70 degrees both Tuesday and Wednesday. With a lighter wind, low temperatures will dip to a cool 50 degrees Wednesday morning.

The end of this week will become more active with showers and a few thunderstorms possible Wednesday night, again late Thursday, and from Friday through this coming weekend. We’re not looking at constant rain in that time frame, but rain will be more common beginning later this week.
