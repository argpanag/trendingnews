Second gales of the season arrive with strong northerly winds and the return of sunshine

TheTools · 16.10.2026 11:03 · source · permalink

Synopsis: Rain will move out of the region with windy and dry conditions ensuing. Temperatures will be on the uptrend, returning back above average by Sunday.

Today: Rain showers will continue to move across the Northland this morning, predominantly across WI and MI. Rapidly clearing skies are expected this afternoon with continued northerly winds @ 15-25 MPH. Gusts may be as high as 40 MPH. Winds will slowly decrease through the afternoon.

Tonight: Winds will become slightly weaker tonight and will turn out of the SW. Widespread lows in the 30s are expected with clear skies.

Tomorrow: Mostly sunny skies will be abundant all day with breezy winds out of the SW @ 10-20 MPH. Gusts as high as 30 MPH will be possible. Highs will be in the 50s.

Sunday: More sunshine will be on tap with continued SW winds @ 10-20 MPH. Gusts as high as 35 MPH will be possible along the higher elevations of the South Shore. Highs will be in the upper 50s and low 60s.
