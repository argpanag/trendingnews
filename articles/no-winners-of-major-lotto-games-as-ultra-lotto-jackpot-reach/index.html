No winners of major lotto games as Ultra Lotto jackpot reaches P337.8 million

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:31 · source · permalink

There were no winners of either of the major lotto games played by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) on Sunday, as the Ultra Lotto 6/58 jackpot steadily approached the P340-million mark.

No bettor picked the winning number combination of 57-40-09-54-56-27 for the Ultra Lotto 6/58 game, whose prize has reached P337,819,635.42.

There were also no winners of the Super Lotto 6/49 game, with the winning numbers of 09-10-41-38-08-26 for the jackpot, which has risen to P25,462,206.59.
