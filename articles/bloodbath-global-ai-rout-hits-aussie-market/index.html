‘Bloodbath’: Global AI rout hits Aussie market

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:05 · source · permalink
  • NVDA -10.94%
  • ^GSPC -3.70%

Australia's sharemarket has brutally fallen along with the rest of the world as investors rushed to sell anything artificial intelligence related.

On the opening bell on Monday, the benchmark ASX 200 slumped55.2 points or 0.6 per cent to 8537.4 points.

Australia avoided most of the US technology-led market rout over the weekend.

The S&P 500 plunged 3.7 per cent, while the technology heavy Nasdaq tumbled 5.3 per cent.

The two major US indices are now in a correction, meaning they have fallen more than 10 per cent from a previous high.

Leading the falls was artificial intelligence chipmaker Nvidia which tumbled nearly 14 per cent, even with the business beating expectations earlier in the week.

Capital.com senior financial market analyst Kyle Rodda said traders were selling off AI related stocks.

"Wall Street's sell-off is turning into a bloodbath, with a deflating AI bubble and the prospects of rate hikes in the United States slamming equity valuations," he said.

"The primary focus right now is what's happening in AI and it mostly pertains to fears about a peak in the investment boom and earnings growth.

"All signs over the past month – since tech earnings kicked off for the US reporting season – point to slowing growth in AI spending and moderating profit growth going forward."
