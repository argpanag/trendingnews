CBI arrests Munitions India Limited official, SMPP CEO in defence leaks-for-money case

TheTools · 15.10.2026 09:02 · source · permalink

    The CBI has arrested Anil Kumar, Munitions India Limited executive director, and SMPP CEO Ashish Kansal. They are charged with accepting bribes and leaking sensitive defense information related to a major supply contract. Cash amounting to over Rs 2.2 crore was recovered during searches at various premises. Additional individuals connected to the case are also implicated for their roles in the conspiracy.
