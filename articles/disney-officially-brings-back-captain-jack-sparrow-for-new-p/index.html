Disney Officially Brings Back Captain Jack Sparrow for New ‘Pirates of the Caribbean’ in 2027

TheTools · 25.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

It'll be ten years in May since we last saw Captain Jack Sparrow on the big screen in Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales, and while there's been plenty of talk about what Disney intends to do with the franchise going forward, we're still waiting to see whether Johnny Depp will ever don the costume again. Given that the franchise was born out of an attraction at Disneyland, though, it's perhaps appropriate that Disney's next venture involving Captain Jack will allow audiences to become part of the story themselves. And for those of us who still consider The Curse of the Black Pearl to be the best of the five movies, there's a particularly good reason to be excited about this one.

Captain Jack Sparrow will officially be back in 2027 as part of Secret Cinema presents Disney's Pirates of the Caribbean: The Immersive Adventure, a theatrical reimagining of the original 2003 movie. Disney and Secret Cinema are bringing the story of Jack, Will Turner, and Elizabeth Swann to life through a combination of live performances, stunts, projections, special effects, and the franchise's iconic music, with the action unfolding around audiences in 360 degrees.

Although the show itself runs for two hours and five minutes, Secret Cinema will be opening the doors to Pirate Cove an hour before each performance, and given how much there is to do before the show even starts, you'll probably want to get there early. Part of the fun of Secret Cinema is getting dressed up and immersing yourself in the world of the movie, and this time around, you'll be able to spend some time with your fellow pirates, listen to a live band, and get something to eat without having to leave the world of Pirates of the Caribbean.

Who Is Playing Captain Jack Sparrow in Disney's New 'Pirates of the Caribbean'?

Taking over from Depp for the theatrical production is George Maguire, with Phil Daniels (Quadrophenia) as Captain Barbossa, Lily Blunsom as Elizabeth Swann, Harrison Wilde as Will Turner, and Gleanne Purcell-Brown as Anamaria. The production will revisit the story that originally brought Depp, Orlando Bloom, Keira Knightley, and Geoffrey Rush together under the direction of Gore Verbinski.

Secret Cinema has brought more than 60 stories to life over the last 18 years, entertaining more than 1.5 million audience members through its immersive productions. Disney's Pirates of the Caribbean: The Immersive Adventure arrives in 2027. Further information is available on the official website.
