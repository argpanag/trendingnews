The winning numbers are out for the Powerball drawing on Sept. 30, but no one has won the jackpot yet.

The top prize increased to $440 million, with a cash value of $183.1 million, after no one won the drawing on Wednesday, according to Powerball's website. But a lucky person in Texas still won the $1 million Match 5 prize.

If no one matches all five white balls and the Powerball during the next drawing on Saturday, Oct. 3, the jackpot will continue to increase.

Here's what to know about the latest drawing and the drawing coming up.

Thomas Cook and Joseph Feeney of Wisconsin agreed years ago to split future lottery winnings. Cook followed through on that promise when he won a $22 million prize on June 10, 2020.

Did anyone win the Powerball jackpot?

No one won the Powerball jackpot or the $2 million Match 5 + Power Play prize, but one lucky person in Texas won the $1 million Match 5 prize.

When is the next Powerball drawing?

The next Powerball drawing will take place on Saturday, Oct. 3, at around 10:59 p.m. ET. The drawing can be watched live on Powerball's website, and players can rewatch clips on its YouTube channel.

Powerball drawings occur every week on Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday.

How to play Powerball

Powerball tickets cost $2 per play and are sold in 45 states, Washington, DC, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Depending on local laws, tickets may also be purchased online.

Players choose five white-ball numbers from 1 to 69 and one red Powerball number from 1 to 26. For an additional $1, players can add Power Play, which multiplies non-jackpot prizes by 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X, or 10X.

Players who don't want to pick their own numbers can choose a Quick Pick and let the terminal generate selections randomly.

To win the jackpot, a ticket must match all five white balls and the Powerball. If no one wins, the jackpot rolls over and continues to grow.
