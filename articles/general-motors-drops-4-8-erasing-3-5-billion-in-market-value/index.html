General Motors Drops 4.8%, Erasing $3.5 Billion in Market Value

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:10 · source · permalink

As seen on the 24/7 Wall St. homepage on September 18, 2026.

A 4.8% intraday slide to $82.21 knocks roughly $3.5 billion off the automaker's $74.4 billion market value in a single session. Whether the $86.35 open gets reclaimed by the close is the level to keep an eye on.

General Motors shed roughly $3.5 billion in market value in a single session, with shares sliding from an open of $86.35 to $82.21 during regular trading hours.

Selling pressure set in almost immediately at the open and barely relented through the afternoon, with no meaningful bounce.
