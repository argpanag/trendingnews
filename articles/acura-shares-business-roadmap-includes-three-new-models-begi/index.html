Acura Shares Business Roadmap: Includes Three New Models Beginning with Adventure-Ready SUV in 2028

TheTools · 13.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink
  • Acura will introduce new products in segments where the brand does not currently compete, building customer lifetime loyalty and attracting new buyers to the brand
  • New Acura adventure-ready SUV set to arrive in the first part of 2028
  • All-new fourth-generation Acura RDX, coming to market in 2028, will be first Acura model to feature the company's award-winning two-motor hybrid-electric technology
  • New Acura 8-passenger SUV – its largest ever – currently in development
  • New V6 hybrid powertrain in development for Acura D-Segment models

Acura today shared a roadmap for the brand's future in North America with its U.S. dealers, including plans to introduce three new SUVs in the coming years. The strategy will significantly expand the Acura lineup into new segments, with a focus on building customer lifetime loyalty while attracting new clients to the Acura brand.

"We are making a big commitment to Acura by delivering products and services that meet the needs of our customers at all life stages, which will create a sustainable long-term direction for the brand," said Eiji Fujimura, president & CEO of American Honda. "The Acura business foundation is sharply focused on the needs of the customer, delivering new products that offer value for money on a premium level, and increasing our speed to market to ensure that our products are highly competitive and meet the latest market conditions."

New Models, New Segments
As a part of the company's commitment to the Acura brand, significant investment is being made into developing new products and technologies built around the needs of the customer.

The new product introductions will begin with an adventure-ready Acura SUV set to arrive in the first part of 2028. The new model will deliver the capability customers need for their outdoor adventures along with modern and premium styling, and the comfort and technology customers expect from an Acura.

Acura also will add a new 8-passenger SUV – its largest ever – designed to capture growing demand for more family-friendly vehicles in the premium space. The new SUV, positioned above the 7-passenger MDX, is currently in development.

Acura previously announced that an all-new fourth-generation RDX Hybrid will come to market in 2028 – the first Acura model to feature the company's award-winning two-motor hybrid technology.

The three new SUVs, along with the ADX, MDX and Integra, will meet customer needs across a range of life stages, a key element of the brand's new business direction. Additionally, a new V6 hybrid powertrain is in development for Acura D-Segment models. This new powertrain will deliver sporty performance, improved towing capabilities and environmental efficiencies.

"Acura is entering the most popular segments in the market while maintaining a leading position in gateway segments with Integra and ADX, to solidify a strong and sustainable future," said Gary Robinson, Vice President of Auto Strategy at American Honda.

Acura Outpacing the Market
By delivering value-driven, premium products, Acura is outpacing the luxury industry through the first 9 months of the year – growing faster than several established luxury competitors.

Through the first three quarters of 2026, Acura has posted a year-over-year sales increase of 4.4%. The Acura MDX, the industry's best-selling three-row luxury SUV of all time, has seen impressive growth of more than 25%, while the brand's gateway models – ADX and Integra – both lead their segments in retail sales. All three models will receive meaningful enhancements for the 2027 model year, further increasing customer value and competitiveness.

"At a time when Acura continues to outpace the luxury market with products focused on delivering customer value, our dealers were incredibly excited about our plans to expand the Acura lineup" said Jessika Laudermilk, assistant vice president of Acura National Sales. "Affordability is more important than ever, so there couldn't be a better time to have strong gateway models like ADX and Integra continue to bring new buyers to the brand while retaining our loyal customers."

About Acura
Acura is a leading premium auto brand committed to delivering expressive styling, innovative engineering and engaging dynamics, all built on the brand's Precision Crafted Performance DNA. The 2026 Acura lineup consists of four models: the Integra sport sedan, and the ADX, RDX, and MDX sport-utility vehicles. Around 85% of Acura vehicles sold in America in 2025 were made in America, using domestic and globally made parts.
