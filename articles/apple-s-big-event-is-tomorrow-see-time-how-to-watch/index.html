Apple's big event is tomorrow. See time, how to watch

TheTools · 08.09.2026 23:59 · source · permalink

Apple is expected to announce some of its most highly anticipated products tomorrow afternoon.

The tech giant is hosting its annual product announcement event at 1 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Sept. 9. Though Apple does not share what products it will announce before the event, tech experts believe it'll be the release of the iPhone Ultra, the rumored foldable iPhone.

Based on a source familiar with Apple's plan, MacWorld reported that the iPhone Ultra could fold like a book, opening to a landscape view about the size of an iPad mini. The smartphone could feature a 7.7- to 7.8-inch inner display, 5.3- to 5.5-inch outer display and four cameras, according to MacWorld.

The big event comes a little more than a week after Tim Cook stepped down as Apple CEO, succeeded by longtime executive John Ternus. Cook announced he was stepping down from the role and joining Apple's board in April.

Here's what to know about streaming Apple's event at home.

When is Apple's big event?

Apple's annual product announcement event is at 1 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Sept. 9.

How to watch Apple's big product announcement event at home

You can livestream the event from home on the Apple website or Apple TV app.

What new products will Apple announce during the event?

Apple does not share details of which products it will announce before its annual event; however, tech experts and outlets like Forbes, MacRumors and 9to5Mac, which follow the company closely, have predictions. Here's a look at the rumored heavy hitters:

  • iPhone Ultra
  • iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max
  • Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4
  • AirPods 5
  • MacBook Ultra, M6 MacBook Pro and M6 iMac
  • Apple TV 4K
  • Home Hub
