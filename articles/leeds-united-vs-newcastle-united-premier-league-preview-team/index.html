Match Facts

  • Leeds United are winless in six Premier League games against Newcastle United (D4 L2) since a 2-1 victory at St. James’ Park in January 2021.

  • Newcastle United have only lost one of their last eight Premier League visits to Leeds (W4 D3), a 5-2 defeat at Elland Road in December 2020.

  • Leeds have lost just one of their last 12 Premier League games (W5 D6) and are unbeaten so far this season. Only in 1995-96, 1998-99 and 2001-02 have they avoided defeat in each of their first four in the competition.

  • Following a 2-0 win at Spurs last month, Newcastle are looking to win consecutive away league games for the first time since April 2025, while they last won both of their first two on the road in a season back in 1995-96.

  • Only three teams have had fewer shots on target than Newcastle in the Premier League this season (8), though they’ve scored six goals from these. The Magpies are overperforming their xG more than any other side across the first three matchdays (+3 – 6 goals, 3 xG).

  • Only Hull (24%) and Nottingham Forest (20%) have played a higher percentage of their passes long than Leeds (18%) in the Premier League this season, with only Hull (69.9%) having a lower passing accuracy than the Whites so far (72.3%).

  • Newcastle have faced more shots than any other side in the Premier League this season (61), averaging 20.3 per game. No side has ever faced 20+ shots per game across a full campaign on record (since 2003-04).

  • As a team, Leeds United have applied the second-most high-intensity pressures in the opposition half (528) in the Premier League this season, with striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin (134) behind only Oliver McBurnie (164) among players in this regard.

  • Against no side has Leeds’ Dominic Calvert-Lewin scored more Premier League goals (7), have more goal involvements (9) or more yellow cards (6) than he does against Newcastle.

  • Newcastle’s Harvey Barnes has scored eight goals in his last nine league appearances against Leeds United, his most against any opponent in his league career. Seven of those goals have come in the Premier League; only Alan Shearer (20), Thierry Henry (10), Teddy Sheringham (9), and Mo Salah (9) have netted more against Leeds in the competition.
