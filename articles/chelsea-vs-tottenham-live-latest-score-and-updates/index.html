Chelsea vs Tottenham LIVE: Latest score and updates

TheTools · 24.10.2026 18:02 · source · permalink

Sport | Football

Chelsea and Tottenham were locked at 0-0 at half-time of their London derby showdown on Saturday evening. One of the Premier League’s fiercest rivalries had yet to ignite at a wet and windy Stamford Bridge, with Spurs certainly having the best of the opening 45 minutes at a ground where they have struggled woefully over the last three decades. Omar Marmoush wasted their best chance with a close-range effort that was fired straight at Mike Penders.

Chelsea initially looked out of sorts after back-to-backs wins under Xabi Alonso, who is without Joao Pedro due to an injury sustained in training on Friday. Summer signing Geovany Quenda was a shock selection to make his first Premier League start in attack, while Penders did likewise in goal amid the absence of usual first-choice No1 Emi Martinez. Both sides will hope to kick on after the break in an attempt to claim bragging rights as the rain begins to ease.
