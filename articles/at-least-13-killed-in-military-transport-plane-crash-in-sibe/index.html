At Least 13 Killed in Military Transport Plane Crash in Siberia – Reports - The Moscow Times

TheTools · 17.11.2026 16:23 · source · permalink

A Russian Antonov An-12 military transport plane crashed in Siberia’s Kemerovo region late Tuesday, Russian media reported.

Unconfirmed reports suggest that between 13 and 17 people were on board and that none survived.

Kemerovo region Governor Ilya Seredyuk confirmed that the aircraft crashed near the village of Yagunovo, about 5 kilometers (3 miles) south of the regional capital, Kemerovo.

Seredyuk said the plane was flying over the region when it crashed, but did not provide any information about casualties. 

The Telegram news channel Baza reported that an aviation fuel leak was a possible cause, citing anonymous sources familiar with the matter.

Baza also said the aircraft may have crashed while attempting an attempted emergency landing while flying from Novosibirsk to Kansk.
