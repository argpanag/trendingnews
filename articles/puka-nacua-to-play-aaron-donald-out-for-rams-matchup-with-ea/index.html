Puka Nacua to play, Aaron Donald out for Rams' matchup with Eagles

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:41 · source · permalink

LOS ANGELES -- The Los Angeles Rams will get Puka Nacua back this week, but they'll be without another one of their stars.

Three-time defensive player of the year Aaron Donald has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Philadelphia Eagles, coach Sean McVay said Friday.

However, Nacua will play after missing the past two games because of hip and groin injuries. He practiced all three days this week.

Tight end Terrance Ferguson will also miss the game with an ankle injury, and cornerback Jaylen Watson will be out with a dislocated shoulder.

Donald missed the full week of practice with a back issue. It popped up after he finished last week's 30-26 loss to the Denver Broncos on "Sunday Night Football."

The 35-year-old returned after a two-year retirement to rejoin the only franchise he has ever played with. He missed the season opener against the San Francisco 49ers in Australia while ramping up, but he then played against the New York Giants and Broncos. He did not record a sack in either game, but he had a couple of key run stops, commanded double-teams and added some pressure along the way.

"He's as tough as it gets but is just not feeling good enough, and I think it would be irresponsible on my part," McVay said. "He would do it, and he could probably go be productive, but part of our job is to protect these tough dudes like him from themselves, and that's kind of what went into the decision.

"You guys know him. He's working as hard as he can to get himself feeling as good as possible, and we're hopeful and optimistic that good days are ahead."

The Rams will now have to play without Donald and reigning defensive player of the year Myles Garrett on the defensive line. Garrett will miss at least two more games while on injured reserve following knee surgery.

Los Angeles will look to Kobie Turner and Braden Fiske to provide interior pressure in Donald's absence, and Byron Young, Josaiah Stewart and Wesley Bailey will carry the load of adding edge pressure in place of Garrett.

The Rams will welcome back the reigning NFL receptions leader in Nacua to add to a passing game where Davante Adams has 332 receiving yards over the past two weeks.

"He's looked really good," McVay said of Nacua. "Physically, he's getting in and out of his breaks. Looks strong, looks sturdy, looks explosive. I think it will really just be a feel for the overall conditioning. Hoping for the magic of the jerseys on Sunday. He's feeling real good there, but what I will tell you is he looked sharp yesterday. It was a good workload. Thursday is always our heaviest workload, and I thought he handled it well. In terms of the snap count, that's the thing we have to be willing to navigate."

Los Angeles is looking to go to 2-2, but a road trip to play the Eagles has its challenges. McVay is 1-6 in his career against the Eagles, who came back last year from a 26-7 deficit against the Rams to win 33-26 on a blocked field goal they returned for a touchdown.

A win would be critical in advance of hosting the Buffalo Bills in Week 5 on "Monday Night Football."
