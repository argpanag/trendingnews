Fair City on Thursday: 'There will be consequences...'

TheTools · 30.09.2026 06:48 · source · permalink

Ger warns Rafferty in Thursday's Fair City on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player.

Scout's recent kidnapping and subsequent return were staged by Rafferty's son James and daughter-in-law Hayley to get money from him - and Ger knew of the plan to pretend that Rafferty's grandson had been kidnapped.

In Thursday's episode, Ger tells the still-in-the-dark Rafferty: "You are not going to the guards!"

"Ger, stop it," says Hayley.

"You report this, there's no going back," Ger continues. "There will be consequences - and they'll be ugly."

Fans can watch what happens next on Thursday on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player at 8:00pm.

Fair City airs on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player on Sundays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays.
