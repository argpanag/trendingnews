India A vs Nepal Highlights: India crush Nepal by 306 runs, get ready for Pakistan clash

Hello and welcome to the live coverage of the Rising Stars Asia Cup clash between India A and Nepal.

IND vs NEP: INDIA WIN BY 306 RUNS!

GAME, SET AND MATCH!

An astonishing collapse from Nepal as they are bundled out for just 26 runs! Harshit Rana and Prince Yadav ripped through the batting line-up, leaving Shahbaz Ahmed with the final say. He tossed one up to Nandan, who charged down the track looking for a big hit but was beaten by the dip and turn. Kumar Kushagra made no mistake behind the stumps, completing a simple stumping to wrap things up. A neat finish from Kushagra, who had already lit up the contest with a brilliant hundred earlier!

WOW, WOW, WOW!

This is just an exhibition of pace bowling at its finest.

Prince Yadav has bowled at the stumps and first cleans up Pratis with a beauty. Captain Sandeep Lamichhane comes in and is cleaned up for a duck as the stumps are rattled again. He misses his hat-trick, but that last wicket might be saved for Harshit to take his fifer.

WICKET! HARSHIT GETS HIS FOURTH!

WHAT IS HAPPENING HERE?

Nepal are falling like nine pins here and they have lost the last three wickets without scoring any runs. Harshit forces Lokesh Bam to play the drive and the ball goes straight into the hands of Abhishek Sharma. That's his fourth and he is on a hat-trick. Pratis is able to prevent that but the pace and swing from Harshit has been sensational.
