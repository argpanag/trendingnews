Forecasters update Latvia’s weekly weather outlook – temperatures could reach +16°C

TheTools · 20.10.2026 05:15 · source · permalink

Latvia is expected to experience mostly dry and partly sunny weather early in the week, followed by increasing cloud cover, rain and stronger winds.

Tuesday will be mostly sunny, although clouds will occasionally cover the sky. By Wednesday, cloud cover is expected to increase, particularly in western Latvia, according to the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre.

The weather will remain dry, with light to moderate easterly winds.

On Wednesday morning, southeasterly and easterly winds will strengthen along the western coast of the Gulf of Riga, with gusts reaching up to 15 metres per second.

During the night leading into Tuesday, temperatures will drop to between -2°C and +3°C across much of Latvia. Coastal areas and parts of Zemgale will remain warmer, with temperatures ranging from +3°C to +8°C.

The following night, temperatures will rise by several degrees, falling to between +1°C and +6°C, while some coastal areas of Kurzeme could experience temperatures of +7°C to +10°C.

Daytime highs on Tuesday and Wednesday are expected to range between +8°C and +13°C.

In the second half of the week, weather fronts bringing precipitation will move across Latvia, resulting in occasional rain throughout much of the country.

On Thursday, southeasterly and easterly winds will strengthen in western and central regions, with gusts reaching 15 metres per second.

Nighttime temperatures across most of Latvia will remain relatively mild, ranging from +6°C to +11°C.

According to the latest forecasts, Thursday is expected to be the warmest day of the week, with temperatures climbing to between +13°C and +16°C nationwide, the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre reports.
