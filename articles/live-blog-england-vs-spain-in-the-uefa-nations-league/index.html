Spain Debuts Its Second Star at Wembley

Spain’s last competitive match ended with Ferran Torres scoring in extra time to secure the world title against Argentina. Now, a different story begins for the team led by Luis de la Fuente.

The RFEF presented this squad as the first since winning the World Cup, and the Spanish coach made it clear that the success achieved during the summer already belongs to the past. “One story has ended and now another one begins,” De la Fuente explained ahead of the start of the Nations League.

La Roja retains much of the core that carried it to the world championship. Lamine Yamal, Nico Williams, Pedri, Rodri, Fabián Ruiz, Dani Olmo, Mikel Merino, and Ferran Torres are part of a generation that must now quickly turn the page and begin another competition with the same ambition.

The setting could hardly be more demanding: Wembley Stadium and an England side that also has unfinished business.

Spain Lineup

England Returns After a World Cup Podium Finish

The Three Lions will play their first match since the 2026 FIFA World Cup, where they finished third.

Thomas Tuchel remains in charge of the team and will once again have Harry Kane as captain and its main attacking reference. The striker also arrives in one of the best stretches of his career, with his name firmly in the Ballon d’Or conversation.

The English camp, however, has undergone several changes because of different physical problems. Cole Palmer, Marcus Rashford, Declan Rice, Kobbie Mainoo, and Tino Livramento withdrew from the squad, while Trent Alexander-Arnold returned to the national team.

Despite those absences, Tuchel still has plenty of attacking options, including Jude Bellingham, Bukayo Saka, Anthony Gordon, Eberechi Eze, Morgan Rogers, and Elliot Anderson.

England Lineup

Harry Kane and Lamine Yamal, Two Ballon d’Or Candidates

Beyond the collective battle, Wembley will bring together two players who are at the center of the Ballon d’Or race.

Harry Kane acknowledged ahead of the match that he considers this his best opportunity to win the individual award after completing an extraordinary season for both his club and England.

On the other side will be Lamine Yamal, one of the biggest stars of world champion Spain and a key piece in Luis de la Fuente’s system.

The matchup also features two very different attacking threats. Kane can decide games inside the penalty area, but he can also drop deeper to participate in the buildup, while Lamine Yamal brings one-on-one ability, speed, and creativity every time he receives the ball out wide.


Remembering the Euro 2024 Final

There is another element that is impossible to ignore whenever England and Spain meet.

La Roja defeated the Three Lions 2-1 in Berlin to win Euro 2024, a result that extended England’s wait to lift another major international trophy.

The overall head-to-head record, however, reflects how evenly matched this rivalry has been. According to UEFA, England has 13 victories, Spain has 11, and four other meetings have ended in draws.

There will be no trophy at stake this time, but the first points of a new Nations League campaign are on the line. Both teams are in League A Group A3 alongside Croatia and Czechia, meaning a victory at Wembley could immediately make a difference in the battle to advance.
