In the third round of the UEFA Europa League league phase for the 2026/27 season, Armenia’s reigning champion, Ararat-Armenia, hosted the Dutch club AZ Alkmaar at the Vazgen Sargsyan Republican Stadium in Yerevan.

The visitors scored four unanswered goals. After three matches, Ararat-Armenia has yet to earn a point in the league phase, while AZ Alkmaar secured their second victory, bringing their total to 6 points.

In the fourth round, on November 5, Ararat-Armenia will face Portuguese side Torreense away.
