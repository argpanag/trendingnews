Pistons' Cade Cunningham: Nets 22 points with eight dimes in win

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:08 · source · permalink

Cunningham posted 22 points (8-18 FG, 1-6 3Pt, 5-5 FT), eight assists, four rebounds, one steal, one block and five turnovers across 35 minutes in Tuesday's 121-113 win over the Celtics.

The turnovers and three-point shooting weren't great, but Cunningham delivered everywhere else as he guided the Pistons to an Opening Night win on their home floor. The 25-year-old star point guard should again be a reliable source of points, dimes and defensive stats this season.
