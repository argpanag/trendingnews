Angelina Jolie makes surprise visit to Ukrainian boxing gym

TheTools · 08.09.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

Her son, Knox, 18, trains with front-line soldiers during a martial arts session at a club miles from the Russian border

Angelina Jolie made a surprise visit to Ukraine as her 18-year-old son joined Ukrainian troops for a martial arts session at a boxing club in the city of Kharkiv.

Knox Jolie took part in a Muay Thai workout alongside front-line soldiers at the Spadkoyemets gym, located just miles from the Russian border.

Photographs released by the Boxing Federation of Ukraine showed the 51-year-old Hollywood star and her son posing alongside local athletes and military staff.

Pavlo Chyshko, the owner of the gym, said he had “no idea” he was hosting Hollywood royalty until the Oscar-winning actress unexpectedly appeared after booking a slot at the weekend.

Posting on social media, Mr Chyshko said: “To everyone who asked me – this is not a joke, not AI. It just happened completely by chance: Angelina Jolie, to our great delight, dropped by our gym.

“She complimented us, said she liked our gym, and we thanked her. She asked my son how long he’d been doing sports... That’s real class. And her son is just the same. He shook hands, back and forth ... It was really cool!”

The Boxing Federation of Ukraine said: “And so, in a Kharkiv boxing gym, sport, Ukrainian defenders, and one of the world’s most famous actresses came together.”

Kharkiv remains a daily target for Russian drone, glide-bomb and missile attacks, forcing much of the city into underground shelters.

The trip marks the former UN envoy’s latest low-key humanitarian visit to the country, following unannounced stops in Lviv, Ternopil, and Kremenchuk.

Local reports suggest the actress entered Ukraine in late August without a public announcement.

Jolie’s stealth visits – which began following Russia’s full-scale invasion in February 2022 – have previously caught both locals and authorities off guard.

During an unannounced visit to the southern cities of Mykolaiv and Kherson last November, her itinerary was nearly scuppered by military recruiters who questioned why her driver had not enlisted in the armed forces.

A 33-year-old driver in her entourage, named only as Dmytro, was detained and taken to a regional recruitment centre after it emerged he lacked military registration documents.

The incident reportedly infuriated government officials in Kyiv, who were unaware Jolie had entered the country on a humanitarian mission to meet volunteers and medics forced to live underground to escape Russian attacks.
