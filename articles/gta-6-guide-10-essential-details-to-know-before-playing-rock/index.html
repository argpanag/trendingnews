GTA 6 Guide: 10 essential details to know before playing Rockstar’s game

TheTools · 16.11.2026 14:01 · source · permalink

Details to keep in mind before the launch of the new Rockstar Games title.

There are just a few days left until the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. Next Thursday, November 19, we’ll witness a historic moment with the arrival of one of the most anticipated games of all time. Expectations for Rockstar Games’ new title couldn’t be higher. Below, we’ll go over 10 key things you should know before its release.

The physical edition does not include a disc

This is a controversial decision that is already a reality in stores. Grand Theft Auto VI does not include a disc in its physical edition, so inside the box you’ll find a code that you’ll need to redeem on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store to start downloading the game.

What’s included in the physical edition

The physical edition of GTA 6 does not include a disc. Instead, inside the PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S box, you’ll find a download code and a game poster. It also does not include a map, which will be available in physical, double-sided format through the Rockstar Store for $0.99.

Pre-download to have the game ready before launch

Since November 10, it has been possible to pre-download GTA 6. To do so, you must have purchased the game in either physical or digital format through the PlayStation or Xbox stores. Once installed, you won’t be able to play immediately; you’ll have to wait until 12:00 a.m. on Thursday, November 19.

GTA 6 download size

Rockstar Games has officially confirmed that the game requires 212 GB of free space on our consoles. This is a substantial download that gives an idea of the scale of the new installment in the series.

The Price of GTA 6 in the United States

Grand Theft Auto VI is available in the United States for $79.99 in both physical and digital editions, while the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99.

The long-awaited return to Vice City

After a long wait, GTA 6 confirmed that its story would take place in Vice City, the city that transported us back to the 1980s with Tommy Vercetti as the protagonist. This new installment not only brings back the Miami-inspired city but also takes us to the fictional state of Leonida, a recreation of Florida in the United States.

The first demale lead in GTA history

Lucía Caminos ushers in a new era by becoming the first woman to play a leading role in Grand Theft Auto. Together with Jason Duval, she forms a criminal duo in the true spirit of Bonnie and Clyde. Of Hispanic descent and with a past in Liberty City, her story begins in the state penitentiary and continues in Vice City.

The first country in the world where the game will be available

That country will be New Zealand, as Rockstar has set the game’s release for midnight on November 19, according to each region’s local time. Due to its location, New Zealand will reach that time before most other countries. Grand Theft Auto VI will be available there 16 hours earlier than in the United States and up to about 20 hours earlier than in some territories where midnight comes later.

The approximate length of GTA 6

Rob Nelson, co-director at Rockstar North, told The New York Times that it took him approximately 80 hours to complete the game. This is a rough estimate for a title like GTA, whose length depends entirely on how each player chooses to approach the adventure.

Consoles on which it will be available

Related stories

The new release from Rockstar Games will be available starting November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. For now, there is no official confirmation of a PC release, so we’ll have to wait for the company to make an announcement.
