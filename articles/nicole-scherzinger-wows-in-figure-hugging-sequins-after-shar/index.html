Nicole Scherzinger wows in figure-hugging sequins after sharing burns injury

TheTools · 11.10.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

Kimberly Wyatt, Nicole Scherzinger and Ashley Roberts of The Pussycat Dolls perform live at the Co-op Arena as part of their 2026 PCD Forever Tour(Image: Andy Von Pip/Shutterstock)

Nicole Scherzinger looked sensational as she stepped out in a series of glamorous outfits alongside her Pussycat Dolls bandmates, just days after revealing she had suffered painful burns in a freak accident. The singer, 48, turned heads in eye-catching stagewear as she joined Ashley Roberts and Kimberly Wyatt for the group's PCD Forever tour stop in Newcastle on Friday evening.

Nicole showcased her stunning figure in a selection of striking looks, including dazzling silver sequins and a ravishing red number that clung to her curves, and hid her painful injury.

The trio brought plenty of glitz to the occasion, with their figure-hugging outfits delivering the signature glamour associated with the Don't Cha hitmakers.

Nicole showed fans her painful injury(Image: nicolescherzinger/Instagram)

Nicole completed her looks with her dark hair styled in loose curls and a glamorous make-up look featuring fluttery false eyelashes, glittery eyeshadow, pink lipstick and rosy cheeks.

Her latest appearance comes just days after she opened up about a painful accident that left her with burns on her leg.

The former X Factor judge revealed that boiling water had spilled over her lower body around an hour before she was due to take to the stage in Dusseldorf, Germany.

Despite the mishap, Nicole was determined to carry on with the show and thanked fans for helping her through the performance.

“Nothing like boiling water falling on your leg an hour before your set. But the show must go on. Thanks Dusseldorf for keeping me going. See you soon Amsterdam!” she wrote on social media.

Nicole said the "show must go on"(Image: nicolescherzinger/Instagram)

Nicole also shared pictures showing the extent of the injury, with red, raw-looking marks visible along her inner thigh and a deep purple scar on her lower leg.

In another post, she shared a photo of herself in a black crop top alongside the caption: “What they see vs. what they don’t see…”

The images prompted an outpouring of support from followers, with one fan commenting: “God heal Nicole of any pain”.

Nicole has since continued her tour dates with Ashley and Kimberly. The group's PCD Forever shows feature a mix of Pussycat Dolls favourites and solo tracks.
