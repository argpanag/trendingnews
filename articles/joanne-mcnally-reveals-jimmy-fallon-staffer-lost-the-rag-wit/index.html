Joanne McNally reveals Jimmy Fallon staffer 'lost the rag' with her

TheTools · 22.10.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Joanne McNally is rightfully soaking up praise after a spectacular US TV debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, but it wasn't all smooth sailing for the star in the lead-up to her appearance.

The comedian confessed a series of behind-the-scenes blunders led to a member of staff 'losing his rag' at her before showtime, after the famously laidback funnywoman got the timing wrong.

She lifted the curtain on the intense prep she was required to do ahead of her appearance, she opened up about the ordeal to her co-host Vogue Williams on their podcast, My Therapist Ghosted Me.

'There is a completely different way of working over here when it comes to late night TV. You have to have a script, you have to send them a script and then you have to stick to the script. Turns out I really struggle with that,' Joanne laughed.

Joanne had to get her routine nailed down to the minute, something that proved a struggle for the star. 'We all know my show is currently running at 1 hour and 45 minutes, like much to the detriment of everyone involved, right?' the funnywoman joked to Vogue.

'So it was, honest to God, one of the, one of the hardest things I've done in my whole career. because He approved the material a month ago, and up to, I'd say seven minutes before I went on, I was trying to change it,' she laughed.

The real blunder came before the show, when Joanne mixed up the time she was set to arrive in New York. The Dubliner was flying from Vancouver and was due to do two sets in the city so that a member of Jimmy Fallon's team could watch her.

'I got my arrival flight time wrong,' Joanne confessed. 'I was due to do the two gigs at 7:30. Turns out I was landing at 9:30 at night.'

'So the booker of Jimmy Fallon is sitting waiting for me, and I'm in the f**king air. The whole thing was an absolute... he lost the rag with me at one point,' she recalled. 'But he got it back and we ploughed on.'

'It came together in the end, thank God, but it was one of the most stressful things that I've had to do so far, so I'm so glad that it went well.'

Vogue gushed over her close pal, saying she is 'so proud' of Joanne for achieving the impressive feat, dubbing Joanne her fifth child.
