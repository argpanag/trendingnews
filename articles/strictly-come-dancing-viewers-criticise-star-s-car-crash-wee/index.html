Strictly Come Dancing viewers criticise star's 'car crash' week 3 performance

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:11 · source · permalink

Strictly Come Dancing viewers have been left unimpressed by Shaun Wright-Phillips’ latest performance.

The former footballer and his professional partner, Alexis Warr, finished ninth after weeks one and two with a combined score of 40.

The pair performed a Quickstep to I’m a Believer by The Monkees during week three of the competition on Saturday (October 10) night.

The dance earned the pair a score of 15 from judges Shirley Ballas, Craig Revel Horwood, Motsi Mabuse, and Anton Du Beke.

Strictly fans criticise Shaun Wright-Phillips’ week 3 performance 

Strictly fans were extremely critical of Shaun and Alexis' week three performance, taking to Reddit to share their thoughts.

One viewer, posting on r/strictlycomedancing, commented: "Oh dear.

"Idk if this is too mean, but he dances like he’s taken too many footballs to the head.

"Bless him, I can see he’s trying."

Another viewer commented: "Emergency services on their way after a terrible car crash."

This person said: "Well, that was, um. An attempt."

Despite the criticism, some praised his enthusiasm, saying: "Well at least he looks like he's having fun, and we do love someone who gives his all!"

A second person posted: "Bless Shaun. He tried."

Who was the first couple eliminated from Strictly Come Dancing 2026 last week?

Chris Appleton became the first contestant to leave the competition last week.

The 43-year-old celebrity hair stylist was partnered with professional dancer Amy Dowden.

Their combined score over the first two weeks was 33 points, just ahead of Melanie Walters and Aljaž Škorjanec (30 points), in last place on the leaderboard.

Appleton and Dowden faced Jaime Winstone and Carlos Gu in the dance-off, following the public vote.

The judges chose to save Winstone and Gu, sending Appleton and Dowden home.

How to vote on Strictly Come Dancing
