It’s finally coming: Riot Games and Pop Mart are bringing VALORANT into the world of collectible toys.

There are three things Riot Games is good at: games, music, and merchandise. While you could argue that the game side can sometimes be a flip, the company is great at recognizing trends in the other two segments. And for merchandise, Riot is working on releasing a collection with Pop Mart.

The partnership was announced by Pop Mart itself through its own social media accounts, telling players to “lock in”. If you’re into collectible items, it’s time to get your wallets ready.

The VALORANT Pop Mart collection will be officially released starting on September 25, the day after the start of the VALORANT Champions Tour. It was confirmed during a livestream on Bilibili.

This is not the first time Pop Mart has worked with Riot Games. Earlier in 2025, the two released an Arcane blind-box series in April 2025, featuring nine standard designs and two rare secret figures.

A similar bundle was also released for Teamfight Tactics/Golden Spatula (Chinese version of TFT) later in the year, and it was sold during Worlds 2025 in China as well. There’s a high chance a similar bundle will be released for VALORANT.

On September 16, Pop Mart announced the full lineup of in-game agents for the collection. The agents included are Omen, Sova, Jett, Viper, Fade, Killjoy, Iso, Sage, Gekko, Neon, Clove, and Cypher. All of them will be sold as blind boxes. Based on the announcement, it looks like Omen is the one receiving two different versions. 

Pop Mart is particularly popular in China as a collectible item, and given the general hype and interest, it’s not surprising it will be the first region to gain access to the figures. Regional availability and pricing are yet to be confirmed.
