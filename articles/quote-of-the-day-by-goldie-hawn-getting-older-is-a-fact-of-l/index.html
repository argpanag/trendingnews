Quote Of The Day By Goldie Hawn: 'Getting Older Is A Fact Of Life. By Facing It With Courage And Humor, We Can...' - Women

TheTools · 13.11.2026 13:17 · source · permalink

No matter how many years pass, Goldie Hawn's bright positivity and irresistible enthusiasm have never faded. So, it comes as no surprise that she made a name for herself as a comic actor, with years of experience and awards under her belt. Born on November 21, 1945, Hawn did not start out with acting at first. Instead, the star felt drawn to dancing and began lessons when she was just 3 years old. Hawn initially made a living as a professional dancer before making the transition into acting. 

And she came out swinging, as not long after kickstarting her acting career, she bagged an Oscar win for 1969's "Cactus Flower." With her acting talents recognized, the floodgates opened for Hawn. She gained critical acclaim for her performance in 1974's "The Sugarland Express," as well as her stint on the show "Laugh-In." Hawn bagged an Oscar nomination for her work on "Private Benjamin," with further credits in films like "Death Becomes Her" and "The First Wives Club." 

But through all her career wins, Hawn has maintained her quintessential cheerfulness and spirited nature. This makes her seem particularly invulnerable to the Hollywood pressures surrounding aging, and her words prove why she's one of the celebs confronting aging with confidence. As such, it's fitting that Hawn's quote be today's quote of the day.

Quote of the Day by Goldie Hawn

"Getting older is a fact of life. By facing it with courage and humor, we can keep our spirits young." Goldie Hawn spoke these words in an exclusive interview with Porter magazine back in 2015 (via E! News). In the interview, Hawn not only reflected on her experiences as a woman in Hollywood, but also on family, marriage, and aging. And as a silver screen veteran who spent decades in show business, Hawn noted that aging is as inevitable as the sun rising each morning. 

According to the Golden Globe winner, it's a privilege to even be experiencing aging. In fact, it's how you perceive aging that matters. Only through acceptance of it will you achieve any peace of mind. Hawn believes that the best thing to do about getting older is to welcome it with open arms and to live life with love and laughter. 

Deeper meaning of Goldie Hawn's Quote -- Keep a positive mindset at all times

According to Goldie Hawn, aging isn't something to be afraid of. It is simply a natural part of life that will come, whether anyone likes it or not. Moreover, it is up to us to choose how to respond to it. For Hawn, she chose to see it in a different manner. Rather than panic over every little sign of aging, the "Best Friends" star opted to perceive it in a positive light. 

In Hawn's perspective, getting older is a fantastic thing, one that proves that she's made it another year and gets to spend more time on Earth with those she loved. The nine-time Golden Globe nominee's words encourages us to prioritize what truly matters to us in life, from family bonds to the simple notion of being alive. What's more, Hawn's words imply that we should never forget to find the funny side of things and learn to laugh at what's going on around us. Once we've accomplished that, then the fear of aging will no longer have any power over our minds.
