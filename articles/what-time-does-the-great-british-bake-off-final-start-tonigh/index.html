What time does The Great British Bake Off final start tonight and who are the contestants?

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:13 · source · permalink

After 10 weeks of technical challenges, showstoppers and signature bakes, The Great British Bake Off is preparing to crown its 2026 winner.

The Channel 4 baking competition reaches its grand final tonight (November 24), with three remaining contestants hoping to impress the judges one last time and take home the coveted cake stand.

This year's series has marked a major change for the show, with Nigella Lawson joining Paul Hollywood on the judging panel, replacing Dame Prue Leith. Alison Hammond and Noel Fielding have returned to host proceedings in the famous tent.

But only one baker can be crowned champion. Tom, Moyin and Mo have all made it through to the final, but who will come out on top?

Here's everything to know about this year's finalists.

Who are the Bake Off 2026 finalists?

Tom

Pronouns: He/him Age: 25

From: West Yorkshire Occupation: Hairdresser

While his older brother was off at football matches, six-year-old Tom would escape to his nan's to bake (GBBO/Channel 4)

Tom describes himself as a northern diva who loves creating bakes that are witty, pretty and, above all, delicious.

His love of baking began when he was six, regularly escaping to his grandmother's house to experiment in the kitchen while his older brother went to football matches. By the age of 10, he had already mastered the art of making brandy snaps.

Away from the kitchen, Tom works in a busy hair salon, where his colleagues are among his biggest taste-testers. His salted caramel brownies are a particular favourite.

He also loves travelling and recently spent seven months exploring Southeast Asia with his boyfriend, Alex. The couple are now saving to buy a house together.

Moyin

Pronouns: He/him Age: 22

From: Essex Occupation: Political Economy Student

Moyin started baking with his mum at five-years-old (GBBO/Channel 4)

Moyin is a high achiever who juggles his studies with athletics, baking and a part-time job.

He first started baking with his mum at the age of five and had mastered the notoriously tricky technique of macaronage by the time he was 14.

His baking combines precision with playful flavours, often drawing inspiration from his Nigerian heritage, including ingredients such as grains of paradise and groundnuts.

Away from the tent, Moyin is a competitive sprinter who has been crowned U20 County Champion in the 100m. He also enjoys fashion, collecting vinyl records and visiting comedy clubs.

He lives with his mum, sister and grandmother and regularly visits relatives in Nigeria, Canada and the US. He is also a dedicated matcha latte fan, often pre-ordering his drink before heading to university seminars.

Mo

Pronouns: He/him Age: 21

From: West Midlands Occupation: Law student

Mo is a self-confessed "chaotic and random" character who is determined to make his family proud (Screenshot 2026-11-24 at 10.56.00.png)

Mo describes himself as a "chaotic and random" character who is determined to make his family proud.

Born and raised in the West Midlands, he lives with his family and their three cats, Pablo, Simba and Gucci. He is currently studying law at university, with ambitions of becoming a solicitor.

His interest in baking began when he was just three, pretending to cook with Play-Doh. At nine, he stumbled across a video about mug cakes and quickly became hooked.

Having started out using boxed vanilla cake mixes from his local corner shop, Mo has since developed a talent for creating precise, professional-looking bakes. He particularly enjoys combining Asian flavours with traditional British classics.

When he isn't studying or baking, Mo can usually be found in the gym lifting weights or practising calisthenics.

How to apply for the next series of Bake Off

Aspiring bakers have until Friday, December 11 to submit applications (PA)

Think you have what it takes to compete in the famous Bake Off tent? Applications for the show's 18th series are now open.

Aspiring bakers have until Friday, December 11 at 1pm to submit their applications, although those interested are encouraged to apply sooner rather than later.

Applications can be made online via applyforbakeoff.co.uk.
