The WNBA Playoffs continue on Wednesday, September 30 when the Golden State Valkyries visit the Dallas Wings in Game 2 of their best-of-three first-round series at 9 p.m. ET on ESPN. 

Golden State seeks its first playoff series victory tonight. The Valkyries dominated the Wings 108-80 in Sunday’s Game 1 rout in San Francisco as 7.5-point favorites.

The Wings, meanwhile, set out to extend their season with a home victory following a 17-win improvement from 2025.

Below, we'll break down our Valkyries vs. Wings prediction and picks for Wednesday's WNBA Playoffs game, including the spread, over/under, and key player picks for PrizePicks.

Golden State lost its first-ever matchup to Dallas on June 17, 2025 in an 80-71 defeat. The Valkyries have captured each of the last seven meetings, though, including all four this season.

In Sunday’s Game 1 of the first round in San Francisco, the Valkyries turned a 1-point lead after one quarter into a 27-point halftime advantage. Golden State outscored Dallas 37-11 in the second frame, highlighted by hitting 7 of 10 attempts from downtown.

The Wings shot 40.9% from deep in the first meeting against Golden State but have converted at a 26.8% clip in the last three matchups. 

Since losing at home to Golden State on August 17, Dallas has won seven straight games at College Park Center. The Wings posted an offensive rating of 107 or higher six times and owned an offensive turnover rate below 14.0% on four occasions. 

The Valkyries are 12-3 in their last 15 road games since suffering a 21-point loss at Las Vegas on June 21. Golden State is 10-5 against the spread (ATS) in this span, failing to cash in a 1-point win at Los Angeles to close out the regular season.
