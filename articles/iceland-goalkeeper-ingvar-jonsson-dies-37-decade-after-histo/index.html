Iceland goalkeeper Ingvar Jonsson dies, 37, decade after historic England upset

TheTools · 26.11.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

A goalkeeper who was part of the famous Iceland squad that dumped England out of Euro 2016 has tragically died aged 37. Ingvar Jonsson has passed away after being diagnosed with cancer earlier this year.

Jonsson was an unused substitute throughout Iceland's remarkable run to the quarter-finals in France, memorably beating England 2-1 in the round of 16. His club, Vikingur Reykjavik, confirmed the devastating news on Thursday.

Vikingur said in a statement posted on social media: "It is with great sadness and deep sorrow that we announce that our dear friend and teammate Ingvar Jonsson, Vikingur's goalkeeper, passed away on Wednesday, November 25, surrounded by his loved ones.

"The thoughts of the club and all Vikingur are with his family and loved ones. We send them our deepest condolences."

Jonsson won the Icelandic title five times, including twice with Vikingur to earn qualification to the Europa League and Conference League. He also had spells in Denmark and Norway.

He lifted the title for the first time in 2014 with Stjarnan and was voted Players' Player of the Year. Stjarnan went unbeaten that season.

The shot-stopper won eight caps for him country between 2014 and 2019. Jonsson leaves behind a wife three children.

Tributes have poured in from around the football world. Belgian club Cercle Brugge commented: "Our deepest condolences."

A fan posted: One is deeply saddened. I extend my condolences to Ingvar's family, friends, loved ones and the Viking community."

Another wrote: "Heartfelt condolences to you all."

A third added: "He is a legend in Icelandic football! Rest in peace, dear friend!"
