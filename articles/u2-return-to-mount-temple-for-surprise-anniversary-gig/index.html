U2 return to Mount Temple for surprise anniversary gig

TheTools · 25.09.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

U2 have returned to their former school in Dublin for a special performance marking 50 years since the band first came together.

Bono, The Edge, Adam Clayton and Larry Mullen Jr took to the stage at Mount Temple Comprehensive School shortly after 2pm, performing for students and staff at the school where their story began.

It comes 50 years to the day since, on 25 September 1976, a group of teenagers gathered at Mullen's home on Dublin’s northside after he placed a notice on a school board looking for musicians.

Those teenagers would go on to become one of the world's biggest bands. Half a century later, the four members made their way back up the familiar driveway at Mount Temple to perform for a new generation of students.

Before they took to the stage, Alice Cooper's School’s Out blared over the loudspeakers to huge cheers.

U2 opened with I Will Follow, and also played Vertigo, Beautiful Day and Out of Control, which appeared on their first release, U2-3, in 1979.

They were joined by young musicians from Music Generation for One, and also worked a couple of lines from Elbow’s One Day Like This into the set.

Excitement had been building in the school halls all morning, with rumours of the band’s return circulating during the week.

Among those in the audience were young members of Music Generation, the national music education programme which has been supported by U2 and The Ireland Funds.

The homecoming comes a day after U2 announced Carnaval De Luz, their first studio album in nine years, which will be released on 13 November.

New track Silencio is out now.
