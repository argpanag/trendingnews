Kings' Precious Achiuwa: Starting vs. Grizzlies

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:56 · source · permalink

Achiuwa is in the starting lineup for Sunday's game against the Grizzlies, Frankie Cartoscelli of KHTK Sactown Sports 1140 reports.

Achiuwa will start at power forward alongside Domantas Sabonis in the frontcourt. The veteran big man has enjoyed an excellent start to the season, averaging 14.5 points, 5.5 rebounds and 2.0 steals per game in two appearances off the bench.
