Ty Jerome goes off again, scores 31 points

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:55 · source · permalink

    Ty Jerome goes off again, scores 31 points

    Ty Jerome

    MEM Guard-Forward #2
    Player Stats

    Ty Jerome amassed 31 points (8-of-17 FGs, 14-of-14 FTs), four rebounds, six assists, one steal and one three-pointer in Sunday’s win over the Kings.

    For those who believed Jerome would cool off after scoring 73 points in the Grizzlies’ first two games, that didn’t happen on Sunday. He did most of his damage inside the arc, making 7 of 11 two-point attempts and shooting 14-for-14 from the foul line. Add in the assists, and it was another excellent night for those rostering Jerome, one of the best players in fantasy basketball so far. The Grizzlies play three games in Week 2, starting with the Jazz on Monday.
    - Raphielle Johnson
