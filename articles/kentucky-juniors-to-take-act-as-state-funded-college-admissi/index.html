Kentucky juniors to take ACT as state-funded college admissions exam starting next spring

TheTools · 19.11.2026 21:50 · source · permalink

FRANKFORT, Ky. (WKYT) - Kentucky high school juniors will take the ACT as their state-funded college admissions exam beginning next spring, after the Kentucky Department of Education awarded ACT a four-year contract to administer the exams.

The department said the new contract costs $28.50 per student.

The state switched to the SAT for the 2026 testing year, but lawmakers required a new contracting process.

The department said the new ACT contract will save nearly $500,000 a year compared with what the state paid for the ACT in 2025.

The contract also includes free support for students, families and educators, including free online practice tests.
