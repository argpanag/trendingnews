Netflix's 'Pride & Prejudice' Adaptation Unveils New First Look & December 2026 Release Date

TheTools · 16.09.2026 09:24 · source · permalink

Emma Corrin and Jack Lowden lead a star-studded cast in Netflix's upcoming 6-part adaptation of the Jane Austen classic.

Picture (L to R): Jack Lowden as Mr. Darcy and Emma Corrin as Elizabeth Bennet in Pride & Prejudice

Netflix has unveiled brand-new first-look images for its upcoming 6-part adaptation of Jane Austen’s beloved novel, Pride & Prejudice, and confirmed a December 3, 2026, release date.

Pride & Prejudice is one of the most beloved British stories ever written, and Jane Austen’s Regency-period drama has been adapted in various forms, including the 1995 BBC adaptation starring Jennifer Ehle and Colin Firth and the Universal Pictures film adaptation starring Keira Knightley and Matthew Macfadyen.

The series has long been in development, but subscribers won’t have to wait much longer, with Netflix confirming that Pride & Prejudice will be released on Netflix on December 3, 2026.

Directing the series is Euros Lynn, who previously directed Netflix’s Heartstopper and episodes of Doctor Who. Emma Corrin marks her debut as an executive producer on the series, joining Euros Lyn and others, including Laura Lankester, Will Johnston, Louise Mutter, and Dolly Alderton, for Lookout Point (Gentlemen Jack, Happy Valley).

Netflix’s official logline for the series reads:

When wealthy bachelors arrive in their neighbourhood, the five Bennet sisters must navigate love, family, and society’s expectations to secure their futures. Jane falls for the charming Mr Bingley, while Elizabeth clashes with his proud friend Mr Darcy – but unexpected encounters and family scandals force Elizabeth to reconsider her feelings and discover the true nature of love.

The cast of Pride & Prejudice

Whilst the cast was confirmed many months ago, here’s a reminder of who will star in the adaptation.

The Main Cast:

  • Emma Corrin as Elizabeth Bennet
  • Jack Lowden as Mr. Darcy
  • Olivia Colman as Mrs. Bennet
  • Rufus Sewell as Mr. Bennet
  • Freya Mavor (Industry) as Jane Bennet
  • Jamie Demetriou (Stath Lets Flats) as Mr. Collins
  • Daryl McCormack (Good Luck to You, Leo Grande) as Mr. Bingley
  • Louis Partridge (Enola Holmes) as Mr. Wickham
  • Rhea Norwood (Heartstopper) as Lydia Bennet
  • Siena Kelly (Black Mirror) as Caroline Bingley
  • Fiona Shaw (Killing Eve, Harry Potter) as Lady Catherine de Bourg
  • Hopey Parish as Mary Bennet (Screen Debut)
  • Hollie Avery as Kitty Bennet (Screen Debut)

Recurring Cast & Supporting Roles:

  • Sebastian Armesto as Mr. Gardiner
  • Anjana Vasan as Mrs. Gardiner
  • Jasmine Blackborow as Charlotte Lucas
  • Rosie Cavaliero as Lady Lucas
  • Justin Edwards as Sir William Lucas
  • Isabella Sermon as Georgiana Darcy
  • Saffron Coomber as Mrs. Hurst
  • James Dryden as Mr. Hurst
  • Liz Kingsman as Anne de Bourgh
  • James Northcote as Colonel Forster
  • Eloise Webb as Harriet Forster
