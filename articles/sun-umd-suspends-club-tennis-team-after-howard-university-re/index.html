COLLEGE PARK, Md. (WBFF) — The University of Maryland, College Park suspended its club tennis team pending an investigation after the Howard University team said its president found imagery resembling Ku Klux Klan hoods in material emailed before a tournament last weekend.

The Howard University club tennis team said its president, Toni Rachal, discovered imagery resembling pointed Ku Klux Klan hoods in a Google document that the University of Maryland club tennis team emailed to teams participating in its fall invitational last Saturday and Sunday.

The images appeared beneath the University of Maryland club tennis team logo and replaced portions of the Crossland coat of arms on the Maryland flag, the Howard club said.

“The imagery is a recognizable symbol of white supremacy, organized hate, and violence,” the club said in a statement Monday.
