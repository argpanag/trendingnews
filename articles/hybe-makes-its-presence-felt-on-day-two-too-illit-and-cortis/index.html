The groups that kicked off the second day of the MAMA Awards were acts under HYBE: ILLIT and CORTIS.

The Chapter 2 ceremony of the 2025 MAMA Awards was held at 3 p.m. on the 21st at Kyocera Dome Osaka in Japan.

ILLIT and CORTIS were the first to be named that day as winners of the ‘Best Song Top 10’ award, making their presence felt.

ILLIT is under BELIFT LAB, a label affiliated with HYBE, while CORTIS is under Big Hit Music. The two groups drew attention by thanking HYBE Chairman Bang Si-hyuk and staff at their respective agencies in their acceptance speeches.

CORTIS members said, “We think this award means even more because we wrote the songs ourselves,” adding, “We’ll keep working this way. We’ll pour our lives into our music, so we hope you’ll continue to show us lots of interest.”

They also revealed that they are working on a new album, building anticipation among fans.

BigBang, CORTIS, KiiiKiii, LE SSERAFIM, Nayeon, ZEROBASEONE, Hwasa, IVE, izna, Koi Kids, WOODZ and &TEAM will take the stage in Chapter 2. In Chapter 1, held the previous day, BTS and ENHYPEN won the grand prizes.

The concept for this year’s MAMA Awards is ‘Music Interstellar.’ On a journey that transcends time and space and drifts among the stars of music, audiences will discover and experience unexpected, thrilling moments and the dazzling power of music in their own way. The concept captures a plan to offer the unique joy of finding one’s own star while creating a wondrous musical universe where artists and global fans connect across time and space through ‘Music Makes One.’

The ceremony will be livestreamed worldwide through major digital platforms, including Mnet Plus and TVING.

Actor Park Bo-gum hosted both Chapter 1 and Chapter 2, and was appointed the MAMA Awards’ first official ambassador.

[Ji Seunghun, Star Today reporter]
