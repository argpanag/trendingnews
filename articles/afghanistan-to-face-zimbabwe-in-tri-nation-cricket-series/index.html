Afghanistan to Face Zimbabwe in Tri-Nation Cricket Series

TheTools · 19.10.2026 12:38 · source · permalink

The Kabul Tribune (KT)—Afghanistan will face Zimbabwe on Monday in the tri-nation cricket series also featuring Bangladesh.

The match is scheduled to begin at 4 p.m. Afghanistan time at Sheikh Zayed Stadium in the United Arab Emirates.

Zimbabwe opened the series with a convincing 99-run victory over Bangladesh in the first match of the tournament, hosted by Afghanistan.
