Boston Celtics fall short to Orlando Magic, 115-105, ending six-game win streak - AOL

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:36 · source · permalink

BOSTON — The first time the Boston Celtics squared off with the Orlando Magic this season, the Green Team clutched up in the fourth quarter to come away with a 108-97 win in early November. Two weeks later, the Magic returned the favor, outscoring the C's 40-28 in the final frame to capture a 115-105 victory at TD Garden on Monday night.

Boston got off to a sluggish start, which can perhaps be explained by the fact that its showdown with Orlando came on the second night of a back-to-back. The Celtics scored just 41 points in the first half. Conversely, they scored 42 points in the first quarter alone during a 131-94 win over the Dallas Mavericks on Sunday evening.

Celtics star Jayson Tatum posted a team-high 24 points in 36 minutes, yet he missed 14 of his 23 shots. Celtics wing Paul George and point guard Payton Pritchard also recorded 22 points and 21 points, respectively. It wasn't enough to overcome the now 6-7 Magic, who were led by wing Desmond Bane in a 24-point performance.

Following the double-digit loss, the Celtics' six-game winning streak is over. Additionally, they are no longer undefeated at home and are in danger of surrendering the top spot in the Eastern Conference. If the Philadelphia 76ers are able to defeat the Brooklyn Nets tonight, Boston will fall to second place.

With that being said, the Celtics are still 11-3 on the early season. Now they'll hop on a flight to begin a four-game road trip, which starts Wednesday night when they face the 7-4 Denver Nuggets at Ball Arena.
