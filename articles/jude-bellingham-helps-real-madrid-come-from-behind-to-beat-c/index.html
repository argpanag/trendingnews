Jude Bellingham helps Real Madrid come from behind to beat Celta Vigo

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

Real Madrid came from behind to defeat Celta Vigo 2-1 and draw level on points with Barcelona at the top of LaLiga.

Pablo Duran shocked Real with the opener after only six minutes but Jude Bellingham equalised midway through the second half and Carlos Espi scored a late winner.

Barca will have the chance to pull ahead again when they take on Villarreal on Sunday.

Jose Mourinho chose to start with Vinicius Junior on the bench following the end of his four-match ban.

Denzel Dumfries, Federico Valverde and Marc Cucurella were also among the substitutes with Trent Alexander-Arnold given a rare start, but it was not a happy night for the England defender, who was hauled off before the hour mark.

There were emotional scenes before kick-off with the father, widow and sister of tragic Tenerife player Jesus de Miguel walking out onto the pitch.

The trio were presented with commemorative shirts following periods of applause and silence, while Real players wore shirts bearing his name.

Former tennis champion Rafael Nadal, who was officially elected to Madrid’s board on Saturday, struggled to hold back tears in the stands.

Celta won 2-0 at the Bernabeu last season in a match that Real finished with eight men, and the locals must have been fearing the worst when the visitors took an early lead, Duran finishing off a flowing team move.

Hugo Alvarez then shot straight at Thibaut Courtois as Real were exposed at the back, while Williot Swedberg offered a real threat on the left.

Kylian Mbappe and Arda Guler were unable to find shots to match their trickery, and Swedberg created two more good chances, first sending a shot into the side-netting and then forcing Courtois into a good save.

Real were given a golden chance to equalise in the 39th minute when Javi Rodriguez grabbed at Mbappe’s shirt and arm, and the referee pointed to the spot.

Contact was minimal and Celta would no doubt have felt justice had been done when the Frenchman sent his penalty sailing over the crossbar – a head-in-hands moment for the watching Nadal.

Mourinho turned to Vinicius with 57 minutes gone, the Brazilian replacing Bernardo Silva, and he swiftly helped set up the equaliser.

After Duran had been denied by a strong hand from Courtois, Vinicius ran down the left before setting up Mbappe, who laid the ball off for Bellingham to find the bottom corner from the edge of the box.

Time was running out for Real but two substitutes combined for their winner five minutes from time.

Vinicius crossed from the left and Espi, who had only been on the field for six minutes, first saw a header saved by Ionut Radu before tucking away the rebound.
