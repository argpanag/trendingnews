Roberto Carlos's Ordeal: "I Lost 99% of Everything I Earned from Football"

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:40 · source · permalink

International

The former Brazilian footballer, speaking at the Portugal Football Summit, recounted the unpleasant events he was involved in.

“I had problems with people who emptied my bank account. Due to agents and family issues, I lost 99% of everything I earned from football.” The former Brazilian footballer, a 2002 World Cup winner, was a guest at the Portugal Football Summit where he recounted the unfortunate events he was caught up in. Almost all of the earnings accumulated during his career – estimated at over €160 million – were wiped out due to problems with his former agent. “I had a serious problem with him. I had a very serious family problem. On Thursday, I went to bed at midnight and on Friday, I received a pile of documents I knew nothing about. That’s when I realised I had lost my entire history.” A true nightmare. 

An appeal to the younger generation

During the three-day conference organised by the Portuguese federation, the former Real Madrid player, who is now an ambassador for the club, added: “I made mistakes, and therefore, I ask younger players not to do the same. I always thought about future plans after my playing career. It’s about building a history as a player and leveraging your image. When I decided to leave football, I already had my sporting director’s licence and was working with many brands.” A negative experience, but one that can certainly serve as a life lesson for future talents.
