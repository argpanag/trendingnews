"What Meadow wants to see is her father": Brian's return rests with Paul Walker's daughter - 3DVF

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Cody Walker didn’t dodge the question when Entertainment Weekly asked him whether Brian O’Conner belongs in Fast Forever. He handed the answer straight to his niece. “My stance is, it’s what Meadow wants to see done; it’s her father,” he told the outlet. Meadow Walker, who slipped into Fast X as an unnamed flight attendant helping Jakob Toretto escape a plane, has quietly become the person Vin Diesel and the studio answer to on this question. Diesel has already promised a Dom and Brian reunion as the price of his cooperation on the finale, and her word may carry more weight than any producer’s.

A daughter’s pivotal decision

The Fast & Furious saga is heading toward its finish line, and one question hangs over the whole enterprise: will Brian O’Conner, the character played by the late Paul Walker, appear in the final chapter? In an interview with Entertainment Weekly, Cody Walker, Paul’s younger brother, said the call belongs to one person. “My stance is, it’s what Meadow wants to see done; it’s her father,” he said, pointing to Meadow Walker, Paul’s only child, as the guardian of the actor’s legacy.

Why Cody won’t step in again

Cody has firsthand experience with this kind of decision. After Paul died in a car accident on November 30, 2013, during production of Furious 7, Cody and his brother Caleb served as body doubles while Weta Digital used face-replacement CGI to complete Brian’s unfinished scenes. The character was not killed off; he retired to raise his family, an exit Cody called beautiful.

This time, he is stepping back. “I would not be interested in doing that again,” he said, adding that if producers revive Brian through AI or archival footage, they would not need him or Caleb, since AI was not a thing back in 2014 when they doubled for their brother.

Meadow, Diesel and a public promise

Meadow has already left her mark on the franchise. In Fast X (2023), she made a cameo as a flight attendant who helps Jakob Toretto, played by John Cena, and Little B escape an airplane. Her bond with Vin Diesel, her father’s longtime co-star and close friend, keeps her tied to the family at the heart of the series.

Diesel, for his part, has raised the stakes. At FuelFest in Pomona, California, in June 2025, he told fans the finale would reunite Dominic Toretto with Brian O’Conner, framing it as one of 3 conditions he set with Universal. Whether that reunion actually happens remains undecided.

The road to March 2028

The finale now has a name and a date. Universal announced in January 2026 that the 11th film is titled Fast Forever, with a theatrical release set for March 17, 2028. Louis Leterrier, who directed Fast X, returns behind the camera. Diesel had initially floated an April 2027 window at FuelFest before the studio locked in the later date, giving the production, and Meadow, extra time to settle the question of Brian’s farewell.
