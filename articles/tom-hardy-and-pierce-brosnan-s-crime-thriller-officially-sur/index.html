Tom Hardy and Pierce Brosnan’s Crime Thriller Officially Surges to No. 1 Before Finale

TheTools · 16.11.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Since it premiered on Paramount+ last year, MobLand has held fans captive — and it is easy to see why. The series is chaotic, funny, witty, and thrilling thanks to sharp writing and performances. Created by Ronan Bennett, MobLand features seasoned actors in the main roles, including Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan, and Helen Mirren. The series gives them plenty to work with as it explores mob and family drama in Northern London with Guy Ritchie's signature style of fast-paced dialogue and action. The show has been winning on Paramount+, becoming one of the most-watched shows on the platform ever.

The streamer did not keep fans waiting, delivering a second season almost a year after the first ended. MobLand Season 2 kicks things up a notch as it finds the family fractured, with everyone fighting to become the new patriarch or matriarch. It would be easy if they all weren't so ambitious, and the current heads, Conrad and Maeve, were willing to give up their empire. Meanwhile, external forces are closing in on the family and what they've built, and they'll stop at nothing to ensure the Harrigans have fallen. MobLand Season 2 essentially gives the family enemies from outside as well as from within.

New episodes of the crime thriller have been streaming on numerous SVOD and PVOD platforms globally, including Paramount+ in the Americas and SkyShowtime in Europe. Fans can also catch up on past episodes on Amazon, iTunes, and Netflix, depending on their country of residence, and MobLand has been dominating these platforms. The series is currently the most-watched on Paramount+ in the U.S. as an explosive season finale looms.
