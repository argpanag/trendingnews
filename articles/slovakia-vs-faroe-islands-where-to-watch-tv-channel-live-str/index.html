Match Preview

Slovakia host Faroe Islands at the Kosice Football Arena in a UEFA Nations League C fixture that carries real weight at the top of Group 3.

Vladimir Weiss's side sit first in the group and will be looking to extend their advantage with a home win in Kosice. Slovakia have been in strong form through this Nations League campaign, picking up three wins from their last four competitive outings.

Faroe Islands arrive in Slovakia sitting second in the group, making this a direct contest between the top two. Eydun Klakstein's side have shown resilience throughout the campaign, drawing four of their last five matches across all competitions.

These two sides met earlier in this Nations League cycle, playing out a 1-1 draw. Slovakia will be eager to settle that score on home soil, while the Faroe Islands will back themselves to frustrate again.
