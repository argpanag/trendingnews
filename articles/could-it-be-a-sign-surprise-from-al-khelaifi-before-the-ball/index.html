Could it be a sign? Surprise from Al-Khelaifi before the Ballon d'Or ceremony | Goal.com

TheTools · 26.10.2026 16:47 · source · permalink

A surprising decision from the Paris president

Nasser Al-Khelaifi, the president of Paris Saint-Germain, will not attend tonight's Ballon d'Or ceremony, held this Monday evening at the London Palladium.

According to French radio station "Monte Carlo", Al-Khelaifi has chosen to stay away from the ceremony despite 10 Paris Saint-Germain players sitting among the 30 nominees for the prestigious award.

His evening will instead be spent at the Parc des Princes, backing his side against Olympique Lyon in the postponed eighth-round fixture of the French league.

The broadcaster added that Khvicha Kvaratskhelia will also skip the ceremony as he prepares to undergo knee surgery. Nuno Mendes misses out too, along with seven other team-mates named in Luis Enrique's squad to face Lyon.
