Forget 'Reacher,' Netflix's Action Thriller Hit Returns in First Teaser as Series Crosses Major Milestone

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:50 · source · permalink

One of Netflix's biggest shows is officially coming to an end, and the streamer just made a major announcement ahead of the explosive final chapter. The Reacher-esque action thriller has been a mainstay on the platform's most-watched titles list since its debut back in 2022. It is coming to an end soon enough, but do not fret. The first footage of the final season has arrived, and it already looks like the most ambitious one yet.

Netflix has announced that The Night Agent Season 4 has officially wrapped filming with a new behind-the-scenes video that shows off some of the mayhem from the highly anticipated final chapter. As the footage reveals, Garbiel Basso is back as Peter Sutherland for one last mission in the action-packed conclusion. An exact release date was not confirmed, but Netflix confirmed the series will return in 2027. Watch the video announcement below:

The Night Agent Season 3 finale had Peter Sutherland (Basso) declare himself ready for a much-deserved break from the chaos. However, his boss, Agent Aiden Mosley (Albert Jones), not-so-subtly suggests that more missions are on the way, and a new partner may be in the picture. Safe to say that Sutherland is officially back for an intense mission that has him on another globe-trotting adventure. Showunner Shawn Ryan confirmed to Netflix that The Night Agent Season 4 was filmed over five different countries, promising "even more twists, turns, and thrills" when the show returns in 2027.

"It's been a wild ride filming The Night Agent in five countries across three continents … and we're so thrilled that the adventures of Peter Sutherland will continue into Season 4. Our writers, our cast, and our crew stand ready to answer the call to bring our incredible fans even more twists, turns, and thrills."

The Night Agent Season 4 sees Basso joined by several familiar faces, including fan-favorite Luciane Buchanan back in action as Rose Larkin after sitting out the last batch of episodes. The new cast features Titus Welliver (Bosch), Li Jun Li (Sinners), Trevante Rhodes (Moonlight), Elizabeth Lail (Five Nights at Freddy's), Abigail Breslin (Zombieland), Annabeth Gish (Midnight Mass) as Holland, and David Denman (Mare of Eastown). If it wasn't clear enough, The Night Agent may be making its final stand, but it is going out with an epic bang.
