Family of Kimberly and Daniel Paxton-Fulton continues push for justice

TheTools · 17.09.2026 22:23 · source · permalink

WATERFORD, Ohio (WTAP) - A second arrest has been made in the case of Kimberly and Daniel Paxton-Fulton. Johnny Ray Ball was taken into custody on a 13-count indictment on September 15. The family is now seeking justice.

“We’re thrilled that we’re finally making progress again on our journey to justice for Kim and Daniel,” said Sandy Oxley, Kimberly’s sister.

“Justice would be they wouldn’t have been brutally murdered so long ago. Justice moving forward looks like everybody involved in the crime would be behind bars for the remainder of their lives with no possibility of parole,” Oxley said.

Oxley said that with the community’s help, the family has been able to learn more information through the trial of Scott Hickman.

“More and more community people are coming forward with information and that’s exactly what we need to get the last person involved in this case. We do believe there is another person who is involved and there is somebody in the community who knows exactly what that’s all about,” Oxley said.

Keeping their memory alive

The family works to keep Kim and Daniel’s memory alive 31 years after their passing.

“There’s not a day that goes by that we don’t have a conversation about Kim and Daniel throughout Kim’s children’s lives as they’ve grown up. My brother, my mom and I have made sure that their mom’s memory is at the height of all of their milestones at the birth of their children, at their weddings, at their high school graduation. Someone took all that away from my sister’s children and that’s a travesty. My mom, a parent should never have to bury their children and my mom has buried her daughter,” Oxley said.

Lives changed forever

March 5, 1995, the Paxton-Fulton family had their lives changed forever.

“The day Kim and Daniel were murdered, our lives were changed forever. So we have life before Kim and Daniel were murdered and we have life after Kim and Daniel were murdered. And so they’ve taken away our sense of normalcy. That normal will never be here again. And they’ve taken all the milestones. They took a mother from three young children. They took a sister from my brother and I. They took a child from my parents and they actually robbed my brother and I of our parents because the day my sister died, a piece of my parents died,” Oxley said.

Kimberly never had the chance to meet her grandchildren, which weighs heavy on the family.

“They’ve taken the normalcy from our family mostly. And the fact that myself as the oldest daughter of Kim and my sister Amy and Michael, she never got the opportunity to meet her grandchildren, which would be her total world. And that it just is devastating that somebody took that from us and we never had that experience,” said Miranda Allen, Kimberly’s eldest daughter.

For the last 31 years, the family has lived with a heavy heart while Oxley says the killers walk free.

“And we’ve had to live with that for the last 31 years. And these people have been wandering free within the community, living their life to their best life. And that’s a travesty. And so we are seeking justice to the fullest,” Oxley said.

Family asks public for information

The family is pleading 31 years later: if you have information, please share it.

“If anybody has any information, please come forward. We’re begging you as a family to get our final justice. We want full justice and we know there’s someone else out there that is still running free. Please come forward,” Allen said.

Anyone with information can call the sheriff’s office cold case unit at 740-373-6623, extension 4042 or 4041. Submissions can be made anonymously.
