DraftKings Makes Third Round of Layoffs in 2026

TheTools · 10.11.2026 14:05 · source · permalink

DraftKings has laid off another group of employees, at least its third round of job cuts this year, as the company spends heavily on its prediction markets push and promises tighter cost control.

(Photo by Michael Reaves/Getty Images)

Affected employees worked in areas including customer experience, engineering and legal, Front Office Sports reported Nov. 6. The size of the cuts was not clear. Two laid-off employees told the outlet they were informed on Monday, Nov. 2, and DraftKings also cut jobs in the spring and summer, according to multiple sources and social media posts.

DraftKings said in its annual report for 2025 that it had over 5,500 employees in 13 countries.

Cost Focus

The layoffs came days before DraftKings reported third-quarter results, in which it raised its planned 2026 investment in its Predictions business and lowered full-year guidance.

Asked about 2027 spending, Robins said cost is "a huge focus of the business right now" and that the company wants to make its cost structure "as efficient and lean as possible."

Front Office Sports noted that FanDuel, Underdog and PrizePicks have also had layoffs this year. Elsewhere in the industry, Pennsylvania skill games operator Republic Amusements filed a notice to lay off 81 workers.
