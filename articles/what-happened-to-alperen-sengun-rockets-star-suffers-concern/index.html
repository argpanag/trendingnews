Alperen Sengun sustained an eye injury in the Rockets-Pacers game after being hit by Aaron Nesmith.

Alperen Sengun left and entered the locker room during the Houston Rockets' Wednesday game against the Indiana Pacers.

He will no longer return with a left eye injury, the Rockets announced.

Alperen Sengun absorbed a hard hit to the eye from Aaron Nesmith following a rebound attempt by the Pacers forward with over seven minutes left in the second quarter. The Turkish star went down in visible pain while grabbing his face.

Steven Adams started the second half for the Rockets and replaced the injured Sengun on Wednesday.

The Houston big man dropped seven points and seven boards in 14 minutes before leaving.

Following this brutal injury development, Sengun's playing status will be monitored in Friday's key NBA Cup clash in Denver against the Nuggets.
