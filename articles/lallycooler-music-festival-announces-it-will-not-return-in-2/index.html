Lallycooler Music Festival announces it will not return in 2027

TheTools · 19.09.2026 00:17 · source · permalink

SIOUX FALLS, S.D. (Dakota News Now) - After two summers, Lallycooler Music Festival will not be making a return in 2027.

In a post on social media, organizers thanked those who showed up and artists who took the stage, as well as volunteers and team members who made it all possible.

Those who purchased passes to next year’s festival will receive a full refund within seven business days.
