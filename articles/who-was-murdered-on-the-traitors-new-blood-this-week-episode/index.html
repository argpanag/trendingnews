Of course, Host Alan Cumming had an extra something up his couture sleeve.

What was different about this week’s Murder on The Traitors: New Blood?

While The Traitors franchise has had many dinner and food related twists, including this season’s “Fruitgate” scenario, this week’s Murder was essentially a modified, pre-selected Murder in Plain Sight. Two hours before the banquet, Alan delivered written instructions for Traitors Joe and Katie to meet in the castle’s secret room where they would decide which player would get the poisoned chalice. 

Joe desperately wanted to give it to Victor, but the blatant Murder of his most public accuser would have sealed his Banishment fate at the next Roundtable. Instead, the duo debated less obvious choices from the existing pack. 

Later in the garden, with the chalices arranged, Cumming let the players know they could swap them with someone else’s chalice so long as the possessor agreed. Kriste immediately grabbed her cup and refused to change, which raised some eyebrows. Meanwhile, a desperate Victor tried to swap with Joe who declined, saying it would be suicide to do any swaps with Victor. In truth, everyone was reluctant to swap. Yet in the last seconds, Shane asked to swap with Joe and he agreed.
