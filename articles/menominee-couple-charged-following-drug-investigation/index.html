Menominee couple charged following drug investigation

TheTools · 07.11.2026 01:25 · source · permalink

MENOMINEE COUNTY, Mich. (WLUC) - Two people from Menominee have been charged with intent to deliver drugs and possession of a felony firearm.

Mason Thomas Berden, 25, and Kirstie Anna Paris, 28, are charged with possession with intent to deliver methamphetamine, a felony punishable by up to 20 Years and/or $25,000.00, and felony firearm, a felony carrying a penalty of two Years consecutively with and preceding any term of imprisonment imposed for the underlying felony conviction. This is according to a Thursday evening press release from Menominee County Prosecuting Attorney Jeffrey Rogg.

The release says documents presented in court said the charges stem from an investigation conducted overnight on Nov. 4-5, 2026, by the Upper Peninsula Substance Enforcement Team. UPSET detectives conducted a traffic stop on a maroon van near the intersection of Northshore Drive and 48th Avenue in the city of Menominee. During the traffic stop, Menominee Police Department officers identified the driver of the vehicle as Paris, and the front seat passenger as Berden, who was arrested on the valid felony warrant.

With the assistance of a K9 unit, the MPD found 54.58 grams of methamphetamine, 36.82 grams of psilocybin, 34.86 grams of marijuana, a digital scale, a pistol and ammunition.

“Because of this interdiction effort, a large quantity of methamphetamine and other narcotics did not wind up on the streets of our community and put our citizens at risk. I appreciate the excellent work of my law enforcement partners in identifying and arresting these suspects so quickly,” Rogg said.

Berden and Paris were arraigned in the 95-A District Court on Nov. 5. Berden’s bond is set at $100,000, cash/surety. Paris’ bond is set at $10,000 cash/surety.

A probable cause conference and a preliminary examination will be scheduled by the court at a future date.
