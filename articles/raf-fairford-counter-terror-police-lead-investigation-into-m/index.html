RAF Fairford: Counter-terror police lead investigation into major incident near air base, officials say

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:44 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Five men have been arrested and remain in custody as counter-terror police are leading an investigation into a major incident near RAF Fairford

  • They were arrested on suspicion of committing offences under the Explosives Act, and have been further arrested on suspicion of preparation of a terrorist act

  • Gloucestershire Police say the force received a call at about 00:45 BST on Sunday morning relating to "three suspicious vehicles" travelling towards the RAF base

  • At a news conference, Assistant Chief Constable Richard Ocone says "work to establish the full nature of the incident is ongoing", as 85 homes have been evacuated

  • Helicopter footage shows robots approaching vans at the scene as a British Army bomb disposal team is carrying out operations

  • RAF Fairford has long been used as a forward operating location by the US military. The country's Air Force says it "remains vigilant" following the incident

  • The context for this is troubling and comes amid a rising number of incidents across Europe of what's termed "hybrid warfare", our security correspondent Frank Gardner writes

Five men have been arrested and remain in custody as counter-terror police are leading an investigation into a major incident near RAF Fairford

They were arrested on suspicion of committing offences under the Explosives Act, and have been further arrested on suspicion of preparation of a terrorist act

Gloucestershire Police say the force received a call at about 00:45 BST on Sunday morning relating to "three suspicious vehicles" travelling towards the RAF base

At a news conference, Assistant Chief Constable Richard Ocone says "work to establish the full nature of the incident is ongoing", as 85 homes have been evacuated

Helicopter footage shows robots approaching vans at the scene as a British Army bomb disposal team is carrying out operations

RAF Fairford has long been used as a forward operating location by the US military. The country's Air Force says it "remains vigilant" following the incident

The context for this is troubling and comes amid a rising number of incidents across Europe of what's termed "hybrid warfare", our security correspondent Frank Gardner writes

    We can now bring you further details from Counter Terrorism Policing.

    Deputy Assistant Commissioner Vicki Evans says they are in the "early stages of an investigation, and five men remain in custody".

    Counter Terrorism are "working closely" with partners to "understand the circumstances of the incident", she says.

    She adds: "We continue to work with Gloucestershire Police to keep the community safe as we work to gather evidence from the scene."

    Repeating details shared earlier, she says a 400m cordon is in place around the vehicles while the Army's Explosive Ordinance Disposal unit carries out examinations, and 85 households have been evacuated.

  11. Resident describes waking to police banging on her front doorpublished at 13:41 BST
    13:41 BST

    One resident who has lived in the area for 18 years said she was woken by police banging on her door.

    She was then ordered to leave straight away, without being able to take her dog with her.

    Jo says she knew it "must be serious" after she was evacuated from her home at about 07:00 BST.

    "We were asleep and they were just banging on our door.

    “They said it was a critical incident and we’ve got to leave straight away, and they basically wouldn’t leave the doorstep until we left.

    “We just had to throw our clothes on and come. We didn’t know anything.”

    Jo says the police waited at the door until they left.

    “I didn’t even brush my teeth or nothing."

    Jo says she had to leave her dog, Coco, after the rush.

    “They just implied that we shouldn’t take him. We fed him, but it’s not about that, is it, it’s just he’s on his own for hours.”
