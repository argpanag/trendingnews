Breezy and cooler with mostly cloudy skies and widely scattered showers today | 10/02 AM

TheTools · 02.10.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

LAWTON, Okla. (KSWO) -We are starting out with mostly cloudy skies and some scattered wrap around showers in the area. A cold front is progressing through the area this morning, so you will feel those breezy northeast winds later on. Temperatures won’t rise by that much with highs in the upper 60s to upper 70s northwest to southeast. A little bit of upper level troughing will keep the shower chance for today and tomorrow morning. Lows will fall into the mid 50s to low 60s overnight, and it will certainly feel like fall this weekend. Highs in the 70s and 80s, and lows in the upper 50s to low 60s. We warm up and remain dry for next week.

Meteorologist Michael Amankwaa
