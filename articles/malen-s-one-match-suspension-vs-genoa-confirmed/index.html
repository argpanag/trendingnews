Malen's one-match suspension vs Genoa confirmed

TheTools · 13.10.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Two days after Como-Roma, the decision regarding Donyell Malen has been made.

The Dutch striker was sent off in the 53rd minute of the match at the Sinigaglia stadium for a foul on Kaiki; his one-match suspension has now been confirmed.

This decision will him to play in Serie A’s matchday eight.

The player will therefore miss the next league match against Genoa, scheduled for Saturday, October 17th at 8:45 PM at the Stadio Olimpico. He will be available for tomorrow’s match against Real Madrid.
