BIGBANG set for 3 HK shows as member ticket presale opens today at 11 am

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:29 · source · permalink

BIGBANG will bring its BIGBANG 2026-2027 WORLD TOUR to Hong Kong's Kai Tak Sports Park from Nov. 13 to 15, 2026, with tickets priced from HK$699 to HK$3,099.

Registered members can take part in the priority ticket sale from 11 am to 11:59 pm today (Sept. 14). Public ticket sales will begin at noon on Sept. 17.

-Dates and times

  • Nov. 13, 2026 (Friday), 7 pm
  • Nov. 14, 2026 (Saturday), 6 pm
  • Nov. 15, 2026 (Sunday), 6 pm

-Venue:

  • Kai Tak Sports Park

-Ticketing details

11 am to 11:59 pm on Sept. 14

  • BOC Visa cardholders: Sept. 15, 2 pm to 7 pm
