There’s a Brand New Toyota Corolla Cross on the Horizon, Is This What It Will Look Like?

TheTools · 22.11.2026 22:08 · source · permalink

Rumor has it that the upcoming one might premiere either in 2027 or maybe in 2028, depending on whom you ask, and if it is a case of the former rather then the latter, than the current model year will be the last of the first generation. However, we think that it might be due in 2028, so it could be a 2029 model by the time it reaches our shores.

Recent information coming from Japan claims that the next-generation Toyota Corolla Cross will grow in size, getting closer to the RAV4. The GR Sport variant should be a thing, with a more aggressive design on the outside, some changes in the cabin, and likely a sharper chassis setup, and due to the rumored larger size, the interior should be a lot more spacious compared to the current one.

We recently saw a camouflaged tester of the upcoming model, though reports speak of a minimalist interior layout, and an exterior design perhaps influenced by the Toyota Corolla Concept shown late last year. This is pretty much the approach taken by Kolesa’s Nikita Chuyko, who stands behind the renderings shared in the gallery.

The side windows are shorter, there are new lines running across its sides, and a quite Jaguar-like rear lighting signature, this writer believes. The CGI model features thin taillights that span into the rear three-quarter panels, and they are linked together in the middle of the tailgate by a light strip. The tailgate is decorated with the Corolla emblem in the middle, and no longer features a recess for the license plate, as this was moved to the redesigned bumper.

Toyota’s next-gen Corolla Cross definitely looks more upscale in these renderings compared to today’s model, and it is definitely not bad at all. We expect it to pack several electrified powertrain options, though details about it remain unknown for now. And before wrapping it up, we have to ask you whether you fancy this rendered new Corolla Cross more than the current one. So, do you?
