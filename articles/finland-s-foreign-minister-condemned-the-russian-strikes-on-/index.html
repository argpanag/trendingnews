Finland's Foreign Minister condemned the Russian strikes on Kyiv and called for an end to the war

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:46 · source · permalink

Finnish Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen condemned the Russian strikes on Kyiv and stressed the need to end the war.

According to Elina Valtonen, over the past week, Russia has attacked schools, hospitals, residential buildings, energy infrastructure facilities, and the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine in Kyiv.

“This is a full-scale, barbaric war against the lives and future of the Ukrainian people. It must end,” the Finnish foreign minister stressed.

Elina Valtonen also stated that Finland is providing Ukraine with air defense systems and other vital equipment to help protect people from Russian attacks.

The Finnish minister’s statement came amid a series of massive Russian strikes on the Ukrainian capital.

On September 28, Russia launched a massive drone attack on Kyiv. Hits and falling debris were reported in several districts of the capital.

In the Shevchenkivskyi district, a strike on an office building was initially reported, followed by a strike on the building of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.

In the Obolon district, a gas station was damaged, after which a fire broke out. In the Holosiivskyi district, a drone struck a café near a gas station.

In the Darnytskyi district, debris fell in an open area, and in the Podilskyi district, a critical infrastructure facility and a residential building were damaged.

The attack also damaged a medical center, residential and office buildings, and vehicles.

According to city authorities, two people were killed and another 26 were injured. However, official figures on the number of casualties were updated throughout the day.

On September 29, Russia again attacked Kyiv with drones and ballistic missiles. Debris falls and damage were reported in the Solomyanskyi, Pecherskyi, Obolonskyi, and Holosiivskyi districts.

The attack damaged residential and non-residential buildings, private homes, and cars.

On September 30, another attack on Kyiv took place. In the Sviatoshynskyi district, fires broke out in a residential area; in the Obolonskyi district, a fire occurred in a store building; and in the Holosiivskyi district, a fire broke out at a warehouse.

Residential and non-residential buildings were also damaged. In the Shevchenkivskyi district, debris fell onto the grounds of one of the Western embassies.

City authorities reported three fatalities.

On October 1, Russian forces continued to attack Kyiv with drones. A strike was recorded at a non-residential complex in the Pecherskyi district.

In the Solomyanskyi district, a drone struck a school building, causing a fire. Strikes on warehouse facilities were also recorded.

Separately, on that day, Russian drones attacked the South Bridge several times. Two strikes were recorded near its support structure.

Due to the attack, metro and ground transportation across the South Bridge were temporarily suspended.

On October 2, Russia continued its strikes on the Ukrainian capital. Among the main targets were once again the South Bridge, as well as residential areas of Kyiv.

In the Darnytskyi district, a Russian drone struck the upper floors of a 25-story residential building. The strike caused a fire on the 24th and 25th floors.

In the Holosiivskyi district, debris fell in an open area, damaging the windows of a non-residential building and several cars.

Against the backdrop of these attacks, Elina Valtonen emphasized the need to end Russia’s war against Ukraine and pledged Finland’s continued support for Kyiv.

The Finnish Foreign Minister wrote about this on the social media platform X.

On the morning of October 3, Russian troops struck the North Bridge in Kyiv. Prior to this, a series of explosions rocked the capital amid an air raid alert and the threat of an attack by strike drones.

In Kyiv, the routes of trolleybuses Nos. 29, 30, 31, and 47 were temporarily changed due to damage to the overhead wire system on the North Bridge. 

Russian troops may be using hidden radio repeaters in Kyiv to guide Shahed attack drones immediately before they strike their targets.
