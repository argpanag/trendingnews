ADE Lab On Track reveals first speakers with Moodymann, Kevin Saunderson and Adam Beyer

TheTools · 11.09.2026 08:57 · source · permalink

Amsterdam Dance Event has announced the first speakers for ADE Lab On Track, a three-day academy programme for emerging producers and performing artists running as part of ADE 2026 from 21 to 25 October.

ADE Lab returns to Lab Village at Westergas, and On Track is the new addition: a guided programme built around masterclasses, mentoring, performances, studio sessions and demo drops, split into a Producer Track and a Performing Artist Track.

The first speakers

Nineteen names are confirmed so far.

  • Armana Khan (US)
  • Conrad Taylor (US)
  • DJ Bone (US)
  • Grace Dahl (NL)
  • Julia Borelli (DE)
  • Jyoty (GB)
  • Kevin Saunderson (US)
  • Lyzza (BR)
  • Moodymann (US)
  • Nene H (DE)
  • Paige Tomlinson (GB)
  • Peven Everett (US)
  • Rooléh (NL)
  • Rossi. (GB)
  • Sammy Virji (GB)
  • State OFFF (NL)
  • Timofey Reznikov (BE)
  • Zack Fox (US)

Kevin Saunderson, Moodymann and DJ Bone are all on the list, and Saunderson is also fronting Roland’s TR-909 workshop in Detroit this month. Adam Beyer brings the Drumcode side, Sammy Virji arrives off one of the biggest years in UK garage, and Jyoty and Zack Fox widen it beyond production.

What the programme involves

ADE describes On Track as built for ambitious emerging music professionals rather than beginners. The format combines masterclasses and mentoring with studio time and demo feedback, with the stated aim that participants leave with new contacts and material to work on.

It sits alongside the rest of ADE Lab at Westergas, which ADE is positioning as a platform for community, collaboration and creation rather than a conference strand.

Applications for ADE Lab On Track are already closed. First approvals began going out on 9 September and continue through 16 September.

Anyone who missed the window can join the waiting list through ADE’s site. Further speakers are expected before the event.

ADE 2026 runs from 21 to 25 October across Amsterdam and marks the event’s 30th year, with Eric Prydz at the Gashouder among the headline bookings.
