What is the WNBA Playoffs format?

The WNBA playoffs feature the league’s top eight teams, regardless of conference, with teams seeded by regular-season record. The postseason began with a best-of-three first round, where the No. 1 seed faces No. 8, No. 2 faces No. 7, and so on. From here on out, the bracket stays fixed, meaning teams are not re-seeded after each round. The semifinals are played as best-of-five series, and the WNBA Finals are a best-of-seven series. Home-court advantage goes to the higher seed in every round, giving teams an added incentive to finish as high as possible in the regular-season standings.
