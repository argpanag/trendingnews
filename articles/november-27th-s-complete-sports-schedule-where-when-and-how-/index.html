Black Friday will bring a packed day of sports action on November 27, with fans having plenty of games to choose from. The schedule includes one NFL game, 11 NBA matchups, and 13 college football games, making it a busy day for sports fans.

And that’s not all. The day will also feature Formula 1 racing, more action from the Davis Cup Finals, and 15 NHL games. Now, here’s a look at the complete TV broadcast schedule for November 27.

NFL

The NFL’s Black Friday schedule will feature a single matchup as the Denver Broncos take on the Pittsburgh Steelers at 3:00 PM ET. The game will stream on Prime Video, giving fans an afternoon showdown to look forward to as Week 13 action gets underway.

NBA

The NBA Cup group stage will reach its final night with 11 games on the schedule. Two matchups will receive national streaming coverage on Prime Video, including the New York Knicks hosting the Miami Heat at 7:00 PM ET and the San Antonio Spurs facing the Los Angeles Lakers at 9:30 PM ET.

The remaining nine contests will be available through NBA League Pass, with local and regional coverage varying by market. Amazon will also make its Black Friday games available to stream without requiring a Prime membership.

  • 7:00 PM ET: Miami Heat vs. New York Knicks – Prime Video.
  • 7:30 PM ET: Milwaukee Bucks vs. Detroit Pistons – League Pass; WCGV and Bucks+ (Milwaukee); The Spot and DAZN (Detroit).
  • 7:30 PM ET: Brooklyn Nets vs. Toronto Raptors – League Pass; YES and DAZN (Brooklyn); TSN and Sportsnet (Canada).
  • 7:30 PM ET: Philadelphia 76ers vs. Indiana Pacers – League Pass and local TV.
  • 8:00 PM ET: Atlanta Hawks vs. Boston Celtics – League Pass; Atlanta News First and Peachtree (Atlanta); NBC Sports Boston and Peacock (Boston).
  • 8:30 PM ET: Memphis Grizzlies vs. Minnesota Timberwolves – League Pass and DAZN.
  • 9:00 PM ET: Dallas Mavericks vs. Phoenix Suns – League Pass; KFAA and MavsTV (Dallas).
  • 9:30 PM ET: Los Angeles Lakers vs. San Antonio Spurs – Prime Video.
  • 9:30 PM ET: Golden State Warriors vs. Portland Trail Blazers – League Pass; NBC Sports Bay Area and Peacock (Golden State); KUNP and BlazerVision (Portland).
  • 9:30 PM ET: Denver Nuggets vs. Utah Jazz – League Pass; Jazz+ and KJZZ (Utah).
  • 9:30 PM ET: New Orleans Pelicans vs. Los Angeles Clippers – League Pass; GCSEN (New Orleans); ClipperVision (Los Angeles).

The Texas-Texas A&M clash will be one of the night’s biggest draws, with both programs looking to make a statement in a storied rivalry.

Formula 1

Formula 1 will head to the Lusail International Circuit for the Qatar Grand Prix, with Friday’s schedule featuring two practice sessions. Drivers will use the opening day to get familiar with the circuit, fine-tune their car setups, and prepare for the competitive sessions ahead.

  • 8:30 AM ET: Practice 1 — Apple TV (free without a subscription)
  • 12:00 PM ET: Practice 2 — Apple TV

The weekend will continue with qualifying on Saturday at 1:00 PM ET, also on Apple TV. The main event will take place Sunday, with the Qatar Grand Prix scheduled to begin at 11:00 AM ET.

Golf

Golf fans will have two tournaments to follow on Friday, with action scheduled on both the Ladies European Tour and the DP World Tour.

  • 8:00 AM ET: Andalucía Costa del Sol Open de España Femenino, Round 2 (Ladies European Tour) — Golf Channel stream
  • 8:00 PM ET: BMW Australian PGA Championship, Round 3 (DP World Tour) — Golf Channel

Tennis

The Davis Cup Finals will continue on November 27 in Bologna, Italy, with the first semifinal set to determine one of the finalists.

10:00 AM ET: Davis Cup Finals, Semifinal 1 — Italy/South Korea winner vs. Czechia/Canada winner — Tennis Channel

The second semifinal will take place on Saturday, followed by the Davis Cup Finals championship match on Sunday at 9:00 AM ET.

MMA

MMA fans will have two events to follow on Friday, November 27, with action scheduled in Bangkok and Manchester, England.

  • 6:30 AM ET: ONE Friday Fights 180 (Bangkok) — beIN Sports USA / ONE YouTube
  • 12:00 PM ET: Cage Warriors 214 (Manchester, England) — UFC Fight Pass
