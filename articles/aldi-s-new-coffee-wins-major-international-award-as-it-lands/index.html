Aldi's new coffee wins major international award as it lands in store from today: '36c a cup'

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:29 · source · permalink

Aldi's Lazzio Luxe Reserve Nicaragua Beans have been crowned the best at the Golden Bean Australasia Awards.

A brand new Aldi coffee product has beaten more than 750 competitors to take out the top prize at the Golden Bean Awards Australasia.

The Lazzio Luxe Reserve Nicaragua, which costs just $17.99 a bag, won gold in the Espresso — Large Chain category.

The 500g bag hits stores nationwide today, and you can make a cup for just 36 cents.

The 500g bags have landed in stores today after winning big.

Aldi's Lazzio Luxe Reserve Guatemala, which is also a new product, won bronze in the Milk – Large Chain category. 

The price is wildly affordable, given that the average cup of coffee in Australia costs $5.90 for a latte or cappuccino.

New Canstar research revealed more than one in four Aussies have ditched their local café, and some are getting their caffeine fix from instant coffee, pods, or beans at home.

The Golden Bean Awards Australasia is a major coffee roasting competition that brings together roasters from Australia, New Zealand, and across the Asia-Pacific region.

Industry experts judge the coffees in a blind taste test across categories, including espresso, milk-based and filter.

Roasters can win Gold, Silver or Bronze medals, with the top performers going on to compete in the Golden Bean World Series for the title of World's Best Coffee Roaster.

Aldi's Lazzio Luxe Reserve Nicaragua Beans (right) won the top prize and the Lazzio Luxe Reserve Guatemala beans (right) won bronze.

Aldi has a strong history at the Golden Bean Awards, winning the Overall Large Chain Champion title in 2025, 2024, 2022, 2021, 2020 and 2019.

The supermarket giant also won Large Chain Champion at the Golden Bean World Series in 2024.
