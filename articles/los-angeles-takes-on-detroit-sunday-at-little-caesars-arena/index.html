Los Angeles Takes on Detroit Sunday at Little Caesars Arena

TheTools · 08.11.2026 15:25 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Lakers visit the Detroit Pistons on Sunday, Nov. 8, at Little Caesars Arena, with tipoff at 6:00 p.m. ET.

The Lakers are 8-2 and have won 4 straight. Los Angeles beat Portland Trail Blazers 124-118 on Friday, Nov. 6 at home. The Lakers average 122.0 points per game and allow 114.3. Luka Doncic averages 40.1 points and 7.7 assists per game, and Walker Kessler averages 10.4 rebounds per game.

The Pistons are 6-2. Detroit lost to 76ers 112-106 on Wednesday, Nov. 4 at home. The Pistons average 111.1 points per game and allow 101.8. Cade Cunningham averages 24.0 points and 7.6 assists per game, and Ausar Thompson averages 8.6 rebounds per game.

The Lakers sit first in the Pacific Division. The Pistons are first in the Central Division. Detroit is 2-1 at home and 4-1 away from home. It is the teams' first meeting this season.

Los Angeles lists Luka Doncic as day-to-day. Detroit lists Jalen Duren and Isaiah Joe as out and Elijah Harkless and Isaac Jones as day-to-day.
