Inter Miami - Seeking a return to winning ways

Let’s take a closer look at Inter Miami’s current situation ahead of this fixture. We will highlight the team’s recent form and analyse how they might approach this new test against FC Cincinnati.

Recent form

Inter Miami is enjoying a solid Major League Soccer campaign, currently sitting 3rd with 55 points. Their attack has been exceptional, scoring an impressive 73 goals in 31 league matches. The man behind this success is undoubtedly Lionel Messi, who has been exceptional, scoring 25 goals and providing 12 assists.

Despite their recent 2-0 defeat to the New York Red Bulls, their form has been largely positive. Prior to this loss, they had recorded three consecutive league victories, showcasing their momentum. The team also recently won the Campeones Cup by beating Cruz Azul, which will undoubtedly boost their confidence.

Interestingly, they have drawn seven and won six of their 15 home league games. They have only lost twice in front of their own fans, proving that they are tough to beat. With no new injuries reported other than to defender Caicedo, Inter Miami will be looking to their attacking stars to secure all three points.

FC Cincinnati - Hoping to continue winning run

Let’s now turn our attention to FC Cincinnati. We will analyse the team’s current form, recent results, and key players for this upcoming fixture.

Recent form

FC Cincinnati currently sits in eighth place in the Major League Soccer standings, having amassed 42 points. Although their recent form has been inconsistent, they are coming into this match with renewed confidence. They secured back-to-back victories against DC United, which suggests a positive turn after a challenging run of results.

However, their away form is a cause for concern for the visitors. FC Cincinnati have struggled on their travels this season, winning only three of 16 away league matches. Their defence has been particularly vulnerable, conceding 41 goals in those away fixtures — a statistic that highlights a significant weakness.

On a positive note, FC Cincinnati will have almost their entire squad available, with defenders Robinson and Malacia being the main absentees. The team’s attack is spearheaded by the prolific K. Denkey, who has scored 17 goals, and Evander da Silva Ferreira, who has contributed 13 goals and 10 assists. If they are to overcome their poor away form, their ability to find the back of the net will be crucial.
