Three men stabbed in street attack outside a Liverpool bar

TheTools · 20.09.2026 10:55 · source · permalink
  • Published
    10 minutes ago

Three men have been stabbed outside a bar in a city centre street attack.

Merseyside Police said the men were found injured just after 06:30 BST in Tithebarn Street, Liverpool. They had been attacked outside the G-Bar on Eberle Street.

Two of the men are in a stable condition while the third sustained minor injuries, the force said. Police have appealed for information.

Det Ch Insp Lisa McNaughton said: "We understand the concern that such a serious incident will cause in the community."

She added: "The suspected weapon has been recovered and will be forensically examined.

"We will do all we can to identify the person responsible and support the victims as this investigation progresses."
