The peaceful death has taken place of Mary O’Donnell at her home in Ballyheerin, Fanad, Co. Donegal on 15th September 2026.

Predeceased by her husband Sean, parents Mick and Alice O'Doherty, her brothers Fr. Dan, John and James, and her sisters Anne (Deeney) and Sr. Eileen.

She will be sadly missed by her children Raymond and his wife Dee, Aidan and Marcella, her brother Michael and his wife Bridie, her sister in law Mary. Her grandchildren Danielle, Joshua, Conor and Isla, nieces; Orla, Mary, Eithne and nephews Damian, John Francis, Conor, Jerome, Garvan, Brian, Paul and Brendan, relatives, neighbours and friends.

Reposing at her home in Ballyheerin (F92 PK73) on Wednesday 16th September at 12pm and Thursday 17th from 12pm-9pm. House private after rosary each night at 9pm and on the morning of the funeral. Requiem mass at 11am on Friday 18th September at The Church of the Immaculate Conception in Ballyheerin with burial afterwards at Massmount Cemetery.

Donations in lieu of flowers to Ramelton Community Hospital, care of Bradley O'Doherty Funeral Directors or any family member.

May her gentle soul Rest in Peace.
