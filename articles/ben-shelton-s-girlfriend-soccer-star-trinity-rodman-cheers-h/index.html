Ben Shelton’s Girlfriend, Soccer Star Trinity Rodman, Cheers Him on After Bonding With Taylor Townsend at US Open - AOL

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:32 · source · permalink

Ben Shelton’s girlfriend, soccer star Trinity Rodman, is back at Flushing Meadows to show her support during his blockbuster clash against Frances Tiafoe in the US Open semifinals.

Ahead of the all-American showdown, Rodman had the opportunity to catch up with Taylor Townsend, who returned to Arthur Ashe Stadium hours after lifting the women’s doubles trophy.

Trinity Rodman Shows Up for Ben Shelton’s US Open Semifinal

Rodman had been in the stands for Shelton’s thrilling quarterfinal against Carlos Alcaraz, proudly cheering the world No. 9 on as he claimed a 6-7(5), 6-1, 6-3, 1-6, 7-6(10-7) victory over the defending champion.

The following morning, Rodman returned to training with the Washington Spirit, as the team continued its NWSL season while sitting second in the league table.

Amid her own professional commitments, Rodman returned to the US Open to support Shelton during his clash with Tiafoe. Ahead of the match, the soccer star caught up with Taylor Townsend, with the duo making each other crack up before posing for pictures together.

Townsend is fresh off winning her maiden US Open women’s doubles title, having triumphed over Ashlyn Krueger and Robin Montgomery 5-7, 6-0, 6-2 in the final alongside her partner Kateřina Siniaková. With the win, the American completed her Career Grand Slam in doubles.

However, instead of celebrating the milestone win, Townsend made time to watch Shelton and Tiafoe’s showdown from the stands. Her decision came as little surprise, given her heartfelt comments about representation during her victory speech.

“I take a lot of pride in this, being a Black woman here. It’s two Black women here in this finals. There are two Black men in the semifinals. This is a phenomenal time for tennis, for inspiration, for representation,” Townsend said.

Meanwhile, Shelton gave Rodman, his family, and his supporters plenty to cheer about in the highly anticipated semifinal. After losing the opening set, the 23-year-old responded admirably by winning the next two sets to take control of the match. At the time of writing, Shelton leads 4-6, 6-3, 6-3.

The winner of the match between Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe will face No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev in the US Open final. Shelton and Tiafoe will each be looking to become the first American man since 2003 to lift a Grand Slam trophy, while Zverev is on the hunt for his second Major title of the season after his French Open triumph.
