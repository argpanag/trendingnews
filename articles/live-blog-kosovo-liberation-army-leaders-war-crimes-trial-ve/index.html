Live Blog: Kosovo Liberation Army Leaders’ War Crimes Trial Verdict

TheTools · 16.09.2026 06:28 · source · permalink

Former Kosovo President Hashim Thaci, former parliament speakers Kadri Veseli and Jakup Krasniqi and former MP Rexhep Selimi – all senior Kosovo Liberation Army officers during the 1998-99 conflict – have been on trial for war crimes and crimes against humanity. Prosecutors at the Kosovo Specialist Chambers in The Hague called for 45-year prison sentences. Defence lawyers requested acquittals for the four men, who all pleaded not guilty.
