Last chance to save up to 42% on Disney Plus with this rare annual discount

TheTools · 14.10.2026 02:34 · source · permalink

Disney Plus is offering a rare discount on its Standard and Premium annual plans until 21 October 2026.

The Standard plan is $54 off, bringing the upfront cost down from $179.99 to $125.99.

It's ad-free and streams in 1080p Full HD.

The Premium plan is $73 off, dropping from $249.99 to $176.99 upfront.

You'll get ad-free 4K Ultra HD streaming, plus Dolby Atmos sound.

Hit the 'Go to Site' button to sign up if you're keen.

You can save even more compared with paying month to month for a Disney Plus subscription.

The Standard plan costs $17.99 a month, which works out to $215.88 over a year.

At $125.99 for the discounted annual plan, you'll save $89.89, or 42%.

The Premium plan costs $24.99 a month, adding up to $299.88 over a year.

The discounted annual plan costs $176.99, saving you $122.89, or 41%.

New subscribers and eligible returning subscribers can get the deal, provided they're Australian residents aged 18 or over with a valid payment method.

It can't be stacked with any other Disney Plus offer.
