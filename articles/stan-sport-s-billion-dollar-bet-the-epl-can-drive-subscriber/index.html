Stan Sport’s billion dollar bet the EPL can drive subscriber growth

TheTools · 10.09.2026 06:52 · source · permalink

Nine Entertainment has extended its deal to put the English Premier League on its Stan Sport streaming platform in a nearly decade-long, billion-dollar bet that it can grow the fan base and charge more as top-tier sporting assets surge in value.

The Australian media group announced on Thursday that it had extended its streaming and broadcast contract with the Premier League until the 2033-34 season in a deal estimated to be worth around $US600 million ($830 million).
