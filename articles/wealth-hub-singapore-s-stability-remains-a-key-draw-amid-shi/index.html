Wealth hub: Singapore's stability remains a key draw amid shifting market trends

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

Singapore wants to build on its global wealth hub status, even as investors navigate geopolitical uncertainty and shifting market trends across Asia. It now manages nearly S$7 trillion in assets, more than double the amount a decade ago. Industry leaders say its stability remains a key draw, while navigating US-China relations and tapping AI opportunities will be important to staying competitive. Beatrice Yap reports.
