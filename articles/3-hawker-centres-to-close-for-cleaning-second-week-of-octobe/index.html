3 hawker centres to close for cleaning second week of October

TheTools · 04.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

A total of three hawker centres across Singapore will close for one to three days from Oct 5 to Oct 7 for cleaning.

According to the National Environmental Agency, such cleaning is conducted regularly so that Singaporeans can enjoy affordable food in a clean and hygienic environment.

The hawker centres undergoing cleaning are Block 112 Bukit Merah Market and Food Centre, Serangoon Garden Market and Hong Lim Food and Market Centre.

Meanwhile, three hawker centres remain closed for repairs and redecoration works:

  • Kim Keat Palm Market and Food Centre at Block 22 Lorong 7 Toa Payoh (till Dec 13)
  • Block 726 Clementi West Street 2 (till Dec 27)
  • Changi Village Block 2 and 3 (till Oct 15)

There are a total of 123 markets and hawker centres across Singapore.
