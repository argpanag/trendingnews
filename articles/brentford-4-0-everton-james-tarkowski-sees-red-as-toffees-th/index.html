Janelt scores stunner as Brentford beat 10-man Everton

Keane Lewis-Potter scored twice as Brentford swept 10-man Everton aside to move sixth in the Premier League.

The Toffees were bidding to secure a third successive league win which would send them fifth in the table.

But they were second best throughout, with the damage done in a woeful first half for David Moyes' side.

It had started as a celebratory moment for the Scot as he took charge of a record-breaking 593rd game with Everton, but the evening started to fall apart for the visitors in the 23rd minute when Lewis-Potter opened the scoring.

The full-back was picked out inside the box by Jaidon Anthony and he drove a shot at goal that took a deflection to leave Jordan Pickford stranded.

Just two minutes later and Brentford scored a second, through Lewis-Potter again. His initial shot was blocked but his second attempt took a big deflection off Thierno Barry to fly into the back of the net.

From then on it was wave after wave of Brentford attacks, and got worse for Everton five minutes before the break when James Tarkowski was sent off for the first time in his Premier League career.

The defender slid in to clear the ball but caught Yehor Yarmolyuk on his shin on the follow-through.

Tarkowski was initially booked but referee Craig Pawson was sent to the screen by the video assistant referee (VAR) and he upgraded the card to a red after checking the replays.

Everton's best chance fell to Barry on the stroke of half-time, but his bullet header was superbly kept out by Caoimhin Kelleher.

The visitors improved after the break and it felt like Brentford needed a third goal to kill the game off.

That arrived in spectacular fashion midway through the second half when Vitaly Janelt hammered in an unstoppable strike from 30 yards.

Fabio Carvalho, making only his second Premier League appearance of the season, added a late fourth with his knee to wrap up a fine evening for Brentford.

Brentford analysis: Lewis-Potter the unlikely scorer

Brentford 4-0 Everton: Keith Andrews post-match interview

Brentford manager Keith Andrews had a conundrum before this game - how he would deal with the absence of suspended striker Igor Thiago.

He handed Callum Wilson a first Premier League start, but could hardly have anticipated it would be Lewis-Potter who would solve his goalscoring concerns.

The 25-year-old was deployed at left-back for Monday night's game but Everton struggled to pick up his attacking runs.

Both his goals involved some luck with deflections, but on both occasions he was given acres of space to get his initial shot off.

Lewis-Potter is something of an unsung hero for Brentford, with his ability to play anywhere down the left making him key to Andrews' side.

But he has not been known as much of a goalscorer, with his best tally before now three in a season. He now has four goals by November.

Lewis-Potter has not yet secured a senior England cap but performances like this will only increase the calls for him to receive international recognition.

Everton analysis: Misery for Moyes on record-breaking night

Brentford 4-0 Everton: David Moyes post-match interview

Moyes will be proud of his place in Everton's record books, having now managed more games for the club than anyone during his two spells.

But aside from that this was a night he will want to forget as almost everything that could go wrong did.

There was little Pickford could do about either of the deflected goals, while the Toffees may feel aggrieved by Tarkowski's red card - the first he has received in his career after being booked 74 times without a sending-off.

There did not appear to be intent from Tarkowski as he managed to clear the ball, but what went against him was the speed and intensity at which he flew in.

Regardless, his dismissal means Moyes cannot put this down as a bad night and move on, with Tarkowski now suspended for the Merseyside derby at home on Sunday (12:00 GMT).
