Devils celebrate goaltender’s milestone, then he gets victory with 31 saves

TheTools · 21.10.2026 12:38 · source · permalink

NEWARK – After an emotional pre-game ceremony celebrating New Jersey Devils goaltender Jake Allen for reaching the 500 NHL games played milestone, the 36-year-old was faced with a difficult task.

After seeing a tribute video from his wife and children and being presented with a silver stick, custom fishing rod, a Rolex and even a Tiffany crystal just minutes before puck drop, the veteran netminder had to lock in and try to shut down the high-powered Colorado Avalanche offense on Tuesday night at the Prudential Center.

Difficult to do, Jake?

“No,” Allen said through a wry smile.  “I’ve played over 500 now, so I can say that.”

The Stanley Cup-winning goalie made 31 saves in his team’s 4-3 win over Colorado, capping off a memorable day that featured all of his teammates wearing his jersey number 34 in warmups and custom “500” hats making their way across the spacious home dressing room in Newark.

“No, honestly. I told myself I really wasn’t thinking about hockey too much after I got off after the warmup, I told myself to really enjoy that.  I’ve figured out how to turn the switch pretty quick, so once that ceremony (ended) and the anthem started, it was another game.”

Luke Evangelista, Declan Chisholm, Arseny Gritsyuk and Jesper Boqvist scored the goals for New Jersey to help Allen earn his 229th career win; Boqvist’s late empty-netter actually turned out to be the game-winner after Arturi Lehkonen scored twice in the final 3:13 of the game to cut it to a one-goal advantage.

Allen, who lifted the Cup as a member of the St. Louis Blues in 2019, is in his fourth season with the Devils after a late 2024 trade sent him from the Montreal Canadiens to Newark.  Now essentially the starter after Jacob Markstrom and his albatross contract were shipped to Florida this offseason, it’s been rocky at times this year on an inconsistent club, but he delivered in a big way to help earn two points and get the Devils to within two of the final Eastern Conference wild card spots.

“That and the Stanley Cup, those are the two highlights of my career for sure,” Allen said of his night. “That’s one of the games I’ll remember forever, but to follow it up with a win, it’s even better.”
