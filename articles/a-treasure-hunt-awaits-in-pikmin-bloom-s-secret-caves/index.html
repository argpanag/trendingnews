A Treasure Hunt Awaits in Pikmin Bloom’s Secret Caves!

TheTools · 01.11.2026 09:05 · source · permalink

First, take a look at the video below!

Something new is waiting beneath the surface of Pikmin Bloom! Send your Pikmin into Secret Caves, where familiar creatures from the Pikmin world await and new treasure is waiting to be discovered. By playing Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy and meeting certain requirements, you can also receive a special Cave Map to use in Pikmin Bloom among other items.

Get ready to join your Pikmin on an adventure into the underground world!

[Coming Soon] Secret Caves: Send Your Pikmin Out to Defeat Creatures and Discover Treasure

To enter the Secret Caves, you’ll need a Cave Map. You may sometimes receive one with a postcard. Once you have a map, send a large squad of Pikmin into the caves. They’ll make their way through the underground world on their own!

When they encounter a creature, a battle will begin, and you’ll be able to command your Pikmin to attack. If a creature is about to strike, blow your whistle to call your Pikmin back to safety.

Even if a creature lands a hit, your Pikmin won’t die or disappear from your squad. They’ll leave the battle and recover after some time.

Win a battle to receive one of many different treasures. The treasures you find will be recorded in a new Treasure Catalog, giving you another way to keep track of your underground discoveries.

Play Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy to Receive Rewards in Pikmin Bloom

On November 12, 2026, Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy arrives on Nintendo Switch 2, bringing a new way to play Pikmin 4 through Dandori Academy. Practice your skills, put them to the test in weekend exams, and play Pikmin Bloom-themed Dandori Challenge stages.

By playing the demo or full version and meeting certain requirements, you can receive special rewards in Pikmin Bloom, including a Cave Map, special postcards, and a Pikmin 4 Oatchi-Rider Costume for your Mii.

For details on the available rewards and how to receive them, visit the Pikmin 4 Dandori Academy page.

Whether you’re sending your squad below ground or sharpening your Dandori skills, there are plenty of new paths—and treasures—to discover together!
