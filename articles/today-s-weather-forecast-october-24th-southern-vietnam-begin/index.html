Today's weather forecast (October 24th): Southern Vietnam begins a new period of high tides, while Northern Vietnam will have sunny days.

TheTools · 25.10.2026 01:03 · source · permalink

Today, October 24th, the weather across the country will be sunny, with thunderstorms expected in the afternoon in the Mekong Delta region, and scattered rain in the Central Highlands.

The weather across the country today is generally sunny, with some areas experiencing rain in the late afternoon and evening - Photo: QUANG DINH

According to the National Center for Meteorological and Hydrological Forecasting, today, October 24th, the weather in Northern Vietnam will be foggy in the early morning, followed by sunny weather during the day.

Other areas will have sunny weather, while the southwestern region will experience scattered showers and thunderstorms in the late afternoon and evening. During thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning, hail, and strong gusts of wind.

Southern Vietnam is entering its high tide period today, with rising water levels in rivers. Be prepared for flooding in low-lying areas and along roads and canals.

At sea, in the northeastern part of the South China Sea, winds will be force 6-7, gusting to force 8-9, with rough seas and waves 4-6 meters high.

Additionally, scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected in the sea areas from Vinh Long to Ca Mau, from Ca Mau to An Giang, the Gulf of Thailand , and the southern South China Sea (including the Truong Sa Special Economic Zone). During thunderstorms, there is a high possibility of tornadoes, strong gusts of wind (level 7-8), and waves exceeding 2 meters.

Detailed weather forecast for today, October 24th:

Hanoi will be cloudy, with fog in the early morning, and sunny during the day. The lowest temperature will be 24-26 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 32-34 degrees Celsius.

The Northwest region will be cloudy, with fog in the early morning, and sunny during the day. The lowest temperature will be 21-25 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 31-34 degrees Celsius.

The Northeast region will be cloudy, with fog in the early morning, and sunny during the day. The lowest temperature will be 23-27 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 31-34 degrees Celsius.

Cloudy skies with sunny periods from Thanh Hoa to Hue . Lowest temperatures 23-26°C, highest temperatures 30-33°C.

The South Central coastal region will be cloudy with sunny periods. The lowest temperature will be 23-26 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 30-33 degrees Celsius.

The Central Highlands will be cloudy, with scattered showers and thunderstorms in the evening. The lowest temperature will be 19-22 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 28-31 degrees Celsius.

Southern Vietnam will be cloudy, with scattered showers and thunderstorms in the evening. The lowest temperature will be 23-26 degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 30-33 degrees Celsius.

Ho Chi Minh City will be cloudy with sunny periods. The lowest temperature will be 23-25 ​​degrees Celsius, and the highest will be 31-33 degrees Celsius.
