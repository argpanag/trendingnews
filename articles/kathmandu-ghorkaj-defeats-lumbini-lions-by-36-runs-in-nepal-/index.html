Kathmandu Ghorkaj Defeats Lumbini Lions by 36 Runs in Nepal Premier League

TheTools · 07.11.2026 16:13 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. In the third edition of the Nepal Premier League (NPL) on Saturday, Kathmandu Ghorkaj defeated Lumbini Lions by 36 runs. Lumbini, chasing a target of 161 set by Kathmandu, was all out for 124 runs in 19 overs and 3 balls.

Sandip Jora of Lumbini scored 44 runs off 45 balls with 3 fours, Abishek Gautam scored 15 runs off 15 balls with 1 four, and Niroshan Dickwella scored 14 runs off 7 balls with 3 fours. Jahandad Khan of Kathmandu took 4 wickets. Similarly, Captain Karan KC took 2 wickets, while Santosh Yadav, Shahab Alam, Sahaj Rachige, and Rashid Khan took 1 wicket each.

Earlier, at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu, after losing the toss and batting, scored 160 runs for the loss of 8 wickets in the stipulated 20 overs. David Warner of Kathmandu scored 66 runs off 50 balls with 1 six and 9 fours. Sahil Patel scored 38 runs off 36 balls with 2 sixes and 2 fours, while Sahaj Rachige remained unbeaten on 34 runs off 15 balls with 1 six and 3 fours.

Ruben Trumpelman, Sher Malla, and Abishek Gautam of Lumbini took 2 wickets each. With this win, Kathmandu has moved up to second place with 6 points from 4 games, while the defeated Lumbini remains in the top spot with 7 points from 5 games. Lumbini, who were unbeaten in 4 consecutive games, were defeated by Kathmandu on Saturday.
