Will White Be Alright at Wembley?

The FA media department could have sent out any player to talk to the press.

That they picked Ben White was clearly a PR strategy, an outside hope of the defender not being booed again at Wembley.

OneFootball Videos

This was the Gunner’s chance to explain why he left the 2022 World Cup early and made himself unavailable for selection for the rest of Southgate’s reign. Yet this was more an attempt to draw a line under the matter and move on rather than an explanation.

To be fair, that’s been the 29-year-old’s stance for the last four years.

He understands why some English fans have their perception. He can reassure them they wouldn’t feel that way if they knew the facts, but he doesn’t feel obliged to discuss his business.

Does Ben White Owe England Fans an Explanation?

Rumours about why he chose to leave Qatar have ranged from clashing with Steve Holland, feeling homesick, feeling isolated in the squad or personal reasons.

Almost immediately, Mikel Arteta made it clear Arsenal were aware of what happened and backed their employee, while whatever transpired, it hasn’t put off Thomas Tuchel from calling up the player.

Those are two managers who put a strong emphasis on an individual’s character, so they wouldn’t tolerate picking someone if they thought he had an attitude problem.

So whatever the truth, it can’t be that terrible?

There is a theory that because the reality isn’t that dramatic, it would hurt White’s image if it turned out he was overly sensitive or egotistical, and that it’s better for him to keep people guessing.

In reality, Gooners know that White is quite laid-back, true to his convictions and that it takes a lot to knock him off his stride.

His chilled demeanour often gets translated as not caring, yet with a Premier League medal around his neck, he’s done something right in his career.

(Photo by Justin Setterfield/Getty Images)

Will the Wembley Crowd Forgive and Forget?

If the interviews set up this week were intended to stop the jeers on Thursday, they probably won’t work because they’ve just recycled a story that won’t go away.

The fact he was happy to sit in front of journalists yet not disclose the information he would have known they were searching for was his way of saying he’s no longer going to hide when he comes on international duty.

If I had to bet on it, yes, I think a section of the crowd will be booing White against Croatia.

I also think he won’t care too much.

Dan Smith

Should England supporters respect White’s decision to keep the events in Qatar private, or does he owe them an explanation after making himself unavailable for so long? And if he gets booed again at Wembley, will the fans have gone too far?
