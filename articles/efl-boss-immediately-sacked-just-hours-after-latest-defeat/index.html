EFL boss immediately sacked just hours after latest defeat

TheTools · 24.10.2026 17:22 · source · permalink
  • Jack Johnson, Sports Writer
  • Published: 18:22, 24 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 20:07, 24 Oct 2026

Burnley have sacked manager Nicky Hayen following their 4-1 defeat away at Lincoln in the Championship.

Hayen was unable to prevent the Clarets from picking up their sixth loss in 12 games on Saturday.

Burnley, who were only relegated from the Premier League last season, are currently sitting in the relegation zone.

Their loss to newly-promoted Lincoln, in another game in which they took the lead, leaves them in 22nd spot in the second tier having won just one game all campaign.

And Burnley, who haven't won at home since October 2025 - the longest streak in the top four tiers of England - have wasted no time in announcing the axing of Hayen just hours after their latest loss.

A club statement read: "Burnley Football Club has dismissed Head Coach Nicky Hayen with immediate effect.

"The Board has taken the decision following a disappointing start to the Championship season."

talkSPORT understands that Kenny Miller is one of several names appreciated by Burnley for the now vacant position, with the Sotsman impressing in charge of Maccabi Tel Aviv.

Hayen's axing comes shortly after he told talkSPORT that his future at the club is in the balance, where he also apologised to fans.

What did Hayen say after the defeat to Lincoln?

When asked post-match if he fears being sacked, Hayen told talkSPORT: "Fear? I know how it goes in football, especially with the results we have had in the last few weeks.

"I spoke with the owner last week in terms of the injuries that we had and the upcoming game, and also that we had a good training week.

"But for one or another reason, we can't bring the same quality in the game that we show on the training pitch.

"That's a little bit of a shame because the boys deserve much better and that is unfortunately the reality."

Then, on whether he hopes to continue in his role as manager, he added: "Yes, of course you hope to continue. But I know how it goes.

"I also feel sorry for the fans as there were a lot of fans here, so I do apologise.

"I know even if I apologise to them it doesn't matter because again we have a big loss and I know they're frustrated and I totally accept it."

'Absolute disaster'

Hayen spoke honestly about his and Burnley's woeful start to the Championship season, but unfortunately for the Belgian, it wasn't enough to save his job.

Burnley legend Ben Mee had declared after the Lincoln defeat that the writing was on the wall for the manager.

Speaking on talkSPORT's GameDay Live, Mee, who made 367 appearances for the Clarets, said: "Absolute disaster today.

"Going there and capitulating as they did, going 1-0 up, the quality in the group, the attitude of the group, it seems to be a theme this season.

"Lose a goal, heads go down, whether the players' mentality is right, whether they want to be at the club...

"I think there's players in there that were trying to leave in the summer, maybe didn't get the wish, the heads and the focus aren't quite there.

"The manager's gone in and maybe taken over a squad that's not as together as what he would have liked.

"He's going to find out, he's going to get the brunt of it, I think."
