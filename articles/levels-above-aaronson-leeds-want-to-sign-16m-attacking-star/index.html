Levels above Aaronson: Leeds want to sign £16m attacking star

TheTools · 04.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

The start of the 2026/27 campaign was seen as a fresh start for Leeds United and an opportunity for the club to build on the success during the second half of last season.

Daniel Farke’s men were unbeaten during the first three games of the Premier League campaign, but their record has now taken a turn for the worse in recent weeks.

The Whites have now failed to win any of their last five matches, which have included two losses, with one area of the squad proving to be a major issue at present.

During the club’s recent poor run of form, Leeds have only managed to score two goals in the last five matches - which has seen the club fall down England’s top flight.

As a result of their struggles in front of goal, the Whites have now only scored nine times in the division this term - a record which is the third-worst to date.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin is the only player within Farke’s senior ranks to have scored more than one league goal this campaign, with the striker netting 44% of the club’s goals.

They have also only created 17 big chances in their nine outings, an average of less than two per 90, which has seen the side only beat four sides in that regard.

No attacking option in the senior side has been able to register more than a single assist, which has no doubt contributed to their lack of quality within the final third.

Over the last couple of seasons, attacking midfielder Brenden Aaronson has been a consistent figure in the senior ranks - and he’s continued to be a key man in Farke’s side this term.

The American international has featured in all but one Premier League game to date, but it’s safe to say that he’s been unable to provide the goods in forward areas.

The 26-year-old has failed to register a single goal or assist within his eight league outings, with his underlying stats further showcasing his inability to offer any positive threat.

Aaronson has only been able to complete 72% of the passes he’s attempted, whilst only picking out a teammate with just 2.2 line-breaking passes per 90.

Both of the aforementioned figures place the forward in the bottom 10% of all attackers in the Premier League, but his lack of quality certainly does not end there.

He’s only completed an average of 0.6 chances created per 90, which, like the previous stats, has seen him fall below 80% of other attackers in such an area.

Given the lack of quality currently on show at Elland Road, it’s no surprise to see the hierarchy already planning to improve the senior ranks ahead of the January transfer window.

AS Monaco striker Folarin Balogun has been one name mentioned with a possible move to Yorkshire in the last few days, with a handful of other Premier League sides also keen on his signature.

He’s not the only attacking option currently being considered by the hierarchy at present, with Sevilla winger Miguel Sierra another talent who is admired by the Leeds hierarchy.

Football Insider have provided the latest on a possible deal for the Spanish star, and claim that the hierarchy have been monitoring his progress over the last few months.

They also state that the LaLiga side are reluctant to lose the 22-year-old at present, but could be resigned to losing him should a club match his £16m release clause.

However, it’s been reported that the forward could be seen as a possible signing for the summer, rather than the upcoming January window due to his ongoing shoulder issue.

Regardless of his current setback, Sierra has already caught the eye in his homeland this term, with the youngster netting twice within his eight league outings.

Such a figure is already higher than that produced by Aaronson, and his underlying figures only further showcase why he would be a mega upgrade on the current Leeds star.

The youngster, who’s been dubbed “spectacular” by coach Manolo Sanchez, has created over two times more chances per 90 this campaign, whilst also registering more line-breaking passes per 90.

Both of the aforementioned figures would allow the likes of Calvert-Lewin to increase his own goalscoring tally and put an end to the club’s recent struggles in front of goal.

Sierra has further bettered Aaronson for dribbles completed, with the Spaniard averaging a remarkable 3.2 successful attempts per 90 - placing him in the top 8% of players in Europe.

Such quality would no doubt allow Farke’s to have more of a progressive threat in forward areas, with the current options at Elland Road struggling to get past their opponents in 2026/27.

The Sevilla attacker’s talents are there for all to see, and there’s no reason why he can’t develop into an elite-level talent in the years ahead given his tender age.

£16m for a player of his quality and potential would be a phenomenal deal for Leeds, with such a move undoubtedly pushing Aaronson closer to the exit door in Yorkshire.
