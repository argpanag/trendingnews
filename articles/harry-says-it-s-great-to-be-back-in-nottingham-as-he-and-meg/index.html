Harry says it's 'great to be back in Nottingham' as he and Meghan tour charities after UK return

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:12 · source · permalink

Summary

  • The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are met with cheers and shouts of "I love you" as they tour Nottingham on their first royal-style joint engagement since returning to the UK

  • Harry and Meghan are visiting Epic Partners, a youth charity in the suburb of Sneinton, and the third stop on their visit - "I've always wanted to meet them," flower-bearing fan Dean Mackenzie says outside

  • Earlier they took part in a cooking class at Himmah, an organisation tackling food poverty. The Duchess was also reunited with her "ladies", who she worked with on a cookbook in 2018

  • "It was really, really meaningful," she tells our senior royal correspondent, while Harry says it's "great to be back in Nottingham"

  • Their first stop was the Community Recording Studio (CRS) charity, a place Harry has a long relationship with

  • The pair are in the city where they made their first joint appearance after announcing their engagement in 2017

  • As a reminder, Harry and Meghan are not working royals. Rather, they are in Nottingham as public figures and supporters of individual projects, our royal correspondent writes

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are met with cheers and shouts of "I love you" as they tour Nottingham on their first royal-style joint engagement since returning to the UK

Harry and Meghan are visiting Epic Partners, a youth charity in the suburb of Sneinton, and the third stop on their visit - "I've always wanted to meet them," flower-bearing fan Dean Mackenzie says outside

Earlier they took part in a cooking class at Himmah, an organisation tackling food poverty. The Duchess was also reunited with her "ladies", who she worked with on a cookbook in 2018

"It was really, really meaningful," she tells our senior royal correspondent, while Harry says it's "great to be back in Nottingham"

Their first stop was the Community Recording Studio (CRS) charity, a place Harry has a long relationship with

The pair are in the city where they made their first joint appearance after announcing their engagement in 2017

As a reminder, Harry and Meghan are not working royals. Rather, they are in Nottingham as public figures and supporters of individual projects, our royal correspondent writes

    Harry and Meghan's visit to Nottingham has reached its third and final destination - back in the Sneinton area.

    Crowds had gathered ahead of the couple's arrival at Epic Partners in Edale Road, which has worked with Harry since his visit to Nottingham in 2013.

    The Full Effect partnership followed between Epic and the Community Recording Studio (CRS), which Harry and Meghan visited earlier.

    The partnership mixed community-based support with school interventions, including music and creative activities.

    During their 2017 royal visit, Meghan met young people supported by Full Effect and spent time with Epic's girls' nurture group.

    Harry's support continued through a large donation to BBC Children in Need, while last year he donated a further £50,000 to Epic-supported apprenticeships organisation Coach Core.
