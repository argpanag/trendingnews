Ratcliffe's Week 2 Report

TheTools · 18.09.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

One game is in the books for everyone, and now the fun really starts. Week 1 of the NFL season gave us our first batch of real information, but the trick is figuring out what matters and what was just one game. That’s where The Report comes in. We’re breaking down every matchup on the Week 2 slate, digging into the usage, injuries and trends that matter for your fantasy lineups. We’ll also have a one-and-done pick for every game. There’s a lot to unpack, so let’s get after it.

Detroit @ Buffalo

Detroit needed overtime to get past New Orleans 31-30, but there was plenty to like from a fantasy standpoint. Jared Goff threw for 206 yards and two touchdowns, while Jahmyr Gibbs was the centerpiece of the offense with 29 carries for 156 yards and two scores to go along with five targets. He handled 29 of Detroit’s 32 designed carries, which is exactly the type of workload we want from one of the most explosive backs in football. Amon-Ra St. Brown saw a massive 14 targets and caught 10 passes for 67 yards and two scores, including the game-winner in overtime. Jameson Williams didn’t have the same box-score success, but the usage was excellent. He ran a route on 95.1% of Detroit’s dropbacks and commanded a 25% target share. Sam LaPorta also ran a route on 82.9% of the dropbacks and saw six targets. The concern is up front, where Detroit will be without Christian Mahogany and Blake Miller. That’s worth noting for Goff against Buffalo, especially on the road.

Fantasy spin: Goff remains a QB1, though the offensive-line injuries add a little risk to the matchup. Gibbs is an elite RB1 play. St. Brown is a WR1, Williams is still a WR2 despite the quiet opener, and LaPorta is a TE1.

Buffalo also had to work for its first win, holding off Houston 36-31 behind another monster performance from Josh Allen Allen threw for 334 yards and two touchdowns while adding two more scores on the ground. James Cook III played 71.9% of the snaps, handled 65% of Buffalo’s designed carries and drew four targets, giving him another healthy workload after leading the league with 1,621 rushing yards last season. But the most interesting development came through the air. DJ Moore immediately commanded a 26.9% target share in his Bills debut, while Dalton Kincaid erupted for 130 yards on six targets with a 23.1% target share and 68.8% route participation. Khalil Shakir was next at 19.2%, leaving Keon Coleman and Joshua Palmer further down the pecking order. Kincaid’s usage is especially encouraging after his 2025 season was disrupted by a knee injury.

Fantasy spin: Allen is an elite QB1 and Cook is an RB1. Moore’s immediate involvement puts him firmly in WR1 territory, while Kincaid is a TE1 with considerable upside if this Week 1 usage sticks. Shakir is only a fringe flex this week.
