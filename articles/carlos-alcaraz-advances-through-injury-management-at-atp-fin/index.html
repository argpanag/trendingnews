Carlos Alcaraz advances through injury management at ATP Finals

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:19 · source · permalink

Carlos Alcaraz controlled his Nitto ATP Finals group-stage match with Italy's Flavio Cobolli, winning 6-4, 6-1 on Friday in Turin, Italy, though the Spaniard revealed that he had taken a rest day following an injury scare Wednesday.

Alcaraz, continuing his comeback from a wrist injury that cost him two Grand Slam tournaments earlier this season, is receiving treatment for a left leg injury he sustained against Daniil Medvedev.

"Yesterday (was a) really important day," Alcaraz said. "Obviously, I felt something in the last match. Yesterday with the test, we decided not to practice, to rest. We went to have a walk, chill. And getting treatment morning, afternoon with my physio, this morning once again, really careful.

"Doing, I'm going to say, as best as we can do the things off the court to be ready and to be in a good shape for this match."

The strategy appeared to work. The world's No. 3 player seemed unencumbered on the court, recording 29 winners, and came away from the experience pleased with the result.

"I try not to go too hard with myself physically, which I just tried to play really hard, really strong, and aggressive, and I think I could do it. So, I'm just happy about it. I'm looking forward to tomorrow."

Alcaraz, 23, will face Ben Shelton in the semifinals Saturday. Alcaraz leads the all-time series against the 24-year-old Shelton 4-1.

Russian Daniil Medvedev advanced out of the Ivan Lendl Group on Friday, courtesy of his 6-3, 6-4 win over Frances Tiafoe. That result will match him with German Alexander Zverev, who won the John McEnroe Group.

Tiafoe finished his season 45-23 with a win in Halle.

"The Finals is a great tournament because whenever you finish it is great because you have some rest at the end of the season," Medvedev said. "But if you continue playing, that is great because I love to win.

"Only positives here. When I am on court, always fighting to win, and I managed to fight well today."

The 30-year-old Medvedev, who won this event in 2020, hammered Tiafoe with 15 aces and won all 10 of his service games. Tiafoe built a 4-3 advantage in the second set, but Medvedev rallied to take the next three.

Zverev, 29, enters their semifinal matchup with the ATP's No. 1 ranking, but Medvedev has had success against him in his career, taking 14 of 22 in the series.
