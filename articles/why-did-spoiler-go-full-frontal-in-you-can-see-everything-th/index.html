Why Did [SPOILER] Go Full Frontal in ‘You Can See Everything’? There’s Some BTS Drama

TheTools · 27.10.2026 11:35 · source · permalink

Truly, you can see everything.

Remember when you were innocently watching You Can See Everything in theaters the other day, and all of a sudden Elizabeth Holmes’s partner, Billy Evans, was full frontal in the shower? If you found yourself wondering “pls, why?” sources, um, differ.

When asked by GQ why Billy would want to appear naked on screen in the middle of an otherwise-clothed documentary, co-director Lance Oppenheim said, “I honestly don’t know. It’s beyond me, but I’ve heard interesting theories that people have when they see it. There’s a camera in the bathroom, there’s a camera that was on Nathan, there’s a camera outside the bathroom, and there’s an interaction that we see after Billy leaves where he sort of looks at the camera and smiles.”

He added, “So I don’t know what that means, but I feel like there’s enough stuff in there that gives me the impression that maybe he was excited that people could see everything and that there was full access, I guess. But it really beats me, honestly. We were in the car ride on the way back from the gym, and I said to Billy, ‘We will give you some time to shower.’ And Billy responded, ‘Want to film me showering?’ I offered to film him in a way that could avoid seeing his genitals, but Billy said he didn’t care about being seen naked.”

Cut to TMZ, which spoke to a production source who claimed, “Billy was told that even if he filmed with his penis out...it would not make the final cut for legal reasons.” Oh, and the outlet reports that Billy “did not ask the director” to go full frontal.
