Uncertainty remains over future of two empty city centre shops

TheTools · 07.09.2026 12:14 · source · permalink

The future of two empty shops in the city centre is uncertain after promises of a new store.

Several shop spaces in Chantry Place have been left empty for months after companies, such as Claire's which stopped serving customers in April this year, faced financial difficulties.

The brand had agreed to sell 156 shops to investment group Modella Capital, although 145 branches were not included in the deal.

Claire's in Chantry Place has closed down (Image: Supplied)

The former site of River Island has also been left empty after closing in April.

Last year, the chain announced a raft of nationwide closures as part of a restructuring plan following a pre-tax loss of £32.3m in 2024. 

In July the shopping centre said it had interest from new tenants and Jo Bates, general manager of Chantry Place said: "We are hoping to be able to bring you news of one new opening next week – watch this space."

There are still no updates on the new shop but a spokeswoman for Chantry Place said they are "hoping to have the go-ahead for the announcement soon".
