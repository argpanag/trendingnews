PENSACOLA, Fla. -- Pensacola International Airport is inviting the community to help welcome home World War II and Vietnam veterans returning from a three-day Honor Flight trip to Washington, D.C.

Among the returning veterans are a 100-year-old World War II veteran and a two-time Purple Heart recipient.

The community is invited to gather at Pensacola International Airport to celebrate the veterans’ return and show gratitude for the service and sacrifice of all veterans participating in the Honor Flight.

Representatives from the Patriot Guard Riders, University of West Florida ROTC, Daughters of the American Revolution, Santa Rosa Pipes and Drums, Sons of the American Revolution Color Guard, Pensacola International Airport, and other community organizations are expected to participate in the homecoming celebration.

The welcome-home event is scheduled for Thursday at Pensacola International Airport’s second-floor, pre-security area near the TSA checkpoint, 2430 Airport Blvd., Pensacola, FL 32504.

The veterans are scheduled to arrive on American Airlines Flight AA5139 at 4:30 p.m., though flight arrival times are subject to change.
