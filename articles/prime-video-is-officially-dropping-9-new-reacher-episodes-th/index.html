Prime Video Is Officially Dropping 9 New Reacher Episodes This Week

TheTools · 14.09.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

Reacher Season 4 is coming to an end. The season finale, called "Cut," will stream on Prime Video on Sept. 16, 2026, and most will be eager to see how the latest conspiracy-heavy saga ends. The seventh episode featured another shocking moment, as Congressman Sampson sacrificed himself to save the guests at a fundraising event from a would-be terrorist.

After the finale, we will have to wait until later in 2027 for Reacher Season 5, which is currently being filmed. However, the current journey isn't over yet. The spin-off, Neagley, which revolves around Jack Reacher’s trustworthy friend, Frances Neagley (Maria Sten), will premiere the same day that the Season 4 finale is available. And, unlike Reacher, all eight of its episodes will be available on the same day. This means we will have nine Reacher-related episodes to watch this week. That's quite the treat!

Alan Ritchson Has a Cameo in 'Neagley'

Alan Ritchson is everywhere in 2026, dominating both the small and big screens and the tabloids. Along with starring in Reacher, he has been in three movies so far this year (War Machine, Motor City, Runner). A Christmas tale (The Man with the Bag) is still on the way. The action star has also made the news a lot this year. I just hope "Ritchson Fatigue" doesn’t become a thing.

Ritchson isn’t going anywhere at the moment, as he will also appear in the Reacher spin-off Neagley in a supporting capacity. While we don't know many plot details, the new show will follow Frances Neagley as she tries to find the truth after an old friend dies in suspicious circumstances. Ritchson’s presence seems necessary to connect the two series. There is also speculation that Sten will appear in the Season 4 finale. That would mean the episode has the dual responsibility of concluding the current chapter and also serving as a backdoor pilot.

For the spin-off, Reacher showrunner Nick Santora is once again in charge, and is assisted by Nicholas Wootton (Law & Order, Prison Break). Even though the series isn't based on a book, there is a chance of future adaptation. At the 2026 San Diego Comic-Con, Maria Sten revealed that Frances Neagley will officially get her first novel, Zero Margin, by Yasmin Angoe (Her Name is Knight), and it will be published in March 2027. Previously, she only appeared in Lee Child’s books as a supporting character.

Prime’s Full-Season Release Pattern Has Been Questioned

Franchise expansion is always good, and for Reacher, it was long overdue. After all, The Terminal List received a spin-off after only one season. But it's unclear if Neagley will top Prime Video’s streaming charts like the original series. Of course, it's possible to be hopeful. Frances Neagley has been one of the original show’s best characters and perhaps the only one who can fight as brilliantly as Jack Reacher. The recent success of female-led action thrillers also suggests audiences are likely to give the spin-off a chance. From Lioness to Prime Video's Ride or Die, several similar offerings are thriving.

However, the choice to release all Neagley episodes at once has worried some people. A deeper look at Prime Video’s catalog reveals that the shows that are released via a binge-model format have a renewal chance of between 50–60%, while the weekly and hybrid shows all have chances of 80% and above. This discussion became heated after the recent cancellation of Spider-Noir. The superhero series starring Nicolas Cage was axed despite being adored by critics (92% RT score) and earning a few Emmy nominations. Some think that the series could have fared better and garnered a bigger audience if episodes were released weekly.

Most of the Prime shows that have been released weekly (The Boys, Rings of Power and Invincible) continue to do well. Perhaps Neagley, as a Reacher spin-off, should have been given the weekly Wednesday slot once Season 4 ends. On the bright side, we’ll all get to enjoy more tales from Lee Child's fictional universe than ever before. No matter what happens, Frances Neagley will always be accessible, since she appears in several more unadapted Jack Reacher books likely to come to the screen soon. And with a new series of novels being commissioned, the fictional character will be around for a very long time.
