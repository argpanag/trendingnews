Arabian Falcons invite Benzema, Pogba and other free agents

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:57 · source · permalink

Dubai football club Arabian Falcons, which plays in the UAE Second Division, has invited players without active contracts to join the team. The club named Karim Benzema, Paul Pogba, Raheem Sterling, Jadon Sancho, Riyad Mahrez, Philippe Coutinho, Dani Carvajal and Jamie Vardy among the potential players, The National reports.

Training without long-term commitments

Arabian Falcons is offering free agents the opportunity to come to Dubai, train with the team and play in matches without a long-term contract. Under the terms of the offer, footballers will be able to leave the club as soon as they receive a suitable offer from another team.

Arabian Falcons coach Jonjo Shelvey urged players not to remain without match practice and to join the project in Dubai. The former Liverpool and Newcastle midfielder made six appearances for the England national team.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Management team and club plans

Arabian Falcons captain is former Manchester United midfielder Ravel Morrison, while former Crystal Palace player Jason Puncheon is responsible for football operations. According to him, daily training sessions and matches can help footballers without contracts maintain their fitness and be ready for a new offer.

Sporting director Harry Agombar said the club can provide players with medical support, physical training and match practice. Arabian Falcons began the new season with a 2-1 victory in its opening match and aims to rise through the UAE football league system. The club also announced the launch of an academy, which it describes as meeting English Premier League standards.
