Como 1-0 AEK Athens: Stunning strike by Luis Milla helps Como scrape past Greek side

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores
  • Tables
  • Line-ups
  • Match Stats
  • Head-to-head
  • Live Text

Como are the first Italian side to win their first three home matches in the Champions League since Sampdoria in 1991-92

Luis Milla scored a stunning goal to help Como scrape past AEK Athens and continue their remarkable debut campaign in the Champions League.

After a goalless first-half in which Como missed a penalty, Milla broke the deadlock in the 57th minute when he curled the ball over Thomas Strakosha and into the top-right corner from the edge of the box.

Como had matched the record for most goals scored in a Champions League game when they put nine past French side RC Lens earlier this month, but they needed a gritty performance to defeat a resilient AEK Athens side.

Nico Paz had another lively game for Como, although he was denied a sixth assist in the Champions League this season as Tasos Douvikas - after running onto a sublime threaded pass from the Argentine - drilled wide.

Como were awarded a penalty in the 28th minute when Lovro Majer stuck out a leg and caught Alex Valle, but Lucas da Cunha hammered the spot-kick wide.

Cesc Fabregas' side had Robert Sanchez to thank for entering the break on level terms after the Chelsea loanee made a sublime double save just before the break to stop two quickfire efforts from Mijat Gacinovic and Lazaros Rota.

Como's rise has been remarkable, with the club playing in Serie D, the fourth tier of Italian football, as recently as 2018-19.

They now have 12 points from a possible 15 in their first European campaign and the result against AEK Athens - while not overly convincing - boosts their hopes of a top-eight finish to qualify automatically for the knockout stage.

For their next Champions League fixtures, AEK Athens host Galatasaray on Tuesday, 8 December, while Como travel to Real Betis the following day.
