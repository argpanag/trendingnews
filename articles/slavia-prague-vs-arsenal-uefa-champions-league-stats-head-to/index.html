  8. Postpublished at 22:36 GMT

    Some more stats from Como's 9-1 win at Lens if you'd indulge us.

    • Como have equalled the most goals scored by any club in their first four major European matches (19), matching Queens Park Rangers in 1976. They have surpassed the Champions League record by five goals, previously held by Juventus (14 in 1995-96).
    • Lens have suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a French club in major European competition, alongside Strasbourg’s 10-2 loss at MTK Budapest in 1961. No French side had previously lost a major European home match by more than five goals.
    • Como are only the second club to score nine goals in a UEFA Champions League match, after Bayern München vs Dinamo Zagreb in September 2024 (9-2). They are the first Italian side to score nine in major European competition since Roma beat Altay SK 10-1 in 1962.
    • Como are the first side to have 11 different players score or assist in a UEFA Champions League match, overtaking the previous record of 10 set by Benfica in their 6-1 win against Maccabi Haifa in November 2022.

Arsenal survive late Slavia scare for hard-fought victory

Viktor Gyokeres scored and provided an assist as Arsenal continued their unbeaten start in the league phase of the Champions League with a win over Slavia Prague.

Manager Mikel Arteta made six changes to the team that beat Liverpool in the Premier League on Sunday, with Gyokeres leading the line in place of Kai Havertz.

The Swedish striker played a huge part in the opening goal when he hooked the ball behind the Slavia Prague defence for Noni Madueke to race on to and finish confidently in the ninth minute.

Gyokeres added the second himself before half-time after he exchanged passes with Mikel Merino and curled an excellent finish in off the post.

Slavia Prague improved after the break, and almost immediately David Raya had to produce a good save to deny Oskar Kubiak who shot from the edge of the box.

Gabriel Magalhaes had to clear the ball off the line after a Mojmir Chytil header following a corner, and the home side eventually pulled one back when Wiktor Nowak scored from close range in the 90th minute.

The Gunners, who had a 100% record in this stage of the competition during the last campaign, are unbeaten after their opening four games this season and are third in the table behind Paris St-Germain and Real Madrid.
