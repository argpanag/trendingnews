Delta flight from Spain to Boston diverted to Portugal

TheTools · 28.09.2026 21:42 · source · permalink

The Airbus A330-300 was carrying 13 crew and 283 customers.

A Delta flight from Barcelona to Boston was diverted to Porto, Portugal, on Sunday due to fumes in the cabin.

In a statement, a Delta spokesperson said the flight landed safely after a mechanical issue.

"Delta flight 251 from Barcelona to Boston diverted to Porto, Portugal following a reported mechanical issue. The flight landed safely, and we apologize to our customers for the delay in their travels as we work to get them to Boston as safely and quickly as possible."

The flight was met by EMS. The airline did not say if anyone was hurt, deferring to local authorities for that information. The Airbus A330-300 was carrying 13 crew and 283 customers.

The airline said it was working with passengers to rebook them to final destinations.
