Catherine, Princess of Wales picks up passenger, 94, in minibus

TheTools · 01.10.2026 01:54 · source · permalink

The Princess of Wales has helped pick up a 94-year-old rural minibus user as she celebrated the 50th anniversary of the service.

Catherine travelled to the village of Pulborough to mark the milestone occasion for the West Sussex Minibus, whose team of volunteers transport the elderly across the area in a fleet of vehicles.

Stella Drewett, 94, said before the princess arrived that she was not fazed by anything, and joked: "When they said 'It will be a celebrity,' I said 'I don't know any celebrities'."

Catherine had been picked up by the minibus in the village and arrived at the home of the retired school cook before helping her board the bus.

Drewett, a new user of the service, said she travels on the minibus for her weekly shopping trip and a lunch held every Wednesday at the Pulborough village hall.

The princess walked to her front door and said: "Good morning", before helping her manoeuvre her walking frame along the garden path and onto the minibus, telling her: "Well done."

When asked about her trips out for lunch, the 94-year-old quipped that they meant "one day of not cooking".

At the weekly Pulborough and District Community Care Association lunch in the village hall, regular Lynda Jones told the princess that the event "makes a lot of difference if you've lost your partner".

She added that the princess was "a natural".

Chatting at the table, Catherine said: "What a lovely way to make friends."

Alan Jenkins, trustee at West Sussex Minibus, told BBC Radio Sussex: "She's done a fantastic job talking to our members.

"It's really to promote what our volunteers do for the slightly older generation, which is fantastic.

"She's done a great job and we're so pleased to have her here."

Before leaving Pulborough, Catherine played a jumping game with two little boys - Arthur, aged three, and his one-year-old brother Albie.

Their mother Thea, 25, said: "She was lovely, I didn't expect her to be playing with my children."
