Europe's airpower future is evolving. Here's what to know. - Breaking Defense

TheTools · 02.09.2026 08:45 · source · permalink

Breaking Defense kicks off a new video series focused on how Europe is modernizing its manned and unmanned aircraft.

Across Europe, fleets of aircraft are aging out just as defense budgets — and threats from bad actors — are rising. That’s created an inflection point where the militaries of Europe are trying to figure out how best to move forward with airpower modernization. Is the future manned? Unmanned? Is it pan-European, or will countries go their own way? It’s a lot to unpack, which led Breaking Defense to film a new roundtable series in London to try to sort it out.

Breaking Defense Editor-in-Chief Aaron Mehta is joined Breaking Defense Europe co-editors Tim Martin and Elisabeth Gosselin-Malo, along with noted aviation expert Douglas Barrie of the International Institute for Strategic Studies. Keep an eye out for future episodes by going to Breaking Defense and Breaking Defense Europe.
