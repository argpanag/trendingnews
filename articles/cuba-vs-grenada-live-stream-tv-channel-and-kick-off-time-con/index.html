Match Preview

Cuba host Grenada in the CONCACAF Nations League, with the group leaders looking to extend their advantage at the top of B Group 3.

Cuba sit first in the standings and have been the dominant force in this group. Their most recent outing produced a commanding 7-1 win over Bonaire, a result that underlines just how sharp they have been going forward across this campaign.

Grenada arrive in difficult shape. They have lost four of their last five matches in this competition and were beaten 3-2 by St. Kitts and Nevis as recently as November 13, a result that leaves their group hopes in serious doubt.

The two sides have met before in this Nations League cycle. Cuba won that reverse fixture 3-0 in Grenada back in September, and the head-to-head record between these teams tilts firmly in Cuba's favour.

For Grenada, this is a match they need to win to keep any realistic ambitions alive. For Cuba, three points would put the group beyond reach for their rivals.
