In 2026, the National League Championship Series features the Los Angeles Dodgers taking on the Milwaukee Brewers to compete for the pennant. The winner of the NLCS moves on to the 2026 World Series and faces off against the winner of the ALCS, Cleveland Guardians or Tampa Bay Rays.

The NLCS features a seven-game series, as Milwaukee has home field advantage since the Brewers have the better record at 103-59 between the two.

At a Glance: Watch MLB NLCS 2026 Live Online

  • When: Until Oct. 19, if the series goes to seven games
  • Matchup: L.A. Dodgers vs. Milwaukee Brewers
  • TV channels: FOX
  • Stream online: DirecTV, Hulu + Live TV, Sling, Fubo, FOX One

The MLB is known for celebrity sightings, so it’s likely there may be a number of celebrities in attendance during games. Famous actors, actresses and recording artists have been known to appear at games in the past, including Brewers fans Austin Aries, Stephen Colbert and others; as well as Dodgers fans Jason Bateman, Rob Lowe, Billie Eilish and many more.
