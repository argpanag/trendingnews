G. Broughton Harwell Boatwright

Daniel Island - Broughton Harwell Boatwright, 84, of Daniel Island, died on August 24, 2026. She was born in Milan, Tennessee on October 8, 1941. Broughton was the center of her family and its driving force. Strong, principled, and fiercely committed to those she loved, she shaped the lives of everyone around her, and she will be deeply missed. She graduated from Jacksonville State College and taught high school mathematics in Georgia. She earned a Master of Arts in mathematics from the University of Georgia and moved into computer systems analysis. Her career led her to McKinsey & Company in New York City, Clemson University, and the U.S. Navy in Charleston, SC. She later taught computer science at the College of Charleston and served as practice manager for Coastal Gynecology and Obstetrics. She lived an adventurous life, traveling to Europe, Saudi Arabia, working with horses on HBO Farm in Seneca, SC, and becoming an accomplished tennis player, horseback rider, and lifelong dressage enthusiast.

She especially valued family and creating new traditions shared with family and friends, including the celebration of St. Nicholas Day and annual trips to the National Storytelling Festival in Jonesborough, TN and to Kanuga in Hendersonville, NC.

A longtime member of Grace Church Cathedral, an Episcopal church in Charleston, she trained as a Godly Play teacher and developed the program there, teaching many children over the years. She became a certified Godly Play trainer, helping teachers throughout the Southeast learn and implement this Montessori-based approach to Christian education.

She was an active member of the American Medical Association Auxiliary, serving as president of the Charleston chapter and as a delegate to the national convention in Chicago.

Broughton is survived by her husband of 56 years, Dr. Harry Wade Boatwright, and her daughters, Dr. Sarah Wade Boatwright of Charleston and Anna Broughton Boatwright of Denver, CO. Her first grandchild, Lila Broughton Teague, was just born this October. She was preceded in death by her father, Alfred Blake Harwell, her mother, Elsie Cornelia Broughton, and her brothers, Benjamin Blake Harwell and William Elliot Harwell.

A memorial service will be held at Grace Church Cathedral on December 12, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. A celebration of her life will follow in the fellowship hall. In lieu of flowers, memorials may be made to Grace Church Cathedral, 98 Wentworth St, Charleston, SC 29401.
