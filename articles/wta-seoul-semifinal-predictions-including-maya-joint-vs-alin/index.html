WTA Seoul Semifinal Predictions Including Maya Joint vs Alina Korneeva - Last Word On Tennis

TheTools · 26.09.2026 06:58 · source · permalink

It is semifinal day at WTA Seoul, as players look to book their spot in the final this year. After an almost completely washed-out day yesterday, most of the quarterfinal action was also played earlier today, making it crucial to see how the players manage any potential fatigue in their second match of the day. As always, we here at LWOT will be predicting every match for you, but who do you think will win?

WTA Seoul Semifinal Predictions

Anna Bondar vs Kimberly Birrell

Head-to-head: Bondar 1-2 Birrell

This WTA Seoul matchup features two players who predominantly like to implement their more physically imposing baseline game, as Anna Bondar takes on Kimberly Birrell. It has been a tightly contested week for Bondar so far, as she has had to win every match in three sets to reach this stage, while Birrell, who was fortunate enough to play much of her quarterfinal yesterday, has dropped just one set so far this tournament.

In what should be a match played out from the back of the court, I expect that difference in minutes spent on court to ultimately show. Both of these players rely more on point construction and grinding out results than raw power, and in that case, the fresher player should have the edge.

Prediction: Birrell in 3

Maya Joint vs Alina Korneeva

Head-to-head: Joint 0-1 Korneeva

It had been a season from hell for Maya Joint in the first six months of the year, as the Aussie, coming into 2026 on the back of a positive 2025 season, struggled to win matches, going on a lengthy losing streak. Joint has recently shown some glimpses of her better tennis, which in part have enabled her to reach the semifinals here in Seoul, where she will be taking on the talented Alina Korneeva.

The Russian player, on the back of her court coverage and suffocating baseline style, has done extremely well so far this WTA Seoul tournament as she looks to win her first WTA Tour title this week. This match should come down to which player is more solid from the back of the court, as Joint will want to counterpunch and be the aggressor at times, while Korneeva will look to make the match physical and grind out points if need be. Korneeva has shown far more consistency so far this year, and if she plays her game, I can see her edging this match to reach the final.

Prediction: Korneeva in 3
