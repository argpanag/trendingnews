2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid Preview: Redesign Info & Release Date

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:50 · source · permalink

Late 2028 (Estimated)

TBA

  • Toyota is gearing up for a redesign.
  • Updated powertrain is expected.
  • Official specs haven't been confirmed.

What To Expect

Toyota’s smallest and most affordable hybrid SUV, the Corolla Cross Hybrid, is expected to receive a full redesign for the 2029 model year. Automotive News reports that the Corolla Cross Hybrid is scheduled to be redesigned for 2029, bringing an end to the SUV’s first generation, which was launched in 2022. Here’s what we expect to see from the 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid. 

The 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid is expected to arrive with a new design compared to the current model on sale. Toyota has not shared any images of the upcoming subcompact SUV, so we don’t know what kind of changes are in store for the 2029 Corolla Cross. We expect to see new front and rear fascias, updated bumpers, new lighting elements, a new grille design, and new wheel options. The SUV could also arrive with a larger body and grow in size. 

Toyota is expected to overhaul the Corolla Cross Hybrid SUV’s cabin for 2029. The redesigned SUV should arrive with a new dashboard design, updated tech features, new interior controls, revised materials, and new convenience features. Since most vehicles grow in size with a redesign, passenger and cargo space could increase with the 2029 model. 

We expect the 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid to come with more advanced tech features than the current model on sale. The current Corolla Cross Hybrid comes standard with an eight-inch touchscreen, a seven-inch digital instrument cluster, six audio speakers, two USB ports, Bluetooth, and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.

2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid Engine & Specs

The 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid is expected to have an updated powertrain that could result in an increase in power and fuel economy. Specifics for the 2029 model year have not been announced yet. We could see the Corolla Cross Hybrid become the standard and only version of the subcompact SUV available, which is something Toyota did with the RAV4 and Camry. 

The current model comes with a 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine that’s paired with two electric motors and a continuously variable automatic transmission. All-wheel drive is standard. 

2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid Safety

The 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid should have an updated list of standard driver-assist safety features. The current model comes with traffic sign recognition, a rear seat alert, adaptive cruise control, Toyota’s Proactive Driving Assist system, lane departure warning, forward collision warning, automatic high-beam assist, lane keeping assist, and automatic emergency braking as standard equipment. 

2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid Price

Official pricing for the 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid has not been announced yet. A fully redesigned model is expected to be more expensive than the current-gen SUV that’s on sale.

2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid Release Date

The redesigned 2029 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid is expected to go on sale before the end of 2028 or in early 2029. More information on the subcompact SUV should become available within the next year.
