How to Watch Clippers vs. Rockets for Free: TV Channels, Live Stream Options - AOL

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:20 · source · permalink

Kevin Durant and the Houston Rockets host Darius Garland and the Los Angeles Clippers on Monday during the 2026-27 NBA regular season.

Houston enters the matchup with a 12-5 record, sitting in second place in the Southwest Division of the Western Conference. The Rockets lost their most recent game against Nikola Jokic and the Denver Nuggets, 133-124, on Friday. Do-it-all center Alperen Sengun leads the team in scoring at 22.1 points per game, rebounding at 9.3 and assists at 7.0.
