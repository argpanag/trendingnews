Johor Darul Ta'zim vs Buriram United Live Streaming: When And Where to Watch AFC Champions League Elite Live in India, Malaysia, Singapore, US, USA And More

TheTools · 15.09.2026 11:50 · source · permalink

Southeast Asian club football witnesses its biggest rivalry reignited on Tuesday night as Malaysian serial champions Johor Darul Ta’zim host Thai powerhouse Buriram United in their opening fixture of the 2026/27 AFC Champions League Elite. The high-voltage East Zone clash is scheduled for Tuesday, September 15, 2026, kicking off at 8:15 PM local time (5:45 PM IST) inside a packed Sultan Ibrahim Stadium in Iskandar Puteri, Johor.

With both sides fighting for regional dominance and crucial early points in Asia’s revamped 12-team league stage format, the stakes could not be higher. JDT will look to use their sold-out home fortress, relentless pressing, and sharp attacking firepower to settle scores after intense past meetings, while Buriram plans to counter with rigid defensive discipline and swift breaks on the counter. Here is the complete live broadcast and streaming guide for supporters tuning in across India, Southeast Asia, and worldwide.

Match Details

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in India?

Fans in India can watch the live stream of the match exclusively on the FanCode app and website with an affordable match pass or an active subscription. There is no live television broadcast scheduled for Indian TV networks.

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in Malaysia?

In Malaysia, television viewers can catch the action live on Astro Arena and Astro SuperSport, while online streaming is available via Astro GO and the Sooka app.

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in Singapore?

Supporters in Singapore can follow the live match stream through Mediacorp’s mewatch platform as well as Singtel TV-GO across compatible devices.

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in the US and USA?

Fans based in the United States can stream the game live on Paramount+, with supplemental coverage and analysis on the CBS Sports Golazo Network.

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in Thailand?

In Thailand, the live match will be accessible online via the BG Sports YouTube channel, alongside a linear television broadcast on the TrueVisions network.

Where to Watch Johor Darul Ta’zim vs Buriram United Live in the UK and Rest of the World?

For territories without a localized broadcast deal, the game will be streamed live and free on the official AFC Hub YouTube channel and the Asian Football Confederation’s digital match center.
