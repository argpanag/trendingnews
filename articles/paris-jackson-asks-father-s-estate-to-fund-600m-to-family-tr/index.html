Paris Jackson Asks Father’s Estate to Fund $600M to Family Trust

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:34 · source · permalink

Advertisement

Supported by

A representative for Michael Jackson’s daughter accused the executors of her father’s estate of incompetence, financial mismanagement and delays in distributing his wealth to his children.

Paris Jackson, the daughter of Michael Jackson, has petitioned a court to compel the executor of her father’s estate to distribute $600 million to a family trust for her and her two brothers, accusing the estate’s managers of incompetence and financial mismanagement.

The petition, filed on Oct. 29 in a state court in Los Angeles, is only from Paris Jackson, 28. Her brothers, Prince Jackson, 29, and Bigi Jackson, 24, are not named.

The 14-page document claims that more than 17 years after their father died, the family trust remains unfunded. The filing was made more than six months after “Michael,” the contentious biopic about the performer’s life and early career, was released in theaters worldwide, earning more than $1 billion at the global box office.

Michael, according to the petition, instructed that each child should begin receiving income from their respective trust shares when they turned 21. More would be distributed at the age of 30 and again at 40. It was not clear from the petition how much each child was supposed to receive when they turned 21.

The estate said that more than $200 million has already been distributed to benefit Paris Jackson and other beneficiaries.

According to the petition, the executor failed to take action, and it asks the court to order a “reasonable, partial distribution” to the family trust. After Michael Jackson died at age 50 in 2009, a court appointed John Branca and John McClain coexecutors of the estate. McClain died in May.
