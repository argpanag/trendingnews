PHI starts Maxey, KCP, Brown, James, Wade

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:57 · source · permalink

    Kentavious Caldwell-Pope

    PHI Guard #1
    Player Stats

    Tyrese Maxey, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Jaylen Brown, LeBron James and Dean Wade will start Sunday’s game against the Pistons.

    The 76ers won’t have VJ Edgecombe or Joel Embiid for Sunday’s game, resulting in two changes to the starting lineup. Caldwell-Pope will replace Edgecombe, marking his first start of the season. The other change is James, who returns after sitting out Saturday’s win over the Bucks. He’ll fill the void left by Embiid.
