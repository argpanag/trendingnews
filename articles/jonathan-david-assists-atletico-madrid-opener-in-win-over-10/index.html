Jonathan David assists Atletico Madrid opener in win over 10-man Alaves

Diego Simeone only gave Canada star Jonathan David 45 minutes away to Deportivo Alavés on Saturday afternoon. He nearly regretted it before Atletico Madrid scored late to earn a 2-1 win.

David continued his fine form with a first half assist, slipping the ball behind the backline for Grimaldo to run onto and power past the goalkeeper. The one-touch pass from the Canadian couldn’t have been any better as the visitors took a 1-0 lead in the 33rd minute. They couldn’t hold onto that lead going into the break, conceding a penalty six into stoppage time, scored by Lucas Boyé.

Simeone decided to shake up his attacking unit at halftime, bringing David and Ademola Lookman off at the interval, replaced by Álex Baena and Julián Alvarez. Baena ended up assisting the winning goal for Atleti, serving up a cross for Cristian Romero to head in from a couple of yards out with three minutes left in the 90.

The win was relief for an Atleti side that played the last 30 minutes up a man. Ángel Pérez was originally given a yellow card for a challenge on Alvarez but the Video Assistant Referee had a look and ejected him with an upgrade to a straight red card. Atleti outshot Alavés 22-8 and 7-3 for shots on target, so a 2-1 result is a bit underwhelming.

Still, a win is a win. They are up to second place now with 19 points through eight games — a 6-1-1 record. Real Madrid and Real Betis, are yet to play this weekend still.

David’s loan move to Spain has worked out incredibly well so far. After continuing his fine form into the international break with Canada over the past couple of weeks, he had a goal contribution for the fourth straight LaLiga match.

David had a goal in each of his three LaLiga appearances so far, including the winner against Spanish giants Real Madrid in the last game before the international break. David also scored against Real Sociedad and had a goal and an assist against Osasuna a few days later. His scoring streak reached four games with Canada’s only goal in a 1-0 win over Chile on Sept. 26, but David was held off the board against Peru and the United States and again on Saturday.

A September Player of the Month nominee in LaLiga, it’s safe to say David is enjoying his time in Spain so far a lot more than his final weeks at Juventus.

Alavés are off to a good start this season as well despite Saturday’s setback. After finishing 14th last season they are up to sixth to begin the new campaign with three wins, two draws and three losses so far. That leaves them 10 points back of 7-0-0 Barcelona, but they were never going to be title contenders. Canadian goalkeeper Grégoire Świderski did not even make the bench for Alavés after serving as the backup once this season but mostly featuring for the B team.

Hanging with the big boys so far is impressive enough — including beating Villarreal 1-0 in August in a match that Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi both featured in, but Nathan Saliba did not debut in that game after his move from Anderlecht.

Saliba, like David, has been turning heads every week for club and country since heading to greener pastures in Spain this summer. Villarreal plays Saturday against Real Madrid, but Buchanan will miss out with a hamstring injury.
