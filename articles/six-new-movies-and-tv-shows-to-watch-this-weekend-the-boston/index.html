Six new movies and TV shows to watch this weekend - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:51 · source · permalink

There are several new titles streaming this weekend with ties to the Bay State, including a romance tale set in Massachusetts and a Boston-themed season of a hit reality series. Here’s what to watch.

New movies to stream

‘Sacrifice’

Before his big return as Steve Rogers in “Avengers: Doomsday” later this year, Sudbury native Chris Evans lends his star power to the new comedy “Sacrifice,” which hits Netflix on Friday. The film revolves around a gala filled with billionaires and celebrities, with Evans playing a movie star who becomes the hostage of a radical activist played by Anya Taylor-Joy. The star-studded cast includes John Malkovich, Salma Hayek-Pinault, Vincent Cassel, Charli XCX, and Jonatan “Yung Lean” Leandoer, marking the rapper’s film debut. Leandoer also reunites with director Romain Gavras, who directed the viral music video for “Storm,” the rapper’s hit song with Gener8ion. Available on Netflix

‘The Death of Robin Hood’

Hugh Jackman stars as the titular archer in “The Death of Robin Hood,” streaming Friday on HBO Max. As in his sendoff to an old man Wolverine in “Logan,” Jackman plays a past-his-prime version of Robin Hood who’s injured on the battlefield. The film also stars Jodie Comer and Bill Skarsgård. Available on HBO Max

New TV to stream

‘The Diplomat’

Keri Russell returns for a new season of “The Diplomat,” streaming Friday on Netflix. The drama’s latest installment finds Russell’s ambassador Kate Wyler dealing with the fallout of a catastrophe that upends a delicate time of peace between America and the UK. Rufus Sewell, Allison Janney, and Bradley Whitford are also all back for season 4. Available on Netflix

‘Tulsa King’

Another hit show kicking off its fourth season this weekend is Taylor Sheridan’s “Tulsa King,” which returns to Paramount+ on Friday. Sylvester Stallone’s Dwight Manfredi finds his crime empire under attack this season, with the mobster forced to “protect his crew as corrupt politicians, dangerous new enemies, and a crushing personal loss hit from every direction,” according to a press release. Available on Paramount+

Binge-worthy TV pick

‘Love Is Blind’

In case you missed it, the Boston season of “Love is Blind” finally arrived on Netflix this week. Fans can binge-watch the first five episodes now, and be sure to stay up to date on all the drama in the Hub throughout the season with the Globe’s “Love Is Blind” tracker page. Available on Netflix

Movie night pick

‘Love of Your Life’

The Boston and Cambridge area serves as the core setting for the new romance flick “Love of Your Life,” streaming Friday on Prime Video. Directed by Cambridge native Rachel Morrison, the film stars Margaret Qualley as Maya, a woman dealing with a devastating loss and struggling to love again. “Love of Your Life” also stars Aaron Pierre, Patrick Schwarzenegger, Gabriel Basso, and Catherine Keener. Available on Prime Video
