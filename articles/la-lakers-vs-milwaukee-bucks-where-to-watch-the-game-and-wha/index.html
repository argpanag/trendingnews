LA Lakers vs Milwaukee Bucks: Where to watch the game and what time the opening tip will be at Fiserv Forum

TheTools · 11.11.2026 16:24 · source · permalink

League-leading Lakers visit a struggling Bucks team; Luka and company look to remain unstoppable at Fiserv Forum

  • Los Angeles Lakers.  "I've never seen an NBA champion whose three stars were white, and it's not going to happen this year"

The NBA schedule gives us a clash of opposite realities. The Los Angeles Lakers, the best team in the league by record, arrive in Milwaukee to face the Milwaukee Bucks, who are going through one of the worst starts in the entire competition.

On paper it looks like a blowout; on the hardwood, the pride of a wounded team in its own home always deserves attention.

LA Lakers vs Milwaukee Bucks: Time and where to watch the game

The matchup between Milwaukee and Los Angeles will be played this Wednesday, November 12, 2026, at Milwaukee's Fiserv Forum, a venue with a capacity of 17,500 fans. The game's start time will be as follows for each time zone:

  • Eastern Time: 8:00 p.m.
  • Central Time: 7:00 p.m.
  • Pacific Time: 5:00 p.m.

The local broadcast will be available on the Bucks App in the Milwaukee market, while Los Angeles fans will be able to follow it through Spectrum Sports Net and Spectrum Sports Net +. Nationally, the NBA will broadcast the game through Amazon Prime Video and NBA League Pass.

The Lakers vs. Bucks landscape: heaven and hell on the same court

The numbers tell the story on their own. The Lakers rule the Western Conference with a record of 10 wins and 2 losses, the best mark in the entire NBA, even surpassing the Philadelphia 76ers and the San Antonio Spurs in the overall standings. With 1,465 points scored and a +92 point differential, Los Angeles' offense is a steamroller.

On the other side, the Bucks are living a nightmare. With only 1 win and 9 losses, they sit at the bottom of the Central Division and in the third-worst spot among the league's 30 teams, ahead of only the Sacramento Kings and the Utah Jazz. Their -132 differential reveals a deep-rooted defensive problem: they have allowed 1,216 points in just ten games.

The Lakers' streak is devastating. They have won nine of their last ten games, including statement victories such as the 138-117 over the Miami Heat and gritty wins over direct rivals: 121-116 on the road against the Detroit Pistons and, on that same November 10, a 124-114 victory on the Atlanta Hawks' home court.

Their only recent blemish was a 136-129 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers. They come in hot and used to winning away from Crypto.com Arena.

Milwaukee, meanwhile, is carrying a deep wound: it comes in with eight losses in its last nine games. The most painful was a 97-117 defeat on its own court against the Boston Celtics on November 9, just days after also falling to those same Celtics (109-102) on the road.

The only respite was a 110-102 win over the Indiana Pacers in late October. Fiserv Forum, which should be a fortress, has turned into the scene of blowouts: 136 points allowed against the Cleveland Cavaliers, 132 against the Houston Rockets.

The players to watch in the Los Angeles and Milwaukee lineups

The main attraction is, without a doubt, Luka Dončić. The 27-year-old Slovenian leads the Los Angeles project and is the compass of a team that keeps racking up wins. Alongside him, Austin Reaves provides steady production from the perimeter, and Collin Sexton adds scoring energy whether coming off the bench or joining the starting five.

In the paint, the Lakers boast rotation with Walker Kessler, Deandre… —or rather, with pieces like Kevon Looney and Jarred Vanderbilt who give them muscle and rebounding.

Milwaukee does not lack talent, though it does lack results. Tyler Herro leads the backcourt, and Myles Turner must anchor a leaky defense. Kyle Kuzma and the young Jaime Jaquez Jr. round out a group that urgently needs to find its identity.

The challenge for the Bucks is to slow down the breakneck pace at which Los Angeles plays and to stop giving away points in transition.

What's at stake in the NBA standings

For the Lakers, every victory is worth gold in a brutally tight Western Conference: the San Antonio Spurs, Oklahoma City Thunder, Golden State Warriors, Houston Rockets, and Minnesota Timberwolves are lurking just a game and a half behind. Staying on top requires not relaxing against any opponent, no matter how low they may be.

Milwaukee, meanwhile, is fighting a different battle: avoiding sinking completely to the bottom of the East, where the Chicago Bulls are its immediate neighbor. Every loss brings them closer to the absolute bottom of the league and further from any aspirations of play-in spots.

What is the prediction for the LA Lakers vs Milwaukee Bucks?

The logic is overwhelming: the Lakers are the best team in the NBA by record and arrive in superb form, while the Bucks are one of the hardest-hit teams in the league.

We expect an away win, probably a comfortable one, if Milwaukee fails to turn the support of the Fiserv Forum into defensive intensity from the first quarter. The Bucks' only path is to turn the game into a physical, slow-paced battle and trust that the talent of Herro and Turner keeps them within striking distance until the final period.

On a neutral court or in any scenario, the balance clearly tilts toward Los Angeles.
