Saoirse Ronan's husband Jack Lowden received 'gorgeous' email from Mr Darcy actors

TheTools · 27.11.2026 14:14 · source · permalink

Jack Lowden has said he received a 'gorgeous' joint email from former Mr Darcy actors Colin Firth and Matthew Macfadyen after securing the Pride And Prejudice role.

The Scottish actor, 36, who is married to Irish star Saoirse Ronan, stars in the upcoming Dolly Alderton Netflix series as the reserved love interest to Emma Corrin’s Elizabeth Bennet.

The role was previously portrayed by Firth in the 1995 series adaptation and later by Macfadyen in the 2005 film.

Speaking on the Graham Norton Show, Jack said the pair reached out after he got the role.

He said: 'They sent me a joint email which was titled, ‘The audacity sir’. It was gorgeous, and they went on to give me so much support. They were lovely.'

The series is based on the Jane Austen novel and also stars The Crown actress Olivia Colman and Enola Holmes actor Louis Partridge.

Lowden said it was an 'honour' to take on the role and that the new adaptation is 'incredibly faithful to the book and the script is funny and beautiful.'

Speaking about his interpretation of Mr Darcy, Jack said: 'Colin Firth’s and Matthew Macfadyen’s portrayals of Darcy are so different from each other that I thought if I watched them enough, I would end up somewhere in the middle.

'Hopefully that’s what I have done. It was an honour being asked to follow in those footsteps.'

Jack is best known for starring in the hit Apple TV spy thriller Slow Horses as River Cartwright, a British MI5 agent who in the first season was exiled to Slough House, a dumping ground for agents who made serious mistakes.

The team is led by veteran agent Jackson Lamb, played by Sir Gary Oldman, who frequently clashes with the calculating Diana Taverner, played by Dame Kristin Scott Thomas, who takes over as head of Britain’s MI5 secret service in season six.

Lowden said: 'I love sneaking in to watch the other actors, particularly Kristin Scott Thomas and Gary Oldman.

'I particularly love watching such great actors making mistakes so I can see how they fix problems. Gary and Kristin have different methods which sometimes collide.'

'They don’t seem to sneak in when I am acting!' he added jokingly.

The Graham Norton Show airs on BBC iPlayer and BBC One on Friday at 10.40pm.
