Family Guy released the trailer for its 2026 Christmas special, which centers on Santa Claus. The long-running show's holiday-themed episode features the kind of gags fans would expect, and the story centers on an audition to become a mall Santa. Peter and his friends show up, but Peter becomes upset when the job goes to Cleveland instead.

The trailer's description reads, "May the Best Santa Win," pointing towards the story that the episode is going to follow. In addition to Peter and Cleveland's rivalry, the episode will feature Brian, Stewie, and Chris in a subplot involving Christmas carols.

What happens in the Family Guy 2026 Holiday Special trailer?

The Family Guy 2026 Holiday Special trailer opens with Cleveland and Peter coming across a brochure for Santa Claus auditions at the Quahog Town Mall. Several people show up for the audition, including Peter, Cleveland, Quagmire, and Joe. When the mall supervisor makes it clear that background checks will be conducted, Quagmire quickly withdraws from the running.

The trailer then cuts to the Griffin household, where a dejected Peter tells his family that he lost the Santa audition after Cleveland gets the job. Lois couldn't care less and instead puts on her earbuds to listen to a podcast. Cleveland takes his new role as Santa seriously, making Peter even more envious. As Peter's jealousy continues to grow, Stewie tries to get Brian to sing "Santa Claus is coming to Town." However, all the slightly obese dog does is fall over and fart, after seemingly eating a piece of chocolate cake.

While Peter sets out to show the town why he deserves to be Santa more than Cleveland, Chris comments on Stewie's choice of song, saying that he opens his carols with Jingle Bells, specifically the Batman version.

The special is scheduled to air on November 30, 2026.
