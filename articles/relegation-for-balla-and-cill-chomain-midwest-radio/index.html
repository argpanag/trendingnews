Balla have been relegated to intermediate level from the Connacht Gold Mayo Senior Football Championship following defeat to Ballaghadereen in today’s relegation final on a scoreline of 0-14/1-13.

Cill Chomain will also move down a tier, playing their football for 2027 in the Treenlaur Catering Exclusive Junior Championship. They lost out to Louisburgh today by 0-08/0-20.

Reports to come on MWR’s Sports Wrap-Up Show from 8pm. 

Games take place on October 10th and 11th.

They beat Islandeady earlier this afternoon.

A majority of the players have voted to play the game.

The duo won Gold in their respective categories in the European Championships this week.

They only trailed by two points with 5 minutes remaining
