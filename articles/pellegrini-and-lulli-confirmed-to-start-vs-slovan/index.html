Pellegrini and Lulli confirmed to start vs Slovan

TheTools · 20.10.2026 06:51 · source · permalink

Roma are back in the hunt for a Champions League victory, with Malen ready to reclaim the center of attack.

After the draw against Fenerbahce and the bitter defeat against Real Madrid, the Giallorossi host Yaya Touré’s Slovan Bratislava tonight.

Kick-off is at 9:00 PM at the Olimpico, with Gasperini aiming for his first three points of the competition.

The coach has Malen back, ready to lead the attack, while behind him, Soulé is expected to remain and Pellegrini will return to the starting lineup. Ndicka may be rested, with Balerdi at the center of defense.

The only doubt in the newspapers’ predictions concerns the right flank, where Lulli is favored over Molina.
