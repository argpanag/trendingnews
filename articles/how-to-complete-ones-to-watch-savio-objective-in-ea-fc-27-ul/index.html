How To Complete Ones To Watch Savio Objective In EA FC 27 Ultimate Team

TheTools · 17.09.2026 16:41 · source · permalink

Ones to Watch is the first campaign of EA FC 27 Ultimate Team, with a host of upgraded cards on offer to kick off the latest release. Whilst a bunch of the cards are available in the Season Pass, a select few are tucked away in the Objectives tabs, including the likes of Tottenham Hotspur’s Savio.

Given that the theme of the campaign is players worth keeping an eye on as the season progresses, Savio is a great inclusion, as the ex-Manchester City winger has been sought after by Spurs for quite some time. Whether he finds success at his new club remains to be seen, but he could be a huge boost for your Ultimate Team—if you know how to get him.

With that in mind, here’s how to complete the Ones To Watch Savio Objective in EA FC 27 Ultimate Team.

How To Complete Ones To Watch Savio

If you want to get a hold of the Ones to Watch Savio card, you’ll need to complete the entire Ones We Watched Challenge Completionist Objective set.

That group has four Objectives that you’ll need to complete, and before long, you’ll realise that each one is tied to yet another batch of Objectives! It might seem a little confusing at first, but all you need to do is take these challenges one at a time and head over to their respective bracket to complete them first.

Every Ones We Watched Objective Set In EA FC 27 Ultimate Team

You’ll need to partake in the offline Ones We Watched challenges if you’re after the Savio card.

You’ll notice that each one is tied to a different difficulty rating, with the final one being Legendary. So, you’ll need to be pretty decent at the game to even stand a chance at earning the Savio card, as you’ll need to win four games in that game mode alone!
