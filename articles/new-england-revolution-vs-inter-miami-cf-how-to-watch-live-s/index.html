Match Preview

New England Revolution host Inter Miami CF at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough in a Major League Soccer fixture that carries genuine weight for both sides as the regular season enters its final stretch.

The Revolution arrive in outstanding form, having won four of their last five league matches. Marko Mitrovic's side beat Red Bull New York 3-2 in their most recent outing and sit second in the Eastern Conference, firmly in contention for a top playoff seed.

Inter Miami come into this fixture having drawn 2-2 with FC Cincinnati in their last match, a game in which Luis Suarez opened the scoring before Cincinnati turned the game around, only for Riquelme Fillipi to equalise in the 90th minute. It was a result that followed a 2-0 defeat to Red Bull New York the week prior.

Messi was booked in that Cincinnati draw and had a quieter night overall, though coach Kily Gonzalez will be encouraged that his side showed character to rescue a point late on. The Argentine captain remains central to everything Miami do going forward.

With Inter Miami sitting third in the Eastern Conference on 55 points, the gap between these two sides in the standings is slim. A win for either team here would have a meaningful impact on the final playoff seedings.

This is a fixture between two teams with contrasting momentum. Revolution are surging, Miami are steadying. Something has to give at Gillette Stadium.
