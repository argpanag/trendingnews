The San Francisco 49ers defend their perfect record against the Washington Commanders on Monday night.

After kicking off their year 5-0, the San Francisco 49ers odds of winning the NFC West has skyrocketed while possessing a two game lead for first in the division standings.

Luckily for the 49ers, they will have a great opportunity to build on their momentum against the Washington Commanders by picking apart a defense that struggles in coverage at every level of the field.

Even though there is just one game taking place on Monday night, there are still a wide range of prop bets and markets to groom through which you can read a detailed breakdown of them below.

Best betting promos and sportsbooks for NFL prop bets

Find the best promos and sportsbooks to use for today’s Football prop bets.

Washington Commanders vs San Francisco 49ers (8:15pm, EST)

Brock Purdy Over 248.5 Passing Yards (-113) at Caesars Sportsbook

Since the start of the regular season, Brock Purdy has built a strong case for winning the MVP award, leading the position in EPA per play while running an offense that ranks first in DVOA, EPA, and in Success Rate.

With San Francisco’s star studded offense firing on all cylinders, the 49ers are able to avoid playing in high variance contests, increasing their chances of winning on a weekly basis.

In a favorable matchup against the Washington Commanders, Purdy will be able to continue to stuff the stat sheet by throwing over the top of a secondary that struggles to defend against the pass per bottom ten marks in Def Pass EPA Allowed per play.

The protection Purdy receives from his offensive line will also help improve the quality of his throws while combatting against less pressure, operating behind a front five that resides near the top of the board in Pass Block Grade.

Brock Purdy Longest Completion Over 37.5 Yards (-108) at FanDuel Sportsbook

With Purdy allowed to go through his progressions, the 49ers pass catchers will have more time in the open field to break away from their assignments, reducing the amount of havoc in his throws.

The Commanders inability to keep a defender on the 49ers pass catchers hips will also make it difficult to create a disruption, improving Purdy’s probability of creating an explosive play.

Christian McCaffrey Under 61.5 Rushing Yards (-111) at Caesars Sportsbook

Even when surrounded by one of the most talented group of wideouts in the league, Christian McCaffrey has been unable to take advantage of the extra space in the middle, ranking below league average in Percentage of Carries Exceeding Expected Yards.

To help keep the 49ers dynamic, McCaffrey is able to make up for it through the air, averaging 7.2 Yards per Reception.

Unfortunately for McCaffrey, his struggles on the ground are poised to persist against the Commanders as he faces off against a front seven who stuffs the run at a high rate while ranking 7th in Def Rush EPA Allowed per play.

For some more exposure, escalator bet McCaffrey’s Rushing Yardage prop by placing smaller wagers on his alternate unders at the best available odds across multiple sportsbooks.

George Kittle Anytime Touchdown Scorer (+105) at BetMGM Sportsbook

When at full health, George Kittle has been dominant inside the 20, converting 50% of his Red Zone Receptions into Touchdowns while leading the team in Red Zone Targets.

With the Commanders forced to anchor their linebackers in the box, Kittle will have more room to work with in the end zone, widening the width of Purdy’s passing lanes to help haul in a touchdown.

Jayden Daniels Under 19.5 Pass Completions (-125) at BetMGM Sportsbook

It has been a turbulent start to the year for Jayden Daniels, limping into Week 6 ranked 21st in Success Rate while completing just 60.5% of his passes.

While Daniels is able to gash opposing front sevens with his legs, his over reliance on his running ability makes it difficult to stretch out the secondary, forcing the Commanders quarterback to weave the ball through tight windows.

Heading into Monday night’s contest against the 49ers, expect Daniels to continue to struggle to connect with his pass catchers while trying to attack a secondary that ranks in the top half of the board in Def Pass DVOA and in Success Rate.

Especially with the 49ers consistently collapsing the pocket, forcing Daniels to scramble to the outside which will trade Pass Attempts for Carries.
