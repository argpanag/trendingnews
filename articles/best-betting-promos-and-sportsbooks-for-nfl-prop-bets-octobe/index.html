The Detroit Lions hit the road to battle it out against the Carolina Panthers on Sunday night.

Even though the Detroit Lions are 2-1, they are flirting with danger while ranking near dead last in most defensive metrics.

Heading into Sunday night’s contest against the Carolina Panthers, the Lions struggles on defense will be on full display while facing off against an offense that has surprisingly been efficient three games into the season.

Even though there is only one game taking the field on Sunday night, there are still plenty of prop bets and markets to wager on which you can read a detailed breakdown of them below.

Best betting promos and sportsbooks for NFL prop bets

Find the best promos and sportsbooks to use for today’s Football prop bets.

Detroit Lions vs Carolina Panthers (8:20pm, EST)

Bryce Young Over 246.5 Passing Yards (-115) at BetMGM Sportsbook

After struggling to find his footing earlier in his career, Bryce Young has been able to round back into competitive form this season, climbing into the top-15 in EPA per play and in CPOE while averaging 9.6 Air Yards.

Young’s resurgence under center has helped elevate the Carolina Panthers offense as a whole, leading a group that ranks above league average in Off DVOA.

In a highly anticipated matchup against the Detroit Lions, Young will be able to build on his momentum by picking apart a secondary that has severely underwhelmed in coverage per their bottom five marks in Def Pass EPA and in Dropback Success Rate.

With the Lions struggling to keep a defender near the vicinity of the catch, the Panthers pass catchers will be able to generate explosive plays at a higher rate to help Young clear the over on his Passing Yardage prop.

Bryce Young Over 34.5 Pass Attempts (-110) at BetMGM Sportsbook

The Lions inability to break through the trenches will also reduce the amount of havoc in Young’s throws, making it easier to connect with his pass catchers and keep their drives alive.

With the Panthers expected to play from behind per the current spread, Young will be forced to air out the ball at a higher rate to keep pace with Detroit, leading to an uptick in his total number of Pass Attempts.

Chuba Hubbard Over 66.5 Rushing Yards (-114) at FanDuel Sportsbook

Since the start of the regular season, Chuba Hubbard has been able to play at a high level, ranking near the top of the board in Yards Over Expected per Attempt while averaging 4.5 Yards per Carry.

The protection Hubbard has received from his offensive line have played a major role in his success this season by widening the width of his running lanes in the trenches, fielding a front five that ranks fifth in Run Block Grade.

With Young stretching out the secondary, Hubbard will have more room to work with once he reaches the second level of the defense, leading to a higher volume of Yards After Contact.

For some more exposure, escalator bet Hubbard’s Rushing Yardage prop by placing smaller wagers on his alternate overs at the best available odds across multiple sportsbooks.

Chuba Hubbard Anytime Touchdown Scorer (-125) at FanDuel Sportsbook

When the Panthers are inside the 20, they have leaned heavily on Hubbard to cap off the drive, leading the team in Red Zone Rush Attempts with a 66.7% usage rate while turning both of his Targets into touchdowns.

With the Panthers expected to get into scoring position at a higher rate, Hubbard will receive a heavier volume of Carries, improving his probability of scoring.

Jahmyr Gibbs Longest Rush Over 18.5 Yards (-115) at BetMGM Sportsbook

On the other side of the field, Jahmyr Gibbs has been dominant on the ground, averaging over 100 Rushing Yards per game. Even though Gibbs has been able to keep the Lions ahead of the sticks, he has actually struggled to create explosive plays, generating just one big gain in three outings.

Fortunately for Gibbs, he will have a great opportunity to flip the field position in a flash against the Panthers by taking advantage of a front seven that ranks in the bottom half of the board in Def Rush DVOA.

If you want to diversify your risk on the Lions star running back, place a separate wager on Gibbs to clear the over of 135.5 on his Rush+Receiving Yardage prop as well.
