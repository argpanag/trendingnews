JJ Gabriel confirms Manchester United exit: ‘You gave me moments I will always treasure’

  • Gabriel expected to join Real Madrid

Teenager says ‘thank you for everything’ in video

Gabriel expected to join Real Madrid

JJ Gabriel has announced his departure from Manchester United by posting a farewell message to the club on social media.

The teenager, who has not played a competitive game for the first team, joined as a 10-year-old in 2020 but has been absent since he requested the cancellation of his registration in September before he turned 16, on 6 October.

It has been widely reported that he will join Real Madrid and while he made no mention of his intended destination, his goodbye video on Instagram appears to have taken him closer to a move to the Spanish giants.

Gabriel said in the video: “Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams. Here I grew as a player and a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award.

“You gave me moments I will always treasure. To every coach and every member of staff, to my teammates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

“As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ.”

Gabriel had remained registered to United, who are understood to be ready to explore legal options against the youngster’s next club over a possible breach of regulations.

The 16-year-old is the son of former Republic of Ireland international, Joe O’Cearuill, and last season became the youngest winner of United’s Jimmy Murphy Award, for their young player of the year.

United had hoped to persuade the teenager to stay but it appears his hat-trick-scoring turn against Sabah on his Uefa Youth League debut, on 10 September, will be his final appearance for the club. Gabriel shone in that 5-0 victory although his representatives were disappointed he was not registered to play later that day in the Champions League fixture against the champions of Azerbaijan.

The teenager was due to appear in the Carabao Cup tie against Brighton the next week but had seemingly decided upon a new direction by that point. Gabriel would have become the club’s youngest player in a competitive match in that tie, having appeared off the bench for Michael Carrick’s side in an August friendly against Atlético Madrid.
