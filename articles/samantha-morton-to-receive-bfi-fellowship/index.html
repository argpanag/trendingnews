Samantha Morton To Receive BFI Fellowship

TheTools · 23.11.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

Breaking News

The UK’s leading film organization, the British Film Institute, is honouring actor Samantha Morton with its highest accolade: a BFI Fellowship.

The BFI said the honor recognizes Morton’s “extraordinary talent for bringing empathetic, raw and complex performances to her work.”

The BFI Fellowship will be presented to Morton at the BFI Chair’s Dinner on January 28. Later in 2027, BFI Southbank will host a retrospective of her decades-long career. The season will take place throughout June and include an in-conversation session with Morton.

Morton will be remembered most recently for her witchy turn in Chris Nolan’s The Odyssey. Some of her other credits include Sweet and Lowdown, Minority Report, The Walking Dead, She Said, Under the Skin, Mister Lonely, and The Serpent Queen. She has also directed one feature, 2009’s The Unloved.

Watch on Deadline

Morton described the BFI Fellowship as the “greatest honour and gift I could imagine” in a statement.

“From my first serious film role in Carine Adler’s Under the Skin to bringing Circe to life for Christopher Nolan in The Odyssey, a truly life-changing experience, the BFI has always been there, a guardian of my hopes as an artist,” she said, adding: “Film changes lives. It has mine.”

BFI Chair Jay Hunt added: “Samantha is an extraordinary creative force and one of the most fearless actors of her generation. From her roots in independent film to her scene-stealing appearance in one of the biggest films of the year, The Odyssey, Samantha has had a powerful impact on audiences around the world for over 30 years. Her commitment to supporting young people and facilitating their access to the arts makes this BFI Fellowship even more meaningful. She epitomises the very best of the screen sector.”

Previous BFI Fellows include David Lean, Bette Davis, Akira Kurosawa, Ousmane Sembène, Elizabeth Taylor, Michael Powell, Emeric Pressburger, Orson Welles, Thelma Schoonmaker, Derek Jarman, Vanessa Redgrave, Maggie Smith, Martin Scorsese, Judi Dench, Satyajit Ray, Yasujirō Ozu and, most recently, Tilda Swinton, Barbara Broccoli, Michael G Wilson, Spike Lee, Christopher Nolan, Tom Cruise, Guillermo del Toro and Laura Mulvey.
