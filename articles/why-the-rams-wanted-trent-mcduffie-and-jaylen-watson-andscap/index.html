Why the Rams wanted Trent McDuffie and Jaylen Watson - Andscape

TheTools · 21.09.2026 23:55 · source · permalink

Celebrating 10 years of Andscape

Former Kansas City Chiefs cornerbacks discuss move to Los Angeles, working with Sean McVay, Steve Spagnuolo

New Los Angeles Rams cornerbacks Trent McDuffie and Jaylen Watson join Andscape senior NFL writer Jason Reid to discuss their reunion in Los Angeles after their championship years with the Kansas City Chiefs.

Rams head coach Sean McVay breaks down the qualities he values in the pair, including physical coverage, run support and the ability to handle different assignments. The conversation also explores the accountability they learned under Chiefs defensive coordinator Steve Spagnuolo and their early impressions of the Rams’ culture.

Jason Reid is the senior NFL writer at Andscape. He enjoys watching sports, especially any games involving his son and daughter.
