Yellow rain warning for Wicklow and Wexford

TheTools · 27.09.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

Met Éireann has issued a Status Yellow rain warning for counties Wicklow and Wexford.

It warned of potential localised flooding, difficult travelling conditions and poor visibility.

The warning came into effect at 9pm and will last until 4am tomorrow morning.

Today's Status Yellow wind warning for 11 counties and a Status Yellow rain warning for Cork, Kerry and Waterford have both now expired.
