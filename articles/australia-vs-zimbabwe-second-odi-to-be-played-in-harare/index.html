Australia vs Zimbabwe: second ODI to be played in Harare

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:29 · source · permalink

According to ABC News Australia, Australia’s men’s national team will play the second of three One Day International (ODI) matches against Zimbabwe in Harare, Zimbabwe, on September 18. The match is the second game of the series after Australia’s victory in the opening encounter.

First match of the series

In the first match, held on September 14, Zimbabwe scored 235 runs. Australia finished the game at 8/294. Matt Renshaw scored his first ODI century and helped Australia win.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Australia is captained by Mitch Marsh, while Zimbabwe is led by Sikandar Raza. The ABC page also provides the score, statistics, scorecard and ball-by-ball timeline.

Series schedule

The third match of the series is scheduled for September 20, also in Harare. ABC’s schedule lists the second match for September 18 and the third for September 20. Both scheduled fixtures are to take place in Harare.
