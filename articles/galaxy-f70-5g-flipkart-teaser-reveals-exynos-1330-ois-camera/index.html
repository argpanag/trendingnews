Samsung’s upcoming Galaxy F70 5G has appeared on Flipkart, revealing several key specifications ahead of its launch in India. The retailer’s promotional material confirms an Exynos 1330 processor, a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), and a 5,000mAh battery.

Interestingly, the specifications also back up something we discovered earlier this month. The Galaxy F70 5G looks increasingly like a rebadged Galaxy A17 5G.

Galaxy F70 5G specifications revealed on Flipkart

The Flipkart promotional page gives us our first proper look at the Galaxy F70 5G, including its design and two color options, Vivid Violet and Bold Black. Samsung appears to be keeping things fairly familiar, with a triple rear camera setup arranged vertically and a traditional waterdrop notch at the front. The phone is pretty slim at 7.5mm.

The 50MP main camera features Samsung’s No Shake Cam branding, referring to the optical image stabilisation designed to reduce camera shake when capturing photos and videos. There’s also a 5MP ultrawide camera for fitting more into the frame and a 2MP macro camera for close-up photography. There’s a 13MP selfie camera on the front.

Additionally, Flipkart highlights the phone’s Super AMOLED display, alongside dust and splash resistance and Corning Gorilla Glass Victus protection. However, the promotional material doesn’t specify an exact IP rating, screen size, or refresh rate.

The Galaxy F70 5G is powered by Samsung’s Exynos 1330 processor. A 5,000mAh battery fuels the phone, with support for 25W wired charging. Other advertised features include Circle to Search with Google, Gemini, Samsung Wallet with Tap and Pay, Quick Share, Voice Focus and Samsung Knox.

Our earlier Galaxy A17 discovery looks spot on

Last week, we reported that the Galaxy F70 5G appears to be a rebadged Galaxy A17 5G. Now we’ve got considerably more to go on. The Exynos 1330 processor, 50MP OIS camera, 5MP ultrawide, 2MP macro, and 13MP selfie camera all match the latter. Even the 5,000mAh battery and 25W charging are identical.

That makes the connection between the two devices difficult to ignore. While Samsung hasn’t confirmed that the F70 is a rebranded A17, the hardware revealed so far certainly points in that direction.

Nonetheless, Flipkart has now confirmed that the Galaxy F70 5G will launch on 18 October, with pricing teased as “starting at ₹1X,XXX”. That places it below ₹20,000 (~$210), although Samsung has yet to reveal exact pricing and memory configurations.
