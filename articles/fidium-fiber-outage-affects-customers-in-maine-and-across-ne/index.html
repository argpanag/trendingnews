Fidium Fiber outage affects customers in Maine and across New England

TheTools · 10.09.2026 20:52 · source · permalink

A Fidium Fiber outage affected customers across New England, including in Maine.

Fidium Fiber announced shortly after 3:40 p.m. Thursday that it was experiencing technical issues and that its teams were working to restore services and systems as quickly as possible.

Shortly after 4:20 p.m., Fidium said that services were beginning to come back online for many customers.

Then shortly after 5:30 p.m., Fidium announced that service was back online for most customers, and that the company's website and phone support had been restored.

"Our teams are continuing to stabilize service for any remaining impacted customers. If you're still experiencing issues, please restart your network equipment. If that doesn’t resolve the issue, contact Customer Service for additional support," Fidium said in its most recent update on Facebook. "We know how disruptive an outage can be and appreciate your patience as we worked to get service back up and running."

The website Downdetector received thousands of reports of Fidium Fiber outages at about 3 p.m. Thursday. The Downdetector outage map showed that Fidium customers in Maine, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Vermont, Rhode Island and Connecticut were affected.

The Portland Conservatory of Music announced shortly before 3:30 p.m. that its internet and phone lines were down due to the Fidium Fiber outage.
