GTA VI Proves Too Demanding For Some PS5 Consoles, Even at 30 FPS

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:32 · source · permalink

Friday, November 20th 2026 GTA VI Proves Too Demanding For Some PS5 Consoles, Even at 30 FPS The PlayStation 5 has been plagued with issues caused by its liquid metal thermal interface material since the year it was launched, having prompted Sony to release revisions of the console with improved designs to mitigate issues with the liquid metal. However, with the launch of GTA VI, those liquid metal issues appear to be rearing their ugly heads once more. As spotted by X user @ghost_motley, several Reddit threads have been posted in the day after the launch of GTA VI about random system crashes while gamers are playing GTA VI. Gamers appear to be frequently encountering error CE-108255-1 while playing GTA VI, in which the console runs fine for a short period of time, then crashes to the home screen or crashes the system entirely and forces the PS5 to shut down or, in some cases, even to enter a boot loop. In some cases, it appears as though rebooting the console into safe mode and rebuilding the database helps, but there are also indications that these issues are being caused by liquid metal having run off the APU, resulting in dry spots and improper cooling contact and overheating consoles. Some gamers report that they are able to play certain games on their PS5 without issue, but other, more demanding titles also result in the same CE-108255-1 error, lending credence to the theory that these issues are being caused by an overheating APU. GTA VI being so demanding is supposedly the reason why thermal issues are showing up more frequently while gamers are playing it. It's already been confirmed that GTA VI is one of the most demanding, if not the most demanding, game to launch on the PlayStation 5 to date, with the game only running at 30 FPS on the PS5 Pro, so it makes sense that it would trigger more thermal issues than other games. Sources: r/PS5HelpSupport on Reddit, @ghost_motley on X, via Wccftech
