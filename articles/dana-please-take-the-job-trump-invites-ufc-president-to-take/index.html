"Dana, please take the job": Trump invites UFC president to take over as White House spokesperson

TheTools · 07.11.2026 20:47 · source · permalink

US President Donald Trump has invited six current or former UFC title holders to join Danny White, including two fighters from the Russian republic of Dagestan, Islam Makhachev and Khabib Nurmagomedov, Americans Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier and "Kyla Harris", presumably referring to Kayla Harrison, as well as Brazilian-born Amanda Nunes, who became a US citizen last year, Reuters reported.

US President Donald Trump has invited Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) president Dana White to take over as White House spokesman, in a post on social media, the British public service BBC reports.

"Dana will be a spokesperson like no other. Dana, please take the job," Trump wrote on his social media account Truth Social, the BBC reported in an article on its website.

The BBC has requested comment from the White House and TKO Holdings, which owns the UFC.

The position of White House press secretary has been vacant since mid-October, when Katie Zakaria left the position after only a few days on the job.

Trump's announcement followed a heavy defeat for his Republicans in the US congressional elections, in which they lost their majorities in both houses.

Reuters also reports that Trump announced today that he has invited mixed martial arts promoter Dana White to take over as White House spokesman, and that he has asked him to bring several fighters as aides, "even though some of them are not from our country."

White, the CEO of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), is "a fierce fighter and warrior," Trump wrote in a post on Twitter.

Trump invited six current or former UFC title holders to join White, including two fighters from the Russian republic of Dagestan, Islam Makhachev and Khabib Nurmagomedov, Americans Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier and "Kyla Harris," likely referring to Kayla Harrison, as well as Brazilian-born Amanda Nunes, who became a U.S. citizen last year, Reuters reported.

"We need these people to defend us from the crazed left," Trump wrote.

White and UFC parent company TKO Group Holdings did not immediately respond to requests for comment, according to Reuters.
