How to watch, stream, and listen | Eagles at Titans

TheTools · 20.09.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

See how to watch and listen to the Eagles’ Week 2 matchup against the Tennessee Titans on Sunday, September 20th at 1:00 PM.

The Philadelphia Eagles travel to Nashville, Tennessee to play the Titans at Nissan Stadium on Sunday, September 20th at 1:00 PM. Here is how to follow the action!

Television and streaming

For fans in the Philadelphia and Tennessee markets, the game will be broadcast on FOX. Adam Amin serves as the play-by-play announcer, Drew Brees as the color commentator, and Kristina Pink as the sideline reporter. The game will also air in other parts of the country, including the rival Dallas and Washington, D.C. markets. Click here to find out if the game is on in your specific area.

FOX can be streamed with a FOX One subscription or other live TV streaming services like Hulu.

In-market fans can stream the game on NFL+, while out-of-market viewers can watch via NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV.

Outside of the United States? DAZN broadcasts NFL games via NFL Game Pass International (except for China).

Philadelphia Eagles Mobile App

Stay connected with the Eagles anytime, anywhere with our official mobile app. Receive the latest news, watch live preseason games (in market), behind-the-scenes videos, and more.

Listen

This year is Merrill Reese's 50th season as the Voice of the Eagles! Tune into SportsRadio 94WIP with Reese and Mike Quick for the radio call of the game. The broadcast will be available everywhere on the desktop version of the Eagles website. Fans in the Philadelphia market can also listen on the Eagles app.

The Spanish radio call of the game will also be available everywhere on the desktop version of the Eagles website. Fans in Philadelphia can listen to the Tico Sports broadcast on the Eagles app. Rickie Ricardo serves as the play-by-play analyst, Oscar Budejen as the color commentator, along with commentator Dave Gerhardt. Eagles en Español can also be heard on La Mega 105.7 FM in Philadelphia, 93.9 FM in Atlantic City, and 103.3 FM in Vineland/Millville.

SiriusXM NFL Radio will also broadcast the game nationally on Channel 194.

The Titans' radio broadcast will be available on 104.5 The Zone in the team's local market as well as other stations part of the Titans Radio Network across the Mid-South. – Compiled by Caroline Connor
