News summary

  • Netflix released the trailer for Schumacher ’94 – The Birth of a Legend, focusing on Michael Schumacher’s breakthrough 1994 F1 season.
  • The documentary revisits the German driver’s first world championship and one of the most dramatic seasons in Formula 1 history.
  • The film will premiere worldwide on Netflix on October 2, with Schumacher’s wife Corinna and several prominent F1 figures featured.

Netflix has released the official trailer for Schumacher ’94 – The Birth of a Legend, a new documentary dedicated to Michael Schumacher’s first Formula 1 world championship in 1994.

The trailer was released Friday, September 4, and features the German band Scorpions on the soundtrack, adding a distinctly German touch to the production that chronicles the season that launched Schumacher toward an unprecedented F1 career.

The documentary will be released globally on October 2 and was produced by LEONINE Documentaries, with Christin Freitag as director. According to the film’s synopsis, it follows Schumacher from his victory in the season-opening Brazilian GP through the dramatic conclusion in Australia.

The 1994 season was a pivotal moment in Formula 1 history. Schumacher, then 25, won his first championship in a campaign marked by sporting controversy and tragedy, including the deaths of Ayrton Senna and Roland Ratzenberger at Imola.

The documentary also promises a more personal perspective on Schumacher. Corinna Schumacher, his wife, features prominently and discusses the period when their relationship was beginning. Former Benetton team principal Flavio Briatore, former F1 chief executive Bernie Ecclestone, Schumacher’s former manager Willi Weber and former F1 driver David Coulthard are also among those featured.

The production seeks to portray the contrast between Schumacher’s uncompromising approach on the racetrack and his personality away from competition. The film’s official synopsis describes 1994 as the beginning of the phenomenon that became known as “Schumi Mania”, as Schumacher rapidly became one of the biggest names in Formula 1.

Schumacher ultimately finished the 1994 season as world champion after a controversial finale in Adelaide against Damon Hill. The title was the first of 7 championships he would win during his F1 career.

The new documentary follows the previously released Schumacher, a 2021 Netflix production that examined the German driver’s career and personal life through interviews and archive footage.
