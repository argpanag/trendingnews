Bandgee Kallra defends Love Gill, says Jay Bhanushali was never mentioned

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:30 · source · permalink

The ongoing controversy between Love Gill and Mahi Vij in Bigg Boss 20 continues to spark debate, with television personalities sharing their opinions on the heated exchange. Joining the discussion, Bandgee Kallra has come out in support of Love Gill while questioning Mahi's emotional reaction and the language she allegedly used during the argument.

Reacting to the incident, Bandgee expressed her confusion over Mahi's outburst, pointing out that nobody had even mentioned Jay Bhanushali's name during the conversation. She questioned why Mahi was shouting and crying when, according to her, neither Jay nor her personal life had been brought into the discussion.

Bandgee also highlighted that Love Gill had not made any comments about Mahi's personal life, making her reaction seem unnecessary in the context of the argument. She questioned what had prompted Mahi to respond so strongly and why she appeared to be portraying herself as the victim.

Taking her criticism further, Bandgee raised concerns about the choice of words used during the confrontation. She questioned how Mahi could allegedly refer to other women as 'ghatiya auratein' while expecting respect from others.

Her reaction has added another perspective to the ongoing disagreement, particularly around the questions of personal boundaries, accountability and the language used during arguments inside the Bigg Boss house.

With Bandgee openly supporting Love Gill, the controversy has gained further attention among viewers. While the disagreement initially centred on the exchange between Love and Mahi, reactions from television personalities have now brought the focus to whether the argument was unnecessarily made personal.

It remains to be seen whether Mahi Vij will respond to Bandgee Kallra's criticism or address the growing discussion surrounding the controversy.
