  2. Scotland 'cohesive from the start' - Townsendpublished at 16:32 GMT
    16:32 GMT

    FT: Scotland 67-5 Japan

    Scotland head coach Gregor Townsend tells BBC Scotland: "Really pleased, they looked a cohesive team right from the start.

    "Japan were tough opposition, I know the scoreline doesn't suggest that, but it took a number of phases, really good focus at the breakdown, really smart decision-making to score in the first half.

    "I think the pressure told, the work we did in the first half not only got points on the board, but put pressure on Japan. Obviously we had an eye on that points total and getting to that number of points was an ambition and they delivered on that.

    "Darcy [Graham] had a few assists too. He isn't someone who just tries to play for himself. I think he's worked really hard on two aspects of his game, the defensive side and the high ball side. You can just tell something will happen around him and he was excellent today.

    "Support play, speed, good footwork, being confident when you have the ball. I thought the back row was excellent. Matt Fagerson went through a lot of work and hopefully his injury isn't serious. Ollie Leatherbarrow was great on both sides of the ball and Liam [McConnell] stands out for his tries.

    "We are watching it [Ireland v South Africa]. It's important for us, we don't know what our week's going to look like and it's good to have that doubt because if we hadn't put the points on the board we'd have known we'd be playing on Sunday."

  4. 'We kept our foot on the throat'published at 16:22 GMT
    16:22 GMT

    FT: Scotland 67-5 Japan

    Scotland captain Stafford McDowell tells BBC Scotland: "Yeah it was pretty good. We got off to a good start, which helped settle a few of the boys' nerves. We had a chat on the pitch before we went in at half-time saying that we didn't want to let the game fizzle out and I'm probably most proud of the fact that we kept our foot on the throat in the second half.

    "The boys that came off the bench did really well. We'll wait and see what happens with South Africa and Ireland and hopefully it's enough to bump us up the table.

    "I think it's a big step for this group. We are looking forward to the Six Nations and all those northern hemisphere teams are going to want to finish on a positive note."
