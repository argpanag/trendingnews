Loyalty doesn't pay off: Sony's Black Friday deals upset longtime PS Plus subscribers

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:49 · source · permalink

Gamers can save big on PlayStation Plus until November 30. Sony is offering 20% off the Essential tier, 25% off Extra and 33% off Premium. There's a catch, though: The deals are primarily aimed at users without an active subscription. Anyone who already has an active PS Plus membership can't simply renew it at the discounted price. Sony only offers existing subscribers a discount if they upgrade to a more expensive tier.

The promotion is currently causing plenty of frustration in the community. Many users criticize Sony for attracting new customers with appealing discounts while leaving longtime subscribers largely empty-handed unless they're willing to upgrade. Players find this particularly frustrating in light of the recent price increases in May 2026, especially since discounted Black Friday membership renewals were reportedly available in previous years.

Adding to the frustration is an unusual side effect of the PSN outages in early 2025. At the time, Sony compensated affected users with a few extra subscription days. For some, this pushed their membership expiration date into December – beyond the end of the current Black Friday promotion. As a result, some players are considering letting their subscriptions expire or switching to a cheaper tier. Others report receiving personalized renewal offers with discounts of 15% to 25% when attempting to cancel their memberships. However, there's no guarantee that everyone will receive such an offer.
