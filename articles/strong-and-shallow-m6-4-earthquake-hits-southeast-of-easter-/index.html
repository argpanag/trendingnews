Strong and shallow M6.4 earthquake hits southeast of Easter Island

TheTools · 29.10.2026 08:42 · source · permalink

A strong and shallow earthquake registered by the USGS as M6.4 hit southeast of Easter Island, Chile at 07:49 UTC on October 29, 2026. The agency is reporting a depth of 10 km (6.2 miles). EMSC is reporting M6.4 at a depth of 3 km (1.9 miles).

Epicenter of M6.4 earthquake southeast of Easter Island, Chile on October 29, 2026. Credit: TW/SAM, Google

The epicenter was located 1 483 km (921 miles) SE of Hanga Roa (population 3 304), 2 154 km (1 338 miles) W of Lebu (population 22 345), and 2 157 km (1 340 miles) WNW of Ancud (population 28 020).

There is no tsunami threat from this earthquake.

The USGS issued a Green alert for shaking-related fatalities and economic losses. There is a low likelihood of casualties and damage.
