Gerwyn Price and Nathan Aspinall Reach World Grand Prix Quarterfinals

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:09 · source · permalink

Gerwyn Price and Nathan Aspinall secured the first quarterfinal spots at the 2026 World Grand Prix in Leicester on Wednesday night following straight-set victories over Ross Smith and Luke Woodhouse.

Price recorded a 3–0 win over Smith, setting up an all-Welsh quarterfinal clash against Jonny Clayton. Clayton advanced to the next round after defeating Wessel Nijman 3–1 with checkouts of 112, 116, and 125 in his opening set.

Aspinall posted the highest average of the evening at 96.27 during his victory against Woodhouse, who had previously eliminated defending champion Luke Littler. Aspinall will meet Ryan Joyce in Friday's quarterfinal round.

Joyce secured his spot in the last eight by defeating Danny Noppert 3–0. The Englishman swept all three sets by 3–1 scores while finishing with an 86.04 average against Noppert's 85.99 average.

In the opening set of his match, Price survived four maximums from Smith, breaking throw with a double eight finish before winning the decider in 13 darts. Price then completed an 80 checkout and a 72 checkout in the final set to seal the win.

Clayton took control against Nijman by starting the match with three consecutive checkout conversions on the bullseye and double finishes. Despite Nijman taking the second set, Clayton broke throw in the third set with a 126 checkout and finished the match on double two.
