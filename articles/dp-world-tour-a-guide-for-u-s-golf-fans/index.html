DP World Tour: a guide for U.S. golf fans

TheTools · 11.09.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

The dp world tour’s 2026 calendar stretches across more than two dozen countries and culminates in the Race to Dubai finals, and U.S. fans can watch most events through a Golf Channel rights deal and the tour’s own streaming channels.

The DP World Tour published its 2026 schedule showing an expanded international swing and season-long Race to Dubai that finishes with the DP World Tour Championship in Dubai; the tour’s official site lists dates and venues for the year’s tournaments. (See the schedule for event dates and the Dubai finale.)

In the United States the Golf Channel holds a multi-year media-rights extension to present live DP World Tour coverage, a deal announced in January 2026 that commits broadcast rights through 2030 and includes hundreds of hours of live coverage each year.

Beyond traditional TV, the DP World Tour has launched a DP World Tour FAST channel and expanded streaming options; the tour’s own watch/how-to pages note that World Feed and FAST-channel coverage is available on platforms including Samsung TV Plus, Amazon Prime Video, Fubo and other FAST providers for selected windows.

What this means for U.S. viewers: expect weekend world-feed coverage across Golf Channel linear broadcasts and the tour’s FAST streams for featured groups and highlights, with full schedules and ticket information posted on the DP World Tour site. The tour’s published calendar and the broadcaster announcement together let fans plan which Rolex Series and signature events to follow live.

For business-minded U.S. readers tracking golf’s media landscape, the DP World Tour’s distribution strategy sits alongside ongoing rival developments in professional golf — including recent news around LIV Golf’s restructuring and player moves that have reshaped rights and competition in the market. See reporting on LIV Golf’s Chapter 11 filing and player commitments for context on the broader market.

The dp world tour remains distinct from the PGA Tour in its global footprint and Race to Dubai structure; the tour’s official pages explain the season-long ranking system that awards points at events and finishes with the season-ending DP World Tour Championship in Dubai.
