What a difference a day makes.

On Thursday, Oktagon fans were gearing up for an all-Czech main event at Oktagon 95, where Dominick Humburger was expected to take on Patrick Kincl. However, at Friday’s weigh-ins, Humburger missed weight, and an incensed Kincl declined to accept a catchweight bout.

That, after citing his younger daughter as one reason, and having had to explain to her during fight camp why daddy couldn’t share a meal with her, despite already being skinny.

With the main event off, light heavyweights Daniel Skvor and Tomasz Narkun found themselves promoted to the top of the card. Skvor had been forced out of a fight with 205lb champ Will Fleury earlier this year (he’d later lose that title to Makhmud Muradov, but still holds heavyweight gold). Narkun, of course, was for a time one of KSW’s most recognizable champions.

Where to Watch Oktagon MMA

Saturday, October 17, 2026 sees Oktagon 95 take place at Mattoni Arena in Karlovy Vary, Czech Republic. The card streams live via Oktagon.tv and DAZN for international viewers; full results can be found below. The action gets underway at 5pm BST/6pm CEST/12pm ET/9am PT.
