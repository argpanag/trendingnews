Pakistan A Choose to Bat Against India A in Rising Stars Clash

TheTools · 22.11.2026 09:54 · source · permalink

Pakistan A elected to bat first against India A after winning the toss in their Group B encounter at the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 on Sunday, November 22, at West End Park International Cricket Stadium. Led by Abdul Samad, Pakistan aim to extend their winning start against a confident Indian side captained by Tilak Varma.

Both teams entered the contest following convincing victories in their opening matches. India A defeated Nepal by 306 runs, while Pakistan A began their title defense with a 61-run win over the United Arab Emirates.

According to Times of India, Pakistan captain Abdul Samad considered the slightly dry pitch suitable for batting first and retained the same playing XI that defeated the UAE. Tilak Varma said India had also preferred to bat but was satisfied with the toss result. India likewise maintained its winning combination.

India A produced an outstanding performance against Nepal, scoring 332 for nine before dismissing their opponents for just 26 in 11.3 overs. Kumar Kushagra led the batting effort with 117 runs, while Tilak contributed 107.

Prince Yadav claimed five wickets, and Harshit Rana added four to complete India's dominant victory. Both fast bowlers had returned from injury and made significant contributions in their opening appearance.

India's batting lineup also includes Abhishek Sharma, 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Rinku Singh and Prabhsimran Singh. Their presence provides additional options alongside the established performers from the Nepal match.

Pakistan A also demonstrated strong batting and bowling against the UAE. The defending champions scored 245 runs before restricting their opponents to 184, securing a 61-run victory.

Shamyl Hussain scored 62, while Maaz Sadaqat contributed 53 to Pakistan's total. Left-arm spinner Sufyan Moqim and fast bowler Ali Raza each collected three wickets to lead the bowling attack.

Captain Abdul Samad also contributed 39 runs against the UAE. Shamyl Hussain, Maaz Sadaqat and fast bowler Mohammad Hasnain are among Pakistan's other important players for the tournament.

The encounter carries additional significance because Pakistan A defeated India A by 128 runs in the 2023 final of the 50-over edition. Pakistan enter the 2026 competition as defending champions and are seeking to retain their title.

The eight-team tournament features two groups, with the top two teams from each group advancing to the semifinals. India A are scheduled to face the United Arab Emirates in their final group-stage match on November 24.

India A Playing XI: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Abhishek Sharma, Ayush Badoni, Tilak Varma (captain), Kumar Kushagra (wicketkeeper), Rinku Singh, Suryansh Shedge, Shahbaz Ahmed, Vipraj Nigam, Harshit Rana and Prince Yadav.

Pakistan A Playing XI: Shamyl Hussain, Maaz Sadaqat, Rohail Nazir (wicketkeeper), Hasan Nawaz, Abdul Samad (captain), Farhan Yousaf, Saad Masood, Mohammad Imran Randhawa, Ahmed Daniyal, Sufyan Moqim and Ali Raza.
