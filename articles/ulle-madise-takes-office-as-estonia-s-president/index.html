Ulle Madise takes office as Estonia’s president

TheTools · 12.10.2026 16:44 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

Ulle Madise, Estonia’s former chancellor of justice, took office as president on Monday at a ceremony in parliament in Tallinn, Estonian public broadcaster ERR reported.

Madise took the oath of office in the Session Hall of the Riigikogu, Estonia’s parliament, after outgoing President Alar Karis delivered his final address as head of state.

Madise then delivered her first speech as president.

The 51-year-old lawyer was elected president in a secret ballot in parliament, receiving 71 votes.

Born and educated in Tartu, Madise has held several public-sector positions, including at the Justice Ministry, the Riigikogu’s Constitutional Committee and the National Audit Office.

She served as chancellor of justice from 2015 and was reappointed for a second term in late 2021.

Madise was nominated by 75 members of parliament representing the Reform Party, Eesti 200, Isamaa, the Social Democrats and unaffiliated lawmakers.
